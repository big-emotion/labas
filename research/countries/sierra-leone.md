## Country at a glance (for someone moving, not visiting)

- **Languages day to day**: Krio is the lingua franca, spoken by about 96% of the population;
  English is the official language (schools, administration, business) [6]. Mende dominates in the
  south-east (Bo, Kenema), Temne in the north (Makeni) and second in Freetown [5][7][8].
  **French is not used in daily life**: a French-only speaker will need English from day one.
- **Religion**: about 78.5% Muslim, 20.4% Christian (Pew 2020 estimate) [6]; Freetown is described as
  religiously tolerant, with Muslim and Christian festivals both part of city life [5].
- **Currency**: the new leone (SLE), introduced 1 July 2022 by redenomination at 1,000 old leones
  (SLL) = 1 SLE; old notes stopped being legal tender on 1 January 2024 [9]. Mid-market rate on
  14 Nov 2026: **1 EUR = 25.33 SLE** [10]. Inflation rose from 6.4% (Jan 2026) to **15.7% (Aug 2026)**
  after energy/food price shocks, per the World Bank [11] — budget for rising prices.
- **Economy**: real GDP growth 4.8% (2025), forecast 4.0% (2026) [11]. Mining (diamonds, rutile,
  iron ore) and agriculture dominate; the agriculture/mining shares on Wikipedia are from 2007 and
  outdated [6].
- **(a) French / EU passport holders**: a visa is required. For UK nationals the FCDO lists a visa
  on arrival (US$80 cash) or an e-visa at evisa.sl, single entry valid 90 days for stays of up to
  30 days, or multiple entry for 365 days; any purpose other than visit/business (i.e. work,
  residence) needs a visa from a Sierra Leonean mission before travel [2] (FCDO page last updated
  19 June 2026 [1]). The equivalent rules for French passports were not verified — France
  Diplomatie's entry section did not load [3]. A **yellow fever certificate** is mandatory [2].
  Since 22 Oct 2026 every traveller must register on the National Public Health Agency (NPHA)
  health portal and get a QR code before boarding to Lungi, after an Ebola (Bundibugyo) case in
  Nigeria [3]; the FCDO also requires a health declaration within 72 h of travel and notes
  enhanced Ebola screening [2]. Work permits, residence permits and their costs: **not found**.
- **(b) Diaspora with family roots**:
  - **Dual nationality** has been allowed since the Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2006 (No. 11);
    the same act also opened a route to repatriation [12].
  - **Descent rule**: citizenship by birth/descent rests on descent from a person "of Negro
    African descent" with a parent or grandparent born in Sierra Leone. Naturalisation needs
    8 years of residence for Black Africans versus 15 for others [12]. That is a racial criterion
    written into the law, worth saying plainly to readers.
  - **Mothers**: since the 2017 amendment, women can pass their nationality to children born
    abroad [12].
  - **DNA-based citizenship**: actor Isaiah Washington was granted Sierra Leonean citizenship
    after an African Ancestry DNA test traced his maternal line there. Wikipedia describes him as
    the first African American to receive full citizenship based on DNA, linked to a May 2006 trip [13].
    Group ceremonies followed: 22 African Americans who traced their roots through DNA were made
    citizens at State House in January 2021 [28], and 59 more on 29 April 2021 [29]. The
    published diaspora guidelines say applicants must go through a certified local tour operator
    and prove lineage with a DNA test (African Ancestry or a firm reporting maternal/paternal
    lines) [30]. Fees, current status of the programme and any ceremony after 2021: **not
    verified** (verification pass, 2026-11-14).

## Cities

### Freetown

Capital and by far the main city: 1,347,559 people at the 2024 census (1.5 million metro) [5].
It is split into the **East End** (largest and poorest), **Central** (downtown, business
district, many international companies) and the **West End** (more affluent: hotels, casinos,
Lumley Beach) [5].

- **Cost of living**: Numbeo (only 3 contributors, 25 entries in 12 months, updated 2 Oct 2026, low
  confidence) gives a 1-bed flat at **US$600–1,200/month in the centre (avg $928)** and
  **$450–900 outside (avg $745)**. A cheap meal costs about $2, a mid-range dinner for two $35+, and
  broadband ($55/month) for 60 Mbps+ [14]. Expat.com estimates **$800/month for a single person**
  and **$2,500 for a family of four** (undated, 2026 page) [15]. EUR/SLE conversions of these USD
  figures were not computed (no USD rate fetched). Expat neighbourhoods by name: West End
  (Aberdeen, Lumley, Hill Station) [5]. Wilberforce and Murray Town come up in security reporting
  [16]. Rents per neighbourhood were **not found**.
- **Work & money**: economic and financial centre; port at Queen Elizabeth II Quay; food
  processing, fish packing, rice milling, diamond cutting and light manufacturing [5]. Expat.com
  names Freetown and Bo as the places to look for work; it lists Lebanese, Nigerian, Chinese,
  Indian and British as the main expat nationalities (30,000–40,000 expats overall) [15]. Salaries,
  NGO/donor employment share and remote-work feasibility: **not found**.
- **Entrepreneurship & tech**: the government's Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation
  (DSTI, est. 2018) leads digital government and innovation policy [17]. Hubs and incubators
  (e.g. Sensi Hub): **not verified**. The domain sensi-sl.org now serves an unrelated gambling page,
  so don't link it [18]. Mobile money is the everyday payment layer: 8.2 million mobile money
  accounts at end 2023, of which 1.7 million active (central bank data), and since February 2025
  a national instant payment service links banks and mobile money wallets in real time [31].
  Orange Money and Afrimoney are the two main wallets (search snippet, unverified). Startup
  funding, crypto adoption and company registration (Corporate Affairs Commission site
  unreachable) [19]: **not found**.
- **Daily infrastructure**: national internet use is about 1.85 million users, around 20.8%, at end
  2025 (uncited figure on Wikipedia, low confidence). The ACE submarine cable lands in Freetown
  (2011); outside Freetown businesses mostly rely on VSAT [20]. Electricity access was about 12% in
  2016, mostly in Freetown. Bumbuna hydro gives about 50 MW (2012 figure) [6]. Current power cuts,
  water supply and ride-hailing apps: **not found**. The airport is at **Lungi, across the
  estuary**. You reach the city by ferry, water taxi, hovercraft, speedboat or helicopter; ferries
  are sometimes suspended for safety reasons and low tide can close piers [5]. The FCDO advises
  against taxis, minibuses, kekes and okadas because of robberies [1].
- **Health & schools**: Connaught Hospital is the historic reference public hospital [5]. The
  FCDO says healthcare is "well below UK standards", ambulances are unreliable, and insurance must
  cover medical evacuation [21]. Private clinics (e.g. Choithram, 34 Military Hospital):
  **not verified**. Universities: Fourah Bay College (1827, oldest in West Africa) [5].
  French/international schools: **not found**.
- **Safety**: FCDO: the main risk is pickpocketing and mugging. In "a small number of incidents"
  British nationals were robbed, sometimes at knifepoint, in **Congo Cross, Wilkinson Road,
  Lumley Beach and Aberdeen**, i.e. the expat West End itself.
  Long-stay residents should secure their homes or hire guards. Protests can turn violent [1].
  On **26 Nov 2023** armed men attacked the Wilberforce barracks armoury and Pademba Road prison
  (about 1,890 inmates freed). 21 people died and a curfew followed; treason convictions came in
  July–Aug 2024 [16]. **Kush** (synthetic cannabinoids/nitazenes) is a visible street crisis. In
  2024 it was reported to cause about a dozen deaths a week in Freetown, and it is linked to
  violence [22]. President Bio declared a national emergency on drugs, kush first, on
  **4 April 2024** [32].
- **Social life & vibe**: nightlife concentrates on the **Aberdeen–Lumley** beach strip (clubs,
  restaurants, hotels) [5]. City traditions: Lantern (Lantan) festival at the end of Ramadan,
  "Paddle" masquerades, Christmas street parades, the November Tangays concerts at the National
  Stadium. Football is huge: East End Lions vs Mighty Blackpool is the big derby [5]. Peninsula
  beaches within reach: River Number 2, Tokeh, Bureh, Laka [5].
- **Diaspora & expat community**: Freetown is twinned with Charleston (2019), Kansas City (1974),
  New Haven, Kingston upon Hull (1979) and Hefei (1984) [5]. Its
  Krio/Creole heritage comes from African American, Liberated African and Afro-Caribbean settlers
  (founded 1792) [5]. That is a strong symbolic pull for African-American returnees. The
  Expat.com Sierra Leone forum is active in 2026 (community, not facts) [15]. Named associations,
  Facebook/WhatsApp groups and returnee events: **not found**.
- **Climate in practice**: one of the wettest capitals in Africa: about 4,400 mm a year. The rainy
  season runs May–November, peaking July (about 1,190 mm) and August (about 1,078 mm).
  January–February are almost dry [5]. Heavy rain triggers floods and landslides: the 14 Aug 2017
  Sugar Loaf mudslide killed 300+ people [5]. The city appointed a "chief heat officer" in 2021 [5].
- **Watch out**: high rents for local incomes (Numbeo, thin data) [14]; robberies in expat areas
  [1]; weak healthcare [21]; airport across the water [5]; kush crisis [22]; inflation of 15.7% in
  Aug 2026 [11]; recurring health-entry controls (Ebola screening) [2][3]; low trust in the
  electoral commission (Afrobarometer, Dec 2025) [23].

### Bo

Second city after Freetown: 223,075 people (2021 mid-term census estimate). It lies about 250 km
east-south-east of Freetown and about 64 km from Kenema [7].

- **Cost of living**: **not found** (no Numbeo page reached). Expect lower rents than Freetown
  (assumption, unverified).
- **Work & money**: described as the main financial, educational and economic centre of the south,
  but Wikipedia gives no source for that [7]. Expat.com names Bo with Freetown as the places to
  find work [15]. Main employers: **not found**.
- **Entrepreneurship & tech**: **not found**.
- **Daily infrastructure**: Bo's airport is no longer in regular use; the railway closed in 1974
  [7]. Outside Freetown, internet often relies on VSAT [20]. Power, water and transport:
  **not found**.
- **Health & schools**: main Njala University campus; Bo School (founded 1906) is a well-known
  secondary school; Bo Teachers' College [7]. Hospitals: **not found**. The FCDO notes many
  facilities outside Freetown accept cash only [21].
- **Safety**: no Bo-specific advice found; national FCDO advice applies [1].
- **Social life & vibe**: Krio is the main daily language, Mende widely understood. Mosques
  predominate; Muslims and Christians are roughly equal in number [7]. A university and
  student town (inference from [7]). Nightlife: **not found**.
- **Diaspora & expat community**: **not found**.
- **Climate in practice**: national rainy season May–November [5]; Bo-specific data not fetched.
- **Watch out**: thin information online; 250 km from the capital and the only international
  airport, with no working local airport [7].

### Makeni (extra city: why it matters)

Northern Province's economic centre and Bombali District capital: 85,116 people (2021). Home of
the **University of Makeni**, the largest private university in the country [8]. Hometown of
ex-president Koroma and an APC stronghold. Holy Spirit Hospital is the largest private hospital
in the north [8]. Mostly Temne and Muslim with a large Christian minority [8]. Costs, safety and
infrastructure: **not found**. Of interest to returnees from the north and university staff.

### Kenema (extra city: why it matters)

Listed as **255,110 people (2021 census)**, which contradicts Bo's page calling Bo the second
city (sources disagree; flag) [7][24]. The commercial hub of the south-east: diamonds (Government
Diamond Office since 1959), timber, cocoa, coffee [24]. Kenema Government Hospital has run a Lassa
fever programme since the 1970s and confirmed the country's first Ebola case on 25 May 2014 [24].
Relevant for diaspora with Mende family roots, and for people working in mining, agriculture or
health research. Costs and safety: **not found**.

## Social atmosphere through a Big Five lens (interpretive, editorial)

These are editorial readings of cited facts, not measurements. No quiz answer measures a trait.
No academic regional personality study was found for Sierra Leone. Hofstede scores exist but
could not be loaded [25]. Afrobarometer (Round 9) finds 88% put national identity at least on
par with ethnic identity [23].

**Freetown**
- Openness: suits people curious about history and identity. The city was founded in 1792 by
  freed and returning Africans and has a layered Krio culture and festivals [5].
- Conscientiousness: rewards planners. Getting to the airport means a water crossing that can be
  suspended [5], healthcare often means cash and evacuation insurance [21], and inflation is rising
  [11].
- Extraversion: nightlife sits in a compact Aberdeen–Lumley strip. Street festivals (Lantan,
  Christmas parades) and football derbies are public, crowd events [5].
- Agreeableness: Muslim and Christian life coexist openly [5][6], which suits people comfortable
  in a mixed-faith, community-oriented setting.
- Emotional stability (stress tolerance): matters. Robberies occur in expat areas [1], the Nov
  2023 attacks brought a curfew [16], kush is visible in the streets [22], and the rainy season is
  extreme [5].

**Bo**
- Openness / Extraversion: a university and school town with a strong Mende identity and less of
  a nightlife scene (inference from [7], low confidence).
- Conscientiousness / Emotional stability: distance from the only airport and cash-based health
  services [7][21] favour self-reliant, patient people.
- Agreeableness: Muslim and Christian residents are roughly equal in number [7].

## Legal & sensitive context (each with a dated source)

- **LGBT**: sex between men is illegal under section 61 of the Offences against the Person Act
  1861, with a maximum penalty of life imprisonment. Sex between women is not criminalised. The law
  is "seldom enforced", but harassment and detentions by police were reported (US State Dept
  2012, dated) [26]. The FCDO (live page, Nov 2026) confirms male same-sex relations are illegal
  and urges discretion [1]. The 2023 Employment Act bans job discrimination on sexual orientation
  [26]. Recent Afrobarometer tolerance figures: **not found**.
- **Treatment of foreigners/diaspora**: the citizenship law reserves citizenship by descent for
  people "of Negro African descent", and naturalisation takes 8 years for Black Africans versus 15
  for others [12]. That favours Black diaspora returnees and disadvantages non-Black residents,
  including long-settled Lebanese families. Afrobarometer (15 May 2026): Sierra Leoneans support
  regional mobility but resist immigration [23]. Colourism reports: **not found**.
- **Conflict / political violence**: the civil war ended in 2002 [6]. Protests in 2022 left 33 dead
  [6]. On 26 Nov 2023 there were armed attacks on barracks and prisons in Freetown, with 21 dead,
  a curfew and treason convictions in 2024 [16]. The FCDO says protests can turn violent [1].
- **Women's daily experience**: the Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act (signed Jan 2023)
  sets a 30% quota for women in public and private positions, 14 weeks' maternity leave and equal
  pay [27]. FGM affected roughly 9 in 10 women (UNICEF 2014, dated), except among Krio/Creole
  women [27]. Afrobarometer (29 Oct 2025): despite progressive gender laws, citizens want stronger
  protection for women and girls [23]. The 18 minimum marriage age is set by the customary
  marriage act, date not given [27].
- **Health security**: Ebola screening and mandatory NPHA registration since 22 Oct 2026 [2][3].
  Kush synthetic-drug crisis; national emergency declared 4 April 2024 [22][32].
