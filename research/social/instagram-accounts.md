## What this says

- The French-speaking *return* scene on Instagram is small and business-led (Repat Africa,
  DEMAY): investing, land and networking dinners rather than "how daily life feels".
- The everyday-life voice is stronger in English and in Ghana (food, Accra life, Year of
  Return-style travel), which matches the dossiers.
- No account found that compares cities for someone moving. That is the gap Là-bas fills.
