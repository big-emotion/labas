## Priority countries — direct search, then verified

The user asked for proportional coverage of the four largest African economies (Nigeria,
Egypt, South Africa, Morocco) and of Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Ghana, Benin and
Gabon. Method: Instagram's own search box with several queries per country ("living in X",
"X guide", "things to do in X", "X expat", "vivre au/à X", "relocate to X", "X bons plans"),
then every candidate profile opened to check size, activity and bio. Follower counts as shown
on 2026-11-14. **Dropped after checking**: accounts with no posts or a handful of followers
(e.g. cairoexpats, livinginnairobi, livinginaccra, expatsinethiopia, thingstodoindar,
diaspora_guide_addis_ababa_), shops that only borrow the words "living" or "lifestyle", and
off-topic name clashes (Lagos Portugal, Dar = Arabic "house").

Legend: ★ = strongest lead for Là-bas (daily life, written for newcomers or returnees).

### Building and housing for the diaspora (shared by the user)

- ★ **christelle_lebrun_arch** — Christelle Lebrun, French-qualified architect (architecte DE),
  36.8K, France-based with Benin and Senegal roots: *"Construire au pays sans regrets"*,
  100+ diaspora clients, feasibility studies and design. Post of 2026-10-28
  ([DfDQhnmDFcr](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfDQhnmDFcr/), 1.2K likes, 168 comments):
  *"la grande maison familiale au pays est le pire placement que la diaspora continue de
  faire"*. She describes houses extended with every remittance and never finished, parents
  ageing alone in them, and children paying the guard and repairs from 5,000 km away. Her
  advice is a rental building rather than the family house. Commercial (free masterclass leading
  to paid services), but she names a real return-project failure pattern. **For Là-bas:** a
  quiz on *moving* should not push "build a house" as the default return path; renting first
  is a recurring piece of advice from people who work with returnees.

## What this says

- The French-speaking *return* scene on Instagram is small and business-led (Repat Africa,
  DEMAY): investing, land and networking dinners rather than "how daily life feels".
- The everyday-life voice is stronger in English and in Ghana (food, Accra life, Year of
  Return-style travel), which matches the dossiers.
- No account found that compares cities for someone moving. That is the gap Là-bas fills.
