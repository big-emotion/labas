## What the map of groups says

- **Strongest expat/returnee networks (≥ 50K members across groups, daily activity):** Dakar,
  Abidjan, Casablanca, Tunis, Cairo, Kigali, Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Madagascar, Mauritius.
  These are the cities where a newcomer can get housing and admin answers within a day.
- **Marketplace-first cities:** in Brazzaville, Ouagadougou, Windhoek, Lilongwe, Harare and
  Eswatini, the busiest groups are buy/sell and rentals rather than expat clubs — newcomers
  integrate through local networks, not an expat bubble.
- **Thin or absent:** Sierra Leone, Chad, Eritrea, Mauritania (one private review group),
  Mogadishu, São Tomé (local only), Cabo Verde (tourism only). This is a useful
  `diaspora-network` signal for scoring and matches the low scores proposed in the dossiers.
- **Diaspora (country-of-origin) groups** are huge for Nigeria, Senegal, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire,
  Benin and Kenya, but almost none are about *returning*: the explicit returnee groups are
  small (Cameroon 4.3K, Ghana "repatriated from America" 236, Benin Afro-descendants 126).
  A "return" community is an unmet need — and a possible audience for Là-bas.
