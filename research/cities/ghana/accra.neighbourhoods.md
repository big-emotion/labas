# Accra: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 14 GHS** and **1 USD ≈ 12 GHS** (dossier, retail, Sept 2026; the cedi moved about 10 % in 2026, recheck before quoting). USD figures are converted with those two rates. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/ghana/accra.md`; `[D]` = the Ghana dossier `research/countries/ghana.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Rent is asked months to years upfront: the law caps advance rent at six months for tenancies over six months, but portals estimate Accra tenants pay about 1.93 years upfront on average, and 12–24 months is described as routine in East Legon, Cantonments and Airport Residential; the rent commissioner announced enforcement of the cap from 1 April 2026 [C cost.rent]. Agency fees are typically 10 % of the annual rent, plus a 1–3-month deposit (Ghana-wide) [C cost.rent].
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15): bag snatching and pickpocketing are relatively frequent in Osu, Cantonments, Ridge, Airport Residential and East Legon; armed attacks on cars and trotros happen mostly after dark on main roads, and night driving is strongly discouraged [C safety.general].
- Power: scheduled outages without a published timetable; ask whether a building's generator runs the air conditioning and who pays the diesel [C daily.power]. Water: eastern Accra (Spintex, Teshie, Nungua, Sakumono, La and others) stayed on a rationing programme in Aug 2026 [C daily.water].
- LGBT readers: same-sex relations are criminal and a harsher bill passed Parliament on 29 May 2026 (not law on 3 Nov 2026) [C rights.lgbt]. This applies to every neighbourhood below.

## Neighbourhoods

### East Legon
- Feel: part of the "expatriate ring" with Cantonments and Airport Residential, cheaper than both [C housing.areas]; recent developments, international schools, malls, restaurants and nightlife, suited to families and young professionals (Expat.com, 2025-11-27) [14]; expat families and the Ghanaian upper middle class [15]; gated compounds typically with backup generators and water tanks [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 1-bed GHS 5,000–14,000/month (≈ €360–1,000), 3-bed GHS 15,000–35,000 (≈ €1,070–2,500) (Rio Times, 2026-09-14) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com gives a 3-bed house at $1,500–3,000 and a 1-bed at $800–1,500 (≈ €1,285–2,570 and €685–1,285) [14]. meQasa lists 1,287 Accra rental apartments here, the largest cluster in the city [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 11 minutes' drive from Kotoka airport [C housing.areas][2]; the Flower Pot interchange was built to ease traffic into East Legon [2]. A 20-minute ride-hail costs about $3–6 (≈ €2.60–5.20); rush-hour commutes can take 60–90 minutes [C daily.transport]. No sourced time to the Ridge/central business district.
- Watch: snatch theft listed for East Legon (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [C safety.general]; FCDO flags vehicle crime at the Accra Mall roundabout and on the GIMPA road (undated page, accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]. Landlords here routinely ask 12–24 months upfront and agents add "goodwill payments" [C cost.rent; cost.negotiation].
- Places:
  - Lycée Français International d'Accra (AEFE French school, primary and secondary, 6 Bathur Street, East Legon; first registration €3,500, re-registration €2,100) — school website [23]; fees [C family.schools]
  - American International School (international school, East Legon; $10,000–16,000/year) — Expat.com [14]
  - A&C Shopping Mall (mall, with a Woodin fabric outlet) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Nubuke Foundation, 7 Lome Close (arts centre) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - University of Ghana, Legon (university; visible from East Legon) — Wikipedia [2], Wikivoyage [1]
  - Lincoln Community School, North Legon (IB school, pre-K to grade 12, founded 1968) — Expat.com [14], Wikipedia [17]
  - East Legon rooftop bars (nightlife, no venue named) — Expat.com [C social.nightlife]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (the French lycée and two international schools are in or next to it); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "La santé et l'école"; Q6 "Français" (the only AEFE school in Ghana [D]); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Cantonments
- Feel: an affluent planned suburb, first a colonial military quarter, of mostly 3–4-bedroom homes for wealthy households, academics and officials; many diplomatic missions [4]; tree-lined, peaceful and residential, for diplomats and senior managers (Expat.com) [14].
- Rent: 1-bed GHS 8,000–20,000/month (≈ €570–1,430), 3-bed GHS 30,000–80,000 (≈ €2,140–5,710) (Rio Times) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com: 1-bed $1,200–2,000 (≈ €1,030–1,715), 3-bed $2,500–5,000 [14]; meQasa: 804 listings [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the Flower Pot interchange also serves Cantonments [4]; traffic builds around the embassies (Rio Times) [9]. No sourced commute time.
- Watch: FCDO says burglary in Airport Residential, Cantonments and Ridge peaks at Christmas [C safety.general]; snatch theft listed for Cantonments (France Diplomatie) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Ghana International School (Cambridge-model international school) — Wikipedia [4]; Expat.com [14]
  - Police Hospital and Cantonments health centre & clinic (hospitals) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Ghana Police Mosque, 2 Cantonments Road (mosque) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - W.E.B. DuBois Memorial Centre, 22 First Circular Road (museum; GH₵20 per foreign adult, listing date not shown) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - US Embassy, 24 Fourth Circular Road (embassy) — Wikivoyage [1], Wikipedia [4]
  - St. Thomas Aquinas Senior High School and Christ the King International School (schools) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (Ghana International School); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" within the city.

### Airport Residential Area
- Feel: quiet, green and well-secured, with luxury villas for the expatriate and diplomatic community; embassies, head offices, upmarket restaurants and malls (Expat.com) [14][15]. The dearest part of the "expatriate ring" [C housing.areas].
- Rent: a 3-bed can pass GHS 90,000/month (≈ €6,430) (Rio Times) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com: studio or 1-bed $1,000–1,800 (≈ €860–1,545), 3-bed $2,000–4,000 [14]; meQasa: 652 "Airport Area" listings [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 5 minutes from Kotoka airport [14]. No sourced commute to Ridge.
- Watch: Christmas burglary peak (FCDO) and snatch theft (France Diplomatie) as for Cantonments [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Kotoka International Airport (airport; official taxi stand fares are regulated) — Wikivoyage [1]; [C cost.negotiation]
  - Lister Hospital, Airport Hills, and Euracare, Labone/airport area (private hospitals) — dossier [D]
  - Nyaho Medical Centre (private clinic, on the FCDO list; exact district not given) — [C health.care]
  - Marina Mall (mall; district not given in the source) — Expat.com [14]
  - Best Western Premier Accra Airport, White Avenue, and African Regent Hotel, Airport West (hotels, from $250 and $140) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Voyager facilement" (five minutes to the airport); Q5 "Salarié·e" for embassy and head-office jobs.

### Osu
- Feel: central Accra's "West End", about 3 km east of the business district, mixing early-20th-century houses with office towers [3]; the entertainment heart of the city, with crowds and late-night noise; suits younger professionals (Rio Times, 2026-10-18) [C housing.areas][9]; Oxford Street is the main nightlife hub, venues fill after 22:00 and close at 3–4 a.m. at weekends [C social.nightlife].
- Rent: flats GHS 4,000–12,000/month (≈ €290–860, bedroom count not stated) (Rio Times) [C cost.rent]; WhereNext gives Osu/Cantonments 1-beds at $500–900 (≈ €430–770) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com gives Labone/Osu $1,200–3,500 [15]. meQasa's indicative 3-bed house sale price: GHS 6.99 million (≈ €499,000) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: central taxi trips GHS 7–15 (≈ €0.50–1.10, before the Sept 2026 fare rise) [C daily.transport]; street taxis have no meter, negotiate first; about $3 within central Accra and about $10 to the airport or Accra Mall (Wikivoyage, undated) [1]. Ring Road separates Osu from Labone [3].
- Watch: snatch theft listed for Osu (France Diplomatie) [C safety.general]; petty theft after dark, use a ride-hailing app at night (Rio Times) [9].
- Places:
  - Oxford Street (bars, restaurants, clubs) — Wikivoyage [1]; Expat.com [C social.nightlife]
  - Frankie's, Oxford Street (restaurant, bakery, hotel from $95) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Tatale Vegan Restaurant, 18th Lane (restaurant) and Asanka Locals (Ghanaian restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Republic Bar & Grill (bar, cocktails with local ingredients) and Carbon Nightclub (club) — Expat.com [14]
  - Ebenezer Presbyterian Church, Osu (church) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Greater Accra Regional Hospital, "Ridge Hospital" (public hospital) — Wikipedia [3]; Expat.com [15]
  - Accra Sports Stadium (football, ~40,000 seats, home of Hearts of Oak) — Wikipedia [3][24]
  - Woodin (fabric store, Osu outlet) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Labone
- Feel: largely residential, with some embassies and head offices, between Cantonments Road, Labadi Road and Ring Road [5]; once quiet and family-oriented, bungalows now giving way to apartment blocks, and one of Accra's buzziest areas for food and nightlife (Time Out, 2025-09-23) [10]; ranked 10th in Time Out's 2025 world "coolest neighbourhoods" [5][11]; the quieter neighbour of Osu (Rio Times) [C housing.areas].
- Rent: Labone/Ridge 1-bed $450–800 (≈ €385–690) (WhereNext, 2026-05-20) [C cost.rent]; Labone 1-bed $600–1,200, 3-bed $1,200–2,500 (Expat.com, presented as cheaper than Cantonments) [C cost.rent][14].
- Getting around: easy access to Labadi Beach (Rio Times) [C nature.access]; ride-hail or negotiated taxis as for Osu [C daily.transport].
- Watch: crowds, late-night noise and petty theft after dark are listed for Osu and Labone together (Rio Times, 2026-10-18) [C housing.areas]. La, on its eastern side, was on the Jan 2026 water-rationing schedule [C daily.water] (whether Labone itself was included is not stated).
- Places:
  - Labone Coffee Shop (café, listed as a Labone landmark) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Vine and Brasa (restaurants, local cuisine) and Abbysinia (Ethiopian restaurant) — Time Out [10][11]
  - Zen Garden (live indigenous bands) and Honeysuckle Pub (pub) — Time Out [10][11]
  - Twist (nightclub) — Time Out [10]
  - Vidya Bookstore (bookshop), Berj Gallery (gallery) and Limbo Museum (design museum) — Time Out [10]
  - Maquis Tante Marie, near Metro TV (Ghanaian restaurant, garden) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Labone Senior High School (school) — Wikipedia [5]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (cafés, quieter streets, close to Osu); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" in a gentler key; Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage" (Labadi).

### Spintex
- Feel: a 14 km road and commercial corridor from Tetteh Quarshie to the Tema Motorway roundabout, mixing offices, shops, banks and light industry with middle- and upper-income homes, many in gated estates; associated with young upwardly mobile professionals [7]. Outside the "expatriate ring" [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 1-bed GHS 3,000–8,000/month (≈ €210–570) (Rio Times) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the Flower Pot interchange (2024) is a grade-separated junction on the road [7]; ride-hail or trotro, rush hours 6–9 h and 16–19 h [C daily.transport].
- Watch: on the Greater Accra water-rationing schedule from 8 Jan 2026, still in force in Aug 2026 after pipeline repairs (MyJoyOnline, GNA) [C daily.water]; FCDO flags vehicle crime at the Accra Mall roundabout and on the Teshie-Nungua road [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Accra Mall, near Tetteh Quarshie (mall with Shoprite and Game, multiplex cinema, food court; opened 2008) — Wikipedia [16]
  - Palace Mall (mall) and China Mall Accra (discount retail centre) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Action Chapel International (church, one of Ghana's largest congregations) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Regimanuel Estates, Manet Court, Baatsona Spintex Estates (gated estates) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Detty Rave's 2025 edition was held in Spintex (Untamed Empire) — [C social.nightlife][D]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen"; Q4 "En couple" or "Avec mes enfants" with a car (estates, malls); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi". Not for readers who want to walk to bars.

### Madina
- Feel: a large, mixed suburb next to the University of Ghana, population 137,162 in 2012; Muslims and Christians described as living together peacefully [6]; local, non-expat rents [D].
- Rent: GHS 600–3,000/month (≈ €40–210, unit size not stated) (Rio Times) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: reachable by trotro (the University of Ghana is reached by trotro to Madina, Wikivoyage) [1]; minimum trotro fare GHS 5.50 since 26 Sept 2026 [C daily.transport]. No sourced commute time to the centre.
- Watch: no Madina-specific flood, water or crime item found. The 18 May 2025 downpour flooded Adenta among other areas (MyJoyOnline) [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Madina Market, Old Road (one of Accra's biggest markets, 8:00–20:00) — Wikipedia [6]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Madina Polyclinic (public clinic) — Wikipedia [6]
  - University of Ghana, Legon (university) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Accra College of Education and Presbyterian Boys' Secondary School (schools) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Coco Vanilla, East Legon–Trasacco Estate Road (restaurant, listed under Madina) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (the lowest rents in the city file); Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (university and colleges next door); readers who want to live among Ghanaian families rather than in the expat ring. Not Q7 "Prend son temps": it is a busy market town.

Returnee note: Villa Diaspora, a co-living compound in Kwabenya (opened 2017), hosts returnees for 3–9 months and helps with schools, land and clubs (Al Jazeera, 2025-09-26) [C community.diaspora]. Its distance from Madina or East Legon was not sourced.

  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Twist, Labone [10], or Carbon Nightclub, Osu [14]; venues close around 3–4 a.m. [C social.nightlife]. Go home by ride-hail [9].

  - School: Lycée Français $7,000–13,000/year × 2 children × 12 ÷ 52 = 3,231–6,000 (Expat.com, undated; the LFIA's own tuition table could not be extracted) [C family.schools]
  - Groceries: two-person $250–400 doubled = 1,386–2,218 (assumption: doubled for four) [C cost.living]
  - Electricity, large family house: $200–400/month × 12 ÷ 4.33 = 554–1,109 (Ghana-wide) [C daily.power]
  - Broadband 115; two mobiles 92; one GP visit 100–300; 10 ride-hail school/shop trips × $3–6 = 360–720; one family dinner 650 [C daily.internet; health.care; daily.transport; cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ GHS 9,950–19,290 a week (≈ €711–1,378)**, of which school ≈ GHS 3,230–6,000. One-off LFIA first registration €3,500 per child [C family.schools].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Osu or Labone, central and walkable by day [3][5]. Wikivoyage listings (dates vary): Frankies, Oxford Street, from $95 a night; Christianborg Hotel, Osu; Dot's Inn, Labone; Somewhere Nice, Kokomlemle, GHS 162 per bed [1]. Avoid starting in December: residents report price rises, crowding and traffic during "December in Ghana" [C social.nightlife].
- Day 1: land at Kotoka; take the official taxi stand (regulated fare) rather than touts [1][C cost.negotiation]. A visa is needed in advance for EU passports; free visas for Africans were announced from 25 May 2026, implementation unconfirmed [D].
- Day 2: SIM and mobile money. MTN MoMo handles most daily payments [C daily.money]; a 10 GB mobile plan costs GHS 225 [C daily.internet]. SIM registration rules for foreigners: not sourced. Cash in cedis is the most reliable way to pay ride-hail drivers [C daily.transport]; France Diplomatie reports card fraud and suggests cash or virtual cards [C daily.money].
- Day 3: flat hunting. Listings: meQasa (asking rents GHS 5,000–120,000 in the expat areas) [C cost.rent]; apartments.com.gh markets monthly payments to avoid 1–2 years upfront (commercial claim) [C housing.search]. Negotiate the advance: the six-month cap is law and enforced since April 2026 [C cost.rent; cost.negotiation]. Inspection or viewing fees: no Accra source found.
- Day 4: test the commute you would really do at 7–8 a.m. (trotro minimum GHS 5.50; some operators charged 20–30 % above approved fares in Aug 2026) [C daily.transport].
- Day 5: check power and water in the flat you like: generator for the air conditioning, water tank, whether the area is on GWL's rationing schedule [C daily.power; daily.water].
- Day 6: bank account. Stanbic, Ecobank and Fidelity are described as foreigner-friendly, 1–5 days to open (blog, low confidence) [C daily.money]. Staying 90+ days a year requires a non-citizen Ghana Card ($120) [C admin.residency].
- Day 7: Sunday service in Osu or a Labadi morning [3][25].
- Rough first-week spend (GHS): lodging 7 nights × GHS 162 (dorm bed) to 7 × $95 = 1,134–7,980 [1] + 10 GB data 225 [C daily.internet] + 14 local meals × 20–40 = 280–560 [C cost.living] + 10 ride-hail trips × $3–6 = 360–720 + 6 trotro hops × 5.50 = 33 [C daily.transport] → **≈ GHS 2,030–9,520 (≈ €145–680)**.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Heritage prices for foreigners are separate and higher than local ones [C cost.negotiation]. Agree a taxi fare before getting in, or use Uber, Bolt, Yango or inDrive [C cost.negotiation; daily.transport].

### One day
- Morning: Makola Market (produce, fabric, school supplies, dominated by women traders) [27][1], then Jamestown: lighthouse, Ussher Fort (GH₵40 for a foreign adult with guided tour) and Brazil House, the ancestral home of the Tabom returnees from Brazil [12][1][C community.diaspora]. A local guide is recommended in Jamestown [1]; France Diplomatie advises against walking there at night [C safety.general].
- Midday: lunch at Osikan, the shoreline bar in Jamestown (jollof with red fish) or Jamestown Café [12].
- Afternoon: short ride-hail to the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park (mausoleum and museum, reopened July 2023) [19] and Black Star Square [1]; or the National Museum, Barnes Road (09:00–16:30, GH₵80 foreign adult) [1].
- Evening: dinner in Labone (Vine, Brasa or Abbysinia) [10], then Oxford Street, Osu [1]. Back by ride-hail [9].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Labone and Osu by day: Labone Coffee Shop, Berj Gallery, Limbo Museum, Vidya Bookstore [10]; W.E.B. DuBois Memorial Centre, Cantonments (GH₵20 foreign adult) [1]; sunset at Labadi Beach [25].
- Day 3: Shai Hills Resource Reserve, 57 km out on the Tema-Akosombo road (antelopes, baboons, zebras, birds; first hour 40 GHS per Wikivoyage, undated) [26][1]. Hire a car and driver (about $75 a 10-hour day, fuel extra, Wikivoyage) [1].
- Day 4: daily-life morning: ride a trotro at rush hour (7–9 h) to Madina Market, one of Accra's biggest [6][C daily.transport]; afternoon at the University of Ghana, Legon [1]; evening at the Nubuke Foundation, East Legon [1].
- Days 5–6: Cape Coast overnight. Ford vans from the Kaneshie Bakano station, 100 cedis one-way (2025), 2 h 15 to 4 h depending on traffic [22]. Cape Coast Castle, UNESCO World Heritage site, dungeons and governors' quarters, hourly guided tours, GH¢80 for an adult foreigner [20][22]; next day Kakum National Park, 33 km north, canopy walkway seven bridges about 40 m up (fee not found) [21].
- Day 7: Sunday: service at Ebenezer Presbyterian, Osu, or Holy Spirit Cathedral [3][1]; if Hearts of Oak play at home, the Accra Sports Stadium (fixture list not sourced) [24]. Friday alternative: prayers at the Ghana National Mosque, Kanda (free) [1].
- Seasonal: Chale Wote street-art festival (Jamestown) and Homowo in August; the December season brings parties and 125,000+ visitors but also price rises and traffic [C social.culture; social.nightlife].
