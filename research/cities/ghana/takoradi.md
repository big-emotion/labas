## Items

- [cost.rent] 3–4-bed villas in European Town or New Takoradi: about $1,200–3,000/month (≈ €1,030–2,570); undated. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, updated 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Modern apartments: about $700–1,800/month (≈ €600–1,545); undated; guide says rents are lower than Accra's. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives 1-bed rents of $350–600/month (≈ €300–515). — WhereNext "Moving to Ghana 2026", 2026-05-20, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Single-person monthly total $550–950 (≈ €470–815), lower than Accra (no percentage). — WhereNext, 2026-05-20, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] National minimum wage GHS 21.77/day from 1 Jan 2026, ≈ GHS 588/month (≈ €42) (national). — Les Afriques, 2026-06-08, intl-media, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] Les Afriques gives an unsourced offshore oil-and-gas range of GHS 8,000–25,000/month (≈ €570–1,790), the highest sector band (national, not Takoradi-specific). — Les Afriques, 2026-06-08, intl-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Takoradi is described as the centre of Ghana's oil and gas industry; expat jobs are mainly in hydrocarbons, engineering and maritime logistics. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] A relocation blog notes a growing oil and gas sector with an expat community linked to it. — WhereNext, 2026-05-20, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Uber and Bolt operate in Sekondi-Takoradi; Yango is not listed there. — Bento travel blog, 2026-10-07 (data checked Aug 2026), blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] National fares rose 8% on 26 Sept 2026: minimum trotro fare GHS 5.50 (≈ €0.39), inter-city minimum GHS 27 (≈ €1.93) (national). — MyJoyOnline, 2026-09-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] The Bonsa headworks, supplying 75% of Tarkwa-Nsuaem's water (Western Region hinterland, not Takoradi itself), shut in Jan 2025 because galamsey mud made the river untreatable. — Citi Newsroom, 2025-01-03, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Western Region treatment plants were among those repeatedly shut by mining pollution in 2025, no plant named. — The Fourth Estate, 2026-05, local-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] FCDO lists two Takoradi facilities: the Ghana Ports and Harbour Authority Hospital (public, all specialisations) and WARA Takoradi (private clinic with paramedic and ambulance service). — UK FCDO medical facilities list, updated 2026-07-13, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Effia Nkwanta Regional Hospital is the main public hospital, with a limited set of private clinics. — The Rio Times healthcare guide, 2026-09-18, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Takoradi International School and Blessed Minds International School offer international curricula at about $8,000–15,000/year (≈ €6,900–12,900); undated. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie names Takoradi among major cities with thefts, vehicle hijackings, bag snatching and burglaries, with tourist and expat areas targeted. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] FCDO cites reports of criminally motivated kidnappings in Takoradi, including of foreign nationals. — UK FCDO, undated (accessed 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [community.expat] The expat community is close-knit and mostly tied to the oil sector, with regular club and association events. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Coastal climate with sea breezes, described as pleasant. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Quieter, less crowded beaches than Accra; within reach of Kakum National Park and the forts of Elmina and Cape Coast. — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] FCDO: southern-coast beaches have dangerous rip tides and undertows with occasional drownings; don't visit beaches alone. — UK FCDO, undated (accessed 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex relations are criminal under s.104; the Family Values Bill passed 29 May 2026 was not law on 3 Nov 2026 (national). — The Rio Times, 2026-11-03, intl-media, confidence: high
- [other] Several restaurants, supermarkets and shopping centres have opened in recent years (undated). — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
- [other] Sekondi-Takoradi, capital of the Western Region, has about 800,000 inhabitants (undated). — Expat.com Takoradi guide, 2025-12-12, blog, confidence: low
