# Kumasi: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 14 GHS** and **1 USD ≈ 12 GHS** (dossier, Sept 2026; The Africanvestor's own EUR conversions match 14 GHS/EUR). Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/ghana/kumasi.md`; `[CA tag]` = an item in `research/cities/ghana/accra.md` (used only when national); `[D]` = the Ghana dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Most rent figures come from one source, The Africanvestor (updated 2026-09-21, data June 2026), built from portal listings and trimmed because "many Kumasi listings are negotiable" [C cost.rent; cost.negotiation]. meQasa's 26 listings average GHS 6,188/month, far above it [C cost.rent]. Expat.com says two years upfront is the usual practice, plus a 10 % agency fee and a 1–3-month deposit [C cost.rent]; The Africanvestor warns against informal cash advances without receipts [C cost.rent].
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15) lists Kumasi among cities with thefts, vehicle hijackings, bag snatching and burglaries, with tourist and expat areas targeted, no neighbourhood named; FCDO cites criminal kidnappings in Kumasi, including of foreigners [C safety.general]. No neighbourhood-level crime data exists in the files.
- Health: Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital is the reference hospital; emergency care is limited, so insurance should cover evacuation to Accra or abroad [C health.care].
- LGBT readers: same-sex relations are criminal and a harsher bill passed Parliament on 29 May 2026 (not law on 3 Nov 2026) [C rights.lgbt].
- Pace: slower than Accra (Rio Times, 2026-10-18) [C social.pace]; small, close-knit expat community tied to NGOs, KNUST and the hospital [C community.expat].

## Neighbourhoods

### Adum (and Kejetia)
- Feel: the central shopping district, bordering Kejetia Market [2]; traders, public-sector workers and general city tenants, busy and central, with strong rental activity (The Africanvestor) [3].
- Rent: not given separately; units near Adum or Kejetia command GHS 300–900 more per month (≈ €21–64) than equivalents elsewhere [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the hub with links to Ahodwo, Daban, Nhyiaeso and Asokwa [3]; the city-centre trotro station has the widest choice of destinations but is chaotic and congested (Wikivoyage) [1]. Minimum trotro fare GHS 5.50, shared-taxi minimum GHS 1.90 since 26 Sept 2026 (national) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: Kejetia Market fires destroyed 200+ shops in Jan and March 2016 and another fire broke out in Jan 2025; the market was cut from the grid on 28 June 2022 over unpaid power bills (Wikipedia, citing press) [12].
- Places:
  - Kejetia Market (Kumasi Central Market, 8,000+ stores, about 50,000 visitors a day; 06:00–19:00 per Wikivoyage) — Wikipedia [12]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - St Peter's Cathedral Basilica, Roman Hill, next to Kejetia (Catholic cathedral, 1913) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [2]
  - Wesley Methodist Cathedral, 5 Denkyemenaso Road, Adum (church) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Centre for National Culture, with the Prempeh II Jubilee Museum and Ike's Cafe and Grill (cultural centre, museum, restaurant) — Wikipedia [2]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Ghana Armed Forces Museum, Kumasi Fort, 22 Stewart Avenue (museum, Tue–Sat) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Asafo Market, west of the Asafo interchange (food market, 06:30–17:30) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Alliance Française Kumasi, Adiembra Adam Anor Street, TUC area (French courses, DELF/DALF, library, events; since 1956) — AF website [17]
  - Vic Baboo's, Prempeh II Road (bar-restaurant, cocktails, Indian/Chinese/continental) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" by day only (no club sourced); Q5 "J'entreprends" for trade; Q6 "Français" (the Alliance is the city's only French institution [D]). Better visited than lived in for families.

### Nhyiaeso and Ridge
- Feel: premium areas with larger homes, better security and roads; professionals, business owners, families, NGO and hospital staff (Nhyiaeso); expats and upper-market tenants (Ridge) [3][C housing.areas]. Expat.com names Ridge and Nhyiaeso as the expat residential areas [4].
- Rent: good 2-beds GHS 3,500–6,000/month (≈ €250–430); expat-facing, often furnished units GHS 4,000–10,000 (≈ €286–714) (The Africanvestor) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com quotes villas at $800–2,500 and flats at $500–1,500 (≈ €690–2,150 and €430–1,290), undated [C cost.rent]. Property rates in premium areas can exceed GHS 2,000/year [3].
- Getting around: good access to Adum and Asokwa [3]; Uber, Bolt and Yango operate, pay cash in cedis, avoid unverified street taxis alone after dark [C daily.transport]. Taxi: GHS 10.34 + 15.47/km (CityCost, AI/user-generated) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced. Landlords add monthly premiums for security (GHS 250–700), air conditioning (GHS 250–700) and water storage or borehole (GHS 250–600) [C cost.living].
- Places:
  - Rattray Park, Victoria Opoku Ware Street, Nhyiaeso (park with gardens, artificial lake, playground, cafés; 08:00–23:00; opened 2015) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Kaase-Nhyiaeso mosque (mosque) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Ridge School (school) — The Africanvestor [3]; [C housing.areas]
  - HapaSpace, 19A Residential Drive, Danyame (incubator, maker space, media services) — HapaSpace website [16]
  - Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital (750 beds, country's second-largest hospital; district not stated) — Wikipedia [2]; [C health.care]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (by Kumasi standards) or "Moyen"; Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Ahodwo and Daban
- Feel: premium, with better roads, stronger security, larger homes and more modern flats; families, professionals, diaspora and expats (The Africanvestor) [3][C housing.areas]. Ahodwo and Patasi are popular with families for nearby schools [C housing.areas].
- Rent: good 2-beds GHS 3,500–6,000/month (≈ €250–430); family 2–3-beds GHS 2,500–6,000 (≈ €179–429); expat-facing GHS 4,000–10,000 [C cost.rent]. meQasa's few rental listings are mostly in Asafo, Ahodwo and Daban [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: good access to Adum and Asokwa [3]. No sourced commute time.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - KNUST Basic School, International Community School, Ridge School and Opoku Ware School (schools named as near Patasi and Ahodwo) — The Africanvestor [C housing.areas]
  - Opoku Ware School, Santasi (Catholic boys' senior high, founded 1952) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Kumasi City Mall, Old Bekwai Road, Asokwa (mall, opened May 2017; Mon–Sat 9–21 h, Sun 10–21 h; tenants not listed) — Wikipedia [8]; mall website [9]
  - Galaxy International School, Kumasi campus ($10,000–18,000/year; district not stated) — Expat.com [C family.schools]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q10 "La santé et l'école" (within Kumasi's more limited offer [C family.schools]); Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (diaspora named among tenants [C housing.areas]).

### Asokwa and Patasi
- Feel: mid-market; Asokwa is noted for easy commutes, shops and restaurants [C housing.areas]; Patasi balances price and lifestyle, with young professionals, families and some expats [3].
- Rent: young-professional 1-beds or compact 2-beds GHS 1,200–3,000/month (≈ €86–214); family 2–3-beds GHS 2,500–6,000 (≈ €179–429); outer Asokwa is among the cheapest for studios and 1-beds [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: easier commutes and better road access, good access to Adum [3]; Asokwa interchange [2]. The Atonsu terminal (trotros toward Lake Bosomtwe) is a departure point for weekend trips [7] (its distance from Asokwa is not sourced).
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Kumasi City Mall, Old Bekwai Road, Asokwa (mall) — Wikipedia [8]; mall website [9]
  - Asokwa interchange (road junction, landmark) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Private schools around Ahodwo, Patasi and Asokwa, including Opoku Ware School — The Africanvestor [3]
  - Lancaster Kumasi City, Rain Tree Street (hotel with pool and gym, about 3 km from Kejetia, about $110 a night; district not stated) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (mall, cafés and easy roads); Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### KNUST corridor (Bomso, Ayeduase, Kotei, Kentinkrono)
- Feel: student and university-linked areas; Bomso is lively, Ayeduase busy and student-oriented; they let fastest on student demand [3][C housing.areas]. KNUST's main campus lies about 13 km east of Kumasi and had 85,000+ students in 2023 [13].
- Rent: young-professional 1-beds GHS 1,200–3,000 (Bomso); units near KNUST or Tech Junction cost GHS 300–900 more per month; Kentinkrono is among the dearest 2-bed areas [C cost.rent][3].
- Getting around: close to KNUST [3]; trotro or ride-hail to the centre (no sourced time).
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (university; Kwame Nkrumah memorial park at the main entrance) — Wikipedia [13]
  - KNUST Botanical Garden (garden, 07:00–17:00) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - KNUST hospital (completed 2023) — Wikipedia [2]
  - University College of Management Studies, Ayeduase campus (college) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Focus FM and TEK TV (campus radio and TV) — Wikipedia [13]
- Fits: Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (academic jobs, international academics [C work.jobs]); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" in a student key.

### Santasi and Kwadaso
- Feel: lower-cost residential areas for families and budget tenants (The Africanvestor) [3]; among the cheapest areas for studios and 1-beds, with Tafo and Suame [C cost.rent].
- Rent: studios within the citywide GHS 500–1,500 range (≈ €36–107), typical GHS 900; 1-beds at the low end of GHS 800–2,500; family 2–3-beds GHS 2,500–6,000 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: not sourced.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Opoku Ware School, Santasi (boys' senior high) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Christian Service University College, Santasi (university college) and Light TV (broadcaster) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Christ Apostolic University College, Kwadaso (university college) — Wikipedia [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" on a tight budget; Q7 "Prend son temps". Few named everyday places: a reader would shop in Adum or at Kumasi City Mall.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Taxi drivers often quote foreigners 1.5–3 times the local fare; trotro fares are fixed (Wikivoyage Ghana) [C cost.negotiation].

### One day
- Morning: Kejetia Market from 06:00 (textiles, kente, beads, sandals, food) [1][12]; St Peter's Cathedral Basilica next to it [1].
- Midday: walk or taxi to the Centre for National Culture: Prempeh II Jubilee Museum (former kings' belongings, a reproduction of the Golden Stool) and lunch at Ike's Cafe and Grill [1][2].
- Afternoon: Manhyia Palace Museum (1925 palace, museum since 1995; 09:00–17:00), about 1 km from the cultural centre [14][1]; or the Armed Forces Museum at Kumasi Fort (Tue–Sat) [1]. Entry fees not found.
- Evening: Rattray Park, Nhyiaeso [1], then dinner at Vic Baboo's or Moti Mahal [1]. Ride-hail back; avoid unverified street taxis alone after dark [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: craft villages: Bonwire, home of kente weaving, about 18 km on the Kumasi–Mampong road (Kente Festival 2024; the kente museum's opening is not confirmed) [11]; Ntonso adinkra village, Mon–Sat 06:00–17:00 [1]; Ahwiaa wood carvers [2].
- Day 3: Lake Bosomtwe, sacred to the Asante, 30 km south-east: trotro from the Atonsu terminal or Ejisu station (GH₵60–100) or private taxi (GH₵150–250), entry GH₵20 (2025), boat cruise GH₵200–600 [6][7]. Book guides through your lodge; Wikivoyage warns of touts at Abono [7].
- Day 4: Owabi Wildlife Sanctuary (Mon–Fri, GH₵6; about 161 bird species; Bamboo Temple) [1].
- Day 5: daily-life morning: rush-hour trotro from the city-centre station [1] to the KNUST campus (botanical garden, student life in Ayeduase and Bomso) [1][3][13]; afternoon at Asafo Market [1].
- Day 6: Asante Kotoko at the Baba Yara Stadium (40,528 seats, Ghana's largest) if a home match is on; fixture list not sourced [10][2].
- Day 7: Sunday service at St Peter's or Wesley Methodist Cathedral [1]; Friday alternative: Kumasi Central Mosque [2]. Akwasidae durbar dates at Manhyia were not found [14].
- Longer option: Cape Coast and its castle by coach, 85–95 cedis (Dec 2025) [18]; see the Accra file for the castle.
