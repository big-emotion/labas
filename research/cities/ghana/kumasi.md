# Kumasi, Ghana

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rates **1 EUR ≈ 14 GHS** and **1 USD ≈ 12 GHS** (Sept 2026; dossier header), so 1 USD ≈ €0.86. The Africanvestor's own EUR conversions match the 14 GHS/EUR rate. Items marked "national" are not Kumasi-specific.
