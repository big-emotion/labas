# Santa Maria, Cape Verde

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier (Sal island resort town). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR = 110.265 CVE** (fixed euro peg, Wikipedia "Cape Verdean escudo", accessed 2026-11-14), rounded. Sources quoting only USD are converted at **US$1 ≈ 97 CVE** (Wikivoyage Cape Verde, rate dated July 2026), so US$1 ≈ €0.88. Research-depth caveat: the shared web-search budget ran out after two searches; this file relies on pages opened directly. Several items describe Sal island as a whole; they are labelled so. Espargos, the island capital 20 km north, is where many workers live.

- [cost.rent] Studio at least US$350/month (≈ CVE 34,000, €308); 2-bed US$550–800 (≈ CVE 53,400–77,600, €484–704); villa in a gated community US$1,300–2,000 (≈ CVE 126,100–194,000, €1,144–1,759); rents run 20–30% above comparable homes elsewhere, and Espargos is cheaper for permanent residents. — Expat.com "Living in Cape Verde" guide, 2025-11-03, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A nomad-budget aggregator puts a short-term furnished 1-bed at US$1,300/month (≈ CVE 126,100, €1,144), almost three times its Praia and Mindelo figure (US$450). — Freaking Nomads Santa Maria, updated 2026-11, blog, confidence: low

- [cost.rent] New seafront flats on Sal sell for €1,500–3,000/m² (≈ CVE 165,400–330,800), roughly double the Praia range in the same guide. — Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A forum reply says land on Sal costs much more than on larger islands, and building is slightly dearer because materials are shipped in; it quotes about €500/m² for medium-quality and €1,000/m² for luxury construction, excluding land. — Expat.com forum "General Questions about Cape Verde/Praia and Sal", c. 2024 (page says "2 years ago"), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] An economist's column says rents on Sal have risen to unsustainable levels and that hotel workers live in degraded neighbourhoods; it uses CVE 3,000,000 (≈ €27,200) as the reference cost of one family housing unit built by the Sal council. — A Nação (opinion), 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Aggregator monthly budget for one person in Santa Maria: US$1,880 (≈ €1,654), of which groceries US$200, eating out US$110, essentials US$270. — Freaking Nomads Santa Maria, updated 2026-11, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The country guide compares Sal prices to the Caribbean, and says euros are commonly accepted on Sal, with change sometimes given in escudos. — Wikivoyage Cape Verde, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A 2023-era forum thread calls Sal "by far the most expensive place to live" in Cape Verde; a local single mother was said to afford about CVE 17,500/month for housing (≈ €159). — Expat.com forum "Affordable social housing … in Santa Maria?", c. 2023 (page says "3 years ago"), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] The same thread put the minimum wage at CVE 14,000 (≈ €127) at the time; the government said on 2026-11-13 it has no short-term plan to raise the minimum wage. — Expat.com forum c. 2023 and Inforpress 2026-11-13, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Sal's GDP per capita was CVE 495,827 (≈ €4,497) in 2015, among the highest in the archipelago. — Wikipedia "Sal, Cape Verde", accessed 2026-11-14 (2015 data, older), academic, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] A buyer reports that the two agencies in Santa Maria price in a similar range, so agency reputation mattered more than price; they suggest renting long term for a year before buying because resale is complicated and can lose money. — Expat.com forum "English speaking solicitor in Sal", c. 2025-12 (page says "11 months ago"), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Taxis are unmetered across the country, so fares are agreed before the ride. — Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] A property lawyer writes that tourism has pushed up land and rent prices on Sal, homes were converted into short-term rentals, and teachers, doctors and police were pushed out of Santa Maria into informal neighbourhoods without sanitation. — A Nação (opinion, Gertrudes do Rosário Morais), 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] A forum resident says affordable housing in Espargos is "a dive", with the cheapest housing in the poorest neighbourhoods; the Casa Para Todos public programme had only a few hundred units per island for thousands of applicants. — Expat.com forum "Affordable social housing … in Santa Maria?", c. 2023, forum, confidence: low
- [housing.search] On Sal and Boa Vista several large foreign property managers reportedly control most rentals. — Expat.com forum "Finding an apartment/house to rent", c. 2024, forum, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Two estate agencies are mentioned in Santa Maria; Ocean Properties is described as the larger. A forum user warns against an unnamed solicitor in Espargos. — Expat.com forum "English speaking solicitor in Sal", c. 2025-12, forum, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Tourism, centred on Santa Maria, replaced salt and fishing as Sal's main income; Sal held 48.2% of the country's hotel beds in 2017. — Wikipedia "Sal, Cape Verde" and "Santa Maria, Cape Verde", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The labour market is described as at or near full employment, drawing workers from other islands, possibly from West Africa, and some specialists from Europe. — A Nação (opinion), 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] An aggregator citing the World Bank marks the grid as not reliable, and says summer heat makes air conditioning essential. — Freaking Nomads Santa Maria, updated 2026-11, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Electra handles water production, distribution and billing on Sal; a foreign owner reported the bill's QR-code payment did not work and the bill carried no bank details. — Electra homepage accessed 2026-11-14 and Expat.com forum "Electra utility bills" c. 2025, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Internet benchmark: 80 Mbps down, 50 Mbps up (Ookla index). — Freaking Nomads Santa Maria, updated 2026-11, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Airport taxi to Santa Maria €12–15 (≈ CVE 1,320–1,650); to Espargos €1–2; shared aluguer to Santa Maria about €1 (2019 price). — Wikivoyage Sal, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Shared minibus (Hiace) between Espargos and Santa Maria cost about CVE 100 each way (≈ €0.90) around 2023. — Expat.com forum "Affordable social housing … in Santa Maria?", c. 2023, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] GOV.UK: on Sal, authorised taxis are blue and yellow; hire only from ranks or through your hotel. — GOV.UK travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Sal's airport is the busiest in the country (1.19 million passengers in 2019) with direct flights to Paris-CDG, Paris-Orly, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Nantes, Toulouse, Brussels, Lisbon, Dakar and Casablanca, among others. — Wikipedia "Amílcar Cabral International Airport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Larger food shops are in Espargos rather than Santa Maria. — Wikivoyage Sal, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] A forum reply describes care on Sal as basic: a health centre locally called a hospital and private clinics; serious cases go to Praia or Mindelo, then Dakar or Portugal. — Expat.com forum "Medical system in Sal", c. 2023, forum, confidence: low
- [health.care] There is no helicopter, no sea rescue and no decompression chamber in Cape Verde, which matters for divers and kitesurfers. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Cabo Verde International School (CVIS) on Sal: English-language, British-style curriculum with IGCSE, about 200 pupils, €4,000–7,000/year (≈ CVE 441,000–772,000) plus about €500 registration. — Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] École Internationale de Sal (EIS) in Murdeira is "still functioning" but lost its website during a management change; a parent described multilevel classes with an assistant per teacher and mixed early-years quality. — Expat.com forum threads "Looking for a school or kindergarten" (2026-10) and "School and education, Sal" (c. 2024), forum, confidence: low
- [family.childcare] An extended-school-day pilot starts 1 December 2026 on Sal: 30 children in Espargos with the Fundação Zaya Lina (government subsidy CVE 80,000/month); talks are under way with the Igreja do Nazareno for Santa Maria. — Inforpress, 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] GOV.UK names Sal (with Praia and Boa Vista) for pickpocketing, bag-snatching, burglary and muggings. — GOV.UK travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The US State Department advisory is Level 1, "Exercise Normal Precautions" (relayed by an aggregator that nevertheless rates the town "Risky"). — Freaking Nomads Santa Maria citing US State Dept, 2026-03-02, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.women] France Diplomatie reports more sexual assaults nationwide, with women travelling alone the main victims. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [social.sport] Surfing, windsurfing and kitesurfing have grown over about 25 years on Sal; the town's east end has a surf and windsurf area. — Wikipedia "Sal, Cape Verde" and Wikivoyage Sal, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] A forum moderator notes the Cape Verde board is not very active; a parent relocating to Sal with a teenager could get no answer on secondary schools. — Expat.com forum "Sal international school", c. 2026-02, forum, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] EU citizens visa-free for 30 days, ECOWAS for 90 days; visa on arrival cancelled for 96 countries from 2026-01-01; EASE pre-registration required. — Wikipedia "Visa policy of Cape Verde", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [admin.residency] Remote Working Program: 6 months renewable once (12 months maximum), €20 per person plus €34 airport fee, no local work; staying over 183 days in 12 months triggers tax residence. — Stamped Nomad, updated 2026-10-27, company, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Arid: about 76 mm of rain on roughly 3 days a year; mean temperatures 22 °C in January to 27 °C in September; sea 23–26 °C. — Wikipedia "Santa Maria, Cape Verde", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hurricane Fred damaged houses, tourist facilities and boats on Sal in late summer 2015. — Wikipedia "Sal, Cape Verde", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Santa Maria salt pans are a protected landscape; Ponta de Vera Cruz lighthouse is nearby. GOV.UK advises swimming only at lifeguarded beaches because currents are strong. — Wikipedia "Santa Maria, Cape Verde" and GOV.UK, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Sal's population grew from 6,921 (1990) to about 35,000 (2020); Santa Maria itself had 6,258 residents in 2010. — A Nação 2026-11-12 and Wikipedia "Santa Maria, Cape Verde", local-media, confidence: medium
