# Praia: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 110.265 CVE** (fixed euro peg, dossier [D]). USD figures are converted at US$1 ≈ 97 CVE, as in the city file. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cap-vert/praia.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/cap-vert.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after eleven searches for this country, so most places come from Wikivoyage and Wikipedia pages opened directly; many Wikivoyage listings are marked "updated May 2015" [1] and should be re-checked before any use.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-10-13) reports a recent rise in violent armed robberies in Praia, day and night, often targeting pedestrians, and advises not walking alone outside busy central areas and taking a taxi at night [C safety.general]. GOV.UK names Praia for pickpocketing, bag-snatching, burglary and muggings and advises authorised taxis from ranks [C safety.general]. No official source rates neighbourhoods one against another.
- Sexual assaults are rising nationwide, with women travelling alone the main victims (France Diplomatie 2026-10-13) [C safety.women].
- Beaches are generally unsupervised with strong currents; there is no sea rescue (France Diplomatie 2026-10-13, GOV.UK) [C nature.access].
- Heavy rain August–October can cause landslides and flooding (France Diplomatie 2026-10-13) [C climate.daily].
- Serious cases need evacuation to Dakar or Europe; Hospital Agostinho Neto in Plateau is the national reference hospital [C health.care][3].
- Distances are short: two US diplomatic post reports say almost everything in Praia is within about 20 minutes' drive (Talesmag, 2024-02-25 and 2024-12-17) [7][8].
- Power grid marked not reliable (aggregator citing the World Bank) [C daily.power]; tap water can be contaminated in rooftop tanks, so bottled water is advised [C daily.water].

## Neighbourhoods

### Plateau (historic centre)
- Feel: the grid-plan colonial centre on its plateau, with the Presidential Palace, City Hall, Supreme Court, main post office and the national hospital; only 1,019 residents at the 2010 census, so it is an office and shopping district more than a residential one [3]. A relocation blog calls it lively and administrative, with morna and coladeira concerts (Jarnias, 2026-01-22, low confidence) [9]. On UNESCO's tentative list since 2016 [C social.culture].
- Rent: from US$300 a month (≈ CVE 29,100, €264) (Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03) [C cost.rent]; downtown studio CVE 20,000–30,000 (≈ €181–272) and 1-bed downtown CVE 35,000–50,000 (≈ €317–453), same guide [C cost.rent]. The Jarnias blog instead says Plateau rents are among the highest in Praia, with no figure [9]: the two sources disagree.
- Getting around: on foot inside the grid; the Sucupira shared-taxi terminal (opened May 2018) sits beside it, at the bottom of the steps below Parque 5 de Julho [C daily.transport][4]; airport taxi CVE 500, CVE 800 after 18:00 (Wikivoyage, listings largely 2015) [C daily.transport]; city bus CVE 43 [C daily.transport].
- Watch: France Diplomatie's armed-robbery warning applies to the urban centre by day and night (2026-10-13) [C safety.general]. No source says how busy Plateau stays after office hours.
- Places:
  - Mercado Municipal (Mercado Central), Avenida Amílcar Cabral (food market, from early morning) — Wikivoyage Praia [1]
  - Calú e Angela, Avenida Amílcar Cabral (supermarket with imported goods; Mon–Sat 8:00–20:00, Sun 8:00–13:00; several branches in Praia) — Arrival Guides [10]
  - Pro-Cathedral of Our Lady of Grace (Catholic, built 1902) — Wikipedia "Plateau (Praia)" [3]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Quintal da Música, Avenida Amílcar Cabral 70-A (live national and traditional music, food) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Restaurante Flor de Liz, Rua 5 de Julho 43 (Cape Verdean cooking) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Museu Etnográfico da Praia (ethnographic museum; no fee published) — Wikipedia [3]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hospital Agostinho Neto (national reference hospital) — Wikipedia "Plateau (Praia)" [3]; [C health.care]
  - Casa do Cidadão (tax number, NIF, same day with a passport per a forum user) and the Electra shop on Rua Serpa Pinto (prepaid power top-ups) — [C business.admin][C daily.power] (forum, low)
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (administration and the NIF office on foot); Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen" (the cheapest rent floor quoted for central Praia); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" by day only; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (the colonial centre and the national ethnographic museum).

### Achada Santo António
- Feel: a plateau neighbourhood of 12,965 people (2010) south-west of the centre [11]; restaurants, embassies and much of the city's nightlife (Wikivoyage) [C housing.areas]; the Jarnias blog calls it the "embassy quarter", calmer than Plateau, home to NGOs and international organisations (2026-01-22, low) [9]. Wikipedia places most embassies in neighbouring Prainha instead [11], so the label is loose.
- Rent: a long-time resident says it is older than Palmarejo but rents about the same (Expat.com forum, c. 2024, low) [C cost.rent], i.e. new-build furnished 2-bed €600–700 (≈ CVE 66,000–77,000), unfurnished €300–400 (≈ CVE 33,000–44,000). Generic "2-bed in a residential area" CVE 40,000–60,000 (≈ €363–544) (Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: across town by taxi no more than CVE 1,500 by day, CVE 1,800 at night (Wikivoyage, 2015 listings) [C daily.transport]; short taxi ride CVE 200–500 (≈ €1.80–4.50) (Expat.com guide 2025) [C daily.transport]; the Taxi Seguro app shows fare and driver before booking [C daily.transport].
- Watch: a property lawyer names Achada Santo António among the areas where land hoarding has pushed up prices per m² (A Nação, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. No area-specific safety source.
- Places:
  - École Internationale Les Alizés (French international school) — Wikipedia "Achada Santo António" [11]; [C family.schools] (fees not found)
  - Chapel of Santo António and a Protestant church — Wikipedia [11]
  - Restaurante Marisqueira Rosita, Hotel América, Largo da Europa (seafood, CVE 500–2,200 a dish, 10:00–23:00) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - O Poeta, next to the Portuguese embassy (upmarket restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Churrasqueira Dragoeira (barbecue chicken) and Churrasqueira Benfica (Brazilian-style barbecue) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Morabeza Kriol Hostel, 21A Rua Chandadi (dorm beds €14–18, price updated June 2016) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Electra shop (cash top-ups for prepaid meters) — [C daily.power] (forum, low)
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (the French school is in the neighbourhood); Q5 "Salarié·e" (embassies and NGOs nearby); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" and "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q6 "Français" (French-language school and diplomatic milieu).

### Palmarejo (with Palmarejo Baixo)
- Feel: a subdivision south-west of the centre, 12,037 residents in 2010, bordered by Achada Santo António, Quebra Canela, Cidadela and Palmarejo Grande [12]. Forum residents single it out as a good area, on a hill 1–1.5 km from the sea, about 15 minutes' walk from Kebra Kanela beach (c. 2024, low) [C housing.areas]; a nomad aggregator calls Plateau and Palmarejo the practical areas (2026-11, low) [C housing.areas]; the Jarnias blog describes wide avenues and newer buildings (2026-01-22, low) [9]. A US diplomat's report mentions a coastal walking and running path in Palmarejo Baixo (Talesmag, 2024-02-25) [7].
- Rent: 2-bed US$500–700 (≈ CVE 48,500–67,900, €440–616) (Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03) [C cost.rent]; waterfront Palmarejo Baixo (Covo Minhoto) about €1,000–1,500+ (≈ CVE 110,000–165,000), one street back €500–600 unfurnished, further from the water around €300 (≈ CVE 33,000) (forum, c. 2024, low) [C cost.rent]. Numbeo 1-bed centre average CVE 56,667 (≈ €514), updated 2026-10-02 [D].
- Getting around: taxi to Plateau within the CVE 200–500 short-ride band [C daily.transport]; SolAtlântico city buses CVE 43 [C daily.transport].
- Watch: land hoarding raises prices per m² here and in Palmarejo Grande (A Nação, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. No area-specific safety source.
- Places:
  - University of Cape Verde main campus (new campus completed 2021) — Wikipedia "University of Cape Verde" [13]
  - Prime Coworking (coworking, partner of the remote-work programme per the blog) — Jarnias blog, 2026-01-22, low [9]; address not confirmed
  - Palmarejo Baixo seafront path (walking, running) — Talesmag 2024-02-25 [7]
  - Praia de Quebra Canela (main city beach, about 15 minutes' walk) — [C housing.areas]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Calú e Angela branch near Avenida Santiago (supermarket) — directory listing only, low (search result, yatmo.com) [14]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (coworking, fibre, sea path); Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Prainha and Quebra Canela (seafront)
- Feel: Prainha has the high-end hotels and a beach (Wikivoyage) [C housing.areas]; Wikipedia says much of the embassies are located there [11]. Quebra Canela is a tiny residential count (19 people in 2010) around a popular beach [15].
- Rent: ocean-view villa in Prainha US$800–1,500 (≈ CVE 77,600–145,500, €704–1,320) (Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03) [C cost.rent]; villa with garden in sought-after areas CVE 80,000–150,000 (≈ €726–1,360), same guide [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: short taxi rides to Plateau and Achada Santo António (CVE 200–500) [C daily.transport]; car hire CVRent is based in Prainha, Europcar has a desk at Hotel Pestana Trópico (about CVE 4,500 a day with insurance) [1].
- Watch: beaches unsupervised with strong currents (France Diplomatie 2026-10-13; GOV.UK) [C nature.access]. A US diplomat's report says the beaches are not clean and sewage reaches some of them (Talesmag, 2024-12-17, anecdotal) [8].
- Places:
  - Praia de Quebra Canela (the most popular city beach) and Praia de Prainha — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [15]
  - Cruz do Papa (viewpoint over Quebra Canela beach) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hotel Pestana Trópico (from €113) and Oásis Atlântico Praiamar (from €100) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - CVRent (car hire), Prainha — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Bar "Belavista", Hotel Praia Mar complex — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage" (beach on the doorstep, with the current warning); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q4 "En couple".

### Várzea and Chã de Areia (central, lower-cost side)
- Feel: Várzea is home to Estádio da Várzea, where most of the city's clubs play [6][C social.sport]. The Jarnias blog describes Várzea as a lively, sometimes chaotic commercial and student hub with supermarkets, pharmacies, cheap restaurants and public transport, rents "generally more affordable" than Palmarejo or Plateau (2026-01-22, low) [9]. Chã de Areia (Gamboa) lies south of Plateau on the shore [3].
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure. City-wide reference: Numbeo 1-bed outside the centre average CVE 28,333 (≈ €257), range 20,000–35,000 (≈ €181–317), updated 2026-10-02 [D]; livingcost.org "cheap" 1-bed US$279 (≈ CVE 27,100, €246), 2026-06-21 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: walking distance to Plateau and to Sucupira [3][4]; buses and aluguer minibuses CVE 50–100 in town [C daily.transport].- Watch: no area-specific official source.
- Places:
  - Estádio da Várzea (football; Sporting, Boavista, Travadores, Académica) — Wikipedia [6]; [C social.sport]
  - Mercado de Sucupira (big bazaar market, clothes, household goods, live animals; next to the shared-taxi terminal) — Wikivoyage [1][4]; Arrival Guides [10]
  - Beramar Grill, Chã de Areia (fish) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Perola Hotel, Chã de Areia (from €56) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Praia da Gamboa (beach, not for swimming; site of the Gamboa area) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (walk to the centre); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q8 "Je reste en ville" (football at the Várzea).

### Outer districts: Palmarejo Grande, Cidadela, Achada Limpo
- Feel: the expansion areas west and north-west of Palmarejo [12]. A property lawyer writes that land hoarding in Palmarejo, Cidadela, Palmarejo Grande and Achada Santo António pushes middle- and low-income families towards distant outskirts (A Nação, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. The city and the housing agency IFH have 100 social housing units under construction in Achada Limpo since September 2026 and 100 more for young people planned in Palmarejo Grande (Santiago Magazine, 2026-11-10) [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 3-bed in the suburbs CVE 50,000–80,000 (≈ €453–726) (Expat.com guide, 2025-11-03) [C cost.rent]; "further from the water" around €300 (forum, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: taxi, about 20 minutes to anywhere [7][8]; across-town taxi cap CVE 1,500 by day [C daily.transport].
- Watch: no area-level safety source; the France Diplomatie national warning in the frames above applies.
- Places: no named everyday place could be sourced (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" with a car or a taxi budget; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" for larger homes at a lower rent, once a school run is solved.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Palmarejo** — the area forum residents and aggregators single out, coworking (Prime, per a blog), a seafront path and the main beach within walking distance [C housing.areas][7][9].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home on fibre (Ookla benchmark 80/50 Mbps) [C daily.internet]; evening run on the Palmarejo Baixo path [7].
  - Tue: day at Prime Coworking (address to confirm) [9]; lunch out (cheap meal ≈ CVE 496) [D].
  - Wed: taxi to Plateau (≈ 10–15 min, CVE 200–500) [7][C daily.transport]: Mercado Municipal and Calú e Angela for the week's shop [1][10].
  - Thu: home office; late-afternoon swim at Quebra Canela where a lifeguard is on duty, otherwise not [C nature.access].
  - Fri: coworking; evening drink in Achada Santo António (taxi) [C housing.areas].
  - Sat: see Saturday-night options.
  - Sun: Mass at the Pro-Cathedral in Plateau or a slow morning at Cruz do Papa viewpoint [1][3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Achada Santo António's nightlife [C housing.areas] or Cockpit in Achada Grande [1]; taxi both ways, as France Diplomatie advises at night [C safety.general].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Marisqueira Rosita (CVE 500–2,200 a dish) or O Poeta, Achada Santo António [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home in Palmarejo; sunset walk on the seafront path before dark [7].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Tarrafal, at the north end of the island, the most accessible sand swimming beach; aluguer from Sucupira (fare not found) [4][16].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Serra Malagueta (1,064 m nature reserve, more humid, hikes in several directions) [1][4].

  - "Je reste en ville": football at Estádio da Várzea [6].

Coworking fees not counted (no fetched source).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week of a trial stay
- Before landing: EASE pre-registration and airport fee CVE 3,400 (rising to 4,400 is contested) [D]; EU citizens 30 days visa-free, ECOWAS 90 days, visa on arrival cancelled for 96 countries since 2026-01-01 [C admin.visa]. A longer trial points to the Remote Working Program (6 months, renewable once, €20 + €34 airport fee) [C work.remote].
- Where to stay first: **Plateau** for walking access to admin and the Sucupira terminal: Residencial Paraíso, Rua Serpa Pinto 73 (from €41) or Hotel Santa Maria, Rua Serpa Pinto 35 (single CVE 4,500) [1]; cheaper, Morabeza Kriol Hostel in Achada Santo António (dorm €14–18, 2016 price) [1]. The Expat.com guide advises 2–4 weeks of temporary housing before signing [C housing.search].
- Week:
  - Day 1: airport taxi to Plateau CVE 500 (CVE 800 after 18:00) [C daily.transport]; prepaid SIM from CVMóvel or Unitel T+ (about CVE 1,000 for ≈ 7 GB in July 2022) [C daily.internet]; cash at an ATM (Visa, MasterCard, Maestro) [C daily.money].
  - Day 2: NIF at the Casa do Cidadão [C business.admin]; walk Plateau and the Mercado Municipal [1].
  - Day 3: viewings in Palmarejo and Achada Santo António; most listings circulate privately, no agent needed (an agent adds about €50 a month) [C housing.search][C cost.negotiation].
  - Day 4: more viewings; ask for deposit terms (one or two months, sometimes first and last month upfront) and whether a 12-month lease brings 10–15% off [C housing.search][C cost.negotiation].
  - Day 5: bank: account opening needs a passport, proof of address and sometimes a home-bank reference letter [C daily.money]. Mobile money: not found.
  - Day 6: Cidade Velha by aluguer from Sucupira (CVE 200) [18].
  - Day 7: Quebra Canela and Cruz do Papa [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Achada Santo António nightlife, taxi both ways [C housing.areas][C safety.general].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Quintal da Música [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the guesthouse.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Tarrafal [16].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Entry with EASE pre-registration and airport fee [D].

### One day
- Morning: Plateau on foot — Mercado Municipal on Avenida Amílcar Cabral from early morning, Praça Alexandre Albuquerque, the Pro-Cathedral and the Museu Etnográfico (fee not published), then the Diogo Gomes monument at the south edge of the plateau [1][3].
- Midday: lunch at Restaurante Flor de Liz, Rua 5 de Julho 43 [1]; or a cheap local meal (≈ CVE 496) [D].
- Afternoon: walk down to Sucupira and take an aluguer to Cidade Velha (CVE 200; taxi up to CVE 1,000) [18]; Forte Real de São Filipe (entry CVE 500, includes a town map), the ruined Sé cathedral, Rua Banana and the Pelourinho square [17][18]; aluguers back leave every few minutes from the Pelourinho square [18].
- Evening: sunset at Cruz do Papa over Quebra Canela [1]; dinner at Marisqueira Rosita in Achada Santo António (CVE 500–2,200) [1] or live music at Quintal da Música [1]; taxi back (CVE 1,500 maximum across town by day, 1,800 at night) [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: Plateau and the evening at Quintal da Música, as above [1][3].
- Day 2: Cidade Velha (UNESCO 2009): fort, Nossa Senhora do Rosário church (1495), Rua Banana [17][18].
- Day 3, daily life: early morning at Mercado de Sucupira and the shared-taxi terminal, to see how the island commutes [1][4]; then the neighbourhoods a resident would rent in — Palmarejo (University of Cape Verde campus, seafront path) and Achada Santo António (Les Alizés school, embassies) [7][11][13]; late swim at Quebra Canela (lifeguard caveat) [C nature.access].
- Day 4, trip out: Serra Malagueta (1,064 m reserve, humid, hikes) on the way north; France Diplomatie advises a guide for hiking in the interior [D][1][4].
- Day 5, trip out: Tarrafal — sand beach under Monte Graciosa and the Tarrafal camp museum (political prison 1936–1974, at least 32 deaths) [16]; aluguer from Sucupira; service thins in the afternoon [4].
- Day 6: São Jorge dos Órgãos botanical garden [1], back via the city for a match at Estádio da Várzea if one is on (fixtures not found) [6].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Pro-Cathedral (72.5% of the country is Catholic, 2021) [D][3]; beach at Prainha; farewell dinner at Beramar Grill, Chã de Areia [1].
- Optional: ferry to Fogo (about 4 hours) for the caldera [1][8]; ferries are unreliable (CV Interilhas pulled the Kriola after an engine failure on Santiago–Fogo, 2026-11-14) [C daily.transport].
