# Sousse: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions. EUR at the dossier rate, 1 EUR ≈ 3.32 TND (Numbeo's own conversion, 2026-11-10). "City file" means `research/cities/tunisie/sousse.md`; the source it cites is named after it. Monthly figures become weekly ones by dividing by 4.3. Source numbers [n] refer to "Sources read" at the bottom. The shared web-search budget ran out after two Sousse queries in this pass; the rest comes from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Expat.com, a school website). Places confirmed only through a search-engine snippet are marked "(snippet)". Hammam Sousse and Port El Kantaoui are separate municipalities in Greater Sousse, not the city proper.

Read first, for every neighbourhood below. France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) reports recent racist assaults in Greater Tunis, Sfax and Gabès and does not name Sousse; the FCDO notes anti-migrant protests and violence in some coastal towns such as Sfax that "might affect anyone perceived to be of sub-Saharan African origin" (both in the city file). France Diplomatie lists terrorist attacks in Sousse in 2015 and September 2020; on 26 June 2015 a gunman killed 38 people on a beach near Sousse hotels (Wikipedia, city file). No district-level safety source was found. Article 230 (up to 3 years for same-sex relations) applies everywhere (city file, Amnesty).

## Neighbourhoods

### Médina and centre (Bab Jedid, the station, the port)

- Feel: the walled old town, UNESCO-listed since 1988 (Ribat, Great Mosque) (city file); "authentic" but flats often need renovation [2] (Expat.com, 2025-12-10). Souks meet the residential lanes around the Great Mosque and the Bou Ftata mosque at the southern gate [10]. The port lies on its eastern flank [10].
- Rent: no Médina-specific rent found. Numbeo centre 1-bed 717 TND (range 500–900; ≈ €216) (city file, Numbeo 2026-11-10, few contributors). Expat.com studios 300–1,000 TND (≈ €90–301) citywide [2].
- Getting around: Gare Sousse is directly north of the Médina, 5–10 min on foot; Tunis 7.6 TND second class, 10.3 first [1]. Sahel metro (light rail) from Sousse Bab Jedid to Monastir (1 TND) and Mahdia [1][10]. Local bus station next to the Médina; buses 0.5–2 TND [1][2]. Louage station south of the Médina on Rue El Masjed El Aksa, about 30 min on foot: Tunis 11.9 TND (about 1.5 h), Monastir 2.1 [1].
- Watch: France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) warns of evening bag snatching in city centres, nationwide (city file).
- Places:
  - Great Mosque of Sousse (mosque, visitor entrance on the north square, 08:00–14:00, 5 TND) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Sousse Ribat (fortress, 8 TND, 08:00–17:30, to 19:00 in summer) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Médina souks and the fixed-price Soula Centre just outside, to check prices before haggling — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Restaurant du Peuple (couscous about 10 TND, next to Hotel de Paris) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Restaurant Lido (by the port, grilled fish about 15 TND, beer about 3) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Musée Dar Essid (house museum with café, Boulevard Yahia Ibn Omar, 5 TND) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Église Saint-Félix (Catholic church built 1911, also hosts Protestant and evangelical worship) — French Wikipedia [10]
  - Hôpital universitaire Farhat Hached (public hospital, founded 1942) — French Wikipedia [10]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (cheap rooms, station, Sahel metro); Q3 "Serré"; Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Corniche and Boujaafar (seafront)

- Feel: seafront with recent buildings close to shops and restaurants [2]; a promenade of cafés, ice-cream stands and restaurants facing Boujaafar beach [2]. The protectorate-era Ville Nouvelle north of the Médina runs along this promenade towards Port El Kantaoui [10].
- Rent: Mubawab asking prices: furnished S+1 of 50 m² at 650 TND (≈ €196), S1 of 85 m² at 1,300 (≈ €392) (city file, 2026-11-14). Expat.com: 2-room 850–1,200 (≈ €256–361), sea view from 2,000 (≈ €602) [2]. Landlords often want several months or a full year upfront (city file).
- Getting around: walking distance to the Médina was not sourced. Taxi meter: 0.900 TND flag fall nationally (Business News 2026-11-03), 0.500–0.54 in older Sousse guides; night rate 21:00–05:00 [1] (city file).
- Watch: FCDO says reports of sexual harassment and assault rise in summer, including on quiet beaches by day (city file).
- Places:
  - Plage de Boujaafar (city beach) — Expat.com Sousse [2]
  - Abou Nawas Boujaafar Hotel (hotel with thalasso and pools, Avenue Habib Bourguiba) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Magasin Général (state supermarket, one of the few allowed to sell spirits; 7 November avenue near the Sousse Palace hotel) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - PLAN B (burger place, Avenue Taïeb Mehiri; 8.5 TND with fries, April 2019) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Edge bar (bar in Cesar Palace hotel, Avenue 14 Janvier, beer 4 TND, 23:00–02:00) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
  - Living/Banana/Saloon disco (hotel zone near Samara and Movenpick, free entry, beer 5 TND, 00:00–04:00) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (in season); Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen".

### Sahloul (new districts, families)

- Feel: developing area of new builds, popular with families [2]. Sahloul 4 draws 18% of Sahel apartment rental searches on Mubawab, far ahead of any other district (city file).
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 Sahloul 4 S+1 865 TND (≈ €261), S+2 1,260 (≈ €380), S+3 1,530 (≈ €461), S+2 up 10% in a semester; asking: new furnished S+1 of 92 m² at 1,200 (≈ €361), unfurnished S+2 of 118 m² at 1,100 (≈ €331), S3 of 160 m² at 2,000 (≈ €602) (city file).
- Getting around: not sourced (no bus line or travel time to the centre confirmed).
- Watch: nothing sourced specific to the area.
- Places:

### Hammam Sousse and Port El Kantaoui (north coast)

- Feel: Hammam Sousse is a calmer residential town still close to the beach [2], 47,520 people in 2022 [9]. Port El Kantaoui is a tourist marina built in 1979 (city file) with a golf course, beach clubs and nightlife [2][3]; El Kantaoui is the priciest Sahel area in Mubawab's report (city file).
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 Hammam Sousse S+1 875 TND (≈ €264), S+2 1,225 (≈ €369), S+3 1,425 (≈ €429); El Kantaoui S+1 1,145 (≈ €345), S+2 1,380 (≈ €416), S+3 1,710 (≈ €515); El Kantaoui S+2 1,290 in H1 2025 (city file). Cheaper coast further north: Chott Meriem S+1 885, Hergla S+1 790 (≈ €267 / €238) (city file).
- Getting around: Sousse to Port El Kantaoui about 12 km; metered taxi about 4.1 TND, 5–6 agreed in advance; purple tuk-tuk 2–2.5 TND and tourist train 2.5 [1][3]. Monastir airport about 30 min by taxi [3].
- Watch: FCDO summer harassment warning on beaches (city file). The 2015 beach attack was "near Sousse hotels" (Wikipedia, city file); the page read does not name the beach.
- Places:
  - Port El Kantaoui marina (yacht pier, square with musical fountain) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Port Gate square (three supermarkets and a Magasin Général) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Hannibal Park (amusement park south of the marina) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Restaurant Familial (traditional Tunisian food) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Neptune VI (floating restaurant in the docks) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Brau Haus (micro-brewery near Hannibal Park) — Wikivoyage Port El Kantaoui [3]
  - Be One / B1 club (disco near Houria Palace and Vincci Resort, free entry, beer 5 TND, 00:00–04:00) — Wikivoyage Sousse [1]
- Fits: Kantaoui for Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin", Q8 "À la plage", Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (by Sousse standards); Hammam Sousse for Q7 "Prend son temps" and Q2 "Tranquille chez moi". No everyday place inside Hammam Sousse itself was sourced.

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly totals use only prices in the city file, the dossier or pages read in this pass. Quantities are assumptions and are stated. Utility, transport and grocery lines use Expat.com's itemised Sousse budget [2] (single: utilities 150, groceries 400, transport 100, internet and phone 50 TND/month; family of four: utilities 300, groceries 800, transport 200, schooling for two 1,500, health insurance 400). Expat.com's own headline budgets disagree with that table (city file), so treat these totals as low.

General notes for every profile: the working week runs Monday–Friday, often with a long lunch break; many restaurants close by day in Ramadan (expected 8 February–8 March 2027) (city file). Cash is the norm (city file).

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Where to stay first: the Médina edge near the station. Hotel de Paris, near the main Médina entrance, singles from 50 TND (June 2025) [1]. Hôtel Gabes is cheaper but has many negative reviews [1].
- Day 1: arrival via Monastir airport (about 20 min away [1]) or Enfidha–Hammamet airport [1]; from Tunis, train 7.6 TND second class or louage 11.9 TND [1].
- Day 2: SIM card at an Ooredoo, Orange or Tunisie Telecom shop with a passport copy; plans 10–20 TND/month (national, Tunis city file, Expat.com). Mobile money: not found. ATMs take Visa and Mastercard; keep receipts, the dinar is a closed currency [7].
- Day 3–5: flat hunting on Mubawab, Tayara and Ballouchi or through a local agency (city file). Leases usually run a year and landlords often want several months, sometimes the whole year, paid upfront (city file). Visit fees and agency fees: not found. Start with Sahloul 4 (most listings) and the Corniche.
- Day 6: residence registration at the local police station within 30 days of arrival, per Expat.com's Sousse guide; its national guide says three months, so confirm locally (dossier). Bank account rules for a newcomer: not found.
- Day 7: Sunday on Boujaafar beach, or the Sahel metro to Monastir [1][4].
- Saturday night and weekend: as profile 1.
- Spend for the week (TND): 7 nights × 50 = 350 (Hotel de Paris) · mobile plan 10–20 · 14 cheap meals × 10–13.5 = 140–189 · 6 bus or metro trips × 0.5–2 = 3–12 [1][2] · train from Tunis 7.6. Total ≈ 511–579 TND (≈ €154–174). If a flat is signed with a full year upfront: a 2-room at 850–1,200 TND is 10,200–14,400 TND (≈ €3,072–4,337) on day one; with three months upfront, 2,550–3,600 (≈ €768–1,084).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day

- Morning: the Great Mosque (5 TND, closes at 14:00, so go first) and the Ribat next to it (8 TND), with the view from its watchtower [1].
- Midday: couscous at Restaurant du Peuple (about 10 TND) [1]; check prices at the fixed-price Soula Centre before haggling in the souks [1].
- Afternoon: the Archaeological Museum in the Kasbah, entrance outside the walls (10 TND, 09:00–17:00) [1], then Musée Dar Essid (5 TND) and its café [1].
- Evening: walk the Boujaafar corniche [2], then taxi (about 4.1 TND metered) or tuk-tuk to Port El Kantaoui; dinner at Neptune VI or Restaurant Familial [1][3].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2, trip: Monastir by Sahel metro from Bab Jedid (1 TND): Ribat (8 TND, includes the Islamic Arts Museum), Bourguiba Mausoleum (free), the marina [1][4].
- Day 3, trip: El Jem amphitheatre by train (about 1 h) or louage (about 6 TND); 12 TND including the museum, closes 17:30 in winter (Wikivoyage, data around 2018) [5].
- Day 4, daily life: a weekday in Sahloul: walk past CHU Sahloul and the Olympic stadium [8][12], view two flats from the Mubawab listings (city file), and ride a city bus back at the end of the working day (0.5–2 TND) [2]. If Étoile du Sahel plays at home, go to the match [8].
- Day 5, trip: Kairouan by bus, about 2 h (no train) [6]; multi-site ticket 10 TND for six sites, Great Mosque courtyard open to all [6].
- Day 6: Port El Kantaoui by day: marina, Port Gate supermarkets to see everyday prices, Hannibal Park [3]; evening at Be One/B1 [1].
- Day 7: Sunday service at Église Saint-Félix, which also hosts Protestant and evangelical worship [10]; afternoon on Boujaafar beach [2].
