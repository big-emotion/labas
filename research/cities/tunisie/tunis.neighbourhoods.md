# Tunis: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions. EUR at the dossier rate, 1 EUR ≈ 3.32 TND (Numbeo's own conversion, 2026-11-10). "City file" means `research/cities/tunisie/tunis.md`; the source it cites is named after it. Monthly figures become weekly ones by dividing by 4.3. Source numbers [n] refer to "Sources read" at the bottom. The shared web-search budget ran out after about a dozen queries in this pass; the rest comes from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, local media, a school fee schedule). Several places were confirmed only through a search-engine snippet; those are marked "(snippet)".

Read first, for every neighbourhood below. France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) reports recent racist assaults in Greater Tunis without naming a district, and Amnesty (Report 2025/26) describes a rise in racist violence against Black migrants from February 2025 (both in the city file). No source read in this pass ranks Tunis districts by risk for Black residents; the OpenShores safety page rates districts but cites no source and does not mention racism, so it is not used for any "Watch" line. Article 230 (up to 3 years for same-sex relations) applies everywhere (city file, France Diplomatie).

## Neighbourhoods

### La Marsa (with Bhar Lazreg)

- Feel: seaside suburb after Carthage, called a "ville banlieusarde" (suburban town) by Le Petit Journal [2] (2026-08-17); OpenShores (2026-03-10) describes a lively corniche with a quiet residential background, chosen by expats who want the sea on foot [3]. It is the most-searched rental district on Mubawab (8% of searches; city file, Mubawab H2 2024). Inside La Marsa, Bhar Lazreg is a working-class quarter whose main street, rue Charles de Gaulle, is lined with crowded men's cafés and street vendors; an ENAU student field study (Jadaliyya, 2022-10-18) records neighbourly tension and residents feeling the area has grown more individualistic [9].
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 averages S+1 1,880 TND (≈ €566), S+2 2,410 (≈ €726), S+3 3,030 (≈ €913) (city file, Mubawab 2025-03). Le Petit Journal gives S+3 1,600–2,000 TND (≈ €482–602), the cheapest of the upmarket coast [2]. OpenShores: 1-bed 1,800–3,000 TND (≈ €542–904) [3]. Expat group asking prices Oct–Nov 2026: La Marsa/Gammarth flat 2,500 TND (≈ €753), S+3 house with pool 4,000 (≈ €1,205) (city file). No Bhar Lazreg-specific rent found.
- Getting around: TGM light rail from Tunis Marine; La Marsa Plage is the terminus, just over 30 minutes, under 1 TND [4]; 0.800 TND per trip (Wikivoyage Feb 2025, city file). Repairs between La Goulette Casino and Khereddine were under way in Feb 2025 [1]. Taxi meter 0.900 TND + about 0.58 TND/km, +50% at night (city file, Business News 2026-11-03; Wikivoyage).
- Watch: FCDO says reports of sexual harassment and assault rise in summer, including on quiet beaches by day (city file, accessed 2026-11-14). France Diplomatie notes curfews or traffic bans have been imposed around Carthage palace, next door (2026-09-15, city file).
- Places:
  - La Marsa Plage (TGM terminus and beach front) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - Place du Safsaf (square with bamboloni doughnut shops) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - Abdelliya Palace (16th-century Hafsid palace, cultural venue) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - Lycée Gustave Flaubert (AEFE French lycée, 16 rue Othman El Kaak, 2078 La Marsa) — ERLM fee schedule 2025–26 [10]
  - Al Seniour (Lebanese restaurant, 12:00–23:00, mid-range) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - Les Ombrelles (seafood restaurant on the seafront, from 20:00) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - O Bar at Hotel Plaza Corniche, with a nightclub below (Rue de Maroc) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
  - Le Plug (rock bar built out over the sea, no entry after 23:00) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage" (beach on foot); Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e" (cafés, TGM to the centre); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" when the French lycée matters; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu". Bhar Lazreg is the only lower-cost pocket here, but no rent was found to back a Q3 "Serré" fit.

### Carthage, Sidi Bou Saïd and Gammarth (upmarket coast)

- Feel: Carthage is a prestige residential area of diplomats, executives and well-off families, calm and green [3] (OpenShores 2026-03-10, company blog). Sidi Bou Saïd is a village on the coast and on the heights above the gulf [2]. Gammarth, north of La Marsa, has "the highest prices in the region" [2].
- Rent: Le Petit Journal S+3 2,000–2,500 TND (≈ €602–753) in Sidi Bou Saïd and 2,200–3,500 (≈ €663–1,054) in Gammarth; Jardins de Carthage S+3 from 2,200 (≈ €663) [2]. Mubawab H2 2024 Jardins de Carthage S+1 1,620 (≈ €488), S+2 2,190 (≈ €660) (city file). Gammarth Forêt furnished S+2 1,900 (≈ €572) and furnished S+3 3,800 (≈ €1,145) asking, Oct–Nov 2026 (city file, expat group).
- Getting around: TGM stations Carthage-Salammbo, -Byrsa, -Dermech, -Hannibal, -Présidence, -Amilcar, then Sidi Bou Saïd [5]. Gammarth is not on the TGM in any page read: car or taxi.
- Watch: curfews or traffic bans have been imposed around Carthage palace (France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, city file). Summer harassment warning on beaches (FCDO, city file).
- Places:
  - Carthage archaeological sites, single 20 TND ticket for 10 sites (April 2026) — Wikivoyage Carthage [5]
  - North Africa American Cemetery (free, 09:00–17:00) — Wikivoyage Carthage [5]
  - Dar El Hout oceanographic museum (0.5 TND) — Wikivoyage Carthage [5]
  - Café des Nattes (traditional café, mint tea 3 TND) — Wikivoyage Sidi Bou Said [6]
  - Ennejma Ezzahra (palace museum and Centre for Arab and Mediterranean Music, 10 TND, closed Mondays) — Wikivoyage Sidi Bou Said [6]
  - Port Plaisance (small beach and yacht harbour below Sidi Bou Saïd) — Wikivoyage Sidi Bou Said [6]
  - Au Bon Vieux Temps (restaurant, 56 rue Hedi Zarrouk, mains 20–45 TND) — Wikivoyage Sidi Bou Said [6]
  - Blanko (kitesurf club, bar and café in Gammarth by the Carrefour mall, April–October, until 02:00) — Wikivoyage La Marsa [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q8 "À la plage".

### Les Berges du Lac (Lac 1 and Lac 2)

- Feel: built on reclaimed lake land in the 1990s; embassies, multinational head offices and offshore IT; OpenShores calls it the most international district and a "very artificial" environment with office traffic jams [3]. Academic fieldwork from 2002 already described the Lac corniche as a strip of cafés and restaurants drawing people from across the capital [11] (old; the district has grown since).
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 Lac 2 S+1 2,065 TND (≈ €622), S+2 2,585 (≈ €779), S+3 3,295 (≈ €992), the priciest area in the report; furnished S1 asking 2,500 (Lac 2) and 1,500 (Lac 1) (≈ €753 / €452); unfurnished high-standard S+3 in Lac 1 at 4,200 (≈ €1,265) (city file). Expat.com €750–1,100 upmarket (city file). OpenShores: 1-bed 1,800–3,000 TND, 2-bed 2,500–4,500 [3].
- Getting around: Tunisia Mall 2 is "a few minutes" from Tunis-Carthage airport (Kapitalis 2017) [13]. No metro or TGM stop in Lac was confirmed in the pages read: car or taxi.
- Watch: nothing sourced specific to the area.
- Places:
  - Tunisia Mall 2 (mall, 28,000 m², with California Gym 1,200 m² and a 1,300 m² children's area, opened 2017-11-11, 09:00–21:00) — Kapitalis [13]
  - Tunisia Mall (first mall, Lac 2, opened December 2015; a Carrefour Market was reported inside at opening) — L'Économiste Maghrébin and Expat.com forum (snippet) [14]
  - Regus Berges du Lac 2 (coworking, Imm. Selim, rue de l'Euro) — regus.com listing (snippet) [15]
  - Spaces Carthage Center (coworking, rue du Lac de Constance, 24/7 for dedicated members) — spacesworks.com listing (snippet) [15]
  - Offices of Capgemini, IBM and Sopra HR (employers) — OpenShores [3]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" and Q5 "Salarié·e" (head offices, coworkings); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" only in the mall-and-corniche sense. Wikivoyage is self-contradictory on alcohol in Lac (one passage says Lac 1 and 2 do not serve it, another lists upmarket Lac restaurants that do) [1].

### Ennasr and El Menzah (north, middle class)

- Feel: Ennasr is a residential and commercial district in Ariana, northwest of Tunis [2]; developed in the 2000s, liked by families, cheaper than Lac [3]. El Menzah is a group of residential neighbourhoods with calm, tree-lined streets, villas and large flats, "often considered" one of the best for families [2]. The 2002 IRMC study describes El Menzah and El Manar as new elite residential quarters whose malls and café streets became the capital's new centres [11]. Cité Ennasr 2 is the second most-searched rental district on Mubawab (7%) (city file).
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 Ennasr 2 S+1 1,070 TND (≈ €322), S+2 1,400 (≈ €422); El Menzah 9C S+1 1,165 (≈ €351), S+2 1,500 (≈ €452) (city file). Le Petit Journal S+3 900–1,500 in both (≈ €271–452) [2]; OpenShores Ennasr 2-bed 1,800–3,000 [3]. Asking: furnished S1 "for foreigners" in Ennasr 2 at 1,200 (≈ €361), new S+2 at 1,800 (≈ €542), negotiable (city file, Expat.com listings).
- Getting around: not sourced (no metro stop or commute time confirmed for Ennasr in the pages read).
- Watch: nothing sourced specific to the area.
- Places:
  - Ennasr 2 and City Stars (shopping centres) — OpenShores [3]
  - Parc urbain d'Ennahli (large park with sports grounds and play areas, Ariana; the local council called it badly neglected on 2026-06-02) — La Presse [16]
  - École internationale française de Tunis (French school, El Menzah) — Le Petit Journal [2]
  - Clinique Taoufik (private clinic, El Menzah; cardiology, surgery, emergencies) — OpenShores [3]
  - Cité olympique d'El Menzah (stadium complex built for the 1967 Mediterranean Games) — El Menzah, Wikipedia (snippet) [17]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and Q4 "Avec mes enfants" on a Q3 "Moyen" budget; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### El Aouina and La Soukra (north-east, near the airport)

- Feel: El Aouina is the fifth most-searched rental district, with demand concentrated on S+1 and S+2 (L'Économiste Maghrébin, 2025-09, snippet) [14]. La Soukra, Riadh El Andalous and Chotrana are known for houses and villas between Ariana and the northern suburbs [2]. The expat-targeted ads in the Facebook housing group cluster in Lac 1, La Marsa, Gammarth and Aouina (city file).
- Rent: Mubawab H2 2024 El Aouina S+1 1,080 (≈ €325), S+2 1,285 (≈ €387); La Soukra S+1 1,260 (≈ €380), S+2 1,450 (≈ €437); asking S1 50–60 m² in El Aouina at 950 (≈ €286) (city file). Le Petit Journal S+3 900–1,300 (≈ €271–392) in La Soukra and Chotrana [2].
- Getting around: not sourced beyond taxi fares (city file).
- Places:
  - American Cooperative School of Tunis (ACST, PreK–12, IB Diploma, 10-acre campus in El Aouina; school buses to La Marsa, Gammarth, El Manar, Carthage) — Wikipedia [18]
  - Le Boeuf sur le Toit (bar, 3 avenue Fatouma Bourguiba, La Soukra) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" when an English or IB school matters; Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Prend son temps". Only two places were sourced here.

### Centre ville, Lafayette and Belvédère

- Feel: livelier, "not too touristy", with many cafés, restaurants, bookshops and cultural venues; students and young people; well served by metro, buses and taxis [2] (Le Petit Journal 2026-08-17). Mutuelleville and Belvédère, next to it, are calmer and family-oriented, with villas and large flats [2]. The Ville Nouvelle north of Place de Barcelone has many budget and mid-range hotels [1].
- Rent: Le Petit Journal S+3 about 1,500 TND (≈ €452) in the centre/Lafayette [2]; Numbeo centre 1-bed 1,285 TND (range 900–1,610; ≈ €387) (city file); Expat.com 2-room €300–400 in the centre (city file). Furnished villa floor S+2 in Mutuelleville asking 3,500 (≈ €1,054) (city file).
- Getting around: light metro hubs at Place de la République and Place de Barcelone, 0.500 TND one section / 0.650 two; TGM from Tunis Marine 0.800; RFR line D reaches Le Bardo in about 15 min, about 1 TND [1]. OpenShores gives a metro monthly pass at 30 TND (≈ €9) [3].
- Watch: France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) warns of bag snatching in city centres in the evening and notes curfews or traffic bans imposed in central Tunis (city file). FCDO: protests mostly in central Tunis, often at weekends and on 14 January and 17 December (city file).
- Places:
  - Marché Central (covered food market, entrances rue Charles de Gaulle and rue d'Allemagne; mornings, usually closed Mondays; cleaned up by the municipality in September 2026) — INHA notice and La Presse 2026-09-20 (snippet) [19]
  - Halfaouine (cheap food market, Place Halfaouine) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
  - Café de Paris (café-brasserie serving alcohol, Avenue Habib Bourguiba, 06:00–24:00) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
  - Cathédrale Saint-Vincent-de-Paul (cathedral, Avenue Habib Bourguiba) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
  - Église Sainte-Jeanne-d'Arc (Catholic parish in Belvédère, with a "remarkable presence" of sub-Saharan faithful) — Webdo 2026-02-02 [12]
  - Parc du Belvédère (park with the zoo and Museum of Modern Art, Métro Palestine) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
  - Lycée Pierre-Mendès-France (AEFE French lycée, Mutuelleville) — Le Petit Journal [2]; an older directory gives 92 bis route de l'Hôpital militaire (snippet, not confirmed current)
  - Bourguiba Institute of Modern Languages (Arabic courses, 47 avenue de la Liberté) — Wikivoyage Tunis [1]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (cheap hotels, metro hub); Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q5 "Salarié·e" with a metro commute; Mutuelleville for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French lycée).

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly totals use only prices in the city file, the dossier or pages read in this pass. Quantities (meals, trips, litres) are assumptions and are stated. Electricity and water bills for Tunis were not found (city file gap), so they are left out of every total.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: La Marsa. Beach and cafés on foot, TGM to the centre in about 30 min [4], broadband around 64 TND/month (dossier, Numbeo).
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; evening walk on the seafront to Place du Safsaf [4].
  - Tue: TGM two stops to Sidi Bou Saïd (time not sourced), work a morning from Café des Nattes (mint tea 3 TND) [6]. No coworking in La Marsa was found; the sourced ones are in Lac (Regus, Spaces) [15], taxi only.
  - Wed: food shopping; OpenShores mentions a La Marsa municipal market without an address [3], so the sourced fallback is the Carrefour mall by Gammarth (Blanko entry) [4].
  - Thu: gym (Expat.com €30–60/month, city file); the only named gym found is California Gym in Tunisia Mall 2, Lac 2 [13], taxi.
  - Fri: TGM to Tunis Marine then walk to the Marché Central (closed Mondays) [1][19]; breakfast or an early lunch at The Corner, 36 rue de Syrie (breakfast café, open daily 07:00–14:00) [1].
  - Sat: see Saturday options.
  - Sun: beach at La Marsa Plage; bike hire at Jhonny Bikes, 30 TND per 24 h (Wikivoyage flags its link as dead) [4].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": the nightclub under O Bar, Hotel Plaza Corniche [4]; Blanko in Gammarth in season (no entry after midnight) [4]. Entry prices not found.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Al Seniour or Les Ombrelles [4]; mid-range meal €9–18 a head (city file, Expat.com).
  - "Tranquille chez moi": cinema €4–7 (city file) or an evening in; alcohol is not sold in most shops on Fridays (Wikivoyage Tunisia [7]).
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": La Marsa beach on foot; Hammamet by louage, about 7 TND (Wikivoyage Hammamet, data 2013–2018) [8].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Boukornine National Park near Hammam-Lif, about 15 km [20]; transport to it not sourced.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Tunis for a weekend. Douz and Tozeur are the desert gateways [7]; no travel time from Tunis was sourced. The dossier's orange zone covers the desert south and east of the Bir Rijm Maatoug–Borj Bourguiba–Ben Guerdane line (France Diplomatie, zones 2026-03-13).
  - "Je reste en ville": the medina and the Bardo Museum (13 TND) [1].
- Weekly spend (TND): rent 1,880 ÷ 4.3 = 437 (Mubawab S+1 average, city file) · groceries 200–400 ÷ 4.3 = 47–93 (Expat.com national single budget, city file) · broadband 64 ÷ 4.3 = 15 (dossier, Numbeo) · mobile plan 10–20 ÷ 4.3 = 2–5 (Expat.com, city file) · 3 cheap meals × 13.5 = 41 (Numbeo, city file) · one mid-range dinner €9–18 × 3.32 = 30–60 (Expat.com, city file) · 4 TGM trips × 0.8 = 3 (Wikivoyage) · gym €30–60 × 3.32 ÷ 4.3 = 23–46 (Expat.com). Total ≈ 598–700 TND (≈ €180–211), utilities excluded. Cross-check: Numbeo's single-person costs excluding rent, 1,633 TND/month, are 380 a week (dossier).

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Where to stay first: the Ville Nouvelle, next to the metro hubs [1]. Hotel Transatlantique (Rue de Yougoslavie, 40 TND) or La Maison Doree (6 bis rue de Hollande, 32–52 TND) [1]; Wikivoyage prices are of uncertain date.
- Day 1: from the airport, bus 104 at 1 TND from outside Arrivals, or taxi 5–7 TND by day and about 10 at night on the meter [1].
- Day 2: SIM card at an Ooredoo, Orange or Tunisie Telecom shop with a passport copy; plans 10–20 TND/month (city file, Expat.com). Ask for a "carte prépayée" [7]. Mobile money: no wallet service was found in any source read (see Gaps); ATMs take Visa and Mastercard, about €1–2 per withdrawal fee, and receipts must be kept because the dinar is a closed currency [7].
- Day 3–5: flat hunting on Mubawab, Houni.tn, Lilkre.tn, Tunisie Annonce or the expat Facebook groups; check that an agency's name matches its bank account; agencies may add service fees on top of the deposit (city file, Le Petit Journal 2026-08-17). Leases run a year, deposit two months (1–3 by another source); searches take 1–2 months, and rents rise from April, so look after summer (city file). Visit fees: not found in any source. Advance rent beyond the deposit is a Sousse habit in the sources, not a Tunis one.
- Day 6: bank. Only the property route is sourced: buyers need a non-resident or convertible-dinar account (city file, Le Petit Journal). Whether a short-stay visitor can open an ordinary account was not found. A stay over three months needs a residence card from the police station, 1–3 months to process (city file, Expat.com 2018, old).
- Day 7: Sunday on the coast by TGM from Tunis Marine (0.800 TND) [1].
- Saturday night and weekend: as profile 1.
- Spend for the week (TND): 7 nights × 40 = 280 (Hotel Transatlantique) · airport bus 1 · mobile plan 10–20 · 14 cheap meals × 13.5 = 189 · 10 metro/TGM trips × 0.5–0.8 = 5–8. Total ≈ 485–498 TND (≈ €146–150). If a flat is signed: first month + two-month deposit; on an Ennasr 2 furnished S1 at 1,200 that is 3,600 TND (≈ €1,084), on a La Marsa S+1 at 1,880 it is 5,640 (≈ €1,699), before any agency fee.

### One day

- Evening: TGM to La Marsa Plage; dinner at Les Ombrelles (from 20:00) or Al Seniour [4]. Taxi back with the 50% night surcharge [1].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.

If a football match is on, Espérance and Club Africain play at the 65,000-seat Hammadi Agrebi stadium in Radès (snippet) [22]; derby tickets have at times been reserved for season-ticket holders [22]; ticket prices not found.
