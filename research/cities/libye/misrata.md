Additional city suggested by the dossier ("the country's business-minded city", "not recommended for scoring yet"). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 7.15 LYD** (Central Bank of Libya, 12 Nov 2026); USD figures were converted at the CBL rate of about 6.45 LYD per USD before converting to EUR. The shared web-search budget ran out after three Misrata searches; no Libyan property portal (OpenSooq) or local blog was reached, so **every price below comes from aggregator sites built partly or wholly on AI estimates** — treat them as rough signals, not observed prices.
