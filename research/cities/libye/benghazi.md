- [safety.general] Political analyst Seraj Daghman died in custody at Benghazi's Internal Security directorate after seven months of arbitrary detention; four others held over his case were freed without charge after 10 months (2024). — HRW World Report 2025, 2025, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.general] January 2025: videos showed torture at the LAAF-run Gernada prison, about 250 km north-east of Benghazi; investigations announced on 16 January produced no published results. — Amnesty International Libya report, 2025/2026, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.general] July–August 2025: municipal elections were cancelled or delayed in 20 municipalities because of LAAF-affiliated armed groups. — Amnesty International Libya report, 2025/2026, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Since 2014 the LAAF has forcibly displaced families from Benghazi, Ajdabiya and Derna. — HRW World Report 2025, 2025, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In the LAAF-held east and south: two mass graves with 93 bodies found in Wahat and Kufrah on 7–8 February 2025; killings, arbitrary arrests and disappearances of the Tebu in Kufra, August–October 2025. — Amnesty International Libya report, 2025/2026, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.women] Libyan law allows reduced sentences for men who harm female relatives over suspected extramarital relations and lets a rapist escape prosecution by marrying the victim; the Family Code discriminates on marriage, divorce and inheritance. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026, 2026, academic, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Bringing in any alcohol is banned; a non-Muslim found drunk in public faces 2–6 months in prison, a 500–1,000 LYD fine (≈ €70–140) and expulsion (Law 4/1994). — France Diplomatie "Informations utiles", 2026-10-29, official, confidence: high
- [admin.visa] French nationals need a visa before arrival from the Libyan Embassy in Paris; passports with any Israeli stamp or visa are refused entry. — France Diplomatie "Entrée / Séjour", 2026-10-29, official, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] There is no French consular presence in Benghazi; consular services for French nationals run from Tunis. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", 2026-10-29, official, confidence: high
- [rights.racism] HRW reports collective expulsions, mass arrests and racial discrimination against Black communities by Libyan authorities in 2025 (national, not Benghazi-specific). — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026, 2026, academic, confidence: high
- [rights.racism] Migrants and asylum seekers, including children, are held in facilities run by armed groups or traffickers, with torture, forced labour and sexual assault reported (national). — HRW World Report 2025, 2025, academic, confidence: high
