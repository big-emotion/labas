# Asmara, Eritrea

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR figures use the dossier's rate **€1 ≈ 17 ERN** (France Diplomatie, Oct 2026,
`research/countries/erythree.md`) and the official peg **US$1 = 15 ERN** (so US$1 ≈ €0.88). The
nakfa is non-convertible; Wikivoyage reports a 2025 black-market rate of 50–58 ERN per US$, which
is illegal to use. Official-rate conversions therefore overstate what a nakfa price "costs" anyone
with black-market access, and understate nothing for a legal resident. Old Tales Mag prices
(2016–2021) are converted at today's rate and flagged by date.

Search note: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after 4 searches, so most items come
from pages fetched directly (dossier URLs, Tales Mag post reports, Africarrières, Shabait,
CityCost).

- [cost.negotiation] Before 2016, visiting and vacationing diaspora Eritreans haggled rents with landlords; after the 2016 rules the state fixes the price, so the rent is in principle not negotiable (2017 report; current enforcement not verified). — Awate, 2017-09-14, local-media, confidence: low
