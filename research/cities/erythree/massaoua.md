# Massaoua (Massawa), Eritrea

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR figures use the dossier's rate **€1 ≈ 17 ERN** (France Diplomatie, Oct 2026)
and the official peg **US$1 = 15 ERN** (US$1 ≈ €0.88). The 2025 black-market rate of 50–58 ERN per
US$ (Wikivoyage) is illegal to use. Search note: the session's shared web-search budget ran out
early, so items come from fetched pages (dossier sources, Wikivoyage, Shabait, Africarrières).
Massawa-specific living material is very thin; national rules that apply here are included and
marked as national.

## Items

- [cost.rent] An AI-assisted job-guide says similar housing in Keren or Massawa costs 1,500–3,000 ERN/month (≈ €88–176), versus 3,000–6,000 ERN for a central 1-bed in Asmara. No listing cited. — Africarrières, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Sea-front hotels in Massawa cost no more than US$65 a night (≈ €57) — a 2007 figure, old. — Wikivoyage Eritrea, 2007 price, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fish at the Dahlak Hotel is described as "somewhat pricy" but worth it; boat charters to the Dahlak islands are expensive and must go through licensed companies (no prices given). — Wikivoyage Massawa / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Sunscreen is hard to find in Massawa and is expensive or expired. — Wikivoyage Massawa, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] An AI-assisted guide claims port and maritime wages are "competitive" versus other Eritrean cities; no figures. — Africarrières villes-emploi, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] No Massawa-specific source on bargaining was found; nationally, Awate reported that rents have been fixed per square metre by the state since 2016 (old, not Massawa-specific). — Awate, 2017-09-14, local-media, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The historic city sits on two islands, Batsi (old town) and Taulud (hotel area), linked by causeways to the mainland districts (Hitumlo, Amaterre, Salinas, Abdelkadir peninsula); Gurgusum beach is about 14 km north and needs a taxi or car. — Wikivoyage Massawa; Wikipedia "Massawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog/academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Hotels named: Corallo, Central, Dahlak and Red Sea on Taulud (air-conditioned, no beach); GB Hotel and Hamassien on Gurgusum beach. — Wikivoyage Massawa, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Wikipedia says the city was shelled in 1990 and, as of 2018, "remains nearly deserted" with trade cut by sanctions and isolation. — Wikipedia "Massawa", accessed 2026-11-14 (2018 status), academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] State media reported a 2025 workshop on a revitalisation plan for Massawa's historic buildings (inventory, digitalisation, conservation); no housing figures. — Shabait (Ministry of Information), 2025-08-28, official, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The port exports copper and zinc from the Bisha mine and imports oil and consumer goods; in 2018 some months saw up to 10 ships and others none. — Wikipedia "Massawa", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] An Italian ministerial delegation toured the port and its ship-repair centre in July 2025 and discussed marine-sector investment with the Marine Resources minister. — Shabait, 2025-07-30, official, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] An AI-assisted guide lists the port authority, shipping firms, freight forwarders and trading companies as employers and notes state control of port operations; it also gives a population (70,000–100,000) that conflicts with Wikipedia's 53,090 (2012). — Africarrières, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] State media reports vocational training for Massawa youth (May 2025) and a workers' training centre plan (Oct 2024). — Shabait, 2025-05-06 / 2024-10-05, official, confidence: low
- [work.remote] National: internet is limited to hotels and a few cafés, no 3G, and phone networks outside Asmara and larger towns may work only part of the day. — UK FCDO safety and security, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Buses and minibuses run Asmara–Massawa many times a day, before dusk, from Asmara bus station; regional buses from Massawa run a couple of times a week; buses to Nakfa only once a week. — Wikivoyage Massawa / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The 115 km mountain road from Asmara is dangerous, especially late afternoon and evening (no parapets, sea fog, animals); a travel permit is required. — France Diplomatie sécurité, 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Massawa airport had no scheduled flights as of mid-2023; the narrow-gauge railway runs only chartered tours. — Wikivoyage Massawa, 2023, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] AI-assisted guide (national): intercity bus 200–1,000 ERN (≈ €12–59), private taxi for longer trips 2,000–5,000 ERN (≈ €118–294). — Africarrières, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [daily.money] National: cash only, no ATMs or cards; exchange at Himbol offices or the Asmara airport bureau. — France Diplomatie informations utiles, 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Massawa and the coast are endemic for malaria and dengue (below 1,500 m); bring all medicines, as imports are limited to the WHO essential list. — France Diplomatie santé, 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Mosquitoes are worst November–February; nets are sold locally as "lamse". — Wikivoyage Massawa, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Massawa Hospital ran an eye-surgery campaign treating 240 patients in April 2025 with a Ministry of Health team; the hospital has a medical director. No fees mentioned. — Shabait, 2025-04-29, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A diving decompression chamber exists in Massawa. — Wikivoyage Massawa, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] State media reported awards for outstanding students in Massawa and Asmara (Nov 2025); no school list or fees found. — Shabait, 2025-11-10, official, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Massawa is in France's yellow "vigilance renforcée" zone, with theft and robbery "très limités". — France Diplomatie sécurité, 2026-11-09 (zones 2026-07-10), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all but essential travel to Eritrea outside the 25 km border strip (Massawa included) and says Eritrea–Ethiopia tensions "are high". — UK FCDO travel advice summary, updated 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Red Sea sailing is called dangerous and pleasure boaters are urged to postpone; the UK notes vessels impounded and crews detained for entering Eritrean waters without permission. — France Diplomatie 2026-11-09; UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK says the southern Red Sea is a hotspot for maritime crime and that military activity against Houthi shipping attacks could change advice at short notice. — UK FCDO safety and security, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] National: women may face verbal and physical harassment; dress should suit local custom, especially in mainly Muslim areas. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14; France Diplomatie 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [social.food] The old town's street restaurant grills the day's catch Yemeni-style, served with limes, flatbread and dates in butter and honey ("khobzen"). — Wikivoyage Massawa / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Heritage: Ottoman-era coral-block houses, the Sheikh Hanafi mosque, St Mary's Orthodox cathedral, the former imperial palace on Taulud and a tank memorial; families of mixed Arab, Turkish, Indian, Tigre and Saho descent. — Wikipedia "Massawa", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] The city has both Orthodox and Muslim landmark places of worship; France asks for modest dress in mainly Muslim regions. — Wikipedia "Massawa"; France Diplomatie 2026-11-09, academic/official, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hot desert climate: July averages a 40.8 °C daily maximum, January a 19.1 °C minimum, mean near 30 °C, about 183 mm of rain over ~15 days a year, high summer humidity. — Wikipedia "Massawa", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Wikivoyage says temperatures reach about 45 °C and air-conditioning is strongly recommended. — Wikivoyage Massawa / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Green Island is under 20 minutes by boat from the Dahlak Hotel (no facilities); Dahlak islands by costly charter; beaches near the port are of "modest to poor quality". — Wikivoyage Massawa / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [rights.lgbt] National: same-sex acts are criminalised (Art. 310, 2015 Penal Code, 5–7 years). — Human Dignity Trust, updated 2026-01-11, academic, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] Any trip from Asmara to Massawa needs a travel permit; tourists beyond 25 km must use a rented vehicle with driver. — France Diplomatie sécurité, 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
