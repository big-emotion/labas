## Items

- [cost.rent] An AI-assisted job-guide says similar housing in Keren or Massawa costs 1,500–3,000 ERN/month (≈ €88–176), about half Asmara's 3,000–6,000 ERN for a central 1-bed. No listing cited. — Africarrières, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Wikivoyage counts five small lodges or "pensions" in Keren, with no names or prices; hotels outside Asmara, Massawa, Keren and Assab are described as modest. — Wikivoyage Keren / Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] No Keren-specific source on prices or bargaining was found; nationally, Awate reported that rents have been fixed per square metre by the state since 2016 (old, not Keren-specific). — Awate, 2017-09-14, local-media, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Named neighbourhoods include La'li Keren, Shuq (the market), Dearit, Ad Habab and Hilat Sudan. — Wikipedia "Keren, Eritrea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] An AI-assisted guide lists regional administration, schools, clinics and regional businesses as employers, with agricultural services, commerce and light manufacturing (dairy, grain milling, textiles); it gives a population (60,000–80,000) that conflicts with Wikipedia's ~120,000 (2016). — Africarrières villes-emploi, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] State media describes Keren as a centuries-old trade hub where merchants brought livestock, camels and butter, on routes linking the Red Sea coast to the interior. — Shabait, 2025-01-20, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Buses run from Asmara to Keren many times a day; Keren is the usual overnight stop on the way to Nakfa. — Wikivoyage Eritrea, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The old railway through Keren is "largely dismantled", with only plans to restore it. — Wikivoyage Keren, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] National: cash only, no ATMs or card payments; exchange at Himbol offices or the Asmara airport bureau. — France Diplomatie informations utiles, 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] Keren is about 91 km from Asmara, so foreigners need a travel permit (required beyond 25 km of the capital or place of residence). — Wikipedia "Keren, Eritrea"; France Diplomatie entrée/séjour 2026-11-09, academic/official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Keren Hospital diagnosed 2,500 patients and performed 427 cataract operations in August 2025 with a visiting Sudanese eye team; no fees mentioned. — Shabait, 2025-08-28, official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] The number of schools in Keren sub-zone rose from 68 to 79, per a 2025–2026 academic-year review; no fees or levels given. — Shabait, 2026-08-15, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all but essential travel to Eritrea outside the 25 km border strip, which covers Keren; France's security page does not mention Keren. — UK FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-11-09; France Diplomatie sécurité 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] State media reported that Keren and Geleb sub-zones declared themselves free of female genital mutilation in November 2024; this is an official claim without independent figures. — Shabait, 2024-11-28, official, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Landmarks: Tigu fort (19th century), St Maryam Dearit chapel built into a baobab, the 1920s railway station, the old mosque and Sayed Bakri mausoleum, and British and Italian war cemeteries from the 1941 battle. — Wikipedia "Keren, Eritrea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] State media reported a book fair with innovation and creativity displays in Keren in May 2026. — Shabait, 2026-05-11 (headline in listing), official, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Thousands of Orthodox pilgrims visit Debre Sina monastery east of the city every June; the city also has St Michael's Greek Orthodox church and the Ala'tiq mosque. — Wikivoyage Keren; Shabait 2025-01-20, blog/official, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Local food named: injera and kuluwa (fried mutton), with local beer. — Wikivoyage Keren, undated, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Residents are mainly Tigrinya, Bilen and Tigre, and the city's name appears in Tigrinya, Tigre, Arabic and Italian. — Wikipedia "Keren, Eritrea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hot semi-arid at 1,590 m: afternoon highs average over 33 °C from March to mid-June, January lows about 13 °C, and about 514 mm of rain mostly June–September (August ~199 mm). — Wikipedia "Keren, Eritrea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] National: same-sex acts are criminalised (Art. 310, 2015 Penal Code, 5–7 years). — Human Dignity Trust, updated 2026-01-11, academic, confidence: high
