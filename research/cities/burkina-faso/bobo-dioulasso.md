# Bobo-Dioulasso, Burkina Faso

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Context only: Bobo-Dioulasso is flagged `unsafe` and is not proposed by the site. This file holds only
what is needed to explain why (official advice, conflict, displacement, civil liberties, dated sources).
No lifestyle or price curation was done on purpose. Items marked "(national)" describe the whole
country. Since the July 2025 territorial reform the former Hauts-Bassins region appears in local press
as "Guiriko"; official advice still uses the old name.

## Items

- [housing.areas] In a December 2022 household survey of displaced-host zones in Bobo's arrondissements 2, 4 and 6, 55% of displaced households lived in unplanned ("non-loti") areas, against 35% of non-displaced households (old data). — AGORA (ACTED/IMPACT) Bobo-Dioulasso household survey synthesis, file dated 2023-03, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] FCDO warns of armed attacks on both the Ouagadougou–Bobo-Dioulasso road and the Bobo-Dioulasso–Côte d'Ivoire road, increasing explosive-device attacks on major routes, and advises against any night travel. — UK FCDO safety and security page, updated 2026-10-26, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] A think-tank analysis cited by the Belgian asylum office says armed groups have cut the main roads linking Ouagadougou to Togo, Benin, Niger, Mali and Côte d'Ivoire and encircle about 130 towns. — Belgian CGRA/Cedoca COI Focus, 2026-01-30 (citing Africa Center for Strategic Studies, 2025-09-09), official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Canada says access to quality health care is limited "even in major cities such as Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso". — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-10-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK FCDO advises against all travel to the whole of Burkina Faso, citing terrorist attacks, terrorist kidnapping and an unstable political situation; the UK has no embassy and may be unable to evacuate. — UK FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-10-26, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Hauts-Bassins, Bobo's region, is one of eight regions under a state of emergency, which lets security forces search, restrict movement and detain people, and may suspend the right to protest or hold large meetings; the capital's region is not on the list. — UK FCDO safety and security page, updated 2026-10-26, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France formally advises against all travel to Burkina Faso, does not single out any town, rates the kidnapping risk as very high nationwide, and warns French nationals of arrest and detention with no French consulate open. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-10, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Canada advises avoiding all travel (highest level) because of terrorism, kidnapping and political instability. — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-10-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The Belgian asylum office says JNIM operated across large areas from January to July 2025, "progressively encircling" Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso. — Belgian CGRA/Cedoca COI Focus, 2026-01-30, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] ACLED counts for 1 Jul 2024–14 Nov 2025 put the Boucle du Mouhoun region first for victims (1,865), ahead of the East (1,762) and the Sahel (1,490). — Belgian CGRA/Cedoca COI Focus, 2026-01-30, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In March 2025 VDP militias were reported responsible for a massacre of civilians at Solenzo (Boucle du Mouhoun). — Belgian CGRA/Cedoca COI Focus, 2026-01-30, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] On the night of 3–4 January 2026 a major security alert hit Bobo-Dioulasso and Ouagadougou; authorities called it an attempted coup, soldiers were arrested and population movements were seen in both cities. — Timbuktu Institute, 2026-01-09, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] On 19 November 2025 hundreds of police and gendarmes sealed off Bobo's central market for the whole day, letting no one in or out, in an anti-terrorism sweep after explosives were found in Ouagadougou markets. — RFI via allAfrica, 2025-11-20, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Villagers fleeing jihadist raids around Djibasso (near the Malian border) since 30 December 2025 reached Bobo-Dioulasso, about 300 km south, with almost no resources or support. — RFI via allAfrica, 2026-01-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] At the garrison's January 2026 ceremony, officials praised "strong" social cohesion in 2025 and better coordination; the fire-and-rescue commander flagged rising road accidents. The piece gives no incident or casualty data. — Sidwaya via allAfrica, 2026-01-28, local-media, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Older context: in September 2017 a gendarmerie anti-terror drill at the Hôtel Sissima set off a rumour of an imminent attack in the city; the security ministry said residents had been warned. — Wakat Séra, 2017-09-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] (national) HRW documented 57 incidents killing more than 1,800 civilians in 11 regions (Jan 2023–Aug 2025) by the army, VDP militias and JNIM, and counts more than 2.3 million displaced. — Human Rights Watch Q&A, 2026-04-02, intl-media (NGO), confidence: high
- [community.diaspora] Displaced people working "peace fields" near Bobo (Farakoba, Tolotalama) come from Mali's Ségou region, Nouna, Mouhoun, Loroum and Faramana; the scheme began in 2021 when local women saw displaced women begging by the roadside. — Sidwaya via allAfrica, 2026-05-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] The same report says displaced families live on informal income (reselling plastic waste, odd jobs) and struggle with school fees, which one school lets them pay in instalments. — Sidwaya via allAfrica, 2026-05-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] In the December 2022 survey, 95% of displaced households in Bobo cited violence at home as a reason for leaving; a quarter came from Boucle du Mouhoun, a quarter from Cascades and a fifth from the Nord (old data). — AGORA (ACTED/IMPACT) Bobo-Dioulasso household survey synthesis, file dated 2023-03, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] The same survey found 17% of displaced households had enough food, against 53% of non-displaced households (old data). — AGORA (ACTED/IMPACT), file dated 2023-03, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] (national) Same-sex sexual activity and the "promotion" of same-sex relationships are criminal offences with sentences of up to 5 years; the FCDO reports little to no public acceptance and a higher risk of harassment or violence. — UK FCDO safety and security page, updated 2026-10-26, official, confidence: high
- [rights.racism] (national) HRW says hate speech, stigmatisation and violent attacks against Fulani (Peul) communities have intensified since the current leader took power, with Fulani civilians often treated as collectively aligned with armed groups. — Human Rights Watch Q&A, 2026-04-02, intl-media (NGO), confidence: high
- [other] (national) The death penalty was reinstated in October 2026 for terrorism, espionage, treason, parricide and importing expired or banned products that cause death. — UK FCDO safety and security page, updated 2026-10-26, official, confidence: high

## Gaps

- Lifestyle categories (rent, prices, salaries, power, internet, schools, nightlife) were deliberately not curated because the city is `unsafe`.
- No dated report of an armed attack inside Bobo-Dioulasso itself in 2025–2026 was found; a customs-depot explosion (two dead, "Monday 26 March") could not be dated to a year and was left out.
- No ACLED figure for Hauts-Bassins/Guiriko alone was readable (the COI gives it only in a chart).
- No city-specific, dated source on women's safety, harassment or foreigners' treatment in Bobo was found.
- `community.diaspora` items above concern internally displaced people, not the international diaspora; no source on diaspora returnees in Bobo was found.
- The session's web-search budget ran out mid-pass; no further searches (e.g. curfews, 2026 road incidents on the Bobo–Banfora axis) could be run.
