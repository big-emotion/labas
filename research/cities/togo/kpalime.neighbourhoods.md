# Kpalimé: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg, dossier). USD figures are kept as their source states them and are not converted. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/togo/kpalime.md`; `[L tag]` = an item in `research/cities/togo/lome.md`; `[D]` = the Togo dossier `research/countries/togo.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

**Read first: this is a thin sheet.** The session's shared web-search budget ran out before any Kpalimé query could run, so every source was opened directly by URL. No neighbourhood guide, local media, rental listing (CoinAfrique's Kpalimé page was empty on 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]) or returnee account exists in this pass. Kpalimé's quarters can be named but not described; most places below can be placed in the town but not in a quarter. Only three neighbourhood blocks could be written, against the 4–8 asked.

Frames that apply everywhere:
- Kpalimé (Plateaux region) is in France's yellow "vigilance renforcée" zone with no town-specific warning (France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15) [C safety.general]. France advises against driving between towns at night (no lighting, little signage, rare armed road robberies) and warns that roads can become impassable in the rainy seasons; a 4×4 is recommended on secondary roads [C daily.transport].
- Wet climate: about 1,442 mm of rain a year, over 100 mm a month from March to October, wettest in September, driest in January [C climate.daily]. Waterfalls shrink in the dry season (Kpimé's water is diverted to a dam) [C nature.access].
- Services: a post office, several banks, cyber-cafés, medical centres, pharmacies and petrol stations (Wikipedia, article tagged for missing citations) [C daily.internet]; a "basic" regional hospital (Expat.com, 2025-12-08) [C health.care]; no international school [C family.schools]. Serious care and international schools mean Lomé, about 120 km and roughly 2 hours by road [C daily.transport].
- About 101,088 people (2023); Ewe is the main local language and French is spoken by anyone who went to school [C social.pace]. Expat.com sums the town up as "calme et nature" and suggests it to retirees, artists and remote workers [C social.pace][C work.remote].

## Neighbourhoods

### Town centre (around the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit and the Place du 30 Août)
- Feel: the commercial and church centre of a town of about 100,000 [C social.pace]; Wikipédia shows the market, the Place du 30 Août and a historic monument in photos without describing them [3]. No dated source on noise or pace at street level.
- Rent: town-wide only: 100,000–300,000 XOF a month (≈ €152–457), type of home not stated, Expat.com 2025-12-08 [C cost.rent]; an aggregator lists a 1-bed in the centre at $240 and a "cheap" 1-bed at $112 (USD as stated, likely modelled, livingcost.org 2026-06-21) [C cost.rent]. Expat.com adds that rents outside Lomé are generally 30–50 % lower than in the capital [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot or by moto-taxi; no Kpalimé fare was found (Lomé reference: about 300 XOF for a ride of decent length, Wikivoyage) [L daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Cathédrale Saint-Esprit (neo-Gothic, built 1913 under German rule; seat of the diocese since 1994) — Wikipedia [4]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Place du 30 Août — Wikipédia (photo caption) [3]
  - Kpalimé market (shown in a photo; name and market days not given) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Hôtel 30 Août (no address or rate) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Mandela Inn (Auberge Mandela), Castle Market Street — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Aklala Batik du Togo (batiks and fabrics, Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00; address not given) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Macumba (Togolese: goat-meat soup, roast chicken, fufu; address not given) and Le Bon Vivant (Belgian food and beer; address not given) — Wikivoyage [1]; their quarter is not confirmed
- Fits: Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" or "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple"; Q5 "Création, culture" (Togo's handicraft capital, 36 workshops and shops and the CEAA craft college) [1][4][C work.jobs].

### The Kloto hills side (road towards Kouma Konda and Missahoé)
- Feel: cooler upland: German colonisers picked Missahoé for its altitude and cool climate (Wikipedia) [7]; Mont Kloto is described as a tourist area known for its "climat frais", hiking trails and butterflies (Wikipédia) [6]. Small Ewe villages in dense forest on the Agou side [5]. A place to live only for someone with a car (editorial inference, see Gaps).
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: Misahöhe forest about 9 km and Mont Kloto about 13 km north-west of town (Wikipedia) [4]; a 4×4 is recommended on secondary roads [C daily.transport]. No minibus or moto fare found.
- Watch: France's night-driving advice applies [C daily.transport]; rainy-season roads can become impassable [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Mont Kloto (hiking trails, butterflies; 741 m on its own Wikipédia page, 825 m on the Kpalimé page — unresolved) — Wikipédia [6][3]
  - Missahoé (Misahöhe) forest reserve, about 5,000 ha, 67 bird species (Wikivoyage fr, 2019) and the old German governor's house, courthouse, prison and cemetery in Missahoé village — Wikipedia [7][4]; Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Château Vial / Viale (built 1940–1944 by a French settler; presidential residence since 1975–1979; visiting not documented) — Wikipédia [8]; Wikivoyage fr places it at Kouma Konda [2]; Wikipedia (en) "near Kouma Konda" [4]
  - Kamalo falls, near Missahoé, called the most visited waterfall in Togo — Wikipedia [7]
- Fits: Q8 "En montagne, en forêt"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q9 "Doux" (cooler than the coast) [6][C climate.daily]; Q5 "Télétravail" only with reliable mobile data (not documented).

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city files, the dossier and the sources below; Kpalimé has almost no XOF prices, so several lines are in USD as stated by an aggregator whose Kpalimé figures look modelled (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21) [C cost.living]. "Assumption" marks a non-sourced step. No coworking, gym, supermarket, school other than "a scientific lycée", clinic name, internet operator or power-cut data was found for Kpalimé.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **town centre**, for walkable services (banks, cyber-cafés, pharmacies) [C daily.internet] and restaurants [1]. Expat.com names remote workers among the people Kpalimé suits [C work.remote], but no internet data exists for the town; an aggregator lists 50 Mbps+ home internet at $45.90 a month [C cost.living]. Plan on mobile data and expect the national 2025 shutdown risk [L daily.internet].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; lunch at Macumba [1].
  - Tue: home office; errands in the centre (bank, pharmacy) [C daily.internet].
  - Wed: home office; late-afternoon visit to the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit [4].
  - Thu: home office; batik shopping at Aklala (Mon–Sat to 17:00) [1].
  - Fri: dinner at Le Bon Vivant (Belgian beer) [1].
  - Sat: forest morning at Missahoé or Mont Kloto by hired car or moto (no fare found) [7][6]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the cathedral [4]; a Gomido FC home game at the Stade Municipal if one is on (fixtures not found) [9].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic in Kpalimé: Expat.com says there are few restaurants and Wikivoyage lists no nightlife beyond hotel bars [C social.nightlife]. Lomé's clubs are 2 hours away and night driving is advised against [C daily.transport].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Bon Vivant or Macumba [1]; palm wine and grilled chicken or fish with fufu and peanut sauce are the local fare [C social.food].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the town's default [C social.pace].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Lomé's private beaches at Baguida (2018 listing) are about 2 hours by road plus 13 km [C daily.transport][L nature.access]; a weekend trip, not a day trip. Swim only at arranged spots [L safety.general].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Mont Kloto trails, Missahoé forest, Kpimé falls (2,500 XOF), Womé falls (2,500 XOF; 4×4 best; swimming possible) [6][7][C nature.access]; Mont Agou (986 m, Togo's highest; summit is a military area with a relay, so access rules are unknown) [5].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Kpalimé. No savanna area within a weekend's reach was sourced; the Savanes region far north is in an orange zone under a state of emergency until 2027-03-13 (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [D].
  - "Je reste en ville": crafts workshops and Aklala Batik [1][4]; the cathedral [4]; a Gomido FC match [9].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: 100,000–300,000 ÷ 4.33 = 23,095–69,284 [C cost.rent]
  - Mobile plan (Lomé price, used as a national reference): 12,500 ÷ 4.33 = 2,887 [L daily.internet]
  - One waterfall entry: 2,500 [C nature.access]
  - **Subtotal ≈ 28,482–74,671 XOF a week (≈ €43–114)**
  - Plus, in USD as stated: 10 lunches × $3.32 = $33.20; home internet $45.90 ÷ 4.33 = $10.60 [C cost.living]. No electricity, water or grocery figure exists for Kpalimé.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **town centre**. Local salaried work is thin: the area grows over 80 % of Togo's coffee and cocoa and lives on crafts [C work.jobs]; an aggregator's median after-tax salary is $125 a month [C cost.salary]. A daily commute to Lomé (about 2 hours each way) is not realistic, so this profile fits only someone with a local post (diocese, regional hospital, schools; none of their employers was sourced) or a job done remotely.
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: walk or moto to work in town (no fare sourced) [L daily.transport].
  - Wed: dinner at Macumba [1].
  - Fri: Le Bon Vivant [1].
  - Sat: market morning (days not found) [4]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit [4]; afternoon at Kpimé falls [C nature.access].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — not realistic locally [C social.nightlife]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Le Bon Vivant [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" — at home.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend (two people): rent 23,095–69,284 [C cost.rent] + two mobile plans 2 × 2,887 = 5,774 [L daily.internet] + two waterfall entries 2 × 2,500 = 5,000 [C nature.access] → **≈ 33,869–80,058 XOF a week (≈ €52–122)**; plus 20 lunches × $3.32 = $66.40 and one dinner for two at $29 (USD as stated) [C cost.living].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **town centre**. The sourced openings are agriculture (coffee and cocoa), crafts (wood sculpture, weaving, batik, pottery, painting; 36 workshops and shops, the CEAA craft college) and nature tourism (waterfalls, Mont Agou, Kloto) [C work.jobs][4][C nature.access]. Expat.com and the dossier found no entrepreneurship data for Kpalimé [C][D]. Company registration happens through Lomé's CFE (2017 figures: 36,000 XOF sole trader, 210,000 SARL) [L business.admin].
- Week:
  - Mon: visit craft workshops and the CEAA craft college [4].
  - Tue: Aklala Batik, a working batik business (Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00) [1].
  - Wed: coffee and cocoa country around town (plantations named by Wikipedia) [4].
  - Thu: waterfall operators: note the guide-price friction at Kpimé, where guides try to charge double the 2,500 XOF entry [C cost.negotiation].
  - Fri: day trip to Lomé for the CFE and banks, bush taxi from/to Kodomé station (2017) [C daily.transport]; back before dark [C daily.transport].
  - Sat: market morning [4]; Saturday night as profile 2.
  - Sun: rest; Mass at the cathedral [4].
- Saturday night: as profile 2.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend: profile 1 subtotal 28,482–74,671 XOF + one Lomé round trip by bush taxi ≈ 2 × 3,600 = 7,200 (derived from the 2017 rate of about 3,000 XOF per 100 km × 120 km; no dated Lomé–Kpalimé fare exists) [C cost.negotiation] → **≈ 35,682–81,871 XOF a week (≈ €54–125)**, plus USD meals and internet as in profile 1, plus one-off registration.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **town centre**, to be near the "scientific lycée" and medical centres [4][C daily.internet]. Honest limits: no international school (Expat.com) [C family.schools]; only a "basic" regional hospital and a community psychiatry centre (Association Saint-Camille-de-Lellis) [C health.care]. Families wanting the French or English curriculum need Lomé's schools (2–5 M XOF a year) [L family.schools], i.e. boarding or moving.
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school on foot or by moto in town (local schools not named beyond the lycée) [4].
  - Wed: cathedral and town centre [4].
  - Sat: Kpimé falls in the wet season, when the water runs [C nature.access]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit [4]; a Gomido FC match (top flight, 6th in 2024–25; fixtures not found) [9].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — not realistic locally [C social.nightlife]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Macumba [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" — at home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Lomé's private beaches, a weekend away [L nature.access].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Missahoé forest trails for birds and butterflies [4][7]; Womé falls, swimming possible [C nature.access].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Kpalimé [D].
  - "Je reste en ville": crafts workshops and batik [1][4].
- Rough weekly spend (two adults, two children): rent 23,095–69,284 [C cost.rent] + two mobile plans 5,774 [L daily.internet] + four waterfall entries 4 × 2,500 = 10,000 (assumption: children pay full price) [C nature.access] → **≈ 38,869–85,058 XOF a week (≈ €59–130)**; the aggregator's family-of-four total is $1,569 a month including rent (USD as stated) [C cost.living]. School fees for local schools were not found.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Before arrival: e-visa from voyage.gouv.tg at least 5 days ahead, no visa on arrival since 12 April 2024, yellow-fever certificate (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [L admin.visa]; Visa-network card [L daily.money]. Land in Lomé, sleep there the first night rather than drive after dark [C daily.transport].
- Day 1 (Lomé): SIM and data, 10 GB with calls ≈ 12,500 XOF a month [L daily.internet]; cash from a Lomé ATM (Kpalimé has "several banks", ATM network not documented) [C daily.internet].
- Day 2: bush taxi from Kodomé station to Kpalimé, about 2 hours, fare negotiated with the driver (2017) [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation]. Check in at Jess Hotel, Kpeta, or Mandela Inn, Castle Market Street (no rates published) [1].
- Day 3: walk the centre: cathedral, market, banks [4][C daily.internet]; ask around for rentals, since the online classifieds were empty [C cost.rent]. Lease rules: the 2022 deposit cap (three months) applies to Grand Lomé only so far [L housing.search; see lome.neighbourhoods.md source 5]; outside Lomé no rule was sourced.
- Day 4: test mobile data at home-office hours in the quarter you like; no coverage data exists [C].
- Day 5: Kpimé falls (2,500 XOF; agree the guide price first) [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 6: Missahoé forest or Mont Kloto [7][6].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit [4]; dinner at Le Bon Vivant [1].
- Rough first-week spend: bush taxi ≈ 3,600 (derived from the 2017 per-km rate) + mobile plan 12,500 + one waterfall 2,500 = **≈ 18,600 XOF (≈ €28)** plus hotel nights (no rates found) and meals ($3.32 per lunch, USD as stated) [C cost.negotiation][L daily.internet][C cost.living].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". About 2 hours by road from Lomé [1]; travel by day [C daily.transport].

### One day
- Morning: Kpimé falls at Kpimé Seva (2,500 XOF; best in the wet season; guides on site try to charge double) [1][C nature.access]. Transport from town by hired moto or car (fare not found).
- Midday: Macumba (goat-meat soup, roast chicken, fufu) [1].
- Afternoon: Cathédrale Saint-Esprit (1913) and the Place du 30 Août [4][3]; Aklala Batik until 17:00 [1].
- Evening: Le Bon Vivant (Belgian beer) [1]; palm wine or sodabi as the local drinks [1][C social.food].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from Lomé by bush taxi (Kodomé station, 2017) [C daily.transport]; settle at Jess Hotel or Mandela Inn [1]; dinner at Macumba [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (Kloto side): Mont Kloto trails (butterflies, cool climate) and the Missahoé forest and German colonial buildings; Château Vial seen from outside (visits not documented) [6][7][8].
- Day 4 (Womé falls): jungle waterfall below ground level, 2,500 XOF, no guides on site so hire one in town; 4×4 best; swimming possible [1].
- Day 5 (Mont Agou): Togo's highest point (986 m) among Ewe villages in dense forest; the summit is a military area with a communications relay, so ask locally how far visitors may go [5].
- Day 6 (daily-life moment): market morning in the centre (days not found; ask at the hotel) [4], then Gomido FC at the Stade Municipal (10,000 seats) if a home game is on [9][1]; or a craft-workshop visit [4].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Esprit [4]; back to Lomé before dark [C daily.transport].
