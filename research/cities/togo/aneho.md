# Aného, Togo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the Togo dossier ("additional — coastal small-town option near Lomé"). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**. Research limitation: web search was unavailable for this whole pass, so items come only from pages opened directly; Aného has very little web coverage in those pages.

## Items

- [cost.rent] An expat guide gives rents of 50,000–200,000 XOF/month (≈ €76–305) in Aného, type of home not specified — the lowest range it quotes for any Togolese town. — Expat.com Guide Togo, updated 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The same guide says rents outside Lomé are generally 30–50 % lower than in the capital (general estimate). — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A land listing of 31 plots at "Lac Togo" (filed under Lomé; the lake lies between Lomé and Aného) was asking 150,000,000 XOF (≈ €228,673) in total. — CoinAfrique Togo, Immobilier category, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low (asking price)
- [cost.negotiation] Bush-taxi fares are negotiated with the driver, about 3,000 XOF per 100 km in 2017 (≈ €4.57); no Lomé–Aného fare was given. OLD. — Expat.com "Moyens de transport au Togo", 2017-10-04, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The coastline and lagoon are eroding by roughly 5–10 m a year. — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Farming and fishing are the main industries; fishing is one of the main activities on Lake Togo. — Wikipedia "Aného" and Wikipédia (fr) "Lac Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The Université de Lomé launched an Institute of Higher Maritime Studies in Aného on 10 November 2026. — Togo First, 2026-11-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Aného and the Chinese city of Xiamen are considering a decentralised-cooperation partnership. — Togo First, c. 2026-11-05, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Aného is about 45 km east of Lomé (the French article also says ~50 km), near the Benin border; bush taxis for Aného left from Lomé's Grand Marché or Hollando stations (2017). — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného" and Expat.com "Moyens de transport au Togo" (2017-10-04), academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The suburb of Sanvee-Condji faces Benin's Hillacondji crossing and is described as an important customs zone. — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Services in Aného are "limited", with Lomé close by for major needs. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The sea is particularly dangerous along the whole Togolese coast, with regular drownings; swim only in areas protected by breakwaters. — Belgian FPS Foreign Affairs, Togo sécurité générale, accessed 2026-11-14 (dossier records update 2026-10-06), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France does not single out Aného; the coast outside Lomé is in the yellow (heightened vigilance) zone, and recreational boating offshore is discouraged because of Gulf of Guinea piracy. — France Diplomatie, Togo security advice, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Festivals: Epe Ekpe (the Guin-Mina New Year) draws visitors from across Africa and the diaspora; the Acofin festival of "black divinities" returned in 2025 after a break; the town also hosts a history festival (FIHA) and the Situ Expo trade fair. — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] A former slave-trading post (Petit-Popo) and twice Togo's capital (1886–1897, 1914–1919); royal palaces, the Glidji temple and a regional museum; on UNESCO's tentative World Heritage list. — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] A Vodun centre on the "Voodoo Trail" route, with a Catholic cathedral (Peter and Paul church, from 1898) and a Protestant church from 1895. — Wikipedia "Aného" and Wikivoyage "Aného", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] About 28,237 inhabitants at the 2022 census; the expat guide describes "tranquillité balnéaire". — Wikipédia (fr) "Aného" and Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08), academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Aného lies between the Atlantic and Lake Togo; the mostly freshwater lake (Togoville, Agbodrafo) offers swimming, sailing and water-skiing, and the expat guide mentions lake water sports. — Wikipédia (fr) "Lac Togo" and Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08), academic, confidence: medium
