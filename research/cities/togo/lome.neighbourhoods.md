# Lomé: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg, dossier). USD figures are kept as their source states them and are not converted. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/togo/lome.md`; `[D]` = the Togo dossier `research/countries/togo.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Research limitation: one web search ran (a neighbourhood query) before the session's shared search budget ran out; every other source below was opened directly by URL. Many French Wikipedia neighbourhood pages (Adidogomé, Agoè, Bè, Hédzranawoé) do not exist (404).

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15) puts Lomé in the yellow "vigilance renforcée" zone; avoid the seafront and beach from sunset (knife attacks reported near the seafront hotels), avoid walking or riding a motorbike at night, keep car doors locked [C safety.general][D]. UK FCDO: violent crime, theft and pickpocketing are common, particularly on beaches and in markets; carjackings sometimes via staged accidents [C safety.general].
- The sea is dangerous along the whole coast, with regular drownings; swim only behind breakwaters or at arranged private beaches east of the port (Belgian FPS, France Diplomatie 2026-09-15) [C safety.general][C nature.access].
- Protests can break out in Lomé and turn violent, cutting traffic and communications (Belgian FPS, 2026-10-06); seven deaths were reported during the 26–30 June 2025 repression, and internet was disrupted from late June to September 2025 (Amnesty 2025/26) [C safety.general][C daily.internet]. Keep a discreet, neutral profile on politics (France Diplomatie) [C other].
- France advises against zémidjans (moto-taxis) because of frequent accidents and recommends yellow-plated taxis [C daily.transport]. Routes below use taxis or Gozem cars by default; zem fares are given because most residents use them. Gozem offers zem, tricycle and car rides and lists its Lomé address in Nyékonakpoè [23]; Wikivoyage calls it usually cheaper than street taxis and also names Yango [1].
- The flat city (about 10 m above sea level, shallow water table) floods repeatedly in heavy rain and loses about 12 m of coastline a year (Wikipédia, accessed 2026-11-14) [C housing.areas]. Rainy seasons: late March–July and mid-September–late October, when roads can become impassable (France Diplomatie) [C climate.daily].
- Power cuts are called frequent; check generator or solar back-up, the Cashpower prepaid meter and water pressure before signing (Expat.com, 2025-12-08) [C housing.search][C daily.power]. Some neighbourhoods have water-supply problems [C daily.water].
- Lease law: a 2022 decree caps the deposit (caution) at three months' rent, applied first in Grand Lomé only (Bénin Web TV, 2022-02-10, reporting the official explainer session) [5]; a 2026 consultancy blog says the deposit and the rent guarantee are each capped at three months, six in total, and that agency fees run about one month's rent [4]. Expat.com still reports 2–3 months' deposit plus rent paid quarterly or half-yearly in advance [C housing.search]. The decree text itself was not read.

## Neighbourhoods

### Kodjoviakopé (4th arrondissement, seafront, west)
- Feel: an old fishing quarter bounded by the Ghana border, the ocean and the former Governors' Palace; long known as the "quartier des blancs", with colonial-era houses, an ageing local population and newcomers from Europe, Lebanon and China; weekend beach bars with a mixed crowd (Wikipédia, accessed 2026-11-15) [12]. A 2026 consultancy blog calls it relaxed and international, popular with NGO staff, consultants, remote workers and childless couples [3].
- Rent: the top of the classified market is here: 6-bedroom duplex villas with pool 1,500,000–1,700,000 XOF (≈ €2,287–2,592), CoinAfrique asking prices 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; no figure for smaller homes in this quarter. The consultancy blog suggests studios and small furnished flats here for stays of a few months [4] and places Kodjoviakopé in the upper range without figures [3]. City-centre furnished studio 150,000–250,000 XOF (≈ €229–381), Expat.com 2025-12-08 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: central, next to the administrative quarter and the Grand Marché [12]; the Aflao border crossing is under 2 km from the centre [1]. Taxi 1,000–3,000 XOF a ride, zem 300–1,000 XOF (Expat.com 2025-12-08) [C daily.transport]; Wikivoyage: 2,000 XOF gets you almost anywhere [1].
- Watch: the seafront frame above applies in full. Wikipédia says the border sectors sometimes have a poor reputation (encyclopedic, uncited; no official source names the quarter) [12]; Wikivoyage advises leaving the beach bar near the Ghana border soon after dark [1].
- Places:
  - Le Galion, Rue Camomille (French-owned hotel-restaurant near the beach; mains 3,000–5,000 XOF, set menu 6,500; rooms 7,000–15,000 XOF, fan or air-con) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Al Sultan's, Kodjoviakopé (Lebanese, most plates 1,000–2,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Christ-Roi church and the Catholic mission founded by Comboni missionaries — Wikipédia [12]
  - Beach bars La Cour des Grands, Le Gouverneur and Bronco City (busy at weekends; beer, grilled chicken and fish) — Wikipédia [12]
  - Embassy of Germany (Boulevard de la République) — Wikipédia [12]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Le Palais de Lomé, Avenue des Nîmes (former Governors' Palace, now a cultural site; daily 10:00–17:00, 2,000–7,000 XOF by nationality and day) — Wikivoyage [1]; it bounds the quarter to the east [12]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple" (the blog's remote-worker and childless-couple crowd) [3]; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (Le Galion, beach bars); Q8 "À la plage" for terraces, not swimming; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Tokoin, especially Tokoin Hôpital (2nd and 5th arrondissements, north of the centre)
- Feel: a former quarter north of the centre, now split into Wuiti, Tamé, Forever, Noukafou, Solidarité, Ouest, Hôpital, Gbadago, Lycée and Élavagnon; home to the airport, a military camp and the CHU (Wikipédia) [11]. Expat.com calls Tokoin the diplomatic quarter [C housing.areas]; the consultancy blog describes calmer streets, secured villas, families and international staff, and calls it overpriced for local budgets [3].
- Rent: villas 800,000–1,500,000 XOF (≈ €1,220–2,287), Expat.com 2025-12-08 [C cost.rent]; at Tokoin Hôpital, a 3-bed single-storey villa 300,000 XOF (≈ €457), CoinAfrique asking 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; a 5-bed duplex villa 750,000 XOF (≈ €1,143) and a 6-bed high-end villa 1,800,000 XOF, both "negotiable", Expat.com listings about two months old on 2026-11-15 [8]. Tokoin Forever: 5-room villa 600,000 XOF (≈ €915) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the airport is about 5 km north-east of the centre [10]; taxi 1,000–3,000 XOF [C daily.transport]. No door-to-door commute time sourced.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - CHU Sylvanus Olympio, 198 rue de l'Hôpital (public reference hospital, 1,138 theoretical beds; surgical emergencies renovated with UNDP in 2022) — Wikipédia [16]; [C health.care]
  - École Arc-en-Ciel, Tokoin Hôpital (French school, maternelle to lycée) — consultancy blog [3]; fees 2–4 M XOF/year for French-curriculum schools [C family.schools]
  - Université de Lomé (founded 1970; about 44,500 students in 2015) — Wikipédia places it in the north of the city [19]; Wikipedia (en) lists it under Tokoin [10]
  - Lomé–Tokoin International Airport — Wikipédia [11]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hôtel Tourist, Tokoin, near the Cap Esso station (air-con or fan rooms) — Wikivoyage fr [2]

- Getting around: central [C housing.areas]; zem 300–1,000 XOF, shared route taxis 200–400 XOF [C daily.transport][1].
- Watch: the blog advises prudence at night without specifics [3]; no official or media source names Bè.

### Adidogomé and Agoè (north-west and north outskirts)
- Feel: Expat.com calls Adidogomé a developing, calm residential area and Agoè greener and cheaper, where a car is needed [C housing.areas]. The consultancy blog: Adidogomé is fast-growing with more space and suits families and long-stay residents on mid-range budgets; Agoè is suburban, semi-rural, with large gardens, families, students and young workers near several universities (unnamed) [3]. Both sit outside the five historic arrondissements; Adidogomé is a former quarter now subdivided [9].
- Rent: Adidogomé villas 400,000–800,000 XOF (≈ €610–1,220), Expat.com 2025-12-08 [C cost.rent]; Adidogomé studios from about 115,000 XOF (≈ €175), furnished 2–3-bed flats 230,000–450,000 (≈ €351–686), furnished 3–4-bed villas 500,000–1,000,000 (≈ €762–1,524), consultancy blog 2026-05-13 [3]; Agoè furnished villas 500,000–1,000,000 XOF in the sought-after parts [3]; a flat at Agoè Kitidjan 65,000 XOF (≈ €99), CoinAfrique asking 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]. Sale: villa at Adidogomé IPG 90,000,000 XOF asking; plot at Agoè Assiyéyé 25,000,000 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: car needed in Agoè [C housing.areas]; the blog recommends a car in Adidogomé too [3]. The Blueline commuter train ran a Lomé–Cacavéli service from 2014 [C daily.transport]; whether it still runs, and where Cacavéli sits relative to these quarters, was not sourced.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places: only one could be sourced:
  - Bar La Fierté, Agoè Anomé, across from Hôtel Maison Blanche (Togolese dishes, delivery, daily 09:00–23:00) — Wikivoyage en [1]; Wikivoyage fr gives Boulevard de la CEDEAO (2022) [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" or "Avec mes enfants" (space, gardens) and Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" for those who will drive [3][C housing.areas]; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Baguida and Avépozo (east, coast towards Aného)
- Feel: a suburb about 13 km east of Lomé, in the commune of Golfe 7 since 2019 per its own page, while the Golfe 6 page lists Baguida and Avépozo among its villages (conflict unresolved); German colonial Togo's first capital until 1893; fishing beach at Avépozo (Wikipédia) [14][15]. The consultancy blog: calm, beachfront, far from the main international schools, liked by expatriates, freelancers and retirees, with rainy-season traffic making commutes harder [3].
- Rent: $850–1,700 (USD as stated) with caretaking, consultancy blog 2026-05-13 [3]; a 7-bed prestige villa at Baguida "Plantation" 2,500,000 XOF (≈ €3,811), "negotiable", Expat.com about two months before 2026-11-15 [8]; a 3-bed villa on the village side rented for 100,000 XOF after negotiation, Expat.com forum, about 2017 (OLD) [7]. Land: a plot at Baguida 40,000,000 XOF asking [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 13 km east of Lomé on the coastal national road [14]; Hotel Clementine describes itself as 10 minutes from the port and 15 from the centre (2020) [2]. Taxi 1,000–3,000 XOF a ride [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the beach frame applies; this is the stretch east of the port where France Diplomatie says arranged private beaches are the only reasonable swimming spots [C nature.access]. Coastal erosion is documented city-wide (≈ 12 m a year) [C housing.areas].
- Places:
  - Plages de Baguida et Avépozo (fine sand, several private beaches, busy at weekends; 2018 listing) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Hotel Clementine, Route nationale Lomé–Cotonou, Baguida (16 rooms from 12,000 XOF, under 500 m from the beach; 2020) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Centenaire germano-togolais monument, Baguida — Wikipédia [14]
  - Baguida square (place) — Wikipédia [15]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage" (private beaches); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Télétravail" (if the commute does not matter); Q4 "En couple"; not Q4 "Avec mes enfants" if the children attend an international school [3].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Before arrival: the e-visa from voyage.gouv.tg at least 5 days ahead; no visa on arrival since 12 April 2024; yellow-fever certificate mandatory (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [C admin.visa]. Wikivoyage (en) still describes a visa on arrival at the airport and the Ghana border [1]: outdated, follow France Diplomatie. A travel wiki quotes the e-visa at 35,000 XOF for 15 days (low confidence) [C admin.visa]. Bring a Visa-network card (France Diplomatie) [C daily.money].
- Where to stay first: Le Galion, Kodjoviakopé (7,000–15,000 XOF a night) [1], Villa Caliendi, Nyékonakpoè (25,000–50,000 XOF with breakfast, 2020) [2] or Kemi Guesthouse, Moyama, facing the Ghana embassy (from 13,000 XOF, 2020) [2]. The blog suggests the centre, Cité du Bénin or Kodjoviakopé for stays of a few months [4].
- Day 1: airport about 5 km from the centre [10]; taxi, fare agreed before boarding [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 2: SIM and data: a 10 GB plan with calls about 12,500 XOF a month [C daily.internet]; operators Togocom (Yas, Mixx money) and Moov (Flooz, figures unverified) [D][C daily.money]. Install Gozem [23].
- Day 3: viewings. Expect a deposit capped at three months in Grand Lomé (2022 decree) [5], rent often asked quarterly or half-yearly in advance, an agency fee of about one month, and a one-year lease with three months' notice [C housing.search][4]. Never pay before seeing the property (2017, OLD) [C cost.negotiation]. Visit fees ("frais de visite") were not found in any source.
- Day 4: test the commute from the flat you like at rush hour; check generator, Cashpower meter, water pressure and internet [C housing.search].
- Day 5: bank account: a 2017 guide asked for a passport or residence card, proof of address, a phone number, two photos and a 10,000–20,000 XOF opening deposit (OLD) [C daily.money]. Stays beyond 90 days need a long-stay visa then a carte de séjour (DGDN) [C admin.residency].
- Day 6: meet people: Français du Monde-ADFE's first-Saturday drop-in, UFE [C community.expat]; Institut français programme [22].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur, then the Grand Marché area [21][20].
- Rough first-week spend: 7 nights at Le Galion × 7,000–15,000 = 49,000–105,000 + mobile plan 12,500 + 14 meals × 1,700–2,450 = 23,800–34,300 + 12 taxi rides × 1,000–3,000 = 12,000–36,000 → **≈ 97,300–187,800 XOF (≈ €148–286)**, before the visa and any lease advance [1][C daily.internet][C cost.living][C daily.transport].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read the frames at the top (seafront at night, the sea, moto-taxis) first. E-visa required before travel [C admin.visa].

### One day
- Morning: Grand Marché, Adawlato (wax cloth, spices; Nana Benz on the first floor) and the Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur next to it (built 1901–1902) [20][21][1]; then the Centre Artisanal (bargaining expected) [1]. Short taxi hops, about 1,000–2,000 XOF [C daily.transport][1].
- Midday: Al Sultan's, Kodjoviakopé (Lebanese, 1,000–2,000 XOF) [1] or a street plate of rice (≈ 200 XOF) [1].
- Afternoon: Le Palais de Lomé, Avenue des Nîmes (use the rear entrance; 2,000–7,000 XOF; last entry 16:15) [1]; then the Independence Monument and the National Museum in the Palais des Congrès (1,500 XOF) [1].
- Evening: dinner at Le Galion, Rue Camomille (set menu 6,500 XOF) [1]; taxi home, no walking on the seafront after dark [C safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Akodésséwa fetish market (3,000 XOF; tourist-trap prices on souvenirs) [1][C cost.negotiation]; Musée International du Golfe de Guinée, 1603 Boulevard du Mono (Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00, 3,000 XOF; may have been renamed) [1].
- Day 3 (Lac Togo and Togoville): the lake is about 15 km east; Agbodrafo on the south shore faces Togoville; the 1884 German protectorate was signed by the lake [24]. In Togoville: the Notre-Dame du Lac Togo church (1910) and the house of a descendant of King Mlapa III [25]. Boat fares not found.
- Day 4 (Aného): about 45 km east; Guin-Mina and Vodun heritage, cathedral, the Glidji royal palace and temple, on UNESCO's tentative list since 2000 [26]; see `research/cities/togo/aneho.md`. Bush taxis from the Grand Marché or Hollando stations (2017, OLD) [C daily.transport].
- Day 5 (daily-life moment): a weekday morning commute from Tokoin to the centre by Gozem or shared route taxi (200–400 XOF) to feel the traffic residents live with [1][C daily.transport], then a viewing of a Tokoin Hôpital or Nyékonakpoè flat; lunch at a maquis (grilled food, local beer) [C social.nightlife].
- Day 6 (Kpalimé, overnight): 2 hours by road, bush taxis from Kodomé (2017, OLD) [C daily.transport]; Kpimé falls (2,500 XOF) and the cathedral; see `kpalime.neighbourhoods.md`. Return before dark (France advises against night driving) [D].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur [21]; brunch at Le Namiélé (25,000 XOF) [1] or a private beach at Baguida [2]; in the evening, if one is on, a Lomé first-division match (AS OTR, Dynamic Togolais, etc.; fixtures not found) [27].
