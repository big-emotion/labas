# Kpalimé, Togo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**. USD figures are kept as stated and not converted. Research limitation: web search was unavailable for this whole pass (shared budget exhausted), so items come only from pages opened directly; Kpalimé-specific local media, blogs and listings could not be found that way.

## Items

- [cost.rent] An expat guide gives rents of 100,000–300,000 XOF/month (≈ €152–457) in Kpalimé, without specifying the type of home. — Expat.com Guide Togo, updated 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The same guide says rents outside Lomé are generally 30–50 % lower than in the capital (a general estimate, not specific to Kpalimé). — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] An aggregator lists (USD as stated) a 40 m² 1-bed in the centre at $240/month and a "cheap" 1-bed at $112; an 80 m² 3-bed at $440 centre / $199 cheap. Its figures are nearly identical to its Lomé page, which suggests modelled rather than observed local data. — livingcost.org Kpalimé, updated 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A classifieds portal has a dedicated Kpalimé property page, but it showed no listings on the day it was opened. — CoinAfrique Togo, /ville/kpalime/immobilier, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Aggregator monthly totals (USD as stated): $665 for one person and $1,569 for a family of four, rent included; $403 / $1,156 without rent. — livingcost.org Kpalimé, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Aggregator item prices (USD as stated): lunch menu $3.32, dinner for two $29, 0.5 L beer in a bar $0.93, 1 kg rice $1.31, 12 eggs $2.12; 50 Mbps+ home internet $45.90/month. — livingcost.org Kpalimé, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] An older expat guide only said rural prices are "nettement plus bas" (clearly lower) than in Lomé, with no figures. OLD (2017). — Expat.com "Se loger au Togo", 2017-10-03, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Aggregator median monthly after-tax salary: $125 (USD as stated), no source or method given. — livingcost.org Kpalimé, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] At the Kpimé waterfall, a travel wiki warns that guides "try to charge twice" the 2,500 XOF (≈ €3.81) entry fee. — Wikivoyage "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14 (latest entry update 2025-11), blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Bush-taxi fares are negotiated with the driver; the 2017 guide put them at about 3,000 XOF per 100 km (≈ €4.57) and gave no Lomé–Kpalimé fare. OLD. — Expat.com "Moyens de transport au Togo", 2017-10-04, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Historic quarters include Domé (Fiakomé), the first settlement, and Numétukodzi, Atakpamékodzi and Zomayi (formerly Avénuié, Dzigbé and Agnigbé). — Wikipédia (fr) "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The area around Kpalimé produces over 80 % of Togo's coffee and cocoa. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Kpalimé is Togo's main crafts centre (wood sculpture, weaving, batik, pottery, painting), with 36 artisan workshops and shops and an artisanal training college. — Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low (article tagged for missing citations)
- [work.remote] The expat guide suggests Kpalimé suits retirees, artists and remote workers looking for calm and nature. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] The town has a post office, several banks, cyber-cafés, medical centres, pharmacies and petrol stations. — Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Kpalimé is about 120 km north-west of Lomé, roughly 2 hours by road, and 15 km from the Ghana border, at a crossroads of roads to other regions and to Ghana. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kpalimé" and Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Bush taxis to Kpalimé leave from the Kodomé station in Lomé. OLD (2017). — Expat.com "Moyens de transport au Togo", 2017-10-04, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Lomé–Kpalimé railway opened in 1907 but has not worked for many years. — Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] France advises against night driving between cities (no lighting, almost no signage, rare armed road robberies) and warns that roads can become impassable in the rainy seasons; a 4×4 is recommended on secondary roads. — France Diplomatie, Togo security advice, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] The expat guide describes a "basic" regional hospital. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] The Association Saint-Camille-de-Lellis runs a community psychiatry centre in Kpalimé. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] No international school in Kpalimé, per the expat guide; a scientific lycée is listed in the town. — Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08) and Wikipedia "Kpalimé" (accessed 2026-11-14), company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Kpalimé (Plateaux region) is in France's yellow "heightened vigilance" zone with no city-specific warning; the orange and red zones are in the far north. — France Diplomatie, Togo security advice, updated 2026-09-15 (map 2026-03-13), official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Local specialities are palm wine and grilled chicken or fish with fufu and peanut sauce; a travel wiki lists Le Bon Vivant (Belgian food and beer) and Macumba (Togolese dishes such as goat soup and fufu). — Wikipedia "Kpalimé" and Wikivoyage "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] The expat guide says there are few restaurants; a travel wiki lists no nightlife beyond hotel bars. — Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08) and Wikivoyage "Kpalimé" (accessed 2026-11-14), company, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Seat of a Catholic diocese (since 1994) with the neo-Gothic Cathédrale Saint-Esprit (1913); a Benedictine monastery stands at Dzogbégan. — Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] About 101,088 people (2023), Togo's fourth-largest town; Ewe is the main local language and French is spoken by anyone who went to school. — Wikipédia (fr) and Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] The expat guide sums up the town as "calme et nature". — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] About 1,442 mm of rain a year over ~111 rain days; more than 100 mm a month from March to October, wettest in September (~207 mm), driest in January (~15 mm). No temperature data in the source. — Wikipedia "Kpalimé" (1991–2020 normals), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] A travel wiki describes the Kpalimé coffee region as cooler than the coast. — Wikivoyage "Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Mont Agou (986 m, Togo's highest point; summit is a military area with a communications relay) and Mont Kloto (825 m) are nearby; the Misahöhe forest reserve covers 5,000 ha with 67 recorded bird species. — Wikipédia (fr) "Mont Agou", Wikipedia "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Waterfall access: Kpimé falls 2,500 XOF per person, but the water is diverted to a dam and can shrink to a trickle in the dry season; Womé falls 2,500 XOF, no guides on site, a 4×4 is best for the track, swimming possible. — Wikivoyage "Kpalimé", accessed 2026-11-14 (Womé entry updated 2025-11), blog, confidence: low
