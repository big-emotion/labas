# Kara, Togo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**; USD figures are kept as stated. ## Items

- [cost.rent] An expat guide gives rents of 100,000–300,000 XOF/month (≈ €152–457) in Kara, type of home not specified. — Expat.com Guide Togo, updated 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] An aggregator lists (USD as stated) a 40 m² 1-bed in the centre at $212/month and a "cheap" 1-bed at $104; an 80 m² 3-bed at $376 centre / $187 cheap. Figures closely track its Lomé page, suggesting modelled data. — livingcost.org Kara, updated 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A classifieds portal has a Kara property page, but it showed no listings on the day it was opened. — CoinAfrique Togo, /ville/kara/immobilier, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] The expat guide says the cost of living is 30–40 % below Lomé. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Aggregator monthly totals (USD as stated): $661 for one person and $1,566 for a family of four, rent included; lunch menu $3.34, 0.5 L beer in a bar $0.94. — livingcost.org Kara, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Travel-wiki prices: Hotel Kara singles from 18,500 XOF (≈ €28) and dinner 6,000 XOF (≈ €9.15); Gîtes chez Noël meals 3,000–7,000 XOF (≈ €4.57–10.67); a local eatery opposite the market about $1 a meal, a German-run restaurant about $5 for a meal with a beer. — Wikivoyage "Kara", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Aggregator median monthly after-tax salary: $126 (USD as stated), no method given. — livingcost.org Kara, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Bush-taxi fares to the north are negotiated with the driver; intercity bus Lomé–Kara was about 6,000 XOF (≈ €9.15). OLD (2017). — Expat.com "Moyens de transport au Togo", 2017-10-04, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Quarters include Tomdè, Tchintchinda, Yéou, Lama-Feing, Kara-Ville (centre), Dongoyo, Kara-Sud and Tchitchao; the Kara river splits the city, with the centre on the north bank. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kara (Togo)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The expat guide calls job opportunities limited, mainly NGOs and agricultural projects. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Seven agricultural processing centres of the Kara agropole are preparing to start operating. — Togo First, c. 2026-11-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The Université de Kara (opened 2004) has five faculties including health sciences, two campuses (Kara and Pya) and about 20,000 students (undated figure). — Wikipédia (fr) "Université de Kara", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Kara sits 413 km north of Lomé at the crossroads of the Lomé–Ouagadougou and Bassar–Parakou roads; international bus lines (Eltrab, LK, Adji, Rakieta) run to Lomé, Ouagadougou and Niamey. — Wikipedia "Kara, Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A daily Rakieta bus leaves at 7 am and takes about 7 hours to Lomé. — Wikivoyage "Kara", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Niamtougou international airport is about 40 km north of Kara and is being developed. — Wikipedia "Kara, Togo" and Wikipédia (fr), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A travel wiki calls the hilly road north of Kara "particularly dangerous". — Wikivoyage "Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.water] The Kara river is the city's main water source. — Wikipedia "Kara, Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [health.care] The expat guide lists a public regional hospital and a few private clinics. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Few international schools, per the expat guide. — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Belgium advises vigilance for travel and stays in the Kara region and formally discourages the Burkina and Benin border zones. — Belgian FPS Foreign Affairs, Togo sécurité générale, accessed 2026-11-14 (dossier records update 2026-10-06), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France classes the country outside the Savanes region in yellow (heightened vigilance) and does not name the Kara region; the Savanes region to the north is orange, under a state of emergency until 13 March 2027, and foreigners need written Armed Forces authorisation to go there. — France Diplomatie, Togo security advice, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against travel within 30 km of the Burkina Faso border (except Dapaong and the N1) and all but essential travel to the rest of the Savanes; it does not mention Kara. — UK FCDO, Togo travel advice, updated 2026-11-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Nearby Sokodé, Bafilo and Mango have a history of violent demonstrations that injured and killed protesters and security forces. — UK FCDO, Togo safety and security, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Evala, the Kabiye male initiation rite built around wrestling, takes place each July in the Kozah prefecture (Kara), usually from the second to the third Saturday, with concerts, dances and theatre; the head of state traditionally attends the finals. — Wikipédia (fr) "Evala" and Wikipedia "Kara, Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] A travel wiki names two bars long popular with Peace Corps volunteers (Chateau, with air conditioning, pool table and darts, and La Besace). — Wikivoyage "Kara", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Mostly Christian churches (Catholic, Presbyterian, Baptist, Pentecostal) and several mosques for a large Muslim population. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kara (Togo)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Kabiye is the main local language; Tem, Zarma, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo are common in the main market; most residents farm (maize, cassava, yams, sorghum, groundnuts; cotton as cash crop). — Wikipedia "Kara, Togo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Population 94,878 in 2010; the French article's infobox gives 158,090 for the 2022 census. The expat guide describes a peaceful setting away from urban bustle. — Wikipédia (fr) "Kara (Togo)" and Expat.com Guide Togo (2025-12-08), academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hot and seasonal: mean maximums 35–38 °C from November to March (38 °C in March), rains of ~1,300 mm a year concentrated June–September (August ~261 mm), almost none December–January. — Wikipedia "Kara, Togo" (1991–2020 normals), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] The expat guide calls Kara's climate "plus tempéré que le sud". — Expat.com Guide Togo, 2025-12-08, company, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Parc Sarakawa (crocodiles, antelopes, buffalo) offers guided tours at 5,000 XOF per person plus 4,000 XOF per vehicle; the Koutammakou tower-houses (UNESCO) are about 2 hours north, with no amenities. — Wikivoyage "Kara", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
