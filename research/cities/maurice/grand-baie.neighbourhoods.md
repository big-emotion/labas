# Grand Baie: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate: the dossier's **1 EUR ≈ 52.5 MUR** (`research/countries/maurice.md`, implied by Numbeo, November 2026). "[city file]" means `research/cities/maurice/grand-baie.md`; "[PL file]" means `research/cities/maurice/port-louis.md`; bracketed numbers refer to "Sources read" at the end. The session's shared web-search budget was already spent when this pass started, so everything below comes from the city files, the dossier and pages fetched directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Westimmo, Expat.com, OpenShores, C-Care). Grand Baie rent sources disagree widely (a 2-bed from Rs 18,000 to Rs 70,000); ranges are quoted as found, never averaged.

Shared caveats for every neighbourhood below:

- Size: a coastal village of 11,910 people (2011 census) run by a village council in Rivière du Rempart district; its western part lies in Pamplemousses district (Wikipedia) [5][city file]. Village councils were to be dissolved on 22 Nov 2026 (Le Mauricien) [city file].
- Safety: FCDO names Grand Baie among tourist areas where petty crime is common, mostly villa burglaries and bag theft; it advises renting only Tourism Authority-registered accommodation and not walking alone on beaches or in poorly lit areas at night, and reports some sexual assaults on tourists (read 2026-11-14) [city file]. France names beaches, beach car parks and isolated places as risk spots and says assaults have risen since 2025 (2026-09-15) [city file].
- Sea: strong currents regularly cause drownings, and beach water-sports touts rarely carry liability insurance (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [city file]; many beaches have sea urchins, so wear water shoes (Wikivoyage) [3].
- Getting around: not on the Metro [city file]; a car is "almost essential" outside Port-Louis and the main roads (Westimmo, 2026-09-16) [city file]; car hire from about Rs 1,000/day for a basic manual (Expat.com, 2026-02-20) [4]. Port-Louis is 20 minutes by motorway (Westimmo), 25 minutes (Wikivoyage) or 30 minutes (Expat.com) [city file][3]; express bus from the northern terminal in Port-Louis [1], about Rs 50 (OpenShores, 2026-03-10) [city file]. Grand Baie itself is small enough to walk, and the coastal walk is pleasant (Wikivoyage) [1]. Taxis have no meters, agree the fare first [city file].
- Up-front cash: about three months' rent for a 1-year lease (deposit, agency commission, first month), a Westimmo benchmark, not a rule (2026-05-13) [city file]; start searching 4–8 weeks before moving in [city file]. December is high season: a Péreybère property took bookings at Rs 25,000 a night (≈ €476) [city file].

## Neighbourhoods

### Centre: La Croisette and the Royal Road

- Feel: Westimmo describes a lively centre around La Croisette, with shops and restaurants on foot (2026-11-11) [city file][6]. Expat.com calls Grand Baie a lively seaside town with full amenities and nightlife (2026-02-17) [2]. Wikivoyage calls it "a shopping and leisure paradise" and the place Mauritians go out at night (undated) [1].
- Rent: observed 1-bed listing around Rs 25,000/month (≈ €476) (Westimmo, 2026-05-13) [city file]; 1-bed Rs 25,000–35,000 (≈ €476–667) (Nucamp, 2026-04-19, low confidence) [city file]; modest apartments from about Rs 25,000 (Expat.com, 2026-03-17) [city file].
- Getting around: on foot inside the village [1]; express bus to Port-Louis [1].
- Watch: petty crime and bag theft in tourist areas (FCDO, read 2026-11-14) [city file]. Wikipedia records an escalator accident at La Croisette on 23 Nov 2024 [5] (a single incident, not a pattern).
- Places:
  - Grand-Baie La Croisette (shopping mall, the island's third) — Wikipedia [5], Westimmo [6]
  - Super U (large supermarket with fruit, vegetables and takeaway grilled chicken; listing link dead) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - La Vieille Rouge (small fish restaurant and bar) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Banana Beach Club (bar and restaurant) — Westimmo [7][city file]
  - C-Care Grand Baie (private hospital; ambulance 118; address not shown on the pages read) — C-Care [8][city file]
  - Shiv Kalyannath Mandir (Hindu temple) and Vadapazhanee Murugan Kovil (Tamil temple; Thaipusam is celebrated in Grand Baie) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Adolphe de Plevitz State Secondary School — Wikipedia [5]
  - Hotel La Residence (budget hotel, rooms from €20 a night) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (the island's night-out village [1]); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" by Mauritian standards; Q5 "Télétravail"; Q8 "À la plage". Not Q3 "Serré".

### Seafront and the quiet back streets (La Cuvette side)

- Feel: Westimmo splits the rest of Grand Baie into the seafront, for bay views, and quieter residential streets set back from the action, a few minutes' walk from the beach, with larger plots (2026-11-11) [6].
- Rent: a well-located 2-bed Rs 60,000–70,000/month (≈ €1,143–1,333) (Expat.com, 2026-03-17) [city file]; good-quality 3-bed residence about Rs 95,000 (≈ €1,810), family houses Rs 80,000–100,000 (≈ €1,524–1,905), premium above Rs 125,000 (Westimmo, 2026-05-13) [city file]; two long-term villas with pools at Rs 80,000/month each (Westimmo snapshot, 2026-11-11) [city file]. A much lower 2-bed estimate, Rs 18,000–40,000, comes from OpenShores without a source (2026-04-29) [city file].
- Getting around: a few minutes on foot to the beach and centre [6]; car for schools.
- Watch: FCDO's villa-burglary warning applies most to detached houses [city file]; no street-level remark found.
- Places:
  - La Cuvette (public beach, about 10 minutes' walk from the centre, coast guard post next to it, food and juice vendors) — Wikivoyage [1], Westimmo [6]
  - Seaman Diving Center (diving and seabed walking, La Cuvette; cheaper booked directly than through downtown agents) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Veranda Grand-Baie Hotel & Spa and Royal Palm (hotels on the Royal Road) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Catholic church of Grand Baie (name not given) — Wikipedia [5]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple" (larger plots, quiet streets [6]); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage".

### Péreybère

- Feel: a former fishing village that keeps an authentic feel, less touristy than Grand Baie and sheltered from the wind; very hot summers November–March (Expat.com, 2026-02-17) [2]. Its public beach is popular for its shops, restaurants and pubs (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Rent: furnished 3-bed of 110 m² at Rs 48,000/month (≈ €914) (Westimmo north listings, undated) [city file]; a property at Rs 75,000 "Négociable" (≈ €1,429) and a contemporary villa at Rs 80,000 (≈ €1,524) (Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", 2026-11-14, agency posts) [city file].
- Getting around: on the coast road north of Grand Baie; time not sourced. Buses not sourced.
- Watch: December short-let pressure (Rs 25,000 a night, minimum stay) [city file]; beach and current warnings above.
- Places:
  - Péreybère public beach (shops, restaurants, pubs) — Wikivoyage [1][9]
  - Ocean Spirit (diving and water-sports club) — Westimmo [7][city file]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps" (less touristy [2]); Q5 "Télétravail"; Q4 "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage". Only two everyday places sourced: see Gaps.

### Pointe aux Canonniers, Mont Choisy and Trou aux Biches (west side)

- Feel: Trou aux Biches grew from a 19th-century fishing village into a small resort town with a 2 km casuarina-lined beach, family-oriented facilities and a supermarket on the B38, and is less commercialised than Grand Baie (Wikipedia) [10]. Westimmo lists new apartments and penthouses at La Pointe (Pointe aux Canonniers) [6].
- Rent: no area figure; the North's 1–2-bed apartments run Rs 25,000–45,000 (≈ €476–857) and 3-bed houses with garden or small pool Rs 80,000–100,000 (≈ €1,524–1,905) (Expat.com, 2026-02-17) [2][city file].
- Getting around: the M2 motorway from Port-Louis ends about a mile east of Trou aux Biches [10]; car or bus.
- Places:
  - Mont Choisy beach (about 2 km long) — Wikivoyage [9], Westimmo [6]
  - Trou aux Biches beach (snorkelling reefs, sunset spot) — Wikipedia [10], Wikivoyage [9]
  - Maheswarnath Mandir (inland from Trou aux Biches; described as the island's largest Hindu temple, founded 1888) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Le Canonnier resort (private peninsula; kids' club in an old lighthouse) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Mauritius Aquarium (about 200 species, Pointe aux Piments; link dead) — Wikivoyage [9]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (family beach town [10]); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" or "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (nearest to the motorway into Port-Louis [10]).

### Cap Malheureux, Grand Gaube and Calodyne (far north)

- Feel: Cap Malheureux is a village of 5,070 people (2011) [11]; Calodyne is small and quiet, close to the beach, for people who want calm without being cut off (Expat.com, 2026-02-17) [2]; a Calodyne development is listed 15 minutes from Grand Baie (Westimmo) [6].
- Rent: a single-storey house in a residence at Cap Malheureux Rs 60,000/month (≈ €1,143), available January 2027 (Facebook group, 2026-11-14) [city file]; in Grand Gaube a furnished 30 m² studio Rs 26,000 (≈ €495) and a 3-bed of 80 m² Rs 45,000 (≈ €857) (Westimmo north listings, undated) [city file]. Groceries reported by a Grand Gaube resident: 1 kg spaghetti Rs 140, 30 eggs Rs 281, chicken drumsticks Rs 263/kg (Expat.com forum, about 8–9 months old) [city file].
- Getting around: car; about 15 minutes to Grand Baie from Calodyne [6].
- Watch: France says burglaries are frequent in isolated places (2026-09-15) [city file].
- Places:
  - Notre Dame Auxiliatrice (Catholic church, Cap Malheureux) — Wikipedia [11]
  - Paradise Cove Hotel & Spa (Anse La Raie, north end of the bay) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Gabriel Island, Flat Island and Gunner's Quoin (islets reached by catamaran) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Télétravail"; Q3 "Moyen" (the cheapest sourced asking rents on this coast). Only three places, none everyday: see Gaps.

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly spends use only prices already in the city files or this pass, at 52.5 MUR/EUR. A month becomes a week as × 12 ÷ 52 (≈ 0.231). Groceries use OpenShores' Port-Louis weekly basket of Rs 2,000–4,500 for one person [PL file], because no Grand Baie basket was found; the north is described as dearer [city file], so read the grocery line as a floor. Electricity uses CEB residential tariffs (1 May 2026): Rs 2,075 for 300 kWh, Rs 4,481 for 500 kWh, Rs 8,165 for 800 kWh; water Rs 45–310 a month (Westimmo, 2026-09-16) [PL file]. Restaurant prices are national (Expat.com, 2026-02-20) [4]: standard restaurant about Rs 400 a head, business lunch about Rs 350, upscale dinner for two Rs 2,000–6,000, local beer 0.3 L about Rs 75. No Grand Baie venue publishes prices in the pages read.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Péreybère or the centre. Grand Baie is the island's most popular expat hub (Expat.com, 2026-03-17) [city file], and remote work is the realistic option here (Premium Visa: USD 1,500/month, no local job) [city file]. No coworking space was found.
- Monday: work from home; groceries at Super U [1].
- Tuesday: early swim at La Cuvette (coast guard post) [1].
- Wednesday: work; sunset at Péreybère public beach [1].
- Thursday: dive with Ocean Spirit or Seaman Diving Center (prices not found) [7][1].
- Friday: errands at La Croisette [5].
- Saturday: catamaran to Gabriel or Flat Island (price not found) [3].
- Sunday: Port-Louis by express bus (about Rs 50) for the market and the Caudan [1][city file].
- Saturday night, by Q2 answer:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Grand Baie is where Mauritians go for bars and discos (Wikivoyage) [1]; Banana Beach Club is the only bar named in the sources [7]. Taxi home, fare agreed first [city file].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Vieille Rouge (fish) [1], or restaurants at Péreybère beach [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; the coast walk at dusk, never alone on the beach at night (FCDO) [city file].
- Weekend outing, by Q8 answer:
  - "À la plage": La Cuvette, Péreybère, Mont Choisy, Trou aux Biches [1][9][10].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Château de Labourdonnais orchards in Rivière du Rempart, with mountain-bike trips and hikes [1]; Le Pouce (812 m) above Port-Louis [PL file]; Black River Gorges in the southwest is a full day by car (trail from the Pétrin information centre) [3].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Grand Baie; Mauritius has neither. Casela park near Flic en Flac is the nearest substitute (Rs 1,525 adult at peak) [4][9].
  - "Je reste en ville": La Croisette and the village centre [5]; Pamplemousses botanical garden and L'Aventure du Sucre (daily 09:00–18:00) inland [12].
- Weekly spend (MUR):
  - Rent: 1-bed 25,000 (Westimmo) to 35,000 (Nucamp) a month [city file] → 5,769–8,077
  - Groceries 2,000–4,500 (Port-Louis basket, a floor) [PL file]
  - Electricity 300–500 kWh, 2,075–4,481/month → 479–1,034; water 45–310/month → 10–72 [PL file]
  - my.t internet from 1,225/month → 283 [PL file]
  - 2 days' car hire × 1,000–1,500 = 2,000–3,000 [4]
  - 3 restaurant meals × 400 = 1,200; 3 local beers × 75 = 225 [4]
  - **Total ≈ Rs 11,966–18,390 a week ≈ €228–350.**

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"

- Base: the quiet back streets or the Trou aux Biches side, for houses with gardens and family beaches [6][10]. International schools are about 15 minutes away (Expat.com) [2]: Northfields International High School (Mapou) and International Preparatory School (Labourdonnais) [city file]. A state secondary school, Adolphe de Plevitz, is in the village [5]. No French-curriculum school near Grand Baie was verified.
- Monday–Friday: school run by car (about 15 minutes) [2]; groceries at Super U [1].
- Wednesday: after-school swim at La Cuvette, by the coast guard post [1].
- Saturday: Château de Labourdonnais orchards, or Pamplemousses garden (giant water lilies) and L'Aventure du Sucre [1][12].
- Sunday: Trou aux Biches beach [10]; Mass at the village church or a temple visit [5].
- Health: C-Care Grand Baie private hospital, ambulance 118 (no prices published) [8][city file]; emergency SAMU 114, police 999 or 112 [3]; complex cases may need evacuation to Réunion or South Africa (FCDO) [PL file]. Dengue: use mosquito protection all year [3].
- Saturday night, by Q2 answer: "Danser jusqu'au matin" the village bars and discos with a babysitter (no price found) [1]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" La Vieille Rouge [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" home.
- Weekend outing, by Q8 answer: "À la plage" Trou aux Biches or Mont Choisy [9][10]; "En montagne, en forêt" Château de Labourdonnais [1]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic from Grand Baie, Casela as a substitute (Rs 1,525 adult, Rs 1,255 child at peak) [4]; "Je reste en ville" La Croisette [5].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Before arriving: no visa under 3 months for French passports; return ticket and USD 100/day may be asked; longer stays or work need a permit arranged before departure (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [city file]. Avoid arriving in December if you can: short-let prices peak (Rs 25,000 a night in Péreybère) [city file].
- Day 1: airport taxi Rs 1,200–1,800 to Port-Louis (OpenShores) [PL file]; the Grand Baie leg was not priced. Buy an Emtel or my.t SIM or eSIM at the airport, from Rs 700 a month, passport needed [3]. Check in at Hotel La Residence (from €20 a night, undated listing) [1]; FCDO advises Tourism Authority-registered accommodation only [city file].
- Day 2: hire a car (from Rs 1,000/day) [4]; drive the bay: centre, La Cuvette, Péreybère [1].
- Day 3: flat hunt through agencies (Westimmo lists north long-term rentals), L'Express Property, Expat.com ads and the Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", where 5 of 14 priced posts said "négociable" or similar [city file][2]. Visit fees: none found.
- Day 4: viewings in Péreybère, Grand Gaube and the back streets; ask what the rent covers (pool, garden, internet, water, electricity, security, parking, minor repairs) [city file]; inventory and photos [2].
- Day 5: banking: MCB's Juice app handles transfers and QR payments [13]; whether a short-stay non-resident can open an account was not found.
- Day 6: drive to Port-Louis (20–30 minutes) to compare city life [city file].
- Day 7: Sunday at the beach and at Cap Malheureux church; decide whether to sign [11].
- Week-one spend (MUR):
  - Hotel La Residence 7 nights × €20 = €140 ≈ 7,350 [1]
  - SIM 700 [3]; airport taxi 1,200–1,800 (to Port-Louis) [PL file]
  - Groceries 2,000–4,500 [PL file]; 5 restaurant meals × 400 = 2,000 [4]
  - Car hire 3 days × 1,000–1,500 = 3,000–4,500 [4]
  - **Total ≈ Rs 16,250–20,850 ≈ €310–397.**
  - Up-front cost of a 1-year lease at about three months' rent (Westimmo benchmark) [city file]: Rs 75,000 on a Rs 25,000 1-bed to Rs 135,000 on a Rs 45,000 2-bed (≈ €1,429–2,571).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day

- Morning: swim at La Cuvette, 10 minutes' walk from the centre (coast guard post, juice vendors) [1]; water shoes for urchins [3].
- Midday: walk the coast road north to Péreybère public beach and eat at one of its restaurants [1].
- Afternoon: taxi (fare agreed first) to Cap Malheureux and its church, Notre Dame Auxiliatrice [11][city file].
- Evening: dinner at La Vieille Rouge, then a drink at Banana Beach Club [1][7]. Taxi back rather than walking an unlit beach (FCDO) [city file].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: catamaran to Gabriel Island and Flat Island (prices not found; book with operators, not beach touts, which France says are rarely insured) [3][city file].
- Day 3 (daily life): morning shop at Super U and La Croisette among residents [1][5]; then the express bus from Grand Baie to Port-Louis's northern terminal with commuters (about Rs 50) [1][city file], for the Central Market, Aapravasi Ghat and the Caudan [PL file].
- Day 4: Pamplemousses: SSR Botanical Garden and L'Aventure du Sucre (daily 09:00–18:00) [12]; Château de Labourdonnais orchards on the way back [1].
- Day 5: west side: Trou aux Biches beach and the Maheswarnath Mandir inland [10]; dive with Seaman Diving Center at La Cuvette instead if the sea is calm [1].
- Day 6: southwest by hired car (from Rs 1,000/day) [4]: Black River Gorges trail from the Pétrin information centre, then Chamarel's waterfall and Seven Coloured Earths [3][14]. Long day; drive times from Grand Baie not sourced.
- Day 7 (daily life): Sunday at a temple (Shiv Kalyannath Mandir) or Mass in Grand Baie or Cap Malheureux [5][11]; afternoon at Péreybère public beach [1]. If the visit falls in Thaipusam season, the festival is celebrated in Grand Baie (date not sourced) [5].
