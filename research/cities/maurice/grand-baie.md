# Grand Baie, Mauritius

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 52.5 MUR** (implied by Numbeo, November 2026, dossier source [3]). Rent sources for Grand Baie disagree widely (a 2-bed is quoted anywhere from Rs 18,000 to Rs 70,000); conflicting figures are listed separately, never averaged. The shared web-search budget ran out early in this pass, so Grand Baie rests on agency pages, Expat.com, official advisories and the social-pass listings (see Gaps).

## Items

- [cost.rent] Westimmo benchmarks for Grand Baie: an observed 1-bed listing around Rs 25,000/month (≈ €476); a good-quality 3-bed residence around Rs 95,000 (≈ €1,810); family houses often Rs 80,000–100,000 (≈ €1,524–1,905); premium listings above Rs 125,000 (≈ €2,381), with a premium segment cited at Rs 98,000–165,000 for semi-residents. — Westimmo "Tamarin / Grand Baie: rent year-round", 2026-05-13, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A well-located 2-bed in Grand Baie typically rents for Rs 60,000–70,000/month (≈ €1,143–1,333); modest apartments from about Rs 25,000, with an apartment average close to Rs 70,000 (PropertyCloud 2026 index as reported). — Expat.com Expat-Mag, 2026-03-17, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A much lower estimate: 2-bed in Grand Baie Rs 18,000–40,000/month (≈ €343–762), "often above Rs 25,000"; no source named. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère de Maurice", 2026-04-29, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Another unsourced 2026 table: 1-bed in Grand Baie Rs 25,000–35,000 (≈ €476–667), 3-bed villa Rs 75,000+ (≈ €1,429+). — Nucamp blog, updated 2026-04-19, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Westimmo's France comparison page gives Rs 60,000–70,000 (≈ €1,143–1,333) for a two-room property in Grand Baie. — Westimmo "Cost of living in Mauritius vs France 2026", undated (rates of 2026-08-31), company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Westimmo's Grand Baie stock on 11 Nov 2026 included two long-term villas with pools (a 5-bed and a 3-bed single-storey) at Rs 80,000/month each (≈ €1,524). — Westimmo "Grand Baie real estate", snapshot 2026-11-11, company, confidence: high
- [cost.rent] North-coast neighbours: a furnished 3-bed of 110 m² in Péreybère at Rs 48,000/month (≈ €914); in Grand Gaube a furnished 30 m² studio at Rs 26,000 (≈ €495) and a 3-bed of 80 m² at Rs 45,000 (≈ €857). — Westimmo north long-term rentals page, accessed 2026-11-14 (undated listings), company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Island-wide guide ranges for the North: 1–2-bed apartment Rs 25,000–45,000 (≈ €476–857); 3-bed apartment or house with garden or small pool Rs 80,000–100,000 (≈ €1,524–1,905). — Expat.com accommodation guide, 2026-02-17, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Family homes with garden in the North/West expat hubs Rs 60,000–130,000 (≈ €1,143–2,476); beachside villas Rs 80,000–150,000+ (≈ €1,524–2,857+). — Expat.com cost-of-living guide, 2026-02-20, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Holiday-season pressure: a Péreybère property was taking December 2026 bookings at Rs 25,000 a night (≈ €476) with a minimum stay and a Rs 10,000 deposit. — Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", feed read 2026-11-14, social-caption, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Entry cost for a 1-year lease is roughly three months' rent: one month's deposit, one month's agency commission and the first month (Westimmo says this is a benchmark, not an official rule). — Westimmo, 2026-05-13, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Sale asking prices in Grand Baie (11 Westimmo listings, 11 Nov 2026): apartments from about Rs 12 M (≈ €228,600; e.g. a furnished 92 m² 2-bed), houses and penthouses Rs 18–45 M (≈ €342,900–857,100), prestige beachfront villas up to about Rs 125 M (≈ €2.38 M). — Westimmo "Grand Baie real estate", 2026-11-11, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Groceries reported by a resident of nearby Grand Gaube: 1 kg spaghetti Rs 140 (≈ €2.67), pork fillet Rs 450/kg (≈ €8.57), chicken drumsticks Rs 263/kg (≈ €5.01), 30 eggs Rs 281 (≈ €5.35); vegetables described as fairly cheap. — Expat.com forum "Cost of living in Mauritius in 2026", post about 8–9 months old, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Westimmo describes Grand Baie and Tamarin as having higher rents and living costs than less touristy regions; Westimmo's own island-wide figures put a couple without children at Rs 110,000–140,000/month all-in (≈ €2,095–2,667). — Westimmo retiree budget page (undated) and France comparison page (2026), company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Of 14 priced posts in a Mauritius rental and sale group, 5 carried "négociable", "or nearest offer" or similar, on rents (e.g. a Péreybère property at Rs 75,000/month "Négociable", ≈ €1,429) as well as sales. — Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", 2026-11-14, social-caption, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Expat.com reads the 2026 PropertyCloud index (foreigner-accessible rents down 5.3%) as giving expats more bargaining power, though it calls it a window that may not last. — Expat.com Expat-Mag, 2026-03-17, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Taxis have no meters; agree the fare before the ride. — Expat.com transport guide, 2026-02-24, intl-media, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Westimmo divides Grand Baie into the centre around La Croisette (shops and restaurants on foot), the seafront, and quieter residential streets set back a few minutes' walk from the beach; La Cuvette and Mont Choisy are the nearby beaches. — Westimmo "Grand Baie real estate", 2026-11-11, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Long-term asking rents in the far north in Nov 2026: a single-storey house in a residence at Cap Malheureux Rs 60,000/month (≈ €1,143); a contemporary villa in Péreybère Rs 80,000 (≈ €1,524). — Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", 2026-11-14, social-caption (agency posts), confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Westimmo advises starting a search 4–8 weeks before moving in and clarifying what the rent covers (pool, garden, internet, water, electricity, security, parking, minor repairs). — Westimmo, 2026-05-13, company, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Non-citizens can buy freehold in approved PDS, RES, IRS and Smart City developments, and apartments in R+2 buildings from USD 175,000; USD 375,000+ in an eligible scheme gives the buyer and family a residence permit. Five of Westimmo's six Grand Baie sale listings were open to foreigners. — Westimmo "Grand Baie real estate", 2026-11-11, company, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Social-pass sale asking prices: a Grand Baie property at Rs 12.95 M (≈ €246,700), a home at Rs 10.2 M "or nearest offer" (≈ €194,300), and 125 toises of residential land at Rs 4.65 M (≈ €88,600). — Facebook group "Location & Vente île Maurice", 2026-11-14, social-caption, confidence: low
- [work.remote] The Premium Visa (up to 1 year, renewable, free) requires remote income of at least USD 1,500/month (+1,500 per extra adult, +500 per child) and forbids working for a local employer. — Wise, 2025-12-17, company, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Westimmo says Grand Baie is within 20 minutes of Port Louis by motorway; Expat.com says 30 minutes. — Westimmo 2026-11-11; Expat.com accommodation guide 2026-02-17, company/intl-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A bus from Port Louis to Grand Baie is about Rs 50 (≈ €0.95). — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Port-Louis", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Grand Baie is not on the Metro Express line (Port Louis–Curepipe, with a Rose-Hill–Réduit branch); Westimmo calls a car "almost essential" outside Port-Louis and the main roads. — Expat.com transport guide 2026-02-24; Westimmo "Living in Mauritius" 2026-09-16, intl-media/company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] An expat forum reply some two years ago said Yugo "doesn't work" as an Uber alternative; another poster relies on a trusted private driver at a fixed weekly fare. — Expat.com forum "Car hire vs Yugo", posts about 2 years old, forum, confidence: low
- [health.care] C-Care runs a hospital named C-Care Grand Baie (and one in Tamarin) alongside its Darné and Wellkin sites; ambulance number 118 listed; no prices published. — C-Care website, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] International schools named for the north: Northfields International High School (Mapou) and International Preparatory School (Labourdonnais); Expat.com says Grand Baie is about 15 minutes from international schools. — Westimmo cost-of-living page (undated); Expat.com accommodation guide 2026-02-17, company/intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Adolphe de Plevitz State Secondary School is in Grand Baie; a Tertiary Education Roadshow stopped in Grand-Baie on 14 Nov 2026. — Wikipedia "Grand Baie" accessed 2026-11-14; Le Mauricien 2026-11-14, academic/local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO names Grand Baie among tourist areas where petty crime is common; most crime is non-violent, such as burglaries of tourist villas and bag theft; it advises renting only accommodation registered with the Tourism Authority. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14 (events to 2026-10-19), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France says assaults are rare but rising since 2025, and names beaches, public car parks (including at the beach) and isolated places as risk spots; burglaries are frequent in isolated places. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] FCDO reports some sexual assaults on tourists and advises against walking alone at night on beaches or in poorly lit areas. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Expat.com describes Grand Baie as the north's hub with full amenities and nightlife; Westimmo names Banana Beach Club in Grand Baie as a leisure spot. No prices found. — Expat.com accommodation guide 2026-02-17; Westimmo cost-of-living page (undated), intl-media/company, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Grand-Baie La Croisette, owned by MaxCity Properties, was the third shopping mall built in Mauritius. — Wikipedia "Grand Baie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Places of worship include the Shiv Kalyannath Mandir, the Tamil Vadapazhanee Murugan Kovil and a Catholic church; Thaipusam is celebrated locally. — Wikipedia "Grand Baie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Water sports operators in nearby Péreybère (Ocean Spirit) are named by Westimmo; France warns that beach water-sports touts rarely carry liability insurance and strong currents regularly cause drownings. — Westimmo (undated); France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, company/official, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] Grand Baie is described as the most popular expatriate hub in Mauritius. — Expat.com Expat-Mag, 2026-03-17, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Cyclone season November–April with possible 2–3-day lockdowns; humidity damages textiles, leather and electronics, especially in closed homes. — France Diplomatie 2026-09-15; Westimmo "Living in Mauritius" 2026-09-16, official/company, confidence: high
- [other] Grand Baie is a coastal village of 11,910 people (2011 census) run by a village council in Rivière du Rempart district; village councils were to be dissolved on 22 Nov 2026 with no election date set. — Wikipedia "Grand Baie" accessed 2026-11-14; Le Mauricien 2026-11-14, academic/local-media, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex acts decriminalised in October 2023; FCDO calls the country relatively conservative, with attitudes varying by region and discrimination persisting (national). — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
