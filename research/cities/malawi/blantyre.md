# Blantyre, Malawi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier's Reserve Bank of Malawi rate of 13 Nov 2026 (EUR ≈ 1,958–1,996 MWK), taken at its midpoint **1 EUR ≈ 1,977 MWK**. These are official rates: the dollar reportedly trades at about twice the official rate on the parallel market (see daily.money). Blantyre here includes Limbe (merged in 1956). Web search ran out early in this pass; Numbeo returned 429 errors and no property portal with dated Blantyre listings was reached (see Gaps).

## Items

- [cost.rent] Private low-density houses comparable to government houses in Nyambadwe and Limbe rent for K600,000–2.5 million/month (≈ €303–1,265); government rents there rose an average 65% from 1 Apr 2026 (K160,000–715,000, ≈ €81–362, is the current range in the article, not the post-rise one). — Nation Online, 2026-04-01, local-media, confidence: medium ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [cost.rent] A fully furnished 4-bed ensuite house with pool in a gated compound at Chigumula is listed at MK 1,500,000/month (≈ €759, ≈ US$850), "negotiable", basic utilities included; posted "last year". — Expat.com listing, 2025 (accessed 2026-11-14), company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A Malawi Housing Corporation compound (3-bed and 2-bed houses, a bedsitter and a 10-room building) near Chiradzulu District Hospital, about 20 minutes' drive from Blantyre CBD, was offered at K555,000/month (≈ €281), paid in advance. — MHC property listing, 2025-05-23, official, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A crowd-sourced price site gives 1-bed flats at about MWK 396,182/month in the centre (≈ €200) and MWK 199,976 in the suburbs (≈ €101); 3-bed at MWK 1,046,783 (≈ €529) centre and MWK 679,241 (≈ €344) suburbs. — combien-coute.net Blantyre, prices said to be collected Nov 2026, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The Centre for Social Concern's survey ranked Blantyre the most expensive city in Malawi in Dec 2025, with households needing K1,072,809/month (≈ €543) for basic needs; the Nation gives the same figure as the national average for Jan 2026, so the city label is uncertain. — Business Malawi (Times Group), updated 2026-01-23; Nation Online, 2026-02-22, local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] ECAM's basic-needs basket for an urban family of six reached K1,152,606/month in April 2026 (≈ €583); monthly transport in it rose 40% to K100,000–140,000, and a 50 kg charcoal bag cost K60,000 (≈ €30). — Nyasa Times via AllAfrica, 2026-05-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Regulated pump price for petrol is K5,619/litre (≈ €2.84) and diesel K6,306 (≈ €3.19) since 19 June 2026; black-market fuel reached up to K20,000/litre (≈ €10) in Oct 2026. — Rio Times (citing Nation Online), 2026-10-07, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Average electricity tariff rose 12% to K160.13/kWh (≈ €0.08) from 20 Jan 2026. — Nation Online, 2026-01-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] The crowd-sourced site gives lunch at about MWK 6,975 (≈ €3.5), a 3-course dinner MWK 11,639 (≈ €5.9), a local 0.5 L beer MWK 1,885 (≈ €0.95), a gym MWK 103,408/month (≈ €52), a cinema ticket MWK 6,739 (≈ €3.4) and electricity plus water MWK 46,535/month (≈ €24). — combien-coute.net Blantyre, Nov 2026, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Minimum wages from 1 June 2026: commercial workers K157,500/month (≈ €80), shop workers K187,500 (≈ €95), domestic workers K83,720 (≈ €42). — Nation Online, 2026-05-24, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] A Blantyre cleaner on the old K126,000 minimum wage told the Nation her household of four can afford little beyond a bag of maize and the rent. — Nation Online, 2026-02-22, local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] In July 2026 civil servants' June salaries were late; a Blantyre primary teacher said landlords were demanding rent "yet we have nothing to give them". — Malawi24, 2026-07-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] The crowd-sourced site puts the average monthly salary at about MWK 582,071 (≈ €294). — combien-coute.net Blantyre, Nov 2026, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Rental asking prices on listings are marked "negotiable" (e.g. the Chigumula house above); some landlords, including the state housing corporation, want rent paid in advance. — Expat.com listing 2025; MHC listing 2025-05-23, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Travel guides advise agreeing taxi and kabaza fares before the ride, since nothing is metered or app-priced, and carrying small kwacha notes. — Hello Travel, 2026-05-26, blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Named residential neighbourhoods include Sunnyside, Mandala, Namiwawa, Mount Pleasant and Kabula, plus Limbe; the universities cluster around Chichiri–Ginnery Corner. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The downtown area and nearby Limbe are full of Indian-run shops, per Wikivoyage. — Wikivoyage Blantyre, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Blantyre is the financial and commercial capital: Malawi Stock Exchange, eight designated industrial areas, services as the largest employer, and an international trade fair usually in May. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] The US dollar reportedly trades on the parallel market at roughly twice the official rate; the IMF says forex shortages limit imports of energy, food and fertiliser. — Malawi24, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Malawians abroad for 12+ consecutive months can open diaspora foreign-currency accounts (since early 2026), withdrawing up to 50% of the balance in hard currency once a month. — Malawi24, 2026-01-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Load-shedding is national: ESCOM confirmed a 77 MW evening deficit in Nov 2025 and cuts during morning, midday and evening peaks across all regions. — Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05; Malawi24, 2025-11-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Parts of Blantyre had three continuous days of dry taps in early Oct 2026 after the main pipeline from Walkers Ferry to Chileka was damaged; the water board blamed rising vandalism and sent water bowsers. — Malawi24, 2026-10-04, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] On 23 Oct 2026, during a heatwave, Blantyre Water Board shut Walkers Ferry pumping station for repairs after vandals cut a valve spindle; it had already cut pumping to 50%, and most areas faced interruptions with no return date. — Malawi24, 2026-10-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] The water board has also been in turmoil: its CEO was suspended in April 2026 and the Consumer Protection Unit called for urgent intervention in Aug 2026. — Malawi24, 2026-04-27 and 2026-08-30 (headlines), local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Ndirande–Limbe minibus fare rose from K1,500 to K2,500 (≈ €0.76 to €1.26) in Oct 2026. — Rio Times (citing Nation Online), 2026-10-07, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A year earlier, Chilomoni Main Market–CBD went from K700 to K1,000 and other township routes rose about K300. — Nyasa Times, 2025-10-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Intercity: Lilongwe–Blantyre bus K55,000 → K85,000 (≈ €28 → €43) and Mzuzu–Blantyre K110,000 → K140,000–180,000 (≈ €56 → €71–91) after the April 2026 fuel rise. — Nation Online, 2026-04-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A Malawi24 spot check in late Sept 2026 found most Blantyre filling stations dry, with drivers moving station to station and waiting hours. — Malawi24, 2026-09-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Wikivoyage lists Gogo, a local app offering car, motorbike and three-wheeler rides in Blantyre; taxis gather at the Mount Soche Hotel. — Wikivoyage Blantyre, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Chileka International Airport is about 14 km from the centre; coaches leave from Wenela bus station and the Sena railway serves the city. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital is the country's largest referral hospital; private options are Mwaiwathu and Blantyre Adventist hospitals, and Beit CURE is known for hip and knee replacements. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] France Diplomatie lists named doctors and pharmacies in Blantyre and Limbe; the FCDO says many facilities expect payment up front and full insurance is essential. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15 (via dossier); UK FCDO, 2026-10-20, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] The health minister said public-hospital fees vary by hospital and will be standardised; paying wards bring in "substantial revenue". — Malawi24, 2026-03-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] St Andrew's International (primary and high school) is in Blantyre; its fees were not found. The crowd-sourced site puts a private primary school at about MWK 1.03 million/year (≈ €523). — Wikipedia "Blantyre" (via dossier); combien-coute.net Blantyre, Nov 2026, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Tertiary institutions include the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences and Kamuzu University of Health Sciences. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The FCDO reports an increase in break-ins in Lilongwe, Blantyre and Limbe, including violent assaults on residents, and occasional kidnappings for ransom. — UK FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-10-20, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie names Blantyre and Lilongwe for night-time theft, car-jacking and assault; it advises against walking alone in poorer districts and says 4x4 theft is common. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Police arrested five suspects over a robbery at the St Lawrence parish priest's house in Oct 2026; in Aug 2026 a gang was jailed (up to 21 years) for a Feb 2023 wave of armed robberies on Blantyre businesses. — Malawi24, 2026-10-07 and 2026-08-03, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] UK tourists were assaulted and robbed in a hotel at Mulanje, in the southern region, in 2025; the FCDO and France Diplomatie advise climbing Mulanje only with a guide checked through the Mountain Club of Malawi. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-20; France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] The FCDO's Malawi page has no Malawi-specific guidance for women; it warns against accepting food or drink from strangers after robberies involving drugged food. — UK FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-10-20, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Founded in 1876 by Church of Scotland missionaries; Mandala House (1882) is among the oldest buildings; Kamuzu Stadium is the country's second largest. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Catholics are the largest single denomination (17.94%) and Muslims 10.11%. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Wikivoyage names restaurants such as Chez Maky, Jungle Pepper, Bombay Palace, L'Hostaria and the Mandala House café; its prices are years old. — Wikivoyage Blantyre, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [community.expat] Expat.com describes Blantyre as a "more Malawian" experience than Lilongwe, with a smaller expat community. — Expat.com Malawi guide, undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [community.diaspora] France's only consular presence in Malawi is an honorary consular agency in Blantyre; the consulate is in Johannesburg. — France Diplomatie "Contacts utiles", 2026-09-15 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [admin.visa] French passport holders have needed a visa since 3 Jan 2026 (e-visa or, since Feb 2026, visa on arrival at the airport; US$50 / $150 / $250); 30 days at first, extendable to 3 months. — France Diplomatie "Entrée / Séjour", 2026-09-15 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [admin.nationality] Dual nationality has been allowed since 2019 (with notification); citizenship by descent stops at the first generation born abroad. — Wikipedia "Malawian nationality law" (via dossier), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex sexual activity is illegal and can lead to a prison sentence; the Constitutional Court upheld these laws on 28 June 2024. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-20; Human Rights Watch, 2024-07-11 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Tropical savanna climate in the hilly Shire Highlands, with most rain November–April; the FCDO says cyclones and floods are common in that season and often close roads with landslides. — Wikipedia "Blantyre", accessed 2026-11-14; UK FCDO, 2026-10-20, official, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The city is ringed by hills including Mount Soche and Michiru Mountain; Majete Wildlife Reserve and Zomba are suggested as nearby trips. — Wikipedia "Blantyre"; Wikivoyage Blantyre, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
