# Lilongwe: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: 1 EUR ≈ 1,977 MWK and 1 USD ≈ 1,734 MWK (Reserve Bank of Malawi midpoints, 13 Nov 2026, dossier and city file). These are official rates; the city file reports the dollar trading at about twice that on the parallel market (Malawi24, 2026-10-15), so euro figures here are an upper bound for anyone changing money informally. Bracketed numbers refer to "Sources read" below. "City file" = `research/cities/malawi/lilongwe.md`, whose items carry their own dated sources.

Research-pass note: this session's shared WebSearch budget ran out after a few searches, so this pass rests mostly on pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, venue and school sites, Malawi24 and Nyasa Times search pages, expat post reports). Lilongwe "Areas" are numbered in the order they were built, not by location [4]. Most venue listings come from Wikivoyage, many tagged 2015–2024, and from US-diplomat post reports of 2016–2020: they prove a place existed, not that it still operates.

## Neighbourhoods

### Areas 10, 12 and 43 (New Town residential belt)
- Feel: where most expats live: three areas a 5–10 minute drive apart, mostly single-storey 1970s houses on big walled plots with 24-hour guards (Talesmag post reports 2017 and Q&A to 2020) [9][11]; shared with international NGO and Malawi government staff (Q&A, Sep 2018) [11]. A 2019 resident calls Areas 10, 11, 12 and 43 "leafy suburbs" with a few restaurants and bars [12]. The city's land-use plan keeps Area 12 and parts of Area 10 for low-density housing [4].
- Rent: private low-density houses comparable to government houses in Areas 10, 11 and 12 ask K600,000–2.5 million/month (≈ €303–1,265) (Nation Online, 2026-04-01, city file). No Area 43 figure found. Expect to have up to three months' rent ready, a large deposit plus the first month (William Russell guide, undated) [19].
- Getting around: commutes to the embassy/city-centre district are about 10 minutes (Talesmag 2017, 2020) [9][11]; the airport is about 20 minutes' drive from most expat housing (Talesmag 2016) [10]. Capital Hill (Area 20) is the seat of government [4]. A car is the norm; a cross-town taxi is MWK 8,000–12,000 (Hello Travel, 2026-05-26, city file); late-night taxis are unreliable without a regular driver (resident interview, 2019) [12]. Sky Energy's Sky E-Ride electric taxis take bookings from 1 Dec 2026 (Nyasa Times, 2026-10-31, city file).
- Watch: the FCDO (2026-10-20) reports more break-ins in Lilongwe, including violent assaults on residents, and France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) names Lilongwe for night-time theft, car-jacking and assault (city file). A 2020 diplomat report, a personal account, adds robberies during walks and runs and phones snatched through open car windows [8]. Power: national load-shedding in 3.5-hour blocks (ESCOM, Jul 2025, city file); a 2019 resident described cuts of up to 6 hours a day for a few months a year [12]. Houses in these areas often have generators and water tanks (Q&A, Oct 2016) [11].
- Places:
  - Blue Ginger (Indian restaurant, Pacific Parade, Area 10) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Four Seasons Centre (restaurants, Chameleon Bar with a Sunday jazz jam; Presidential Way, New Town, near the Capital Sunbird hotel; Area number not given) — Wikivoyage [3]; a 2016 report names Buchanan Steak House, Kathmandu, the Ama Khofi lunch spot and a plant nursery there [10]
  - Foodworths (expat-oriented supermarket, Kenyatta Rd) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Lilongwe Golf Club (18-hole course, lessons, camping; off Glyn Jones Rd) — Wikivoyage [3]; also named in a 2016 report [10]
  - Lilongwe Wildlife Centre (forest trails, boardwalk, café; Kenyatta Rd entrance between Old Town and City Centre) — Lilongwe Wildlife Trust [5]
  - Kamuzu Mausoleum and Parliament gate (memorial; Presidential Way) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (10-minute commute to the government, embassy and NGO district); Q4 "En couple" and Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (big gardens, guarded houses); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" to "Moyen"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (the main expat cluster, though the sources describe expatriates, not the African diaspora); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Old Town and Areas 2, 3 and 9
- Feel: Old Town (Area 2 Bwalonjobvu and Area 3 Kang'ombe) is the old commercial centre with street and walled markets, bus stations, cafés and restaurants [4]. Areas 3 and 9 sit close to Old Town's restaurants, shops and banks, and Old Town is easy to cross by day on a tuk-tuk or bicycle taxi (resident interview, 2019) [12]. The land-use plan mixes low-density housing, high-rise flats and government land in Area 3 [4].
- Rent: no Area figure. The crowd-sourced "city centre" 1-bed is MWK 935,224/month (≈ €473), low confidence (combien-coute.net, Nov 2026, city file). Lodges for a first stay: St Peters Guesthouse single 10,000 K (listing Apr 2023) and Mufasa Lodge, 4th St, Area 2, private rooms from US$28 (≈ K48,550, ≈ €25) [3].
- Getting around: the main bus station is on Malangalanga Rd, Area 2 [3]. Wikivoyage gives minibuses at 1,000–2,000 K and moto-taxis at 1,500–2,500 K (Aug 2024) [3], but after the 2026 fuel rises the Nation reports a moto-taxi ride at about K12,000 (2026-04-05) and Area 25–Old Town minibuses at K6,000 (2026-10-07) (city file). Airport: 30–40 minutes; Wikivoyage gives about US$20 by taxi from Old Town [3].
- Watch: France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) says thefts concentrate in bus stations and markets, both of which are in Old Town [2]. Water is short citywide: Lilongwe Water Board produced 125 against 151 million litres a day demanded (Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05, city file).
- Places:
  - Old Town Mall (art galleries, grocery, bookshop, cafés; off Chilambula Rd) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mamma Mia (Italian restaurant, Old Town Mall) — [3]
  - Diplomats Pub (bar, 17:00–02:00, weekend braai, Old Town) — [3]
  - Chipiku (bulk supermarket, near Chilambula and Mzimba roads) — [3]
  - Crossroads Complex (post office, pharmacy, ATMs, Indian grocery; Mchinji Roundabout) — [3]
  - Bishop Mackenzie International School (IB school, Barron Avenue, Area 3) — BMIS [7]
  - Civo Stadium (football ground, Area 9) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e" and Q5 "Télétravail" (cafés and shops within reach by day); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (BMIS in Area 3); Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Area 47 (western side, towards the Mchinji road)
- Feel: home to Silver Stadium [4] and to the Chez Ntemba club [3]; the dossier's reading of the 2030 land-use plan lists Area 47 for medium/high-density housing [2]. An Area 47 resident told Nyasa Times her family goes up to two days without water and relies on a private borehole (2025-11-05, city file).
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: the Gateway mall stands in the western suburbs at the junction of the Kaunda Road western bypass and the Mchinji road (Nyasa Times, 2012-03-06) [14]; a search summary placed it in Area 47, but no page opened in this pass confirms the Area number. Cross-town taxi MWK 8,000–12,000 (Hello Travel, city file).
- Watch: water, as above. Two suspected bag-snatchers were caught at Gateway Mall in June 2025 and one was nearly burnt by a mob (Malawi24, 2025-06-08, city file).
- Places:
  - Silver Stadium (football ground, Area 47) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Chez Ntemba (nightclub, behind the Total station, Area 47; Fri–Sat) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Gateway Mall (shopping mall; Pick n Pay named as anchor at planning stage, current tenants not verified) — Nyasa Times [14]; How We Made It In Africa [15]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (the city's only named late club is here); Q3 "Moyen"; Q8 "Je reste en ville".

### Area 25 (north, towards Kanengo)
- Feel: Area 25/2 (Bvunguti) is the Kanengo primary commercial centre, next to Kanengo (Areas 28–29), the heavy-industry zone with food processing and tobacco and maize storage [4]. Area 25 is a commuter area: one commuter estimated his Area 25–Old Town minibus at K200,000 a month (Nation, 2026-04-05, city file). In Feb 2024 the district commissioner promised to settle a row over the demolition of illegal structures in Area 25 [16].
- Rent: no rental figure. A house in Area 25 was offered for sale at MK 260 million (≈ €131,500) on a Facebook group, 2026-11-14 (city file, low confidence).
- Getting around: the Area 25–Old Town minibus rose from K4,000 to K6,000 (≈ €2.0 to €3.0) on 7 Oct 2026 (Rio Times citing the Nation, city file). Minibuses north to Mzuzu leave from Kanengo, Area 28 (25,000 K, 6–7 hours, Aug 2024) [3].
- Watch: nothing Area-25-specific and dated found beyond the citywide FCDO and France Diplomatie remarks (city file).
- Places:
  - Area 25 Health Centre (public health centre; Lilongwe Water Board donated equipment in Mar 2024) — Malawi24 [16]
  - Bvunguti commercial centre (Area 25/2, district shopping centre) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Kanengo industrial area (Areas 28–29, workplaces) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (the only affordable area with a sourced commute fare); Q5 "Salarié·e" for jobs in Kanengo's industries; Q10 "Une vie abordable", with the caveat that the commute itself costs up to K200,000 a month.

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come from the city file (dated there) unless marked. Weekly = monthly ÷ 4.33. Quantities are stated assumptions. Many prices are from the crowd-sourced combien-coute.net page (Nov 2026, low confidence); the ECAM food figures are national urban figures for April 2026. Prices in dollars were converted at 1,734 MWK.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Old Town / Area 3. Cafés and shops are reachable by day without a car, the Wildlife Centre trails are next door, and it is the only area with a 1-bed price, even a crowd-sourced one [3][4][5]. Working from home needs a generator or solar and a backup connection, because load-shedding hits the 05:30–07:30 and 16:30–20:30 peaks (ESCOM, Jul 2025, dossier).
- Mon: work from home, planned around the load-shedding block; evening coffee at Old Town Mall [3].
- Tue: a day at mHub, the coworking space and accelerator (directory listing modified 2026-10-05) [23]; it is said to be at Corporate Mall, Suite 10, but that address comes from a search summary and was not opened. Cross-town taxi there.
- Wed: groceries at Chipiku [3]. The Wildlife Centre is closed on Wednesdays [5].
- Thu: morning loop on the Wildlife Centre's 3.5 km forest trail (weekdays 09:00–17:00) [5].
- Fri: after-work drink at Diplomats Pub (from 17:00) [3].
- Sat: one of the monthly farmers' markets that 2016 and 2020 reports mention, venue not named [8][10]; evening per Q2.
- Sun: brunch at Kumbali Country Lodge, a 15-minute drive from the city centre (lodge site) [13].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Chez Ntemba in Area 47 (Fri–Sat, Wikivoyage, old prices) [3]; book a known driver both ways, since France Diplomatie warns of night-time car-jacking (city file). "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Mamma Mia at Old Town Mall (book at weekends) [3]. "Tranquille chez moi" — at home; Debonairs pizza delivers from Crossroads (Wikivoyage, old prices) [3].
- Weekend outings (Q8): "À la plage" — Lake Malawi at Salima, about 2 hours' drive (Talesmag 2017) [9] or 1.5 hours (resident 2019) [12]; Kumbali Lake Retreat at Salima, from US$80 per person (lodge site) [13]. "En montagne, en forêt" — Dedza, 85 km south on the M1: a pottery with a coffee shop, and a walk up Dedza Mountain (2,198 m) on the road to the masts [20]; Nkhoma Mountain hiking is named in 2016 and 2020 reports, distance not sourced [8][10]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — Liwonde National Park, about 3.5 hours' drive (2019) [12], US$25 international day fee, US$10 for residents (Wikivoyage) [21]; Kuti Wildlife Reserve near Salima is named but its fees were not found [3][10]. "Je reste en ville" — Wildlife Centre membership and trails [5], Lilongwe Golf Club [3].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: 1-bed "centre" K935,224 ÷ 4.33 = K215,987 (combien-coute)
  - Internet K105,869 ÷ 4.33 = K24,450; gym K53,362 ÷ 4.33 = K12,324 (combien-coute)
  - Electricity: 50 kWh assumed × K160.13 = K8,006 (Nation, 2026-01-21)
  - 3 lunches 3 × K8,654 = K25,962; 2 cappuccinos 2 × K5,550 = K11,100 (combien-coute)
  - Food: 1 kg tomatoes K5,700 + 1 kg beans K4,250 (midpoint of K4,000–4,500) = K9,950 (ECAM, Apr 2026)
  - Wildlife Centre resident day entry K8,000 [5]
  - 4 short taxi rides 4 × K3,000–6,000 = K12,000–24,000 (Hello Travel)
  - Total ≈ K327,780–339,780/week (≈ €166–172). Generator fuel, data bundles and mHub fees are not included because no dated price was found.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: Old Town / Area 3. It puts you near the street and walled markets and the bus stations [4], with mHub for the startup network [23]. Lilongwe's economy runs on government and public institutions (about 27% of formal jobs) plus Kanengo's agro-industry (dossier) [2]. Forex shortages mean delayed supplier payments and hard-to-get dollars (CCJP via Malawi24, 2026-06-05, city file).
- Mon: register the business and permits through MITC's One Stop Service Centre (dossier) [2]; office location not sourced. MITC announced an investment summit for 23 Nov 2026 (dossier).
- Tue: mHub: incubator, accelerator and Standard Bank challenges (dossier) [2][23].
- Wed: price-check suppliers in Old Town's markets, keeping valuables close (France Diplomatie) [2][4].
- Thu: visit Kanengo industrial area (Areas 28–29) [4].
- Fri: client lunch at the Four Seasons Centre [3].
- Sat: monthly farmers' market (small producers; venue not named) [10]; evening per Q2.
- Sun: Gateway Mall, to see which retailers trade there now [14].
- Saturday night: as profile 1.
- Weekend outings (Q8): as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent K215,987; internet K24,450; electricity K8,006; food K9,950 (as profile 1)
  - 5 lunches 5 × K8,654 = K43,270; one client dinner for two 2 × K29,533 = K59,066 (combien-coute)

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: Area 10 (or 12/43). Big walled gardens, guards and a 5–10 minute drive between the expat areas [9][11]. Bishop Mackenzie International School is on Barron Avenue, Area 3 [7]; reports from 2016 and 2017 also name the ABC (African Bible College) Christian School and preschools such as The Learning Center, without locations [9][10].
- Mon: school run to BMIS (driving time from Area 10 not sourced) [7]; BMIS taxpayer fees for 2026–27 are US$11,855/year for primary, and families who do not pay Malawian tax pay about 65% more (city file).
- Tue: after-school swimming, football or ballet, which a 2017 report names as children's activities [9].
- Wed: groceries at Foodworths [3].
- Thu: check-up: Kamuzu Central Hospital (public referral hospital, Area 33) [6]; reports from 2016–17 name CMED for lab work and X-rays, and the Partners in Hope and African Bible College clinics [9][10]. The FCDO says to expect payment up front (city file).
- Fri: family dinner at Mamma Mia [3].
- Sat: Wildlife Centre family morning: boardwalk, bird hide, recycled-material animal sculptures [5]; evening per Q2.
- Sun: an English-language church service; CCAP Presbyterians and Catholics are the largest groups (2018 census, city file), and 2016–2020 reports say many denominations hold English services [8][10]. Then Kumbali brunch [13].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — not a family option; Chez Ntemba [3] with a sitter. "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Four Seasons Centre [3][10]. "Tranquille chez moi" — at home.
- Weekend outings (Q8): "À la plage" — Lake Malawi at Salima (≈ 2 h) [9]; ask about bilharzia (Wikivoyage advises it for the lake) [22]. "En montagne, en forêt" — Dedza [20]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — Liwonde (child day fee US$12 for international visitors) [21]. "Je reste en ville" — Wildlife Centre [5].
- Rough weekly spend (one child):
  - Rent K138,568–577,367 (Nation); petrol K112,380; electricity K16,013; internet K24,450 (as profile 2)
  - Food ×3 = K29,850 (ECAM)
  - Wildlife Centre resident family membership K36,000/month ÷ 4.33 = K8,314 [5]
  - School, mid-priced option: crowd-sourced private primary K4.07 million/year ÷ 52 = K78,269 (combien-coute) → total ≈ K407,845–846,645/week (≈ €206–428)
  - School, BMIS primary taxpayer rate: US$11,855 ÷ 52 × 1,734 = K395,319 → total ≈ K724,895–1,163,695/week (≈ €367–589); at the non-taxpayer rate (US$19,590) add a further K257,933 a week, plus an entry levy and a refundable deposit (BMIS, city file)

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week of a trial stay
- Base: a lodge in Areas 2–3 near Old Town, then a viewing round of Areas 10/12/43 and Area 3.
- Day 1 (arrival): Kamuzu International Airport is 7 km north of the city centre [4]. French passport holders need a visa: e-visa or, since Feb 2026, a visa on arrival at the airport (photo, passport, host's invitation letter), US$50 single entry, 30 days, extendable to 3 months by reporting to immigration within those 30 days (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, dossier). Declare foreign cash; holding more than US$1,000 needs Reserve Bank permission (FCDO, city file). Airport taxi MWK 18,000–30,000 (Hello Travel, 2026-05-26) but Sky Energy cites about K80,000 one way (2026-10-31) (city file). Sleep at Sunrise Motel, Mzimba Rd (double with breakfast 64,500 K, Aug 2024) or Mufasa Lodge, Area 2 (from US$28) [3].
- Day 2 (SIM and mobile money): Airtel and TNM both run 4G [3]. TNM sells pay-as-you-need "Mixit SuperFlex" bundles from K600 (Nyasa Times, 2026-09-15, city file), and users of both networks complain that data bundles run out too fast (MACRA inquiry, 2026-09-29, city file). TNM Mpamba is the TNM mobile-money service, also used to buy Super League match tickets [18]. SIM-registration rules and Airtel Money were not verified in a source opened.
- Day 3 (money): banks named in a 2025–26 guide are National Bank of Malawi, FDH Bank, NBS Bank, First Capital Bank and Standard Bank Malawi [19]; account-opening rules for non-residents were not found. Since early 2026, Malawians who have lived abroad for 12+ months can open a diaspora foreign-currency account (RBM rules via Malawi24, 2026-01-30, city file). The parallel-market dollar rate is about twice the official one, and the CCJP calls that trading illegal (city file).
- Day 4 (finding a flat): listings are scarce online. On 2026-11-14 Expat.com had two ads for the whole country, none in Lilongwe, and the Facebook group "Lilongwe home makert" had no rentals (city file); on 2026-11-15 Seeff Malawi's rentals were all in Blantyre [6b]. Ask employers, the expat network and agents in person. Viewing or agency fees: not found. Have up to three months' rent ready [19].
- Day 5 (viewings): visit at a load-shedding hour (05:30–07:30 or 16:30–20:30) and ask about the generator, water tank or borehole. Reports from 2016 and 2020 say such houses commonly have them, and Area 23 and Area 47 residents describe water coming rarely (Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05, city file) [11].
- Day 6 (paperwork): find out how to extend the visa at the Department of Immigration (the online portal is epermit.gov.mw for residence permits, dossier); for a longer stay, the Temporary Residence Permit and Business Residence Permit are listed, but their requirements and fees are not shown online (dossier).
- Day 7: Wildlife Centre trails [5] or Kumbali brunch [13]; Saturday night per profile 1.
- Rough first-week spend:
  - Lodging 7 × 64,500 = K451,500 (Sunrise Motel, 2024 price) or 7 × US$28 × 1,734 = K339,864 (Mufasa)
  - Visa US$50 = K86,700 (France Diplomatie)
  - Airport taxi K18,000–80,000 (Hello Travel; Sky Energy)
  - 7 lunches 7 × K8,654 = K60,578 (combien-coute); 6 cross-town taxis 6 × K8,000–12,000 = K48,000–72,000 (Hello Travel); TNM starter bundle K600
  - Total ≈ K665,380–751,380 (≈ €337–380) with Sunrise Motel, or ≈ K553,740–639,740 (≈ €280–324) with Mufasa.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Lilongwe Wildlife Centre, Kenyatta Rd (open daily except Wednesday; weekdays 09:00, weekends 08:00, to 17:00). Walk the 2 km or 4.8 km forest trail, then the boardwalk and the riverside bird hide. Entry: international adult K15,000 (≈ €7.6), resident K8,000, citizen K3,000 [5]. Wikivoyage's older two-tier prices are out of date.
- Midday: a short taxi (MWK 3,000–6,000, agree the fare first; Hello Travel, city file) to Old Town Mall for lunch at Mamma Mia or a café, and a look at the galleries and bookshop [3].
- Afternoon: Capital Hill and Presidential Way by taxi (MWK 8,000–12,000): Parliament can only be seen from the gate, then the Kamuzu Mausoleum next to it and the War Memorial clock tower [3].
- Evening: Kumbali Cultural Centre for traditional dance, drumming and a traditional dinner (Wikivoyage, Jan 2023; price not listed) [3], or the Four Seasons Centre's Chameleon Bar [3]. Go back with a booked driver, since France Diplomatie warns of night-time car-jacking (city file).

### One week
- Day 1: arrival, visa on arrival, lodge in Area 2/3 (see profile 5); late afternoon at Old Town Mall [3].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 ("what daily life looks like"): take the Area 25–Old Town minibus at morning rush hour (K6,000 since Oct 2026, city file) and spend the morning in Old Town's street and walled markets [4], with valuables out of sight (France Diplomatie: theft concentrates in markets and bus stations) [2]. Lunch at Diplomats Pub or Sana (budget curries) [3].
- Day 4 (trip out): Dedza, 85 km south on the M1: Dedza Pottery and its coffee shop, then a walk up Dedza Mountain (2,198 m). The town is the highest in Malawi and cold on June–July mornings [20]. Minibus fare not sourced; a car is simpler.
- Days 5–6 (trip out): Lake Malawi at Salima, about 2 hours [9]: overnight at Kumbali Lake Retreat (from US$80 pp) [13], with an optional visit to Kuti Wildlife Reserve nearby [3][10]. Ask about bilharzia before swimming [22].
- Day 7: Sunday service at an English-language church [10], Kumbali brunch [13], then, if a Super League match is on, football at Bingu National Stadium (40,000 seats), Silver Stadium (Area 47) or Civo Stadium (Area 9) [4]. Tickets can be bought by TNM Mpamba e-ticketing since 2024 [18]. Fixtures were not sourced.
