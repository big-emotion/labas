# Lilongwe, Malawi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier's Reserve Bank of Malawi rate of 13 Nov 2026 (EUR ≈ 1,958–1,996 MWK), taken at its midpoint **1 EUR ≈ 1,977 MWK**; USD figures use the same day's midpoint, 1 USD ≈ 1,734 MWK (≈ €0.88). These are official rates: the dollar reportedly trades at about twice the official rate on the parallel market (see daily.money), so real euro costs for someone changing cash informally can be far lower. Web search ran out early in this pass; Numbeo returned 429 errors and no property portal with dated Lilongwe listings was reached (see Gaps).
