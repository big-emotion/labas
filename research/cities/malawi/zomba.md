# Zomba, Malawi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier's Reserve Bank of Malawi rate of 13 Nov 2026 (EUR ≈ 1,958–1,996 MWK), taken at its midpoint **1 EUR ≈ 1,977 MWK** (official rate; the parallel-market dollar is reportedly about twice the official rate). Extra city suggested by the dossier. Web search ran out early in this pass, so Zomba rests on fewer sources than the two main cities; national items are repeated here so the file stands alone.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Off-campus rooms rented by University of Malawi students in Chikanda cost K75,000–120,000/month (≈ €38–61) after rises of 60–67% in a year, against 27.9% inflation; an on-campus bed costs K35,000 (≈ €18). — Nation Online, 2026-01-08, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] A crowd-sourced price site gives lunch at about MWK 4,854 (≈ €2.5), a local 0.5 L beer MWK 1,684 (≈ €0.85) and electricity plus water MWK 48,932/month (≈ €25); several of its other Zomba figures are clearly wrong (e.g. petrol far below the regulated price). — combien-coute.net Zomba, Nov 2026, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Supermarkets (Shoprite, Peoples) are pricier than in Blantyre because of transport costs, while the central market behind the bus station sells cheap fruit, vegetables and meat. — Wikivoyage Zomba, accessed 2026-11-14 (some listings tagged 2015), blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Regulated pump price for petrol is K5,619/litre (≈ €2.84) and diesel K6,306 (≈ €3.19) since 19 June 2026; black-market fuel reached up to K20,000/litre (≈ €10) in Oct 2026. — Rio Times (citing Nation Online), 2026-10-07, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Minimum wages from 1 June 2026: commercial workers K157,500/month (≈ €80), shop workers K187,500 (≈ €95), domestic workers K83,720 (≈ €42). — Nation Online, 2026-05-24, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] A taxi up the Zomba Plateau costs about 7,000 MK each way (≈ €3.5) and is negotiable; a plateau guide about 5,000 MK a day (≈ €2.5) — old prices. — Wikivoyage Zomba, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] There are no standard rules on when, how much or with what notice landlords can raise rent, so they adjust largely at will; some now raise student rents every semester. — Nation Online, 2026-01-08, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Zomba is a centre for surrounding tobacco and dairy farms; its market sells local produce, second-hand clothes and fish from Lakes Malawi and Chilwa. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.admin] Zomba City Council's 2026/27 development budget is K13.8 billion, K10.5 billion of it from central government; the finance director says residents evading levies limit local revenue. — Malawi24, 2026-01-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.money] The dollar reportedly trades at roughly twice the official rate on the parallel market, amid chronic forex shortages. — Malawi24, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Load-shedding is national: ESCOM confirmed a 77 MW evening deficit in Nov 2025 and cuts across all regions at morning, midday and evening peaks. — Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05; Malawi24, 2025-11-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Zomba had no piped water for about seven days in May 2023 after thieves damaged the supply pipe; the Southern Region Water Board said over 600,000 residents were affected (old source). — Nyasa Times, 2023-05-29, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Only about one filling station in ten had fuel on the day before the Nyasa Times visit in Nov 2025; a motorbike-taxi operator said the shortage left "no relish" at home. — Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In late Oct 2026 Zomba motorists queued at filling stations under police watch, some pushing vehicles with passengers inside. — Malawi24, 2026-10-27, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Minibuses and shared taxis run to Blantyre on the M3 (about an hour, per Wikivoyage); a daily coach serves Lilongwe, about four hours away; the nearest airport is Chileka, about 75 km south. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi"; Wikivoyage Zomba, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Police roadblocks are frequent for drivers, per Wikivoyage; France Diplomatie also warns of unmarked police roadblocks nationally. — Wikivoyage Zomba; France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Zomba Central Hospital is one of the country's largest hospitals; the city also hosts a mental hospital. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi"; Malawi24, 2026-10-11 (headline), academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A measles outbreak in Zomba District since Aug 2026 had reached 65 cases and 3 deaths by early Nov, mostly children; officials link it to low vaccination driven by some churches' beliefs. — Malawi24, 2026-11-06, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Sir Harry Johnston International School has operated since 1958; Zomba Catholic Secondary (founded 1942, ~600 pupils) and Zomba Theological College are also in town; fees not found. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Chancellor College, the largest college of the University of Malawi, is in Zomba. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie lists Zomba among places where pickpocketing and theft occur, and advises against travelling outside towns after dark. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Wikivoyage mentions Casa Rossa (Italian), Hotel Masongola (chicken kawawa), Annie's (mid-priced lunches of rice, nsima and stew) and Domino's bar with live sport (old listings). — Wikivoyage Zomba, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] French passport holders have needed a visa since 3 Jan 2026 (e-visa or visa on arrival at the airport; US$50 / $150 / $250); 30 days at first, extendable to 3 months. — France Diplomatie "Entrée / Séjour", 2026-09-15 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [admin.nationality] Dual nationality has been allowed since 2019 (with notification); citizenship by descent stops at the first generation born abroad. — Wikipedia "Malawian nationality law" (via dossier), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex sexual activity is illegal and can lead to a prison sentence; the Constitutional Court upheld these laws on 28 June 2024. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-20; Human Rights Watch, 2024-07-11 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Humid subtropical, about 1,340 mm of rain a year, mostly November–March; the FCDO says cyclones and floods are common November–April nationwide. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi"; UK FCDO, 2026-10-20, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Zomba Plateau (~1,800 m) rises right above town: a tarmac road leads up, the walk takes about 1.5 hours, and guides show waterfalls (Williams Falls), dams and viewpoints such as Queen's View; the forest is easy to get lost in. — Wikipedia "Zomba, Malawi"; Wikivoyage Zomba, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
