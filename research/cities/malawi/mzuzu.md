# Mzuzu, Malawi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier's Reserve Bank of Malawi rate of 13 Nov 2026 (EUR ≈ 1,958–1,996 MWK), taken at its midpoint **1 EUR ≈ 1,977 MWK** (official rate; the parallel-market dollar is reportedly about twice the official rate). Extra city suggested by the dossier. Web search ran out early in this pass, so Mzuzu rests on fewer sources than the two main cities; national items are repeated here so the file stands alone.

## Items

- [cost.rent] No dated Mzuzu rent figure was found; the only nearby listing on Expat.com was a beach hotel for sale in Nkhata Bay District at US$700,000, negotiable. — Expat.com Malawi housing, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Regulated pump price for petrol is K5,619/litre (≈ €2.84) and diesel K6,306 (≈ €3.19) since 19 June 2026; black-market fuel reached up to K20,000/litre (≈ €10) in Oct 2026. — Rio Times (citing Nation Online), 2026-10-07, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] ECAM's urban basic-needs basket for a family of six reached K1,152,606/month in April 2026 (≈ €583); not broken down by city. — Nyasa Times via AllAfrica, 2026-05-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Students in Luwinga and Lupaso buy water from neighbours with storage tanks at K200 per 10-litre bucket (≈ €0.10). — Nation Online, 2026-03-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Minimum wages from 1 June 2026: commercial workers K157,500/month (≈ €80), shop workers K187,500 (≈ €95), domestic workers K83,720 (≈ €42). — Nation Online, 2026-05-24, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] Mzuzu City Council workers downed tools in Oct 2026 demanding a 25% raise after three years without an adjustment; a 17% government rise for 2024/25 was never applied to council staff. — Malawi24, 2026-10-06, local-media, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Named neighbourhoods include Chibavi, Luwinga, Area 1B, Chibanja, Katoto, Zolozolo, Masasa, Mchenga-utuba, Chimaliro, Kaning'ina, Katawa, Nkholongo and Lupaso; most Mzuzu University students live in Luwinga and Lupaso. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu"; Nation Online 2026-03-12, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] A new trading space, Vigwagwa Market, is under construction in Mzuzu under the constituency development fund. — Malawi24, 2026-10-30, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] The dollar reportedly trades at roughly twice the official rate on the parallel market, amid chronic forex shortages. — Malawi24, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Wikivoyage lists Standard Bank, National Bank of Malawi and Ecobank ATMs in town (old listing). — Wikivoyage Mzuzu, listings dated 2015–2023 (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Load-shedding is national: ESCOM confirmed a 77 MW evening deficit in Nov 2025 and cuts across all regions at morning, midday and evening peaks. — Nyasa Times, 2025-11-05; Malawi24, 2025-11-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Northern Region Water Board's Lunyangwa Dam system produces about 20 million litres a day against about 30 million demanded; the network was designed for ~2005 demand while the population grew to about 285,000. — Nation Online, 2026-03-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Taps at Mzuzu University run dry two to three times a week; piped water reaches about 30% of the Nkhorongo–Lupaso ward, and Area 1B has pipes but dry taps. — Nation Online, 2026-03-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] About 30% of treated water is lost to leaks, illegal connections and faulty meters; a €47 M EIB-funded water project is under way. — Nation Online, 2026-03-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] After the April 2026 fuel rise, a local Mzuzu city route went from K5,000 to K10,000 (≈ €2.5 to €5.1), and the Mzuzu–Blantyre bus from K110,000 to K140,000–180,000 (≈ €56 to €71–91). — Nation Online, 2026-04-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Mzuzu has an airport (Mzuzu Airport); a new airport proposed in 2015 had not started by 2022. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Mzuzu Central Hospital is in the city (Wikipedia's claim that it is one of four such hospitals is unsourced); the Northern Region had no working dialysis unit in July 2026, so patients travel hundreds of kilometres; the hospital's unit was built and awaiting machines. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu"; Malawi24, 2026-07-08, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Mzuzu University and the private Mzuzu Academy are in the city; about 20,000 commuters, mostly university students, add to the population. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie lists Mzuzu among places where theft occurs, mostly in crowded places such as bus stations and markets; the whole country is on "vigilance renforcée", mainly for road risk. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Emergency services are basic and night driving is hazardous (unlit vehicles, potholes, animals); the FCDO advises avoiding road travel after dark. — UK FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-10-20, official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Wikivoyage names A-1 Restaurant (Indian, called the best in town), Obrigado Leisure Park (nsima by day, a beer den at night) and Casablanca in Ekwendeni, 18 km out, as a lively bar for locals and expats (old listings). — Wikivoyage Mzuzu, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.sport] Moyale Barracks FC and Mzuni play in the top flight; Mzuzu Stadium holds up to 15,000. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] The Tumbuka are the largest group (Wikipedia's table gives 51.7%; its text gives an unsourced 80.5%). — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] French passport holders have needed a visa since 3 Jan 2026 (e-visa or visa on arrival at the airport; US$50 / $150 / $250); 30 days at first, extendable to 3 months. — France Diplomatie "Entrée / Séjour", 2026-09-15 (via dossier), official, confidence: high
- [admin.nationality] Dual nationality has been allowed since 2019 (with notification); citizenship by descent stops at the first generation born abroad. — Wikipedia "Malawian nationality law" (via dossier), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex sexual activity is illegal (FCDO 2026); in Mzuzu specifically, a Feb 2016 High Court injunction let arrests resume despite a moratorium, and a man was arrested there in April 2018 and charged under s.156. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-20; Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in Malawi" (via dossier), official, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Subtropical highland climate: about 1,289 mm of rain a year, heaviest December–April; average highs from ~20 °C in July to ~27 °C in October–November, July lows ~6.5 °C. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Mzuzu City Council appealed for help for flood survivors in Jan 2026. — Nation Online, 2026-01-07 (headline), local-media, confidence: low
- [nature.access] The city sits in a gap in the Viphya Mountains; Lake Malawi, Vwaza Marsh Wildlife Reserve and Nyika National Park (Malawi's largest) are nearby. — Wikipedia "Mzuzu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
