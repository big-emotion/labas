# Nakuru, Kenya

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city (suggested by the dossier as Kenya's newest city, a quieter highland base 160 km from Nairobi). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 143 KES** (Numbeo, November 2026, dossier source [5]). Web search was exhausted before Nakuru-specific searches, so this file relies on Wikipedia, official advisories and a few dated press items (see Gaps).

## Items

- [housing.areas] Nakuru became Kenya's fourth city in December 2021; its urban population was 570,674 in 2019; Milimani is a named residential area and Lanet a nearby residential town. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The economy rests on agriculture (coffee, wheat, maize, potatoes), manufacturing (soap, motorcycles, batteries) and tourism; the Naivas supermarket chain was founded here. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In Maella (Naivasha, Nakuru County, not the city itself), residents went about five months without piped water after road works cut the pipes, buying water at KES 100 per 20 litres (≈ €0.70). — The Standard, 2026-09-03, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] About 160 km from Nairobi; the Eldoret–Nairobi highway runs through the city; Lanet airstrip was approved for an upgrade to international status in 2017. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Matatu fares rose 25% in April 2026 and operators announced a further 50% and a nationwide strike in May 2026 over fuel prices. — The Star 2026-04-15; Citizen Digital 2026-05-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Nakuru Hospital is one of the county's largest; Nakuru War Memorial Hospital dates from just after World War I. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Canada says good medical facilities are available in Nairobi but very limited elsewhere in Kenya. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Egerton University (Njoro, nearby), Kabarak University and campuses of Mount Kenya, Nairobi and Kenyatta universities; Nakuru Boys and Menengai High are named public secondary schools. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Dozens of buildings were burned in Nakuru during the 2007 post-election violence. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France flags a pre-election period ahead of the 10 Aug 2027 general election, with possible deadly violence at rallies (national advisory, no Nakuru detail). — France Diplomatie, 2026-10-29, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] A government working group named Nakuru, with Nairobi and Meru, among the counties with the highest femicide counts in 2022–2024. — The Standard, 2026-01-26, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.women] UN Women cites Africa Data Hub analysis that Nairobi, Kiambu and Nakuru consistently top intimate-partner femicide counts, mostly inside homes, and flags women aged 18–34 in the Nairobi–Kiambu–Nakuru corridor. — UN Women technical brief, 2026 (file dated 2026-04), academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Afraha Stadium (8,200 seats, opened 1948) and the Rift Valley Sports Club (founded 1907). — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Highland city at about 1,850 m with a temperate climate, cooler nights from June to August and about 963 mm of rain a year. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lake Nakuru National Park (188 km², created 1961) lies on the southern edge of town; it is a rhino sanctuary (since 1983) and a Ramsar site (1990) known for flamingos. — Wikipedia "Lake Nakuru National Park", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [nature.access] The lake grew from 40 to 68 km² between 2010 and 2020; in 2024 the main park gate was submerged and 677 households and parts of town were flooded. — Wikipedia "Lake Nakuru National Park", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Menengai Crater (a dormant volcano) and the Hyrax Hill prehistoric site are close to the city. — Wikipedia "Nakuru", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex acts carry up to 14 years in prison nationally; public and police harassment is common. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
