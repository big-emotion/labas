# Mombasa: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate: the dossier's **1 EUR ≈ 143 KES** (`research/countries/kenya.md`, implied by Numbeo, November 2026; not re-verified). US-dollar prices are kept in dollars. "[city file]" means `research/cities/kenya/mombasa.md`; bracketed numbers refer to "Sources read" at the end. Many venue prices come from Wikivoyage listings dated 2013–2018 and are flagged as old. Expat.com publishes two Mombasa guides: the French one (city file) quotes rents in euros, the English one [10] in US dollars; they do not match exactly.

Shared caveats for every neighbourhood below:

- Water: the four coastal counties receive about 42% of the water they need (Water CS to the Senate, June 2026; Eastleigh Voice) [city file]; the Mwache Dam was due to start filling from October 2026 [city file]. Expect tanks, vendor water and dry spells, worst on the mainland west (Changamwe, Jomvu, Mikindani) [city file].
- Security: UK advice names beaches among possible terror targets and extends a high kidnap risk to coastal counties (FCDO, read 2026-11-14) [city file]; several governments call the city centre unsafe at night and report robberies on beaches after sunset, so take a taxi at night rather than a tuk-tuk, boda-boda or walking (Wikivoyage) [2]. Dress conservatively away from resorts, especially in Mombasa (FCDO) [city file].
- Up-front cash: the Mombasa guide says one month's deposit plus the first month; the Kenya-wide guide says a 3-month deposit plus 1–2 months in advance (Expat.com) [city file][10].
- Getting around: matatus, tuk-tuks and boda-bodas [city file]; 2018 fares tuk-tuk KES 50–100, boda about KES 50, matatu from KES 20 [city file], before the 2026 fare rises; Uber operates in Mombasa and Bolt also covers Diani, Kilifi and Malindi [city file]. Most expats drive or use ride-hailing [10].

## Neighbourhoods

### Nyali

- Feel: a relatively affluent area of low-density, high-value housing, malls and beach resorts on the mainland north of the island (Wikipedia) [4]; "the premier residential area", with secure compounds, malls, international schools and hospitals (Expat.com) [10]; Mombasa's most upscale suburb (Huduma Global, 2026-02-17) [11]. 216,577 residents in 2019 [city file].
- Rent: 1-bed KES 34,000–35,000/month (≈ €238–245), 2-bed KES 48,000 (≈ €336), 3-bed flats KES 42,500–145,000 (≈ €297–1,014), Property24 14 Nov 2026 [city file]; 1-bed €350–600 (≈ KES 50,050–85,800) and 3-bed €550–1,100, Expat.com FR 2025-12-15 [city file]; 2-bed KES 60,000–100,000 (≈ €420–699), Expat.com 2025-11-07 [city file].
- Getting around: linked to the island by the New Nyali Bridge [4], where traffic is heavy at peak times [10]; tuk-tuks and ride-hailing; matatus to Malindi leave near the bridge [2].
- Watch: Wikivoyage reports poor sea-water quality on Nyali beach (water, not safety) [2]. Beach robberies after dark and the FCDO beach-target warning apply here as everywhere on the coast [2][city file].
- Places:
  - City Mall Nyali (mall with Carrefour, Malindi Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Naivas Nyali (supermarket, Nyali Bazaar Mall, Links Road, opened Aug 2024) — Newsline [14]
  - Kongowea Market (one of the region's largest open-air markets, Links Road, 03:00–13:00; pickpockets) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [3]
  - Nyali Golf Club (18 holes, pool, gym, tennis, Links Road) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [4]
  - Nyali Cinemax (cinema, bowling, Tamarind Road; tickets KES 500, 2013–2018 listing) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mamba Village (crocodile farm, horse and camel rides, Links Road) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [4]
  - New Dwarikadham Temple (Hindu temple with a vegetarian restaurant, Beach Road) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [4]
  - Tamarind Mombasa and Tamarind Dhow (restaurant and dinner cruise on Tudor Creek, Cement Silo Road; reservations) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (schools and hospitals nearby [10]); Q5 "Télétravail" (fibre in most residential areas [city file]); Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Bamburi and Shanzu

- Feel: Bamburi mixes commercial, industrial, residential and tourist uses, with lively nightlife between Front Line and the cement factory (Wikipedia) [5]; about 15 minutes' drive along the Malindi road; Kiembeni Estate has about 100,000 residents (Wikipedia) [3]. Similar amenities to Nyali at lower prices; Shanzu is quieter, resort-style and popular with families (Expat.com) [10].
- Rent: 4-room house in Bamburi €300–600 (≈ KES 42,900–85,800), Expat.com FR 2025-12-15 [city file]; 3-bed house USD 350–700, Expat.com EN [10]. A Shanzu development sells at KES 6.4–12.9 M (≈ €44,755–90,210) [city file].
- Getting around: matatus and buses run from the island through Bamburi towards Mtwapa and Malindi [5]; about 15 minutes by car [3].
- Watch: beach robberies after dark (Wikivoyage) [2]; no Bamburi-specific official remark found.
- Places:
  - Haller Park (nature park on a former quarry, Malindi Road; 08:30–17:30, feeding 14:00–15:00; KES 1,600 in 2013) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [5]
  - Bamburi Forest Trails and Butterfly Park (3.5–10 km trails, bike hire; butterfly pavilion KES 200) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Jomo Kenyatta Public Beach, "Pirates" (public beach) and Pirates Beach Club (beach bar and dancing) — Wikipedia [3][5], Wikivoyage [2]
  - Big Tree (dance club, Bamburi beachfront) and Cheers (24-hour bar, Malindi Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mombasa Go-Kart (track, restaurant and bowling, Shanzu; Tue–Sun 16:00–22:00) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Nguuni Nature Sanctuary (KES 500 entry, 2017) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Bombolulu Workshops and Cultural Centre (crafts by disabled artisans, Kisauni) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" and "Moyen"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Bamburi strip); Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (Shanzu); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Kizingo (south of the island)

- Feel: considered the prime residential area of Mombasa, with State House, the law courts and the municipal council (Wikipedia) [3]; international schools cluster here and in Nyali (Expat.com) [10].
- Rent: no Kizingo-specific figure found. Island-centre benchmark: 1-bed about KES 30,000 (≈ €210), range 20,000–50,000, Numbeo 2026-10-03 with only 13 contributors [city file].
- Getting around: on the island, close to Old Town and government offices [10]; tuk-tuks; the Likoni ferry leaves from Kilindini and Mtongwe, free for pedestrians [3][2].
- Watch: FCDO flags the Likoni ferry area for crime, and Canada advises avoiding the ferry (overcrowding, safety gaps) [city file].
- Places:
  - Aga Khan Academy Mombasa (IB school, Kizingo) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Coast Academy (IGCSE school, Kizingo; €2,200–4,500 a year per Expat.com FR) — Wikipedia [3], [city file]
  - Mama Ngina Park (free seafront park with views of the port and ferry) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mombasa Florida Golf Club (9 holes since 1911, Mama Ngina Drive) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Aga Khan Hospital (Vanga Road, 82 beds) and Mombasa Hospital (private, Hospital Road, 124 beds) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mbaraki Pillar and the mosque beside it (14 m coral tower; mosque about 1400–1450) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (two international schools on the doorstep); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (courts, port, county offices nearby [3]).

### Old Town, Tudor and the island centre (Mvita)

- Feel: Mvita is the city centre; Wikivoyage finds the old town "calmer than the rest of Mvita" [2]; Old Town has Swahili architecture, silver and souvenir shops [2] and a UNESCO-listed Fort Jesus [city file]. Tudor is a middle-class area of homes and shops with the Technical University of Mombasa (Wikipedia) [3]; Expat.com calls it the affordable island option near Old Town and Nyali [10].
- Rent: island-centre 1-bed KES 20,000–50,000 (≈ €140–350), Numbeo 2026-10-03, 13 contributors [city file]. No Tudor-specific figure from a page that opened.
- Getting around: walking and tuk-tuks on the island; Makupa Bridge to Changamwe and the airport, Nyali Bridge north [2]; SGR terminus at Miritini, about 20 km away [city file].
- Watch: FCDO flags Old Town for crime and Canada advises extra caution there [city file]; families and rights groups reported 23 people missing on the Coast in Aug–Oct 2026, one seized on CCTV in Bondeni, Mombasa; police deny involvement (The Star, 2026-11-12) [city file]. The Buxton Point housing redevelopment is in dispute (The Star, 2026-11-14) [city file].
- Places:
  - Fort Jesus (fort and museum, 08:30–18:30; from 7 May 2026: KES 550 citizens, USD 9 African visitors, USD 17 others) — Wikivoyage [2], The Star [12]
  - Swahilipot Hub (non-profit tech and creative hub; search results place it beside Fort Jesus, not confirmed on an opened page) — WordCamp Mombasa [18]
  - Jahazi Coffee House (café, Ndia Kuu Road, 08:00–19:30) and Island Dishes (Kenyan and North African food, Kibokoni Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mackinnon Market (market hall) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mandhry Mosque (1570) and Holy Ghost Cathedral (Catholic, Nyerere Avenue) — Wikipedia [3], Wikivoyage [2]
  - A One Supermarket (Hospital Street) and Quick Mart (24 hours, Haile Selassie Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mombasa Sports Club (since 1896; non-members can play tennis and squash; Mnazi Moja Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Coast General Hospital (public referral hospital, Kisauni Road) — Wikivoyage [2], [city file]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" and "Création, culture" (Swahilipot Hub, heritage); Q3 "Serré"; Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine". Not Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" near the busy centre.

### Mtwapa (Kilifi County, 16 km north)

- Feel: a town about 16 km north of Mombasa on the Malindi road; cosmopolitan, with many foreign residents and visitors, known for nightlife and cheap lodgings; population 90,677 in 2019, up from 18,397 in 1999 (Wikipedia) [6]. Known for nightlife and beach bars (Expat.com) [10]. Wikipedia also notes a reputation for sex tourism, including of children [6].
- Rent: 3-bed townhouse KES 55,000/month (≈ €385), Property24 14 Nov 2026 [city file]; sale 1-bed KES 4.4 M, 2-bed KES 5.4 M [city file].
- Getting around: matatus and buses on the Mombasa–Malindi road [5]; across Mtwapa Creek from Mombasa County [3]. No commute time sourced.
- Watch: Canada's avoid-travel zone on the Kilifi coast starts north of Malindi, not at Mtwapa (2026-11-02) [city file]; FCDO's coastal kidnap and terror warnings still apply [city file].
- Places:
  - Naivas Mtwapa (24-hour supermarket, Petrocity, opened Sept 2024) — The Star [15]
  - Jumba la Mtwana (14th-century ruins near the creek) — Wikipedia [6], Wikivoyage [2]
  - Monsoon (restaurant near Jumba ruins) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mtwapa Creek (birdwatching, deep-sea fishing departures) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - The Beach Africa Backpackers (dorm KES 600) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q4 "Seul·e".

### Diani (Kwale County, about 30 km south)

- Feel: a 17 km beach with a tourism economy (dossier) [7]; "Kenya's premier beach destination" with an active expatriate community (Huduma Global, 2026-02-17) [11]; expats cluster here and in Nyali (Expat.com) [city file]; developing fast, with the Galu end quieter (social post, 2026-11-01) [city file].
- Rent: seafront villas KES 80,000–200,000/month (≈ €559–1,399), Expat.com 2025-11-07 [city file]. No flat rents found.
- Getting around: about 30 km south [7]; via the Likoni ferry or the Dongo Kundu southern bypass (the dossier says completed in 2024; the Wikipedia page still says "expected" in 2024) [7][city file]; door-to-door shared taxi about KES 1,000 and 1.5 hours (Wikivoyage, undated) [2]; Ukunda airstrip [7]; Bolt covers Diani [city file].
- Watch: Canada advises avoiding the Likoni ferry [city file]; coastal kidnap and terror warnings (FCDO) [city file].
- Places:
  - Colobus Conservation (primate rescue charity at the southern end of Diani; small visitor fee) — Discover Wildlife, 2025-12-25 [16]
  - Kongo Mosque (16th-century Swahili mosque at the northern tip of the beach) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Diani Backpackers (camping or dorm KES 600/1,150) and Diani Hostel (dorm KES 800, double KES 1,500) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Ukunda airstrip — Wikipedia [7]
  - Diani-Chale marine reserve (KWS coastal group: USD 25 non-residents, USD 15 African citizens, KES 675 residents) — HapaKenya [13]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Télétravail" (fibre reaches Diani [city file]; no coworking sourced); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" for villas.

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly spends use only prices already in the city file or this pass, converted at 143 KES/EUR. A month becomes a week as × 12 ÷ 52 (≈ 0.231). The single-person lines come from Expat.com's French Mombasa guide (2025-12-15): groceries €220 (≈ KES 31,460) and utilities €90 (≈ KES 12,870) a month [city file]. Local transport fares are 2018 figures and predate the 2026 rises [city file]. Mombasa has no sourced coworking price, gym price by venue or delivery app.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Nyali, for fibre, malls and the beach [10][city file]. Class N permit as in Nairobi (USD 24,000 a year) [city file]. Power: the 29 July 2026 blackout hit the Coast too [city file]; keep a mobile-data backup.
- Monday: work from home; Naivas Nyali, Links Road, for groceries [14].
- Tuesday: work; sunset walk on Nyali beach (swim elsewhere: water quality) [2].
- Wednesday: Nyali Golf Club gym or pool (membership price not found) [2].
- Thursday: Tamarind Dhow dinner cruise when friends visit [2], or Island Dishes in Old Town by ride-hailing [2].
- Friday: morning at Kongowea Market (pickpockets) [2].
- Saturday: Bamburi Forest Trails by bike [2].
- Sunday: Holy Ghost Cathedral Mass or the Dwarikadham temple lunch [2][3].
- Saturday night, by Q2 answer:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Bob's Bar, Malindi Road, Nyali (until sunrise), or Pirates Beach Club and Big Tree in Bamburi [2]; taxi both ways [2].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Tamarind Mombasa, Cement Silo Road, booked ahead [2].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": an early film at Nyali Cinemax (KES 500), then home [2].
- Weekend outing, by Q8 answer:
  - "À la plage": Shanzu or Pirates beach [2][3]; snorkelling in Mombasa Marine National Park (KES 675 residents, USD 25 non-residents, USD 15 African citizens; boats extra) [13][city file]; dive schools at Nyali hotels (Buccaneer Diving, Peponi Divers) [2].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Shimba Hills National Reserve, 33 km (elephants, Kenya's only sable antelope) [8]; KES 675 residents, USD 50 non-residents, USD 20 African citizens [13].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Tsavo East via Voi, 153 km, about 2 hours by car [2]; KES 1,350 residents, USD 80 non-residents, USD 40 African citizens [13]. Realistic as an overnight trip.
  - "Je reste en ville": Fort Jesus and Old Town [2][12].
- Weekly spend (KES):
  - Rent: 1-bed Nyali 34,000 (Property24) to 85,800 (Expat.com €600) a month [city file] → 7,846–19,800
  - Groceries 31,460/month → 7,260; utilities 12,870/month → 2,970; Zuku 40 Mbps 2,999/month → 692 [city file]
  - 7 cheap meals × 350 = 2,450 [city file]
  - 10 tuk-tuk rides × 50–100 = 500–1,000 (2018) [city file]
  - 1 cinema ticket 500 [2]
  - **Total ≈ KES 22,200–34,700 a week ≈ €155–242.**

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". National Museums of Kenya sites charge foreigners in US dollars, with a lower rate for African visitors [12].

### One day

- Morning: Fort Jesus at 08:30 (USD 17, or USD 9 for African visitors) [2][12], then an Old Town walk with a guide from the fort gate (about KES 1,000) [2]; Mandhry Mosque from outside [3].
- Midday: Jahazi Coffee House or Island Dishes [2]; Mackinnon Market [2].
- Afternoon: tuk-tuk (KES 50–100, 2018) to Mama Ngina Park and the Mbaraki Pillar [2]; or Uber to Haller Park in Bamburi for the 14:00 feeding [2].
- Evening: Tamarind Dhow cruise on Tudor Creek, or Tamarind Mombasa, both booked ahead [2]; taxi back [2].

### One week

- Day 1: arrive (SGR or airport), Nyali base [2][city file].
- Day 2: the one-day loop above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Kongowea Market at dawn (opens 03:00), then a matatu over the New Nyali Bridge into the island at morning rush [2][10]; keep the phone hidden [2].

- Day 5 (trip out): Diani via the bypass or ferry: Colobus Conservation [16], Kongo Mosque [7], beach; Diani-Chale marine reserve USD 25 / USD 15 [13].

- Day 7: north: Jumba la Mtwana and lunch at Monsoon near Mtwapa [2][6]. Farther, the Gedi Ruins (UNESCO since 2024, Kilifi County; USD 8 non-African, USD 6 African visitors) [9][12]; Gedi's coordinates place it south of Malindi, and Canada's avoid-travel zone begins north of Malindi [city file] — confirm the latest advice first.
