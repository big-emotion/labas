# Nairobi: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate: the dossier's **1 EUR ≈ 143 KES** (`research/countries/kenya.md`, implied by Numbeo, November 2026; not re-verified). US-dollar prices are kept in dollars, as the city file does. "[city file]" means `research/cities/kenya/nairobi.md`; bracketed numbers refer to "Sources read" at the end. Property portals (BuyRentKenya, Kenya Property Centre, Jumuika) and Expat Arrivals returned HTTP 403 in this pass, so rents for South C and Kasarani are thin.

Shared caveats for every neighbourhood below:

- Water: on 9 Nov 2026 Nairobi Water cut production by 35% and warned of longer rationing (Kenyans.co.ke, 2026-11-09) [city file]; rationing schedules already reached Lavington, Kileleshwa, Kilimani, Karen, Westlands and Riverside in June 2025 (The Star, 2025-06-12) [city file]. Ask a landlord about storage tanks.
- Rains: the short rains (October–December) and long rains (March–May) flood roads; on 6–7 March 2026, 23 people died in Nairobi floods (National Police Service via The Star, 2026-03-07) [23].
- Up-front cash: a 3-month deposit plus 1–2 months in advance, sometimes a month's agency fee (Expat.com Kenya guide, 2025-11-07); the same publisher's Nairobi guide says one month's deposit, so the custom varies [city file].
- Traffic: peak congestion is 7–9 am and 5–8 pm, when a 20-minute trip can pass an hour (Expat.com Nairobi guide) [24]. Matatus cost KES 50–150 (≈ €0.35–1.05) a ride; Uber, Bolt and Little start around KES 300 (≈ €2.10) [city file][24]. Canada calls matatus unsafe after dark and advises against boda-boda (2026-11-02) [city file].
- Protests and elections: frequent Nairobi protests since 2024 and a pre-election period before the 10 Aug 2027 vote (France Diplomatie, 2026-10-29) [city file].

## Neighbourhoods

### Westlands (with Parklands)

- Feel: a former residential area turned one of Nairobi's main commercial districts, with high-rises along Waiyaki Way, Woodvale Grove and Mpaka Road and upper-middle and high-income residents; rapid building has strained infrastructure (Wikipedia) [4]. The main nightlife area, centred on Woodvale Grove and Mpaka Road (Wikivoyage) [2]; "lively and popular with young professionals" (Expat.com, via dossier) [city file].
- Rent: 1-bed KES 70,000–100,000/month (≈ €490–699), Expat.com Nairobi guide 2025-12-12 [city file][24]; 2-bed KES 80,000–150,000 (≈ €559–1,049), Expat.com Kenya guide 2025-11-07 [city file]; a 2-bed asked KES 90,000 (≈ €629) on Property24, 14 Nov 2026 [city file]; furnished flats USD 1,200/month all included (Facebook housing group, 14 Nov 2026) to USD 2,200 (Property24) [city file]. Q2 2026 rents +1.8% (HassConsult via Kenyans.co.ke, 2026-08-04) [city file].
- Getting around: about 3 km (3.2 km by road) north-west of the CBD [4]; the toll Expressway runs from Westlands past JKIA to Mlolongo (27 km) [city file]; matatus and ride-hailing as above.
- Watch: parts of Westlands were among the areas flooded on 7 March 2026 (The Star) [23]. UK advice warns against accepting drinks from strangers in bars because of spiking (FCDO, read 2026-11-14) [city file]; Al-Shabaab could target malls, hotels and nightclubs (FCDO) [city file].
- Places:
  - Sarit Centre (mall with a small cinema, 08:00–21:00) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [4]
  - Westgate Shopping Mall (mall, Mwanzi Road, 08:00–midnight) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [4]
  - Java House, ABC Place (café chain; full meal KES 500–750) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Havana (bar, Woodvale Grove; live band on Saturdays) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mercury Lounge, ABC Place (cocktail bar, cocktails USD 3–6) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - K1 Klub House (live music and cocktails, Ojijo Road, Parklands) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - M. P. Shah Hospital (private hospital) — Wikipedia [4]; Aga Khan University Hospital (3rd Parklands Avenue, Limuru Road) — Wikipedia [13]
  - City Park (free park with a fruit and vegetable market, near Limuru Road) — Wikivoyage [2]

### Kilimani (with Kileleshwa)

- Feel: upper-middle-income, long popular with expatriates, a mix of flats, shops and offices with many restaurants, bars and clubs; a centre of the tech and startup scene since the early 2010s; residents complain about high-rises and nightclub noise (Wikipedia) [3]. Kileleshwa next door went from leafy suburb to high-rise flats after early-2000s zoning changes, mainly upper-middle-income, 22,216 residents in 2019 (Wikipedia) [9]. Modern compounds with pools, gyms and 24-hour security, popular with young professionals (Expat.com Nairobi guide) [24].
- Rent: 1-bed KES 70,000–100,000 (≈ €490–699), Expat.com 2025-12-12 [city file]; 2-bed KES 60,000–120,000 (≈ €420–839), Expat.com 2025-11-07 [city file]; a 3-bed of 154 m² asked KES 96,000 (≈ €671) on Property24, 14 Nov 2026 [city file]. Kilimani rents dipped 0.3% in Q2 2026 (HassConsult) [city file]. Cytonn's older guide put Kilimani flats at KES 40,000–150,000 [city file].
- Getting around: about 5 km west of the CBD for both (Wikipedia) [3][9]; Ngong Road matatus; Upper Hill is the next neighbourhood east [3].
- Watch: Kilimani and Kileleshwa were on the June 2025 water-rationing schedule (The Star) [city file]. Wikipedia notes residents' complaints about nightclub noise [3].
- Places:
  - Yaya Centre (mall since 1992, Hurlingham edge, Chandarana Food Plus supermarket; Maasai Market on Sundays) — Wikipedia [3], Wikivoyage [2]
  - Prestige Plaza (mall, Ngong Road) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Lycée français Denis Diderot (French school, Kilimani) — Wikipedia [3]; 2026–27 fees in the city file
  - Nairobi Arboretum (30.4 ha forest reserve between Kilimani and Kileleshwa; entrance fee) — Wikipedia [9], Wikivoyage [2]
  - Basic Mall with a Quickmart supermarket, and Kasuku Centre (Kileleshwa) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Mama Oliech (Kenyan restaurant, Marcus Garvey Road), Ashaki Grill (nyama choma, Kindaruma Road), Roadhouse Grill (Dennis Pritt Road) — The Kenya Times, 2026-06-10 [20]
  - Habesha (Ethiopian restaurant, Argwings Kodhek Road near Yaya Centre, about USD 5–6) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Nairobi Hospital (Argwings Kodhek Road, Upper Hill, next door) — Wikipedia [14]
  - Nairobi Garage Kilimani (coworking) — nairobigarage.com [25]

### Lavington

Quiet suburban living with easy access to international schools (Expat.com Nairobi guide) [24]; a 2026 agency guide says it suits people who drive rather than walk (search snippet, not opened).
- Rent: 3-bed house KES 100,000–200,000 (≈ €699–1,399), Expat.com 2025-11-07 [city file]; furnished flat USD 1,000 or KES 135,000/month (≈ €944), or KES 5,500 a night (Facebook housing group, 14 Nov 2026) [city file]; 6-bed house KES 500,000 [city file]. Q4 2025 rents +2.6% (HassConsult) [city file].
- Getting around: about 5.5 km north-west of the CBD (Wikipedia) [7]; James Gichuru Road and Ngong Road; mostly car or ride-hailing.
- Watch: on the June 2025 water-rationing schedule (The Star) [city file].

### Karen

- Feel: affluent low-density suburb with large houses, tree cover and minimum plot sizes; residents mainly wealthier Kenyan households, politicians and expatriates (Wikipedia) [5]; leafy and close to international schools (Expat.com) [city file]; known for Brookhouse, Hillcrest and The Banda School (Expat.com Nairobi guide) [24].
- Rent: 3-bed house KES 150,000–300,000 (≈ €1,049–2,098), Expat.com 2025-11-07 [city file]; 3-bed houses of 180 m² KES 100,000–250,000 (≈ €699–1,748), Property24 14 Nov 2026 [city file]; a house at KES 300,000 "negotiable" plus service charge (Facebook group, 14 Nov 2026) [city file]; 4–5-bed KES 300,000–450,000 [city file]. Q2 2026 rents +2.7% [city file].
- Getting around: south-west of the CBD along Langata Road and Ngong Road (Wikipedia) [5]; Karen Hospital is about 17 km from the CBD (Wikipedia) [15]. Car-dependent; the BRT pilot route 111 runs CBD–Ngong Town (Expat.com) [24].
- Watch: on the June 2025 water-rationing schedule (The Star) [city file]. No Karen-specific crime remark found in FCDO or Canada advice.
- Places:

  - Karen Hospital (private, 102 beds, off Langata Road) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Karen Blixen Museum (house and gardens; from 7 May 2026: KES 550 citizens, USD 9 African visitors, USD 17 others) — Wikivoyage [2], The Star [18]
  - Kazuri Beads (clay-bead workshop since 1977, next to the Karen Blixen Museum) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Giraffe Centre (Lang'ata; KES 1,500 non-resident adults) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Ngong Racecourse (horse racing three Sundays a month) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [5]
  - The West Nairobi School (school) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Nairobi Garage Karen (coworking in gardens) — nairobigarage.com [25]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "Dans la savane ou le désert" (Nairobi National Park and Sheldrick nearby); Q3 "Le prix compte peu". Poor for Q3 "Serré" and for anyone without a car.

### Gigiri and Runda

- Feel: one of the most upscale suburbs, home to the UN Office at Nairobi (UNEP, UN-Habitat), the US Embassy and "one of the largest expat communities in Africa" (Wikipedia) [8]; many diplomatic families on large secure plots (Expat.com Nairobi guide) [24]. Cytonn calls Runda and Muthaiga top-security, strictly zoned areas [city file].
- Rent: 2-bed guesthouse in Runda KES 200,000/month incl. water and internet (≈ €1,399); new 4-bed villa KES 400,000 (≈ €2,797) (Facebook group, 14 Nov 2026) [city file]; Runda/Kitisuru often above KES 300,000 (Expat.com) [city file]. Runda rents +3.4% in Q2 2026 [city file].
- Getting around: north of the centre along Limuru Road; no sourced distance or commute time.
- Places:
  - Village Market (mall, Limuru Road, 07:00–23:00; food court; Maasai Market on Fridays) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [8]
  - United Nations Office at Nairobi (UN complex between Karura Forest and the US Embassy) — Wikipedia [8], Wikivoyage [2]
  - Karura Forest (about 1,040 ha, 50 km of trails; KES 600 non-residents; bike hire KES 500 for two hours; River Cafe) — [city file], Wikivoyage [2]
  - International School of Kenya (pre-K to grade 12, North American diploma and IB, end of Peponi Road) — Wikipedia [12]
  - Two Rivers Mall (Runda, opened 2017, 67,000 m², 60 m "Eye of Kenya" wheel) — Wikipedia [11]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (ISK); Q5 "Salarié·e" for UN or embassy staff; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Karura on the doorstep); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" only.

### South C (with South B)

- Feel: a residential estate next to Wilson Airport, bordering Nairobi National Park to the south; Wikipedia calls it upper-class and lists the Red Cross, NEMA and KEBS head offices and several sports clubs [6]. The city file names South B, South C and Madaraka among lower- and upper-middle-income areas (Wikipedia "Nairobi", via dossier) [city file]. Online sources disagree on its standing (search summary).
- Rent: 2-bed unfurnished asking KES 50,000/month (≈ €350) in two listings seen only as search snippets (Kenya Property Centre and BuyRentKenya pages returned 403), undated — low confidence.
- Getting around: south of the CBD off Mombasa Road; no sourced commute time. The Expressway runs along Mombasa Road to JKIA [city file].
- Watch: South B and South C were among the areas flooded on 7 March 2026 (The Star) [23]; floods at the South C substation cut power to several Embakasi estates for three days or more from 4 March 2026 (Citizen Digital) [city file]. FCDO names Airport South Road for carjacking (read 2026-11-14) [city file].
- Places:
  - South C Sports Club, MOW Sports Club, Kenya Motor Sports Club (sports clubs) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Drive-in cinema at Bellevue, Mombasa Road — Wikipedia [6]
  - Capital Centre (Mombasa Road; Maasai Market on Wednesdays) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - The Carnivore (restaurant, Langata Road near Uhuru Gardens; pricey, levies added) — Wikivoyage [2], The Kenya Times [20]
  - Nairobi National Park (borders South C; main gate details in Visiting) — Wikipedia [6], [city file]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen"; Q4 "En couple" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q8 "Dans la savane ou le désert" (park next door); Q5 "Salarié·e" for jobs along Mombasa Road or at the airport. Flood and power record is a drawback.

### Kasarani and Roysambu (Thika Road)

- Feel: a mixed-use area along Thika Road, in the city's second most populated sub-county, with Moi International Sports Centre and several malls (Wikipedia) [10]. Cytonn lists Kasarani among the cheapest rental areas [city file].
- Rent: flats KES 8,000–20,000 (≈ €56–140), Cytonn, undated and probably older [city file]. Search snippets of 2025 listings put basic 1-beds at KES 15,000–35,000 (≈ €105–245) — pages not opened (403), low confidence.
- Getting around: about 12 km by road north-east of the CBD (Wikipedia) [10]; Thika Superhighway matatus; Thika Road was among the highways cut by the March 2026 floods [23].
- Watch: on 28 April 2026 a woman drowned in floodwater near Roysambu and the Street 13 bridge to Mwiki was submerged; KDF led the response in Kasarani (Citizen Digital, 2026-04-29) [22]. Roysambu, Kahawa West and Githurai were among the worst-hit areas on 7 March 2026 (The Star) [23].
- Places:
  - Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani (60,000-seat stadium, pool, gym; 2029 World Athletics host) — Wikipedia [10], [city file]
  - Garden City Mall (Thika Superhighway, 09:30–21:00) and Thika Road Mall (08:00–22:00) — Wikivoyage [2], Wikipedia [10]
  - USIU-Africa (university) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Ashaki Grill (nyama choma, Thome Estate off Thika Road) — The Kenya Times [20]
  - Safari Park Hotel — Wikipedia [10]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" on a budget; Q5 "Salarié·e" with a long commute; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" in a working-class sense. Check flood maps before signing.

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly spends use only prices already in the city file or this pass, converted at 143 KES/EUR. A month is converted to a week as × 12 ÷ 52 (≈ 0.231). Every line is an asking price or a guide's budget figure, mostly low or medium confidence. The single-person lines (groceries KES 25,000, utilities KES 12,000, mobile KES 2,500 a month) come from Expat.com's Kenya guide, 2025-11-07 [city file].

- Wednesday: home; early run or walk in the Nairobi Arboretum [9].
- Thursday: dinner at Habesha on Argwings Kodhek Road [2].
- Friday: work; after-work at Mama Oliech, Marcus Garvey Road [20].
- Saturday: morning at Karura Forest (bike hire KES 500 for two hours) [2], by ride-hailing (travel time not sourced).
- Sunday: Maasai Market at Yaya Centre [2]; Holy Family Basilica or All Saints' Cathedral in the centre for a service [2].
- Saturday night, by Q2 answer:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Westlands, Woodvale Grove and Mpaka Road clubs, Havana's Saturday live band [2]; ride-hailing both ways; FCDO warns about drink spiking [city file].

  - "Tranquille chez moi": home with Zuku fibre [city file]; no food-delivery app was sourced.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week

- Before flying: French/EU passports need an eTA (USD 32.50) with compulsory Kenyan travel health insurance since 5 Oct 2026; most African passports have been exempt since 30 May 2025 [city file].
- Where to stay first: a backpackers' in Milimani/Kilimani (Milimani Backpackers, Oldeani Crescent; Manyatta Backpackers, Milimani Road; singles from about KES 1,000, doubles under KES 3,000) [2], or a furnished flat in Lavington at KES 5,500 a night [city file].
- Day 1: land at JKIA; Uber or Bolt from KES 900, airport bus 34 KES 100 (2023), street taxi about KES 2,000 [city file]. Buy a Safaricom SIM (starter KES 50–100) and set up M-Pesa in the same visit [city file]; 5 GB data KES 1,000 (2023) [city file].
- Day 2: cash: Ecobank, Co-operative Bank, NCBA, DTB and I&M charge no fee on foreign cards; Equity, KCB, Absa and Standard Chartered charge KES 400–700 [2]. Opening a bank account needs a passport, residence permit and proof of address [city file], so on a visitor's eTA expect to live on M-Pesa and foreign cards.
- Day 3: visit Kilimani and Kileleshwa flats (1-bed KES 70,000–100,000 asking) [city file]; ask about water tanks and rationing [city file].
- Day 4: compare Westlands and South C [city file][6]; ride-hailing between viewings.
- Day 5: negotiate. Budget a 3-month deposit plus 1–2 months in advance and possibly a month's agency fee; some landlords ask only one month's deposit [city file]. 3 of 14 upmarket posts said "negotiable" [city file]. A returnee warns furnished flats can cost three to four times normal rent [city file].
- Day 6: errands at The Junction or Yaya Centre [2].
- Day 7: Karura Forest [2].
- Saturday night and weekend outings: as profile 1.
- First-week spend (KES): lodging 7 × 1,000–5,500 = 7,000–38,500; SIM 100; JKIA ride 900; 10 ride-hailing trips × 300 = 3,000; 14 meals × 500–850 = 7,000–11,900 [2][city file]; data 1,000. **Total ≈ KES 19,000–55,400 ≈ €133–387**, before deposit and advance rent.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Museums and parks charge foreigners in US dollars, with lower rates for African citizens [18][19].

### One day

- Morning: Nairobi National Park at opening, by ride-hailing to the main gate (USD 80 non-residents, USD 40 African citizens) [19][city file]; or, for a shorter morning, the Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage visit at 11:00 (arrive 10 minutes early; USD 15 per Expat.com) [2][24].
- Midday: Giraffe Centre in Lang'ata (KES 1,500 non-resident adults) [2]; lunch in Karen at The Hub [2].
- Afternoon: Karen Blixen Museum (USD 17 non-African, USD 9 African visitors) and Kazuri Beads next door [18][2].
- Evening: dinner at The Carnivore, Langata Road [2][20]; ride-hailing back, not walking after dark [2].

### One week

- Day 1: arrive, SIM and M-Pesa [city file]; Yaya Centre and Kilimani dinner [2][20].
- Day 2: the one-day loop above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Maasai Market morning at whichever site runs that day (Tue Kijabe Street, Wed Capital Centre, Thu The Junction, Fri Village Market, Sat High Court car park, Sun Yaya Centre); pay about half to two-thirds of the first price [2]. Then a Ngong Road matatu at evening rush hour with a local, phone out of sight [2][24].
- Day 4: centre by day: Nairobi National Museum on Museum Hill (USD 18 / USD 9) [18], Nairobi Gallery, All Saints' Cathedral, Jamia Mosque from outside (interior closed to non-Muslims), August 7 Memorial Park (KES 30) [2]. Leave the CBD before dark (mugging and "devil's breath" reports, 2025) [city file].
- Day 5 (trip out): Hell's Gate, about 90 km, gorge walk and cycling [16]; USD 50 non-residents, USD 20 African citizens [19]. Lake Naivasha is next to it [16].
- Day 6: Karura Forest and Village Market in Gigiri [2]; or a Gor Mahia home match if one is on (2026–27 opener at Nyayo Stadium; regular season ticket KES 4,080 for 17 games ≈ KES 240 a game) [21].
- Day 7 (trip out, optional): Ngong Hills ridge with a Kenya Forest Service guide [17], or the SGR to Mombasa (about 5 h, KES 1,000–3,000) to compare the coast [city file].
