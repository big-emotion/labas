- [housing.areas] Nyali, on the mainland north across the New Nyali Bridge, is the affluent low-density area with malls, beach resorts and a golf club; 216,577 residents (2019). — Wikipedia "Nyali", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] On the island, Kizingo is the prime residential area (State House, law courts, international schools) and Tudor is middle class; Likoni to the south is lower and lower-middle income. — Wikipedia "Mombasa", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
