## Items

- [housing.areas] Kisumu had about 398,000 people in the urban area and about 1.16 million in the metro area (2019), including suburbs such as Kondele, Maseno and Ahero; the page gives conflicting city figures. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Kenya's main Lake Victoria port and a regional trade hub; key activities are agricultural processing, brewing, textiles, sugar and irrigated rice. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Fish farmers in the Kisumu Gulf were warned in Nov 2026 after tests found dissolved oxygen of 2.1 mg/L, low enough to kill caged fish; pollution and organic waste are among possible causes. — The Star, 2026-11-06, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.startup] A fireworks and drone-show company recently set up in Kisumu plans the city's first drone light show for the 2026 festive season and is hiring freelance pilots and technicians; a basic show starts at KES 200,000 (≈ €1,399). — The Star, 2026-11-06, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Kisumu International Airport has regular daily flights to Nairobi and Mombasa. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Passenger trains to Nairobi on the old metre-gauge line stopped in 2013 and the planned standard-gauge extension to Kisumu has not materialised; the Busia–Kisumu highway is described as increasingly congested. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Matatu fares rose 25% in April 2026 and operators announced a further 50% and a nationwide strike in May 2026 over fuel prices. — The Star 2026-04-15; Citizen Digital 2026-05-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Canada says good medical facilities are available in Nairobi but very limited elsewhere in Kenya. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Universities nearby include Maseno University, JOOUST, Great Lakes University and campuses of the University of Nairobi, Catholic University of East Africa and Mount Kenya University; Aga Khan Primary is one named primary school. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Canada lists Kisumu among Kenya's high-crime cities, without a neighbourhood-level advisory. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-11-02, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Road accident black spots around the city include Ojola/Kisian, Kisat Bridge, Ahero, Sondu and Awasi. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] A memorial park unveiled in July 2026 on the former Kachok dumpsite honours victims of police brutality, named after a six-month-old who died after a police assault in Nyalenda during the August 2017 post-election unrest; in Nov 2026 residents protested an investor's claim on the land. — The Star, 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France flags a pre-election period ahead of the 10 Aug 2027 general election, with possible deadly violence at rallies (national advisory, no Kisumu detail). — France Diplomatie, 2026-10-29, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Kisumu Museum (with a recreated Luo homestead), the Kisumu Impala Sanctuary, the Kit Mikayi rock and the old town clock on Oginga Odinga Road are local landmarks. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] English and Kiswahili are official, but many residents speak Dholuo; most residents are Luo, with a recent rise in other groups. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Moi Stadium and Jomo Kenyatta Sports Ground are the main venues; Kisumu hosts a leg of the national rugby sevens circuit. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mostly Christian (including a Catholic cathedral), with mosques and a Hindu temple. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Tropical rainforest climate with no true dry season: average about 22.9 °C and about 1,388 mm of rain a year; January driest, April wettest. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lakeside spots include Dunga Beach and its papyrus wetland (community-run centre), Hippo Point for sunsets, and Ndere Island national reserve in the Winam Gulf. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Downtown and Lower Town are being revitalised (lakefront, pedestrian streets) and a Lake Victoria ring road is under construction, with no completion date given. — Wikipedia "Kisumu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex acts carry up to 14 years in prison nationally; public and police harassment is common. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
