# Gabú, Guinea-Bissau

## Items

- [cost.living] Hotel Visiom: about 12,000 XOF for a double room (≈ €18.29) in 2023; fan, private toilet, electricity only at night, no mosquito nets. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", listing dated 2023, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A street vendor sells mocha-pot espresso at 100 XOF small / 200 XOF normal (≈ €0.15 / €0.30); black-and-white printing costs 200 XOF a page. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Shared taxi Gabú–Labé (Guinea): about 15,000 XOF (≈ €22.87) plus 3,000 XOF per bag (≈ €4.57), up to 16 hours on rough roads. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Gabú is a market town with the country's second-largest market, trading coal, ceramics and farm produce, with traders from Guinea, Senegal, Mali and Sierra Leone. — Wikipedia "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The local economy produces fruit, vegetables, charcoal, fish, meat, crafts and pottery; donkeys are used for farming and moving goods and people. — Wikipédia (pt) "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Banque Atlantique Guinée-Bissau put out a tender to build a new branch in Gabú. — O Democrata GB, 2026-10-15, company, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Tenders were issued for rehabilitating farm tracks in the Bafatá and Gabú regions and for a 100 ha rice scheme (COPIRO) in the Gabú region. — O Democrata GB, 2026-04-13 and 2026-09-16, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The 2023 hotel listing says electricity worked only at night; no recent town-wide power source found. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", 2023, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] BAO bank has an ATM outside its branch (2023); travel guides say BAO ATMs work only with a local account; Western Union is present (10 % commission). — Wikivoyage "Gabú" and "Guinea-Bissau", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Wikivoyage's description of the drive from Bissau is contradictory ("a three-hour drive" and "1-hr drive with a good road"); no fare given. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Gabú lies on the main road east from Bissau via Bafatá to the Buruntuma border with Guinea, described in 2025 as "sometimes good, sometimes not" but usually passable. — Scoot West Africa (Matthew Christie), 2025-07-24, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] National roads link Gabú to Bafatá, Piche, Pirada (Senegal border) and Madina do Boé; the old aerodrome sees only occasional flights and its runway is used as a street. — Wikipédia (pt) "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Selling fuel from roadside containers was banned nationwide after a container exploded in nearby Bafatá, burning 163 people (Feb 2026). — Lusa via AMAN Alliance, 2026-02-16, intl-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] The Hospital Regional de Gabú hosted a Chinese medical team in May 2026; ultrasound was unavailable for lack of equipment, and the hospital reports 20+ TB inpatients and worsening HIV. — O Democrata GB, 2026-05-27, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] The clinical director reported five moto accidents in one day, blaming alcohol and disregard of traffic rules; a seriously injured victim was sent to Simão Mendes in Bissau. — O Democrata GB, 2026-05-27, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Canada advises a high degree of caution nationwide; crime is higher outside major cities, with road banditry and carjacking; avoid travelling after dark. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Outside Bissau some landmines remain; follow local advice and stay on paved roads. — UK FCDO, updated 2025-12-10 (read 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Discotheques close during Ramadan. — Wikivoyage "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Main ethnic group Fula and main religion Islam. — Wikipedia "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Named Nova Lamego under Portuguese rule; renamed after independence after the pre-colonial Kaabu empire; a local writer published a book on the "Reino de Gabú" in 2026. — Wikipedia "Gabú" and O Democrata GB (2026-08-23), academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Local media include Rádio Sol Mansi and Rádio Gandal. — Wikipédia (pt) "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [social.sport] Gabú hosts national-league football matches (Guiné-Liga). — O Democrata GB, 2026-06-23, local-media, confidence: low
- [social.pace] Population 37,525 (2010), the second city of the country. — Wikipedia "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] The Portuguese Wikipedia gives 43,556 (2009) in its infobox and 41,612 for the urban area (2009 census). — Wikipédia (pt) "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] About a quarter less rain than Bissau (≈ 1,444 mm/year); dry Nov–May, wet Jun–Oct; mean daily maximum from 29.2 °C (Aug) to 38.0 °C (Apr). — Wikipedia "Gabú", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
