# Bissau, Guinea-Bissau

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**. USD figures are converted with the Wikivoyage rate "as of October 2026" (**1 USD ≈ 585 XOF**), so USD→EUR conversions are approximate. Research limitation: the shared web-search budget was exhausted after a handful of queries, so most items come from pages fetched directly (dossier sources, O Democrata GB's own site search, Wikivoyage, official advisories, one travel-company blog).
