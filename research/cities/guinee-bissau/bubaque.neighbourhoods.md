# Bubaque: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: fixed peg, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (dossier). Source numbers in brackets refer to "Sources read" below. "City file" = `research/cities/guinee-bissau/bubaque.md`.

Research-pass notes, read first:
- Bubaque town had 4,299 inhabitants and the island 6,427 at the 2009 census (city file); the 2018 EU guide gives about 11,300 for the island [3]. No source names **bairros** inside the town, so the "neighbourhoods" below are the four parts of the island that sources describe: the port town, the channel shore facing Rubane, the inland village of Bijante, and Bruce beach.
- There is **no long-term rent figure** for the island (city file). Every "Rent" line says so; the weekly budgets use lodge room rates instead, which overstate what a resident would pay.
- Almost all prices come from one travel-company article (Scoot West Africa, first published 2021, updated Feb 2024) [4]; venues and opening days from the EU-funded guide date from **March 2018** [3].
- The WebSearch budget ran out after four queries; the rest of this sheet rests on pages opened directly.
- Living here full-time is a serious undertaking: no mains electricity per a 2025 visitor (city file), a health centre with five doctors and no working X-ray in 2025 (city file), and one scheduled ferry a week (city file). The weeks below describe what a week could look like, not a recommendation.

## Neighbourhoods

### Port and town centre
- Feel: the town lives around its port and the market beside the pontoon; disordered streets, mixed building styles, a few colonial traces (2018) [3]. The port is crowded on boat days, when fish, poultry, livestock and people all come off the ferry [3]. Most commerce is around the port, and shops stock only what locals buy (2024) [4].
- Rent: none found (city file). Short stays: Mango Lodge 20,000 XOF a room with breakfast (≈ €30), 20 % off Sunday–Thursday; Saldo Mar rooms from 20,000 XOF (city file, Scoot 2024).
- Getting around: on foot; bikes about 5,000 XOF/day, often in poor condition (city file). There is one basic road and few vehicles (city file). No moto-taxi fare found.
- Watch: the port was in "very bad condition" with no proper quay in 2018 [3]. The Bubaque health centre's solar power cannot guarantee 24-hour supply, and it reports drinking-water and sanitation problems, especially in the rainy season (O Democrata, 2025-07-05, city file). No ATM or card information found (city file).
- Places:
  - Bubaque port and pontoon (port; ferry arrivals) — [3]
  - Old market (market, right on leaving the port: vegetables, fish, meat, rice, medicines, small appliances) and new market (left on leaving the port) — [3]; an EU project (2013–2016) renovated the central market [3]
  - Super Mercado Agoueinitt (supermarket, about 100 m up the main road from the port, on the right) — [4]
  - Restaurant Nuna (restaurant behind the first row of shops; whole fish with rice and salad 1,500 XOF, large beer 750 XOF) — [4]
  - Restaurant Djass (restaurant-bar overlooking the port) — [4]
  - Djiu Mancebo (African food, near the port, 2018) — [3]
  - Museu de Bubaque "Padre Biasutti" (museum of Bijagó art: masks, statues, canoes; up the avenue to the left of the port; Tue–Sun 10:00–13:00 and 16:00–19:00 in 2018) — [3]; Wikipedia via city file
  - Discothèque Online (nightclub, Thursday–Sunday, late) — [4]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (cheapest rooms, market at the door); Q3 "Serré" for food (1,500 XOF fish plate); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" only Thursday to Sunday.

### Channel shore facing Rubane
- Feel: the colonial administrator's house faces the narrow channel between Bubaque and the sacred island of Rubane [3]; Cajou Lodge opened in 2023 above the same channel [4]; Saldo Mar is on the north coast [4]. Lodge country rather than village: most of the nicer accommodation is run by Europeans, according to a 2025 visitor (city file).
- Rent: none found (city file). Cajou Lodge 120,000 XOF/night full board (≈ €183), 25 % off Sunday–Thursday (2024) [4].
- Getting around: walking distance or a short bike ride from the port (not timed in any source). Hotel boats cross to Rubane; at Ponta Anchaca a three-course lunch is about 15,000 XOF with the boat taxi included for small groups [4].
- Watch: Cajou Lodge powers its air-conditioning by generator in the evenings (city file). Nothing else area-specific.
- Places:
  - Maison de l'ancien administrateur (colonial building facing the channel, until recently the island's administrative seat) — [3]
  - Cajou Lodge (lodge with restaurant, bar, small pool and bike hire; concerts and parties mostly Thursday–Sunday) — [4]
  - Saldo Mar (guesthouse and Mediterranean restaurant with wood-fired pizza; meals by advance booking) — [3]; [4]
  - Hotel Ponta Anchaca, across the channel on Rubane (hotel with pool; lunch stop) — [3]; [4]
- Fits: Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (Saldo Mar, Cajou Lodge); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (lodge rates); Q5 "J'entreprends" for anyone looking at tourism.

### Bijante (Tabanca Nova) and the hidden beach
- Feel: the largest village after the town, about 4 km inland from the museum, first along a street of houses and then through dense forest; it keeps distinctive Bijagó rituals and receives visitors warmly, children quickly gathering round (2018) [3]. Courtesy calls for bringing tobacco or cane spirit for the village chief [3]. Bijante residents live part of the year on sacred Rubane to grow rice [3].
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: on foot from the museum, about 4 km [3]; paths continue to the hidden beach [4].
- Watch: visitors are asked to greet people, ask permission before leaving usual paths and respect sacred areas (Scoot 2024, city file). Wikivoyage warns of Nile crocodiles and hippos in the marshes (city file).
- Places:
  - Tabanca Nova de Bijagó / Bijante (village) — [3]; [4]
  - Forest path from the museum to Bijante (walk, ~4 km) — [3]
  - Bubaque's hidden beach (horseshoe bay reached on foot from Bijante; go at high tide; say you are heading to the "praia") — [4]; Trailing Ivy 2025 [5]
- Fits: Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (forest walk) and "À la plage"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" for those drawn to Bijagó culture; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Bruce beach (south coast)
- Feel: a white-sand beach with calm, warm water, palm trees and dense forest almost touching the sea, at the far end of the island; a village of the same name lies nearby (2018) [3]. A 2025 visitor found it large and nearly empty [5].
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: about 18 km from town by the island's only paved road per the 2018 guide [3] (Scoot also says 18 km [4]; a 2025 blog says about 14 km by bike [5]; the guide's bike route gives 15 km in about 40 minutes [3]). About 35 minutes by car (Wikivoyage, city file). Ride out before 10:00 and back after 16:00 to avoid the midday sun [3].
- Watch: stingrays abound; wear sandals and shuffle your feet entering the water [3][4]. Dakosta Eco Retreat's buildings were damaged in the 2023 rainy season and it moved towards Wefa Natura (Scoot, 2024) [4], so do not count on food or shelter at the beach.
- Places:
  - Praia de Bruce (beach) — [3]; [4]; [5]; Wikivoyage via city file
  - Bruce village (tabanca next to the beach) — [3]
  - Bubaque Island Hotel (hotel at Bruce beach; Wikivoyage marks its link dead) — [6]
  - Bubaque–Bruce bike route (only paved road, monkeys and sacred trees along the way) — [3]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

Common facts (city file unless marked): Consulmar ferry 16,500 XOF one way for foreigners, about 4 hours, once a week each way; fast boats 30,000 XOF in the dry season when demand allows; local canoes 3,000–3,500 XOF, 3.5–5 hours — **France strongly advises against crossing by pirogue** (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [7] and Canada calls them unsafe (2026-10-13). Air Bijagos advertised about 90,000 XOF each way, booking by WhatsApp in Nov 2026; France says there is no air link to the islands and private companies land on Bubaque only in high season [7] (sources conflict). MTN and Orange both work on the island (2024). No ATM found: bring cash from Bissau. Weekly lodging below = Mango Lodge 5 nights Sunday–Thursday at 16,000 XOF (20 % off) + 2 nights at 20,000 XOF = 120,000 XOF (Scoot 2024, city file); the room price is per room.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **port and town centre**, for the shops, the market and the strongest chance of a phone signal near the town (Orange worked for internet and calls in town in 2024, city file). Power is the constraint: no mains electricity per a 2025 visitor; lodges run on solar and generators (city file). Remote work here is untested by any source.
- Week:
  - Mon: work from the lodge; lunch at Restaurant Nuna (1,500 XOF) [4]; groceries at Super Mercado Agoueinitt [4]. On foot.
  - Tue: work; museum visit (opens 10:00–13:00, 16:00–19:00 in 2018) [3].
  - Wed: work in the morning; walk to Bijante (~4 km) at 16:00 [3].
  - Thu: work; drink at Restaurant Djass overlooking the port [4].
  - Fri: market morning (old and new markets) [3]; restock before the weekend boat traffic.
  - Sat: bike to Bruce beach, out before 10:00, back after 16:00 (5,000 XOF hire) [3][4].
  - Sun: rest; dinner booked at Saldo Mar [4].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Discothèque Online (Thursday–Sunday) or a party at Mango Lodge / Cajou Lodge [4].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Saldo Mar, booked ahead [4], or Cajou Lodge [4].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the island default.
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Bruce beach [3] or the hidden beach [4].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": the forest walk to Bijante [3]; no mountains.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bubaque.
  - "Je reste en ville": the port on ferry day and the market [3].
- Weekly spend:
  - Lodging: 120,000 XOF (Mango Lodge, see above)
  - Meals: 7 × 1,500 = 10,500 XOF (Restaurant Nuna) [4]
  - Drinks: 3 × 750 = 2,250 XOF (large beer at Nuna) [4]
  - Bike: 2 days × 5,000 = 10,000 XOF [4]
  - Total: 142,750 XOF (≈ €218) a week, before phone data (no bundle price found) and a ferry to Bissau.

Salaried work on the island is not sourced: most families farm and fish; tourism is limited (city file). The island hosts the biosphere-reserve headquarters (city file), and the health centre has five doctors (city file), but no vacancies or salaries were found.

  - Tue: evening drink at Cajou Lodge [4].
  - Thu: concert at Mango Lodge or Cajou Lodge [4].

  - Sun: Bruce beach by bike [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" Discothèque Online [4]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Saldo Mar [4]; "Tranquille chez moi" at home.
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" Bruce [3]; "En montagne, en forêt" Bijante forest walk [3]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic from Bubaque; "Je reste en ville" the town market and port [3].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **port and town centre**, where all commerce is (2024) [4].
- Context: tourism is limited and mostly boat charters from Senegal (city file); a 2018 EU project set up the islands' first tourism-training school, run by the local organisation Faspebi [3]. Shops carry only what locals buy [4], and much food comes from the mainland (2025, city file). Company-registration steps and any island business licence: not found.
- Week:
  - Mon: market morning to see what sells [3]; talk to Super Mercado Agoueinitt about supply [4].
  - Tue: visit the museum to understand Bijagó crafts [3].
  - Wed: tour the channel-shore lodges (Cajou Lodge, Saldo Mar) [4].
  - Thu: ferry day planning (Consulmar timetable posted weekly; tickets at the port booth, cash only, booths open 2 hours before departure) [8].
  - Fri: supply run or pick-up at the port [3].
  - Sat: Bijante visit, with the customary gift for the chief [3].
  - Sun: rest at Bruce beach [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" Discothèque Online [4]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Cajou Lodge [4]; "Tranquille chez moi" at home.
- Weekend outings: as profile 1.
- Weekly spend:
  - Lodging: 120,000 XOF; meals 10,500 XOF; one bike day 5,000 XOF [4]
  - One supply round trip to Bissau by Consulmar: 2 × 16,500 = 33,000 XOF (city file)
  - Total: 168,500 XOF (≈ €257) a week, before stock and freight.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **port and town centre**, nearest the market and the health centre.
- Read first: Bubaque has a high school; bilingual Kriol–Portuguese schooling in the archipelago ended in 2022 (city file). Primary schools, fees and any French- or English-language school: not found. The health centre has five doctors who also cover nearby islands, X-ray was not working in 2025, and several areas of the health region have no evacuation boat (O Democrata 2025-07-05 and 2026-05-10, city file); Canada says there are no clinics on the islands (2026-10-13, city file). On current sources this is a hard place to raise children.
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school in town (no timetable found); afternoons at the market or the museum [3].
  - Sat: Bruce beach with sandals for the stingrays [3].
  - Sun: Bijante visit, children are welcomed by village children [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not a family option; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Saldo Mar (pizza) [3]; "Tranquille chez moi" the default.
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" Bruce [3]; "En montagne, en forêt" the Bijante walk [3]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic from Bubaque; "Je reste en ville" the port and market [3].
- Weekly spend (two adults, two children):
  - Lodging: 120,000 XOF (one room; whether it sleeps four is not stated)
  - Meals: 4 × 7 × 1,500 = 42,000 XOF [4]
  - Total: 162,000 XOF (≈ €247) a week, before school costs (not found), bikes and evacuation insurance.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Before leaving Bissau: buy an Orange SIM (500 XOF with ID) and take enough cash, since no ATM is reported on Bubaque (city file).
- Day 1: cross on the Consulmar ferry (16,500 XOF, about 4 hours; timetable posted weekly; tickets only at the Avenida 3 de Agosto nº 40 office or port booths, no cards) [8]; check in at Mango Lodge (20,000 XOF with breakfast) [4].
- Day 2: walk the town: port, old and new markets, Super Mercado Agoueinitt [3][4].
- Day 3: housing. No long-term listing exists (city file). The only route sources suggest is asking around in person; no visit-fee or deposit custom found.
- Day 4: Orange Money for payments (open to all operators since 2023, city file); no bank branch found.
- Day 5: health check-in: locate the health centre (city file) and confirm what it can do.
- Day 6: Bruce beach by bike [3].
- Day 7: Bijante and the hidden beach [3][4].
- Saturday night: as profile 1.
- First-week spend:
  - Ferry: 16,500 XOF (or Air Bijagos ~90,000 XOF, see conflict above)
  - Lodging: 120,000 XOF; meals 14 × 1,500 = 21,000 XOF [4]
  - SIM: 500 XOF; bikes 3 days × 5,000 = 15,000 XOF
  - Total: 173,000 XOF (≈ €264) by ferry, 246,500 XOF (≈ €376) by plane.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". The dry season is the time to go: a travel company advises avoiding the rainy season, when many hotels close (city file); boats do not run in rough seas (city file).

### One day
- Morning: ride the paved road to Bruce beach, leaving before 10:00 (bike ~5,000 XOF/day; about 40 minutes for 15 km on the guide's route) [3][4]; swim with sandals on [3]. Take water and food: the beach retreat moved after 2023 damage [4].
- Midday: back in town after 16:00 if following the guide's sun advice [3]; otherwise a late lunch at Restaurant Nuna (1,500 XOF) [4].
- Afternoon: Museu de Bubaque (16:00–19:00 in 2018; entry fee not found) [3]; walk up to the administrator's house on the channel [3].
- Evening: drink at Restaurant Djass overlooking the port [4], dinner at Saldo Mar booked ahead [4]; Thursday to Sunday, Discothèque Online [4].

### One week
- Day 1: ferry from Bissau (about 4 hours) [8]; arrival scene at the port [3]; evening at Restaurant Djass [4].
- Day 2 (daily life): **market morning** at the old and new markets beside the port [3], then the museum [3].
- Day 3: forest walk to Bijante (~4 km), with tobacco or cane spirit for the chief, and on to the hidden beach at high tide [3][4].
- Day 4: Bruce beach by bike [3].
- Day 5 (trip out): **Rubane** across the channel: lunch at Ponta Anchaca (~15,000 XOF) and kayaking from Bob's (€20/hour) [4]. Rubane is sacred: no permanent buildings, no burials [3].
- Day 6 (trip out): **Canhabaque**, about an hour by motorised pirogue from Bubaque; its eastern villages (Inorei, Meneque, Inhodá, Ambeno) are the most traditional, and a guide is essential [3][4]. Pirogue warnings apply [7]; ask a lodge for an equipped boat, as France suggests [7]. Orango (hippos, park fee 5,000 XOF/day, about €130/day full board at its only hotel) is a separate multi-day trip from Bissau or Quinhamel [4].
- Day 7: return ferry or fast boat (30,000 XOF in the dry season) [4]. If the visit falls at Easter, the Festival de Bubaque fills the island for three days of music (2018 guide) [3]; current dates not found.
