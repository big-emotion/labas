## Neighbourhoods

### Bissau Velho (old colonial centre and port)
- Feel: straight-streeted colonial quarter beside the port, in an advanced state of decay but worth walking; two-storey houses with a shop or warehouse below, the Supreme Court, some banks and services [6] (2018). A 2026 blog says it suits short-term consultants, single NGO staff and couples without children, with cramped housing (low confidence) [7].
- Rent: no Bissau Velho figure. Citywide centre: 1-bed 300,000 XOF/month (≈ €457), Numbeo 2025-05-16 with 0 recent contributors (city file); a 2026 blog gives a centre studio at $400–440 (≈ 234,000–257,400 XOF, ≈ €357–392) and a centre 1-bed at $433–437 (≈ €386–390) [8] (low confidence).
- Getting around: this is the centre. Shared taxis 300 XOF for one or two people, 600 XOF for three or four; toca-toca minibuses 100 XOF (city file, Wikivoyage). The 2018 guide gives 250–500 XOF per shared-taxi trip in town [6]. The airport is 9 km north-west [4].
- Watch: described as the only area with a "minimally satisfactory" water supply and, as of 2010, the only sewer network (Portuguese Wikipedia, undated) [5]. The Geba waters by Avenida 3 de Agosto are very polluted and can smell (2018) [6]. The Amura fortress is a military zone with restricted access [6]; the UK advises avoiding military sites and government buildings (FCDO, updated 2025-12-10, city file). France advises not walking at night and warns of snatch thefts in the capital, more so at festive times (France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15) [9].
- Places:
  - Porto de Pidjiguiti and its fish market on the pontoon (port and market; busiest in the morning) — [6]; [3]
  - Mão de Timba (monument with a children's playground, by the port) — [6]
  - Casa dos Direitos (human-rights NGO house with a library, resource centre and a space to meet and work) — [6] (2018)
  - Ta Mar / Tamar (restaurant, Rua 12 de Setembro; live music at weekends, terrace) — [6]; [3]
  - Guiné Doce (café-restaurant, Rua A Mbana) — [6]
  - Kais (café, Largo do Pidjiguiti) — [6]
  - Sabores da Tabanca (shop for local honey, rice, jams and salt, Rua Guerra Mendes near the port) — [6]
  - Hotel-Residência Proquil (budget hotel, Rua 2 nº 5-6; 12,500–20,000 XOF/night, Mar 2018) — [3]; [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" (the best water in town and a workspace at Casa dos Direitos); Q5 "J'entreprends" (banks and commerce); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Pidjiguiti massacre memorial, Amura); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" (walkable core).

### Centre: Praça dos Heróis Nacionais, Avenida Amílcar Cabral and Praça Che Guevara
- Feel: the city's main square fills every weekend with families, children and young couples, and young people come for the public Wi-Fi (2018) [6]. Avenida Amílcar Cabral runs from the port to the Presidential Palace past the cathedral, the post office and the old UDIB club [6].
- Rent: as Bissau Velho (citywide centre figures above). A 2026 blog lists 3-room downtown flats at $584–890+ (≈ 341,600–520,700 XOF, ≈ €521–794) (low confidence) [7].
- Getting around: central; Avenida Amílcar Cabral links the Praça to the port and is meant to be walked [6]; shared taxi 300 XOF (city file).
- Watch: the Presidential Palace sits on the square [6]; on coup day, 26 Nov 2025, gunfire was heard outside the presidential palace (dossier, Wikipedia). O Democrata reported on 2023-01-04 that the Praça and Chão de Pepel risked flooding, in a story tied to works at Lagoa N'Batonha [14] (headline only, OLD). France: avoid walking at night (2026-09-15) [9].
- Places:

  - Hotel Coimbra & SPA (hotel with nightly buffet 2,000–7,500 XOF, gym, next to the cathedral) — [6]; [3]
  - Hotel Império and Café-Pastelaria Império (hotel; café; top-floor restaurant, Praça dos Heróis Nacionais) — [6]
  - Centre culturel franco-bissau-guinéen (cultural centre: concerts, films, French classes, the country's only equipped auditorium; Praça Che Guevara) — [12]; [11]; [3]
  - Le Grimaldi, Hotel Kalliste (pizzeria with live music most nights, Praça Che Guevara; small pizza 4,000–5,000 XOF) — [3]

### Alto Bandim and Bandim (market, sports and fishing port)
- Feel: Bandim is the old grumete stronghold absorbed into the city in 1908 and home to the country's largest market, estimated at about $1 M daily turnover in 2001 [5]. Bandim market starts at the Mãe de Água water-tower roundabout, a toca-toca hub, and fills both sides of the avenue [6]. A 2026 blog calls Alto Bandim a choice of diplomats and NGO families, with townhouses (low confidence) [7]; it says a furnished 3-bed with air conditioning there would cost "several hundred dollars" a month [8].
- Rent: no area figure. Citywide 3-bed: $416 "cheap" to $687 "centre" (≈ 243,000–402,000 XOF, ≈ €371–613; livingcost.org 2026-06-21, city file); 3-bed outside centre ~$457 (≈ 267,345 XOF, ≈ €408) [8] (low confidence).
- Getting around: toca-tocas link the bairros to Mãe de Água, by the Palácio Colinas de Boé, 100 XOF (2018 guide [6]; fare from city file). The 2018 "Tour de Bissau" bike route runs from Cuntum Madina through rice fields to Alto Bandim and the 24 de Setembro stadium, passable in the dry season [6].
- Watch: a fire at Bandim market exposed the risk of clandestine electrical connections, according to the transitional prime minister (O Democrata, 2026-02-02) [13]. Storms left three families homeless in Bandim on 3 Oct 2024 [13]. Canada names markets among pickpocketing spots (2026-10-13, city file). France: snatch thefts in the capital, avoid walking at night (2026-09-15) [9].
- Places:
  - Mercado de Bandim (main market: food, fish, clothes, tools, medicines) — [6]; [5]; [3]
  - Darling (supermarket, Airport Road opposite Mercado de Bandim; other branches in the centre and on Estrada de Bor) — [3]
  - Campo Sueco (restaurant, pool at 3,000 XOF, tennis; Alto Bandim) — [3]
  - Estádio Nacional 24 de Setembro (national stadium; running lanes and gym) — [5]; [3]
  - Tennis club at Bandim harbour (tennis) — [3]
  - Alto-Bandim fishing port (fishing port; supply tender published 2025-03-06) — [13]
  - Palácio Colinas de Boé and the Brazilian cultural centre (parliament building, 2005; cultural centre next to it) — [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" is not this area's draw; Q8 "Je reste en ville" (stadium, market); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" if the low-confidence townhouse claim holds (pool, tennis).

### Bairro da Ajuda, Bairro Militar and Bairro da Penha (the airport avenue)
- Feel: extremely populated peripheral bairros along the 7.5 km Avenida dos Combatentes da Liberdade da Pátria to the airport; along it stand the Grand Mosque, the EU delegation, the BCEAO, the courthouse and the Primatura (prime minister's office and ministries) (2018) [6]. The Universidade Amílcar Cabral rectorate is in Ajuda [5]. A 2026 blog describes Ajuda as calm, tree-lined and family-oriented (low confidence) [7].
- Rent: no area figure; the blog's "normal area" 85 m² furnished at 300,000 XOF/month (≈ €457) [8] and Numbeo's outside-centre 1-bed at 100,000 XOF (≈ €152, very weak data, city file) frame the range.
- Getting around: hotels on this avenue sat 15–20 minutes from the centre by car in 2018 (Hotel Lisboa-Bissau 15 min; Residencial Almagui 20 min, near the airport) [6]; shared taxi 300 XOF (city file). Run Run app offers moto and car rides with fare estimates (city file); moto-taxis face a June 2026 licensing directive (city file).
- Watch: the ministries and courthouse on this avenue are government buildings the FCDO says to avoid during unrest (city file). France: avoid walking at night (2026-09-15) [9]. Nothing area-specific dated.
- Places:
  - Grande Mosquée de Bissau (mosque, right side of the airport avenue past the Chapa de Bissau roundabout) — [6]
  - Ambassade de France (embassy, Av. Combatentes da Liberdade da Pátria, Bairro de Penha) — [12]
  - Centro Artístico Juvenil (wood-carving workshop with fixed prices, near Chapa on the airport road) — [6]; [3]
  - Kings Club (restaurant, bar and nightclub, Bairro da Ajuda; live music at weekends) — [6]
  - Bambu (nightclub, Bairro da Penha, open daily; mostly Guinean crowd) — [6]; [3]

### Antula (northern outskirts)
- Feel: a very populated bairro passed by the Volta de São Paulo ring road, with views over rice fields (2018) [6]. A 2026 blog calls it a lively middle-class area for people wanting immersion, with the Antula church as a landmark (low confidence) [7].
- Rent: no area figure; outside-centre 1-bed 100,000 XOF (≈ €152; Numbeo, 0 recent contributors, city file) or $225–252 (≈ 131,600–147,400 XOF, ≈ €201–225) [8] (low confidence).
- Getting around: by the ring road to the airport avenue, or the port road (5.5 km of new road from the Simão Mendes hospital area to the Antula roundabout, 2018 bike route) [6]; local public transport to downtown per the blog [7]; no fare beyond the citywide 300 XOF shared taxi (city file).
- Watch: a new borehole with 350 m³ storage serves about 73,949 people (O Democrata, 2026-04-09, city file). UN-Habitat's sanitation project planned to close the Safim dump and clear a site in Antula (O Democrata, 2025-07-08, headline read) [15]. The Safim dump, just north, was reported as a health threat to waste pickers (2024-07-22) [15].
- Places:
  - Antula church (church, landmark) — [7] (low confidence; no second source)
  - Volta de São Paulo ring road and the São Paulo / Antula rice fields (walk, bike or run route) — [6]
  - Antula roundabout to the Geba river arm (dirt-road bike route, 23 km loop from Simão Mendes, tides visible) — [6]
  - Antula borehole and water point (public water point) — city file (O Democrata 2026-04-09)
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest citywide figures are "outside centre"); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" in a small way (rice-field walks); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

## Living: typical weeks

Common facts used below (all from the city file unless marked): shared taxi 300 XOF for 1–2 people, 600 XOF for 3–4; toca-toca 100 XOF; cheap meal 3,000 XOF; mid-range meal for two 15,000 XOF; Orange CASA BOX home internet 20,000–24,000 XOF/month; Orange SIM 500 XOF with ID. Electricity and water from a 2026 blog [8] (low confidence): electricity $25–85/month single (≈ 14,600–49,700 XOF), ~$100 family (≈ 58,500 XOF); water $6–25 (≈ 3,500–14,600 XOF). Weekly share of a monthly cost = monthly × 12 / 52. Power cuts are frequent, especially at night (Portuguese Wikipedia, undated) [5]. Day times below are planning slots, not sourced travel times, unless marked.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Bissau Velho**, for the best-described water supply [5] and Casa dos Direitos as a place to work outside the flat [6] (2018; check it still hosts outside workers). Avoid relying on home power alone: blackouts are frequent at night [5].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home on the Orange CASA BOX; lunch at Ta Mar; evening walk on Avenida 3 de Agosto for the estuary view [6]. On foot.
  - Tue: morning at Casa dos Direitos [6]; coffee at Kais on Largo do Pidjiguiti [6]; groceries at Darling (centre branch) [3]. On foot / shared taxi 300 XOF.
  - Wed: work; run at the Estádio 24 de Setembro running lanes or gym [3]. Shared taxi each way.
  - Thu: work; French or cultural event at the Centre culturel franco-bissau-guinéen, Praça Che Guevara [12][3]. Shared taxi.
  - Fri: early visit to Pidjiguiti fish market on the pontoon [6]; work; dinner buffet at Hotel Coimbra (2,000–7,500 XOF) [3].
  - Sat: Bandim market morning [6]; afternoon at Campo Sueco pool (3,000 XOF) [3].
  - Sun: Praça dos Heróis Nacionais, full of families at weekends [6]; Parque Europa – Lagoa N'Batonha walk [6].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Tabanka nightclub (Rua Justino Lopes, Thu–Sun) or X Club (Rua Osvaldo Vieira, Wed–Sun), both in the 2018 guide [6]; Wikivoyage notes X-Klub as popular with expats [3] and advises avoiding nightclubs not attached to major hotels [16]. Taxi home: France advises against walking at night [9].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Ta Mar (live music at weekends) [6] or Le Grimaldi (live music most nights) [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": a quiet evening, but plan for a night-time power cut [5].
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Ilhéu do Rei, 10 minutes by canoe from the small port behind customs; ~1,000 XOF return per Wikivoyage [3], no more than 7,500 XOF return per the 2018 guide [6]. Or Suru beach near Prábis, ~20 km [6] (Wikivoyage says about two hours' drive, dry season [3]; the two disagree).
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no mountains. Saltinho waterfall, ~3 hours' drive, Pousada do Saltinho rooms 20,000 XOF [3]; Cantanhez forests (chimpanzees possible) [16], travel time not found. A weekend at least.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bissau (no savanna or desert outing sourced; the 2018 guide mentions only patches of savanna in Biombo [6]).
  - "Je reste en ville": the Volta de Bissau, a ~4-hour walking loop from Porto Canoa past rice fields and cemeteries [3].
- Weekly spend (asking prices; groceries not included because no sourced basket exists):
  - Rent: 300,000 XOF/month × 12/52 = 69,231 XOF (Numbeo centre 1-bed, very weak data, city file)
  - Internet: 24,000 × 12/52 = 5,538 XOF (Orange CASA BOX 8 Mbps, city file)
  - Electricity: 14,625–49,725 × 12/52 = 3,375–11,475 XOF [8]
  - Weekday lunches: 5 × 3,000 = 15,000 XOF (Numbeo cheap meal, city file)
  - Two buffets at Coimbra: 2 × 2,000–7,500 = 4,000–15,000 XOF [3]
  - Saturday dinner with a friend: 15,000 XOF (Numbeo meal for two, city file)
  - Pool: 3,000 XOF [3]
  - Taxis: 10 × 300 = 3,000 XOF (city file)
  - Total: 118,144–137,244 XOF (≈ €180–209) a week, before groceries, water and phone data.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Alto Bandim**, the only area a source links to family townhouses, with Campo Sueco's pool and tennis and the stadium nearby (2026 blog, low confidence) [7][3]. Ajuda is the alternative the same blog calls family-oriented [7].
- Schools, read first: the **Lycée Français International de Bissau** opened in September 2023 and teaches maternelle to terminale in French, Portuguese and English, with all elementary classes accredited by the French Education Ministry; its operator **decided to stop running it during the 2025–2026 school year and a parents' takeover is "in progress"** (France Diplomatie, updated 2026-10-23) [11]. Its embassy page (updated 2026-04-08) gives hours 8:00–16:00 and an admissions contact [12]. The Escola Portuguesa da Guiné-Bissau exists (blog, city file). International-school fees: $2,000–5,000/year (blog, undated, city file). Public schools: frequent teacher strikes (same blog).
- Health: Hospital Nacional Simão Mendes is the reference hospital; its cleaners worked without detergent in Feb 2026 over unpaid contracts (city file). Guides advise insurance covering evacuation to Dakar (city file).
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: shared taxi to school (600 XOF for three or four, city file); school 8:00–16:00 [12].
  - Wed afternoon: tennis or swimming at Campo Sueco [3].
  - Fri: groceries at Darling opposite Bandim market [3]; Mini Marché Alvalade for cheese and frozen meat [3].
  - Sat: Praça dos Heróis Nacionais, where children play at weekends [6]; Mão de Timba playground by the port [6].
  - Sun: Parque Europa – Lagoa N'Batonha (play area, café) [6]; church or mosque.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not a family option; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Papa Loca, known for grilled chicken, Av. Francisco Mendes (2018) [6]; "Tranquille chez moi" the default.
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" Hotel Mar Azul at Quinhamel (pool and river swimming) [6] or Suru [6]; "En montagne, en forêt" Quinhamel riverside walks [6]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic from Bissau; "Je reste en ville" a league football match [5].
- Weekly spend (one child):
  - Rent: 3-bed $416–457/month (≈ 243,000–267,345 XOF; livingcost.org city file and [8]) × 12/52 = 56,077–61,695 XOF
  - School: $2,000–5,000/year (≈ 1,170,000–2,925,000 XOF) / 52 = 22,500–56,250 XOF (city file, blog; LFIB's own fees not found)

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: **Santa Luzia / Avenida Pansau na Isna** apart-hotels (Appart Hotel Diarama ~40,000 XOF a double after bargaining [3]; Aparthotel Lobato and Jordani, no price [6]), or the budget Hotel-Residência Proquil in Bissau Velho (12,500–20,000 XOF, Mar 2018) [3][4] or Pensão Creola (fans, shared toilets) [6].
- Day 1: arrive at Osvaldo Vieira airport; visa on arrival $120 or €85 cash per a 2023 travel-company post, "between €60 and $120" per Wikivoyage (city file); airport taxi asks ~€10, about 5,000 XOF with cash and bargaining (2023, city file). Tourist desks in the arrivals and departures halls (2018) [6].
- Day 2: SIM — Orange, 500 XOF with ID, shop in front of the Spanish embassy [3]; Orange Money is open to all operators and the BCEAO raised the account cap to 4 M XOF in Oct 2026 (city file). Cash from an Ecobank ATM (Visa/Mastercard); BAO ATMs need a local account (city file).
- Day 3: flat hunting. No online listings exist (Expat.com housing empty; O Democrata's rental page holds one 2018 post) (city file). A 2026 blog suggests monthly Airbnb, Agentiz.com, Sublet.com, and asking colleagues, shopkeepers and building caretakers [8].

- Day 5: bank account: steps not found. Residence card: issued by the migration office in Bissau, cost and steps not found (Wikivoyage fr, city file). French nationals staying over six months are advised to register with the consulate (dossier).
- Day 6: Bandim market and Darling to price a week of food [6][3].
- Day 7: rest at Campo Sueco pool [3]; Sunday evening at Praça dos Heróis Nacionais [6].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". France advises avoiding walking at night and staying away from any gathering (2026-09-15) [9]; check the situation around the 6 Dec 2026 elections.

### One day
- Morning: Porto de Pidjiguiti at its busiest, pirogues landing fish, women selling seafood on the pontoon; the massacre memorials and Amílcar Cabral bust at the entrance; Mão de Timba [6]. Walk Bissau Velho's streets and Casa dos Direitos' permanent photo exhibition [6]. The Amura fortress (Cabral's mausoleum) needs a written request to the army chief of staff [6], so view it from outside. All on foot.
- Midday: lunch at Ta Mar in Bissau Velho [6], or the Coimbra buffet beside the cathedral (2,000–7,500 XOF) [3].
- Afternoon: walk up Avenida Amílcar Cabral past the Ministry of Justice, the cathedral and the post office to the Praça dos Heróis Nacionais and the Presidential Palace [6]; the National Ethnographic Museum is on the square per the 2018 guide [6] but on the university campus and open mornings only per Wikivoyage [3] (location unresolved). Shared taxi (300 XOF) to Mãe de Água and Bandim market [6][3]. Entry fees: not found.
- Evening: Le Grimaldi at Praça Che Guevara for pizza and live music [3], or a concert at the CCFBG next door [12]. Taxi back.

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily life): **Bandim market from opening**, then a toca-toca ride (100 XOF) from Mãe de Água to see the city's commute [6]; Mercado dos Coqueiros craft market and Lojinha da Terra, the Tiniguena NGO shop in Bairro de Belém [6][3]; dinner at Dona Fernanda, Santa Luzia [6].
- Day 3: Ilhéu do Rei by canoe (10 min; ruined groundnut-oil factory and the fishing tabanca; ~1,000–7,500 XOF return) [3][6]; afternoon on the Volta de Bissau walk from Porto Canoa [3].
- Day 4 (trip out): **Quinhamel**, 37 km on a good road: roadside market, Artissal weaving workshop and small Papel museum, a cane-spirit distillery, lunch and a swim at Hotel Mar Azul [6]; Esplanada Omay oyster buffet at weekends, booking required [6]. Fare for the trip not found.
- Day 5 to 7 (trip out): **Bubaque** by Consulmar speedboat, Friday out and Sunday back, 30,000 XOF each way (2024) [19]; the regular ferry is 16,500 XOF, about 4 hours, once a week each way (city file). France strongly advises against crossing by pirogue [10]. See `bubaque.neighbourhoods.md`. Alternative for those staying: Suru beach (Day 5), a Sunday Mass at the cathedral or the weekend crowd at the Praça (Day 6), and a league football match if one is on (Day 7) [5][6].
