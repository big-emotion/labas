# Cacheu, Guinea-Bissau

## Items

- [cost.negotiation] A chartered park boat from Cacheu to São Domingos cost 180,000 XOF (≈ €274.41) for 6 people and 6 motorbikes, about 2 hours; the price was negotiable. — Scoot West Africa, 2023-03-23, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] In a 2023 update a private covered pirogue did the same trip for 100,000 XOF for the whole boat (≈ €152.45), possibly less for small groups. — Scoot West Africa, 2023, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A regular canoe to São Domingos left around 10:00 daily, for "less than €3" in 2008 (≈ 1,970 XOF). OLD. — Wikivoyage "Cacheu", 2008, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Few places to stay: a riverside Carrene Tours Village Hotel (pool, bungalows) opened in 2023 opposite the slavery museum; no prices given. — Scoot West Africa, 2023-03-23, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Economy: fishing around a small river port, trade in coconuts, palm oil and rice, and cattle, sheep and goat raising nearby. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Nearly 70 % of the people in the Jeta–Pecixe–Cacheu area live close to and depend on marine resources; fishing in the Grande Rio is closed June–September. — O Democrata GB, 2026-01-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Environmentalists warn that expanding cashew orchards are destroying forests in the Cacheu region. — O Democrata GB, 2026-01-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] A tender sought studies for a climate-resilient ramp at the Cacheu artisanal fishing port (GEF/UNDP Coastal project). — O Democrata GB, 2025-03-06, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Cacheu is about 100 km from Bissau; Regional Road 1 runs south to Canchungo and on to Bula. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] From Bissau, take a sept-place (shared taxi) to Canchungo and change for Cacheu (2007–2008 info). — Wikivoyage "Cacheu", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A 2023 traveller calls the road "in great shape" and Cacheu an easy day trip from Bissau. — Scoot West Africa, 2023-03-23, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Civil-society groups in Canchungo (Cacheu region) warned about moto-taxi accidents (headline only). — O Democrata GB, 2026-08-10, local-media, confidence: low
- [family.schools] A campus of the Escola Normal Superior Tchico Té (teacher-training degrees) is in Cacheu. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Canada advises against non-essential travel within 20 km of the Senegal border, including the São Domingos–Mpack road (Cacheu region), and a high degree of caution elsewhere. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Outside Bissau some landmines remain; stay on paved roads and follow local advice. — UK FCDO, updated 2025-12-10 (read 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [social.food] A few harbour restaurants serve rice dishes, probably only at lunch; the guide suggests bringing bread and tinned food (2007–2008). — Wikivoyage "Cacheu", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] Butu de Cacheu is the one nightclub listed, with dancing and African music (2007–2008). — Wikivoyage "Cacheu", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] The Memorial da Escravatura e do Tráfico Negreiro (slavery memorial) sits on the waterfront in Casa Gouveia; a 16th-century fort holds colonial statues, interior visits via the museum caretaker. — Wikipedia "Cacheu" and Scoot West Africa (2023-03-23), academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] NGOs ACEP and AD launched a culture and local-economy project covering Bissau and Cacheu. — O Democrata GB, 2026-02-06, local-media, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Cacheu hosts the Santuário de Nossa Senhora da Natividade; the 2025 national Catholic pilgrimage to Cacheu was cancelled because of the post-coup political situation. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)" and O Democrata GB (2025-12-02), local-media, confidence: high
- [social.pace] A 2023 visitor describes "a sleepy town" that was mostly taking a siesta in the afternoon. — Scoot West Africa, 2023-03-23, company, confidence: low
- [social.pace] Population 9,882 at the 2009 census. — Wikipedia "Cacheu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] The Portuguese Wikipedia gives 5,674 for the urban area (2009 census) and 18,563 for the sector. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [nature.access] The Cacheu River Mangroves Natural Park (Tarrafes) faces the town; a 2023 visitor saw birdlife and a caiman on a guided trip. — Wikipédia (pt) "Cacheu (cidade)" and Scoot West Africa (2023-03-23), academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Guinea-Bissau is preparing a UNESCO bid for the Jeta–Pecixe–Cacheu landscape. — O Democrata GB, 2026-06-25, local-media, confidence: medium
