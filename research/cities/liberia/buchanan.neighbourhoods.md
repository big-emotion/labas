# Buchanan: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches; second pass 2026-11-15 (web search available) added named places from the national tourism portal, Liberian local media, Human Rights Watch and FAO. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **US$1 ≈ €0.86** (US$1 ≈ L$180), the Wikivoyage Liberia cross rate "as of July 2026" used in the city file; the dossier gives no dated rate [B currency note]. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[B tag]` = an item in `research/cities/liberia/buchanan.md`; `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/liberia/monrovia.md` (used only for country-level facts); `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/liberia.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

The second pass (2026-11-15) found named lodgings, restaurants, markets, schools, churches, a fish-processing site and the Edina boat landing, so the weeks below now have real places in them. Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Scale and pace.** City population 34,270 in 2008 (old); Grand Bassa County 293,557 in 2022 [B other]. The centre is small and grid-patterned [1][B housing.areas]. One travel-wiki page calls it laid-back, another describes little more than shanty housing; both undated [B social.culture].
- **Work.** Jobs tie to the ArcelorMittal iron-ore port at the end of the 250 km railway from Nimba, plus fishing [B work.jobs].
- **Power.** [daily.power] In June 2026 the electricity regulator held a hearing in Buchanan on the state utility's bid to restore grid power to the city after about 36 years without it; substations, poles and streetlights were already up in several parts of the city and the rollout is planned in three phases. — The New Dawn via allAfrica, 2026-06-23, local-media, confidence: medium [30]. Which streets are connected is not stated; check on site.
- **Road to Monrovia.** About 110 km; the road is mainly paved and in reasonable condition (UK) [B daily.transport][6]; about two hours by car per a 2012-dated note [B daily.transport]; intercity bus US$5–10 (≈ €4.30–8.60) [B daily.transport]. The UK advises getting security advice before road travel outside Monrovia; roads are unlit and robbery at illegal checkpoints is likelier at night [6].
- **Health.** The Liberian Government Hospital is the county's main public hospital (2016 report) [B health.care][4]; Canada says public facilities are "generally unavailable" outside Monrovia [7]. Plan on Monrovia for anything serious.
- **Beaches.** Rip currents are common on Liberian beaches; the UK advises against canoes and fishing boats offering passenger services [6].
- **Sea erosion.** [housing.areas] Waves were eating into Upper Buchanan, Atlantic Street, Korkorwein and Big and Small Fanti Town in 2016. — FrontPage Africa, 2016-06-21, local-media, confidence: low (old) [19]. A UNDP-backed rock revetment runs from the port to Atlantic Street, but did not reach Corcorwein. — New Narratives, c. Feb 2023 (date inferred from image uploads), local-media, confidence: medium [20]. Ask about erosion before renting near the shore.
- **LGBT readers.** Same-sex activity is a criminal offence nationally [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Downtown Buchanan (the grid and the port)
- Feel: the small, grid-patterned centre [1]; in 2016 most travellers looked for vehicles here rather than at the official bus terminal [3]. The port and the ore-handling area lie on Waterhouse Bay near the mouth of the St John River [2][B nature.access]. Atlantic Street, once one of the busiest streets with stores and chop shops, is now almost deserted because of erosion [20].
- Rent: no figure found for Buchanan. The only national floor is Africarrieres' minimum local housing line of US$100–250/month (≈ €85–215), not Buchanan-specific [C cost.living].
- Getting around: the centre is compact (no distance data); transport to Monrovia loads downtown per the 2016 report [3]. Motorbike fare from Monrovia Junction to Tubman Street L$40–50 (≈ €0.19–0.24), undated report, probably c. 2019 [35].
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated was found. (Wikivoyage mentions landmines off the beaten path; it is an undated travel wiki and not an official source, so it is not relied on here.)

### Upper Buchanan
- Feel: a township on the Benson River side, with the county circuit court and a jail compound [31]; named in 2016 as an area whose residents would have to travel to reach the bus terminal [3], and as threatened by sea erosion [19]. It is the departure point for boats to Edina [17].
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: boat to Edina in 10–15 minutes from the Benson River bank [17]; no road distance found.
- Places:
  - [place.worship] Charles A. White United Methodist Church is in Upper Buchanan; a residents' development association held its induction there on 1 Jan 2023. — FrontPage Africa, 2023-01-10, local-media, confidence: medium [31]
  - [place.civic] The Second Judicial Circuit Court and a jail compound are in the township. — FrontPage Africa, 2023-01-10, local-media, confidence: medium [31]
  - [place.transport] Wooden boats leave from the Benson River bank in Upper Buchanan, cross the meeting point of the Benson, St John and Mechlin rivers through mangroves, and land in Edina 10–15 minutes later; boats can also be chartered on the Benson or St John. No price listed. — Liberia Tourism "Edina Heritage Tour" and "Confluence Boat Ride", undated, official, confidence: medium [17]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" as the gateway to Edina.

### Fanti Town and the shore (Lower Buchanan)
- Feel: [housing.areas] Fanti Town, in Lower Buchanan, is the town's artisanal fishing quarter, split into Big and Small Fanti Town; over a thousand fishermen worked from there in 2016. — FrontPage Africa, 2016-06-21, local-media, confidence: low (old) [19]; New Dawn via allAfrica, 2026-03-23, local-media, confidence: medium [26]. This is where the Ghanaian Fante community noted in the city file lives, by its name [B social.culture].
- Work: [work.jobs] Canoe operators earn about L$6,000 (≈ €29) on a good day and L$1,500–2,000 (≈ €7–10) on a poor one; annual licences fell on 1 Jan 2026 to US$30 (≈ €26) for a paddle canoe and US$175 (≈ €150) for a small motorised one. — The New Dawn via allAfrica, 2026-03-23, local-media, confidence: medium [26]
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: no fare found.
- Watch: [safety.general] The shore here is the most exposed to erosion: Korkorwein and Bardor Wleh Town lost houses, 20 of them in Bardor Wleh in 2008, and the rock wall stops short of Corcorwein. — New Narratives, c. Feb 2023, local-media, confidence: medium [20]
- Places:
  - [place.market] The Fanti Town fish-processing facility, built with Japanese funds and handed to the national fisheries authority through FAO in June 2024, has improved smoking ovens (FAO-Thiaroye technique) and a fish market for women processors. — FrontPage Africa, 2024-06-11, local-media, confidence: medium [25]

### Edina (across the St John River mouth)
- Feel: a historic settler town (1832) on the north shore of the St John River mouth, about 5.6 km north of Buchanan; birthplace of President Joseph James Cheeseman [8]. The tourism portal pitches 1800s-style homes, churches and monuments on a walking tour [15][17]. Small and residential by every indication, but no source describes daily life.
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: wooden boats from Upper Buchanan, 10–15 minutes [17]; a Wikipedia photo caption also shows water transport between Edina and Buchanan [8]. The UK advises against canoes and fishing boats offering passenger services [6].
- Watch: a 2002 diarrhoea outbreak killed six people; ArcelorMittal began subsidising medical care in 2007 (old) [8].
- Places:
  - First Baptist Church (19th-century church) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Cheeseman's mansion ruins, his grave and a monument (heritage site) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Settlers' landing site (heritage site) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Edina Public Health Center (government clinic) — Wikipedia [8]
  - [place.school] J.J. Cheeseman Elementary School in Edina reopened after renovation on 20 Jul 2026. — FrontPage Africa, 2026-07-21, local-media, confidence: high [22]
  - [place.beach] The heritage tour continues by boat past the river-mouth bar to a beach for lunch and a swim. Beach not named. — Liberia Tourism "Edina Heritage Tour", undated, official, confidence: medium [17]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (one of the original 1830s settlements of the Commonwealth of Liberia) [8].

## Living: typical weeks

Base for every profile: **Downtown Buchanan**, because it has the hospital, the General Market, the Kilby Street parking station and most of the listed lodgings and restaurants. In-town commute times are not sourced; the town is small.

Country-level budget used for all weekly spends (Africarrieres, "Cost of Living Overview in Liberia", 06/08/2026, low confidence, not Buchanan-specific) [C cost.living]: housing US$100–250, food 150–200, transport 20–40, utilities 30–60 a month. Divided by 4.33 → housing 23–58, food 35–46, transport 5–9, utilities 7–14 a week; total **US$69–127 a week (≈ €59–109)** for one person on a local budget. A senator said in Oct 2026 that goods cost more outside Monrovia, but Grand Bassa was not among the counties named [B cost.living].

- Base: Downtown. Caveat: grid power was only being restored in 2026 [30]; Canada says mobile coverage exists in major towns but not in many remote areas [B daily.internet].   - Fri: dinner at Hotel Buchanan's poolside restaurant on Roberts Street [16], or the Gardner Street drinking spot [1]; back before dark.
  - Sat: beach about 5 km south of the centre (unmaintained, eroding, rough seas) [1].
  - Sun: boat from Upper Buchanan to Edina for the First Baptist Church and the heritage sites [17][8].

  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic; the only sourced nightlife is drinking on Gardner Street [1] and hotel bars [16].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Hotel Buchanan (pizza and Liberian food, poolside) or Aroma Restaurant at Watco Camp (open to 21:00) [16]; no prices listed.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Buchanan works as a one- or two-night trip from Monrovia rather than a week-long base on current sources.

### One day
- Morning: leave Monrovia early; about 2 hours on the paved road (bus US$5–10 or a hired car) [B daily.transport][6].
- Midday: the downtown grid and a look at the port and the ore train from outside [1][2]; City Hall and the Fairgrounds' Unification Pavilion [5]; lunch at Hotel Buchanan on Roberts Street [16].
- Afternoon: the beach about 5 km south of the centre, beautiful but eerie, unmaintained, with rough seas [1]; do not swim alone [6].
- Evening: back to Monrovia before dark (unlit roads, night checkpoints) [6], or a night in one of the listed hotels [16].

### One week
Day-by-day, staying mostly in Monrovia with Buchanan as the anchor of the second half:
- Day 1: arrive in Monrovia; see monrovia.neighbourhoods.md for the city days.
- Day 2: Monrovia downtown, Ducor Hotel and Waterside (see that file).
- Day 3: drive to Buchanan (2 h) [B daily.transport]; downtown, port, City Hall [1][2][5]; dinner and night at Hotel Buchanan or the Crowne Hotel with Aroma Restaurant [16].
- Day 4 (trip): the Edina Heritage Tour from the Benson River bank in Upper Buchanan: boat through the three-river confluence, the First Baptist Church, Cheeseman's mansion ruins and grave, the landing site, then a beach past the river mouth for lunch [17][8]. Choose a listed operator; the UK warns that passenger canoes are often overwhelmed by waves [6].
- Day 5 (daily life): morning in Fanti Town, where the fishing canoes land and women process fish at the FAO-built facility [19][25][26]; then the General Market on Kilby Street [34]; afternoon at the southern beach [1].
- Day 6 (daily life): Sunday service at Charles A. White United Methodist Church in Upper Buchanan [31] or Edina's First Baptist Church [8] (service times not sourced), or a match at Doris Williams Stadium if one is scheduled [1][28].
- Day 7: return to Monrovia in daylight [6].
