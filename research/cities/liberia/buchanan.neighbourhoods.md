# Buchanan: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **US$1 ≈ €0.86** (US$1 ≈ L$180), the Wikivoyage Liberia cross rate "as of July 2026" used in the city file; the dossier gives no dated rate [B currency note]. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[B tag]` = an item in `research/cities/liberia/buchanan.md`; `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/liberia/monrovia.md` (used only for country-level facts); `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/liberia.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

**Read this first: this is a thin file.** The session's web-search budget was exhausted, so only direct fetches were possible, and the reachable Buchanan reporting is mostly from 2016. No source gives a Buchanan rent, a named restaurant, market, school, church service, café or supermarket. Neighbourhood "feel" is reduced to what the sources actually say. The weeks below are therefore skeletons, and the weekly spends use country-level prices, not Buchanan ones. The honest reading is that Buchanan cannot yet be recommended at neighbourhood level from desk research; a field contact or a second search pass is needed.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Scale and pace.** City population 34,270 in 2008 (old); Grand Bassa County 293,557 in 2022 [B other]. The centre is small and grid-patterned [1][B housing.areas]. One travel-wiki page calls it laid-back, another describes little more than shanty housing; both undated [B social.culture].
- **Work.** Jobs tie to the ArcelorMittal iron-ore port at the end of the 250 km railway from Nimba, plus fishing [B work.jobs].
- **Road to Monrovia.** About 110 km; the road is mainly paved and in reasonable condition (UK) [B daily.transport][6]; about two hours by car per a 2012-dated note [B daily.transport]; intercity bus US$5–10 (≈ €4.30–8.60) [B daily.transport]. The UK advises getting security advice before road travel outside Monrovia; roads are unlit and robbery at illegal checkpoints is likelier at night [6].
- **Health.** The Liberian Government Hospital is the county's main public hospital (2016 report) [B health.care][4]; Canada says public facilities are "generally unavailable" outside Monrovia [7]. Plan on Monrovia for anything serious.
- **Beaches.** Rip currents are common on Liberian beaches; the UK advises against canoes and fishing boats offering passenger services [6].
- **LGBT readers.** Same-sex activity is a criminal offence nationally [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Downtown Buchanan (the grid and the port)
- Feel: the small, grid-patterned centre [1]; in 2016 most travellers looked for vehicles here rather than at the official bus terminal [3]. The port and the ore-handling area lie on Waterhouse Bay near the mouth of the St John River [2][B nature.access].
- Rent: no figure found for Buchanan. The only national floor is Africarrieres' minimum local housing line of US$100–250/month (≈ €85–215), not Buchanan-specific [C cost.living].
- Getting around: the centre is compact (no distance data); transport to Monrovia loads downtown per the 2016 report [3]. No in-town taxi or keke fares found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated was found. (Wikivoyage mentions landmines off the beaten path; it is an undated travel wiki and not an official source, so it is not relied on here.)
- Places:
  - Port of Buchanan (iron-ore export port; Lumco industrial complex with the ore train) — Wikipedia [2]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Buchanan City Hall (opened April 2016) — FrontPage Africa [5]
  - Unification Pavilion on the Fairgrounds (public pavilion) — FrontPage Africa [5]
  - Temple of Justice, Buchanan (courthouse; hosted a 2016 judicial workshop) — FrontPage Africa tag page [9]
  - Liberian Government Hospital (main public hospital; maternity, paediatric beds; 2016) — FrontPage Africa [4]
  - Teepro Lodge, near the Buchanan Recyclables office (lodge; prices reported as inconsistent) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Gardner Street (a busy drinking spot the wiki does not name) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Doris Williams Stadium (town stadium; district not stated) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Salarié·e" for port and mining jobs; Q3 "Serré" (likely cheaper than Monrovia, but unproven); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Own-Your-Own community (outskirts)
- Feel: on the city's outskirts; site of the National Transit Authority bus terminal, more than a mile from downtown, built in 2014 and standing unused in 2016 [3][B housing.areas; daily.transport].
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: over a mile to downtown [3]; no fares found.
- Places:
  - NTA bus terminal (built 2014, unused in 2016; current status unknown) — FrontPage Africa [3]
  - No other place sourced.
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" at best; too little evidence to recommend.

### Edina (across the St John River mouth)
- Feel: a historic settler town (1832) on the north shore of the St John River mouth, about 5.6 km north of Buchanan; birthplace of President Joseph James Cheeseman [8]. Small and residential by every indication, but no source describes daily life.
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: a photo caption on Wikipedia shows water transport between Edina and Buchanan; road route not described [8]. The UK advises against canoes and fishing boats offering passenger services [6].
- Watch: a 2002 diarrhoea outbreak killed six people; ArcelorMittal began subsidising medical care in 2007 (old) [8].
- Places:
  - First Baptist Church (19th-century church) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Cheeseman's mansion ruins, his grave and a monument (heritage site) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Settlers' landing site (heritage site) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Edina Public Health Center (government clinic) — Wikipedia [8]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (one of the original 1830s settlements of the Commonwealth of Liberia) [8].

## Living: typical weeks

Base for every profile: **Downtown Buchanan**, because it is the only area with any sourced places, transport and the hospital. In-town commute times are not sourced; the town is small.

Country-level budget used for all weekly spends (Africarrieres, "Cost of Living Overview in Liberia", 06/08/2026, low confidence, not Buchanan-specific) [C cost.living]: housing US$100–250, food 150–200, transport 20–40, utilities 30–60 a month. Divided by 4.33 → housing 23–58, food 35–46, transport 5–9, utilities 7–14 a week; total **US$69–127 a week (≈ €59–109)** for one person on a local budget. A senator said in Oct 2026 that goods cost more outside Monrovia, but Grand Bassa was not among the counties named [B cost.living].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

Starlink has had a provisional licence since January 2025 [D]. Treat a remote-work move as unverified until connectivity is tested on site.
- Week:

  - Fri: evening walk to the Gardner Street drinking spot (unnamed) [1], back before dark.
  - Sat: beach about 5 km south of the centre (unmaintained, eroding, rough seas) [1].
  - Sun: Edina by water transport for the First Baptist Church and the heritage sites [8].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic; the only sourced nightlife is drinking on Gardner Street [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": no restaurant named in any source; gap.
  - "Tranquille chez moi": DStv packages from US$7/month across Liberia (Nov 2026) [C daily.internet].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": the beach 5 km south [1]; swim with care, rip currents [6].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Buchanan on current sources (no forest site sourced nearby).
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Buchanan.
  - "Je reste en ville": Monrovia, about 2 hours by road, for a city weekend (see monrovia.neighbourhoods.md) [B daily.transport].
- Rough weekly spend: local budget US$69–127 (above) + home internet US$50–150/month (Monrovia figure) → 12–35 → **≈ US$81–162 (≈ €70–139)**; whether home internet exists in Buchanan at that price is not sourced [C daily.internet].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: Teepro Lodge, near the Buchanan Recyclables office; prices were reported as inconsistent and rising between booking and check-out, so agree the total in writing [1][B cost.negotiation]. The wiki suggests cheaper places on the main road but names none [1].
- Week:
  - Mon: arrive via Monrovia: Roberts International, then the paved road south (about 2 h from Monrovia; bus US$5–10) [B daily.transport]; travel in daylight [6].
  - Tue: SIM card and mobile money in Monrovia before leaving is the safer plan (GSM SIM about US$1; Orange Money and MTN MoMo exist; no Buchanan outlet sourced) [11][D]. Bring US-dollar cash: ATMs are scarce nationally and often empty [D].
  - Wed: walk the downtown grid; ask about flats (no listing site, agent or fee found).
  - Thu: Liberian Government Hospital to locate care [4]; City Hall [5].
  - Fri: Edina [8].
  - Sat: residence registration is done with the Bureau of Immigration; extensions at the Immigration Office in Sinkor, Monrovia (US$25) [C admin.visa; admin.residency]. No Buchanan immigration office sourced.
  - Sun: southern beach [1].
- Saturday night: Gardner Street [1] or nothing; no restaurant sourced.
- Weekend outing: southern beach [1] or back to Monrovia.
- Rough spend: lodging price unknown (gap); bus Monrovia–Buchanan 5–10; SIM 1; food at the local floor 35–46 → **≈ US$41–57 plus lodging (≈ €35–49 plus lodging)**.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Buchanan works as a one- or two-night trip from Monrovia rather than a week-long base on current sources.

### One day
- Morning: leave Monrovia early; about 2 hours on the paved road (bus US$5–10 or a hired car) [B daily.transport][6].
- Midday: the downtown grid and a look at the port and the ore train from outside [1][2]; City Hall and the Fairgrounds' Unification Pavilion [5]. No restaurant sourced: plan to eat in Monrovia or bring food.
- Afternoon: the beach about 5 km south of the centre, beautiful but eerie, unmaintained, with rough seas [1]; do not swim alone [6].
- Evening: back to Monrovia before dark (unlit roads, night checkpoints) [6], or a night at Teepro Lodge [1].

### One week
Day-by-day, staying mostly in Monrovia with Buchanan as the anchor of the second half:
- Day 1: arrive in Monrovia; see monrovia.neighbourhoods.md for the city days.
- Day 2: Monrovia downtown, Ducor Hotel and Waterside (see that file).
- Day 3: drive to Buchanan (2 h) [B daily.transport]; downtown, port, City Hall [1][2][5]; night at Teepro Lodge [1].
- Day 4 (trip): Edina across the river mouth by water transport: First Baptist Church, Cheeseman's mansion ruins and grave, the landing site [8]. Choose a licensed operator; the UK warns that passenger canoes are often overwhelmed by waves [6].
- Day 5: southern beach [1]; a Ghanaian Fante fishing community lives in town [B social.culture], but no landing site or fish market is named, so this "daily life" morning has to be found on the spot.
- Day 6 (daily life): Sunday service at Edina's First Baptist Church (service times not sourced) [8], or a match at Doris Williams Stadium if one is scheduled [1].
- Day 7: return to Monrovia in daylight [6].
