# Monrovia, Liberia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the dossier gives no dated exchange rate. EUR equivalents use the cross rate on Wikivoyage's Liberia page "as of July 2026" (US$1 ≈ L$180, €1 ≈ L$210, so US$1 ≈ €0.86). FrontPage Africa cites a Central Bank rate of about L$174 per US$ in October 2026. Rent and most larger prices are quoted in USD; the economy runs on both currencies.

Coverage note: this pass ran after the session's shared web-search budget was used up, so it relies only on direct fetches of known URLs (dossier sources, Wikipedia/Wikivoyage, official advisories, and front pages/tag pages of FrontPage Africa, Liberian Observer and The New Dawn). Numbeo (HTTP 429), Expatistan (403), Jobicy (429) and the US State Department advisory (403) could not be read.
