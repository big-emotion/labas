# Buchanan, Liberia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

## Items

- [cost.living] A senator reported in Oct 2026 that goods and fuel cost much more outside Monrovia (rice US$22 vs US$17 per 25 kg bag in Gbarpolu); Grand Bassa was not among the counties named. — FrontPage Africa, 2026-10-27, local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] A travel wiki reports inconsistent room prices at Teepro Lodge, with costs rising between booking and check-out, and suggests cheaper places on the main road give better value (undated). — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The centre is small and grid-patterned; the NTA bus terminal was built in the "Own-Your-Own" community, more than a mile from downtown. — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)" (accessed 2026-11-14) and FrontPage Africa (2016-08-04, old), blog / local-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Buchanan is the export port for the 250 km iron-ore railway from Nimba; ArcelorMittal rebuilt the line and returned it to service in 2011. — Wikipedia "Rail transport in Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The port was a ghost town until ArcelorMittal took over LAMCO's facilities in 2005, bringing jobs; under the 2005 deal the company was to pay the county US$1 million a year (≈ €860,000), terms revised in 2007. — Wikipedia "Grand Bassa County", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Fishing is an important local industry; rubber and palm oil were once shipped from the port. — Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] In Nov 2026 ArcelorMittal Liberia held a rail-safety dialogue in Compound #3, Grand Bassa, along the Yekepa–Buchanan corridor, part of an ongoing community programme. — FrontPage Africa, 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.invest] A 2022 proposal (HPX/Ivanhoe Liberia) would share the Buchanan–Tokadeh line to export Guinean iron ore; status not given. — Wikipedia "Rail transport in Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The road to Buchanan is mainly paved and in reasonable condition, unlike most other roads; UK advice is to take security advice before travelling outside Monrovia. — GOV.UK FCDO "Safety and security", page undated (accessed 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Intercity bus Monrovia–Buchanan US$5–10 (≈ €4.30–8.60). — Africarrieres "Cost of Living Overview in Liberia", 06/08/2026, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Buchanan sits about 110 km south-east of Monrovia; a 2012-dated travel note put the drive at about two hours once the sealed road was finished. — Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia" and Wikivoyage, accessed 2026-11-14, academic / blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A US$50,000+ National Transit Authority bus terminal completed in 2014 stood unused two years later for lack of funds (old). — FrontPage Africa, 2016-08-04, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Buchanan airport has an unpaved runway. — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Mobile coverage exists in Monrovia and major towns but not in many remote areas or road sections. — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-10-15, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Liberian Government Hospital in Buchanan saw over 4,750 patients in May 2016, added beds and midwives, and was seeking regional-referral status for Grand Bassa, Rivercess and Sinoe; drug supply was "a work in progress" (old). — FrontPage Africa, 2016-06-02, local-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] Public medical facilities are poor in Monrovia and "generally unavailable elsewhere"; private care is very expensive and evacuation needs upfront payment. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Roads outside Monrovia are mostly unlit; travellers are more exposed to robbery at illegal checkpoints at night; crowds after road accidents can turn hostile. — GOV.UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] During the first civil war Buchanan largely escaped the fighting and became a refuge. — Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] A travel wiki says there is not much to do besides drinking or going to the beach, naming a busy drinking spot on Gardner Street (undated). — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.culture] A sizeable Ghanaian Fante community lives in Buchanan; the town was founded in 1835 as Bassa Cove by black Quaker settlers after the earlier Port Cresson colony was destroyed. — Wikivoyage and Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, blog / academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] One travel wiki calls Buchanan "a laid-back city"; another page of the same wiki says Buchanan offers "little more than" shanty housing with no decent hotels — contradictory, both undated. — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)" and "Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.sport] Doris Williams Stadium is the town's stadium. — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Same coastal monsoon pattern as Monrovia; roads deteriorate sharply in the May–November rainy season. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Isolated beaches and lagoons; the town lies on Waterhouse Bay near the mouth of the St John River. — Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The main beach, about 5 km south of the centre, is unmaintained, eroding and has rough seas, per an undated travel-wiki note. — Wikivoyage "Buchanan (Liberia)", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [other] City population 34,270 at the 2008 census (old); Grand Bassa County had 293,557 people in the 2022 census, sixth in the country. — Wikipedia "Buchanan, Liberia" and "Grand Bassa County", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
