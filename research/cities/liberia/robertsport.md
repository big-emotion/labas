## Items

- [cost.living] Prices remain high in Kinjor (Grand Cape Mount) and other rural towns, according to a senator in Oct 2026, while the official petrol ceiling is US$5.09/gallon (≈ €4.35); no Cape Mount figures were given. — FrontPage Africa, 2026-10-27, local-media, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Lodging named by sources: Kwepunha Retreat (listing last updated 2017) and a surfers' guesthouse run by a local surfer; an older travel-wiki note mentions a holiday lodge and a beachside tent camp. No prices found. — Wikivoyage "Robertsport" and "Liberia", Wikipedia "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, blog / academic, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Most residents are rice farmers or fishers; about 30% of the population fishes. — Wikipedia "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [work.remote] Canada notes mobile coverage exists in major towns but not in many remote areas or road sections. — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-10-15, official, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] About 80 km north-west of Monrovia; a travel wiki says the drive takes "several hours" depending on road and weather. — Wikipedia and Wikivoyage "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic / blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Roads outside Monrovia are mostly unpaved and unlit and deteriorate from May to November; fuel shortages are common in remote areas. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Public medical facilities are "generally unavailable" outside Monrovia; private care is expensive and medical evacuation needs upfront payment. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Rip currents are common along the coast; avoid canoes and fishing boats offering passenger services, which are often overwhelmed by waves. — GOV.UK FCDO "Safety and security", page undated (accessed 2026-11-14), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Lake Piso's history includes the 2002 capsizing of the overcrowded ferry Papa Friends 2000, which killed most of its passengers. — Wikipedia "Lake Piso", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Five point breaks; the main ones are Fisherman's Point (closest to town), Cotton Trees and Cassava Point. The surf scene grew after 2003 when visiting Americans taught local men to surf. — Wikipedia "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [social.sport] As of 2022 the Robertsport Surf Club expected to own beach property for lessons and fishing trips; the film "We The Surfers", about Liberia's national surf titles, screened at the Ocean Film Festival in 2025. — Wikipedia "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Surf forecast site: exposed reef break, best in November, "uncrowded" even when working, hazards are rips and rocks; water about 29.6 °C on 14 Nov 2026. — surf-forecast.com "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Vai are the dominant group, alongside Gola, Mende, Mandingo, Fante, Kru and Bassa; plantation-style houses line the hills, many in disrepair after looting in both civil wars. — Wikipedia and Wikivoyage "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic / blog, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Population 3,933 at the 2008 census (old); the town remains "mostly a fishing village". — Wikipedia "Robertsport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [community.expat] InterNations Monrovia organises outings to Robertsport; it is a weekend destination for the capital's expats rather than a settled expat base. — Cyril Jarnias "Moving to Liberia as an Expat", 2026-01-26, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] In September 2023 heavy rain flooded Robertsport, destroying homes and displacing families; a political coalition handed L$1.8 million (≈ US$10,000) in aid. — FrontPage Africa, 2023-09-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lake Piso, Liberia's largest lake (about 103 km²), is a Ramsar wetland since 2003 and an Important Bird Area; islands such as Massatin host monkeys and birds. — Wikipedia "Lake Piso", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lake Piso Reserve covers about 97,159 ha; Grand Cape Mount had about 129,817 people in 2008, overwhelmingly rural, with rice the main crop. — Wikipedia "Grand Cape Mount County", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference wiki), confidence: medium
