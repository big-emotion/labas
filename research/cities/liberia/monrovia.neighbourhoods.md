# Monrovia: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **US$1 ≈ €0.86** (and US$1 ≈ L$180), the Wikivoyage Liberia cross rate "as of July 2026" that the city file uses; the dossier gives no dated rate, and FrontPage Africa cites about L$174/US$ in October 2026 [C currency note]. Rents and most larger prices are in US dollars. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/liberia/monrovia.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/liberia.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Coverage warning: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after one search for this file (the search returned only result titles and a summary, so it is not used as a source for places). Everything else comes from direct fetches of Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, official advisories, Talesmag post reports and the aggregator pages already used in the city file. Several useful pages could not be read (see Gaps), so venue coverage is thinner than for most capitals and leans on travel-wiki listings, some of which the wiki itself flags as dead links.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Getting around.** Canada (updated 2026-10-15) advises against commercial taxis, buses, motorbike taxis and kekes, and the UK reports assaults and robberies in taxis [C daily.transport; safety.general][23][24]. Most expats use a car with a driver at US$300–600/month (≈ €255–515) [C daily.transport]. Local ride-hailing apps (Transix, DamyRide, Workorbor) agree the fare before the trip and take cash or mobile money; no fares were found [C cost.negotiation][1]. Shared minibus L$10–20 a trip, okada L$50–100, pre-arranged taxi or ride US$3–10 (≈ €2.60–8.60) (Africarrieres, 2026, low confidence) [C daily.transport]. The UK advises against walking around Monrovia after dark [23].
- **Rent customs.** One guide says landlords often ask 6–12 months up front; another says a one-year lease with one or two months' deposit plus the first month [C cost.negotiation][25]. Expat housing comes with a generator, water tower and filtration; guards are a separate line at US$150–300/month [C housing.search; cost.living].
- **Power and night.** Canada links night-time crime partly to the lack of electricity in many parts of the capital, and says armed home break-ins are common there [24]. LEC said in May 2025 that load shedding was "practically eliminated" [C daily.power]. Expat-standard electricity, generator and water: US$400–800/month (≈ €345–685) [C cost.living].
- **Floods.** Monrovia is described as especially vulnerable to sea-level rise, with low coastal zones and swamps at the edges of residential areas where floodwater spreads waste [3]; Canada warns of flash floods in the May–November rains [24]. No street-level flood map was found.
- **Health.** JFK Medical Center in Sinkor is the national medical centre [9]; serious cases need evacuation [D]. No reliable ambulance service anywhere in the country (UK) [C health.care].
- **LGBT readers.** Same-sex activity is a criminal offence; a harsher bill was deferred in committee in Nov 2025, later status unknown [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Mamba Point (with Snapper Hill and the Broad Street edge)
- Feel: the old diplomatic quarter west of downtown, with the US and UK embassies and the EU delegation [3][C housing.areas]; a 2015 expat report names it, with Sinkor, as the most common expat address [21]. 360Nations calls it upscale with luxury flats and ocean views [25].
- Rent: US$600–1,200/month, bedroom count not stated (≈ €515–1,030) (360Nations, 2026-02) [C cost.rent]; furnished expat flats US$800–2,500 (≈ €685–2,140) are "dearer" here, per Africarrieres (2026, citing 2024 conditions) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 4 miles from Sinkor, roughly 15 minutes without traffic and 20–45 minutes at rush hour (2015 report) [21]. Downtown and Capitol Hill are adjacent [3].
- Watch: the UK says foreign nationals have been mugged in Mamba Point and Sinkor, where most international visitors stay (GOV.UK, read 2026-11-15) [23].
- Places:
  - Mamba Point Hotel, United Nations Drive (hotel with 24-hour power, bar and international restaurant; rooms from US$194) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Cape Hotel, United Nations Drive (upscale hotel near the US Embassy) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Paddy's Sports Bar, United Nations Drive (lounge bar with Atlantic-view patio; the wiki flags its link as dead, so verify it still trades) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Ducor Hotel ruins, Ducor Hill at the end of Broad Street (abandoned landmark, fenced and guarded; entry US$5 in 2019; views over the ocean, the Saint Paul River and West Point) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [8]
  - Antoinette Tubman Stadium, United Nations Drive at Lynch Street (10,000-seat stadium; national team and LFA First Division home games, concerts) — Wikipedia [10]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (the city's highest asking rents); Q5 "Salarié·e" for embassy, UN and NGO posts nearby; Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (hotel restaurants at walking distance by day, car at night).

### Downtown, Capitol Hill and Waterside
- Feel: the historic centre at the tip of the Cape Mesurado peninsula; Capitol Hill holds the Capitol, the Executive Mansion and the Temple of Justice; Waterside, just north and facing the harbour, is the big market district [3]. More a place to work and shop than a residential pick for newcomers in the sources.
- Rent: studio or 1-bed downtown US$220–230/month on a low estimate (≈ €190–200), US$600–830 on other data (≈ €515–710) (Jarnias, 2026-01-26); modest unfurnished 1-bed in central Monrovia US$150–400 (≈ €130–345) (Africarrieres) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the government, port and business district itself [3]; Spriggs Payne in Sinkor is about 5 km away [14]. The Freeport of Monrovia is the main harbour [3].
- Watch: Canada says pickpocketing and purse snatching are common in busy areas, especially markets (updated 2026-10-15) [24].
- Places:
  - Waterside Market (largest indoor-outdoor market, narrow, busy walkways) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Benson Street around Newport Street (best street for fresh produce) and Monoprix, Benson Street (imported-goods supermarket beside the produce market) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Exclusive Supermarket, Center Street at Carey Street (smaller branch, big housewares section) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Ecobank ATMs, Randall Street (Visa and Mastercard, popular with foreigners) — Wikivoyage [2][C daily.money]
  - St. Teresa's Convent, Randall Street (the city's only hostel, beds US$10–15 a night) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - University of Liberia, Capitol Hill campus (main campus) — Wikipedia [12]
  - Providence Island, mouth of the Mesurado River (1822 landing site; replica settler-era buildings, an old well, a monument; nominated to UNESCO's tentative list in 2017; access and fees not stated) — Wikipedia [13]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (ministries, port); Q8 "Je reste en ville" for a visitor's history day; Q3 "Serré" only for the low end of the downtown range.

### Sinkor (with Airfield)
- Feel: once suburban, now Monrovia's midtown, with diplomatic missions, hotels, businesses, the LBDI bank and telecom headquarters [3][5]. Tubman Boulevard carries many restaurants, guesthouses and supermarkets [C housing.areas]. The Airfield area beside Spriggs Payne airport is the main nightlife district [3][C social.nightlife]. It also contains informal communities such as Plumkor, Jorkpentown, Lakpazee and Fiamah [3].
- Rent: US$400–800/month, bedroom count not stated (≈ €345–685) (360Nations, 2026-02) [C cost.rent]; furnished flats US$500–3,500 in a c. 2018 forum (old anecdote) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 5 km from downtown [14]; 15 minutes to Mamba Point off-peak, 20–45 at rush hour (2015) [21]. Tubman Boulevard links Sinkor to Capitol Hill and downtown [5].
- Watch: the UK names Sinkor, with Mamba Point, for muggings of foreigners [23]. FrontPage Africa reported charges over an assault on a police officer in Sinkor on 2026-11-13 (headline only) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Exclusive Supermarket, Tubman Boulevard at 19th Street (imported goods, spices, wine, small bakery) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Sajj House, Tubman Boulevard at 18th Street (Lebanese restaurant with outdoor seating and Wi-Fi) — Wikivoyage [1]; a 2015 report mentions dancing at "Sajj" [21]
  - FuZion D'Afrique, Tubman Boulevard at 15th Street (burgers, pizza and Liberian food; sports screenings) — Wikivoyage [1]; quiz nights there in 2015 [21]
  - P.A.'s Rib House, near Spriggs Payne airfield (American-style ribs) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Living Room Sushi, Royal Hotel, Tubman Boulevard (sushi, expensive) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Fresh fish sellers on the beach side of Tubman Boulevard past the Vavoma building (fish market) — Wikivoyage [1][C social.food]
  - JFK Medical Center, overlooking Tubman Boulevard (national medical centre with emergency department and maternity hospital) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Immigration Office, 24th Street (visa extensions, US$25) — GOV.UK via [C admin.visa]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (by Monrovia standards); Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Airfield); Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" (cafés with Wi-Fi, short trips to everything); Q3 "Moyen".

### Congo Town
- Feel: an independent township at the south-eastern base of the peninsula [3]. The dossier, citing an expat guide, calls it quiet and residential with schools [D]; 360Nations instead describes a busy area mixing traditional and modern housing [25], so the two sources disagree. Jarnias names "parts of Congo Town" among preferred expat areas [C housing.areas].
- Rent: US$300–600/month, bedroom count not stated (≈ €255–515) (360Nations, 2026-02) [C cost.rent]; Africarrieres places furnished expat flats here in the dearer band (US$800–2,500) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: AISM is about 5 miles from downtown on Old Road [6]. Sinkor turns into Congo Town past the Vavoma building on Tubman Boulevard [C housing.areas].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the city-wide frames.
- Places:
  - American International School of Monrovia, Old Road (American curriculum; reopened 2007; 93 pupils in 2021; Wikipedia gives both Pre-K–12 and "through grade 10"; fees not published) — Wikipedia [6][C family.schools]
  - Corina Hotel, Tubman Boulevard at 24th Street (mid-range hotel; check restaurant bills) with Sam's BBQ next door (restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1][C cost.negotiation]
  - University of Liberia medical school campus (A. M. Dogliotti College of Medicine) — Wikipedia [12]
  - Greenland supermarket, Tubman Boulevard (the only supermarket the wiki finds open on Sundays, about 08:00–noon; exact district not stated) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (the one international school sourced); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q10 "La santé et l'école" only partly (school yes, serious care means evacuation [D]).

### ELWA and Rehab (coastal Paynesville)
- Feel: 360Nations calls ELWA a quiet area mixing modern and traditional homes, popular with families [25]. It lies within Paynesville district, where the ELWA mission hospital sits [7]; Rehab is named on Wikipedia as the community where President Boakai lives [4].
- Rent: US$250–500/month, bedroom count not stated (≈ €215–430) (360Nations, 2026-02) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on the airport road east of Congo Town [2][4]; the UK warns the Monrovia–airport roads are under roadworks, congested and hazardous [23]. No commute time sourced.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced. The UK says the SK Doe stadium nearby can become overcrowded on big match days [23].
- Places:
  - ELWA Hospital (mission hospital run by SIM, 120 beds, founded 1965; kept working through the wars and Ebola) — Wikipedia [7]
  - ELWA beach (named among beaches near Monrovia, on the airport road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Thinkers beach (named among beaches near Monrovia; location not detailed) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Samuel Kanyon Doe Sports Complex (22,000 seats, largest stadium; national team; marathon finish in 2022) — Wikipedia [11]
  - Kendeja Resorts and Villas, Robertsfield Highway past the SKD stadium (resort with spa and beach access) — Wikivoyage [1]; a 2013 report praises its beach and pool [20]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille" (family-oriented area, mission hospital); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Paynesville (Red Light and the inland side)
- Feel: a separate city of over 300,000 (2008) with its own city corporation, a largely informal economy of retail, transport and trade, and strong football culture [4]. A 2013 expat report mentions expats living in Paynesville [20]; the dossier calls it cheaper, with big markets [D].
- Rent: US$200–400/month, bedroom count not stated (≈ €170–345) (360Nations) [C cost.rent]; 1-bed outside the centre US$95–150 (≈ €80–130) (Jarnias) and residential-suburb 1-bed US$100–250 (≈ €85–215) (Africarrieres) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: directly east of Monrovia on the airport route [4]. No sourced travel time to downtown; the airport roads are under roadworks and congested [23].
- Watch: Canada says pickpocketing is common in markets [24]. (Wikivoyage advises keeping car doors locked and windows up around Redlight, but it is an undated travel wiki, not an official source.)
- Places:
  - Red Light Market (large commercial district with thousands of traders a day) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Paynesville City Hall Community Park (park opened in 2024) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Blo Degbo (rock formation shaped like a face) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - E.S. Grant Mental Health Hospital (psychiatric hospital affiliated to JFK) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Samuel Kanyon Doe Sports Complex (see ELWA) — Wikipedia [4][11]
  - Roots & Returns "Palava Hut Pitch", 21 Dec 2026, Paynesville (grant pitch event for existing small businesses, US$250–5,000) — FrontPage Africa via [C business.startup]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (trade and market economy, diaspora grant event); Q10 "Une vie abordable".

Not given a block: Old Road, Caldwell, Gardnersville and Logan Town are named as cheaper with mixed security, and West Point as high-risk, by one expat guide [D][C housing.areas]; no further sourced detail was found to describe them responsibly.

## Living: typical weeks

Commute times below are only given when sourced; otherwise "time not sourced". "Car" means a hired car with driver or a ride-hailing app, per the official advice against street taxis and kekes [23][24].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Sinkor**. Everything a remote worker needs is on Tubman Boulevard (supermarket, cafés with Wi-Fi, JFK Medical Center), Mamba Point is 15 minutes away off-peak, and the Airfield is the nightlife district [1][3][21]. Choose a flat with generator and water tank; power and connectivity make remote work costly [D].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; lunch at Sajj House (Wi-Fi) on Tubman at 18th, a few blocks by car [1].
  - Tue: groceries at Exclusive Supermarket, Tubman at 19th [1]; fresh fish from the sellers past the Vavoma building [1].
  - Wed: hot desk if found (US$50/month is the sourced price, no coworking named) [C work.remote]; Orange Digital Center runs free tech training (location not stated) [D][26].
  - Thu: work; evening sports screening at FuZion D'Afrique, Tubman at 15th [1].
  - Fri: car to Mamba Point (15–45 min) for a drink at Paddy's Sports Bar, if still open [1][21].
  - Sat: market morning at Waterside and Benson Street, by car (time not sourced) [1].
  - Sun: Greenland supermarket, open about 08:00–noon [1]; afternoon at ELWA beach (by car, time not sourced) [2].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": the Airfield nightlife district next to Spriggs Payne [3]; a 2015 report mentions dancing at Sajj [21]. No club is named in a current source. The UK warns criminals operate in nightclubs, so go and return by car [23].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": P.A.'s Rib House or The Living Room Sushi at the Royal Hotel [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": DStv from US$7/month (≈ €6) after the 5 Nov 2026 repricing [C daily.internet]; a 2015 report says many restaurants deliver [21].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": ELWA, Thinkers or CE CE beaches near the city [2], or Kendeja's beach and pool [1][20]; rip currents are common and isolated beaches are unsafe after dark [23].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Careysburg, about 30 minutes out, with Quelu and Wulki Farms (pools, horse riding) [1][16].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Monrovia.
  - "Je reste en ville": Ducor Hotel rooftop at sunset (US$5 in 2019) [1][8].
- Rough weekly spend (US$, monthly ÷ 4.33):

  - Electricity 50–150 + water 20–40 (modest-flat figures) → 70–190/month → 16–44 [C daily.power; daily.water, Africarrieres]
  - Home internet 50–150 → 12–35 [C daily.internet]
  - Hot desk 50 → 12 [C work.remote]
  - Food, varied, one person 250–400 → 58–92 [C cost.living]
  - Two mid-range restaurant meals at 5–12 → 10–24 [C social.nightlife]
  - Six car trips at 3–10 → 18–60 [C daily.transport]
  - Gym 25 → 6 [C social.nightlife]
  - **Total ≈ US$224–458 a week (≈ €193–394)**. With the expat-standard generator line (400–800/month → 92–185) instead of the modest-flat utilities, add US$76–141.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Mamba Point**. Salaried newcomers mostly arrive with a posting in government, the port, NGOs, UN agencies or embassies [D][C work.jobs]; the embassies and EU delegation are in Mamba Point [3], and the Freeport is the main harbour [3]. Work permits cost up to about US$1,000/year and are tied to the employer [C work.jobs].
- Week:
  - Mon: office by car (Mamba Point–Sinkor 15–45 min if the job is in Sinkor) [21].
  - Tue: after work, groceries at Exclusive Supermarket, Center Street at Carey Street [1].
  - Wed: dinner at Mamba Point Hotel [1].
  - Thu: football at Antoinette Tubman Stadium, United Nations Drive, if an LFA fixture is on (fixtures not sourced) [10].
  - Fri: drinks at Paddy's Sports Bar (verify it trades) [1].
  - Sat: Waterside Market in the morning [1]; Ducor Hotel at sunset [8].
  - Sun: church (85% of Liberians are Christian [C social.religion]; Trinity Cathedral and Sacred Heart Cathedral are named but not located in the sources [1][3]); Kendeja for the afternoon [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Airfield, Sinkor [3], by car.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Move by pre-booked car or ride-hailing app, not on foot after dark [23][24].

### One day
- Morning: Waterside Market and the produce stalls on Benson Street around Newport Street (watch bags: pickpocketing is common in markets) [1][24]. Then the National Museum (civil-war exhibits; hours and fee not stated) [1].
- Midday: Lebanese sandwiches or falafel at Diana (address not stated) [1], or a Liberian plate (palm butter, cassava leaf, jollof) at a cook shop for US$2–3 [C social.food].
- Afternoon: Capitol Hill by car (Capitol, Executive Mansion, Temple of Justice, seen from outside) [3]; then the Ducor Hotel ruins on Ducor Hill for the view (US$5 in 2019) [1][8].
- Evening: dinner at Mamba Point Hotel, a short drive away on United Nations Drive [1], or Sajj House in Sinkor [1].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive at Roberts International (2–4 h to town) [1]; check in on Tubman Boulevard, Sinkor (Moko's or Hotel Provident on 9th Street) [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above (downtown, Ducor, Mamba Point).
- Day 3: Sinkor and Congo Town as a resident would see them: Exclusive Supermarket on Tubman at 19th, fish sellers past the Vavoma building, a drive past AISM on Old Road [1][6]; evening at the Airfield [3].
- Day 4 (trip): Careysburg, about 30 minutes away, an 1850s settler town with American-South-style houses and churches; lunch or a ride at Quelu or Wulki Farms [1][16].
- Day 5 (daily life): early morning at Red Light Market in Paynesville, the city's trading engine [4], then the Paynesville City Hall Community Park [4] and Blo Degbo [1]; afternoon at ELWA beach [2]. If a fixture is on, an LFA First Division match at Antoinette Tubman Stadium is the other everyday scene [10] (fixtures not sourced; the UK warns of crowding at SKD on big days [23]).
- Days 6–7 (trip): Robertsport, 80 km north-west, "several hours" by road: surf at Fisherman's Point, Cotton Trees or Cassava Point, and Lake Piso (Ramsar wetland) [18][19][27]; stay at Kwepunha Retreat (2017 listing, verify) [27]. Return in daylight: roads outside Monrovia are unlit, with night robberies at illegal checkpoints [23]. Alternative: Buchanan, about 2 hours on the paved road (bus US$5–10) [C daily.transport; see buchanan.neighbourhoods.md]. Sunday morning before leaving: Greenland supermarket's Sunday market [1][C social.food], or a church service (the country is 85% Christian [C social.religion]; no church is located in the sources).
