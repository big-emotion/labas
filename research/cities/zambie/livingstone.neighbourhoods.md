# Livingstone: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (city file, dated): **1 USD ≈ ZMW 19.78** (Rio Times, 2026-11-11) and **1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR** (dossier), so **1 EUR ≈ ZMW 21.5**. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/zambie/livingstone.md`; `[L tag]` = an item in `research/cities/zambie/lusaka.md` (used only for national figures); `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/zambie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Method note: one keyword search ran for Livingstone before the session's shared search budget was exhausted; everything else comes from direct fetches. Livingstone is documented online for tourists, hardly at all for residents. Rents below come only from an aggregator snippet (page returned 403); everyday-life places come mostly from Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Zambia Tourism and one travel blog. Treat the "Living" section as a frame to test on a trial stay, not as a picture of resident life.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Rent: the Rio Times found no official rent series for foreign residents in Livingstone, and Expat.com gives no Livingstone rents [C cost.rent].
- Safety: the UK FCDO calls larger cities "generally safe during daylight hours" and advises pre-arranged transport at night; it also warns that adventure sports around the Falls have caused serious accidents and deaths [C safety.general]. Southern Province recorded 629 criminal gender-based-violence cases in July–September 2026 [C safety.women]. Human Rights Watch documents a national post-election crackdown on opposition figures, lawyers and journalists (2026-11-01) [D].
- Health: private care is concentrated in Lusaka and Ndola; medical-evacuation cover is recommended (Expat.com) [C health.care]. A 70-bed cancer hospital has been under construction since May 2026 [C health.care]. A French honorary consul is based in Livingstone [D].
- Money: ATMs give kwacha only; Wikivoyage says card networks are "often down" at the Falls and activities are usually priced in USD cash [C daily.money][C cost.negotiation].
- LGBT readers: same-sex activity is illegal nationwide, up to life imprisonment [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Town centre (Mosi-oa-Tunya Road)
- Feel: small and walkable; the main road carries the banks, supermarkets, backpacker hostels and museums (Wikivoyage, Zambia Tourism) [3][5]. On 15 Oct 2026 the council cleared more than 130 street vendors from the CBD to designated markets (News Diggers) [C business.admin].
- Rent: no figure found for the centre. Nightly options as a first base: Fawlty Towers dorm US$12, double US$55–85; Kozo Lodge, Kabila Street, double with breakfast US$27 (Wikivoyage, undated) [3].
- Getting around: on foot in the centre; shared taxis from the depot next to City Market, across from Shoprite: ZK20 to the Falls (≈ €0.93), ZK100 to Kazungula (≈ €4.65) [3][C daily.transport]; official taxis are blue; Yango works, cash only [C daily.transport]. The Falls are about 10 km away [3]; Zambia Tourism gives about 11 km to the Victoria Falls Bridge and border [5].
- Watch: the FCDO advises pre-arranged transport at night [C safety.general]; at a July 2026 rally the President promised "more security street lights" for the town (Mwebantu, 2026-07-27) [C safety.general], a sign that lighting is thin.
- Places:
  - Shoprite, Mosi-oa-Tunya Square and Kapondo Street (supermarket, 08:00–20:00; Stanbic 24-hour ATM in the Shoprite mall) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Livingstone City Market and the shared-taxi depot opposite Shoprite (market, transport) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mukuni Park Curio Market (craft market, town centre) — Zambia Tourism [5]; travel blog [6]
  - Livingstone Museum, Plot 567 Mosi-oa-Tunya Road (national museum, US$5 for non-Zambians) — Wikivoyage [3]; Zambia Tourism places it "on the main road through the town centre" [5] (a Wikipedia photo caption places it in Maramba [4])
  - Olga's Italian Corner, behind the Catholic church (pizza and pasta up to about US$8; social enterprise funding a youth training centre) — Wikivoyage [3]; blog [6]
  - Fawlty Towers and its Hippos bar (hostel, bar) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Pub and Grill, opposite the main banks, and Wonder Bake (restaurants) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Intercity Market (market; 140 empty stands counted by the council in Oct 2026) — News Diggers via [C business.admin]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (everything on foot, nightly beds); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Maramba
- Feel: the former African township to the east and south, named after the Maramba River, historically home to workers, craftsmen, tradesmen and families on welfare (Wikipedia) [4]. A search snippet from an aggregator calls it the cheapest area, lively, near the river and markets (expatlife.ai, "verified June 2026", page 403, low confidence) [10].
- Rent: 1-bed ZMW 1,500–4,000/month (≈ €70–186) (aggregator snippet, low confidence) [10].
- Getting around: no sourced fare or time to the centre. Taxi or Yango (cash) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Maramba Market (produce, chitenge fabric) — travel blog, updated 2022-08-02 [6]
  - Maramba Cultural Museum (traditional dance, song and costume; exact location not given) — Wikipedia [4]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Livingstone Museum (Wikipedia's caption places it in Maramba; see Town centre for the conflicting placement) — [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" for a low-cost base near markets; readers who want to live among Zambian families.

### Dambwa and Linda
- Feel: two separate residential suburbs, grouped here because sources say little about either. Dambwa North: "mid-range", larger plots, quieter streets, a growing number of guesthouses and lodges (expatlife.ai snippet, low confidence) [10]. Linda: described in a snippet from the same aggregator as a budget-friendly, peaceful residential compound [10]; Wikipedia names it only for its secondary school [4]. Dambwa village sits on the route of a local cycle tour [6].
- Rent: Dambwa North 1-bed ZMW 3,500–8,000/month (≈ €163–372) (aggregator snippet, low confidence) [10]. Linda: no figure.
- Getting around: no sourced fare or time to the centre; taxi or Yango (cash) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Dambwa market (small local market) — travel blog [6]
  - Local Cowboy Cycle Tours (about 4 hours through villages, a local market and a school it funds) — travel blog [6]
  - Linda Secondary School (secondary school) — Wikipedia [4][C family.schools]
  - Hillcrest Technical Secondary School (secondary school; neighbourhood not stated) — Wikipedia [4][C family.schools]
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Riverside and the road to the Falls
- Feel: Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park lies between the town and the Zambezi (Wikipedia) [4]; lodges and hotels line the river toward the Falls and upstream (Wikivoyage, travel blog) [3][6]. This is hospitality territory: it is where many jobs are (hotel occupancy reached 70 %, minister quoted 2025-09-26) [C work.jobs], not a place with sourced residential lets.
- Rent: no residential rent found. Nightly: Maramba River Lodge from K1,200 (≈ €56; Zambia Tourism, period not stated) [5]; Waterberry Zambezi Lodge US$365; Tongabezi Lodge US$495 [5].
- Getting around: shared taxi ZK20 from the City Market depot, or blue taxi about US$10 each way (negotiable); Wikivoyage advises against walking the 8 km to the Falls [3][C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: bull elephants in musth can be aggressive (Expat.com) [C nature.access]; human–wildlife conflict killed 83 people nationally in 2026 (Rio Times, 2026-11-11, no local breakdown) [C nature.access]; the FCDO notes deaths in Falls adventure sports [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Victoria Falls park entrance and curio market (US$20 per Wikivoyage, US$30 per Expat.com; full-moon visits US$25) — [3][7][C cost.living]
  - Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park (white rhino, giraffe, zebra) — Wikivoyage [3]; Expat.com [7]
  - The Royal Livingstone (hotel; afternoon tea open to non-guests around 15:30) and Avani Victoria Falls next door — travel blog [6]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Maramba River Lodge, inside the park about 10 minutes' drive toward the Falls, and Zambezi Waterfront nearby (lodges) — travel blog [6]
  - David Livingstone Safari Lodge with Kalai restaurant (lodge) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - 18-hole golf course with clubhouse, restaurant and gym (location not given) — Zambia Tourism [5]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (tourism jobs); Q8 "Dans la savane ou le désert"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" for lodge stays.

Not built out: Constitution Hill (higher ground settled by Europeans after 1900) and Old Livingstone (the original river crossing) are historical names only in the sources [4]; Kutaway, which Wikivoyage calls historically "second class" and where Sunrise serves Zambian food, has no other source [3].

## Living: typical weeks

Read with the method note: the resident data is thin and the rents are snippet-only.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Dambwa (North)**. Quieter streets and larger plots per the only source [10], a mid-range rent band. Big caveat: no fibre or internet availability for Livingstone was found [C gaps]; test the connection and the backup power during a trial stay (load-shedding hurt tourism in 2024; ZESCO said supply was stable in June 2026) [C daily.power].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home.
  - Tue: into town by taxi or Yango (cash) [C daily.transport]; lunch at Olga's [3]; groceries at Shoprite [3].
  - Wed: home; evening at Hippos, Fawlty Towers [3].
  - Thu: work; late afternoon at the Livingstone Museum (US$5 for non-Zambians; resident pricing not found) [3].
  - Fri: groceries at Super Spar, Falls Park shopping centre [3].
  - Sat: Dambwa market [6]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Catholic church in the centre (Olga's sits behind it) [3]; the Roman Catholic Diocese of Livingstone is seated here [4].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Chez nTemba (nightclub, open daily, door charge) [3]; take a pre-arranged ride home [C safety.general]. The Mosi Day of Thunder festival (25 Apr 2026) is the yearly big night [C social.culture].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Rhapsody's, Falls Park centre, or The Waterfront (river views) [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; on a full-moon night in high water, the Falls night visit (US$25, 20:00–22:00) [3].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Livingstone (landlocked; riverside only, beach score 0 in the dossier) [D].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no mountain or forest outing sourced; the nearest equivalent is the Batoka Gorge (rafting Aug–Dec, full day US$155) [C cost.living][C nature.access] or canoeing on the Upper Zambezi with Bundu Adventures (price not listed) [6].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park [3]; Chobe (Botswana) day trip via Kazungula, from about US$190 (blog, 2022) [6].
  - "Je reste en ville": Railway Museum (Chishimba Falls Road per Zambia Tourism, 726 Limulunga Road per Wikivoyage) [5][3]; Livingstone Museum [3].
- Rough weekly spend (one person; national averages where Livingstone figures are missing):
  - Rent: ZMW 3,500–8,000 ÷ 4.33 = ZMW 808–1,848 = €38–86 [10]
  - Utilities: US$70 (85 m², national Numbeo) ÷ 4.33 = US$16 ≈ €15 [8]
  - Internet: US$46 ÷ 4.33 = US$10.6 ≈ €9.8 (national figure; Livingstone availability unknown) [8]; mobile 10 GB US$12 ÷ 4.33 ≈ €2.6 [8]
  - Food at home: US$250–350/month ÷ 4.33 = US$58–81 ≈ €53–75 (national) [8]
  - 2 meals at Olga's × up to US$8 = up to US$16 ≈ €14.7 [3]
  - 4 taxi trips × ZK30–50 (the airport fare, used as an upper proxy because no in-town fare is sourced) = ZK120–200 ≈ €5.6–9.3 [C cost.negotiation]
  - 4 local beers × under US$1 (national) ≈ under €3.7 [8]
  - **Total ≈ €142–216 a week.**

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Dambwa**, commuting toward the riverside lodges. Jobs are in tourism, hospitality, guiding and conservation [C work.jobs]; the employer has to apply for the employment permit (K24,000 issuance fee, ≈ €1,116) [L admin.residency]. Seasonality matters: Falls flow peaks in March–April and is lowest September–November; rafting runs August–December [C nature.access].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: shared taxi from the City Market depot to the Falls road, ZK20 each way [3]; staff transport by employers not sourced.
  - Mon: groceries at Choppies, Plot 1386/19 Kafubu Road (08:00–20:00) [3].
  - Tue: evening at home.
  - Wed: dinner at Olga's [3].
  - Thu: Livingstone Performing Arts Foundation show at Wasawange Hotel (US$25) [3].
  - Fri: drinks at The Waterfront [3].
  - Sat: Maramba Market [6]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mount Zion Christian Centre or another of the town's churches (no address) [4]; afternoon tea at the Royal Livingstone [6].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Chez nTemba [3].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Kalai at David Livingstone Safari Lodge [3]; a sunset cruise US$45–75 per person [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Livingstone [D].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Batoka Gorge rafting or Jungle Junction on Bovu Island (about 1.5 hours; transfers US$30, camping US$10 per person, blog 2022) [6].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Chobe day trip from about US$190 (2022) with the KAZA UniVisa (US$50, 30 days, allows day trips to Botswana) [6][C admin.visa]; Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park [3].
  - "Je reste en ville": Livingstone Museum and Railway Museum [3][5].
- Rough weekly spend (two people):
  - Rent: €38–86 (Dambwa North 1-bed, snippet) [10]
  - Utilities €15; internet €9.8; two mobile plans 2 × €2.6 = €5.2 [8]
  - Food at home for two: 2 × €53–75 = €106–150 [8]
  - Commute: 2 people × 5 days × 2 legs = 20 shared-taxi rides × ZK20 = ZK400 ≈ €18.6 [3]
  - One dinner for two at Olga's, 2 × up to US$8 ≈ €14.7 [3]
  - **Total ≈ €207–299 a week**, before any sunset cruise (2 × US$45–75 = €83–138).

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"

Business context: about 2.2 million international arrivals nationally in 2024 [C work.jobs]; 210 empty stands in three of the town's 18 markets, 140 of them at Intercity Market (Oct 2026) [C business.admin]; a US$90,000 town-centre design competition under GREAT-TDP, with no build budget yet [C business.invest]; an opposition party claimed a festive-season visitor slump in Dec 2024 without figures [C work.jobs]. Founders without Zambian nationality face the US$250,000 investor-permit floor [D].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois"
- Base for the first nights: **Town centre**. Everything on foot, nightly beds, banks and the taxi depot together [3]. EU passport holders get 90 days visa-free per 12 months [L admin.visa]; the KAZA UniVisa (US$50) is sold at Livingstone airport and the land borders for trips into Zimbabwe and day trips to Botswana [C admin.visa].
- Week:
  - Mon: land at Harry Mwaanga Nkumbula International (flights from Lusaka, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Mbombela, Nairobi) [C daily.transport]; taxi ZK30–50, no more than US$10 [C cost.negotiation]. Check in at Jollyboys (dorm about US$12) or Kozo Lodge (US$27 double with breakfast) [3][C cost.living].
  - Tue: SIM card (Airtel, MTN; about US$1 per Wikivoyage, undated) [9]; cash from a Stanbic or FNB 24-hour ATM [3]. Avoid street money-changers; use the official bureau [C cost.negotiation].
  - Wed: walk the neighbourhoods by taxi (Maramba, Dambwa); ask lodge staff and agents for lets (no agency, portal or Facebook group was found for Livingstone) [C gaps].
  - Thu: Victoria Falls morning (US$20–30) [3][7]; look at seasonal job boards at lodges and operators (no job board sourced).
  - Fri: Maramba Market [6]; Shoprite for the basics [3].
  - Sat: Saturday night as in profile 1.
  - Sun: church service in town [4].
- Lease terms, visit fees and advance customs for Livingstone: not found; the national pattern is a 1–3-month deposit and a 12-month minimum lease [L cost.rent]. Bank account: needs a permit and proof of address, so not on a trial stay [L daily.money].
- Weekend outing: as in profile 1 (no beach; gorge; Mosi-oa-Tunya or Chobe; museums).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best season depends on the goal: full Falls March–April, balanced views June–July, rafting August–December, Devil's Pool late in the dry season [C nature.access][3].

### One day
- Morning: shared taxi from the depot next to City Market to the Falls, ZK20 (≈ €0.93), or a blue taxi about US$10 [3]; Victoria Falls park, US$20 (Wikivoyage) or US$30 (Expat.com) [3][7]; curio market at the entrance [3].
- Midday: back to town; lunch at Olga's Italian Corner (up to about US$8) [3].
- Afternoon: Livingstone Museum, Plot 567 Mosi-oa-Tunya Road (US$5 non-Zambians) [3]; Mukuni Park Curio Market [5].
- Evening: Zambezi sunset cruise, US$45–75 [C cost.living]; drinks at Hippos, Fawlty Towers [3]; pre-arranged ride if out after dark [C safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2, daily life: market morning at Maramba Market (produce, chitenge) [6]; Railway Museum and Maramba Cultural Museum in the afternoon [5][3].
- Day 3: Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park, white rhinos [3]; afternoon tea at the Royal Livingstone [6].
- Day 4: one adventure: full-day Batoka Gorge rafting US$155 (Aug–Dec) or Livingstone Island and Devil's Pool about US$98 (seasonal, from about mid-year) or a 15-minute helicopter flight US$140 [C cost.living][3]. FCDO: serious accidents and deaths have happened in Falls adventure sports [C safety.general].
- Day 5, out of town: Chobe National Park, Botswana, via the Kazungula crossing (shared taxi ZK100, then a day tour from about US$190, 2022 prices); the KAZA UniVisa (US$50) covers the Botswana day trip [3][6][C admin.visa].
- Day 6, out of town: walk across the Victoria Falls Bridge to the Zimbabwe side (KAZA visa; Zimbabwe-side park entry US$30 in 2022) [3][6].
- Day 7, daily life: Sunday service at one of the town's churches [4]; Local Cowboy Cycle Tours through Dambwa village and its market (about 4 hours) [6]; Mukuni village, 9.6 km south-east, the area's oldest settlement, on an organised tour [4][5].
- If the dates fit: the full-moon night visit at the Falls (US$25) [3]; the July action festival [D].
