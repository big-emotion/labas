# Livingstone, Zambia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: USD amounts are converted at the dossier's rate of **1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR**. Kwacha uses a dated market rate, **ZMW 19.78 per USD** (Rio Times, 2026-11-11), so **1 EUR ≈ ZMW 21.5**. The dossier's own ZMW 25/USD was only an inference. No keyword searches were possible in this session, because the shared search budget was exhausted. Livingstone is thinly covered online for residents (as opposed to tourists), so several items come from a travel wiki and are flagged as such.

## Items

- [cost.rent] The Rio Times found no official rent or household-budget series for foreign residents in Livingstone; agent and forum rents are unverified. — Rio Times "Moving to and Living in Zambia in 2026", 2026-11-12, intl-media, confidence: high

- [cost.living] JCTR's basket for August 2026 puts Livingstone among the seven of 16 districts above the national average of K8,480.72 (≈ €394) for a family of five. Its exact total was not reported. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-09-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] A 25 kg bag of roller meal (the maize-flour staple) cost K180.83 in Livingstone in August 2026 (≈ €8.4), down from K204.99 in July. In Lusaka it cost K159.28. — Zambia Monitor (JCTR August 2026), 2026-09-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Fuel is priced uniformly nationwide. In November 2026 petrol cost K33.06/L (≈ €1.54) and diesel K34.45/L (≈ €1.60). — Energy Regulation Board pump price build-up, 2026-10-31, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Victoria Falls park entry: US$30 per adult on the Zambian side, according to Expat.com (≈ €28). — Expat.com Livingstone guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Wikivoyage gives US$20 for Falls park entry and US$25 for full-moon night visits. Bring cash, as card networks are "often down". — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Activity prices: full-day rafting US$155; bungee US$168; 15-min helicopter flight US$140; sunset cruise US$45–75; Livingstone Island trip about US$98. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Backpacker dorms cost about US$8–12; Jollyboys US$12/night; a Zig Zag guesthouse room from US$50 with breakfast. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] An airport taxi costs about ZK30–50 (≈ €1.4–2.3). Foreigners may be charged more, but Wikivoyage says the fare should not exceed US$10 (a 2026 note). — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] The going taxi rate to the Falls (about 10 km) is US$10 each way (≈ €9.2), described as negotiable. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Activities are often priced and paid in US dollars cash; paying in kwacha works out more expensive. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Curio stalls near town and at the Falls entrance charge similar prices, which may be higher than elsewhere. Souvenirs are cheaper on the road to Lusaka. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Two-tier pricing: the Livingstone Museum charges US$5 for non-Zambians. Money touts use sleight-of-hand tricks; use the official bureau. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Historic areas: Maramba (the former African township, east and south), Constitution Hill (higher ground settled by Europeans after 1900) and Old Livingstone by the river. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The economy runs on tourism, hospitality, adventure activities and conservation; Expat.com targets "tourism professionals". — Expat.com Livingstone guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The tourism minister said Livingstone hotel occupancy reached 70%, up from 40% before 2023. National international arrivals doubled from 1.1M (2022) to 2.2M (2024). — Zambia Monitor, 2025-09-26, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] An opposition party (Citizens First) claimed a "drastic decline" in festive-season visitors in Dec 2024. It blamed load-shedding and the drying Falls, but gave no figures. — Zambia Monitor, 2024-12-30, local-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] The town has other small industries: timber, hides, tobacco, cotton and textiles. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [business.admin] More than 130 street vendors were cleared from the CBD on 15 Oct 2026 and sent to designated markets. The council counted 210 empty stands in three of the town's 18 markets, 140 of them at Intercity Market. — News Diggers, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.invest] A Town Centre Development Programme (under the tourism ministry's GREAT-TDP project) launched a US$90,000 landscape-design competition for streets and tourist hotspots. No implementation budget or dates were given. — Zambia Monitor, 2025-08-26, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] During the 2024 drought crisis, load-shedding was blamed for hurting Livingstone tourism. ZESCO said in June 2026 that supply was stable nationwide. — Zambia Monitor, 2024-12-30 and 2026-06-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Harry Mwaanga Nkumbula International Airport has flights to Lusaka, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Mbombela and Nairobi. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Buses to Lusaka cost ZK250–350 (≈ €12–16; a 2022 figure). They leave hourly, take 6–9 hours, and no longer run overnight. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The weekly Zambezi train to Lusaka and Kitwe takes about 12 hours to Lusaka; a sleeper costs ZK145 (≈ €6.7), economy ZK75 (≈ €3.5). — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Shared taxis from the depot near City Market cost ZK20 to the Falls (≈ €0.93) and ZK100 to Kazungula (≈ €4.65). Official taxis are blue. Yango works but takes cash. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Livingstone–Sesheke road is among the roads in K4.9bn rehabilitation contracts signed in July 2026 (headlines and summaries only). — Zambia Monitor search listing 2026-07-10; News Diggers 2026-07-13, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.money] ATMs dispense only kwacha; Stanbic, Standard Chartered and FNB have 24-hour Visa/Mastercard machines. Kwacha cannot be exchanged in Zimbabwe. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Expat.com says private care is concentrated in Lusaka and Ndola, and recommends medical-evacuation cover. — Expat.com Livingstone guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Building of a 70-bed cancer hospital (K422.3M, ≈ €19.6M) began in May 2026, with completion expected within 12 months. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-05-03, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Zambia and Zimbabwe held a joint Ebola preparedness meeting in Livingstone in October 2026 covering screening at the Victoria Falls border; no case was reported in either country. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-10-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Wikipedia names only two secondary schools (Hillcrest Technical and Linda); no international or French school was found. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The FCDO notes that adventure sports around Victoria Falls carry risks, with serious accidents and deaths reported. — gov.uk FCDO Zambia safety and security, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The FCDO calls larger cities "generally safe during daylight hours" and advises pre-arranged transport at night. Wikivoyage adds that downtown Livingstone is risky at night with poor street lighting, and that walking the 8 km to the Falls is not advised. — gov.uk FCDO (accessed 2026-11-14), official; Wikivoyage (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] At a July 2026 campaign rally in Livingstone, President Hichilema promised "more security street lights". — Mwebantu, 2026-07-27, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.women] Southern Province, where Livingstone sits, recorded 629 criminal gender-based-violence cases in July–September 2026. Nationally, 80.5% of victims were women and girls. — Zambia Monitor (Zambia Police data), 2026-10-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Nightlife centres on tourist bars such as Hippos (at Fawlty Towers), plus Chez nTemba (door fee) and a casino with slightly higher drink prices. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] The Mosi Day of Thunder music festival, sponsored by Mosi Lager, was held in Livingstone on 25 April 2026, with Nigerian artist Ruger headlining. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-04-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Museums: the Livingstone Museum, the Maramba Cultural Museum, the Railway Museum and the Victoria Falls Field Museum. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Supermarkets include Shoprite (two branches), Super Spar at Falls Park mall and Choppies; Olga's Italian Corner sells pizza and pasta for up to about US$8. — Wikivoyage "Livingstone", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Mostly Christian (Catholic, United Church, Reformed, Baptist, Pentecostal), with mosques and Hindu temples too. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [admin.visa] The KAZA UniVisa (US$50, 30 days) is issued at Livingstone airport and at the Victoria Falls and Kazungula land borders. It allows unlimited Zambia–Zimbabwe travel and day trips to Botswana. — gov.uk FCDO entry requirements, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Hot semi-arid: about 646 mm of rain a year, nearly all November–March. Highs average about 36 °C in October and 26–27 °C in June–July, with nights near 8 °C. The record is 43.6 °C. — Wikipedia "Livingstone, Zambia" (1991–2020 normals), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Falls run at maximum flow in March–April, with balanced views in June–July and low flow from September to November. Rafting in Batoka Gorge runs August–December. — Expat.com Livingstone guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park sits between town and river (giraffes, white rhinos, zebras). Bull elephants in musth can be aggressive. — Expat.com Livingstone guide / Wikivoyage, accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Human–wildlife conflict killed 83 people and injured 77 nationally in 2026 (to the latest figures); no breakdown by area was given. — Rio Times, 2026-11-11, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex activity is illegal nationwide, with up to life imprisonment; 91.9% would not want gay neighbours (Afrobarometer 2021–23). — Equaldex, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
