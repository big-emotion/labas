# Ndola, Zambia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city: the dossier proposes Ndola as the Copperbelt commercial hub. Currency note: kwacha is converted at **1 EUR ≈ ZMW 21.5**, using ZMW 19.78 per USD (Rio Times, 2026-11-11) and the dossier's 1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR. No keyword searches were possible in this session (the shared search budget was exhausted); all items come from pages fetched directly.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Expat.com puts Ndola rents at the same level as Kitwe's or slightly lower, and below Lusaka's. It gives no figures. — Expat.com Ndola guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] JCTR's basket for a family of five in Ndola was K10,444.93 in August 2026 (≈ €486), against K12,000.94 in Lusaka and a national average of K8,480.72. — Zambia Monitor (JCTR), 2026-09-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Ndola had the highest charcoal price among monitored towns in August 2026: K770 per 90 kg bag (≈ €35.8), K590 above the cheapest town (Mpika). — Zambia Monitor (JCTR), 2026-09-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Fuel is priced uniformly nationwide; in November 2026 petrol cost K33.06/L (≈ €1.54) and diesel K34.45/L (≈ €1.60). The Ndola fuel terminal fee is built into the price. — Energy Regulation Board pump price build-up, 2026-10-31, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Budget guesthouses charge K250–350 a night (≈ €12–16), from listings updated 2022–2023. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Two-tier pricing at the Copperbelt Museum: residents pay about K10 and non-residents US$5 (K100). Wikivoyage says the visit is "not worth it" for non-residents. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Neighbourhoods include Kansenshi, Northrise, Itawa, Ndeke, Hillcrest, Dola Hill, Masala, Chifubu, Kabushi, Twapia, Kawama and Mushili. — Wikipedia "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Work began on 10 Apr 2026 on a US$1.1bn oil refinery (a joint venture between the state IDC and Fujian Xiang Xin). It is expected to create 2,200+ construction jobs and 600 direct jobs once running; no completion date was given. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-04-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] Existing industry includes Ndola Lime, Elsewedy Electric's transformer and meter plants, and the Indeni refinery, where the Tazama pipeline ends. Clothing and vehicle assembly largely disappeared after 1980–2000. — Wikipedia "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] The 60th Zambia International Trade Fair opened in Ndola in July 2026. The President ordered the return of about 8 ha of fair land taken over by encroachers. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-07-05, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe International Airport (opened 2021) is about 15 km from downtown. It has Ethiopian, Kenya Airways, Airlink and Air Tanzania international flights, plus domestic Proflight and Zambia Airways. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] By road, Lusaka is 4–5 hours (UBZ, Juldan, Power Tools buses) and Kitwe about 45 minutes. The weekly Zambezi train runs to Lusaka in about 12 hours. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Ndola–Sakania highway, a public–private partnership (PPP) project, had potholes in March 2026, prompting the Copperbelt minister and engineers to criticise the PPP works (headlines and summaries). — Zambia Monitor, 2026-03-14/15, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.water] The FCDO, as reported by the Rio Times, advises against bathing in Copperbelt rivers or drinking untreated local water or local fish after the February 2025 heavy-metal discharge. — Rio Times, 2026-11-12, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.money] Free 24-hour Visa/Mastercard ATMs at Ecobank (Broadway) and FNB (President Avenue); Standard Chartered at Ndola Square Mall. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Ndola Teaching Hospital is the public reference. Expat.com says private facilities are concentrated in Lusaka and Ndola, and that most expats prefer private care. — Expat.com Zambia/Ndola guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Simba International School (Airport Road) takes boarders (Tau House) from Ndola, Luanshya and Kitwe, plus secondary pupils from Lusaka. Its 2025/26 year began 29 Aug 2025. No fees were given. — Expat.com Ndola guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Higher education includes Northrise University, Copperbelt University's School of Medicine, the Ndola College of Biomedical Sciences and NORTEC (technical college). — Wikipedia "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] During the July 2026 campaign, opposition supporters threw stones at police at unauthorised roadshows in Chifubu and Kawama (14 arrests), and a ruling-party office at Twapia was burgled. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-07-25, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.women] The Copperbelt recorded 984 criminal gender-based-violence cases in July–September 2026. — Zambia Monitor (Zambia Police data), 2026-10-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] An immigration operation on 13 Oct 2026 in Minsundu, Itawa, Ndeke, Kansenshi, Northrise and Mitengo stopped 86 people (35 Somali, 25 Congolese, 2 British among others). 29 had valid permits. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.sport] Levy Mwanawasa Stadium seats 50,000; Zesco United plays in the top flight. There is a membership tennis club, a public swimming pool and a motorbike track about 10 km out. — Wikipedia "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Malls: Jacaranda (Pick n Pay, open 07:00–22:00), Kafubu (Shoprite) and Ndola Square (Choppies). Nightlife listings are thin: one pub-pizzeria (Bo'Jangles) last updated 2017. — Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [community.expat] Expat.com calls Ndola calmer than Kitwe. On its forum, a member recommended Ndola about 10 years ago for its expat community, restaurants, malls and airport (an old anecdote). — Expat.com Ndola guide (2025-10-31) / Expat.com forum "Best cities to live in Zambia" (post about 10 years old), forum, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Humid subtropical, with about 1,127 mm of rain a year, mostly November–March. Highs are 26–33 °C, and nights drop to about 6–7 °C in June–July. — Wikipedia "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lake Chilengwa (about 14 km east, a collapsed limestone sinkhole), Nsobe Game Camp, and the Dag Hammarskjöld crash-site memorial (about 10 km). — Wikipedia / Wikivoyage "Ndola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Ndola sits about 10 km from the DR Congo border, with freight rail to Lubumbashi. Population is about 625,000 (2022 census); Expat.com gives 628,595. — Wikipedia "Ndola" / Expat.com, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex activity is illegal nationwide, with up to life imprisonment. — Equaldex, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
