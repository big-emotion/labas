# Lusaka: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (city file, dated): **1 USD ≈ ZMW 19.78** (Rio Times close, 2026-11-11) and **1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR** (dossier), so **1 EUR ≈ ZMW 21.5**. The kwacha lost 3.4 % in October 2026 [C daily.money], so recheck before quoting. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33 and a yearly one by 52. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/zambie/lusaka.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/zambie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Method note: this pass had a single keyword search before the session's shared search budget ran out; everything else comes from direct fetches of known pages (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, the Lusaka365 neighbourhood guide, Nkwazi Magazine, Zambia Tourism, school and hub sites). Lusaka365 is a commercial city-guide site (KAPVOLK Services Ltd): good for the existence and street of a venue, weak for "feel" claims, and never used here for safety.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Rent: expat-area 1–2-bed flats are quoted in USD at US$800–2,000/month; local-market Numbeo averages are far lower (1-bed centre US$211, suburbs US$121); paid monthly or quarterly, deposit 1–3 months, lease 12 months minimum [C cost.rent]. The Rio Times found no official rent series for foreign residents [C cost.rent].
- Safety: the UK FCDO calls larger cities "generally safe during daylight hours", advises pre-arranged transport at night and warns of carjackers at locked gates after dark; Lusaka demonstrations can turn violent [C safety.general]. Expat.com advises 24/7 guards, especially with children [C housing.search]. Human Rights Watch (2026-11-01) documents a post-election crackdown aimed at opposition members, lawyers, journalists and critics [C safety.general].
- Power and water: ZESCO said on 2026-06-10 it expects no nationwide load management, but outages still happen and generators or inverters are common [C daily.power]; France Diplomatie flagged El Niño vigilance on 2026-11-03 [C health.care]. Lusaka was named the main cholera transmission hub in Feb 2026, linked to informal settlements and sanitation gaps; 1,090 of 1,622 national cases by late Oct 2026 were in Lusaka Province [C daily.water][C health.care].
- LGBT readers: same-sex activity is illegal, up to life imprisonment [C rights.lgbt]. This applies to every neighbourhood below.

## Neighbourhoods

### Kabulonga
- Feel: a quiet, upscale suburb of older houses and large gardens, home to expatriates, diplomats and affluent Zambians, with several embassies (Lusaka365, undated) [7]; described as "serene" and relaxed [8]. Listed by Expat.com among the expat areas [C cost.rent]. Lusaka365 puts it about 15 km south-east of the CBD; that figure was not cross-checked [7].
- Rent: 1–2-bed flats US$800–2,000/month (≈ €736–1,840); 3–4-bed houses US$2,500–5,000 (≈ €2,300–4,600) (Expat.com, 2025-12-12) [C cost.rent]. An aggregator (expatlife.ai) quoted in a search snippet gives Kabulonga and Roma 1-beds at US$500–700; the page itself returned 403 and its method is unknown, so treat it as low confidence [25].
- Getting around: Yango or Bolt, ZMW 20–50 a short trip (≈ €0.93–2.33); minibuses ZMW 6–15 on fixed routes into the four CBD terminals [C daily.transport]. No sourced commute time.
- Watch: on 14 Aug 2026 former minister Mutotwe Kafwaya was fatally shot during a police raid in Kabulonga, part of the post-election arrests (Wikipedia, 2026 election article) [D]. This is a political event, not street crime; no Kabulonga crime data was found.
- Places:

  - Melisa Supermarket, Chindo Road (supermarket) — Lusaka365 [7]; Melisa also named by Expat.com [C social.food]
  - Siesta by Mint, 4 Bishops Road (café) and AfricaWorks Kabulonga, "4 on Bishop" (coworking) — Lusaka365 [7]
  - The Quorum, corner of Kabulonga and Martin Luther King Roads (business club with coworking space, gym, rooftop bar, 7th Sense Grill House) — Lusaka365 [7][8]
  - 37d Gallery, 37d Middleway (gallery and café) — Lusaka365 [7]; Expat.com [C social.culture]
  - Dutch Reformed Church Craft Market, Kabulonga Road (monthly craft market; Lusaka365 says first Saturday, Wikivoyage says last Saturday) — [7][3]

New developments along Leopards Hill Road (Expat.com) [D]; Nkwashi new town about 36 km out on the same road, about 30 min to the CBD per its developer [C housing.areas].

### Kabwata and Chilenje
- Feel: Kabwata is a township split by Chilimbulu Road: Kabwata Estates (modern homes, a shopping mall, high-rise flats and preserved pre-colonial houses) to the north, New Kabwata (council houses and site-and-service plots) to the south (Wikipedia, edited 2026-10-30) [5]. Chilenje has colonial low-cost houses in New Chilenje and middle-cost houses in Chilenje South, with slightly higher education and income than Mtendere (Wikipedia, citing Africa Directions) [6]. Wikipedia also groups Chilenje among the unplanned settlements on the fringes [C housing.areas].
- Rent: no area figure found; use the local-market Numbeo averages, 1-bed suburbs US$121 (≈ €111), 3-bed suburbs US$745 (≈ €685) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: minibus ZMW 6–15 into the CBD terminals [C daily.transport]. No sourced time.
- Watch: the Health Minister linked Lusaka's cholera spread to informal settlements and sanitation gaps (Zambia Monitor, 2026-02-09) [C daily.water]; residents of Chilenje complained of long clinic queues in 2012 (Wikipedia, old) [6].
- Places:
  - Kabwata Cultural Village, Burma Road (preserved pre-colonial houses and crafts market, daily 08:00–18:00; bargaining expected) — Wikipedia [5]; Wikivoyage [3]; [C cost.negotiation]
  - Kabwata Market (market) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Kabwata Clinic (public clinic; a maternity annex was 90 % complete per a council update archived 2026-05-20) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Kabwata Primary School and St. Patrick's Girls Primary School (schools) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Chilenje House 394 (museum in a typical New Chilenje house) — Wikipedia [6]; Nkwazi lists "Chilenje House" as Kenneth Kaunda's former home [13]
  - Chilenje Clinic (public clinic) — Wikipedia [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" for readers who want to live among Zambian families; Q8 "Je reste en ville". Not for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" if an international school is needed (none sourced here).

Other areas named but not built out: Roma (family-friendly, expatriates and middle-income families, Roma Park mixed-use estate; Lusaka365) [12]; Makeni (Pick n Pay at Makeni Mall; Wikivoyage) [3]; Kalundu and Jesmondine (backpacker hostels; Wikivoyage) [3]. Mtendere saw 91 arrests on 9 Nov 2026 after a July–October wave of break-ins [C safety.general]; it was not considered as a base.

- Day 1: the one-day route above.

- Day 3, daily life: market morning at Soweto Market, Lumumba Road (opens 06:30) and Lusaka City Market [3]; then a minibus ride from one of the four CBD terminals (ZMW 6–15) during the 16:30–18:30 rush to feel the commute [C daily.transport].
