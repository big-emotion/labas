# Kitwe, Zambia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city: the dossier proposes Kitwe as the Copperbelt mining capital. Currency note: kwacha is converted at **1 EUR ≈ ZMW 21.5**, using ZMW 19.78 per USD (Rio Times, 2026-11-11) and the dossier's 1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR. ## Items

- [cost.rent] Expat.com says Kitwe rents are generally below Lusaka's and that mining companies often house their staff in company houses and compounds. It gives no figures. — Expat.com Kitwe guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Kitwe is one of seven of 16 JCTR-monitored districts whose August 2026 basket for a family of five was above the national average of K8,480.72 (≈ €394). Its exact total was not reported. — Zambia Monitor (JCTR), 2026-09-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Expat.com describes the cost of living as lower than in Lusaka, without Kitwe-specific prices. — Expat.com Kitwe guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fuel is priced uniformly nationwide; in November 2026 petrol cost K33.06/L (≈ €1.54) and diesel K34.45/L (≈ €1.60). — Energy Regulation Board pump price build-up, 2026-10-31, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Budget guesthouses charge K200–285 a night (≈ €9–13), from listings updated in 2023. — Wikivoyage "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Neighbourhoods include Parklands, Riverside, Nkana East, Nkana West, Garneton, Ndeke, Buchi, Chimwemwe, Kwacha, Mindolo, Wusakile, Chachacha and Race Course. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Expat.com says Kitwe mainly draws mining engineers, technicians and extractive-industry managers, and has an established international mining community. — Expat.com Kitwe guide, 2025-10-31, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Mining employers listed (as of 2019): Mopani's Nkana Cobalt Plant, the Konkola Copper Mines refinery, Sandvik Mining and several contractors. Nkana is called the deepest mine on the Copperbelt. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Mopani halted its South Ore Body shaft in Kitwe after an underground accident killed two contract workers on 13 Jan 2026. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-01-14, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] After two deaths in three weeks, Mopani suspended underground work by contractor Reliant Mining in Kitwe and Mufulira in February 2026 (headline and summary). — Zambia Monitor, 2026-02-03, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] A tailings dam at Sino-Metals Leach in Chambishi collapsed on 18 Feb 2025, sending acidic waste into the Mwambashi and then the Kafue River. A government-backed clean-up began in March 2026. Water and sediments carried heavy metals above safe levels. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-03-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] The FCDO, as reported by the Rio Times, advises against bathing in Copperbelt rivers or drinking untreated local water or local fish after the February 2025 heavy-metal discharge. — Rio Times, 2026-11-12, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Kitwe is 57.7 km from Ndola on a double carriageway (under an hour). The nearest airline airport is Ndola's, about 50 km away; Southdowns Airport has no scheduled flights and is due to go to the Air Force (announced April 2024). — Wikivoyage / Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Zambia Railways runs passenger trains from Livingstone via Lusaka. — Wikivoyage "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] 24-hour Visa/Mastercard ATMs: Ecobank (Mega Shopping Complex) and FNB. — Wikivoyage "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Hospitals include Kitwe Central, Wusakile Mine Hospital, Progress, Sinozam and Ndeke Village. Clinics include Hillview Medical Centre, Royal Medical Centre and Kitwe Polyclinic. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Copperbelt had only 2 of the 1,622 cases in the 2026 cholera outbreak, which ran from 28 July to late October. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-10-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Schools include Lechwe School (international, Cambridge), Kitwe International School (opened 2012), Mpelembe Secondary (Zambian and Cambridge) and Nkana Trust School. Wikipedia flags the education section as out of date. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Simba International School, a boarding school in Ndola, takes pupils from Kitwe. Copperbelt University (11,000+ students) is in town. — Expat.com Ndola/Kitwe guide; Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Rival taxi-driver factions rioted at Savage Yard bus station in the CBD on 19 Aug 2026 over control of the station; police arrested 14. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-08-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] During the July 2026 campaign, police opened seven cases of malicious damage to opposition vehicles at Sherbourne Hotel, Boma Street and near Mukuba Mall. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-07-25, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.women] The Copperbelt recorded 984 criminal gender-based-violence cases in July–September 2026. — Zambia Monitor (Zambia Police data), 2026-10-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] An immigration sweep on 13 Oct 2026 in Nkana East, Nkana West, Riverside, Ndeke and other areas stopped 67 people. 37 had valid papers, 5 had expired ones, and 24 (mostly Congolese and Tanzanian) had none. — Zambia Monitor, 2026-10-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.sport] Nkana FC, Power Dynamos and Kitwe United play at Arthur Davies Stadium; there is also Nkana Golf Club. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Malls include Mukuba Mall in Parklands (Game, Shoprite, Pick n Pay) and ECL Mall on Freedom Avenue (open 08:00–21:00). Chisokone and Obote Avenue are the markets, the latter selling copper crafts. — Wikivoyage / Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Restaurants include Arabian Nights, Mona Lisa (Italian, Parklands) and cheap Indian places with Sunday buffets (Dannys, Hotel Edinburgh, Havmore). — Wikivoyage "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Wikipedia estimates about 98.5% of residents are Christian, with Muslim and Hindu minorities; the figure is uncited. — Wikipedia "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Mindolo Dam (about 7 km; boating, swimming), Chembe Bird Sanctuary (about 20–28 km; malaria warning) and Chimfunshi chimp sanctuary (about 2 h away, roughly 130 chimps). — Wikipedia / Wikivoyage "Kitwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
