# Saint-Louis: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed CFA franc peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/senegal/saint-louis.md`; `[D]` = the Senegal dossier `research/countries/senegal.md`; `[Dakar C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/senegal/dakar.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. Almost every Saint-Louis price in the city file comes from one commercial publisher (OpenShores, 2026-03-10, low confidence); Wikivoyage EN restaurant and hotel prices are undated except where noted.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- No official advisory names the city for crime; the only city-level safety remark is a relocation blog saying a foreigner can walk alone without constant solicitation (low confidence) [C safety.general]. The orange zone in France Diplomatie covers the south of the Mauritania border in Matam department, not Saint-Louis [C safety.general].
- The sea on the Grande Côte is dangerous and often unsupervised, with drownings (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-29) [C safety.general].
- Heat: extreme March–June, often about 40°C, peaks of 45–47°C; many expats leave in those months; flood risk August–September [C climate.daily][C community.expat].
- Power and water cuts are described as more frequent than in Dakar; generator or inverter and a water tank strongly advised (OpenShores, low) [C daily.power].
- Jobs: Université Gaston Berger, a few NGOs, small tourism; remote work for a foreign employer described as effectively required [C work.jobs][C work.remote].
- Serious medical cases go to Dakar, 4–6 hours by road; there is no commercial flight and no passenger train [C health.care][1][2].
- Women: national figure only, 31% say harassment in public places is frequent (Afrobarometer, 2025-11-28) [C safety.women]. LGBT law of 30 March 2026 applies [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### The Island (Île de Saint-Louis, historic centre)
- Feel: the UNESCO-listed island of colonial houses (OpenShores) [C cost.rent]; a walkable historic centre with horse-drawn carriages for visitors [1]; the dossier calls the city slow, calm and intellectual [D]. Many guesthouses and restaurants sit on the river quays (Quai Roume, Quai Henri Jay) [1].
- Rent: furnished studio or 1-bed 80,000–150,000 XOF a month (≈ €120–230); furnished 2-bed 120,000–220,000 (≈ €185–335); a whole 3–4-bed colonial house 200,000–400,000 (≈ €305–610), a renovated house with river terrace up to 500,000 (≈ €760); OpenShores 2026-03-10, low confidence [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot; taxis 500 XOF standard, 600 from the bus garage or at night [1]; bicycle rental 5,000 XOF a day and scooter 10,000, corner of Rue Blanchot and Quai Roume [1]. The Faidherbe bridge links the island to Sor [1][3].
- Watch: on the island, pick an upper floor (OpenShores advice) [C housing.areas]; flood risk August–September [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Saint-Louis Cathedral (Catholic, opened 1828, described as the oldest functioning church in West Africa) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Musée de l'Aéropostale Jean Mermoz (2,000 XOF, Mon–Sat, next to the tourist office) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Exotica and D.S.K. Mini Prix, Rue Khalifa Ababacar Sy (supermarkets with Western foods) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Aux Délices du Fleuve, Rue Blanchot / Quai Roume (pastry shop, pastries from 400 XOF, Wi-Fi) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - La Linguère, 195 Rue Khalifa Ababacar (Senegalese dishes from 1,500 XOF, fish and meat from 2,500) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - La Kora chez Peggy, Avenue Blaise Diagne (French dishes from 3,000 XOF, beer 1,000, Wi-Fi, patio) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - École française Saint-Exupéry, Quai Roume (homologated, petite section to CM2; 6e–3e by CNED) — Le Petit Journal [8]
  - Syndicat d'Initiative / Office de Tourisme, Rue Milles Lacroix (walking-tour maps, guided tours) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Télétravail" and "Création, culture" (jazz festival, photo museum); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (colonial-era heritage).

### Sor (mainland)
- Feel: the mainland side across the Faidherbe bridge, with the city's largest market and the cheaper housing (OpenShores calls it the mainland side) [1][C cost.rent]. No dated description of who lives there was found.
- Rent: 1-bed 50,000–100,000 XOF (≈ €75–150); 2-bed 80,000–150,000 (≈ €120–230); small villa with garden 150,000–250,000 (≈ €230–380); OpenShores 2026-03-10, low [C cost.rent]. CoinAfrique listings for the city (2026-11-14): a 2-room rental at 45,000 XOF (≈ €69) and a furnished room at 12,000 (≈ €18), area not stated [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: car rapide up to about 100 XOF a trip, taxi 500 XOF [1]; the bus garage for Dakar is here (taxi 600 XOF from the garage to downtown) [1]; Dem Dikk's station is described as in the Pikine quarter [2]. Université Gaston Berger is about 10 km out on the N2; CROUS buses run to the centre for students [5].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced; city-wide flood season applies [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Sor market (largest in the city) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Sor central market prices: 25 kg rice 11,000–14,000 XOF, vegetables 200–600/kg (OpenShores, low) [C cost.living]
  - Daily market, Avenue Général de Gaulle (produce, fabric, second-hand clothes) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Village artisanal, Rue de la Corniche (crafts, bargaining) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Bar La Source, off Rue de la Corniche (garden, brochettes, cheap beer) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Sor's Catholic church (second church of the city, unnamed) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Centre Hospitalier Régional, Boulevard Abdoulaye Mar Diop (24/7) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; quarter not confirmed
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants" (villas with gardens); Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (towards the university and the hospital); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Guet Ndar (fishing quarter, Langue de Barbarie)
- Feel: the fishing district on the sandbar opposite the island, described as one of the densest places in the world; noisy and busy with boats, fish drying, carts and livestock, the muezzin setting the day's rhythm (Wikipedia, edited 2026-10-26) [4]. More than 25,000 people (Wikipedia FR Saint-Louis) [3].
- Rent: 1-bed 40,000–80,000 XOF (≈ €60–120), with OpenShores warning families against it because of flooding [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: walk or taxi from the island (500 XOF standard fare) [1].
- Watch: in March 2018 house walls collapsed and injured residents as the sea attacked the spit [4]; the 2003 breach of the Langue de Barbarie, opened to relieve river flooding, widened to 6 km by January 2020 [6]; Wikipedia cites the IPCC listing Saint-Louis among African sites at risk by 2100 [3]. Never rent a ground floor here (OpenShores) [C housing.areas].
- Places:
  - Guet N'Dar fishing beach and port (boat-building, fish smoking; landings priced at capitaine 1,500–3,000 XOF/kg, thiof 2,000–4,000) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Wikipedia [4]; [C cost.living]
  - Guet N'Dar market (smaller and calmer than Sor) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Fishermen's cemetery, past the village — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - École Cheikh Toure (primary school with a donated computer room) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: a place to visit and understand, not a base. Q3 "Serré" only for a single adult accepting the flood risk; not for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" [C cost.rent].

### Hydrobase (Langue de Barbarie, beach strip)
- Feel: the beach-hotel strip on the Langue de Barbarie, with the city's main swimming beach, popular with residents and visitors [1].
- Rent: no long-let figure found for Hydrobase. Hotels publish nightly rates (below).
- Getting around: on the Route de Hydrobase; taxi to the island at the 500 XOF standard fare (assumption: within the town fare) [1].
- Watch: plastic waste reported on the beach [1]; the Grande Côte sea is dangerous and often unsupervised [C safety.general]; the spit is eroding (see Guet Ndar) [6].
- Places:
  - Plage Hydrobase (main swimming beach) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Hotel Mermoz, Route de Hydrobase (spa, pool, childcare, horse riding 5,000 XOF; rooms from 13,200 XOF low season) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Hotel Diamarek, beachfront (rooms 27,500 to bungalows 67,500 XOF) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Hotel Dior, Route de Hydrobase (single 27,600, double 40,600 XOF with breakfast) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Auberge du Pélican (in disrepair as of late January 2026 per Wikivoyage) — [1]; do not rely on it
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (hotel base while flat-hunting); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Considered and set aside
- **Sanar / Université Gaston Berger**: about 10 km out on the N2, campus of 250 ha with CROUS housing and buses (Wikipedia, edited 2026-10-30) [5]. Relevant for lecturers (Q5 "Santé, enseignement") but no off-campus housing or places were found.
- **Gandiol**: the village area by the Langue de Barbarie park, 18–20 km south [1][2]; used below for outings, not as a base.

## Living: typical weeks

  - Fri: home office; lunch at La Linguère (from 1,500 XOF) [1].
  - Sat: Sor market morning [1]; Saturday night below.

- Saturday night:

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best from November to April (Djoudj season; before the spring heat) [C nature.access][C climate.daily]. The Jazz Festival runs in late May (34th edition 13–17 May 2026) [1][D].

### One day
- Morning: guided walk or carriage tour of the island from the Syndicat d'Initiative (1h30–3h; two routes: "the two islands" or the fishermen's village) [2]; Musée Jean Mermoz (2,000 XOF, Mon–Sat) [1][2]; the Cathedral [1].
- Midday: lunch at La Linguère (from 1,500 XOF) [1].
- Afternoon: MuPho photography museum (1,000 XOF, Tue–Sun) [2]; then cross on foot or by taxi (500 XOF) to Guet Ndar for the late-afternoon boat landings and the fishermen's cemetery [1]. Ask before photographing people (assumption from general practice; the Mbour page gives this advice for fish markets).
- Evening: dinner aboard the Bou El Mogdad if docked (jazz on Saturdays) or at La Kora chez Peggy [1]; late drinks at L'embuscade or Iguane Café [1].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from Dakar (sept-place about 5,000 XOF, 4–6 h) [1]; evening on the quays.
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): Parc national des oiseaux du Djoudj, about 60 km north-east (16,000 ha UNESCO site, nearly 3 million birds; pirogue trip 15,000–30,000 XOF), best with Sahel Découverte or another operator [2][7][C cost.living][1].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): Sor market at opening, then the daily market on Avenue Général de Gaulle and a car rapide back (up to 100 XOF) [1]: the city as residents shop and commute.
- Day 5 (trip out): Langue de Barbarie national park via Gandiol (Ramsar site since 2021; boat tours) [1][6]; Réserve de Guembeul on the way back [1][2].
- Day 6: Hydrobase beach (no swimming far out; Grande Côte currents) [1][C safety.general]; evening at Iguane Café [1].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathedral, or the Grande Mosquée from outside (colonial style, clock and bell on its minarets) [1]; Institut français if open [1]. Return to Dakar.
