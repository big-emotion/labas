# Saint-Louis, Senegal

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed CFA franc peg), rounded. Most city-specific prices come from one commercial relocation blog (OpenShores); treat them as indicative. Listing prices are asking prices seen on 2026-11-14.

## Items

- [cost.rent] On the UNESCO-listed island: furnished studio or 1-bed 80,000–150,000 XOF/month (≈ €120–230); furnished 2-bed 120,000–220,000 (≈ €185–335). — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Saint-Louis", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Island houses: a whole 3–4-bed colonial house 200,000–400,000 XOF/month (≈ €305–610); a renovated house with river terrace up to 500,000 (≈ €760); one room in an 18th-century house quoted at 150,000. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Sor (mainland side): 1-bed 50,000–100,000 XOF (≈ €75–150); 2-bed 80,000–150,000 (≈ €120–230); small villa with garden 150,000–250,000 (≈ €230–380). — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Guet Ndar (Langue de Barbarie fishing district): 1-bed 40,000–80,000 XOF (≈ €60–120), with a warning against it for families because of flooding. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Another page of the same publisher gives a 2-room flat at 50,000–110,000 XOF (≈ €75–170); a third gives a 2-bed in a decent area at 80,000–150,000 (≈ €120–230). — OpenShores "Coût de la vie au Sénégal", 2026-04-30; "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Asking rents on a classifieds portal: a 2-room "chambres" rental at 45,000 XOF/month (≈ €69, posted about a month ago); a furnished room at 12,000 (≈ €18, posted ~10 months ago). — CoinAfrique Sénégal, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Asking sale prices: villas at 22 million XOF (≈ €33,500, leasehold "bail"), 55 million (≈ €83,800) and 60 million (≈ €91,500); a 9-room villa at 230 million (≈ €350,600); a building at 110 million. — CoinAfrique Sénégal, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Asking prices for building plots vary widely: 400 m² at 10 million XOF (≈ €15,200); 270 m² at 4 million (≈ €6,100); two 300 m² plots at 4.5 million and 25 million (≈ €6,900 vs €38,100). — CoinAfrique Sénégal, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fresh fish at the Guet Ndar landings: capitaine 1,500–3,000 XOF/kg (≈ €2.30–4.60); thiof 2,000–4,000; shrimp 2,000–5,000; smoked fish 1,000–2,000. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Sor central market: 25 kg local rice 11,000–14,000 XOF (≈ €17–21); vegetables 200–600/kg; fruit 200–500/kg; baguette 150–200; whole chicken 3,000–4,500 (≈ €4.60–6.90). — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Single person's local-market food budget 35,000–60,000 XOF/month (≈ €55–90); imported European goods (cheese, cold cuts, wine) are rare and pricey, people stock up in Dakar. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Eating out: neighbourhood thiéboudienne 1,500–2,500 XOF (≈ €2.30–3.80); guesthouse meal 5,000–15,000; tourist restaurant on the island 8,000–20,000 per person (≈ €12–30); café or bakery breakfast 500–1,500. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Utilities: electricity with air-con 20,000–50,000 XOF/month (≈ €30–76; air-con described as nearly essential March–June); water 3,000–10,000; 4G mobile internet 15,000–25,000 (≈ €23–38). — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Sample monthly budgets: single retiree on the island 280,000 XOF (≈ €427); "comfortable remote worker" 495,000 (≈ €755); well-off retired couple in a colonial house 660,000 (≈ €1,006). — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A sister page gives a full monthly budget of 200,000–400,000 XOF (≈ €305–610) and says someone earning €800–1,000 a month can live very comfortably. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Saint-Louis", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A third page of the same publisher gives a much lower total monthly budget, 140,000–240,000 XOF (≈ €215–365). — OpenShores, 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Leisure: guided tour of the island 5,000–15,000 XOF; Djoudj bird-park pirogue trip 15,000–30,000 (≈ €23–46); jazz-festival concerts 5,000–20,000 (many street concerts free); Wolof or kora lessons 10,000–20,000/month. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Estimated average formal-sector salary in Saint-Louis: 120,000–180,000 XOF/month (≈ €185–275), against 280,000–350,000 in Dakar; source not named. — Les Afriques, 2026-06-08 (mod. 2026-11-12), local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] In-town taxi 500–1,500 XOF, with the advice to negotiate before the ride; moto-taxi (Jakarta) short trip 500–1,000 XOF. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Advice: never rent a ground floor in low-lying zones, especially Guet Ndar; on the historic island pick an upper floor. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Local jobs are limited to the Université Gaston Berger (lecturers), a few NGOs and small tourism (guesthouses, guides); no multinationals, big NGOs, oil or banking employers. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] GTA offshore gas, ITIE 2024 report: the region generated 39.6 billion XOF (8.7% of national extractive revenue); companies bought 7.2 billion from local suppliers vs 23.8 billion abroad; the sector employs 207 people in the region, which officials call insufficient. — Le Soleil, 2026-06-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.remote] Remote work for a foreign employer is described as effectively required for anyone who needs a salary. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Electricity (Senelec) and water cuts are described as more frequent than in Dakar; a generator or inverter and a water tank are strongly advised. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Outside Dakar, 4G is available in the main cities but fibre is mainly in Dakar and parts of Thiès; the Saint-Louis budget assumes 4G "plus fibre if available". — OpenShores, 2026-04-30 and 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] To Dakar (about 340 km): car rapide or Ndiaga Ndiaye 3,000–5,000 XOF per person (≈ €4.60–7.60), 5–6 hours; private car with driver 25,000–40,000; the same publisher elsewhere says about 4 h 30 by road. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] No functional international airport; the local airport has only irregular links; a basic bicycle (50,000–100,000 XOF) is suggested for getting around. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] GP consultation 5,000–15,000 XOF (≈ €8–23), specialist 10,000–25,000; the regional hospital handles routine care and emergencies "free or very cheap"; serious cases go to Dakar. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] École française Saint-Exupéry: homologated French school from petite section to CM2, with CNED distance learning for 6e–3e; there is no AEFE lycée in the city. — lepetitjournal.com Dakar, updated 2026-06-07, intl-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The relocation blog says a foreigner can walk alone without being constantly approached; it gives no crime figures. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] On the Grande Côte (northern Dakar suburbs to south of Saint-Louis) the sea is dangerous and often unsupervised, with drownings; the beach drive Dakar–Saint-Louis needs a qualified guide because of tides. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-29, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Remote areas near the Mauritanian and Malian borders call for particular care (JNIM active in Mali); France Diplomatie's orange zone covers the south of the Mauritania border in Matam department, not Saint-Louis city. — GOV.UK FCDO regional risks, accessed 2026-11-14; France Diplomatie, zones 2026-03-12, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] National figure: 31% of Senegalese say women are often or always sexually harassed in markets, streets or transport (no city breakdown). — Afrobarometer AD1086, 2025-11-28, academic, confidence: high
- [social.culture] 33rd Saint-Louis Jazz Festival, 28 May–1 June 2025 on Place Baya: more than 15 concerts and 30 artists. — au-senegal.com, 2025-05-03, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.pace] Residents describe teranga hospitality and direct, informal social relations. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [community.expat] Many expatriates leave between March and June because of the heat. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: low
- [community.expat] A French consular agency is listed in Saint-Louis by one source; France Diplomatie's own page lists honorary consuls only in Cap-Skirring and Tambacounda. — au-senegal.com, 2026-10-08; France Diplomatie, 2026-09-29, local-media, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Extreme heat March–June, often about 40°C, peaks of 45–47°C during the March–April harmattan; air-con needed day and night; flood risk in August–September. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, company, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Djoudj bird sanctuary is about 60 km away, busiest November–April; pirogue trips to the Langue de Barbarie cost 5,000–15,000 XOF. — OpenShores, 2026-03-10, company, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] National law of 30 March 2026: 5–10 years for same-sex relations and new offences for "promotion, support or funding"; enforced with arrests. — GOV.UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14; France Diplomatie, 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
