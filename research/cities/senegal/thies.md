- [cost.living] The same page's FAQ says a single person living simply needs 300,000–400,000 XOF/month in secondary cities — higher than its own Thiès budget above. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Estimated average formal-sector salary in Thiès: 150,000–220,000 XOF/month (≈ €230–335), against 280,000–350,000 in Dakar; source not named. — Les Afriques, 2026-06-08 (mod. 2026-11-12), local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Shared "sept-places" taxi Dakar–Thiès about 1,500–2,500 XOF (≈ €2.30–3.80). — OpenShores "Coût de la vie au Sénégal", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Thousands commute from Thiès to Dakar each morning; a commuter says "we never know what time we'll arrive"; high transport costs weigh on daily life. — AFD, 2026-06-17, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] No passenger train has served Thiès since the 1990s; the TER's third phase (Dakar–Thiès) is planned with Alstom trains and Seter (SNCF subsidiary) as operator. — AFD, 2026-06-17, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] On 24 July 2026 the transport ministry, Senter, AFD and the EU signed a memorandum for €10 million of studies on the TER extension to Thiès; no construction date or fares announced. — Lejecos via AllAfrica, 2026-07-24, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] CETUD is preparing a sustainable urban mobility plan for Thiès, with an initial €20–30 million investment and the challenge of integrating informal transport. — AFD, 2026-06-17, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Fibre is mainly in Dakar "and in some areas of Thiès"; 4G in the main cities. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Textile firm NSTS has operated since 1991 with about 200 staff; a local entrepreneur says industry is gradually moving to Thiès because Dakar is saturated and expensive. — AFD, 2026-06-17, official, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The urban area has nearly 400,000 residents and sits about 60 km from Dakar (other sources say 70 km). — AFD, 2026-06-17; OpenShores, 2026-04-30, official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] École française Dr René Guillet: AEFE-conventionné, homologated from TPS to CM2, with CNED distance learning from 6e to terminale. — lepetitjournal.com Dakar, updated 2026-06-07, intl-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] A diphtheria outbreak declared 4 Sept 2026 mainly affects Kédougou, Dakar and Thiès; vaccination check advised. — France Diplomatie, posted 2026-09-21, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Road accidents are very frequent nationally and intercity travel at night is discouraged (no lighting, pedestrians on roads) — relevant for Dakar–Thiès commuters. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-29, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] National figure: 31% of Senegalese say women are often or always sexually harassed in markets, streets or transport (no city breakdown). — Afrobarometer AD1086, 2025-11-28, academic, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] National law of 30 March 2026: 5–10 years for same-sex relations and new offences for "promotion, support or funding"; enforced with arrests. — GOV.UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
