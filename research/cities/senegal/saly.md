# Saly, Senegal

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed CFA franc peg), rounded. Most Saly prices come from one commercial relocation blog (OpenShores) and are indicative only. Listing prices are asking prices seen on 2026-11-14; the portal does not say whether rents are monthly. Saly (Saly-Portudal) sits next to Mbour on the Petite Côte, about 80 km from Dakar.
