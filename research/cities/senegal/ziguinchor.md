- [work.jobs] The SONACOS plant resumed industrial activity in 2025, helping the regional economy; the cashew campaign went smoothly though processors want bigger quotas; good rains and timely farm inputs. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Described as enclaved: reaching Dakar means flying or taking the ferry (no fares given). — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Air Sénégal has served Ziguinchor since November 2018 and Transair flies several times a week to Ziguinchor and Cap Skirring; domestic links are run by small carriers and are not regular. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-29, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Announced works: a second bridge in the Ziguinchor area and rehabilitation of the Ziguinchor–Cap Skirring road; the Tilène market, burnt in 2018, was handed over but is not yet fully running. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Outside Dakar, 4G covers the main cities and fibre is mainly in Dakar and parts of Thiès. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-30, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] Health workers joined several national strikes in 2025, disrupting services in local facilities. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A university study cited by Au Sénégal finds Casamance's local healthcare short of needs; urgent or specialist cases are referred to Oussouye, Ziguinchor or Dakar. — au-senegal.com, 2026-10-08, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] École française François Rabelais (AEFE partner): homologated from petite section to CM2, CNED for 6e–3e. — lepetitjournal.com Dakar, updated 2026-06-07, intl-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] First stone laid at end-2025 for the LYNAQE high school in Sindone (Adéane), estimated at 10 billion XOF; 2025 baccalauréat results fell short of expectations. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie: Casamance's borders with The Gambia (off the main roads to Ziguinchor via Bignona) and the strip south of Ziguinchor towards Guinea-Bissau (off the main road) are advised against except for imperative reasons; mines remain, stay on tarred roads, forests strongly discouraged. — France Diplomatie, zones updated 2026-03-12, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The rest of Casamance is a "heightened vigilance" zone: prefer the main axes (Diouloulou–Bignona–Ziguinchor; Sénoba–Bignona–Ziguinchor–Cap Skirring), avoid travelling alone; the coast–Ziguinchor–Cap Skirring–river triangle stays open and the Cap Skirring hotel zone has no particular danger. — France Diplomatie, 2026-03-12, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] FCDO: decades of low-intensity separatist violence, mostly aimed at security forces; risk of clashes near the Gambian and Bissau borders, lower in tourist areas; avoid night driving and stick to main roads. — GOV.UK FCDO regional risks, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In April 2025 armed groups infiltrated the Djignaki area (Bignona department); army sweeps followed and a soldier was held hostage for several months before release. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] A Konrad-Adenauer-backed study presented in Ziguinchor describes "neither peace nor war": mines have caused 870 victims since 1982 (186 civilians); 2.2 million m² cleared since 2008, 1.3 million m² left, with 42 dangerous zones in Bignona department. — Senego, 2026-09-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.women] A series of assaults on women in Kafountine in 2025 caused outrage and a mobilisation of Casamance women demanding firm action. — Sud Quotidien via AllAfrica, 2026-01-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.returnee] Per the state reintegration agency ANRAC, 203 of the 244 villages emptied by the conflict have been repopulated, but returns are not yet durable; many children born in exile lack birth certificates. — Senego, 2026-09-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] Casamance property is attracting European buyers and diaspora members (per the PAP property site, as reported). — au-senegal.com, 2026-10-08, local-media, confidence: low
- [community.expat] France has an honorary consul in Cap-Skirring (the nearest French consular presence; the consulate general is in Dakar). — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-29, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Ziguinchor is described as very green and very cheap; Cap Skirring as less urban with more vegetation but fewer services. — OpenShores, 2026-04-30; au-senegal.com, 2026-10-08, company, confidence: low
- [rights.lgbt] National law of 30 March 2026: 5–10 years for same-sex relations and new offences for "promotion, support or funding"; enforced with arrests. — GOV.UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
