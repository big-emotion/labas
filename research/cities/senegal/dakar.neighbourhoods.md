# Dakar: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed CFA franc peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/senegal/dakar.md`; `[D]` = the Senegal dossier `research/countries/senegal.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. OpenShores and Numbeo figures are commercial or crowd-sourced and flagged low confidence in the city file; they are used here only where nothing better exists.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- FCDO (accessed 2026-11-14): robbery, burglary and assault increasingly common in Dakar, sometimes armed; hotels used by foreigners have been robbed at gunpoint; bag-snatching rising after dark [C safety.general]. France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-29): muggings day and night, pedestrian attacks on the **Corniche** after nightfall, permanent guarding advised at home [C safety.general]. Crime spikes before Tabaski and Korité [C safety.general].
- Protests are frequent, can turn violent and block the airport road; foreigners have been prosecuted for attending unapproved ones (FCDO) [C safety.general].
- Power cuts returned in September 2026 (works cuts 8:00–16:00 in named areas; residents describe night cuts) [C daily.power]. Plan an inverter wherever you live.
- Rainy season July–October: violent storms and sudden flooding; do not drive during or right after storms [C climate.daily]; avoid ground floors in low-lying suburbs [D].
- Swimming: Wikivoyage FR (beach entries updated June 2026, citing an RFI report of 2026-04-24) discourages swimming at Yoff, Ngor island and around Hann [2]; Yoff beach is "often closed" for rubbish and sewage, with drowning risk (EN, notes of Dec 2024) [1].
- Women: 31% of Senegalese say women are often or always harassed in public places and transport (Afrobarometer, 2025-11-28) [C safety.women].
- Rush hours 7:00–9:00 and 17:00–19:30 [C daily.transport]. No door-to-door commute time between neighbourhoods was found in a dated source; trips below give fares, not minutes.
- LGBT residents: the law of 30 March 2026 (5–10 years) is enforced with arrests [C rights.lgbt]. Nothing here is a "safe area".

## Neighbourhoods

### Almadies and Ngor (incl. the Virage)
- Feel: the expat and high-budget end of the city: Le Petit Journal (2024-05-22) calls Almadies very secure, full of shops and schools and among the most expensive areas, while Ngor and the Virage sit in its "more affordable" group [9][D]. Westernmost tip of mainland Africa, seafood restaurants on the point [1]. Returnee founders profiled by Le Soleil run a civic-tech NGO based in Ngor (2025-07-21) [C community.returnee]. The dossier places the club scene here (older travel guides, flagged) [D].
- Rent: Almadies/Ngor 7,000–18,000 XOF per m² a month (≈ €11–27), au-senegal.com 2026-04-02 [C cost.rent]; furnished studio or 1-bed 250,000–400,000 XOF (≈ €380–610), furnished 2-bed 400,000–650,000 (≈ €610–990), OpenShores 2026-03-10, low confidence [C cost.rent]; a studio in Ngor asked 180,000 XOF (≈ €275) on CoinAfrique, 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; luxury Almadies homes 2–5 million XOF before the 2024–2026 fall of 10–30% in the upper market [C cost.rent]. Upscale homes now sit empty about 8 months, so tenants negotiate harder (2026-10-14) [C cost.negotiation].
- Getting around: an agreed taxi from Plateau costs 2,000–3,000 XOF (≈ €3–4.60), 20–50% more at night, in rain or at rush hour (Expat.com, 2026-03-24) [C cost.negotiation]. Yango 10–15 km 2,000–4,000 XOF (OpenShores) [C daily.transport]. Dem Dikk line 1 runs along the VDN and line 10 along the Corniche Ouest, 150 XOF (Wikivoyage, Dec 2024) [1].
- Watch: Almadies was on the Senelec list of scheduled 8:00–16:00 works cuts in September 2026 (Senego, 2026-09-18) [C daily.power]. At the Pointe des Almadies beach, guards at building sites may ask for an illegitimate "fee" (Wikivoyage, Dec 2024) [1].
- Places:
  - Pointe des Almadies and its beach (westernmost point, seafood restaurants) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Le N'Gor, Corniche des Almadies (open-air seafood restaurant, about 10,000 XOF for a meal with beer and dessert) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Île de Ngor (beach, bars, eateries; boat about 500 XOF, 5 min, from the Embarcadère) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; surf spots, swimming discouraged (June 2026) [2]
  - Plage du Virage (rocky, surfing for all levels, bar nearby) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - École française des Almadies, sea front (AEFE-homologated nursery, bilingual option) — Le Petit Journal [10]
  - Regus Ngor Virage, Diama Les Almadies, Rue NG 233 no. 55, Route de l'aéroport (coworking; open-space desk from 125,900 XOF a month per person, private office from 156,500) — Regus listing seen only in a search-result summary [13]; confirm before use
  - Sao Brasil, Ngor (pizza) and Mogador, Almadies (upscale) — Wikivoyage FR, entries of Feb 2018 (OLD) [2]
  - Hôtel Sunugal, Ngor-Almadies (62 € a double, pool, private beach) — Wikivoyage FR, 2018 (OLD) [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage" (surf, Ngor island, with the swimming caveat); Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats"; Q5 "Télétravail" (coworking, to be confirmed); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Mermoz and Sacré-Cœur (commune of Mermoz-Sacré-Cœur)
- Feel: about 26,600 residents in 2007 on 6 km², with most Senegalese groups plus Cape Verdeans, Guineans and Beninese; Wikipedia (edited 2024-09-27) notes very poor residents living beside wealthy villa owners and a saturated centre [3]. Le Petit Journal (2024-05-22): Mermoz suits families on a moderate budget; Sacré-Cœur is calm, affordable, well served by public transport and close to universities, bars and clubs [9].
- Rent: Mermoz 3,000–7,000 XOF per m² (≈ €5–11), Sacré-Cœur 3,000–6,500 (≈ €5–10), au-senegal.com 2026-04-02 [C cost.rent]; 1-bed 180,000–280,000 XOF (≈ €275–425), 2-bed 280,000–450,000 (≈ €425–685), OpenShores 2026-03-10, low confidence [C cost.rent]; top of the range: a 4-room flat in Mermoz-Sacré-Cœur asked 1,300,000 XOF (≈ €1,980) and a Mermoz flat 900,000 (≈ €1,370), CoinAfrique 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Dakar Dem Dikk has its headquarters in the south of the commune [3]; buses 150–250 XOF, "Tata" minibuses under 200 (Expat.com, 2026-03-24) [C daily.transport]; Yango 3–5 km 1,000–1,800 XOF [C daily.transport]. No sourced time to Plateau.
- Watch: Cité Mermoz 1 was on the September 2026 scheduled-cut list [C daily.power].
- Places:
  - Grande mosquée de Mermoz (and mosques at Sacré-Cœur 3, Sacré-Cœur VDN, Sicap Karack) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Saint Pierre des Baobabs (Catholic parish founded 1960) and the Sacré-Cœur church — Wikipedia [3]
  - École Actuelle Bilingue: primary at Fenêtre Mermoz, collège at Sacré-Cœur 3 (MLF network, primary homologated) — Le Petit Journal [10]
  - Lycée public Galandou Diouf — Wikipedia [3]

  - Sonatel headquarters, Cité Keur Gorgui (major employer) — Wikipedia [3]
  - BD Passion Dakar, Sicap Karack (comics and manga shop) — Wikivoyage FR [2]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants" (bilingual school, calm streets per [9]); Q3 "Moyen"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (Sonatel, central); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Point E and Fann (commune of Fann-Point E-Amitié)

### Ouakam and Les Mamelles
- Feel: a former Lebou fishing village turned residential suburb, still led by a traditional Jaraaf and council of notables, with a strong military presence (army camp, gendarmerie school, air base) (Wikipedia, edited 2026-11-03, sources tagged insufficient) [4]. Le Petit Journal: fairly calm, popular with students, many leisure options, close to the Corniche beaches [9]. A broker's list in a public expat group on 2026-11-14 is dominated by Ouakam rooms and flats [C cost.rent].
- Rent: Ouakam/Mamelles 2,800–8,000 XOF per m² (≈ €4–12), au-senegal.com 2026-04-02 [C cost.rent]; broker asking prices 2026-11-14: room with bathroom at Ouakam market 80,000–85,000 XOF (≈ €122–130), mini-studio 125,000 (≈ €191), studio at Cité Avion 175,000 (≈ €267), 2-bed 225,000–250,000 (≈ €343–381) [C cost.rent]; 2-room flat in Ouakam 210,000 (≈ €320) and in Mamelles 500,000 (≈ €760), CoinAfrique 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: agreed taxi Plateau–Mamelles 1,500–2,000 XOF (Expat.com, 2026-03-24) [C cost.negotiation]; Dem Dikk line 10 follows the Corniche Ouest [1].
- Watch: in November 2025 a man posing as a Yango driver picked up a student in Mamelles and committed an obscene act during the ride; he was charged (Dakaractu, 2025-11-11) [C safety.women]. The Corniche after dark is France Diplomatie's named pedestrian hotspot [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Mosquée de la Divinité (1992–1997) and the Ouakam beach below it (right-hand wave for experienced surfers; paddle when flat) — Wikipedia [4]; Wikivoyage FR [2]
  - Monument de la Renaissance africaine (outside free; view and exhibition 6,500 XOF) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Wikipedia [4]
  - Phare des Mamelles (lighthouse, views, restaurant) and Plage des Mamelles (sunset, big waves) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Loman Art Gallery, Villa no. 1, rue de Pharmacie, Mamelles (free, daily 10:00–18:00) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Lycée français Jean Mermoz, Route de Ouakam (AEFE direct management, CP–Terminale) — Le Petit Journal [10]; address "Route Ouakam 99" in the school's 2026 application form, seen via search summary [14]
  - Spaces, Immeuble Futura, Route de la Corniche Ouest (coworking; dedicated desk from 3,500 XOF per person per day, meeting room from 10,900 XOF an hour, prices "from", read 2026-11-14) — spacesworks.com [12]

### Yoff (incl. Yoff-Tonghor, Ouest Foire, Nord Foire)
- Feel: Dakar's largest commune, a Lebou fishing village founded in 1432, run partly by its own traditional bodies and shaped by the Layène brotherhood; Yoff-Tonghor towards the Virage is popular with expatriates; Djily Mbaye and Yoff BCEAO are coastal residential areas; Nord and Ouest Foire are the fair quarter (Wikipedia, edited 2025-05-01) [5]. Le Petit Journal: seafront, low rents, very popular with tight-budget expats [9].
- Rent: Yoff/Djily Mbaye 3,000–6,000 XOF per m² (≈ €5–9), Ouest/Nord Foire 2,800–5,000 (≈ €4–8), au-senegal.com 2026-04-02 [C cost.rent]; Yoff/Grand Yoff 1-bed 70,000–130,000 XOF (≈ €105–200), OpenShores, low confidence [C cost.rent]; 4-room flat in Sicap Foire 350,000 (≈ €535), CoinAfrique 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: borders the old Léopold Sédar Senghor airport [5]; the Dem Dikk airport bus to AIBD leaves from HLM Grand Yoff roughly hourly 06:00–21:00, 6,000 XOF (Dec 2024) [1].
- Watch: after the rains of 15 August 2025 the sanitation office pumped standing water in Yoff, and a pump and generator were installed at the Philippe Maguilène Senghor health centre (Le Soleil, 2025-08-18) [11]. Ouest-Foire was on the September 2026 scheduled-cut list [C daily.power]. Yoff beach often closed for rubbish and sewage (Dec 2024) [1].
- Places:
  - Mausolée de Seydina Limamou Laye (major Layène pilgrimage site) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Plage de Yoff / Plage BCEAO (kitesurf December–March; swimming discouraged) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Centre de santé Philippe Maguilène Senghor (public health centre) — Le Soleil [11]
  - CPSML (Yoff's first private school, 1988) and École El Hadji Abdou Seck ("École 4") — Wikipedia [5]
  - Auberge Le Poulagou, Yoff Tonghor Plage (guesthouse about 2 km from the old airport; from 16 € in 2018, OLD) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" and "Moyen"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (cheap first base); Q7 "Prend son temps" (village fabric, inference from [5]); Q8 "À la plage" (kitesurf, not swimming).

### Plateau (city centre)
- Feel: the administrative and business centre: presidency, National Assembly, ministries, BCEAO, embassies, banks and head offices; congested, with Sandaga and Kermel markets (Wikipedia, edited 2026-10-12) [8]. About 34,700 residents (2013) [8].
- Rent: 6,000–15,000 XOF per m² (≈ €9–23), au-senegal.com 2026-04-02 [C cost.rent]; upscale Almadies/Plateau/Mermoz 500,000–1,000,000 XOF (≈ €760–1,525), OpenShores 2026-04-30, low confidence [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the TER leaves from the central station towards Diamniadio and AIBD (4,000 XOF 2nd class to the airport since 12 Oct 2026; last train 22:00) [C daily.transport][1]; Gorée ferry terminal north of Place de l'Indépendance [1]. The BRT's 18 km line starts by the Grand Mosque area per Wikivoyage [1]; city file: 18.3 km Petersen–Guédiawaye, 400–500 XOF [C daily.transport].
- Watch: Sandaga's sidewalks are taken by vendors, cars and moto-taxi garages, pushing pedestrians into traffic on avenues Blaise Diagne, Jean Jaurès and Lamine Gueye (Le Soleil, 2026-09-28) [C safety.general]; Wikivoyage calls Sandaga notorious for pickpockets [1].
- Places:
  - Marché Sandaga (corner of Rue Sandinière and Av. Lamine Gueye) and Marché Kermel — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Wikipedia [8]
  - Hôpital Principal de Dakar (military, open to civilians) — Wikipedia [8]; France Diplomatie emergency list [C health.care]
  - Cathédrale du Souvenir africain (1929) and the Grande Mosquée de Dakar — Wikipedia [8]
  - Institut français Léopold Sédar Senghor, 89 rue Joseph Gomis (daily 09:00–20:00; theatre, library, garden bar-restaurant) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Eric Kayser, Boulevard de la République (bakery-café) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Chez Loutcha, 101 rue Moussé Diop (Cape Verdean and Senegalese) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Institution Sainte Jeanne d'Arc, Av. Lamine Guèye (AEFE partner, CP–Terminale) and École Aimé Césaire, rue de Thann — Le Petit Journal [10]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (ministries, banks, embassies); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis". Few newcomers live here; it is where many work.

### Parcelles Assainies
- Feel: about 200,000 residents on 4 km² (49,140 per km²), "very cosmopolitan" and commercial, organised in twenty Unités (Wikipedia, edited 2026-09-23) [7].
- Rent: Parcelles/HLM 1-bed 60,000–120,000 XOF (≈ €90–185); studio or room in Grand-Yoff/Parcelles 50,000–100,000 (≈ €75–150), OpenShores 2026-03-10 and 2026-04-30, low confidence [C cost.rent]. Small units rose 15–20% between 2024 and 2026 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: a Dem Dikk terminus (the Dakar–Banjul bus leaves from it at 07:00) [1]; Dem Dikk 150–250 XOF, cars rapides 100–400 [C daily.transport].
- Watch: after the 15 August 2025 rains, pumps ran at Unités 13, 22, 24 and 25 (Le Soleil, 2025-08-18) [11]. None of the public health posts has a working maternity (Wikipedia) [7].
- Places:
  - Four main markets at Unités 11, 14, 17 and 20 — Wikipedia [7]
  - Centre de santé, Unité 17, and Clinique Yacine, Unité 17 (private) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Parcelles Dem Dikk terminus — Wikivoyage EN [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q4 "Seul·e" on a tight budget. Few sourced leisure places.

### Considered and set aside
- **Grand Yoff and Diamaguène**: Le Petit Journal (2024-05-22) says they have a reputation for crime and insecurity and lists them as areas to avoid [9]; Grand Yoff was among areas with weather-related deaths in 2025 (search-result summary of Seneweb, not opened). Not proposed.
- **Sicap / Liberté / Amitié**: 3,000–6,500 XOF per m² [C cost.rent]; a 3-bed flat in Liberté 400,000 XOF and a 2-bed in Liberté 4 near IPG 280,000 (2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; Liberté 6 Extension on the September 2026 cut list [C daily.power]. Too few sourced places for a block.
- **Médina / Gueule Tapée**: cheapest per m² (2,000–4,500 XOF) [C cost.rent]; broker rooms near Soumbédioune at 125,000 "négociable" and a mini-studio in Gueule Tapée at 115,000 (2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. Places not researched beyond Marché Soumbédioune (fish market 08:00–19:00, dead link noted) [1].
- **Hann Maristes**: 2,500–6,000 XOF per m² [C cost.rent]; Cours Sainte-Marie de Hann (AEFE partner, nursery to higher education) [10]; Hann Bel-Air residents were evacuated by Zodiac in a flood (Senego, date not confirmed, search summary only). Kept for the family profile as a school, not as a base.

## Living: typical weeks

- Evening: sunset at Plage des Mamelles or the Phare [1][2], dinner at Le N'Gor on the Corniche des Almadies (about 10,000 XOF) [1]; Just 4 U for live music late [1]. Ride back by app [C daily.transport].
