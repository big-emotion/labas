# Saly: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed CFA franc peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/senegal/saly.md`; `[D]` = the Senegal dossier `research/countries/senegal.md`; `[Dakar C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/senegal/dakar.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. Nearly all Saly prices come from one commercial publisher (OpenShores, 2026-03-10, low confidence); Wikivoyage's Mbour fares and hotel prices carry no date.

Saly (Saly-Portudal) is a resort commune of 41,811 residents (2023 census) built from 1984 next to an old trading post, about 80 km south of Dakar and next to Mbour [3][2]. French Wikipedia counts 17 hotels, 33 residences and about 10,000 beds, and 15 quarters [2].

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-29) names the Somone/Saly-to-Joal coast as a place where assaults "are not rare", some violent, notably in burglaries of rental villas [C safety.general].
- Local press: a series of burglaries and murders worried residents and a German resident was robbed in daylight (Dakaractu, c. 2025-03-03); pickpocketing and aggressive soliciting keep visitors in at night and a French tourist was followed for 500 m in August 2025 (Sud Quotidien, 2025-11-15) [C safety.general][12]. OpenShores is more upbeat; sources conflict [C safety.general].
- Evenings: a shopkeeper says the street is empty by 8 pm where tourists once strolled until midnight (Sud Quotidien, 2025-11-15) [12]. Plan rides home, not walks.
- Power cuts last from hours to days; older villas can lose water for days; a generator (500,000–800,000 XOF to buy) and a water reserve are advised (OpenShores, low) [C daily.power][C cost.living].
- Serious care means Dakar, 80 km and about 1 h 30; evacuation insurance described as indispensable [C health.care]. Hivernage (June–September): 33–38°C, 85–95% humidity, high malaria risk [C climate.daily].
- Beaches are often strewn with plastic, no public toilets, rubbish on roads; the monthly "Set Setal" clean-ups were suspended [C nature.access]. Beaches have shrunk from erosion since the 2000s; dike works began in early 2019 [2].
- Local taxes rising: household-waste tax 72,000 → 108,000 XOF, built-property tax 120,000 → 180,000 (Dakaractu, c. 2025-03-03) [C cost.living].
- Women: national figure only (31% say harassment in public places is frequent, Afrobarometer 2025-11-28) [C safety.women]. LGBT law of 30 March 2026 applies [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Saly Portudal resort core (Saly Plages, Saly Station, Saly Golf)
- Feel: the tourist centre with hotels, golf and a small harbour at Saly Plages [2]; a European retiree hub with more than a thousand French retirees living full-time (Retraite sans frontières estimate, au-senegal.com 2026-10-08) [C community.expat]; WhatsApp groups and lists of trusted tradespeople (OpenShores) [C community.expat]. Sud Quotidien (2025-11-15) calls it a dormitory town with crumbling infrastructure [12]. Much livelier in the European winter (dossier, editorial) [D].
- Rent: secured residences: studio 70,000–120,000 XOF (≈ €105–185), 2-room with shared pool 120,000–200,000 (≈ €185–305), 3-room 180,000–280,000 (≈ €275–425); villas: 2-bed with private pool 150,000–250,000 (≈ €230–380), 3-bed with pool, garden and guard 200,000–350,000 (≈ €305–535); OpenShores 2026-03-10, low [C cost.rent]. CoinAfrique asking rents 2026-11-14: 3-room flat 120,000 (≈ €183), 3-room villa 500,000 (≈ €760) [C cost.rent]. Short stays in high season cost 2–4 times the long-term rate [C cost.rent]. Owners away for the summer leave villas with managers, creating negotiable offers [C cost.negotiation]; the property market is a buyer's market [C cost.negotiation].
- Getting around: taxi within Saly 1,000–2,500 XOF, Jakarta moto-taxi 500–2,000 (OpenShores, low) [C daily.transport]; to AIBD 8,000–15,000 by private taxi; to Dakar shared taxi 3,000–5,000 a person, private 12,000–20,000 [C daily.transport]. Main roads potholed; secondary roads turn to mud in the rains (2025-11-15) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: Jakarta motorbikes ridden at speed on the Plein Sud and Safari beaches and in front of the Mövenpick, endangering bathers, especially weekend afternoons (Dakaractu, c. 2025-03-03) [11][C safety.general]. The beach of the Bougainvillées hotel is shrinking from erosion [2].
- Places:
  - Casino Saly (supermarket with imported goods; baguette 400–600 XOF, camembert 4,000–7,000) — OpenShores [9][10]
  - Clinique de Saly (routine care and emergencies) — OpenShores [10]; [D]
  - Lycée français Jacques Prévert, Route de Saly Station (AEFE partner, petite section to seconde; 1re–Tle by CNED) — Le Petit Journal [8]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Saly golf course and the small harbour, Saly Plages — Wikipedia FR [2]
  - Plage de Plein Sud, Plage de Safari, and the beach in front of the Mövenpick (ex-Lamantin Beach Hotel) — Dakaractu [11]
  - Saly-Expo (trade-fair venue) — Sud Quotidien [12]
  - NBA Academy Africa (basketball training centre since 2017) — Wikipedia FR [2]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" up to seconde (Lycée Jacques Prévert); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" only in the sense of a European retiree network (returnee community not found) [C community.expat].

### Saly Niakhniakhal and the outer hamlets
- Feel: one of the original hamlets around the resort (with Saly Bambara, Saly Vélingara, Saly Tapé, Saly Koulang) [2]; OpenShores describes the outer areas as local housing [9][C cost.rent]. No dated description of residents was found.
- Rent: room or simple studio 25,000–50,000 XOF (≈ €38–76); local 2–3-room house 40,000–90,000 (≈ €60–135); small recent villa without pool 100,000–180,000 (≈ €150–275); OpenShores, low [C cost.rent]. CoinAfrique: a room at 28,000 XOF (≈ €43), 2026-11-14, area not stated [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: taxi 1,000–2,500 XOF within Saly [C daily.transport]; clando Mbour–Saly 200 XOF (Wikivoyage, undated) [1].
- Watch: villa burglaries frame above; nothing area-specific sourced. Its beach has held up better against erosion than others [2].
- Places: only the Saly Niakh Niakhal beach (less eroded) — Wikipedia FR [2]. Everyday services are the resort-core ones (Casino Saly, Clinique de Saly) a taxi ride away [9][10]. Fewer than three places sourced; see Gaps.
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" on a budget; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Mbour (town next door)
- Feel: a town of 284,189 people (2023 census), department capital, built on fishing and tourism and described as Senegal's second port; home of the Kankourang mask tradition (UNESCO, 2008); Wikipedia flags insecurity and prostitution, especially in Saly, and seasonal flooding [4]. The dossier names Mbour as the cheaper local-housing option (editorial) [D].
- Rent: 2-room flat in Saly/Mbour 80,000–250,000 XOF (≈ €120–380), OpenShores 2026-04-30, low [C cost.rent]; a villa in Mbour asked 32 million XOF to buy (≈ €48,800) on CoinAfrique, 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: clando (shared car) 100 XOF in town, 125 to the end of the route, 200 to Saly; charette 50; taxis 500–800 (Wikivoyage, undated) [1]. Saly centre to Mbour market by taxi 500–1,500 (OpenShores) [C daily.transport]. Dakar–Mbour: sept-place 1,500 XOF plus up to 500 for luggage, mini-car 1,100, Ndiaga Ndiaye 700 (Wikivoyage, undated, likely older) [1].
- Watch: seasonal flooding in the rainy season (Wikipedia, undated statement) [4].
- Places:
  - Mbour fish market on the beach (free; busiest late afternoon when boats land; per kg thiof 2,500–5,000 XOF, capitaine 2,000–4,000) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; OpenShores [9][10]
  - Large market (reached by clando 100 XOF) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; OpenShores [9]
  - Hôpital de Mbour (routine care, common emergencies) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; OpenShores [10]
  - Holidays and Avalanche supermarkets, Grand Mbour road intersection — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Stade Caroline Faye (stadium) — Dakaractu sidebar [11]; Ligue 1 clubs Stade de Mbour, Mbour Petite-Côte FC and Diambars FC — Wikipedia [4]
  - Blue Africa (beachfront campement; rooms 20,000 XOF plus breakfast, restaurant 2,500–4,000) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Auberge Ndaali (eco-lodge, 6 huts; 17,500 XOF single, 25,500 double with breakfast; restaurant from 2,500) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Gare routière (transport garage) on Rue Nationale — Wikivoyage EN [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Salarié·e" and "J'entreprends" (the working town, port and market) [4]; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q8 "À la plage".

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Saly Portudal, a secured residence** (studio, buffer water tank more common in modern residences) [C cost.rent][C daily.power]. Internet: Sonatel fibre "where available" 30,000–50,000 XOF a month, 4G 50 GB 15,000–30,000; Starlink is suspended [C daily.internet]. Budget a generator share or inverter for multi-day cuts [C daily.power].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; groceries at Casino Saly [9].
  - Tue: home office; late-afternoon run to the Mbour fish market by taxi (500–1,500 XOF) [1][C daily.transport].
  - Wed: home office; lunch in a local eatery, thiéboudienne 2,000–3,500 XOF [C cost.living].
  - Thu: home office; beach walk at Saly Niakh Niakhal [2] (avoid weekend afternoons when motorbikes ride the Plein Sud and Safari beaches) [11].
  - Fri: home office; Mbour large market by clando [1].
  - Sat: surf lesson at Warang (15,000–25,000 XOF a session) [C cost.living]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Somone lagoon by paddle [7].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no club or dance venue was sourced in Saly; the streets empty by 8 pm per Sud Quotidien [12]. Realistically Dakar, 80 km away (private taxi 12,000–20,000 XOF each way) [C daily.transport]; not a weekly option.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": an expat restaurant (grilled capitaine 8,000–15,000 XOF, Flag 1,000–2,000; no restaurant named in the sources) [C cost.living], or the Blue Africa beach restaurant in Mbour (2,500–4,000 XOF) [1]. Ride back by taxi.

- Weekend outing:

  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Saly (no mountains or forests sourced); the nearest wooded outing is the Accrobaobab baobab course [7] or the Réserve naturelle de Popenguine (listed, no details) [7].

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read the frames first: no walking alone after dark, motorbikes on weekend beaches, villa burglaries. Best season October–May (25–32°C) [C climate.daily].

### One day
- Morning: Réserve de Bandia, on the Dakar road (fenced savanna, giraffes, rhinos; by vehicle only; fee not found) [5]. Taxi or hired car (monthly local rental quoted at 150,000–300,000 XOF; no day rate in the files) [C cost.living].
- Midday: lunch in Saly: thiéboudienne 2,000–3,500 XOF or dibi (grilled mutton) 2,500–5,000 [C cost.living]; bissap 300–500 [C cost.living].
- Afternoon: beach at Saly Niakh Niakhal [2], then a taxi to Mbour (500–1,500 XOF) [C daily.transport] for the fish market on the beach when the boats come in, late afternoon (free; ask before portraits, dress decently) [1].
- Evening: dinner at Blue Africa on the Mbour beachfront (2,500–4,000 XOF) [1]; taxi back to Saly.

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from AIBD (8,000–15,000 XOF) [C daily.transport]; the one-day route the next morning.
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: La Somone lagoon: mangroves, birds, paddle or kitesurf [7]; Ngaparou beach on the way back [2].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): Mbour large market in the morning by clando (100 XOF), the ride residents take [1]; afternoon Ligue 1 football at Stade Caroline Faye if a match is on (fixtures not sourced) [4][11].
- Day 5 (trip out): Joal-Fadiouth: the shell island and its footbridge, the mixed cemetery, the Église Saint-François-Xavier, Senghor's childhood home (fees not found) [6].
- Day 6: Accrobaobab treetop course and the Popenguine reserve [7]; surf lesson at Warang (15,000–25,000 XOF) [C cost.living].
- Day 7 (trip out): a day in Dakar (shared taxi 3,000–5,000 XOF each way) for Gorée and the Plateau [C daily.transport]; see `dakar.neighbourhoods.md`. The Sine Saloum delta is named as nearby by Wikivoyage but no details were sourced [1].
