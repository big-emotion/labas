## Items

- [cost.rent] Baie Sainte Anne (villas and flats): €800–1,800/month (≈ 12,800–28,800 SCR). — Expat.com FR guide, 2025-11-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Praslin is often 15–30% cheaper than Mahé for comparable property, but supply is limited. — Cyril Jarnias, "Living in Seychelles", 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] 1-bed ranges: Anse Volbert (Côte d'Or) USD 560–1,000/month (≈ €509–909); Grand' Anse USD 450–700 (≈ €409–636). — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A much higher range from the same author's property article: Praslin long-term rents USD 1,000–3,000/month (≈ €909–2,727). — Cyril Jarnias, "Seychelles real estate prices", 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Holiday villas fetch USD 300–800 a night in high season (≈ €273–727), which competes with long-term letting. — Cyril Jarnias, "Seychelles real estate prices", 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Airbnb's "monthly stays" pages for Praslin show furnished holiday units (Anse Lazio area, Anse Kerlan, Grand' Anse, Au Cap) rather than ordinary leases; no long-term agency listing for Praslin was found. — Airbnb Praslin monthly page, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [business.invest] Praslin sale prices: average about 8,736 SCR/m² (≈ €546/m²); apartments USD 250,000–500,000 (≈ €227,000–455,000); villas USD 700,000–2 M (≈ €636,000–1.82 M); a 260 m² 3-bed at Côte d'Or listed at USD 830,000 (≈ €755,000). — Cyril Jarnias, "Seychelles real estate prices", 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [business.invest] Foreigners may buy only in designated areas or approved developments, with a 10 M SCR minimum for built residential property (≈ €625,000) and total transaction costs up to 18–20%. — Cyril Jarnias, "Seychelles real estate prices", 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] About 90% of consumer goods in Seychelles are imported, and European products carry a 40–60% markup; outer islands add ferry costs on top. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Mahé–Praslin ferry about 965 SCR one way on average (≈ €60); flight 890–1,450 SCR (≈ €56–91). — Global Health insurance page, accessed 2026-11-14 (2025 data), company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Mahé–Praslin ferry return 1,600–2,100 SCR (≈ €100–131); Air Seychelles domestic return from 2,400 SCR (≈ €150). — Nucamp blog, 2026-04-24, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Bus single ticket about 10–12 SCR (≈ €0.63–0.75) and monthly pass about 430 SCR (≈ €27), given for Mahé and Praslin together. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Taxis run on Praslin but not all are metered; agree the fare beforehand. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Praslin has two districts, Baie Sainte Anne and Grand' Anse; main settlements are Baie Sainte Anne (about 2,500 people), Anse Volbert and Grand' Anse; the island had about 7,500 people in 2014. — Wikipedia "Praslin", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The island's economy rests on hotels, resorts and beaches; Seychelles visitor arrivals were down 6.3% year on year to early November 2026. — Wikipedia "Praslin", accessed 2026-11-14; NBS week-45 bulletin 2026-11-11, academic/official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Cat Cocos ferry times: Mahé–Praslin 1 h 15; Praslin–La Digue 15 min; some sailings call at Praslin Jetty in Baie Sainte Anne; it runs multiple trips every day with five catamarans. — Cat Cocos website, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Mahé–Praslin flight takes about 15 minutes; sailings can be cancelled in bad weather. — Expat.com EN guide, 2025-11-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] SPTC runs daily bus trips on Praslin as well as Mahé; Canada describes buses on both islands as limited. — SPTC website, accessed 2026-11-14; Government of Canada 2026-06-09, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Seven regional health centres serve Mahé, Praslin and La Digue; serious cases are evacuated to Mauritius, India or South Africa. — Expat.com EN guide, 2025-11-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Praslin has a secondary hospital; the main hospital is in Victoria. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Medical facilities are limited on isolated islands, some with no doctor; insurance covering evacuation is advised. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Praslin has local public schools only and no international school. — Expat.com EN guide, 2025-11-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Conflicting claim: a Vijay International School is listed on Praslin. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Baie Sainte Anne Primary runs a crèche; in Nov 2026 a bank donated dining tables because the school lacked them. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Petty crime (pickpocketing, bag snatching) is reported at Côte d'Or beach, as well as Beau Vallon and Victoria. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advisory has no Praslin-specific section; its national warning covers beaches, walking trails, parked cars and holiday accommodation. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Vallée de Mai Nature Preserve (est. 1979, coco de mer palms) and the Praslin National Park bird area (Seychelles black parrot, Seychelles bulbul) are on the island; Anse Lazio and Anse Georgette are its best-known beaches. — Wikipedia "Praslin", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] The December–March rainy season brings strong winds and heavy rain that can disrupt travel, which matters for an island reached by boat or small plane. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex activity is legal but homosexuality is "not widely socially tolerated" (Canada); the UK says "attitudes vary". — Government of Canada 2026-06-09; UK FCDO accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
