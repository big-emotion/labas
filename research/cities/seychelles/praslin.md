- [work.jobs] The island's economy rests on hotels, resorts and beaches; Seychelles visitor arrivals were down 6.3% year on year to early November 2026. — Wikipedia "Praslin", accessed 2026-11-14; NBS week-45 bulletin 2026-11-11, academic/official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Cat Cocos ferry times: Mahé–Praslin 1 h 15; Praslin–La Digue 15 min; some sailings call at Praslin Jetty in Baie Sainte Anne; it runs multiple trips every day with five catamarans. — Cat Cocos website, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium

- [health.care] Praslin has a secondary hospital; the main hospital is in Victoria. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Medical facilities are limited on isolated islands, some with no doctor; insurance covering evacuation is advised. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Praslin has local public schools only and no international school. — Expat.com EN guide, 2025-11-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Conflicting claim: a Vijay International School is listed on Praslin. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-07-06, blog, confidence: low

- [safety.general] The UK advisory has no Praslin-specific section; its national warning covers beaches, walking trails, parked cars and holiday accommodation. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Vallée de Mai Nature Preserve (est. 1979, coco de mer palms) and the Praslin National Park bird area (Seychelles black parrot, Seychelles bulbul) are on the island; Anse Lazio and Anse Georgette are its best-known beaches. — Wikipedia "Praslin", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] The December–March rainy season brings strong winds and heavy rain that can disrupt travel, which matters for an island reached by boat or small plane. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-06-09, official, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex activity is legal but homosexuality is "not widely socially tolerated" (Canada); the UK says "attitudes vary". — Government of Canada 2026-06-09; UK FCDO accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high

- [cost.living] An old resident's budget from Amitié (2015), useful only as a baseline: food about 2,000 SCR/month for two (≈ €125 at today's rate), electricity 150 SCR (≈ €9), water 80 SCR, cooking gas 80 SCR, prepaid internet 800 SCR (≈ €50), a Grand Anse clinic visit 200 SCR plus about 80 SCR of medicine, cinema 60 SCR, takeaway meal 50 SCR, local beer 29 SCR, bus 5 SCR. — Expat.com forum thread "Cost of living in the Seychelles 2015", ~2015, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The state trading company's (STC) supermarket at the Pension Fund Complex in Grand Anse promised the same prices as on Mahé for basic goods, with STC covering shipping and handling. — Seychelles Nation, 2013-08-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] The national minimum wage is SCR 40.95 an hour (≈ €2.56), or SCR 6,210.75 gross a month for a 35-hour week (≈ €388); there is no island-specific rate. — ILO news item, 2026-08-17, official, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] One guide advises opening property offers about 10–15% below the asking price and says a Praslin villa can cost up to 30% more than a similar one on Mahé, which conflicts with the same author's claim that Praslin rents are 15–30% cheaper; it names Côte d'Or among the most sought-after areas in 2024. — Cyril Jarnias, 2025-10-02, blog, confidence: low
- [business.invest] Grand'Anse (Praslin) sale prices: flats €2,000–3,000/m² (≈ 32,000–48,000 SCR), the most prestigious villas €4,000–6,000/m² (≈ 64,000–96,000 SCR). — Cyril Jarnias, "Seychelles real estate price comparison by city", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Social housing: a 16-unit public project at Amitié (two blocks of eight) was 70% built in July 2026; handover slipped from June to September 2026 because of material shortages. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-07-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] From 1 June 2026, Seychellois passengers pay half the port fee: Mahé–Praslin R25 → R12 (≈ €1.56 → €0.75) and Praslin–La Digue R15 → R8; visitors' fees are unchanged. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-06-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Residents and tourists pay very different ferry fares. An old Cat Cocos tariff (about 2013) charged 835 SCR one way Mahé–Praslin for tourists against 210 SCR for citizens on the main deck (1,080 vs 325 SCR in business), with a 33% rebate for seniors and under-12s. Current resident fares were not found. — ATC News, ~2013-01, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Car hire on Praslin from about €50 a day (≈ 800 SCR) at one Baie Sainte Anne firm, plus 200 SCR (≈ €12.50) to deliver and collect the car. — Sea View Car Rental listing on koek.sc, accessed 2026-11-15, company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] On 21 April 2026 a private contractor felling trees at Cap Samy cut power from the Baie Sainte Anne power station to all of Praslin and La Digue from about 1:50 p.m.; supply was fully back by 4 p.m. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-04-22, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Recurrent outages in early 2025 were blamed on an intermittent fault (a fuse carrier on a 200 kVA transformer at Grand Anse, since replaced) and on trees touching overhead lines; PUC sent crews from Mahé and launched an island-wide tree-clearance programme, which some residents opposed. — Seychelles Nation, 2025-04-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Four new 2 MW generators (8 MW in all), bought with a €14.6 M loan, arrived in February 2026 for the Baie Sainte Anne power station to meet rising demand on the inner islands; no commissioning date was given. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-02-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Night-time water cuts from 9 p.m. to 5 a.m. were reintroduced on Praslin (and La Digue and parts of Mahé) in November 2025 during a dry spell. — Seychelles Nation, 2025-11-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] The upgraded desalination plant on Eve Island has been fully running since 16 October 2025, and PUC says it has noticeably improved water supply on Praslin during the drought. — Seychelles Nation, 2025-10-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Water restrictions returned on Praslin in July 2026 after two months with very little rain. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-07-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] A presidential visit to Baie Sainte Anne Hospital in February 2025 reviewed staff shortages and needed building upgrades, and looked at a site for a planned cancer hospice nearby. — State House, 2025-02-21, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Baie Sainte Anne Hospital is the island's hospital (+248 423 2333); a pharmacy is listed at Cap Samy; emergency numbers are 999, and 151 for the free A&E line. — SeyVillas health guide, © 2026, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] A "sober living" home for people recovering from drug and alcohol addiction opened as a pilot on Praslin in January 2026; the report says Praslin, as a smaller island with fewer resources, has been hit especially hard by substance abuse. — Seychelles Nation, 2026-01-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The former Anse Boudin community centre is to become a facility of the national drug prevention and rehabilitation agency (presidential visit, 25 May 2026). — Seychelles Nation, 2026-05-26, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.women] No Praslin-specific data. Nationally, 26% say women often or always face sexual harassment in public spaces (markets, streets, buses), and 75% want police and courts to do more. — Afrobarometer dispatch AD1105, 2026-01-07 (2025 survey), academic, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Praslin's evening life is small: Jungle Club and Oxygen nightclub for dancing, and quieter spots such as the Honesty Bar for sundowners; no prices or hours given. — Shore Africa, 2025-11-30, blog, confidence: low
