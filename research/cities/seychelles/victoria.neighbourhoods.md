# Victoria: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 16 SCR** (dossier [D], implied by Expat.com Nov 2025) and **1 EUR ≈ 1.10 USD** (city file). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/seychelles/victoria.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/seychelles.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after four searches in this pass, so places come mainly from Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, the Expat.com guide, a KOEK directory listing and venue or institution pages opened directly. Wikivoyage listings are undated and several carry "updated 2015" or "updated Feb 2021" tags [1][2], so treat opening hours and even existence as leads to re-check.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Victoria is small: the Central region (nine districts in and around the capital) had 30,145 people in the 2022 census; Victoria proper covers English River, Saint Louis, Mont Fleuri and Bel Air [14][C housing.areas].
- Crime is mostly non-violent (break-ins, bag snatching, car and boat break-ins); the UK FCDO names the back streets of Victoria, Beau Vallon and isolated areas at night (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]. Canada puts the country at "take normal security precautions" (2026-06-09) [C safety.general].
- December to March brings heavy rain, floods and landslides that can disrupt travel and services (FCDO; Canada 2026-06-09) [C climate.daily][D].
- Buses: SPTC, 12–15 SCR a ride (≈ €0.75–0.94) on a reloadable Travel Card, cash not accepted (Nucamp, 2026-04-24, low) [C daily.transport]; another source gives 10–12 SCR and a monthly pass of about 430 SCR (Jarnias, 2026-07-06, low) [C daily.transport]. Wikivoyage's older "Rs 7, small notes in cash" note conflicts and is probably out of date [2]. Most buses stop after 8 p.m. (FCDO) [C daily.transport]. SPTC publishes an August 2026 weekday timetable and a July 2026 weekend timetable as PDFs [20].
- Taxis: base fare 97 SCR plus 29 SCR/km (Global Health, 2025 data, low); not all are metered, so agree the fare first (Canada, FCDO) [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation].
- Healthcare for foreigners is private: insurance about €150–500/month single, €300–800 for a family of four (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Victoria centre (English River, Saint Louis)
- Feel: the working capital, chosen by professionals who want offices, banks, shops and schools close by; urban and busy, and the lack of a beach puts some expats off (Expat.com FR, 2025-11-26) [3]. English River is the central district, about 3,737 people (2019 estimate) [13]. Modern flats are scarce [C cost.rent].
- Rent: 3-room flat €900–1,800/month (≈ 14,400–28,800 SCR) (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C cost.rent]; downtown flats 12,000–18,000 SCR (≈ €750–1,125) (Nucamp, 2026-04-24, low) [C cost.rent]; Victoria/English River 1-bed USD 850–1,300 (≈ €773–1,182) (Jarnias, 2026-07-06, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot inside the centre; it is the hub of the SPTC network (SPTC's office is at English River) [20]; a monthly pass is about 430–600 SCR (Jarnias; Nucamp, low) [C daily.transport][4].
- Watch: the FCDO's "back streets of Victoria" warning applies at night (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]; Canada reports petty crime in Victoria and at ATMs (2026-06-09) [C safety.general]; few buses after 8 p.m. [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Sir Selwyn Selwyn-Clarke Market (fresh fish, fruit, spices; Monday to Saturday; go early) — Wikipedia [14]; Wikivoyage Mahé [2]; Expat.com [3]
  - STC Supermarket, Albert Street (supermarket) — Wikivoyage Victoria, listing "updated Jul 2015" [1]
  - Immaculate Conception Cathedral, Olivier Maradan Street (Catholic cathedral) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [14]
  - St Paul's Cathedral (Anglican cathedral) — Wikipedia [14]
  - Arulmigu Navasakti Vinayagar Temple, near the fish market and clock tower (Hindu temple) — Wikipedia [14]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Natural History Museum, Independence Avenue (museum) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Barrel Night Club, Revolution Avenue (bar-club, reggae and disco, billiards) — Wikivoyage, "updated Feb 2021" [1]
  - Seychelles Hospital (main public hospital, 404 beds; GP visit 250 SCR in a public hospital) — Expat.com [3]; [C health.care]; exact district not confirmed (Wikipedia page 404)
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" and "J'entreprends" (offices and banks are in the capital, and new businesses register with the Investment Board and the SRC) [D]; Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" by Seychelles standards only (nightlife is modest) [C social.nightlife].

### Mont Fleuri
- Feel: hillside residential district with houses and small blocks of flats, home to families and working people, no direct beach access, a few kilometres from the centre (Expat.com FR, 2025-11-26) [3]. About 3,418 people (2019 estimate); the district also covers the Sainte Anne Marine Park islands [9].
- Rent: 3-room €800–1,400/month (≈ 12,800–22,400 SCR) (Expat.com, 2025-11-26); the same guide also gives €800–1,600 for the Mont Fleuri / Roche Caïman / Mont Buxton group [C cost.rent][3].
- Getting around: "a few kilometres" from Victoria (no time given) [3]; bus at 12–15 SCR, or taxi at 97 SCR + 29 SCR/km [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Seychelles National Botanical Gardens, Mont Fleuri, just outside Victoria (garden: coco de mer, giant tortoises, fruit bats; fee not found) — Wikivoyage Mahé [2]
  - University of Seychelles, Mont Fleuri campus (information-technology programmes) — Wikipedia [15][14]
  - Sheikh Mohamed bin Khalifa Mosque (mosque, built 1982, capacity 600; Wikipedia places it in Mont Fleuri in the text and Bel Air in the infobox) — Wikipedia [18][9]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (short commute); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Roche Caïman and Mont Buxton (eastern hills and reclaimed land)
- Feel: Expat.com groups them with Mont Fleuri as hillside family areas chosen to be close to work and schools, no beach access (2025-11-26) [3]. Roche Caïman was created in 1998 from reclaimed land, about 2,905 people (2019 estimate) [12].
- Rent: 3-room €800–1,600/month (≈ 12,800–25,600 SCR) (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: a few kilometres from Victoria (no time given) [3]; bus or taxi as above.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:

  - No other everyday place inside these two districts could be sourced (see Gaps).

### Beau Vallon (north-west coast, with Bel Ombre)
- Feel: Mahé's most popular beach, about 2 km of sand from Bel Ombre to Mare Anglaise, with hotels, dive centres, restaurants and bars [2][10]; Expat.com calls it a lively tourist area where families and many expats live (2025-11-26) [3]. About 4,020 people (2019 estimate) [10]. A relocation blog says it has a noticeable French-speaking community (Jarnias, 2026-07-06, low) [5].
- Rent: 3-room €900–2,200/month (≈ 14,400–35,200 SCR) depending on view (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C cost.rent]; 2-bed 20,000–25,000 SCR (≈ €1,250–1,563), studios from about 12,000 SCR (≈ €750) (Nucamp, 2026-04-24, low) [C cost.rent]; 1-bed USD 1,000–1,550 (≈ €909–1,409) (Jarnias, low) [5].
- Getting around: about 15 minutes to Victoria by car (Expat.com) [3]; Nucamp suggests a scooter (second-hand 15,000–25,000 SCR) [C daily.transport]; airport taxi USD 50–80 (Jarnias, low) [5].
- Watch: named by the FCDO as a place to take care, especially at night (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]; petty crime reported by Canada (2026-06-09) [C safety.general]; rip currents when the sea is rough, few lifeguards, and the west coast is unsafe for swimming in the north-west monsoon, about December to March (FCDO) [C nature.access].
- Places:
  - Beau Vallon Beach (beach) — Wikipedia [10]; Wikivoyage Mahé [2]
  - Wednesday-evening market at Beau Vallon (market) — Expat.com [3][C social.culture]
  - STC and Codevar supermarkets (supermarkets, Beau Vallon) — Expat.com FR [3]; exact addresses not given
  - Boat House (Creole buffet, opens 07:30) — Wikivoyage Victoria [1]
  - La Perle Noire (restaurant, pricier) — Wikivoyage Victoria [1]
  - Anse Major trail from Bel Ombre (about 3 hours return, mostly inside Morne Seychellois National Park) — Wikipedia [16]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q5 "Télétravail" (check fibre at the address) [C work.remote]; Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q6 "Français" (French-speaking community, low source) [5].

### Eden Island
- Feel: a man-made island with a private marina, shops, restaurants, 24-hour security and concierge services; home to wealthy expats and retirees and the densest English-speaking international community, about 5 minutes from the centre (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [3][C cost.rent]. Nucamp notes it is a special economic zone hosting outsourcing and tech firms (2026-04-24, low) [4].
- Rent: from €2,000/month for a 2-room flat (≈ 32,000 SCR); villas above €4,000–5,000 (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C cost.rent]; long-term rents often above USD 2,500 (≈ €2,273) (Jarnias, low) [C cost.rent]; 1-bed USD 1,400–2,500 (Jarnias, low) [5].
- Getting around: a 300 m bridge links it to Mahé [6]; Eden Plaza sits just off the Providence Highway [7]; about 5 minutes to Victoria [3].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced. Foreigners may buy only in designated developments, from 10 M SCR for built residential property (Jarnias, low) [C business.invest].
- Places:
  - Eden Plaza (shopping centre, 50+ shops, restaurants and business services) — KOEK directory [7]; Savoy blog [6]
  - Supermarket in Eden Plaza: Savoy's blog says a large supermarket without naming it [6]; search snippets from edenisland.sc and wanderlog call it SPAR (pages not opened) [21]
  - Boardwalk Bar & Grill (bar-restaurant, live music, late on Fridays) — Savoy blog [6]; Wikivoyage Victoria, "updated Feb 2021" [1]
  - Sey Si Bon (café for children: juices, milkshakes, frozen yoghurt) — Savoy blog [6]
  - Eden Island Marina (marina) — Savoy blog [6]; search snippets [21]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (24-hour security) [3]; Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (economic zone, low) [4]; Q4 "En couple"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Anse Royale (south-east coast)
- Feel: quiet residential area with a sheltered beach and a more local atmosphere, chosen by families who want calm; fewer expats than Beau Vallon or Eden Island (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [3]. Nucamp calls it more residential than Beau Vallon (low) [4]. About 4,665 people (2019 estimate) [11].
- Rent: 3-bed house €700–1,500/month (≈ 11,200–24,000 SCR) (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [3]; 1-bed 8,000–12,000 SCR (≈ €500–750) and 3-bed family home 20,000–30,000 SCR (≈ €1,250–1,875) (Nucamp, 2026-04-24, low) [C cost.rent]; 1-bed USD 560–920 (Jarnias, low) [5].
- Getting around: about 30 minutes to Victoria (Expat.com) [3]; a daily bus commute to Victoria costs 500–600 SCR/month (≈ €31–38) (Nucamp, low) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the east coast is unsafe for swimming in the south-east winds, about May to September (FCDO) [C nature.access].
- Places:
  - Anse Royale beach (beach) — Wikivoyage Mahé [2]
  - Anse Royale health centre ("Hospital – Anse Royale": consultations, antenatal care, dental, physiotherapy) — KOEK directory [8]; Expat.com names a health centre in Anse Royale [3]
  - University of Seychelles, main campus (university) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Seychelles Polytechnic School of Humanities (college) — Wikipedia [11]
  - Public schools in Anse Royale (unnamed) — Expat.com [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest Mahé rents in the files); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q8 "À la plage".

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Beau Vallon**, a studio. The beach and the expat crowd make a solo remote worker less isolated (Expat.com; a French family's testimony describes early isolation) [3][C community.expat]. Check fibre before signing: fibre runs at 20–100 Mbps in urban Mahé [C work.remote]. Cheaper alternative: Anse Royale.
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; swim at Beau Vallon Beach before 9 a.m. (west coast unsafe December–March) [10][C nature.access].
  - Tue: bus to Victoria (about 15 min by car; bus time not sourced), shop at Sir Selwyn Selwyn-Clarke Market, back before the last buses around 8 p.m. [3][14][C daily.transport].
  - Wed: work; evening at the Beau Vallon Wednesday market [3].
  - Thu: work; supplies at STC or Codevar in Beau Vallon [3].
  - Fri: breakfast at Boat House (opens 07:30) [1]; afternoon bus into Victoria, drinks at Boardwalk Bar & Grill on Eden Island (taxi back after 8 p.m.) [6][1].
  - Sat: market morning in Victoria; afternoon on the beach.
  - Sun: Anse Major trail from Bel Ombre (about 3 hours return) [16].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Barrel Night Club, Revolution Avenue, Victoria (taxi; agree the fare) [1][C cost.negotiation].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Perle Noire, Beau Vallon [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": fish from the market at 30–50 SCR/kg cooked at home [C cost.living].
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Beau Vallon, or Anse à la Mouche and Anse Soleil on the west coast by car [2].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Morne Seychellois National Park (twelve trails of 1–2 hours, peak 905 m) [2][16].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Victoria.
  - "Je reste en ville": Victoria market, Natural History Museum, Botanical Gardens [1][2].
- Weekly spend (SCR, single, studio): rent 12,000/4.33 = 2,771 [C cost.rent, Nucamp, "from" price] + groceries 800–1,200 [C cost.living] + electricity and water 2,000/4.33 = 462 [C daily.power] + fibre 1,500–2,500/4.33 = 346–577 [C daily.internet] + 4 bus rides × 12–15 = 48–60 [C daily.transport] + 2 local-eatery meals × 100–150 = 200–300 + 3 coffees × 50–80 = 150–240 + Saturday out (main 300–500 + 2 bar beers × 125) = 550–750 [C cost.living] + gym €85.20/4.33 ≈ 315 [C cost.living, CityCost] + insurance €150–500/4.33 = 554–1,848 [C health.care] = **about 6,200–8,520 SCR (≈ €387–533) a week**. Air conditioning pushes the power bill up [C daily.power].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: the only priced short-stay in the sources is the Belair Hotel, from €165 a night (Wikivoyage, undated) [1]; the budget Calypha Guest House closed in September 2024 [1]. Furnished flats cost about 20–30% more than unfurnished (Expat.com) [C cost.rent], i.e. €480–1,040/month for a 1-bed. No guesthouse with a dated price could be sourced (see Gaps).
- Paperwork: a free Visitor's Permit for 3 months, extendable in paid 3-month blocks up to 12 months; a paid online Travel Authorisation before flying [D]. Working without a GOP carries a 40,000 SCR fine [C admin.visa].
- Week:
  - Mon: arrive; airport taxi to Beau Vallon about USD 50–80 (Jarnias, low) [5]. SIM from Airtel or Cable & Wireless [3][C work.remote]; a 10 GB+ plan about €37/month (CityCost, low) [C daily.internet]. Mobile money: not found.
  - Tue: Victoria: buy and load an SPTC Travel Card (cash not accepted on buses, Nucamp) [C daily.transport]; ATMs give rupees only, and unofficial money changing is illegal (Canada) [C daily.money]; market [14].
  - Wed: viewings in Mont Fleuri and Roche Caïman; search runs through agencies, expat Facebook groups and word of mouth [C housing.search]; Beau Vallon market in the evening [3].
  - Thu: viewings in Anse Royale (bus about 30 minutes) [3]. No viewing or agency fee was found.
  - Fri: price the move: leases run a year, with the first month plus 1–2 months' deposit; deposits have no specific legal protection, so photograph the flat [C cost.rent]. Bank account: needs an employment contract, so a trial stayer probably cannot open one [C daily.money].
  - Sat: market morning; Barrel Night Club or Marie Antoinette at night [1].
  - Sun: Anse Major hike or Beau Vallon beach [16].
- Weekly spend (SCR, furnished flat route): furnished 1-bed €480–1,040/4.33 ≈ 1,773–3,843 + airport taxi USD 50–80 ≈ 727–1,164 + mobile plan €37 ≈ 592 (a month, paid once) + groceries 800–1,200 + 10 bus rides × 12–15 = 120–150 = **about 4,010–6,950 SCR (≈ €251–434)**. Hotel route: Belair €165 × 7 = €1,155 ≈ 18,480 SCR before food. Advance for an annual lease on a 6,400–12,800 SCR 1-bed: 2–3 months = 12,800–38,400 SCR (≈ €800–2,400) [C cost.rent].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Sir Selwyn Selwyn-Clarke Market early, for fish, fruit and spices [2][14]; then the Clock Tower and the Arulmigu Navasakti Vinayagar Temple nearby, and Immaculate Conception Cathedral on Olivier Maradan Street, all on foot [1][14]. No entry fees found.
- Midday: Natural History Museum on Independence Avenue (hours and fee not found) [1]; lunch at a local eatery, about 100–150 SCR [C cost.living].
- Afternoon: taxi to the Seychelles National Botanical Gardens in Mont Fleuri (fee not found) [2]; taxi fare 97 SCR plus 29 SCR/km [C daily.transport].
- Evening: taxi over the bridge to Eden Island, sunset drinks at Boardwalk Bar & Grill [6]; or bus to Beau Vallon before 8 p.m. for the beach and dinner at La Perle Noire [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: glass-bottom boat from Victoria to Sainte Anne Marine National Park, the Indian Ocean's first marine park (1973), about 20 minutes away; fee applies, amount not found [2][17].
- Day 3: Beau Vallon. Anse Major trail from Bel Ombre in the morning (about 3 hours return) [16]; if it is Wednesday, the evening market [3].
