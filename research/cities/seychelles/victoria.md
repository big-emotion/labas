# Victoria, Seychelles

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: SCR→EUR conversions use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 16 SCR** (implied by Expat.com, Nov 2025; CityCost gives 15.99 on 2026-11-14). USD→EUR conversions use **1 EUR ≈ 1.10 USD** (implied by Expat.com FR vs EN salary figures, Nov 2025). Caution: the English edition of the Expat.com guide shows most prices as the French edition's EUR figures multiplied by about 1.30, so its USD figures are not converted again here. Victoria is a small capital; many items cover Mahé as a whole or the commuter areas around it (Mont Fleuri, Roche Caïman, Beau Vallon, Anse Royale, Eden Island). The shared web-search budget ran out after the first three searches of this pass, so the file rests on direct fetches of known pages (see Gaps).
