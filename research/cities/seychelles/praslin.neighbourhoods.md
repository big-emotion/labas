# Praslin: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 16 SCR** (dossier [D]) and **1 EUR ≈ 1.10 USD** (city file). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/seychelles/praslin.md`; `[V tag]` = an item in `research/cities/seychelles/victoria.md` (used only where no Praslin figure exists, and labelled as a Mahé price); `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/seychelles.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after four searches in this pass. Places come from Wikivoyage (undated listings, many "updated Jan 2021"), the Seychelles Islands Foundation, Seyvillas guide pages, Wikipedia stubs, Seychelles Nation and two search-result snippets. Praslin is an island of about 7,500 people (2014), not a city: there is no business district, so "the centre" below means Baie Sainte Anne, the largest settlement and the ferry port [5][C housing.areas].

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Two districts: Baie Sainte Anne (about 4,786 people, 2019 estimate, also covering Curieuse) and Grand'Anse Praslin (about 3,876, also covering Cousin, Cousine, Aride and Booby) [6][7].
- Getting there: Cat Cocos Mahé–Praslin 1 h 15, Praslin–La Digue 15 min, several sailings a day calling at the Praslin Jetty in Baie Sainte Anne [11][C daily.transport]; ferry about 965 SCR one way or 1,600–2,100 SCR return; flight 15 minutes, 890–1,450 SCR one way or from 2,400 SCR return (low sources) [C cost.living]. December–March weather can cancel sailings [C climate.daily].
- Buses: SPTC runs daily trips on Praslin (SPTC; Canada calls service limited) [C daily.transport]; Wikivoyage lists routes 61, 62 and 63, Monday to Friday 06:00–19:00, timetables at the Grand Anse tourist office and the jetty office (undated) [1]. Single ticket about 10–12 SCR, monthly pass about 430 SCR (Jarnias, Mahé and Praslin together, low) [C cost.living]. Car hire about SR 1,300 a day [1]. Taxis are not all metered: agree the fare first [C cost.negotiation].
- Health: the island has the Baie Ste Anne Praslin Hospital (Seychelles Nation, 2026-06-22) [10] and a health centre at Grand Anse (search snippets of Seychelles Nation 2023 and a US-funded clinic article) [12]. Serious cases go to Mahé or abroad [C health.care].
- Schools: local public schools only per Expat.com; a blog lists a Vijay International School, unverified [C family.schools].
- Daily life prices for Praslin were not found; Mahé prices are used below and are probably a floor, since imported goods add ferry costs (Jarnias, low) [C cost.living].

## Neighbourhoods

### Baie Sainte Anne (port and main village, with Eve Island)
- Feel: the island's transport hub and service centre, mainly a residential area for local people, with no beach of its own and little holiday atmosphere (Seyvillas, undated) [4]; Expat.com describes Praslin's main port and administrative centre as home to families, retirees and people working in tourism, with a calmer pace than Mahé (2025-11-26) [13]. Eve Island, a man-made island linked by bridges, holds mostly offices and shops [4].
- Rent: villas and flats €800–1,800/month (≈ 12,800–28,800 SCR) (Expat.com, 2025-11-26) [C cost.rent]; Praslin is said to be 15–30% cheaper than Mahé for comparable property but with little supply (Jarnias, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: ferries leave from the Praslin Jetty here [11][4]; buses and taxis to the rest of the island [1].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Praslin Jetty / Praslin Ferry Terminal, Baie Sainte Anne (ferry port) — Cat Cocos [11]; Seyvillas [4]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Baie Ste Anne Praslin Hospital (hospital; Chinese medical team donated supplies in June 2026) — Seychelles Nation, 2026-06-22 [10]
  - Baie Sainte Anne Primary, with a crèche (state school; a bank donated dining tables in November 2026) — Seychelles Nation, 2026-11-14 [C family.schools]
  - Root's Juicery, by the harbour (juice bar, breakfast bowls) — Seyvillas [4]
  - Oxygen (nightclub, Friday and Saturday nights; no caps or sandals) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Church in the main town (unnamed; the village is named after St Anne) — Seyvillas [3][4]
  - Banks, pharmacies and small supermarkets (unnamed) — Seyvillas [3][4]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille" (hospital, primary school with crèche, ferry to Mahé); Q5 "Salarié·e" (port and public services); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Oxygen is the only club found on the island).

### Anse Volbert / Côte d'Or (north coast)
- Feel: one of the two tourist hubs, with accommodation, restaurants and shops (Seyvillas) [3]; Côte d'Or is the main beach, lined with hotels and restaurants, and most of the island's dive sites lie along it (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Rent: 1-bed USD 560–1,000/month (≈ €509–909, ≈ 8,150–14,550 SCR) (Jarnias, 2026-07-06, low) [C cost.rent]; a 260 m² 3-bed for sale at USD 830,000 shows the top of the market (low) [C business.invest].
- Getting around: bus or taxi; distance to Baie Sainte Anne not sourced. Water taxis to Curieuse leave from Anse Volbert [1].
- Watch: Canada reports pickpocketing and bag snatching at Côte d'Or beach (2026-06-09) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Côte d'Or beach (beach) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Café des Arts, Côte d'Or esplanade (seafood restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Les Lauriers, Côte d'Or (restaurant, breakfast to dinner) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Chill out tapas lounge bar, edge of Côte d'Or beach (bar, themed DJ nights at weekends) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Praslin Museum, Côte d'Or (Seychellois culture, medicinal plant garden) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Whitetip and Octopus Diver (dive schools) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Berjaya Praslin Resort, Anse Volbert (hotel) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (cafés, beach; fibre not verified) and "J'entreprends" (tourism micro-business) [C work.jobs]; Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Grand'Anse (west coast, airport side)
- Feel: the second tourist hub, with accommodation, restaurants and shops (Seyvillas) [3]; seat of the Grand'Anse Praslin district [6]. No dated description of residents was found.
- Rent: 1-bed USD 450–700/month (≈ €409–636, ≈ 6,550–10,180 SCR), the cheapest Praslin range in the files (Jarnias, 2026-07-06, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Praslin Airport is at Grand Anse (Wikivoyage) [1]; buses on routes 61–63 (timetable at the Grand Anse tourist office) [1].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Praslin Island Airport (airport) — Wikipedia [5]; Wikivoyage [1]

- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest Praslin rents); Q5 "Salarié·e" (hotel jobs); Q4 "En couple"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Vallée de Mai); Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport).

### North-west: Anse Kerlan, Anse Lazio and Anse Possession side
- Feel: the island's best-known beaches and its golf course; Anse Kerlan is described as a secluded beach at the far north-west (Wikivoyage) [1]. Housing here appears as furnished holiday units on Airbnb's monthly pages (Anse Lazio area, Anse Kerlan, Grand' Anse, Au Cap) rather than ordinary leases (accessed 2026-11-14) [C housing.search].
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; the Praslin-wide range of USD 1,000–3,000 (≈ €909–2,727) applies (Jarnias, low) [C cost.rent]; holiday villas fetch USD 300–800 a night in high season, which competes with long lets [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: car or taxi; bus coverage of the north-west not confirmed.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced; the FCDO's national warning on beaches and walking trails applies [C safety.general].
- Places:

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Anse Volbert / Côte d'Or**: restaurants and cafés within walking distance cut the isolation of solo island life [1][3]. Internet on Praslin was not sourced; check the line at the address first (the city file has no Praslin internet item).
- Week:
  - Mon: work at home; swim at Côte d'Or beach [1].
  - Tue: work; lunch at Les Lauriers [1].
  - Wed: morning dive with Whitetip or Octopus Diver (prices not found) [1].
  - Thu: bus to Baie Sainte Anne for the bank, pharmacy and groceries; juice at Root's Juicery [3][4].
  - Fri: work; evening at the Chill out tapas lounge bar [1].
  - Sat: water taxi to Curieuse (water-taxi fare not found; Curieuse Marine National Park entrance about SR 200 for non-residents) [1].
  - Sun: Anse Lazio [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Oxygen, Baie Sainte Anne (taxi, agree the fare) [1][C cost.negotiation].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Café des Arts, Côte d'Or [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Anse Lazio, Anse Georgette, Côte d'Or [1][5].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Vallée de Mai (SCR 450) or Fond Ferdinand Nature Reserve (122 ha, guided trails, fee not found) [2][1].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Praslin.
  - "Je reste en ville": not realistic on Praslin, which has no town; the nearest is Victoria, 1 h 15 by ferry [11].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Vallée de Mai at opening, 08:30 (SCR 450, payable in rupees, euros, dollars or pounds; public bus stops at the visitor centre) [2].
- Midday: lunch in Grand Anse at Breeze Garden, or Creole takeaway at Coco Rouge, Cap Samy [1].
- Afternoon: taxi or hire car (about SR 1,300 a day) to Anse Lazio (no fee) [1].
- Evening: Côte d'Or for dinner at Café des Arts or Les Lauriers [1].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive by Cat Cocos at Baie Sainte Anne (1 h 15 from Mahé) [11]; settle at Côte d'Or [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): water taxi from Anse Volbert to Curieuse (fare not found; park entrance about SR 200 for non-residents): Doctor's House at Anse José, tortoises at Baie Laraie, mangrove boardwalk [1][8].
- Day 4 (trip out): Inter Island Ferry to La Digue (15 min, up to 8 departures a day) [9][11]; bike hire about RS 100 a day; L'Union Estate SCR 150, the route to Anse Source d'Argent; Veuve Nature Reserve free [9].
- Day 5: Fond Ferdinand Nature Reserve guided walk (fee not found) and the Praslin Pearl Farm [1].
- Day 6 (daily-life moment): weekday morning at Baie Sainte Anne: ferry arrivals at the jetty, banks and pharmacies, juice at Root's Juicery, past the hospital and the primary school [4][10][C family.schools]; afternoon at the Praslin Museum, Côte d'Or [1].
- Day 7 (daily-life moment): Sunday Mass at the church in the main town [3]; last swim at Anse Georgette or Anse Lazio [5]; evening ferry or flight to Mahé.
