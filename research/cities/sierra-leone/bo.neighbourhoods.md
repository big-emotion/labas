# Bo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (dossier and city file): **1 EUR = 25.33 SLE** and **1 USD = 22.98 SLE**, so **1 USD ≈ €0.907** (XE mid-market, 14 Nov 2026). Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33 and a yearly figure by 52. "Le 120,000" on older pages means old leones (= NLe 120). Southern-region inflation was 8.48 % to June 2026, far below Freetown's 21.38 % [C cost.living]. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/sierra-leone/bo.md`; `[CF tag]` = an item in `research/cities/sierra-leone/freetown.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/sierra-leone.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

**Coverage warning.** Bo is thinly documented online. No source describes Bo's neighbourhoods for a newcomer. The neighbourhood names below come from two academic population studies (PLoS ONE 2014 and 2015) that surveyed Bo's 68 "sections" [5][6], and from hotel and venue listings. Wikivoyage's Bo listings date from July 2020 [3]. No rent figure in leones and no neighbourhood-level rent exists for Bo. The web-search budget ran out after three searches, so the rest of this file comes from direct fetches.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Rent.** National practice is months to a year upfront, often without a formal deposit [C cost.negotiation]. City-level asking figures only: a careers portal gives 1-beds in secondary cities (Bo, Kenema) at $150–300/month (≈ €136–272) (Africarrières, 2026-08-06). A modelled aggregator gives a central 1-bed at $638 (≈ €579) and a 3-bed at $1,157 (≈ €1,049), which the city file flags as unreliable (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21) [C cost.rent].
- **Power and water.** Bo, Kenema and Kono together got about 15 MW from the grid in April 2026, against 56–70 MW for Freetown [C daily.power]. A new 33 kV Kenema–Bo line was commissioned in October 2025 to replace a fault-prone 1985 line [C daily.power]. In 2007–08 there was "virtually no piped water"; most households used backyard hand-dug wells, and water-related epidemics were rising (One World Link report, dated) [7]. No current water source was found.
- **Safety.** The Minister of Internal Affairs held a security meeting in Bo over an "alarming" rise in armed robbery in the city and surroundings (Sierraloaded, 2025-08-31) [C safety.general]. The FCDO says to avoid road travel at night outside the Freetown peninsula (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general].
- **Health.** Many facilities outside Freetown take cash only, and ambulances are unavailable across most of the country (FCDO) [C daily.money; health.care].
- **LGBT readers.** Sex between men is illegal nationwide (FCDO, Nov 2026) [D]. This applies to every neighbourhood below.
- **Language.** Krio is the everyday language and Mende is widely understood; English is used for school and government [C social.culture]. For the quiz this means Q6 "Anglais", never "Français".

## Neighbourhoods

### Bo Central (around the Clock Tower)
- Feel: the town centre. Bo School sits "in the heart of Bo Town", within walking distance of the government hospital, post office, police station and main shopping area [4][C housing.areas]. In 2007–08 the only central market, built for about 300 traders, was used by over 5,000, who spilled onto the surrounding streets (dated) [7]. Mosques predominate, though Muslims and Christians are roughly equal in number [C social.religion].
- Rent: city-level only (see frames). A 1-bed at $150–300/month comes to ≈ €136–272 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the Clock Tower is the orientation landmark [3]. In-town okada and keke fares were not found for Bo. The April 2026 official short-ride fare of NLe 7 (≈ €0.28) is from Freetown and is not confirmed for Bo [CF daily.transport]. Freetown–Bo official fare is NLe 182.50 (≈ €7.20) since 8 Apr 2026, about 4 hours on a paved road [C daily.transport].
- Watch: armed-robbery concern (2025, above) [C safety.general]. In 2007–08 a 30-year-old central dump sat beside a food market and homes, and the council planned to remove it (dated) [7]. Bo Regional Hospital closed its mortuary for an upgrade from August to October 2026 [C health.care].
- Places:
  - Clock Tower (landmark, town centre) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Bo Market (fresh produce, spices, clothing, baskets, carved masks) — Sierra Leone Tourism, 2024-05-31 [12]
  - Bo School (boys' boarding school, ages 11–18, founded 1906, 13.5-acre central campus) — Wikipedia [9][4]
  - Bo Government Hospital (public hospital; entrance on Fenton Road per Wikivoyage) — Wikipedia [4]; Wikivoyage [3]. Possibly the same as "Bo Regional Hospital" in local news, not confirmed [C health.care]
  - Americana Bakery and Cafe, near the Government Hospital entrance at 4 Fenton Road (café, bakery) — Wikivoyage, July 2020 [3]
  - Black and White Bar (bar; Guinness on tap, live football on a first-floor balcony) — Wikivoyage, July 2020 [3]
  - Hotel Sir Milton, 6 Kissy Town Road, north of the Clock Tower (hotel with an internet café and small restaurant, mixed reviews) — Wikivoyage, July 2020 [3]
- Fits: Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" (school, hospital, post office and shops within walking distance of one another [4]); Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (hospital, schools); Q5 "J'entreprends" for trade around the market.

### Reservation (Bo Government Reservation)
- Feel: a section originally built as housing for government officials, with large lots and a relatively high share of non-residential buildings. Its average residential roof is 255 m², against 172 m² in dense Njai Town (PLoS ONE, 2014) [5]. It was the largest section in the 2015 sample, at 2.33 km² [6].
- Rent: no figure (city-level frames only) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no distance or transport source found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific found.
- Places: **none sourced** (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" and Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (large lots, built for officials); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu". These are inferences from the housing type, not from residents' accounts.

### Kulanda Town, Njai Town and New London (residential sections)
- Feel: Njai Town is a densely populated residential area with few commercial structures: 269 structures, 127 of them homes, in 2014 [5]. Kulanda Town hosts the Mercy Hospital Research Laboratory [5]. New London had the most residential structures (208) of any surveyed section in 2015, and it was grouped with Kulanda Town and Nduvuibu in the same sampling stratum [6] (the stratum criteria were not visible).
- Rent: no figure (city-level frames only) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Kulanda Town and Njai Town are next to each other (both chosen as near the laboratory) [5]. No distance to the centre was sourced.
- Watch: nothing area-specific found.
- Places:
  - Mercy Hospital Research Laboratory, Kulanda Town (health research lab; its staff and Njala University public-health students ran the surveys) — PLoS ONE [5][6]
  - No other everyday place sourced (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (research lab, Njala University links); Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Bo–Kenema Highway (south side of town)
- Feel: the hotel strip south of town [3]. Dohas Hotel was described as Bo's most reputable lodging as of July 2020, and Countryside Hotel as having declined since opening, with a risk of loud music late at night [3].
- Rent: no figure (city-level frames only) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Bo–Kenema is 68 km, about 1 hour, with poda-podas throughout the day (Gola Rainforest page, undated) [13]. The official fare was NLe 73.70 (≈ €2.91) in March 2026, before a 14.28 % rise [C daily.transport].
- Watch: five people died in a fuel-tanker crash on the Bo–Kenema Highway (Sierraloaded, 2026-06-02, headline only) [C daily.transport]. FCDO night-road advice applies [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Dohas Hotel, 103 Bo–Kenema Highway (hotel with pool, gym, restaurant, secured perimeter, Wi-Fi, A/C) — Wikivoyage, July 2020 [3]
  - Countryside Hotel, Bo–Kenema road (hotel with pool, tennis court, A/C, night electricity; rooms from Le120,000, probably outdated) — Wikivoyage [3][C housing.search]
  - Imperial Hotel, Bo–Kenema road (hotel, no details) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" only with a hotel's generator and Wi-Fi as backup (2020 listing); Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (the road to Kenema and Gola); Q3 "Le prix compte peu".

## Living: typical weeks

Shared caveat: Bo has no sourced weekly basket. The figures below combine Bo's modelled prices (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21, low confidence) with a few national prices. Where a Freetown figure is used as a proxy, it is labelled.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Bo–Kenema Highway**, near Dohas Hotel. Its gym, pool and Wi-Fi are the only sourced backup to home power and data [3]. Be honest about the limits: outside Freetown internet often relies on VSAT [D], hotel Wi-Fi is often unreliable [C daily.internet], and Bo shared about 15 MW with two other cities in April 2026 [C daily.power]. Remote work here is a gamble without your own solar or generator.
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home on Orange mobile data; town 3G/4G is "reasonable" (US Commercial Service, 2026-01-23) [C daily.internet].
  - Tue: market run at Bo Market [12]; coffee at Americana Bakery and Cafe [3].
  - Wed: gym and pool at Dohas Hotel (access terms for non-guests not sourced) [3].
  - Thu: work; evening football on the balcony at Black and White Bar [3].
  - Fri: day trip to Kenema (1 hour by poda-poda) [13].
  - Sat: see options below.
  - Sun: Bo Rangers home game at Southern Arena Stadium (6,000 seats) when scheduled [10]; Bo Rangers vs Nepean Stars is an "intense" local rivalry [10][11].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no nightclub sourced in Bo. The only late-music venue named is the Countryside Hotel bar [3]. Nightlife was not found [D].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Dohas Hotel restaurant [3]. Restaurant menus are limited to beef, chicken and river fish [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home, with local radio (Kiss FM 104, Radio New Song 97.5, SLBS 96.5) [C social.culture].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": no sea beach is realistic for a weekend. The nearest sourced sea beaches are on the Freetown peninsula, about 4 hours away [3]. Tiwai Island has river beaches for swimming in the dry season (multi-day trip) [16].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Gola Rainforest National Park via Kenema (4WD, dry season Nov–May) [13][14], or Tiwai Island [15][16].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bo; no savanna site was sourced within reach.
  - "Je reste en ville": Bo Market, the Clock Tower, football [12][3][10].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent, 1-bed $150–300/month (Africarrières) [C cost.rent] → $34.60–69.30/week → **€31–63**
  - Lunch menus 7 × $2.49 (modelled) [C cost.living] → $17.40 → **€16**
  - Utilities for one $68.40/month (modelled) [C cost.living] → $15.80/week → **€14**
  - Data: Orange 20 GB at NLe 300/month (national price list) [CF daily.internet] → **€2.70**; or modelled 50 Mbps+ broadband $50.40/month [C cost.living] → **€10.60**
  - Saturday dinner: half of $29.60 for two [C cost.living] → **€13.40**
  - **Total ≈ €78–117/week**, before groceries for other meals (no Bo grocery prices sourced) and any generator or solar.

Exact campus locations were not sourced, so commute times are unknown.

  - Wed: evening at Black and White Bar for a match [3].
  - Thu: Dohas Hotel gym [3].
  - Fri: mosque or a quiet evening. Mosques predominate (none named) [C social.religion].
  - Sat: market morning at Bo Market [12]; evening below.
  - Sun: Mass in the Roman Catholic Diocese of Bo, or a Methodist service (churches named without addresses) [4]; then football [10].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Countryside Hotel bar, the only late-music venue named [3].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Dohas Hotel restaurant [3] or Hotel Sir Milton's small restaurant [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outing:

  - "En montagne, en forêt": Gola, which charges a $5 entry and $5 community fee for nationals and $15 + $15 for international visitors, plus a $15/night guesthouse [14].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bo.
  - "Je reste en ville": Kenema day trip [13].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: the only sourced range runs from a 1-bed at $150–300 [C cost.rent] to the modelled 3-bed at $1,157/month [C cost.rent] → $34.60–267.20/week → **€31–242**

  - Saturday dinner for two $29.60 [C cost.living] → **€27**
  - **Total ≈ €110–321/week**. The national minimum wage is NLe 1,200/month (≈ €47) and the modelled median net pay in Bo is $114 (≈ €103), so local salaries rarely cover expat-standard rent [C cost.salary].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: Dohas Hotel, 103 Bo–Kenema Highway (pool, generator-backed facilities as of 2020) or Hotel Sir Milton downtown [3]. Room prices: not sourced (Countryside's Le120,000 is probably outdated) [C housing.search].
- Day 1: Lungi to Freetown by government ferry (first class NLe 76.20) or water taxi (about $50) [CF daily.transport]. The airport security fee is $25 [18]. Register on the NPHA portal before flying [D].
- Day 2: Freetown to Bo. The government bus leaves at 06:00 (listed NLe 150, may predate 2026), and the official fare is NLe 182.50 from 8 Apr 2026; the drive takes about 4 hours on a paved road [C daily.transport]. Don't drive at night [C safety.general].
- Day 3: buy an Orange or Africell SIM [18]. Orange 6.5 GB costs NLe 100 [CF daily.internet]. Open an Orange Money wallet [CF daily.money]. Banks and ATMs in Bo: not named; carry cash, since health facilities may take cash only [C daily.money].
- Day 4–5: flat hunting. No agency or listing for Bo was found (city file, Gaps); ask your employer or university contact. Expect months to a year upfront [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 6: walk the centre: Clock Tower, Bo Market, Bo School's surroundings [3][12][9].
- Day 7: day trip to Kenema [13].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" at the Countryside Hotel bar [3]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at Dohas Hotel [3]; "Tranquille chez moi" at the hotel.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" not realistic; "En montagne, en forêt" Gola via Kenema [13]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic; "Je reste en ville" Bo Market [12].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best season for trips out: the dry season, November to May [13].

### One day
- Morning: Bo Market [12], then the Clock Tower [3] and the area around Bo School, a 1906 boarding school founded for sons of paramount chiefs [9]. Entry to the campus is not sourced; view it from outside. Moving around: in-town fares were not found, so a hotel car or driver is the safe option (FCDO advises against okadas and kekes nationally) [CF safety.general].
- Midday: lunch at Americana Bakery and Cafe, 4 Fenton Road area [3].
- Afternoon: a Premier League match at Southern Arena Stadium if Bo Rangers are at home [10]. Otherwise the pool at Dohas Hotel [3].
- Evening: football on the balcony at Black and White Bar [3], then dinner at Dohas Hotel [3]. No entry fees were sourced for any of these.

### One week
- Day 1: Freetown to Bo by the 06:00 government bus or a hired car, about 4 hours [C daily.transport]. Evening at Black and White Bar [3].
- Day 2: Bo Market morning and the centre walk [12][3]. This is the "what daily life looks like" moment: the market was already overflowing its building in 2007–08 [7].
- Day 3: Kenema, 68 km and about 1 hour by poda-poda (NLe 73.70 in March 2026). The Gola park office is at 164 Dama Road [13][C daily.transport].
- Days 4–5 (trip out): Gola Rainforest National Park, 4WD on unpaved roads. Entry $15 plus a $15 community fee for international visitors ($5 + $5 for nationals). Guesthouse $15/night, meals $10/day, required community guide $3/day (undated price page) [14]. Activities include birdwatching and forest walks [13].
- Day 6 (alternative trip out): Tiwai Island, a primate sanctuary on the Moa River run by the Environmental Foundation for Africa, about 15 km from Potoru [15]. It offers forest walks, boat tours, community visits and dry-season river swims [16]. Book by email; prices are not on the site [16]. Visit Sierra Leone sells a three-day Tiwai Island Heritage Trail [17].
- Day 7: Sunday in Bo. Church (Catholic Diocese of Bo or Methodist) or a Friday mosque earlier in the week [4], then a football match [10][11]. Finish with a look at the residential sections (Reservation, New London) to judge where you would live [5][6].
