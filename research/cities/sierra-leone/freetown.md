# Freetown, Sierra Leone

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR = 25.33 SLE** (XE mid-market, 14 Nov 2026). For USD figures, the XE mid-market rate fetched the same day, **1 USD = 22.98 SLE**, is used, so 1 USD ≈ €0.91. The new leone (NLe/SLE) replaced the old leone at 1,000 old = 1 new in July 2022; prices written "Le 60,000" on older pages are old leones (= NLe 60). Some official sites still label new-leone prices "SLL" or "Le"; this is flagged where it matters. Inflation reached 15.7% in Aug 2026 (World Bank), so every price ages fast.

Coverage note: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after a handful of searches, so most items come from direct fetches of known pages (Sierraloaded, Awoko, FCDO, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, operator sites, cost-of-living aggregators).
