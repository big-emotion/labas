# Freetown: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (dossier and city file): **1 EUR = 25.33 SLE** and **1 USD = 22.98 SLE**, so **1 USD ≈ €0.907** (XE mid-market, 14 Nov 2026). Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33 and a yearly figure by 52. Prices written "Le 120,000" on older pages are old leones (÷ 1,000 = NLe); Wikivoyage listings are flagged where the leone unit is ambiguous. Inflation was 15.7 % in Aug 2026 and 21.38 % in the Western Area to June 2026, so every price below ages fast [C cost.living]. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/sierra-leone/freetown.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/sierra-leone.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Rent is asked yearly and upfront.** A full year in advance is common, often without a formal deposit; one portal says 3–6 months plus a one-month deposit. Employers often pay the annual rent directly [C cost.rent; cost.negotiation]. Agency commission: not found.
- **Transport advice.** The FCDO (live page, accessed 2026-11-14) advises against taxis, poda-podas, kekes and okadas because of robberies [C safety.general]. Most expats drive or hire a driver; a 2022 expat report recommends an SUV because many roads, including the main road to the beach, are unpaved [6]. A 10 km commute can take over an hour at rush hour [C housing.areas]. Okadas have been banned from the central business district since 1 Feb 2025 [C daily.transport].
- **Power and water.** Mains power is unreliable and outages are long; villas and hotels run generators and private water [C daily.power]. Tap water is often not drinkable and cholera peaks in the rainy season [C daily.water]. Expat housing comes with walls, gate and 24/7 guards; check water pressure at viewings [C housing.search].
- **LGBT readers.** Sex between men is illegal (FCDO, live page, Nov 2026) [C rights.lgbt]. This applies to every neighbourhood below.
- **Entry.** Since 22 Oct 2026 every traveller must register on the NPHA health portal before boarding to Lungi (France Diplomatie, 2026-10-27) [C admin.visa].

## Neighbourhoods

### Aberdeen–Lumley (beach strip, West End)
- Feel: the affluent West End with hotels, casinos and Lumley Beach [C housing.areas]; Aberdeen was founded in 1829 for liberated Africans and now holds upscale restaurants, hotels and nightclubs [10]. The nightlife cluster of the city: clubs, bars, restaurants and casinos along the Aberdeen–Lumley beach road [C social.nightlife]. Lumley Beach is the main public beach; the Aberdeen end has rubbish, the stretch near the golf club is better [3].
- Rent: no figure for these two areas alone. Western Freetown, local market: single self-contained room NLe 5,000–10,000/year (≈ €197–395/year), 3–4-bedroom house NLe 20,000–50,000/year (≈ €790–1,974/year) (Sierraloaded, 2026-10-22) [C cost.rent]. Expat-standard: a careers portal names Aberdeen among "desirable" areas with 3-bed houses at $1,000–1,800/month (≈ €907–1,633) (Africarrières, 2026-08-06) [C cost.rent]; an expat blog gives furnished 1-beds at $800–1,300 (≈ €726–1,179) and sea-view villas at $5,000–20,000 (2026-01-26) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: official fare Lumley or Aberdeen to Regent Road NLe 7 (≈ €0.28) from 8 Apr 2026; Lumley–Bawbaw NLe 8.60 and Lumley–Borbor NLe 11.10 in March 2026, before a 14.28 % rise [C daily.transport]. Poda-podas marked "Lumley" and "Aberdeen" run from town [3]. Chartered taxi within Freetown should stay under about $4 (≈ €3.60) [C cost.negotiation]. Sea Coach Express to the airport leaves from the Aberdeen bridge (NLe 1,080 ≈ €43) [3].
- Watch: FCDO lists Lumley Beach and Aberdeen (with Congo Cross and Wilkinson Road) among places where a small number of robberies, some at knifepoint, hit British nationals (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]; Wikivoyage notes night incidents near clubs and bars in Lumley and Aberdeen [C social.nightlife]; a 2022 expat report says crime is high along Lumley Beach [6]. Police recovered kush in an Aberdeen raid (Sierraloaded, 2026-08-26) [C safety.general]. Kamayama, off Regent Road in Lumley, had been without power for nearly a month after its only transformer exploded (Sierraloaded, 2026-11-10) [C daily.power].
- Places:
  - Lumley Beach (public beach) — Wikivoyage [3]; Wikipedia [10]
  - Radisson Blu Mammy Yoko, 17 Lumley Beach Road (hotel; its café is used as a drop-in workspace) — Wikivoyage [3]; expat blog [5]
  - Freetown Golf Club, Lumley Beach Road (golf and tennis) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Aberdeen craft market, behind the Tourist Office (crafts, "less hassle" than Big Market) — Wikivoyage [3]; Lumley Beach Arts and Crafts Market (woodcarvings, textiles, jewellery) — Sierra Leone Tourism, 2024-05-31 [9]
  - Indochine, 64 Sir Samuel Lewis Road, Aberdeen (Chinese/Thai restaurant) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Roy's Beach Bar and Gigibonta (café, cakes and ice cream), Lumley Beach Road — Wikivoyage [3]; Gigibonta also named for pizza in a 2022 expat report [6]
  - Chapter One, 30 Lumley Beach Road (restaurant, bar, nightclub; special events about $100 ≈ €91) — Wikivoyage [3]; [C social.nightlife]
  - Papaya, Family Kingdom, Aberdeen (raised bar, kebabs and pizza) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" and Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (the city's only real nightlife strip); Q8 "À la plage" without leaving town; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu" at expat standard. Not for Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" first: it is the FCDO's named robbery zone.

### Hill Station
- Feel: elevated West End area of colonial stilt houses overlooking the city, with the Country Lodge and the old Hill Station Club [3]; listed with Wilberforce, Spur Road, Signal Hill and Juba Hill as elevated residential areas "prized for relative safety" (expat blog, 2026-01-26) [5]; affluent per Wikipedia [C housing.areas].
- Rent: an agency lists a 3-bed apartment off "Old Skul" at $3,500 (≈ €3,175), period not stated, undated [C cost.rent]; a 5-bedroom duplex for sale at $400,000 (≈ €362,800), undated [C housing.search]. Western Freetown local ranges above also apply [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Regent Road runs through Hill Station and Leicester towards Tacugama [3]. No sourced commute time to Central; the 10 km/over-one-hour rush-hour warning applies [C housing.areas].
- Watch: nothing area-specific found beyond the city-wide power and water frames above.
- Places:
  - Country Lodge Hotel and restaurant, HS 51 Hill Station (live jazz Thursdays, "Mongolian night" Fridays) — Wikivoyage [3]; Lebanese food at Country Lodge named in a 2022 expat report [6]
  - Hill Station Club (colonial-era club, serves beer) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Hill Station colonial stilt houses and old railway remains (heritage walk) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary (reached via Regent Road through Hill Station and Leicester) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps" within the city; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (closest base to Tacugama and the peninsula forest).

### Wilberforce
- Feel: West End neighbourhood founded in 1810 for liberated Africans, whose descendants form the Creole (Krio) people; home to the main military barracks and embassies including China, Germany, Lebanon, Liberia and Austria [11]. Named by the expat blog among elevated, relatively safe residential areas [5].
- Rent: western local ranges as above [C cost.rent]; a 323.7 m² plot asked $45,000 (≈ €40,800), search snippet, with $500/year property tax to the city council quoted in one listing [C housing.search].
- Getting around: official March 2026 fare Wilberforce–Jui NLe 13.50 (≈ €0.53, before the April 14.28 % rise) [C daily.transport]. A 2022 expat report says diplomats mostly live near their missions, so commutes are short [6].
- Watch: on 26 Nov 2023 armed men attacked the Wilberforce barracks armoury; 21 died and a curfew followed (Wikipedia via dossier) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - The Hub Restaurant and Hotel, 4 Regent Road, Wilberforce (pizza takeaway and delivery, air-conditioned) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Wilberforce military barracks (landmark, not visitable) — Wikipedia [11]
  - Embassies of China, Germany, Lebanon, Liberia and others (institutions) — Wikipedia [11]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" for embassy, NGO and diplomatic jobs; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (The Hub); Q4 "En couple".

### Murray Town – Congo Cross – Wilkinson Road
- Feel: Wilkinson Road is the main commercial artery of the western neighbourhoods "where most expatriates live"; Murray Town and Congo Cross sit near embassies and institutions (US Embassy, British High Commission, UN) (expat blog, 2026-01-26) [5]. Murray Town, founded 1829, keeps colonial board houses from around 1900 [12].
- Rent: Africarrières names Wilkinson Road among "desirable" areas: 3-bed house $1,000–1,800/month (≈ €907–1,633) (2026-08-06) [C cost.rent]; western local ranges above apply [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: poda-podas marked "Aberdeen" leave from Murray Town via the north bridge [3]; chartered taxi under about $4 within the city [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: FCDO names Congo Cross and Wilkinson Road among the robbery locations (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Crown Bakery Express, Wilkinson Road (sandwiches, Wi-Fi) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Oasis, Murray Town Road (smoothies and food) — Wikivoyage [3]; Oasis Juice Bar & Guesthouse named in the expat blog [5]
  - Sierra Leone Grammar School, Murray Town (secondary school) — Wikipedia [12]
  - Amputees and War Wounded Association, Murray Town (civil-war memory, organisation) — Wikipedia [12]
  - Congo Cross railway remains (heritage) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (café with Wi-Fi, central position between town and beach); Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Central Freetown (downtown, business district)
- Feel: business district, government buildings and embassies [C housing.areas]; the main commercial area, with colourful, noisy markets (Visit Sierra Leone) [7]. Okadas banned from the CBD since Feb 2025 [C daily.transport].
- Rent: central/eastern single self-contained room NLe 2,000–5,000/year (≈ €79–197); 3–4-bedroom NLe 8,000–30,000/year (≈ €316–1,184) (Sierraloaded, 2026-10-22); one resident pays NLe 5,000/year for a central room [C cost.rent]. Expat blog: modest studio/1-bed in the centre $200–250/month (≈ €181–227) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: walkable core; Waterloo–Bombay Street poda-poda NLe 14.70 (March 2026) [C daily.transport]; poda-podas marked "Tong" serve downtown [3]. Government ferry to Lungi (first class NLe 76.20 ≈ €3.01) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: protests can turn violent (FCDO) [D]; Pademba Road prison was attacked on 26 Nov 2023 [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Big Market (fruit, vegetables, fabrics, baskets, woodcarvings, street food) — Sierra Leone Tourism, 2024-05-31 [9]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Cotton Tree remains (the tree collapsed on 24 May 2023) and the Supreme Court building, 26 Siaka Stevens Street — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Sierra Leone National Museum, junction of Siaka Stevens Street and Pademba Road — Wikipedia [16]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - St George's Cathedral (Anglican, completed 1828) — Wikipedia [17]; Sacred Heart Cathedral, Siaka Stevens Street, and St John's Maroon Church — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Portuguese Steps, Old Wharf (heritage) — Wikivoyage [3]; Visit Sierra Leone [7]
  - Crown Bakery Restaurant, Wilberforce Street (Western-style restaurant) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - YMCA, 37 Fort Street (budget lodging; Le200,000–350,000, unit ambiguous) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - King Jimmy Market (market, site of the De Ruyter Stone) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (traders, government offices); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Cotton Tree, Maroon church, museum).

### East End (Kissy, Texaco, Fourah Bay, Cline Town)
- Feel: the most populous and poorest part of the city [C housing.areas]; Kissy is an East End neighbourhood with over 500 Liberian refugees [13]. More than 30 % of Freetown households live in about 60 informal settlements, including Portee Rokupa and Cockle Bay (African Cities Research Consortium, 2022) [C cost.rent].
- Rent: central/eastern ranges above; a single room and parlour at Texaco NLe 8,000/year (≈ €316) (Sierraloaded, 2026-10-22) [C cost.rent]; zinc "panbody" rooms in informal settlements Le 60,000–120,000/month in 2022 (= NLe 60–120 ≈ €2.40–4.70), with two years upfront, dated [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: poda-podas marked "Eastern Police" go to the Kissy Road clock tower [3]; Kissy ferry terminal runs to Tagrin Point near Lungi airport [13][3].
- Watch: informal-settlement water and toilets are often pay-per-use (ACRC, 2022) [C cost.rent]. No East End-specific safety source found.
- Places:
  - Kissy Ferry Terminal (ferry to Tagrin; charter boats to Bunce Island) — Wikipedia [13]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Fourah Town Mosque (Jamiatul Atiq), Fourah Bay (mosque; Visit Sierra Leone dates the "Foulah Town Mosque" to the 1830s) — Wikivoyage [3]; [7]
  - Fourah Bay College, Mount Aureol (university, founded 1827) — Wikipedia [14]
  - National Railway Museum, Cline Street (Mon–Sat 10:30–17:00, by donation) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Church of the Holy Trinity, Kissy Road, and the United Methodist Church health and maternity ward (church, clinic) — Wikipedia [13]
  - Kissy Mental Hospital (psychiatric hospital, heir to the 1820 asylum) — Wikipedia [13]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q10 "Une vie abordable"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (college, hospitals). Not a first base for a newcomer without local contacts.

### Hill villages: Leicester, Regent, Bathurst
- Feel: mountain villages of the early settlements; Regent is the starting point for walks to Sugar Loaf Mountain and Charlotte Falls (Visit Sierra Leone) [7]. Tacugama sits about 20 minutes from downtown in Bathurst [8].
- Rent: a single room at Leicester Peak NLe 5,000/year (≈ €197) (Sierraloaded, 2026-10-22) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: via Regent Road through Hill Station; Tacugama needs a 4WD (Wikivoyage) [3]. No commute time sourced.
- Watch: the Leicester Peak resident has no piped water and pays over NLe 100/month (≈ €4) to have it fetched [C daily.water]; the 14 Aug 2017 Sugar Loaf mudslide killed 300+ in Regent [C nature.access]; the peninsula forest suffers steady deforestation from urban expansion [C nature.access].
- Places:
  - Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary, Bathurst (tours 10:30, 12:00, 14:30, 16:00; six eco-lodges; forest walks) — Visit Sierra Leone [8]; Tacugama website [20]
  - Sugar Loaf Mountain and Charlotte Falls (walks from Regent) — Visit Sierra Leone [7]
- Fits: Q8 "En montagne, en forêt"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Serré". Not for Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" in the rainy season (landslide history).

### Peninsula beaches: Goderich, Lakka, Tokeh
- Feel: further west and south with sea-view and beachfront villas, longer travel times and sometimes difficult roads (expat blog, 2026-01-26) [5]. Tokeh is the weekend and holiday escape of the city's going-out crowd (social post, 2026-11) [C nature.access].
- Rent: no rental figure; asking price for a 5.5 town-lot plot at Marjay Town, Goderich: $137,000 (≈ €124,300), search snippet [C housing.search].
- Getting around: Lakka Beach is about 10 minutes' drive from Lumley [3]; poda-podas stop at Lakka junction, then okada [3]; official Waterloo–Tokeh fare NLe 35.10 (≈ €1.39) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: FCDO names peninsula beach resorts among robbery sites and warns of children's rope "roadblocks" asking for donations on beach roads at weekends; avoid night driving [C safety.general]. A kush seizure with a pistol in Goderich (Sierraloaded, 2026-08-30) [C safety.general]. Hamilton Beach is now a mining site [3].
- Places:
  - Lakka Beach (Lakka Beach Island Resort; Hard Rock Café, Paul's and Club Med restaurants) — Wikivoyage [3]; Paul's named for seafood in a 2022 expat report [6]
  - Franco's, Sussex Beach (restaurant, slow service) — Wikivoyage [3]; [6]
  - River No. 2 Beach and market (village-run beach, women's crafts) — Wikivoyage [3]; Sierra Leone Tourism [9]
  - The Place resort, Tokeh Beach (resort; Freetown Brunch Club beach edition on 20 Dec 2026) — [C social.nightlife]
  - Choitrams supermarket, Adonkia, peninsula road — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Goderich Beach (few visitors) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Télétravail" only with your own power and data backup; Q3 "Le prix compte peu".

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: a guesthouse on Lumley Beach Road (Sweet Mother's Guesthouse, $25 ≈ €23 a night; Roy Guest House, 54 Lumley Beach Road, $120) or the YMCA, 37 Fort Street, downtown (price unit ambiguous) [3].
- Day 1: land at Lungi. The airport security fee is $25 [4]. Take a water taxi (about $50) or Sea Coach Express (NLe 1,080) to Aberdeen, or the government ferry (first class NLe 76.20) to Kissy [C daily.transport][3]. Register on the NPHA portal before flying [C admin.visa].
- Day 2: buy SIM cards. Orange (prefixes 76/78) or Africell (77/88/30) [4]. Orange 6.5 GB costs NLe 100 (≈ €3.95) [C daily.internet]. Open an Orange Money wallet; cash-out costs about 1–3 % [C daily.money]. There were 117,419 mobile-money agents at end-2025 [C daily.money].
- Day 3: money. Change USD at banks or licensed bureaus only [C daily.money]. Rokel Commercial Bank, Standard Chartered and GTCO have Visa ATMs; Ecobank and UBA accept international cards (Wikivoyage, Aug 2026) [4]. Opening an account as a non-resident: requirements **not found**.
- Day 4–5: flat hunting with a chartered driver ($50/day) [C daily.transport] in Murray Town–Wilkinson Road, Wilberforce and Aberdeen. Check water pressure and the generator, and that guards are included [C housing.search]. Expect a year upfront, or 3–6 months plus a one-month deposit [C cost.negotiation]. Agency and viewing fees: **not found**. Compare actual annual rents rather than assuming by area [C housing.search].
- Day 6: a test commute at rush hour to your future workplace (10 km can exceed an hour) [C housing.areas].
- Day 7: beach day at Lakka [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" on Lumley Beach Road [3]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at Indochine [3]; "Tranquille chez moi" at the guesthouse.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" Lakka [3]; "En montagne, en forêt" Tacugama [8]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic; "Je reste en ville" Big Market and the museum [9][16].

  - Guesthouse 7 × $25 [3] → $175 → **€159**
  - Water taxi $50 [C daily.transport] → **€45**
  - Airport security fee $25 [4] → **€23**
  - Data NLe 100 [C daily.internet] → **€4**
  - Meals: 7 lunch menus at $2.41 and 3 dinners at half of $29.60 [C cost.living] → $61.30 → **€56**
  - Two days of chartered driver at $50 [C daily.transport] → $100 → **€91**
  - **Total ≈ €378**, plus the $80 visa (≈ €73) for UK-type passports [C admin.visa] (French passport rules not found [D]).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: a heritage walk downtown. Start at the Cotton Tree remains and the Supreme Court building (Siaka Stevens Street) [3]. Then the National Museum at Siaka Stevens Street and Pademba Road (fee not found) [16], St George's Cathedral [17], St John's Maroon Church and the Portuguese Steps [3]. All of it is on foot.
- Midday: lunch at Crown Bakery Restaurant, Wilberforce Street [3]. Then the National Railway Museum, Cline Street (Mon–Sat 10:30–17:00, by donation) [3], by chartered taxi (okadas are banned in the CBD) [C daily.transport].
- Afternoon: Tacugama, about 20 minutes from downtown in Bathurst. Take the 14:30 tour and arrive 30 minutes early; the price was not given on the operator page [8]. Wikivoyage lists entry at "Le30,000", which is probably a pre-2022 figure (= NLe 30) [3]. Hire a 4WD with driver [3].
- Evening: sunset on Lumley Beach, then dinner at Roy's Beach Bar or Indochine [3]. Chapter One if you want to dance [3]. Come back with the same driver (FCDO night advice) [C safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive at Lungi; water taxi to Aberdeen [C daily.transport]. Evening on Lumley Beach [3].
- Day 2: the downtown heritage walk above [3][16][17]. Afternoon at Big Market for fabrics and baskets [9].
- Day 3: Tacugama morning tour [8]. Afternoon drinks at Hill Station Club and a look at the stilt houses [3].
- Day 4 (trip out): Bunce Island, the 18th-century slave-trading fort about 32 km upriver, tied to the Gullah people of the US Lowcountry [19]. Go on an organised tour, or charter a boat from Kissy (at least £115) or a speedboat from Aberdeen (about $300). The budget route is a dugout from Big Wharf to Pepel [3]. This is the strongest "mémoire de la diaspora" day.
- Day 5: beaches. River No. 2 (village-run, crafts market) then Tokeh [3][9].
- Day 6 (trip out, overnight): Banana Islands via Kent. Stay at Daltons Banana Guest House or the Banana Island Chalets; see the 1881 church ruins and the old slave dock on Dublin [18]. Group charter from Freetown $300–400 [4].
- Day 7, "what daily life looks like": a Saturday morning at Big Market [9], then the Kissy Road poda-poda at rush hour (watch from a car, given FCDO advice) [3][C safety.general]. Or a Premier League match: eight of the fifteen clubs are from Freetown [C social.sport]. Visit Murray Town–Wilkinson Road and Wilberforce on the way back to see where people settle [5].
