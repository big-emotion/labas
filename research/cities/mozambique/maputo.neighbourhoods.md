# Maputo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **76 MZN ≈ €1** (dossier rate, implied by France Diplomatie's 3,050 MZN ≈ €40 visa fee, Nov 2026); USD at **63.25 MZN = US$1** (city file currency note). Rand figures stay in rand (no rand rate in the files). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/mozambique/maputo.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/mozambique.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

**Research limit:** the session's shared web-search budget was already used up when this file started, so no keyword search ran. Everything new comes from direct fetches of Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, The Africanvestor, venue sites and official pages. Many Wikivoyage venue entries carry no date (some hotel prices date from 2009); treat them as "existed when last edited".

**Quarter assignment:** Wikipedia lists the bairros of each urban district [2]. Venues addressed only by an avenue (Av. Julius Nyerere, Av. 24 de Julho, Av. Lenine) are grouped under the neighbourhood the avenue crosses; where the source does not name the bairro this is flagged "quarter not confirmed".

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Crime:** armed robbery, assault and burglary have risen since 2025, especially downtown and where the international community goes; take extra care on Avenida Marginal, Avenida Friedrich Engels and the Baixa port area near Jardim Tunduru; limit walking at night (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.general]. FCDO: street crime with knives and firearms; carjacking risk, be alert arriving at or leaving homes after dark; avoid driving alone at night; use bank ATMs, not at night [C safety.general][17].
- **Kidnapping** for ransom targets local business owners; foreigners not targeted so far but not excluded (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.general].
- **Protests** can start at short notice, especially at toll gates (FCDO) [D]; police fired live rounds in Hulene on 5 March 2025 (Amnesty) [C safety.general].
- **Flooding:** low-lying areas flood at short notice in the rainy season (Nov–Apr) and roads can become impassable (FCDO) [17][D].
- **Water:** do not drink tap water (France Diplomatie Santé, 2026-11-09) [16][C daily.water]. Wikivoyage's Mozambique page says the opposite; the official advice is used here [18].
- **Fuel:** in April 2026 most filling stations were closed and sales capped at 1,000 MZN per customer [C daily.transport].
- **Language:** Portuguese is the working language; English is used in business and tourism [D].
- **Health:** reference private facilities are ICOR, Hospital Privado and Clínica de Sommerschield; serious procedures abroad, South Africa first [16][C health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Polana Cimento (A and B), towards Ponta Vermelha
- Feel: a bairro of the central KaMpfumo district [2]; with Sommerschield it is counted among the upscale "bairros nobres" where incomes are highest (Wikipedia PT) [3]. The Africanvestor (2026-09-21) describes corporate executives, consultants and expat professionals in walkable high-rise living [12].
- Rent: young-professional range (Polana Cimento, Coop, Bairro Central) 45,000–95,000 MZN a month (≈ €592–1,250); a good 2-bed in Polana Cimento, Sommerschield or Ponta Vermelha 120,000–180,000 (≈ €1,579–2,368) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [C cost.rent][13]. CASA SAPO asking rents in Polana "A" 110,000–224,000 (≈ €1,447–2,947), accessed 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]. Premium 3-bed 160,000–224,000 (≈ €2,105–2,947) [12]. Furnishing adds 15,000–35,000 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: described as walkable to the business districts [12]. Chapa 15 MZN minimum for about 10 km (fares frozen by the government from Nov 2026) [C daily.transport]; one chapa hub is "Museu" [1]. Yango is the most used ride-hailing app [C daily.transport]; street taxis have no meter, agree the fare first [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: Avenida Friedrich Engels is one of the streets France Diplomatie names for pedestrian care (2026-11-09) [C safety.general]; carjacking at residential gates after dark (FCDO) [17].
- Places:
  - Polana Serena Hotel, Av. Julius Nyerere 1380 (hotel since 1922; Maisha Health Club gym, pool; Polana Bar open 24 h) — Serena Hotels [15]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Mundos, Av. Julius Nyerere next to Hotel Avenida (bar, pizza, South African sport on TV) — Wikivoyage [1]

  - Piri Piri, Av. 24 de Julho at Av. Julius Nyerere (grilled chicken and prawns) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Cristal, Av. 24 de Julho / Av. Tomás Nduda (Portuguese menu) — Wikivoyage [1]; quarter not confirmed

  - Museu de História Natural, Praça Travessia do Zambeze (entry 50 MZN) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][20]; quarter not confirmed

### Sommerschield (I and II)
- Feel: upscale bairro with the city's largest concentration of embassies; farmland subdivided and residential from 1960; density under 93 people per hectare in 1994 (Wikipedia PT) [3]. The Africanvestor (2026-09-21): embassy staff, NGO workers, senior diplomats, affluent local business owners; embassies and corporate headquarters cluster here [12].
- Rent: CASA SAPO 107,500–155,000 MZN (≈ €1,414–2,039), accessed 2026-11-14; a 185 m² 3-bed advertised at US$3,000 (≈ €2,490) [C cost.rent]. Good 2-bed 120,000–180,000 (≈ €1,579–2,368); premium 3-bed 160,000–224,000 (≈ €2,105–2,947) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [12][13]. Amenities (guard, generator, tank, parking) add 5,000–25,000 each [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Yango or agreed-fare taxi [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation]; no Sommerschield-to-Baixa time found.
- Watch: robberies are rising where the international community goes (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.general]; older flats in Sommerschield are named targets of rental scams (fake owner abroad, reservation fee before papers are checked; Africanvestor, 2026-09-21, low confidence) [C housing.search].
- Places:
  - FEIMA craft, flower and food fair, Parque dos Continuadores (corner Av. Julius Nyerere / Av. Mao Tse Tung; bargaining expected; weekend live music) — Wikipedia PT [3]; Wikivoyage [1]; [C social.culture]
  - Jardim dos Cronistas (park) — Wikipedia PT [3]
  - Mamma Mia, Av. dos Mártires da Machava, Parque dos Continuadores (Mozambican and Italian) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hospital Privado, rua do Rio Inhamiara, Sommerschield II (private hospital) — France Diplomatie Santé, 2026-11-09 [16]
  - Clínica de Sommerschield / Policlínicas de Moçambique, rua Pereira do Lago — France Diplomatie Santé [16]
  - Café Sol, Sommerschield II (café, free Wi-Fi) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - ICOR, avenida Kenneth Kaunda 1111 (cardiology institute named as a reference facility) — France Diplomatie Santé [16]; quarter not confirmed
  - American International School of Mozambique, Rua de Rio Raraga 266 (K–12, IB; about 600 pupils from 52 countries in 2015) — Wikipedia [11]; bairro not stated in the source
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille" (clinics and the international schools named for this area [12]); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q10 "La santé et l'école".

### Bairro Central, Alto Maé, Malhangalene and Coop (central belt)
- Feel: KaMpfumo bairros [2]. The Africanvestor (2026-09-21) names Bairro Central, Alto Maé and Malhangalene as the cheapest places for a 1-bed, Polana Cimento, Coop and Bairro Central as young-professional areas with short commutes, cafés, offices, hospitals and Eduardo Mondlane University (UEM) nearby; well-priced flats near UEM and the Av. Eduardo Mondlane corridor stay listed 20–45 days [13][C housing.areas]. Alto Maé borders Mafalala, a historic working-class bairro (Wikipedia PT) [4].
- Rent: 45,000–95,000 MZN for young-professional flats (≈ €592–1,250) [C cost.rent][13]; citywide 1-bed 55,000–95,000 (≈ €724–1,250) [13]; one KaMpfumo studio at 35,000 (≈ €461) on CASA SAPO, 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]. Numbeo's thin 1-bed centre average 34,750 (≈ €457, 17 contributors) [C cost.rent]. Alto Maé sale prices of 14,000–15,000 MZN/m² show much cheaper stock than Sommerschield [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: walking distance or one chapa (15 MZN) to the Baixa [C daily.transport]; paid minivan lifts 15–20 MZN for 4 km [C daily.transport].
- Watch: pickpocketing on busy streets and at chapa stops (Wikivoyage) [1]; France Diplomatie advises avoiding chapas (poor condition, dangerous driving) [C daily.transport]; an NGO paper reports recurrent harassment of women in crowded chapas (2019, old) [C safety.women].
- Places:

### Costa do Sol and Triunfo (northern seafront)
- Feel: Costa do Sol is a bairro of the KaMavota district [2], about 5 km north of the centre along the Marginal [1]. The Africanvestor (2026-09-21): young expat couples and families, upper-middle-class local families, beach access and managed condominiums; newer buildings outpace demand and some lack proper maintenance [12][C housing.areas]. Triunfo is "near the beach, far from the city" (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Rent: CASA SAPO 3-beds 100,000–110,000 MZN (≈ €1,316–1,447), 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; mid-range 2-bed 60,000–130,000 (≈ €789–1,711) [12]; families' 2–3-bed range in Sommerschield, Polana Cimento and Triunfo 100,000–180,000 (≈ €1,316–2,368) [13].
- Getting around: by the Avenida Marginal; Yango or agreed-fare taxi [C daily.transport]. No commute time found.
- Watch: pedestrians should take extra care on the Avenida Marginal (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.general]; beaches are not policed and alcohol is illegal on public beaches (FCDO) [17]; flooding of low-lying roads in the rainy season [17].
- Places:
  - Costa do Sol restaurant, Av. Marginal, Costa do Sol (seafood institution with a few rooms; booking advised at weekends) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Coconut Club, Costa do Sol (beach club from 22:00; Afro-house; listed price 301 MZN) — Wikivoyage [1]; [C social.nightlife]
  - Estádio do Costa do Sol (about 10,000 seats) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Mercado do Peixe, Av. Marginal behind the Sasol garage (fish market; small restaurants cook what you buy) — Wikivoyage [1]; quarter not confirmed
  - Maputo B&B Backpackers, Triunfo, 4 Avenida, House 98 — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Coconut Club) or "Un bon dîner entre amis" (Costa do Sol restaurant); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### KaTembe (across the bay)
- Feel: urban district on the south shore of Maputo Bay, about 30,285 residents (2024 estimate), mainly of Tembe lineage; known for beaches, informal seafood restaurants and skyline views; tourism has grown since the bridge, and a Chinese-assisted master plan is being implemented slowly (Wikipedia) [5]. Wikivoyage: quieter on weekdays [1].
- Rent: Pam Golding lists 2- to 6-bed houses at R 10,626–21,252 a month (rand only), accessed 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]. The Africanvestor calls it a higher-risk area tied to development momentum [12].
- Getting around: the Maputo–KaTembe suspension bridge (inaugurated November 2018, 680 m main span) [5][2]; small "mapapai" water taxis carry foot passengers across the bay [5]. A tarred road runs south from KaTembe to Ponta do Ouro (opened November 2018) [7].
- Places:
  - Maputo–KaTembe bridge — Wikipedia [5][2]
  - Mapapai water-taxi crossing to the city (also the easiest way to the Inhaca ferry) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Catembe Gallery Hotel, Rua B 77 (14 artist-decorated rooms; about €60–380; about 45 min from the centre via public boat and taxi) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [5]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Télétravail" only with a backup connection (no local internet data found).

### Considered and set aside
- **Baixa** (historic downtown): where people work, shop and catch chapas (Mercado Central, the station, the cathedral) [1][2], but Wikivoyage advises steering clear of it at night and France Diplomatie names the port area near Jardim Tunduru [1][C safety.general]. No Baixa rent figure found. Used below for weekday and visiting routes.
- **Matola**: the largest suburb, about 1.03 million people (2017 census), industrial with a port [6]; growing middle-class Mozambican families and budget-conscious expats, gated condominiums along the N4; basic family homes from 40,000 MZN (≈ €526), outer Matola 40,000–65,000, central 2-beds 60,000–130,000 (Africanvestor, low confidence) [C cost.rent][12]. Only one named everyday place was found (Estádio da Machava [2]), so it is not given a full block.
- **Mafalala**: historic KaMaxaquene bairro tied to anti-colonial culture (home of José Craveirinha, Noémia de Sousa, Eusébio, Samora Machel); recurrent flooding; Casa-Museu José Craveirinha and NGO-run guided visits (Wikipedia PT, last edited 2020-02-09) [4]. A visit, not a base: no rent data found.

  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Cristal (Portuguese) or Restaurante 1908 [1]; mid-range dinner for two about 4,000 MZN [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Mimmos takeaway pizza [1].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Visa first: the 30-day e-visa (≈ €40) covers a short stay only; long stays go through the embassy in Paris, overstaying costs US$50 a day [C admin.visa]. A "few months" test needs that long-stay visa before leaving.
- Where to stay first: Malagueta Inn, Av. 24 de Julho 14 (about US$46 with breakfast) [1]; or Fatima's Place, Av. Mao Tse Tung 1317 (dorms from US$15, Jan 2023) [1].
- Day 1: airport taxi about 500 MZN (agree first; drivers open high) [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation]. ## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". The dry, cooler months (July is coolest, 18.8 °C mean) avoid the Nov–Apr rains and the Dec–Mar cyclone season [D].

### One day
- Morning: Mercado Central, Av. 25 de Setembro (opens 07:00) [1]; walk to Praça da Independência for the Cathedral (1936–1944) and City Hall [10][1]; the Fortaleza (1785 remains) and Jardim Tunduru [1][2]. Stay alert near the Baixa port area and Tunduru (France Diplomatie) [C safety.general].
- Midday: the railway station on Praça dos Trabalhadores (1916) [1]; lunch at Mercado Janeta (40–60 MZN) or Cantinho do Brasil [1]. Move by Yango or an agreed-fare taxi (Baixa to Mercado Central about 150–200 MZN) [C cost.negotiation].
- Afternoon: Museu de História Natural (50 MZN) [1]; FEIMA at Parque dos Continuadores (bargain) [1][3].
- Evening: dinner at the Costa do Sol restaurant or at the Mercado do Peixe [1]; Friday or Saturday: live music at the station or Gil Vicente bar [1]. Back by Yango, not on foot along the Marginal [C safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive (airport taxi about 500 MZN) [C daily.transport]; evening at the Polana Serena terrace [15].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Mafalala guided visit by the local NGO and the Casa-Museu José Craveirinha [4], then Xipamanine market by chapa from the Baixa at the morning rush [1] — how most of the city shops and commutes.
- Day 4: KaTembe across the bridge or by mapapai water taxi; seafood on the south shore and the skyline view [5][1].
- Day 5 (trip out): Inhaca Island by the local ferry (Tue, Thu, Sat or Sun 07:30, back about 15:00) [C nature.access]; reefs and the marine biology station [9].
- Day 6 (trip out, overnight): Ponta do Ouro via the bridge and tarred road (dolphins, diving) or Maputo National Park (about 100 km south-east) [7][8].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathedral or Santo António da Polana [10][1]; FEIMA; Núcleo de Arte live music in the evening [1].
