## Items

- [cost.rent] A property-data publisher gives secondary cities (Beira, Nampula, Pemba) a formal-apartment rent range of 20,000–40,000 MZN/month (≈ €263–526), about 250–500 MZN per m² of floor area, versus 65,000–90,000 MZN for central Maputo. — The Africanvestor "Mozambique rents", updated 2026-09-21 (data June 2026), company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Same publisher: Beira city-centre sale prices around 43,000 MZN/m² (≈ €566/m²); fewer expat tenants, higher vacancy and slower resale than Maputo. — The Africanvestor "Mozambique: which area", updated 2026-09-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Hotel examples: Hotel Moçambique from 1,400 MZN (≈ €18) with breakfast; Jardim das Velas near the airport US$110 (≈ €92); undated. — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The Náutico club charges non-members a small entry fee, listed as 20 MZN (≈ €0.26). — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] National fuel rise on 7 May 2026: diesel 116.25 MZN/L (≈ €1.53), petrol up 12.1%; May 2026 inflation 2.32% in one month, taxi fares +23.5%. — The Rio Times 2026-10-20; Observador 2026-06-10, intl-media, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] The municipality has 26 bairros in five administrative posts; the central post includes Chaimite, Macuti and Ponta-Gêa; Munhava and Manga are other posts. — Wikipedia (pt) "Beira (Moçambique)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] About 70% of Beira's population lives in informal settlements, which face the highest climate vulnerability. — ICLEI Africa "Facing climate challenges beyond flooding in Beira" (via search summary), 2025-02-26, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Northern suburbs are described as gentrifying, helped by commercial growth and post-cyclone reconstruction. — The Africanvestor "Mozambique: which area", updated 2026-09-21, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Beira has Mozambique's second port for international cargo and is the outlet for landlocked Zimbabwe, Zambia and Malawi. — Wikipedia "Beira, Mozambique", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] A water treatment plant at Mutua opened in May 2007 and doubled supply to Beira and Dondo; a €62.65M sanitation system was completed in 2012. — Wikipedia "Beira, Mozambique", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Daily buses to Maputo (City Link, Nagi, JM Transport since Sept 2025) leave around 04:30, about 2,500 MZN (≈ €33, Oct 2025). — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] To Chimoio: chapa 300 MZN (≈ €3.9) or small bus 400 MZN (≈ €5.3), about 4 hours; third-class train 250 MZN (≈ €3.3), about 5 hours (Oct 2025). — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] App-based taxis have spread in Beira as in Maputo, drawing thousands of drivers. — Club of Mozambique, 2025-08-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The international airport is about 10 km from town, with LAM, South African Airways and Malawi Airlines flights. — Wikivoyage / Wikipedia "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Cholera outbreaks recur in Sofala (Beira). — France Diplomatie "Santé", updated 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Cyclone Idai damaged all 17 of the city's hospitals and health centres in 2019; the main hospital lost six of its seven operating theatres; over 4,000 cholera cases were confirmed by 10 April 2019. — Wikipedia "Cyclone Idai", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Public universities (Zambeze, Licungo, Higher Institute of Health Sciences) and private ones (Catholic University, Jean Piaget and others); the Escola Portuguesa da Beira follows the Portuguese curriculum. — Wikipedia (en/pt) "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Kidnapping for ransom recurs in Maputo and the main urban centres (notably Beira); foreigners and tourists are not targeted, but the risk cannot be ruled out. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", updated 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [safety.general] FCDO says street crime is increasing in cities beyond Maputo. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14 (advice updated 2026-08-20), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] A travel guide advises not walking alone in the Baixa at night and says most crime is phone or wallet theft at knifepoint (undated section). — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] Friday to Sunday nights are heavy drinking nights; Beira Bar on the beach has a band late in the week and at weekends. — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.sport] Clube Ferroviário da Beira and Sporting Clube da Beira share the 7,000-seat Estádio do Ferroviário. — Wikipedia (pt) "Beira (Moçambique)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Population 533,825 (2017 census) per Portuguese Wikipedia; English Wikipedia gives 592,090 for the same census. — Wikipedia (en/pt), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Cyclone Idai made landfall near Beira on 15 March 2019; the IFRC reported 90% of the area totally destroyed and over 500,000 people without power. — Wikipedia "Cyclone Idai", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Cyclone Eloise landed just north of Beira on 23 Jan 2021: 1,069 homes destroyed and 3,343 damaged across Buzi, Dondo, Nhamatanda and Beira; over 8,700 people in Beira shelters. — Wikipedia "Cyclone Eloise", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] The city sits on marshland at the Púnguè river mouth; drainage channels overflow in Munhava and downtown during floods (Lusa, Jan 2021). — Wikipedia (pt) / Notícias ao Minuto via search, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Coastal protection is in an advanced state of decay; the municipal recovery plan puts the need at about US$90.85M (≈ €75.6M). — ICLEI Africa (Portuguese version, via search summary), 2025, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Savanna Beach, 20–30 km away, is reached by chapa and a small boat; Rio Maria beach (10–15 km) has no facilities. — Wikivoyage "Beira", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [other] The city has been run by the opposition MDM; Albano Carige took office as mayor in 2021 and was re-elected in 2024. — Wikipedia (pt) "Beira (Moçambique)", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium

- [daily.power] A fire at the Munhava substation on 17 June 2023 cut power to more than 160,000 EDM customers in bairros including Chaimite, Maquinino, Esturro, Macurungo, Matacuane, Palmeiras, Manga Loforte and the industrial zone. — Club of Mozambique, 2023-06-17, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] EDM had already named the Munhava substation in Beira as a point that would stay under strain even after planned grid upgrades (2020, old). — Club of Mozambique (AIM, Notícias), 2020-10-28, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In March 2025 about two dozen city-centre filling stations had no petrol and only two stations, both over 15 km out, had fuel; taxi and chapa operators threatened to stop. — Club of Mozambique (Lusa), 2025-03-25, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] Chapa fares are set by the municipal assembly, not bargained: a 2022 rise of 5 MZN per 10 km (e.g. Baixa–Macuti 10 → 15 MZN, ≈ €0.13 → €0.20) was approved in June, and its delay triggered a drivers' strike in July. — Club of Mozambique, 2022-07-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] In March 2024 a Pakistani trader was abducted in Beira and freed after more than 24 hours; police said the kidnappers had mistaken him for his boss and that it was the city's first kidnapping or abduction in almost two years. — Club of Mozambique, 2024-03-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Water coverage in Beira and Dondo is put at 76%; power cuts lower pressure in some areas, and FIPAG bought four generators for the pumping stations. Article date not confirmed (dated 2025 in one search summary). — Club of Mozambique via search summary, accessed 2026-11-15, local-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] A diaspora careers guide says private clinics in Maputo and Beira give better care than the public sector but cost more, and advises health insurance. — Africarrières "Diaspora", updated 2026-08-06, blog, confidence: low
