# Vilankulo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **76 MZN ≈ €1** (dossier rate, Nov 2026); USD at **63.25 MZN = US$1** (city file currency note). Pam Golding shows most rents in rand only; they stay in rand because no rand rate exists in the files, and its rand figures disagree with each other [C cost.rent]. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/mozambique/vilankulo.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/mozambique.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

**Research limit:** the session's shared web-search budget was already used up when this file started, so no keyword search ran. New material comes only from direct fetches (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Pam Golding, The Africanvestor). Many Wikivoyage entries are marked dead links or undated; they are used only where the page still lists them, and flagged.

**Neighbourhood names:** no source read names Vilankulo's bairros (Wikipedia says some are named after the sons of a former chief, without listing them) [4]. The four areas below are the zones the sources themselves use: central town and the Marginal, North Vilankulo, the airport side, and South Beach towards Chibuene [9][1][6].

Frames that apply to every area:
- **Size and pace:** a town about 5 km long with a walkable centre [C daily.transport][4]; 38,432 people in the 2007 census (PT Wikipedia) or 24,433 (2010 estimate, EN) [C social.pace]; city status since 25 Feb 2020 [5].
- **Who comes:** higher-spending tourists, remote workers and retirees seeking a slower pace (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21, low) [10]; the expat presence is small [C business.invest].
- **Safety:** not named by France Diplomatie; national "heightened vigilance" only [C safety.general]. FCDO: street crime increasing in tourist destinations, beaches and offshore islands not policed, alcohol illegal on public beaches [C safety.general][13]. Do not walk alone on beaches at night; swimming is unsupervised with strong currents (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.general]. Only travel by road outside cities in daylight (FCDO) [13].
- **Cyclones:** season roughly December–March [C climate.daily]; Cyclone Favio (22 Feb 2007) destroyed the hospital and reportedly about 80% of the town [C climate.daily].
- **Health:** a hospital exists in North Vilankulo, services unknown; France Diplomatie says serious care should be sought abroad, South Africa first [C health.care].
- **Utilities:** listings stress borehole water, solar and backup generators [C housing.search]. Vodacom 4G "works fine" (Wikivoyage, undated) [1]; no local speed data [C daily.internet].
- **Language:** Portuguese is official; English is used in tourism [D].

## Neighbourhoods

### Central Vilankulo and the Marginal (beachfront)
- Feel: Pam Golding's listings cluster here, "a short walk to the beach", near the big hotels and on Rua do Outubro, mostly walled plots [9][C housing.areas]. Hotels, lodges and dive operators line the beach (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Rent: 3-bed house on the Marginal opposite the beach, R 39,848 a month; furnished 4-bed "Front House" in the centre US$3,500 (≈ €2,913); central 2-suite house with pool R 66,413; garden cottage on Rua do Outubro R 53,131 (Pam Golding, accessed 2026-11-14) [9][C cost.rent]. A T3 at 150,000 MZN (≈ €1,974, area not stated) [C cost.rent]. New furnished 2-bed flats at R 17,267 (area not stated) are the cheapest long let seen [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot [1][C daily.transport]. No taxi, chapa or tuk-tuk fares found.
- Watch: the beachfront is where FCDO's unpoliced-beach and France Diplomatie's night-beach warnings bite [C safety.general]; the 2007 cyclone damage [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Kilimanjaro Coffee Shop, 22M Av. Eduardo Mondlane (coffee, meals, Wi-Fi; updated Jul 2024) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hotel Massunguine, Av. Eduardo Mondlane (27 rooms, 1,800 MZN) — Wikivoyage [1]; [C cost.living]
  - Varanda (beachfront; Portuguese and Mozambican food; suites US$70) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Casa Rex (harbour views, pool; US$95–130 per person) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Tourist Information office and TDM (public computers for internet) — Wikivoyage [1]; [C daily.internet]
  - Sailaway Dhow Safaris (catered dhow trips; day US$70, overnight US$230) — Wikivoyage [1][2]; [C cost.living]
  - Big Blue at Aguie Negra (PADI dive centre, snorkelling, island trips) — Wikivoyage [1]; exact location not given
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (the listed long lets are high-end).

### North Vilankulo
- Feel: one listing describes a secure condominium "opposite the hospital and school", two minutes' walk from the beach and close to shops and restaurants (Pam Golding) [8]. Wikivoyage placed a lodge at the northern end of town (dead link) [1].
- Rent: furnished 4-bed, 3-bath house with pool on a 1 ha plot, R 59,772 (header) or R 57,469 (rates section) a month, 6-month minimum (Pam Golding, accessed 2026-11-14) [8][9].
- Getting around: on foot to the beach [8]; the town is about 5 km long [4].
- Watch: borehole water and "alternate" power listed, no grid detail [8][C housing.search].
- Places:
  - The town hospital (name and services not given; the 2007 cyclone destroyed the earlier building) — Pam Golding [8]; [C climate.daily]
  - A school opposite it (name and level not given) — Pam Golding [8]
  - Only these two unnamed facilities were found; no named shop, church, mosque or restaurant in this part of town was sourced.
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" only in the sense that the school and hospital are close [8]; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Airport side (Bairro Aeroporto)
- Feel: large walled plots: a furnished house "south of the airport" on 10,000 m² with solar, generator and borehole (Pam Golding) [C cost.rent][9]; Casa Chibububo Lodge is at "Bairo du Airporto, Stand 10" (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Rent: furnished 3-suite house, 6-month minimum, US$2,000 a month (≈ €1,664) including water, electricity, wifi, TV, garden and house staff [C cost.rent]; shown at R 39,848 on the listings page [9].
- Getting around: the airport sits on the edge of town (Airlink, LAM, Federal Air; daily regional flights including Johannesburg and Maputo) [1][4][C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Vilanculos airport — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [4]
  - Casa Chibububo Lodge, Bairo du Airporto, Stand 10 (doubles, airport shuttle) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Only two named places were found for this area.
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q10 "Voyager facilement" (next to the airport); Q5 "Télétravail" (utilities included in the listed house).

### South Beach and Chibuene
- Feel: lodges and backpacker places a few minutes south of the centre (Wikivoyage) [1]; Chibuene, 5 km south of South Beach, is an archaeological trading site occupied roughly AD 600–1700 and on a proposed World Heritage tentative list (Wikipedia) [6].
- Rent: no long-let figure found. Short-stay benchmarks: Archipelago Resort at Chibuene US$150 a house per night or US$40 per person sharing; Beach chalets at South Beach US$40–50 per person sharing (Wikivoyage, undated) [1][2].
- Getting around: about 5 minutes south of the centre (Baobab Beach entry) [1]; no transport fares found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the beach warnings above [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - South Beach Vilankulo (beach) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Baobab Beach (huts, dorms, camping; about 5 minutes south of the centre) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Odyssea Dive at Baobab Beach (PADI centre, inflatable boats) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Archipelago Resort, Chibuene (18 houses) — Wikivoyage [1][2]
  - Beach chalets, South Beach — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Chibuene archaeological site (visitor access not stated) — Wikipedia [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" for a trial stay in dorm or shared chalet terms only; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" is not met here (no bar sourced); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Indian Ocean trade history at Chibuene).

## Living: typical weeks

Prices below come only from the city file, the dossier and the sources listed here. "Assumption" marks a non-sourced step. **No food, grocery, restaurant-meal, transport, internet-plan or utility price exists for Vilankulo in any file or source read**, so every weekly figure below is rent plus the few priced activities, and says so. Long-let rents come from one high-end agency only [C cost.rent]. A secondary summary of an expat guide gives US$300–600 a month for a 1-bed or small house, unverified (403) [C cost.rent]; it is shown as a possible floor, never as a fact.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Central Vilankulo**, walkable, with the coffee shop and tourist office as Wi-Fi fallbacks [1]. Internet is the risk: Vodacom 4G is reported to work, with no speed data; national medians 12.6 Mbps mobile [C daily.internet]. Or the **airport-side** furnished house where wifi and power backup are included in the rent [C cost.rent].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office on 4G [1]; coffee and lunch at Kilimanjaro Coffee Shop (Wi-Fi) [1].
  - Tue: home office; late swim at the town beach (no lifeguards) [C safety.general].
  - Wed: morning dive with Big Blue or Odyssea Dive before work (prices not found) [1].
  - Thu: work from Kilimanjaro Coffee Shop [1]; Machilla Magic crafts project (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00) [1].
  - Fri: home office; sunset dinner at Varanda [1].
  - Sat: Machilla Magic until 12:00 [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: dhow day to the islands with Sailaway (US$70) [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no club found; Smugglers, a lodge with a sports bar, pool table and pool (not a club; Tuesday and Thursday volleyball nights), is the only lead (Wikivoyage, formerly a dead link) [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Varanda (Portuguese and Mozambican, beachfront) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; the town's social life is small and tourism-paced (dossier, editorial) [D].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": the Bazaruto Archipelago by dhow (Bazaruto, Benguerra, Magaruque, Santa Carolina; national park since 1971, about 120 dugongs) [2][3][C nature.access]; South Beach [1].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Vilankulo (none sourced).
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic as a weekend. Wikivoyage lists flights out to Kruger and Mpumalanga, and a weekly light-aircraft link with Gorongosa serving the island resorts, with no prices [1][2]. Horse rides on the coast and on Benguerra with Mozambique Horse Safari are the nearest "wild" outing [1][2].
  - "Je reste en ville": Chibuene archaeological site, 5 km south (access not stated) [6]; Machilla Magic [1].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: airport-side furnished house US$2,000 = 126,500 MZN ÷ 4.33 = 29,215 (utilities, wifi and staff included) [C cost.rent]; or the unverified US$300–600 small house = 18,975–37,950 ÷ 4.33 = 4,382–8,764 [C cost.rent]
  - One dhow day trip US$70 = 4,428 [1][C cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ 33,643 MZN a week (≈ €443) on the only verified long let**, or ≈ 8,810–13,192 (≈ €116–174) on the unverified small-house range, in both cases **before food and transport, which have no price in any source**.

Employers that exist on the sources: the dive centres (Big Blue, Odyssea Dive), lodges (Casa Rex, Archipelago Resort, Varanda) and dhow operators [1][2]; whether any of them hires is not known.
- Week:

  - Tue: coffee at Kilimanjaro [1].
  - Wed: horse ride on the coast with Mozambique Horse Safari (price not found) [1].
  - Thu: dinner at Varanda [1].
  - Fri: drinks at Smugglers [1].
  - Sat: South Beach and Baobab Beach [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: dhow day to Magaruque or Benguerra [1][2].

  - Two dhow day trips × US$70 = US$140 = 8,855 [1]

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Central Vilankulo**. The Africanvestor names Vilankulo a top short-term-rental area because Bazaruto draws higher-spending visitors; top beach Airbnbs earn 100,000–400,000 MZN a month in peak season (≈ €1,316–5,263), with heavy seasonality (2026-09-21, low confidence) [10][C business.invest]. Commercial stock exists: a warehouse on the main road into town at R 60,436 a month, and a brand-new beach resort (four cottages, restaurant, pool) to let at R 69,070 (Pam Golding) [9]. Foreigners cannot own land, only buildings plus a DUAT use right (national rule) [Maputo C housing.search].
- Week:
  - Mon: visit the beach-resort and warehouse listings (Pam Golding) [9].
  - Tue: talk to dhow and dive operators about partnerships (Sailaway, Big Blue, Odyssea) [1].
  - Wed: work from Kilimanjaro Coffee Shop [1].
  - Thu: Machilla Magic for local craft supply [1].
  - Fri: dinner at Varanda [1].
  - Sat: South Beach lodges to benchmark prices [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: rest.
  - Monthly: Maputo for paperwork by coach (about 12 h, 1,500 MZN one way, Jan 2024) or by air [C daily.transport].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Smugglers [1]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Casa Rex [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" — home.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend: house rent 29,215–34,642 [C cost.rent] + a monthly Maputo coach round trip 3,000 ÷ 4.33 = 693 [C daily.transport] → **≈ 29,908–35,335 MZN a week (≈ €394–465)**, before food, business premises and set-up costs (not found).

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"

The volunteer-run African Impact Community Project works with a preschool and an orphanage (Wikivoyage) [1] — a sign of local need, not a school option.
- Week:

  - Wed: horse ride with Mozambique Horse Safari [1].
  - Fri: snorkelling trip with Big Blue (age rules not found) [1].
  - Sat: South Beach [1]; Saturday night below.

- Rough weekly spend (two adults, two children):

  - **Total ≈ 68,836 MZN a week (≈ €906)**, before school fees, food and transport (none found).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Visa: the 30-day e-visa (≈ €40) does not cover months; long stays go through the embassy in Paris [C admin.visa].
- Getting there: fly via Maputo or Johannesburg [1][C daily.transport]; or the coach from Maputo, leaving 04:00, about 12 h, 1,500 MZN (Jan 2024); from Beira 7 h, 550 MZN (Oct 2025) [C daily.transport].
- Where to stay first: Hotel Massunguine, Av. Eduardo Mondlane (1,800 MZN a night) [1]; Casa Chibububo Lodge (airport side) [1].
- Day 1: arrive; walk the centre and the beach [1].
- Day 2: SIM and data: Vodacom 4G is reported to work in town [1]; a store starter pack costs about 50 MZN nationally (undated) [11]. Mobile money and banks/ATMs in Vilankulo: not found [C Gaps].
- Day 3: flat hunting: Pam Golding is the only listing source found; most lets want 3- to 6-month minimums [C cost.rent][8]; the unverified guide mentions 2–3 months' deposit for a beach house [C cost.rent]. Visit fees: not found.
- Day 4: ask every landlord about borehole, solar and generator [C housing.search]; check cyclone-season roofing (assumption, after Favio) [C climate.daily].
- Day 5: walk to the hospital in the north to see what it offers [8].
- Day 6: Saturday at Machilla Magic and South Beach [1].
- Day 7: a dhow day trip with Sailaway (US$70) [1].
- Bank account opening: not found.
- Rough first-week spend: 7 nights × 1,800 = 12,600 [1] + coach from Maputo 1,500 [C daily.transport] + SIM 50 [11] + dhow day US$70 = 4,428 [1] + e-visa 3,050 [C admin.visa] → **≈ 21,628 MZN (≈ €285)**, before meals (no price found).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Rain falls mostly November–March, wettest January–February; July is coolest [C climate.daily]. Marlin season runs early October to end of January [1].

### One day
- Morning: coffee at Kilimanjaro Coffee Shop [1]; dive or snorkel with Big Blue or Odyssea Dive (sites 12–30 m, visibility up to 40 m) [1].
- Midday: lunch at Varanda on the beach [1].
- Afternoon: Machilla Magic crafts (Mon–Fri until 16:00, Sat until 12:00) [1]; walk south to South Beach and Baobab Beach (about 5 minutes from the centre) [1].
- Evening: sunset drinks at Casa Rex (harbour views) [1], then Smugglers [1]. No walking alone on the beach after dark [C safety.general].
- Moving between them: on foot; the centre is compact [1]. Entry fees: none sourced.

### One week
- Day 1: fly in via Maputo or Johannesburg; check in [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): dhow day to the Bazaruto Archipelago with Sailaway (US$70, catered): Magaruque or Benguerra, dugong waters [1][2][3]. Park fees: not found.
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): the town market in the morning (shown in a Wikipedia photo; name and hours not found) [4], then the Tourist Information office and a walk through North Vilankulo past the hospital and school [1][8] — the town as residents use it. If a home game is on, Vilankulo F.C. (promoted to the Moçambola in 2009; no fixtures found) [C social.sport].
- Day 5: horse ride on the coast with Mozambique Horse Safari [1].
- Day 6 (trip out): Chibuene archaeological site, 5 km south (access to confirm locally) [6]; or north along the EN1 to Inhassoro, a small fishing port and tourist town facing Santa Carolina (distance not stated) [7].
- Day 7: overnight dhow trip in the archipelago (US$230 with Sailaway) [1], or a quiet beach day and departure.
