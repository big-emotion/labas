# Inhambane / Tofo, Mozambique

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier ("Inhambane / Tofo (suggested addition)"). Inhambane is the provincial capital; Tofo is the beach village about 22 km away. Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **76 MZN ≈ €1** (France Diplomatie, Nov 2026); USD is converted at **63.25 MZN = US$1** (The Rio Times, 2026-11-10), i.e. US$1 ≈ €0.83.
