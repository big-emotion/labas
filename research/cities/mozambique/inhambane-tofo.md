# Inhambane / Tofo, Mozambique

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier ("Inhambane / Tofo (suggested addition)"). Inhambane is the provincial capital; Tofo is the beach village about 22 km away. Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **76 MZN ≈ €1** (France Diplomatie, Nov 2026); USD is converted at **63.25 MZN = US$1** (The Rio Times, 2026-11-10), i.e. US$1 ≈ €0.83. TravelTables prices date from July 2023 and are converted at 2026 rates, so they understate today's euros. - [daily.transport] Inhambane airport has flights from Maputo on most days (LAM, some via Vilankulo) and Johannesburg flights several times a week. — Wikipedia / Wikivoyage "Inhambane", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium

- [safety.general] FCDO says street crime is increasing in tourist destinations; beaches are not policed. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14 (advice updated 2026-08-20), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Swimming is unsupervised and often dangerous; shark attacks are exceptional but possible; avoid walking alone on beaches at night. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", updated 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] A travel guide says many beach resorts are culturally isolated, with about 95% of guests white visitors from South Africa and Europe. — Wikivoyage "Inhambane", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Inhambane's old quarter has a 170-year-old cathedral and an old mosque from the same period, a museum and a central market. — Wikipedia "Inhambane", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Local dishes named: peri-peri prawns and matapa (cassava leaves, peanut, coconut milk, often with crab); Tofo market sells fruit, cashews and capulana cloth. — Wikivoyage "Inhambane", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.pace] Inhambane city had 79,724 residents in 2017 (54,157 in 1997); Tofo about 15,000. — Wikipedia "Inhambane" / "Tofo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Tropical savanna climate, about 950 mm of rain a year, record high 39.8 °C. — Wikipedia "Inhambane", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Cyclone Dineo (15 Feb 2017) brought up to 200 mm of rain to Inhambane province; about 20,000 homes were destroyed nationwide. — Wikipedia "Cyclone Dineo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Tofo is known for whale sharks and manta rays (Manta Reef, Gallaria); Tofinho, 2 km south, is a powerful reef break for experienced surfers. — Wikipedia "Inhambane" / Wikivoyage, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
