# Bata: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XAF** (fixed peg, from the dossier). Bata's only prices are livingcost.org figures in USD, which the city file already converted to EUR at 595 XAF/USD; those EUR figures are used as they stand. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/guinee-equatoriale/bata.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/guinee-equatoriale.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

**Read this first.** Bata has even less material than Malabo. The shared web-search budget ran out after two searches, both about Malabo, so every Bata item below comes from pages fetched by URL. The sources are Wikipedia (EN, ES, FR), Wikivoyage (EN, FR; listings 2018–2024), the Spanish cultural centre's website and the city file. The only named districts in any source are Bata-Centro, Bata-Sur and Bata-Norte, plus place names (Nkoantoma, Bome, Ngoló) that appear in landmark listings. No source describes who lives where, and no rent exists for any district. The neighbourhood blocks are therefore skeletal and must not be read as a guide to where to live.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Taxis and night travel**: the FCDO (updated 2026-01-08) reports robberies and violent assaults on taxi passengers in Bata, especially at night. Crime runs from petty theft to armed robbery, with higher risk in remote parts of town after dark [C safety.general]. Avoid road travel after dark [C daily.transport]. Taxis are unmetered, so agree the fare first [C cost.negotiation]. No in-town fare in XAF was found; livingcost gives about €7.21 for an 8 km ride and €1.59 for a local-transport ticket [C daily.transport].
- **Checkpoints**: roadblocks with ID checks and bribe requests are common. Carry your passport or residence permit (or copies) [C safety.general].
- **Power, water and fuel**: power cuts are described as regular [C daily.power]. The city went several weeks without running water in September 2019 [C daily.water]. Daily nationwide cuts were reported in November 2026 [C daily.power]. The November 2026 fuel shortage hit Bata too [C daily.transport]. Plan for a water tank and a generator (editorial inference from these items).
- **Growth**: fast-growing unplanned suburbs around the city lack drinking water, electricity or postal service [C housing.areas]. A master plan launched in 2016 runs in phases to 2070 [C housing.areas].
- **Arrival**: the e-Visa is valid only for arrival by air at Malabo. Arriving directly in Bata or by land needs an embassy visa (about USD 200) [C admin.visa]. Land borders toward Cameroon and Gabon can refuse entry without a clear reason [C admin.visa].

## Neighbourhoods

### Bata-Centro and the Paseo Marítimo
- Feel: the centre around the Plaza del Reloj (also called Plaza de la Libertad) and the Torre de la Libertad, with the Gran Mercado Central [C social.pace][5]. The seafront promenade runs about 20 km [3][4]. Bata is known for its nightlife and its market [7]. Wikivoyage mentions fine-sand beaches near the centre, but names none [4]. No source describes residents or noise.
- Rent: no district figure. City-centre 1-bedroom (40 m²) is $464/month (≈ 276,100 XAF, ≈ €421); a "cheap" 1-bedroom is $272 (≈ €247); a centre 3-bedroom is $733 (≈ €665). All from livingcost.org, 2026-06-21 [C cost.rent]. Livingcost is the only rent source for Bata.
- Getting around: the centre itself. The airport is about 5 km north [4]. No in-town fare was found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific beyond the city-wide frames.
- Places:
  - Torre de la Libertad, by the promenade (tower lit in changing colours at night; revolving restaurant at the top) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [3][4]; Wikipedia ES/EN [5][7]
  - Plaza del Reloj / Plaza de la Libertad (main square) — Wikipedia ES/FR [5][6]
  - Gran Mercado Central (central market; phone +240 222091493) — Wikivoyage EN [3]; Wikipedia ES/FR [5][6]
  - Catedral de Bata, Santiago y Nuestra Señora del Pilar (Catholic cathedral; ES Wikipedia dates it to 1954, while Wikivoyage and FR Wikipedia say "17th century") — [3][5][6]
  - Centro Cultural de España en Bata, Paseo Lumu Matindi s/n (library Mon–Fri 9:00–20:00 and Sat 9:00–14:00; children's library; workshops) — CCE Bata website [10]; Wikipedia ES [5]
  - Ibis Bata, Paseo Marítimo (hotel with pool, ocean-view rooms, Wi-Fi) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [3][4]
  - Hotel Elik Melen, Calle Patricio Lumumba (45 rooms and suites, free Wi-Fi, two restaurants) — Wikivoyage FR [4] (district not stated)
  - Centro de salud María Gay, Bata-Centro (public health centre) — Wikipedia ES [5]; [C health.care]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (nightlife known city-wide [7]; clubs fill late [C social.nightlife]); Q5 "Création, culture" (CCE Bata); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Bata-Sur and the south-east edge (Nkoantoma)
- Feel: no description of Bata-Sur found. Nkoantoma is a neighbourhood on the south-eastern edge of the city, around the Nkoantoma military barracks [8]. Wikivoyage lists an "Urbanización Ncoantom" and a Church of the Nazarene there [3]. The sources do not say whether Nkoantoma lies inside the Bata-Sur district.
- Rent: no figure; livingcost "cheap" 1-bed $272 (≈ €247) is the nearest proxy [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: not sourced.
- Watch: on 2021-03-07, explosions at the Nkoantoma barracks killed at least 107 people and injured more than 600; 243 structures were badly damaged or destroyed, and munitions were scattered into surrounding areas [8][C safety.general]. No source reports on clearance or rebuilding since. Ask locally before renting nearby.
- Places:
  - Centro de salud María Rafols, Bata-Sur (public health centre) — Wikipedia ES [5]; [C health.care]
  - Nkuantoma Iglesia del Nazareno (church; oddly filed under "Eat" by Wikivoyage) — Wikivoyage EN [3]
  - Urbanización Ncoantom (housing estate; listed under "Sleep") — Wikivoyage EN [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (inferred only from the cheapest city-wide rent proxy). Do not choose without a visit.

## Living: typical weeks

Grocery basket, using livingcost.org Bata prices (2026-06-21, EUR as converted in the city file): milk €1.86/L, rice €1.53/kg, a dozen eggs €2.04, chicken €7.35/kg, tomatoes €2.98/kg, bananas €1.61/kg [C cost.living]. **One adult's weekly basket** = 3 L milk + 2 kg rice + 12 eggs + 1.5 kg chicken + 1 kg tomatoes + 1 kg bananas = 5.58 + 3.06 + 2.04 + 11.03 + 2.98 + 1.61 = **€26.29**. The quantities are an assumption; the prices are sourced.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Bata-Centro**, near the CCE Bata library (open until 20:00 on weekdays) [10] and the GETESA agencies [C daily.internet]. Home internet (TRINET5, 5 Mbps, 33,000 XAF/month) depends on local technical availability [C daily.internet]. Power cuts are regular [C daily.power], so a backup (generator or UPS) matters more than the plan.
- Monday: sign up at a GETESA agency for TRINET5 and buy a data pack (3 GB for 1,000 XAF) [C daily.internet].
- Tuesday: work from home; evening walk on the Paseo Marítimo [3].
- Wednesday: afternoon work session at the CCE Bata library [10].
- Thursday: Gran Mercado Central for food [3]; work from home.
- Friday: lunch at Terraza San Miguel or Restaurante Beirut [C social.food]; evening at the Ibis Bata bar on the seafront [3].
- Saturday: a CCE Bata workshop or event (the agenda in November 2026 listed weekly storytelling sessions to 11 December) [10].
- Sunday: Mass at the cathedral [5]; then an outing.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": La Casa Bar de Chabeli, Bar Oscar F or the Hotel Millennium bar; clubs fill only late and run all night [C social.nightlife]. Use a known driver home [C safety.general].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": the revolving restaurant atop the Torre de la Libertad, or Terraza el Gustazo [C social.food]. Dinner for two is about €38 [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outings (Q8):
  - "À la plage": the Plage des Cocotiers in town [4], or Mbini, 44 km south-west (about 50 km per Wikivoyage), with undeveloped white-sand beaches and seafood [11][C nature.access]. Go and return by daylight [C daily.transport].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Monte Alén National Park (gorillas, chimpanzees, forest elephants, laid-out trails), on the Niefang road inland [C nature.access]. In town, the Complejo Bome preserve [3].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bata (equatorial forest and coast).
  - "Je reste en ville": the promenade, the Torre de la Libertad, the Parque Infantil Tarsicio Obama Nseng, and a match at the Estadio de Bata (35,700 seats, a 2015 Africa Cup of Nations venue) [3][9].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: €57.04 (cheap 1-bed €247 ÷ 4.33) to €97.23 (centre 1-bed €421 ÷ 4.33) [C cost.rent]
  - Groceries: €26.29
  - Eating out: 3 lunch menus × €15.87 = €47.61 [C cost.living]
  - Transport: 5 local tickets × €1.59 + 2 taxi rides × €7.21 = €22.37 [C daily.transport]
  - Utilities: €181 ÷ 4.33 = €41.80 [C cost.living]
  - Internet: TRINET5 33,000 XAF ÷ 4.33 = 7,621 XAF ≈ €11.62, plus two 3 GB packs (2,000 XAF ≈ €3.05) [C daily.internet]
  - Gym: €73 ÷ 4.33 = €16.86 [C cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ €227–267 a week.** Cross-check: livingcost's one-person total with rent is €1,097/month (≈ €253 a week) [C cost.living].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Arrive via Malabo with the e-Visa, then fly to Bata [C admin.visa]. Do not photograph the presidential palace (Palacio África) or military sites [3][C safety.general].

### One day
- Morning: Gran Mercado Central [3], then the Plaza del Reloj and the cathedral [5].
- Midday: lunch at Restaurante Beirut or Terraza San Miguel [3]. No prices were found; livingcost gives about €15.87 for a lunch menu [C cost.living].
- Afternoon: the CCE Bata on the Paseo Lumu Matindi (free library; check the agenda) [10]; then walk the Paseo Marítimo to the Torre de la Libertad [3].
- Evening: dinner in the revolving restaurant atop the Torre de la Libertad as its lights change colour [3][4]. Then, for night owls, La Casa Bar de Chabeli, which gets going late [3]. Travel by known taxi at night [C safety.general].
- Moving around: on foot along the promenade by day. No in-town taxi fare was sourced.

### One week
- Day 1: fly in from Malabo (≈ €183 return) [C daily.transport]; GETESA SIM [C daily.internet]; an evening on the promenade [3].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: the daily-life moment. Be at the Gran Mercado Central early to watch the city stock up [3]; then the CCE Bata children's library when it opens at 15:00 [10] to see the after-school rhythm.
- Day 4: Mbini, 44 km south-west, for the beaches and seafood. Leave and return in daylight [11][C daily.transport].
- Day 5: Monte Alén National Park on the Niefang road, with a guide, for the trails and wildlife [C nature.access].
- Day 6 (Saturday): a match at the Estadio de Bata or La Libertad Stadium [3][9]; Saturday night out [C social.nightlife].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the cathedral or an Assemblies of God service [C social.religion]; the afternoon at the Complejo Bome preserve [3] or the Plage des Cocotiers [4].
- Option: a day trip to Ciudad de la Paz, the official capital since 2026-01-02 (Grand Hotel Djibloho, golf course, AAUCA campus) [12]. It shows where civil-service jobs are heading. The distance was not found; drive only by day.
