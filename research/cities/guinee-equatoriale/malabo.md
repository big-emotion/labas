# Malabo, Equatorial Guinea

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier's fixed peg **655.957 XAF = 1 EUR**. Sources quoting US dollars only are converted at **1 USD = 595.05 XAF**, the rate Spanish Wikivoyage states for 2026-11-14, then to EUR at the peg; those items say "at 595 XAF/USD". The shared web-search budget ran out after one search in this pass, so nearly every item comes from pages fetched directly by URL (see Gaps). Cost-of-living aggregators below disagree widely and some are AI-generated; each is listed separately and never averaged. Expat.com forum posts show only relative dates ("11 years ago"); they are converted to an approximate year and marked "≈".
