# Malabo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XAF** (fixed peg, from the dossier). USD figures are used only where the city file already converted them (at 595 XAF/USD). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/guinee-equatoriale/malabo.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/guinee-equatoriale.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

**Read this first.** Malabo is the thinnest-sourced city in this batch. Only two web searches ran before the session's shared search budget was used up; everything else comes from pages fetched by URL. No dated rent exists for any named neighbourhood except a ≈2015 forum post for Malabo II. Most venue listings come from Wikivoyage and US-embassy "post reports" (Talesmag, 2014–2021). They show that a place existed, not that it is open today. The neighbourhood list below is therefore shorter, and its "Feel" lines are thinner, than for other capitals.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Taxis and night travel**: the UK FCDO (updated 2026-01-08) reports robberies and violent assaults on taxi passengers in Malabo, especially at night. It advises using pre-booked taxis or taxi apps and not sharing a taxi with strangers [C daily.transport]. Risk is higher in remote parts of town after dark, and it advises against road travel at night [C safety.general]. US-embassy staff were barred from taking taxis in 2014, 2017 and 2021 [17][18][21]. A newcomer without a car should budget for a trusted driver or pre-booked rides after dark.
- **Checkpoints and photos**: police and military roadblocks are common. Carry your passport or residence permit (or copies) at all times. Photographing government or military buildings or the airport can lead to arrest [C safety.general]. The presidential palace and its grounds east of the centre are off-limits [3]. Spanish Wikivoyage adds: do not photograph police or the military, and avoid talking politics even in private [4].
- **Fuel**: in November 2026 long queues blocked Malabo's main avenues as drivers waited hours at empty pumps (Diario Rombe, 2026-11-06) [C daily.transport]. Expect taxi supply and fares to move with it.
- **Power and water**: an exile outlet reports daily nationwide power cuts (2026-11-13) [C daily.power]. Drink only bottled water [C daily.water]. No neighbourhood-level reliability data was found.
- **Taxi fares**: about 500 XAF (≈ €0.76) by day anywhere in town, and about 3,000 XAF (≈ €4.57) out to the Marathon Oil compound. Fares go up at night and during police traffic operations [C daily.transport][3]. There is no meter, so agree the fare first [C cost.negotiation].
- **Traffic**: main roads are fluid, but there are bottlenecks around markets and schools at early-morning drop-off, around 13:00 and at the 15:00 pick-up (US-embassy post report, 2020-07) [19]. Typical expat commutes are under 20 minutes [22].
- **Housing**: there is no rental-agency network, many people live in employer housing, and Expat.com showed zero listings on 2026-11-14 [C housing.search]. Every rent below is an aggregator estimate or an old forum figure.

## Neighbourhoods

### Santa Isabel (old town and centre)
- Feel: the colonial grid around the cathedral, with harbour views [C housing.areas][3]. It is the city's restaurant and café district, around the old Banco Nacional between Av. Hassan II, Av. 3 de Agosto and Calle de Rey [C housing.areas][4]. The banks are here [4]. English Wikivoyage calls Malabo walkable [3]. No source describes who lives here or the noise level.
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure. City-centre 1-bedroom (40 m²) is $896/month (≈ 533,000 XAF, ≈ €813), livingcost.org, 2026-06-21 [C cost.rent]. CityCost (2026-11-08, partly AI-based) is far lower: 3-bedroom in the centre 198,100 XAF (≈ €302) [C cost.rent]. The two sources disagree by a factor of two to four.
- Getting around: you are in the centre and can walk [3]. A day taxi anywhere in town costs about 500 XAF [C daily.transport]. The airport is 7–9 km west [6].
- Watch: the palace zone east of the centre is off-limits and must not be photographed [3][4]. The cathedral roof was badly damaged by fire on 2020-01-15 [12].
- Places:
  - Catedral de Santa Isabel, west side of Plaza de la Independencia (neo-Gothic, finished 1916, 40 m towers) — Wikipedia ES [12]; Wikivoyage [3][4]
  - United Methodist church near the cathedral, with an English-language service (church) — US-embassy post report, 2020-07 [19]
  - SGBGE headquarters and Banco Ecológico, Avenida de la Independencia (banks with ATMs; EC/Maestro cards mostly refused) — Wikiviajes ES [4]
  - Sofitel Malabo President Palace, Plaza de la Independencia behind the cathedral (5-star hotel, from US$300) — Wikiviajes ES [4]
  - Terraza D'Ambo (local food: braised fish, octopus, couscous on Fridays; outdoor tables) — Wikiviajes ES [4] (district not stated, listed with the old-town eating area)
  - Aviator Pub & Café, Avenida de la Libertad 322 (café, eat-in and take-away; listing updated 2023-01) — Wikivoyage EN [3] (district not stated)
  - Museo de arte moderno de Guinea Ecuatorial (museum) — Wikipedia ES/EN [5][6] (address not stated)
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (banks and business lunches within walking distance); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" (a small, walkable centre); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Caracolas and the airport road (Carretera del Aeropuerto, Paraíso)
- Feel: the strip between the old town and the airport, where the embassies (France, United States, Germany, China), business hotels and the Spanish cultural centre sit [4][8]. English Wikivoyage describes the Paraíso side road, off the airport road, as a "peaceful neighbourhood" [3]. The cultural centre runs free shows, films, a library and Spanish classes [8].
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; the city-wide ranges in Santa Isabel apply. For a short stay, Suites Mango charges €179 for a standard double, breakfast included [4].
- Getting around: on the main road between the centre and the airport (the airport is 7–9 km west of the centre [6]); a taxi into town costs about 500 XAF by day [C daily.transport]. Malabo II is reached from this road (see below).
- Watch: nothing area-specific was found beyond the city-wide frames.
- Places:
  - Centro Cultural de España en Malabo (CCEM), Carretera del Aeropuerto at Av. de los Parques de África, next to the Spanish embassy, in Caracolas (free events, library of about 20,000 items, free Wi-Fi, Spanish classes A1–B2; open Mon–Sat 9:00–21:00) — Wikipedia ES [8]; CCEM website [9]
  - Malvaloca bar-restaurant and the "Aire" café-restaurant at the CCEM — Wikipedia ES [8]; CCEM website [9]
  - Suites Mango (Mango Suites), off the Carretera del Aeropuerto on the Carretera Principal de Paraíso (boutique hotel, double €179) — Wikivoyage EN/ES [3][4]
  - Ibis Malabo, Carretera del Aeropuerto (hotel, about €135–160; SGBGE ATM that takes Visa) — Wikivoyage EN/ES [3][4]; [C daily.money]
  - El Paraíso restaurant, next to the US embassy (named only as a landmark in the source; no address or listing) — Wikiviajes ES [4]
  - Embassy of France, Carretera del Aeropuerto — Wikiviajes ES [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (the CCEM library has free Wi-Fi; hotels are where internet works [C daily.internet]); Q5 "Création, culture" (CCEM programme); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" (the Paraíso side is described as peaceful); Q10 "Voyager facilement" (close to the airport).

### Malabo II (Malabo Dos)
- Feel: the newer district. In ≈2015, Expat.com forum members called it the place they had heard was best to live, and one couple lived there [23]. It has the 87-hectare Parque Nacional de Malabo [4]. Its character is otherwise unsourced: an AI-generated guide calls it a modern business district, which was not verified.
- Rent: about 500,000 XAF unfurnished and 700,000 XAF furnished a month for an apartment (≈ €762 / €1,067), posted ≈2015 and already a year old then [C cost.rent][23]. Treat it as a historical floor, not today's price.
- Getting around: by taxi (about 500 XAF by day across town) [C daily.transport]. No sourced time to the centre; expat commutes are typically under 20 minutes [22].
- Watch: nothing area-specific was found.
- Places:
  - Parque Nacional de Malabo ("Parque de la Nación"), Malabo II (87 ha, adventure activities, entry 500 XAF ≈ €0.76; built by China, 2016) — Wikiviajes ES [4]; Wikivoyage EN [3]
  - Total petrol station, Malabo Dos (SGBGE ATM that takes international Visa) — Wikiviajes ES [4]; [C daily.money]
  - Supermercado Martínez Hermanos (grocery; named by an embassy family, 2020) — Wikivoyage EN [3]; post report [19] (district not stated)
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants" (the park; the forum's preferred area); Q5 "Salarié·e"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" at the ≈2015 forum rent.

### Ela Nguema (Elá Nguema)
- Feel: a residential barrio linked to the centre by the city's public bus line, the only named bus route in any source [5][6]. Its parish church is San Fernando [C housing.areas]. One of the league clubs, Sony Elá Nguema, plays at the Estadio de Malabo [11]. No source describes its housing, income level or pace.
- Rent: no figure. CityCost's 1-bedroom outside the centre at 131,500 XAF (≈ €201), 2026-11-08, partly AI-based [C cost.rent], is the only "outside the centre" figure available.
- Getting around: public bus to the centre [5]. A single public-transport ticket is about 1,044 XAF (≈ €1.59) per an aggregator [C daily.transport], and a taxi about 500 XAF by day [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific was found. Keep the FCDO's "remote parts of town after dark" warning in mind [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Iglesia de San Fernando, Elá Nguema (Catholic church) — Wikipedia ES/EN [5][6]
  - Bus line centre–Elá Nguema (public bus) — Wikipedia ES/EN [5][6]
  - Estadio de Malabo (15,250 seats; Sony Elá Nguema is one of six clubs playing there; opened 2007) — Wikipedia EN [11] (stadium location not stated)
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (the only bus-served area; the cheapest outside-centre figure); Q7 "Prend son temps" (residential, though this is inferred, not sourced). Thin evidence: visit before choosing.

### Sipopo
- Feel: a purpose-built resort and conference town about 15 km east of Malabo, opened on 5 June 2011 for an African Union summit [7]. It has luxury hotels and gated communities [C housing.areas][3]. Embassy staff came here for brunch buffets, the spa and the beach [17][18].
- Rent: no figure found; the gated-community rents are unpublished. Rio Times (2026-10-02, AI newsroom) also says compound rents are not published [25].
- Getting around: car or taxi only, about 15 km from Malabo [7]. Given the night-taxi warnings [C daily.transport], a car is effectively needed.
- Watch: nothing area-specific was found.
- Places:
  - Sofitel Malabo Sipopo Le Golf (5-star hotel, 200 rooms, golf) — Wikipedia ES [7]; Wikivoyage EN/ES [3][4]
  - Sipopo Conference Palace (Palacio de Conferencias) — Wikipedia ES [7]
  - Sipopo Beach (beach) — US-embassy post report, 2014-09 [17]
  - Sofitel spa and Sunday brunch buffets (named by embassy staff) — post report, 2017-08 [18]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" or "Un bon dîner entre amis" (hotel restaurants); Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Caracolas / airport road**. The CCEM library with free Wi-Fi (Mon–Sat 9:00–21:00) gives a backup connection [8][9], and a day taxi to the centre costs about 500 XAF [C daily.transport]. Internet is the constraint: GETESA home internet is 5 Mbps for 33,000 XAF/month [C daily.internet], and a resident says island internet drops in and out (≈2017) [C daily.internet].
- Monday: book GETESA TRINET5 home internet in person; the office is open on weekdays only (8:00–16:00) [C daily.internet][4]. Afternoon work session at the CCEM library [8].
- Tuesday: work from home on GETESA mobile data (7.5 GB weekly pack, 4,500 XAF) [C daily.internet]. Evening: sunset walk on the Paseo Marítimo, the seafront boardwalk with exercise equipment [19][21] (taxi, about 500 XAF).
- Wednesday: CCEM library in the morning; lunch at the CCEM's Malvaloca [8].
- Thursday: work from home; groceries at Supermercado Martínez Hermanos [3][19] (taxi).
- Friday: lunch at Terraza D'Ambo, which serves couscous on Fridays [4]; a free evening show or film at the CCEM [8].
- Saturday: Parque Nacional de Malabo in Malabo II (500 XAF) [4].
- Sunday: weekend outing (below).
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": L'Atelier (live music, "popular for nightlife") or the Olympus Skybar or Kristal Lounge [C social.nightlife][3][4]. Go home in a pre-booked taxi [C daily.transport].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Luna (seafood, Atlantic view, open 7:30–23:00) [4] or L'Oriental (Lebanese; embassy family, 2020) [19]. A mid-range dinner for two costs about 41,000 XAF (≈ €62.50) [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home, streaming over GETESA. Streaming worked "sometimes only partially" in 2017 [18].
- Weekend outings (Q8):
  - "À la plage": Arena Blanca near Luba, about an hour's drive south (Luba is 52 km from Malabo) [14][27]. Lonely Planet warns that the car-park bars get rowdy on Sundays and that drunk drivers use the road back [27]. Nearer, Sipopo Beach is about 15 km east [7][17].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Moka (1,380 m) and the Ilachi waterfall, about an hour's hike through forest with river crossings [15][3]. A rural travel permit may be needed: Spanish Wikivoyage says the Ministry of Tourism rarely grants them (1,500 XAF stamp in 2017) [4], while embassy staff described free permits for Ureca and Pico Basilé (2017) [18].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Malabo (a humid volcanic island) [C climate.daily].
  - "Je reste en ville": cathedral and old town, Parque Nacional, CCEM, and a league match at the Estadio de Malabo [11].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: 46,882 XAF (livingcost "cheap" 1-bed, 203,000/month ÷ 4.33) to 123,095 XAF (centre 1-bed, 533,000 ÷ 4.33) [C cost.rent]
  - Groceries: 14,176 [C cost.living]
  - Eating out: 3 budget meals × 5,099 + 2 cappuccinos × 2,845 + 4 beers × 999 = 24,983 [C cost.living]
  - Taxis: 10 day rides × 500 = 5,000 [C daily.transport]
  - Internet: weekly mobile pack 4,500 + home internet 33,000 ÷ 4.33 = 7,621, so 12,121 [C daily.internet]
  - Gym: 20,100 ÷ 4.33 = 4,642 [C cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ 107,800–184,000 XAF (≈ €164–281) a week**, plus utilities, which livingcost puts at $201/month (≈ €182, so ≈ €42 a week) [C cost.living].
  - The aggregators disagree widely, so this is an order of magnitude, not a budget.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: a hotel on the airport road. The Ibis is about €135–160 a night and has a Visa-capable ATM [3][4]. Hotel de Federaciones, by the stadium in the Ciudad Deportiva, has singles at €120 with Wi-Fi [4]. A month on Airbnb is an AI estimate of $1,055 (≈ €957) [C cost.rent].
- Arrival: the e-Visa is valid only for arrival by air at Malabo. Print the approval letter and make sure your passport is stamped [C admin.visa].
- Monday: SIM card. Foreigners can buy one only at the GETESA office (Mon–Fri 8:00–16:00), with a photo and an ID copy; the process is slow [C daily.internet][4]. Muni is the other operator embassy staff name (2017, 2021) [18][20]. Buy a 7.5 GB weekly pack (4,500 XAF) [C daily.internet].
- Tuesday: cash. Withdraw at an SGBGE ATM (Ibis, airport, Total station in Malabo Dos, headquarters) [4]. Bring euros, sterling or dollars to change at banks [C daily.money]. **Mobile money: not found** in any source [C].
- Wednesday: start the flat search. There is no agency network and no online listings [C housing.search], so ask your employer, the Expat.com forum (5,828 members) [C community.expat] and contacts met at the CCEM [8]. Visit Malabo II and the airport road by day.
- Thursday: more visits. **Visit fees, advance rent and deposit customs were not found in any source**; ask before you pay anything.
- Friday: bank account. **No source describes how a foreigner opens one.** Ask SGBGE or Banco Ecológico in person [4].
- Saturday: Parque Nacional (500 XAF) and the Paseo Marítimo [4][19].
- Sunday: Mass at the cathedral; lunch at La Luna (9,500) [4].
- Residence: for longer stays, apply for the one-year permit from the Ministry of National Security; local police stations process extensions [C admin.residency].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": L'Atelier [C social.nightlife].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Luna or Terraza D'Ambo [4].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the hotel.
- Weekend outings (Q8): Sipopo Beach is the easy one ("À la plage") [17]. Moka needs a car and possibly a permit ("En montagne, en forêt") [4][18]. Savannah or desert is not realistic.
- Rough first-week spend (one person):
  - Day costs: 14 taxi rides × 500 + data 4,500 + 7 budget meals × 5,099 + 3 beers × 999 + 2 cappuccinos × 2,845 + half a grocery basket 7,088 = 62,968 XAF (≈ €96) [C]
  - Lodging: 7 nights at Hotel de Federaciones (€120) = €840, so **≈ €936 for the week**; at a pro-rated Airbnb month (€957 ÷ 4.33 = €221, AI estimate), **≈ €317**.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". The e-Visa only works for arrival by air at Malabo [C admin.visa]. Do not photograph the palace, police, the military or the airport [C safety.general][4].

### One day
- Morning: Plaza de la Independencia and the Catedral de Santa Isabel (exterior; roof fire in 2020) [12]. Walk the old-town grid with its harbour views [3]. Coffee in the café district around the old Banco Nacional [4].
- Midday: lunch at La Luna (seafood, Atlantic view, lunch menu 9,500 XAF) [4] or Terraza D'Ambo [4].
- Afternoon: taxi (about 500 XAF) to the CCEM in Caracolas, for an exhibition and the library (free entry) [8][9]. Then taxi to the Parque Nacional de Malabo in Malabo II (entry 500 XAF) [4].
- Evening: sunset on the Paseo Marítimo, which is best in the dry season [19]. Dinner at L'Atelier (live music) [4]. Return in a pre-booked taxi [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1 (a weekday): arrival. Buy a SIM at the GETESA office before 16:00 [4]; withdraw cash at an SGBGE ATM [4]. Evening in the old town.
- Day 2: the daily-life moment. Be on the road at 7:30–8:00 to see the school-run bottleneck [19]; then the old town, banks and CCEM; and at 13:00 the lunchtime traffic [19]. This is the rhythm a resident lives with.
- Day 3: Moka day trip by hired car. The Bioko Biodiversity Protection Program centre, the Ilachi waterfall (over 250 m, about an hour's hike, fog can hide it), and guides arranged by the research centre to the Ilyadi falls and Lake Biao [3][4]. Lunch at Trocadero [19][21]. Check the permit rules first [4][18].
- Day 4: Luba (52 km south; seafront promenade, 4 km white-sand beach) [14] and Arena Blanca [27]. Go on a weekday to avoid the Sunday crowds and drunk driving [27].
- Day 5: Sipopo, 15 km east, for the beach and lunch at the Sofitel [7][17]. From November to February/March, an overnight turtle camp at Ureca instead (the wettest place in Africa, so bring rain gear) [13][C nature.access].
- Day 6 (Saturday): a league match at the Estadio de Malabo, home of six local clubs [11]; dinner with friends at La Luna or L'Oriental [4][19].
- Day 7 (Sunday): a second daily-life moment. Take the public bus from the centre to Ela Nguema for Mass at the Iglesia de San Fernando [5][6]. Afternoon in the Parque Nacional [4].
- Option: a day or two in Bata. Flights run a couple of times a day, about 120,000 XAF return (≈ €183); domestic carriers' safety standards are not enforced [C daily.transport][3].
