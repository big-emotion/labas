# Manzini: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = E18.7** (lilangeni pegged 1:1 to the rand; the city file uses the South Africa dossier's Numbeo-derived rate of 2026-11-13). Seeff quotes rents in rand (R = E). livingcost.org figures are already in EUR in the city file. `[Z tag]` = an item in `research/cities/eswatini/manzini.md`; `[M tag]` = an item in `research/cities/eswatini/mbabane.md` (national items); `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/eswatini.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read". Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- **Manzini's online rental market is thin.** On 2026-11-15, Seeff showed only 4 Manzini rentals and 9 in Matsapha [1][2]. RE/MAX showed 4 Manzini houses and 1 Matsapha house, by town only [4]. Each neighbourhood rent below is therefore one or two asking prices, not a range. livingcost.org models Manzini rents about 40% below Mbabane's [Z cost.rent].
- **Distances are straight-line**, from OpenStreetMap coordinates, measured from Manzini Market [6]. Downtown is small: about 15 minutes on foot end to end [7].
- **The bus rank is in flux.** Since 27 June 2026, routes run from temporary ranks while the new rank is built:
  - western routes (KaKhoza, Ngwane Park, Fairview, Zakhele, Mbabane, Nhlangano, Mankayane) leave from the Old Trade Fair Site;
  - eastern routes leave from the Satellite Bus Rank at the Manzini Traffic Interchange;
  - cross-border transport leaves from Riverstone Mall [29].
  - The new rank's cost rose to E86 million, with completion expected "the following February" [Z daily.transport].
- **Fares:**
  - Regulated bus fares from 16 Nov 2026: E12.50 for the first 8 km. Matsapha–Manzini is the source's own example [Z daily.transport].
  - Manzini–Mbabane (35 km) was E30 by kombi before the revision [Z daily.transport].
  - A metered taxi costs up to E60 for 3 km [Z daily.transport].
  - Richer suburbs have little or no kombi service, so take a metered taxi there [7][Z cost.negotiation].
- **Safety:** FCDO advises against walking downtown after dark; crime rises before Christmas [Z safety.general]. Wikivoyage says to watch belongings at ranks and avoid them at night [8].

## Neighbourhoods

### Downtown (CBD) around the market and the malls
- Feel:
  - The commercial, agricultural and transport hub of the country [Z work.jobs]. A busier, more commercial town than Mbabane [D].
  - The council's CEO says the people who use the town each day far outnumber its ratepayers (2026-07-16) [Z business.admin].
  - Downtown is small and walkable [7].
- Rent:
  - Seeff "Manzini CBD" 3-bed R8,800 (≈ €471) [1].
  - RE/MAX "Manzini" houses [4]: 1-bed E2,420 (≈ €129); 3-bed E10,000 (≈ €535, available immediately) and E25,000 (reduced). Suburb not given.
  - livingcost.org models a centre 1-bed at €131 [Z cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot [7]. Western-route kombis leave from the Old Trade Fair Site, about 0.9 km north of the market; cross-border transport leaves from Riverstone Mall [6][29].
- Watch:
  - "avoid walking in the downtown areas … after dark" (FCDO, read 2026-11-14) [Z safety.general].
  - Avoid ranks at night [8].
- Places:
  - Manzini Market (Mon–Sat 6:00–18:00: produce, ironwork, beadwork, basketry). Wikivoyage, listing updated Aug 2020, gives the address as Mhlakuvane St [7]; OpenStreetMap gives Mancishane Street [6].
  - Gil Vicente Restaurant, Ngwane Street, next to The George Hotel (Portuguese, family-friendly; Tue–Sun 11:00–23:00, listing Aug 2020) — Wikivoyage [7]; OpenStreetMap [6]
  - The George Hotel (hotel with pool, restaurant and bar) — Wikivoyage [7]
  - Bhunu Mall, Sandlane Street, with an FNB agency (mall, bank) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - Riverstone Mall, Stanley Street (mall; cross-border taxi point since June 2026) — OpenStreetMap [6]; Times of Eswatini [29]
  - Spar, Ngwane Street (supermarket) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - The Hub (large Spar selling imported goods at high prices, plus coffee shops) — Wikivoyage [8]; [Z cost.living]
  - Mavuso Sports Centre / Trade Fair Ground, about 0.9 km north (5,000-seat stadium; home of Manzini Wanderers and Moneni Pirates; venue of the Mavuso trade fair) — Wikipedia [11][12]; [Z social.culture]
  - St Theresa High School, Tenbergen Street (school) — OpenStreetMap [6]
- Fits:
  - Q5 "J'entreprends": the market, the malls and the trade-fair ground are all within a kilometre.
  - Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" only by Eswatini standards: busier than Mbabane [D], but the centre empties after dark [Z safety.general].
  - Q3 "Serré": the cheapest 1-bed listing (E2,420) [4].

### Fairview (north-west of the centre)
- Feel:
  - Built in 1964 as the country's first integrated residential area, later split into Fairview North and South [9][Z housing.areas].
  - It has a dense cluster of schools and churches [6].
  - Active church life: FEA Fairview launched a couples' fellowship in Oct 2025 [30].
- Rent: no rental found. Sale listings: a Fairview North 5-bed for R1,940,000 [3]; a 3,000 m² plot in Fairview South for R1,100,000 [3].
- Getting around: Fairview Church of Christ is about 1.3 km north-west of the market [6]. Wikivoyage says Fairview is harder to reach by kombi [7], but since June 2026 a Fairview route runs from the Old Trade Fair Site [29].
- Places:

### Ngwane Park (south)

### Matsapha and Tubungu Estate (the industrial town to the west)
- Feel:
  - The main industrial estate since 1965, on the South Africa–Mozambique corridor, about 11 km from Manzini [10][Z work.jobs].
  - Textiles employ over 22,000 people, but the sector warned of retrenchments in Feb 2026 [Z work.jobs][Z cost.salary].
  - One-room "titimela" rental blocks for factory workers are spreading around Matsapha, including Eteni and Logoba, and councils say they are too dense to service (2025-11-09) [15][Z housing.areas].
- Rent:
  - Seeff Tubungu Estate (read 2026-11-15) [2]: 1-bed R5,000 (≈ €267); 2-bed R7,500 (≈ €401), five identical listings, which suggests a new block; 3-bed R13,000; 4-bed R16,000.
  - Seeff Matsapha CBD 3-bed R8,500 (Rented) [2].
  - RE/MAX Matsapha 3-bed house E10,000 [4].
  - Tubungu plot: R600,000 [5].
- Getting around: Tubungu is about 6.4 km west-south-west of Manzini Market [6]. Matsapha–Manzini falls in the first 8 km fare band, E12.50 [Z daily.transport].
- Watch:
  - Police warned of escalating robberies ahead of the 2025 festive season. The one incident described is a shop near Sigodvweni, Matsapha, broken into through the roof; Tubungu is named only as where the owner lives (Times of Eswatini, 2025-12-15) [16].
  - Electricity is about 40% of factory costs [Z work.jobs], so tariff rises hit local jobs.
- Places:
  - Ruyi 1 Supermarket, Police College Road (supermarket) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - N.K.S. Restaurant & take-away, Police College Road; S&B Restaurant, Ludvonga Street, industrial site (eateries) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - Evangelical Church, Airport Road (church) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - Swazi National High School, D38 (school) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - University of Eswatini, Kwaluseni (main campus) — Wikipedia [13]; Ezulwini Valley reaches down to Kwaluseni [17]
- Fits:
  - Q5 "Salarié·e" in industry or logistics: you live next to the estate.
  - Q4 "En couple": 2-bed units at a fixed E7,500.
  - Q3 "Moyen".

### Malkerns (lifestyle valley, about 16 km west)
- Feel:

  - Home of House on Fire and the MTN Bushfire festival, held since 2007 [18][24].
  - Malkerns Country Club hosts the MoMo Xperience festival and the Eswatini Farmers Market [Z nature.access][M social.nightlife].
- Rent: Seeff Malkerns 2-bed R10,000 (≈ €535); 3-bed R25,000; 4-bed R30,000 (reduced) [22]. An earlier Seeff 2-bed at R14,000 was available from 1 Jan 2026 on a 12-month lease (search-result summary, not re-read) [22].
- Getting around: Malandela's is about 15.7 km west of Manzini Market in a straight line [6]. No fare was sourced.
- Places:
  - Malandela's, MR27 (farmhouse restaurant and pub, guesthouse, Gone Rural shop, botanical garden trails) — malandelas.com [23]; OpenStreetMap [6]
  - House on Fire (performance venue and arena) — house-on-fire.com [24]
  - Swazi Candles craft centre (workshop, market, Yebo Art Gallery, Sambane Tea Garden with children's playground; daily 8–17) — swazicandles.com [25]
  - Save Mor (Spar), MR103 (supermarket) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - Light Primary School, MR103 (school) — OpenStreetMap [6]
  - University of Eswatini, Luyengo campus (agriculture), between Malkerns and Bhunya — Wikipedia [13]
- Fits:

  - Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" on festival nights only.
  - Q5 "Création, culture": the arts venue and the craft centre.
  - Q8 "En montagne, en forêt": Mlilwane is next door.
