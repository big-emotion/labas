# Mbabane, Eswatini

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the lilangeni (E, SZL) is pegged 1:1 to the South African rand. The Eswatini dossier has no EUR rate, so EUR conversions use the South Africa dossier rate **18.7 ZAR (= E) per €1** (Numbeo-derived, 2026-11-13); Wikivoyage's "as of October 2026" rate (€1 ≈ E19) is close. livingcost.org figures are in USD and are converted at **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (Zimbabwe dossier's indicative rate). The shared web-search budget was exhausted, so no search-engine queries ran in this pass: sources are pages reached by known URL and the Times of Eswatini's own site search. National items (fuel, bus fares, electricity, MoMo, visas) are recorded here and only the ones that bite differently are repeated in manzini.md.
