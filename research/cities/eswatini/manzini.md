# Manzini, Eswatini

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the lilangeni (E, SZL) is pegged 1:1 to the South African rand. The Eswatini dossier has no EUR rate, so EUR conversions use the South Africa dossier rate **18.7 ZAR (= E) per €1** (Numbeo-derived, 2026-11-13). livingcost.org figures are in USD, converted at **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (Zimbabwe dossier's indicative rate). - [cost.rent] Modelled rents (not listings): 1-bed (40 m²) in the centre USD 152/month (≈ €131), cheaper 1-bed USD 96.50 (≈ €83); 3-bed (80 m²) in the centre USD 277 (≈ €238), cheaper 3-bed USD 177 (≈ €152) — about 40% below the same site's Mbabane figures. — livingcost.org Manzini, updated 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled monthly budget with rent: USD 494 (≈ €425) for one person and USD 1,254 (≈ €1,078) for a family of four. — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled staples: milk USD 1.26/L (≈ €1.08), bread USD 0.96/500 g (≈ €0.83), rice USD 1.84/kg (≈ €1.58), eggs USD 1.17/dozen (≈ €1.01), chicken breast USD 3.08/kg (≈ €2.65), potatoes USD 0.62/kg (≈ €0.53). — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled going-out prices: lunch menu USD 4.14 (≈ €3.56), dinner for two USD 28 (≈ €24), 0.5 L beer in a pub USD 2.17 (≈ €1.87) or USD 0.62 from a shop (≈ €0.53), cinema USD 3.71 (≈ €3.19), gym USD 26.20/month (≈ €22.50). — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled utilities USD 45.30/month (≈ €39) single, USD 69.80 (≈ €60) family; unlimited 50 Mbps+ internet USD 30.60/month (≈ €26). — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fuel from 1 Oct 2026 (national): unleaded 95 E29.40/L (≈ €1.57), diesel E32.25/L (≈ €1.72); fuel is up 51.2% since April 2026. livingcost.org's older model says USD 1.35/L. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-10-01 and 2026-11-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Wikivoyage says the Hub supermarket in Manzini sells imported goods at "an appropriately inflated price". — Wikivoyage "Eswatini", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Modelled median salary after tax: USD 397/month (≈ €341). — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Textile and apparel (concentrated around Matsapha, next to Manzini) employs over 22,000 emaSwati; the union seeks to raise average monthly pay from E2,400 (≈ €128) to about E3,900 (≈ €209) over three years; some firms moved hourly rates from E15 to E18 (≈ €0.80 to €0.96). — Times of Eswatini, 2026-02-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] Kombis have no meter and fixed prices; Wikivoyage advises asking the driver the fare before boarding, and using a metered taxi for the richer suburbs that kombis serve poorly. — Wikivoyage "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Coates Valley, Extension 6 and Madonsa (east of downtown) are upscale to middle class; Helemesi Estates (north, early 1990s) is middle class; Fairview (1964) was the first integrated area; Ngwane Park (south) is lower to middle class; KaKhoza (west, towards Mbabane) looks like an informal settlement. — Wikipedia "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Informal one-room rental blocks ("titimela"), built for low-paid factory and retail workers, are spreading in Manzini's peri-urban areas (KaShali, Nhlambeni, Sicelwini, Makholweni, Ticantfwini) and around Matsapha; councils say they are too dense for water, sewer and road services. Rents not given. — Times of Eswatini, 2025-11-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] A property analyst reports steady demand for high-footfall retail space in central Manzini and Mbabane, and almost no supply of industrial sites over 1 ha in Matsapha. No prices given. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-04-18, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Manzini is the commercial, agricultural and transport centre; Matsapha, the main industrial estate, sits on its western edge. — Wikipedia "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Textile employers warned of "imminent retrenchments": Drake Clothing closed (about 350 jobs), Golden Jubilee Textiles stopped (about 650), and Kasumi Apparels closed in 2023 (1,782) before reopening. Electricity is about 40% of factory costs. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-02-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] More than 1,000 people queued at Manzini Regional Police Headquarters for a limited number of Constable II posts. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-11-03, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.admin] Manzini council posted a E7.7 million deficit (≈ €412,000) for the year to March 2024 after government subventions stopped in 2021; it leans increasingly on property rates, and its CEO notes ratepayers are far fewer than daily users of the town. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-07-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] A new Manzini bus rank is under construction; its cost rose from E66 million to E86 million (≈ €4.6 million), with completion expected the following February. The Bosco bridge cost about E90 million. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-07-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Bus fares from 16 Nov 2026: first 8 km (e.g. Matsapha–Manzini) E12.50 (≈ €0.67), up from E10; Manzini–Mbabane (35 km) was E30 by kombi (≈ €1.60) and E25 by bus (≈ €1.34) before the change. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-11-10 and 2026-08-18, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] livingcost.org models a local ticket at USD 0.62 (≈ €0.53), a monthly pass at USD 37.80 (≈ €32.50) and an 8 km taxi at USD 25 (≈ €21.50); the Times reports a regulated 3 km taxi maximum of E60 (≈ €3.20), proposed E72. — livingcost.org 2026-06-21 / Times of Eswatini 2026-08-18, company / local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Downtown is walkable in about 15 minutes; MobiRide is listed as a local ride-hailing app. Wikivoyage still lists Matsapha airport with Airlink flights, which conflicts with Wikipedia (airport replaced by King Mswati III International, about 57 km east). — Wikivoyage "Manzini" (listings c. 2020) and Wikipedia "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, blog/academic, confidence: low
- [health.care] FCDO: basic care, shortages of common medicines, serious cases likely sent to South Africa; ambulance 977 or 933 (national). Raleigh Fitkin Memorial (RFM) Hospital is in Manzini; the only recent Times item found on it concerns a clerk charged over an alleged E133,754 fraud (2026-08-21). — UK FCDO health page, accessed 2026-11-14 / Times of Eswatini site search, official / local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Modelled GP visit: USD 20.20 (≈ €17.40). — livingcost.org Manzini, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Nazarene High School in Manzini is known for its choir; St Paul's Methodist Church and School sits on a hill north of downtown. Fees not found. — Wikipedia "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO advises to "avoid walking in the downtown areas of Mbabane and Manzini after dark"; theft and muggings mainly on town edges and at night, rising before Christmas. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Wikivoyage advises watching belongings at bus ranks, avoiding ranks at night, and taking a taxi after dark (save drivers' numbers, expect possible overcharging). — Wikivoyage "Eswatini", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The national police commissioner, speaking in Manzini, criticised officers who wait in offices instead of preventing crime. — Times of Eswatini, 2026-06-08 (headline/snippet), local-media, confidence: low
- [social.food] Manzini Market (Mhlakuvane St) opens Mon–Sat 6 am–6 pm with farm produce, ironwork and crafts (beadwork, basketry); the centre has a Portuguese restaurant (Gil Vicente) plus Chinese and Indian options, and Manzini Mall. — Wikivoyage "Manzini", listings c. 2020, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Manzini hosts the Mavuso International Trade Fair (since 2004); the Eswatini Stock Exchange ran a business seminar at the 2026 edition. — Wikipedia "Manzini" / Times of Eswatini 2026-09-01, academic/local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Christian radio station Voice of the Church is based in Manzini, and the Roman Catholic diocese is named after the city. — Wikipedia "Manzini" and "Mbabane", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Warmer than Mbabane: humid subtropical, mean daily max about 25.7 °C (28.5 °C in January, 22.4 °C in June), June minimum about 8.9 °C; ~923 mm rain, February wettest, June almost dry (8 mm). — Wikipedia "Manzini", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Malkerns, home of the big MoMo Xperience and Farmers Market events at its country club, and the Ezulwini Valley lie between Manzini and Mbabane on the MR3. — Times of Eswatini 2026-03-27 / Wikipedia "Mbabane", local-media/academic, confidence: medium
