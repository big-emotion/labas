# Manzini, Eswatini

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the lilangeni (E, SZL) is pegged 1:1 to the South African rand. The Eswatini dossier has no EUR rate, so EUR conversions use the South Africa dossier rate **18.7 ZAR (= E) per €1** (Numbeo-derived, 2026-11-13). livingcost.org figures are in USD, converted at **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (Zimbabwe dossier's indicative rate). National items (fuel, electricity, MoMo, schools, visas, LGBT law) are in mbabane.md; only those that bite differently in Manzini are repeated here.
