# Le Caire: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions: EUR at the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 59 EGP** (dossier, Numbeo Cairo 2026-11-14). "City file" means `research/cities/egypte/le-caire.md` (curated 2026-11-14) and the item tag cited. Wikivoyage listings carry their own "updated" stamp, given in brackets; many are old, so a listing proves a place existed, not that it still opens the same hours or charges the same price. The session's web-search budget ran out after one search, so this sheet rests on direct page fetches (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, House Solution Egypt) plus the city file.

City-wide watch points that apply to every neighbourhood below (not repeated in each block):
- Police check ID and may detain foreigners without a passport and visa or a copy (UK FCDO, travel advice updated 2026-10-27; city file [daily.transport]).
- Amnesty documented mass police stops and ID checks in Cairo and Giza that targeted Black people (Amnesty International, 2024-06-19), and plain-clothes arrests of Sudanese, South Sudanese and Syrian refugees in Cairo, Giza and 6th of October between late Dec 2025 and 5 Feb 2026 (Amnesty, 2026-02-16) (city file [rights.racism]). For a Black diaspora reader this is the first point to weigh, whichever neighbourhood.
- Women: FCDO advises care in taxis alone at night and sharing live location (2026-10-27); Arab Barometer 2020 found 63% of women harassed in the past year (Egyptian Streets, 2025-11-09) (city file [safety.women]).
- Opening hours: since 26 Apr 2026 cafés and restaurants may open 5 am–1 am, shops 7 am–11 pm (Egyptian Streets, 2026-04-27; city file [social.nightlife]).

## Neighbourhoods

### Zamalek (Gezira island)
- Feel: affluent, "pseudo-European" residential island with many expatriate residents, small enough to walk, green, on the Nile (Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira, read 2026-11-14). Upscale, with galleries and restaurants (lepetitjournal.com, city file [housing.areas]). Less green space than Maadi, parking difficult (House Solution Egypt 2026 guide, updated 2026-09-11).
- Rent: furnished Nile-view 2-bed E£45,000–110,000/month (≈ €763–1,864); 3-bed E£70,000–180,000 (≈ €1,186–3,051) (House Solution Egypt, 2026-09-11; city file [cost.rent]). No 1-bed figure for Zamalek found.
- Getting around: central island next to Downtown; getting on or off at rush hour adds about 20 minutes (House Solution Egypt; city file [housing.areas]). Uber or Bolt in central Cairo about E£60–120 per trip (≈ €1.0–2.0) (city file [daily.transport]). A Facebook-group commenter recommends Zamalek as quieter, with a car and driver rather than driving (city file [daily.transport], low confidence).
- Places:
  - Diwan (bookstore with small café), 159 26th of July St — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira
  - Sip (specialty coffee with stable Wi-Fi for working, 8 am–11 pm; drinks E£80–100 [Jan 2023]), 18 Kamal Khaleel St — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira
  - Sunny Market (supermarket), Al-Aziz Osman St — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira
  - Gezira Sporting Club (sports club: pool, tennis, squash) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira; also House Solution Egypt 2026 guide
  - Cairo Opera House (performing arts, shows most nights), El Borg Gezira — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira; city file [social.culture]
  - El-Sawy Culture Wheel (outdoor concerts, no alcohol), 26 July St — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira
  - Pub 28 (British-style pub; Stella E£100, mains from E£300 [Oct 2024]), 28 Shagaret El Dorr St — Wikivoyage Cairo/Gezira
  - Lycée français du Caire, Zamalek primary site — LFC homepage via city file [family.schools]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" and Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (walkable cafés, pubs, Opera); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (highest asking rents in the file); Q6 "Français" helped by an LFC primary site and francophone events at the Opera.

### Maadi: Degla and Old Maadi
- Feel: "leafy and formerly suburban" district south of the centre on the east bank (Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi); "the historic expat heart of Cairo", tree-lined and walkable (House Solution Egypt 2026 guide). Degla is the most walkable sub-area with cafés, gyms and supermarkets; Old Maadi is nearer the Nile and often better value (House Solution Egypt 2026 guide). Pleasant and well supplied (lepetitjournal.com, city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: 1-bed asking Old Maadi E£12,000–16,000 (≈ €203–271), Degla E£10,000–14,000 (≈ €169–237) (House Solution Maadi cost page, 2026-09-11, low confidence, furnished/unfurnished not stated); furnished 2-bed E£35,000–80,000 (≈ €593–1,356), furnished 3-bed E£65,000–125,000 (≈ €1,102–2,119) (House Solution 2026 guide). Both via city file [cost.rent]; the two agency pages conflict.
- Getting around: about 12 km south of downtown (House Solution "Why expats love living in Maadi", updated 2026-09-11). Metro Line 1 runs along Road 9 with three Maadi stations: Hadayeq El-Maadi, Maadi, Sakanat El-Maadi (Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi). Metro fare E£10 up to 9 stations, E£12 up to 16 (Egyptian Streets 2026-03-27; city file [daily.transport]). Airport about 45 minutes off-peak (House Solution, 2026-09-11).
- Watch: agency advice to check the building has a generator or backup before signing (House Solution, city file [daily.power], low confidence); no Maadi-specific outage data found.
- Places:
  - Farmer's market behind Maadi metro station, next to Road 9 (market) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi
  - Café Greco (café, coffee and waffles), 64 Road 9 — Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi; Cairo360 review, 2010-03-21
  - Jared's Bagels (café with Wi-Fi, 6:30 am–9:30 pm), 82 Road 9 — Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi
  - Maadi Grand Mall (mall) [Apr 2024] — Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi; also House Solution 2026-09-11
  - Wadi Degla Protected Area (desert valley for hiking, climbing, cycling; E£5 for foreigners plus E£5 per car [Apr 2026]) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Ma'adi
  - CSA Maadi (community association for newcomers) — House Solution 2026 guide
  - Cairo American College, Midan Degla (American curriculum and IB school) — House Solution "Why expats love living in Maadi", 2026-09-11
  - Lycée français du Caire, Maadi primary site (Road 12, Sarayat) — LFC homepage via city file; House Solution 2026-09-11
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (CAC, LFC, CSA); Q5 "Télétravail" (Wi-Fi cafés on Road 9, metro to the centre); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" (walkable, leafy, inside a megacity); Q3 "Moyen" for a 1-bed, "Le prix compte peu" for a furnished family flat.

### Downtown, Garden City and Mounira (Wust el-Balad)
- Feel: commercial centre with late-19th-century Paris-style boulevards, busy around Tahrir and Ataba (Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown). Garden City, just south of Tahrir, is leafy, quiet, high-security, with embassies (Wikivoyage Cairo/Garden City). Mounira, near the Institut français, is lively but noisy (lepetitjournal.com, city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure found. Citywide averages: Numbeo centre 1-bed E£11,800 (≈ €200), Expat.com centre E£5,000–7,500 (≈ €85–127), conflicting (city file [cost.rent]). Old-rent reform (2025) raises pre-1996 controlled rents twentyfold in prime areas over 7 years; downtown Azbakeya tenants fear eviction (Reuters via Al-Monitor, 2025-09-03; city file [cost.rent]).
- Getting around: the metro hub. Sadat station (Tahrir) is where Lines 1 and 2 meet; Al-Shohadaa under Ramses Station is the other interchange; Nasser, Mohamed Naguib, Ataba and Orabi also serve the area (Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown, Cairo/Garden City).
- Places:
  - Egyptian Museum, Tahrir (museum; E£550, students E£275) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - Koshary Abou Tarek (budget koshari) [Jan 2023] — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - Estoril, Talaat Harb (Egyptian restaurant since 1962) [Aug 2026] — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - El Horreya Cafe and Bar (classic baladi bar) [Oct 2025] — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - Shorouk Bookshop, Talaat Harb Square (Arabic and English books) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - Attaba bookstalls (100+ kiosks, new and used books) — Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown
  - Institut Français d'Égypte, Mounira (cultural centre) — dossier [9]; lepetitjournal via city file [housing.areas]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (hostels and metro hub for a first base); Q5 "Création, culture" (Institut français, bookshops, galleries).

### Heliopolis (Masr El-Gedida)
- Feel: Cairo's early-20th-century garden suburb (1905), "one of Cairo's nicest places to live", near the airport, little for tourists (Wikivoyage Cairo/East). About 387,000 people in Masr El-Gedida and El-Nozha (Jan 2022 estimate); Korba Square on Baghdad St is the strolling, shopping and café hub (Wikipedia "Heliopolis, Cairo"). After the spring 2026 curfew, Heliopolis cafés filled again with shisha and coffee crowds (AFP via Al-Monitor, 2026-04-28; city file [social.nightlife]).
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure found.
- Getting around: Metro Line 3 Heliopolis stations include Koleyet El Banat, Al Ahram, Haroun, Heliopolis Square, Alf Maskan, El-Shams Club and El-Nozha (Wikipedia, extension completed 2018); the old tram was removed after 2015, and streets were widened and bridges built in 2019–20 (Wikipedia). Eastern Cairo is car-dependent; buses crowded and unreliable (Wikivoyage Cairo/East).
- Places:

  - Merryland (park with lake and small amusement park) — Wikipedia
  - L'Amphitryon, 18 Ibrahim al-Laqqani St (open-air café and pub) [Jun 2022] — Wikivoyage Cairo/East
  - Cleopatra Hospital (private hospital) — Wikivoyage Cairo
  - Lycée La Liberté Héliopolis (school) — Wikipedia
  - City Stars, Nasr City (mall with cinema, 11 am–1 am) — Wikivoyage Cairo/East

### New Cairo (Fifth Settlement and compounds)
- Feel: planned satellite city on the eastern desert plateau, established 2000; about 3 million people per a 2026 decree (Wikipedia "New Cairo"). Modern compounds, car-dependent, walking mostly inside the compound (House Solution 2026 guide); new but far from the historic centre (lepetitjournal.com, city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: 3-bed compound apartment E£50,000–120,000/month (≈ €847–2,034); Katameya Heights villas from about E£200,000 (≈ €3,390) (House Solution 2026 guide; city file [cost.rent]).
- Getting around: Katameya Heights 45–75 minutes from central Cairo or Maadi (House Solution; city file [housing.areas]); the drive downtown "can be punishing" (House Solution 2026 guide). The monorail's eastern line (Nasr City to the New Administrative Capital) opened May 2026 with fares E£20–80 (Al-Monitor 2026-05-26; city file [daily.transport]); which New Cairo compounds it serves was not checked.
- Places:

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: Downtown, for the metro hub and cheap rooms. Wikivoyage lists hostels such as Dahab Hostel (beds from about E£100, undated) and Museum Plaza Hostel (described as long-stay friendly), and the Talisman Hotel de Charme (US$61) (Wikivoyage Cairo/Downtown). These prices are old; recheck.
- Day 1: arrive on a visa on arrival (US$30 cash, 30 days) or a card-only digital visa at Cairo airport (US$36 since 1 Aug 2026); for a trial of several months, the tourist e-visa runs up to 3 months, extensions at passport and immigration offices (FCDO, city file [admin.visa]). Keep a copy of passport and visa on you (FCDO).
- Day 2: SIM card. Operators Vodafone, Orange, Etisalat (e&) and WE; a passport is needed to set up a line (Wikivoyage Cairo and Egypt). Warning: Wikivoyage Egypt reports a 38.5% import tax on foreign-made phones used on a local SIM, payable within 90 days or the phone is locked; check before inserting a local SIM. Mobile money: Vodafone Cash holds 53% of wallets (Zawya/NTRA, H1 2026); eligibility rules for foreigners not checked.
- Day 3: neighbourhood scouting by metro: Line 1 to Maadi (Road 9), Line 2 to Dokki, Line 3 to Heliopolis (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia).
- Day 4: flat hunting. Word of mouth beats listings; building concierges (bawab) tip you off for a small fee; a simsar (agent) is usually paid half a month's rent after signing (lepetitjournal, city file [housing.search]). Agency fee up to one month; deposit 1–2 months; insist on a bilingual Arabic–English lease (House Solution). Standard long leases are one year paid quarterly in advance; agree EGP or USD in writing (House Solution).
- Day 5: Caire Accueil (francophone newcomers' association, 180 member families, WhatsApp help groups) (lepetitjournal 2023/2025, city file [community.expat]).
- Day 6: opening a bank account: rules for foreigners not found; Wikivoyage Egypt names HSBC and CIB with branches, hours Sunday–Thursday 8:30 am–2 pm.
- Day 7: rest; Al-Azhar Park.
- Saturday night: as for the remote worker (Cairo Jazz Club / Pub 28 or El Horreya Downtown / hostel rooftop).
- Weekend outing: Saqqara or Faiyum for desert; Ain Sokhna for the beach; mountain not realistic.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Foreigner prices apply; Arab nationals pay them at antiquities sites since 1 Jan 2026 (SceneNow, city file [cost.negotiation]); a residence permit halves the Grand Egyptian Museum price (Curly Tales 2026-10-19).

### One day
- Morning: Giza pyramids (E£700 [Apr 2026], 7 am–5 pm, 4 pm in winter), then the Grand Egyptian Museum next to it. GEM price: E£1,830 for foreign visitors from 1 Nov 2026 (Curly Tales, city file); Wikivoyage Giza gave E£1,590, online only, in Apr 2026. Getting there: Uber from anywhere in Cairo should cost at most about E£150 (Wikivoyage Giza). Agree any horse or camel price before riding (Wikivoyage Giza).
- Midday: Uber to Downtown; koshari at Koshary Abou Tarek.
- Afternoon: Uber to Islamic Cairo: Al-Azhar Park (E£20 [Mar 2019]), then walk to Khan el-Khalili and Al-Muizz Street (pedestrian 8 am–11 pm) (Wikivoyage Islamic Cairo).
- Evening: tea at El Fishawy in the Khan, or an evening felucca from the Garden City corniche (Wikivoyage Garden City).

### One week
- Day 1: Downtown and the Egyptian Museum (E£550); evening on Talaat Harb (Estoril, Shorouk Bookshop).
- Day 2: Giza pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum (allow four hours for the GEM, Wikivoyage Cairo).
- Day 3: Islamic Cairo: Citadel (E£550 [May 2026]), Sultan Hassan and Al-Rifa'i mosques (E£180 for both [Jul 2024]), Bab Zuweila, Khan el-Khalili.
- Day 4, daily life: Line 1 at morning rush hour from Sadat to Maadi (women-only cars exist, dossier [15]); the farmer's market behind Maadi metro; coffee on Road 9; Wadi Degla in the afternoon. This is the day to judge Maadi as a place to live.
- Day 5, trip out: Faiyum (90 km): Tunis pottery village, Lake Qarun, Wadi El Rayan; microbus from Ramsis Square or Giza (Wikivoyage Faiyum). Fees for Wadi El Rayan and Wadi al-Hitan not found.
- Day 6: Zamalek (Diwan, Gezira Centre of Arts, Cairo Tower: E£70 for non-Egyptians [Jan 2023]) and Heliopolis by Uber (Korba, Basilica, Baron Empain Palace from outside). Between October and April, a top-flight football match at Cairo International Stadium, Nasr City (Wikivoyage Cairo).
- Day 7, trip out: Alexandria by train, about 2 h 40 min on the fastest services, about US$35 AC2 one way (Oct 2025) (Wikivoyage Alexandria; Cairo). Or Saqqara and Dahshur, south of Cairo (Wikivoyage Cairo).
