# Assouan: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions: EUR at the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 59 EGP** (dossier, Numbeo Cairo 2026-11-14). "City file" means `research/cities/egypte/assouan.md` (curated 2026-11-14) and the item tag cited. Wikivoyage listings carry their own "updated" stamp in brackets. The session's web-search budget was exhausted, so this sheet rests on direct fetches (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Expat.com) plus the city file. Aswan data is thin: rents rest on one expat-platform page and a handful of listings.

City-wide watch points (not repeated in each block):
- Heat: average highs about 42 °C in June–August (Wikipedia via city file); 49.6 °C on 7 Jun 2024 with over 40 deaths in four days reported (Zawia3, 2024-06-26); late July 2026 highs above 45 °C, nights around 29 °C, streets empty from midday to late afternoon (Masrawy, 2026-07-25) (city file [climate.daily], [social.pace]). Every weekly plan below assumes October–April; in summer, move everything to early morning and evening.
- Power: during the June 2024 national load-shedding some Aswan outages lasted 5–6 hours and transformer failures left areas dark for days (Zawia3, 2024-06-26; city file [daily.power]). No 2025–2026 Aswan data.
- Nile: residents warn of strong currents and drownings where families cool off on the banks (Masrawy, 2026-07-25; city file [nature.access]).
- Amnesty documented mass arrests of Sudanese refugees in Aswan city and border areas, transfer to a Shallal detention site, and arrests of injured refugees in Aswan public hospitals (Amnesty, 2024-06-19; city file [rights.racism]). For a Black reader, carry passport and visa or a copy at all times (FCDO, 2026-10-27). Nubian volunteers fed Sudanese arrivals in 2023 (Africanews, 2023-09-15).
- FCDO names Aswan and Abu Simbel as exceptions to its Western Desert advice (UK FCDO, updated 2026-10-27; city file [safety.general]).

## Neighbourhoods

### The Corniche and the souq (east-bank centre)
- Feel: prime riverside location with views and easy access to restaurants, markets and transport (Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01). The Corniche was redeveloped as the Ahl Misr Walkway with cafés, shops, bazaars and a multi-level glass walkway (Wikipedia "Aswan", undated).
- Rent: furnished 1-bed E£2,000–4,500/month (≈ €34–76) "in decent areas", with premium spots 20–30% more (Expat.com 2025-12-01, low confidence); one 120 m² flat in Aswan City asked E£9,000/month (≈ €153) on Aqarmap (accessed 2026-11-14) (city file [cost.rent]).
- Getting around: taxis E£20–50 in town, mostly without meters; microbuses E£5–10; motor ferries to the islands E£5–10 (Expat.com; city file [daily.transport]). The railway station is at the north end of the souq street (Wikivoyage Aswan). Ride-hailing is limited; Uber drivers reportedly message higher prices, inDrive suggested instead (Wikivoyage; city file [daily.transport], low confidence).
- Places:
  - Sharia as-Souq (main market street, from the railway station southwards) — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Ahl Misr Walkway (Corniche promenade with cafés and shops) — Wikipedia "Aswan"
  - Panorama (budget restaurant), Corniche [Jan 2017] — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Al-Masry Restaurant (budget restaurant), city centre [Sep 2022] — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Chef Khalil (fish restaurant, priced by weight), near the station [Sep 2022] — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Ferial Gardens (park, E£10 [Nov 2018]), southern end of the Corniche — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Archangel Michael's Coptic Cathedral (church, consecrated 2006), south end of the Corniche — Wikivoyage Aswan; Wikipedia
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Télétravail" (walkable, central) with the caveat of unverified internet quality; Q3 "Serré" (lowest rents of the three cities on Expat.com's figures).

### Al-Aqqad (El Akkad) extension
- Feel: middle-class zone with newer buildings, popular with Egyptian professionals and some expat families (Expat.com 2025-12-01; city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: "moderate", no figure (Expat.com). Sale: a 160 m² flat in El Akkad asked E£2.5M (≈ €42,373) on Aqarmap (accessed 2026-11-14, only 4 listings; city file [housing.search]).
- Getting around: by taxi or microbus to the centre (city fares above); distance not sourced.
- Places: none sourced for this area (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants" (newer buildings, professional neighbours); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q6 "Arabe" (Egyptian professional area).

### Elephantine Island
- Feel: quiet island near archaeological sites, reached by boat, so daily travel adds logistics (Expat.com 2025-12-01). Its formerly separate community is now part of the city (Wikipedia); Nubian villages on the island (Wikivoyage).
- Rent: no figure found.
- Getting around: public ferry E£5 return for foreigners, E£1 for Egyptians (Nov 2018) (Wikivoyage; city file [cost.negotiation]).
- Places:
  - Aswan Museum and Elephantine ruins (museum and site; E£100, students E£50 [Mar 2022]) — Wikivoyage Aswan; Expat.com
  - Baba Dool Guesthouse (guesthouse) — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - King Jamaica (café) [Sep 2022] — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Steigenberger Elephantine, ex-Mövenpick (resort hotel, from US$160/night, reopening Dec 2026 [Oct 2026]) — Wikivoyage Aswan
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" in a guesthouse; not for Q5 "Salarié·e" with a daily commute.

### West Bank: Gharb Soheil and the tombs side
- Feel: Gharb Soheil is a Nubian community on a sandy slope west of the Nile, founded about a century ago when the first dam was built (Wikipedia "Aswan"); Nubian guesthouses (Wikipedia via city file [housing.areas]). Young Nubians mostly speak Egyptian Arabic and use Nubian among themselves (Egyptian Streets, 2025-04-14; city file [social.culture]).
- Rent: no long-term figure. Bet el Kerem guesthouse, near the tombs: double €30, house rental €45 (unit not stated) (Wikivoyage Aswan, undated).
- Getting around: West Bank public ferry E£5 for foreigners, E£1 for locals (Nov 2018) (Wikivoyage).
- Watch: Nubians displaced by the High Dam still press for return; recent decrees made many Nubian lands military zones (Egyptian Streets, 2025-04-14; city file [other]). Context for anyone looking to buy or build here.
- Places:
  - Tombs of the Nobles (rock-cut tombs; E£200, students E£100) — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Monastery of St Simeon (desert monastery ruins, about 3 km from the tombs; E£100 [Jan 2019]) — Wikivoyage Aswan; Wikipedia via city file [social.religion]
  - Bet el Kerem (guesthouse) — Wikivoyage Aswan
  - Seheyl Island (inscriptions and beadwork village; E£100 [Jun 2026]), just north of the old dam — Wikivoyage Aswan
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Création, culture" and "J'entreprends" for Nubian hospitality and crafts; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Nubian heritage, Nubian Museum across the river).

### New Aswan City and Sahari (north)
- Feel: New Aswan is a planned development about 10 km north of downtown, designed for 70,000 residents and less established than older areas (Expat.com 2025-12-01). Aswan University's main campus (400 acres) is at Sahari on Airport Road (Expat.com).
- Rent: no figure found. The Al Safwa compound had a 120 m² flat for sale at E£2.1M (≈ €35,593) on Aqarmap (accessed 2026-11-14; location of the compound not given) (city file [housing.search]).
- Getting around: about 10 km to downtown (Expat.com); airport about 10 km from town (Wikipedia via city file).
- Places:
  - Aswan University, New Aswan headquarters (university) — Expat.com
  - Aswan University main campus, Sahari, Airport Road (university) — Expat.com
  - Other everyday places: none sourced.
- Fits: Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (university jobs); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

## Living: typical weeks

Weekly costs convert monthly figures as × 12 ÷ 52. Aswan's figures come mostly from Expat.com (2025-12-01, low confidence); Numbeo has no rent and only 3 contributors. Home internet uses the national 140 GB plan E£287.28/month (Zawia3, May 2026); Aswan fibre coverage is unknown.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: the Corniche and souq area. Everything walkable, ferries to the islands, station for trips; the caveat is unverified internet quality (city file gaps) and the 2024 outage record. Freebird runs daily winter flights to seven German cities since Sep 2026 (ch-aviation, 2026-09-18), useful for European time-zone work trips.
- Monday: work from home early; evening on the Ahl Misr Walkway.
- Tuesday: morning groceries in Sharia as-Souq (souks and small grocers rather than supermarkets, Expat.com); lunch at Al-Masry.
- Wednesday: ferry to Elephantine (E£5) to work an afternoon at King Jamaica café (Wi-Fi not sourced).
- Thursday: Nubian Museum (E£400 [Apr 2026]) late afternoon.
- Friday: felucca at sunset (E£50–200, Expat.com; E£50–80 haggled, Wikivoyage Nov 2018).
- Saturday: West Bank ferry, walk to the Tombs of the Nobles; evening per Q2.
- Sunday: work week.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no dance venue sourced; the dossier calls nightlife limited. Not realistic in Aswan.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": riverside restaurant at E£150–400 per person (Expat.com), e.g. Panorama or Chef Khalil. The Old Cataract high tea (E£2,750–3,250) is listed, but the hotel is shown as reopening in summer 2027 (Wikivoyage, Oct 2026).
  - "Tranquille chez moi": Nile view at home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Aswan (no sea coast; Nile banks carry drowning warnings).
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Aswan.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": West Bank to the Monastery of St Simeon in the desert; Abu Simbel by road (coach E£300–500, Expat.com; entry E£765 [Nov 2024], Wikivoyage).
  - "Je reste en ville": Botanical Garden on Kitchener's Island (E£45 [Dec 2021]) and the Corniche.
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent, furnished 1-bed E£2,000–4,500/month → E£462–1,038/week (Expat.com).
  - Groceries E£2,000–3,500/month → E£462–808 (Expat.com).
  - Utilities E£300–600/month → E£69–138 (Expat.com).
  - Internet, 140 GB plan → E£66.
  - Four restaurant meals: 4 × E£80–250 = E£320–1,000 (Expat.com).
  - Six taxis: 6 × E£20–50 = E£120–300 (Expat.com).
  - Total ≈ E£1,499–3,350/week ≈ €25–57. Using the one E£9,000 Aqarmap rent instead (E£2,077/week) raises it to about E£3,114–4,389 (≈ €53–74).

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: Al-Aqqad extension. Newer buildings and Egyptian professional neighbours. Jobs are mainly tourism, Aswan University, sugar and language teaching; entry- to mid-level pay US$600–1,500/month per Expat.com (low confidence); foreign hires capped at 10% of staff (city file [work.jobs]).
- Monday to Thursday: commute by taxi or microbus to the centre or by road to the university campus at Sahari (times not sourced).
- Monday: groceries in the souq.
- Tuesday: evening on the Corniche.
- Wednesday: dinner at Chef Khalil.
- Thursday: Feryal Gardens at sunset.
- Friday: felucca and Kitchener's Island.
- Saturday: Elephantine and the Aswan Museum.
- Sunday: work.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not realistic; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at a riverside restaurant (E£150–400 per person); "Tranquille chez moi" at home.
- Weekend outing: beach and mountain not realistic; desert via St Simeon or Abu Simbel; town via the Nubian Museum. Luxor is 3–4 hours by train (Wikivoyage) for a weekend away.
- Rough weekly spend (couple):
  - Rent, 3-bed E£4,000–8,000/month → E£923–1,846/week (Expat.com); or the Aqarmap E£9,000 → E£2,077.
  - Groceries, 2 × single E£2,000–3,500/month → E£924–1,616 (no couple figure exists).
  - Utilities E£69–138; internet E£66.
  - Two dinners for two: 4 × E£150–400 = E£600–1,600.
  - Ten taxis: 10 × E£20–50 = E£200–500.
  - Total ≈ E£2,782–5,997/week ≈ €47–102 (the high end uses the Aqarmap rent).

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: the Corniche, with weekly time on the West Bank. Tourism is the economy (dossier); Aswan was named D-8 Tourist City of the Year for 2026 (Wikipedia via city file); the 13th Aswan International Festival of Culture and Arts ran 3–9 Feb 2026 at 17 venues (Middle East Observer, 2026-02-03); new German winter flights since Sep 2026 (ch-aviation). Entrepreneurship and tech ecosystem: not found (dossier).
- Monday: souq suppliers (Nubian handicrafts said to be good value, Wikivoyage).
- Tuesday: West Bank ferry to meet guesthouses around Gharb Soheil (Bet el Kerem as an example of the market).
- Wednesday: Elephantine guesthouses (Baba Dool).
- Thursday: admin in town; foreign work permit costs about E£5,205, HIV test E£850, residence processing E£7,100 in the first year (Expat.com; city file [work.jobs]).
- Friday: felucca operators (Wikivoyage warns of overcharging, so posted fares and haggling matter).
- Saturday: Seheyl Island beadwork village; evening per Q2.
- Sunday: accounts.
- Saturday night: as for the remote worker.
- Weekend outing: Abu Simbel (desert road), Kom Ombo (taxi or tour E£150, or local train E£10 plus tuk-tuk; Wikivoyage), beach and mountain not realistic.
- Rough weekly spend: as for the remote worker, E£1,499–3,350 (≈ €25–57), plus about four extra ferry crossings at E£5–10 each (E£20–40) and the work-permit costs above spread over a year: (E£5,205 + E£850 + E£7,100) ÷ 52 = E£253/week. Total ≈ E£1,772–3,643/week ≈ €30–62.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: Al-Aqqad extension. The hard constraint: no full international school in Aswan; the nearest are in Cairo or Alexandria; Egyptian "language schools" cost E£15,000–40,000/year and home-schooling or distance learning is common among expats (Expat.com; city file [family.schools]). Healthcare: Aswan Heart Center, Aswan University Hospitals and others; complex treatment may need Cairo or Alexandria (Wikipedia, Expat.com). Aswan joined Universal Health Insurance in July 2025; foreigners' eligibility not stated (SceneNow, 2025-07-05).
- Monday to Thursday: school run to a language school (none named in sources) or distance learning at home.
- Monday: souq groceries.
- Tuesday: Ferial Gardens after school (E£10).
- Wednesday: Nubian Museum.
- Thursday: dinner at Panorama on the Corniche.
- Friday: felucca to the Botanical Garden.
- Saturday: Elephantine by ferry.
- Sunday: school.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not realistic; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at a riverside restaurant; "Tranquille chez moi" at home.
- Weekend outing: as above; summer outings for children are limited by heat and Nile currents.
- Rough weekly spend (family of four):
  - Rent, 3-bed E£4,000–8,000/month → E£923–1,846/week (Expat.com).
  - Groceries E£5,000–8,000/month → E£1,154–1,846.
  - Utilities E£69–138; internet E£66.
  - Healthcare and insurance E£1,000–2,000/month → E£231–462.
  - Two pupils at a language school E£15,000–40,000/year each → E£576–1,538/week.
  - Total ≈ E£3,019–5,896/week ≈ €51–100. A French-system education would mean CNED-type distance learning; its cost was not researched.

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: short furnished lets around E£500/day (≈ €8.5) on Aqarmap (accessed 2026-11-14; city file [cost.rent]); guesthouses on Elephantine (Baba Dool) or the West Bank (Bet el Kerem, double €30, undated).
- Day 1: arrive by the Abela night sleeper from Cairo (US$110–170 one way, about 13.5–14 hours, Seat61 2026 timetable) or the first-class day train (AC1 US$80 [Apr 2026], Wikivoyage); airport about 10 km out (taxi to the station E£92 in Nov 2018, Wikivoyage). Visa as for Cairo (FCDO).
- Day 2: SIM (passport needed; national 38.5% import tax on foreign phones per Wikivoyage Egypt). Mobile money: Vodafone Cash leads nationally (Zawya/NTRA H1 2026, Cairo city file).
- Day 3: scout the Corniche, Al-Aqqad and Elephantine by taxi and ferry.
- Day 4: flat hunting: listings are informal, by word of mouth, local agents and the bawwab; deposit 1–2 months plus first month; leases usually one year (Expat.com; city file [housing.search]).
- Day 5: Nubian Museum and the Corniche to read the town.
- Day 6: bank account rules not found; souk and ferry payments are cash.
- Day 7: felucca to Kitchener's Island.
- Saturday night and weekend: as for the remote worker.
- Rough first-week spend (one person): lodging 7 × E£500 = E£3,500 (Aqarmap short let); groceries E£462–808; four meals out E£320–1,000; six taxis E£120–300. Total ≈ E£4,402–5,608 (≈ €75–95), plus the train (US$80–170, not converted: no USD rate in the dossier). Move-in on a E£4,500 furnished 1-bed: 1–2 months' deposit + first month = E£9,000–13,500 (≈ €153–229).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best season: October–April (see heat above); the culture festival falls in early February (2026 edition 3–9 Feb). Foreigner prices apply; ferries charge foreigners E£5 vs E£1 for Egyptians (Nov 2018).

### One day
- Morning: taxi south to Philae Temple (E£550, students E£275 [Jun 2026]; boat about E£150 return in Nov 2018) and the Aswan High Dam (E£200 [Jun 2026]) (Wikivoyage Aswan).
- Midday: back to town; Unfinished Obelisk (E£220 [Apr 2026]) on the way; lunch at Al-Masry.
- Afternoon: Nubian Museum (E£400 [Apr 2026]); late afternoon felucca to the Botanical Garden on Kitchener's Island (E£45 [Dec 2021]; motorboat quoted E£200 for two, haggled to about E£100, Wikivoyage).
- Evening: Sharia as-Souq from the station southwards; dinner on the Corniche at Panorama.

### One week
- Day 1: Corniche and Ahl Misr Walkway, Archangel Michael's Coptic Cathedral, Ferial Gardens, the souq.
- Day 2: Philae, High Dam, Unfinished Obelisk.
- Day 3: West Bank by public ferry: Tombs of the Nobles (E£200), walk to the Monastery of St Simeon (E£100), Gharb Soheil.
- Day 4, daily life: early-morning souq shopping as residents do (souks over supermarkets, Expat.com), then the public ferry to Elephantine at the E£1/E£5 two-tier fare alongside commuters; Aswan Museum and the island's Nubian villages; at Friday noon, El-Tabia Mosque (landmark built on an early-19th-century fort site, Wikipedia) seen from outside.
- Day 5, trip out: Abu Simbel (entry E£765 [Nov 2024]; coach E£300–500 per Expat.com, or private car about E£2,500 return [Nov 2024]; public bus at 8 am [May 2019]) (Wikivoyage, Expat.com).
- Day 6, trip out: Kom Ombo (taxi or tour E£150, or local train E£10 plus tuk-tuk; Wikivoyage), or Luxor by train in 3–4 hours.
- Day 7: Seheyl Island (E£100) and Kalabsha Temple (E£200 [Jun 2026]); sunset at Nubian House café, south Aswan (Wikivoyage).
