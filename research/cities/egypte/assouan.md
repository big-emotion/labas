- [cost.living] Home-cooked meal E£30–50 per person; restaurant meal E£80–250 (≈ €1.4–4.2). — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low

- [cost.salary] Entry- to mid-level roles typically pay US$600–1,500/month per Expat.com; main sectors are tourism, Aswan University, sugar and language teaching. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Nile Corniche is the prime area; the Al-Aqqad extension is middle-class with newer buildings; Elephantine Island is reached by boat; New Aswan City, about 10 km north, is planned for 70,000 people but less established. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Nubian villages such as Gharb Soheil on the west bank host Nubian guesthouses. — Wikipedia "Aswan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Listings are informal: word of mouth, local agents and the building's bawwab; deposit 1–2 months plus first month; leases usually one year. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Economy rests on tourism, farming (dates, hibiscus, tamarind), quarries and the KIMA chemicals plant; logistics zones in Wadi Karkar and a high-speed rail link are planned for trade with Africa. — Wikipedia "Aswan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Foreign hires are capped at 10% of staff and 20% of payroll; first-year costs about E£5,205 for the permit, E£850 HIV test and E£7,100 residence-visa processing. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In June 2024 national load-shedding cut power 2–3 hours a day, but some Aswan outages lasted 5–6 hours and transformer failures left areas dark for days; residents launched a "Stop cutting electricity in Aswan" hashtag. — Zawia3, 2024-06-26, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In the July 2026 heat, families relied on desert coolers, which work poorly in humid air; those who switched to AC saw higher bills. — Masrawy, 2026-07-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Aswan was exempt from the March–April 2026 national early-closing and energy-saving rules as a tourist destination. — Euronews, 2026-03-24, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Internet E£200–400/month (≈ €3.4–6.8) per Expat.com; Numbeo logs broadband at E£500 (≈ €8.5). — Expat.com, 2025-12-01 / Numbeo, 2026-02-01 (dossier), blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Taxis E£20–50 in town, mostly without meters (agree the fare first); ride-hailing has limited presence; microbuses E£5–10, crowded and without AC; motor ferries to the islands E£5–10. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Night sleeper Cairo–Aswan (Abela, since 2023): US$110–170 one way for foreigners depending on class and occupancy; about 13.5–14 hours; foreigners and Egyptians book separate quotas. — Seat61, 2026 timetable (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] First-class day train to Cairo about E£250–400 (≈ €4–7), roughly 12 hours; domestic flights to Cairo E£2,500–4,000 (≈ €42–68). — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Freebird Airlines started daily winter flights linking Aswan with seven German cities (Stuttgart, Berlin, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Munich, Hamburg, Hannover); first arrival 14 Sep 2026. — ch-aviation, 2026-09-18, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] The airport is about 10 km from town; Aswan is the southern end of the national rail network; a road runs south to Abu Simbel and Wadi Halfa. — Wikipedia "Aswan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Aswan joined Universal Health Insurance in July 2025, the last governorate of phase one: eight hospitals and 89 clinics, over E£12bn invested. Eligibility of foreign residents not stated. — SceneNow (CairoScene), 2025-07-05, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Facilities include Aswan University Hospitals, Aswan Specialized, Aswan Heart Center (Magdi Yacoub), Aswan General, Nile Specialized and an Evangelical Mission Hospital. — Wikipedia "Aswan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Complex treatment may require travel to Cairo or Alexandria; Expat.com recommends insurance with medical evacuation. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] In June 2024, residents described hospitals about 15 km from some homes being hard to reach during the heatwave. — Zawia3, 2024-06-26, local-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] A November 2021 storm drove scorpions into homes: at least 503 people hospitalised, all discharged after antivenom; officials said no deaths were caused by stings. Dated. — AP via NBC News, 2021-11-15, intl-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] No full international school; nearest are in Cairo or Alexandria. Egyptian "language schools" (English for maths and science) cost E£15,000–40,000/year (≈ €254–678). - [family.schools] Aswan University (est. 2012) has 15 faculties, mostly Arabic-medium; Al-Azhar and the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport have branches. — Expat.com 2025-12-01 / Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO advises against all but essential travel to most of the Western Desert but names Luxor, Qina, Aswan and Abu Simbel as exceptions. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] FCDO says crime is "generally low" nationwide, terrorist attacks are "likely", and drivers should avoid roads outside main cities at night. — UK FCDO safety page, updated 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
