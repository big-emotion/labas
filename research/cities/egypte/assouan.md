- [daily.transport] Taxis E£20–50 in town, mostly without meters (agree the fare first); ride-hailing has limited presence; microbuses E£5–10, crowded and without AC; motor ferries to the islands E£5–10. — Expat.com Aswan guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
