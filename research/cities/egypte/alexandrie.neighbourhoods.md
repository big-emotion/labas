# Alexandrie: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions: EUR at the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 59 EGP** (dossier, Numbeo Cairo 2026-11-14). "City file" means `research/cities/egypte/alexandrie.md` (curated 2026-11-14) and the item tag cited. Wikivoyage listings carry their own "updated" stamp in brackets; old stamps prove a place existed, not current hours or prices. The session's web-search budget was exhausted, so this sheet rests on direct fetches (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Expat.com) plus the city file.

City-wide watch points (not repeated in each block):
- FCDO lists terrorist shootings in Alexandria in 2023 (two Israeli tourists and an Egyptian guide killed) and 2024 (an Israeli-Canadian killed); crime otherwise "generally low" (UK FCDO, updated 2026-10-27; city file [safety.general]).
- Amnesty's Feb 2026 report lists Alexandria among governorates where plain-clothes police arbitrarily arrested refugees from Sudan, South Sudan and elsewhere, late Dec 2025 to 5 Feb 2026 (Amnesty, 2026-02-16; city file [rights.racism]). Carry passport and visa or a copy (FCDO).
- Old buildings: building collapses rose from about one a year to about 40 a year over a decade as seawater intrusion raises groundwater (USC study, ScienceDaily 2025-02-20); a ceiling collapse in Moharram Bek killed three in April 2026 (Gulf News, 2026-04-19) (city file [housing.search]). Check the building's age and condition before signing.
- The 163-year-old Raml tram was due to stop in April 2026 for a 24-month rebuild; 153 replacement buses and minibuses run its route every 3–5 minutes (Africanews 2026-02-25; Egyptian Streets 2026-02-02; SceneNow 2026-06-01; city file [daily.transport]).

## Neighbourhoods

### Raml Station and the Latin Quarter (downtown, Mansheya)
- Feel: the historic downtown: cafés and restaurants along Saad Zaghloul St around Raml Station (Wikivoyage Alexandria). No resident profile sourced.
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure. Citywide 1-bed centre E£7,167/month (≈ €121) (Numbeo 2026-11-10 via dossier); first Alexandria Rent Index median for new unfurnished lets E£6,900/month (≈ €117), Q2 2026 (Marsad Omran, 2026-08-11) (city file [cost.rent]).
- Getting around: Raml Station was the terminus of the Raml tram, now replaced by buses and minibuses during the rebuild (city file [daily.transport]). Taxis start around E£30, cross-city E£40–50 (Expat.com 2025-11-28); taxis do not use meters, agree the fare first (Wikivoyage, city file [cost.negotiation]).
- Watch: the USC study covers six historic districts (data 2001–2021); the district list was not read, so whether it includes this quarter is unconfirmed (city file [housing.search]).
- Places:
  - Trianon 1905 (historic café-restaurant), Saad Zaghlul Square, 8 am–midnight [Oct 2021] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Brazilian Coffee Stores (café), Saad Zaghloul St, 7 am–11 pm — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Foul Mohamed Ahmed (budget fuul and falafel), 17 Shakour St [Mar 2022] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - L'Apéro Bistrot (bistro serving alcohol), 30 Al Naby Daniel St [Apr 2024] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Nabi Daniel street booksellers (second-hand books, prices negotiable) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Graeco-Roman Museum (museum; E£150 foreigners [Oct 2024]) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Université Senghor (francophone graduate university), Manshiya Square headquarters — Wikipedia "Senghor University"
- Fits: Q6 "Français" (Senghor, Lycée nearby in Chatby); Q5 "Création, culture" and "J'entreprends"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" as a first base.

### Chatby, Rushdy and Kafr Abdo
- Feel: Kafr Abdu and Rushdi are described as central and calm (OpenShores, undated, low confidence); Gleem and Kafr Abdo as established, traditional, good value near the Corniche (Expat.com 2025-11-28) (city file [housing.areas]). Chatby holds the Bibliotheca and the Lycée français.
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; city averages above (E£6,900–7,167 for a 1-bed or median new let).
- Getting around: along the Corniche and the Raml line corridor (replacement buses); taxis E£40–50 cross-city (Expat.com).
- Places:
  - Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Shatby (library and museums; E£150 foreigners, E£10 Egyptians [Oct 2024]); its 2,000-seat reading hall opened free after hours during the June 2024 power cuts — Wikivoyage Alexandria; Arab News 2024-06-27 via city file [daily.power]
  - Lycée Français d'Alexandrie, Chatby (Mission laïque française school, 826 pupils in 2025) — Wikipedia via city file [family.schools]
  - Club21 (café with garden shisha, 9 am–1 am), Roushdy [Oct 2021] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Deeb Mall, Roushdy (mall) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Ole Cafe (Spanish food), Roushdy — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Clay Cafe (café with Wi-Fi), Omar Lotfy St near Small Sporting tram station [Oct 2021] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (calm, Wi-Fi cafés, Bibliotheca reading hall); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Serré" or "Moyen"; Q6 "Français" for a family using the Lycée.

### Sidi Gaber and Sporting
- Feel: near the Alexandria Sporting Club and the main train station, with transport links, schools, healthcare and shopping; suited to professionals and families (Expat.com 2025-11-28). Smouha and Sidi Gaber are "modern upper-middle-class" (OpenShores, low confidence) (city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; city averages.
- Getting around: Sidi Gaber station for trains to Cairo, about 30 a day, 2 h 40 min on the fastest services, about US$35 AC2 one way (Oct 2025) (Wikivoyage Alexandria).
- Places:
  - Alexandria Sporting Club (sports club, known for basketball) — Expat.com 2025-11-28; Wikipedia via city file [social.sport]
  - Balbaa (meat and seafood restaurant, 10 am–4 pm), Sidi Gaber near El Gaish Rd [Oct 2021] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Clay Cafe, near Small Sporting tram station (see above) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Stanley Bridge and Stanley beach (Corniche landmark lined with beach restaurants; beach named for residents) — Wikivoyage Alexandria; Expat.com 2025-11-28
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (station, links to the ports and Cairo); Q4 "En couple"; Q10 "Voyager facilement" (trains to Cairo).

### Smouha
- Feel: "an upper-class district in the centre of Alexandria" (Wikipedia "Smouha"); described as the main expat area, near business districts and international schools (Expat.com 2025-11-28; city file [housing.areas]).
- Rent: unfurnished 3-bed, 210 m², E£25,000/month (≈ €424), 2 months' deposit plus 1 month's rent as commission, available from 21 Jun 2026 (Property Finder listing; city file [cost.rent]). Numbeo citywide 3-bed centre E£14,111 (≈ €239, Nov 2025, via Expat.com). Sale: average asking E£46,395/m² over 762 listings (Aqarmap, accessed 2026-11-14).
- Getting around: Victor Emmanuel Square roundabout links to the Cairo desert road and the Tanta agricultural road (Wikipedia, flagged "citation needed"); City Centre mall on the desert road is taxi-only (Wikivoyage).
- Places:
  - Smouha Sporting Club (sports club; non-member day ticket E£50 [Feb 2026]) — Wikipedia "Smouha"; projectsegy.com via city file [social.sport]
  - Zahran Mall (mall) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Kushari Bondok (koshari) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - International Cardiac Centre, Smouha (private hospital) — UK gov medical list (2022-07-18) via city file [health.care]
  - Hilton Green Plaza (hotel) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Carrefour City Centre, Cairo Desert Road (hypermarket and mall, 10 am–midnight) [Oct 2021] — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Schutz American School: Expat.com places it in Smouha; Wikipedia gives 51 Schutz St, Raml. Location conflicts; not counted for Smouha.
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (international schools nearby, cardiac centre, club); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu" by Alexandria standards; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Gleem and San Stefano
- Feel: San Stefano is upscale with sea views, high-end dining and shopping, popular with affluent families; Gleem is established, traditional and good value near the Corniche (Expat.com 2025-11-28).
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; a relocation site quotes US$200–500 for a sea view (OpenShores, undated, low confidence; city file [cost.rent]).
- Getting around: along the eastern Corniche; taxis (Expat.com fares above).
- Watch: Corniche stretches in Sidi Bishr, Cleopatra, Sporting and Camp Chezar were turned into cafés; only 20 free public beaches remain (ARIJ investigation, data to 2025; city file [nature.access]).
- Places:
  - San Stefano Grand Plaza (mall with about 10 cinemas and a food court) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Four Seasons San Stefano (hotel) — Wikivoyage Alexandria; Expat.com
  - Ibn El-Balad Gleem (restaurant), Mustafa Kamel St — Wikivoyage Alexandria
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (San Stefano) or "Moyen" (Gleem); Q8 "À la plage"; Q4 "En couple".

### Montaza and Maamoura (east)
- Feel: family-friendly and quieter, apartments and villas, many with sea views, near Montaza Palace and its gardens (Expat.com 2025-11-28).
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure; city averages (outside-centre 1-bed E£3,556, ≈ €60, Numbeo 2026-11-10 via dossier).
- Getting around: far east end of the Corniche; taxi or bus; no times sourced.
- Watch: a dawn storm on 31 May 2025 brought hail and flash floods and cut power in several eastern districts (Daily News Egypt, 2025-05-31; city file [daily.power]); which districts was not stated.
- Places:
  - Montaza Palace gardens (park, 150 ha; E£25 park entry [Mar 2019]) — Wikivoyage Alexandria; Expat.com
  - Montazah Bay beach (paid beach, E£350 per person with garden entry) — ARIJ via city file [nature.access]
  - Maamoura beach (beach, paid in summer, crowded) — Wikivoyage Alexandria; Expat.com
  - Maamoura Plaza Mall (mall) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
  - Sheraton Montazah (hotel) — Wikivoyage Alexandria
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

Weekly costs convert monthly figures as × 12 ÷ 52. Numbeo's "single person without rent" (E£16,900/month, ≈ €287) already includes food and transport, so extra lines overlap a little.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Rushdy or Kafr Abdo. Calm and central, Wi-Fi cafés, the Bibliotheca's reading hall a short ride away, sea front.
- Monday: work at home (broadband E£700/month, Numbeo); evening Corniche walk to Stanley Bridge.
- Tuesday: work at Clay Cafe near Small Sporting; groceries (cash for small shops and markets, Expat.com).
- Wednesday: Bibliotheca Alexandrina reading hall (E£150 entry [Oct 2024]); replacement bus along the Raml corridor.
- Thursday: Club21, Roushdy, for an evening shisha.
- Friday: morning at Montaza gardens (E£25 [Mar 2019]) by taxi.
- Saturday: Anfushi fish market (Expat.com), lunch by the harbour; evening per Q2.
- Sunday: work week restarts.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Mermaid Bar, sea view with an upstairs dance floor (Wikivoyage, undated); closing hours under the April 2026 national rules (cafés and restaurants 5 am–1 am, city file for Cairo) not confirmed for bars.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": White and Blue at the Greek Club (East Mediterranean, mostly fish, low prices) or Trianon 1905 (Wikivoyage).
  - "Tranquille chez moi": Corniche walk, then home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Maamoura or Montazah Bay (E£350); only 20 free public beaches remain (ARIJ).
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Alexandria.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Abu Mena, 45 km south, or El Alamein, 120 km west, WWII memorials (Wikivoyage Alexandria).
  - "Je reste en ville": Latin Quarter museums, Nabi Daniel booksellers.
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent, Numbeo centre 1-bed E£7,167/month → E£1,654/week.
  - Living without rent, Numbeo E£16,900/month → E£3,900.
  - Broadband E£700/month → E£162.
  - Four cheap meals out: 4 × E£200 = E£800 (Numbeo).
  - Four cross-city taxis: 4 × E£40–50 = E£160–200 (Expat.com).
  - Total ≈ E£6,676–6,716/week ≈ €113–114.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: Sidi Gaber. Near the main station, the Sporting Club and the eastern business areas; employers cited are logistics, shipping, manufacturing, IT, tourism and education (Expat.com; city file [work.jobs]). Foreign hires are capped at 10% of staff (Expat.com).
- Monday to Thursday: commute by taxi (E£40–50) or replacement buses along the Raml corridor; no commute times sourced.
- Monday: after work, gym (Expat.com average E£678/month).
- Tuesday: dinner at Balbaa (lunch hours 10 am–4 pm per listing; check).
- Wednesday: Alexandria Sporting Club (membership fee not found; Sporting Club joining fee E£450,000 per projectsegy.com, low confidence).
- Thursday: evening at Stanley Bridge restaurants.
- Friday: beach in summer or Montaza gardens.
- Saturday: train to Cairo for the day (2 h 40 min).
- Sunday: work.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Mermaid Bar (Wikivoyage, undated).
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Trianon 1905 or seafood at White and Blue, Greek Club.
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; cinema ticket about E£140 (Expat.com) at San Stefano Grand Plaza.
- Weekend outing: Maamoura beach; mountain not realistic; El Alamein or Abu Mena for the desert; Bibliotheca or the Graeco-Roman Museum in town.
- Rough weekly spend (couple):
  - Rent, from the Rent Index median E£6,900/month to the Numbeo 3-bed centre E£14,111 → E£1,592–3,256/week.
  - Living without rent, 2 × E£16,900 → E£7,800.
  - Two gym memberships, 2 × E£678/month → E£312.
  - Two cheap dinners out for two: 4 × E£200 = E£800.
  - Total ≈ E£10,504–12,168/week ≈ €178–206.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: Raml Station and the Latin Quarter. Central for meetings, cafés, Senghor's francophone network, consulates (France and the UK keep consulates in Alexandria, Wikivoyage).
- Monday: work at Brazilian Coffee Stores; meetings on Saad Zaghloul St.
- Tuesday: Université Senghor, Manshiya Square, for francophone and African contacts (graduate programmes in development, Wikipedia); whether it runs public events was not checked.
- Wednesday: train to Cairo for investors or clients (about 30 trains a day).
- Thursday: Nabi Daniel booksellers; evening at L'Apéro Bistrot.
- Friday: Foul Mohamed Ahmed breakfast; afternoon Corniche.
- Saturday: Anfushi fish market; evening per Q2.
- Sunday: admin; work-permit rules: employer sponsorship, 2–3 months, HIV test (Expat.com; city file [work.jobs]).
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Mermaid Bar; Sheikh Ali (Cap d'Or), one of the oldest bars (Wikivoyage), for a late drink.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Trianon 1905.
  - "Tranquille chez moi": Cafe de la Paix on the waterfront (24 hours) then home.
- Weekend outing: as above.
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent E£1,654 + living E£3,900 + broadband E£162 (Numbeo).
  - Five cheap meals out: 5 × E£200 = E£1,000.
  - Six taxis: 6 × E£40–50 = E£240–300.
  - Total ≈ E£6,956–7,016/week ≈ €118–119. Coworking prices not found.

- Monday to Thursday: school run by car or taxi; Expat.com names Schutz American School, the British School of Alexandria, Alexandria International Academy (IB), IBSA, IAS and the German school (locations mostly not given).
- Monday: Carrefour City Centre run (taxi, desert road).

- Wednesday: Zahran Mall.
- Thursday: Kushari Bondok dinner.
- Friday: Montaza gardens and beach.
- Saturday: Bibliotheca Alexandrina children's visit or the planetarium (Wikipedia via city file [social.culture]).

- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not a family plan; no childcare price found for Alexandria.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Ibn El-Balad Gleem or Balbaa.
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home in Smouha.
- Weekend outing: Montazah Bay beach (E£350 per person); mountain not realistic; El Alamein for the desert; Montaza gardens in town.
- Rough weekly spend (family of four):
  - Rent: Smouha 3-bed listing E£25,000/month → E£5,769/week; or Numbeo 3-bed centre E£14,111 → E£3,256.
  - Groceries E£12,000/month → E£2,769 (Expat.com).
  - Healthcare and insurance E£2,500/month → E£577 (Expat.com).
  - Two primary pupils at E£70,000–120,000/year each → E£2,692–4,616/week (Expat.com).
  - Total ≈ E£9,294–13,731/week ≈ €158–233. Lycée Français d'Alexandrie fees not found.

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: around Raml Station. Wikivoyage lists Ithaka Hostel and the Alexandria Youth Hostel, and basic hotels near the Triomphe Hotel with singles from about E£100–150 (undated, surely old); monthly flats "about E£180 to E£1,000 per week" (undated). Recheck all prices.
- Day 1: arrive by train from Cairo (2 h 40 min) or at Borg El Arab airport (taxi at least E£400, 60–90 minutes, agree the fare) (Wikivoyage). Visa as for Cairo: on arrival US$30, e-visa up to 3 months (FCDO, dossier).
- Day 2: SIM: Alexandria and its airport have 5G from all carriers (Wikivoyage Alexandria); a passport is needed (Wikivoyage Cairo); the 38.5% import tax on foreign phones reported by Wikivoyage Egypt applies nationally. Mobile money: Vodafone Cash is the main wallet nationally (Zawya/NTRA, H1 2026, Cairo city file).
- Day 3: scout Rushdy, Sidi Gaber and Smouha by taxi.
- Day 4: flat hunting on Property Finder and OLX (Expat.com); the Smouha listing asked 2 months' deposit plus one month's rent as commission (Property Finder).
- Day 5: Université Senghor and the French consulate area for francophone contacts; no Alexandria equivalent of Caire Accueil found (city file gaps).
- Day 6: bank account: rules not found; cash is needed for markets, small shops and transport (Expat.com).
- Day 7: Montaza gardens.
- Saturday night and weekend: as for the remote worker.
- Rough first-week spend: lodging not costable (no dated price); food and transport at Numbeo's E£3,900/week (≈ €66); move-in on a Smouha-type 3-bed listing: 2 months' deposit + 1 month's commission + first month = 4 × E£25,000 = E£100,000 (≈ €1,695); on a median new let: 4 × E£6,900 = E£27,600 (≈ €468), assuming the same terms.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Foreigners pay about 4–5× the Egyptian rate at several sites; Arab nationals pay the foreigner rate at antiquities sites since 1 Jan 2026 (Wikivoyage; SceneNow 2025-07-10; city file [cost.negotiation]).

### One day
- Morning: Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Shatby (E£150, Antiquities Museum E£100 extra [Oct 2024]).
- Midday: taxi to Raml Station; fuul at Foul Mohamed Ahmed, then coffee at Trianon 1905.
- Afternoon: Corniche double-decker bus from Al-Anfushi (E£25 [Oct 2024]) or taxi to Qaitbay Citadel (E£150 foreigners [Oct 2024]); walk to Abu al-Abbas al-Mursi Mosque, Anfushi (seen from outside; visiting rules not sourced).
- Evening: seafood near the Anfushi fish market (Expat.com), then Cafe de la Paix on the waterfront (24 hours).

### One week
- Day 1: Raml Station, Latin Quarter, Graeco-Roman Museum (E£150).
- Day 2: Bibliotheca Alexandrina and its museums; Stanley Bridge at sunset.
- Day 3: Kom el-Shoqafa catacombs (E£150) and Pompey's Pillar (E£150), Karmouz; Attarine Mosque.
- Day 4, daily life: a weekday rush hour on the Raml replacement buses and minibuses (every 3–5 minutes) from Raml Station east to Sidi Gaber; morning at the Anfushi fish market; Nabi Daniel booksellers. The day to judge whether daily movement works without the tram until its rebuild ends (24-month schedule from 2026).
- Day 5: Montaza Palace gardens and Montazah Bay beach (E£350 in season).
- Day 6, trip out: El Alamein, 120 km west, WWII memorials (Wikivoyage); transport not detailed on the page read.
- Day 7, trip out: Abu Mena, Christian pilgrimage site 45 km south (Wikivoyage); or Rosetta (Fort Julien, Amasyali House, Zaghloul Mosque; Rosetta railway station) (Wikivoyage Rosetta, an outline page without fares). Football: Al Ittihad at Alexandria Stadium (1929) if a home match falls that week (Wikipedia via city file; fixtures not checked).
