- [cost.living] Cheap meal E£205 (≈ €3.5); broadband about E£494/month (≈ €8.4). — Numbeo Hurghada, updated 2026-11-10 (as logged in the dossier), company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Expat.com budgets: single person about US$670–1,055/month; family of four about US$1,820–3,120; comfortable retirement US$1,000–1,500. Its key-facts box gives different totals. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Hospitality salaries E£7,000–15,000/month (≈ €119–254). — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] El Kawther and El Mamsha (central) are popular with long-term expats; Al Ahyaa is cheaper and near international schools; Sahl Hasheesh is gated and retiree-oriented; El Gouna, 25 km north, is the priciest. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Tourism dominates: over 9 million visitors in 2024; oil-service ports nearby since oil was found in 1913. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Work permits take 1–3 months via the Ministry of Manpower with employer sponsorship; foreign staff capped at 10% of workforce and 20% of payroll. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Hurghada was exempt from the March–April 2026 national early-closing rules as a tourist destination; hotels usually have generators. — Euronews, 2026-03-24, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In July–August 2024 pipeline breaks (Qena–Safaga, then Kureimat) left districts such as Mubarak 7, El Kawther and Magawish without tap water for days to weeks; vendor water rose from about E£30 to E£300 a ton until the governorate fixed E£60. — Zawia3, 2024-08-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Hurghada depends on desalinated and piped water: the local Al-Yusr plant produced about 33,397 m³/day per the water company. — Zawia3, 2024-08-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] No formal public transport: white minibuses about E£5–10 a ride; taxis E£50–100 in the centre; Uber and Careem operate in the main city. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Cairo is 455 km by the Sokhna road; the Safaga–Qena–Luxor road has dangerous curves and a high accident rate; the airport has flights to Cairo and several European cities. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The UK list for Hurghada includes Nile, El Salam, El Gouna and El Haya private hospitals and the government Red Sea and General hospitals. — UK gov list of medical facilities, updated 2022-07-18, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Expat.com says El Gouna Hospital offers international-level care and many doctors speak English; retiree insurance about US$500–1,000/year. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Schools include El Gouna International School (British), Deutsche Schule Hurghada, New Sunrise, MES and Continental; fees about US$3,000–8,000/year. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Wikipedia lists a French School of Hurghada, a German school and four Russian-language schools. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The tourist submarine "Sindbad" sank off Hurghada on 27 Mar 2025 with 45 Russian tourists aboard; the governor's office reported six dead. — Cyprus Mail, 2025-03-27, intl-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] FCDO warns that Red Sea dive-operator safety varies and some are linked to deaths; it also reports overcrowded Red Sea ferries and quad-bike accidents in resorts. — UK FCDO safety page, updated 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] FCDO: most sexual assaults reported to the British Embassy happened in Red Sea resorts, often by someone the victim had just met (hotel or excursion staff); some staff discouraged reporting. — UK FCDO safety page, updated 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [social.religion] English-language evangelical services and Coptic Orthodox churches exist alongside mosques. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] Expat.com estimates 20,000–30,000 foreigners, mainly Russian, German, Ukrainian, British and Italian; its Hurghada forum has 555 members. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: low
- [community.expat] About 20,000 Russians were estimated in 2015 (about 3,000 documented); a Russian consulate opened in 2017. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [admin.residency] No retirement visa: renewable residence permits of up to five years need proof of income or property; some seasonal residents use 30-day tourist visas. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Summers (June–September) 35–40 °C, AC essential; winters 20–25 °C; sea 21 °C (Feb–Mar) to 28 °C (Aug). — Expat.com 2025-12-01 / Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Over 200 dive centres; Giftun Islands snorkelling, kitesurfing in El Gouna, desert trips. — Expat.com Hurghada guide, updated 2025-12-01, blog, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] A cited study found coral reefs down about 50% over three decades. — Wikipedia "Hurghada", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] LGBT people are prosecuted under "debauchery" laws nationwide; public acceptance is low. — UK FCDO safety page, updated 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
