- [cost.living] Regulated pump prices rose on 28 March 2026: petrol from 775 to 875 XOF/L (≈ €1.18 → €1.33) and diesel from 725 to 940 XOF/L (≈ €1.11 → €1.43); the authorities cited the Gulf energy crisis, and the article also notes the jihadist fuel embargo. — RFI (David Baché) via AllAfrica, 2026-04-01, intl-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] During the fuel crisis, REACH's minimum food basket cost 42,725–48,723 XOF (≈ €65–74) across Bamako communes I, II, IV, V and VI in December 2025; in Commune V it had been 60,529 XOF (≈ €92) in August 2025. — REACH ICSM market factsheet, collected 2025-12-09 to 19, academic (humanitarian survey), confidence: medium (read from PDF table layout)
- [daily.power] At the end of November 2025, after convoys restored fuel, most neighbourhoods still got about six hours of electricity a day, sometimes less. — AFP via Connaissance des Énergies, 2025-11-28, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] In early January 2026, residents of the Golf district reported a cut from 07:00 to 23:00 on one day, and residents of Yirimadjo (Commune VI) said they got 4–5 hours of power a day; no official explanation was given. — Studio Tamani, 2026-01-06, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In May 2026, residents of Médine (Commune II) and Sikoroni said they sometimes went three to four days without water or electricity; neighbours shared a mosque borehole and a free tap. — Studio Tamani, 2026-05-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In early July 2026 (outages two days old on 7 July), sabotage of the high-voltage line from the Manantali dam cut power to several Bamako districts and stopped tap-water distribution; residents queued before dawn at solar boreholes and wells. Nobody claimed it. — Africanews, 2026-07-07, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] At the height of the 2025 fuel blockade, a resident described a filling-station queue of "at least two kilometres"; by 28 November 2025 the queues had gone, but some stations stayed shut. — AFP via Connaissance des Énergies, 2025-11-28, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] On 19 November 2025, Bamako residents said people still spent the night queueing for fuel and many stations were closed; businesses slowed because generators had no fuel during power cuts. — RFI via AllAfrica, 2025-11-19, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] In early March 2026, petrol was available in Bamako but diesel had been "drastically" short for several days. — RFI via AllAfrica, 2026-03-05, intl-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Malian schools closed nationwide from 26 October to 9 November 2025 because of the fuel shortage. — Wikipedia "Mali fuel blockade" citing BBC 2025-10-27, intl-media (digest), confidence: high
- [safety.general] France formally advises against travel to Mali "quel que soit le motif" and tells French nationals still there to plan a temporary departure while commercial flights run. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-10, official, confidence: high
