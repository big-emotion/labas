# Abuja: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 1,500 NGN** (dossier, mid-market, 7 Nov 2026). Annual rents are shown as a monthly EUR figure for comparison only: tenancy law allows 12 months in advance, but many Abuja landlords ask up to two years [C1 cost.rent]; agency and legal fees of 15–25 %, a caution fee and a service charge recur in listings [C1 housing.search]. USD figures are kept as their source states them and are not converted. `[C1 tag]` = an item in `research/cities/nigeria/abuja.md`; `[D]` = the Nigeria dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- FCDO (updated 10 Nov 2026) puts "the Federal Capital Territory, including Abuja" in the heading of its all-but-essential-travel section, though the FCT is not in the bullet list (Niger, Kogi, Plateau, Taraba, Kwara, Benue) [19][C1 safety.general]. Check your insurer before relying on either reading.
- FCDO reports kidnapping in Abuja's outer suburbs and lists the FCT among places with recent terrorist attacks; malls, hotels, bars and large gatherings are possible targets [C1 safety.general]. This touches the very places a newcomer uses (Jabi Lake Mall, Wuse II lounges); it is a city-wide frame, not a district warning.
- Police warn against unpainted taxis because of "one chance" robberies; in Jan 2026 a woman lawyer was killed and found on the Kubwa Expressway [C1 safety.general; safety.women]. Use ride-hailing or official green cabs [3].
- Ebola: Nigeria's first Bundibugyo case was confirmed in Abuja on 20 Oct 2026; contact monitoring was due to end on 13 Nov 2026 [C1 health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Maitama (with Asokoro)
- Feel: the most prestigious district: embassies, officials, government agency headquarters [C1 housing.areas][5]; Asokoro, next to the Aso Villa, is upscale and quiet [C1 housing.areas][8].
- Rent: central districts (Maitama, Asokoro, Wuse, Jabi, Gwarinpa, Guzape) up to ₦3 M/year for a 1-bed (≈ €167/month, Oct 2025) [C1 cost.rent]; a 2026 Maitama 1-bed listing at ₦2.5 M (≈ €139/month) [D S13b]; Expat.com's city-centre range is far higher, ₦5–8 M (≈ €278–444/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 2-bed $2,500–4,500/month (USD as stated) [C1 cost.rent]; duplexes up to ₦20 M/year (≈ €1,110/month) [C1 cost.rent]; one Asokoro 5-bed listing ₦70 M + 20 % fees [C1 cost.rent].
- Getting around: most trips take 15–30 min outside rush hour [C1 daily.transport]; ride-hailing ₦1,500–3,500 a trip [C1 daily.transport].
- Watch: on 10 July 2026 much of Maitama lost power after the AEDC C2 Maitama substation was damaged, with no restoration date [C1 daily.power]. On 16 March 2026 the FCT minister ordered an estate in neighbouring Guzape demolished for being built on park land [C1 housing.search]. Access near Aso Rock is restricted [C1 nature.access].
- Places:
  - Millennium Park (32 ha public park, opened 2003; closes 6 pm) — Wikipedia [11]; hours, Wikivoyage [3]
  - Transcorp Hilton, 1 Aguiyi Ironsi St (hotel; pool open to non-guests) — Wikipedia Maitama [5]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Sarius Planetum, 3259 Ibrahim Babangida Blvd (palm-tree botanical garden, not a planetarium; ₦1,000 adults, undated listing) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - IBB International Golf & Country Club (golf) — Wikipedia Maitama [5]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Unity Fountain (landmark, public space) — Wikipedia Maitama [5]
  - National Children's Park and Zoo, just east of Millennium Park (park, zoo; animal-welfare concerns reported) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Chopsticks, Plot 66 Mississippi St; Albasha Palace, 11 Cassandra St (restaurants; listings 2017–2024, check still open) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (embassies, ministries, agencies); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" relative to the outer districts (central districts are where expats live, mostly in compounds [D]).

### Jabi (with Utako and Life Camp)

- Rent: Jabi is in the "up to ₦3 M/year" 1-bed central group (≈ €167/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 2-bed $1,200–2,000/month (USD as stated) [C1 cost.rent]; neighbouring Life Camp self-contained ₦1.2 M (≈ €67/month), up from ₦500,000 [C1 cost.rent].
- Getting around: 15–30 min to most districts off-peak [C1 daily.transport]; the light rail runs mainly on the west side but with very few trains [3][9].
- Watch: in May 2026, dispenser bottled water was hard to find in Life Camp, Wuye and Guzape [C1 daily.water].

### Gwarinpa
- Feel: Nigeria's largest single housing estate (~1,090 ha, seven residential areas) [7]; family-oriented and more affordable [C1 housing.areas]; mid-to-high-class, traditionally residential (agency guide, undated) [16].
- Rent: in the "up to ₦3 M" 1-bed central group (≈ €167/month) [C1 cost.rent]; a nomad guide gives ₦1–2 M (≈ €56–111/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 3-bed ₦4–6 M (≈ €222–333/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 2-bed $800–1,500/month (USD as stated) [C1 cost.rent].
- Getting around: 15–20 min to the Central Area via the Murtala Mohammed Expressway, Ahmadu Bello Way or Ring Road 2 (agency guide) [16][7]. Petrol at NNPC Gwarinpa ₦1,325/litre on 4 Nov 2026 [C1 daily.transport].
- Watch: the Kubwa Expressway, used by north-west commuters, is where the Jan 2026 "one chance" victim was found [C1 safety.general]. Gwarinpa borders Kubwa and Dawaki to the north-west [7]; Kubwa had at least 16 people abducted in early March 2026 [C1 safety.general].
- Places:
  - Gwarinpa Estate Market (market) — search summary of Gwarinpa guides [20]
  - Pathfield Mall (mall) and Evelyn's Event Centre (events, open 24 h) — Villa Afrika [16]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Crush Café, Yahuza Suya, Domino's Pizza, Cold Stone Creamery (café, grill, fast food) — Villa Afrika [16]
  - Laura Foods, Emto Plaza (restaurant) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Garden restaurants and lounges under trees, often hosting markets and events — Africa.com via [C1 social.nightlife]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

Not proposed.
- **Mararaba, Karu, Suleja, Madalla, Zuba**: rents from ₦400,000–700,000 [C1 cost.rent], but transport can cancel the saving [C1 cost.rent], and Madalla and Zuba are named among border communities hit by repeated kidnappings [C1 safety.general]. Not proposed.

## Living: typical weeks

Prices used below are only those in the city file and in sources listed here. Weekly figures convert monthly ones by ÷ 4.33 and yearly rent by ÷ 52. "Assumption" marks the only non-sourced step (multiplying a one-person grocery figure). Abuja power costs exist only in USD in the files, so they are shown apart.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Jabi**. Central-group rent, a mall with a supermarket and cinema, a lake for breaks, quieter than Wuse II [C1 cost.rent; housing.areas][3]. Abuja averaged 22.3 Mbps in a 2025 ranking (Lagos 40.26) [C1 daily.internet].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; groceries at Shoprite, Jabi Lake Mall [17].
  - Tue: coworking day (₦50,000–120,000/month range) [C1 daily.internet]; no named Abuja coworking was confirmed.
  - Wed: lunchtime walk at Jabi Lake Park [3].
  - Thu: meetings in Wuse II, 15–30 min by ride-hailing [C1 daily.transport]; lunch at City View, Wuse Zone 4 [3].
  - Fri: home office; evening at a garden restaurant [C1 social.nightlife].
  - Sat: Wuse Market in the morning [4]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Millennium Park (closes 6 pm) [3][11].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Abuja has "more lounges than clubs" [C1 social.nightlife]; Jake's Club, Silverbird Galleria, Central Business District, and Blakes Resort, Garki (DJ or live music) [3] (listings 2017–2024); Lost in Abuja publishes a weekly events line-up [C1 social.nightlife].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Wuse II restaurants on Aminu Kano Crescent [3]; Awari Restaurant Week runs in Abuja too, 2–22 Nov 2026 [C1 social.food].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": cinema at Jabi Lake Mall [C1 social.nightlife], or home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Abuja (inland) [D].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Millennium Park [11]; rocks, trails and hills around the city (Pulse) [C1 nature.access]; Usuma Lower Dam trails near the Kubwa reservoir [3] — but in the Bwari/Kubwa area where kidnappings were reported in 2026 [C1 safety.general]; Zuma Rock is in Niger State [13], an FCDO all-but-essential state [19].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Abuja sits in savanna, but the northern savanna and Sahel states are under FCDO restrictions [D]; Farin Ruwa Falls (Nasarawa, not on FCDO lists) is ~3 h 45 away on the Plateau border [14][19]. Treat as not realistic for a regular weekend.
  - "Je reste en ville": Jabi Lake boats [3]; Thought Pyramid Art Centre [3].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: ₦3,000,000 ÷ 52 = ₦57,700 [C1 cost.rent]
  - Groceries: ₦50,000–70,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦11,500–16,200 [C1 cost.living]
  - 4 meals out (supermarket or mid-range): 4 × ₦3,000–5,000 = ₦12,000–20,000 [C1 cost.living]
  - Coworking: ₦50,000–120,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦11,500–27,700 [C1 daily.internet]
  - Home Wi-Fi: ₦15,000–25,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦3,500–5,800 [C1 daily.internet]
  - 6 ride-hailing trips: 6 × ₦1,500–3,500 = ₦9,000–21,000 [C1 daily.transport]
  - **Total ≈ ₦105,200–148,400 a week (≈ €70–99)**, plus generator fuel $50–200/month ÷ 4.33 = $12–46 a week (USD as stated) [C1 daily.power].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Wuse II hotels such as Check Inn Hotels (37 Durban St) or Immaculate (noisy at weekends) [3]; their listed prices date from 2017–2024 and are not used here.
- Day 1: Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport is ~40 km from the centre; take an official green cab or ride-hailing [3][C1 daily.transport]. Visa before travel, yellow fever certificate and online Health Declaration Form required [C1 admin.visa].
- Day 2: SIM and data: MTN 20 GB ₦7,500, 75 GB ₦18,000 for 30 days [C1 daily.internet]; SIM registration rules for foreigners not sourced. Fintech wallets (OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint) and transfers are the default way to pay [D].
- Day 3: viewings. Listings add agency + legal 15–25 %, a caution fee, a service charge and sometimes an inspection fee (₦15,000 in one listing) [C1 cost.rent; housing.search]; "pay and pack in" is used as a selling point [C1 housing.search]. Tenancy law allows 12 months upfront, but landlords ask up to two years [C1 cost.rent]. The calculator site's claim that agency fees can often be halved with a direct landlord is low-confidence [C1 cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: test the real commute at rush hour from the flat you like [C1 daily.transport]; try the light rail if your line fits (Yellow Line Abuja Metro–Airport, four trips a day after the 2024 relaunch) [9].
- Day 5: check water and power in the building: satellite towns go long periods without piped water [C1 daily.water]; bottled-water shortages hit several districts in May 2026 [C1 daily.water]; most expat compounds have backup power [C1 daily.power].
- Day 6: bank account: no source found on what a foreigner needs. Diaspora with a Nigerian parent can claim citizenship by birth [C1 admin.nationality].
- Day 7: Sunday at Millennium Park and Jabi Lake [3][11].
- Rough first-week spend: data ₦7,500 + 10 ride-hailing trips × ₦1,500–3,500 = ₦15,000–35,000 + 14 meals × ₦3,000–5,000 = ₦42,000–70,000 → **₦64,500–112,500 (≈ €43–75)** excluding lodging, which has no current sourced price [C1 daily.internet; daily.transport; cost.living].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read the FCDO frame at the top first: the FCT sits in the heading of the all-but-essential section, which can affect travel insurance [19].

### One day
- Morning: Abuja National Mosque (open to non-Muslims except during congregational prayers) and the National Christian Centre opposite [12]; then Millennium Park, Maitama [11]. Ride-hailing between them (₦1,500–3,500 a trip) [C1 daily.transport].
- Midday: Wuse Market [4]; lunch at City View, Wuse Zone 4 [3], or a ₦3,000–5,000 mid-range meal [C1 cost.living].
- Afternoon: Jabi Lake boat ride or walk [3]; Jabi Lake Mall [17].
- Evening: Wuse II restaurants or a garden lounge [3][C1 social.nightlife].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (Maitama): Sarius Planetum palm garden (3259 Ibrahim Babangida Blvd) [3]; Unity Fountain [5]; Transcorp Hilton pool [3].
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): a weekend morning at Wuse Market, when it is busiest [4]; on a Friday, watch the crowds arrive at the National Mosque from outside (non-Muslims are not admitted during congregational prayer) [12].
- Day 4 (Garki): Cyprian Ekwensi Arts & Culture Centre, Area 10 [6]; Area 10 Market and Area 1 Shopping Complex [3]; Thought Pyramid Art Centre, Wuse [3].
- Day 5 (residential Abuja): Gwarinpa estate and its market [7][20]; ride the light rail Yellow Line from Abuja Metro (four trips a day in 2024; check current timetable) [9].
- Day 6 (trip out, with caveats): Farin Ruwa Falls, Nasarawa State (~150 m drop, ~3 h 45 via Keffi–Akwanga; tourism underdeveloped) [14]. Nasarawa is not on the FCDO lists, but the falls sit on the Plateau State border, and Plateau is [14][19]. Zuma Rock, on the Kaduna road at Madalla in Niger State, is visible from the road [13], but Niger State is an all-but-essential state [19] and Madalla is named in kidnapping reporting [C1 safety.general]: do not plan a stop without checking advice and insurer.
- Day 7 (Sunday): a service at the National Christian Centre [12]; afternoon in Millennium Park or the National Children's Park and Zoo [3].
