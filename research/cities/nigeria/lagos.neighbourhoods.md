# Lagos: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 1,500 NGN** (dossier, mid-market, 7 Nov 2026). Annual rents are shown as a monthly EUR figure for comparison only: landlords ask for one to two years upfront, plus agency, legal and caution fees of roughly 25–30 % [C1 cost.rent]. USD figures are kept as their source states them and are not converted (the dossier gives no USD rate). `[C1 tag]` = an item in `research/cities/nigeria/lagos.md`; `[D]` = the Nigeria dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Two frames to keep in mind for every neighbourhood:
- Lagos is not on the FCDO no-travel or all-but-essential lists, but FCDO reports high violent crime nationwide (muggings, armed robbery, kidnap), foreigners targeted for perceived wealth, and robbery risk even in app taxis: check the driver and plate match the booking [C1 safety.general; 20].
- Flooding peaked on 28 June 2026, with mainland districts, Lekki and Victoria Island under water; the state blames high lagoon levels that stop storm water draining [C1 climate.daily].

## Neighbourhoods

### Ikoyi
- Feel: the city's wealthiest residential district, home to the very rich upper class, embassies and old social clubs [5]; most expats live on the Island (Ikoyi, VI, Lekki) near offices and international schools [C1 housing.areas].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦8 M/year in 2025 (≈ €445/month; Edala via News Express) [C1 cost.rent]; Expat.com gives ~₦6 M (≈ €333/month), 3-beds up to ₦25 M/year (≈ €1,390/month) [C1 cost.rent]; a corporate blogger quotes 2-beds at $3,500–6,000+/month (USD as stated) [C1 cost.rent]. Serviced residences add ₦2–8 M/year in service charges [C1 housing.areas].
- Getting around: no sourced commute time to VI (adjacent district). Okada (motorbike taxi) banned in Ikoyi-Obalende; 65 bikes seized in Ikoyi in Feb 2026 [C1 daily.transport]. Lagos Ferry Services runs boats between Lagos Island, Banana Island, Mile 2 and the mainland [3]. Expats mostly hire a driver ($150–250/month, USD as stated) [C1 daily.transport].
- Watch: the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 cap of one year's advance rent does **not** apply in Ikoyi [C1 cost.rent; D]. Starlink residential sign-ups showed "Sold Out" in Ikoyi in Feb 2026 [C1 daily.internet].
- Places:
  - Ikoyi Club (social club) — Wikipedia, Ikoyi [5]
  - Ikoyi Golf Club (golf course) — Wikipedia, Ikoyi [5]; Wikivoyage calls it one of Africa's largest golf courses [3]
  - Awolowo Road (high street of shops and boutiques) — Wikipedia, Ikoyi [5]
  - Falomo garden / green space along Alfred Rewane Road (park) — Wikipedia, Ikoyi [5]
  - Lagos Preparatory School (school) — Wikipedia, Ikoyi [5]
  - French Consulate, which houses the Franco-Nigerian Chamber of Commerce (FNCCI) (consulate, business network) — dossier S30 [D]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (highest rents in the file outside Eko Atlantic and Banana Island); Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (consulate and chamber community); Q6 "Français" only marginally (FNCCI, consulate) — Lagos stays English-speaking [D].

### Victoria Island (VI)
- Feel: Nigeria's main business and financial hub, with some of the most expensive homes in Lagos [6]; Akin Adesola Street, where Adeola Odeku runs into it, is a nightclub hotspot [6]; VI has the main bar and rooftop scene [C1 social.nightlife; 3].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦7.5 M/year in 2025 (≈ €415/month) [C1 cost.rent]; Expat.com ~₦4 M (≈ €222/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 2-bed $2,500–4,500/month (USD as stated, corporate blogger) [C1 cost.rent].
- Getting around: okada ban covers VI [C1 daily.transport]. Ride-hailing short trips ₦2,000–5,000 (≈ €1.33–3.33), more at peak [C1 daily.transport]. Uber left Nigeria on 2 Sept 2026; Bolt, inDrive, LagRide, Rida, Shuttlers, SimpliRide remain [C1 daily.transport].
- Watch: VI was under water in the June 2026 floods [C1 climate.daily]; flooding in Aug 2025 too [D]. Tenancy Law advance-rent cap does not apply in VI [C1 cost.rent]. Starlink residential "Sold Out" in VI (Feb 2026) [C1 daily.internet].
- Places:
  - Lycée Français Louis Pasteur (AEFE school, nursery to baccalauréat; grades 7–10 ₦16.0 M/year, 2025) — Wikipedia VI [6]; fees [C1 family.schools]
  - British International School Lagos and American International School of Lagos (schools) — Wikipedia VI [6]
  - Reddington Hospital (private hospital; consult ₦25,000 + ₦10,000 registration, late 2025) — Wikipedia VI [6]; fee [C1 health.care]
  - EKO Hospital (private hospital) — Wikipedia VI [6]
  - Silverbird Galleria (mall and cinema) — Wikipedia VI [6]
  - Workstation NG and 989 Workspaces (coworking, VI/Oniru; prices on request) — Roadbook, updated 2026-03-19 [19]
  - TOP BEANS Coffee & More, WINGS complex, Ozumba Mbadiwe Ave (café; ₦1,200 a coffee) — Roadbook [19]
  - Bar Beach (Atlantic seafront) — Wikivoyage Lagos [3]; Eko Atlantic, reclaimed land next to VI, where expats walk and jog — Wikipedia VI [6], [C1 nature.access]
- Fits: Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (office district); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" for families who can pay international fees; Q3 "Le prix compte peu".

### Lekki Phase 1
- Feel: Expat.com's "middle ground" between Ikoyi/VI prices and the mainland [C1 housing.areas]; a reputation for some of the state's most expensive real estate [7]; dense with megachurches, international schools and malls [7][19].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦4.5 M/year in 2025 (≈ €250/month); Lekki Peninsula II ₦3.5 M (≈ €195/month) [C1 cost.rent]; 2-bed $2,000–3,500/month (USD as stated, corporate blogger) [C1 cost.rent]. Land ₦1.5 M/m² in 2026 versus ₦421,000 in 2022 [C1 business.invest].
- Getting around: linked to Ikoyi by the Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge (2013) [7]. Eti-Osa is in the okada-ban zone [C1 daily.transport]. No sourced commute time to VI.
- Watch: Lekki flooded in Aug 2025 [D] and again on 28 June 2026 [C1 climate.daily]; check a flat's flood history before signing [D]. Starlink "Sold Out" in Lekki (Feb 2026) [C1 daily.internet].
- Places:
  - Lekki Conservation Centre (78 ha nature reserve, canopy walkway, 1.8 km trail, picnic area and playground) — Wikipedia [10]; canopy walkway 401 m [C1 nature.access]
  - Nike Art Gallery (gallery, four floors, free entry) — Wikipedia [11]; [C1 social.culture]
  - Lekki Arts and Crafts Market / Oba Elegushi Market (craft market) — Wikipedia, Lekki [7]
  - The Palms Shopping Mall with Pause Cafe (mall, café; ₦2,700 a coffee) — Roadbook [19]
  - Moonshine Cafe (café, ₦1,500 a coffee) and Cafe One by Sterling, Lennox Mall (café-cowork, from ₦3,780 a day) — Roadbook [19]
  - Lekki British School; American International School of Lagos, Lekki campus (schools) — Wikipedia, Lekki [7]; Lekki British secondary ~₦4.6 M/year (2025) [C1 family.schools]
  - Lekki Central Mosque; House on the Rock (mosque, church) — Wikipedia, Lekki [7]
  - Evercare Hospital Lekki (private hospital, 165 beds; consult ₦25,000) — dossier S31 [D]; fee [C1 health.care]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple" (schools, malls, churches close by); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (returnee crowd in Lekki/VI [D]); Q8 "À la plage" (eastern beaches are along this side [3]).

### Yaba
- Feel: Lagos's tech district around CcHub (since 2011) [9][C1 business.startup]; students, tech workers, creatives, young families and founders (company guide, undated) [17]; home of the University of Lagos and Yaba College of Technology [9].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦2.2 M/year in 2025 (≈ €120/month) [C1 cost.rent]. A property-company guide (dated "Feb 2", year not shown) gives self-contained ₦0.7–1.1 M (≈ €39–61/month) and 2-bed ₦1.4–2.3 M (≈ €78–128/month) [17] — lower than the Edala average, treat as optimistic. Sale: 2-bed flat ~₦140 M (asking) [C1 business.invest].
- Getting around: 20–40 min to VI/Ikoyi off-peak via Third Mainland Bridge, much longer at peak [17]. BRT corridors on Ikorodu Road to TBS/CMS, ₦300–1,000+, Cowry card only [3][C1 daily.transport]. Mobolaji Johnson Central Station (Lagos–Ibadan standard-gauge rail) is in Yaba [9]. Okada banned in Yaba [C1 daily.transport].
- Watch: drainage problems in some low-lying streets (company guide, undated) [17]; "mainland districts" were flooded in June 2026 [C1 climate.daily]. Makoko, the floating settlement, is part of Yaba [9].
- Places:
  - Co-Creation Hub (CcHub) (innovation hub, coworking; private startup offices since June 2026) — Wikipedia Yaba [9]; [C1 business.startup]
  - HubOne, Alagomeji (coworking) — Roadbook [19]
  - Tejuosho Market (market, rebuilt as a shopping complex) and Yaba Market — Wikipedia Yaba [9]
  - E-Centre, Commercial Avenue, with Ozone Cinemas (mall, cinema) — Wikipedia Yaba [9]
  - University of Lagos (university, Akoka) — Wikipedia Yaba [9]
  - All Saints Anglican Church; Yaba Baptist Church (churches) — Wikipedia Yaba [9]
  - Ojez Night Club, 7 Chief Onitana St (club) — Wikivoyage Surulere page [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (hubs and coworking); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Surulere
- Feel: older mainland district of professionals, civil servants, small-business owners and young families (company guide, undated) [18]; known for its two stadiums and the commercial strip of Adeniran Ogunsanya Street [4].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦1.5 M/year in 2025 (≈ €83/month), the cheapest of the named central districts in the Edala report [C1 cost.rent]. Company guide (undated): mini flat ₦0.9–1.2 M (≈ €50–67/month), 2-bed ₦1.5–2.5 M (≈ €83–139/month), varying by street (Bode Thomas, Shitta) [18].
- Getting around: 15–45 min to VI/Ikoyi depending on traffic [18]. Danfo buses and BRT; okada banned in Surulere [4][C1 daily.transport]. Blue Line rail stops at National Theatre (Iganmu) and Suru Alaba, fare ₦375 [3].
- Watch: drainage problems in some low-lying streets in heavy rain (company guide) [18]. Starlink residential "Sold Out" in Surulere (Feb 2026) [C1 daily.internet].
- Places:
  - Teslim Balogun Stadium (football, ~23,000 seats, home of First Bank F.C.) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - National Stadium Lagos (multi-sport complex, Olympic pool; condition disputed) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - Adeniran Ogunsanya Shopping Mall, with Shoprite (mall, supermarket) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]; Shoprite from search summary of the same pages
  - Leisure Mall with Filmhouse Cinema (8 screens, ₦1,000–2,500, listing undated) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - Census Market (50 Baba Animashaun St) and Aguda Market (Kusimo St) (markets) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - Ofadaboy, 1 Mba St (restaurant, ofada rice); Suya Spot, Akerele St (grill) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - Afro-Spot, Fela Kuti's first shrine (music heritage) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
  - National Theatre (performing-arts centre, 1976) — Wikivoyage Surulere [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (lowest rent among the named central districts); Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (young families, malls); Q10 "Une vie abordable"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (local restaurants).

### Ikeja (incl. Ikeja GRA)
- Feel: state capital since 1976, home to the airport, TV studios, Computer Village and Fela Kuti venues; congested around Computer Village, with unreliable electricity [8].
- Rent: 1-bed average ₦3.2 M/year in 2025 (≈ €180/month) [C1 cost.rent]; Magodo/Ikeja GRA 2-bed $1,500–2,500/month (USD as stated, corporate blogger) [C1 cost.rent].
- Getting around: Murtala Muhammed International Airport is in Ikeja [3]. The Red Line rail runs Agbado–Oyingbo [C1 daily.transport]; the Lagos–Ibadan line stops at Ikeja and Agege [16]. Okada banned in Ikeja [C1 daily.transport]. No sourced commute time to the Island.
- Watch: Ikeja Electric apologised seven times in nine days (27 Oct–4 Nov 2026) for missing the 20-hour Band A promise on some feeders [C1 daily.power]. The Tenancy Law cap does not apply in Ikeja GRA [C1 cost.rent]. Police reported "one chance" robberies (fake commuter buses) on the Oworonshoki, Alapere, Ogudu and Ketu axes nearby (Gazette, 2024-02-12) [C1 safety.general].
- Places:
  - Ikeja City Mall (largest mall on the mainland) — Wikipedia Ikeja [8]
  - Computer Village, Otigba (electronics market) — Wikipedia Ikeja [8]
  - New Afrika Shrine (music venue; concerts Thursday and Sunday) — Wikipedia Ikeja [8]; [C1 social.nightlife]
  - Kalakuta Republic Museum (Fela Kuti museum, rooftop café; ~₦7,500 entry) — Wikipedia Ikeja [8]; fee [C1 social.culture]
  - EridanSpace, Alausa (coworking, ₦5,000 a day) — Roadbook [19]
  - Johnson Jakande Tinubu Park (park) — Wikipedia Ikeja [8]
  - Lagoon Hospitals Ikeja (private hospital; consult ₦20,000 + ₦8,000 registration) — [C1 health.care]; Duchess International (private hospital) — dossier S31 [D]
  - Lagos State University Teaching Hospital (LASUTH), 1–5 Oba Akinjobi Way (public hospital, 24 h) — Wikivoyage Lagos [3]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (Computer Village trade, Alausa coworking) and "Création, culture" (Fela heritage); Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport on the doorstep); Q3 "Moyen".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices used below are only those in the city file and in sources listed here. Weekly figures convert monthly ones by ÷ 4.33 and yearly rent by ÷ 52. "Assumption" marks the only non-sourced step (doubling a one-person figure for two people).

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Yaba**. Cheapest rent among the districts with a coworking scene (1-bed ₦2.2 M/year) [C1 cost.rent], hubs within the district [9][19], 20–40 min to VI off-peak for meetings [17].
- Week:
  - Mon: work at HubOne, Alagomeji [19]; lunch at a neighbourhood buka (₦1,500–3,000) [C1 cost.living]; walk.
  - Tue: work from home; if the grid fails, café with sockets is the usual workaround [C1 work.remote]; evening errands at Yaba Market [9].
  - Wed: CcHub event or day pass [9]; danfo or BRT inside the mainland (₦300–1,000+ per BRT trip) [3].
  - Thu: client meeting on VI: 20–40 min by ride-hailing off-peak (₦2,000–5,000 a short trip, more at peak) [17][C1 daily.transport]; coffee at TOP BEANS, Ozumba Mbadiwe Ave (₦1,200) [19].
  - Fri: home; evening film at Ozone Cinemas, E-Centre [9].
  - Sat: Tejuosho Market in the morning [9]; Saturday-night options below.
  - Sun: All Saints Anglican Church or Yaba Baptist Church [9], or rest.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Ojez Night Club, Yaba [4], or VI clubs along Akin Adesola Street [6].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": mid-range restaurant ₦8,000–20,000 per person [C1 cost.living]; Awari Restaurant Week runs 2–22 Nov 2026 with app vouchers [C1 social.food].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": delivery in about 45 minutes is now the norm (Lost in Lagos) [C1 social.pace]; film at Ozone Cinemas [9].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": eastern beaches (Lekki, Alpha, Eleko, Akodo) [3]; no dated fee found.
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no mountains; Lekki Conservation Centre's marsh forest and canopy walkway is the forest option in town [10]; Olumo Rock, Abeokuta, by Lagos–Ibadan rail from Yaba's Mobolaji Johnson station [9][15][16].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Lagos (the savanna and Sahel states are under FCDO travel restrictions [D]).
  - "Je reste en ville": Freedom Park, Lagos Island [12]; Nike Art Gallery, free [11].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: ₦2,200,000 ÷ 52 = ₦42,300 [C1 cost.rent]
  - Home cooking: ₦60,000–100,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦13,900–23,100 [C1 cost.living]
  - 3 buka lunches: 3 × ₦1,500–3,000 = ₦4,500–9,000 [C1 cost.living]
  - 1 mid-range dinner: ₦8,000–20,000 [C1 cost.living]
  - Coworking: ₦60,000–150,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦13,900–34,600 [C1 work.remote]
  - Power (prepaid + generator fuel): ₦30,000–70,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦6,900–16,200 [C1 daily.power]
  - Home internet: ₦30,000–50,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦6,900–11,500 [C1 daily.internet]
  - Public transport: ₦30,000–75,000/month ÷ 4.33 = ₦6,900–17,300 [C1 daily.transport]
  - **Total ≈ ₦103,300–174,000 a week (≈ €69–116)**, before the first-year fees (25–30 % of rent: ₦550,000–660,000 once) [C1 cost.rent].

  - Schools: Lekki British secondary ~₦4.6 M/year × 2 children ÷ 52 = ₦176,900 [C1 family.schools]; the Lycée would be ₦16.0 M × 2 ÷ 52 = ₦615,400 for grades 7–10 [C1 family.schools]

  - Power ₦6,900–16,200; internet ₦6,900–11,500 [C1 daily.power; daily.internet]
  - **Naira items ≈ ₦246,300–297,000 a week (≈ €164–198) with Lekki British**, plus €43–85 insurance and $462–808 housing (USD as stated).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Ikeja for a first night near the airport [3], then a short-let on the Island or in Yaba. No dated nightly price was found; in December, short-let rates doubled or tripled during Detty December 2025 [C1 social.nightlife] — avoid starting a trial in December.
- Day 1: arrive at Murtala Muhammed (Ikeja) [3]; online landing card and Health Declaration Form done before travel, yellow fever certificate required [C1 admin.visa]. Ride-hailing only, check driver and plate [C1 safety.general].
- Day 2: SIM and data. MTN 30-day bundles: 20 GB ₦7,500, 75 GB ₦18,000 [C1 daily.internet]. How to register a SIM as a foreigner: not sourced. Fintech wallets (OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint) are how most people pay [D]; transfers and mobile payments common but cash still needed for small purchases [C1 daily.money]. A retiree from London could get neither cash nor card without a NIN (national ID number), 2024 [C1 return.experience].
- Day 3: viewings. Never pay an "inspection" fee before seeing a property; genuine agents take commission after signing [C1 housing.search]. Expect 1–2 years upfront, agency ~10 % + legal ~10 % + caution 5–10 % [C1 cost.rent]. The one-year advance cap applies by law except in Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and VI [C1 cost.rent]. Ask for a generator or air conditioners to be included; agree whether a dollar-quoted rent is fixed in dollars or naira [C1 cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: test the commute you would really do at rush hour (BRT, Blue Line ₦375 with a Cowry card costing ₦500–1,000) [C1 daily.transport].
- Day 5: test power and internet in the flat you like: ask the feeder's Band (A promises 20 h) [C1 daily.power]; Starlink residential is sold out in several districts [C1 daily.internet].
- Day 6: bank account: no source found on what a foreign national needs; diaspora with a Nigerian parent can claim citizenship by birth [C1 admin.nationality].
- Day 7: rest, church or mosque, and a beach afternoon [3][7].
- Rough first-week spend: 7 days of data (20 GB ₦7,500) + 10 ride-hailing trips × ₦2,000–5,000 = ₦20,000–50,000 + 14 buka meals × ₦1,500–3,000 = ₦21,000–42,000 → **₦48,500–99,500 (≈ €32–66)** excluding lodging, which has no sourced price [C1 daily.internet; daily.transport; cost.living].

### One day
- Morning: Lekki Conservation Centre: canopy walkway (401 m), tree house, 1.8 km trail [10][C1 nature.access]; entry fee not found.

### One week
- Day 1 (Lekki): the one-day route above, ending on VI.
- Day 2 (Lagos Island): Balogun and Idumota markets in the morning [3]; National Museum and Freedom Park [3][12]; Blue Line from Marina to National Theatre, ₦375 [3].
- Day 3 (Ikeja): Kalakuta Republic Museum (~₦7,500) [C1 social.culture]; Computer Village [8]; Shrine at night on Thursday or Sunday [C1 social.nightlife].

- Day 5 (daily-life moment, mainland): a weekday morning at Tejuosho Market [9], then ride the BRT at rush hour on the Ikorodu Road corridor (Cowry card) [3]; evening football at Teslim Balogun Stadium if First Bank F.C. play [4].
- Day 6 (trip out, Abeokuta): Lagos–Ibadan rail from Ebute Metta/Yaba to Abeokuta [16][9]; climb Olumo Rock [15]. Ogun State is not under FCDO restriction [20]. Fares and times not found.
- Day 7 (Sunday): a service at a Lekki church (House on the Rock) or Lekki Central Mosque [7]; afternoon on an eastern beach [3].
