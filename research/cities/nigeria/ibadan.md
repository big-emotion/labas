## Items

- [cost.rent] 2-bed in an "emerging" area (Akala Way) ~₦800,000/year in 2025 (≈ €44/month), forecast ₦1.2 M for 2026; the same guide says Bodija and Jericho rents rose ~50 %. — Nigeria Housing Market via dossier S13, updated 2026-01-03, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Room-and-parlour in Kuola (off Akala Expressway): ₦600,000/year rent but ₦910,000 total first payment once service charge, agreement, damages, form and agency fees are added. — Gazette rent survey, 2024-08-29 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] One-room self-contained in Akala Estate (Akobo): ₦500,000/year, ₦700,000 with fees. — Gazette rent survey, 2024-08-29 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Ologuneru: room-and-parlour ₦500,000 (₦750,000 with fees); one-room self-contained ₦400,000 (₦620,000 with fees). — Gazette rent survey, 2024-08-29 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] In that survey the Kuola package itemised ₦60,000 each for service charge, agreement and damages, ₦10,000 form fee and ₦120,000 agency fee — fees adding ~50 % to the first year. — Gazette rent survey, 2024-08-29 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] A cost aggregator puts a single person at ~$335/month excluding rent (USD as stated; method unclear). — Living Cost Index via dossier S39, 2026, company, confidence: low
- [work.remote] Ibadan ranked 3rd among Nigerian cities for remote work in 2025, cited as one of the cheapest places to live with a growing tech community; average download 23.03 Mbps. — Technext, 2025-06-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] StartupBlink 2026 places Ibadan 3rd in Nigeria and 522nd worldwide, with 3.8 % of the national ecosystem score (Lagos 78 %). — Nairametrics, 2026-08-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The College of Medicine area of University College Hospital (UCH) was without grid power for ~7 months of 2026 after a breaker failure; resident doctors began a 3-day warning strike on 12 Oct 2026. — AllAfrica, 2026-10-13, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] The same outage caused a chronic water shortage in UCH residential areas; doctors reportedly bought water from private sellers and places of worship on site. — AllAfrica, 2026-10-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] A 2016 investigation found only a "negligible fraction" of Ibadan homes on mains water; residents relied on wells, boreholes and vendors, and boreholes needed ~180 ft depth versus ~120 ft in 1998. — ICIR, 2016-06-23 (old), local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Lagos–Ibadan by road: about 1 h 15 min from Berger to Challenge on a clear day; Kara Bridge is the worst choke point and 2026 bridge repairs added 45–60 min at peak. — Nigeria Housing Market, 2026-02 (updated 2026-06-03), company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Bolt and inDrive both operate in Ibadan after Uber's exit on 2 Sept 2026. — Condia, 2026-09-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] UCH resident doctors said their congress would decide after the October 2026 warning strike whether to go on indefinite strike — a risk of disruption at the city's teaching hospital. — AllAfrica, 2026-10-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Regulators seized ₦100 bn of counterfeit drugs in Ibadan in March 2025; the NAFDAC Greenbook app checks a drug's registration number. — Rio Times, 2026-09-17, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Oyo State (Ibadan's state) is not on the UK FCDO all-travel or all-but-essential lists. — UK FCDO via dossier S1, 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 15 May 2026 gunmen abducted 39 students and 7 teachers from schools in Oriire area, Oyo State; Al Jazeera notes such attacks are rare in the southwest. — Al Jazeera, 2026-05-19, intl-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie cites the Oyo school abduction as a sign that the terrorist threat "tend à s'étendre plus au sud". — France Diplomatie via dossier S3, 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In July 2026 an alleged phone robbery at a POS stand in Ojoo, Ibadan, escalated into a mob killing and reprisal attacks; at least two dead, six shops burnt, and police warned against "jungle justice". — Premium Times, 2026-07-09, local-media, confidence: high
