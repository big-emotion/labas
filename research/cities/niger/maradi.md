## Items

- [cost.rent] Modest housing in Niger's other main cities (Zinder, Maradi, Tahoua): 15,000–35,000 XOF/month (≈ €23–53). — Africarrières "Coût de la vie au Niger", 2026-08-07, company (job-board guide), confidence: low
- [cost.rent] "Decent" housing in the same cities: 30,000–70,000 XOF/month (≈ €46–107). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Upscale villa in the same cities: 80,000–200,000 XOF/month (≈ €122–305). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A different guide gives a two-room flat "in the provinces" (Maradi, Zinder, Dosso, not split) at 100,000–200,000 XOF/month (≈ €152–305), far above the Africarrières range; both kept. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère du Niger", 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same guide puts monthly food at 80,000–120,000 XOF (≈ €122–183), urban transport at 15,000 XOF (≈ €23) and electricity plus water at 20,000–30,000 XOF (≈ €30–46) in the provinces, for a minimum subtotal of about 250,000 XOF (≈ €381), half its Niamey figure. — OpenShores, 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The guide also says living costs fall 20–30% in the provinces compared with Niamey, which does not match its own subtotals (half); treat both as rough. — OpenShores, 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Nigerien minimum wage: 42,000 XOF/month (≈ €64) since January 2026 per the guide's verified box; its body text still gives 30,047 XOF (≈ €46) under a 2012 decree. (national) — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Maradi's economy turns on a large daily Grand Marché (wholesale, retail, farm produce from south-central Niger) and cross-border trade with Nigeria; it is a groundnut-growing area. — Wikipedia "Maradi, Niger", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [business.invest] A ministerial order of 11 November 2026 bans groundnut exports nationwide (exceptional permits only for AES countries); goods caught being exported are seized. Relevant to Maradi's groundnut trade. — ActuNiger, 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Intercity bus from Niamey to Maradi: 4,000–6,000 XOF (≈ €6.10–9.15). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Maradi sits on National Road 1, about 662 km east of Niamey, at the junction of roads to Katsina and Kano in Nigeria, and has an airport. — Wikipedia (FR) "Maradi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Wikipedia says both the CFA franc and the Nigerian naira can be used in Maradi (claim marked as unsourced in the article). — Wikipedia "Maradi, Niger", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Nationally, 23.2% of people used the internet at the end of 2025 (6.52 million) and social-media user identities were only 2.7% of the population. (national) — DataReportal "Digital 2026: Niger", 2025-11-08, intl-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] France says medevac capacity outside Niamey is very limited and strongly advises repatriation to France for surgery. (national) — France Diplomatie (santé), updated 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Maradi has Université Dan Dicko Dankoulodo (2008), the private Université libre de Maradi (2004) and Université internationale Aboubacar Ibrahim (2020); the English article also lists an IUT and Maryam Abacha American University Niger. — Wikipedia (FR and EN) "Maradi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK says banditry and smuggling are common in border areas, "particularly Maradi", Tillabéri and Diffa, and advises not crossing borders after dark; it advises against all travel outside Niamey. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14 (dossier: updated 2026-10-23), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France reports kidnapping for ransom by organised criminal groups coming from Nigeria in the south of the country; the whole country is red. — France Diplomatie (sécurité), updated 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Belgium says leaving Niamey requires prior approval from the authorities and a paid armed escort. — Belgian MFA, updated 2026-10-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Maradi is a centre of Hausa culture founded in 1807 by exiled Katsina rulers; Hausa dominates, with Fulani and Tuareg residents and Igbo and Yoruba traders from Nigeria. — Wikipedia (EN and FR) "Maradi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [social.religion] The city is mostly Muslim (Sultanate and Tchana mosques), with a Catholic diocese and cathedral, Protestant churches and an Assemblies of God presence. — Wikipedia "Maradi, Niger", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [social.sport] The Yacouba Ango traditional-wrestling arena (5,000 seats) opened in June 2021. — Wikipedia (FR) "Maradi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hot semi-arid: about 14–40°C over the year, April the hottest month (mean highs near 40°C); about 491 mm of rain, mostly June–September, almost none November–February. — Wikipedia "Maradi, Niger", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Nationwide, the penal code in force since June 2026 punishes same-sex relations with 5 to 10 years in prison and same-sex marriage with 10 to 20 years. (national) — Belgian MFA (législation locale), updated 2026-10-14, official, confidence: high
- [other] Population: 267,249 at the 2012 census; a 2024 projection gives 412,364. A 2018 figure puts the regional fertility rate at 8.4 children per woman. — Wikipedia (FR) "Maradi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
