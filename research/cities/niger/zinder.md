## Items

- [cost.rent] Modest housing in Niger's other main cities (Zinder, Maradi, Tahoua): 15,000–35,000 XOF/month (≈ €23–53). — Africarrières "Coût de la vie au Niger", 2026-08-07, company (job-board guide), confidence: low
- [cost.rent] "Decent" housing in the same cities: 30,000–70,000 XOF/month (≈ €46–107); upscale villa 80,000–200,000 XOF (≈ €122–305). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A different guide gives a two-room flat "in the provinces" (Maradi, Zinder, Dosso, not split) at 100,000–200,000 XOF/month (≈ €152–305), far above the Africarrières range; both kept. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère du Niger", 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same guide puts provincial monthly food at 80,000–120,000 XOF (≈ €122–183), urban transport at 15,000 XOF (≈ €23) and electricity plus water at 20,000–30,000 XOF (≈ €30–46). — OpenShores, 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Pump prices nationwide: 499 XOF/L super (≈ €0.76), 618 XOF/L diesel (≈ €0.94), held after the government rejected a rise on 9 October 2026. (national) — La Nouvelle Tribune, 2026-10-10, intl-media, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Zinder has five communes; the main quarters are Birni (old Hausa town with the Sultan's palace and Great Mosque), Zengou (old Tuareg suburb) and Sabon Gari between them, the commercial centre with the Grand Marché; it also extends to Karkada, Garin Mallam and Gawon Kollia. — Wikipedia "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The local economy rests mainly on farming (over 80% of jobs in the region), livestock and gathering, plus trade, transport and small industry: a tannery, food and forest-produce processing, and the SORAZ refinery. — Wikipedia (FR and EN) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In March 2026 Zinder had a fuel shortage; the governor ordered restocking of stations and banned selling fuel from jerrycans, saying fraudulent fuel made up "more than 50%" of consumption. — Seneweb, 2026-03-26, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Niger's only working refinery is SORAZ, about 50 km north of Zinder (20,000 barrels/day, opened 2011), yet the national petrol shortage of October 2026 still hit several cities before Niamey. — AFP via Connaissance des Énergies, 2026-10-18; Wikipedia "Zinder", intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Intercity bus from Niamey to Zinder: 3,500–5,000 XOF (≈ €5.35–7.60). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Zinder lies 861 km east of Niamey and 240 km north of Kano, on the N1 (Niamey–Dosso–Maradi–Zinder–Diffa) and the trans-Saharan Algiers–Lagos route, with an airport (ZND). — Wikipedia (EN and FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The city has a long history of water shortages; most of it is supplied by a pipeline from the north run by a Chinese-owned company, and the article expects the problem to continue as the population grows. — Wikipedia "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Catholic mission runs several clinics, including one in Karakara, the leprosy quarter. — Wikipédia (FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: low
- [health.care] France says medevac capacity outside Niamey is very limited and strongly advises repatriation to France for surgery. (national) — France Diplomatie (santé), updated 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] The public university (André-Salifou University; the English article dates it to 2010, the French one to 2008) teaches medicine, urban planning and natural-resource management; the Saint-Joseph complex in Jaguindi runs from primary to lycée, and hundreds of Koranic schools operate. — Wikipedia (EN and FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Canada says tourists travelling at night have been attacked in Zinder city and bandit gangs operate north of the Tahoua–Zinder–Diffa axis; it advises avoiding remote areas around Zinder. — Global Affairs Canada, updated 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France reports kidnapping for ransom by groups coming from Nigeria in the south; whole country red and road travel outside Niamey ruled out. — France Diplomatie (sécurité), updated 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 8 November 2026 the drugs police seized 54,000 psychotropic tablets (Exol and diazepam) from a public minibus at the Tanout checkpoint on the way out of Zinder. — ActuNiger, 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 15 January 2015, riots after the Charlie Hebdo march damaged most of the city's churches, destroyed the Assomption school and hit many Christian homes and businesses. — Wikipédia (FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14 (event 2015, older), academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The population is mainly Hausa and Kanuri, with Fulani and Tuareg groups; Zinder was the capital of the Damagaram sultanate and Niger's capital until 1926. — Wikipédia (FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Music scene: griot kalangou music, ganbara (from the 1990s Haské group), rap since the 2000s, and church gospel choirs with an annual Christmas concert; the regional museum is the main museum. — Wikipédia (FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: low
- [social.religion] Mostly Muslim, with more than a dozen churches; Christianity has been officially present since 1930. — Wikipédia (FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Hot arid to semi-arid: about 410 mm of rain a year, three-quarters in July–August and almost none October–May; temperatures rarely below 12°C or above 42°C. — Wikipedia "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] In September 2024 Zinder's old mosque, one of Niger's oldest, collapsed after intense rainfall. — Wikipedia "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Nationwide, the penal code in force since June 2026 punishes same-sex relations with 5 to 10 years in prison and same-sex marriage with 10 to 20 years. (national) — Belgian MFA (législation locale), updated 2026-10-14, official, confidence: high
- [other] Population: 235,605 at the 2012 census; the French article gives 376,994 for 2016. — Wikipedia (EN and FR) "Zinder", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (reference digest), confidence: medium
