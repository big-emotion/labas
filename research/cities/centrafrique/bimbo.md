# Bimbo, Central African Republic

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

> Extra city (dossier "additional", part of Grand Bangui; not in cities.json). Bimbo-specific
> sources are scarce, so many price items are Grand Bangui or national figures and are marked
> "(Grand Bangui)" or "(national)". **Advisory note:** France treats Bimbo like Bangui (not formally
> advised against), but the UK FCDO text read today puts Bimbo in its **all-travel** zone, not the
> "all but essential" Bangui zone the dossier describes; see the safety items. Whoever scores this
> city should weigh it as at least as restricted as Bangui.
> Rates: 655.957 XAF = €1 (fixed CEMAC peg, dossier rate). USD-only figures are converted at the
> rate implied by REACH June–July 2026 (115 USD ≈ €99, i.e. 1 USD ≈ €0.86).

## Items

- [cost.rent] A 2026 online cost-of-living guide gives monthly rents in Bangui of 600–1,200 USD (≈ €516–1,033) for an air-conditioned 2-bed in "premium" Bé-Guma or Koudougou, 300–600 USD (≈ €258–516) mid-range and 100–300 USD (≈ €86–258) for basic housing; it calls them estimates to verify. (Grand Bangui) — Africarrières "Prix et budget 2026", 2026-02-25, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The same guide's FAQ gives 50,000–150,000 XAF/month (≈ €76–229) for a "decent" Bangui flat, far below its own table; listed separately, not averaged. (Grand Bangui) — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The typical monthly rent for the cheapest newly built formal house was 100,000 XAF (≈ €152) in 2020 data. (national) — Africa Housing Finance Yearbook 2024 (CAHF), published 2025-03, academic, confidence: medium

- [cost.living] A 50 kg bag of cement averages 11,500 XAF (≈ €17.53) in Bangui against a regulated 9,500 XAF (≈ €14.48), because of import costs, road conditions and dealer mark-ups. (Grand Bangui) — Africa Housing Finance Yearbook 2024 citing Radio Ndeke Luka 2024-05, published 2025-03, academic, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] REACH's June–July 2026 survey put the monthly survival basket (food, non-food, hygiene) at 60,274 XAF (≈ €92) at Bangui-Centre, 79,695 XAF (≈ €121) at Bangui-Fleuve, 82,776 XAF (≈ €126) at Bangui-Kagas and 77,359 XAF (≈ €118) at Bangui-Rapide; Bimbo was not surveyed separately. (Grand Bangui) — REACH/ICSM factsheet, collected 2026-06-17 to 2026-07-08, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Between April and June 2026 the basket fell slightly in most of the country but rose 16–25% on three Bangui markets, which REACH links to shortages and bad roads; cassava rose 17% and rice 25%. (Grand Bangui) — REACH/ICSM factsheet June–July 2026, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] National median market prices in June–July 2026: rice 313 XAF per 500 g (≈ €0.48), beef 2,500 XAF/kg (≈ €3.81), vegetable oil 1,275 XAF/L (≈ €1.94), dried cassava 175 XAF per 500 g (≈ €0.27), tomatoes 225 XAF/kg (≈ €0.34), one egg 125 XAF (≈ €0.19), soap 250 XAF (≈ €0.38). (national) — REACH/ICSM factsheet June–July 2026, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] REACH's national median market price for petrol was 1,250 XAF/L (≈ €1.91) and a 20-litre can of water 50 XAF (≈ €0.08) in June–July 2026. (national) — REACH/ICSM factsheet June–July 2026, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Official pump prices since 1 October 2026: petrol 1,150 XAF/L (≈ €1.75, from 1,050) and diesel 1,400 XAF/L (≈ €2.13, from 1,250). (national) — RFI via AllAfrica, 2026-10-07, intl-media, confidence: high

(Grand Bangui) — Africa Housing Finance Yearbook 2024, published 2025-03, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] In 2021, 37.6% of Bangui-area residents lived in the south-west (2nd and 6th arrondissements and Bimbo), and 38.5% in the north (4th, 8th and Bégoua). — Africa Housing Finance Yearbook 2024, published 2025-03, academic, confidence: medium

- [daily.power] Phase one (15 MW) of the Sakaï 2 solar farm in Bimbo began feeding the Enerca grid on 12 August 2026; it is said to serve Bangui's 3rd and 9th arrondissements first, with wider rollout planned by December. — Alwihda Info, 2026-08-12, intl-media, confidence: medium
