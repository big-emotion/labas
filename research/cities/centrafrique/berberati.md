- [safety.general] In September 2026 MINUSCA described the area as generally calm but reported persistent insecurity on the Berbérati–Sosso-Nakombo road, especially around Benewayo, where herders and traders are still targeted. — MINUSCA police via UN.org, 2026-09-28, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] MINUSCA's Senegalese police unit runs daily patrols on the roads from Berbérati to Carnot, Sosso-Nakombo, Nola and Gamboula; traders and transporters quoted say they feel more confident going to market. — MINUSCA police via UN.org, 2026-09-28, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The same MINUSCA piece notes tensions in the Gamboula and Berbérati–Nola areas and farmer–herder disputes linked to transhumance corridors. — MINUSCA police via UN.org, 2026-09-28, official, confidence: medium
