- [cost.living] Street meal USD 1–2 (≈ €0.92–1.84); smaller towns overall said to be 30–50% cheaper than Brazzaville. — OpenShores "ville la moins chère", 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Chambers of commerce, including Dolisie's, called for the return of a national price reference list (mercuriale), blaming its absence for unstable market prices. — ADIAC search excerpt, 2026-07-03, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Local economy: transit hub for goods within Congo and to CEMAC neighbours; timber (nicknamed "capitale de l'or vert"); clay used for bricks and tiles; small wood industries. — French and English Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Mostly draws humanitarian experts, NGO staff and short-mission consultants. — OpenShores, 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Youth employment and apprenticeship programmes (Essor, World Bank social protection) run in Dolisie alongside Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire. — ADIAC search excerpts, 2026-10-19 and 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: low
- [business.admin] An ACPCE one-stop shop for company creation exists in Dolisie (also Nkayi, Owando, Ouesso). — lepetitjournal.com entrepreneurship guide, 2025-09-08, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Dolisie depends heavily on motorcycles for transport (with Nkayi, Madingou, Sibiti); the consumer group O2CD warned that the Oct 2025 import ban on motorcycles and machetes hit this mobility and risked price speculation. The ban was lifted on 1 Dec 2025. — ADIAC, 2025-11-04 and 2025-12-02, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] On the RN1 and the Congo-Océan railway, about 350 km from Brazzaville; Mbinda branch line runs north towards Gabon; small airport. — French and English Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] France strongly advises against passenger rail between Pointe-Noire and Brazzaville (which runs via Dolisie) until works finish, and calls the Dolisie–Pointe-Noire road through the Mayombe risky in rain (logging trucks, speeding, poor vehicles). — France Diplomatie "informations utiles" and "sécurité", 2026-10-30, official, confidence: high
- [daily.water] A March 2025 column lists Dolisie among cities whose health facilities have had no running water since they were built. — ADIAC search excerpt, 2025-03-24, local-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] Dolisie's public hospital is described as "rudimentary" and access to international private care as "quasi nul". — OpenShores, 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] The paediatrics building of the Dolisie reference hospital was renovated by donors and handed over on 27 July 2026: water supply restored, wiring and septic system redone, after simultaneous shigellosis, typhoid and cholera outbreaks in the Niari department. — ADIAC, 2026-07-27, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Italy is upgrading equipment at the Dolisie general hospital (with Owando and Oyo) under a Congo–Italy hospital modernisation deal; the city also has a military regional hospital. — ADIAC search excerpts, 2025-04-23, 2025-05-02 and 2026-02-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] A new interdepartmental lycée was inaugurated in Dolisie on 19 Nov 2025; reputable schooling is otherwise described as nearly nil, and higher education means going to Brazzaville. — ADIAC search excerpts 2025-12-28 and 2025-02-28 / OpenShores 2026-05-02, local-media/company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie gives no Dolisie-specific advice; intercity travel only by day, in a convoy of at least two 4x4s, on the RN1, which runs from Brazzaville via Ignié and Mindouli toward Dolisie; the Pool department is under heightened vigilance because ex-rebels are not fully disarmed. — France Diplomatie "sécurité", 2026-10-30, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] The Espace Tiné storytelling centre moved from Brazzaville to Dolisie and runs the itinerant RIAPL spoken-word festival (2017 article); the FITAAS theatre festival stops in Dolisie between Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire (2025). — ADIAC, 2017-09-27 / search excerpt 2025-04-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Catholic diocese since 2013 and more than twenty evangelical "réveil" churches. — French Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [community.diaspora] A couple pledged 25M XAF (≈ €38,100) for the Dolisie hospital paediatrics renovation; the article presents it as the couple's pledge, not the association's. — ADIAC, 2026-07-27, local-media, confidence: medium ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [climate.daily] Tropical savanna climate; mean 25 °C; about 1,306 mm of rain a year; wettest November and March, driest July. — Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Lac Bleu about 10 km away and the Madiaba falls about 20 km away; the town sits on the eastern edge of the coastal rainforest. — French and English Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [other] Population 178,172 at the 2023 census, the country's third city; two arrondissements (Foundou-Foundou, Youlou-Poungui); twinned with Riom, France. — Wikipedia "Dolisie", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
