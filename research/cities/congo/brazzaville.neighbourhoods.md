# Brazzaville: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = 655.957 XAF** (fixed peg, from the dossier). Items quoted in US dollars by their source are shown at the city file's editorial rate of **600 XAF per USD** and marked "USD-converted". `[B tag]` = an item in `research/cities/congo/brazzaville.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/congo.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- Rent sources disagree by an order of magnitude. A blog's local-market grid puts a Poto-Poto 2-room at 45,000–100,000 XAF [B cost.rent], while lepetitjournal's expat-standard examples run 750,000–2,000,000 [B cost.rent]. Both are asking prices. Upfront: 2 months' deposit, 1 month's agency fee and several months at signing, plus about 10,000 XAF per agency viewing [B cost.rent].
- City-wide safety frame: France says insecurity is low in the central districts where foreigners live, but advises care at night and in outlying districts, with checkpoints in tense periods (updated 2026-10-30) [B safety.general]. The UK FCDO advises against walking after dark (read 2026-11-14) [B safety.general]. Youth gangs ("bébés noirs") are a recurring local-media topic (ADIAC, 2025-12-02) [B safety.general].
- Power and water: cuts are national news. A World Bank grid project aims to reduce them by end-2026 (ADIAC, 2026-11-11) [B daily.power]. Expats value generators and water tanks [B daily.power].
- Brazzaville sits in the Pool region [13]. France advises intercity travel by day only, in a convoy of two 4x4s, staying on the RN1 (2026-10-30) [B nature.access]. This shapes every weekend outing below.
- Search note: the shared web-search budget ran out after six searches in this pass. Most places come from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage fr/en, Wikipédia, Expat.com, lepetitjournal, an Expertise France heritage-trail booklet). See Gaps.

## Neighbourhoods

### Centre-ville (Plateau, La Plaine, the Corniche)
- Feel: the historic seat of political, military and religious power. The first colonial buildings went up here (heritage booklet, texts 2024) [3]. Expat.com places it near embassies and restaurants and says professionals favour it. It describes the riverside Corniche as quieter, with river views (2025-12-17) [7][B housing.areas]. Newer buildings are reaching the market along avenue Nelson Mandela [8].
- Rent: blog grid for Plateau–OCH–Glacière: studio 150,000–300,000 XAF (≈ €229–457), 2 rooms 400,000–800,000 (≈ €610–1,220) (2026-03-21, low confidence) [B cost.rent]. Expat.com: 1-bed in the centre USD 500–1,000, about 300,000–600,000 XAF USD-converted (≈ €460–920) [B cost.rent]. OpenShores: centre studio USD 250–350, about 150,000–210,000 USD-converted [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: central. The official taxi fare is 700 XAF (ADIAC, 2024) [B daily.transport], and Wikivoyage gives 1,000 XAF anywhere in town [2]. The airport is a few kilometres away [7]; airport to centre costs 3,000 XAF by taxi [13].
- Watch: France advises care at night even in the centre, with checkpoints in tense periods (2026-10-30) [B safety.general].
- Places:
  - Marché du Plateau (covered food and craft market, in its hall since 1938) — fr.Wikipédia [6]; en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Hôtel de Ville and Mémorial Pierre Savorgnan de Brazza (memorial and history museum, Mon–Sat 09:00–17:00) — heritage booklet [3]; en.Wikivoyage (Oct 2018) [2]
  - Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur (Catholic cathedral, views over Poto-Poto and towards Kinshasa) — heritage booklet [3]; en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Hippocampe, rue Béhangle (Vietnamese and local food; buffet 8,000 XAF on Friday and Saturday evenings) — fr/en.Wikivoyage [1][2]
  - La Mandarine, avenue Foch (Lebanese pastry, ice cream, brunch) and La Mie Dorée (bakery) — fr/en.Wikivoyage [1][2]
  - Le Buffet de la Mairie (restaurant next to the town hall, river views) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Bantou Hub (coworking; 5,000 XAF a day, 60,000 a month for a fixed desk) — I am Beezy blog, 2026-03-20 [9]
  - CHU de Brazzaville and Clinique Netco, avenue Lyautey (hospital, 24/7; private clinic) — [B health.care]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (ministries, embassies, bank HQs [Dos]); Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Plateau des 15 Ans (with Camp Clairon and Les Flamboyants)
- Feel: lepetitjournal calls it lively and central, with many expatriates. Camp Clairon and Les Flamboyants, near the École militaire, hold the residences expats seek most, and some are ageing (2025-07-26) [8][B housing.areas]. The same author warns of bars, neighbours' generators and loud revivalist churches, so visit at different times before signing [B housing.areas].
- Rent: furnished 3-room flat at 750,000 XAF (≈ €1,143) after negotiation, including 24/7 guard, generator, shared water tank, electricity and daily cleaning [B cost.rent]. Camp Clairon / Flamboyants 2-bed about 1,500,000 (≈ €2,287). "Correct"-standard villas cost 1,500,000–2,000,000 (≈ €2,287–3,049) [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: central, by taxi at 700–1,000 XAF a ride [B daily.transport][2]. No commute times found.
- Places:
  - Clinique Les Clairons (private clinic, dental) — [B health.care]
  - Makosophie (MKS), rond-point Moungali, behind the petrol station (hall with live rumba and karaoke; Roga-Roga and others on its July–August 2026 bill) — Les Dépêches de Brazzaville via AllAfrica, 2026-07-07 [10]. It is in neighbouring Moungali; no source gives a walking distance.
  - Only one place could be sourced inside the quarter itself; see Gaps.
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (furnished all-inclusive flats suit a first stay [B housing.search]); Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats".

### Bacongo (with La Glacière)
- Feel: one of the oldest arrondissements, about 80,000 people, on the river south of Poto-Poto. Wikipédia calls it the birthplace of the SAPE, with avenue Matsoua as its fashion street [4]. Expat.com calls it family-friendly and says the French lycée is here (2025-12-17) [7]. en.Wikivoyage notes street food and bars at night [2].
- Rent: blog grid: studio 30,000–60,000 XAF (≈ €46–91), 2 rooms 50,000–120,000 (≈ €76–183), 3 rooms or house 80,000–200,000 (≈ €122–305) (2026-03-21, low confidence) [B cost.rent]. Expat-standard villas in La Glacière or Bacongo cost 1,500,000–2,000,000 (≈ €2,287–3,049) [B cost.rent]. Sale ask: a 3-room villa at 120M XAF, unsold for months [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: Bacongo has a bus terminus [4]. Minibus 150 XAF official [B daily.transport]. On the Total–Madibou/Mayanga line, touts push fares to 200–500 XAF from 4 p.m. (ADIAC, 2024-06-14) [B daily.transport].
- Watch: AFD-funded works are canalising the Tsiémé and draining neighbouring Makélékélé to cut flooding in several neighbourhoods (ADIAC, 2026-06-26) [B daily.water]; check a street's flood history before signing.
- Places:
  - Marché Total (the city's largest market; en.Wikivoyage says prices are fixed) and Marché Commission — fr.Wikipédia [4]; en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Lycée International Français Saint-Exupéry (AEFE school, maternelle to terminale, founded 1971) — Expat.com places it in Bacongo [7]; en.Wikipedia [16]
  - Églises Saint-Pierre-Claver and Notre-Dame-du-Rosaire (Catholic churches) — fr.Wikipédia [4]
  - Hôpital Blanche Gomez (hospital) — [B health.care]
  - Case de Gaulle (De Gaulle's wartime residence, now the French ambassador's; seen from outside) — fr.Wikipédia [4]; heritage booklet [3]
  - Avenue Matsoua (SAPE fashion street) — fr.Wikipédia [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French lycée, family housing [7]); Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen" on the local grid; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (SAPE heritage [4]).

### Poto-Poto
- Feel: one of the oldest working-class central arrondissements, laid out on a grid around 1900. It became a meeting point for people from northern Congo arriving in the rural exodus (fr.Wikipédia) [5]. Expat.com describes it as lively, with markets, the painting school and a cultural atmosphere [7], and places bars and clubs with live Congolese music here and in the centre [7].
- Rent: blog grid: studio 25,000–50,000 XAF (≈ €38–76), 2 rooms 45,000–100,000 (≈ €69–152), 3 rooms or house 70,000–180,000 (≈ €107–274) (2026-03-21, low confidence) [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: next to the centre [11]. Taxi 700–1,000 XAF [B daily.transport][2]; shared taxis 150–300 [B daily.transport]. Moto-taxis are blamed for many road accidents (ADIAC, 2025-10-25) [B daily.transport].
- Places:
  - École de peinture de Poto-Poto (painting school founded 1951; visitors can watch artists work) — fr.Wikipédia [5]; Expat.com [7]
  - Basilique Sainte-Anne du Congo (1949 basilica by Roger Erell) — fr.Wikipédia [5]; heritage booklet [3]
  - Stade Félix-Éboué (stadium, 1944, grandstand by the same architect) — fr.Wikipédia [5][6]
  - Marché de Poto-Poto (food market) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; Expat.com [7]
  - CanalOlympia Poto-Poto (300-seat cinema and open-air stage, opened April 2019) — adweknow, 2019-04-23 [11]
  - Maison commune de Poto-Poto (1943 municipal building) — fr.Wikipédia [5]; heritage booklet [3]
  - Clinique Elikia (private clinic) — [B health.care]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q5 "Création, culture"; Q3 "Serré".

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": first week of a trial stay
- Before landing: the visa is bought online before travel, with no visa on arrival. You need an accommodation certificate (mairie, certified by the DDST) or a paid hotel booking, plus a yellow-fever certificate (France, 2026-10-30) [B admin.visa]. The first hotel booking therefore doubles as a visa document.
- Where to stay first: Hotel Hippocampe, rue Béhangle (26,000–36,000 XAF a night, undated listing) [2]; Hotel Africa, 41 rue Kouyou (37,710, Aug 2020) [2]. lepetitjournal advises renting furnished short-term first and searching on the ground [B housing.search].
- Day 1: airport taxi to the centre about 3,000 [13]; agree every taxi fare before getting in [B cost.negotiation]. Change money only at banks, bureaux and ATMs [B daily.money].
- Day 2: SIM from MTN or Airtel, mobile plan 5,000–15,000 a month [B daily.money][B cost.living]. Mobile-money tariffs were not found (city file Gaps).
- Day 3: flat viewings: informal agents charge about 10,000 XAF per viewing even with no rental [B cost.rent]. Agencies ask for ID, proof of income and a passport copy; leases run 12 months, sometimes 6 [B housing.search]. Do a photo inventory [B housing.search].
- Day 4: move-in maths: a 120,000 XAF 2-room needs about 480,000–600,000 upfront [B cost.rent]. Offer 5–15 % below asking, more with months paid in advance [B cost.negotiation].
- Day 5: bank: BGFI, CBAO, Ecobank [B daily.money]; account-opening rules for non-residents were not found (see Gaps).
- Day 6: residence: register at the commissariat or mairie within 15–30 days in some areas [B admin.residency]; morning at Marché Total [4].
- Day 7: Basilique Sainte-Anne, then the heritage trail [5][3].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Visa before departure, none on arrival; yellow-fever certificate required [B admin.visa]. Move by taxi with the fare agreed first [B cost.negotiation]; avoid walking after dark (FCDO) [B safety.general]. Most restaurants close on Sundays [2].

### One day
- Morning (centre): the heritage trail launched in 2024: Hôtel de Ville and the Mémorial Pierre Savorgnan de Brazza (Mon–Sat 09:00–17:00), then the Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur and the CFRAD [3][2]. Some sites have information panels; the Marien Ngouabi mausoleum and museum sit inside the army headquarters and open by appointment only [3]. No entry fee was found for the Mémorial.
- Midday: lunch at Le Buffet de la Mairie, with river views, or Hippocampe [1][2].
- Afternoon (Poto-Poto): taxi (700–1,000) [B daily.transport] to the Basilique Sainte-Anne, Stade Félix-Éboué and the Maison commune [3][5], then the École de peinture to watch painters work [7]. Return past the gare de Brazzaville and place de la Liberté [3].
- Evening: Corniche at sunset (by day; the FCDO advises against walking after dark) [2][B safety.general]; dinner at Mami Wata on the river [2], or Bacongo street food [2], and back by taxi.

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route.
- Day 2: Bacongo: Marché Total, avenue Matsoua (SAPE), Saint-Pierre-Claver church, the Case de Gaulle from outside [4][3]; evening film at CanalOlympia Poto-Poto [11].
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): early morning at Marché Total or Marché de Poto-Poto, then a minibus ride at the 4 p.m. rush (150 XAF official, 200–500 with touts on the Madibou line) [4][2][B daily.transport].
- Day 4: Djoué rapids and Les Rapides bar [2]; craft shopping at the Marché du Plateau craft market (bargaining expected) [2].
- Day 5 (trip out): Lésio-Louna gorillas (~110 km straight line) [13][7] only with an organised operator and by day, under France's Pool advice [B nature.access]. Or Île Mbamou, about an hour away [13]. No operator or fee was sourced for either.
- Day 6 (daily-life moment): a Saturday-night rumba concert at Makosophie [10]; check the bill first.
- Day 7 (daily-life moment): Sunday Mass at the Basilique Sainte-Anne [5], then a league match at Stade Alphonse-Massamba-Débat (33,037 seats; home of CARA Brazzaville and Étoile du Congo) [14]. Fixtures were not found.
- Optional extension: fly to Pointe-Noire (USD 100–250 one way) for the coast [B daily.transport]. The passenger train is "formellement déconseillé" by France until works finish [B daily.transport].
