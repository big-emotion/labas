# Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: XAF (Central African CFA franc) is pegged at **1 EUR = 655.957 XAF** (the fixed rate stated in the dossier); EUR figures below use it. Sources quoting USD are converted at the rate implied by the dossier's own conversions, **1 USD ≈ 0.92 EUR (≈ 600 XAF)**, which is editorial and unsourced. Listings and guide figures are asking prices or estimates, not transaction prices. Web search budget ran out after two searches, so this file relies on direct reads of known sites (France Diplomatie, FCDO, lepetitjournal, Expat.com, Africarrières, OpenShores, I am Beezy blog, combien-coute.net, ADIAC / Les Dépêches de Brazzaville, Afrobarometer, Wikipedia).
