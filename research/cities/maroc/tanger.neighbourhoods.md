## Neighbourhoods

### Centre Ville and Boulevard Pasteur

- Feel: the walkable heart of the modern city, with shops, banks and services within walking distance [1] (Expat.com, upd. 2025-12-05). Wikipedia places the French Consulate General at the start of Boulevard Pasteur and lists the Gran Teatro Cervantes and the Mohammed V Mosque in the downtown outside the walls [2].
- Rent: studio 2,500–4,000 MAD (≈ €231–370), 1-bed 3,500–5,500 MAD (≈ €324–509) (city file, OpenShores 2026-03); city-centre 1-bed 3,000–6,000 MAD (city file, Expat.com Dec 2025); ReaConsult Centre/Bd Pasteur 50–70 MAD/m²/month (city file).
- Getting around: on foot; Tanger-Ville station is 2 km from the medina, taxi about 15 MAD [3]; short petit-taxi trips 10–25 MAD (city file, OpenShores).
- Watch: authorities used force to disperse Gen Z 212 demonstrators in Tanger on 1 Oct 2025 (city file, Wikipedia). Wettest months November–January, 134.6 mm in November (city file, Wikipedia normals).
- Places:
  - Terrasse des Paresseux (viewpoint over the Strait, near Boulevard Pasteur; free) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Gran Teatro Cervantes (historic theatre) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Roman Catholic Cathedral of Tangier (church, downtown outside the walls) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Mohammed V Mosque (mosque, downtown) — Wikipedia [2]
  - Chez Poppeye (restaurant near Hotel El Minzah, 100–150 MAD) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - La Española (bakery and tea house, city centre) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Bocadillo Abdelmalik (sandwich shop, about 14 MAD) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Lycée Regnault (AEFE school, collège and lycée; addresses rue Chapi and rue Allal Ben Abdellah) — school website [4]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (central, cheap studios, easy to move from); Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Marshan (plateau over the Strait)

- Feel: panoramic views of the Strait of Gibraltar and a more refined residential feel [1]; OpenShores lists it as quiet residential (rent table) [5]. A ridge west of downtown along the sea [2].
- Rent: 1-bed 4,000–7,000 MAD (≈ €370–648); villa or house 12,000–25,000 MAD (≈ €1,111–2,315) (city file, OpenShores 2026-03); ReaConsult Ibéria/Marshan 52–70 MAD/m² (city file).
- Getting around: petit taxi to the centre (10–25 MAD); the Kasbah is next door on foot [2].
- Places:
  - Café Hafa (tea café on terraces over the Strait, open since 1921) — Expat.com [1], Wikivoyage [3]
  - Marshan Palace (government residence, not a museum) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [2]
  - Stade de Marchan (old stadium, IR Tanger's ground until 2011) — Wikipedia [2][6]
  - Mendoub's Residence (historic residence) — Wikipedia [2]
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Télétravail" (quiet, views; no fibre data for the district, see Gaps); Q3 "Moyen".

### Malabata and the bay (Playa, Marina Bay)

- Feel: OpenShores calls Malabata the modern residential area preferred by expatriate managers working at the Tanger Free Zone, Renault or Tanger Med, with secured residences, caretakers, parking and sometimes a shared pool [5] (2026-03-10). Playa/Marina Bay is upscale waterfront living [1]. ReaConsult classes Malabata, Marina Bay, Iberia and Tanja Balia as premium (city file).
- Rent: Malabata studio 3,000–4,500 MAD, 1-bed 4,000–6,500 (≈ €370–602), 2-bed 6,000–10,000 (≈ €556–926) (city file, OpenShores); Playa/Marina Bay 2-bed 6,000–15,000 MAD (≈ €556–1,389) (city file, Expat.com); ReaConsult Malabata 55–75 MAD/m² (city file).
- Getting around: taxi to the Free Zone (20 km) 60–100 MAD (city file, OpenShores); production jobs often come with a free shuttle or a 300–800 MAD transport allowance (city file, jobsquare). Tanger-Ville station is in the eastern outskirts [3].
- Watch: FCDO says petty crime is common on beaches, including motorbike theft (national, city file).
- Places:
  - Beachfront along Avenue Mohammed VI (city beach lined with hotels and restaurants) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Cap Malabata (headland, free) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mnar Park (leisure park to the east; about €5 children, €10 adults) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Expat-oriented restaurants of Malabata (200–500 MAD a meal; none named) — city file, OpenShores
  - Tangier Grand Stadium / Ibn Batouta Stadium (IR Tanger's ground, Avenue des FAR; AFCON 2025 semi-final) — Wikipedia [6]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" in industry; Q4 "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" at Marina Bay, "Moyen" for a Malabata 1-bed.

### California and Iberia (families)

- Feel: quieter family areas near international schools and parks [1].
- Rent: ReaConsult California/Nzaha 58–78 MAD/m²/month, Ibéria/Marshan 52–70 (city file); a 3-bed in the city 6,000–12,000 MAD (≈ €556–1,111) (city file, Expat.com). No California listing sample was found.
- Getting around: car or petit taxi; new CTM-Transdev buses since Dec 2025 (fares not stated, city file).
- Places:
  - Perdicaris Park / Rmilat Forest (70 ha public forest park facing the Strait, Rmilat) — Wikipedia [7]
  - American School of Tangier (K–12, 149 Rue Christophe Colomb; district not stated) — school website [8]
  - Only two places could be sourced near these districts (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (schools and parks); Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Rmilat forest at hand); Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Médina and Kasbah

- Feel: the old walled city; the 2024 census counted 4,531 of Tanger's roughly 6,900 foreign residents in the Médina arrondissement, which also covers more than the old town (city file, Wikipedia).
- Rent: small flat or room 1,500–3,000 MAD (≈ €139–278); renovated house (dar) 5,000–12,000 MAD (≈ €463–1,111) (city file, OpenShores).
- Getting around: on foot; the Tanger Ville ferry terminal sits beside the medina [3].
- Watch: touts near the harbour expecting large tips [3]; petty crime in medinas (FCDO, national, city file).
- Places:
  - Grand Socco and Petit Socco (squares) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [2]
  - Mendoubia Gardens (park beside the Grand Socco; free) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Cinémathèque de Tanger (art-house cinema and café in the Cinéma Rif, Grand Socco, founded 2006) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Kasbah Museum (museum; 20 MAD per Wikivoyage 2026, 30 MAD per Expat.com) — [3][1]
  - American Legation Museum (museum; 20 MAD per Wikivoyage, 50 MAD per Expat.com) — [3][1]
  - St Andrew's Church (Anglican church near the Grand Socco) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [2]
  - Darna (restaurant, four-course menu 60 MAD) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Restaurant Ahlen (tajine or couscous 50–90 MAD) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest rooms in the city file); Q5 "Création, culture"; Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### Boukhalef (affordable, near the universities)

- Feel: near the universities, the most affordable options, fewer expat amenities [1].
- Rent: from about 2,500 MAD/month (≈ €231) [1]; ReaConsult 28–38 MAD/m²/month, with the Free zone/Gzenaya at 28–40 (city file).
- Getting around: the airport (formerly named Boukhalef [2]) is 12 km south-west of the centre; airport bus 40 MAD, about 40 min (city file, Expat.com).
- Watch: in July 2015 police evicted about 400 sub-Saharan migrants from occupied flats in Boukhalef after clashes with residents (city file, TelQuel snippet, old); no recent source on the area.
- Places: none sourced in this pass (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Salarié·e" for jobs in the Free Zone/Gzenaya side (cheapest per-m² band, city file).

## Living: typical weeks

Weekly totals use only prices in the city file or pages read in this pass; quantities are assumptions and stated.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Centre Ville, walking distance to cafés and services [1]; cheaper than Marshan with the same taxi range. No Tanger-specific internet speed was found; fibre is sold at 250–400 MAD/month (city file, OpenShores).
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; evening at the Terrasse des Paresseux [3].
  - Tue: Technopark Tanger offers coworking and private offices (address and price not found) [10].
  - Wed: market shopping in the souks, cheaper for produce than Marjane or Carrefour (city file, Expat.com).
  - Thu: work from La Española [3].
  - Fri: quieter afternoon, family couscous custom (city file, Expat.com); Friday prayer at the Mohammed V Mosque [2].
  - Sat: see options.
  - Sun: Café Hafa in Marshan [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": a modest scene; Wikivoyage lists Casa Pepe, Morocco Palace, Marco Polo and the hotel bars of the Ramada and El Minzah as bars and discos, no prices [3].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": San Remo (mains about 150 MAD) or Spanish tapas places at 100–250 MAD (city file, OpenShores) [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": a film at the Cinémathèque [9] (ticket price not found) or tea at Café Hafa at sunset.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": city beach on Avenue Mohammed VI [3]; Cap Spartel, with a kilometre of beach at Le Mirage, and the Caves of Hercules (10 MAD) [3]; Asilah, 20 km south: bus 11 MAD or shared grand taxi 50 MAD (a private grand taxi is 200 MAD by day) [3].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Perdicaris Park [7]; Chefchaouen in the Rif, about 2 h by shared taxi at 70 MAD a person (city file, Expat.com). France Diplomatie advises avoiding the Rif's secondary roads at night (city file).
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Tanger.
  - "Je reste en ville": Tétouan's UNESCO medina, about 1 h (city file); or Tarifa in Spain by fast ferry, 35 min, 350–650 MAD one way on foot (city file, OpenShores).
- Weekly spend (MAD): centre 1-bed 3,500–5,500 ÷ 4.3 = 814–1,279 (OpenShores) · groceries 1,500–2,000 ÷ 4.3 = 349–465 (Expat.com) · fibre 250–400 ÷ 4.3 = 58–93 · mobile 80–200 ÷ 4.3 = 19–47 · power and water 350–700 ÷ 4.3 = 81–163 · 3 tajine meals × 50–90 = 150–270 · 5 café tea-and-pastry × 20–40 = 100–200 · 4 taxi rides × 10–25 = 40–100 (all OpenShores). Total ≈ 1,611–2,617 MAD (≈ €149–242). Cross-check: Expat.com's single budget of 7,500–11,000 MAD/month ≈ 1,744–2,558 a week.

- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: commute to the Free Zone (taxi 60–100 MAD, or the employer's shuttle; 2x8/3x8 shifts are the norm in production, city file, jobsquare).
  - Wed: evening walk on the Avenue Mohammed VI beachfront [3].
  - Fri: dinner at an expat-oriented restaurant in Malabata (200–500 MAD a meal, OpenShores).
  - Sat: see options. Sun: Mnar Park or Cap Malabata [3].
- Saturday night: as profile 1; plus an IR Tanger match at the Grand Stadium when one is on [6].
- Weekend outing: as profile 1; the city file's couple budget includes two Spain trips a month (OpenShores).
- Weekly spend (MAD): Malabata 2-bed 6,000–10,000 ÷ 4.3 = 1,395–2,326 · groceries for two 2,000–3,500 ÷ 4.3 = 465–814 · power and water 81–163 · fibre 58–93 · two Malabata dinners × 200–500 = 400–1,000 (OpenShores; per meal, assumed per person). Commute assumed covered by the employer shuttle. Total ≈ 2,399–4,396 MAD (≈ €222–407). Cross-check: OpenShores' couple with a car and two Spain trips a month, 23,300 MAD ≈ 5,419 a week, includes car and travel.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"

- Weekend outing: "À la plage" Cap Spartel or Asilah; "En montagne, en forêt" Perdicaris or Chefchaouen; desert not realistic; "Je reste en ville" Kasbah Museum.
- Weekly spend (MAD), two children at Lycée Regnault primary: 3-bed 6,000–12,000 ÷ 4.3 = 1,395–2,791 (Expat.com) · tuition 2 × 42,000 ÷ 52 = 1,615 (French pupils) to 2 × 51,000 ÷ 52 = 1,962 (Moroccan and third-country pupils) (Lycée Regnault 2026–27) · groceries, twice the couple figure, 4,000–7,000 ÷ 4.3 = 930–1,628 (OpenShores; doubling is an assumption) · family health insurance 1,500–3,000 ÷ 4.3 = 349–698 (Expat.com) · power and water 81–163 · fibre 58–93 · fuel, one full tank per fortnight, 700–950 ÷ 2 = 350–475 (OpenShores). Total ≈ 4,778–7,810 MAD (≈ €442–723). One-off: first registration 30,000 MAD per child (25,000 for first nursery year).

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Where to stay first: Centre Ville or the medina. Wikivoyage lists a budget hotel at about 600 MAD a night, negotiable to 450 [3] (city file).
- Day 1: arrive by ferry at the Tanger Ville terminal beside the medina [3], or by plane: airport bus 40 MAD, about 40 min (city file); airport grand taxi to the centre 100 MAD by day, 150 at night (city file, Wikivoyage).
- Day 2: SIM card: prepaid SIMs are easy to get from Maroc Telecom, Orange or Inwi; plans 80–200 MAD/month (city file). No mobile-money service found. ATMs that accept foreign cards are available (city file).
- Day 3–5: flat hunting on Avito.ma, Mubawab and Sarouty or through a local agent (simsaar); deposit one to two months; leases usually one year minimum (city file, Expat.com). No agency fee or visit fee was sourced for Tanger.
- Day 6: residence: the residence card must be requested within 3 months of arrival (city file, Expat.com); a bank account will usually need it (national, Casablanca city file, low confidence).
- Day 7: Café Hafa and the Kasbah.
- Spend for the week (MAD): hotel 7 nights × 450–600 = 3,150–4,200 · airport bus 40 · mobile plan 80–200 · 7 tajine meals × 50–90 = 350–630 · 7 café stops × 20–40 = 140–280 · 6 taxi rides × 10–25 = 60–150. Total ≈ 3,820–5,500 MAD (≈ €354–509). If a centre 1-bed is signed: first month + deposit (1–2 months) = 2–3 months × 3,500–5,500 = 7,000–16,500 MAD (≈ €648–1,528) upfront, before any agent's fee.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day

- Morning: Kasbah Museum (20–30 MAD) and the Kasbah lanes [3][1].
- Midday: down through the medina to the Petit Socco and the American Legation Museum (20–50 MAD) [3][1]; lunch at Darna (60 MAD menu) [3].
- Afternoon: Grand Socco, Mendoubia Gardens and St Andrew's Church [3]; coffee at the Cinémathèque café [9]. Walk or take a petit taxi (10–25 MAD) to Café Hafa in Marshan for the late afternoon [1].
- Evening: the Terrasse des Paresseux off Boulevard Pasteur [3]; dinner at the port seafood stalls (about 60 MAD) or San Remo [3].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules (10 MAD; taxi about 15 min) [3]; Perdicaris Park on the way back [7].
- Day 3, trip: Asilah, 20 km south, bus 11 MAD [3].
- Day 4, trip: Chefchaouen, about 2 h by shared taxi at 70 MAD (city file, Expat.com). Return before dark (Rif roads, France Diplomatie, city file).
- Day 5: Tétouan, about 1 h (city file).
- Day 6, daily life: morning at Casa Barata market, about 6 km out, shared grand taxi about 3 MAD a person from near the English Church [3]; or an IR Tanger match at the Grand Stadium if one is scheduled [6]. Ride a taxi out to Malabata at the evening rush to judge the commute.
- Day 7: Sunday service at St Andrew's or the Catholic cathedral [2], then the Avenue Mohammed VI beachfront [3]. Optional: the fast ferry to Tarifa (35 min, 350–650 MAD) to test the "one foot in Europe" argument (city file).
