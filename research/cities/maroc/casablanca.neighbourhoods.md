# Casablanca: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Conventions. EUR at the dossier's editorial rate, 1 EUR ≈ 10.8 MAD (unsourced in the dossier). "City file" means `research/cities/maroc/casablanca.md`; its source is named after it. Monthly figures are turned into weekly ones by dividing by 4.3. Source numbers [n] refer to "Sources read" at the bottom. The web-search budget ran out after the first query of this pass, so every new place comes from pages opened directly (Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, venue sites, two neighbourhood guides).

## Neighbourhoods

### Maârif

- Feel: the city's main going-out and shopping district, with restaurants, cafés and upmarket shops in mid- and high-standing blocks; public transport described as good [2] (Le Petit Journal, upd. 2024-09-10). An arrondissement of 139,669 residents in 2024, down from 180,394 in 2004 [3].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed 5,000–12,000 MAD/month (≈ €463–1,111) (city file, Rio Times 2026-09); unfurnished 2-bed 5,500–8,000 MAD (≈ €509–741) plus 500–800 MAD building charges (city file, OpenShores 2026-03); asking prices in the public expat group, Oct–Nov 2026: furnished studio 6,500 MAD (≈ €602), flats 6,000–7,000 MAD (city file, "Réseau des expatriés à Casablanca").
- Getting around: petit taxi inside Maârif 10–18 MAD, Maârif to Casablanca Finance City 20–35 MAD (city file, OpenShores 2026-03). Neither tram page read lists a Maârif stop by name [7], so tram access is not confirmed here.
- Watch: Le Petit Journal lists Derb Ghallef, the market area beside Maârif [3], among the districts "less frequented, mainly for safety reasons" (guide upd. 2024-09-10, no incident data) [2]. Citywide, France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) asks for heightened vigilance over motorbike thieves snatching bags and phones (city file).
- Places:
  - Stade Mohammed V (stadium, home of Raja CA and Wydad AC) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Derb Ghallef (souk of small stalls: phones, clothing; hard bargaining) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Parc Murdoch (park) — French Wikipedia, Maârif [3]
  - Le Kobe D'Or (restaurant, 9 Rue Abou Salt El Andaloussi) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Shops around the Twin Center, Boulevard al Massira al Khadra (fashion chains) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Nightclubs Tiger House and La Notte (Maârif/Gironde cluster) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Galleries Atelier 21, Galerie Shart, Loft Art Gallery (Maârif/Gauthier, usually free) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (clubs and late cafés in the district); Q5 "Télétravail" (a relocation blog lists Maârif among the most reliable fibre areas, city file, getwherenext 2026-02); Q3 "Moyen".

### Gauthier and the Boulevard Mohammed V centre

- Feel: calmer, more cultural and residential than Maârif, mostly modern blocks, easy to reach the rest of the city [2]. Part of the "golden triangle" with Racine and Anfa [1] (Expat.com, upd. 2022-07-14). Expat.com (2025-12) names Gauthier among expat-favoured districts near international schools (city file).
- Rent: furnished 1-bed 4,000–9,000 MAD (≈ €370–833) (city file, Rio Times 2026-09); unfurnished 2-bed 5,000–7,500 MAD (≈ €463–694) (city file, OpenShores 2026-03); 2-bed on Bd Abdelmoumen 6,500 MAD and a large city-centre studio 5,500 MAD (≈ €602 / €509) (city file, expat group, Oct–Nov 2026).
- Getting around: tram T1 stops at Central Market, Mohammed V Square and Place des Nations Unies, and T4 ends at Arab League Park (opened 2024-09-23) [7]. Ticket 8 MAD, 6 MAD on a card, weekly pass 60 MAD, monthly 230 MAD (≈ €21) (city file, Rio Times 2026-09).
- Watch: motorbike bag and phone snatching (France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, city file). Downtown traffic described as heavy [1].
- Places:
  - Marché Central (covered food market, Bd Mohammed V, built 1917–1920: meat, fish rotunda, fruit, oysters) — French Wikipedia [9]
  - Parc de la Ligue Arabe (city park, about 30 acres; renovation from 2016, current state not confirmed) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Former Sacré-Cœur church (deconsecrated, now exhibitions and cultural events, Bd Rachidi) — French Wikipedia [11]
  - Villa des Arts (art gallery, 30 Bd Brahim Roudani, free; Wikivoyage marks its link dead) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Brasserie La Cigale (budget restaurant, Bd Brahim Roudani) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - La Cocina (restaurant, 55 Rue Mustapha el Manfalouti, Gauthier) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Le Kazbar (bar, 7 Rue Najib Mahfoud, Gauthier) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Petit Poucet (bar, 86 Bd Mohamed V) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (central, on the T1 line, furnished stock); Q5 "Salarié·e" (short tram rides to the centre); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Bourgogne

- Feel: centre-west, very dense, with large upscale residential blocks housing young middle-class residents and some retired civil servants; the Hassan II Mosque sits on its shoreline [12]. Le Petit Journal calls it central, with wide tree-lined avenues, green spaces and modern buildings liked by expat families [2].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed 3,000–6,000 MAD (≈ €278–556) (city file, Rio Times 2026-09); ReaConsult reference 65–90 MAD/m²/month (≈ €6.0–8.3) (city file, Apr 2026).
- Getting around: walking distance to the Hassan II Mosque and the corniche [12][8]; tram stops for Bourgogne were not confirmed in the pages read.
- Watch: in the storm blackout of 11 Nov 2026, commenters said power came back in Bourgogne around 19:00 (city file, social comments, low confidence; a one-off).
- Places:
  - Hassan II Mosque (mosque; guided tours 130 MAD, museum 30, combined 140) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - The Corniche (seafront with cafés, west of the mosque) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - El Hank lighthouse (256 steps) — Wikivoyage FR [13]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen" (cheaper than Gauthier for a 1-bed); Q4 "En couple" or Q4 "Avec mes enfants" on a moderate budget (described as liked by expat families [2]); Q8 "Je reste en ville" with the sea on foot.

### Californie and Sidi Maarouf (south)

- Feel: Californie is very quiet, with villas on shaded streets next to green spaces, popular with families and expats [2]. Sidi Maarouf is a growing district of modern buildings and offices about halfway to the airport, chosen by expats to be near work [2].
- Rent: new studio in Californie (Arcadia Loft) asking 5,700 MAD (≈ €528) (city file, expat group, Oct–Nov 2026). No 2- or 3-bed rent sourced for Californie; ReaConsult gives only sale prices there (10,000–15,000 MAD/m²).
- Getting around: no tram line serving Californie was confirmed in the pages read; car or petit taxi.
- Places:
  - Casablanca American School (international school, IB; Route de la Mecque, Lotissement Ougoug, Quartier Californie) — school website [14]
  - Only one everyday place could be sourced here (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (quiet streets, the American school in the district); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Salarié·e" for jobs in the Sidi Maarouf office area.

### Anfa, Racine and Aïn Diab (upmarket, coastal)

- Feel: Anfa is one of the wealthiest areas, villas and ocean views near the corniche; Racine mixes new building with large older homes; Aïn Diab is touristic, with shops, restaurants, bars, hotels and beach access [2]. Wikipedia calls Aïn Diab affluent [15].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed Racine/Anfa 10,000–20,000 MAD (≈ €926–1,852); Aïn Diab 12,000–25,000 MAD (≈ €1,111–2,315); Casa Finance City 12,000–22,000 MAD (city file, Rio Times 2026-09); unfurnished 3-bed in Anfa 10,000–18,000 MAD (≈ €926–1,667) (city file, OpenShores 2026-03); furnished 2-bed of 106 m² at CFC 13,000 MAD (city file, expat group, Nov 2026).
- Getting around: tram T2 serves Anfa Park and Casablanca Finance City and ends at Aïn Diab Beach [7]; taxi city centre to Aïn Diab 25–40 MAD (city file, OpenShores).
- Watch: FCDO says knives have been used in street attacks on tourists in major cities and on beaches (national, city file). Rio Times says the market "changes character" from about 10,000 MAD (city file).
- Places:
  - Aïn Diab Beach and Lalla Meryem Beach (beaches) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Shrine of Sidi Abderrahman (island shrine off the corniche, closed to non-Muslims, reachable at low tide) — Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Morocco Mall (mall, opened 2011-12-01, Aquadream aquarium; far south-west beyond Aïn Diab/Anfa) — Wikipedia [16], Wikivoyage EN [8]
  - Sindibad (amusement park, Aïn Diab) — Le Petit Journal [2]
  - Au Four à Bois and La Toscana (Italian restaurants, Racine) — Wikivoyage FR [13]
  - Casablanca Finance City (business district on the former Anfa airport) — Wikipedia [17]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage" (beaches on the tram line); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" with a large budget; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (the corniche concentrates nightclubs and cabarets [13]).

### Hay Hassani and Oulfa (affordable, south-west)

- Feel: named among Casablanca's working-class districts; livelier, but expats are quickly noticed and may feel less secure; generally far from international schools, with few green spaces [1] (Expat.com, upd. 2022-07-14). About 468,000 inhabitants in 2014 (figure flagged as unsourced on the page) [4].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed 2,500–5,000 MAD (≈ €231–463) (city file, Rio Times 2026-09); ReaConsult reference 40–55 MAD/m²/month (≈ €3.7–5.1), the cheapest mid-city band (city file, Apr 2026).
- Getting around: tram T2 serves Hay Hassani and runs on to Anfa Park, CFC and Aïn Diab Beach [7].
- Places: none sourced in this pass (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest asking rents on a tram line); Q8 "À la plage" (same T2 line as Aïn Diab). Less suited to Q4 "Avec mes enfants" if an international school is needed [1].

### Dar Bouazza (coastal suburb)

- Feel: a suburb about 15 km from the centre by the sea, with residences, villas and some blocks, known for surfing and private beaches [2]; Expat.com calls these newer developments far from the centre [1].
- Rent: a house at 9,000 MAD/month (≈ €833) plus one month's agency fee; a larger property at 23,000 MAD (≈ €2,130) (city file, expat group, 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-06).
- Getting around: no public transport sourced; plan on a car (fuel 15.36 MAD/L on 2026-11-09, city file).
- Watch: Dar Bouazza was among the areas hit by the 11 Nov 2026 storm blackout (city file, social caption citing SRM, one-off).
- Places:
  - Tamaris (beach about 20 km from Casablanca, cheap restaurants) — Wikivoyage FR [13]
  - Only one everyday place sourced (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" with a car; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

All weekly totals use only prices in the city file or in the pages read in this pass. Quantities (how many meals, litres, trips) are assumptions and are stated.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Maârif. Fibre reputation (city file, getwherenext), cafés and nightlife on foot [2], taxis of 10–18 MAD inside the district.
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; evening walk to the shops around the Twin Center [8].
  - Tue: coworking day (the city file names WeWork Casablanca, New Work Lab and Casanearshore Park, about $80–150/month, blog source, addresses not found); taxi 10–35 MAD.
  - Wed: market run at Marché Central, T1 or taxi about 15 min (time not sourced) [9].
  - Thu: gym after work (350–600 MAD/month, city file, OpenShores); no named gym in Maârif was found.
  - Fri: Friday prayer at a neighbourhood mosque or the Hassan II Mosque [8]; evening at Le Kobe D'Or [8].
  - Sat: see Saturday options.
  - Sun: T2 or taxi to the Aïn Diab corniche (25–40 MAD), run or cycle along it (city file, talesmag).
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Tiger House or La Notte in Maârif/Gironde [8]; pubs about 100 MAD a head, half at happy hour 19:00–23:00 (city file, Wikivoyage).
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Sqala, in an old fortress, Bd des Almohades [8]; three-course dinner for two about 300 MAD (city file, Rio Times).
  - "Tranquille chez moi": groceries delivered from Marjane or Carrefour (city file, talesmag); or a Megarama film at 55–80 MAD (city file, OpenShores).
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Aïn Diab and Lalla Meryem beaches [15]; or El Jadida, bus about 1.5 h, 25 MAD [8].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no mountain or forest within a day was sourced; the Atlas means going via Marrakech (train about 3 h, 84 MAD second class [18]) and Imlil, 50 km south of Marrakech [19], so it is a two-night trip.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Casablanca for a weekend; the nearest sourced desert trip is Agafay from Marrakech (city file of Marrakech, Expat.com).
  - "Je reste en ville": Rabat by train, about 1 h (dossier), or the Habous quarter [5].
- Weekly spend (MAD): rent 5,000–12,000 ÷ 4.3 = 1,163–2,791 (Rio Times) · groceries 1,300 ÷ 4.3 = 302 (Expat.com) · broadband 418 ÷ 4.3 = 97 (Rio Times) · utilities 300–700 ÷ 4.3 = 70–163 (Expat.com) · 3 cheap meals × 47 = 141 (Rio Times) · half of one dinner for two, 300 ÷ 2 = 150 (Rio Times) · tram weekly pass 60 (Rio Times) · gym 350–600 ÷ 4.3 = 81–140 (OpenShores) · 5 espressos × 10–15 = 50–75 (OpenShores). Total ≈ 2,114–3,919 MAD (≈ €196–363). Cross-check: Rio Times' single-person month of 9,000–14,000 MAD is 2,093–3,256 a week.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"

- Base: Gauthier. On tram T1 (Mohammed V Square, Central Market) and close to the banking head offices of the centre (dossier); calmer than Maârif [2].
- Week:
  - Mon–Thu: tram T1 to work, a few stops (journey time not sourced); one evening market shop at Marché Central [9].
  - Wed: after-work drink at Le Kazbar [8].
  - Fri: lunch at Brasserie La Cigale [8]; evening walk in the Parc de la Ligue Arabe area [10].
  - Sat: see options.
  - Sun: Hassan II Mosque visit or the corniche (T2 to Aïn Diab Beach) [7][8].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Caesar's nightclub at the Sheraton, 100 Avenue des FAR [8]; corniche clubs [13].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Cocina in Gauthier [8], or Rick's Café near the medina, about 800 MAD dinner for two [8].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": a film (55–80 MAD each) or a quiet evening at home.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1 ("À la plage" Aïn Diab or El Jadida; mountains and desert not realistic for a weekend; "Je reste en ville" Rabat or Habous).
- Weekly spend (MAD): unfurnished 2-bed 5,000–7,500 ÷ 4.3 = 1,163–1,744 (OpenShores) · groceries 2 × 1,300 ÷ 4.3 = 605 (Expat.com) · two monthly tram passes 460 ÷ 4.3 = 107 (Rio Times) · utilities for 85 m² 467 ÷ 4.3 = 109 (Rio Times) · broadband 97 · one dinner for two 300 (Rio Times) · two cinema tickets 110–160 (OpenShores). Total ≈ 2,491–3,122 MAD (≈ €231–289).

  - Sat/Sun: as profile 1.

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"

- Base: Californie (quiet, family streets, Casablanca American School in the district [2][14]); on a smaller budget, Bourgogne [2]. For the French system, Lycée Lyautey (AEFE, collège and lycée; entrance on boulevard Ziraoui) [21].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school run by car (no tram confirmed in Californie); one evening of sport (the city file reports local clubs as the best way to meet Moroccans, talesmag).
  - Wed: Marché Central or a supermarket run (Marjane, Acima [8]).
  - Sat: Morocco Mall and its aquarium [16]; or Sindibad [2].
  - Sun: Aïn Diab beach [15]; Mass at Notre-Dame-de-Lourdes, a parish church frequented by many sub-Saharan faithful (address not given on the page) [22].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": corniche clubs [13] (with a sitter; a housekeeper costs 3,500–4,000 MAD/month, city file).
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Au Four à Bois or La Toscana in Racine [13].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": cinema as a family, 55–80 MAD a ticket.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" Tamaris [13] or El Jadida [8]; the rest as profile 1.
- Weekly spend (MAD), two children at Lycée Lyautey (French nationality, primary): 3-bed 7,500–15,000 ÷ 4.3 = 1,744–3,488 (Expat.com central 3-bed, used as a proxy since no Californie 3-bed was found) · groceries 4 × 1,300 ÷ 4.3 = 1,209 (Expat.com per-person figure; children assumed to eat as adults, so this is high) · tuition 2 × 40,800 ÷ 52 = 1,569 (Lycée Lyautey 2026–27) · utilities 109 · broadband 97 · fuel 30 L × 15.36 = 461 (globalpetrolprices 2026-11-09; 30 L is an assumption) · 4 cinema tickets 220–320. Total ≈ 5,409–7,253 MAD (≈ €501–672). One-off: first registration 30,000 MAD for the first child and 15,000 for the second (50 % off) (city file). Third-country children pay 50,100 MAD in primary instead of 40,800.

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Where to stay first: Gauthier or the Bd Mohammed V centre, on tram T1 [7]. No hotel or short-let nightly price was sourced (see Gaps).
- Day 1: airport train to the city, 50 MAD second class, about 45 min, hourly [8] (Wikivoyage FR gives 35 MAD, older); grand taxi about 280 MAD [8]. Buy a tram card (15 MAD, Rio Times) and a weekly pass (60 MAD).
- Day 2: SIM card. Wikivoyage FR gives about 20 MAD for a chip at Derb Ghallef [13]; a 10 GB plan is about 98 MAD/month (city file, Rio Times). Networks are Maroc Telecom, Orange and Inwi (national, city file of Tanger). Mobile money: no M-Pesa-style service found; daily life runs on cash and bank cards (city file gap).
- Day 3–5: flat hunting. Leases usually go through a local agent with a formal lease; deposit one to three months; landlords may ask for a passport, proof of income and sometimes a residence permit (city file, Expat.com). Agency fee commonly one month's rent excluding VAT (city file, Trovit listings). The public group "Réseau des expatriés à Casablanca" carries owner listings; tenants there try to rent from owners to skip the fee (city file). Separate "visit fees" were not found in any source.
- Day 6: bank. Convertible dirham accounts at Attijariwafa, BMCE Bank of Africa and Banque Populaire; passport, residence permit and proof of income usually needed (city file, getwherenext, low confidence), so a full account may wait for the carte de séjour (1–3 months, dossier).
- Day 7: Sunday on the corniche (T2 to Aïn Diab Beach).
- Saturday night and weekend: as profile 1.
- Spend for the week (MAD, excluding lodging, not sourced): airport train 50 · SIM 20 · data plan 98 · tram card 15 + weekly pass 60 · 14 cheap meals × 47 = 658 · groceries 302. Total ≈ 1,203 MAD (≈ €111) plus lodging. If a flat is signed: first month + deposit (1–2 months) + agency fee (1 month) = 3–4 months; on a Gauthier 1-bed at 4,000–9,000 MAD that is 12,000–36,000 MAD (≈ €1,111–3,333) upfront.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day

- Morning: Hassan II Mosque guided tour (130 MAD; 140 with the museum). Wikivoyage EN lists tours Saturday–Thursday at 08:00–11:00 and 14:00, Friday 08:00–10:00 and 14:00 [8]; Wikivoyage FR says no visits on Fridays [13]. Check before going. Walk west along the corniche cafés [8].
- Midday: petit taxi (minimum 7 MAD) to the Habous quarter [5][8]: Mahkama du Pacha (Mon–Sat 08:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00, free but usually with a guide) and Pâtisserie Bennis Habous (bastilla to pre-order) [8]. T3 has a Habous stop [7].
- Afternoon: T3/T1 or taxi to the centre: Marché Central for oysters [9], then the Villa des Arts (free) and the former Sacré-Cœur by the Parc de la Ligue Arabe [8][11]. Museum of Moroccan Judaism, 81 Rue Chasseur Jules Gros, is an alternative (fee not given) [8].
- Evening: T2 or taxi (25–40 MAD) to Aïn Diab for the sunset, then dinner at La Sqala or Rick's Café (about 800 MAD for two) [8].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Maârif. Shops around the Twin Center, Derb Ghallef souk (bargain hard), galleries in Maârif/Gauthier [8]; evening in a Maârif bar.
- Day 3, daily life: market morning at the "marché sénégalais" at Bab Marrakech in the old medina: tailoring, textiles, garba and attiéké, hair salons; 200+ businesses, threatened by a planned avenue (city file, RFI and Le360, May 2026). Then ride tram T1 at rush hour from Casa-Voyageurs to Place des Nations Unies [7] to feel the commute.
- Day 4, trip: Rabat by train (about 1 h, trains about every half hour) [8] and dossier; the capital's calmer pace is the useful comparison.
- Day 5, trip: El Jadida by bus, about 1.5 h, 25 MAD [8].
- Day 6: a Raja or Wydad match at Stade Mohammed V if one is scheduled [6] (ticket price not found); evening dinner in Gauthier.
- Day 7: Sunday at Aïn Diab Beach and Morocco Mall [15][16]; Sunday Mass at Notre-Dame-de-Lourdes for Christian visitors who want to meet the sub-Saharan congregation [22].
