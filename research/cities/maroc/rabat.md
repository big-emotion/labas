## Items

- [cost.rent] Numbeo 2025 figures: 1-bed in the centre 4,250 MAD/month (≈ €394), outside the centre 2,400 MAD (≈ €222); 3-bed centre 10,090 MAD (≈ €934), outside 6,490 MAD (≈ €601). — expat.com Rabat guide, updated 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Mubawab's 2025 review puts the average unfurnished apartment rent in Agdal at 11,999 MAD/month (≈ €1,111), and Bladi reports supply shortages pushing rents up (Aug 2026). — bladi.net, 2026-08-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Apartment rents per m² (ReaConsult barometer, 2025–26): Souissi 75–105 MAD/m²/month (≈ €6.9–9.7), Hay Riad 65–92, Agdal 60–85, Hassan/Centre 55–78, Témara 32–45 (≈ €3.0–4.2). — reaconsult.ma, 2026 (undated page), company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives a 1-bed in Agdal or Hassan at $350–550/month and says flat quality tends to be higher than in other cities. — getwherenext.com, 2026-02-28, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Sample single budget about 8,850 MAD/month (≈ €819): rent 4,250, utilities 700, groceries 2,000, transport 400, dining 1,500. Family of four about 29,390 MAD (≈ €2,721) incl. school for two and health insurance. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Local meals from about 40 MAD (≈ €3.7); imported goods are expensive, local goods and services cheap; the guide calls Rabat cheaper than Casablanca. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Indicators quoted: average monthly salary 6,721 MAD (≈ €622); minimum wage 17.10 MAD/hour in 2025 (about 3,269 MAD/month), now 17.92 MAD/hour from 1 Jan 2026. — expat.com 2025-12-05 / bladi.net 2026-01-01, intl-media + local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Agdal: modern, cafés, young professionals and families. Souissi: villas, embassies, high security. Hay Riad: newer high-end flats. Hassan: historic centre. Youssoufia and Mabella: more affordable. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Deposit one to two months plus first month's rent; agency commission about one month; landlords prefer 12-month leases; word of mouth finds flats before they are listed. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] In March 2025, demolitions in the Océan (Al Mouhit), Sania Gharbia and Douar El Askar areas drew protests; Douar El Askar residents rejected relocation to Tamesna, far from work and schools. Seen via search snippets. — fr.hespress.com / medias24.com, 2025-03, local-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Government is the largest employer, alongside embassies and international organisations; Rabat-Salé-Kénitra holds about 24% of national offshoring jobs (IBM, Dell, CGI, Capgemini named). French is essential for most roles. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Work permit route: ANAPEC approval, then the employment ministry authorises the contract (2–8 weeks); the residence permit is handled by the DGSN. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Rabat hosts the AU's African Migration Observatory, FIFA's African office and UCLG Africa, among other international bodies. — en.wikipedia.org (Rabat), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] Technopark Rabat offers free coworking to registered startups; The Hive costs $60–100/month. — getwherenext.com, 2026-02-28, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Rabat–Salé tram: two lines, 27 km, 43 stations, 5:30–23:00; single ride 6 MAD (≈ €0.56), monthly pass about 250 MAD (≈ €23). Careem and inDrive operate. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A tram extension is planned by 2028; a new local station, Rabat Riad, opened in late 2025; a 54 km Kénitra–Skhirat regional express line is under construction. — en.wikipedia.org (Rabat), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Trains: Casablanca about 1 hour, Fès about 2.5 hours, Tanger by Al Boraq 1h20. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Private health insurance costs 500–2,000 MAD per person per month (≈ €46–185); the guide names no clinics. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The new 33-storey Ibn Sina university hospital entered its final building phase in mid-2026; in March 2026 it advertised about 30 head-of-department posts. Opening date not confirmed. Snippets only. — en.le360.ma 2026-07 / medias24.com 2026-03-18, local-media, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Rabat American School: about $7,600 (half-day pre-K) to $29,300 (high school) a year, plus a $9,800 one-time registration fee; Khalil Gibran School 39,500–46,000 MAD/year (≈ €3,657–4,259); Lycée Descartes (AEFE) in Agdal. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Mohammed V University (62,000+ students); public university fees for international students 1,000–5,000 MAD/year (≈ €93–463). — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie's security page (15 Sep 2026) names no Rabat-specific crime hazard; Expat.com calls Rabat one of Morocco's safest cities but gives no statistics. — diplomatie.gouv.fr 2026-09-15 / expat.com 2025-12-05, official + intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Gen Z 212 protests: first protests in Rabat on 27-28 Sep 2025, with demonstrations and clashes on 29 Sep; the CNDH reported protests on 3 and 4 Oct as peaceful; protests resumed in Rabat on 18 Oct. — en.wikipedia.org (2025 Moroccan Gen Z protests), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [safety.women] A relocation blog calls Rabat the most orderly city and reports it as more comfortable for solo women than Marrakech or Fès; France Diplomatie's national warning on harassment of women still applies. — getwherenext.com 2026-02-28 / diplomatie.gouv.fr 2026-09-15, blog + official, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Kasbah of the Oudayas, Hassan Tower, Chellah, Mohammed VI Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art; Mawazine music festival every June. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium

- [nature.access] Beaches: Plage de Rabat, and Temara beach about 15 minutes south, described as cleaner; the Bouregreg corniche for evening walks. — expat.com Rabat guide, 2025-12-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
