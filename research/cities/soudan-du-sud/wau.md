## Items

- [cost.living] In July 2026, with some Wau filling stations closed, motorists bought fuel in bottles at 30,000 SSP (small) or 50,000 SSP (large) (≈ €4.12 / €6.87; bottle sizes not given). — Eye Radio, 2026-07-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] REACH's national survival basket for a six-person household reached a median 782,787 SSP in July 2026 (≈ €107), with the steepest monthly rises in Ganyiel and Wulu (+53 %) and, in the wider Bahr el Ghazal region, Aweil Town (+28 %) and Tonj (+40 %); Wau's own figure is not legible in the text. (national) — REACH JMMI factsheet, data 1–10 July 2026, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In March 2026 Wau residents said a drum of water had doubled from 1,500 to 3,000 SSP (≈ €0.21 → €0.41), larger loads reached 10,000 SSP (≈ €1.37), and some areas went two days without water; borehole operators blamed fuel for pumps and donkey-cart vendors blamed feed costs. — Eye Radio, 2026-03-19, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Older context: in August 2022 Wau Teaching Hospital spent nearly a month without power because of a fuel shortage, until an NGO donated generator fuel. — Eye Radio, 2022-08-24 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] REACH traders reported the Tonj–Wau road closed by seasonal flooding in July 2026, and the Raja crossing to Sudan closed throughout the period, raising transport costs. — REACH JMMI factsheet, July 2026, academic, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] A Wau resident told Eye Radio in July 2026 that fuel prices had left many people unable to pay for transport, so some now walk. — Eye Radio, 2026-07-15, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] In October 2025 the Wau–Tambura road was closed at the Bushere Bridge checkpoint south of Wau for about a week amid reported SSPDF–SPLA-IO fighting; the SSPDF had reportedly carried out airstrikes in Jur River County the week before. — Radio Tamazuj, 2025-10-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The UK says health facilities are "extremely limited" nationwide, most serious cases need evacuation to Nairobi or Kampala, and there is no public emergency medical number. (national) — UK FCDO health page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France lists Wau (spelled "Way") in its orange zone, go only for a compelling reason, with the surrounding country red. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-18, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all travel to South Sudan; Canada says avoid all travel; Eye Radio reports the US at Level 4 "Do Not Travel". None of the three pages names Wau specifically. (national) — UK FCDO 2026-08-25 / Canada 2026-09-28 / Eye Radio 2026-11-09, official / local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 10 November 2026 the SSPDF deployed troops to Wau County for a presidential disarmament campaign; after gunfire near Bringi, residents of Bagari Payam fled into the forest and had not returned by 12 November. — Radio Tamazuj, 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Bagari's paramount chief said homes, a health centre, a women's centre and the court were looted or vandalised; SPLA-IO forces (Division 6A) are also present in Wau County. — Radio Tamazuj, 2026-11-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Western Bahr el Ghazal civil society asked on 14 November 2026 for oversight of disarmament troops over looting reports; the operation runs under a state of emergency the commander said would last six months, and exam centres in Wau County were flagged as possibly unsafe. — Radio Tamazuj, 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In July 2026 a secondary-school teacher was shot dead in the afternoon on Wau's eastern bank by armed men on a motorbike; Warrap State called it targeted and linked it to the Marial Wau–Apuk Giir border dispute, which Wau authorities had not confirmed. — Radio Tamazuj, 2026-07-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Police said calm returned to parts of Wau County in April 2026 after cattle-related violence left several dead, injured and displaced; hotspots named were Bazia, Baselia/Buseri and the Wau–Aweil road. — Eye Radio, 2026-04-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 13–14 June 2026, two incidents in Western Bahr el Ghazal (a boda-boda rider stabbed in Raja County, cattle raids in Jur River County) left seven dead and 16 injured, per state police. — Eye Radio, 2026-06-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Heavy clashes in 2016 displaced much of Wau's Fertit population; in April 2017 soldiers and allied militia killed up to 50 non-Dinka civilians, and about 8,500 displaced people sheltered at the Catholic cathedral in 2016–18. — Wikipedia "Wau, South Sudan", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (digest), confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Older context: a night curfew imposed after civil unrest that killed dozens in Wau was eased in March 2013 to 10 pm–5 am after nearly four months. — Radio Tamazuj, 2013-03-26 (older), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] The UK says international cards are rarely accepted and ATMs are hard to find; Canada says ATMs give SSP only and old or marked dollar notes are refused. (national) — UK FCDO 2026-08-25 / Canada 2026-09-28, official, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] The UK says sex between men can bring up to 14 years in prison; the 2008 Penal Code art. 248 sets up to 10 years (sources conflict). (national) — UK FCDO 2026-08-25 / Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in South Sudan", accessed 2026-11-14, official / intl-media, confidence: medium
- [other] Wau lies on the Jur River about 650 km north-west of Juba, had about 320,752 inhabitants in 2022 and has an airport with a 2,500 m paved runway. — Wikipedia "Wau, South Sudan", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (digest), confidence: medium
- [other] The troop deployment coincides with voter registration (14–28 November 2026) for elections the electoral commission has now pushed to 11 February 2027. — Radio Tamazuj, 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: high
