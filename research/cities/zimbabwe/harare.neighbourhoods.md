# Harare: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (dossier `research/countries/zimbabwe.md`, "to be recomputed"); the ZiG traded at about ZiG 26.43 per USD officially in November 2026 [C daily.money]. Prices are quoted in USD almost everywhere, so local currency here means USD. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/zimbabwe/harare.md`; `[D]` = the dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Lease terms**: a 2–3-month deposit, sometimes plus 1–2 months in advance, so moving in can cost 4–5 months' rent; leases usually run 12 months or more, paid in USD (Expat.com, 2025-10-28) [C cost.rent].
- **Water and power**: 423 of 674 portal listings advertise a borehole [C housing.search]; frequent and sometimes long blackouts, traffic lights may fail (FCDO, page set 2026-07-15) [C daily.power]; outages of power and water across Harare (Canada, 2026-10-13) [C daily.water]. A property-portal guide says homes with full backup rent for 15–20 % more (Propertybook, 2025-05-15) [3].
- **Safety, city-wide**: mugging and bag-snatching in the centre after dark; smash-and-grab at main intersections on the airport road, Liberation Legacy Way (Borrowdale Road) and Churchill Road (FCDO, 2026-07-15) [C safety.general]. Burglaries rising, sometimes armed; France advises an alarm linked to a security company and not walking in the centre at night (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [C safety.general]. Demonstrations in the CBD and high-density suburbs can turn violent (Canada, 2026-10-13) [C safety.general].
- **LGBT readers**: sex between men is criminal (Section 73), with a small underground scene in Harare and rare prosecutions (FCDO 2026-07-15; HRW 2026-01) [C rights.lgbt]. This applies to every neighbourhood below.
- **Transport**: no integrated public transport; kombis carry most residents, ZUPCO runs buses, metered taxis must be called [C daily.transport]. Wikivoyage (revision read 2026-11-15) gives kombis at about USD 0.50 between the centre and suburbs, taxis at about USD 2–3 by day and USD 5 at night for the whole cab, and inDrive and Hwindi as the apps to use [1]. livingcost.org's modelled 8 km taxi at USD 20.90 [C daily.transport] is far above that; treat both as rough. Rush hours run about 6:30–8:30 and 16:30–18:30 [3].

## Neighbourhoods

### Borrowdale (with Borrowdale Brooke and Vainona)
- Feel: a mostly residential northern suburb, historically tied to the elite and once "the richest community in Zimbabwe"; since the late 2000s non-resident and foreign buyers have pushed prices far above local incomes [8]. A portal guide calls it the country's most prestigious address, drawing diplomats, executives, diaspora buyers and families who want gated estates (property.co.zw, 2025-09-03) [2]. The diaspora invested heavily in the northern suburbs before COVID-19 [C community.returnee].
- Rent: 4-bed house with pool USD 2,200/month (≈ €1,890); 3-bed house in Borrowdale Brooke USD 2,500 (≈ €2,150); furnished 2-bed townhouse in Vainona USD 1,600 (≈ €1,380) (property.co.zw, "as of October 2026") [C cost.rent]. Expat.com range USD 1,500–3,500 (≈ €1,290–3,010) (2025-10-28) [C cost.rent]; executive homes USD 1,200–3,500 (property.co.zw blog, 2025-09-03) [2].
- Getting around: about 12 km from the CBD (Propertybook, 2025-06-04) [4]. Car or ride-hailing; no kombi route sourced. Rush hours as above [3].
- Watch: smash-and-grab robberies at main intersections on Liberation Legacy Way (Borrowdale Road) (FCDO, 2026-07-15) [C safety.general]. Activists are suing to stop housing on the Borrowdale West wetland, which their lawyer calls one of Harare's key water sources; hearing set for 7 December 2026 (ZimLive, 2026-11-06) [C daily.water].
- Places:
  - Sam Levy's Village (shopping centre, 100+ stores, Bon Marché, cinema, cafés; 9:00–20:00 daily per the 2025 guide) — Wikipedia [8]; Propertybook [3]; property.co.zw [2]
  - Borrowdale Brooke Centre and Rowland Square (shopping centres) — Wikipedia [8]
  - École française de Harare, Groupe scolaire Jean de La Fontaine (French AEFE-network school, preschool to Upper 6, 1 Teign Road, Vainona; founded 1983) — France Diplomatie [19][20]; school site [21]
  - St John's College, The Heritage School, Westminster International, Hartmann House Preparatory (schools) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Ballantyne Park and Greystone Park (parks) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Borrowdale Race Course and Borrowdale Country Club (racecourse, sports club) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Borrowdale Trauma Centre, Borrowdale Lane (private emergency care, 24/7) — Propertybook [3]. Wikipedia instead lists a "Trauma Centre & Hospital" in Belgravia [17]; check which one is meant.
  - St Gerard's Borrowdale Parish (Catholic church) — Wikipedia [8]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French school in Vainona, several private schools); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q6 "Français" (the only French school found in the country); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Mount Pleasant
- Feel: a northern suburb, once white and now multiracial, one of Harare's more affluent; the University of Zimbabwe gives it an academic, bohemian feel with bookshops, cafés and restaurants, and many older homes now house businesses [10]. A 2025 portal guide calls it central and popular with expatriates (property.co.zw, 2025-10-15) [5].
- Rent: Expat.com range USD 1,200–2,800 (≈ €1,030–2,410) (2025-10-28); a 4-bed flat at USD 3,000 (≈ €2,580) on the portal (October 2026) [C cost.rent].

### Avondale (with Avondale West and Belgravia's edge)
- Feel: one of Harare's oldest suburbs, about 3.5 km north of the centre, gentrified in the 1980s–1990s with coffeehouses, restaurants, bars, theatres and shops; Avondale West is wooded and residential, the King George Road side more commercial [9]. Inner suburbs such as Avondale and Belgravia have gentrified with high prices [C housing.areas]. A portal page says it draws expats, NGO households, medical staff and families, and residents call it convenient but noisy on main roads (Propzone, undated 2026) [6].
- Rent: 3-bed garden flat (80 m²) USD 1,700/month (≈ €1,460); 3-bed house with pool USD 1,500 (≈ €1,290) (property.co.zw, October 2026) [C cost.rent]; an Avondale West townhouse example at USD 1,200 (≈ €1,030) (property.co.zw blog, 2025-10-15) [5].
- Getting around: 3.5 km to the centre [9]; taxi about USD 2–3 by day, USD 5 at night [1]. Propzone claims peak-hour bottlenecks add about 45 minutes (portal estimate, undated) [6].
- Watch: a portal page rates property crime high, but that is not an official source; the city-wide FCDO and France Diplomatie warnings above apply. The Belgravia Sports Club is named by a resident as a source of late event noise (Propzone review) [6].
- Places:
  - Avondale Shopping Centre (shopping centre) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Avondale Flea Market (curio and farmers' market near the shopping centre, running since 1997; Wikipedia says weekly, a 2015 guide says daily) — Wikipedia [9]; AFK Travel [24]
  - Cafe Espresso, Cork Road (café with garden and Wi-Fi) and 40 Cork Road (outdoor café, gallery, sculpture garden) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Jam Tree, Avondale (bar and live music) — VisitZimbabwe [23]
  - St Anne's Hospital, King George Road side (hospital) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Drs Campbell and Cowper, 27 Lanark Road (private GP practice; list updated 2020-12-03) — FCDO [18]
  - Avondale Primary, Twin Rivers, Blakistone and Littlerock International (schools) — Wikipedia [9]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (cafés, central); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" by Harare standards; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" and "Danser jusqu'au matin".

### Chisipite and Highlands
- Feel: Chisipite is described as tranquil, upscale and family-centred, with green space (property.co.zw, 2025-09-03) [2]; Highlands as quiet and leafy with old-school charm, linked to the CBD by Enterprise Road (Propertybook, 2025-06-04) [4].

### Mabelreign and Marlborough (north-west, middle class)
- Feel: a mid-density north-western suburb with an established, owner-occupier character (Propzone, undated 2026) [7]; several shopping centres (Wikipedia) [12]. ### Western high-density suburbs (Kuwadzana, Budiriro)
- Feel: Kuwadzana lies on the western border just south of the Harare–Bulawayo road, upgraded from a slum in the 1980s (Wikipedia) [16]. Budiriro is the most populous suburb in the country, about 90,000 people, with only two clinics and five primary schools (Wikipedia) [15]. The south and south-west are industrial and high-density [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 3-room cottages in Kuwadzana 7 at USD 250 and 400 (≈ €215–344), the dearer one with borehole backup (Facebook group, 12–14 Nov 2026) [C cost.rent]. No Budiriro rent found.
- Getting around: kombis at about USD 0.50 to the centre [1]; no distance sourced.
- Watch: demonstrations in high-density suburbs can turn violent (Canada, 2026-10-13) [C safety.general]; Wikivoyage advises avoiding high-density suburbs after dark [1]. Budiriro had flash floods in late 2016 and half the country's cholera cases in 2008 (Wikipedia) [15]; nearby Highfield and Mbare flooded on 4 January 2026 [13][14]. Borehole backup is a selling point in Kuwadzana ads [C daily.water].
- Places:
  - OK Budiriro 5 and TM Budiriro 1 (supermarkets), Current Shopping Centre (shops) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Budiriro Sports Club (sports) — Propertybook [4]
  - Budiriro Polyclinic (public clinic, on solar) — [C daily.power]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; only for readers who already have family or work there; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" in the daytime sense.

### Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Base: **Mabelreign / Westgate** to start: Simba Harare in Westgate runs on borehole and solar and offers airport pickup on request (USD 60–70 B&B) [1]. ## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Move by inDrive or a called taxi; the centre is lively by day but walking after dark is advised against by France and FCDO [C safety.general]. Do not photograph police, soldiers, demonstrations or State House [C other].

### One day
- Morning: National Gallery of Zimbabwe, USD 10, with Shona stone carving and a small café [1]. Then the Kopje, a granite hill in the south-west of the centre with city views, by day only [1].
- Midday: Coimbra, 61 Selous Avenue, half chicken and chips about USD 10; about USD 5 by taxi from the main hotels [1].
- Afternoon: Mukuvisi Woodlands, Hillside Road, 08:00–17:00, adults USD 5, trails of 3–10 km [1].
- Evening: Avondale. Dinner at a Cork Road café [1], then live music at The Jam Tree [23]. Taxi back (about USD 5 at night) [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 ("what daily life looks like"): early morning at Mbare Musika, the country's largest produce market, where farmers bring crops every morning [14]; it is also the main long-distance bus terminal, large and confusing, so go by taxi [1]. Afternoon at National Heroes Acre, USD 10 [1] (no photos of soldiers).
- Day 3: Domboshava, 27 km north: climb the granite hill and see rock paintings [28]; fee not confirmed. Afternoon at Chapungu Sculpture Park [1].
- Day 4: Avondale Flea Market [9]; galleries at Doon Estate and Msasa Park for stone sculpture [C social.culture]; afternoon tour at Wild Is Life (USD 100 adults, Tue–Sat 15:00–18:00, cash or local card) [1].
- Day 5 (trip out): Lake Chivero Recreational Park, south-west of the city [29]; USD 10 international day fee (ZimParks 2023) [27].
- Day 6: Sam Levy's Village [3][8]; afternoon football: Dynamos at Rufaro or CAPS United at the National Sports Stadium, or cricket at Harare Sports Club, 3 Lanark Road (USD 10–15 for rugby or cricket) [1][C social.sport].
- Day 7: Sunday service at the Cathedral of St Mary and All Saints [1]; afternoon at the National Botanic Garden (68 ha, 900+ native species) [1].
- Optional longer trip: Chinhoyi Caves, 135 km north-west on the A1, with the cobalt-blue "Sleeping Pool" and a parks campsite [30]; USD 10 international fee (ZimParks 2023) [27]. Wikivoyage lists Victoria Falls, Bulawayo and the Eastern Highlands as next stops [1].
