## Items

- [cost.rent] Modelled estimates (not listings): 1-bed flat in the centre USD 315/month (≈ €271), a cheap 1-bed USD 226 (≈ €194), 3-bed in the centre USD 583 (≈ €501), a cheap 3-bed USD 399 (≈ €343). — livingcost.org Mutare, updated 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The national portal listed only two rentals: a 5-bed house in Fairbridge Park at USD 800/month (≈ €688) and a CBD office. — property.co.zw Mutare rentals, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled monthly total with rent: USD 754 (≈ €648) for one person, USD 1,839 (≈ €1,582) for a family of four. — livingcost.org Mutare, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Modelled prices: lunch menu USD 9.43 (≈ €8.11), dinner for two USD 50.10 (≈ €43.10), 0.5 L beer in a pub USD 1.15 (≈ €0.99), milk USD 1.26/L (≈ €1.08), eggs USD 1.90/dozen (≈ €1.63), gym USD 40.20/month (≈ €34.60). — livingcost.org Mutare, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Modelled median monthly salary after tax: USD 251 (≈ €216). — livingcost.org Mutare, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Upscale suburbs to the north and east: Murambi, Fairbridge Park, Morningside, Tiger's Kloof, the Avenues, Palmerstone, Darlington; Weirmouth and Fern Valley to the south have plots over an acre; Yeovil, Westlea and Florida are middle-class; Chikanga and Dangamvura are high-density; working-class Sakubva holds nearly half the population. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Sale prices: portal averages USD 175,000 (≈ €150,500) for houses and USD 180,000 overall across 65 listings ("as of October 2026"). — property.co.zw Mutare sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Asking prices: 4-bed house in Darlington USD 150,000 (≈ €129,000); 3-bed houses in Weirmouth USD 220,000 and 350,000 (≈ €189,200 and €301,000); 3-bed flat in Murambi USD 230,000 (≈ €197,800); residential stand in Chikanga USD 17,500 (≈ €15,050). — property.co.zw Mutare sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [other] Population figures conflict: 224,804 at the 2022 census versus 260,567 listed for 2012; the metro area is put at about 500,000. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Main employers and sectors: farming, forestry (pine, gum, wattle), dairy, Cairns Foods, Tanganda Tea, Willowton edible oils, Quest bus and truck assembly, and gold mining at Redwing and Penhalonga. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The city is about 8 km from the Mozambique border; the A3 runs to Harare and the EN9 east to Chimoio and Beira; the nearest international airport is Harare, about 214 km away; heavy traffic and potholes are increasingly common. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Wikipedia describes passenger trains to Harare (overnight, three times a week), but FCDO says passenger rail is suspended nationwide; the two sources conflict. — Wikipedia "Mutare" and FCDO travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, academic/official, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Modelled fares: local bus ticket USD 0.57 (≈ €0.49); 8 km taxi USD 19 (≈ €16.30). — livingcost.org Mutare, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Modelled price for 50 Mbps+ unlimited internet: USD 59.50/month (≈ €51.20). — livingcost.org Mutare, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Problems at the wastewater works have at times let untreated sewage reach the river in Sakubva. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [health.care] Clinics in every suburb, a main general hospital, an infectious-diseases hospital and maternity hospitals. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Private schools include Hillcrest Preparatory and Hillcrest College; public secondaries include Mutare Boys' High, Mutare Girls' High and Hartzell. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Africa University (Methodist, about 5,000 students) has its main campus in Old Mutare and draws students from across Africa, including DR Congo; Manicaland State University and Mutare Polytechnic are also in town. — Wikipedia "Mutare" and africau.edu, accessed 2026-11-14, academic/company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France advises against the Marange diamond fields, about 50 km south-west of Mutare: access is controlled, and buying diamonds from unlicensed sellers is a crime. — France Diplomatie (sécurité), 2026-09-15 (zones 2026-03-13), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Canada says do not enter Marange, and urges extra caution at the Honde Valley waterfalls and on roads near the Mozambique border, where highway robberies occur, especially at night. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Anti-personnel mines remain along the northern border with Mozambique, and it is dangerous to leave the main roads there. — France Diplomatie (sécurité), 2026-09-15 (zones 2026-03-13), official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Mostly Shona, speaking the Manyika dialect; Mutare Museum, Utopia House, a National Gallery branch and Murahwa Hill (rock paintings, Iron Age village). — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Mild, altitude-moderated climate, about 19 °C on average: July around 6–20 °C, October around 16–32 °C; about 790–820 mm of rain a year, mostly December–February. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Bvumba mountains (Afromontane forest, hiking, riding, kayaking, Leopard Rock Hotel) are next door; the Cecil Kop game park in Tiger's Kloof is said to lack proper management; Gorongosa (Mozambique) is within reach. — Wikipedia "Mutare", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
