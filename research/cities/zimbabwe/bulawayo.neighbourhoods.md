# Bulawayo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (dossier `research/countries/zimbabwe.md`, "to be recomputed"); prices are quoted in USD. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/zimbabwe/bulawayo.md`; `[H tag]` = a national item kept in `research/cities/zimbabwe/harare.md`; `[D]` = the dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Thin rental market**: the main portal showed only 27 Bulawayo rentals, most of them commercial (October 2026) [C cost.rent]. Many prices below are 2024–2025 portal-blog ranges or sale prices, not live listings.
- **Water** is the first question to ask a landlord: shortages are more frequent than in Harare and many residents add a borehole (Expat.com, 2025-10-28) [C daily.water]; dam capacity has not kept up with population and burst sewers can contaminate tap water (Wikipedia) [C daily.water]. Effective water cost about USD 5.86 per kilolitre once levies are added; Burnside, Hillside and Sunninghill reportedly pay more than some Harare areas (CITE, 2026-07-01) [C cost.living]. National power cuts apply [D].
- **Safety**: no FCDO or France Diplomatie advice specific to Bulawayo; the national warnings (street crime after dark, poor roads at night, roadblocks) apply [C safety.general]. Canada urges extra caution and says demonstrations in the CBD and high-density suburbs can turn violent (2026-10-13) [C safety.general]. Police warned of growing drug use in the city (CITE, 2026-11-04) [C safety.general].
- **LGBT readers**: sex between men is criminal nationally (FCDO 2026-07-15; HRW 2026-01) [H rights.lgbt].
- **Transport**: kombis (18-seat minibuses) about USD 0.50 from the CBD to the suburbs; taxis about USD 2 inside the CBD and about USD 1/km outside it; inDrive recommended (Wikivoyage, revision read 2026-11-15) [1]. livingcost.org models a bus ticket at USD 0.65 and a monthly pass at USD 30.10 [C daily.transport]. City-centre parking costs USD 1/hour on weekdays 8:00–17:00 and Saturday mornings, clamping USD 40 (CITE, 2026-07-02) [C cost.living].

## Neighbourhoods

### Suburbs and Khumalo (inner low-density, next to Centenary Park)
- Feel: "Suburbs" was Bulawayo's first suburb and kept the name; tree-lined avenues, with Centenary Park, the Natural History Museum and the Bulawayo Athletic Club (Wikipedia) [4]. Khumalo, named after the royal clan, is described by a portal blog as tranquil and tree-lined, for families and professionals (property.co.zw, 2024-11-19) [3].
- Rent: 3–4-bed houses in Hillside, Suburbs and Matsheumhlope USD 800–2,500/month (≈ €688–2,150) (Expat.com, 2025-10-28) [C cost.rent]; a 3-bed double-storey house in Kumalo USD 1,500 (≈ €1,290) (property.co.zw, October 2026) [C cost.rent]; Khumalo 3–4-bed homes sell for about USD 120,000 (2024) [3].
- Getting around: next to the centre; taxi about USD 2 for CBD hops [1]. No sourced distance.
- Places:
  - Centenary Park (park with amphitheatre, lawns and a large fountain) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Natural History Museum of Zimbabwe, corner Park Road and Leopold Takawira Avenue (museum, daily 09:00–17:00, USD 10 for non-residents) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [4]
  - Bulawayo Athletic Club, 12th Avenue / 3rd Street (bar, live music) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [4]
  - Khumalo hockey stadium (sports ground) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Khumalo Primary School (school) — property.co.zw [3]
  - Queens Sports Club, First Avenue and Queens Street (Test cricket ground) — Wikivoyage [1]; [C social.sport]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" and "Création, culture" (walk to the centre, museums); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis".

### Hillside
- Feel: an upper-middle-class suburb with wide jacaranda-lined streets, about 5 km from downtown (Propertybook guide, undated 2026) [5]; green and scenic, popular with young professionals, families and retirees (property.co.zw, 2024-11-19) [3]; a portal says many residents are academics and students tied to NUST (Propzone, undated) [7].
- Rent: apartments USD 300–450/month (≈ €258–387) (property.co.zw blog, 2024-11-19) [3]; townhouses listed at USD 1,300 (≈ €1,118) (Propertybook, 2026) [5]; 3–4-bed houses USD 800–2,500 (Expat.com, 2025-10-28) [C cost.rent]. Sale: 4-bed USD 275,000–280,000 (portal, October 2026) [C housing.search].
- Getting around: about 5 km to the centre [5]: at about USD 1/km a taxi is roughly USD 5 (my arithmetic on Wikivoyage's rate) [1]; kombi about USD 0.50 [1].
- Watch: Hillside residents reportedly pay higher water rates than some Harare areas (CITE, 2026-07-01) [C cost.living].
- Places:
  - Hillside Dams Conservancy (several dams, walking, biking, fishing, birdwatching) — Wikipedia [4]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hillside Shopping Centre, Burn Street (supermarket, pharmacy) — Propertybook [5]
  - Zonkizizwe (Bradfield) Shopping Complex, Hillside Road, with Roosters Pub & Grill (shops, pub) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - New Orleans Restaurant, Banff Road (restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hillside Presbyterian Church (church) — Propertybook [5]
  - Hillside Primary School (founded 1954) and Petra Primary and High School (private) (schools) — Propertybook [5]
  - Hillside Hospital (hospital; exact street not given) — Wikipedia list [11]; [C health.care]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (quiet, near NUST); Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen" for the flats.

### Burnside
- Feel: one of the most prestigious residential areas, wide tree-lined streets, large plots, family-oriented (Propertybook guide) [6]; spacious, private and quiet (property.co.zw, 2024-11-19) [3]; a resident describes a quiet, rocky suburb with large yards (Propzone review, about 7 months before Nov 2026) [8].
- Rent: typical USD 800–2,000/month (≈ €688–1,720) (Propertybook, 2026) [6]; two homes (5 beds in all) on one property USD 2,000 (≈ €1,720) (property.co.zw, October 2026) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: about 10 km to the CBD (Propertybook) [6]; Wikivoyage places two Burnside guesthouses at about 4.8–6 km from the centre [1], so the distance depends on the street. Car or taxi (about USD 1/km) [1].
- Watch: higher water rates reported (CITE, 2026-07-01) [C cost.living]; many homes have boreholes and tanks [6]. A resident says power cuts are scheduled and limited (Propzone review) [8].
- Places:
  - Burnside Dam (fishing, picnics) — Propertybook [6]
  - Girls' College (private girls' secondary, Form 1–6) and Burnside Primary School (public) — Propertybook [6]
  - Burke's Backpackers Paradise, 11 Inverleith Drive (backpackers; dorm USD 15, double USD 30, camping USD 7) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Fleming Place, 6 Fleming Drive (guesthouse, double USD 39) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Free to be Wild (wildlife sanctuary named as nearby) — Propertybook [6]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (the road to Matobo).

### Western high-density suburbs (Makokoba, Mzilikazi, Barbourfields, Nkulumane, Pumula, Cowdray Park)
- Feel: Makokoba is "the oldest African dwelling in the city"; Mzilikazi and Barbourfields sit either side of Ambulance Drive, which leads to Mpilo hospital (Wikipedia) [4]. The "Western Suburbs" are the city's high-density areas [C housing.areas]. A portal blog calls Cowdray Park, Nkulumane and Pumula affordable, community-focused and fast-growing (property.co.zw, 2024-11-19) [3]. Entumbane, where King Mzilikazi is buried, is where the first disturbances that led to Gukurahundi broke out [4]: a history to raise with care.
- Rent: not found. Sale prices: small homes in Cowdray Park from about USD 15,000; Nkulumane and Pumula USD 20,000–30,000 (2024) [3]. Buyers face "land baron" plot scams (CITE, 2026-11-11) [C housing.search].
- Getting around: kombi about USD 0.50 to the CBD [1]; the portal blog cites good links to the centre [3].
- Watch: well water in Pumula and Robert Sinyoka exceeds coliform limits (Wikipedia) [C daily.water]; demonstrations in high-density suburbs can turn violent (Canada, 2026-10-13) [C safety.general]; in October 2025 police and suspected ruling-party supporters shut down a civil-society meeting in Bulawayo (HRW) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Barbourfields Stadium (football, home of Highlanders FC; about 40,000 standing) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Mpilo Central Hospital (largest hospital in the city; radiotherapy starting within three to four months of Nov 2026) — Wikipedia [4]; [C health.care]
  - Makokoba (historic township) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Entumbane (burial place of King Mzilikazi) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" for buyers with family ties; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Ndebele history); not a first base for a newcomer.

## Living: typical weeks

### Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Hillside**. Quiet, cheap flats by portal-blog standards (USD 300–450), a dams conservancy for breaks, 5 km to town [3][5]. Ask about borehole water and backup power before signing [C daily.water]. Starlink residential is waitlisted here too [C daily.internet].
- Monday: work from home; groceries at Hillside Shopping Centre [5].
- Tuesday: Bulawayo Tech Hub, a coworking space and incubator (USD 5/day, USD 75/month for founders, CITE, 2024-05-24; address not given) [10]; taxi about USD 5 [1].
- Wednesday: work at Indaba Book Cafe, 92 Josiah Tongogara Road (Tue–Fri 08:00–16:00) [1].
- Thursday: walk at Hillside Dams Conservancy at lunch [1][4]; dinner at New Orleans Restaurant, Banff Road [1].
- Friday: Tech Hub again [10]; live music at 3 Daughters, a jazz sports bar, from 21:00 [1].
- Saturday: Natural History Museum and Centenary Park [1][4] (no Bulawayo market day could be sourced); evening below.
- Sunday: Hillside Presbyterian Church [5]; lunch at Roosters Pub & Grill, Zonkizizwe complex [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Cafe Baku, Bulawayo Centre, now mainly a late-night disco (Wikivoyage) [1]; Club 263, city centre (VisitZimbabwe, undated) [12]. Taxi both ways.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": The Cattleman steakhouse, corner Josiah Tongogara Street and 12th Avenue [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Expat.com calls the city calmer than Harare [D].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Bulawayo (landlocked [D]); canoeing at Matobo Sailing Club is the nearest water sport [C nature.access].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Matobo Hills, about 35 km south, a World Heritage granite landscape with over 3,000 rock-art sites [13]; gate USD 15 for non-residents [14][15].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": the Matobo game park with white and black rhinos and viewing hides [13]; Tshabalala sanctuary (USD 10 international, ZimParks 2023) [15].
  - "Je reste en ville": National Art Gallery (USD 10) and Railway Museum (USD 5) [1].

  - Electricity + water: no Bulawayo figure; Harare's USD 100–200 + 20–50 = 120–250 a month used as a proxy ÷ 4.33 = 27.7–57.7 [H cost.living]
  - Coworking 2 days × USD 5 = 10 [10]
  - Groceries: 3 × 500 g bread at USD 1 + 3 L milk × 1.63 + eggs 1.86 + 1 kg chicken 3.88 + 1 kg tomatoes 1.03 + 1 kg potatoes 1.24 = 15.90 (quantities are my assumption) [C cost.living]
  - Meals: one Bulawayo Club dinner about USD 11 [1] + one dinner for two USD 24.90 [C cost.living] = 35.90
  - Four beers × USD 1.29 = 5.16; gym USD 31.30 a month ÷ 4.33 = 7.2 [C cost.living]
  - Taxis 4 × about USD 5 = 20; kombis 6 × USD 0.50 = 3 [1]
  - **Total ≈ USD 202–266 a week (≈ €173–229)**.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Taxis or inDrive between sights [1]; do not walk after dark [C safety.general]; do not photograph police or soldiers [H other].

### One day
- Morning: Natural History Museum, corner Park Road and Leopold Takawira Avenue (09:00–17:00, USD 10 non-residents) [1], then a walk through Centenary Park and to City Hall, where artists sell sculpture [4].
- Midday: The Coffee Shoppe inside the National Art Gallery (breakfast and lunch only), and the gallery itself (Tue–Sat, USD 10) [1].
- Afternoon: Bulawayo Railway Museum, Prospect Avenue and Crew Road (08:00–16:00, USD 5) [1], by taxi.
- Evening: dinner at The Cattleman [1], then live music at the Bulawayo Athletic Club [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route.
- Day 2 (trip out): Matobo National Park, about 35 km south; World Heritage since 2003 [13]. Gate USD 15 for non-residents, World's View and cave paintings USD 10; no bus, so a car or taxi at USD 30–40 [14]. World's View (Malindidzimu) is a short climb [13][14].
- Day 3: stay in Matobo: rhino viewing in the game park, rock art at Pomongwe Cave with its small museum, the Maleme Dam lakeside walk [13][14]; Maleme Rest Camp has lodges, chalets and camping [13].
- Day 4 (trip out): Khami, the ruined capital of the Torwa state, 22 km west, World Heritage since 1986 [17]; USD 10, taxi about USD 20 one way [1].
- Day 5 ("what daily life looks like"): a weekday morning kombi from the CBD into the western suburbs at about USD 0.50 [1], passing Barbourfields and Mpilo [4]; back via Haefelli's Bakery [1].
- Day 6: a Highlanders match at Barbourfields Stadium if one is scheduled [9] (ticket price not sourced); evening Cafe Baku or Club 263 [1][12].
- Day 7: Sunday service at Hillside Presbyterian Church [5]; afternoon at Hillside Dams and Indaba Book Cafe (open Sunday 08:00–14:00) [1].
- Season: in September the Intwasa Arts Festival and the King Mzilikazi commemorations at City Hall [C social.culture].
- Optional: Fastjet flies Bulawayo–Victoria Falls on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays (since August 2025; fares not sourced); the road is in poor condition [C daily.transport].
