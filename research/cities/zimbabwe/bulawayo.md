- [housing.search] Sale prices: portal average USD 220,000 (≈ €189,000) across 568 listings; houses average USD 200,000 (≈ €172,000) and flats USD 91,500 (≈ €78,700). Prices "as of October 2026". — property.co.zw Bulawayo sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Asking prices: 3-bed house in Waterford USD 110,000 (≈ €94,600); 3-bed in Ilanda USD 170,000 (≈ €146,200); 3-bed townhouse in Burnside USD 150,000 (≈ €129,000); 4-bed in Hillside USD 275,000–280,000 (≈ €236,500–240,800); residential land in Lower Rangemore at USD 27–35/m². — property.co.zw Bulawayo sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] With too few affordable council stands, residents buy plots from private "land barons"; in one case land outside the city was pledged as loan collateral, so buyers risk losing their stands. — CITE, 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Bulawayo has run out of state land for development; ministers want ageing low-density government houses replaced with multi-storey flats, and developers must reserve at least 40% of land for multi-storey buildings. — Bulawayo24, about 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Once the industrial hub (cars, textiles, building materials, food), Bulawayo has seen many factories close or move to Harare; service delivery has declined and unemployment has risen, with some renewed investor interest after 2017. — Wikipedia "Bulawayo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.admin] In a chamber-of-commerce survey of 30 firms, over 70% reported licensing delays, some of six months or more; one applicant waited over eight months and moved the investment to another city; most called compliance costs "simply too high". — CITE (ZNCC survey), 2026-07-01, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Water shortages hit in droughts because population growth has outpaced dam capacity; well water in Pumula and Robert Sinyoka exceeds coliform limits, and burst sewers can contaminate tap water. — Wikipedia "Bulawayo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Water shortages are more frequent in Bulawayo than in Harare, and many residents add a private borehole. — Expat.com Zimbabwe guide, 2025-10-28, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] United Bulawayo Hospitals (UBH) has a 350 kW solar plant, fully running since February 2026, which cut its diesel spending on generators. — Health Policy Watch via NewZimbabwe, 2026-11-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.internet] Starlink residential customers in Bulawayo, as in Harare, are offered only expensive Priority plans or a waiting list. — TechZim, 2026-09-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.internet] Modelled price for 50 Mbps+ unlimited internet: USD 31.90/month (≈ €27.40); most residents go online through mobile phones. — livingcost.org Bulawayo (2026-06-21) and Wikipedia "Bulawayo", company/academic, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Modelled fares: local bus ticket USD 0.65 (≈ €0.56), monthly pass USD 30.10 (≈ €25.90), 8 km taxi USD 19.10 (≈ €16.40). — livingcost.org Bulawayo, 2026-06-21, company, confidence: low

- [daily.transport] Bulawayo is the rail hub and headquarters of National Railways of Zimbabwe; about 70% of its 2,100 km of roads were in poor condition in 2017 (old figure). — Wikipedia "Bulawayo", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
