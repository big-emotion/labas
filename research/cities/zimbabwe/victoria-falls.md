# Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city: the dossier suggests it as a tourism-economy town with an international airport. Currency note: prices are in USD; EUR equivalents use the dossier's indicative rate **1 USD ≈ €0.86** (to be recomputed). ## Items

- [cost.rent] Rents run about USD 700–2,000/month (≈ €600–1,720), with limited schools and medical care. — Expat.com Zimbabwe guide, 2025-10-28, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] The national property portal had only one rental listing in Victoria Falls, a shop at USD 1,050/month (≈ €903), so long-term homes are clearly not let through this channel. — property.co.zw Victoria Falls rentals, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Activity prices residents would also pay: 15-minute helicopter flight USD 150–170 (≈ €129–146), rafting USD 125–140 (≈ €108–120), bungee USD 160 (≈ €138), sunset cruise USD 60–80 (≈ €52–69). — Expat.com Zimbabwe guide, 2025-10-28, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Elephant Walk and Sawanga Mall serve tourists, while the Victoria Falls Shopping Centre and Phumula Centre are the more affordable options for residents. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Sale prices: portal averages USD 530,000 (≈ €455,800) for houses and USD 215,000 (≈ €184,900) for flats across 56 listings ("as of October 2026"), well above Harare's averages. — property.co.zw Victoria Falls sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Asking prices: 3-bed house in Chinotimba USD 65,000 (≈ €55,900); 1-bed flats at Eagle Heights USD 115,000 and 180,000 (≈ €98,900 and €154,800); 4-bed houses in Old Victoria Falls USD 550,000–594,000 (≈ €473,000–510,800); residential stand in Mkhosana USD 58,000 (≈ €49,900). — property.co.zw Victoria Falls sales, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Housing was the second least affordable in Zimbabwe after Harare at the start of 2017 (old figure). — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Chinotimba and Mkhosana appear as the high-density townships (clinics and schools carry their names); listings also cite Eagle Heights, Old Victoria Falls, Mahogany Road and a "CBZ buffer zone". — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe" and property.co.zw, accessed 2026-11-14, academic/company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Tourism is the main industry; employment grew 10.3% in 2018, the fastest in Zimbabwe, mostly in travel and accommodation; more than 200 adventure activities were on offer in 2019. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population figures conflict: 35,199 at the 2022 census, and 35,761 in the infobox labelled "2026 census". — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The airport, 18 km south, has flights to Johannesburg, Cape Town, Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Windhoek, Gaborone and Kasane, plus Harare and Bulawayo. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Fastjet has flown Bulawayo–Victoria Falls four times a week since August 2025; the road to Bulawayo (440 km) is described as in poor condition and under roadworks. — ZimLive, 2025-07-24, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The national railway's daily train to Bulawayo stopped in 2020; only the luxury Rovos Rail runs, two to four times a month. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Health facilities: Victoria Falls District Hospital, the Chinotimba and Mkhosana clinics, a private hospital (The Health Bridge) and a few surgeries. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Mosi-oa-Tunya High is the largest school, followed by Mkhosana Adventist Secondary; about ten primary schools and several private colleges; no international school is named. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Canada lists Victoria Falls among the places needing extra caution because of crime. — Government of Canada travel advice, 2026-10-13, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Swimming in lakes and rivers is forbidden (hippos, crocodiles, parasites, drowning); on safari, never leave the vehicle; check your insurance for bungee, canyoning and rafting. — France Diplomatie (sécurité), 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Animal attacks have injured and killed visitors; check that operators are licensed with the Safari Operators Association of Zimbabwe. — FCDO travel advice (safety and security), accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Hotter than the plateau cities: average highs of 24.8 °C in July up to 33.4 °C in October, July lows around 5.6 °C; about 657 mm of rain, nearly all December–February (1961–1990 data). — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The town is surrounded by the Zambezi, Mosi-oa-Tunya and Victoria Falls national parks, with Hwange nearby; chacma baboons live in town. — Wikipedia "Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
