## Items

- [housing.areas] Dodoma counted 765,179 residents in 2022; Wikipedia projects about 1 million by end-2026 as government and NGO staff relocate; it passed Arusha as the third-largest city in July 2024. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Meals at a central hotel run TZS 3,000–12,000 (≈ €1.0–4.1); Indian thalis about TZS 5,000 (≈ €1.7); grilled pork about TZS 2,500 per kilo (≈ €0.86). Guide listings, some years old. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Shoppers Plaza has the city's largest supermarket; a deli on Hatibu Avenue stocks cheese, yoghurt, frozen food and wine. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Parliament sits in Dodoma, the President's office and ministries completed their move to Mtumba in 2019, and a new State House opened in 2023; Dar es Salaam remains the commercial capital. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Embassies and UN agencies have stayed in Dar es Salaam, while most government meetings now happen in Dodoma. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Official audit figures show Dodoma's water deficit at about 70,000 m³ a day, 47% of demand, in 2024/25. — The Citizen, 2026-09-07, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] The 27 Jun 2026 national grid failure stranded electric SGR passengers for hours; the minister ordered faster work on the 400 kV Chalinze–Dodoma line. — The Chanzo, 2026-06-28, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Electric standard-gauge trains to Dar es Salaam run a few times a day: about 2½ hours by express, an hour longer by ordinary train; the SGR station is 13 km north of the centre. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] SGR passenger service Dar–Dodoma began in 2024; Msalato International Airport (14 km north, US$272M) was about 85% complete for infrastructure in 2026, while the current airport takes only small aircraft. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Bolt operates in the city; dala dalas are cheap; buses to Dar take 7–8 hours. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Rio Times names only an Aga Khan medical centre (outpatient, refers complex cases to Dar) and no full private hospital in Dodoma. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-22, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Expat.com says services for expatriates in Dodoma are "limited compared to other cities" and names no hospital. — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] The University of Dodoma (about 35,000 students, opened 2007) is here; Canon Andrea Mwaka School is the only international school (English National Curriculum). — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK advisory has no Dodoma-specific warning; France warns that fuel-price protests could bring curfews and supply problems in large cities. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14; France Diplomatie, updated 2026-06-08, official, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Nightlife is thin: one disco on Friday nights popular with younger people, a quiet bar, and an outdoor wine-and-cheese bar serving local wine. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] About 19% of residents are Roman Catholic; Anglican, Lutheran, Baptist and Assemblies of God churches, several mosques (including the Gaddafi Mosque), a Hindu mandir and a Sikh gurdwara serve the city. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Tanzanian wine is produced around Dodoma. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Photographing the Parliament buildings is restricted and visits need written permission. — Wikivoyage "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Semi-arid: about 610 mm of rain a year, mostly December–April; the rest is dry; average lows dip to about 13°C in July. — Wikipedia "Dodoma", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
