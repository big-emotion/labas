# Dar es Salaam: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: ~2,920 TZS = 1 EUR (dossier, implied by Numbeo 2026-11-08). USD figures stay in USD, as in the dossier and city file. Source numbers in brackets refer to "Sources read" below; "city file" = `research/cities/tanzanie/dar-es-salaam.md`, cited with the original source and date it logs. Weekly figures divide a monthly price by 4.33.

Research-pass note: the session's shared WebSearch budget ran out after two queries in this pass, so almost everything new comes from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia ward pages, Zoom Tanzania's neighbourhood guide, The Rio Times, a coworking guide). Wikivoyage listings are often undated or several years old; they are used to prove that a place exists and where, and their prices are flagged as undated. Wikivoyage safety remarks are not used on their own (rule: safety needs an official, media, NGO or academic source).

City-wide context that applies to every neighbourhood below (all from the city file):
- Rush hours 7–9 am and 4–7 pm; a 20-minute trip can take more than an hour (Expat.com Dar guide, 2025-12-16).
- Uber left Tanzania on 30 Jan 2026; Bolt dominates, with inDrive and Farasi (The Chanzo, 2026-02-01).
- DART BRT: TZS 1,000 main line (≈ €0.34), TZS 500 feeder (The Rio Times, 2026-11-09). Daladala about TZS 400 (≈ €0.14), undated (Wikivoyage).
- Water: DAWASA rationing since Dec 2025; urban supply averaged 14 h a day in 2024/25 (Circle of Blue 2026-01-06; The Citizen 2026-09-07). Power: nationwide blackout 27 Jun 2026 (The Chanzo, 2026-06-28); Expat.com advises a backup generator.
- Politics: France advises avoiding gatherings and night travel; fuel-price protests could bring curfews and internet cuts (France Diplomatie, security item 2026-06-08). X has been blocked since May 2025 (HRW World Report 2026).
- Leases: 3–12 months upfront plus 1–3 months' deposit (Expat.com Dar, 2025-12-16); brokers (madalali) usually take one month's rent and charge viewing fees "rarely below TZS10,000" per house (Minority Africa, 2023-08-30); stamp duty 1% of annual rent (The Rio Times, 2026-09-26).

## Neighbourhoods

### Masaki and Oyster Bay
- Feel: affluent area with many expats, ambassadors and foreign-mission staff, though all income levels live there; planned streets, security guards at homes and businesses, English heard almost as often as Swahili (Zoom Tanzania, 2026-01-31 [4]). Expat social life and nightlife centre here (Expat.com Dar 2025-12-16, city file).
- Rent: 2-bed average US$1,500 unfurnished, US$2,500–3,000 furnished (Zoom Tanzania 2026-01-31 [4]). Good flats in Masaki, Oyster Bay and Msasani TZS 2.5M–6M/month (≈ €856–2,055) (The Africanvestor 2026-09-21, city file). Masaki 2-bed asking US$1,200–1,500 (The Rio Times 2026-11-09, city file); furnished 3-bed in Masaki US$2,850–3,000 (BeForward listings 2026-11-12/14, city file); 3-bed house US$2,500–4,000 (Expat.com 2025-12-16, city file). Some Masaki landlords ask six months upfront (The Rio Times, city file).
- Getting around: the Tanzanite Bridge links Msasani ward (which contains Masaki and Oysterbay) to Kivukoni in the centre [6]. Taxi centre to the peninsula about TZS 7,000, more commonly TZS 10,000 (≈ €2.4–3.4), undated [3]; a bajaji costs about US$2 anywhere on the peninsula (Tales Mag post report, 2020, city file).
- Watch: the UK warns of armed crime in Masaki and Oysterbay, especially Coco Beach, and along Toure Drive, and of express kidnappings (UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, city file).
- Places:
  - The Super Dome (events venue: concerts, boxing, gospel awards, real-estate expo, November 2026) — The Rio Times [13]
  - Coco Beach (town beach, very busy at weekends) — Wikivoyage [3]; Expat.com Dar (city file)
  - Q Bar (large bar-restaurant, Haile Selassie Road, live music on Friday nights) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Tinga-tinga artists' collective (painters' alley off Haile Selassie Road) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - A Novel Idea (bookshop, Oysterbay Shopping Centre) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Oysterbay Primary and Secondary Schools (public schools) and Oysterbay Clinic, IST Clinic (clinics) — Wikipedia "Msasani" [6]
  - Coworking: independent spaces cluster in Masaki, Mikocheni, Oyster Bay and Msasani, day pass TZS 10,000–30,000 (≈ €3.4–10.3), hot desk TZS 150,000–400,000/month (≈ €51–137); none named — mctaba guide, 2026-06-04 [12]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and "Un bon dîner entre amis" (bars, Super Dome nights); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (the expat hub). Not Q3 "Serré".

### Msasani (peninsula, outside Masaki proper)
- Feel: low to high incomes side by side, families and singles; narrow crowded streets, mixed homes and businesses [4]. Wanted in Africa calls the peninsula the popular choice, with most expatriates living there (undated [5]). Ward of 48,920 people in 2012 [6].
- Rent: TZS 400,000–2,500,000/month (≈ €137–856), high-end and low-end housing close together (Zoom Tanzania, 2026-01-31 [4]). Sale asking price in Msasani: 1-bed 82 m² US$170,000 (Facebook agency post 2026-11-14, city file).
- Getting around: same as Masaki: Tanzanite Bridge to Kivukoni [6]; taxi to the centre TZS 7,000–10,000 (undated [3]); boats leave the Slipway for Bongoyo Island (about 30 min) (Expat.com Dar, city file).
- Watch: Toure Drive and Coco Beach are named in the UK's crime warning (FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, city file).
- Places:
  - The Slipway (waterfront shopping centre, market with tinga-tinga paintings and batiks) — Wikivoyage [3]; Expat.com Dar (city file)
  - Shrijee's Supermarket (supermarket, Slipway) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - SeaCliff Village (shopping centre with food court: Spurs, Karembezi Cafe) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Arizona and BBQ Village (semi-local eateries) — Zoom Tanzania [4]
  - Yacht Club (members' club, guests can swim or boat) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Msasani Peninsula Hospital and AfyaMedicare Polyclinic (health) — Wikipedia "Msasani" [6]
  - Msasani Islamic Primary School and Peninsula English Medium School (schools) — Wikipedia "Msasani" [6]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e" (coworking day passes, cafés and the sea within the peninsula); Q3 "Moyen" (wide rent spread); Q8 "À la plage" (Bongoyo boats from the Slipway).

### Mikocheni
- Feel: mid-to-high incomes, growing in popularity; more local bars and offices than Masaki; heavy traffic at peak hours on Mwai Kibaki Road and old Bagamoyo Road [4]. Wanted in Africa calls it a slightly more downmarket but livelier alternative to the peninsula (undated [5]). Expat.com says it is popular with expat families because it is near the International School of Tanganyika and slightly cheaper than Masaki (2025-11-07 [14]). Ward of 32,947 people in 2012 [7].
- Rent: 2-bed average TZS 3M unfurnished (≈ €1,027), 5.5M furnished (≈ €1,884) [4]; 2–3 bed TZS 2M–4.5M (≈ €685–1,541) (Expat.com, 2025-11-07 [14]); 2-bed asking US$1,500 (BeForward 2026-11, city file). The Africanvestor puts Mikocheni in its TZS 500k–1.8M young-professional band (≈ €171–616) (2026-09-21, city file). The sources disagree; not averaged.
- Getting around: on Mwai Kibaki Road and old Bagamoyo Road [4]; Expat.com calls it highly accessible but congested at peaks [4][14]. No BRT line sourced through Mikocheni.
- Places:
  - Kairuki Hospital and TMJ Hospital (private hospitals) — Wikipedia "Mikocheni" [7]; The Rio Times 2026-09-22 (city file)
  - Makumbusho Village Museum (open-air museum of 16 traditional houses, 09:00–18:00, ngoma dances some afternoons) — Wikipedia [7]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Al-Basha (restaurant, Mayfair Plaza, Mikocheni) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - CEFA Hostel (hostel with sea-view terrace, Mikocheni B, Old Bagamoyo Road, TZS 58,125/night) and Passionist Fathers House (guest house, Mikocheni B, TZS 35,000 single) — Wikivoyage, undated prices [3]
  - St Florence Primary School and Mikocheni Primary School (schools) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Hubert Kairuki Memorial University (university) — Wikipedia [7]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (IST nearby, hospitals); Q5 "Salarié·e" (central position between peninsula and New Bagamoyo Road); Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Mbezi Beach, Bahari Beach and Tegeta (northern suburbs)
- Feel: Mbezi is a newer area about 20 km north of the centre [5]; mid-to-high incomes, high car ownership, more bars, supermarkets and fuel stations each year [4]. Bahari Beach: families, managers, retirees and expats, mainly residential and not congested, car practically necessary [4]. Tegeta is more commercial and congested, near the new Bagamoyo Road and the Twiga cement factory [4]. Expat.com names Mbezi Beach for larger homes and beach access (city file).
- Rent: Mbezi 3-bed average TZS 2.1M unfurnished (≈ €719), lower-cost TZS 200k–400k (≈ €68–137); Bahari Beach 2-bed TZS 500k–700k unfurnished (≈ €171–240), TZS 1.5M furnished (≈ €514); Tegeta TZS 400k–600k unfurnished (≈ €137–205) [4]. Cheapest 2-beds include Tegeta and Mbezi (The Africanvestor 2026-09-21, city file). Kawe 2-bed asking US$490 (BeForward 2026-11, city file).
- Getting around: about 20 km to the centre [5] along New Bagamoyo Road, a car-dependent area [4]; buses now reach Ununio village by Bahari Beach [4]. With the 7–9 am peak (city file) the commute is long; no timed trip sourced.
- Watch: The Citizen (2026-09-11) lists "Mbezi" among areas buying water from private tankers, without saying whether this is Mbezi Beach or Mbezi on Morogoro Road (city file).
- Places:
  - Braeburn International School, Mbezi Juu (British-curriculum school, US$12,000–18,000/year) — Wikipedia "Mbezi Juu" [9]; fees Expat.com (city file)
  - MHS-Massana Hospital (hospital, Mbezi Juu) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Bahari Beach Hotel (beach access for a small fee to non-guests, ~20 km north on New Bagamoyo Road) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - White Sands Hotel (departure point for Mbudya Island boats, near Kunduchi) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mediterraneo Hotel & Restaurant (Italian restaurant with ocean view, Kawe) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Akiba, CRDB and NBC branches (banks, Tegeta) — Zoom Tanzania [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (space, Braeburn, beaches); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage". Not for anyone without a car who works in the centre.

### Sinza and Kijitonyama
- Feel: Sinza is very lively and noisy at all hours, with shops, food stands, budget hotels and wholesale outlets; students, entry-level workers, large families and retirees; mixed roads, litter and congested housing [4]. Kijitonyama: university students, young families and single seniors [4]. Sinza ward had 40,546 people on 3.3 km² in 2012 and borders Kijitonyama, Manzese and Ubungo [8].
- Rent: Sinza TZS 150k–500k (≈ €51–171), some from TZS 100k (≈ €34) [4]; Kijitonyama 2-bed house TZS 300k–400k (≈ €103–137), entry level TZS 150k–250k (≈ €51–86) [4]. Same 300–400k for a Kijitonyama 2-bed in a French-language blog (Jarnias, 2026-01-28, city file). Sinza is among the cheapest areas for 1-beds (The Africanvestor, city file).
- Getting around: Sinza borders Ubungo and Manzese [8], on the Morogoro Road side; BRT Ubungo–Posta TZS 650 (undated [3]) or TZS 1,000 main line (The Rio Times 2026-11-09, city file). One passenger paid TZS 6,000 for a Posta–Ubungo boda ride priced TZS 3,000 on the app (The Citizen, Feb 2026, city file).
- Watch: on flood days water crosses Morogoro Road at Jangwani, buses stop and commuters wade; the raised bridge was 41% built in Aug 2026 (AllAfrica, 2026-10-12, city file).
- Places:
  - Buni Hub (COSTECH tech hub and coworking, Kijitonyama) — mctaba guide 2026-06-04 [12]; TanzaniaInvest 2026-03-16 (city file)
  - Sinza Health Center and Afya Care Health Center (clinics) — Wikipedia "Sinza" [8]
  - Sinza Primary School and Mugabe Secondary School (schools) — Wikipedia [8]
  - CybeBase Internet Cafe (internet café, Shekilango Road, Sinza) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mlimani City (mall with supermarket and Century Cinemax, Sam Nujoma Road) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - The Blues Bar & Restaurant (bar-restaurant, Mawasiliano Towers, Sam Nujoma Road) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mwenge Carvers' Market (craft market, closes at dusk) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (Buni Hub, wholesale trade); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin". Not Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Upanga, the city centre and Kariakoo
- Feel: the centre is home to middle-to-high-income professionals in banking, business and politics, noisy near main roads but quiet at night [4]. Kariakoo is a bustling commercial and residential area around its market [5]. Upanga lies due north of the centre [5]. The Lutheran, Anglican and Catholic cathedrals stand in a part of town their congregations no longer live in [5].
- Rent: city centre TZS 1.5M–5M (≈ €514–1,712) [4]; central 1-bed average TZS 1,397,326 (≈ €480) (Numbeo 2026-11-08, city file); 1-bed US$500–800 (Expat.com 2025-12-16, city file). Upanga and Kariakoo sit in The Africanvestor's TZS 500k–1.8M band (≈ €171–616) (city file).
- Getting around: BRT Phase I terminates at Kivukoni and Phase II runs Gerezani–Mbagala (dossier; Wikivoyage [3]); most daladalas end at Posta [3].
- Watch: the UK lists the city centre among armed-crime areas (FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, city file); the post-election curfew of Oct–Nov 2025 was centred on Dar (dossier, Wikipedia 2025 election).
- Places:
  - Kariakoo Market (main market, haggling expected, best on Saturday morning) — Wikivoyage [3]; city file
  - National Museum (5 Shaaban Robert Street, 09:30–18:00, botanical garden opposite on Samora Avenue) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - St Joseph's Cathedral (Catholic, completed 1902) and Azania Front Lutheran Church — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Upanga Club (club restaurant, Ali Hassan Mwinyi Road, non-members TZS 1,000) and Alliance Française nearby — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Diamond Jubilee Expo Centre (trade-fair venue, Malik Road, Upanga) — The Rio Times [13]
  - Gymkhana (tennis and golf club, Gymkhana Road) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - YWCA canteen (Tanzanian meal under TZS 2,000, near the cathedral) and Chef's Pride (restaurant, Chagga Street) — Wikivoyage, undated [3]
  - City Mall (mall, Bibi Titi and Morogoro roads, 09:00–23:00) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (walk to offices, banks, the BRT hub); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q3 "Moyen". Kariakoo side: Q3 "Serré".

### Kigamboni
- Feel: Dar's largest district by area (578 km²), nicknamed "the holiday district", with about 65 km of coast; mostly Muslim, largely rural and farming outside the beach strip; 317,902 people in 2022 [10]. Expat.com calls it more peaceful than the peninsula (2025-11-07 [14]).
- Rent: Expat.com says it is 30–40% cheaper than Masaki but gives no source [14]; Kigamboni is among the cheapest areas for 1- and 2-beds (The Africanvestor 2026-09-21, city file). Sale asking prices TZS 1.3M–2.3M/m² (≈ €445–788) (city file). No neighbourhood rent figure found.
- Getting around: ferry from Kivukoni, TZS 100, about 5 minutes, strict no-photo rule [3]; the Nyerere Bridge (opened April 2016, 680 m, toll plaza, pedestrian and cycle lanes) links Kurasini to Vijibweni [11]. Expat.com warns the ferry or bridge lengthens commutes [14].
- Watch: a 132 kV underground-line fault reported on 3 Nov 2026 cut power to Kigamboni and Mbagala while TANESCO built a temporary bypass (Daily News, Nov 2026, city file).
- Places:
  - Kigamboni ferry and the Ferry market (market) — Wikivoyage [3]; Wikipedia [10]
  - Masjid Annur Ferry (mosque) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Vijibweni public hospital (district hospital) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Mikadi Beach Lodge (beach, 1 km from the ferry, entry TZS 3,000) — Wikivoyage, undated [3]
  - Sunrise Hotel (beach, TZS 5,000 entry of which 3,000 is a food voucher) — Wikivoyage, undated [3]
  - South Beach Resort (beach hotel) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi". Weak for Q5 "Salarié·e" with a daily commute to the centre.

## Living: typical weeks

Arithmetic convention: weekly = monthly ÷ 4.33; EUR at 2,920 TZS; USD kept apart. Every unit price cites its source; quantities (how many meals, rides) are assumptions stated in the line.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Msasani, for the widest rent spread close to the expat services (TZS 400k–2.5M [4]), coworking day passes on the peninsula [12] and the sea.
- Week:
  - Mon: work at home on fibre (60 Mbps about TZS 89,875/month, Numbeo, city file); groceries at Shrijee's, Slipway [3]; on foot or bajaji (~US$2, 2020).
  - Tue: coworking day pass in Msasani or Masaki (TZS 10,000–30,000 [12]); lunch at a local eatery such as BBQ Village [4].
  - Wed: work from home; evening walk at the Slipway waterfront [3] (daylight; FCDO names Toure Drive and Coco Beach for crime).
  - Thu: coworking again; after work, cappuccino (TZS 5,000–8,000, Expat.com, city file) at SeaCliff Village [3].
  - Fri: Buni Hub event in Kijitonyama [12] by Bolt (no 2026 per-km fare sourced) across peak traffic: leave after 7 pm (rush 4–7 pm, city file).
  - Sat: see Saturday-night options; morning boat to Bongoyo (below).
  - Sun: slow morning, A Novel Idea bookshop at Oysterbay Shopping Centre [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": a concert night at The Super Dome, Masaki (Mbili Abel concert, Sat 7 Nov 2026; prices unpublished [13]); nightclubs exist downtown and on the peninsula but no current named club was sourced (see Gaps).
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Karembezi Cafe at SeaCliff Village (seafood, ocean view) or Addis in Dar (Ethiopian, Regency Estates, about TZS 13,000 a dish, undated) [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": cook at home from Shrijee's [3]; or the Century Cinemax at Mlimani City [3].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Bongoyo Island from the Slipway: boat TZS 25,000 return, which includes the US$10 marine-park fee, snorkel set TZS 6,000 (Wikivoyage, undated [3]); about 30 min (Expat.com, city file).
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Pugu Hills forest, about 12 km west of the airport; bandas US$80–100 for two with breakfast, camping US$10 (Wikivoyage, undated [3]).
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Mikumi National Park lies on the Dar–Mbeya road (Wikivoyage Tanzania [17]); no travel time or fee sourced, so a weekend is plausible but unpriced here.
  - "Je reste en ville": National Museum [3]; Simba or Young Africans at Benjamin Mkapa Stadium (city file).
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: TZS 400,000–2,500,000 ÷ 4.33 = TZS 92,400–577,400 (≈ €32–198) [4].
  - Coworking: 3 day passes × TZS 10,000–30,000 = 30,000–90,000 [12].
  - Home internet: 89,875 ÷ 4.33 = 20,800 (Numbeo, city file).
  - Meals: 10 cheap meals × 5,000 = 50,000; one shared mid-range dinner, half of 50,000 = 25,000 (Numbeo 2026-11-08, city file).
  - Coffee: 5 × 5,000–8,000 = 25,000–40,000; bar beer: 4 × 3,000–5,000 = 12,000–20,000 (Expat.com 2025-11-07, city file).
  - Gym: TZS 100,000–200,000/month ÷ 4.33 = 23,100–46,200 (Expat.com, city file).
  - Subtotal without rent: 185,900–292,000 (≈ €64–100). Bajaji: 6 hops × ~US$2 (2020) = US$12.
  - Total: TZS 278,300–869,400 (≈ €95–298) + US$12.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: Kijitonyama, next to Sinza: cheap rents [4], Buni Hub in the same ward [12], and Sinza's wholesale outlets and students as a test market [4].
- Week:
  - Mon: morning at Buni Hub (COSTECH; Tanzania Ventures Lab runs with DTBi, Buni and Sahara Ventures, TanzaniaInvest 2026-03-16, city file).
  - Tue: supplier run at Kariakoo by BRT from Ubungo (TZS 650–1,000) [3]; expect quotes for foreigners "sometimes three times" the local price (Wikivoyage, city file).
  - Wed: admin in the centre; mobile-money set-up (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money are widely used, Expat.com, city file); a CRDB current account needs a TZS 100,000 deposit, and NBC asks foreigners for a permit (The Rio Times 2026-09-23, city file).
  - Thu: client meetings at Mlimani City or The Blues Bar, Sam Nujoma Road [3].
  - Fri: AFRICON-type summits at JNICC, Shaaban Robert Street (9–11 Nov 2026, free registration) [13]; Friday prayers for Muslim partners (about 70% of the city is Muslim, Wikipedia, city file).
  - Sat: Sinza at night (see below).
  - Sun: rest; Coco Beach afternoon in daylight [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Sinza is noisy and lively at all hours [4]; no venue name was sourced (Gaps).
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Rohobot Ethiopian Restaurant, off Bagamoyo Road near Twiga Pub [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Kijitonyama is quieter than Sinza [4].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Kigamboni by ferry (TZS 100) and taxi (TZS 10,000–15,000) to Mikadi Beach (entry TZS 3,000) [3].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Pugu Hills [3].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Mikumi (unpriced).
  - "Je reste en ville": Bagamoyo day trip, see Visiting.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: Mbezi Beach, for larger homes, relatively quiet streets and Braeburn in neighbouring Mbezi Juu [4][9]. Alternative: Mikocheni, for IST [14] (fees far higher, see below).

- Weekend outing:

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: Mikocheni B, between the peninsula and the northern suburbs: Passionist Fathers House (TZS 35,000 single) or CEFA Hostel (TZS 58,125, sea-view terrace) (Wikivoyage, undated [3]). Budget alternative in the centre: YWCA, Ghana Street, TZS 20,000 [3].
- Before landing: e-visa (single entry US$50, Expat.com 2025-11-07; the UK still mentions visa on arrival — conflicting, city file); US$44 state inbound insurance since 1 Oct 2026 (NIC on the mainland), not accepted by most facilities, so keep private cover (France Diplomatie 2026-11-12, city file); online Afyamsafiri health declaration since 17 Oct 2026 (city file).
- Day by day:
  - Day 1: airport to Mikocheni by taxi, about TZS 30,000 and negotiable (Wikivoyage, city file); an expat forum (about 2018) says airport–Oyster Bay should not exceed TZS 50,000 (city file).
  - Day 2: SIM card at an operator shop with a passport (Expat.com says passport and residence permit; rules for a visitor without a permit not verified, see Gaps); prepaid bundle about TZS 10,000 for several GB (Expat.com 2025-11-07 [14]); set up M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa or Airtel Money (city file). ATMs with no foreign-card fee: Ecobank, GT Bank, KCB, I&M, DTB (Wikivoyage Tanzania, city file).
  - Day 3: flat hunt in Kijitonyama and Sinza (cheap) or Mikocheni; viewing fees "rarely below TZS10,000" per house; broker takes one month's rent although the government banned this in 2021 (Minority Africa 2023-08-30, city file).
  - Day 4: Mbezi Beach and Bahari Beach viewings by car [4]; check for a generator and water filtration before signing (Expat.com, city file).
  - Day 5: bank: CRDB current account with passport, proof of address, photos and TZS 100,000 deposit; NBC wants a resident or work permit (The Rio Times 2026-09-23, city file). Foreigners over 30 days must hold health cover (The Rio Times 2026-09-22, city file).
  - Day 6: Kariakoo market morning [3], then Coco Beach in daylight.
  - Day 7: Mass at St Joseph's or rest; plan week two.
- Advance customs: 3–12 months upfront plus 1–3 months' deposit (Expat.com, city file); some Masaki landlords ask six months (The Rio Times, city file). Example for a Kijitonyama 2-bed at TZS 300,000: minimum 3 months + 1 deposit + 1 broker = TZS 1.5M (≈ €514); maximum 12 + 3 + 1 = TZS 4.8M (≈ €1,644); stamp duty 1% of annual rent = TZS 36,000 (The Rio Times 2026-09-26, city file). Negotiating levers: pay quarterly, or commit 12–24 months for a lower rent (Jarnias blog 2026-01-28, low confidence, city file).
- Saturday night (week one): "Danser jusqu'au matin": Super Dome event if one is listed [13]; "Un bon dîner entre amis": Al-Basha, Mikocheni [3]; "Tranquille chez moi": CEFA terrace [3].
- Weekend outing: "À la plage": Bongoyo [3]; "En montagne, en forêt": Pugu Hills [3]; "Dans la savane ou le désert": Mikumi, unpriced; "Je reste en ville": Village Museum [3].
- Rough spend for the week:
  - Room: 7 × 35,000 = 245,000 (Passionist Fathers) or 7 × 58,125 = 406,875 (CEFA) [3].
  - Airport taxi 30,000; SIM bundle 10,000; 5 viewing fees × 10,000 = 50,000 (minimum); 14 cheap meals × 5,000 = 70,000.
  - Total: TZS 405,000–566,900 (≈ €139–194), plus US$50 visa and US$44 insurance, plus TZS 100,000 bank deposit if eligible.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read with the city file's safety items: avoid walking at night, avoid gatherings (France Diplomatie 2026-06-08), do not film protests, and expect phone checks after the 2025 election (UK FCDO, city file).

### One day
- Morning: National Museum, 5 Shaaban Robert Street (opens 09:30; Nutcracker Man skull, Laetoli footprints cast; fee not given) and the botanical garden across Samora Avenue [3]; walk past St Joseph's Cathedral and the Azania Front Lutheran Church [3].
- Midday: lunch at the YWCA canteen (under TZS 2,000) or Chef's Pride, Chagga Street [3]; then Kariakoo Market by taxi or on foot (pickpocket caution, haggle) [3].
- Afternoon: taxi to Mikocheni (centre–peninsula fares TZS 7,000–10,000 as a guide [3]) for the Makumbusho Village Museum (09:00–18:00; ngoma dances 14:00–18:00 on some days) [3][7].
- Evening: The Slipway at sunset and dinner at SeaCliff Village (Karembezi Cafe, seafood) [3]; taxi or Bolt back after dark (The Rio Times advice [13]).

### One week
- Day 1: centre as above.
- Day 2 ("what daily life looks like"): a weekday rush-hour BRT ride from Kimara or Ubungo to Kivukoni (TZS 1,000, 7–9 am peak, city file), then Kariakoo at its busiest; the BRT crosses the flood-prone Jangwani stretch of Morogoro Road on the way, where a raised bridge is being built (AllAfrica 2026-10-12, city file).
- Day 3: Bongoyo Island from the Slipway (boat TZS 25,000 return, park fee included, boats from 09:30) [3].
- Day 4 (trip out): Bagamoyo, about 70 km north: daladala Makumbusho → Bunju (TZS 1,100) → Bagamoyo (TZS 1,500), 2.5–3 h (fares as of Aug 2025); Kaole ruins TZS 10,000, Slavery & Missionary Museum TZS 10,000 for foreigners, old German boma free to view [15].
- Day 5: Kigamboni: ferry from Kivukoni (TZS 100, 5 min, no photos), Ferry market and Masjid Annur [3][10], afternoon at Mikadi Beach (TZS 3,000) [3]; return over the Nyerere Bridge to see the southern side [11].
- Day 6 (Saturday): Kariakoo morning [3]; Simba or Young Africans match at Benjamin Mkapa Stadium if one is scheduled (city file; no fixture or ticket price sourced); evening Super Dome event if listed [13].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Sunday Mass at St Joseph's Cathedral [3]; afternoon drive through Mikocheni and Mbezi Beach to see where families live [4]; sunset at Bahari Beach Hotel [3].
- Optional (trip out): Zanzibar by Azam fast ferry, about 1 h 45, US$35–50 for non-residents (Expat.com Zanzibar, Zanzibar city file); avoid overloaded boats (UK FCDO, Zanzibar city file).
