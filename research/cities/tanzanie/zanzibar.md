# Zanzibar, Tanzania

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Scope: Zanzibar City / Stone Town on Unguja, plus the island areas residents commute from (Fumba, Mbweni, Paje, Nungwi). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 2,920 TZS** (Numbeo, 2026-11-08, dossier [S8]). USD figures stay in USD, as in the dossier. - [housing.areas] Stone Town for culture and the ferry; Nungwi and Kendwa for beach and nightlife; Paje, Jambiani and Bwejuu for a quieter life popular with remote workers and kitesurfers; Fumba and Mbweni (near the airport) for newer, family-oriented developments. — Expat.com Zanzibar guide, updated 2025-12-09, blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Zanzibar City has two halves: historic Stone Town (UNESCO, 2000) and the larger post-revolution Ng'ambo with the Michenzani apartment blocks; 219,007 people live on 15.4 km². — Wikipedia "Zanzibar City", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] On 9 Oct 2026 the minister halted hotel works in Shangani (Stone Town) after residents said lanes were blocked and some homes damaged or demolished without consultation. — The Rio Times, 2026-10-11, intl-media, confidence: high

- [housing.search] Furnishing adds about TZS 400k–1.2M/month (≈ €137–411) and backup power TZS 250k–600k (≈ €86–205) to a rent; ordinary rentals leave electricity, internet, gas and water to the tenant. — The Africanvestor, updated 2026-09-21, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Many expats live in compounds — groups of buildings in an enclosure, usually owned by one family; availability and prices shift with tourist seasons. — Travelling King blog, updated 2024-03, blog, confidence: low

- [business.startup] "Silicon Zanzibar" launched in Aug 2022 with a promised fast-track work visa; Wasoko moved 40+ tech staff to Fumba, then closed its Zanzibar office around its MaxAB merger; IIT Madras opened a campus in 2023. — Wikipedia "Silicon Zanzibar", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium

- [daily.power] Unguja receives 135.7 MW against demand of 165 MW; it relies on two undersea cables (one from 1980); load-shedding hit homes and businesses for three months; the government said it cannot compensate losses. — The Citizen, 2026-02-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Emergency fixes announced in Feb 2026: 30 generators (+20 MW) and a power ship off Mtoni (+35 MW). — The Citizen, 2026-02-23, local-media, confidence: high

- [daily.water] Power and water interruptions are described as the persistent challenge; most expats budget for generators and water storage. — The Africanvestor "Zanzibar expat guide", 2026, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] ZanBus electric buses (R1, R2 to the airport) run 5 am–9 pm at a flat TZS 700 (≈ €0.24) with a travel card; dala dalas leave from Darajani Market. — Wikivoyage "Zanzibar City", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Bolt works but waits can reach 15–20 minutes; Uber does not operate on the island; bajaji and boda bodas outnumber cars. — Wikivoyage "Zanzibar City", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Dala dala about TZS 2,000 a ride (≈ €0.68), boda about TZS 1,000 per 5 minutes; the Azam fast ferry to Dar takes about 1 h 45 and costs US$35–50 for non-residents. — Expat.com Zanzibar guide, updated 2025-12-09, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Ferry disasters on Dar–Zanzibar and inter-island routes have killed hundreds; the UK advises not boarding overloaded boats and watching for aggressive ticket touts. — UK FCDO travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.money] Resident foreigners pay NIDA ID fees via a control number at banks including the People's Bank of Zanzibar; CRDB accepts a Zanzibar ID card for account opening. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-23, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Mnazi Mmoja (Stone Town) is the public referral hospital; MedExpress in Paje offers emergency care, surgery and ICU; serious cases are evacuated to Dar or Nairobi. — Expat.com Zanzibar guide, updated 2025-12-09, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Private consultations cost about TZS 40k–120k (≈ €14–41), specialists TZS 120k–300k (≈ €41–103); Tawakal Hospital is named for private care. — The Africanvestor "Zanzibar expat guide", 2026, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] Zanzibar sits outside the mainland's Universal Health Insurance Act and runs its own visitor policy, from which island residents are excluded. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-22, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] International School of Zanzibar (Mazizini, Cambridge curriculum, ages 3–18): about US$2,925/year for early years to US$16,410 for Years 12–13; the British School of Zanzibar is said to be similar; waitlists mean applying 3–6 months ahead. — The Africanvestor "Zanzibar expat guide", 2026, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] ISZ also charges a US$500 registration fee and a yearly capital fund of US$600 (private) or US$1,500 (corporate); public schools teach mainly in Swahili. — Expat.com Zanzibar guide, updated 2025-12-09, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Expat.com's country guide says there are few decent private schools and families often weigh distance learning or mainland boarding. — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: medium

- [admin.residency] Buying a ZIPA-endorsed property worth US$100,000+ qualifies for a 2-year Class C11 permit at US$550 (US$300 for East Africans), covering spouse and up to four children; it does not allow work. - [admin.residency] Retirees use Class C-2: The Rio Times says applicants must be 55+, fee US$500 (US$250 for EAC citizens and diaspora), 2 years renewable, no permanent-residence route. — The Rio Times, updated 2026-11-08, intl-media, confidence: medium

- [admin.visa] France says Zanzibar's US$44 ZIC visitor insurance is mandatory from 1 Oct 2026 (valid 90 days); The Rio Times says the Zanzibar requirement dates from 1 Oct 2024. Residents are exempt. — France Diplomatie 2026-11-12; The Rio Times 2026-11-08, official, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Highs of 29–33°C all year; April is the wettest month (about 409 mm), July–September the driest. — Wikivoyage "Zanzibar City" chart, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Snorkelling at Mnemba Atoll, red colobus monkeys in Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park, tortoises on Prison Island. — Expat.com Zanzibar guide, updated 2025-12-09, blog, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Entry to Changuu (Prison) Island rose from US$3 to US$10 in Sep 2025. — Wikivoyage "Zanzibar City", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low

- [other] VAT is 15% in Zanzibar (18% on banking, telecom, insurance and digital services) versus 18% on the mainland. — The Rio Times "Taxes in Tanzania for expats", 2026, intl-media, confidence: medium
