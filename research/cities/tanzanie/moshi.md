## Items

- [housing.areas] Moshi counted 221,733 residents in 2022 on about 59 km², the smallest municipality by area, at 700–950 m on the lower slopes of Kilimanjaro. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Shantytown (north, up Kibo Road) is described as relatively wealthy with restaurants and hotels; Soweto (west) has pricier homes; Majengo (east) is described as much poorer. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Most restaurant meals cost TZS 2,000–8,000 (≈ €0.68–2.7), per a travel guide with listings partly from 2018 or earlier. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A Land Cruiser with driver costs about TZS 100,000/day (≈ €34) and a motorbike about TZS 35,000/day (≈ €12). Undated guide prices. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] As regional capital Moshi is a centre for government, trade and tourism; coffee is a major crop, grown through a co-operative founded in 1929; a brewery, a Coca-Cola bottler and sugar and dairy plants operate nearby. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [work.remote] Kili Hub is a coworking space with fast internet and backup power for "a couple of dollars" a day; all internet cafés share the same backhaul, so outages hit them together. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Kilimanjaro International Airport is about 40 km away; Moshi Airport (5 km) handles charters and private flights. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Buses to Dar es Salaam take about 8 hours for TZS 20,000 (≈ €6.8); local dala dalas cost TZS 500–3,000 (≈ €0.17–1.03); a taxi to the airport about TZS 60,000 (≈ €21), negotiable. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [daily.transport] Arusha is about 1 hour by bus for TZS 2,000 (≈ €0.68). — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Kilimanjaro Christian Medical Centre (KCMC) is a zonal referral hospital with 450+ beds; Mawenzi Regional Referral Hospital (about 300 beds) is described as severely underfunded (older information). — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] KCMC handles many mountain and road-accident cases; mainland health-insurance rules apply. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-22, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A KCMC doctor visit reportedly costs around TZS 50,000 (≈ €17); St Joseph's Hospital in Soweto, run by nuns, charges only for tests and medicines. Undated guide claims. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] UWC East Africa (boarding) costs about US$48,000/year including board, with need-based scholarships. — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Higher education includes KCMC College, Mwenge Catholic University, Moshi Co-operative University and the College of African Wildlife Management (Mweka); English-medium private schools exist. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK advisory has no Moshi-specific warning; national road-safety warnings (frequent fatal accidents involving boda bodas and minibuses) apply. — UK FCDO travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] A nightclub listing gives TZS 5,000 entry (≈ €1.7) and beer at TZS 2,500 (≈ €0.86); local beers are Kilimanjaro, Safari and Tusker, and the common spirit is Konyagi. Undated. — Wikivoyage "Moshi", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.sport] The Kilimanjaro Marathon (since 2003, a Comrades qualifier) runs 7 Feb 2027 from MoCU Stadium; residents and EAC work-permit holders pay US$55 for the marathon or half against US$80 for international runners. — The Rio Times, 2026, intl-media, confidence: high
- [social.sport] The city has two golf courses, a hockey pitch and Ushirika Stadium. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Moshi has long been an opposition stronghold, with strong Chadema support in 2010 and 2012. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Nights average 15–17°C all year; days are often above 30°C from October to March and 25–26°C from May to August; March–May brings about 71% of the year's rain. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Kilimanjaro National Park, Lake Chala and Lake Jipe are nearby. — Wikipedia "Moshi, Tanzania", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
