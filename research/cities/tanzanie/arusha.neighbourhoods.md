# Arusha: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: ~2,920 TZS = 1 EUR (dossier, implied by Numbeo 2026-11-08). USD figures stay in USD. Source numbers in brackets refer to "Sources read"; "city file" = `research/cities/tanzanie/arusha.md`, cited with the original source and date it logs. Weekly figures divide a monthly price by 4.33.

Research-pass note: the session's shared WebSearch budget was exhausted before this city was researched, so new material comes only from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Expat.com, The Rio Times). Many Wikivoyage Arusha listings date from 2015–2019; they prove a place existed and where, and their prices are flagged. Wikivoyage is not used alone for safety; its warnings about Old Moshi Road and Sokoine Road are therefore left out (as the city file also did).

City-wide context (city file unless stated):
- Altitude about 1,400 m: highs 27–28°C in Jan–Mar, 20–21°C in Jun–Jul, night lows about 8°C in Jun–Sep (Wikipedia, Wikivoyage). Short rains forecast from October 2026 into January 2027, heavier in November (The Rio Times 2026-10-09).
- Transport: Uber left Tanzania on 30 Jan 2026; Bolt remains (The Chanzo 2026-02-01). Average city ride TZS 5k–15k (≈ €1.7–5.1) (Expat.com 2025-11-07). Taxis have no meters, town trips TZS 4,000–16,000; dala dala fares are government-set and posted on the vehicle (Wikivoyage). Expat.com does not recommend daladalas for expats [5].
- Safety: citing the US advisory (31 Oct 2025), The Rio Times reports home invasions in Arusha and pickpocketing in tourist areas, and advises a hotel or operator driver after dark and not walking alone at night (The Rio Times 2026-10-09 [7]). The UK has no Arusha-specific warning; its national ones apply (FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14).
- Water: a travel guide advises against drinking tap water, citing high fluoride (Wikivoyage, low confidence). Power: the 27 Jun 2026 national blackout hit Arusha (The Chanzo 2026-06-28).
- Leases: many homes are let directly by owners; brokers (dalali) generally take one month's rent (Jarnias blog 2026-01-28, low confidence). No 2025–2026 rent survey exists for Njiro, Sakina or the centre (The Rio Times 2026-11-09).

## Neighbourhoods

### City centre (clock tower, Sokoine Road, Old Boma Road)
- Feel: the central business area around the clock tower [4], with markets, cafés and the downtown hospital; the city grew from 416,442 (2012) to 617,631 (2022) people [4]. A new master plan bars buildings under six storeys in the centre, and the state housing company is replacing old buildings on Masai, Swahili, Azimio and Sokoine streets with a 350-unit "Sokoine Square" (The Rio Times 2026-11-02, city file).
- Rent: 1-bed in the centre about TZS 750k/month (≈ €257), 6 contributors (Numbeo 2026-11-01, city file); The Rio Times converts it to about US$284 (city file); a cost estimator gives US$228 (livingcost.org 2026-06-21, model, city file).
- Getting around: walkable core; taxi or Bolt elsewhere (TZS 4,000–16,000 taxi, TZS 5k–15k Bolt, city file). Buses: Moshi about 1 h for TZS 2,000, Dar es Salaam 11 h+ for about TZS 33,000, Nairobi about 6 h for TZS 22,000–25,000 [3]. Kilimanjaro International Airport is about 60 km east, taxi about US$50 [3][4].
- Watch: home invasions reported in Arusha and pickpocketing in tourist areas (The Rio Times citing the US State Department, 2026-10-09 [7]). The 43 former tenants of the Sokoine Square blocks have already left on notice; new rents are not set (The Rio Times 2026-11-02, city file).
- Places:
  - Arusha Central Market (fruit and vegetables, bargaining expected) — Wikivoyage [3]; The Rio Times [7]
  - Maasai Market (crafts; prices "not inflated" but negotiable) — Wikivoyage [3]; The Rio Times [7]
  - Shoppers (supermarket, Sokoine Road, 8 am–10 pm) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Africafe Coffee House (café, Old Boma Road near the clock tower, 8 am–8 pm, cakes from TZS 5,000) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Via Via Cultural Cafe (café-bar, Old Boma Road, meals from TZS 6,000, concert and party on Thursday nights) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Arusha Lutheran Medical Centre (private faith-based hospital, downtown) — Wikipedia [4]; The Rio Times 2026-09-22 (city file)
  - Arusha Declaration Museum and National Natural History Museum (museums) — Wikivoyage [3]; Wikipedia [4]
  - Sheikh Amri Abeid Memorial Stadium (home of Arusha FC) and the Catholic Cathedral of Arusha — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (markets, tour trade, bus links); Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q3 "Moyen". Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" only if you avoid the main roads.

### Njiro (with the PPF estate)
- Feel: the PPF gated community sits in Njiro near the Village supermarket [4]; Wikivoyage describes a shopping and restaurant complex with a cinema and supermarket, and places nyama choma spots in Njiro [3]. Expat.com groups Njiro with Kaloleni and Themi Hill as residential areas where expats in tourism and NGO work often live [5].
- Rent: 2-bed TZS 0.8M–2M/month (≈ €274–685), 3–4 bed villas TZS 1.5M–3.5M (≈ €514–1,199) (Expat.com 2025-11-07 [5]); 2-bed house with garden in Sombetini or Njiro US$300–500 (Jarnias 2026-01-28, low confidence, city file).
- Getting around: taxi from Njiro's cinema to town "shouldn't cost more than TZS 4,000" (Wikivoyage, undated, city file); Bolt TZS 5k–15k (city file). No travel time sourced.
- Places:
  - Village Supermarket (supermarket, Njiro complex, 8:30 am–9 pm) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Century Cinema (cinema, Njiro Road, TZS 5,000–7,000) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Spice Retreat (restaurant, 1st floor, Njiro Complex, noon–midnight, TZS 5,000–15,000) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Khan's Chicken on the Bonnet (grill, branch at the Njiro cinema, TZS 4,500–9,000 per person) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Liquid Blue and Nick Bar (Indian food and nyama choma, Njiro) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - PPF (gated estate) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e" (supermarket, cinema and restaurants in one complex); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (gated estate); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Inner residential ring (Kijenge, Themi Hill, Kaloleni, Sekei, Sakina)
- Feel: Expat.com names Kaloleni and Themi Hill as residential areas for expats in tourism and NGO work [5]. Westlands, a gated community in Sakina, is known for its view of Mount Meru [4]. Little else is described in the opened sources (Gaps).
- Rent: 2-bed TZS 0.8M–2M (≈ €274–685) for Kaloleni and Themi Hill (Expat.com [5]); 1-bed outside the centre about TZS 625k (≈ €214), 6 contributors (Numbeo, city file).
- Getting around: taxi TZS 4,000–16,000 or Bolt TZS 5k–15k (city file). No distances sourced.
- Places:
  - BaseCamp Tanzania (lodge, Kijenge) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - L'Oasis Lodge (lodge and restaurant, Sekei, 7 am–late) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Westlands (gated estate, Sakina) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Kwa Mawala (gated estate on "a quiet side of Majengo") — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple" (short rides to the centre's institutions); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q9 "Doux" (applies city-wide). Thin data: three to four sourced places only.

### Usa River and Tengeru (east, towards Kilimanjaro)
- Feel: Dolly Estate at Usa River is a gated community focused on country living, popular with expats (Wikipedia [4]; dossier). The Rio Times calls Usa River a village outside Arusha town [6]. Tengeru hosts the Tengeru Institute of Community Development [4] and lies by Lake Duluti [9].
- Rent: no figure found for Usa River or Tengeru (Gaps).
- Getting around: Lake Duluti is about 14 km from the centre, 1.3 km off the Arusha–Moshi road [9]; Arusha National Park's main Ngongongare Gate is on the Arusha–Moshi road, about 25 km from the city (45 min–1 h) [8]. The Rio Times advises arranging a driver back from Usa River at night [6].
- Places:
  - Dolly Estate (gated estate, Usa River) — Wikipedia [4]
  - River Trees Country Inn (country inn, venue of the "Rooted" evening on 28 Nov 2026, TZS 130,000) — The Rio Times [6]
  - Dunia Salama Foundation (volunteering organisation, Usa River) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Lake Duluti and the Duluti Forest Reserve (crater lake, canoes for hire at a restaurant on the north shore; 19 ha forest) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Tengeru Institute of Community Development (college) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" and "Dans la savane ou le désert" (park gates on the doorstep); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" with a car. Weak for Q5 "Salarié·e" in town without a car.

## Living: typical weeks

Arithmetic convention: weekly = monthly ÷ 4.33; EUR at 2,920 TZS; USD kept apart. Numbeo's Arusha figures rest on 6 contributors (low confidence); livingcost.org figures are model estimates.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Njiro, where the supermarket, cinema and restaurants sit in one complex [3] and expats in tourism and NGO work live [5].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; internet about TZS 125k/month (Numbeo, city file); backup for blackouts (27 Jun 2026 national outage, city file). No coworking space was found (Gaps).
  - Tue: groceries at Village Supermarket [3]; lunch at Spice Retreat [3].
  - Wed: Bolt to town (TZS 5k–15k, city file) for a work session at Africafe [3].
  - Thu: Via Via's Thursday concert and party, Old Boma Road [3].
  - Fri: Khan's Chicken on the Bonnet at the Njiro cinema [3], then a film at Century Cinema [3].
  - Sat: Central Market morning [3]; evening below.
  - Sun: Lake Duluti walk and canoe [9].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": the Dance Battle Festival (Elimu Yetu, central Arusha, Sat 28 Nov 2026, TZS 10,000) [6]; Wikivoyage lists clubs (Velocity, Colobus Club, Maasai Camp, Triple A, 777, Polygon Triangle) that "start late and close very late", undated listing, check they still operate [3].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Bay Leaf, 102 Vijana Road (meals from TZS 20,000) [3]; or Spice Retreat, Njiro [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Century Cinema as the low-key outing [3].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Arusha (inland city; a Dar flight costs TZS 80k–150k, Expat.com, city file, but no coast weekend was priced).
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Arusha National Park (US$45 per day for foreigners, walking safari with armed ranger, canoeing on the Momella lakes) [8]; Lake Duluti [9].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Ngorongoro: residents with valid status pay US$35.40 a day, non-East African visitors US$70.80, crater US$295 per vehicle (The Rio Times 2026-11-09, city file).
  - "Je reste en ville": Maasai Market and the museums [3].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: 2-bed TZS 0.8M–2M ÷ 4.33 = 184,800–461,900 (≈ €63–158) [5].
  - Internet 125,000 ÷ 4.33 = 28,900; utilities 75,000 ÷ 4.33 = 17,300 (Numbeo, city file).
  - Meals: 10 cheap meals × 8,850 = 88,500; half a dinner for two = 35,400 (Numbeo, city file).
  - Cinema 5,000–7,000 and Khan's 4,500–9,000 [3]; 2 Africafe cakes × 5,000 = 10,000 [3].
  - Bolt: 4 rides × 5,000–15,000 = 20,000–60,000 (Expat.com, city file).
  - Subtotal without rent: 209,600–256,100 (≈ €72–88).
  - Total: TZS 394,400–718,000 (≈ €135–246).

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: the city centre, near the markets, the bus links to Moshi, Nairobi and Dar [3], and the safari trade (Arusha is the gateway to the northern circuit, dossier). Horticulture is a major employer exporting flowers to Europe (Wikipedia, city file).
- Week:
  - Mon: suppliers at the Central Market and Maasai Market (bargaining is part of buying) [3].
  - Tue: Moshi by bus (1 h, TZS 2,000) [3] for the Moshi market and Union Cafe, the coffee cooperative's café [10].
  - Wed: bank: DTB's 24-hour ATM on Old Moshi Road charges no fee on foreign cards (Wikivoyage, listing 2023-07, city file); mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Expat.com, city file).
  - Thu: trade-fair circuit: HortiLogistica Africa at the TGT Grounds, Burka Estate (8–10 Nov 2026; US$20 a day for foreign visitors, online only) [6] — annual, check next edition.
  - Fri: meetings at Africafe [3].
  - Sat: evening below.
  - Sun: rest.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": Dance Battle Festival [6]; "Un bon dîner entre amis": Kiti Moto, Makao Mapya (pork, TZS 10,000–20,000 for two, open to about 9–10 pm) [3]; "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage": not realistic from Arusha; "En montagne, en forêt": Lake Duluti [9]; "Dans la savane ou le désert": Arusha National Park [8] or Ngorongoro (city file); "Je reste en ville": Arusha Cultural Heritage Centre, a large private gallery [3][4].

  - Day 1: Kilimanjaro airport (about 60 km) by taxi about US$50 or airline shuttle free to about US$10 [3] (city file).
  - Day 2: SIM at an operator shop (passport; Expat.com also mentions a residence permit), prepaid about TZS 10,000 for several GB [5]; mobile money; withdraw at the no-fee DTB ATM, Old Moshi Road (city file).

  - Day 4: viewings in the outer areas (Sombetini, Usa River) by taxi.
  - Day 5: bank: CRDB needs passport, proof of address, photos and TZS 100,000 deposit; NBC asks for a permit (The Rio Times 2026-09-23, Dar city file).
  - Day 6: Central Market and Maasai Market [3].
  - Day 7: Sunday service; Lake Duluti [9].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": Dance Battle Festival if it is on [6]; "Un bon dîner entre amis": Via Via or Bay Leaf [3]; "Tranquille chez moi": guest house.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage": not realistic from Arusha; "En montagne, en forêt": Lake Duluti [9]; "Dans la savane ou le désert": Arusha National Park day [8]; "Je reste en ville": museums [3].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Use a hotel or operator driver after dark (The Rio Times citing the US advisory, 2026-10-09 [7]); carry shillings for markets and taxis [7].

### One day
- Morning: Central Market (produce) and the Maasai Market (crafts, bargain) on foot from the clock tower [3][7].
- Midday: Africafe, Old Boma Road (cakes from TZS 5,000) or Via Via (meals from TZS 6,000) [3]; Arusha Declaration Museum by the Uhuru monument and the National Natural History Museum (fees not sourced) [3][4].
- Afternoon: taxi to Lake Duluti (14 km, about 1.3 km off the Arusha–Moshi road) for a canoe from the north-shore restaurant [9] (canoe price not sourced).
- Evening: back to Njiro: Khan's Chicken on the Bonnet (TZS 4,500–9,000) and a film at Century Cinema (TZS 5,000–7,000), taxi about TZS 4,000 [3].

### One week
- Day 1: centre as above.
- Day 2 (trip out): Arusha National Park: Ngongongare Gate, 45 min–1 h; walking safari with an armed ranger, Ngurdoto crater viewpoints, canoeing on the Momella lakes when water allows; US$45 per day for foreigners (undated) [8].
- Day 3 ("what daily life looks like"): Central Market at opening, then a dala dala ride on a posted-fare route (city file) and a weekday lunch at Big Bite, Swahili Road [3]; afternoon drive through Njiro's PPF estate and Themi Hill to see where expats and families live [4][5].
- Day 4 (trip out): Ngorongoro Crater: US$70.80 per day for non-East African visitors plus US$295 per vehicle for the crater (The Rio Times 2026-11-09, city file); book through a licensed operator and ask whether the fee is included [7]. Travel time to the crater was not sourced; a bus to Karatu, on that road, takes 3 h for TZS 5,000 [3].
- Day 5 (trip out): Moshi by bus (1 h, TZS 2,000) [3]: Moshi market, Union Cafe's Kahawa Shamba coffee tour, Marangu waterfalls [10] (no prices sourced).
- Day 6 (Saturday): a Tanzania Tourist Board cultural village visit (TZS 15,000–50,000; villages listed include Ng'iresi and Tengeru) [3]; evening Dance Battle Festival or a Usa River dinner if listed [6].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathedral of Arusha [4]; Arusha FC at Sheikh Amri Abeid stadium if at home [4]; Maasai Market for gifts [3].
