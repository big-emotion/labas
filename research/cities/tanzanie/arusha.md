- [housing.areas] Njiro has a shopping and restaurant complex with a cinema and a supermarket open 8:30 am–9 pm (listing updated Jan 2023). — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] The state housing company NHC plans "Sokoine Square", replacing old buildings on Masai, Swahili, Azimio and Sokoine streets with 350 shops and flats; its 43 former tenants have already left on 30 + 90 days' notice, and new rents are not set. — The Rio Times, 2026-11-02, intl-media, confidence: high
- [housing.search] Arusha's new master plan bars buildings under six storeys in the centre; Sokoine Square is valued at Sh64.5bn (about US$24.4M) and should take 18 months once started. — The Rio Times, 2026-11-02, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Cheap meal about TZS 8,850 (≈ €3); mid-range dinner for two about TZS 70,800 (≈ €24); basic utilities about TZS 75k/month (≈ €26). — Numbeo Arusha, updated 2026-11-01 (as logged in the dossier), company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The Rio Times estimates a modest single life at about US$700–750/month including rent, but says this is a proxy built from Dar's non-rent costs. — The Rio Times, updated 2026-11-08, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Street food from about US$0.40; a local grilled-chicken place charges TZS 4,500–9,000 per person (≈ €1.5–3.1); high-end mains up to about US$15. Prices mostly from 2015–2019 listings. — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Market vendors may not take cards, so carry shillings. — The Rio Times, 2026-10-09, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Arusha hosts the East African Community headquarters, the African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights and a branch of the UN residual mechanism for criminal tribunals; foreign tribunal staff pushed up local costs. — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Horticulture is a major employer, with several firms exporting flowers to Europe; the HortiLogistica Africa trade fair ran 8–10 Nov 2026 (day pass TZS 20,000 ≈ €6.8 for citizens, US$20 for others). — Wikipedia "Arusha"; The Rio Times 2026-11-06, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The 153rd Inter-Parliamentary Union Assembly met at the Arusha International Conference Centre on 5–9 Oct 2026, the first time Tanzania hosted it. — The Rio Times, 2026-10-09, intl-media, confidence: high
- [work.remote] Home internet about TZS 125k/month (≈ €43). — Numbeo Arusha, updated 2026-11-01 (as logged in the dossier), company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] The 27 Jun 2026 grid failure blacked out every region on the national grid, Arusha included; no Arusha-specific outage data found. — The Chanzo, 2026-06-28, local-media, confidence: medium

- [daily.transport] Kilimanjaro International Airport is about 60 km east; Arusha Airport in the west of the city is a regional hub with 87,000+ passengers a year. — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Taxi to Kilimanjaro airport about US$50; airline shuttles free or about US$10. — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Uber and Bolt both work in Arusha; an average city ride costs TZS 5k–15k (≈ €1.7–5.1). — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Taxis have no meters; town trips TZS 4,000–16,000 (≈ €1.4–5.5); dala dala fares are government-set and posted on the vehicle. — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Buses: Nairobi about 6 h for TZS 22,000–25,000 (≈ €7.5–8.6); Dar es Salaam 11 h+ for about TZS 33,000 (≈ €11.3); Moshi about 1 h for TZS 2,000 (≈ €0.68). — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Domestic flights Dar–Arusha cost TZS 80k–150k (≈ €27–51) on Precision Air or Air Tanzania. — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Arusha Lutheran Medical Centre (private, faith-based) and Mount Meru Regional Referral Hospital (public); Aga Khan runs an outpatient medical centre that refers complex cases to Dar. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-22, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] KCMC in Moshi, about 80 km away, is the zonal referral hospital and handles many mountain and road-accident cases. — The Rio Times, 2026-09-22, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Arusha International School (IB and British) costs about US$10,000–15,000/year and Braeburn Arusha US$11,000–16,000; UWC East Africa (Moshi) about US$48,000 with boarding. — Expat.com Tanzania guide, updated 2025-11-07, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Five international schools operate in and around the city: International School Moshi (Arusha campus), Arusha Meru International, Braeburn, St Constantine's and Kennedy House. — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Citing the US advisory, The Rio Times reports home invasions in Arusha and pickpocketing in tourist areas, and advises using a hotel or operator driver after dark. — The Rio Times, 2026-10-09, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK advisory gives no Arusha-specific crime warning; its national warnings (armed robbery, bag snatching, road danger) apply. — UK FCDO travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] No Arusha-specific source; nationally the UK reports verbal harassment of women alone and sexual assaults on foreigners. — UK FCDO travel advice, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Late-November 2026 listings: "Rooted", a wine, food and music evening at Usa River (TZS 130,000 ≈ €45), and a dance-battle festival in central Arusha (TZS 10,000 regular ≈ €3.4). — The Rio Times, 2026-11-06, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [social.food] A travel guide says Indian food is the most common restaurant food and often considered the best in town; nyama choma spots are in Njiro. — Wikivoyage "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] Catholic, Anglican, Lutheran, Baptist and Assemblies of God churches, mosques, and Sikh and Hindu communities; the population mixes African, Arab-Tanzanian and Indian-Tanzanian residents with a small Western minority. — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Cultural sites include the National Natural History Museum and the Arusha Cultural Heritage Centre; the Maasai Market sells local crafts and the central market fruit and vegetables. — Wikipedia "Arusha"; The Rio Times 2026-10-09, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Arusha FC plays at Sheikh Amri Abeid Memorial Stadium and the city hosts the national rugby team; a first Arusha tourism marathon was held in June 2026. — Wikipedia "Arusha"; The Rio Times 2026-10-09, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] The international community centres on staff of the EAC, the African Court and the UN mechanism; the city counted 617,631 residents in 2022 (416,442 in 2012). — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [admin.residency] Same national system as Dar: Class B employee permit US$2,050 (US$550 for EAC citizens); Class A US$1,000 for diaspora investors. — The Rio Times, updated 2026-11-08, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] At about 1,400 m the city is cooler than the coast: highs around 27–28°C in Jan–Mar and 20–21°C in Jun–Jul; about 1,180 mm of rain a year; record low 7°C. — Wikipedia "Arusha", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] April is the wettest month (about 360 mm); night lows fall to about 8°C in June–September. — Wikivoyage "Arusha" chart, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Short rains were forecast from early October 2026 into January 2027, heavier in November, possibly shaped by a strong El Niño; a warm layer is advised for evenings. — The Rio Times, 2026-10-09, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Arusha National Park (Mount Meru, Momella lakes) is a day trip; the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Kilimanjaro and Lake Manyara are within about two hours, per Expat.com. — The Rio Times 2026-10-09; Expat.com Tanzania guide 2025-11-07, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Residents with valid immigration status pay half the Ngorongoro daily fee: US$35.40 against US$70.80 for non-East African visitors; the crater costs US$295 per vehicle per trip. — The Rio Times, 2026-11-09, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] National law applies: same-sex acts between men carry 30 years to life; public displays of affection can lead to arrest. — Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in Tanzania" and UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
