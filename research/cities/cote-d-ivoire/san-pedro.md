# San-Pédro, Côte d'Ivoire

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**, rounded. Where a source gives USD only, the USD figure is kept as stated.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Relocation-blog grid (monthly): studio or 1-room 50,000–120,000 XOF (≈ €76–183); 1-bed (F2) 80,000–200,000 (≈ €122–305); 2-bed (F3) 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534); simple villa 250,000–600,000 (≈ €381–915); villa with garden 400,000–900,000 (≈ €610–1,372). — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Another page from the same blog: a "decent" flat costs 80,000–200,000 XOF in San-Pédro versus 350,000–700,000 in Cocody, and overall living costs run 40–50% below Abidjan. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Company housing from agro-industrial firms is often better than what the open market offers, and port and agro jobs often include housing. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A third page from the same site gives no figures and says the expat rental market is underdeveloped with limited supply. — OpenShores "Grandes villes : top 5", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Local markets are abundant and cheap, taxis are cheap, and local restaurants cost a fraction of Abidjan prices; imported goods (wine, cheese) are very limited, so residents stock up in Abidjan or order delivery. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Tabaski 2026: imported sheep sold for over 150,000 XOF (≈ €229) each, so several families shared one ox at 600,000 XOF (≈ €915) split seven ways. — AIP, 2026-05-27, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Fuel ceilings are national: super 905 XOF/litre (≈ €1.38), diesel 725 since 1 Aug 2026; butane 12.5 kg ceiling 5,200 XOF per the Oct 2026 DGH grid (national). — I am Beezy "Prix carburant gaz butane octobre", 2026-10-15, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] In May 2026 the hydrocarbons directorate seized 1,200 gas bottles and about ten decanting machines in San-Pédro, part of a crackdown on diverted subsidised butane, some of it used to fuel shared taxis. — AIP, 2026-05-16, official, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] An unsourced salary page says Abidjan pay runs 20–40% above Bouaké, San-Pédro and Yamoussoukro. — I am Beezy "Salaire moyen par métier", 2026-02-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] National minimum wage 75,000 XOF/month (≈ €114), unchanged since Jan 2023 (national). — I am Beezy "SMIG Côte d'Ivoire 2026", 2026-02-09, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Not found for San-Pédro specifically (see Gaps); the only related signal is that shared taxis within town range 500–1,500 XOF per ride per the blog, i.e. no fixed fare. — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Neighbourhoods: Balmer is the most residential; Centre-ville the commercial core; Bardot the industrial port zone; Cité des Cadres company housing; Plage de la Baleine and Bois Joly beachfront with some upscale homes. — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Port staff housing "Cité Hilaire Lamizana" (nearly 300 units, 3-room flats to duplexes) is under construction on the San-Pédro–Sassandra road near the Laurent Pokou stadium. — AllAfrica, 2026-03-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Stable, well-paid jobs are in the port, agro-industry (cocoa, palm oil) and industrial fishing. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] The port's 2024–2026 plan was over 65% complete by Feb 2026; a 4 km, 4-lane access road (4 bn XOF, 6 months, by CHEC) serves industrial units that make up about 60% of port activity, plus a planned cement and nickel terminal. — AllAfrica, 2026-03-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.remote] The blog rates it a poor fit for remote workers who need a stable connection. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Power cuts are described as more frequent than in Abidjan; a generator or inverter is often essential. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Electrification in San-Pédro region rose from 35% (2019) to 43% (2023), per the regional review of the 2021–2025 development plan. — AIP, 2025-05-06, official, confidence: high
- [daily.internet] 4G from Orange CI and MTN CI is decent in town but can be unreliable depending on the area; fibre is rare. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Tap water is described as not drinkable. — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Moto-taxi 200–500 XOF per ride (≈ €0.30–0.76); shared taxi in town 500–1,500 XOF. — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Abidjan is 350 km away: about 5 hours by road, 6–8 hours by coach (CTM, UTB), or about 50 minutes by air with infrequent flights. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A truck and a passenger minibus collided on the San-Pédro–Sassandra road about 20 km from town on 8 Feb 2026, killing 19. — AIP, 2026-02-09, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] The regional public hospital (EPHR), on over 7 hectares, cost nearly 30 bn XOF; its 2026 priorities are quality of care and patient satisfaction after "persistent constraints" in 2025. — AIP, 2026-02-08, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] A relocation blog describes a general hospital and 1–2 private clinics with limited equipment; serious cases go to Abidjan or are evacuated; malaria risk "very high". — OpenShores "Vivre à San Pedro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] No international school: families board children in Abidjan or change school system. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Université polytechnique de San-Pédro (opened Oct 2021) had 2,484 students in 2025-26 and 175 teacher-researchers; units in marine sciences, agriculture and agro-industry, logistics-tourism-hospitality and health sciences, plus civil and naval engineering. — AIP, 2026-10-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Canada lists areas within 25 km of the Liberian border, including in San-Pédro region, as "avoid non-essential travel" because of crime and intercommunal conflict. — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie puts the Liberian border area (Tabou, Taï, Grabo) in orange; the rest of the country, San-Pédro included, is yellow (updated 15 Sept 2026). — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] UK FCDO reports armed robbers attacking private cars on main roads in the west, by day and night, and "coupeurs de route" roadblocks on secondary roads. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Late June 2024 rains killed five people in San-Pédro region; in the Thanry 2 neighbourhood 92 people were evacuated by pirogue, and 165 people were displaced. — AIP, 2024-06-30, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Cultural life is very limited: no quality cinema, museums or galleries, few concerts beyond local maquis. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [social.sport] The Laurent Pokou stadium pitch was reseeded in mid-2026 and declared ready for competitions. — Koaci, 2026-06-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.food] Fishermen land barracuda, tuna, capitaine and shrimp each morning; fresh fish sells daily at the central market. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [social.pace] Much less traffic, noise and pollution than Abidjan; the small expat community is described as close-knit; an agronomist quoted found weekends isolating. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Hotter and more humid than Abidjan on average; March–April and October are described as the hardest months despite the sea breeze. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Plage de la Baleine and Bois Joly beaches are under 10 minutes by car from most neighbourhoods; Taï National Park (UNESCO, chimpanzees, pygmy hippos) is about 1h30 away. — OpenShores "San Pedro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] A 31 bn XOF management plan was validated in March 2025 for the Monogaga classified forest. — AIP site search listing, 2025-03-14, official, confidence: medium
- [other] Population 390,654 in the sous-préfecture (1,060,724 in the region), 2021 census figures cited by the blog. — OpenShores "Plus grandes villes de Côte d'Ivoire", 2026-10-15, blog, confidence: medium
- [other] Residents' priorities voiced in the April 2025 consultations: more paved roads, water towers and hand pumps, more health centres, secondary schools (Tabou has only two) and fishing ports. — AIP, 2025-05-04, official, confidence: medium
