# Grand-Bassam: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cote-d-ivoire/grand-bassam.md`; `[A tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cote-d-ivoire/abidjan.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/cote-d-ivoire.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **The sea**: France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) says sea and lagoons are often unsupervised and dangerous, with frequent drownings even for strong swimmers; Canada (2026-11-02) names Grand-Bassam; UK FCDO says there is "no lifeboat or rescue service" [C safety.general]. Every beach outing below is for the sand and the terrace, not the water.
- **Terrorism**: FCDO says attacks "cannot be ruled out" at beach resorts, hotels and cafés [C safety.general]; the March 2016 beach attack killed 19 (blog recollection) [C safety.general]; Wikipedia lists a memorial to it [3].
- **Yellow zone**: the town is in France Diplomatie's "vigilance renforcée" zone, like Abidjan [D].
- **Commute**: 40–43 km to Abidjan [3][C daily.transport]; about 45 minutes off-peak, 1h30–2h each way at rush hour, motorway toll about 300 XOF (blog, 2026-03-10) [C daily.transport]. A blog says many residents leave after a few months because of it [C daily.transport].
- **Car**: effectively needed (blog) [C cost.living]; inside town, taxis and moto-taxis cover short hops [C daily.transport]; Wikivoyage says Yango is available [1].
- **Health**: one communal health centre and small dispensaries; serious emergencies go to Abidjan (Clinique Sainte-Anne-Marie under an hour without traffic) [C health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Quartier France (Ancien Bassam, the old town)
- Feel: the historic core on the strip between lagoon and sea, UNESCO World Heritage since 2012 [2][3]; colonial buildings, creole houses and artist studios; the blog calls it a calm residential zone (2026-03-10) [4][C housing.areas]. Artists are moving in from Abidjan for affordable studios (Africa.com, 2026-08-02) [5].
- Rent: renovated colonial house 300,000–900,000 XOF/month (≈ €457–1,372); 1-bed (F2) town-wide 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]. No listing found in the old town itself.
- Getting around: the centre is compact and walkable; the Pont de la Victoire (150 m, 1928) links it to the rest of town [1][2]. Abidjan as in the frames.
- Watch: a local association links piled-up household waste in France, Petit Paris and Moké to malaria, typhoid and dengue (AIP, 2026-11-07) [C health.care]. UNESCO's 2025 decision cites storm surges and erosion and asks for a conservation report by 1 Dec 2026 [D]; weeks-long floods hit the quarter in late 2019 (Quartz, older) [D].
- Places:
  - Musée national du costume, Boulevard Treich-Laplène, former governor's house (museum, Tue–Sun 9:00–17:00, 1,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Centre céramique de Grand-Bassam, Rue Bouët (ceramics centre and shop, Mon–Sun 9:00–18:00; listing Aug 2018) — Wikivoyage fr [2]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Maison des Artistes, Boulevard Treich-Laplène (artists' collective, workshops, sales) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Maison du patrimoine (former post and customs offices, photo exhibitions on the town's history), Boulevard Treich-Laplène — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur (built 1910, renovated 2004) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Le Quai, northern lagoon edge (African and French cuisine, 3,000–4,000 XOF) and Maquis l'Estomac, lagoon shore (from 2,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - La Playa, Boulevard Treich-Laplène (hotel with private beach and pool, doubles from 35,000 XOF; listing Aug 2018) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - SGBCI, Boulevard Gouverneur Angoulvant (bank with ATM, Western Union, exchange; Mon–Fri 7:45–15:30; listing Aug 2018) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Création, culture" (artists' studios, residencies) and "Télétravail"; Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (colonial-era heritage site).

### The beach road east: Azuretti, Modeste, Gbamelé
- Feel: the seafront with villas and beach restaurants [C housing.areas]; busy at weekends with Abidjan visitors, quiet on weekdays [1]. The retiree quoted by the blog calls it her paradise with the beach two minutes away (anecdote) [C return.experience].
- Rent: beachfront villa 700,000–2,000,000 XOF/month (≈ €1,067–3,049), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]; a 6-room villa in Modeste was for sale at 135,000,000 XOF (≈ €206,000), negotiable, Nov 2026 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: by car or taxi from town; no fares sourced. Wikivoyage places La Maison de la Lagune about 7 km along the Route d'Azuretti [1].
- Watch: about 7 tonnes of crude oil leaked from an SIR buoy on 27 Dec 2025 and reached about 10 km of beaches (Modeste, Gbamelé, Azuretti); 145 t of polluted waste were removed and beaches reopened on 1 Jan 2026 (AIP, 2026-01-05) [C safety.general]. On 4 Nov 2026 the prefecture demolished structures to reopen a blocked path to the beach near the Jah Live space (AIP) [C nature.access]. Sea and terrorism frames apply.
- Places:
  - Hotel Boblin la Mer, Route d'Azuretti (small beach hotel, 12,000 XOF a night; undated) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - La Maison de la Lagune, Route d'Azuretti (7-room guesthouse with pool and internet) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Taverne la Bassamoise (beach lodge known mainly as a restaurant, large pool) — Wikivoyage [1]; location on the beach strip not stated
  - Azuretti village and its chief's court (traditional authority) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Jah Live space (beach venue; access path reopened 4 Nov 2026) — AIP via [C nature.access]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage" (terraces, not swimming); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" for space and a garden, if the school run to Abidjan is accepted; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" on weekdays.

### Nouveau Bassam (town centre and market, around Place de la Paix)
- Feel: on the mainland north of the lagoon, the business centre with shops, the market and "more standard, accessible" homes (blog) [1][3][C housing.areas]. The OpenShores grid calls the market "reasonable", no figures [C cost.living].
- Rent: studio or room 80,000–180,000 XOF (≈ €122–274); 1-bed (F2) 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534); 2-bed (F3) 250,000–600,000 (≈ €381–915); modest villa 400,000–800,000 (≈ €610–1,220), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]. No new-town-specific figure.
- Getting around: the bus station is in the centre beside the Place de la Paix roundabout [1]. To Abidjan: shared taxi 2,000–4,000 XOF (1–2 h) or private taxi 15,000–25,000 (about 1 h), blog 2026-03-10 [4][C cost.negotiation]; Wikivoyage gives about 500 XOF by bush taxi from Abidjan's Gare de Bassam or bus from Adjamé (undated) [1]. The two sources disagree.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the frames.
- Places:
  - Central market, next to Place de la Paix (produce, occasional souvenirs) — Wikivoyage [1]; OpenShores [4]
  - Épilogue, Rue Congo opposite the Trésor public, north town (nightclub, from 18:30 to 05:00 or 06:00; Tuesday karaoke, Thursday live orchestra; listing gives a price range of 1,000–250,000 XOF, not labelled as a cover charge) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Cyber café on the east side of the Place de la Paix roundabout — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Artisan stalls along the western edge of town on the Abidjan road (crafts) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Mosquée Sylla (mosque; quarter not stated) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Stade municipal de Bassam (football ground; quarter not stated) — Wikipedia [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q5 "Salarié·e" in Abidjan (closest to the shared taxis); Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Épilogue); Q4 "Seul·e".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city files, the dossier and the sources below; the Grand-Bassam grid is one relocation blog (OpenShores, 2026-03-10, confidence low). No electricity, water or in-town taxi price was found for Grand-Bassam: Abidjan figures are used only where marked [A].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Quartier France**. Walkable, calm, an artists' scene [4][5]. A freelance developer quoted by the blog finds the connection "correct"; 4G is available and fibre exists in some recent residential complexes [C work.remote]: ask about fibre before signing, and keep an Orange data plan as backup.
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; lunch at Maquis l'Estomac (from 2,000 XOF) [1].
  - Tue: home office; walk to the Centre céramique and Maison des Artistes [2].
  - Wed: day in Abidjan for a coworking session (e.g. Babi Startup House, Marcory, 35,000 XOF/month) [A cost.living]; shared taxi there and back [4].
  - Thu: home office; dinner at Le Quai [1].
  - Fri: home office; market run at Place de la Paix [1].
  - Sat: beach restaurant lunch on the Azuretti road [4]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Sacré-Cœur [1]; lagoon canoe trip with a local boatman (no price sourced) [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Épilogue, north town (until 05:00–06:00) [1]; Zone 4 in Abidjan means a late road trip back (Wikivoyage warns of carjackings at red lights late at night) [9].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": a beach restaurant, 8,000–20,000 XOF per person [C cost.living]; Le Quai [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the town is calm on weekdays and busy at weekends [1]; home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": on the doorstep; no swimming (see frames). Assinie lies further east (94 km from Abidjan) [7]; no Bassam–Assinie distance sourced.
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no forest sourced near town; the Parc national des îles Ehotilé on the Aby lagoon (UNESCO tentative list) is listed nearby [2], but no access, fee or route was found. Banco National Park is in Abidjan [A].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Grand-Bassam (northern savanna parks are in red zones) [D].
  - "Je reste en ville": Quartier France museums [1][2]; the Abissa festival in late October–early November [1][C social.culture].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: F2 150,000–350,000 ÷ 4.33 = 34,642–80,831 [C cost.rent]
  - Data: Orange unlimited 25,000 ÷ 4.33 = 5,774 (national operator) [A cost.living]
  - 10 maquis meals × 2,000–5,000 = 20,000–50,000 [C cost.living]
  - 1 beach-restaurant meal 8,000–20,000 [C cost.living]
  - 1 Abidjan round trip by shared taxi 2 × 2,000–4,000 = 4,000–8,000 [C cost.negotiation]
  - Épilogue: price range from 1,000 XOF (not labelled as a cover charge) [1]
  - **Total ≈ 73,416–165,605 XOF a week (≈ €112–252)**, before electricity, water and the Abidjan coworking fee.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Nouveau Bassam**, near the shared taxis at Place de la Paix [1]. Honest frame: there is little local market for managers and professionals [C work.jobs]; the job is almost certainly in Abidjan, at 1h30–2h each way at rush hour [C daily.transport]. An HR director quoted by the blog found the first 18 months ideal, then moved to Cocody because of the commute (anecdote) [C return.experience].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: leave early by shared taxi (1–2 h, 2,000–4,000 XOF each way) or by car (toll about 300 XOF) [4]; Abidjan workday (see `abidjan.neighbourhoods.md`, profile 2).
  - Wed: evening market shopping at Place de la Paix [1].
  - Fri: dinner at Le Quai [1].
  - Sat: lazy morning; beach restaurant lunch [4]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Musée national du costume (1,000 XOF) [1]; Maison des Artistes [2].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Épilogue [1]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — beach restaurant, 8,000–20,000 XOF per person [C cost.living]; "Tranquille chez moi" — the point of living here.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1; Abidjan's Zone 4 and Banco are 45 minutes away off-peak [C daily.transport].
- Rough weekly spend (two people):
  - Rent: F3 250,000–600,000 ÷ 4.33 = 57,737–138,568 [C cost.rent]
  - Commute: 2 people × 10 shared-taxi trips × 2,000–4,000 = 40,000–80,000 [C cost.negotiation]
  - 2 beach-restaurant dinners for two: 4 × 8,000–20,000 = 32,000–80,000 [C cost.living]
  - 20 weekday lunches in Abidjan × 1,000–4,000 = 20,000–80,000 [A cost.living]
  - 2 phone plans 2 × 10,000 ÷ 4.33 = 4,618 [A cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ 154,355–383,186 XOF a week (≈ €235–584)**, before utilities and groceries. The commute alone is 40,000–80,000 XOF a week for two.

  - Sat: market at Place de la Paix [1]; Saturday night below.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **the beach road (Azuretti/Modeste)** or the **Cité Rosier** estates, for a villa with a garden [C cost.rent]. Hard constraint: there is no international school; families commute to Abidjan [C family.schools], 1h30–2h per morning run [C daily.transport]. Locally, Wikipedia counts 2 lycées, 5 collèges and 28 public primary schools [3]. Blaise-Pascal, the nearest AEFE school, is in Riviera 3, Cocody [11].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school run to Abidjan by car, leaving early (toll about 300 XOF each way) [C daily.transport]; or local public school.
  - Wed: after school, beach (sand only) [1].
  - Sat: Musée national du costume (1,000 XOF) [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Sacré-Cœur [1]; lunch at a beach hotel. Étoile du Sud listed a Sunday buffet at 20,000 XOF (listing Aug 2018, old) [2].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — needs a sitter (no wage sourced) and a drive to Épilogue [1]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — beach restaurant [C cost.living]; "Tranquille chez moi" — garden evening.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" — at home, no swimming; "En montagne, en forêt" — Banco in Abidjan [A]; "Dans la savane ou le désert" — not realistic [D]; "Je reste en ville" — Quartier France museums and the ceramics centre [2].
- Rough weekly spend (two adults, two children):
  - Rent: modest villa 400,000–800,000 ÷ 4.33 = 92,379–184,758 [C cost.rent]
  - School: 2 children at Blaise-Pascal 2026-27, 2 × 2,865,000 to 2 × 3,570,000 ÷ 52 = 110,192–137,308 [A family.schools]
  - Toll: 10 × 300 = 3,000 [C daily.transport] (fuel not counted: no consumption sourced; super 905 XOF/litre [C cost.living])
  - Food: market for four 50,000–120,000 a month (Abidjan figure) ÷ 4.33 = 11,547–27,714 [A cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ 217,118–352,780 XOF a week (≈ €331–538)**, before fuel, utilities and car costs; local public schooling would remove the fee line but no fee or class-size figure was found for Bassam.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Hotel Boblin la Mer at 12,000 XOF a night (undated) [1], La Nouvelle Paillote at 20,000–30,000 (website dead) [1], or La Playa from 35,000 (2018) [2]. Many larger hotels offer free Wi-Fi [1].
- Day 1: arrive at Abidjan airport (e-visa via SNEDAI) [D]; taxi to Bassam: private 15,000–25,000 XOF [4].
- Day 2: SIM and data: Orange 15 GB for 10,000 XOF [A cost.living]; mobile money (Wave, Orange Money, MTN, Moov) [D]; SGBCI ATM in the old town [2].
- Day 3: viewings in Quartier France and the new town; Abidjan agents charge 5,000–10,000 XOF per viewing [A housing.search] (assumption that Bassam agents do the same; nothing local found). All Bassam listings found were marked "négociable" [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: test the commute: shared taxi to Abidjan at 7:00 and back at 17:30 [4].
- Day 5: ask landlords about generator, water and fibre (no local data on cuts) [C work.remote].
- Day 6: Saturday market at Place de la Paix [1]; evening at Épilogue or Le Quai [1].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Sacré-Cœur [1]; beach lunch.
- Rough first-week spend: 7 nights × 12,000–30,000 = 84,000–210,000 + data 10,000 + 14 meals × 2,000–5,000 = 28,000–70,000 + 2 Abidjan round trips 4 × 2,000–4,000 = 8,000–16,000 + 3 viewings × 5,000–10,000 = 15,000–30,000 → **≈ 145,000–336,000 XOF (≈ €221–512)**, excluding the airport taxi and any lease advance.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". From Abidjan: 35–43 km, 30–45 minutes off-peak [6][1]; the motorway is reached at the Gonzagueville exit, or take the coastal road past the Bassam artisan market [2]. No swimming (see frames).

### One day
- Morning: cross the Pont de la Victoire into Quartier France [2]; Musée national du costume when it opens at 9:00 (1,000 XOF) [1]; Maison du patrimoine for the town's history in photos [2]. On foot.
- Midday: Le Quai on the lagoon edge (3,000–4,000 XOF) or a maquis on the northern lagoon edge (about 1,900–2,300 XOF) [1].
- Afternoon: Centre céramique (to 18:00) and Maison des Artistes [2]; the merchant houses of the N'zima commercial zone (Maison Varlet 1918, Maison Ganamet 1920) [2]; the Cathédrale du Sacré-Cœur [1]; then the beach for the view.
- Evening: a beach restaurant (8,000–20,000 XOF per person) [C cost.living]; return to Abidjan before late night or stay over [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily-life moment): morning at the central market by Place de la Paix, when produce is in [1]; Mosquée Sylla from outside [3]; the artisan stalls on the Abidjan road [1].
- Day 3 (lagoon): canoe trip with a local boatman to see traditional fishing (price to agree) [1]; La Maison de l'Art [5].
- Day 4 (Abidjan): Plateau and Treichville route, see `abidjan.neighbourhoods.md` "One day".
- Day 5 (Assinie, overnight): beach resort about 94 km east of Abidjan, a 15 km peninsula between ocean and lagoon [7]; Coucoué Lodge is the one Wikivoyage listing (Nov 2023) [8].
- Day 6 (commute test): ride a shared taxi to Abidjan at 7:00 to feel the 1–2-hour run residents describe [4][C daily.transport]; back for Épilogue's Thursday live orchestra if the day fits [1].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Sacré-Cœur [1]; the beach busy with Abidjan visitors [1]. In late October–early November, the Abissa festival of the N'zima (2026 edition opened early Nov with the sacred Edo-N'Gbolé drum) [C social.culture].
