# San-Pédro: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cote-d-ivoire/san-pedro.md`; `[A tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cote-d-ivoire/abidjan.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/cote-d-ivoire.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Advisories**: the town is in France Diplomatie's yellow zone; the Liberian border area (Tabou, Taï, Grabo) south-west of it is orange (2026-09-15) [C safety.general]. Canada (2026-11-02) puts areas within 25 km of the Liberian border, including in San-Pédro region, at "avoid non-essential travel" [C safety.general]. UK FCDO reports armed robbers attacking private cars on main roads in the west, by day and night, and "coupeurs de route" on secondary roads [C safety.general]. No dated source on crime inside the town was found.
- **Roads**: a truck and a passenger minibus collided on the San-Pédro–Sassandra road on 8 Feb 2026, killing 19 (AIP) [C daily.transport].
- **Utilities**: power cuts described as more frequent than in Abidjan, a generator or inverter often essential; 4G decent in town but uneven, fibre rare; tap water not drinkable (blog, 2026-03-10) [C daily.power][C daily.internet][C daily.water]. Regional electrification was 43% in 2023 (AIP) [C daily.power].
- **Floods**: late-June 2024 rains killed five people in the region; 92 people were evacuated by pirogue in Thanry 2 and 165 displaced (AIP) [C safety.general]. Thanry 2 could not be placed on a map here.
- **Getting there and around**: coaches from Abidjan by CA Trans (from Yopougon), UTB and SBTA at 6,600 XOF (listings Nov 2026) [1]; 354 km and about 6h40 by car per Wikivoyage's table [7], 5 hours per a blog [C daily.transport]; flights about 50 minutes, 2–4 a week (blog) [3]. In town: blue taxis communaux 200 or 300 XOF, possibly double after 23:00, 500–1,000 XOF or more for outlying places; SOTRA bus 200 XOF, Mon–Fri 06:00–21:00, about hourly (listings Sept 2026) [1]. A blog gives moto-taxis 200–500 XOF and taxi-ville 500–1,500 [C daily.transport]; Yango has little presence (blog) [3].
- **Health**: Centre hospitalier régional (CHR, 24/7) and Clinique Médicale Notre Dame (24/7) [1]; Wikipedia also names Clinique la Renaissance, Clinique les Rochers and Clinique Centrale [2]. Serious cases go to Abidjan or are evacuated; malaria risk "very high" (blog) [C health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Balmer (and the Corniche)
- Feel: the most residential quarter, where senior company staff and expatriates live; paved streets and guarded residences (OpenShores, 2026-03-10) [3][C housing.areas].
- Rent: no Balmer-specific figure. Town-wide grid: 1-bed (F2) 80,000–200,000 XOF (≈ €122–305); 2-bed (F3) 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534); villa with garden 400,000–900,000 (≈ €610–1,372), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]. The blog adds that port and agro jobs often include housing [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: blue taxis communaux 200–300 XOF [1]; beaches under 10 minutes by car from most quarters (blog) [C nature.access].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the frames.
- Places:
  - Les Jardins d'Ivoire, Balmer, Corniche, boulevard Houphouët-Boigny (hotel with seaside-terrace restaurant, Italian and local dishes; rooms 30,000–45,000 XOF, breakfast 5,000; listing Nov 2023) — Wikivoyage fr [1]

### Plage de la Baleine and Bois Joly (beaches north of town)
- Feel: beach areas north of the city where some upscale homes have ocean views; La Baleine is a large sandy beach with paillotes and beach restaurants, Bois Joly forested and quieter (blog, 2026-03-10) [3][C housing.areas].
- Rent: no area-specific figure; villa with garden 400,000–900,000 XOF town-wide [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: under 10 minutes by car from most quarters (blog) [C nature.access]; a 4x4 or SUV is recommended for remote beaches (blog) [3].
- Watch: currents in places at La Baleine (blog) [3]; France Diplomatie's general warning on dangerous, unsupervised beaches applies country-wide [D].
- Places:
  - Plage de la Baleine (beach with paillotes and restaurants) — OpenShores [3]
  - Plage du Bois Joly (forested beach) — OpenShores [3]
  - Surf spot: beach and reef breaks, best September to March (Oct 2026) — Wikivoyage fr [1]; exact break not named
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi". Only three places: a weaker block.

### Considered and set aside
- **Bardot**: the industrial port zone with little housing (blog) [3]; it has one of the two big public markets and the Kante mosque (Bardot 18) (Wikipedia) [2]. Not a base for a newcomer.
- **Cité des Cadres**: company housing built by agro-industrial firms (SIFCA, Olam) for their staff; usually provided by the employer and often better than the open market (blog) [3][C cost.rent]. Relevant only if the job comes with it.
- **Cité Hilaire Lamizana**: nearly 300 port-staff homes under construction on the San-Pédro–Sassandra road near the Laurent Pokou stadium (AllAfrica, 2026-03-09) [C housing.areas]; not yet a neighbourhood.

  - Wed: home office; walk the Front de mer to the Rochers des amours [1].
  - Thu: home office; dinner at Toit Rouge, Lac [1].
  - Fri: home office; Grand Marché run [1].
  - Sat: Plage de la Baleine [3]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Pierre [1]; surf session in season (Sept–March) [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": The VOX, quartier Cité (until 05:00) [1]. Taxi fares may double after 23:00 [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Toit Rouge (braised sole) [1]; Palm Rock Beach (about 20,000 XOF per person) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the default here; an agronomist quoted by the blog found weekends isolating (anecdote) [C social.pace].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": La Baleine and Bois Joly [3]; Monogaga, about 22 km east in a straight line (computed from Wikipedia coordinates) [5]; Grand-Béréby, under an hour by road [2]: Ménéké beach near the Kara Krou ecolodge is a turtle nesting site [4].   - "En montagne, en forêt": the Monogaga classified forest (31 bn XOF management plan validated March 2025) [C nature.access][5]; Taï National Park (UNESCO, chimpanzees) about 1h30 away per the blog [C nature.access], with access Wikipedia calls challenging [8], but Taï is named in France Diplomatie's orange zone and Canada's 25 km border band [C safety.general]: not proposed without checking the current advice.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from San-Pédro.
  - "Je reste en ville": Grand Marché and the Port de pêche [1]; a match at the Laurent Pokou stadium (pitch declared ready June 2026; no fixtures sourced) [C social.sport].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Come by coach (6,600 XOF from Abidjan) or plane [1][3]. Read the frames first; move by day between towns.

### One day
- Morning: the Port de pêche when the night's catch lands (fresh fish, shrimp, crab) [1][C social.food]; the Grand Marché next (fish, yams, spices, textiles) [1]. Blue taxi between them, 200–300 XOF [1].
- Midday: lunch on the seaside terrace of Les Jardins d'Ivoire, Balmer Corniche [1].
- Afternoon: the Front de mer, the ruins of the Balmer hotel (free) and the Rochers des amours (paid) [1]; then Plage de la Baleine for the paillotes [3].
- Evening: dinner at Toit Rouge, quartier Lac (braised sole) [1]; The VOX if dancing (Tue–Sun from 20:00) [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (Monogaga): beach and classified forest about 22 km east in a straight line [5][C nature.access]; car or hired taxi, fare not sourced.
- Day 3 (Sassandra, overnight): fishing port and fish market, colonial quarter, 1951 wharf (free), beaches Batélébré, Niézéko and Lateko; Hôtel Le Pollet 18,000–40,000 XOF (listing 2023) [6]. Daylight driving on the San-Pédro–Sassandra road [C daily.transport].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): back in San-Pédro, the morning rush at the Grand Marché and the Port de pêche [1]; Friday prayers seen from outside the Grande mosquée de la cité, or a Sunday Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Pierre [2][1].
- Day 5–6 (Grand-Béréby, overnight): under an hour by road [2]; Ménéké beach (turtle nesting) near the Kara Krou ecolodge, Tortues Hôtel La Flotte (sport fishing, turtle protection), La Baie des Sirènes [4]; canoe trips on the Dodo river and mangroves [4]. Check France Diplomatie's orange-zone map first (Tabou is named) [C safety.general].
- Day 7: surf in season (Sept–March) [1]; a match at the Laurent Pokou stadium if one is on [C social.sport]; dinner at Palm Rock Beach [1].
