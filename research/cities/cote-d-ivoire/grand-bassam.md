# Grand-Bassam, Côte d'Ivoire

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**, rounded. Where a source gives USD only, the USD figure is kept as stated.

- [cost.rent] Sale asking prices: 6-room villa in Modeste at 135,000,000 XOF (≈ €206,000), negotiable (Nov 2026); 5-room duplex in Mockeyville on 600 m² at 180,000,000 (≈ €274,000) and in Cité Rosier on 350 m² at 145,000,000 (≈ €221,000), both negotiable but posted 2–3 years ago. — Expat.com listings, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium (asking prices; two are OLD)

- [cost.living] Eating out: maquis in the old town 2,000–5,000 XOF per person (≈ €3–8); beach restaurant 8,000–20,000 (≈ €12–30); gourmet restaurant 15,000–40,000 (≈ €23–61). — OpenShores "Vivre à Grand-Bassam, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The new-town market is described as "reasonable", no figures; imported, branded and technical goods have to be bought in Abidjan. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A car is effectively needed, which adds purchase, upkeep and fuel to the budget. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low

- [cost.negotiation] All three Grand-Bassam sale listings and the one rental found on the portal are marked "négociable": asking prices are a starting point. — Expat.com listings, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Transport to Abidjan comes in very different price tiers for the same trip: shared taxi 2,000–4,000 XOF (≈ €3–6) versus private taxi 15,000–25,000 (≈ €23–38). — OpenShores "Vivre à Grand-Bassam, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The blog distinguishes the old colonial town (Quartier France, renovated houses), the beach strip (villas, beach restaurants) and the new town (market, more standard homes). — OpenShores "Vivre à Grand-Bassam, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Property listings name Cité Rosier, Mockeyville and Modeste as residential areas. — Expat.com listings, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The local economy runs on tourism and on residents who work in Abidjan; there is little local market for managers and professionals. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] A national data centre is being built at the VITIB technology park in Grand-Bassam (42.361 bn XOF, over 20% done in Dec 2025, delivery planned end 2026). — AIP, 2025-12-09, official, confidence: high
- [work.remote] A freelance developer quoted by the blog says the connection is "correct" for remote work; 4G is available and fibre exists in some recent residential complexes. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Distance to Abidjan 40 km: about 45 minutes off-peak, 1h30–2h each way at rush hour (3–4 h a day); motorway toll about 300 XOF. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The same blog says many residents leave after a few months because of the commute; families with children at international schools face 1h30–2h per morning run. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Inside town: taxis and moto-taxis over short distances between old town, beach and new town. — OpenShores "Vivre à Grand-Bassam, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] Only one communal health centre plus small dispensaries; serious emergencies go to Abidjan (Clinique Sainte-Anne-Marie under an hour away without traffic). — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] A local association links piled-up household waste in several neighbourhoods (France, Petit Paris, Moké) to malaria, typhoid and dengue, and launched a clean-up and sorting project aiming at 10,000 residents. — AIP, 2026-11-07, official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] No international school: local primary schools and a lycée only; families commute to Abidjan. — OpenShores guide, 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Beaches: France Diplomatie says sea and lagoons are often unsupervised and dangerous, with frequent drownings even for strong swimmers (updated 15 Sept 2026). — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Canada names Grand-Bassam: avoid swimming there, dangerous even for strong swimmers. — Government of Canada travel advice, updated 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] UK FCDO: very strong coastal currents and rip tides cause many drownings and there is "no lifeboat or rescue service"; terrorist attacks "cannot be ruled out" at beach resorts, hotels and cafés. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The relocation blog recalls the March 2016 beach attack that killed 19 people and says security has been reinforced since. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] About 7 tonnes of crude oil leaked from an SIR buoy on 27 Dec 2025 and reached about 10 km of beaches (Modeste, Gbamelé, Azureti); 145 t of polluted waste were removed and beaches reopened on 1 Jan 2026 after tests. — AIP, 2026-01-05, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Civil protection ran awareness sessions in Dec 2025 on household fires caused by misuse of B6/B12 gas bottles and on road accidents. — AIP, 2025-12-29, official, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Artists are moving from Abidjan for affordable studios and a calmer setting; La Maison de l'Art (restored contemporary art museum) and residencies at La Fourchette de Rōze and La Cocoteraie des Arts are named. — Africa.com, 2026-08-02, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Abissa 2026 festive week of the N'zima opened in early Nov 2026 with the sacred Edo-N'Gbolé drum; the new king made his first official outing. — AIP (search listing of articles dated 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-05), official, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] The town is described as calm with a slower pace and fewer jams than Abidjan; the expat community is small and close-knit. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [social.food] Seafood restaurants are the local highlight; authentic Ivorian cooking in old-town maquis. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [return.experience] Testimonies on the blog: an HR director found the first 18 months ideal before the commute wore her down and she moved to Cocody; a retiree calls it her paradise with the beach two minutes away. — OpenShores "Grand-Bassam : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] On 4 Nov 2026 the prefecture demolished structures next to the Jah Live space to reopen a blocked path to the beach; more operations were announced. — AIP, 2026-11-05, official, confidence: high
- [other] Population 124,567 (2021 census figure cited by the blog). — OpenShores "Plus grandes villes de Côte d'Ivoire", 2026-10-15, blog, confidence: medium
