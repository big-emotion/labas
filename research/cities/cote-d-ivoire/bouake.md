# Bouaké, Côte d'Ivoire

## Items

- [cost.rent] A 2-bedroom flat costs 60,000–150,000 XOF/month (≈ €91–229); the blog says rents are 40–50% below Abidjan. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère de Côte d'Ivoire", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] An app blog puts a room at 25,000–40,000 XOF/month (≈ €38–61) versus 60,000–90,000 in Abidjan. — I am Beezy "Gagner de l'argent à Bouaké", 2026-02-24, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Land is described as among the cheapest of the large cities (no figure). — OpenShores "Grandes villes : top 5", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same app blog compares Bouaké with Abidjan: garba 300–500 XOF vs 500–800; daily transport 500–1,000 vs 1,500–3,000; CIE bill 10,000–20,000 XOF/month vs 20,000–40,000; overall 30–40% cheaper. — I am Beezy "Gagner de l'argent à Bouaké", 2026-02-24, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Late April 2026, a bag of rice (size not stated) sold at 17,500–18,000 XOF (≈ €27) in Bouaké, down from 22,000–23,000 two to three months earlier; a 25-litre drum of oil about 25,000 XOF, under the recommended 25,750. — AIP, 2026-04-29, official, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Food budget for one person in secondary cities about 60,000 XOF/month (≈ €91); local market food 20–35% cheaper than Abidjan, imports about the same. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Official beef prices in Bouaké are 3,000 XOF/kg boneless and 2,500 with bone; in March 2025 more than ten butchers were fined or had goods seized for overcharging (2025 source). — AIP, 2025-03-25, official, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] An unsourced salary page says Abidjan pay runs 20–40% above Bouaké. Informal earnings quoted by an app blog: experienced tailor 100,000–300,000 XOF/month; private tutoring 3,000–10,000 XOF/hour. — I am Beezy pages (2026-02-13, 2026-02-24), company, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Construction of 1,520 social and economic flats (760 three-room, 760 four-room, for rent and rent-to-own) began on 16 Oct 2026 at Ahougnansou 2 extension, BOAD-financed (over 40 bn XOF); no prices given. — AIP, 2026-10-17, official, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] Bouaké commune has 13 industrial firms (turnover above 147 bn XOF) employing about 3,000 people against 6,000 at full capacity; cashew is the key crop as cotton has nearly disappeared. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] A technical-lycée employment desk opened in Sept 2026. — AIP site search listing, 2026-09-08, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The Loka treatment plant produces over 88,000 m³ but part of the water does not reach residents because the pipe network is insufficient; wastewater from settlements pollutes the Kan river and Lac de Gonfreville sources; a pipeline project awaits a government decision. — AIP, 2026-08-24, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Coach Abidjan–Bouaké with UTB (from Adjamé): 5,000 XOF standard (≈ €7.62), 6,500 confort, 8,000 VIP; 4–5 hours. — I am Beezy "Horaires et tarifs UTB Abidjan-Bouaké", 2026-02-07, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Train (Sitarail) Abidjan–Bouaké: 8,000 XOF 2nd class, 12,000 1st, 18,000 sleeper (≈ €12–27), indicative Feb 2026. — I am Beezy "Prix billet Sitarail", 2026-02-07, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] A university hospital (CHU) and a new regional hospital (opened 28 April 2025), plus 14 urban health centres and private clinics. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Université Alassane Ouattara (two campuses) and private universities; 117 secondary schools, most of them private. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Belgium (updated 10 Nov 2026) reports continuing road ambushes in the Bouaké region, on the Bouaké–Korhogo axis and around Bouaké and Bouaflé, and advises checking conditions before travel. — Belgian MFA, 2026-11-10, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Cultural centre Jacques Aka (hall, library); crafts nearby: Dar-es-Salam dyers, Tanou-Sakassou potters, N'Gattakro and Djébonoua weavers. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Stade de la Paix holds 40,000 after its AFCON renovation; pitch work finished in June 2026. — AIP monograph (2025-07-30) and Koaci (2026-06-23), official/local-media, confidence: high
- [social.religion] Catholic cathedral in the centre, many Protestant and evangelical churches, and Islam "well established" with several mosques. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Four seasons: long dry season Nov–Feb, long rains March–July, short dry July–Aug, short rains Sept–Oct; 25–38 °C; rainfall 1,000–1,700 mm and increasingly irregular. — AIP "Monographie du département de Bouaké", 2025-07-30, official, confidence: medium
