# Abidjan: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cote-d-ivoire/abidjan.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/cote-d-ivoire.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15): Abidjan is in the yellow "vigilance renforcée" zone; do not go alone at night to Adjamé, Yopougon, Abobo or Gonzagueville (Port-Bouët); stay alert leaving maquis, restaurants, banks and ATMs [C safety.general]. UK FCDO: armed burglaries and street hold-ups "not common" but occur; use taxi apps, avoid public transport after dark [C safety.general]. Canada (2026-11-02): violent crime mostly at night; passengers sometimes robbed by taxi drivers [C safety.general]. Belgium (2026-11-10): burglaries and armed robberies occur even in expat areas [C safety.general].
- Floods: the late-June 2026 rains killed at least 12 at Mossikro (between Yopougon and Attécoubé); Cocody, Yopougon, Abobo, Treichville, Marcory and Bingerville also flooded (GSPM toll, Tchadinfos 2026-06-30) [C safety.general]. The main rainy season runs late May to late July [D].
- Power: outages multiplied from early 2026; a 700 bn XOF plan was announced [C daily.power]. Plan an inverter wherever you live.
- Upfront cost of a lease: the legal rule is 2 months' deposit + 2 months' advance, but AFP (2026-08-31) reports 1,000,000 XOF (≈ €1,524) cash at signing for a 180,000 XOF flat in Cocody, and 5,000–10,000 XOF per viewing paid to the agent [C housing.search]. Nov 2026 listings ask 5 months for a studio [C housing.search].
- Transport: Uber left in Sept 2025; Yango dominates, with Heetch and InDrive [D]; Wikivoyage also names Bolt [1]. Yango 1,500–5,000 XOF a ride (Feb 2026 blog) [C daily.transport]. SOTRA bus fares conflict: 200, 300 or 500 XOF depending on the source [C daily.transport][D]. Shared taxis (wôrô-wôrô) stay inside one commune and are colour-coded; gbaka minibuses 200–500 XOF; SOTRA Wibus air-conditioned lines 701–711 serve Cocody and Bingerville, 300–500 XOF, last departure 20:30 [2]. Wikivoyage calls moto-taxis "probably the number one way to die" while travelling [1]. The metro is not open [C daily.transport].

## Neighbourhoods

### Cocody Riviera (Riviera 2/3/4, Riviera Golf, Palmeraie, M'Badon)
- Feel: the family address. Le Petit Journal names Riviera Golf and Les Jardins de la Riviera as residential family areas [18]; Expat.com (updated 2025-12-03) calls Riviera Golf quiet and secure and Riviera 3 the area of the largest villas, near the French lycée [21]; Accor (2025-02-21) describes Riviera and Deux-Plateaux as calm, upscale and popular with French and expatriate families for the schools and medical centres [20].
- Rent: Cocody Riviera 2 rooms 180,000–350,000 XOF (≈ €274–534), 3 rooms 300,000–600,000 (≈ €457–915), app blog 2026-02-07 [C cost.rent]; Riviera Golf 2-bed above 1,200,000 XOF (≈ €1,829+), Jan 2026 [C cost.rent]; 4-room high-standard flat in Riviera 4 1,100,000 XOF (≈ €1,677); new 4-room flats in Riviera M'Badon with generator and lift 1,000,000 XOF (≈ €1,524); villas in Riviera 3/4 2,000,000–3,500,000 XOF (≈ €3,049–5,336), Expat.com and expat groups, Sept–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent][C cost.negotiation]. Furnished 3-room in Riviera 400,000–600,000 XOF [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Wibus lines 701–711 serve Cocody, 300–500 XOF [2]; yellow wôrô-wôrô inside Cocody [2]. From Cocody to the airport, 45 minutes to 1h30 (blog, 2026-03-10) [23]. No sourced Riviera–Plateau time; Plateau–Cocody is about 15 minutes without traffic, with 20–40-minute queues on each bridge 7:00–9:30 [24].
- Watch: Cocody was among the communes flooded in late June 2026 [C safety.general]. Nothing else area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Lycée Blaise-Pascal and École Jacques-Prévert, Riviera 3 (AEFE French school, nursery to lycée, about 2,700 pupils in 2026) — Wikipedia [12]; Le Petit Journal [19]

  - Impact Hub Abidjan, Cocody Riviera (coworking, hot desk 50,000 XOF/month, dedicated 150,000; 2026-03-18) — I am Beezy [25]

### Angré (7e/8e tranche, Nouveau CHU, Djorogobité)
- Feel: Cocody's newer, middle-class north. Angré 8e Tranche has the Cocovico food market (created 2008) (Wikipedia) [3]. Agency copy sells Djorogobité as calm and family-oriented (Oct–Nov 2026, not resident testimony) [C housing.areas]. A Fratmat editorial (2025-12-17) says part of Angré "lives to the rhythm of Yopougon" [28].
- Rent: Cocody Angré 2 rooms 120,000–250,000 XOF (≈ €183–381), 3 rooms 200,000–400,000 (≈ €305–610), app blog 2026-02-07 [C cost.rent]; 3-room flat at Angré Nouveau CHU 250,000 XOF (≈ €381), 4-room high-standard flat in Angré 8e Tranche 850,000 XOF (≈ €1,296), 2026-11-14 listings; new 5-room duplex in Djorogobité 500,000 XOF (≈ €762), Oct–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. Coliving room in Angré 80,000–150,000 XOF [C cost.rent].

- Watch: on 11 Oct 2025 an opposition march banned by the authorities led to unrest at Anono and Angré château, with 237 arrests (Fratmat editorial via AllAfrica, 2025-12-17) [28]. Election-year tension is a national frame [D].

### Marcory Zone 4 and Biétry
- Feel: the expat and Lebanese-community district near the airport, known for nightlife (Accor 2025-02-21) [20]; Expat.com says it attracts younger expats [21]. A blog (2026-03-10) describes music until 3–4 am Thursday to Sunday, plus daytime noise from traffic, generators and shop speakers [23][C social.nightlife]. A telecom engineer quoted there found it very noisy at first and pays about 40% less than in Cocody (anecdote) [23].
- Rent: furnished studio in Zone 4 150,000–300,000 XOF (≈ €229–457); 1-bed (F2) 250,000–500,000 (≈ €381–762); 2-bed (F3) 400,000–800,000 (≈ €610–1,220), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]. An app blog gives Zone 4 2 rooms 130,000–250,000 (≈ €198–381) [C cost.rent]; The Rio Times quotes 900,000–1,500,000 XOF for a 2-bed (≈ €1,372–2,287) [C cost.rent]; a 3-room flat in Biétry is listed at 1,300,000 XOF (≈ €1,982), 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Plateau 10–15 minutes via Pont Houphouët-Boigny outside traffic, jams at peak hours especially mornings and Friday evenings; airport 15–25 minutes (blog) [23]. An orange taxi Zone 4–Plateau costs about 2,000 XOF (Wikivoyage, undated) [1]. Gbaka 300–500, Yango/Bolt 1,500–6,000, SOTRA 200–600 XOF (blog, 2026-03-10) [22].
- Watch: Marcory flooded in late June 2026 [C safety.general]. Wikivoyage (undated) warns that sex workers may approach visitors in Zone 4 [1]. Popular parts of the commune see more power cuts than Cocody's residential areas (blog) [23].

### Le Plateau
- Feel: the business district ("Petit Manhattan"), home to most big companies' headquarters; only 7,186 residents in 2021 (Wikipedia, edited 2026-10-19) [5]. A blog (2026-03-10) says it is for working, not living: housing unsuited to families, no play areas, restaurants and shops closed Saturday afternoon and Sunday, streets quiet after about 21:00 [24].
- Rent: IvoireDomi benchmark 513,333–953,333 XOF (≈ €783–1,453), May 2026 [C cost.rent]; 2-bed 200,000–600,000 XOF (≈ €305–915), blog Apr 2026 [C cost.rent]; 2 rooms 200,000–450,000 (≈ €305–686), app blog [C cost.rent]; studio 120,000–200,000 [C cost.rent]; 4-room flat near the AfDB 2,000,000 XOF (≈ €3,049) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Marcory about 10 minutes, Cocody about 15 without traffic, airport 30–40; evening rush 17:30–19:30 can take over an hour across Pont de Gaulle (blog) [24]. Lagoon ferry Plateau (Blockhaus)–Treichville about 15 minutes, 150 XOF [1]; SOTRA bateau-bus links Yopougon, Cocody, Plateau and Treichville from 200 XOF [2]. Gare d'Abidjan-Lagune is the SOTRA terminus for Monbus 6 to the airport (200 XOF) [2].
- Watch: a blog describes it as fairly safe 7 am–7 pm but empty after 9 pm (blog only, no official source names the Plateau) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Cathédrale Saint-Paul, Avenue Jean-Paul II (cathedral) — Wikivoyage fr [2]; Wikipedia [5]
  - Mosquée Salam du Plateau, Avenue Crosson Duplessis (mosque) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Musée des civilisations de Côte d'Ivoire, Boulevard Carde (museum, Tue–Fri 9:00–17:00, 2,000 XOF non-African adults; listing Feb 2023) — Wikivoyage fr [2]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Institut français de Côte d'Ivoire, avenue Franchet d'Esperey (exhibitions free; festival 5,000 XOF, Oct 2026) — I am Beezy [26]
  - Stade Félix-Houphouët-Boigny (national stadium) — Wikipedia [5]; Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Saarkan, Avenue Chardy (modern Ivorian, 6,000–12,000 XOF) and Restaurant des Combattants, opposite the French embassy (Ivorian food in a colonial villa) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Regus (coworking, 80,000 XOF/month) and Orange Digital Center (free on selection) — I am Beezy [25]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (if the job is here); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" (dead at weekends); not a fit for Q4 "Avec mes enfants".

### Treichville
- Feel: "the commune of Abidjan's old indigenous families", one of the liveliest areas, especially at night; Rue 12 and avenue 16 are its best-known streets (Wikipedia, edited 2026-09-28) [6]. Le Petit Journal says students and night-owls like it [18]; a Ghanaian engineer quoted by a blog calls it the "soul" of Abidjan (anecdote) [23].
- Rent: 1-bed (F2) 100,000–200,000 XOF (≈ €152–305), blog 2026-03-10 [C cost.rent]; studio 60,000–100,000 XOF (≈ €91–152), app blog [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: next to Plateau over Pont Houphouët-Boigny, 10–30 minutes (blog) [22]; lagoon ferry to Plateau 150 XOF [1]; Gare de Bassam, for Grand-Bassam taxis, is here [6][29].
- Watch: Le Petit Journal (2024-03-21, updated 2025-07-23) lists Treichville among areas where you should not walk alone at night [18]; France Diplomatie does not name it [C safety.general]. Treichville flooded in late June 2026 [C safety.general].
- Places:

  - Palais de la Culture Bernard Binlin-Dadié (performance venue; comedy show 5,000 XOF general, Oct 2026) — Wikipedia [6]; I am Beezy [26]
  - Palais des sports and Piscine d'État de Treichville (sports hall and public pool) — Wikipedia [6]
  - CHU de Treichville (public teaching hospital, emergencies) — Wikipedia [6]; OpenShores [22]
  - Le Débarcadère and Chez Ambroise (restaurants) — Wikivoyage fr [2]
  - Collège Saint-Jean-Bosco (Catholic secondary school) — Wikipedia [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q5 "Création, culture" (Palais de la Culture); Q4 "Seul·e".

### Considered and set aside
- **Yopougon**: the largest commune (1,571,065 people in 2021), maquis capital ("Yop City"); Rue Princesse was demolished in August 2011 (Wikipedia, 2026-10-08) [7][20]. 2 rooms 50,000–100,000 XOF (app blog) [C cost.rent]. France Diplomatie advises against going alone at night [C safety.general], and Mossikro, on its edge, had the deadliest June 2026 flood [C safety.general]. Used below for a daytime visit, not proposed as a base for a newcomer.
- **Koumassi**: south-zone commune of 412,282 people (2021), 26% of its surface precarious housing, with the residential Abri 2000 quarter (Wikipedia, 2026-09-27) [8]; 2 rooms 60,000–120,000 XOF [C cost.rent]. Only one named place (Marché Koumassi 2000 "Djê Konan") could be sourced, so no block.
- **Abobo, Adjamé, Attécoubé**: cheapest rents (Abobo 2 rooms 35,000–70,000 XOF) [C cost.rent] but named by France Diplomatie for night caution and hit hardest by floods [C safety.general][D].

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city file, the dossier and the sources below. Most are company-blog estimates (I am Beezy, OpenShores), confidence low. "Assumption" marks an unsourced step.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Angré**. Coworking at Akendewa for 35,000 XOF a month [25], Cocovico market [3], gyms in the 8e Tranche [C social.sport], CHU d'Angré [14], and rents at roughly half of Riviera's [C cost.rent]. Fixed broadband median 60.99 Mbps (Ookla H1 2026), fibre-to-the-home about 15% coverage [C daily.internet]: ask whether the building has fibre and keep a 4G backup.
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; morning shop at Marché Cocovico [3].
  - Tue: Akendewa coworking [25]; Wibus or Yango home (no time sourced).
  - Wed: home office; gym in Angré 8e Tranche [C social.sport].
  - Thu: Impact Hub Riviera for an event or a change of desk [25]; dinner at the Allocodrome de Cocody [2][20].
  - Fri: home office; evening at Fondation Donwahi (exhibitions, screenings) [16].
  - Sat: Playce Palmeraie for a big shop (Carrefour) [27]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Banco National Park walk (5,000 XOF for foreigners per Wikivoyage, undated) [1][11].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Zone 4 bars and clubs, Thursday–Sunday until 3–4 am [C social.nightlife]; Bidul Bar or Havana Club [1]. Yango both ways (rush and rain surcharges) [D].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Jardyland, Deux-Plateaux (11:30–23:30) [2]; Bushman Café, Riviera (Thu–Sat until midnight) [2].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": a film at Pathé Cap Sud on Sunday instead [26], or home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Grand-Bassam, 35–43 km, about 30–45 minutes off-peak [2][29]; the sea is dangerous even for strong swimmers (France Diplomatie, Canada, FCDO) [C safety.general in grand-bassam.md]. See `grand-bassam.neighbourhoods.md`. Assinie, about 94 km east, 1h10 by car [17][2].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Banco National Park, about 34 km² of primary forest with trails and some 80 km of cycle paths, at the junction of Abobo, Yopougon, Adjamé and Attécoubé [11]; go by car, by day (the surrounding communes are flagged at night) [C safety.general]. No mountains within a weekend's reach were sourced.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Abidjan for this file: the northern savanna parks (Comoé) are in France Diplomatie's red zone [D].
  - "Je reste en ville": Musée des civilisations (Tue–Fri only) [2]; Institut français exhibitions (free) [26]; Bingerville botanical garden [10].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: Angré 2 rooms 120,000–250,000 ÷ 4.33 = 27,714–57,737 [C cost.rent]
  - Coworking: Akendewa hot desk 35,000 ÷ 4.33 = 8,083 [25]
  - Mobile data backup: Orange unlimited 25,000 ÷ 4.33 = 5,774 [C cost.living]
  - Electricity with air conditioning: 25,000–60,000 ÷ 4.33 = 5,774–13,857; water 3,000–8,000 ÷ 4.33 = 693–1,848 [C cost.living]
  - 10 garba plates × 1,000 = 10,000; 4 maquis meals (attiéké and fish) × 1,500–4,000 = 6,000–16,000 [C cost.living]
  - 6 Yango rides × 1,500–5,000 = 9,000–30,000 [C daily.transport]
  - Saturday: 3 beers in a maquis × 500–700 = 1,500–2,100 [C cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ 74,538–145,399 XOF a week (≈ €114–222)**. A blog's single-person budget of 150,000–250,000 XOF a month [C cost.living] is far below this; Africarrières' 300,000–500,000 [D] is closer.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read the frames at the top first. Wikivoyage says to agree taxi fares before leaving, since meters rarely work, and warns of pickpockets in crowds, stations and markets [1].

### One day
- Morning (Plateau): Cathédrale Saint-Paul, Avenue Jean-Paul II [2]; Musée des civilisations, Boulevard Carde (Tue–Fri 9:00–17:00, 2,000 XOF non-African adults) [2]; La Pyramide building (1973) seen from outside [2]. On foot within the Plateau.
- Midday: lunch at Restaurant des Combattants or Saarkan (6,000–12,000 XOF) [1]; then the lagoon ferry from Blockhaus to Treichville, 15 minutes, 150 XOF [1].
- Afternoon (Treichville and Marcory): Grand marché de Treichville for pagnes and crafts upstairs [2]; CAVA crafts village, Rue du Canal (8:00–19:00) [2]. Short taxi; bargain in small shops and with artisans [2].
- Evening (Zone 4 or Cocody): dinner at Chez Léon, drinks at Bidul Bar [2][1]; or the Allocodrome de Cocody for grilled chicken and alloco [2]. Yango back.

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (Grand-Bassam): 35–43 km, 30–45 minutes; Quartier France (UNESCO 2012), Musée national du costume (Tue–Sun 9:00–17:00, 1,000 XOF) [29]; no swimming. See `grand-bassam.neighbourhoods.md`.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): morning market at Cocovico, Angré [3]; then ride the Plateau-bound traffic at 7:30–9:30 across a bridge to see the commute residents live with [24]; afternoon at CEFAC, Riviera 2 [2].
- Day 4 (forest): Banco National Park by car in the morning [11][1]; MuCAT opposite Abobo town hall (Tue–Sun 9:00–18:00) [2]; back before dark (Abobo and Yopougon are flagged at night) [C safety.general].
- Day 5 (Bingerville): botanical garden (created 1904) and Musée Combes [10][9]; Wibus 701–711 or car [2].
- Day 6 (Assinie, overnight): about 94 km east, 1h10 by car; a 15 km peninsula between ocean and lagoon with villas and paillotes [17][2]. Coucoué Lodge is the one Wikivoyage listing (Nov 2023) [30].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Paul or Notre-Dame d'Afrique, Attécoubé [2]; boat lunch on Île Boulay from the Biétry landing (10,000 XOF per adult) [2]; a match at the Stade Félix-Houphouët-Boigny if one is on (no fixtures sourced) [5].
