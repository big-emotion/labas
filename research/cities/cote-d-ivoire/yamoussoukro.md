# Yamoussoukro, Côte d'Ivoire

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg stated in the dossier, **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**, rounded.

## Items

- [cost.rent] A 2-bedroom flat costs 55,000–140,000 XOF/month (≈ €84–213). — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère de Côte d'Ivoire", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Same blog, another page: room or studio 40,000–90,000 XOF (≈ €61–137); 1-bed (F2) 70,000–160,000 (≈ €107–244); 2-bed (F3) 120,000–280,000 (≈ €183–427); villa 250,000–600,000 (≈ €381–915). — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A 2015 expat guide put an unfurnished 2–3-room flat at about 75,000–200,000 XOF/month and named Quartier Millionnaire (Abla Pokou) as the upscale, priciest area. OLD. — Expat.com "Se loger à Yamoussoukro", 2015-11-20, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Housing from INPHB or government institutions is often included in job packages and of good quality. — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Maquis meal 1,000–3,000 XOF (≈ €1.52–4.57); meal at the Hôtel Président 8,000–20,000; market groceries said to be 30–40% cheaper than Abidjan. — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Food budget for one person in secondary cities about 60,000 XOF/month (≈ €91) versus 90,000+ in Abidjan; imported goods cost about the same everywhere. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère", 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Big purchases (imported goods, large supermarkets, fashion) are usually made on trips to Abidjan. — OpenShores "Yamoussoukro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] An unsourced salary page says Abidjan pay runs 20–40% above Yamoussoukro, Bouaké and San-Pédro. — I am Beezy "Salaire moyen par métier", 2026-02-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] No source found on bargaining in Yamoussoukro (see Gaps). An app blog says running a business there is cheaper than in Abidjan: rent, labour and stock all cost less. — I am Beezy "E-commerce Yamoussoukro Daloa", 2026-02-13, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] About ten districts including Habitat (centre, main market), Quartier Millionnaire (upscale, best hotels), Assabou, Dioulako, Kokrenou, Morofé, N'zuessy and 220 Logements; the bus station area is the busiest. OLD. — Expat.com "Se loger à Yamoussoukro", 2015-11-20, company, confidence: low
- [housing.search] A BOAD loan signed in Aug 2025 contributes to 420 social and economic housing units in Yamoussoukro. — AIP (Bouaké housing article), 2026-10-17, official, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Private job market very limited, almost no multinationals; work is mainly academic (INPHB) and civil service. — OpenShores "Grandes villes : top 5" and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] The blog says the capital gets priority supply and fewer outages than Abidjan on average. — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] 4G is available but less stable than in Abidjan; the INPHB campus connection is adequate for academic work. — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] Wave and Orange Money are described as widespread in interior cities; Ecobank, BGFI and Société Générale CI have branches. — I am Beezy "E-commerce Yamoussoukro Daloa" (2026-02-13) and OpenShores guide (2026-03-10), company/blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Abidjan is 240 km, about 3 hours on the A3 toll motorway (coaches CTM, SOGEBEF); Bouaké 200 km, about 2h30; in town moto-taxis are everywhere and wide, empty avenues make trips about 5 minutes. — OpenShores guide and "avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] The regional hospital (CHR) is described as fine for routine cases, with 2–3 private clinics; serious cases go to Abidjan (3 h) or are evacuated. — OpenShores "Vivre à Yamoussoukro, guide complet", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] No international or French-system school; children attend local private schools or the INPHB lycée. — OpenShores "Yamoussoukro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie names no special zone for Yamoussoukro; outside the listed red and orange zones the country is yellow ("vigilance renforcée"), with advice not to drive at night (updated 15 Sept 2026). — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Social life centres on the INPHB campus and a few hotels; no active Alliance Française or art scene; weekends are very quiet as students and staff leave for Abidjan or Bouaké. — OpenShores "Yamoussoukro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] First "Zouglou Life Campus" festival (14 hours of zouglou) held at Espace Moh Faitai on 29 Aug 2026. — AIP, 2026-08-13, official, confidence: high
- [social.sport] Charles Konan Banny stadium pitch work started 26 May 2026 and was nearly ready by late June. — Koaci, 2026-06-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.pace] Testimonies on the blog: an INPHB professor enjoys the calm and low costs and visits Abidjan on weekends; a government executive left after a year finding it too quiet. — OpenShores "Yamoussoukro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Lake Kossou is about 50 km away; classified forests and village plantations nearby. — OpenShores "Yamoussoukro : avantages et inconvénients", 2026-03-10, blog, confidence: low
- [other] Population 340,234 in the sous-préfecture, 422,072 in the autonomous district (2021 census cited). — OpenShores "Plus grandes villes de Côte d'Ivoire", 2026-10-15, blog, confidence: medium
