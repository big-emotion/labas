# Gitega, Burundi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.
> EUR conversions: the country dossier states no rate, so this file uses the xe.com official mid-market rate of 14 Nov 2026, 1 EUR ≈ 3,311 BIF. Iwacu (11 Nov 2026, citing IMF data) puts the parallel-market franc at roughly twice the official rate, so at parallel-market cash rates the EUR cost is about half the figure shown. 2019 rents are converted at today's rate for scale only.

- [cost.rent] Same 2019 report: a house in the affluent CNAR quarter rented at 300,000 FBu (≈ €91) kept its price for a new tenant; rents in Nyabisindu and Karera did not rise because roads there are poor in the rainy season. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2019-02-28, local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] National rents rose 18.4 % year on year to Sept 2026 (INSBU); no Gitega breakdown. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Meat, Oct 2026: the usual price is 30,000 FBu/kg boneless (≈ €9.1) and 22,000 FBu/kg with bone (≈ €6.6); a commune–butchers deal cut this to 28,000 / 20,000 but prices were back up days later. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-16, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] During that short-lived meat deal, residents say scales were rigged so buyers got about 0.75 kg for each kilo paid. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Beer, Aug 2026: Primus 72 cl has an official price of 4,500 FBu (≈ €1.36) but sells for 6,000 FBu (≈ €1.81) in Gitega bars, 5,500 in several places; Amstel 65 cl official 5,000 FBu (≈ €1.51), sold at 8,000 FBu (≈ €2.42). — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-08-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] Scarce or old-label beers have no reference price and are sold "à la tête du client" (price depends on the customer); one barman says it's take it or leave it. - [cost.negotiation] Motorbike taxi fares in the centre are negotiated: drivers at a stand next to a loud sound billboard say the noise makes bargaining with customers difficult. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Informal intermediaries ("commissionnaires") handle house rentals and sales; in 2019 they waited for clients at a former market square turned bar, and said the capital move had pushed rents up in most quarters. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2019-02-28, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Since 13 March 2026, road works have cut residents of Nyamugari's 5th avenue off from Regideso water; a 20 L jerrycan sells for 3,000 FBu (≈ €0.91) informally and people queue from about 4 am. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-25, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Fuel quotas cut in Oct 2026: private vehicles 30 → 20 L, public transport 60 → 50 L. Sopebu publishes a weekly list of supplied stations and drivers race to register. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-08, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] On 6 Oct 2026 two motorcyclists and a motorist died in a crash during a rush to a supplied station; motorbikes ride on pavements to overtake, putting pedestrians at risk. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-08, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] About 62 km from Bujumbura, reachable by bus or taxi; Gitega's airport is defunct and Bujumbura holds the international airport. — en.wikipedia.org Gitega / en.wikivoyage.org Gitega, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: medium
- [health.care] Public hospital specialist consultation costs 10,000–30,000 FBu (≈ €3–9); residents describe whole days waiting, specialists who see only about twenty patients, and pharmacies lacking drugs. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Private "bio-medical" and natural-medicine centres have multiplied, charging from 50,000 FBu (≈ €15) for a quick exam and up to 300,000 FBu (≈ €91) for a sachet of powder; one family says it spent over 5 million FBu (≈ €1,510) before the patient died. Recruiters at hospitals and bus stations earn 15,000 FBu per patient brought in. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-10-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] The École internationale de Gitega (736 pupils) was ordered closed by a ministerial letter of 16 April 2026 over occupation of state buildings without a lease; its accounts were frozen, salaries suspended, and parents petitioned the Prime Minister's office. Status after July 2026 not found. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] A technical secondary school in town struggles with a lack of permanent teachers, with late-evening classes for students (Sept 2026). — iwacu-burundi.org site search "Région Centre/Gitega" (article 2026-09-11), local-media, confidence: low (excerpt only)

- [social.pace] A sound billboard in the centre loops ads from about 7 am all day; traders, hoteliers and moto drivers say it breaks the town's usual calm and drives customers away. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.pace] The central market is partly open-air since traders demolished unauthorised stalls to open fire-truck access after market fires elsewhere; rainy days are described as dead days for business. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-05, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Small neighbourhood bars ("bistrots", cabarets) are the documented drinking scene, e.g. a Nyamugari bar with about five plastic chairs; deliveries of 10–15 crates a week are irregular. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-08-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] High-alcohol local spirits that had been seized as harmful were back on sale in Aug 2026, and sellers faced a sudden ban again in Sept 2026. — iwacu-burundi.org site search "Région Centre/Gitega" (articles 2026-08-13, 2026-09-24), local-media, confidence: low (excerpts only)

- [cost.living] Charcoal, the main cooking fuel: a sack at Shatanya market went from 70,000 to 85,000 FBu (≈ €21 → €26) in about a month (Feb 2026); residents say cooking now costs some households more than food, and gas cookers are not available locally. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-02-06, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] Residents of the commune say earnings of about 5,000 FBu a day (≈ €1.5) do not cover a midday meal; a 4 m plank went from 4,000 to 20,000 FBu (≈ €1.2 → €6.0) and varnish from 12,000 to 20,000 FBu over a few years. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-06-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Rukoba, a hill neighbourhood now inside Gitega's urban commune, has never had mains power although it sits next to the Zege substation; Regideso blames a lack of transformers and poles. Residents use candles, paraffin lamps and torches, and only the better-off have solar panels; shops close early and cannot chill drinks. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-19, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Other unconnected areas named in 2025: Jimbi hill, several cells of Rango quarter, Nyabututsi sub-quarter and the Zege site. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-06-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Drinking water is insufficient with regular household shortages (May 2026); studies to rehabilitate the urban network are under way and the commune proposes treating Ruvubu river water for the outskirts. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-05-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The Zege site, home to more than 90 Batwa households, shares one public tap that can stay dry for over a week; taps on Jimbi hill are often dry too. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-06-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Gitega has no structured urban bus network, unlike Bujumbura, which residents say makes daily trips hard for workers and students. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-05-12, local-media, confidence: medium

- [daily.transport] After a fuel price cut in Feb 2024 the official Bujumbura–Gitega fare was set at 9,300 FBu (≈ €2.8); fares have since risen during shortages. - [nature.access] The Gishuha hot springs charge 2,000 FBu for Burundians and 10,000 FBu for foreigners (≈ €0.6 / €3.0) — an official two-tier price; visitors are often put off by transport costs. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-05-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] The reference hospital is the Hôpital Régional de Gitega on Avenue de l'Enseignement, Musinzira; the commune also has the Itaba communal hospital, now a district hospital, and health centres planned on Jimbi and Mugutu hills. — hopital-gitega.bi / iwacu-burundi.org 2026-05-12, official + local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Residents call care expensive and insufficient and say health-insurance cards are sold fraudulently (May 2026); in 2025 they said free care for under-fives and pregnant women was not always applied, so parents bought medicines themselves. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-05-12 / 2025-06-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] The commune is short of more than 765 teachers, with four or five pupils per bench in some classrooms; boarding schools are planned at Giheta and Nyabugogo within two to three years. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-05-12, local-media, confidence: medium

- [business.startup] Women's savings cooperatives are common (one on Jimbi hill: 53 members, about 4,000 FBu saved per member per month), but members cannot get bank loans without land to pledge, and some husbands stop wives from joining. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-06-16, local-media, confidence: medium
