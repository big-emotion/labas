# Bujumbura, Burundi

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.
> EUR conversions: the country dossier gives prices in FBu only and states no rate, so this file uses the xe.com official mid-market rate of 14 Nov 2026, 1 EUR ≈ 3,311 BIF, and 1 USD ≈ 0.907 EUR. Iwacu (11 Nov 2026, citing IMF data) says the parallel-market franc trades at roughly twice the official rate, so for anyone changing cash on the parallel market the real EUR cost of an FBu price is about half the figure shown. Old FBu prices are converted at today's rate and are therefore only indicative.
> Method note: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after one query, so almost every item comes from pages opened by URL (Iwacu articles and its site search, official advisories, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, AirROI, cost-of-living guides). No blog by a diaspora member living in Bujumbura could be found this way.
