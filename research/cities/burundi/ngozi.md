## Items

- [cost.salary] Day rates in Ngozi commune (Jul 2026): construction helper about 15,000 FBu/day (≈ €4.5), mason about 30,000 FBu/day (≈ €9.1). — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Moto-taxi drivers who rent their bike pay the owner about 12,000 FBu a day (≈ €3.6), or about 100,000 FBu a week, with the owner covering taxes. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Fuel shortages pushed a typical moto-taxi trip from 2,000 to 3,000 FBu (≈ €0.60 → €0.91). — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] National context: inflation 8.4 % in Aug 2026 after 34.4 % a year earlier; rents +18.4 % year on year to Sept 2026. No Ngozi breakdown. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Local economy is farming (maize, avocado as a commune priority, coffee, tea); farmers say official maize buying is slow and its price "dérisoire" against production costs. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Coffee growers in Ngozi and Kayanza complained in Oct 2025 about prices, fraud and late payment, and an energy shortfall at processing plants. — iwacu-burundi.org site search "Ngozi" (article 2025-10-08), local-media, confidence: low (excerpt only)
- [business.startup] Young people say no one has explained how to get start-up funding from the national youth programme (PAEEJ); the development bank BIDF has no local branch, so applicants travel to Gitega. Each of the commune's 92 hills has at least one women's cooperative. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The Kigoma–Butare–Ngozi–Gitega high-voltage line linking the Rwandan and Burundian grids passes through the town. — en.wikipedia.org Ngozi, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Mobile-money agents (some of them University of Ngozi students working part-time) say telecom commissions are too low to build a business on. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Ecobank is said to have a branch in town (Wikipedia flags this as uncited). — en.wikipedia.org Ngozi, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: low
- [family.schools] The private University of Ngozi opened its campus in 1999. — en.wikipedia.org Ngozi, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Older reporting on university costs: medical-faculty tuition rose by more than 60 % (2013) and students were asked for 25,000 FBu towards a food-science lab (2013). — iwacu-burundi.org site search "université de Ngozi" (articles 2013), local-media, confidence: low (old, excerpts only)
- [family.schools] Commune schools face a shortage of benches and overcrowded classrooms; dropouts are linked to unemployment, early pregnancy and child labour. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The central market burned on the evening of 9 July 2026, with traders estimating 80 % of goods lost; witnesses said the fire truck was in Gitega, over 80 km away. It was the third fire there in a few years. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against the RN6 and RN10 roads west from Kayanza through Kibira National Park towards Cibitoke, but does not name Ngozi town. — gov.uk FCDO travel advice, updated 2026-05-21, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Street children, many from outside the commune, are numerous; land disputes and alcohol-related family conflicts are also reported. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-07, local-media, confidence: medium
- [other] Press-freedom context: a journalist was prosecuted over an offence placed in Ngozi; an appeal court ruled in June 2025 it lacked jurisdiction for that reason. — hrw.org World Report 2026, 2026-01, intl-media (NGO), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] 1,806 m altitude: mean highs about 21–24 °C, mean lows about 11 °C; about 1,454 mm of rain on about 181 days, driest June–August (1961–1990 normals). — en.wikipedia.org Ngozi, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: medium
- [other] About 125 km north-east of Bujumbura and 83 km north of Gitega; 39,884 people at the 2008 census (no recent count). Chief town of the new Butanyerera province. — en.wikipedia.org Ngozi / iwacu-burundi.org 2026-07-10, academic + local-media, confidence: medium
