## Items

- [cost.living] Fish, Jul 2026: ndagala (the small lake sardine) reached 140,000–160,000 FBu per kg depending on quality (≈ €42–48), which consumers say is a first; buyers are switching to cheaper, lower-quality "Mwanza" fish from Lake Victoria. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-27, local-media, confidence: medium (figure as printed; unit not cross-checked)
- [cost.living] Palm oil, the area's staple product: a 20 L drum went from 70,000–80,000 FBu in the April harvest season to over 150,000 FBu (≈ €21–24 → €45+), according to growers. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Fishers say they pay up to 700,000 FBu, or over 1 million FBu, for 40 litres of fuel (≈ €211–302), far above the official pump price. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Women selling fish or ndagala earn about 10,000–15,000 FBu a day (≈ €3–4.5); ordinary fishers say boat owners pay them about 10,000 FBu (≈ €3). Vegetable sellers pay a 500 FBu daily market tax, sometimes before selling anything. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Since Feb 2026 palm growers on state land are asked for 700,000 FBu per hectare per year (≈ €211); they call it unaffordable. (Farmland, not housing.) — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Moto-taxi fares rose by 1,000–5,000 FBu after a police crackdown took non-compliant bikes off the road (Jul 2026): Rumonge–Kigwena 7,000–10,000 FBu (≈ €2.1–3.0), Rumonge–Gitaza 10,000–15,000 (≈ €3.0–4.5), Rumonge–Buruhukiro 5,000–7,000 (≈ €1.5–2.1). The ranges reflect fares agreed ride by ride. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The economy rests on lake fishing and palm oil; output of palm oil has been falling for years. A women's cooperative working with Tamura Oil produces about 1,250 L of oil a day. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] A USD 26 million World Bank-funded port was launched on 6 Nov 2026, with 18 months of works; it is meant to link Kigoma (Tanzania), Kalemie (DRC) and Mpulungu (Zambia). Merchants say some port staff demand bribes. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.invest] Wikipedia says the early-2025 M23 offensives on Goma and Bukavu "severely reduced trade" at the port, citing BBC Kinyarwanda (2025). Single source. — en.wikipedia.org Rumonge, accessed 2026-11-14, academic (secondary aggregator), confidence: low
- [daily.power] Power cuts are frequent; a waste-to-charcoal cooperative shrank from 19 to 5 workers because of electricity and fuel costs. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The town has a serious drinking-water shortage: some hotels ask guests to wash from buckets, residents spend hours at public taps, and lakeside households cook and wash with lake water. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] The RN3 lakeside road is in poor condition except about 20 km rehabilitated between Nkayamba and Kagongo, which lengthens trips and raises transport costs; few boats carry passengers across the lake. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Jul 2026 police crackdown on moto-taxis: bikes without plates removed, two weeks to get papers, one passenger per bike; drivers report bribe demands even with papers in order, and passengers have had no insurance cover. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-10, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Rumonge Hospital is the public regional hospital (it began as a 1922 dispensary). In 2026 patients at the Gatete health centre were sent there for something as basic as an IV drip. — en.wikipedia.org Rumonge / iwacu-burundi.org 2026-07-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Health centres are overcrowded with waits of several hours, drug shortages are recurrent and patients are sent to private pharmacies. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-02, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] The commune is often hit by cholera. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Ebola watch (Oct–Nov 2026): six unofficial lake landing points used for night canoe crossings to and from the DRC bypass health screening; three suspected cases tested negative; some town residents said they had no information on the disease. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-11-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Sept 2026: more than twenty goats stolen since early September and bush fires in several localities. — iwacu-burundi.org site search "Rumonge" (article 2026-09-24), local-media, confidence: low (excerpt only)
- [safety.general] Neither the UK nor the French advisory names Rumonge; France formally advises against the zone bordering the DRC and describes the South Kivu side as highly volatile. — gov.uk FCDO travel advice updated 2026-05-21 / diplomatie.gouv.fr updated 2026-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] In May 2024 the Murembwe river flooded the Kanyenkoko district, displacing hundreds of families; the port was hit by major floods in 2023 and 2024. — en.wikipedia.org Rumonge / iwacu-burundi.org 2026-11-13, academic + local-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] More than 50 km of Lake Tanganyika shoreline, hot springs, nature reserves and beaches that local observers call under-used. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [other] Since Aug 2025 Rumonge is a commune (absorbing Burambi and Buyengero) with an estimated 809,769 inhabitants over 806 km², per the commune; the town had about 36,000 people at the 2008 census. — iwacu-burundi.org 2026-06-17 / en.wikipedia.org Rumonge, local-media, confidence: medium

- [cost.living] Fish at Rumonge market roughly doubled in June 2026: a heap of mukeke from 20,000 to 50,000 FBu (≈ €6.0 → €15.1), a heap of fresh ndagala from 5,000 to 10,000 FBu (≈ €1.5 → €3.0), dried ndagala from 80,000 to 150,000 FBu/kg (≈ €24 → €45); low-income buyers switch to Lake Victoria fish, and the large species go to Bujumbura hotels. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-06-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Market prices vs government-fixed prices (Apr 2025): unrefined palm oil 8,500 FBu/L against an official 5,000 (≈ €2.6 vs €1.5); sugar 10,000 FBu/kg against 6,000 (≈ €3.0 vs €1.8), sold under the counter; cooking salt 4,000 against 2,000; meat with bone 22,000 FBu/kg (≈ €6.6), as fixed; cassava flour 1,700–2,500 FBu/kg (≈ €0.51–0.76). — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-04-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] Traders answer price caps with "supply and demand"; a consumer association asks shoppers to report speculators and wants traders and consumers to agree a profit margin per product. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2025-04-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The official Bujumbura–Rumonge bus fare has been 6,000 FBu (≈ €1.8) since a May 2022 decree; a provincial governor later summoned carriers who were charging more. Seen as search excerpts; no newer official fare found. — abpinfo.bi, 2022 / undated, official (state news agency), confidence: low
- [daily.power] The local radio station says power cuts stop its broadcasts for two to three hours or longer and that its generators break down often (Sept 2026). — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-09-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Regideso's PROSECEAU water-intake works for Rumonge and Nyanza-Lac were reviewed in Jan 2026 and pushed to speed up in July–Aug 2026 in response to the drinking-water shortage. — regideso.bi news, 2026-01-28 to 2026-08, official, confidence: medium
- [community.returnee] Sept 2026: refugees returning from exile ask for land, seeds and roofing materials; some can no longer afford rent in town and others stay with friends or relatives; land disputes between returnees and residents are long-running, and public health centres often lack drugs even for those with insurance cards. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-09-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [housing.search] Rumonge has more than 4,000 land and property files before the Truth and Reconciliation Commission, the most of any commune, rooted in the 1972 exodus when others occupied land left behind; anyone buying land should check its history. — iwacu-burundi.org, 2026-07-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Cross-check: the national statistics office's Bujumbura reference price for dried ndagala was 120,000 FBu/kg in Q1 2026 (≈ €36), consistent with the high Rumonge prices already listed. — INSBU Référentiel des prix, published 2026-06, official, confidence: high
