# Bujumbura: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 3,311 FBu** (xe.com official mid-market, 14 Nov 2026, the rate the city file uses) and 1 USD ≈ 0.907 EUR ≈ 3,003 FBu. The dossier itself states no rate because **the parallel-market franc trades at roughly twice the official rate** (Iwacu citing IMF, 11 Nov 2026) [D]: anyone changing cash on the parallel market pays about half the EUR figure shown. `[B tag]` = an item in `research/cities/burundi/bujumbura.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/burundi.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read". Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Frames that apply to the whole city:
- **After dark:** UK FCDO (updated 2026-05-21, current 2026-11-14) advises not to walk or take public transport after dark, even in the centre; armed robbery, bag snatching and carjacking occur; grenade attacks hit crowded central areas on 10 May 2024; police control access in and out of the city at night [B safety.general][31]. Every evening plan below assumes a taxi door to door.
- **Off limits:** FCDO advises against all travel to parts of Ntahangwa commune: the RN5 north of the airport towards Cibitoke and the area west of the Rusizi river, **including Rusizi National Park** [30].
- **Lake:** FCDO advises against swimming in Lake Tanganyika (wildlife and waterborne disease) [31]. The rising lake flooded homes, leisure spots and roads on the shore in 2020 (Iwacu) and shore districts again in April 2024 (OCHA) [33][24]; Wikipedia notes the threat to low-lying districts [D].
- **Utilities:** repeated power cuts (Aug 2026), dry taps for days or weeks in several districts (Oct 2026), chronic fuel rationing [B daily.power][B daily.water][B daily.transport].
- **Rents:** rose 18.4 % year on year to Sept 2026 (national INSBU index) [B cost.rent]. Leases priced in foreign currency were banned by the mayor in 2020 [B cost.rent], yet guides still quote USD; both are kept as stated.
- **Getting around:** city buses at 600 or 800 FBu (≈ €0.18–0.24, Jan 2025), exact change or 100-franc notes expected [B daily.transport]; taxis unmetered, negotiate first [B cost.negotiation]; one guide gives 3,000–8,000 FBu a ride (≈ €0.9–2.4), another USD 27.80 for 8 km [B daily.transport]. Motorbike taxis have been banned from Mukaza commune (the centre, Rohero, Buyenzi, Bwiza, Nyakabiga) since 2016 [B daily.transport]. Wikivoyage lists a local ride app, ITEKA Ride, for taxis and airport transfers (undated listing, not verified) [3]. The airport is about 9 km north-west of Place de l'Indépendance [3]. **No door-to-door travel times between neighbourhoods were found in any source**, so none are given below.

## Neighbourhoods

### Kiriri (hill above the centre, with Kiriri-Vugizo)
- Feel: the hill where ministers, ambassadors and senior executives live; quiet, villas and upmarket hotels, lake and city views; the expat-services blog calls it the most secure area, with patrols and guarded homes (cyriljarnias.com, 2026-03-08) [4]. Wikivoyage lists it for panoramic restaurants and hotels [B housing.areas][3]. Kiriri-Vugizo is administratively a quarter of Rohero [6].
- Rent: no Kiriri-specific monthly rent found. A 3-bed in Kiriri or Rohero "can exceed" USD 700/month (≈ €635) (cyriljarnias.com, undated housing guide) [5]; a careers guide gives 3+-bed villas in "secure private areas" at USD 1,500–3,000+ (≈ €1,361–2,721, 2026-02-25) [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: on the hill above Rohero; no bus line or travel time sourced. In practice a taxi or private car (night travel by car is the norm, [4]).
- Watch: the University of Burundi's Kiriri campus buildings were reported in critical condition, halls and sports facilities "gradually falling into ruin" (Iwacu, 2022-09-28) [26]. No safety incident specific to Kiriri found in the sources read.
- Places:
  - Le Belvédère, Avenue Belvédère (restaurant with panoramic view; mains 25,000–30,000 FBu in a 2016 listing) — Wikivoyage [3]; Burundi Travel review (undated) [23]
  - Kiriri Garden Hotel (3-star; gym free for guests, day passes at reception, no price given) — Burundi Travel 2024-01-18 [13]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Monument de l'Unité Nationale (monument; 10,000 FBu entry, guide tip ≈ 2,000 FBu, Aug 2016 listing) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mausolée du Prince Louis Rwagasore (memorial; free, 2016 listing) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - War Memorial, top of Avenue Belvédère (viewpoint, sunsets) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Best Outlook Hotel, Chaussée Prince Louis Rwagasore on Kiriri hill (hotel; USD 80 double, 2016 listing) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - University of Burundi, Kiriri campus — Wikipedia [6]; Iwacu 2022-09-28 [26]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (no sourced rent below USD 700 for family size); Q7 "Prend son temps" (quiet hill); Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (the only area a source calls the most secure, a company blog, low confidence); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (Belvédère on the doorstep).

### Rohero (centre-ville, Rohero I, INSS, Asiatique, Kabondo, Mutanga-Sud)
- Feel: residential and lively, tree-lined avenues with many cafés, restaurants, bars and gyms; diplomats, NGO staff, consultants and some digital nomads (cyriljarnias.com, 2026-03-08) [4]. Administratively Rohero covers Centre-Ville, Kabondo, Mutanga-Sud, Asiatique and Kiriri-Vugizo [6]. Wikivoyage places restaurants, the cathedral and the stadium here [B housing.areas].
- Rent: 1-bed 200,000–350,000 FBu/month (≈ €60–106; cyriljarnias.com 2026-03-08) [4]. Wider city-centre figures in the city file: 1-bed (~40 m²) USD 273–381 (≈ €248–346), studio USD 600–900 (≈ €544–817) in a different guide — listed separately, not reconciled [B cost.rent]. Coworking 30,000–100,000 FBu/month (≈ €9–30) [4].
- Getting around: this is the centre; city buses from the central bus area (600–800 FBu) [B daily.transport]; no motorbike taxis in Mukaza [B daily.transport]; taxis to other districts, negotiate [B cost.negotiation].
- Watch: two shopping galleries in the city centre burned down on 22 Aug 2026 and Iwacu's editorial spoke of an unknown arsonist; the national human-rights commission asked for independent inquiries into market fires (Iwacu, 2026-08-25 to 09-02) [25]. Thefts reported on the downtown footbridge at Boulevard de l'Uprona / Avenue de la Mission (Mar 2024) [B safety.general]. Grenade attacks in crowded central areas, 10 May 2024 (FCDO) [B safety.general].
- Places:
  - Buja Café, Avenue du 18 Septembre 32, Rohero (café with Wi-Fi described as reliable for work; drinks 2,000–10,000 FBu ≈ €0.6–3.0; daily 7:00–22:00) — Burundi Travel 2023-12-13 [11]
  - Carrington Coffee, corner Avenue du 18 Septembre / Rue de l'Imbo (café, two levels, from 7:30 daily) — Burundi Travel 2025-12-19 [12]
  - The Fitness Factory, 30 Boulevard de l'Uprona (gym, no price) and Inkuba Fitness and Health Center, Kabondo Ouest, Avenue du Bonheur 9 (gym) — Burundi Travel 2024-01-18 [13]
  - Cathédrale Regina Mundi, Boulevard Patrice Lumumba (Catholic cathedral) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - École Française de Bujumbura, Boulevard/Avenue de l'Uprona near the Prince Rwagasore stadium (AEFE-linked French school) — Wikipedia via search excerpt; school documents (not opened) [10]
  - Super Nounou, Rohero I, quartier INSS, Avenue de la Démocratie 36 (daycare and holiday programme; 200,000–250,000 FBu/month, summer 2024) — Burundi Travel 2024-06-24 [19]
  - Musée Vivant, 11 Avenue du 13 Octobre (living museum; 5,000 FBu adults, 2,000 children, 2014-era prices) with Kiosque Saint-Luc crafts beside it (9:00–17:00) — Wikivoyage [3]; Burundi Travel 2024-01-22 [20]
  - BUMEREC (Burundi Medical and Research Center), Kabondo (private clinic) — gov.uk medical facilities list, updated 2021-09-09 [9]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (cafés with Wi-Fi, coworking price range); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (by Bujumbura's standard); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q3 "Moyen".

### Kinindo (south, with Zeimet, Kinanira and Kibenga)
- Feel: the expat-services blog's pick for families with young children who do not want Kiriri prices: houses with gardens, parks, restaurants and shops, hospitals and a Belgian school nearby (cyriljarnias.com, 2026-03-08) [4]. Kinanira and Zeimet are presented as quieter, developing residential zones where comfort and security "vary from street to street" [4]. Kinindo's quarters include Kibenga, Kinanira I–IV and Zeimet-OUA [6].
- Rent: 1-bed 150,000–250,000 FBu/month (≈ €45–76; cyriljarnias.com 2026-03-08) [4]. Family sizes: 3-bed outside the centre USD 205–318 (≈ €186–288) in the city file [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: just south of the Muha river on the Rumonge road (Harmonie Woods' directions start at the Muha bridge) [15]; buses for the southern routes were moved to Kinindo market's parking in Aug 2021 [7]; city bus 600–800 FBu [B daily.transport].
- Watch: Kibenga Lac was among the Bujumbura districts flooded in April 2024 when the Kanyosha river overflowed and the lake rose (OCHA Flash Update, 2024-04-26) [24]. Kinindo market burned on 25 Aug 2026, days after two central galleries (Iwacu) [25]. Neighbouring Kanyosha (Kajiji, Busoro, Nkenga) went two to three days or more without tap water in Oct 2026 [B daily.water].
- Places:
  - Kira Hospital, No. 47 Avenue Nzero, Kinindo (private hospital, all specialities) — gov.uk medical facilities list, 2021 [9]; Wikipedia list [8]
  - CMCK, Centre Médico-Chirurgical de Kinindo (private clinic) — Wikipedia [6][8]
  - Harmonie Woods, Zeimet, first alley right after the Muha bridge on the Rumonge road (family park, playground and bar; Sundays only) — Burundi Travel 2025-12-17 [15]
  - Splash Bujumbura, Kinindo-large (water park with pools, slides and restaurant; daily 10:00–22:00) — Burundi Travel 2024-06-24 [19]
  - Kinindo market (food wholesale and supply market, 1,200 stalls; fire 25 Aug 2026) — Wikipedia [7]; Iwacu [25]
  - La Cervoise du Gaulois, Rue Mutaho (burgers and steak, closed Mondays) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Ecofo Kinindo (public basic school) — Iwacu 2020-11-15 [25]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (Kira, CMCK, a park and a water park nearby); Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q10 "La santé et l'école".

### Gihosha, Kigobe and Mutanga-Nord (north-east, administrative and university side)
- Feel: Gihosha is described as green, with hill views and a mix of housing, home to middle-class Burundian families and a growing number of expatriates; Mutanga-Nord as a quiet residential area with schools and sports clubs; Kigobe as a secure option for couples and single people on moderate budgets (cyriljarnias.com, 2026-03-08, thin detail) [4]. Gihosha's quarters include Gihosha, Kigobe, Mutanga-Nord and Taba [6].
- Rent: no figure for these quarters found. Use the city-wide ranges in the city file [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: east/north-east of the centre [4]; north-east bus fare 800 FBu (e.g. to Gisandema, Jan 2025) [B daily.transport].
- Watch: Taba households went a whole month without tap water in Oct 2026 [B daily.water]; Taba and Nyabagere (Kamenge) reported power cuts of two to four days in Aug 2026 [B daily.power].
- Places:
  - Commissariat Général des Migrations, Avenue de l'OUA, Kigobe (immigration office: visas, residence) — dossier, citing migration.gov.bi [D]
  - Kamenge Military Hospital, Mutanga 1 (public hospital) — gov.uk medical facilities list, 2021 [9]
  - Kamenge University Hospital, CHUK (public teaching hospital, northern health zone) — Wikipedia list [8]
  - University of Burundi, Mutanga campus — Iwacu 2026-06-30 (photo caption) [26]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (ministries, university); Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine". Thin on everyday places; see Gaps.

### Nyakabiga, Buyenzi and Bwiza (dense quarters beside the centre)
- Feel: Buyenzi is popular, dense and noisy, chosen by budget-conscious expats and volunteers who want immersion; Nyakabiga is a busy commercial and nightlife hub for young people (cyriljarnias.com, 2026-03-08) [4]. Wikivoyage lists Bwiza and Nyakabiga for budget hotels [B housing.areas]. Mosques are present notably in Buyenzi (fr.wikipedia via the dossier) [D]; Wikivoyage mentions a new mosque in Nyakabiga [3].
- Rent: Buyenzi 1-bed 100,000–200,000 FBu/month (≈ €30–60; cyriljarnias.com 2026-03-08) [4]. Working-class areas "simple housing" USD 150–400 in another guide, which does not reconcile its USD and FBu figures [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: next to the centre; walkable by day; no moto-taxis (Mukaza) [B daily.transport].
- Watch: Nyakabiga residents report repeated cuts of two to four days and say the dark makes getting home at night frightening because of criminals (Iwacu, 2026-08-21) [B daily.power][B safety.general]; the blog advises against walking alone at night in Nyakabiga [4]. Overflowing waste in Bwiza, collection delayed for months (Iwacu, 2026-11-04) [D].
- Places:
  - Ruvumera market, Buyenzi (wholesale dried fish, hardware, second-hand clothes per a 2010 plan) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Jabe market, Bwiza (2,095 stalls; renovated with EU funds from 2016, current state not confirmed) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Hotel Alexestel Nyakabiga, Avenue Muyinga (hotel; USD 25–30, older listing) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Camel Africa Hotel, Bwiza 6e Avenue no 15 (budget hotel; 35,000 FBu, Aug 2023) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Ceprodilic, Avenue du Commerce 17 (budget rooms; 30,000 FBu, Oct 2024) and Hotel Pacific, Avenue du Palmier 6 (from 35,000 FBu, Oct 2024) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (only with a taxi home); Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (cheap first rooms).

### Ngagara and Kamenge (north, affordable, around the Cotebu hub)
- Feel: no lifestyle description found in the sources read. Ngagara holds the Cotebu wholesale market and the parking for buses and shared taxis to the provinces (Iwacu 2025-01-14) [28]; Kamenge is a large commune with a 3,000-stall market and the military hospital nearby [7][9].
- Rent: no figure for these quarters. City-wide affordable range: apartments 200,000–500,000 FBu/month (≈ €60–151) in one guide's FAQ [B cost.rent].
- Getting around: interprovincial departures from the Cotebu parking, Ngagara [28]; city buses 600–800 FBu [B daily.transport]. Motorbike taxis faced a 7 pm curfew in the north in 2022 [B daily.transport].
- Watch: Kinama (next door) and Buterere were among the districts flooded in April 2024; Buterere was flooded by the Kinyankonge river (OCHA, 2024-04-26) [24]. Nyabagere (Kamenge) reported two-to-four-day power cuts (Aug 2026) [B daily.power]; a Kamenge restaurant said tap water came about once a month (Jul 2025) [B daily.water].
- Places:
  - Cotebu market, Ngagara II (wholesale fruit and vegetables, 1,500 stalls; Nov 2026 prices in the city file) — Wikipedia [7]; Iwacu [B cost.living]
  - Cotebu parking (buses and shared taxis to the provinces, incl. Gitega) — Iwacu 2025-01-14 and 2025-12-25 [27][28]
  - Ngagara market (900 stalls; Bubanza route parking since 2021) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Kamenge market (3,000 stalls) — Wikipedia [7]; Burundi Travel 2024-02-08 [14]
  - Abajinama Association, 8e Avenue no 52, Kamenge (basket weaving by about sixty women) — Burundi Travel 2024-01-22 [20]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (trading, near the wholesale hub); Q8 "Je reste en ville". Not a sourced fit for Q4 "Avec mes enfants".

## Living: typical weeks

Shared price items used in every budget below (all from the files):
- Groceries "one-person basket" = 2 kg local rice (2 × 7,500) + 1 kg beef (35,000) + 2 kg potatoes (2 × 3,500) = **57,000 FBu (≈ €17.2)**, Cotebu prices Nov 2026 [B cost.living].
- Simple lunch out: USD 3.43 (≈ 10,301 FBu, ≈ €3.1), compiled dataset 2026-03-08, low confidence [B cost.living].
- Meal for two in a good restaurant: USD 27 (≈ 81,083 FBu, ≈ €24.5), same dataset [B cost.living].
- Starlink USD 44/month (≈ 132,135 FBu; 30,516 FBu ≈ €9.2 a week), kit USD 372 one-off [B daily.internet].
- Taxi 3,000–8,000 FBu a ride (careers guide; another dataset is far higher) [B daily.transport]; bus 600–800 FBu [B daily.transport].
- Water when taps are dry: bicycle delivery 100,000–120,000 FBu/month (23,095–27,714 FBu ≈ €7.0–8.4 a week) [B daily.water].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Rohero**, because it has the sourced work cafés (Buja Café's Wi-Fi), the coworking price range and gyms within the centre [4][11][13]. Install Starlink: the city file reports criticism of mobile internet quality [B daily.internet].

  - Mon: work from home on Starlink; late-morning coffee at Buja Café (Av. du 18 Septembre, on foot) [11]; evening at The Fitness Factory (Bd de l'Uprona, on foot) [13].

  - Wed: home; afternoon at Carrington Coffee (Av. du 18 Septembre / Rue de l'Imbo) [12]; evening Zion Beach on the lake shore, live music on Wednesdays (taxi) [17].
  - Thu: groceries at Cotebu market, Ngagara (city bus 600–800 FBu each way) [7][B daily.transport]; afternoon work.
  - Fri: work; dinner at Tandoor, 22 Avenue de la Culture, INSS (Indian, 2016 prices) [3].
  - Sat: football at the Rohero stadium (Wikivoyage: matches Saturdays 14:00 and 16:00) [3]; evening per Q2 below.
  - Sun: Mass at Regina Mundi (Bd Lumumba) [3]; afternoon at the Entente Sportive pool, Avenue du Stade (33 m, daily 7:00–19:00) [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Arena Lounge Bar (themed DJ nights, prices "relatively high", Burundi Travel 2024-08-19) [16]; L'Archipel, Bd de la Liberté (dance venue, older listing) [3]; Cœur d'Afrique, Quartier Asiatique (open-air weekend nights) [3]. Taxi both ways (FCDO) [31]. Note: in 2019 the mayor tried to limit weekday bar hours to 17:00–21:00 and then backed down (Iwacu, 2019-11-26) [29]; check current rules.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Belvédère on Kiriri (view; 25,000–30,000 FBu mains in 2016) [3]; Restaurant Tanganyika, 1 Avenue de la Plage (lakeside) [3][23].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home on Starlink, with the caveat of power cuts reported across several districts (Aug 2026) [B daily.power].
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Saga Plage and Bora Bora Plage on the Uvira road, Karera Beach about 20 minutes out (Wikivoyage) [3]; Hotel Club du Lac Tanganyika pool 4,000 FBu weekdays / 10,000 weekends [B cost.living]. Do not swim in the lake (FCDO) [31].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Bugarama viewpoint on the RN1, 32 km east, about 2,200 m (Wikivoyage) [3]; Kibira Park Lodge near Bugarama advertises itself as a base for Kibira National Park [23], but FCDO advises against travel through Kibira on the RN6/RN10 and in communes where a rebel group may be in the park [30]: day trip to the viewpoint only, back before dark [31].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bujumbura. Rusizi National Park, the nearby savanna-and-wetland park, is in an FCDO no-travel zone [30]; Ruvubu is beyond Gitega (see Gitega file).
  - "Je reste en ville": Musée Vivant (5,000 FBu, 2014-era) [3]; Palais des Arts opposite the central post office (drumming and craft displays, Burundi Travel 2025-12-18) [21]; La Maison Fleurie crafts, Chaussée Rwagasore [20].
- Rough weekly spend (one person): rent 46,189–80,831 (200,000–350,000 ÷ 4.33) [4] + Starlink 30,516 [B daily.internet] + coworking 6,928–23,095 [4] + groceries 57,000 [B cost.living] + 3 simple lunches 30,903 [B cost.living] + 3 café drinks 6,000–30,000 [11] + 4 bus rides 2,400–3,200 [B daily.transport] + 2 night taxis 6,000–16,000 [B daily.transport] + gym 23,095 (Safari Gate gym and pool, 100,000 FBu/month, 2024) [13] = **209,031–294,640 FBu ≈ €63–89 a week** at the official rate (about half that in EUR at parallel-market cash rates). Add 23,095–27,714 FBu if taps run dry and water is delivered [B daily.water].

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Kinindo** (Kira Hospital, CMCK, Harmonie Woods, Splash water park) [4][9][15][19]. The international schools named in Bujumbura are École Belge, École Française, King's School, Bujumbura International Montessori School, Burundi English School, Cubahiro and International Maarif [B family.schools]; only the French school's address was found (Av./Bd de l'Uprona, Rohero) [10], and the blog places a Belgian school near Kinindo [4].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week of a trial stay
- Before landing: complete the online "Airport entry visa" form, then pay USD 90 on arrival (one month, single or multiple entry; UK-national rules, check migration.gov.bi); yellow fever certificate mandatory [B admin.visa]. Older notes say USD only, recent notes, no cards; extensions USD 20–50 (2023) [B admin.visa].
- Where to stay first: a budget room in the centre — Ceprodilic, Avenue du Commerce 17 (30,000 FBu) or Hotel Pacific, Avenue du Palmier 6 (from 35,000 FBu), both Oct 2024 listings [3]; or a short-term flat (42 active listings, average booked night 157,558 FBu ≈ €48, AirROI 2026-11-05) [B cost.rent].
- Week:
  - Mon: arrive at the airport, 9 km north-west of the centre; taxi (negotiate first) [3][B cost.negotiation].
  - Tue: SIM and mobile money: Lumitel (Lumicash) or Econet (Ecocash); Lumicash charges 696 FBu to withdraw 10,000 (June 2024) [B daily.money]. Cash from ATMs at DTB, Ecobank or KCB (≈ 5,000 FBu interbank fee) [B daily.money]. **How to open a local bank account as a non-resident: not found.**
  - Wed: neighbourhood walk by day in Rohero and Kinindo; ask about tank, generator and solar backup, which raise rents [5].
  - Thu: flat hunting with a commissionnaire; older forum replies (≈ 2012) mention a fee for viewing listings through an agency office [B housing.search]; Iwacu (2013) described intermediaries pushing rents up [B cost.negotiation]. The deposit should not exceed two months' rent and, since a 2021 reform, is meant to be lodged in a ministry account at a bank (cyriljarnias.com) [5]. Leases in foreign currency banned since 2020 [B cost.rent]. **No current agency commission or advance-rent norm found.**
  - Fri: Commissariat Général des Migrations, Avenue de l'OUA, Kigobe, to ask about extension or residence (carte de séjour rules and costs not found) [D].
  - Sat: Cotebu market morning for prices [7]; Saturday night per Q2 (as profile 1).
  - Sun: Regina Mundi, then Harmonie Woods [3][15].
- Customs: **no source found on advance customs clearance or shipping household goods.**
- Rough first-week spend: 7 nights at 30,000–35,000 = 210,000–245,000 [3] + groceries 57,000 + 3 lunches 30,903 [B cost.living] + 4 taxis 12,000–32,000 [B daily.transport] + 3 Lumicash withdrawals 2,088 [B daily.money] = **311,991–366,991 FBu ≈ €94–111**, plus the USD 90 visa (≈ €82) [B admin.visa].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily life): early morning at Cotebu market, Ngagara, where Iwacu took its Nov 2026 prices (maize flour, beef, rice) [7][B cost.living]; then crafts at La Maison Fleurie and Abajinama in Kamenge [20]. City bus 600–800 FBu with small notes [B daily.transport].

FCDO: no road travel outside Bujumbura after dark [31].
- Day 5: return from Gitega by midday; evening Zion Beach live music if a Wednesday [17].
- Day 6: south along the lake: Livingstone–Stanley Monument at Mugere, 12 km, free [3]; Blue Bay resorts about 60 km south for grilled fish (Wikivoyage) [3]. Back before dark.
- Day 7 (daily life): Sunday Mass at Regina Mundi, then Harmonie Woods with Bujumbura families (Sundays only) [3][15]; or a Saturday match at the Rohero stadium (14:00 and 16:00) or the 22,000-seat Intwari stadium for national and first-division games [3][18].
