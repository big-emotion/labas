> Conversion note: EUR figures use the dossier's rate of 1 EUR ≈ 205 DJF (an unchecked estimate built from the
> 177.721 DJF/USD peg), so 1 USD ≈ €0.87. Wikivoyage gives €1 ≈ Fdj 203 as of July 2026, which is close. Several
> aggregator figures below conflict sharply; they are listed separately, never averaged.
