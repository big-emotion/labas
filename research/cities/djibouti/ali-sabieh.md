- [daily.transport] Minibus Djibouti → Ali Sabieh about 600 FDJ (≈ €2.90), leaving when full. — Cyril Jarnias blog, 2026-03-08, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Buses run to the capital roughly every hour along National Route 5 (about 90–98 km). — Wikipédia "Ali Sabieh" / Wikipedia "Ali Sabieh", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Auto-rickshaws (tuk-tuks) are the most common local vehicle; intercity buses serve Djibouti, Dikhil and Arta; the town is on the Addis Ababa–Djibouti railway, opened in 2016. — Wikipedia "Ali Sabieh", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Large trucks use the main Djibouti–Ethiopia road, and police wire-coil roadblocks are hard to see at night; France Diplomatie calls driving on the Ethiopian border axis dangerous and the last stretch to the border very poor. — UK FCDO 2026-07-27 / France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [daily.internet] Mobile service comes mainly from Djibouti Telecom with 3G and 4G; France Diplomatie says the network is often faulty in the regions. — Wikipedia "Ali Sabieh" / France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Hôpital Dr. Absieh is the town's key hospital and the region's medical hub; a 2011 Islamic Development Bank loan of about 13.5 M USD was agreed for a regional hospital. — Wikipedia / Wikipédia "Ali Sabieh", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [health.care] No private clinic in regional towns, per Expat.com. — Expat.com Djibouti guide, 2025-11-04, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Primary schools, nurseries and state and private secondary schools exist; teaching is in French and Arabic. No international school. — Wikipedia "Ali Sabieh" / Expat.com 2025-11-04, academic/company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Ali Sabieh lies in France Diplomatie's yellow "enhanced vigilance" zone, 10 km from the Ethiopian border; no specific warning for the town. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Public sale of alcohol is banned in inland regions outside hotels and gîtes, per France Diplomatie. — France Diplomatie "Informations utiles", 2026-09-15, official, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Founded in 1899 as a military post for the railway; coral-stone colonial houses and WWII casemates remain; residents are mainly Issa and Gadabursi Somali and mostly Muslim. — Wikipédia / Wikipedia "Ali Sabieh", accessed 2026-11-14, academic (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.sport] The Ali Sabieh athletics centre is described as one of the "viviers" of national athletics; football and basketball are the popular sports, and AS Ali-Sabieh won the national football title in 2013–14 and 2014–15. — ADI 2026-11-13 / Wikipédia "Ali Sabieh", local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] The Ali-Addeh refugee camp is in the region; no source on an expat or diaspora community in town was found. — ADI, 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: medium
