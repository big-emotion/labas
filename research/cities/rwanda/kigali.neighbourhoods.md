## Neighbourhoods

### Kiyovu and the CBD edge (Nyarugenge)

- Feel: established, green, colonial-era streets, "minutes from downtown", home to diplomats, long-term expats and professionals (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3]; many embassies (Wikivoyage) [2]; a 2020 portal guide calls it commercial, high-class and expensive with few residences [4]. Lower Kiyovu was expropriated in 2008 (Wikipedia, via city file) [city file].
- Rent: 2-bed USD 800–1,500/month (≈ €676–1,268), 3–4-bed villa USD 2,000–4,000 (≈ €1,690–3,380) (Expat.com, 2025-10-27) [city file]; premium 2–3-bed in Kiyovu or Kimihurura 800,000–2,500,000 RWF (≈ €491–1,534) (Africarrières, 06/08/2026, low confidence) [city file].
- Getting around: walkable to the CBD (Kigali City Tower, Avenue du Commerce) [3][21]; city buses are cashless, Tap&Go card only, FRw110–517 per route per Wikivoyage [2]; regulated fare 59.28 RWF per passenger-km since 6 Apr 2026 [city file]. Airport about 10 km from the CBD (Wikipedia) [25].
- Watch: FCDO lists burglary, bag-snatching and mugging in Kigali and warns that some poorly lit, unmarked military zones can lead to arrest (current 2026-11-14) [city file]; nothing Kiyovu-specific found.
- Places:
  - Norrsken House Kigali (coworking and startup campus on the old École Belge grounds in the financial district; plans listed, no prices) — norrsken.org [city file]; the old École Belge site was in Kiyovu (KT Press 2018, search snippet only) [29]
  - École Francophone Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, EFASE (French AEFE-partnered school, KN 41 St, Nyarugenge; 2026/27 fees in the city file) — efase.rw [city file]; street from a 2025 EFASE notice seen in search snippets [29]
  - Kandt House Museum (natural-history museum, KN 90, open 08:00–18:00; fee not stated) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Kigali City Tower (offices, shops, food court, fitness centre, Avenue du Commerce; original supermarket and cinema have since closed) — Wikipedia [21]
  - Sainte-Famille Church (Catholic, 1913, Muhima, downtown) — Wikipedia [20]
  - Khana Khazana (Indian restaurant, KN 31 St) and The New Cactus (pizza, KN 47 St) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Hotel des Mille Collines (hotel, Avenue de l'Armée, from €151) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (Norrsken on the doorstep); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" with Q6 "Français" (EFASE close by); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine". Not Q3 "Serré".

### Kimihurura and Rugando (Gasabo)

- Feel: sector of 5.1 km² and 16,425 residents (2022), cells Kamukina, Kimihurura and Rugando, holding Parliament, several ministries and the Kigali Convention Centre (Wikipedia) [16]; "lively but still safe and well-organized", favoured by young expats, entrepreneurs and digital nomads (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3]; "glamorous and fun" with strong nightlife (HouseInRwanda, 2020) [4]. Rugando is leafy and steep, mostly residential (Living in Kigali, undated) [8]. Expat beauty, wellness and café venues use Kimihurura addresses (@livinginkigali, 2026-11-13) [city file].
- Rent: 2-bed USD 600–1,000/month (≈ €507–845) (Expat.com, 2025-10-27) [city file]; premium tier as Kiyovu above [city file].
- Getting around: KN5 borders Rugando; KCC sits on KN5 at the KG2 roundabout, about 6 km from the airport and 7 km from Nyabugogo (Wikipedia) [19][8]. Moto 500–1,000 RWF for most rides (Wikivoyage, haggled) [2]; Move app 1,800 RWF first km then 1,080 RWF/km (New Times, 2026-10-27) [city file].
- Watch: no area-specific safety, power or water item found. City-wide water deficit (~145,000 vs ~210,000 m³/day, RURA, 2026-08-22) [city file].
- Places:
  - Kigali Convention Centre and Radisson Blu (KN5, KG2 roundabout) — Wikipedia [19]
  - Kigali Heights (mall; Simba Supermarket anchor; RIDERS Lounge with a Friday night) — Wikivoyage [2]; KT Press 2025-02-08 [14]
  - Campaign Against Genocide Museum (Parliament building, RN3; open 08:00–17:00) — Wikivoyage [2]; Wikipedia [16]
  - Africa Bite (lunchtime African buffet), Repub Lounge (KG 674 St), Boho Restaurant (KN 14 Ave, 08:00–01:00) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mamba Club (pool; the ten-pin bowling beside it costs FRw3,000 for the first game, FRw2,500 for the second) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Waka Fitness (gym) — HouseInRwanda 2020 [4]
  - Centre Culturel Francophone du Rwanda / Institut français (Rugando) — French embassy [city file]
  - Green Carpet running track near the Office of the Prime Minister (mayor, June 2026, search snippet only) [29]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (cafés, coworking, short rides); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" by Kigali standards; Q6 "Français" (Institut français). Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Nyarutarama and Kacyiru (Gasabo)

Kacyiru is the government and embassy district, "calm, professional and structured" [3]; US, Japan, Kenya and Uganda embassies (Wikivoyage) [2].

- Getting around: Kimihurura and Gishushu are close (Living in Kigali) [3]; Move/Yego and motos as above [city file]. Lounges and clubs cluster in Nyarutarama and Remera (Expat.com) [city file].

### Kibagabaga and Kimironko (Gasabo)

- Feel: Kibagabaga is residential, quieter, mixing locals and internationals, popular with young professionals, NGO staff and first-time expats, cheaper than Gacuriro or Nyarutarama (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3]; an older Living in Kigali page calls it far from everything with limited nightlife and good for runners (undated, comments 2014–2022) [5]. Kimironko is one of the largest middle-income areas, built around its market and bus terminal (HouseInRwanda, 2020) [4].
- Rent: "modern" Kibagabaga rentals USD 500–1,500 (≈ €423–1,268) per a 2021 reader reply (low confidence) [5]; top end: luxury furnished house in Kimironko USD 2,500/month (KIGALI EXPATS group, 2026-11-14) [city file]. 3-bed in expat areas 1,000,000–2,500,000 RWF (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [city file].
- Getting around: Kimironko Gare bus terminal connects to all major areas (HouseInRwanda) [4]; a bus route and motos pass through Kibagabaga [5]. Bus-only lanes at peak (6–10 am, 5–9 pm) since April 2026 (KT Press) [city file].
- Watch: in 2018 taps in Kimironko and Kibagabaga ran about three days a week (Global Press Journal, 2018-04-29, old) [city file]; WASAC rationed Gasabo in the 2026 dry season (wasac.rw, 2026-07-03) [city file].
- Places:
  - Kimironko Market (KG 11 Ave; produce, rice, second-hand clothes; vendors quoted on 2026 prices) — Wikivoyage [2]; New Times 2026-04-14 [city file]
  - Simba Supermarket, Kimironko branch — Wikivoyage [2]
  - International School of Kigali (American-style, AP; Kibagabaga campus since Dec 2021) — Wikipedia [23]
  - Kibagabaga Hospital (district hospital, the area's landmark) — Living in Kigali [5]; Wikipedia via city file [city file]
  - Kigali Parents School — HouseInRwanda 2020 [4]
  - Kimironko Gare (bus terminal) — HouseInRwanda 2020 [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (ISK, hospital); Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "La santé et l'école".

### Gacuriro, Kagugu and Gisozi (north, Gasabo)

- Feel: quiet, private, standalone homes and upscale flats with gardens and guards; diplomats, senior professionals and families (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3]; "expensive and luxurious" (HouseInRwanda, 2020) [4]. A US diplomatic family lived in Kagugu, "where most embassy families live" (Tales Mag, 2023-10-24) [city file]. A French family asked on Expat.com about the Vision City, Gacuriro and Nyarutarama areas (~2026-07, no replies) [city file].
- Rent: "cheaper new builds" in Gacuriro per Expat.com (2025-10-27), no figure [city file]. No sourced figure for Kagugu.
- Getting around: car-dependent; the diplomatic writer advises a car because the city is hilly and spread out (2023) [city file]. Kigali Genocide Memorial is about ten minutes' drive from the centre (kgm.rw) [11].
- Watch: nothing area-specific found.
- Places:
  - Kigali Genocide Memorial (Gisozi; 09:00–17:00 daily, 13:00–17:00 on umuganda Saturday; Wikivoyage: free or by donation) — kgm.rw [11]; Wikivoyage [2]
  - Gisozi running circuit (10 km route behind Gacuriro, free) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - École Belge de Kigali, now reported as École Polaris (IB), KG 784 St, Gisozi, moved from Kiyovu in 2018 — search snippets (doris.school, KT Press 2018, New Times), not opened [29]
  - ULK Gisozi (university campus, a Car Free Day sports venue) — New Times Feb 2026, search snippet [29]
  - Kigali Guestlux (guesthouse garden restaurant, KG 416) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" or "Pour fonder une famille" with a car; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu".

### Remera and Gisimenti (Gasabo)

- Feel: lively and sporty, with nightlife and "some rough edges" (HouseInRwanda, 2020) [4]; Gisimenti is a car-free entertainment hub (New Times, 2026-07-23) [15]. Borders Kimihurura to the east [16].
- Rent: mid-range 2–3-bed in Remera or Gikondo 300,000–800,000 RWF/month (≈ €184–491) (Africarrières, 06/08/2026, low confidence) [city file].
- Getting around: a main bus route (Wikivoyage) [2]; airport buses run via Remera [2]; neighbours Kimironko, Gisimenti and Giporoso [4].
- Watch: nothing area-specific found; Canada advises against public transport at night (2026-09-09) [city file].
- Places:
  - Amahoro Stadium (45,000 seats) and BK Arena (10,000, opened 2019) — Wikipedia via city file [city file]
  - Sole Luna (wood-fired pizza, KG 599, mains from FRw5,000) and Tangren (Chinese, KG 181 St, 07:30–23:00) — Wikivoyage [2]

### Kicukiro, Kagarama, Kabeza and Kanombe (Kicukiro district)

- Feel: mostly Rwandan, quieter, hillside views, growing fast (Living in Kigali, 2019-10-02) [6]; families and professionals wanting space, near the airport (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3]; Kanombe has new estates and mixed incomes (HouseInRwanda, 2020) [4]. Kabeza is up-and-coming, few foreigners, with a calmer market than Kimironko (Living in Kigali, undated ~2019) [9].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed in Kanombe 600,000 RWF (≈ €368) with internet, TV, water and cleaning, or 700,000 RWF (≈ €429) with electricity and gas; furnished 1-bed in Kicukiro 1,000,000 RWF (≈ €613); single listings, not averages (HouseInRwanda, ~Nov 2025) [city file]. Renting a house is "considerably cheaper" than in Kimihurura (2019) [6].
- Getting around: about 15 minutes by car to town; moto ~800–1,000 RWF and bus ~200 RWF with a 20-minute wait in 2019 (pre-2026 fares) [6]. Airport (Kanombe) 5 km from the centre per Wikivoyage, 10 km per Wikipedia [2][25].
- Watch: Kanombe, Nyarugunga, Busanza and Kabeza had dry taps for weeks; WASAC apologised publicly (New Times, 2026-08-22); residents bought jerrycans at 500 RWF [city file]. REG upgrades at Gahanga and Gikondo make some planned power cuts unavoidable (New Times, 2026-09-10) [city file].
- Places:
  - Kicukiro Market (Kicukiro Centre; produce, flour, dried fish, fabric) — Living in Kigali 2019 [6]
  - Kabeza Market — Living in Kigali [9]
  - Simba Supermarket, Kicukiro branch — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Chez John (sports bar, Sonatubes roundabout) — Living in Kigali 2019 [6]
  - Suuka/Coffee Bar and PADDOCK (party venues, Kicukiro); Wakaanda Villa (DJs, Kagarama) — KT Press 2025-02-08 [14]
  - IPRC Kigali (Car Free Day route end, from Sonatube) — KT Press 2026-09-13 [12]
  - Nyanza Genocide Memorial (Kicukiro) and Presidential Palace Museum (Kanombe, KK 106 St, FRw6,000, FRw5,000 for resident foreigners) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (space); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport). Check the water tank before signing.

Biryogo is a car-free entertainment hub (New Times, 2026-07-23) [15].

- Getting around: public transport links to major parts of the city (HouseInRwanda) [4]; Nyabugogo, the main bus and share-taxi station, is in the same district (Wikipedia) [25].
- Watch: nothing area-specific found in an official or media source.
- Places:

  - Kigali Pelé Stadium (APR FC and Rayon Sports; reopened and renamed 15 Mar 2023; Car Free Day sports venue) — Wikipedia [18]; New Times Feb 2026 snippet [29]
  - Auberge de Nyamirambo (guesthouse, FRw5,000–10,000) and Anthurium Residential Hotel (KN 159 St, from FRw31,636) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q6 "Français" (Women's Centre tours in French); Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" not sourced.

### Gikondo and Rebero (Kicukiro)

- Feel: Gikondo is busy, mixed-income and residential beside an industrial park, with nicer houses up Mount Rebero (Living in Kigali, undated, comments to 2020) [7]; Rebero is peaceful, on a hill with skyline views, chosen by remote workers and creatives (Living in Kigali, 2026-01-08) [3].
- Rent: mid-range 2–3-bed in Remera or Gikondo 300,000–800,000 RWF (Africarrières, low confidence) [city file]; top end: villa with city views in Rebero USD 3,000/month (KIGALI EXPATS group, 2026-11-14) [city file].

- Watch: REG says upgrades at Gikondo make some planned power cuts unavoidable (New Times, 2026-09-10) [city file].
- Places:
  - Canal Olympia Rebero (cinema and concert venue) and Agaseke Centre; a 2 km running loop due by end of December 2026 — New Times 2026-06-25 [13][15]

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Entry: Francophonie, AU and Commonwealth passport holders get a fee-waived 30-day visa on arrival; no remote-work permit exists (DGIE) [city file]. People of Rwandan ancestry can apply for nationality by origin (5,000 RWF via Irembo) [city file].
- Where to stay first: a guesthouse while looking: Center St Paul (KN 32 St, FRw15,000–20,000), Hotel Isimbi downtown (US$30–40), Auberge de Nyamirambo (FRw5,000–10,000) or Iris Guest House (US$75) (Wikivoyage, listings of mixed dates) [2].
- Day 1 (arrival): plastic bags and vapes are confiscated at entry (FCDO) [city file]; airport to town by KBS bus about 1,000 RWF, taxi 8,000–12,000 RWF (agree the price first) or moto 1,500–2,000 RWF [city file][2].

- Day 4: viewings. Expect a one-year lease, 3–6 months upfront plus 1–2 months' deposit (another Expat.com page says 1–3 months plus advance); have a lawyer read the lease [city file]. Agency or viewing fees: **not found**. Ask about water storage (2026 dry taps in Kicukiro areas) and backup power [city file].
- Day 5: bank account needs passport, residence permit and proof of address, so not possible on a visitor's stay [city file]; Bank of Kigali has a diaspora offer (bk.rw/diaspora) [dossier]. Use MoMo meanwhile.
- Day 6: Saturday: Kimironko Market and a Nyamirambo walk [2][10]; check if it is umuganda Saturday [city file].
- Day 7: Car Free Day if 1st/3rd Sunday [12]; Kigali Genocide Memorial in the afternoon [11].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Kigali is a gentle first visit: the memorials set the historical frame, and the
markets and buses show daily life.

### One day

- Morning: Kigali Genocide Memorial, Gisozi, from 09:00 (last entry 16:00; 13:00 opening on umuganda Saturday); free or by donation per Wikivoyage, photography extra [11][2]. Move or Yego cab from the centre, about ten minutes [11][city file].
- Midday: moto to Kimihurura (500–1,000 RWF) [2]; lunch buffet at Africa Bite [2]; Campaign Against Genocide Museum in the Parliament building (08:00–17:00) [2].
- Afternoon: Nyamirambo: walking tour from the Nyamirambo Women's Centre (with lunch if taken earlier; English or French; price by phone) [10]; Green Mosque from outside [17].
- Evening: Kandt House closes at 18:00, so go before; dinner at Chez Robert (buffet about €7) or Khana Khazana, Kiyovu [2]. Taxi or Move home rather than a night bus (Canada, 2026-09-09) [city file].

### One week

- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily life): Kimironko Market morning, KG 11 Ave, with light haggling on fruit and veg [2][city file]; then a bus from Kimironko Gare with a Tap&Go card at the 5–9 pm rush, on the new bus lanes [4][city file]; evening Inema Arts Centre [2].
- Day 3: Kicukiro: Nyanza Genocide Memorial, then the Presidential Palace Museum in Kanombe (FRw6,000; guided tour mandatory) [2]; lunch at Chez John, Sonatubes (2019 listing) [6].
- Day 4 (trip out): Akagera National Park day trip by hired car, about 2 h each way; US$50 day fee in an old listing, vehicle fee 10,000 RWF [26][city file].
- Day 5: Nyandungu Eco-park (06:00–18:00, about 70 bird species) [24]; evening a game or concert at BK Arena (Remera) [city file][4], or Zaria Court, the sports complex where fans watched the 2026 World Cup final (location not sourced) [15].
- Day 6 (trip out): bus Nyabugogo–Musanze (3,821 RWF, about 2 h) for Volcanoes National Park with a guide; gorilla permits are expensive and the two sourced prices conflict [musanze city file]. Or Lake Kivu at Rubavu (6,554 RWF, about 3 h) after reading Canada's caution [rubavu city file].
- Day 7 (daily life): if 1st or 3rd Sunday, Car Free Day from 7 to 10 am, sports at the KCC roundabout or Kigali Pelé Stadium [29][12]; else Mass at Sainte-Famille [20]; afternoon Rebero for the skyline and Canal Olympia [3][13].
