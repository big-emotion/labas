# Maseru, Lesotho

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the loti (LSL, "M") is pegged 1:1 to the rand. EUR equivalents use the dossier's working rate **~20 LSL = 1 EUR** and **USD→EUR ~0.9** (both unsourced, approximate, as stated in `research/countries/lesotho.md`). Web-search budget ran out after two searches in this pass; everything else comes from direct page reads (local media site search, official pages, aggregators).
