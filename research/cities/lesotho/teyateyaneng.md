- [housing.areas] Founded in 1886 as Berea's capital by Chief Gabasheane Masupha, a son of Moshoeshoe I; named after the sandy-bedded Teja-tejana river. — Wikipedia "Teyateyaneng", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Economy: retail plus carpet and tapestry factories; little industry overall. — Wikipedia "Teyateyaneng", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The ITC Ethical Fashion Initiative launched a wool and mohair roadshow for youth in TY (Sept 2026), running to 2030 in five districts; officials say youth involvement in Berea's wool cottage industry is minimal and it is run mainly by older women. — LENA, 2026-09-11, official, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The Teyateyaneng wool shed resumed sheep shearing in Aug 2025 ahead of early wool exports. — LENA, 2025-08-04 (search excerpt), official, confidence: low

- [business.startup] A TY producer of dried and canned peaches lost much of her fruit to heavy rains in 2026 and bought from other growers to keep supplying customers. — LENA, 2026-07-08, official, confidence: medium

- [daily.transport] A new TY taxi rank was nearing completion in Aug 2026 (expected within two months): 64 lockable trader cubicles, a terminal for 3–4 buses, a police post, municipal offices, shops and toilets, on the former Lioli FC ground. — LENA, 2026-08-24, official, confidence: high

- [safety.general] Canada names Maseru, Leribe and Maputsoe as the highest-crime urban areas; TY is not named (absence is not evidence of safety). — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: medium
