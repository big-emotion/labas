## Items

- [cost.rent] Aggregator range for Roma: USD 100–300/month (≈ €90–270), the cheapest area it lists in the Maseru region; unit size implied 1-bed. — 360nations, updated 2026-02, blog (aggregator), confidence: low
- [cost.living] National pump prices after the May 2026 rise: petrol 93 M25.40/L (≈ €1.27), diesel M34.75/L (≈ €1.74), paraffin M22.50/L (≈ €1.13) — paraffin matters where homes lack power (see below). — Newsday, 2026-05-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] National 2026 minimum-wage rise: +4.5% most sectors, from 1 May; no Roma-specific wage data. — LENA, 2026-03-23, official, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Settlement in Maseru district in a broad, fertile valley ringed by sandstone cliffs, altitude 1,610 m; Wikipedia puts it 34 km south-east of Maseru (its Maseru article says 32 km), LENA says about 45 km. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14; LENA, 2025-12-31, intl-media/official, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Founded in 1862 by Catholic missionaries, whose institution became the National University of Lesotho; the area holds three seminaries and several novitiates. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Many NUL students rent rooms off campus in nearby villages such as Hata-Butle; LENA reports "growing security concerns" for them. — LENA, 2026-08-05, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Roma residents say power cuts have hit for years "almost every time it rains" or there is even slight wind, often for hours; LEC first blamed trees, then ageing lines, and did not comment. — LENA, 2025-12-31, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The same outages disrupt teaching and research at NUL and procedures at St Joseph's Hospital. — LENA, 2025-12-31, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In Aug 2026 Ha-Koili, in the Roma Valley, still had no power lines; residents use wood, paraffin lamps and candles while neighbouring villages are connected. — LENA, 2026-08-14, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] LEC condemned vandalism of its equipment in the Roma Valley (Oct 2025). — LENA, 2025-10-22 (search excerpt), official, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Roma Taxi Association and police have been clamping down since June 2025 on informal paid lifts; one person allegedly charged M100 (≈ €5) per passenger from Roma to Morija or Maseru Mall. — LENA, 2025-08-19, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] National local taxi fares from 11 May 2026: 4+1 taxis M17 (≈ €0.85), minibuses M15 (≈ €0.75); the Roma–Maseru long-distance fare was not published in the sources read. — Newsday, 2026-05-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The main road through Roma recently got street lights; the Masianokeng–Roma road was being rebuilt in May 2024. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14; LENA, 2024-05-28 (search excerpt), intl-media/official, confidence: low
- [health.care] St Joseph's Hospital (opened 1937, run by a board under the Archbishop of Maseru) is the only hospital for Roma and 20+ surrounding villages; the Roma College of Nursing trains nurses and midwives. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14; LENA, 2025-12-31, intl-media/official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] NUL is in Roma; the NUL International School (NULIS) is on campus, with 2027 applications open until 31 Aug 2026. NUL publishes a 2026–27 fee structure as a PDF (not read). — National University of Lesotho homepage, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Two church-founded secondary schools: Christ the King High School (boys; a high school since 1961) and St Mary's High School (girls, started by the Holy Family Sisters). — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] Roma street vendors lost a Sept 2025 court case against removal of their stalls, which the Roads Directorate then demolished; most now use mobile stalls set up each morning while they seek a site. — LENA, 2025-10-11, official, confidence: medium
- [other] NUL governance turbulence: litigation affecting university governance (June 2026) and a new 16th University Council inaugurated by King Letsie III on 1 July 2026. — National University of Lesotho homepage, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.women] In Aug 2026 a female NUL student was allegedly raped at gunpoint in her rented room in Hata-Butle; no arrest at publication; the student union called for street lights around the campus villages. — LENA, 2026-08-05, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] LENA reported a rise in revenge and retaliation killings in Roma in Dec 2024; the High Court sentenced two men for a Roma murder in May 2026. — LENA, 2024-12-31 and 2026-05-13 (search excerpts), official, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Canada lists Maseru, Leribe and Maputsoe as the highest-crime urban areas and says incidents also occur in remote areas; Roma is not named. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Strong Catholic footprint: mission town, three seminaries, novitiates. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] US Peace Corps training was once held in Roma; local Makhetha mountain is still nicknamed "Peace Corps Mountain". — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Dinosaur footprints on the NUL campus and the Roma Plateau; basalt mountains to the east. — Wikipedia "Roma, Lesotho", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] El Niño conditions expected through 2026–27 (drought, floods, severe storms); violent summer thunderstorms Nov–Feb — relevant given Roma's rain-triggered power cuts. — Government of Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
