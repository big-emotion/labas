# Teyateyaneng: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **~20 LSL = 1 EUR** and **USD→EUR ~0.9**, the dossier's working rates (unsourced, approximate) [D]. `[T tag]` = an item in `research/cities/lesotho/teyateyaneng.md`; `[M tag]` = an item in `research/cities/lesotho/maseru.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/lesotho.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- **Teyateyaneng ("TY") has no published neighbourhood map, no rent figure and no local price of any kind** [T Gaps]. No aggregator, property portal or expat blog covers it [T cost.rent]. The "neighbourhoods" below are the four parts of town that the sources describe around named places (the town centre on the A1, the Blue Mountain Inn area, St Agnes Mission and the northern exit), not official wards. Every spend figure uses **national** fares or **Maseru** aggregator prices as a stated proxy.
- Basics: capital of Berea district, about 40 km north-east of Maseru on the Main North 1 (A1), at 1,693 m; 75,115 people in 2005 [T housing.areas]. About 45 minutes' drive from Maseru on a road Wikivoyage describes as nearly free of potholes [3]. Most jobs and paperwork are in Maseru [D].
- Safety frame: Canada names Maseru, Leribe and Maputsoe as the highest-crime urban areas and does not name TY, which is not evidence of safety [T safety.general]. TY police warned in April 2026 of rising drug use and linked many sexual-offence and assault cases to entertainment venues [T safety.general]. Gender-based violence is "very high" nationally (UK FCDO) [M safety.women].
- Health: TY has the district referral hospital [T health.care]; LENA reports on Berea Hospital as the district's hospital (2026-03-24) and on Maluti Adventist Hospital at Mapoteng, also in Berea [9]. Canada calls care outside Maseru inadequate; serious cases go to Bloemfontein [T health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Town centre along the A1 (taxi rank, bus terminal, traders)
- Feel: TY's economy is retail plus carpet and tapestry workshops [T work.jobs]. A street-vendors' association formed in Feb 2025 to push back against unfair competition [8]. LENA described mixed Easter 2026 trade and ongoing pressure on Berea's small businesses [T business.startup].
- Rent: no figure exists for TY [T cost.rent]. As a ceiling only, a 1-bed outside central Maseru asks 2,250–4,500 LSL/month (≈ €110–225), 360nations, updated 2026-02 [M cost.rent]; the dossier expects TY to be lower (unsourced) [D].
- Getting around: on foot in town. The new taxi rank on the former Lioli FC ground was nearing completion in Aug 2026 (64 trader cubicles, a terminal for 3–4 buses, a police post, municipal offices, toilets) [T daily.transport]. 16 km of urban roads were being prepared from April 2025 [T daily.transport]. Local 4+1 M17, minibus M15 since 11 May 2026 [T daily.transport]; the TY–Maseru fare after May 2026 was not found [T Gaps]. Wikivoyage's undated national page caps intercity minibus fares at M50 one way [4].
- Watch: a taxi marshal and two other people were shot dead in TY in Sept 2024 (LENA, 2024-09-23) [8]. Police link assaults to entertainment venues (LENA, 2026-04-20) [T safety.general].
- Places:
  - Teyateyaneng taxi rank and bus terminal, on the former Lioli FC ground (transport hub with trader cubicles) — LENA 2026-08-24 [T daily.transport]; LENA 2024–2025 [8]
  - Thakaneng Youth Resource Centre (youth centre; hosted a 50-person youth camp in Sept 2024) — LENA 2024-09-09 [8]
  - Khotsong Skills Training Centre (vocational school, 92 students in 2026) — LENA 2026-02-12 [T family.schools]
  - Teyateyaneng wool shed (wool-shearing shed; resumed shearing Aug 2025) — LENA 2025-08-04 [T work.jobs]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (no price data, but the dossier expects it below Maseru); Q5 "J'entreprends" for crafts or trading near the new rank; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Blue Mountain Inn and Setsoto Design area
- Feel: the visitor end of town: the Blue Mountain Inn and, next to a primary school nearby, the Setsoto Design tapestry workshop, signposted from the A1 [3][5]. A TY weaving-gallery owner told LENA in Sept 2026 that tourist demand is strong and one mat can pay a salary [T business.startup].
- Rent: none found [T cost.rent].
- Getting around: signposted off the A1 through town [3]; 4+1 M17 [T daily.transport].
- Watch: in March 2025 a 29-year-old woman was stabbed to death in daylight near the Blue Mountain Inn, allegedly by her police-officer boyfriend; hundreds of Berea women marched to the magistrate's court (LENA, 2025-03-04) [T safety.women].
- Places:
  - Blue Mountain Inn (hotel; rates not found) — Wikivoyage [3]; LENA 2025-03-04 [T safety.women]
  - Setsoto Design Weaving Gallery (tapestry workshop and shop) — Wikivoyage [3]; Lonely Planet [5]
  - An unnamed primary school next to Setsoto Design — Lonely Planet [5]
- Fits: Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the only sourced place to stay; Q5 "Création, culture" — the weaving workshops.

### St Agnes Mission
- Feel: Helang Basali, a weavers' company of 11 women that began as a cooperative in 1990, works at St Agnes Mission, using local raw mohair for tapestries and rugs and taking commissions [6]. Nothing else on the area was found.
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: distance from the A1 not sourced.
- Places:
  - Helang Basali Weavers, St Agnes Mission (weaving workshop; page updated Feb 2022) — African Craft Market [6]
  - St Agnes Mission (mission; denomination and service times not found) — African Craft Market [6]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q5 "Création, culture".

### Northern exit on the main road (towards Peka and Maputsoe)
- Feel: Elelloang Basali ("be aware, women"), a weaving centre founded in 1997 by fifteen women who left an older group, sits by the main road just north of town in a red building made of aluminium cans; no street address, cash only [7]. Its continued operation is reported by a 2016 blog only [7].
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: on the A1 north of town, near the Peka Bridge turn [7]; 4+1 M17 [T daily.transport].
- Watch: the road leads to Maputsoe, which Canada lists among the highest-crime urban areas (2026-10-15) [T safety.general]; nothing specific to this stretch of TY.
- Places:
  - Elelloang Basali Weaving Centre (weaving workshop and shop) — 2Summers blog, 2016-12-06 [7]; Lonely Planet listing [5]
- Fits: Q5 "Création, culture"; not suited to families (nothing else sourced nearby).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: from Maseru, minibus or car up the A1, about 45 minutes [3]. Setsoto Design Weaving Gallery, near the Blue Mountain Inn, to watch tapestries being made [3][5]; then Helang Basali at St Agnes Mission [6].
- Midday: no restaurant sourced in TY (Wikipedia mentions unnamed cafés) [2]; the Blue Mountain Inn is the only named hotel [3].
- Afternoon: Kome Caves, 25 km east, by 4x4 or high-clearance car only [11]; the site is run by Ha Kome villagers with the Lesotho Tourism Development Corporation [11]. Fee unclear: R70 per person with guide (Evendo, seen in search results only) versus free (ShowCaves, 2023 data) [13].
- Evening: on the way back north or to Maseru, Elelloang Basali by the main road (cash only) [7]; overnight at the Blue Mountain Inn [3] or back to Maseru before dark [4].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from Maseru; walk the town centre, the new taxi rank and traders [T daily.transport][8].
- Day 2: the three weaving workshops: Setsoto Design, Helang Basali, Elelloang Basali [3][6][7].
- Day 3: Kome Caves, 25 km east (4x4) [11]; 2026 festive-season visitors were mostly day trippers (LENA, 2026-01-15) [11].
- Day 4 (daily life): an early weekday at the rank as traders set up, then the morning commuter run to Maseru by minibus, the trip most TY workers would make [T daily.transport][D].
- Day 5: Maseru: Kingsway, Basotho Hat, Our Lady of Victories Cathedral (see Maseru file).
- Day 6: Thaba Bosiu, 24 km east of Maseru, the cultural village and Moshoeshoe I's plateau [14]; or Morija's museum and 1833 French Protestant mission, about 35 km south of Maseru [15].
- Day 7: Sunday at St Agnes Mission [6]; afternoon Lioli FC home match if scheduled [10]; a community-guided walk to the hill villages east of town [T nature.access].
