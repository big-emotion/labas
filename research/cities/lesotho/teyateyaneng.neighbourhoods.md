# Teyateyaneng: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches; places added 2026-11-15 from OpenStreetMap, LENA and booking listings. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **~20 LSL = 1 EUR** and **USD→EUR ~0.9**, the dossier's working rates (unsourced, approximate) [D]. `[T tag]` = an item in `research/cities/lesotho/teyateyaneng.md`; `[M tag]` = an item in `research/cities/lesotho/maseru.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/lesotho.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- **Teyateyaneng ("TY") has no published neighbourhood map, no rent figure and no local price of any kind** [T Gaps]. No aggregator, property portal or expat blog covers it [T cost.rent]. The "neighbourhoods" below are five parts of town grouped around named places (the town centre, the Blue Mountain Inn area, St Agnes Mission, the east side by Berea Hospital, and the northern exit); since 2026-11-15 they carry the suburb labels OpenStreetMap uses (Ha Rats'iu, Ha Mts'eremeli, Ha Ramonaheng, St. Agnes, Ha Ntjabane) [18], which are map labels, not confirmed official wards. Every spend figure uses **national** fares or **Maseru** aggregator prices as a stated proxy.
- Basics: capital of Berea district, about 40 km north-east of Maseru on the Main North 1 (A1), at 1,693 m; 75,115 people in 2005 [T housing.areas]. About 45 minutes' drive from Maseru on a road Wikivoyage describes as nearly free of potholes [3]. Most jobs and paperwork are in Maseru [D].
- Safety frame: Canada names Maseru, Leribe and Maputsoe as the highest-crime urban areas and does not name TY, which is not evidence of safety [T safety.general]. TY police warned in April 2026 of rising drug use and linked many sexual-offence and assault cases to entertainment venues [T safety.general]. Gender-based violence is "very high" nationally (UK FCDO) [M safety.women].
- Health: TY has the district referral hospital [T health.care]; LENA reports on Berea Hospital as the district's hospital (2026-03-24) and on Maluti Adventist Hospital at Mapoteng, also in Berea [9]. OpenStreetMap places Berea Hospital in TY, about 1.1 km east of the centre [17]. Canada calls care outside Maseru inadequate; serious cases go to Bloemfontein [T health.care].

### Town centre: Ha Rats'iu, Mofolo Street and Moshoeshoe Road (taxi rank, shops, banks)
- Feel: TY's economy is retail plus carpet and tapestry workshops [T work.jobs]. OSM maps a dense block of chain stores, supermarkets, banks, butchers, pharmacies and small bars along Mofolo Street, Moshoeshoe Road and the A1 [17]. A street-vendors' association formed in Feb 2025; its launch was held at the Teyateyaneng bus stop, where vendors sell grass brooms, half cabbages and other small goods (LENA, 2025-02-15) [22][8]. LENA described mixed Easter 2026 trade and ongoing pressure on Berea's small businesses [T business.startup].
- Rent: no figure exists for TY [T cost.rent]. As a ceiling only, a 1-bed outside central Maseru asks 2,250–4,500 LSL/month (≈ €110–225), 360nations, updated 2026-02 [M cost.rent]; the dossier expects TY to be lower (unsourced) [D].
- Getting around: on foot in town; the centre's shops sit within about 1 km of each other [17]. The new taxi rank on the former Lioli FC ground was nearing completion in Aug 2026 (64 trader cubicles, a terminal for 3–4 buses, a police post, municipal offices, toilets) [T daily.transport]. 16 km of urban roads were being prepared from April 2025 [T daily.transport]. Local 4+1 M17, minibus M15 since 11 May 2026 [T daily.transport]; the TY–Maseru fare after May 2026 was not found [T Gaps]. Wikivoyage's undated national page caps intercity minibus fares at M50 one way [4].
- Watch: a taxi marshal and two other people were shot dead in TY in Sept 2024 (LENA, 2024-09-23) [8]. Police link assaults to entertainment venues (LENA, 2026-04-20) [T safety.general]; the bars and the nightclub listed below are map entries, not recommendations.

### Blue Mountain Inn area: Ha Mts'eremeli and Ha Ramonaheng (Setsoto Design, Taoana Square, district offices)

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: the Blue Mountain Inn area to start, then a house near the town centre for walking access to the rank [3][T daily.transport]. - Tue: home; afternoon at Setsoto Design [3].
- Wed: minibus to Maseru (about 45 min by road) [3] for groceries at OK Shoprite or Pick n Pay and a café session (see `maseru.neighbourhoods.md`).
- Thu: home; walk the town.
- Fri: home; Helang Basali at St Agnes Mission [6].
- Sat: market morning at the rank and TY Shopping Centre; bread from Batsoeneng Bakers on Mapoteng Road [17]; evening below.
- Sun: service at St Agnes Mission (Anglican on OSM; times not found) [6][17], or Assumption Mission (Catholic) on the east side [17]; Lioli FC home match if scheduled [10].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Wikipedia calls TY an "entertainment capital" [T social.culture]; OSM maps two nightclubs, The End Point on Mofolo Street and Provocateur on the A1 north of the centre, with no hours or reviews [17], and police link venues to assaults [T safety.general]; otherwise Maseru, 45 min away, with the drive back at night that Wikivoyage warns about [4].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": the Blue Mountain Inn's restaurant, or Ka Pitseng's bar-restaurant on the western edge (both from booking listings seen in search results only) [23][24]; Maseru's Lancer's Inn or Avani otherwise.

  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from TY.
  - "Je reste en ville": Lioli FC, 2024–25 league champions, at Lioli Football Stadium (3,000 standing, unsourced figure on Wikipedia) [10].
