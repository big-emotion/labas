- [cost.living] Pump prices are national: from 23 Oct 2026 unleaded 95 costs P19.75/l (≈ €1.32) *(national)*. — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 2026-10-22, official, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] Taxi fares are agreed with the driver before the ride. — Wikivoyage "Kasane", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Tourism is the activity documented: lodges, campsites, river safaris and day trips into Chobe National Park and to Victoria Falls. — Wikipedia "Kasane", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Kasane airport has Air Botswana flights to Gaborone, Maun, Cape Town, Johannesburg, Mbombela and Windhoek, and Airlink to Johannesburg. — Wikipedia "Kasane Airport", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Kazungula border post, about 8 km east, serves the bridge to Zambia; another post leads into Zimbabwe, 70 km from Victoria Falls; Namibia's Ngoma border is 51 km west by tar road. — Wikipedia "Kasane", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Mahube Express bus runs to Francistown twice daily, at 13:30 and 15:30. — Wikivoyage "Kasane", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] Banks with ATMs: ABSA, FNB, Stanbic and Standard Chartered; shops include Spar, Shoprite (08:00–19:00) and Choppies (08:00–18:00). — Wikipedia and Wikivoyage "Kasane", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] Malaria is endemic in the Chobe area, with peak risk November to April. — Expat.com Botswana guide, updated 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] The UK reports room break-ins and theft at lodges in the Chobe area, especially those facing the river. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] The UK notes a high number of sexual assaults nationally and advises women not to walk alone at night *(national)*. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Chobe National Park borders the town to the north-east; Wikivoyage warns that elephants roam near the airport. — Wikipedia and Wikivoyage "Kasane", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Wetter than the south: about 1,044 mm of rain a year, mostly November–March; mean highs about 36 °C in October and lows about 8.5 °C in July. — Wikipedia "Kasane" (1991–2020 normals), accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [other] Population 9,244 at the 2011 census (old). Expat.com calls Kasane a small border town with minimal infrastructure. — Wikipedia "Kasane" and Expat.com Botswana guide (2025-11-03), intl-media, confidence: medium
