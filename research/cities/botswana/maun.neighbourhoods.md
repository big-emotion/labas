# Maun: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: 15 BWP = 1 EUR, the dossier's *indicative* rate (not from a fetched source). Source numbers in brackets refer to "Sources read" below. "City file" = `research/cities/botswana/maun.md`; items cited "(city file)" keep the source and date given there.

Research-pass note: the session-wide WebSearch budget ran out in this pass, so places come from pages opened directly: Wikivoyage Maun (community-written, latest listing update Sep 2025, many entries undated), Wikipedia, and the local weekly *The Ngami Times* (articles of Oct–Nov 2026). Maun is officially still a village with a rural feel (city file), and a 2026 relocation blog says the exact location of a house (near the centre, the river or the lodges) matters more than the ward [9]. Only three areas could be described with three or more sourced places; the brief asks for four to eight, and the shortfall is explained in Gaps. Maun has no sourced rent below the family-house band, and almost no local price list, so the weekly spends below borrow Gaborone prices, marked "(Gaborone proxy)".

Townwide watch (applies to every area below):
- Power: "near-hourly outages" were battering Maun and surrounding villages in October 2026; a planned 10-hour cut on Sunday 18 October (07:00–17:00) covered Maun and 18 other localities, and power often came back hours after the announced time; BPC had not explained the cause by print time (The Ngami Times, 2026-10-26) [6].
- Water: the utility flagged that a sewage line along the Thamalakane River could contaminate water if it leaks (Daily News 2026-05-21, city file).
- Both the UK and France name Maun, with Gaborone and Francistown, as places where violent crime is rising or vigilance is needed (FCDO, read 2026-11-14; France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, city file).
- Wildlife and livestock make night driving dangerous; the UK advises against bathing in rivers; flash floods possible November–March (FCDO, city file).
- Malaria is endemic around Maun, with peak risk November–April (Expat.com 2025-11-03, city file).

## Neighbourhoods

### Town centre and the airport side (Old Mall, Riley's, Mathiba I Road)
- Feel: the airport terminal is "right in town", about two blocks from the major shopping centre [3]; farmers arrive by taxi to sell goods at the kerb [4]; red lechwe graze along the Thamalakane beside donkeys, goats and cattle [3][4]. Many safari and air-charter firms have their head offices here (city file).
- Rent: family house BWP 4,000–8,000/month (≈ €267–533) — Expat.com 2025-11-03 (city file); the same band in a 2026 blog, which adds that good listings rent quickly [9]. No 1-bed or flat figure exists in any source. Office at the Old Mall: 28 m² P3,080/month (≈ €205), 118 m² P7,840 (≈ €523) — TswanaHome, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: the core from Riley's to the airport is walkable but hot [3]; combis leave the bus station on numbered routes (Route 1 north to the airport and camps, Route 7 south-east to the Education Park) [3]; blue-plate taxis, no central dispatch, fewer after dark [3].
- Places:
  - Riley's Hotel (the town's oldest hotel, central, small pool; restaurant serving oxtail and pap) — [3][4]
  - Old Mall and Laha House (shops and offices; Gumasema Adventures safari office, Unit 8, 08:00–18:00) — [3]
  - Bus station and market (Tsaro Street, about 1.5 km from the Riley's roundabout) — [3]
  - Choice (local eatery with ready-made staples, across from Riley's Garage) — [3]
  - Hilary's Coffee Shop (cheap café near the airport, popular with locals at lunch) and Bon Arrivée (restaurant across from the airport) — [3]
  - French Connection (bistro, croissants and shawarmas, Mophane Avenue) — [3]
  - Maun Airport and the Mathiba I Road charter offices (Delta Air, Mack Air, Sefofane, Merlin Air) — [3][5]
  - Maun Education Park (wildlife park within walking distance of downtown; giraffe, zebra, kudu, wildebeest) — [3]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (Old Mall offices, tourism head offices); Q4 "Seul·e" without a car (walkable core, combis); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "Dans la savane ou le désert".

### Matlapaneng and the Shorobe road (riverside camps, north-east)
- Feel: campsites and hotels cluster on the road to Moremi and Shorobe, about 6–10 km north-east of the airport [3]; two of the lodge bars are described as noisy [3]. The Maun Private Hospital is on the Sedia road (opened July 2021) [4].
- Rent: no residential rent found. Stays: Audi Camp house at P200 per person per night with breakfast, en-suite tents P490 for two; Sedia Riverside Hotel camping P120 per person, rooms from P950 (Wikivoyage, undated) [3]. Riverfront plot for sale P300,000 (≈ €20,000) on TswanaHome (city file).
- Getting around: taxi to the camps about P30 (≈ €2); combi into town about P3 [3]. Fewer taxis run at night because of animals on the road [3].
- Watch: night driving with stray wildlife and livestock (FCDO, city file); no river bathing (FCDO, city file).
- Places:
  - Motsana (cultural centre with seven shops, two dance studios, a stage and the Arts Cafe; on the Moremi road before the Audi Camp turn-off) — [3]
  - Audi Camp (camp with a restaurant under a large thatch roof) — [3]
  - The Old Bridge Backpackers and bar (lodge and bar, noisy) — [3]
  - Okavango River Lodge and Crocodile Camp (riverside lodges serving food) — [3]
  - Sedia Riverside Hotel (hotel, Shorobe Road, 6 km north-east of the airport; dining room and poolside terrace) — [3][4]
  - Maun Private Hospital (private hospital, Sedia road) — [4]
  - Matlapaneng tree bridge (old bridge on living trees, on the way to Moremi; in poor condition) — [3]
- Fits: Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (lodge restaurants); Q8 "Dans la savane ou le désert" (Moremi road starts here); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (camps and chalets by the night).

## Living: typical weeks

Price basis: Maun items from the city file and [3]; national prices (petrol P19.75/l from 23 Oct 2026, Daily News 2026-10-22; BTC 100 Mbps P379/month; SIM about BWP 5; 10 GB plan ≈ BWP 247) from the city files. Gaborone proxies: groceries BWP 504/week per person and utilities BWP 182/week (Wise, Gaborone city file); dinner for two BWP 400–600 (Expat.com, Gaborone city file). The city file judges imported goods likely dearer in Maun because of distance (editorial), so the proxies probably understate.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: town centre — walkable to cafés, the airport and the bus station [3]. Caution for remote work: near-hourly power cuts in October 2026 [6], and mobile coverage only 20–25 km beyond town (old figure, city file). A solar or battery backup looks necessary (editorial, from [6]).
- Mon: work from home; evening walk to the Thamalakane bank to see lechwe [3].
- Tue: lunch at Hilary's Coffee Shop [3].
- Wed: work at French Connection, Mophane Avenue [3].
- Thu: early morning at Maun Education Park (2–3 hours suggested) [3].
- Fri: groceries and ready-made dishes at a supermarket; magunya from street sellers, about 50 thebe each (city file).
- Sat: Motsana and the Arts Cafe, Matlapaneng [3]; evening per Q2.
- Sun: rest; sundowner at a riverside lodge [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Trekkers Nightclub or Bar 2000 [3]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Audi Camp or the Sedia Riverside Hotel dining room [3]; Wine and Dine for a sampler of local dishes [3]. "Tranquille chez moi" — home, power permitting [6].
- Weekend outings (Q8): "À la plage" — not realistic from Maun (landlocked; rivers not for bathing, FCDO, city file). "En montagne, en forêt" — not realistic as a weekend: the Tsodilo Hills (up to 1,400 m, UNESCO rock art) are about 40 km from Shakawe [14], and the Maun–Shakawe bus takes about 6 hours for BWP 141 (July 2024) [11]; a 3-day trip at least. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — Moremi Game Reserve, about 80 km [3]; a mokoro trip in the delta through any agency [3]. "Je reste en ville" — Education Park, Motsana, the Nhabe Museum (exhibitions and gift shop; hours and fees not listed) [3].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: no 1-bed figure; a family house BWP 4,000–8,000 ÷ 4.33 = 924–1,848 (city file) — an upper bound for one person
  - Groceries 504 and utilities 182 (Gaborone proxy); internet 88 (BTC, availability in Maun not verified)
  - 4 taxi rides × P30 = 120 [3]
  - Total ≈ BWP 1,818–2,742/week (≈ €121–183), before any backup-power equipment (no price sourced).

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: town centre — closest to Letsholathebe II Memorial Hospital and the private hospital (city file; [4]). Honest caveat: there is no real international school; families use distance learning or boarding in Gaborone or South Africa (Expat.com, city file), and Expat.com judges Maun isolated and poorly suited to young children (dossier). Maun Senior Secondary School is the only school named in the sources [4]. Serious emergencies mean evacuation to Gaborone (about 1h30 by air) or Johannesburg (city file).
- Mon: CNED or other distance-learning lessons at home (editorial, from city file); power cuts are a real constraint [6].
- Tue: Maun Education Park in the early morning [3].
- Wed: groceries in town [3].
- Thu: Motsana dance studios or the Arts Cafe [3].
- Fri: dinner at Riley's Hotel [3].
- Sat: Market Night on the last Saturday [7]; evening per Q2.
- Sun: scenic flight over the delta as a special treat (Kavango Air P1,500 for up to three passengers plus P50 each) [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — needs a sitter; Trekkers [3]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Audi Camp [3]. "Tranquille chez moi" — home.
- Weekend outings (Q8): "À la plage" — not realistic. "En montagne, en forêt" — not realistic as a weekend. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — Moremi or Nxai Pan with a booked campsite [10][12]; malaria peak November–April (city file). "Je reste en ville" — Education Park, Nhabe Museum [3].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week
- Where to stay first: Motsebe Backpackers in Wenela (dorm P180) or Audi Camp in Matlapaneng (en-suite tents P490 for two with breakfast; camping P45 per person); Laha Hotel in Boseja for self-catering from BWP 550 [3]. Prices undated.
- Day 1: arrive by air (Air Botswana from Gaborone, Johannesburg, Kasane; Airlink from Cape Town and Johannesburg) [5], or by bus from Gaborone, 9–10 hours, P278 (2023) [3]. The terminal is in town [3].
- Day 2: SIM about BWP 5 with passport registration and a 10 GB plan ≈ BWP 247 (national, Gaborone city file). Mobile money: Orange Money or BTC Smega (national, Gaborone city file).
- Day 3: bank — ABSA, FNB, Stanbic and Standard Chartered had branches (2011 data) [4]; opening an account needs a work permit and proof of address (national rule, Gaborone city file), so a visa-free visitor generally cannot; use Wise.
- Day 4: house hunting — the market is informal, with deals often made directly between individuals (Expat.com, city file); TswanaHome showed no homes to rent in Maun (city file). Ask at lodges and offices (editorial). Deposit of one to two months plus the first month is the national norm reported for Gaborone (city file); not confirmed for Maun.
- Day 5: Maun Education Park; get a taxi driver's mobile number, since there is no dispatch [3].
- Day 6: Saturday — Market Night if it is the last Saturday (next: 28 November 2026, by the monthly rule in [7]); evening at Audi Camp [3].
- Day 7: Sunday — Motsana [3].
- Rough weekly spend: 7 nights × P180 = 1,260 [3]; SIM 5 + 10 GB 247; 4 taxis × P30 = 120 [3]; groceries 504 (Gaborone proxy) → ≈ BWP 2,136 (≈ €142), plus the bus (P278) or a flight (price not sourced).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". The Okavango flood peaks June–August, in the dry winter (city file); the dry season May–September is the easier time for Nxai Pan [12].

### One day
- Morning: Maun Education Park at opening, 2–3 hours, by combi Route 7 or on foot from the centre (entry fee not listed) [3].
- Midday: lunch at Hilary's Coffee Shop near the airport [3].
- Afternoon: scenic flight over the delta from Maun Airport — Kavango Air P1,500 for up to three plus P50 departure tax each; Moremi Air US$290 for up to five, tax included; Delta Air from US$700 per plane for 45 minutes [3].
- Evening: taxi (about P30) to Matlapaneng; dinner at Audi Camp or the Sedia Riverside Hotel [3].

### One week
- Day 1: as the one-day route.
- Day 2: the Nhabe Museum (hours not listed), the Old Mall and the Tsaro Street market [3].
- Day 3–4 (trip out): mokoro trip into the delta, often with an overnight bush camp, arranged by any Maun agency; Gumasema Adventures lists mokoro trips at Boro/Daunara (prices not listed) [3].
- Day 5 (trip out): Moremi Game Reserve day trip, about 80 km [3]; the reserve is on the delta's eastern side, explored by 4x4, on foot or by mokoro [10].
- Day 6: Motsana and the Arts Cafe in Matlapaneng; the Matlapaneng tree bridge [3].
- Day 7: daily-life moment — the morning market at the Tsaro Street bus station, where farmers sell at the kerb and combis leave for the outlying villages [3][4]; or, on the last Saturday of the month, the Maun Market Night among local makers [7].
- Longer option: Nxai Pan and Baines' Baobabs, only with a campsite booked at the DWNP office [12]; Tsodilo Hills via Shakawe, 6 hours by bus plus 40 km of graded road [11][14].
