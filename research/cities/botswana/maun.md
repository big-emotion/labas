# Maun, Botswana

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR figures use the dossier's indicative rate **15 BWP = 1 EUR** (not from a fetched source). Maun has almost no online rental market: the one local portal shows plots, offices and businesses but no homes to rent, and the Pam Golding Maun listing link opened on unrelated properties. Many prices below come from Wikivoyage, which is undated (its latest dated note is Sep 2025) and community-written. The web-search budget ran out after one Maun search, so national items are reused where they apply to Maun and are marked *(national)*.

- [cost.living] Street snack: magunya (fried dough balls) about 50 thebe each. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Most supermarkets sell ready-made local dishes; the market and bus station are on Tsaro Street. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Avis 4x4 hire costs P550/day (≈ €37) plus per-km and insurance charges. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Pump prices are set nationally: from 23 Oct 2026 unleaded 95 costs P19.75/l (≈ €1.32) and diesel P23.76/l (≈ €1.58) *(national)*. — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 2026-10-22, official, confidence: high
- [cost.negotiation] The Maun housing market is informal and deals are often made directly between private individuals. — Expat.com Gaborone guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: low
- [business.invest] Businesses listed for sale: a hotel at P7,500,000 (≈ €500,000); a bar and bottle store at P1,000,000 (≈ €67,000); a multi-residential plot at P6,000,000. — TswanaHome, Maun, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Maun is called the country's tourism capital: many safari and air-charter firms are headquartered here, and it supplies safari and mineral-exploration camps. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Lodge managers earn about BWP 15,000–25,000/month (≈ €1,000–1,670) *(national figure)*. — Expat.com Botswana guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Taxis have blue plates; a ride to the camps north of town costs about P30 (≈ €2), a combi into town about P3; combis run numbered routes from the bus station, Route 1 to the airport. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] There is no central taxi dispatch: you need a driver's mobile number for a pickup, and fewer taxis run at night because of animals on the road. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Bus to Gaborone takes 9–10 hours for P278 (2023, ≈ €18.50), paid on board; to Francistown about 6 hours for about P167. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Wikivoyage's Gaborone page gives a lower Francistown–Maun fare of P110 for the same 6-hour trip. — Wikivoyage "Gaborone", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Scheduled flights from Maun reach Gaborone, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Kasane, Victoria Falls and Windhoek (Air Botswana, Airlink, CemAir, Fastjet) and Addis Ababa (Ethiopian). — Wikipedia "Maun Airport", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The A3 highway links Maun to Francistown; donkeys and goats are common in town. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Mobile coverage reaches about 20–25 km beyond town, depending on weather (undated, possibly old). — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Maun's water comes from well fields along the Thamalakane and Shashe rivers. — Wikipedia "Thamalakane River", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The water utility flagged that a sewage line along the Thamalakane River could contaminate water if it leaks, and plans online leak sensors. — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 2026-05-21, official, confidence: high
- [daily.water] A P40 million treatment project for Ngamiland villages (Toteng, Sehithwa and others) was cancelled; 39 of 517 villages served nationally rely on water bowsers. — Botswana Daily News (BOPA), 2026-05-21, official, confidence: high
- [daily.power] The national grid had load shedding in March 2025 and again for two days in April 2026, after Morupule B breakdowns *(national)*. — Business Weekly & Review 2025-04-09 and AllAfrica 2026-04-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.money] Banks with ATMs (as of 2011, unsourced on the page): Barclays (now ABSA), FNB, Stanbic and Standard Chartered. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] Letsholathebe II Memorial is the main referral hospital; Maun Private Hospital on the Sedia road opened in July 2021. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Serious emergencies require evacuation to Gaborone (about 1h30 by air) or Johannesburg. — Expat.com Botswana guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Malaria is endemic around Maun, the Okavango and Chobe, with peak risk November to April. — Expat.com Botswana guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] There is no real international school: families use distance learning or boarding schools in Gaborone or South Africa. — Expat.com Botswana guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Both the UK and France name Maun, with Gaborone and Francistown, as places where violent crime is rising or vigilance is needed. — UK FCDO (accessed 2026-11-14) and France Diplomatie (2026-09-15), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Driving outside towns is dangerous because of stray wildlife and livestock, especially at night. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against bathing in rivers and lakes because of wild animals and waterborne disease. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] The UK notes a high number of sexual assaults nationally and advises women not to walk alone at night *(national)*. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Trekkers Nightclub is popular with locals; expatriates warn against it, though the guide calls it safe with reasonable caution. Bar 2000 is also listed. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [social.food] Cafés and restaurants named: Hilary's Coffee Shop (cheap, popular with locals), French Connection, RAW, Bon Arrivée and Audi Camp. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] The Maun Festival takes place in August. — Expat.com Gaborone guide, 2025-11-03, blog, confidence: low
- [social.pace] Maun is officially still a village (85,293 people in 2022) with a rural feel; cinder-block houses have largely replaced rondavels. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Maun Education Park, within walking distance of downtown, has giraffe, zebra, kudu, impala and wildebeest; red lechwe graze along the Thamalakane. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] The Okavango flood peaks in June–August, in the dry winter, when the delta swells to about three times its permanent size; the delta became a UNESCO site on 22 June 2014. — Wikipedia "Okavango Delta", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Scenic flights over the delta: Kavango Air charges P1,500 for up to three passengers plus P50 per person departure tax (≈ €100). — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Botswana's highest temperature, 44.0 °C, was recorded in Maun on 7 January 2016; about 452 mm of rain a year, mostly November–April, with June–September almost dry. — Wikipedia "Maun, Botswana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Winter (June–August) brings freezing mornings and hot afternoons; central Maun is walkable but usually hot. — Wikivoyage "Maun", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] The UK flags flash-flood risk from November to March, with roads and border crossings possibly closed at short notice. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
