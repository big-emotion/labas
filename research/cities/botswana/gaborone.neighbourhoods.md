# Gaborone: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Exchange rate: 15 BWP = 1 EUR, the dossier's *indicative* rate (not from a fetched source). Source numbers in brackets refer to "Sources read" below. "City file" = `research/cities/botswana/gaborone.md`; items cited "(city file)" keep the source and date given there.

Research-pass note: the session-wide WebSearch budget ran out after three searches in this pass, so most places come from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, the Alliance Française site, a 2026 relocation blog, Business Weekly). Wikivoyage Gaborone is community-written and mostly undated (latest dated note Oct 2025); its prices are flagged as such. Commute times between suburbs were not found in any source, so distances are given where sourced and times are left out.

Citywide watch (applies to every neighbourhood below, not repeated in each block):
- The UK says violent crime is rising in Gaborone; cars are targeted at traffic lights, restaurants have been robbed at peak hours, armed gangs burgle houses; women are advised not to walk alone at night (FCDO, undated page read 2026-11-14, city file).
- France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) reports water cuts of varying frequency and length in Gaborone and its region (city file).
- National load shedding: rotational cuts from 22 March 2025; two days of national shedding from 21 April 2026 (Business Weekly 2025-04-09; AllAfrica/Daily News 2026-04-23, city file). No Gaborone report after April 2026 was found.
- Homes: walled plot, lockable gates and a guard or alarm are recommended (Expat.com 2025-11-03, city file).
- Neighbourhood-level crime data: not found (city file gap). Wikivoyage names pickpocketing areas (Old Naledi, Mogoditshane, Broadhurst, Phase I and II), but it is an undated community wiki, so it is not used as a Watch item here.

## Neighbourhoods

### Phakalane (and Phakalane Golf Estate)
- Feel: commercial and residential suburb north of the city [5]; a 1991 estate development meant, its developer says, for citizens and non-citizens alike, now adding flats to shed a wealthy-only image (Business Weekly, 2021-11-05) [6]. A 2026 relocation blog describes a secure gated golf estate with villas, gardens and pools, popular with families [7]; Expat.com also lists it among family suburbs (city file).
- Rent: 1-bed flat in Phase 1 ≈ BWP 5,500 (≈ €367); furnished air-conditioned 1-bed ≈ BWP 9,500 (≈ €633); 3-bed flat in a gated complex ≈ BWP 8,000 (≈ €533) — Pam Golding, asking, read 2026-11-14, rand converted by the city file. Golf-estate villas BWP 15,000–30,000 (≈ €1,000–2,000) — Expat.com 2025-11-03 (city file); the blog gives BWP 15,000–25,000 for villas with pools [7].
- Getting around: about 12.5 km north of the centre by the A1 [5]. Shared taxi P10, combi P9, special taxi P36 (official fares after the April 2026 reversal, Daily News 2026-04-06, city file). Car-dependent in practice (editorial, from distance).

### Tlokweng and the Riverwalk side (east, across the Notwane)
- Feel: peri-urban village east of Gaborone, part of the conurbation, with an older unplanned area and a newer planned one; people move there for jobs in the capital; 35,982 people in 2011 [8]. Tribal-land rules restrict purchases by non-citizens [7].
- Rent: no Tlokweng rent found. Nearest proxy: Wise's "outside the centre" 1-bed ≈ BWP 2,740 (≈ €182), which the city file's spot-check could not verify (Wise URL 404) — use with caution.
- Getting around: on the road to the South African border, which is about 15 km east [8]; Riverwalk Mall sits on the Tlokweng Road, with Oasis Motel 2 km further [3].
- Watch: in 2022 Greater Gaborone including Tlokweng had been "running dry taps for years" (Business Weekly, 2022-03-02, old; city file).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". French and EU passport holders enter visa-free for up to 90 days a year (France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, dossier).

### One day
- Morning: Main Mall on foot, then the Three Dikgosi Monument in the CBD and the National Museum and Art Gallery on Independence Road, both just north-west of the Mall [4][12][13]. Entry fees and hours not found.
- Midday: white-box lunch in the government district (P10–20) [3], or SPAR on the Main Mall [3].
- Afternoon: special taxi (P36) to Game City, then Kgale Hill — Wikivoyage suggests weekday late afternoons, 16:00–18:00, and warns of baboons; free [3][11].
- Evening: Gaborone Yacht Club at the dam on a Wednesday or Friday (P10 entry) [3], or Caravela [3]. A cab home rather than walking at night [3]; FCDO also advises licensed blue-plate taxis (city file).

### One week
- Day 1: city centre as above.
- Day 2: Mokolodi Nature Reserve, about 10 km south — game drive, reptile park, vulture aviary [10][3].
- Day 3: Phakalane and Oodi Weavers (weekdays only, 10:00–16:30; tapestries about P1,000) [3]; evening at Tounel or a Phakalane mall [6] (city file).
- Day 4 (trip out): Otse, about 55 km south — Manyelanong Game Reserve's Cape vultures and Baratani Hill [3].
- Day 5–6 (trip out): Khama Rhino Sanctuary, 25 km outside Serowe, overnight (distance from Gaborone, transport and fees not sourced) [18]. Alternative across the border, a little over 10 km away: Pilanesberg National Park and Sun City in South Africa [3].
- Day 7: daily-life moment — a weekday lunch-hour among civil servants buying white-box lunches in the government district, then a combi ride (P9) at the evening rush [3] (city file); or a Township Rollers or Gaborone United home match at the National Stadium (fixtures and ticket prices not found) [14]. On a Friday, an Alliance Française film night [15].
