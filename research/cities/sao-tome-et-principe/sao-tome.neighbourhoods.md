# São Tomé: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = 24.5 STN** (fixed euro peg, dossier [D]); USD at US$1 ≈ 21.4 STN ≈ €0.87, as in the city file. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/sao-tome-et-principe/sao-tome.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/sao-tome-et-principe.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after three queries in this pass. Everything else comes from pages opened directly: Wikivoyage, Wikipedia (en/pt/fr), two July 2026 relocation-blog articles, a tour operator's restaurant list, a backpacker guide and Téla Nón's on-site search. Wikivoyage's city listings date from 2017–2023 and may be stale [5]. The backpacker guide [7] gives euro price bands without listing methods (low confidence). The Saiga Tours restaurant prices [8] are in dobras, but its own euro conversions do not match the peg (180 STN shown as €13 when it is €7.35), so this file re-converts its dobra figures at the peg.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Crime is generally low, but burglaries and armed robberies occur. The FCDO advises carrying little cash and leaving valuables at home when going to the beach (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]. France Diplomatie keeps the whole archipelago at yellow "vigilance renforcée", reports armed robbery and pickpocketing with extra care in markets, and advises avoiding demonstrations (updated 2026-09-15) [C safety.general]. Téla Nón reported an unprecedented wave of robberies and home invasions on the island (2026-01-01) [C safety.general]. **No source rates individual neighbourhoods** [C Gaps].
- Power: national capacity was 12.8 MW against 17 MW demand in February 2026 (Téla Nón) [C daily.power]. Cuts reached 12 h a day in August 2025 (IMF via Immigrant Invest), and a generator costs US$500–1,500 (≈ €440–1,310) [C daily.power]. Cuts also stop pumped water [C daily.power].
- Fuel: gasoline ran out for more than 15 days in October 2026. Taxis, moto-taxis and water vans stopped [C daily.transport].
- Transport is taxis, shared yellow minivans and moto-taxis only. The FCDO calls them often unsafe and in poor condition [C daily.transport]. Bands from a backpacker guide (2026-11-02, low): shared taxi or minibus €0.40–2; private taxi in town €2–4; moto-taxi €0.50–2; driver or rental car €60–100 a day [7]. The main taxi rank is next to the Municipal Market (Wikivoyage, undated) [5].
- Cash economy, and no ATM takes overseas cards [C daily.money]. Fibre covers only parts of the city, so a backup SIM is advised [C daily.internet].

## Neighbourhoods

### Cidade / Baixa and the Avenida Marginal (centre, Ana Chaves Bay)
- Feel: the administrative, commercial and cultural heart, with government offices, banks, shops and restaurants. One relocation blog says it suits singles, couples and short-assignment staff who want to do everything on foot, and calls the Mercado Grande "lively and sometimes chaotic" (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3][4]. Expat.com names downtown and the Ana Chaves waterfront among expat areas [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 1-bed downtown about €233/month (range €100–300, ≈ 2,450–7,350 STN). 3-bed downtown about €575 (range €400–750, ≈ 9,800–18,375 STN). Both are Numbeo April 2025 figures via Expat.com, 2025-10-21 [C cost.rent]. "Expat-standard" homes €1,000–3,000 (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3]. Guesthouse rooms in the centre "from about €15 to 35 a night" (backpacker guide, 2026-11-02, low) [7].
- Getting around: walking. Taxi rank at the Municipal Market [5]. Private taxi in town €2–4 [7]. Airport taxi about US$5 (≈ 107 STN, €4.40) (Expat.com) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: France Diplomatie advises extra care against pickpocketing in markets (2026-09-15) [C safety.general]. A relocation blog calls the centre exposed to flooding but says it "benefits from better drainage projects" (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, commercial blog, low) [3].
- Places:
  - Mercado Grande / Mercado Municipal (main covered food market; Lonely Planet describes two adjacent halls, the Mercado Municipal and the newer Mercado Novo; entry free, snacks under €2) — Wikipedia [11][12]; Lonely Planet listing (search snippet) [23]; backpacker guide [7]
  - Sé Catedral de Nossa Senhora da Graça (Catholic cathedral, free) — Wikipedia [12]; Wikivoyage [5]; backpacker guide [7]
  - Museu Nacional in the Forte de São Sebastião (museum; entry about €2–3) — Wikipedia [12]; backpacker guide [7]
  - Xico's Café, Rua Patrice Lumumba, next to the National Museum (café: coffee 30–50 STN ≈ €1.20–2.05; sandwiches 120–220 STN ≈ €4.90–9) — Saiga Tours, 2025-07-11 [8]; Expat.com also uses it as a landmark for exchange offices [C daily.money]
  - Hotel Central rooftop bar, Rua de Angola (bar-restaurant: mains 180–500 STN ≈ €7.35–20.40, cocktails 150–250 STN ≈ €6.10–10.20) — Saiga Tours [8]; Wikivoyage [5]
  - Xquizeat, Rua de Angola near the Presidential Palace (modern São Toméan cuisine: mains 300–500 STN ≈ €12.25–20.40) — Saiga Tours [8]
  - Café Camões (seafood, petiscos, coffee and Wi-Fi; Fri–Sat until 01:00) — Wikivoyage, listing dated Aug 2019 [5]
  - Ossobó (non-profit craft, coffee and chocolate shop opposite the fort) — Wikivoyage country page [6]

### Pantufo (south-east seafront)
- Feel: one relocation blog calls it an "upscale" residential area with more space, beach access, sometimes guards and power or water backup, chosen by expat families and long-term executives (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3]. A backpacker guide calls it a quiet neighbourhood with beach bars, a few minutes south of the centre (2026-11-02, low) [7]. It is a fishing and beach locality with social housing: 25 social houses were planned there from 2015 (Téla Nón, 2014-05-16) [17].
- Rent: standard local 3-bed ≈ €450/month; "premium local" 2-bed plus one ≈ €850; expat-standard villa or flat €1,000–3,000 (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3]. Guesthouse rooms around €20–40 a night (backpacker guide, low) [7].
- Getting around: "a short drive from the centre". A personal vehicle or regular driver is advised, especially at night (Jarnias) [3]. Private taxi €2–4 in town [7].
- Watch: a cargo ship ran aground on Pantufo beach in May 2014 (Téla Nón) [17]. No recent area-specific safety, flood or power report found.
- Places:
  - Pantufo beach — Téla Nón, 2014-05-21 [17]; backpacker guide (beach bars, unnamed) [7]
  - Vila de Bombom primary school (public; over 1,500 pupils in 2015, named in a headline alongside Pantufo and São Marçal schools; exact locality not confirmed) — Téla Nón, 2015-03-26 [17]
  - Inter de Bombom (football club; plays national-league matches against Vitória do Riboque in 2025–2026) — Téla Nón, 2026-10-12 [18]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple" (space, sea, near the centre); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Campo de Milho (near the central hospital)
- Feel: Téla Nón called it an "elite neighbourhood" in 2010 and blamed its water use for a shortage at the central hospital, Ayres de Menezes. An outdoor sports space was planned there in 2012 [19]. Both reports are old, and no recent description was found.
- Rent: no figure. The nearest proxy is the expat-standard €1,000–3,000 band (Jarnias, low) [3]. Do not treat that as a Campo de Milho price.
- Getting around: not documented. City taxi bands apply [7].
- Watch: water competition with the hospital (Téla Nón, 2010-04-29, old) [19].
- Places:
  - Hospital Ayres de Menezes (national reference hospital, limited capacity) — Wikipedia [11]; Expat.com [C health.care]; Téla Nón ties it to this area [19]

- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (hospital nearby); Q3 "Le prix compte peu". A lead to check on the ground, not a recommendation.

### Riboque and the peripheral belt (Água Porca, Boa Morte, Bobô Fôrro)
- Feel: Riboque is a listed city locality [11] and home of Vitória FC [11][13]. The club led the national league in 2026 and got diaspora backing for its season (Téla Nón, 2026-01-07 and 2026-10-12) [18]. Água Porca, Boa Morte, Bobô Fôrro and Penha are what Téla Nón calls peripheral neighbourhoods [C daily.water]. A relocation blog describes the suburbs in general as cheaper but simpler, with more trips to the centre (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [4].
- Rent: 1-bed outside the centre €163/month (range €90–200, ≈ 2,200–4,900 STN), Numbeo April 2025 via Expat.com [C cost.rent]. Suburban local 1-bed "around $100 to $200" (≈ €87–174) (Jarnias, low) [4]. Modelled cheap 1-bed US$183 (≈ €160) (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared taxi or minibus €0.40–2, moto-taxi €0.50–2 [7]. About 6,000 moto-taxi riders work in the trade (Téla Nón, 2026-10-23) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: in October 2026 the piped network in Bobô Fôrro, Água Porca, Penha and Boa Morte had been dry for some time; Bobô Fôrro residents said three months. Residents bought water from vans at more than 1,000 STN per 1,000 litres (≈ €41) (Téla Nón, 2026-10-20) [C daily.water]. The vans stopped during the fuel shortage [C daily.transport].
- Places: none of the everyday places could be sourced to these areas, apart from the club:
  - Vitória FC (Vitória do Riboque) (football club; home games at the Estádio Nacional 12 de Julho) — Wikipedia [11][14]; Téla Nón [18]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré", for someone who speaks Portuguese and accepts water and power self-reliance; Q8 "Je reste en ville" (league football). Not a first base for a newcomer.

### Trindade (Mé-Zóchi, 7 km inland)
- Feel: seat of Mé-Zóchi district, 7 km south-west of the capital at 254 m, with 16,140 people in 2012. A secondary school for 720 pupils opened in 2011 [15]. The dossier calls Mé-Zóchi the nearest rural option, green and cheaper, which suits remote workers with their own power backup (Immigrant Invest) [D]. A relocation blog calls it a cooler "green belt" of plantations, with longer trips to the hospital, private schools and supermarkets (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3].
- Rent: no Trindade figure. livingcost.org covers Trindade separately, but its figures were not pulled into the city file [C Sources]. Use the outside-centre 1-bed €163 as a reference only [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared taxi to Trindade and Bom Sucesso "around €1 to 2" (backpacker guide, low) [7].
- Watch: the same blog says roads are prone to landslides and heavy rain, and that power and water cuts "may be even more frequent" than in the city (Jarnias, commercial blog, low) [3]. On New Year's Eve 2026 a surveillance camera fired on a burglar in Belém-Trindade (Téla Nón, 2026-01-01) [24].
- Places:
  - Trindade FC (football club) — Wikipedia [15]; Téla Nón (lost to Riboque, 2026-05-25) [18]
  - Trindade secondary school (opened 2011, 720 places) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Bom Sucesso botanical garden (uphill from Trindade; entry about €2–5) — backpacker guide, 2026-11-02 [7]; Jarnias [3]
  - Roça Monte Café (colonial coffee plantation with a coffee museum) — Wikivoyage [6]; Jarnias [3]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (only with a generator and a backup SIM); Q8 "En montagne, en forêt"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Guadalupe (Lobata, 11 km north-west)
- Feel: seat of Lobata district, about 11 km north-west of the capital, with 7,604 people in 2012. Lagoa Azul is listed in its geography [16]. Expat.com names Guadalupe among the areas expats choose [C housing.areas]. A relocation blog calls it "quieter", suited to remote workers who go into town occasionally (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low) [3][4].
- Rent: no Guadalupe figure found.
- Getting around: the EN-1 road to the capital was recently rehabilitated, but travel time depends on the weather (Jarnias) [3]. Shared taxi to Lagoa Azul €1–3; private €10–15 (backpacker guide, low) [7].
- Watch: exposed to heavy rain, flooding and coastal erosion (Jarnias, commercial blog, low) [3].
- Places:
  - Lagoa Azul (bay with snorkelling; lighthouse) — Wikipedia [16]; backpacker guide [7]; Outlook Travel [9]

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Pantufo**. One blog names it as where expat families settle, with space and the beach close by [3]. Campo de Milho is the hospital-side alternative, but it is thinly sourced [19].
- Schools: all teach the Portuguese curriculum. The options are Escola Portuguesa de STP, Escola Internacional de STP, Instituto Diocesano João Paulo II, Escola Bambino and Colégio Lusíada [C family.schools][4]. None of their locations were found. There is no IB, British, US or French school, and expat families often homeschool or use online programmes [C family.schools].
- Week:
  - Mon: school run by taxi or driver [7]. One parent shops at Supermercado Nacional or Padrão [10].
  - Tue: school. Afternoon on Pantufo beach [17].
  - Wed: school. Paediatric check-up at Centro Médico Gnóstica or Hospital Ayres de Menezes [C health.care].
  - Thu: school. Mercado Grande after drop-off [7].
  - Fri: school. Family dinner at 90 Graus Esplanada (pizzas) [8].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: a **guesthouse in the centre** (€15–35 a night, backpacker guide 2026-11-02, low) [7]. Immigrant Invest advises 2–4 weeks in a guesthouse before signing anything [C housing.search]. Pestana Miramar is the upmarket fallback (from €155, 2019 listing) [5].
- Entry: EU passports are visa-free for 15 days [C admin.visa]. A trial of several months needs an e-visa through the SMF portal: 7 working days, €100/day proof of funds, fee paid at the border [D]. Tourist e-visa €20 for 30 days; business e-visa €60 for 6 months, multiple entry [C admin.visa].
- Day by day:
  - Day 1: arrive at TMS. Airport taxi about US$5 (≈ €4.40) [C daily.transport]. Bring €500–1,000 in cash [C daily.money].
  - Day 2: money. Exchange offices near Xico's Café [C daily.money]. Street changers outside Hotel Central quoted 25 STN per €1 (Wikivoyage, undated) [5]. A Visa withdrawal may work at the main bank [C daily.money].
  - Day 2: SIM. CST or Unitel, at the airport or in their shops (no price found) [10]. Mobile 10 GB about €24.50/month [C cost.living]. **No mobile-money service was found** [C Gaps].
  - Days 3–5: flat hunt. Use word of mouth, local contacts and Facebook expat groups; there are no real agencies or portals [C housing.search][4]. Visit the centre and Pantufo. Check water pressure, the storage tank, the generator or inverter, and real internet speed [C housing.search]. No viewing or agency fee was found.
  - Day 6: costs before moving in. The deposit is 1–3 months, often in cash [C cost.negotiation]. For a €233 1-bed, deposit plus first month comes to €466–932. Equipping an unfurnished flat costs US$2,000–5,000 (≈ €1,750–4,370) [C cost.living].
  - Day 6: bank account. You need a passport, proof of address and proof of income [C daily.money].
  - Day 7: Sunday at the Mercado Grande or the Sé, then Lagoa Azul by shared taxi [7].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Envy Bar STP (2019) [5].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": the Hotel Central rooftop, in the centre within walking distance of the guesthouses [8].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": at the guesthouse.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Lagoa Azul [7].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Bom Sucesso [7].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from São Tomé.
  - "Je reste en ville": the Museu Nacional [7].

  - Guesthouse: 7 × €15–35 = €105–245 [7]

  - Meals: 7 × €8–15/day = €56–105 [7]
  - Local transport: 7 × €2–5 = €14–35 [7]

  - **Total ≈ €204–414**, before any deposit.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". The dry season (gravana, June–September) is the comfortable window [D].

### One day
- Morning: start early at the **Mercado Grande** (free; snacks under €2) [7][23]. Walk to the **Sé Catedral** (free) [12], then the **Museu Nacional** in the Forte de São Sebastião (≈ €2–3) [7]. Buy coffee or chocolate at **Ossobó**, opposite the fort [6]. All on foot.
- Midday: lunch at **Xico's Café**, next to the museum (light meal 120–220 STN ≈ €4.90–9) [8].
- Afternoon: walk the **Avenida Marginal** along Ana Chaves Bay (free) [7]. Swim at **Praia Gamboa** [7], or take a taxi (€2–4) to Pantufo's beach bars [7].
- Evening: sundown at the **Hotel Central rooftop** (cocktails 150–250 STN) [8]. Dinner at **Xquizeat** next door on Rua de Angola, or **Papa-Figo** by taxi [8].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day city route above.
- Day 2, north coast: shared taxi towards Guadalupe [16][7]. See **Roça Agostinho Neto** [9], swim and snorkel at **Lagoa Azul** (shared €1–3, private €10–15) [7], and **Praia das Conchas** [16]. Back for dinner at Papa-Figo [8].
- Day 3, inland: **Trindade**, then **Bom Sucesso** (entry €2–5) and **Roça Monte Café** with its coffee museum [7][6]. Shared taxi €1–2 [7]. The Cascata de São Nicolau needs a 4×4 [5].
- Day 4, daily life: be at the Mercado Grande at opening [7]. Ride a shared yellow minivan for a short hop and see how the morning commute and moto-taxis work [5][C daily.transport]. Afternoon at CACAU (exhibitions; check its programme) [20]. On a match day, watch Vitória do Riboque or Sporting Praia Cruz at the **Estádio Nacional 12 de Julho** [14][18].
- Day 5, the south: a driver for the day (€50–90, split) [7]. Stops at **Roça Água-Izé** (shared taxi €2–4 each way) [7], **Boca do Inferno** [6], lunch at **Roça São João dos Angolares** (fixed menu; rooms from €60, 2017) [6], then **Praia Jalé or Praia Inhame** (turtle beaches) [7].
- Day 6, two options. Rest day in town: Museu Nacional, Praia Gamboa, Cesar Café on a Saturday [5][7]. Or fly to Príncipe: return flight US$180–220 (≈ €157–192), about 30 minutes [C daily.transport]; see `santo-antonio.neighbourhoods.md`.
- Day 7, Sunday: Mass at the Sé Catedral, a real neighbourhood moment [12]. Then a last Lagoa Azul swim, or a talk at the Aliança Francesa if one is on [21].
- What to watch while deciding:
  - Whether the power and water hold for a week [C daily.power].
  - How long the fuel queues are [C daily.transport].
  - Whether your Portuguese gets you through the market [D].
