- [work.jobs] Neves hosts some of the island's few industrial sites: a deep-water fuel port (2012 deal with Nigeria; a 400 m jetty for two Panamax ships) and the Rosema brewery. — Wikipedia "Neves", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The Neves fish dock, funded by the African Development Bank, had been out of service since December 2024 when fishers and fish sellers (palaiês) pressed the minister in April 2025; its refrigeration had failed. - [work.jobs] A Portuguese-funded vocational training centre (three classrooms) was due to start construction in Neves in October 2025 under the ERGUES project. — Téla Nón, 2025-10-10, local-media, confidence: medium

- [daily.power] (national) The power crisis had lasted six months by February 2026, with capacity of 12.8 MW against demand of 17 MW. — Téla Nón, 2026-02-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Neves is linked to the capital by the EN1 road. — Expat.com guide, 2025-10-21, company, confidence: medium

- [daily.transport] The Lembá river bridge, the country's largest, fell in a December 2021 storm; a US$11.3 million rebuild had its first stone in June 2025 but had not started by October 2026. The stand-in crossing floods when it rains, and people carry goods across on foot. — Téla Nón, 2026-10-07, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] (national) Roads outside the capital are often poor and unlit, with animals on the road at night and landslides after heavy rain. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high

- [daily.transport] (national) In October 2026 gasoline ran out for more than 15 days, stopping taxis, moto-taxis and fishers. — Téla Nón, 2026-10-20, local-media, confidence: high
