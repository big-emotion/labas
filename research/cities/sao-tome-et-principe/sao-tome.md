# São Tomé, São Tomé and Príncipe

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **€1 = 24.5 STN** (fixed euro peg since 2010), rounded. Sources quoting only USD are converted at **US$1 ≈ 21.4 STN** (Wikivoyage São Tomé and Príncipe, rate dated August 2026), so US$1 ≈ €0.87. Expat.com quotes some figures in pre-2018 "old dobras" (1,000 old = 1 new STN); those are flagged. Research-depth caveat: the session's shared web-search budget was exhausted on the first search, so this file relies on pages opened directly (dossier URLs, Téla Nón articles and its on-site search, Expat.com guide and forum, livingcost.org, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, official advisories). Numbeo (429), Expatistan (403) and the US State Department page (403) could not be opened; no São Tomé property portal or agency site could be found without search.
