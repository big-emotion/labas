# Santo António: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = 24.5 STN** (fixed euro peg, dossier [D]); USD at US$1 ≈ 21.4 STN ≈ €0.87, as in the city file. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/sao-tome-et-principe/santo-antonio.md`; `[CS tag]` = an item in the São Tomé city file; `[D]` = the dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget was spent after one query on Príncipe. Places come from Wikipedia (en/pt), Wikivoyage, Timbuktu Travel, the Principe Collection site, pousadas.st, a relocation blog and Téla Nón's on-site search. **No source gives a single price for rent, food, taxis or restaurants in Santo António.** Every spend line below is therefore built from São Tomé prices with a dated multiplier, or from a single low-confidence blog band, and is flagged as such.

Scale first: Santo António is a town, not a city. It had 2,620 people at the 2012 census, or about 1,500 according to Expat.com (2025). The whole island had about 8,420 in 2018 [C social.pace]. It is the island's only town [5]. "Neighbourhoods" below are the town itself, its bay edge, the airport road and the plantation coast where lodge jobs are.

Frames that apply everywhere:
- Burglaries: in October 2026 a growing wave of burglaries of homes and shops worried residents, who asked for more police patrols. They linked it to night-time power cuts that leave areas dark (Téla Nón, 2026-10-29) [C safety.general]. National advice applies: crime is generally low, but burglaries and armed robberies happen (FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14), and France Diplomatie keeps yellow "vigilance renforcée" (2026-09-15) [C safety.general].
- Supply: the inter-island cargo ship Rehoboth sank at the island's only port on 26 Aug 2026 and blocked it, and fuel queues followed (Téla Nón) [C daily.transport]. In 2021, island businesses said consumer goods cost three to five times São Tomé prices [C cost.living].
- Water: in the September 2026 campaign an opposition leader claimed only part of Santo António gets water, every other day. The ruling party disputed this (Téla Nón, 2026-09-25, a campaign claim) [C daily.water]. Drinking water returned to the town's taps in March 2012 after about three months without it (Téla Nón, 2012-03-05) [14].
- Power: the regional plant ran at reduced output in the March 2024 crisis [C daily.power]. A solar park at Azeitona was being tendered in October 2026 [C daily.power].
- Cash: there is no ATM for foreign cards in the country, so bring cash from São Tomé [C daily.money].
- Health: Hospital de Príncipe is very limited, and complex cases go via São Tomé [C health.care].

## Neighbourhoods

### Town centre (Cidade de Santo António)
- Feel: a small colonial town, the colonial capital from 1753 to 1852, known for its colonial architecture and churches [C social.culture][5]. In 2024 the regional president sought UCCLA co-funding to restore its heritage buildings (Téla Nón, 2024-10-26) [C social.culture]. Most of the island's residents live in or around it (Timbuktu Travel, undated) [8]. The pace is slow, and the social circle is very small [D].
- Rent: **no figure from any source** [C Gaps]. One relocation blog says Príncipe offers guesthouses at €1,000–2,500 a month or ultra-luxury at €12,000+, "with virtually no mid-range option" (Jarnias, 2026-07-24, commercial blog, low) [3][4]. Treat that as lodging pricing, not a rent market. Wikivoyage mentions several small pousadas in town, without names or prices [6].
- Getting around: on foot in town. The airport is 3 km north [5]. Outside town, hire a driver with a car or motorbike, or a boat; roads are few and often unpaved (Wikivoyage) [C daily.transport]. No fare found.
- Watch: the October 2026 burglary wave (Téla Nón, 2026-10-29) [C safety.general]; water supply only in parts of town, every other day (campaign claim, 2026-09-25) [C daily.water].
- Places:
  - Igreja de Nossa Senhora da Conceição (Catholic church) — Wikipedia [5][7]
  - Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Rosário (Catholic church) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Palácio do Governo Regional (regional government seat) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Estádio Regional 13 de Junho (the island's only sports ground; home of Sporting Clube do Príncipe and GD Os Operários) — Wikipedia [5]; [C social.sport]
  - Hospital de Príncipe (only hospital, very limited) — Expat.com [C health.care]
  - Centro de Dia e Lar de Idosos São Francisco (elderly day centre; 9 residents, 67 daily meals) — Téla Nón, 2026-10-14 [C community.diaspora]
  - Escola Internacional de Santo António, EISA (small private school, mostly in English, mainly for younger children) — Jarnias, 2026-07-24, low [4]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple" for someone posted to a job here; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (emigrants return every August for the culture month) [C social.culture].

### Ponta da Mina and the bay
- Feel: the headland at the entrance to Santo António bay, with the ruined 1695 fort [5][7]. The regional president promised to start the island's first mooring port in Santo António bay in the next term (Téla Nón, 2026-09-25) [C daily.transport]. Nobody describes it as a residential area.
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: on foot from the centre (no distance given).
- Watch: the port blockage after the Rehoboth sinking (2026-08-26) [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Fortaleza de Santo António da Ponta da Mina (fort ruin, built 1695 and destroyed in 1706) — Wikipedia [5][7]
  - Santo António Bay beach (town-bay beach; listed by Expedia as a place to visit) — Expedia place page title seen in search results only, low [11]
- Fits: Q8 "Je reste en ville" (a walk); a viewpoint, not a place to live.

### Airport road (Aeroporto locality, north)
- Feel: "Aeroporto" is a listed locality of Pagué district [5]. Príncipe Airport is 3 km north of town and has a 1,750 m runway. Its only flights go to São Tomé, and it is not certified for instrument flight [C daily.transport]. No description of residents was found.
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: 3 km to town; driver or motorbike hire [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Príncipe Airport, PCP (domestic flights to São Tomé; return US$180–220 ≈ €157–192) — Wikipedia [5]; Expat.com [C daily.transport]
- Fits: Q1 "Un pied ici, un pied là-bas" (shortest trip to the capital's flights). Otherwise a lead only.

### Plantation coast: Sundy, Belo Monte, Ponta do Sol (west and north)
- Feel: restored roças and beach lodges, where ecotourism jobs are [C nature.access][8]. Sundy and Porto Real are the island's smaller settlements besides the town [7]. This is the luxury-tourism side of the island, not a rental market.
- Rent: no residential rent. Lodge rates per person per night from Timbuktu Travel (undated): Roça Sundy from US$370 (≈ €322), Belo Monte from US$310 (≈ €270), Bom Bom from US$460 (≈ €400), Sundy Praia from US$920 (≈ €800) [8]. Roça Sundy was "temporarily closed for refurbishment" on the operator's site [9]. Pousada Roça Ponta do Sol, about 8 km from town: four rooms, a shared bathroom and no electricity; no price [10].
- Getting around: driver or boat [C daily.transport]. Ponta do Sol is about 8 km from the airport and town [10].
- Watch: nothing sourced.
- Places:
  - Roça Sundy (UNESCO-listed plantation; Eddington's plaque for the 1919 eclipse test of relativity; a science space chosen there in 2024) — Wikivoyage [6]; Téla Nón [C social.culture]
  - Belo Monte (plantation hotel with a restaurant, above Banana Beach) — Timbuktu Travel [8]; Wikivoyage [6]
  - Banana Beach (beach below Belo Monte) — Timbuktu Travel [8]; Wikivoyage [6]
  - Praia Grande (turtle-watching November–April) — Timbuktu Travel [8]
  - Pousada Roça Ponta do Sol ("Casa do João", former overseer's house, near Banana Beach and Baía das Agulhas) — pousadas.st [10]
  - Bom Bom Island Resort (lodge with restaurant and bar, reached by a 230 m walkway) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Claudio Corallo cocoa plantation (factory tours on request) — Wikivoyage [6]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (lodge and conservation work) [D]; Q8 "À la plage" and "En montagne, en forêt"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" for anyone staying rather than working.

  - Day 5: Roça Sundy [6].
  - Day 6: a match at the Estádio 13 de Junho if one is on [5].
  - Day 7: Sunday Mass [5].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". July is the driest month and October the wettest [C climate.daily]. Turtles nest November–April [8]. The culture month and the Auto de Floripes (15 Aug) bring emigrants home [C social.culture].

### One day
- Morning: land at Príncipe Airport and drive the 3 km into town [5]. Walk past Nossa Senhora da Conceição, Nossa Senhora do Rosário and the regional palace [5].
- Midday: walk out to the Fortaleza da Ponta da Mina at the bay entrance [5]. For lunch, no town restaurant could be sourced, so use a lodge restaurant (Belo Monte) [8].
- Afternoon: Banana Beach, below Belo Monte [8].
- Evening: dinner at a lodge (Belo Monte or Bom Bom) [8][6]. Move between places with a hired driver, the only option sourced [C daily.transport]. No entry fees found.

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route.
- Day 2: Roça Sundy and the Eddington eclipse plaque [6]. Driver needed.
- Day 3: the Obô do Príncipe natural park, towards the Pico do Príncipe (947 m) [7]. A guide is advisable; no operator or price was sourced.
- Day 4, daily life: a morning in town, then an evening match of Sporting Clube do Príncipe or GD Os Operários at the Estádio 13 de Junho, if one is scheduled [5]. Or, in August, the Auto de Floripes street play [C social.culture].
- Day 5: Claudio Corallo's plantation (tour on request) [6], then Praia Grande, with turtles November–April [8].
- Day 6: Pousada Roça Ponta do Sol area, Banana Beach and Baía das Agulhas [10].
- Day 7, Sunday Mass in town: watch who is there and how small the community really is [5]. Fly back to São Tomé.
- What to note while deciding: how often power and water drop; whether your phone has data; how many flights a week actually run [C daily.power][C daily.water][5].
