## Items

- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives 1-bed rents of US$130–220/month in Kezira/centre (≈ €113–191) and US$90–160 in Sabian (≈ €78–139); no method or sources stated. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same blog puts a single person's budget at US$209–403/month (≈ €182–351) and says air-conditioning adds US$10–20 to the power bill in the hot season. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] A peace-research study says new settlement patterns are making neighbourhoods more ethnically homogeneous, sharpening fault lines. — Rift Valley Institute, 2025, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The study describes an informal economy of bajaj drivers, labour associations, informal traders and contraband dealers, with rural migrants competing with local youth for scarce jobs. — Rift Valley Institute, 2025, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] The Dire Dawa Free Trade Zone opened in August 2022 on 150 ha (expandable to 4,000 ha), with 15 factory sheds, linked to the Djibouti railway and 2.8 km from the dry port. — Wikipedia "Dire Dawa Free Trade Zone", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In June 2026 the city faced a critical drinking-water shortage: a normal 50 % gap between demand and supply, rotating distribution, pumps damaged by wind and frequent power cuts. — Borkena (citing DW Amharic), 2026-06-28, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Hotel owners said they pay over Br5,000 (≈ €27) for a water tanker that lasts under two days. — Borkena (citing DW Amharic), 2026-06-28, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The water utility said frequent power outages worsen supply because pumps need electricity. — Borkena (citing DW Amharic), 2026-06-28, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] A travel wiki says Addis-Abeba and Dire Dawa have the best internet service in the country. — Wikivoyage "Ethiopia", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Addis train now runs overnight with sleeper cabins (as of Nov 2025), every second day, about 11 hours; the station is ~10 km out at Melka Jebdu and a bajaj into town costs about Br900 (≈ €4.9). — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The 2018 launch tariff was Br308 for a seat from Addis and Br410–821 for bunks (≈ €1.7–4.4 today; old prices). — The Reporter, 2018-01-01, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The airport is Ethiopia's second largest, with several Ethiopian Airlines flights a day and Air Djibouti service. — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.money] ATMs at Dashen Bank and Ogaden Bank (undated listing). — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [health.care] FCDO says medical facilities outside Addis-Abeba are "extremely poor"; Dire Dawa (1,276 m) lies in the malaria-risk band below 2,000 m. — UK FCDO health page / Wikipedia "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Researchers describe the peace since the 2019–2020 violence as "fragile"; residents contest the informal 40:40:20 power-sharing deal between Oromo, Somali and others. — Rift Valley Institute, 2025, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Police said in October 2025 they killed 43 OLA fighters hiding in rural areas around Dire Dawa and detained 34 suspects; OLA did not comment. — Borkena, 2025-10-29, local-media, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The FCDO advice page (updated 30 Oct 2026) and its regional-risks page do not mention Dire Dawa. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-30, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] 2007 census: Oromo 46 %, Somali 24 %, Amhara 20 %, Gurage 4.5 %. — Wikipedia "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Many residents chew khat in the afternoons with coffee; the wiki advises avoiding political talk, especially about Somalia. — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.culture] Afaan Oromo is not an official city language, and renaming Millennium Park to Ali Birra Park alarmed non-Oromo residents. — Rift Valley Institute, 2025, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Local specialities listed: baklava at Al Hashim (sells out before 9 am) and roasted goat with rice at Paradiso. — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.religion] 2007 census: Muslim 70.9 %, Ethiopian Orthodox 25.6 %, Protestant 2.8 %. — Wikipedia "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.religion] The Kulubi pilgrimage nearby (July and December) doubles or triples hotel prices for three days. — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Hot all year: average highs from 29.3 °C (January) to 35.6 °C (June); rain peaks in August (158 mm). — Wikipedia "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: high
- [nature.access] Harar is about an hour away by shared minibus, which leaves when full. — Wikivoyage "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [other] Population about 600,000 for the chartered city (2025 estimate), 277,000 in the city proper. — Wikipedia "Dire Dawa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
