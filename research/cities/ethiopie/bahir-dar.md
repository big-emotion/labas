# Bahir Dar, Ethiopia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Context first: the UK FCDO advises against **all travel to the Amhara region**, which includes Bahir
Dar (page updated 30 Oct 2026), and the dossier recommends flagging the city `unsafe`. Most recent
material is about the Fano conflict; daily-life sources are few and mostly old travel listings, flagged
by date. Currency note: EUR figures use the dossier's assumed **≈ 185 ETB per EUR** and US$1 ≈ €0.87
(EUR/USD ≈ 1.15), not re-verified.
