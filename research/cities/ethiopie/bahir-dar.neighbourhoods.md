## Neighbourhoods

### Lakeside (Lake Street / Lake Road)

- Feel: the hotel and café strip along Lake Tana, with lakeside beer at sunset and azmari music in the evening (Wikivoyage listings 2015–2023) [2]. Resident profile, noise and pace not sourced.
- Rent: no area figure. City-wide aggregator: 1-bed in the centre US$458/month (≈ €400), cheaper 1-bed US$301 (≈ €262), modelled and partly crowdsourced (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21) [city file].
- Getting around: bajaj (three-wheel taxi) about Br2 per route segment or Br30–50 chartered (undated, likely old); cycling is the most common way to get around [city file][3]. Airport 8 km west of downtown [2].
- Watch: city wastewater generally flows straight into the lake [4]; malaria is common below 2,000 m and the city sits at about 1,800 m (FCDO health page) [city file].
- Places:
  - Checheho Cultural Restaurant (azmari music and dance, Lake Rd; listing updated Jan 2023) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Wude Coffee (quiet drinks and Ethiopian food, Lake Street; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Desset Restaurant (lakeside, behind Grand Resort and Spa; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Grand Resort and Spa with its Hot Spot bar (Lake Street; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Blue Nile Resort Hotel, formerly Avanti (Lake Street) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage" only as a lake shore (no beach sourced). Not proposable (FCDO).

### Centre: Giorgis Road and the market

- Feel: the commercial centre around the Kidus Giorgis church and the daily market, busiest on Saturday and quiet on Sunday [2]. The settlement's old name was Bahir Giyorgis [3].
- Rent: as above (aggregator only) [city file].
- Getting around: walkable; bajaj as above [2].
- Watch: a hospital surgery director was shot dead by unidentified gunmen in the Koshe area around 7:30 pm in February 2025 (Borkena, 2025-02-02) [city file]; the Koshe area's position relative to Giorgis Road is not sourced. Grenade attacks on a revenue office and the regional high court were reported in April 2025 (Borkena, claimed by Fano, unverified) [city file].
- Places:
  - Kidus Giorgis church (Orthodox church near the lake, Giorgis Road; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Bahir Dar market (daily market, Giorgis Road; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Goat-skin and basket shops (about twenty shops, Giorgis Road; 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Papyrus Hotel (mid-range hotel with pool, Giorgis Road at Arba Meter Road) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Ours Guest House (Giorgis Road; 2019) and the hornbill roost trees opposite it (2019) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Dashen Bank and Commercial Bank of Ethiopia ATMs (take Visa and Mastercard; undated) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (if the aggregator's cheaper rents hold); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q8 "Je reste en ville". Not proposable (FCDO).

### Beg Tera, Abafasilo Road and the stadium side

- Feel: hotels and guesthouses away from the lake front, near the new 60,000-seat stadium [2][3]. Character not sourced.
- Rent: as above (aggregator only) [city file].
- Getting around: bajaj; the university and its hospital are elsewhere in the city (locations not sourced).
- Places:
  - Bahir Dar Stadium (football, 60,000 seats) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Delano Hotel (near the new stadium; card machine often fails, 2021) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Blue Nile Hotel (Beg Tera; arranges boat and car trips, 2015) — Wikivoyage [2]

## Living: typical weeks

**None of these weeks is a recommendation.** Every profile is blocked by the FCDO all-travel advice
for Amhara [1]; the war has been ongoing since April 2023, with a new offensive across central
Amhara in February 2026 [city file]. Road links to Addis-Abeba were halted in August 2024 and FCDO
reports attacks on vehicles on the A3 [city file]. Mobile internet was cut for about 11 months in
2023–2024 [city file]. The outlines below are kept only for the day the advice is lifted.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Lakeside, if anywhere. Remote work is the weakest fit: connections were described as slow and inconsistent after the 2024 restoration, with possible throttling [city file].
- Monday–Friday: work from home or a hotel; lunch near Giorgis Road; evenings at Wude Coffee or Desset [2]. Saturday: market [2]. Sunday: Kidus Giorgis church [2].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": Dream House or the bar above Friendship Cafe (western music) [2], subject to any curfew; "Un bon dîner entre amis": Checheho Cultural Restaurant [2]; "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage": lake shore only, no beach sourced; "En montagne, en forêt": Blue Nile Falls about 30–35 km south (Br50 + mandatory guide Br200, July 2019) [2][3]; "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Bahir Dar (none sourced); "Je reste en ville": market and lakeside.
- Weekly spend: rent US$301–458/month [city file] × 12 ÷ 52 = 69.5–105.7; aggregator single-person costs without rent US$373/month [city file] × 12 ÷ 52 = 86.1. **Total ≈ US$156–192 a week ≈ €135–167** (modelled aggregator figures only).

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"

- Base: Centre (Giorgis Road), walkable to commerce; main employers are commerce, health, education, the university and tourism [city file].
- Weekdays: bajaj or bicycle to work [3]; Saturday market; Sunday church [2].
- Saturday night and weekend outings: as profile 1.
- Weekly spend for two: rent 69.5–105.7 (as above) + 2 × 86.1 = 172.2 (doubling the single figure is an assumption). **Total ≈ US$242–278 a week ≈ €210–242**.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"

- Base: Centre. No hub, co-working or startup programme was found [city file]. During an April 2026 visit the US ambassador called the city an economic hub, but no deals were announced [city file]. Not a realistic plan under current conditions.
- Weekly spend: as profile 1.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"

- Base: not proposable. Schools named (Bahir Dar Academy, Rispins International School) have no published fees or locations [city file]; FCDO calls medical facilities outside Addis "extremely poor" [city file]; teachers were abducted and killed in Merawi, in the wider region, in March 2025 (HRW) [city file].
- Weekly spend: not built (no school fee; the aggregator's US$29,678/year international primary figure is modelled [city file]).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week

- Not proposable. If advice changes: fly from Addis (up to ten flights a day; foreigner fare Br4,377 vs Br2,421 for residents, July 2021) [3][2]; stay at a lakeside hotel or the backpackers hostel [2]; SIM and telebirr are best set up in Addis first [Addis sheet]; ATMs at Dashen and CBE [2]. Rent advance customs, deposit and agency fees in Bahir Dar: not found.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". **Not proposable**: FCDO advises against all travel to Amhara [1].

### One day (if advice is lifted)

- Morning: boat to the Lake Tana island monasteries (Ura Kidane Mehret, Azwa Maryam on the Zege peninsula, Debre Maryam) [4]; Br100 per monastery (2016); some do not admit women; boat rates are fixed, so avoid brokers [2].
- Midday: lunch on Lake Street (Desset or Wude Coffee) [2]; boat past the Blue Nile outflow, where hippos live [4].
- Afternoon: Haile Selassie palace viewpoint (interior closed) [2] and the Martyr's Memorial Monument [2], by bajaj.
- Evening: Checheho Cultural Restaurant [2].

### One week (if advice is lifted)

- Day 1: arrive by air from Addis [3]; lakeside walk.
- Day 2: the one-day loop above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Saturday market morning on Giorgis Road, the busiest day [2]; Sunday service at Kidus Giorgis church [2].
- Day 4 (trip out): Blue Nile Falls (Tis Abay), about 35 km south; flow reduced by a dam, best May–September [2][city file].
- Day 5: longer lake tour (2–12 hours) to further monasteries such as Narga Selassie or Tana Qirqos [4][2].
- Day 6: football at Bahir Dar Stadium if a match is on [3]; Balageru Cultural Club for tej and azmari [2].
- Day 7: return flight.
- Ethiopian New Year eve (11–12 Sep): deacons chant and drum on the lake shore at night [city file].
