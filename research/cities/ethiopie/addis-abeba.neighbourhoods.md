## Neighbourhoods

### Bole (incl. Bole Medhanealem, Atlas, Rwanda and Japan areas, Chechenia)

- Feel: the airport district in the east and the most convenient area for shops, restaurants and the airport [10]; named with Old Airport and Kazanchis as the main expat areas [4]; busy and tourist-friendly per a 2026 culture feature [12]. Listed among the wealthier areas [1].
- Rent: furnished 1-bed asked US$700/month (≈ Br112,600 ≈ €610), 2-bed Br170,000–250,000 (≈ €920–1,350), Rio Times 2026-11-09 [city file]; relocation blog 1-bed US$350–500 (≈ €305–435), 2-bed US$500–800 (≈ €435–696), villa US$1,200–3,000 (≈ €1,044–2,610), OpenShores 2026-04-13 [3]; 4-bed in Bole Bulbula Br100,000 (≈ €541), Addis Fortune 2025-02-23 [city file]. Expat rents are mostly quoted in dollars [city file].
- Getting around: Bole Road links the airport to Meskel Square [1]; ride-hailing (Ride, Feres, Little, Yango), Ride base fare Br150 + Br19/km in Aug 2025 (≈ €0.81 + €0.10/km) [1]; a blog puts a Ride trip at US$2–6 (≈ €1.74–5.22) [3]. Light-rail Green Line terminates at Ayat, not in Bole [6]. Time to Meskel Square not sourced.
- Watch: FCDO reports rising muggings, including at knifepoint, in Bole Medhanealem and Bole Atlas and advises not travelling alone in these areas if possible, being cautious on foot and preferring road transport, especially after dark (FCDO safety page, read 2026-11-14) [city file]. Pickpocketing and scams around the airport per the travel wiki [1].
- Places:
  - Medhane Alem Cathedral (Orthodox cathedral, Cameroon St) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - iceaddis (incubator and co-working, Bole Medhanialem building, 5th floor) — iceaddis.com [city file]
  - Friendship Hypermarket (supermarket, airport end of Bole Rd, takes Visa/Mastercard) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Shoa and Novis (supermarkets, Bole Rd) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Edna Mall (mall with Matti Multiplex cinema, Cameroon St; closed for refurbishment as of Feb 2026) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - 2000 Habesha Cultural Restaurant (dinner with music and dance, Tele Bole Rd) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Black Rose Lounge (bar, Boston Building, Bole Rd, above Boston Day Spa) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Fendika Cultural Center (azmari music house, now at the Hyatt Regency) — OkayAfrica 2026-08-21 [12]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q5 "J'entreprends" (iceaddis, cafés, airport); Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (clubs near Edna Mall per [1]); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (dollar rents); a poor fit for Q3 "Serré".

### Old Airport (with Lebu and Kera nearby)

- Feel: many families of International Community School pupils live here; fewer restaurants than Bole (Tales Mag, 2018, old) [10]; listed among the wealthier areas [1] and among the dearer areas [city file, Africarrières]. Lebu is described as a gated community near Old Airport, with slow traffic in and out (2018) [10].
- Rent: no dated 2025–2026 figure for Old Airport itself. Large houses were about US$4,500/month around 2016 (≈ €3,915, very old) [10]. Citywide agency range: 2-bed US$600–1,500 (≈ €522–1,305), Ethiopian Properties 2026-02-27 [city file].
- Getting around: ride-hailing as above [1]; commute times not sourced. From Bole Homes, Gerji or CMC the school run to ICS is described as long (2018) [10].
- Places:
  - International Community School (American curriculum + IB Diploma, EC3 to grade 12, Mauritania Rd, Bisrate Gabriel) — Wikipedia [7]

### Kazanchis (Kirkos sub-city)

- Feel: an old, dense central neighbourhood that the corridor redevelopment largely cleared; The Reporter (2025-02-01) calls it unrecognisable, with former residents moved to outskirts such as Gelan Gura and Bole Arabsa [11]. Still named as an expat area by a 2026 job-board guide [4]. Its long-running azmari house Fendika was forced out after 17 years [12].
- Rent: 1-bed US$280–400 (≈ €244–348), 2-bed US$400–650 (≈ €348–566), 3-bed US$600–900 (≈ €522–783), OpenShores 2026-04-13 [3]. Aggregator snippets put Bole/Kazanchis 1-beds at US$400–1,200 (low confidence) [dossier].
- Getting around: central; Africa Hall (UNECA) is across Menelik II Ave from the Palace [1]; ride-hailing [1]. Light-rail stops for Kazanchis not confirmed.
- Watch: evictions in Jan–Feb 2025 with about three days' notice and no compensation for those Amnesty interviewed (Arab News/AFP, 2025-04-14) [city file]; ask whether a building is in a planned corridor before signing.
- Places:
  - Bambis (supermarket with imports, pricey) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Yohannes Kitfo Bet (Ethiopian restaurant, Kazanchis Rd) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Yewedale (azmari music, Zewditu St; may be affected by the redevelopment, not re-checked) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Africa Hall (UNECA headquarters, visits by appointment) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Unity Park and the National Palace (nearby, Arat Kilo side) — Wikipedia, Wikivoyage [8][1]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (UN/AU/NGO offices) [city file]; Q4 "En couple"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q3 "Moyen".

### East side: Megenagna, CMC, Summit and Ayat (Yeka and Bole sub-cities)

- Feel: newer residential districts with condominiums and villas; CMC and Ayat listed among the wealthier areas by the travel wiki [1], while Gerji and Yeka are named as cheaper by a French guide [city file, Africarrières]. Megenagna is the city's busiest transport interchange (IGC, 2023) [city file].
- Rent: CMC/Summit 1-bed US$220–350 (≈ €191–305), 2-bed US$350–550 (≈ €305–479), 3-bed US$450–750 (≈ €392–653), OpenShores 2026-04-13 [3]; 3-bed in Megenagna or CMC Br85,000 (≈ €459) and run-down villas in Ayat from Br60,000 (≈ €324), Addis Fortune 2025-02-23 [city file]; duplex at Megenagna (Shola) Br95,000 (≈ €514), Facebook housing group 2026-11-14 [city file].
- Getting around: Green Line light rail from Ayat westward through the centre, fare Br2–6, trains about every 15 minutes on weekdays (Apr 2025) [6]; minibus Br4.5–65 and city bus Br7–35 (Oct 2024 tariff) [city file].
- Watch: in 2023 residents of Mekanisa and Abado condos reported at least three blackouts a day and Ayat was described as worst hit (The Reporter, 2023-08-26, old) [city file]; a Yeka Abado resident paid at least Br2,000/month extra for water trucks (Addis Fortune, 2023-06-24, old) [city file]; FCDO names Yeka Hills among mugging spots (read 2026-11-14) [city file]. The light rail suffers power-related stoppages, sometimes whole days [6].
- Places:
  - Ayat light-rail terminus (Green Line) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Megenagna interchange (minibus and bus hub) — IGC policy brief 2023 [city file]
  - Top View (restaurant, Megenagna) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Five Loaves Bistro and Bakery (Aware, Yeka) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Dashen Terara Traditional Restaurant (garden restaurant, Aware, Yeka) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen" and Q3 "Serré" (cheapest of the five here); Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (3-bed for under €500 asking); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps". Not for Q5 "Télétravail" without a backup for power.

### Piassa and Arat Kilo (Arada, historic centre)

- Feel: the old centre with Italian-era buildings, cafés and the oldest theatre [2]; Arat Kilo holds Holy Trinity Cathedral, Parliament and Unity Park [1][8]. Students around Sidist Kilo (Addis Ababa University campus hosts the Ethnological Museum) [1].
- Rent: no neighbourhood figure. A French AI-assisted guide puts a centre 1-bed at Br8,000–15,000 (≈ €43–81), flagged low-confidence and conflicting with listings [city file]. Gulele (just north) is named as cheaper [city file].
- Getting around: Blue Line light rail runs from Menelik II Square (Piazza) south to Kality, Br2–6 [6]; city bus line 31 runs from Meskel Square to Trinity Church [1].
- Watch: the city plans to lease cleared land on the Piassa–Wello Sefer corridor to private developers (The Reporter, 2025-02-01) [11]; the travel wiki warns of pickpockets and a "spitting" scam in Piazza (travel wiki, confirm) [1]; in 2023 Gulele and Addis Ketema had among the worst piped-water supply (Addis Fortune, 2023-11-04, old) [city file].
- Places:
  - St George's Cathedral (cathedral and museum, north end of Churchill Rd; museum Br200) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Holy Trinity Cathedral (Arat Kilo) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hager Fikir Theatre (oldest theatre in Ethiopia, John Melly St) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Itegue Taitu Hotel (historic hotel with a popular vegan buffet) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Castelli's (long-running Italian restaurant) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Lycée Guebre-Mariam (French MLF/AEFE school, Churchill Road; exact distance from Piassa not sourced) — Wikipedia [city file]

## Living: typical weeks

Shared caveats: the Ethiopian clock starts at 6 am, so "6 o'clock" can mean noon, and the
calendar runs about 7–8 years behind [city file]. Many restaurants serve a fasting menu on
Orthodox fasting days [city file]. Power and water cuts are frequent and unannounced [1]. All
weekly spends below use only prices already in the city file or this pass, converted at the rates
above; ranges are low–high, and every source is low or medium confidence.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

- Base: Bole, because cafés, iceaddis co-working and the airport are close [1][city file]; budget for a flat with a generator, since expat housing usually has one plus water tanks [city file]. Daily power and water cuts make a co-working backup useful.
- Monday: work from home; evening groceries at Shoa or Novis on Bole Rd (walk or Ride) [1].
- Tuesday: day at iceaddis (Bole Medhanialem; co-working price not published) [city file]; avoid travelling alone on foot after dark in Bole Medhanealem (FCDO) [city file].
- Wednesday: home work; gym (gyms US$20–50/month per OpenShores; Bole Rock named in 2018) [3][10].
- Thursday: evening drink at Black Rose Lounge, Bole Rd [1].
- Friday: work; lunch at a local injera place (US$1.50–3) [city file].
- Saturday: morning at Friendship Hypermarket [1]; afternoon light rail from Ayat into the centre for Piassa cafés [6][2].
- Sunday: Medhane Alem Cathedral service, Cameroon St [1]; quiet afternoon.
- Saturday night, by Q2 answer:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": the weekly Jubilation Africa night at The Venue Warehouse (Afro house, amapiano) [city file], or clubs near Edna Mall [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": 2000 Habesha Cultural Restaurant (buffet, music, fasting menu) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": takeaway via Deliver Addis (named in 2018, check it still runs) [10].
- Weekend outing, by Q8 answer:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Addis-Abeba (landlocked). Closest water is the Bishoftu crater lakes and resorts, about 48 km away [9]; FCDO exempts the Addis–Bishoftu expressway and 10 km either side of it from its East Shewa advice, and whether the town sits inside that strip should be checked on the FCDO map [5].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Entoto, the 3,200 m ridge with a 1,300 ha natural park and the Entoto Maryam and Raguel churches on the city's edge [13]; FCDO reports muggings at Entoto, so go in a group or with a guide [city file].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic under current advice. Awash National Park (about 200 km east) [1] lies beyond Adama, and FCDO advises against all but essential travel in East Shewa outside the expressway [5].
  - "Je reste en ville": Unity Park (zoo, palace grounds) and the National Museum [1][8].
- Weekly spend (US$ unless stated):
  - Rent: furnished 1-bed Bole US$350–500/month [3] to US$700 [city file, Rio Times] × 12 ÷ 52 = 81–162
  - Groceries: single person US$180/month [city file, OpenShores] × 12 ÷ 52 = 41.5
  - 4 local injera meals × 1.50–3 = 6–12; 1 international dinner in Bole 12–25 [city file, OpenShores]
  - 7 macchiatos × 0.30–0.80 = 2.1–5.6 [city file, OpenShores]
  - 6 Ride trips × 2–6 = 12–36 [3]
  - Ethio Telecom unlimited data Br400/week (Oct 2024) ≈ 2.5 [city file, Wikivoyage]
  - Gym US$20–50/month × 12 ÷ 52 = 4.6–11.5 [3]
  - Bills: electricity 10–25 + water 3–8 + gas 5–10 + home internet 15–30 = 33–73/month × 12 ÷ 52 = 7.6–16.8 [city file, OpenShores]
  - **Total ≈ US$169–312 a week ≈ €147–272** (co-working not included: no price published).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week

- Where to stay first: a Bole serviced flat, US$40–70 a day (≈ €35–61), agency blog 2026-02-27 [city file]; or a guesthouse (Z Guest House from about US$30) [1].
- Day 1: arrive at Bole airport (tourist e-visas valid at Bole only) [city file]; buy an Ethio Telecom or Safaricom SIM, which needs a passport and a photo [city file][1]; unlimited data Br400/week (Oct 2024) [city file].
- Day 2: set up telebirr (Ethio Telecom mobile money, 60.6 m customers) [city file]; withdraw cash at Dashen, CBE or Awash ATMs (Br4,000–6,000/day limits) [city file]. Bank account: the central bank waived the national-ID requirement for diaspora for two years (Shega, 2025-02-10) [city file]; rules for other foreigners not found.
- Day 3: flat visits in Bole and CMC with an agent; commission half to one month's rent [city file].
- Day 4: Megenagna and Ayat by Green Line to compare prices [6].
- Day 5: negotiate; expect 6 months to 2 years' rent in advance, sometimes a non-refundable "goodwill" payment, and dollar quotes; write the currency into the contract [city file]. Renewed leases must be registered at sub-city housing offices (from 8 Jul 2026) [city file].
- Day 6: Merkato or Shiro Meda for household basics (haggle) [1][city file].
- Day 7: rest; Medhane Alem Cathedral [1].
- Visa note: a 30-day tourist e-visa can be extended by 30 or 90 days at Immigration HQ (Gola Saint Michael church is near the federal immigration office) [city file][1]; a tourist visa forbids any work, even volunteering (FCDO) [city file], so remote work on it is a question to raise with the embassy.
- Saturday night and weekend outings: as profile 1.
- First-week spend (US$): serviced flat 7 × 40–70 = 280–490; data 2.5; 10 Ride trips × 2–6 = 20–60; 14 local meals × 1.50–3 = 21–42; 7 macchiatos 2.1–5.6. **Total ≈ US$326–600 ≈ €283–522**, before advance rent and agency fee.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Unity Park and the National Palace charge foreigners in dollars and
need a passport [1].

### One day

- Morning: Holy Trinity Cathedral at Arat Kilo [1], then the National Museum (Lucy replica; much of it was closed for renovation) and the Ethnological Museum in Haile Selassie's former palace on the university campus (Br250 for foreigners) [1]. Move by Ride.
- Midday: Piassa: lunch at Itegue Taitu Hotel (vegan buffet) or Castelli's [1]; St George's Cathedral museum (Br200, plus Br200+ for a guide) [1].
- Afternoon: Unity Park before last entry at 15:00 (US$20; US$70 VIP with palace tour) [1]; Wikipedia says it is closed on Mondays [8]. Then the Red Terror Martyrs Memorial Museum at Meskel Square (donation) [1].
- Evening: Ride to Bole for dinner and dancing at 2000 Habesha [1], or Fendika at the Hyatt Regency [12].

### One week

- Day 1: arrive, Bole; SIM and cash [city file]; Medhane Alem Cathedral [1].
- Day 2: the one-day historic loop above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Merkato morning (Mon–Sat), Africa's largest open market, with the Grand Anwar Mosque from outside [1][2]; ride the Blue Line from Menelik II Square at rush hour [6]. Note the September 2026 demolitions of historic market hubs [city file].
- Day 4: Entoto with a guide or group: churches of Entoto Maryam and Raguel, eucalyptus forest [13]; FCDO warns of muggings there [city file]. Afternoon Shiro Meda market (traditional dress) [1].
- Day 5 (trip out): Bishoftu crater lakes and resorts, about 48 km by expressway [9]; first confirm the town sits in FCDO's exempted 10 km strip [5].
- Day 6–7 (trip out): Hawassa by the Addis–Hawassa expressway, which FCDO exempts from its East Shewa advice; coaches about 5–5.5 hours (Sky Bus, Selam; 2020 fares) [hawassa.md]; lakeside fish market and Piazza Road bars [hawassa.md]. Or, if on a Saturday in the Addis season, a Saint George match at Addis Ababa Stadium (shared with Ethiopian Coffee and others) [15]. Late November: the Great Ethiopian Run, 10 km, over 45,000 runners in 2023 [16].
- Not proposed: Debre Libanos monastery (named by OpenShores [3]) lies in North Shewa, Oromia [17], where FCDO advises against all travel south and west of the A3 and all but essential travel elsewhere [5]. Bahir Dar and Lake Tana are under FCDO all-travel advice [5].
