# Addis-Abeba, Ethiopia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR figures use the dossier's assumed rate **≈ 185 ETB per EUR** and EUR/USD ≈ 1.15
(so US$1 ≈ €0.87), both from `research/countries/ethiopie.md` and not re-verified. The birr floated in
2024 and lost value fast, so an older birr price converted at today's rate understates what it was
worth when published; older items are flagged by date.
