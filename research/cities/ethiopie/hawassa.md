## Items

- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives 1-bed rents of US$120–200 by the lake (≈ €104–174), US$80–150 in the centre (≈ €70–130) and US$50–100 on the outskirts (≈ €44–87); no method or sources stated. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same blog's table puts a single person's budget at US$201–378/month (≈ €175–329), while its text says US$400–650 (≈ €348–566); the page contradicts itself. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] It says inflation hits imported goods and fuel most, while local produce prices stay fairly stable. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Hawassa Industrial Park (apparel, opened 2016) peaked above 35,000 workers; factories closed and jobs were lost after the US removed Ethiopia from AGOA in 2022. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Toyo Solar says it has invested about US$110 million in a solar-panel plant in the Hawassa Special Economic Zone, employing over 1,000 Ethiopians (ministry figures). — Borkena, 2025-08-15, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] In 2021 a dormitory project for 6,500 park workers (Br600 million) was launched because of a lack of affordable housing; thousands of workers commuted for hours (old). — Addis Fortune, 2021-09-18, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] At least 150 lakeside homes were demolished by June 2026; residents allege police beatings and unpaid compensation, and say many landlords refuse families with children. — Borkena, 2026-06-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The relocation blog says power cuts are frequent, though electricity is cheap. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Hawassa is 273 km south of Addis-Abeba via Bishoftu; FCDO exempts the Addis–Hawassa expressway from its East Shewa advice. — Wikipedia "Hawassa" / UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Coaches from Addis: Sky Bus Br149 and Selam Br200 one way (2020 prices, ≈ €0.81–1.08 today), about 5–5.5 hours. — Wikivoyage "Awasa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The town is compact and walkable; blue motorbike rickshaws (bajaj) are common. — Wikivoyage "Awasa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Awasa Airport opened in 1988. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [health.care] FCDO says medical facilities outside Addis-Abeba are "extremely poor"; at 1,708 m the city is below the 2,000 m line under which malaria is common. — UK FCDO health page / Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Hawassa University (1999) includes a health-sciences college and the Wondo Genet forestry college; private colleges include Info Link and Africa Beza. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO (updated 30 Oct 2026) does not name Hawassa; in Sidama it advises only against areas within 10 km of the Kenya and South Sudan borders, and the Hawassa–Mega road is exempted from the Guji-zone advice. — UK FCDO, 2026-10-30, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] 2007 first languages: Sidamo 47.97 %, Amharic 31.01 %, Welayta 9.58 %. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.culture] An opinion column argues the city's multi-ethnic identity is eroding ("Sidamaization and Oromoization"); headline-level only, body not read. — Borkena (opinion), 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: low
- [social.food] Outdoor cafés by the lakeside fish market served fresh cooked fish for about Br30 (2020 listing, ≈ €0.16 today). — Wikivoyage "Awasa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] Small bars line Piazza Road where it meets the lake, with street seating and fried fish; Bera Beer is a local microbrewery (undated listing). — Wikivoyage "Awasa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.sport] Football and swimming are the most popular sports; Hawassa City SC was the first club outside Addis to win the Ethiopian Premier League; basketball and skateboarding are growing. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.religion] 2007 census: Protestant 52.71 %, Ethiopian Orthodox 39.99 %, Muslim 7.30 %. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Wet season March–October, dry November–February; highs 23.6–29.3 °C; December nights average about 9 °C. — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: high
- [nature.access] A small park next to the fish market charged Br50 (2020); monkeys at lakeside resorts may steal food. — Wikivoyage "Awasa", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [other] Hawassa became capital of the new Sidama region in June 2020 and still hosts the former southern region's seat for two electoral cycles; population ~577,000 (2023 estimate). — Wikipedia "Hawassa", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
