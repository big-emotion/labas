# Conakry: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout: **10,000 GNF ≈ €1** (dossier rate, marked approximate and unverified) and **1 USD ≈ 8,600 GNF ≈ €0.86** (city-file rate from nomad.watch, March 2026). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/guinee/conakry.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/guinee.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33. Almost every everyday price in the files comes from two commercial guides (Africarrières 2026-08-07 and OpenShores 2026-04-13, both low confidence). The Lonely Planet listings [3] date from the 2017 edition and are used only to show that a place exists and where; their prices are old.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (2026-09-15) puts Conakry in the yellow zone. It reports rising armed robbery, night carjackings and break-ins, pickpocketing at the Niger, Madina and Taouyah markets, and travellers followed from the airport. It advises against living **east of Kipé and Taouyah** without reinforced security, and against gatherings on the Route Le Prince (Hamdallaye–Bambeto–Cosa) [C safety.general][C housing.areas].
- GOV.UK: violent carjackings, especially in the outer suburbs; fake police; police extortion [C safety.general]. Both advisories advise against moto-taxis and shared taxis [C daily.transport]. Women are advised not to walk alone at night [C safety.women].
- Power cuts are the defining daily problem across the city. Examples: weeks of long cuts in August 2026 (RFI), and EDG maintenance cuts in Dixinn, Matam, Ratoma and Gbessia in May 2026 [C daily.power]. Running water is irregular, so plan a tank and a generator or inverter [C daily.water][C cost.living].
- Floods: roads flood May–October (GOV.UK) or June–November (France Diplomatie) [C climate.daily].
- Money: cards are rarely accepted, ATMs are unreliable and street money-changing has led to arrests (GOV.UK) [C daily.money]. Orange Money is widespread [C daily.money].
- Reference clinics: Clinique Pasteur and Clinique Ambroise Paré; the Centre mère et enfants paediatric clinic also follows children from the French school (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [12][C health.care]. Pasteur's quarter is not confirmed (see Gaps).

## Neighbourhoods

### Kaloum (peninsula tip, downtown)
- Feel: the city centre: seat of government, ministries, banks, the port and the artisanal fishing port, with "residential areas and slums" (Wikipédia, 2025 census 49,364 people) [6]. A relocation guide calls it administrative and not very residential, with heavy traffic [C housing.areas].
- Rent: 3-room flat in Kaloum/Almamya 2–8 million GNF a month (≈ €200–800) (Africarrières, 2026-08-07, low) [C cost.rent]. Kaloum is also among the sought-after areas where a small 2-bed costs 2.5–3 million GNF (≈ €250–300) (Guineenews, 2026-01-12) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the end of the Conakry Express commuter line from Kagbélen (two trains a day, Mon–Fri; Wikivoyage EN calls it unsuitable for tourists) [1][C daily.transport]. The airport is about 13 km north-east of the centre [2]. An agreed hotel-to-airport taxi fare of 150,000 GNF (≈ €15) was reported, against 500,000 asked of a foreign passenger [C cost.negotiation]. Boats to the Îles de Loos leave from Boulbinet (Guineenews, 2022) [23] or the fish market behind the Novotel [1].
- Watch: on 14 August 2024, floods in Coronthie and Tombo reached about 1.5 m in homes and jammed the entrance to Kaloum (Guineenews) [22]. Pickpocketing at Marché du Niger (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Marché du Niger, Route du Niger (produce and street food; Lonely Planet gives 6am–5pm; pickpockets) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]; Wikivoyage EN (Aug 2024) [1]
  - Cathédrale Sainte-Marie (Catholic cathedral) — Wikivoyage EN (May 2021) [1]
  - Musée national de Sandervalia (masks, statues, ethnography; GFr10,000 and Tue–Sun hours in 2017) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]; Wikipédia [6]
  - Hôpital Ignace Deen (colonial-era public hospital) — Wikipédia [6]
  - Le Cèdre, 6th Avenue (Lebanese restaurant) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Le Loft (quiet bar, good early in the night; coordinates at the Kaloum tip) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Maison d'Accueil, Catholic mission near Stade de la Mission (budget rooms, €13 in July 2020) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Lonely Planet 2017 [3]
  - Centre culturel franco-guinéen (CCFG), Pont du 8 Novembre (shows, films, library) — Wikivoyage FR (entry Feb 2018; current name and status not checked) [2]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (ministries, banks, port) and "J'entreprends" (meetings); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit". Not for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (flooding at Coronthie and Tombo, not residential) [22][C housing.areas].

### Camayenne and Landréah (Dixinn, Corniche Nord)
- Feel: Dixinn hosts the university and many embassies (Wikipédia, 2025 census 186,553 people) [7]. A relocation blog lists Camayenne for embassies and secure residences [C housing.areas]. The dossier names Dixinn/Landréah among the areas expats usually pick [D].
- Rent: small 2-bed in Dixinn 2.5–3 million GNF (≈ €250–300) (Guineenews, 2026-01-12) [C cost.rent]. 3-room flat in Dixinn/Ratoma 0.8–2.5 million GNF (≈ €80–250) (Africarrières, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on the Corniche Nord between Kaloum and Ratoma. Travel times are not sourced. Individual taxi 15,000–30,000 GNF for a short trip (≈ €1.50–3) (Africarrières) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: Dixinn was in the EDG 08:00–16:00 maintenance cut of 7 May 2026 [C daily.power]. A worker died falling from a 7th-floor building site facing Camayenne beach in October 2025 (Guineenews) [21].
- Places:
  - Clinique Ambroise Paré, Corniche Nord (private hospital, surgery, ambulance) — Wikipedia [11]; France Diplomatie [12]
  - Conakry Botanical Garden (1894, kapok trees) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; [C social.culture]
  - Grande mosquée Fayçal (built 1981, Dixinn) — Wikipédia [7]; Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Plage de Camayenne (hosted the djembé festival in December 2024) — Guineenews [21]
  - Hotel Palm Camayenne (upmarket hotel) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Riviera Royal, off Corniche Nord (pool, gym, nightclub) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]; Wikivoyage EN (2016) [1]
  - Centre d'Art Acrobatique Keita Fodeba, Dixinn stadium (free acrobat rehearsals 10am–2pm Mon–Fri in 2017) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]
  - CHU Donka (public teaching hospital) and Université Gamal Abdel Nasser — Wikivoyage FR [2]; Wikipédia [7]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and Q5 "Salarié·e" (short ride to Kaloum, embassies); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (Ambroise Paré nearby); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q8 "À la plage" (Camayenne beach); Q3 "Moyen".

### Taouyah (Ratoma)
- Feel: chef-lieu of Ratoma commune [5][9]. Ratoma is "known for its nightlife" [D], and a relocation blog lists Taouyah "near the corniche" [C housing.areas]. It sits on France Diplomatie's eastern line (see frames).
- Rent: Ratoma small 2-bed 2.5–3 million GNF (Guineenews, 2026-01-12) [C cost.rent]. A Ratoma tenant says landlords keep raising rents [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on the Corniche (Kipé–Taouyah). Individual taxi 15,000–30,000 GNF per short trip [C daily.transport].
- Watch: on 23 April 2024, anti-load-shedding protests barricaded Taouyah-Corniche and a schoolboy was shot dead (Guineenews) [16]. A fire on 7 January 2026 destroyed much of the Rogbanet beach leisure site and killed a restaurant manager (Guineenews) [17]. Pickpocketing at Taouyah market (France Diplomatie) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Marché Taouyah (6am–4pm, rice bowls in 2017; closed by Ratoma town hall in June 2024 after protests, reopening not confirmed) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]; Guineenews headlines 2024-06-21/25 [16]
  - Plage de Rogbanet (beach leisure site with restaurant and straw huts; burnt January 2026, current state unknown) — Guineenews [17]
  - Anglican church of Taouyah — Wikipédia (photo caption) [9]
  - Pension Les Palmiers, Ratoma (guesthouse, €70/75 single/double in 2017) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (Ratoma nightlife) [D]; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q3 "Moyen". Not for Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" (protest axis).

### Kipé (Ratoma)
- Feel: a business district with some of the city's largest hotels (Wikipédia) [4]. A relocation blog calls it seaside with restaurants [C housing.areas]. Another guide says embassy and NGO staff favour it, with modern compounds and 24-hour security [C housing.areas]. New gated estates are going up: Plaza Platinium (Kipé Centre Émetteur), part occupied with guards and cameras in October 2026 [14].
- Rent: Ratoma small 2-bed 2.5–3 million GNF (Guineenews) [C cost.rent]. One furnished 2-bed, 2-bath agency listing in Kipé was at 8,000,000 "Fr" a month (≈ €800 if GNF; currency not stated) [C cost.rent]. In a 2022 report a Kipé tenant thought three months' advance reasonable [C cost.negotiation].
- Getting around: Kipé–Taouyah corniche towards Kaloum; time not sourced. OpenShores warns that 10 km can take two hours on the peninsula [C daily.transport]. Ride-hailing: WONSIGA app with fare shown in advance (2025) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: EDG cited the Kipé substation during multi-day cuts in April 2026 [C daily.power]. A Kaporo Rail resident (next door) reported almost a month of nights without power [C daily.power]. On 20 April 2026, clashes between pupils and security forces in Kipé disrupted traffic (Guineenews) [18]. Kipé is the eastern limit in France Diplomatie's housing advice [C housing.areas].
- Places:
  - Lycée français Albert Camus (AEFE, ages 3–18, French bac; in Kipé since 1986) — Wikipedia [10]; Guineenews [14]
  - Franprix Kipé (supermarket, opened 2026 with ZCO Trading) — Food Business MEA, 2026-06-30 [13]
  - Le Waffou, off Route de Donka (Ivorian attiéké, mains GFr25,000–40,000 in 2017; live bands some weekends) — Lonely Planet 2017 [3]
  - Sheraton Grand Conakry and Mariador Palace (hotels) — Wikipédia [4]; Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - École nationale des postes et télécommunications (ENPT) — Wikipédia [4]; Guineenews [18]
  - Kipé cemetery (burial place of Mory Kanté, 2020) — Wikipédia [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French lycée); Q5 "Télétravail" (secured compounds, supermarket); Q1 "Tester quelques mois"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Lambanyi and Nongo (Lambanyi commune)
- Feel: a commune created in January 2024 out of Ratoma (2025 census 385,884 people). It is coastal and mixes housing, universities and sports grounds [8]. A relocation blog calls Nongo "developing, cheaper" [C housing.areas], and another says embassy and NGO staff choose Nongo [C housing.areas]. The 650-hectare "Nouvelle Ville de Conakry" from Lambanyi to Kobaya was presented in June 2026 and has a 10–15 year horizon (Guineenews headline) [19].
- Rent: Lambanyi small 2-bed 2.5–3 million GNF (Guineenews, 2026-01-12) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on the far side of the Route Le Prince, which marks the commune's southern boundary [8], from Kaloum; time not sourced. The Cosa–Nongo road had kiosks cleared in January 2026 [19].
- Watch: this is **east of Kipé and Taouyah**, where France Diplomatie advises against living without reinforced security [C housing.areas]. In June 2026 the state ordered the evacuation of buildings judged dangerous after cracks appeared in the Bowal sector of Nongo Morykantéyah; the cause was still under technical investigation (Guineenews, 2026-06-08) [20][19]. Two pupils were shot at Lycée Sainte-Marie de Nongo in March 2025 (Guineenews headline) [19]. In December 2024, bulldozers cleared a "criminogenic" zone in Nongo and people were arrested [19].
- Places:
  - Stade Général Lansana Conté (national stadium; final certification still pending in August 2026) — Wikipédia [8]; Guineenews headlines [19]
  - Stade Petit Sory (football; cultural events allowed again from June 2026) — Wikipédia [8]; Guineenews headlines [19]
  - Hôpital les Flamboyants — Wikipédia [8]
  - Clinique Vitalis, Nongo Tady (private clinic, opened 2026) — Guineenews headline 2026-02-10 [19]
  - Paroisse Anne-Marie Javouhey de Nongo (Catholic parish) — Guineenews headline 2024-12-25 [19]
  - Plage de Tayaki — Wikipédia (photo caption) [8]
  - UNIC and Université Mercure International (universities) — Wikipédia [8]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen", with care. Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" only inside a secured compound. Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (universities, new clinic).

### Matam (Coléah, Madina)
- Feel: one of the five historic communes [D], with Madina, described as one of the largest markets in West Africa [1]. A relocation blog advises against Madina as very dense [C housing.areas] but cites it as the cheapest place for groceries [C social.food].
- Rent: 3-room flat in Matam/Hamdallaye (popular) 0.3–1.2 million GNF (≈ €30–120) (Africarrières, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the Conakry Express stops at Madina market [C daily.transport]. Shared taxi 2,000–5,000 GNF (advised against) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: residents of Matam reported near-daily unannounced cuts in May 2026 [C daily.power]. Pickpocketing at Madina market (France Diplomatie) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Marché Madina (very large market) — Wikivoyage EN (Aug 2024) [1]
  - Franprix Coléah (supermarket, opened 2026) — Food Business MEA [13]
  - La Brasserie U, above the U Express supermarket in Coléah (said to be known among expats) — Petit Futé, seen only in a search-result summary (page returned 403); treat as unconfirmed
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" for a single adult; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" is not sourced here. Not for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (density, cuts).

### Considered and set aside
- **Hamdallaye–Bambeto–Cosa (Route Le Prince)**: France Diplomatie advises avoiding gatherings on this axis, where protests have turned deadly; Cyril Jarnias flags heavy traffic at Bambeto [C safety.general][C housing.areas]. Bambeto is useful only as the shared-taxi station for Kindia (50,000 GNF, 2023) and Freetown [1].
- **Sonfonia, Matoto and the outer suburbs**: France Diplomatie (east of Kipé) and GOV.UK (carjackings in outer suburbs) both warn [C housing.areas][C safety.general]. Gunfire was reported in Sonfonia on the eve of the December 2025 vote [D].

## Living: typical weeks

- **Home running costs per week**: generator fuel 30–80 USD/month = 258,000–688,000 GNF ÷ 4.33 = 59,584–158,891 (OpenShores); EDG 15,000–80,000 ÷ 4.33 = 3,464–18,476; SEG water 8,000–25,000 ÷ 4.33 = 1,848–5,774 (Africarrières) [C cost.living]. High-speed internet 250,000–600,000 GNF/month ÷ 4.33 = 57,737–138,568 (Africarrières) [C daily.internet].
- **Rent, small 2-bed in Kaloum, Dixinn, Lambanyi or Ratoma**: 2.5–3 million GNF ÷ 4.33 = **577,367–692,841 per week** (Guineenews, 2026-01-12) [C cost.rent].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Kipé**, in a compound with a guard and a generator. Reasons: relocation guides cite secured compounds there, and the Franprix supermarket is close [C housing.areas][13]. Internet is the risk. Power cuts also knock out telecom masts [C daily.power], cybercafés lost connections in 2026 [C daily.internet], Starlink is not authorised [C daily.internet], and the regulator is cracking down on resold "Wi-Fi zones" [C daily.internet]. Keep a 4G backup: 5 GB costs 5–10 USD (OpenShores) [C daily.internet].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office on the fixed line plus inverter; groceries at Franprix Kipé [13].
  - Tue: lunch at Le Waffou (attiéké) [3]; home office.
  - Wed: home office. Evening swim and gym at Riviera Royal, Corniche Nord, about 15–30 min by individual taxi (time is an assumption). Day-pass price not found [3][1].
  - Thu: home office; dinner at Le Cèdre, Kaloum (Lebanese) by taxi, before dark [1].
  - Fri: home office. Friday prayer at the Grande mosquée Fayçal (Dixinn), or a morning at Marché du Niger [7][3].
  - Sat: Madina market for cheaper groceries (pickpockets) [1][C social.food]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Sainte-Marie [1], or rest; beach at Camayenne [21].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": nightlife starts just after midnight (Wikivoyage EN) [1]. Options are Timi's (small and lively, location not stated) [1] and the Riviera Royal nightclub [3]. Concert tickets through Orange Money's Max it app start at 100,000 GNF (≈ €10) [C social.nightlife]. Take a booked driver home: France Diplomatie reports night carjackings [C safety.general].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Cèdre (Lebanese) or Jardin de Chine (location not stated) [1]. A mid-range meal costs 50,000–120,000 GNF (≈ €5–12) (Africarrières) [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; the generator decides the evening [C daily.power].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Îles de Loos by motorised pirogue from Boulbinet or behind the Novotel. Roume has the Plage du Gouverneur; there are no cars on the islands [C nature.access][1][23]. A day trip costs 15–30 USD (≈ €13–26) (OpenShores) [C cost.living]. Pirogues were banned over the 2023–24 holidays [23]. Closer options are Plage de Camayenne [21] and Plage de Tayaki [8].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Soumba waterfalls, a short way past Dubréka, about 2 hours' drive (swimming, restaurant) [1]. Kindia is 128.8 km (about 1h45) [2], but France Diplomatie warns of armed night roadblocks around Kindia, so travel by day only [D].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Conakry.
  - "Je reste en ville": the Musée national (Sandervalia) [3], the Botanical Garden [1] and the acrobats at the Centre Keita Fodeba (weekday mornings) [3].

  - 6 individual taxi rides × 15,000–30,000 = 90,000–180,000 [C daily.transport]

  - **Total ≈ 1,092,723–1,771,996 GNF a week (≈ €109–177)**. Cross-check: OpenShores' all-in single month of 600–1,000 USD is about 1.19–1.99 million GNF a week [C cost.living]. A guard (150,000–300,000/month) is extra if the compound does not include one [C cost.living].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Kipé**, walking distance to the Lycée français Albert Camus (in Kipé since 1986, ages 3–18, French bac) [10]. Expat post reports (talesmag) also name the American International School of Conakry (AISC, K-12), with mixed reviews from 2016–2022, and Tom Pouce, a French-language school up to about age 10. No addresses were found [15]. Care: Clinique Ambroise Paré (Corniche Nord) and the Centre mère et enfants paediatric clinic [11][12]. Families heading for Nongo should note the June 2026 crack evacuations and France Diplomatie's eastern line [20][C housing.areas].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school run on foot or by short taxi to the Lycée Albert Camus [10][C daily.transport].
  - Tue: groceries at Franprix Kipé [13].
  - Wed: half-day afternoon at Plage de Camayenne [21].
  - Thu: paediatric check at the Centre mère et enfants if needed [12].
  - Fri: dinner at Le Waffou [3].
  - Sat: Riviera Royal pool (price not found) [3]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Paroisse Anne-Marie Javouhey de Nongo or the Cathédrale [19][1]; Botanical Garden [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — needs a sitter: a full-time housekeeper costs 50–80 USD a month (OpenShores) [C cost.living]; venues as profile 1. "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Le Cèdre or Jardin de Chine [1]. "Tranquille chez moi" — home.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" — Îles de Loos, Roume (no cars on the islands) [C nature.access]. "En montagne, en forêt" — Soumba falls, by day [1]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — not realistic from Conakry. "Je reste en ville" — the Musée national and the acrobats' rehearsals [3].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Before arrival: apply online on the DCPAF platform (paf.gov.gn/visa) for the "visa volant". Biometrics and the visa itself are done at Conakry airport [C admin.visa]. Bring a yellow-fever certificate [C admin.visa].
- Day 1: land at Ahmed Sékou Touré airport (Gbessia, about 13 km from the centre) [2]. Take the motorway rather than the airport road at night (France Diplomatie) [D]. Agree the taxi fare before getting in. 150,000 GNF hotel-to-airport is the documented fair fare; 500,000 was asked of a foreign arrival [C cost.negotiation]. Stay first at the Maison d'Accueil, Catholic mission, Kaloum (€13 in 2020; GFr100,000–130,000 in 2017) [1][3], or Pension Les Palmiers, Ratoma (€70/75 in 2017) [3].
- Day 2: SIM from Orange, MTN or Cellcom; 5 GB costs 5–10 USD [C daily.internet]. Open an Orange Money wallet (mobile money is the practical alternative to cards and unreliable ATMs) [C daily.money]. Never change money in the street (arrests reported by GOV.UK) [C daily.money].
- Day 3: flat hunting through brokers and the "Conakry - Guinée Expats" Facebook group, which carries agency rental posts [C cost.rent]. Brokers charged a 50,000–70,000 GNF visit fee (≈ €5–7) plus about one month's rent as commission (Guineenews, 2022) [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: compare advances. One November 2026 listing (Samatra, 2.5 million GNF) asked 2 months' advance plus 2 months' deposit = 10 million GNF (≈ €1,000) [C cost.rent]. In 2022, 12 months was the common minimum, with up to five years asked [C cost.negotiation]. Landlords may take 10–20% off for a solvent, stable tenant (OpenShores) [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 5: check the flat for a generator, water tank and guard. Visit at night to see the power pattern (a common 2026 pattern was power from 18:00 to 08:00 only) [C daily.power][C daily.water].
- Day 6: walk the candidate area by day: Kipé or Camayenne. Do not settle east of Kipé/Taouyah without reinforced security [C housing.areas].
- Day 7: Sunday at Plage de Camayenne or Mass at the Cathédrale [21][1].
- Bank account: needs passport, residence permit and work contract (Cyril Jarnias), so it is not realistic in the first week [C daily.money]. Without a residence permit, foreigners "can be arrested" (France Diplomatie), so start the permit question early [C admin.residency].
- Rough first-week spend (GNF): 7 nights × 100,000–130,000 = 700,000–910,000 [3] + airport taxi 150,000 [C cost.negotiation] + 3 visits × 50,000–70,000 = 150,000–210,000 [C cost.negotiation] + 14 popular meals × 15,000–35,000 = 210,000–490,000 [C cost.living] + 10 taxi rides × 15,000–30,000 = 150,000–300,000 [C daily.transport] + 5 GB data 43,000–86,000 [C daily.internet] → **≈ 1,403,000–2,146,000 GNF (≈ €140–215)**. The 2017 hotel price is old. Add the move-in cash: on a 2.5 million flat this is 10 million GNF (2+2 months) up to 30 million (12 months), plus one month's commission [C cost.rent][C cost.negotiation].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best from December to April, the dry season with harmattan dust; July–August is the rainfall peak [D]. Lonely Planet (2017) names November–December as the best time [3].

### One day
- Morning: the acrobats' free rehearsal at the Centre d'Art Acrobatique Keita Fodeba, Dixinn stadium (10am–2pm on weekdays in 2017) [3]. Then the Grande mosquée Fayçal from outside and the Botanical Garden next door [7][1].
- Midday: taxi down the Corniche to Kaloum (fare 15,000–30,000 GNF) [C daily.transport]; lunch at Le Cèdre [1].
- Afternoon: Musée national de Sandervalia (GFr10,000 in 2017) [3]; the Cathédrale Sainte-Marie [1]; Marché du Niger before it closes around 5pm (watch your bag) [3][C safety.general].
- Evening: back towards Camayenne before dark. Dinner at Hotel Palm Camayenne or the Riviera Royal; a late night at the Riviera Royal nightclub if going out, with a booked driver [1][3][C safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive. Agree the airport taxi fare (about 150,000 GNF) and settle in Kaloum or Camayenne [C cost.negotiation][1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): Îles de Loos by motorised pirogue from Boulbinet or behind the Novotel. Visit Roume and its Plage du Gouverneur, and Kassa, about 4.5 km offshore [C nature.access][23][1]. A day trip costs 15–30 USD [C cost.living]. Check for holiday bans on pirogues [23].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): Madina market at opening, then back on the Conakry Express to Kaloum (weekdays, two trains a day). This is how the city shops and commutes, but Wikivoyage advises tourists against the train, so a taxi back is the safer option [1][C daily.transport].
- Day 5 (trip out): Soumba waterfalls past Dubréka, about 2 hours each way; swimming and a restaurant on site. Back before dark [1][D].
- Day 6: Plage de Camayenne; evening dinner in Kaloum or Camayenne [21][1].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathédrale Sainte-Marie or the Anglican church of Taouyah [1][9]. Or a football match: the 28-Septembre and Nongo stadiums were still under works in 2026, and Petit Sory in Nongo is football-only again [24][19].
- Note: a week reaches only the coast. The Fouta Djallon (Labé) is about 430 km away [D].
