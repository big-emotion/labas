# Conakry, Guinea

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: GNF → EUR uses the dossier rate **10,000 GNF ≈ €1** (the dossier marks it approximate and unverified). USD → EUR uses **1 USD ≈ €0.86**, derived from 1 USD ≈ 8,600 GNF (rate printed on nomad.watch, March 2026) and the dossier's GNF rate. Commercial relocation blogs (OpenShores, Cyril Jarnias, Africarrières) and crowd-sourced Numbeo are flagged as such; their figures are indicative and often disagree. Listing prices are asking prices.

Research limitation: the shared WebSearch budget ran out after four searches, so this pass relies on direct fetches of known pages and Guineenews' internal search. No social media, forums or Expat.com threads were reached beyond one listing.
