# Labé: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout: **10,000 GNF ≈ €1** (dossier rate, approximate and unverified) and **1 USD ≈ 8,600 GNF** (city-file rate). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/guinee/labe.md`; `[Ck tag]` = an item in `research/cities/guinee/conakry.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/guinee.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

**Read this first.** Labé is thinly documented online. No rent listing, restaurant, café, taxi fare, school fee or clinic name was found. Wikivoyage FR's Labé page is an empty outline, and the EN page lists one hotel [2][3]. The neighbourhood blocks below are therefore short, and several are set aside. The only dated everyday prices are the February 2026 central-market prices reported by Guineenews [11].

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Labé is in France Diplomatie's general yellow zone. The advisory warns of armed night robbers on the road to Labé, notably around Kindia and Mamou, and says to reach safety before nightfall (2026-09-15) [C safety.general].
- Conservative, strongly Muslim Peul city [D]. Police jailed 16 people in an October 2026 shisha crackdown [C social.nightlife]. Female genital mutilation persists, now practised discreetly (Guineenews, 2026-10-31) [C safety.women]. France Diplomatie urges extreme vigilance for uncut girls of Guinean origin from France visiting family [C safety.women]. Same-sex activity is illegal nationally [C rights.lgbt].
- Power: a 2023 interconnection stabilised supply in the urban commune, though about ten peri-urban neighbourhoods were still unconnected. A substation fault blacked out Labé, Pita and Dalaba in July 2025 [C daily.power]. Water: the mayor said in 2022 that the city "has no water", and Guineenews flagged unregulated boreholes in March 2024 [C daily.water][14].
- Altitude about 1,026 m, January lows around 12 °C, rains May–October [C climate.daily]. Roads to Lélouma and Tougué are hard in the rains [C daily.transport].
- Transport: Conakry is about 430 km away; the intercity fare is 80,000–120,000 GNF (≈ €8–12) (Africarrières, low) [C daily.transport]. A transporters' strike from 8 September 2026 paralysed the Kouroula bus station [C daily.transport]. Moto-taxis operate (a driver was shot near Hafia in September 2026) [C safety.general]; no fare was found. The national advisories advise against moto-taxis [Ck daily.transport].
- Land and housing: titles are rarely issued and double sales are common, per the deputy regional urbanism inspector [C housing.search].

## Neighbourhoods

### Centre-ville (Mairie, Mosquée, central market)
- Feel: the commercial heart. The central market sits between the Paraya and Sassé sectors [1] and draws traders from Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Gambia and Sierra Leone [C business.invest]. Land here sells at 4–5 billion GNF a plot; a broker says Mairie and Mosquée have reached city-centre prices (Guineenews, 2026-02-22) [12].
- Rent: no figure from a local source. An aggregator seen only in a search summary puts a central 1-bed at about 300 USD a month (≈ 2,580,000 GNF ≈ €258) (GlobalCostData, low) [C cost.living]. Residents complain of rising rents without figures [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot in the centre; moto-taxis (fare not found) [C safety.general].
- Watch: the city council shut the central-market butchery and other meat outlets in August 2026 over ignored meat tariffs [C cost.living][10]. Shops at the central market were broken into at night in May 2020 and December 2022 (Guineenews) [10]. All public toilets in the urban commune had been closed for more than two years in September 2023 [10].
- Places:
  - Marché central (Paraya and Sassé sectors; regional market, February 2026 staple prices in [11]) — Wikipédia [1]; Guineenews [10][11]
  - Grande mosquée de Karamoko Alpha (main mosque, tied to the city's founding about 1755) — Wikipédia [1]; Wikivoyage FR [2]
  - Paraya wholesale stalls near the big market (rice, oil, sugar) — Guineenews [11]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (regional trade); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" for Fouta families [C community.diaspora].

### Kouroula
- Feel: home to the city's main bus station [8][C daily.transport]. The broker in the land article says plots here now reach centre prices [12]. No description of daily life was found.
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: departures for Conakry, Kankan, N'Zérékoré, Senegal and Gambia leave from here when no strike is on [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the September 2026 transporters' strike paralysed the Kouroula station [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Gare routière de Kouroula (intercity bus and taxi station) — Guineenews 2026-09-09 [8]
  - École primaire de Kouroula (used as a polling station for Senegal's March 2024 presidential vote) — Guineenews [8]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (traders on the Senegal–Gambia routes); Q1 "Tester quelques mois" for someone moving often between cities. Two sourced places only: below the 3-place bar, so kept for orientation.

### Tata 1 and 2 (north, aerodrome)
- Feel: the airport quarter, near the regional governor's residence [13]. The Labé–Dakar road passes through Tata II [13].
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: Aérodrome de Tata (LEK) has no regular flights according to Wikipédia [C daily.transport]. Its reconstruction began in April 2023 and was 25% done in June 2024 [13]. A blog lists Labé flights at 40–100 USD without naming an airline [C daily.transport].
- Watch: five children drowned in unsecured water-filled pits on the aerodrome building site in Tata 1, May 2025 (Guineenews) [13]. A head-on taxi-moto and truck crash near the governor's residence killed two people in April 2026 [13]. The Labé–Dakar road at Tata II was closed after a large crack opened in the July 2023 rains [13].
- Places:
  - Aérodrome de Tata (LEK/GULB, 2,000 m runway) — Wikipédia [1]; Wikivoyage FR [2]; Guineenews [13]
- Fits: none clearly. It is described here because it is where an air link would arrive. Only one sourced place.

### Peri-urban university belt (Hafia, Garambé, Dara Labé)
- Feel: sub-prefectures around the urban commune, home to the Université de Labé campuses [1]. Plots here sell for 50–500 million GNF (≈ €5,000–50,000) (Guineenews, 2026-02-22) [12]. About ten peri-urban neighbourhoods had no grid power in 2023 [C daily.power].
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: by moto-taxi or car to the centre; distances and fares are not found.
- Watch: on 12 September 2026, a moto-taxi driver was shot and his motorbike taken at Kalimaso (Hafia sub-prefecture) (Guineenews) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Université de Labé (campuses at Hafia, Garambé and Dara Labé; autonomous since 2016) — Wikipédia [1]
  - Hotel project of KPC at Garambé (three-star hotel, works launched February 2021; opening not confirmed) — Guineenews headline [9]
- Fits: Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (university). Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" only once power and water are checked plot by plot [C daily.power].

## Living: typical weeks

Prices below come only from the city file, the dossier and the sources listed here. "Assumption" marks a step that has no source. There is **no sourced rent, restaurant meal, taxi fare, internet plan, utility bill or school fee for Labé**. Weekly spends are therefore partial and say what is missing.

Fixed items used below:
- **Grocery basket, one adult, one week** (Labé central market, Guineenews 2026-02-20) [11]: imported rice 270,000 per 50 kg = 5,400/kg × 3 kg = 16,200; potatoes 12,000/kg × 2 kg = 24,000 (a vendor's pre-Ramadan price, reported as rising, so a floor); one loaf a day × 4,000–4,500 = 28,000–31,500; vegetable oil 110,000 per 5 L = 22,000 for 1 L; sugar 375,000 per 50 kg = 7,500 for 1 kg. **Total 97,700–101,200 GNF** (quantities are an assumption; no meat, fish or vegetable price, since meat tariffs were in dispute) [11][C cost.living].
- **Rent placeholder**: central 1-bed about 300 USD/month = 2,580,000 ÷ 4.33 = **595,843 GNF a week** (GlobalCostData via search summary, low) [C cost.living].
- **All-in cross-check**: OpenShores' single-person month of 250–400 USD = 2.15–3.44 million GNF ÷ 4.33 = **496,536–794,457 GNF a week (≈ €50–79)**, the lowest of the Guinean cities it lists [C cost.living].
- **Conakry round trip**: 2 × 80,000–120,000 = 160,000–240,000 GNF [C daily.transport].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Centre-ville**, near the market and the grid, which is stable in the urban commune since 2023 [C daily.power][1]. Honest caveat: no Labé internet data exists. Keep a generator or inverter and a 4G backup (Conakry price for 5 GB: 5–10 USD) [Ck daily.internet].

  - Tue: Marché central for the week's food [1][11].
  - Wed: home office; late-afternoon visit to the Musée du Fouta Djallon (hours not found) [1].
  - Thu: home office; programme at the Ka Werdhe cultural centre, if any (programme not found) [1].
  - Fri: Friday prayer at the Grande mosquée de Karamoko Alpha, or a walk past it [1].
  - Sat: Fello Star home game at the Stade régional, if scheduled (fixtures not found) [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: day out in the Fouta (below), back before dark [C safety.general].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic in Labé. No club is sourced, the city is conservative, and shisha venues were raided in October 2026 [C social.nightlife][D].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": the bar at Hotel Djamtum serves food (2023 entry) [3]; no restaurant is sourced.
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the city's default; cool evenings at altitude [C climate.daily].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Labé (inland, about 430 km from the coast) [D].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": the Saala falls in Diari sub-prefecture, a weekend spot shared with Timbi Madina. Do not swim: students drowned there in 2018 and 2022 (Guineenews) [7]. Mont Kolima [1]. Near Pita, which lies between Labé and Dalaba: Kinkon falls (15 km from Pita), Kambadaga falls (16 km) and the Doucki cliffs (62 km) [5]. Dalaba, the old climate resort with the Jardin Chevalier botanical garden (1906) [6].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Labé (nothing sourced). Koundara, 4–5 hours north by shared taxi (150,000 GNF, 2023), is the road to Senegal [3], but no savanna park visit is sourced.
  - "Je reste en ville": the Musée du Fouta Djallon, Ka Werdhe, and the market [1].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Day 1: Conakry to Labé by road, about 430 km, 80,000–120,000 GNF, leaving early to arrive before nightfall [C daily.transport][C safety.general]. Check first for transporters' strikes [C daily.transport]. Stay at Hotel Djamtum (double 300,000 GNF in August 2023) [3].
- Day 2: SIM and Orange Money are best bought in Conakry, where agents and cash are documented [Ck daily.money]. No Labé agent or price was found. Bring enough cash in GNF; street money-changing is illegal [Ck daily.money].
- Day 3: Marché central and the centre on foot. Ask about rentals (no Labé listing site, broker practice, visit fee or advance custom was found) [1][C cost.rent].
- Day 4: check power and water at any candidate home. Ask whether the street is on the grid (peri-urban areas were not in 2023) and whether there is a borehole [C daily.power][14].
- Day 5: the university campuses or Tata, depending on work [1][13].
- Day 6: Saala falls or Pita's falls, back before dark [7][5].
- Day 7: Friday prayer or the Sunday service of one of the city's churches (none named) [1]. Bank account requirements (passport, residence permit, work contract) are the national ones from a Conakry guide [Ck daily.money].
- Rough first-week spend (GNF): 7 nights × 300,000 = 2,100,000 [3] + one-way fare 80,000–120,000 [C daily.transport] + bread and staples 97,700–101,200 [11] → **≈ 2,277,700–2,321,200 GNF (≈ €228–232)**. Meals out and moto-taxis are not priced. The 2023 hotel price is dated.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Come in the dry season, November–April. Rain falls May–October and peaks July–September [C climate.daily][D]. Travel only by day on the Conakry road [C safety.general]. The Festival des arts et du rire has run since 2015 (dates not found) [1].

### One day
- Morning: Marché central, Paraya and Sassé sectors, at opening [1][11]. Then the Grande mosquée de Karamoko Alpha from outside [1].
- Midday: no restaurant is sourced; the Hotel Djamtum bar serves food [3].
- Afternoon: Musée du Fouta Djallon (fee and hours not found) [1]; Ka Werdhe cultural centre [1].
- Evening: a Fello Star match at the Stade régional if scheduled [1], or a quiet evening; there is no sourced nightlife [C social.nightlife].
- Moving between them: on foot in the centre. Moto-taxis exist but are advised against nationally [Ck daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from Conakry by road (80,000–120,000 GNF, all day), before dark [C daily.transport][C safety.general].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): the Saala falls in Diari sub-prefecture. Look, do not swim (drownings in 2018 and 2022) [7].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): early morning at the Kouroula bus station as the long-distance cars load for Senegal, Gambia and Conakry, then the Marché central as traders arrive [8][C business.invest]. This is the city's working rhythm.
- Day 5 (trip out): towards Pita for the Kinkon falls (15 km from Pita) and the Kambadaga falls (16 km from Pita) [5][4]. Or further to the Doucki cliffs, 62 km from Pita, with a guide; Wikivoyage says the best-known Fouta guides are based in Labé, without naming one [5][4].
- Day 6 (trip out): Dalaba, the 1930s climate resort: the Jardin Chevalier (1906) and the villa Sili [6].
- Day 7: Friday prayer at the Grande mosquée if the day falls right, or a quiet morning; leave early for Conakry [1][C safety.general].
- Note: the Fouta roads need a 4WD according to Wikivoyage [4].
