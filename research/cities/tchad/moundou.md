## Items

- [cost.living] A young welder in Moundou says diesel costs him 1,000 XAF/L (≈ €1.52), against the official N'Djamena price of 800 XAF/L; provincial pump prices add transport costs on top of the capital's. — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-01 and 2026-08-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Fuel shortages and a price surge returned to Moundou in November 2024; in July 2023 stations were reported not respecting the regulated price. — Tchadinfos, 2024-11-06 and 2023-07-21 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] The national minimum wage (SMIG) is 60,000 XAF/month (≈ €91), unchanged since 2011, per a sociologist; a household "survival budget" is put at 2,000 XAF a day (≈ €3) for 5–6 people (national figures). — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] A Moundou moto-taxi driver (a law graduate waiting for a civil-service post) says he sometimes makes 7,500 XAF in a day (≈ €11.40). — Tchadinfos, 2025-02-24, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] A phone repairer at the Grand marché says good days bring in up to 50,000 XAF (≈ €76). — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-01, local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] When petrol rose in 2023, moto-taxi fares became driver-dependent: 250–300 XAF day trips went to 500–750 XAF (≈ €0.38–0.46 → €0.76–1.14) "depending on the driver", the usual 200 XAF trip doubled, and a 500 XAF night trip went to 1,500 XAF (≈ €2.29). Older item. — Tchadinfos, 2023-09-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In September 2023 petrol sold at 700–900 XAF/L in town (≈ €1.07–1.37) and only three stations sold retail; others were said to resell by the drum on the side. Older item. — Tchadinfos, 2023-09-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Moto-taxis ("clando") arrived in the early 1990s and are the main way to make short trips, from dawn to sunset; drivers wait at crossroads, hospitals and markets. Accidents are frequent, some drivers lack training, and drivers face bike theft, assault and killings. — Tchadinfos, 2025-02-24, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Moundou sits on the southern road axis Léré–Pala–Kélo–Doba–Koumra–Sarh and has an airport (MQQ) with a paved runway; no scheduled routes are listed. Leaving N'Djamena by road requires Interior Ministry authorisation, per FCDO. — Wikipedia "Moundou" / UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, reference / official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] FCDO: in the rainy season (July–October) roads are poor and often impassable, "especially in the south", and heavy rain can cause major flooding. — UK FCDO safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] The city has four arrondissements and 20 quartiers: 1st Dombao, Doyon, Tayé, Bebandji, Koudjiriko, Ngara; 2nd Baguirmi, Bornou, Guelbe, Haoussa; 3rd Dombeur, Guelkoura, Quinze ans, Doheri, Mbagué; 4th Mbombaya, Gueldjem, Dombeur 2, Koutou Guelmbagué, Madana. — Wikipédia (fr) "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The urban-planning office says peripheral quarters are growing faster than roads, drainage and services; a master plan exists but lacks money; local commissions settle land disputes over subdivisions and some plots are being regularised. — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-04, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] The mayor temporarily suspended all subdivisions and land allocations by decree no. 107/2026 — relevant for anyone planning to buy a plot. — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-11 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] A 4.6 MW dual-fuel plant at the Bélaba gas site was handed to the national utility SNE in August 2023; during trials the city had power continuously for over a month. Older item. — Tchadinfos, 2023-08-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In late May–June 2024 the Bélaba gas turbine broke down and Moundou went over a week without power; only the administrative district was supplied, during working hours. The SNE said Bélaba supplies most of the city and Perenco injects only 1 MW; shops ran generators or solar panels. — Tchadinfos, 2024-06-04, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Young entrepreneurs list energy supply difficulties, high equipment costs, taxes and hard access to financing as their main obstacles. — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-01, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] A community multimedia centre opened in September 2026 to give residents access to digital tools; Starlink is declared available across the whole national territory since July 2025 (plans from 12,000 XAF/month, ≈ €18). — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-29 (excerpt via site search) and 2025-07-04, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Main employers: CotonTchad (cotton ginning, oil mill, soap plant), the Brasseries du Logone (Gala beer) and the Manufacture des Cigarettes du Tchad. — Wikipedia "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Salaried jobs are described as very hard to find for young graduates; many open tailoring shops, salons, garages, phone/computer repair, small restaurants or digital-service centres instead (one centre now has over 100 computers). — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-01, local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Agro-value chains in the news: a Caritas Switzerland project linking sesame producer cooperatives with buyers (Nov 2026), and cotton growers near Moundou asking for a higher purchase price (Jan 2026). — Tchadinfos, 2026-11-13 and 2026-01-28 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] ARC EN ZIK launched the first cohort of a "Music Tech Hub" training young people in digital music production (Sept 2026), and an arts-and-entrepreneurship week centred on second-hand clothing was announced at the Maison de la culture (Nov 2026). — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-02 and 2026-11-09 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [business.admin] Local businesses were briefed on mandatory standardised electronic invoicing at a provincial workshop in October 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2026-10-16 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] About 15 public lycées and CEGs (e.g. Lycée Adoum Dallah) plus many private schools; higher education at IUTEM (2002), Université de Moundou (2008), Université catholique d'Afrique centrale (2022), a national health-sciences school and a teacher-training school. No international school found. — Wikipédia (fr) "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Nearly 1,000 candidates sat the Université de Moundou first-year medicine entrance exam in September 2026; the 2026–27 school year in the province launched with a focus on bilingual teaching (languages not named in the excerpt). — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-28 and 2026-09-03 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Local-drink "cabarets" serving bilibili, djala, cochette and bodo (cereal-based brews) are booming: authorities estimate 700–1,000 in the 1st arrondissement alone, often in homes or near markets, and they work as places to meet and debate the news. No prices given. — Tchadinfos, 2025-03-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Festival International Moundou-Mouve returned on 22–25 October 2026 after 11 years' interruption, with concerts by artists from Chad, Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire; the comedy festival "Le Rire de Bambé" holds its 5th edition on 28–31 December 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-16 and 2026-11-02, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Other recurring events: a community library's 10th "Mois du livre" (Nov 2026), the Koura Gosso festival (April 2025) and a Hausa language-and-culture day (July 2026). — Tchadinfos, 2026-11-05, 2026-09-16, 2026-07-27 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mosques are the most numerous places of worship; Christian communities include the Catholic Diocese of Moundou, the Evangelical Church of Chad and Brethren assemblies. Moundou is the main city of the Ngambai people. — Wikipedia "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Local club USM FC was reinstated for the national promotion play-offs starting 28 October 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2026-10-11 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO advises against all but essential travel to all of Chad outside the red zones; Moundou is not named, so it falls under that rating. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-11-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In May 2025, clashes over grazing and farmland at Mandakao (Logone-Occidental province) killed at least 41 people and wounded 6, per the government; opposition leader Succès Masra was arrested afterwards. — HRW World Report 2026 (events of 2025), intl-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The mayor announced solar street lamps along the Koutou road into town to improve security (Aug 2026); authorities held drug-awareness sessions (Aug 2026) and farmer-herder conflict-prevention visits in rural Moundou (Sept 2026). — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-25, 2026-08-22, 2026-09-20 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] Moundou has been twinned with Poitiers since 1990 (and Shanghai since 2007); France keeps a French-language centre (CALF) and an honorary consulate there. — Wikipédia (fr) "Moundou" / France Diplomatie, page updated 2026-11-04, reference / official, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Tropical savanna: about 1,083 mm of rain a year (1961–1990 normals), August wettest, December–January dry; hottest March–May before the rains. — Wikipedia "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Built-in flood risk: the 1926 street plan drained toward the river, and the mayor has said that when the Logone rises its water flows into Lac Wey and floods the city. — Wikipédia (fr) "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] An AFD-funded flood-control programme (PLIM 2) launched in March 2025, after a first phase from 2017; drain-clearing ahead of the rains began in the 2nd arrondissement in April 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2025-03-17 and 2026-04-06 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The city lies on the north bank of the Logone next to Lac Wey. — Wikipédia (fr) "Moundou", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [other] The commune banned child begging on public roads and in public buildings in September 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2026-09-08 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
