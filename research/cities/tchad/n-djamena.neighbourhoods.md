# N'Djamena: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = 655.957 XAF** (fixed CEMAC peg, dossier rate). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/tchad/n-djamena.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/tchad.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- **Travel advice frames everything below.** The UK FCDO advises against all but essential travel to N'Djamena and warns that insurance may be void (updated 2026-11-04) [C safety.general]; the US rates Chad Level 4 "Do Not Travel" (28 Apr 2026) [Dos]. FCDO prefers driving or pre-arranged secure transport to walking after dark, and leaving the city needs Interior Ministry authorisation [C daily.transport]. Every evening and every trip out of town below assumes a pre-arranged car and the permit.
- **Health, Nov 2026:** a dengue epidemic is filling the capital's hospitals; on 28 Oct 2026 the Prime Minister found patients on the floor at the Hôpital général de référence nationale and a doctors' strike at the Hôpital de l'Amitié Tchad-Chine [26]. Lamadji cemetery alone logged 2,072 burials in October 2026; the causes are not attributed [25]. Cholera was reported in Sept 2026 [C health.care].
- **Two rent markets that do not meet.** Expat-standard housing in the centre was quoted at 650,000–2,000,000 XAF a month around 2020 [C cost.rent]; local media in 2023–2026 quote 35,000 XAF for a single room and 50,000–150,000 XAF for a two-bedroom [13][14][15]. No dated listing ties a price to a named neighbourhood except Ambassatna and Habbena [13].
- **Power:** cuts lasting days or weeks in several quarters (Oct 2026) [C daily.power]; the Farcha 1 power station caught fire on 26 Jul 2026 [22].
- **Search note:** the shared web-search budget ran out after two searches in this pass, so places come from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, Talesmag, TripAdvisor list, Tchadinfos site search). Many venues have no sourced quarter; they are listed once under "Places with no sourced quarter" rather than guessed into a neighbourhood.

## Neighbourhoods

The city has ten arrondissements; quarter names below follow fr.Wikipédia's 2009 table [6].

### Centre: Amriguébé, Klémat and the colonial core along the Chari (2nd and 5th arr.)
- Feel: the old colonial core along the Chari holds most administrative institutions; Avenue Charles de Gaulle is the main commercial avenue and the "Nassara Strip" a commercial centre [5]. Klémat hosts institutions: the UN common office [21], the ruling MPS party's national headquarters (inaugurated Nov 2025) [21] and the CNPS business centre (provisionally received Oct 2024) [34]. Older central quarters are preferred for being near hospitals and markets, but houses to rent there are "almost impossible to find" (Tchadinfos, 2023-11-05) [15].
- Rent: centre is "where most expatriates live"; a worn but secured 3-bedroom with AC and generator 800,000 XAF (≈ €1,220), a two-room "studio" 650,000 (≈ €991), 3–4 bedrooms from 1,000,000 (≈ €1,525), villa with garden about 2,000,000 (≈ €3,049) (Expat.com forum, c. 2020) [C cost.rent]. Local-standard: a self-contained house (bedroom, living room, shower, kitchen) in central N'Djamena 100,000–250,000 XAF (≈ €152–381) (Tchadinfos, 2023-11-05) [15].
- Getting around: a diplomat reports 10-minute commutes, 15–20 in heavy traffic (Talesmag, 2026-05-03) [11]. Taxi 3,000 XAF by day, 5,000 at night, agree the price first (en.Wikivoyage, undated listing) [3]. Minibus fares from the Grand marché: to Gassi 250 XAF, to Farcha on a contested fare (Feb 2024) [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: FCDO warns of fatal shootings on the road in front of the Presidential Palace (accessed 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]; Wikivoyage advises caution about photographing the palace [3]. In Feb 2026 the housing minister ordered informal settlements cleared from the state reserve in the "Bouta Klemat" depression [21].
- Places:
  - Musée national du Tchad, Amriguébé, 5th arr., facing the Palais du 15 Janvier (national museum; Sao collections, casts of Toumaï) — fr.Wikipédia [7]
  - Bibliothèque nationale du Tchad, next to the museum (national library) — fr.Wikipédia [7]
  - Cathédrale Notre-Dame de la Paix, near Avenue Charles de Gaulle and Avenue Félix Éboué (Catholic cathedral, rebuilt 1983–86, restored 2013) — en.Wikipedia [8]
  - Avenue Charles de Gaulle (colonial-era houses and embassies) — en.Wikivoyage [3]
  - Centre Al-Mouna, avenue de Gaulle (cultural centre; music lessons for children at "very low" prices) — [C family.childcare]; hosted the Euro-African film festival in Nov 2025 [23]
  - Institut français du Tchad, Avenue Mobutu (French institute; main Fête de la musique concert, 20 Jun 2026) — fr.Wikipédia [10]; Tchadinfos [32]
  - Place de la Nation (central square; the Oct 2026 anti-dengue spraying campaign started there) — fr.Wikipédia [6]; Tchadinfos [27]
  - N'Djamena Grand Mosque (Grande Mosquée Roi Fayçal, completed 1978; coordinates 12°06′38″N 15°03′03″E, no quarter given) — en.Wikipedia [9]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (UN, NGO and ministry addresses); Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (expat-standard rents); Q10 "Ma carrière".

### Sabangali, Kabalaye and Ambassatna (3rd arr.)
- Feel: riverside quarters. Sabangali has the Radisson Blu [3], the Catholic University Centre [23], an arts residency (Knock On Art) [23] and the ANVOL-T volunteer office [23]; its roads were resurfaced in Jan 2026 [23]. Kabalaye is named among the city's night hotspots (Tchadinfos, 2026-09-08) [18]. Ambassatna is a popular quarter: a family of five shares one room with no ventilation or private toilet [13].
- Rent: Ambassatna, one room for a family 35,000 XAF/month (≈ €53), raised every year without explanation (Tchadinfos, 2025-07-08) [13]. No Sabangali listing found.
- Getting around: no sourced time to the centre; taxis 3,000/5,000 XAF day/night [3].
- Watch: an association ran a solidarity day for Sabangali flood victims in Oct 2024 [23]; quarrying on the Chari riverbed at Sabangali was closed by ministerial order in Jul 2026 [23]. Riverbank protection works have run since 2025 [28].
- Places:
  - Radisson Blu, Quartier Sabangali (hotel; about US$200+ a night, listing Nov 2025) — en.Wikivoyage [3]
  - Centre universitaire catholique, Sabangali (Catholic university centre; hosts public meetings) — Tchadinfos [23]
  - Knock On Art premises, Sabangali (arts association; creative residency for young talent) — Tchadinfos [23]
  - OLA Energy fuel station, Sabangali (launch site, Feb 2026) — Tchadinfos [23]
  - Chari riverbank at Sabangali (planned "mini-plage" site, no date) — Tchadinfos [28]
- Fits: Q5 "Création, culture" (arts residency, university centre); Q3 "Serré" in Ambassatna, with the caveat that rooms there are cramped; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Moursal and Paris-Congo (6th arr.)
- Feel: named by Wikipedia among the residential areas [5]; the dossier reads that as the best-known areas for expatriates and the middle class [Dos]. Moursal is also one of the city's night hotspots [18]. Local news shows a busy, mixed quarter: a market, a public primary school, churches and bars [20].
- Rent: no Moursal or Paris-Congo listing found.
- Getting around: no sourced time or fare.
- Watch: weeks of gunfire into the air during late-night traditional dances on a vacant plot; residents filed a complaint on 4 Sep 2026 [C safety.general][20]. A man was stabbed to death in a quarrel (Tchadinfos, 2026-10-12) [20]. One death from adulterated whisky at an informal outlet behind the "Chin" school, the second recorded at that spot (2026-08-11) [20]. A businessman was shot in front of his bar (2023-05-05) [20]. Fire in a compound, no deaths (2026-09-06) [20].
- Places:

  - COST headquarters site (national Olympic committee; first stone Dec 2023, completion not found) — Tchadinfos [20]

### Chagoua, Gassi and Habbena (7th arr.)
- Feel: the most populous arrondissement (223,231 in 2009) [6]. Gassi became a night hotspot in 2026: braziers on the pavement, bars, "caves", football screenings, crowds from about 18:00 "jusqu'à l'aube" at weekends (Tchadinfos, 2026-09-08) [18]. Chagoua has a parish choir, a new mosque and a community centre with jazz and slam events [17]. The US embassy is near Chagoua [17].
- Rent: Habbena, a room with an internal shower 55,000 XAF/month (≈ €84) (Tchadinfos, 2025-07-08) [13].
- Getting around: minibus Gassi–Chagoua 200 XAF, Grand marché–Gassi 250 XAF, Gassi–double-voies roundabout 150 XAF (after the Feb 2024 fuel decree) [C daily.transport]. A new six-lane, 320 m bridge at Chagoua was announced to open on 1 Dec 2025 [17]; the city file's Pont de la Refondation, opened that day, may be the same bridge (not confirmed).
- Watch: power cuts left Habbena shops without yoghurt in the hot season (May 2026) [C daily.power]. Three classrooms of a Chagoua school occupied by suspected criminals for years (2025-04-05) [17]. A pedestrian killed at the Chagoua viaduct (2025-10-08) [17]. A journalist was shot dead in Chagoua near the US embassy in 2022 [17]. Fires destroyed shops and homes in Gassi in March 2026 [19]. Waste piles up at the Abena market (2026-09-20) [17].
- Places:
  - Hôpital Notre-Dame des Apôtres, Chagoua (hospital) — fr.Wikipédia [6]
  - Sacré-Cœur Chagoua parish (Catholic church; choir releasing an album, 2025) — Tchadinfos [17]
  - Salmane Al-Farisi mosque, Chagoua (inaugurated 22 May 2026) — Tchadinfos [17]
  - Maison de quartier de Chagoua (community centre; Ronel Jazz festival Nov 2025) — Tchadinfos [17]
  - Gassi night strip (bars, grills, "caves", football screenings) — Tchadinfos [18]
  - Marché d'Abena / Habena (market, 7th arr.) — fr.Wikipédia [6]; Tchadinfos [17]
  - Stade d'Abena (stadium; Olympic torch run finish, Sept 2026) — Tchadinfos [17]
  - Commercial Bank Tchad, Gassi branch (bank, opened Dec 2025) — Tchadinfos [19]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (markets, bank branch, dense clientele).

### Diguel (8th arr.)
- Feel: a large quarter with a market, a stadium with a multipurpose hall, a public lycée and the national health-insurance agency's headquarters [24]. Fête de la musique events were held in Diguel and Habena in 2025 [24].
- Rent: no listing found.
- Getting around: no sourced time or fare. City authorities asked traders to clear the road between the Diguel viaduct and Tacha Moussoro, which was blocking traffic and drainage (May 2026) [24].
- Watch: the Lycée de Diguel-Est yard flooded in Sept 2024 and had to be pumped [24]; a woman was rescued from a drainage pit (2026-11-06) [24]; night fires at the Diguel market in Mar 2025 and Aug 2026 [24]. An opinion piece describes impassable streets and overflowing retention basins citywide in the rains (2026-07-21) [24].
- Places:
  - Marché de Diguel (market; livestock and sheep markets nearby) — fr.Wikipédia [6]; Tchadinfos [24]

- Fits: Q8 "Je reste en ville" (stadium life); Q3 "Moyen"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (a hotel to start from).

### Walia and Toukra (9th arr., south bank)
- Feel: across the Chari, linked by the Pont de la Refondation (opened 1 Dec 2025); since then most residents take the bus [C daily.transport]. Walia is named among night hotspots [18].
- Rent: no listing found; local media say peripheral quarters are cheaper but have longer, costlier trips and fewer services (Alwihda, 2026-08-13) [14].
- Getting around: bus Walia–Dembé 150 XAF (≈ €0.23); moto-taxis quoted 1,000 XAF for the same trip (Tchadinfos, 2026-01-03) [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: floods. The Chari reached 8.14 m in Oct 2022, and in Oct 2024 Walia, Ngoumna, Digangali and Kabé were again on alert [C climate.daily]. In Toukra the road along the cemetery stays dark and residents report frequent assaults; a solar lighting project has stalled (2026-10-25) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Hôpital universitaire Le Bon Samaritain, Walia (university hospital) — fr.Wikipédia [6]; [C health.care]
  - Hôpital de Walia (hospital) — en.Wikivoyage [3]
  - Pont de la Refondation (bridge, Walia side) — [C daily.transport]
  - Marché de Dembé (market at the other end of the bus line) — fr.Wikipédia [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest bus links), only outside the flood months; Q7 "Prend son temps"; not Q4 "Avec mes enfants" in the rains.

- Where to stay first: Hotel L'Amitié, Diguel Est (from €245, Aug 2024), Radisson Blu, Sabangali (about US$200+, Nov 2025), or the cheaper Chez Wou near La Tchadienne [3].
- Day 1: arrive (Air France from Paris; Turkish via Istanbul) [Dos]; car to the hotel.
- Day 2: police registration within 72 hours [Dos].
- Day 3: SIM card with identity documents (ARCEP checks operators for anonymous SIM sales, May 2025) [33]; mobile money via Moov Money or Airtel Money [34][Dos]. Bundle prices not found.
- Day 4: flat hunting. Go direct to owners (cheaper than intermediaries); furnished flats are rare but a landlord can be asked to furnish [C cost.negotiation]. Brokers sometimes pre-reserve homes at high prices [15]. A Tchadinfos headline reports landlords refusing to rent to single women (2025-03-20; seen in search results, not opened) [35].
- Day 5: visit fees and advance: several months in advance plus a deposit (Alwihda, 2026) [14]; 3–6 months in advance (forum c. 2009) [C cost.rent]. No agency-fee norm found.
- Day 6: bank: Commercial Bank Tchad has a Gassi branch [19]; account-opening rules not found. Most places want cash; ATMs exist [11][12].
- Day 7: Sunday at the museum and the riverside at sunset [7][3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": Gassi [18]. "Un bon dîner entre amis": Layalina [16]. "Tranquille chez moi": hotel.
- Weekend out (Q8): first week, stay in town: no permit yet.

## Visiting: discovery

### One day
- Morning: Musée national du Tchad and the Bibliothèque nationale, Amriguébé (no fee or hours found) [7].
- Midday: Côte Jardin, Rue Idriss Miskine (opens 11:00) [3], or Hybah Coffee House [3].
- Afternoon: Cathédrale Notre-Dame de la Paix and Avenue Charles de Gaulle's colonial houses [8][3]; the Grand Mosque from outside [9]. No photos near the Presidential Palace [3][C safety.general].
- Evening: sunset over the Chari, which Wikivoyage calls spectacular [3]; then dinner at La Cave [16] or a programme at the Institut français [10].
- Moving: taxis at 3,000 XAF by day, 5,000 at night [3]; agree the fare first.

### One week
- Day 1: arrival, hotel, police registration [Dos].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: **daily-life moment**: a market morning at the Marché de Diguel or the Abena market, then minibus Grand marché–Gassi at rush hour (250 XAF) [24][17][C daily.transport].
- Day 4: Gaoui, about 10 km north-east: Sao-Kotoko museum (reopened 2023) and traditional architecture [29][3]; the access road was nearly cut in Jul 2024 [29].
- Day 5: WenakLabs at the Marché à Mil [C business.startup]; Centre Al-Mouna [C family.childcare].
- Day 6: football at the Stade de Diguel, or a Gazelle FC or Geyser FC match if scheduled [24][C social.sport]; evening at Gassi [18].
- Day 7: Douguia / Rocher des Éléphants, about an hour away, only with the permit [C nature.access][30]. The forum spells it "Dougia" and Tchadinfos "Douguia" (Hadjer-Lamis province); treated as the same place, not confirmed. Zakouma (about 600 km) is not a weekend trip [C nature.access].
