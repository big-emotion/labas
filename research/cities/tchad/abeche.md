## Items

- [cost.living] Water from vendors cost up to 1,500 XAF (≈ €2.29) per "pousse-pousse" handcart of ten jerrycans in March 2025, and up to 2,000 XAF (≈ €3.05) in outlying quarters. — Tchadinfos, 2025-03-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] A "coro" measure of onions rose from 500 XAF to 1,250–1,500 XAF (≈ €0.76 → €1.91–2.29) in mid-2025, amid fuel shortages; Tchadinfos said living costs had become "untenable" and some traders raised prices without good reason. — Tchadinfos, 2025-06-04, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] The national minimum wage (SMIG) is 60,000 XAF/month (≈ €91), unchanged since 2011, per a sociologist (national figure). — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] In March 2025 some taps had been dry for days, cuts lasted two to three days, and water vendors queued for hours or a whole day; in Chetyé (east) drinking water was described as almost non-existent and delivered by tricycle. Agaté was also named. — Tchadinfos, 2025-03-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The Biteha 2 water project, launched in 2019 to build two large water towers about 30 km away, had not ended the shortage five years on; it reported "record" production in March 2025, and its progress was still being discussed with civil society in May 2026. — Tchadinfos, 2025-03-02, 2025-03-06, 2026-05-04 (last two excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] A water tower collapsed in Hilélé Icher (3rd arrondissement) in August 2026, killing two people, reportedly after being filled. — Tchadinfos, 2026-08-31 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Fuel shortage in April 2025: tankers were said to empty Abéché's stations toward war-torn Sudan, where a drum sold for up to 500,000 XAF at the border; tricycle-taxi ("rackha") drivers raised fares and spent days waiting at the few supplied stations. — Tchadinfos, 2025-04-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The coach fare Abéché–N'Djamena was 25,000 XAF (≈ €38) in July 2023, which civil society called unjustified. Older item. — Tchadinfos, 2023-07-21 (excerpt via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Abéché airport (AEH) has flights to N'Djamena and Faya-Largeau; roads link the city to N'Djamena, Sarh and Sudan. — Wikipedia "Abéché", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] The economy rests on trade and livestock; Abéché is Chad's main camel export market, and its market sells vegetables, onions, spices, textiles, crafts and leather goods. — Wikipedia / Wikipédia (fr) "Abéché", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Humanitarian employers present: an IMC / Première Urgence Internationale / Solidarité International consortium serving Sudanese refugees, host communities and returnees in eastern Chad (Oct 2026), and UNHCR and WFP running aid-targeting workshops (Sept 2026), plus OCHA's sub-office. — Tchadinfos, 2026-10-14 and 2026-09-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A neonatology unit opened at the university hospital (CHU) in February 2026, and new CHU buildings were visited with Islamic Development Bank and UNICEF representatives. — Tchadinfos, 2026-02-28 and 2026-02-14 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Université Adam Barka (2003) and INSTA (1997); ten lycées including a Franco-Arabic national lycée and a girls' lycée. — Wikipédia (fr) "Abéché", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] A city with a strong Islamic heritage (Grand Mosque, Koranic masters "faki"), Chadian Arabic in daily use; a church is also mentioned. — Wikipedia / Wikipédia (fr) "Abéché", accessed 2026-11-14, reference, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Abéché is to host the 28th Fête nationale de la jeunesse and the national media forum (announced Oct 2026). — Tchadinfos, 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-30 (excerpts via site search), local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO advises against all travel within 30 km of Chad's borders and all but essential travel elsewhere; Abéché itself is about 164 km from Adré on the Sudan border, so it falls under "all but essential". — UK FCDO, updated 2026-11-04 / Wikipedia, official / reference, confidence: medium
