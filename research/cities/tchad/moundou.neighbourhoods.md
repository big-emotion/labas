# Moundou: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 = 655.957 XAF** (fixed CEMAC peg, dossier rate). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/tchad/moundou.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/tchad.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- **No rent, meal or drink price exists for Moundou in any source read.** Every weekly spend below covers only transport and connectivity; rent and food are marked unknown. This file is thinner than most and says so rather than filling gaps.
- **Travel advice:** FCDO advises against all but essential travel to all of Chad outside the red zones; Moundou is not named, so it falls under that rating (updated 2026-11-04) [C safety.general]. Reaching Moundou by road from N'Djamena needs Interior Ministry authorisation [C daily.transport].
- **Region:** clashes over grazing and farmland at Mandakao (Logone-Occidental) killed at least 41 people in May 2025 [C safety.general]; Tchadinfos reported an attack in neighbouring Logone Oriental on 13 Nov 2026 (headline only) [Dos]. In July 2025 armed home robberies hit families in Doyon and Djarabé [9].
- **Floods and roads:** the 1926 street plan drains towards the river, so a rising Logone floods the town [C climate.daily]; southern roads are often impassable July–October [C daily.transport].
- **Power:** a week-long blackout in 2024 [C daily.power]; the Bélaba plant restarted on 1 May 2026 after four months off [11]; in June 2026 one 3.5 MW plant supplied the city "without major interruption" and a downtown cybercafé owner called power almost constant for a month [10].
- **Quarter names:** fr.Wikipédia lists four arrondissements and 20 quarters [4]; Tchadinfos places Bélaba in the 1st (2024) and the 5th arrondissement (2026) and Tayé in the 6th [6][11][7]. The arrondissement count has probably changed; quarter-to-arrondissement links below follow the most recent source.
- **Search note:** the shared web-search budget was exhausted before this file; everything comes from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipédia, Tchadinfos articles and site search).

## Neighbourhoods

### Centre-ville and the administrative district
- Feel: during the 2024 blackout only the administrative district was supplied, during working hours [C daily.power]; in 2026 downtown businesses report a more stable supply [10]. No source describes the pace or who lives there.
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: moto-taxis ("clando") wait at crossroads, hospitals and markets and run from dawn to sunset [15]. Day trips that cost 250–300 XAF rose to 500–750 XAF (≈ €0.76–1.14) "depending on the driver" in Sept 2023; a 500 XAF night trip went to 1,500 XAF (≈ €2.29) [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: fire in the second-hand clothes section of the central market (19 Jun 2024) [5].
- Places (the sources give no quarter; they are placed here by name or function, not by an address):
  - Marché central de Moundou (central market, with a friperie section) — Tchadinfos [5]
  - Hôtel de ville de Moundou (town hall; a history of Moundou was presented there in Jun 2024) — Tchadinfos [16]
  - BEAC branch (central bank agency) — fr.Wikipédia gallery [4]
  - Maison de la culture Maoundoé Naindouba (culture house; press events and book signings, Jan 2024 and Nov 2026) — Tchadinfos [12]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (the most stable power reported in 2026); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois".

### Doyon and Djarabé (1st arr. for Doyon)
- Feel: Doyon has a teacher-training school and an HIV-support centre [11]; Djarabé is large enough to owe about 334 million XAF in unpaid power bills (2026) [10] and has its own football club [8]. No source describes daily feel.
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: no sourced distance or fare beyond the citywide clando fares [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: armed home robberies against several families in Doyon and Djarabé; security meetings at the town hall and governorate and a 72-hour ultimatum to security forces (Tchadinfos, 2025-07-18) [9]. A Doyon merchant reports bills rising during outages (2026) [10]. Residents called a new borehole site in front of the school unsafe (2026-02-08) [11].
- Places:
  - École normale, Doyon (teacher-training school) — Tchadinfos [11]
  - Centre ADNS+, Doyon (HIV-support association centre; open day announced Nov 2025) — Tchadinfos [11]
  - Djarabé FC (football club, Lac Wey league) — Tchadinfos [8]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" only with the robbery caveat; not Q2 "Tranquille chez moi" for someone wanting low risk; Q8 "Je reste en ville" (local football).

### Bélaba
- Feel: home of the fruit and vegetable market opened in early 2014 and of the dual-fuel power plant [6][C daily.power]. A wholesaler says he can sell 200,000–250,000 XAF a day (2024) [6].
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: clando, fares as above [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: the Bélaba plant broke down for over a week in mid-2024 and stopped for four months until May 2026 [C daily.power][11]. A man's body was found in the quarter (2026-03-24) [11].
- Places:
  - Marché de Bélaba (fruit and vegetables, much of it from Cameroon: avocados, pineapples, plantains, yams, palm oil) — Tchadinfos [6]
  - Centrale de Bélaba (4.6 MW dual-fuel plant) — [C daily.power]; Tchadinfos [11]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (trade hub); Q3 "Serré" (market prices not sourced).

### Gueldjem and the Koutou road (3rd arr. per Tchadinfos; 4th per fr.Wikipédia)
- Feel: the Guelmbagué market is here; the city launched its clean-city campaign there with 1,000 young volunteers (4 Jul 2026) [5]. The Koutou road into town is getting solar street lamps for security, and a public space is planned at the city entrance (Aug 2026) [11][C safety.general].
- Rent: not found.
- Getting around: clando, fares as above [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: the street lamps were announced, not installed (Aug 2026) [11]; in June 2026 residents of Kaga complained of an unlit wooded road and of safety [10] (Kaga's location relative to Koutou not given).
- Places:
  - Marché de Guelmbagué, Gueldjem (market) — Tchadinfos [5]
  - Koutou road (city entrance; solar lighting project) — Tchadinfos [11]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (market); Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

Every week assumes the Interior Ministry permit to travel from N'Djamena [C daily.transport], clando trips by day only (they run until sunset [15]) and no night road travel (a night crash between Kana and Moundou killed six, 2025-12-23 [7]).

- Base: **centre-ville**, where power was most stable in 2026 [10]; Starlink for internet (declared available nationwide since Jul 2025) [C daily.internet]; a solar kit or generator, given the 2024 and 2026 plant stoppages [C daily.power][11].

- Tuesday: work; afternoon at the community multimedia centre to meet local users [C daily.internet].
- Wednesday: Bélaba market for fruit [6].

  - "Tranquille chez moi": the realistic default.

  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Moundou (no sourced site).
