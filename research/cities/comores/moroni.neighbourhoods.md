## Neighbourhoods

### Médina (Badjanani and Mtsangani) and Place de France
- Feel: the historic core; Badjanani holds the old Friday mosque and Mtsangani sits by the beach and the old port [3]. Inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage list in July 2026 (city file, [social.culture], Mayotte Hebdo 2026-07-28). RFI (30 Aug 2026) describes it as still lived in, with children playing in the alleys, some houses turned into shops and some families gone [9]. Place de France next to it is the banks-cafés-taxi hub, noisy, with patchy pavements [2].
- Rent: no medina figure. Citywide: studio or 1-bed in Moroni 150,000–300,000 KMF/month (≈ €305–610) (Africarrières 2026-02-25, city file); furnished 45 m² studio 75,000 KMF (≈ €152) "standard area" (Jarnias 2026-01-25, city file, low confidence). A blog notes back-street medina rooms can be cheaper but may lack reliable water or power [2].
- Getting around: medina, port, Volo Volo market and the centre are within about 20 minutes' walk of each other [2]. Shared taxis are the only public transport in town: 200 KMF (≈ €0.41) per person within the city per Wikivoyage [1] (undated); Africarrières gives 500–1,500 KMF (≈ €1–3) per trip (2026-02-25, city file).
- Watch: the FCDO notes low crime but pickpocketing, and that walking alone at night raises the risk (accessed 2026-11-14, city file [safety.general]).
- Places:
  - Old Friday Mosque / Badjanani Mosque (mosque, 1427, minaret 1921; exterior visit, ask before entering) — [4], [1], [2]
  - Medina and old harbour / dhow quay (historic district, free) — [2], [1]
  - Café de la Medina (café, Badjanani, breakfast ≈ 1,500 KMF) — [1]
  - The New Select (restaurant, Place des Banques; breakfast ≈ 1,500 KMF, mains from 4,000) — [1]
  - Petite marché (open-air market along the old city walls) — [8]
  - Hôtel Les Arcades (hotel, Rue de la Corniche, US$40–50 double) — [1]
  - National Museum / CNDRS (museum; Mon–Thu 07:30–12:30 and 13:30–16:45, Fri 07:30–11:00, Sat 08:00–12:00; ≈ €2–5) — [1], [8], [2]
- Fits: Q1 "D'abord des vacances" and Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (walkable base for the first nights); Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; less so Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (no family housing named, water and power doubts [2]).

### Hankounou and the Corniche
- Feel: the seafront strip north of the centre with the city's restaurant cluster, the tennis club and the only two night venues a source names [1]. Expat.com calls seafront neighbourhoods relatively secure and says Moroni concentrates the expats, embassies, banks and hotels (2025-10-14, city file, low confidence). Airbnb listings cluster in Hankounou (2026-11-14, city file).
- Rent: no Hankounou figure. Citywide expat-type flat €300–600/month, utilities excluded (Expat.com 2025-10-14, city file); furnished 45 m² studio in an "expensive area" 112,450 KMF (≈ €229) (Jarnias 2026-01-25, low). Short stays from €27/night on Airbnb (2026-11-14, city file).
- Getting around: a few minutes by shared taxi to Place de France; El-Maarouf hospital sits in the "Moroni Ambassadeur" area [11]. The Corniche is poorly lit after dark and shared taxis stop around 20:00–21:00 [2].
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated found.
- Places:
  - New Mumbai (Indian restaurant, Rue de la Corniche, Hankounou; mains €6–10) — [1]
  - L'Escale (restaurant, Rue de la Corniche; €6–12) — [1]
  - Tennis Club / Club House (sports club and restaurant, Rue de la Corniche) — [1]
  - River Gauche (street barbecue, Corniche) — [1]
  - VIP club (nightclub, Route de la Corniche, Fri–Sat; entry 5,000 KMF ≈ €10 with a drink) — [1]
  - Le Rose Noir (nightclub, Route de la Corniche, Saturday nights; fee not given) — [1]
  - La Grillade, formerly Vacoa Lodge (hotel with restaurant, Rue de la Corniche) — [8]
  - CHN El-Maarouf (national reference hospital, Moroni Ambassadeur; emergency, maternity, dialysis listed) — [11], [10]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (the only sourced club and restaurant strip); Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Moyen".

### La Coulée (Coulée de lave) and Caltex / Asgaraly
- Feel: residential districts on the lava flow and around the Caltex crossroads [3]; Coulée has primary schools, the collège pilote and a mosque, so it reads as a family quarter, though no source describes who lives there [3].
- Rent: no figure by area. Local family budget for essentials 250,000–400,000 KMF/month, of which rent and charges 100,000–200,000 (≈ €203–407) (Africarrières 2026-02-25, city file).
- Getting around: the Culée roundabout is the stop for shared taxis east to Oichili and Dimani [1]; town fares as above.
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated found.
- Places:
  - Achadhuli Al-Yashrutwi mosque (mosque, Coulée de laves) — [3]
  - Collège pilote de la Coulée and Coulée primary schools EPP1 (public schools) — [3]
  - Mag supermarket (supermarket, Asgaraly, open until at least 21:00) — [1]
  - Wasta (fast food / restaurant, Caltex, Rue Itsambuni; €3–10) — [1]
  - Coraya Express (burgers ≈ 3,000 KMF, beside École Abdulhamid) — [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" with Q3 "Serré" or "Moyen" (public schools nearby, supermarket); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Hamramba
- Feel: residential quarter that hosts the French school and a hotel on Avenue du Président Ali Soilihi [12], [8]; one Airbnb studio here offers long-term stays (2026-11-14, city file). No source describes its pace or residents.
- Rent: no Hamramba figure; villa with garden, generator and air-conditioning €800–1,500/month citywide (Expat.com 2025-10-14, city file); family 2–3-bed 300,000–600,000 KMF (≈ €610–1,220) (Africarrières 2026-02-25, city file).
- Getting around: shared taxi to the centre (town fare); no distance found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated found. Citywide, Sonelec reports ≈ 20 h of power a day in 2026 (La Gazette 2026-10-21, city file), so a generator matters for a family house.
- Places:
  - École française Henri-Matisse (AEFE-convention school, BP 2516 Hamramba; ≈ 400 pupils in 2025–26; fee rises +€155 in 2026–27 and +€444 in 2027–28 contested by parents) — [12]; Mayotte Hebdo 2026-03-27 (city file)
  - Hamramba school (school building put up by US Navy Seabees in 2010) — [3]
  - Ravinala (hotel with restaurant, Av. du Président Ali Soilihi; 16,000–35,000 KMF, entry last changed 2020) — [8]
  - Retaj Moroni (hotel, "Soihili"; breakfast buffet €6 for non-guests) and Sawa Prix (supermarket near Retaj) — [1], [8]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" + Q10 "La santé et l'école" (the only French-curriculum school sourced); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" for a villa.

### Itsandra (Itsandra Mdjini, Voidjou)
- Feel: a separate small town (≈ 3,757 people, 2012) on Moroni's northern edge, home to the Beit-Salam presidential palace [6]; it has a white-sand public beach, a medina and the remains of a sultan's fort [1], [8]. Airbnb listings cluster here (city file).
- Rent: no Itsandra figure; use the citywide ranges above. Golden Tulip (former Hotel Itsandra) from €104–120 a night [1], [8].
- Getting around: about 3 km north, reachable on foot; shared taxi 250 KMF (≈ €0.51) per Wikivoyage [1], 300 KMF per Journeum (undated, city file); 10–15 minutes, evening taxis scarce [2].
- Watch: in May 2025 Itsandra was among villages around Moroni reporting nights or days without power (Mayotte Hebdo 2025-05-19, city file).
- Places:
  - Itsandra public beach (beach, next to Le Sim Sim restaurant) — [1], [8]
  - Golden Tulip Resort, formerly Hotel Itsandra (hotel, RN 1 Voidjou; private-beach entry €4) — [1], [8]
  - Le Coraya (restaurant, Bandamadji, Itsandra; mains €6–20) — [1]
  - Comores Plongée (dive operator, Route de la Corniche, Itsandra) — [8]
  - Dolphin trips from Itsandra (≈ 10,000 KMF ≈ €20) — [5]
  - Itsandra fort (ruins of a 17th-century sultan's fort) — [8]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q5 "Télétravail" only with backup power.

### Mvouni and the university side (south-east, towards Karthala)
- Feel: a locality south of Moroni in Bambao ya Djou [3], [4]; the Université des Comores' Nvuni site holds 4,000 students (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-09-15, city file). No source describes daily life here.
- Rent: no figure; "outside Moroni" 2–3-room home 80,000–200,000 KMF/month (≈ €163–407), shared charges 20,000–50,000 not always included (Africarrières 2026-02-25, city file).
- Getting around: shared taxis south leave from the Gobadjou and Karthala stops [1]; no fare to Mvouni found.
- Watch: nothing area-specific and dated found.
- Places:
  - Karthala International Hotel (hotel, Mvouni, next to the university; US$60) — [1]
  - Université des Comores, Nvuni site (university) — city file (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-09-15)
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (outskirts rents); Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (on the Karthala side); Q5 "Santé, enseignement" for university staff.

### Iconi (Ikoni), 3 km south
- Feel: an old town south of the capital with the Kapviridjeo palace, a Friday mosque, a bangwe (public meeting square) and a small volcano; Wikivoyage notes no restaurants [1]. Airbnb listings cluster here (city file).
- Rent: no figure; "outside Moroni" range as for Mvouni.
- Getting around: shared taxi 250 KMF (≈ €0.51) from Moroni [1]; about 15 minutes [2]; Gobadjou stop for the south [1].
- Watch: during the May 2026 fuel strike roads were barricaded in Iconi and Château and cars burned; 26 people were detained (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-05-15, city file); Iconi held out against the army for six days [13].
- Places:
  - Kapviridjeo palace ruins and Iconi rock viewpoint (heritage) — [1], [8]
  - Iconi Friday mosque and bangwe (mosque, public square) — [1]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Serré"; returnees with family on the south side.

### Peripheral quarters (Sahara, Mbweni, Madjadjou, Dashe, Porini and others)
- Feel: the city's densest areas, built up by rural migration; residents rely on cisterns, wells and public fountains and there is no sanitation network, per a 2021 contributor piece in Masiwa that cites older studies [7].
- Rent: no figure; Inseed's 2024 survey found real rent spending up 49.8% since 2020 and speaks of an accessibility crisis (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-08-21, city file).
- Getting around: shared taxi; no specific fare.
- Watch: water and power figures in [7] are old (2006, 2015) and citywide; no dated area-specific item.
- Places: none sourced beyond the Airbnb listings that name Sahara (city file). Left empty on purpose.
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" only for someone with family there; not recommended as a first base.

## Living: typical weeks

Prices below come only from the city file and [1], [2], [5]. Monthly costs are turned into a week by dividing by 4.33. Shared-taxi fares disagree: 200–300 KMF (Wikivoyage [1], Journeum in city file, both undated) against 500–1,500 KMF (Africarrières 2026-02-25); both bounds are shown.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Hankounou / Corniche — restaurants and the downtown coworking are close, and seafront areas are described as relatively secure. Internet is the risk: ≈ 9 Mbps 4G average, fixed lines rare (Jarnias, city file); a Maluzini coworking burned ≈ 100 L of fuel a week during 2023 cuts (Mayotte Hebdo 2023-08-18, city file).
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home on a 4G router; lunch at a small eatery inside Volo Volo market (shared taxi, a few minutes) [1].
  - Tue: day at Com'Work, downtown (10-desk open space; 2023 profile, prices not found) [14]; dinner at New Mumbai [1].
  - Wed: home; evening walk on the Corniche before dark [2].
  - Thu: Com'Work; grocery run at Mag (Asgaraly) [1].
  - Fri: morning work; Friday is a short day for offices and the museum (closes 11:00) [1]; evening at Tennis Club [1].
  - Sat: Volo Volo market in the morning [2]; night as below.
  - Sun: Itsandra beach (taxi 250–300 KMF, 10–15 min) [1], [2].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": VIP club, Corniche, 5,000 KMF entry with a drink [1]; or Le Rose Noir [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": L'Escale or Le Coraya in Itsandra (€6–20 mains) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; alcohol only from hotels or a few shops near Volo Volo (FCDO and Wikivoyage, city file).
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Itsandra (3 km) or Maloudja beach at Mitsamiouli, 45–60 min north, shared taxi €5–10 [2], [5].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Karthala one-day hike with guide ≈ €60, crater ≈ €100 [5]; foothills at Boboni with driver and guide €20–40 [2].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Moroni.
  - "Je reste en ville": medina, CNDRS museum, Corniche [1], [2].
- Rough weekly spend (KMF, from the files):
  - Rent: studio 75,000–150,000/month (Jarnias standard to Africarrières low end) → 17,321–34,642
  - Water + power 15,000–40,000/month → 3,464–9,238 (Africarrières)
  - Fixed internet 15,000–50,000/month → 3,464–11,547; mobile 5,000–15,000/month → 1,155–3,464 (Africarrières)
  - Groceries: 2 kg rice 3,000–5,000 + 2 kg fish 6,000–12,000 + 2 kg tomatoes/onions 2,000–5,000 + 12 eggs 3,000–4,500 = 14,000–26,500 (Africarrières)
  - 5 market lunches at under 1,000 = up to 5,000 [1]; 1 dinner out at €6–12 ≈ 2,950–5,900 [1]
  - 10 shared-taxi rides at 200–1,500 = 2,000–15,000
  - Club night 5,000 [1]
  - Total ≈ 49,000–116,000 KMF a week (≈ €100–236), coworking not included (price not found).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: a short let from €27/night in Hankounou or Hamramba (Airbnb, city file), or a central guesthouse: Pension Faïda next to the market, 12,500–17,500 KMF [8] (12,500–15,000 per [1]).
- Arrival: visa on arrival €30, 45 days (FCDO, dossier); airport to town by shared taxi at most 500 KMF, private taxi €8–15 [1], [2]; tourists have reportedly been asked €20 (Journeum, city file).
- Week:
  - Day 1: settle in the centre; cash from one of the four central ATMs, often reported broken, ≈ 10% fees [5]; bring euro notes.
  - Day 2: SIM from Comores Telecom (Huri) or Yas; mobile plan 5,000–15,000 KMF/month (Africarrières, city file). Mobile money exists as MVola (Yas), Huri Money and Holo Money (Osiris 2026-02-27, city file); Huri Money halved its staff in 2024 (city file). SIM price and ID requirement: not found.
  - Day 3: look at Hankounou and the Corniche; housing is found by word of mouth, employers or agents (Expat.com, city file).
  - Day 4: Hamramba and La Coulée; check generator and water tank (Jarnias, city file).
  - Day 5: Itsandra and the northern side.
  - Day 6: negotiate: landlords ask several months' deposit plus an advance; no figure given (Expat.com, city file). Agent fee: not found.
  - Day 7: bank account: not found as a procedure for non-residents; the economy runs on cash (Jarnias, city file).
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" VIP club [1]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Le Lagon Blue, halfway to Itsandra [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" guesthouse.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" Itsandra; "En montagne, en forêt" Karthala foothills; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic; "Je reste en ville" medina.
- Rough weekly spend: guesthouse 7 × 12,500 = 87,500 KMF (≈ €178) or Airbnb 7 × €27 = €189; visa €30; food at €8–12/day [2] = €56–84; airport taxi ≤ 500 KMF; 10 town taxis 2,000–15,000. Total ≈ €270–335 (guesthouse low end to Airbnb high end).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Volo Volo market early (Mon–Sat, sunrise to sunset) with street snacks such as mkatra foutra and sambusa [1], [2], [5]; walk (≈ 20 min) into the medina [2]; breakfast at Café de la Medina, ≈ 1,500 KMF [1].
- Midday: Old Friday Mosque from outside and the dhow quay [2]; National Museum / CNDRS (≈ €2–5; closed Friday afternoon and Sunday) [1], [2]; lunch at The New Select [1].
- Afternoon: shared taxi (250 KMF) north to Itsandra beach next to Le Sim Sim [1]; or the Golden Tulip private beach (€4) [1].
- Evening: Corniche at sunset [2], grilled fish at River Gauche [1] or dinner at Le Coraya [1]; taxi home before 20:00–21:00 [2].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Itsandra: dolphin trip (≈ 10,000 KMF) [5], fort ruins [8], beach.
- Day 3: north: Mitsamiouli, Maloudja beach, Trou du Prophète and Lac Salé; shared taxi €5–10 or a car split €15–20 each; 45–60 min [2], [5]. Stade international Saïd Mohamed Cheikh in Mitsamiouli hosts the national team [15].
- Day 4 (daily life): Volo Volo at opening, then ride a full shared taxi at morning rush from the Gare du Nord or Volo Volo stop [1]; afternoon in Iconi (bangwe, palace ruins) [1].
- Day 5: Karthala foothills at Boboni with driver and guide (€20–40) [2], or a one-day hike (≈ €60) [5]; go in the morning before clouds [2].
- Day 6: east coast: Chomoni beach via the Chalma stop [1], [5]; east-coast taxis may stop by 15:00 (Journeum, city file).
- Day 7: visit Hamramba, Hankounou and La Coulée as a would-be resident; Friday is the day to see the medina at prayer time, from outside and dressed modestly (FCDO, city file).
- Optional: Anjouan by plane from Hahaya or the SGTM ferry (see `mutsamudu.neighbourhoods.md`).
