# Mutsamudu, Comoros

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 491.97 KMF** (fixed euro peg, see `research/countries/comores.md`), rounded. Wikivoyage writes "FC" for the Comorian franc. Mutsamudu-specific price data is very thin online: most items below are national, or come from travel pages with undated prices. The shared web-search budget ran out after five searches; sources were reached by direct fetch and Mayotte Hebdo's site search.

## Items

- [cost.rent] No Mutsamudu rent figure was found; Expat.com only says the cost of living on Anjouan is slightly lower than on Grande Comore and that expat housing is scarcer (mostly individual houses or employer housing). — Expat.com "Living in Comoros", 2025-10-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] National household survey (Inseed, 2024): real rent spending rose 49.8% between 2020 and 2024 and rent is the largest household spending item; Inseed speaks of an accessibility crisis. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-08-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Budget hotels in Mutsamudu: air-conditioned rooms about 10,000 KMF/night (≈ €20); fan rooms 10,000, air-conditioned 12,500 (≈ €25) at another; a restaurant main course about 4,000 KMF (≈ €8.13). — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", undated (page edits to 2026-07), blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] National staple prices apply (rice 1 kg 1,500–2,500 KMF ≈ €3.05–5.08; fish 1 kg 3,000–6,000 ≈ €6.10–12.20) — Africarrières does not give island-specific figures. (national) — Africarrières "Coût de la vie, Comores", 2026-02-25, company, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Port dockers at Mutsamudu say they were verbally promised €100/month (≈ 49,200 KMF) and have not been paid since 2023; about fifty sent a formal notice in July 2026. — Mayotte Hebdo "À Anjouan, des dockers réclament des rémunérations impayées depuis 2023", 2026-08-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] At Hombo hospital, a proposed per-day guard contract paid 5,000 KMF/day (≈ €10), which staff said would halve monthly pay to roughly €60 instead of €120; some staff were owed up to eight months' salary. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2025-06-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Hitchhikers may be asked for a tip, so the Wikivoyage page advises stating intentions early; on cargo boats, ask to see the vessel before buying a ticket. — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Nationally, negotiating rents and informal services is common, and taxi fares are agreed before the ride (no meters). (national) — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The medina has narrow, sometimes covered lanes around seventeen mosques; government offices and the university campus are at Patsy; the town covers about 30 km². — Wikipédia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Mutsamudu has the country's only deep-water port (built 1982); three quarters of its cargo is trans-shipment to the other islands; main imports are rice, cement, sugar, flour and fuel, exports ylang-ylang, cloves and vanilla. — Wikipedia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Professional opportunities are more limited than in Moroni. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Anjouan's electricity access rate is 49% (Grande Comore 95%, Mohéli 87%); the government says Anjouan will get particular attention to reach universal access by 2030. — Union des Comores / World Bank "Pacte national sur l'énergie (Mission 300)", 2025-09, official, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Sonelec says Anjouan reached 18 h 19 min of supply per day in September 2026, after near-blackout conditions in 2024. — La Gazette des Comores, 2026-10-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In August 2024 a Mutsamudu meat seller reported power from 7:00 to 14:00, then waits of four to six hours; a TV repairer reported cuts roughly every thirty minutes. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2024-08-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] During Ramadan 2024, the Nyumakele area of Anjouan had whole days of repeated short cuts and the announced load-shedding schedule was not respected. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2024-04-09, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.water] Anjouan's forests were about 80% cleared between 1995 and 2014 and most rivers have dried up, worsening drought and landslides. — Wikipédia "Anjouan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Yas Comores planned to extend 5G to Mutsamudu after Moroni (announced May 2025; no launch date confirmed). — Mayotte Hebdo, 2025-05-20, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] In March 2026 a resident reported long fuel queues in Mutsamudu after rationing, with even retailers out of fuel. — Mayotte Hebdo "Les bidons interdits dans les stations-service", 2026-03-06, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Local bus Mutsamudu–Dindri: 500 KMF (≈ €1), about 30 minutes; buses usually stop around sunset. — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Boats: Maria Galanta (SGTM) to Moroni 18,000 KMF (≈ €37) about two hours; to Mayotte €54; cargo boats to Moroni take about 10 hours with no beds; a flight from Ouani to another island is about €50. — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A new AFD-funded ferry, the Massiwa (272 passengers), is to link Moroni and Mutsamudu three to six times a week from summer 2027; the operator Yameela already runs a Mutsamudu route. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-08-27, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In March 2026 Mutsamudu port refused ships from Mayotte for about a week over Mpox controls (21-day period, medical certificate); links resumed on 19 March. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-03-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Ouani airport, north of town, has flights to Moroni, Mohéli, Mayotte and Antananarivo. — Wikipédia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Expat.com describes healthcare on Anjouan as non-existent; serious cases need evacuation. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] At Hombo (Anjouan's reference hospital), staff in 2025 described radiology equipment broken since 2023, an incomplete pharmacy and sometimes one pair of gloves per day in the lab; contract staff went on indefinite strike. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2025-06-12, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] In May 2026, at least five people seriously injured near Mutsamudu, including a child, were evacuated to Mayotte. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-05-18, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] The university campus is at Patsy; the Alliance française (founded 1969) has a library, a 250-seat theatre and classrooms. — Wikipédia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] During the May 2026 fuel-price strike, riots broke out in the Mpagé area near Mutsamudu; the prosecutor reported one death, later accounts two deaths by gunfire; the price rise was then suspended. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-05-18, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The FCDO says protests can occur on Anjouan, as on Grande Comore and Mohéli, especially after elections; it gives no Anjouan-specific advice. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Opposition meetings were multiplying on Ndzuani (Anjouan) in early November 2026. — Al-Watwan homepage, 2026-11-02, local-media, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Anjouan declared unilateral independence in 1997; a military intervention in March 2008 ended Mohamed Bacar's rule. — Wikipédia "Anjouan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.women] Most women wear bright traditional dress; Wikivoyage advises asking before photographing them. — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Mutsamudu's medina was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage list in July 2026 together with Domoni and four Grande Comore medinas; its citadel (1782–1789) is reached by a 280-step stair. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-07-28, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.culture] The Ujumbé palace, former sultans' residence, was restored between 2016 and 2019. — Wikipédia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] An Expat.com forum reply (~2019) described daily life on Anjouan as very simple, with little to do beyond walks and chatting with friendly locals, and said few people speak French beyond older educated residents and students. — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Anjouan avec un bébé", ~2019, forum, confidence: low
- [community.diaspora] Anjouan is the main departure point for kwassa crossings to Mayotte; an estimated 12,000 people have died; Mayotte's immigrant population was about 45% of adults in 2018, mostly Anjouanais. — Wikipédia "Anjouan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] Mamoudzou (Mayotte) twinned with Mutsamudu in July 2022, which caused a controversy in Mayotte. — Mayotte Hebdo search results (titles dated 2022-07-29 and 2022-08-02), local-media, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] Arrivals on Anjouan from Mayotte must complete formalities at the local immigration office. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [admin.visa] A French consulate-general is in Mutsamudu (contact listed May 2020). — Wikivoyage "Anjouan", 2020-05, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] 27–32 °C all year, hottest December–April, humidity often above 90%; January is the wettest month. — Wikipédia / Wikipedia "Mutsamudu", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Mont Ntringui (1,595 m) park and Lac Dzialandzé: entry 2,000 KMF (≈ €4) per person; the climb takes about 3 hours; Moya beach and the Tratringa waterfall are other sites. — Wikivoyage "Anjouan" (undated) / Wikipédia "Anjouan", blog/academic, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Beaches are mostly black sand; much household and industrial waste ends up on beaches. — Wikipédia "Anjouan", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex sexual activity is illegal nationwide; the FCDO cites up to 5 years' prison. (national) — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
