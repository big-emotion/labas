# Moroni, Comoros

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 491.97 KMF** (fixed euro peg, see `research/countries/comores.md`), rounded. Where a source gives only USD, the USD figure is kept and not converted. The session's shared web-search budget ran out after five searches, so most sources were reached by direct fetch from the dossier's list and from Mayotte Hebdo's site search. Listing prices are asking prices; no Comorian property portal with dated listings was found.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Furnished 45 m² studio: 75,000 KMF/month (≈ €152) in a standard area, 112,450 KMF (≈ €229) in an expensive area. — Cyril Jarnias "S'installer aux Comores en tant qu'expatrié", 2026-01-25, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Furnished 85 m² flat: 158,325 KMF/month (≈ €322) standard area, 227,554 KMF (≈ €463) expensive area. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-01-25, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The same blog also gives far higher, crowd-sourced-looking figures: 1-bed 40 m² city centre ≈ USD 675, cheaper area ≈ USD 566; 3-bed 80 m² centre ≈ USD 1,440 — contradicting its own KMF figures above. — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-01-25, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Studio or 1-bedroom in Moroni: 150,000–300,000 KMF/month (≈ €305–610); 2–3-bedroom family flat 300,000–600,000 KMF (≈ €610–1,220). — Africarrières "Coût de la vie, Comores", 2026-02-25, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Outside Moroni, a 2–3-room home costs 80,000–200,000 KMF/month (≈ €163–407); shared charges (power, water) of 20,000–50,000 KMF (≈ €41–102) are not always included. — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Expat-type housing: flat €300–600/month, residential villa with garden, generator and air conditioning €800–1,500; utilities usually excluded. — Expat.com "Living in Comoros", 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Asking purchase price in Moroni: 80–200 million KMF (≈ €163,000–407,000), property type not specified. — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Purchase per m²: city-centre flat ≈ USD 1,626/m², peripheral house ≈ USD 1,385/m². — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-01-25, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A forum reply (about 4 years old, ~2022) put building land at €25–60/m² depending on location, rising closer to the capital; another user said they sold land near the airport at €10/m². — Expat.com forum "Pris du m2 au comores", ~2022, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] National household survey (Inseed, May–Nov 2024, 5,487 households): real rent spending rose 49.8% between 2020 and 2024 and house rent is the largest spending item; Inseed speaks of an accessibility crisis and calls for rental-market regulation. — Mayotte Hebdo "Comores : les dépenses du loyer en hausse de 49.8%", 2026-08-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Food prices: rice 1 kg 1,500–2,500 KMF (≈ €3.05–5.08); baguette 600–1,000 (≈ €1.22–2.03); fresh fish 1 kg 3,000–6,000 (≈ €6.10–12.20); chicken 1 kg 4,000–7,000 (≈ €8.13–14.23); dozen eggs 3,000–4,500 (≈ €6.10–9.15). — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] More food: tomatoes/onions 1 kg 1,000–2,500 KMF (≈ €2–5); coconut 500–1,000 (≈ €1–2); imported UHT milk 1 L 2,500–3,500 (≈ €5.08–7.11). — Africarrières, 2026-02-25, company, confidence: medium

- [work.remote] A coworking space in Maluzini (Comores Lab) closed early during the 2023 power cuts and burned about 100 litres of generator fuel a week. — Mayotte Hebdo "Le retour des délestages…", 2023-08-18, local-media, confidence: medium
