# Fomboni, Comoros

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city: suggested by the Comoros dossier (capital of Mohéli, third town of the country, 21,987 inhabitants in 2019 per Wikipédia). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 491.97 KMF** (fixed euro peg), rounded. Fomboni-specific prices online are almost all from travel pages with old or undated figures; each is flagged. The shared web-search budget ran out after five searches; sources were reached by direct fetch and Mayotte Hebdo's site search.

## Items

- [cost.rent] No Fomboni rent figure was found; Expat.com says expat housing options there are even fewer than in Moroni, mostly individual houses or employer housing. — Expat.com "Living in Comoros", 2025-10-14, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Guesthouse rates in Fomboni listed in Feb 2018: Les Abouts 12,500–15,000 KMF/night (≈ €25–30), La Grande Plaine 15,000–17,000 (≈ €30–35), Relais de Singani 13,500–16,500 (≈ €27–34). — Wikivoyage (fr) "Mohéli", 2018-02, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A brochette meal can cost as little as 250 KMF (≈ €0.51) on Mohéli, against up to 1,500 KMF on Grande Comore. — Wikivoyage "Comoros", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Mohéli's poverty rate rose from 41.8% to 57% between 2020 and 2024, making it the poorest island (Inseed household survey). — Mayotte Hebdo "Les dépenses du loyer en hausse de 49.8%", 2026-08-21, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Residents rely mainly on subsistence farming and fishing; more than half live below the poverty line. — Wikipédia "Fomboni", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] A taxi from the boat landing to Nioumachoua costs about 5,000 KMF (≈ €10); Wikivoyage advises bargaining. — Wikivoyage "Moheli", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Expat.com sees Fomboni as suited to marine-conservation, ecotourism or rural-development work, and warns that isolation can be hard. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Sonelec says Mohéli has power 24 hours a day in 2026; Mohéli's electricity access rate is 87%. — La Gazette des Comores 2026-10-21 / Mission 300 energy compact 2025-09, local-media/official, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Mohéli was reported spared by the power cuts that hit Grande Comore and Anjouan in August 2023 and Ramadan 2024. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2023-08-18 and 2024-04-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Mohéli has no fuel depot; in March and April 2026 fuel was rationed at Fomboni stations (motorbikes 4 litres, cars 10–20 litres) and lamp oil ran short. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-03-06 and 2026-04-16, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Petrol up to 1,000 KMF/L (≈ €2.03) on Mohéli versus about 600 KMF on Grande Comore; Mohéli has no car-rental agencies. — Wikivoyage "Comoros", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Island buses (called "taxis") leave from Fomboni market and stop running before midday; shared taxis run afterwards; Monday mornings are crowded with school commuters. — Wikivoyage "Moheli", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The Itsamia–Nioumachoua road was destroyed by the 2019 cyclone, turning a 15-minute trip into about 3 hours. — Wikivoyage "Moheli", 2019, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Boats: speed boat or cargo boat to Grande Comore or Anjouan 10,500 KMF (≈ €21); cargo boat Moroni–Fomboni 10,000 KMF, up to 12 hours; a flight to another island is about €50. — Wikivoyage "Moheli" / "Comoros", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The AFD-funded ferry Massiwa will serve Fomboni once Boïngoma port works are done (service planned from summer 2027); the company Yameela recently launched a Moroni–Fomboni link. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-08-27, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Bandar Es Salam airport is 2 km from town; part of its terminal burned on 21 August 2026 and seven people were charged with arson. — Wikipédia "Fomboni" / Mayotte Hebdo 2026-09-09, academic/local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Yas Comores planned to extend 5G to Fomboni after Moroni (announced May 2025; no date confirmed). — Mayotte Hebdo, 2025-05-20, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.money] Several bank branches, including a central-bank branch, a post office and three petrol stations. — Wikipédia "Fomboni", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [health.care] A hospital centre and about ten pharmacies. — Wikipédia "Fomboni", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [health.care] Expat.com calls healthcare on Mohéli non-existent and says Fomboni has no international medical services. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] No international schools in Fomboni. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] In December 2025 young people blocked Fomboni's main roads and burned tyres for two days after a detainee's death; schools, offices and shops closed and taxis stopped (Salamani district). — Mayotte Hebdo "Journée tendue et arrestations à Fomboni", 2025-12-18, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] From 9 January 2026 the prefect banned private political meetings in homes within Fomboni, citing public-order risk; opponents saw it as a restriction of freedoms. — Mayotte Hebdo, 2026-01-12, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The FCDO says protests and unrest can happen on Mohéli as on the other islands, especially after elections. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] About twenty mosques, a covered market (opened 2015), an Alliance française, a football stadium and club; restaurants include one on Place de l'Indépendance. — Wikipédia "Fomboni", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [social.food] Supermarkets have a limited choice, market produce is seasonal and local, and restaurants are very few. — Wikivoyage "Moheli", undated, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] About 2,000 mm of rain a year, average 25.2 °C (19–32 °C); hot humid season November–April; cyclones roughly once or twice a decade. — Wikipédia "Fomboni", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Mohéli marine park (created 1999 with village associations): green turtles nest on white-sand beaches; Lac Dziani Boundouni is a Ramsar site; the island has been a UNESCO biosphere reserve since 2020. — Wikipédia "Mohéli", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Sea turtles, dolphins and humpback whales in season; the Nioumachoua islets are reachable by pirogue. — Expat.com, 2025-10-14, company, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex sexual activity is illegal nationwide; the FCDO cites up to 5 years' prison. (national) — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
