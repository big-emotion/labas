## Neighbourhoods

### Médina (old town)
- Feel: a quarter of narrow, sometimes covered alleys around seventeen mosques [2]; one source places it between the town's two parallel main streets (search snippet, not opened, so not relied on). Inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage list in July 2026 with Domoni and four Grande Comore medinas (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-07-28, city file; [9]). An Expat.com forum reply (~2019) found daily life on Anjouan very simple, with walks and chatting (city file, low confidence).
- Rent: no figure. Expat.com says the cost of living on Anjouan is slightly lower than on Grande Comore but expat housing is scarcer, mostly individual houses or employer housing (2025-10-14, city file).
- Getting around: on foot; no taxi fare within town was found (city file gap).
- Watch: Wikivoyage mentions unrest in the medina in October 2018, but the dated news report could not be opened, so no item is written (city file gap).
- Places:
  - Medina of Mutsamudu (UNESCO heritage quarter) — [1], [2]
  - Citadel (fort, 1782–1789, ruins; reached by an old stone stair) — [2]; Mayotte Hebdo 2026-07-28 (city file)
  - Palais d'Ujumbé (sultans' residence until 1909, restored 2016–2019) — [2]
  - Alliance française (cultural centre, 1969; library, 250-seat theatre, classrooms) — [2]
  - Mutsamudu town market (market) — [3]
  - Pangahari and Escalier de Bandrakowa (listed tourist sites) — [2]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "Je reste en ville"; Q1 "D'abord des vacances" for a heritage stay; Q6 "Français" helps only partly: the forum reply says French is spoken mostly by older educated residents and students (city file, low).

### Port and waterfront
- Feel: the country's only deep-water port (1982); three quarters of cargo is trans-shipped to the other islands; imports rice, cement, sugar, flour, fuel; exports ylang-ylang, cloves, vanilla (Wikipedia, city file). Home port of the inter-island ferry Acadie [2].
- Rent: no figure. Hotels "behind the port" charge ≈ 10,000 KMF (≈ €20) a night for an air-conditioned room [1].
- Getting around: walkable to the medina; Ouani airport is about 5 km north-east [5], fare not found.
- Watch: about fifty dockers sent a formal notice in July 2026 over pay unpaid since 2023 (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-08-17, city file); in March 2026 the port refused ships from Mayotte for about a week over Mpox controls (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-03-19, city file); March 2026 fuel queues and rationing in Mutsamudu (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-03-06, city file).
- Places:
  - Port of Mutsamudu (deep-water port, ferries to Moroni and Mayotte) — [2]; Mayotte Hebdo (city file)
  - One EGG Hotel (hotel, behind the port; air-conditioned rooms 10,000 KMF) — [1]
  - Le Papillote (hotel with a lively restaurant; fan rooms 10,000, air-con 12,500, mains 4,000 KMF) — [1]
  - Consulate-General of France (consulate; contact listed May 2020) — [1]
  - SGTM / Maria Galanta and Yameela (ferry operators: Moroni 18,000 KMF ≈ €37, about 2 h, Saturdays; Mayotte €54, Mondays; undated) — [1]; Mayotte Hebdo 2026-08-27 (city file)
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" for import-export (vanilla, ylang-ylang, cloves); Q10 "Voyager facilement" relative to the rest of Anjouan; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" for the first nights.

### Patsy (inland, university and government side)
- Feel: hosts the university campus and administrative buildings, including the governorate [2]. The French Wikipedia "Anjouan" page lists Patsy as a village in Bazimini commune [3], so it is outside the town proper. No source describes daily life there.
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: no distance or fare found; the Mro wa Mouji river rises at Patsy and runs down to Ouani [5].
- Watch: nothing dated and area-specific found.
- Places:
  - Université des Comores campus at Patsy (university) — [2]
  - Anjouan governorate (administration) — [2]
  - Only two places could be sourced; no school, market or clinic was found.
- Fits: Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (university staff); Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Pagé (Mpagé)
- Feel: a locality within Mutsamudu commune [2], [3]. No description of who lives there was found.
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: not found.
- Watch: the May 2026 fuel-price riots started in Mpagé; the prosecutor reported one death and five injured, and later accounts two deaths by gunfire, including the son of Pagé's village chief; the price rise was suspended afterwards (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-05-18 [6]; city file).
- Places: none sourced. Left empty on purpose.
- Fits: no profile can be recommended on current evidence; listed so a reader with family there knows the May 2026 context.

### Mirontsy (Mirontsi), across the bay
- Feel: a town of about 9,000 (2003) in Mutsamudu bay, historically bounded by a river; farming, fishing, crafts and trade; two Friday mosques, a large madrasa and the reef ruins of the Mkiri wa Poini mosque [4].
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: next to Mutsamudu in the same bay [4]; no fare or distance found.
- Watch: in May 2026, a meeting between the town hall and striking fishermen failed, roads were blocked and tear gas was used [6].
- Places:
  - Mkiri wa Poini mosque ruins (heritage, on the reef) — [4]
  - Madzi ya lozi / Mkiri wa pwani (seaside quarter) — [4]
  - Only these could be sourced; the Friday mosques and madrasa are unnamed in [4].
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Serré" for a returnee with family there.

### Ouani (airport town) and Hombo (hospital)
- Feel: Ouani is a town of about 10,000 (undated figure) in a river valley 5 km north-east of Mutsamudu, with Anjouan's airport just to its north [5]. Hombo hosts the regional hospital and the official residence of the island's head [3].
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: flights from Ouani to Moroni, Mohéli, Mayotte and Antananarivo [2]; one-way inter-island flight about €50 [1]. Road fare to Mutsamudu not found.
- Watch: the May 2026 strike movement spread to Ouani before the road was cleared [6]. At Hombo, staff in 2025 described radiology broken since 2023, an incomplete pharmacy and an indefinite strike by contract staff (Mayotte Hebdo 2025-06-12, city file); in May 2026 seriously injured people near Mutsamudu were evacuated to Mayotte (Mayotte Hebdo 2026-05-18, city file).
- Places:
  - Anjouan-Ouani airport (AJN) — [5], [2]
  - Centre hospitalier régional d'Anjouan, Hombo (regional reference hospital) — [3]; Mayotte Hebdo 2025-06-12 (city file)
  - Only these two places could be sourced.
- Fits: Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport); not Q10 "La santé et l'école": Expat.com calls healthcare on Anjouan non-existent (2025-10-14, city file).

## Living: typical weeks

No rent figure exists for Mutsamudu, so each budget below **excludes housing**. Prices come from the city file (national Africarrières figures, 2026-02-25, apply to Anjouan only by default) and Wikivoyage Anjouan [1] (undated). Monthly costs are divided by 4.33. Power: Anjouan's access rate is 49% (Mission 300, 2025-09) and supply reached 18 h 19 min a day in September 2026 (La Gazette 2026-10-21), both via the city file.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Médina or port side, the only walkable areas with sourced cafés and hotels. Honest caveat: 5G for Mutsamudu was only announced (Mayotte Hebdo 2025-05-20, city file); 2024 reports describe cuts every thirty minutes for a repairer in town (Mayotte Hebdo 2024-08-23, city file). Remote work is not documented on Anjouan (dossier).
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home on 4G; evening walk through the medina [2].
  - Tue: work; lunch main at Le Papillote, 4,000 KMF [1].
  - Wed: Alliance française library as a quiet place to read (no coworking found) [2].
  - Thu: work; market shopping at the town market [3].
  - Fri: short day; Friday prayer is the week's big moment in a 17-mosque medina [2] (dress code per FCDO, city file).
  - Sat: climb to the citadel [2]; night as below.
  - Sun: bus to Dindri (500 KMF, 30 min) for Mont Ntringui [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no club or bar was found in Mutsamudu; not realistic on current evidence.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Papillote's restaurant [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; drinking in public is illegal and alcohol is sold in hotels (FCDO, dossier).
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Moya beach, bungalows and restaurant [1], [3]; beaches are mostly black sand and some carry washed-up waste [3].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Mont Ntringui (1,595 m) and Lake Dzialandzé, park entry 2,000 KMF (≈ €4), about 3 h climb from Dindri [1], [3].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Mutsamudu.
  - "Je reste en ville": medina, citadel, Ujumbé palace [2].
- Rough weekly spend excluding rent (KMF):
  - Water + power 15,000–40,000/month → 3,464–9,238; fixed internet 15,000–50,000 → 3,464–11,547; mobile 5,000–15,000 → 1,155–3,464 (national, Africarrières)
  - Groceries: 2 kg rice 3,000–5,000 + 2 kg fish 6,000–12,000 + 2 kg tomatoes/onions 2,000–5,000 + 12 eggs 3,000–4,500 = 14,000–26,500 (national, Africarrières)
  - 2 mains at Le Papillote: 8,000 [1]; 2 bus rides to Dindri and back: 1,000 [1]; park entry 2,000 [1]
  - Total ≈ 33,000–62,000 KMF (≈ €67–126) plus rent (not found).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: One EGG Hotel (10,000 KMF, behind the port) or Le Papillote (10,000–12,500) [1].
- Arrival: fly into Ouani (≈ €50 from another island) [1] or the ferry from Moroni (18,000 KMF) [1]. Arrivals from Mayotte must complete formalities at the local immigration office (Expat.com, city file). Visa on arrival €30 at Hahaya "or other entry points" (FCDO, dossier); whether Ouani issues it was not confirmed.
- Week:
  - Day 1: settle by the port; bring euro cash: ATMs are rare outside Moroni (Jarnias, city file).
  - Day 2: SIM from Comores Telecom (Huri) or Yas; mobile plan 5,000–15,000 KMF/month (national). Mobile money: MVola, Huri Money, Holo Money nationally (Osiris 2026-02-27, city file); Anjouan coverage not found.
  - Day 3: walk the medina and ask around: housing is found by word of mouth or employers (Expat.com, city file).
  - Day 4: Mirontsy and Ouani side [4], [5].
  - Day 5: check power and water at any flat: Anjouan rivers are drying up (city file) and supply averages ≈ 18 h a day.
  - Day 6: deposit and advance customs: several months' deposit is the national norm (Expat.com, city file); Anjouan-specific terms not found.
  - Day 7: bank account: not found for Anjouan.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not realistic; "Un bon dîner entre amis" Le Papillote [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" hotel.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage" Moya; "En montagne, en forêt" Ntringui; "Dans la savane ou le désert" not realistic; "Je reste en ville" medina.
- Rough weekly spend: hotel 7 × 10,000–12,500 = 70,000–87,500 KMF; 7 dinners at 4,000 = 28,000 [1]; 6 bus rides 3,000 [1]. Total ≈ 101,000–118,500 KMF (≈ €205–241), plus visa €30 and arrival ticket (≈ €50 flight or 18,000 KMF ferry). Lunch prices not found.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: town market [3], then the medina's covered alleys and mosques (outside only, dressed modestly per FCDO) [2].
- Midday: lunch at Le Papillote, mains 4,000 KMF [1].
- Afternoon: the stone stair up to the citadel for the bay view [2]; Ujumbé palace (restored 2016–2019) [2]; Alliance française [2]. Entry fees: not found.
- Evening: back down to the port waterfront [2]; dinner at the hotel restaurant [1]. All on foot.

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: bus to Dindri (500 KMF, 30 min), climb Mont Ntringui (≈ 3 h) and see Lake Dzialandzé; park 2,000 KMF; buses stop around sunset; an eatery owner in Dindri arranges homestays [1].
- Day 3: Moya beach; bungalows 7,500–10,000 KMF, meals 4,000–8,000 [1]; transport not sourced.
- Day 4: Domoni medina (UNESCO 2026) and the mausoleum of Ahmed Abdallah Abderamane [3]; city file.
- Day 5 (daily life): town market at opening [3], then the port as dockers and ferries work [2]; afternoon in Mirontsy, a fishing town across the bay [4].
- Day 6: Tratringa waterfalls at Bambao Mtrouni [3]; transport not sourced.
- Day 7: Ouani and departure by air (≈ €50 inter-island) [1], [5].
- Note for the deciding visitor: Hombo's state (city file) and the May 2026 Mpagé riots [6] are the two facts worth weighing during the stay.
