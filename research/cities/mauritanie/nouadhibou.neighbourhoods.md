# Nouadhibou: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 44.78 MRU** (the rate the city file uses; donneesmondiales.com, 2026-11-13; the dossier itself states no rate). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/mauritanie/nouadhibou.md`; `[NKC C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/mauritanie/nouakchott.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/mauritanie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

**Read first.** Nouadhibou is very thinly documented online. There is **no real rent figure** (only an AI-generated estimate the city file flags as a placeholder), no utility or school price, and almost every place below comes from one community guide (Wikivoyage EN, mostly undated, some entries around 2023). Wikivoyage writes prices in "ouguiyas" without saying old or new (1 MRU = 10 old ouguiyas); the city file converts them "if MRU", and this sheet does the same. WebSearch was unavailable for the whole session (shared budget used up), so new sources were opened by known URL only.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- No dated official or press source on crime inside the city was found [C Gaps]. The UK advises against all but essential travel within 25 km of the Western Sahara border but explicitly excludes the city and the Nouakchott road (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [C safety.general]. France rates the border strip from Nouadhibou to Zouérate orange, except the PK55 crossing, whose track edges are mined (2026-11-09, zones 2026-03-11) [C safety.general][10].
- Black sub-Saharan Africans: HRW (2025-08-27) documents a police-run immigration detention centre at the regional police directorate and abuses against migrants concentrated in Nouakchott, Nouadhibou and the desert near the border [C rights.racism]. The city has been a departure point for boats to the Canary Islands since 2006 [C community.diaspora]. Carry papers at all times.
- Alcohol is a narcotic in law; LGBT acts are criminal [C social.nightlife][C rights.lgbt].
- Climate is the city's strong point: tempered by the Canary Current, mean 21.3 °C, daily highs around 27 °C [C climate.daily], against 40 °C+ months in Nouakchott [D].
- Health: the Regional Hospital (opened 2017) is the largest in the region; France gives no Nouadhibou-specific health information and advises evacuation abroad for serious cases [C health.care].
- Money: some bank ATMs take foreign cards (Société Générale: Visa and Mastercard) [C daily.money]; ATMs are fewer than in Nouakchott [C daily.money].
- Power, water and internet reliability: no Nouadhibou-specific source [C Gaps]. The city's fish trade depends on Nouakchott's cold chain, which the 2026 cuts disrupted [C work.jobs].

## Neighbourhoods

Only three districts are described in any source: the centre (Keran), Cansado and Numerowatt [C housing.areas]. Wikipedia names a fourth area, a commuter settlement for harbour workers near the ore port, with nothing else sourced about it, so it gets no block.

### Keran (city centre)
- Feel: the centre, built in 1958, with the central police, customs, banks and the central market (Wikivoyage) [C housing.areas][1]. Boulevard Median is the main shopping street [1]. Wikipedia places the airport in the centre [C housing.areas].
- Rent: no district figure. The only city figure is an AI-generated estimate of about 15,000 MRU (≈ €335) for a 1-bed, which the city file treats as a placeholder (nomad.watch, 2026-06-08) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: unmarked shared taxis, 20 ouguiyas one way on the main roads; a car that honks as it approaches is usually a taxi (Wikivoyage) [C daily.transport]. CarApp and Yango are said to operate; Yango's own site does not list Mauritania [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing dated and area-specific found.
- Places:
  - Central market with the "chep-chep" second-hand market, behind the main middle road — Wikivoyage [1][C social.food]
  - Carrefour (first intersection of the centre), Bon Marché and 28 Novembre (supermarkets on the main street, within 200 m; prices sometimes high) — Wikivoyage [1][C cost.living]
  - Pleine de Lune (snacks, cakes, mini pizzas), across from the Commissariat Central — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Alliance Franco-Mauritanienne (garden and reading space), behind the Central Bank — Wikivoyage [1]; Alliance Française de Nouadhibou since 2015-01-29 — Ambassade de France [6]
  - Port de pêche artisanal (Senegalese boats; fishermen return and sell fish), city centre — Wikivoyage [1]; Routard [4]
  - La Paillotte, Boulevard Maritime, inside Hotel Mauritalia (Italian) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - French consular agency, boulevard Maritime (by appointment) — Ambassade de France [6]
  - Bureau de change at the Carrefour crossroads, Keran entrance — Wikivoyage [1][C daily.money]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e" and Q5 "Télétravail" (shops, banks and cafés on foot); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q9 "Doux".

### Cansado
- Feel: about 10 km south of Keran, built by the French in 1960 for SNIM mine workers, with a hotel, tennis courts and petanque grounds (Wikivoyage) [1]; Wikipedia calls it the main residential area [C housing.areas]. fr.wikipedia describes it as a mining town near the Point Central ore port (edited 2026-08-09) [3].
- Rent: no figure (see Keran).
- Getting around: shared taxi 20 ouguiyas plus 20 extra to reach Cansado (Wikivoyage) [C daily.transport], so about 40 to the centre. No time sourced.
- Watch: nothing dated and area-specific found.
- Places:
  - Cansado grocery store — Wikivoyage [1][C cost.living]
  - Tennis courts and petanque grounds — Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Oasean Hotel (restaurant, tennis courts, ocean view) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Ship graveyard, Baie de Cansado (dozens of rusting boats in shallow water) — Routard [4]; over 300 scuttled ships in the bay per Wikipedia [2][C nature.access]
  - French coastal gun emplacement ruins with tunnels, between Port Autonome and Cansado (free) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" for SNIM, port and ore jobs [C work.jobs]; Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants" for space and calm (but see the schools gap); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Numerowatt
- Feel: the most populous and fastest-growing district, north of the centre, built in 1982–83 and organised by six water taps ("robinets"); it starts with the Socogim area (Wikivoyage) [C housing.areas][1].
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: shared taxis on the main roads, 20 ouguiyas [C daily.transport]; the gare routière for Nouakchott is at Robinet 6 [1].
- Watch: nothing dated and area-specific found.
- Places:
  - FC Nouadhibou restaurant (burgers, fries, TV screens), between the 2nd and 3rd robinets on the middle road — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Gare routière, Robinet 6 (shared Mercedes taxis to Nouakchott at first light) — Wikivoyage [1][C daily.transport]
  - Somatir (air-conditioned vans to Nouakchott), Robinet 3; As-Salaam bus, 5th Robinet — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Fresh camel milk sold by herders at the town entrance, usually in the evening — Wikivoyage [1][C social.food]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (inference from its being the mass-housing district; no rent source); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" relative to the rest of the city. Thin sourcing: visit before choosing.

## Living: typical weeks

Weekly spend in Nouadhibou cannot be built properly: the files hold no real rent, utility, school or restaurant-range price for the city. Each block shows what can be added up and names what is missing.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Keran**, to walk to the supermarkets, banks and market [1]. The mild climate (highs around 27 °C) [C climate.daily] makes working without constant air-con more realistic than in Nouakchott (inference).
- Connectivity: no Nouadhibou-specific internet source [C Gaps]. Mattel publishes national prices for its Wifina 4G box (300 GB for 1,500 MRU / 30 days) but no coverage map was read [NKC C daily.internet]; check coverage on site.
- Week:
  - Mon: work; evening shopping at Bon Marché or 28 Novembre [1].
  - Tue: work; late afternoon at the Port de pêche artisanal when the fishermen come in [1][4].
  - Wed: work; reading space at the Alliance Franco-Mauritanienne [1].
  - Thu: work; dinner at La Paillotte, Boulevard Maritime [1].
  - Fri: prayer day; quiet work; camel milk at the town entrance in the evening [1].
  - Sat: central market and chep-chep in the morning [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Centre de Pêche (pier, restaurant, boat and fishing-gear hire, ferries to a picnic island) [1], or Cap Blanc (see outings).
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic (national alcohol ban; no venue sourced) [C social.nightlife].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Paillotte (Italian), Nomada (European cuisine), or the Chinese restaurants on the lower road [1]; a Senegalese fish-and-rice plate at about 70 ouguiyas [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home and tea; the Mattel night data window if covered [NKC C daily.internet].
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": Baie de l'Étoile for kitesurfing (Routard) [4]; bay winds steady nearly all year (Wikivoyage) [1]. No swimming beach with safety information was sourced.
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Nouadhibou.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": the Cap Blanc peninsula (cliffs, beaches, about 60 km long, shared with Western Sahara) [4]; Banc d'Arguin, entrance near the half-way stop at the 235 km mark on the Nouakchott highway [1], 200 MRU per person per night, guide essential, 4x4 and GPS recommended [7]. The SNIM ore train toward Choum and the Adrar is **not** proposed: France rates the Nouadhibou–Zouérate border strip orange [10], riding the ore wagons is forbidden, and the UK added new train-risk information in October 2026 [C daily.transport].
  - "Je reste en ville": FC Nouadhibou match at the Stade Municipal (10,300 seats; league champions 2023–24 to 2025–26) [5]; Alliance Française; ship graveyard [4].
- What can be added up (one person):
  - Rent placeholder 15,000 MRU / 4.33 = 3,464 MRU (≈ €77.4), AI-generated, not a market figure [C cost.rent]
  - 14 Senegalese fish-and-rice plates × 70 = 980 MRU (≈ €21.9 if MRU) [C cost.living]
  - 10 shared-taxi rides × 20 = 200 MRU (≈ €4.5 if MRU) [C daily.transport]
  - Wifina 1,500 / 4.33 = 346 MRU (≈ €7.7), coverage unconfirmed [NKC C daily.internet]
  - **≈ €111 a week** (77.4 + 21.9 + 4.5 + 7.7 = 111.5), **without** utilities, groceries, backup power or any real rent: not a budget, an illustration of what is missing.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Cansado** when one partner works for SNIM, the ore port or the fishing industry: it was built for mine workers and Wikipedia calls it the main residential area [1][C housing.areas]. Fishing and iron-ore processing are the city's two employers [C work.jobs]; free-zone incentives include a capped income tax for expatriate employees [C business.invest].
- Week:
  - Mon–Thu: commute by shared taxi (20 + 20 ouguiyas to the centre) [C daily.transport]; evenings at the Cansado tennis courts or petanque grounds [1].
  - Wed: dinner at The Oasean Hotel restaurant, ocean view [1].
  - Fri: prayer day; supermarket run in Keran [1].
  - Sat: ship graveyard and the gun-emplacement ruins on foot from Cansado [4][1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Centre de Pêche boat to the picnic island [1] or a Banc d'Arguin night [7].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic [C social.nightlife]. "Un bon dîner entre amis": The Oasean Hotel or La Paillotte [1]. "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outings: as profile 1.
- What can be added up (two people): 2 × 14 plates × 70 = 1,960 MRU (≈ €43.8 if MRU) [C cost.living]; 10 commutes × 40 = 400 MRU (≈ €8.9 if MRU) [C daily.transport]; rent placeholder 3,464 MRU (≈ €77.4) [C cost.rent] → **≈ €130 a week** (43.8 + 8.9 + 77.4 = 130.1), missing utilities, groceries and real rent.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Keran**, near the banks, customs and the market [1].
- Why Nouadhibou for this profile: it is a free zone since early 2013 (over 1,000 km², urban area included), with exemption from import duties and taxes, tax exemptions and an administration fee of 2% of turnover or none; its one-stop shop is free and promises processing within 5 days [C business.invest][C business.admin]. The authority is planning a new Central Business District (call of 2025-06-26) [C business.invest]. These are the authority's own promises, not measured delays.
- Week:
  - Mon: free-zone one-stop shop (location not sourced) [C business.admin]; bank account (requirements not found).
  - Tue: Port de pêche artisanal and the fish and vegetable market near the port to meet the trade [1][4][C social.food].
  - Wed: supplier visits along Boulevard Median [1].
  - Thu: Point Central and the ore port area to understand SNIM logistics (view only; no photographs of ports) [3][D].
  - Fri: prayer day; admin.
  - Sat: market morning [1]; Saturday night as profile 1.
  - Sun: rest or Cap Blanc [4].
- Weekend outings: as profile 1.
- Spend: as profile 1; company set-up costs other than the free one-stop shop and the 2% fee: not found.

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Cansado** (main residential area, tennis, calm) [C housing.areas][1].
- **Blocking gap:** no school of any kind (French, international or named public school) could be sourced in Nouadhibou [C Gaps]. A family with school-age children cannot plan schooling from these files; on current evidence the realistic option is Nouakchott (Lycée Théodore Monod, AISN, TLC) [NKC C family.schools].
- Health: Regional Hospital (2017), largest in the region [C health.care]; evacuation abroad advised for serious cases [C health.care].
- Week (outside school hours): Wed afternoon at the Alliance Franco-Mauritanienne garden [1]; Sat Centre de Pêche (restaurant with a playground, boat to the picnic island) [1]; Sun Cap Blanc monk-seal reserve with a 4x4 and a guide [4][1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic. "Un bon dîner entre amis": FC Nouadhibou restaurant (TV screens, burgers) or the Centre de Pêche restaurant [1]. "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outings: "À la plage": Centre de Pêche and Baie de l'Étoile [1][4]; "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic; "Dans la savane ou le désert": Cap Blanc, Banc d'Arguin [4][7]; "Je reste en ville": FC Nouadhibou match [5].
- Spend: not computable (no rent, school or utility figure).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Getting there: Mauritania Airlines flies several times a week from Nouakchott and Gran Canaria [1]; or a bus from Nouakchott, about 8 hours, 600–700 ouguiyas (≈ €13–16 if MRU), several companies daily [C daily.transport]; or the minibus from Carrefour Sabah in Nouakchott, 700 UM (January 2026) [NKC C daily.transport]. Entry rules are national: e-visa authorisation, then €55 for 30 days at the border [C admin.visa].
- Where to stay first: Hotel Sahel, Boulevard Maritime (from €50) [1]; Camping Baie du Lévrier, about 200 m from the central police station (500 new ouguiyas for a double, ≈ €11) [1]; Auberge Sahara, across from BMCI near the fishing market (from 300 ouguiyas in 2023) [1].
- Week:
  - Day 1: arrival; cash from a Société Générale ATM or the bureau de change at the Keran entrance (passport photocopied) [C daily.money].
  - Day 2: SIM card (operators are national: Mauritel, Mattel, Chinguitel) [D]; Bankily wallet [D]; walk Keran: market, Boulevard Median [1].
  - Day 3: Alliance Française and the French consular agency (by appointment) to ask about housing contacts and registration [6].
  - Day 4–5: housing search in Keran and Cansado. No agency, listing site, deposit, visit-fee or advance-rent source exists for the city [C Gaps]; apply the national guidebook advice to negotiate (open at least 20% below) [C cost.negotiation] and the Nouakchott forum advice not to pay strangers from public posts [NKC C housing.search].
  - Day 6: ask at the free-zone one-stop shop if you plan to work or trade [C business.admin].
  - Day 7: ship graveyard and Cansado [4][1].
- Opening a bank account, residence card: not found.
- Rough first-week spend: visa €55 [C admin.visa] + bus from Nouakchott ≈ €13–16 [C daily.transport] + 6 nights at Camping Baie du Lévrier × €11 = €66, or at Hotel Sahel × €50 = €300 [1] + 14 plates × €1.60 = €22.4 [C cost.living] + 15 taxi rides × €0.45 = €6.7 [C daily.transport] → **≈ €163–400** (low: 55 + 13 + 66 + 22.4 + 6.7 = 163.1; high: 55 + 16 + 300 + 22.4 + 6.7 = 400.1).

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best season November–February per Routard [4]. Night driving outside towns is dangerous (France) [C safety.general]; at road checks, declare passengers, contacts, plate and exact route and keep to it [10].

### One day
- Morning: Port de pêche artisanal as the boats come in, then the fish and vegetable market near the port and the central market in Keran [1][4]. On foot within Keran; shared taxi 20 ouguiyas otherwise [C daily.transport].
- Midday: Senegalese fish-and-rice plate, about 70 ouguiyas [C cost.living]; or snacks at Pleine de Lune [1].
- Afternoon: hired 4x4 with driver (national guidebook price 2,000–2,500 MRU a day, fuel extra) [NKC C cost.living] to the southern tip of the Cap Blanc peninsula: monk-seal reserve, entry "a small fee" (no amount) [1][4]; stop at the ship graveyard in the Baie de Cansado on the way back [4].
- Evening: camel milk at the town entrance [1]; dinner at La Paillotte [1].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily life): central market and chep-chep at opening time, then the fish market by the port [1][C social.food]; ride shared taxis between Keran and Numerowatt to see how most residents live and move [1][C daily.transport].
- Day 3: Cansado: gun-emplacement ruins (free), ship graveyard, lunch at The Oasean Hotel [1][4].
- Day 4: Centre de Pêche, salt-marsh estuary birdwatching on the way, boat to the picnic island [1]; or kitesurfing in the Baie de l'Étoile [4].
- Day 5–6: Banc d'Arguin from the 235 km entrance on the Nouakchott road [1]: guide essential, 4x4 and GPS, 200 MRU per person per night, stay on authorised tracks [7]; two million migrant waders in the northern winter, Imraguen fishing villages, UNESCO site since 1989 [8]; Arguin island with 18th–19th-century fortifications (Wikivoyage) [11]. France asks for two vehicles and experienced guides [10]. Back before dark.
- Day 7 (daily life): a FC Nouadhibou home match at the Stade Municipal if the calendar allows (fixtures not sourced) [5]; otherwise a Friday morning in Numerowatt and the Alliance Française in the afternoon [1].
- Not proposed: La Güera (in Western Sahara, reached via a border crossing near a Mauritanian military post; Wikivoyage itself says check with authorities first) [1], and the ore train (orange border strip, new UK train-risk information, October 2026) [10][C daily.transport].
