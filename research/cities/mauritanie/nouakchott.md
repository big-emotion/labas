# Nouakchott, Mauritania

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the country dossier states no exchange rate, so EUR equivalents use **1 EUR = 44.78 MRU** (donneesmondiales.com, rate dated 2026-11-13), rounded. Watch the unit trap: since the 2018 reform 1 MRU = 10 old ouguiyas, and many sources (and sellers) still quote in old ouguiyas, often written "UM". Where a source's unit is ambiguous, the item says so. Commercial property-data sites (The Africanvestor) and cost aggregators (combien-coute.net, Africarrières) are flagged as such; their ranges are indicative, not surveys. Session note: the shared web-search budget ran out after six searches, so most sources below were opened directly.
