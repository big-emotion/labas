# Nouadhibou, Mauritania

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the country dossier states no exchange rate, so EUR equivalents use **1 EUR = 44.78 MRU** (donneesmondiales.com, rate dated 2026-11-13), rounded. Since the 2018 reform 1 MRU = 10 old ouguiyas; Wikivoyage mixes "old" and "new" ouguiyas, so its fares are flagged as ambiguous. Session note: the shared web-search budget ran out after six searches, so the sources below were mostly opened directly. Nouadhibou is thinly covered online: there is no recent rent source, no local press and no expat forum thread about the city.

## Items

- [cost.rent] An AI-generated cost site (its own label: "generated by an AI model without cited sources") estimates a 1-bed flat at about $375/month (≈ 15,000 MRU, ≈ €335) and a local's housing budget at $250. Treat as a placeholder only: the same page lists a pint of beer, though alcohol is illegal. — nomad.watch "Nouadhibou costs", updated 2026-06-08, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Nationally, almost everything is negotiated and prices are posted only in Nouakchott supermarkets; a guidebook advises opening at half or a third of the asking price, or at least 20% below. — Routard "Argent et budget Mauritanie", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Sellers often quote in old ouguiyas, ten times the MRU figure, so the guidebook advises dividing announced prices by 10. — Routard, undated (accessed 2026-11-14), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] A Senegalese fish-and-rice plate costs around 70 ouguiyas (≈ €1.60 if MRU) in town. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated, prices around 2023 (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Three supermarkets (Carrefour, Bon Marché, 28 Novembre) sit within 200 m on the main street, with a grocery in Cansado; the guide notes their prices are sometimes high. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The city has four main areas: the centre (with the airport), Numerowatt to the north, Cansado to the south, and a commuter settlement for harbour workers near the ore port; Wikipedia calls Cansado the main residential area. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Keran is the centre (police, customs, banks, central market); Cansado, about 10 km south, was built for SNIM mine workers and has tennis courts; Numerowatt is the most populous and fastest-growing district. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Fishing is the main economic activity and iron-ore processing the largest industry; the ore arrives by SNIM train from the Zouérate mines to the Point Central port. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou" (en and fr), undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] A fish seller from Nouadhibou lost 700 of 1,000 cartons of fish in Nouakchott freezing plants during the August 2026 power cuts, a sign of how the city's fishing trade depends on the capital's cold chain. — Le360 Afrique, 2026-09-17, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Nouadhibou has been a free zone since early 2013, covering over 1,000 km² and about 100,000 residents, including the urban area, part of the peninsula and bay and waters up to 2 nautical miles offshore. — Autorité de la Zone Franche de Nouadhibou website, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [business.invest] Free-zone incentives: exemption from all import duties and taxes, tax exemptions, a capped income tax for expatriate employees, and an administration fee of 2% of turnover or none. — Autorité de la Zone Franche de Nouadhibou, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [business.admin] The free zone runs a one-stop shop (guichet unique) that is free and promises processing within 5 days. — Autorité de la Zone Franche de Nouadhibou, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] The free-zone authority called for a master plan for a new Central Business District (2025-06-26) and ran a PPP prequalification for a deep-water port (2024-04-29, postponement notice 2024-01-17). — Autorité de la Zone Franche de Nouadhibou, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Unmarked shared taxis charge 20 ouguiyas one way on the main roads, plus 20 to reach Port Autonome or Cansado; a car that honks as it approaches is usually a taxi. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Ride-hailing apps CarApp (local) and Yango are said to operate in town; Yango's own international site does not list Mauritania. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou" and yango.com, accessed 2026-11-14, blog/company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] At least five bus companies run to Nouakchott daily, mostly in the morning, taking about 8 hours for 600–700 ouguiyas (≈ €13–16 if MRU); shared Mercedes taxis leave at first light from the gare routière at the town entrance. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A guidebook gives about 400 MRU (≈ €9) for a bush taxi Nouakchott–Nouadhibou, varying with fuel, seat and passenger numbers. — Routard, undated (accessed 2026-11-14), intl-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The SNIM ore train has a daily passenger car; a ticket to Choum is quoted at 300 ouguiyas. Riding in the ore wagons is dangerous and strictly forbidden (France) and illegal outside the passenger carriages (UK, which added new train-risk information in October 2026). — Wikivoyage (ca. 2023); France Diplomatie 2026-11-09; UK FCDO 2026-10-19, blog/official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] The airport has flights to the Canary Islands and several Mauritanian cities; the RN2 links the city to Nouakchott, about 450 km south. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou" (fr and en), undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Several banks have ATMs that take foreign cards (Société Générale's accept Visa and Mastercard); a bureau de change at the Keran entrance photocopies your passport. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [daily.money] A guidebook says ATMs are fewer in regional capitals such as Nouadhibou than in Nouakchott; the UK says only a few hotels in Nouakchott and Nouadhibou accept credit cards. — Routard, undated; UK FCDO, 2026-10-19, intl-media/official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Nouadhibou Regional Hospital opened in 2017 after five years of construction and is the largest hospital in the region. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] France's health advice for Mauritania gives no information on care in Nouadhibou; for the country it advises evacuation abroad after stabilisation. — France Diplomatie "Santé", 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all but essential travel within 25 km of the Western Sahara border but explicitly excludes Nouadhibou city and the Nouakchott–Nouadhibou road. — UK FCDO, last updated 2026-10-19, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France rates the border strip from Nouadhibou to Zouérate orange (travel only if essential), except the PK55 crossing; the edges of the PK55 track are mined, so stay on it. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", 2026-11-09 (zones 2026-03-11), official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Poor roads and vehicles, animals and sand on the asphalt cause many serious accidents; night driving outside towns is especially dangerous. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [community.expat] France keeps a consular agency in Nouadhibou, run by an honorary consul and open by appointment. — Ambassade de France "Présence française", accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] An Alliance Française de Nouadhibou has existed since 2015-01-29. — Ambassade de France "Présence française", accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Herders sell fresh camel milk at the town entrance, often in the evening; there is a fish and vegetable market near the port and a second-hand ("chep-chep") market in Keran. — Wikivoyage "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] Alcohol is legally a narcotic nationwide and its consumption is severely punished. — France Diplomatie "Informations utiles", 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [community.diaspora] Since 2006 the city has been a departure point for boats to the Canary Islands; as of 2024 over 80% of arrivals there came from Mauritania, many of them Malians. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou" (en and fr), undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] UNHCR counted about 2,600 refugees and asylum seekers in Nouadhibou in May 2025, down from about 4,000 in late 2024. — Human Rights Watch report, 2025-08-27, academic, confidence: high ⚠ spot-check 2026-11-14: unsupported — figure not found in the HRW report text read
- [rights.racism] Nouadhibou has a police-run immigration detention centre at the regional police directorate, still operating in July 2025; an EU-funded reception centre for people intercepted at sea was due to open in September 2025. — Human Rights Watch report, 2025-08-27, academic, confidence: high
- [rights.racism] HRW found abuses against migrants concentrated in Nouakchott, Nouadhibou and the desert near the Western Sahara border, including a 2020 account of women raped after interception; local police told HRW they generally do not search for people with expired visas. — Human Rights Watch report, 2025-08-27, academic, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex acts are criminal nationwide (penal code art. 308) and there is no anti-discrimination law. — Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in Mauritania"; UK FCDO 2026-10-19, academic/official, confidence: high
- [admin.visa] Same national rules as the capital: an e-visa authorisation from anrpts.gov.mr before travel, then a €55 30-day single-entry visa at the border; PK55 border formalities run 08:00–18:00 with visas issued at the post. — France Diplomatie "Entrée/séjour" and "Sécurité", 2026-11-09, official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Hot desert climate tempered by the Canary Current: mean temperature 21.3 °C, daily highs around 27 °C, record 41.3 °C, 18–23 mm of rain a year (text and table disagree), about 71% humidity. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou" (en and fr), undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The bay holds over 300 scuttled ships, described as the world's largest ship graveyard; Mediterranean monk seals live nearby. — Wikipedia "Nouadhibou", undated (accessed 2026-11-14), academic, confidence: medium
