# Nouakchott: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 44.78 MRU** (the rate the city file uses; donneesmondiales.com, 2026-11-13; the dossier itself states no rate). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/mauritanie/nouakchott.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/mauritanie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33. Almost every rent figure comes from one commercial publisher (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21, low confidence) or a cost aggregator (combien-coute.net, low confidence); treat them as orders of magnitude.

**Unit trap.** 1 MRU = 10 old ouguiyas. Wikivoyage and the aggregator write "UM" without saying which ouguiya they mean. Where a price below is in "UM", the unit is ambiguous and the note says which reading is more plausible, and why [C cost.negotiation].

**Session limit.** The shared WebSearch budget was already used up when this sheet was built (0 searches available), so every new source below was opened directly from a known URL. Local press, venue websites and neighbourhood blogs could not be found that way. Expect thin "Places" lists outside the centre (see Gaps).

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Crime is "relatively low" for a city this size, but violent snatch thefts by motorcyclists are rising, including in the safest districts, and crime grows on the outskirts (France Diplomatie, updated 2026-11-09) [C safety.general][20]. The UK names Le Cinquième, Dar Naïm, Cité Plage and the unlit isolated beaches for knife-point robberies, and notes burglaries where foreigners live (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [C safety.general][21].
- Women: risk of assault in isolated places such as beaches and outlying districts; do not go alone, especially at night (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [C safety.women].
- Power: load-shedding of 5–6 hours at a time in July 2025, several cuts a day in August 2026, then substation fires around 22 August 2026 that cut Tevragh Zeina, parts of Ksar and Sebkha; load-shedding of up to 15–18 hours continued into September 2026 [C daily.power]. The government resigned on 2026-10-09 after an inquiry into the fires [C daily.power]. Budget for backup power wherever you live.
- Water: taps ran dry for weeks in summer 2025, and residents said poor districts suffered more than wealthy ones (Le360, 2025-08-08) [C daily.water].
- Black sub-Saharan Africans: HRW (2025-08-27) documents police extortion and beatings of migrants in Nouakchott, and detention centres in Ksar (closed for renovation per the government, July 2025), Dar Naïm, Arafat and Sebkha [C rights.racism]. Carry your papers and residence documents at all times; HRW reports that valid papers did not always protect people [C rights.racism].
- Alcohol is legally a narcotic; there is no legal bar or club scene [C social.nightlife]. LGBT acts are criminal (art. 308) [C rights.lgbt]. Nothing here is a "safe area".
- Cash economy: shops accept neither cards nor cheques [C daily.money]. Mobile money: Bankily (BPM), USSD *888#, pays SOMELEC and SNDE bills [D].
- No dated door-to-door commute time between neighbourhoods was found. Trips below give fares, not minutes.

## Neighbourhoods

### Tevragh Zeina (with Las Palmas)
- Feel: the prime residential commune of villas, large houses and premium flats, favoured by diplomats, NGO staff, senior professionals, business owners, diaspora renters and some families, who value security, water access, parking and proximity to embassies and offices (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [8][9]. 74,942 residents in 2023 per the infobox; hosts government services, diplomatic missions and international organisations (fr.wikipedia, edited 2026-03-14) [5]. Las Palmas is described as upper-middle and expat-friendly [9].
- Rent: good 2-bed 22,000–35,000 MRU (≈ €491–782); expat-facing stock 18,000–50,000 (≈ €402–1,117); young-professional units 10,000–22,000 (≈ €223–491); vacancy in prime furnished units about 3–5% (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [C cost.rent][8]. Furnished adds 2,000–8,000 MRU a month; each amenity (air-con, water storage, backup power, security, parking) about 1,000–4,000 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the most central of the residential communes for embassies and offices [8]; "tout droit" shared taxis on the paved main roads 10 MRU (≈ €0.22), a taxi with detours 20–30 MRU (≈ €0.45–0.67) (Routard, undated) [C daily.transport]; fares +50% in rain and after 22:00 [C cost.negotiation]. Airport about 25 km (fr.Wikivoyage, 2018) or 40 km (en.Wikivoyage) north; a taxi into town can cost up to €50 [27][1].
- Watch: much of Tevragh Zeina lost power in the 22 August 2026 substation fires; two residents reported 48 hours without power (Le360, 2026-08-25) [C daily.power]. Burglaries where foreigners live (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Hôpital National and Centre National de Cardiologie (public hospitals) — fr.wikipedia "Tevragh-Zeina" [5]
  - Bibliothèque El Irvan (library) — en/fr.wikipedia "Tevragh-Zeina" [5][7]
  - ASC/FC Tevragh-Zeïna (football club) and Collège Tevragh Zeina (school) — en.wikipedia "Tevragh-Zeina" [7]
  - Ambassade de France, Rue Ahmed Ould Hamed (consular registration for stays over 6 months) — Ambassade de France "Présence française" [24][D]
  - ANRPTS office (visa extensions) — France Diplomatie "Voyages d'affaires", 2026-11-09 [D]
  - Chez Lina, Avenue du Général de Gaulle (restaurant and snack) and Deja-Vu market on De Gaulle (shop for American products) — en.Wikivoyage [1]; fr.wikipedia places the avenue across the business and embassy quarter, not a named commune [4]
  - Hotel Tfeila, Avenue Charles de Gaulle (former Novotel) — en.Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q4 "En couple" (security, parking, water access per [8]); Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats" (diaspora renters, embassies); Q5 "Salarié·e" (offices nearby).

### Centre-ville, Ksar and Ilot K
- Feel: Ksar is a "practical central area" of older flats and family houses; Ilot K a central business area with larger family properties (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [9]. Young professionals pick both for closeness to offices and services, and Ksar is a common choice for families seeking schools [8]. The old Ksar village merged into the commune; the former airport lies to its east (fr.wikipedia) [4]. Avenue Gamal Abdel Nasser divides residential areas to the north from the medina quarter to the south (Wikivoyage) [C housing.areas][1].
- Rent: young-professional units in Ksar, Ilot K and Tevragh Zeina 10,000–22,000 MRU (≈ €223–491); good 2-bed in Ksar 22,000–35,000 (≈ €491–782) [C cost.rent][8]. Aggregator: 1-bed in the centre 11,723 UM (≈ €260 if read as MRU) [C cost.rent]. Ilot K sale asks 12–35 million MRU, Ksar 8–25 million [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: central, so short tout-droit hops (10 MRU) or detour taxis (20–30 MRU) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: several sectors of Ksar lost power in the August 2026 fires [C daily.power]. HRW names a police migrant detention centre in Ksar, in use until late 2024 or early 2025 (2025-08-27) [C rights.racism].
- Places (commune boundaries for these central places are not stated in the sources; they are "centre" per the source):
  - Marché Capitale (largest market: spices, fabrics, crafts) — Routard [10]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Prince (sandwich shop near Marché Capitale) — en.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Musée National de Mauritanie (archaeology, ethnography; two permanent rooms), near the Azalaï/Mercure Marhaba hotel and the Hôtel de Ville — en.wikipedia (edited 2025-09-18) [16]
  - Mosquée Ibn Abbas (1963, the city's oldest mosque) — fr.wikipedia "Nouakchott" [4]; en.wikipedia places the museum northeast of it [16]
  - Azalaï Hotel (Marhaba), 160 Avenue Gamal Abdel Nasser — en.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hotel Houda, Avenue Kennedy (centre-ville), and handicraft shops at the top of Avenue Kennedy — en.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Institut Français de Mauritanie (concerts, exhibitions, screenings; address not given) — Routard [10]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (walk or one tout-droit ride to offices and services); Q4 "Seul·e" (young-professional stock); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" by Nouakchott standards; Q3 "Moyen".

### Cité Plage (the beach side)
- Feel: one of the three most expensive areas, with strong expat demand and many furnished units (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [8]. The beaches lie about 5 km west of the centre; locals go on weekend evenings (Wikivoyage) [C nature.access]. During the August 2026 blackout, families fled to the beach to walk and swim (Le360, 2026-08-25) [22].
- Rent: good 2-bed 22,000–35,000 MRU (≈ €491–782); expat-facing stock 18,000–50,000 (≈ €402–1,117) [C cost.rent][8].
- Getting around: no fare or time to the centre found specifically for Cité Plage; general taxi fares above [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the UK names Cité Plage for daytime and night-time knife-point robberies and the unlit, isolated beaches (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [21]. Women: risk of assault on isolated beaches (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [20]. Swimming is dangerous because of strong currents (Wikivoyage) [C nature.access]. The coastal strip is "mostly left to flood" and parts of the city lie below sea level (Wikipedia) [C housing.areas].
- Places (exact distance from Cité Plage not sourced for any of them):
  - Nouakchott beaches, about 5 km west of the centre — en.Wikivoyage [1]; Le360, 2026-08-25 [22]
  - Port de Pêche artisanal / Plage des Pêcheurs (fish sold straight from the pirogues; boats return every afternoon) — Wikivoyage [1]; Routard [10]; fr.wikipedia [4]
  - Complexe Sabah, Plage des Pêcheurs (air-conditioned bungalows, opened 2022; partly under construction in Dec 2023) — en.Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage" (with the safety caveats above); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi". Not a fit for Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" on its own: it is one of the three districts the UK names.

### Sebkha and El Mina (the Cinquième)
- Feel: Sebkha holds the city's large shopping area, the Cinquième, with an open-air market (Wikipedia) [3][C social.culture]. The Africanvestor calls Sebkha an entry-level area with older housing and El Mina an entry-level dense area (2026-09-21) [9]. El Mina's kébbé (shanty town) was restructured in 2003–2007, each family getting a 120 m² plot, which fr.wikipedia calls a success [4].
- Rent: parts of Sebkha are among the cheaper areas for 2-beds (no figure) [8]; city-wide 2-bed range 13,000–35,000 MRU, average 18,000 (≈ €290–782, avg €402) [C cost.rent]. Sale asks Sebkha 3–9 million MRU, El Mina 3–8 million [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: the "tout droit" route BMD–5ème–Arrêt bus at a flat 100 old ouguiyas (10 MRU, ≈ €0.23) (Le360, 2022-02-12) [23][C daily.transport].
- Watch: the UK names Le Cinquième for robberies (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [21]. Several sectors of Sebkha lost power in August 2026 [C daily.power]. HRW reports a migrant detention centre in Sebkha from early 2025 [C rights.racism].
- Places:
  - Marché du Cinquième (large open-air market and shopping area) — en.wikipedia "Nouakchott" [3]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Stade Cheikha Ould Boïdiya (8,200 seats; home of ASC Nasr de Sebkha; also used by the national team) — en.wikipedia, edited 2026-11-10 [18]
  - Marché Sixième (local goods, souvenirs; neighbourhood location not stated) — Wikivoyage [1][C social.culture]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (market density, by day); Q5 "J'entreprends" for trade and retail. Not for Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" as a first base.

### Arafat, Teyarett and Dar Naïm (family districts)
- Feel: the Africanvestor groups these as family-friendly, with larger homes, nearby shops and easier family support; Arafat is "family-oriented, value-focused" and Dar Naïm a budget area with strong local demand and older houses (2026-09-21) [8][9]. Arafat is the most populous commune, 216,919 residents in 2023 (fr.wikipedia, edited 2026-03-14) [6]; its "Kosovo" area received families relocated from slums in 2009 and fr.wikipedia describes it as having high crime and poor services [3][4].
- Rent: 2–3-bed family homes 13,000–25,000 MRU (≈ €290–558); among the cheapest studios and 1-beds (city studio range 5,500–13,000) [C cost.rent][8]. Aggregator: 3-bed in the suburbs 12,818 UM (≈ €285 if MRU) [C cost.rent]. Sale asks Arafat 5–14 million MRU, Dar Naïm 3.5–10 million [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no corridor, fare or time found for these communes beyond the citywide tout-droit and taxi fares [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the UK names Dar Naïm for knife-point robberies (FCDO, 2026-10-19) [21]; France notes crime growing on the outskirts (2026-11-09) [20]. HRW reports migrant detention centres in Dar Naïm and Arafat from early 2025 (2025-08-27) [C rights.racism].
- Places: none could be sourced to these communes in this session (no market, school, clinic or mosque page reached; see Gaps).
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille" on Q3 "Serré" or "Moyen" (space for the money), but only for readers with family already there: the area-specific safety warnings and the lack of sourced services make them a poor first base for a newcomer.

### Riyad and Toujounine (budget outskirts)
- Feel: Riyad is "affordable and growing", Toujounine "expanding, budget-oriented" (The Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [9]; both are among the cheapest areas for studios and 1-beds, Toujounine also for 2-beds [8].
- Rent: no area figure; the citywide studio range 5,500–13,000 MRU (≈ €123–290) and the aggregator's 1-bed in the suburbs 7,011 UM (≈ €156 if MRU) are the closest [C cost.rent]. Careers guide: outskirts 30–50% cheaper than the centre [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Garage Riyadh, 7 km south of Carrefour Madrid, has sept-places to Rosso at about 300 MRU a seat (Wikivoyage) [1]. No commute time to the centre found.
- Watch: crime is growing on the outskirts (France Diplomatie, 2026-11-09) [20]; poor districts suffered more in the 2025 water shortage (residents quoted by Le360) [C daily.water].
- Places:
  - Garage Riyadh (sept-place station to Rosso) — en.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Camel market on the Boutilimit road (outskirts; commune not stated) — Wikivoyage [1]; Routard [10]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" only; not recommended as a newcomer's first base (thin sourcing, outskirts crime remark).

## Living: typical weeks

Notes for every profile: Friday is the main prayer day; shop hours change and driving is erratic around dusk during Ramadan (FCDO) [C social.religion]. April–October is very hot, often above 40 °C [C climate.daily]. Bring a phone charged before cuts and a cash float: cards are refused in shops [C daily.money].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

Walkable to the market and the museum quarter [1][16].
- Connectivity: Mattel Wifina 4G box, 300 GB for 1,500 MRU / 30 days, unlimited 00:00–07:00 [C daily.internet]; no speed stated anywhere; internet was cut during the August 2025 exam session [C daily.internet]. No coworking space could be sourced (Gaps).
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; evening tea and groceries at Marché Capitale (on foot or a 10 MRU tout droit) [10][C daily.transport].
  - Tue: work; late afternoon at the Port de Pêche when the pirogues come in, buy fish for the week [10][C social.food] (detour taxi 20–30 MRU each way).
  - Wed: work during the 00:00–07:00 unlimited window for big uploads [C daily.internet]; dinner at Chez Lina, Avenue du Général de Gaulle [1].
  - Thu: work; evening event at the Institut Français de Mauritanie (programme not sourced) [10].
  - Fri: prayer day; quiet work morning; afternoon at the Musée National [16].
  - Sat: Marché du Cinquième in the morning (go in daylight; FCDO names Le Cinquième) [3][21]; see Saturday-night options below.
  - Sun: beach in a group in the late afternoon [C nature.access][20].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic legally. Alcohol is a narcotic in law [C social.nightlife]; Wikivoyage's claims about hotel clubs serving drinks are unverified and legally risky, and are not recommended here [C social.nightlife].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Iman (steaks, brochettes, seafood, curry) or Chez Lina (De Gaulle) [1]; expat-restaurant meal €8–20 per person [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": tea at home and streaming in the 00:00–07:00 unlimited window (Wifina, or 1–5-hour passes at 10–30 MRU usable midnight to noon) [C daily.internet].

The nearest relief is the Adrar plateau (Terjit oasis gorge, 45 km south of Atar) [15]; buses to Aoujeft through Terjit cost about 500 MRU from Nouakchott (Wikivoyage, 2023) [15]: a long weekend at minimum, and the FCDO advises a convoy and experienced guide outside urban areas [21].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": dunes on the city's edge as a short excursion (Routard) [10]; Banc d'Arguin overnight, "a few hours' drive" (Routard), 200 MRU per person per night, guide essential, 4x4 and GPS recommended (PNBA) [11][12].
  - "Je reste en ville": Musée National, Port de Pêche at pirogue time, Marché Capitale, a match at Stade Cheikha Ould Boïdiya [16][10][18].

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Ksar** (named by the Africanvestor as a common pick for families seeking schools) [8] or **Tevragh Zeina** for budget readers with Q3 "Le prix compte peu" [8]. The family districts (Arafat, Teyarett, Dar Naïm) are cheaper [8] but carry area safety warnings and have no sourced services [21][C rights.racism].
- Schools: Lycée français Théodore Monod (primary, collège, lycée; uses AEFE services; fee tables did not load; address not given on its site) [25][C family.schools]; American International School of Nouakchott and TLC International School (named by Wikipedia; locations and fees not found) [3][C family.schools]. French children at AEFE- or MLF-homologated schools may get means-tested scholarships [C family.schools].
- Health: Hôpital National and Centre National de Cardiologie in Tevragh Zeina [5]; France advises evacuation abroad after stabilisation for serious cases, and private insurance with repatriation [C health.care]. Malaria peaks August–November [C health.care].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school run by detour taxi (20–30 MRU per trip) or a car with driver [C daily.transport]; after school, homework at home in the cooler evening.
  - Wed: afternoon at the Bibliothèque El Irvan [5].
  - Thu: Port de Pêche at pirogue time with the children (busy, keep them close) [10].
  - Fri: prayer day; family meal.
  - Sat: Musée National in the morning [16]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: beach in a group in the late afternoon, no swimming in strong currents [C nature.access]; or a football match in daylight (avoid walking near the Stade Olympique at night on match days) [20][17].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic (alcohol ban) [C social.nightlife].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": a family dinner at Iman or Chez Lina [1]; Nouakchott Accueil for meeting other families [24].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home, tea, films in the night data window [C daily.internet].
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" (as above, in a group); "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic from Nouakchott; "Dans la savane ou le désert": city-edge dunes [10] or a guided Banc d'Arguin night (200 MRU per person) [12]; "Je reste en ville": museum, markets, matches [16][10][18].
- Rough weekly spend (family of four, school fees excluded because none were found):
  - Rent: family home in Arafat/Teyarett/Dar Naïm 13,000–25,000 / 4.33 = 3,002–5,774 MRU (≈ €67–129), or aggregator 3-bed in the centre 29,049 UM / 4.33 = 6,709 (≈ €150 if MRU) [C cost.rent]
  - Food at the careers guide's modest per-person budget, €100–150 a month: ×4 / 4.33 = €92.4–138.6 [C cost.living]
  - Power and water €6.9–26.6 [C cost.living]
  - School run: 10 detour taxis × 20–30 MRU = 200–300 MRU (≈ €4.5–6.7) [C daily.transport]
  - **Total ≈ €171–322 a week, before school fees.** Low: 67 + 92.4 + 6.9 + 4.5 = 170.8. High: 150 + 138.6 + 26.6 + 6.7 = 321.9. Imported products cost 2–3 times their price in France or Belgium, so a family buying imported goods will exceed this [C cost.living].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Before flying: e-visa authorisation on anrpts.gov.mr, then a €55 single-entry 30-day visa with biometrics at the border; overstays must be regularised at the same cost; extensions at the ANRPTS office in Tevragh Zeina [C admin.visa][D]. Arrive with euros or dollars: the airport ATM may not work and airport exchange rates are poor [C daily.money].
- Where to stay first: the city file's forum advice is a short-term rental while searching on site [C housing.search]. Sourced options: Résidence Zahra, opposite Hotel Halima (air-con, bathroom, Wi-Fi, from 12,000 "UM"); Auberge Menata, Rue Aber Baker (dorm from 2,500 "UM", tent from 1,500; helps with car rental and guides); Azalaï Marhaba and Hotel Tfeila (no price) [1]. Unit: Wikivoyage's other UM fares (shared taxi 100 UM) match old ouguiyas, so 12,000 UM most likely means 1,200 MRU (≈ €27) a night; this is an inference, confirm on booking.
- Week:
  - Day 1 (arrival): airport taxi (can cost up to €50 in euros or dollars) [1]; check in.
  - Day 2: SIM card from one of the three operators (Mauritel, Mattel, Chinguitel) [D]; mobile plan ~883 UM a month (aggregator) or Mattel data passes [C cost.living][C daily.internet]; change money at a bureau de change (open afternoons) [C daily.money].
  - Day 3: open Bankily (BPM wallet; transfers, merchant payments, utility bills via *888#) [D]; walk the centre: Marché Capitale, Musée National [10][16].
  - Day 4: flat hunting in Ksar / Ilot K / Tevragh Zeina through agencies, never paying a contact found in a public post (the Expat.com moderator removed one for scam risk) [C housing.search]. Correctly priced flats let in 30–45 days, prime 1–2-beds in 15–30; September–October, January–February and May–June are peak seasons [C housing.search].
  - Day 5: viewings; ask what backup power and water storage the flat has (the amenity premium is 1,000–4,000 MRU each) [C cost.rent]. Visit fees, deposits and months of advance rent: **not found** in any source [C Gaps]; ask each agency in writing.
  - Day 6: register with the consulate if you will stay over 6 months [24][D]; meet Nouakchott Accueil (WhatsApp listed on the embassy page) [24].
  - Day 7: rest; beach in a group at the end of the day [C nature.access].
- Opening a bank account: no source on requirements for a foreign or diaspora resident was found (Gaps). Residence card procedure: not found; the ANRPTS site returned an empty page [C Gaps].
- Saturday night and weekend outings: as profile 1.
- Rough spend for the first week: visa €55 [C admin.visa] + airport taxi up to €50 [1] + 6 nights × ~€27 = €162 (old-UM reading) [1] + SIM/plan ≈ €20 [C cost.living] + 14 local meals × €2–4 = €28–56 [C cost.living] + 15 taxi rides × 20–30 MRU = 300–450 MRU (≈ €6.7–10) [C daily.transport] → **≈ €322–353** (low: 55 + 50 + 162 + 20 + 28 + 6.7 = 321.7; high: 55 + 50 + 162 + 20 + 56 + 10 = 353), counting the airport taxi at its €50 ceiling, before any deposit.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Best season November–March (Routard) [10]; April–October often above 40 °C [C climate.daily]. Dress modestly; no photographs of official, military or religious buildings, ports or airports [D].

### One day
- Morning: Marché Capitale for spices, fabrics and crafts, haggling down toward a third of the first price [10][C cost.negotiation]; then the Musée National (archaeology from Koumbi Saleh, Ouadane, Tichit; ethnography) [16]. Fee not sourced. Move by detour taxi (20–30 MRU), agreeing the fare first [C daily.transport][10].
- Midday: sandwich at The Prince near Marché Capitale or lunch at Chez Lina on De Gaulle [1]; local meal €2–4 [C cost.living]. Look at the Mosquée Saoudienne from outside (the interior is mainly for worshippers, per Routard) [10].
- Afternoon: taxi to the Port de Pêche / Plage des Pêcheurs for the return of the pirogues and the fish sale [10][1]. Do not photograph the port installations [D].
- Evening: a walk on the beach in a group before dark, no swimming (currents) [C nature.access][21]; dinner at Iman [1]; or an event at the Institut Français [10].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (daily life): a market morning at the Cinquième (open-air, in daylight, in company) [3][21]; ride a "tout droit" shared taxi on the BMD–5ème corridor at the morning rush for 10 MRU to see how most residents commute [23]; afternoon at the Marché Sixième [1].
- Day 3: Banc d'Arguin, departing early with a guide and a 4x4 (guide essential for a first visit; 4x4 and GPS recommended; 200 MRU per person per night; stay on authorised tracks) [12]; UNESCO site since 1989, two million migrant waders in the northern winter, Imraguen fishing villages [13]. France asks for two vehicles and experienced guides there [20]. 4x4 with driver 2,000–2,500 MRU a day, fuel extra [C cost.living].
- Day 4: Banc d'Arguin, second day; at Nouamghar, Imraguen fishermen work with dolphins to herd fish (Wikivoyage) [2]. Return before night: the FCDO and France advise against night driving outside towns [21][20].
- Day 5: rest in town; camel market on the Boutilimit road in the morning [1][10]; afternoon in Tevragh Zeina to see how expat and diaspora renters live [8].
- Day 6 (daily life): a football match in daylight at Stade Cheikha Ould Boïdiya (ASC Nasr de Sebkha) or the Stade Olympique; leave before dark on match days, when gangs have threatened spectators with blades near the Stade Olympique [18][17][20].
- Day 7: Friday or weekend morning around the Mosquée Ibn Abbas (the oldest, 1963) from outside [4]; final market shopping; airport.
- Alternative to Days 3–4, for a desert rather than coast trip: Adrar by minibus from Garage Atar (about 500 MRU) to Atar, then Terjit oasis (45 km south of Atar, entry 200 ouguiya in 2023) [1][15]; this needs more than two days and the FCDO advises a convoy with an experienced guide outside urban areas [21]. The zone colour for the Adrar is not stated in the text of France's security page (only on its map) [20].
