# Libreville: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XAF** (fixed peg, from the dossier). USD figures are converted only where the city file already did so. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gabon/libreville.md`; `[D]` = the Gabon dossier `research/countries/gabon.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. Most venue listings come from Wikivoyage and are dated 2020–2023: they prove a place existed, not that it is open today.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15, zones 2026-03-05) rates crime "modéré jusqu'à présent" and, with Belgium (2026-06-25), lists districts for extra vigilance: Petit Louis, Mont-Bouët, the bus station, Plaine de Rio, London, Lalala, Oloumi, Nkembo, Awendje, Nzeng-Ayong behind the prison, PK5, PK9, PK12 and lower Gué-Gué; also beaches away from the busiest spots, Cap Estérias and Pointe Denis included, especially at night [C safety.general][D]. Armed night burglaries happen; do not resist [C safety.general].
- Taxis: negotiate before boarding, fares usually double after 21:00, avoid riding alone at night, and book an exclusive *course* rather than a shared seat (Canada 2026-10-15, UK FCDO) [C daily.transport][C safety.general]. Gozem runs in Libreville and takes Airtel Money, Moov Money or card [C daily.transport][1].
- Power: rotating load-shedding hit Libreville, Akanda and Owendo from 8 to 15 November 2026; SEEG has been told to publish a daily cut schedule by neighbourhood (Gabonreview, 2026-11-10) [C daily.power]. Plan for a generator wherever you live; expat compounds usually have one [C housing.areas].
- Water: the president called water cuts in Grand Libreville a problem going back decades (Gabonreview, 2026-03-04) [C daily.water]. Drink bottled or filtered water [C daily.water].
- Floods: low-lying areas flood in the rainy season [C housing.areas]; on 28 January 2026 the government listed Nzeng-Ayong, Belle-Vue, Awendjé, Batterie IV and the PK8 Marché Banane area among the worst hit [5][6]. Canada warns El Niño may strengthen in late 2026 and 2027 [C climate.daily].
- Renting: most flats go through informal brokers. Expect a 5,000 XAF (≈ €7.62) fee per visit, then one month's rent, one month's deposit and an agency fee before moving in (Facebook listings, Jul–Nov 2026) [C housing.search]. Expat.com quotes heavier terms (2–3 months' deposit plus up to 3 in advance) [C cost.rent].

## Neighbourhoods

### Quartier Louis (and the Montée de Louis)
- Feel: established, pricey area popular with expatriates and well-off Gabonese families, near embassies and schools [3]; the city's densest cluster of restaurants, lounge bars and clubs [C social.nightlife][2]. One of the old Mpongwé villages of the Quaben area [1]. Expect late-night noise near the Montée.
- Rent: studio-type garçonnière near Hôtel Louis 210,000 XAF/month (≈ €320), Facebook listing Jul–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; a 3-bedroom villa with pool 1,700,000 (≈ €2,592), Expat.com Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. Africarrières (2026-08-07) and OpenShores (2026-04-15) both put Louis among the most expensive areas [C housing.areas].
- Getting around: Gozem's fixed airport fare to Louis is 4,000 XAF (≈ €6.10), the lowest band (Gabon Media Time, 2026-04-24) [C daily.transport]. Wikivoyage describes Hôtel Le Patio in Louis as walkable to the Mbolo mall [2]. Door-to-door time to the centre not sourced.
- Watch: Louis had more than three years without running water until boreholes were connected in April 2024 (Gabon Media Time, 2024-04-07) [9]. Petit Louis is on the France/Belgium vigilance list [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - No Stress, start of the Montée de Louis (bar-restaurant with live music, open since 2002; burgers and cocktails) — Wikivoyage FR [1]; Expat.com [3]
  - La Rôtisserie and the Murmure piano bar, on the Montée de Louis in the former Hollywood Café (restaurant from 17:00, mains about 8,500–11,000 XAF in 2018; piano bar with Wednesday karaoke) — Gabonreview, 2018-11-22, OLD [10]
  - Les ateliers Olima, Descente Jeanne Ebori (contemporary art gallery, Mon–Sat 8:30–16:30) — Wikivoyage FR, Feb 2023 [1]
  - Motel La Pirogue de Louis, top of the Montée (doubles from 20,000 XAF, 2023) and Hôtel Kreg Le Patio (≈ €60 a night) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [1][2]
  - Small market at the Montée de Louis — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen" (garçonnière) to "Le prix compte peu" (villas).

### Glass, Centre-ville and the Bord de Mer
- Feel: Glass is an older commercial area, cheaper than Louis and close to both the centre and the airport [1][3]; the centre holds the Sainte-Marie mission (1844), banks and insurers [1]; the seafront Boulevard has restaurants and nightlife, and locals gather there every day [C social.nightlife][2]. New roads and utilities were being built in Glass in 2025 [3].
- Rent: garçonnières at Glass 250,000 XAF (≈ €381) and Montagne-Sainte 160,000 (≈ €244), Facebook listings Jul–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; a 2-bedroom villa with pool at Glass 1,000,000 (≈ €1,524), Expat.com Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; seafront flats from about 700,000 (≈ €1,067), forum ≈2026-02 [C cost.rent]; centre 1-bed average 543,000 (≈ €828), range 350,000–800,000, Numbeo Aug 2025 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: you are in the centre. Gozem airport fare to Glass 6,000 XAF (≈ €9.15) [C daily.transport]. A private 15-minute taxi ride costs about 1,000 XAF by day [2]; standard shared rides were 200 XAF before the October 2026 fuel shortage pushed them to 300–400 [C daily.transport].
- Watch: Mont-Bouët, Nkembo and the bus station, all next to the centre, are on the vigilance list [C safety.general]; phones are snatched and cars broken into around markets (Canada, 2026-10-15) [D].
- Places:
  - Mbolo shopping centre, Boulevard Triomphal (mall with a Géant Casino supermarket and a pharmacy; Mon–Sat 8:30–20:30, Sun 9:00–19:30) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [1][2]
  - Géant CKDO, Boulevard Léon Mba (hypermarket, Mon–Sat 8:30–20:00) — Wikivoyage FR [1]; Wikipédia [4]
  - Institut français du Gabon, Boulevard Omar Bongo Ondimba, next to Mbolo (cinema, concerts, French classes) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [1][2]
  - Musée national des arts, rites et traditions (Tue–Sat 10:00–17:00; about 3,000 XAF alone, 4,000 guided, 2023) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Village des artisans, Avenue du Colonel Parant (crafts, prices negotiable; 08:30–20:00) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [1][2]
  - Maison de la Presse, Glass, opposite Hôtel Re-Ndama (newsagent and bookshop) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Le Shalimar, Rue d'Alsace (Indian buffet, Mon–Sat 09:30–19:00) — Wikivoyage EN [2]
  - Pelisson, rue Gustave Anguilet (bakery-café, from 6:00) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (central, fibre [C daily.internet]); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen" (Montagne-Sainte and Glass garçonnières); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Batterie IV and Haut de Gué-Gué
- Feel: upmarket residential zone favoured by international families, especially those at École Ruban Vert; property values up 25–30 % in recent years per Expat.com (2025-12-04) [3]. Named after a WWII artillery battery; part of the Quaben area [1].
- Rent: no Batterie IV rent figure found; a plot there was listed for sale at 450,000,000 XAF (≈ €686,021) [C cost.rent]. Nearby, lower Gué-Gué 3-bed flats were listed at 550,000–620,000 (≈ €838–945), Expat.com Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. City-wide 3-bed in the centre about 1,250,000 (≈ €1,906) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Gozem airport fare to Gué-Gué 4,000 XAF (≈ €6.10) [C daily.transport]. No sourced time to the centre.
- Watch: Batterie IV was among the flooded areas on 28 January 2026 [5][6]. Lower Gué-Gué is on the France/Belgium vigilance list [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - École Ruban Vert (IB primary and diploma, ages 2–18; 2025 IB average 34.4) — Expat.com [3]; [C family.schools]
  - Club Saoti, Haut de Gué-Gué (25 m pool from 5,000 XAF a day or about 20,000 for 15 days; tennis, squash, bowling; 2023) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Agatour, national tourism agency, Bel Espace 2 building — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Stop on the Libreville City Tour bus — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (IB school on the doorstep); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q10 "La santé et l'école".

### La Sablière (Akanda)
- Feel: the wealthiest quarter of Akanda; the head of state, ministers, diplomats and senior officials live there (Gabonreview opinion, 2022-10-21) [7]; luxury villas and gated complexes [3][C housing.areas]. Quiet, car-based.
- Rent: 4-bedroom duplex villas with pool 1,700,000–2,000,000 XAF (≈ €2,592–3,049), Expat.com Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; garden villas city-wide 2–3 million [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Gozem airport fare 4,000 XAF [C daily.transport]. Taxis were barred from the quarter from December 2020, which pushed non-drivers to walk and worsened jams on Akanda's single road out (Gabonreview, 2022-10-21) [7]; whether the ban still applies in 2026 was not confirmed (see Gaps). Plan on a car.
- Watch: Akanda shares Libreville's load-shedding [C daily.power]; beaches away from busy spots are flagged at night [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Plage de la Sablière (Plage du remorqueur), on foot past the Sablière bridge (family beach, snack stalls at weekends) — Wikivoyage FR, Feb 2023 [1]
  - Akanda tennis club, La Sablière — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Les nageurs de Libreville, swimming lessons at the Beach Club, La Sablière (Tue–Sun) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - L'Orchidée (rooms from 45,000 XAF, 2023) and Hôtel de la Sablière — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage".

### Okala, Angondjé and the Akanda road
- Feel: the northern growth corridor of Akanda, the commune that became the Estuaire capital in 2022 [D][4]. Mixed: Angondjé has social housing (the Amissa estate) and one of the two university hospitals [C cost.rent][4], while Angondjé Village still lacked paved roads and running water in mid-2025 (Gabon Media Time, 2025-07-31) [8]. Social-media commenters name Akanda among the areas newcomers go out to eat and shop (Nov 2026, low confidence) [C housing.areas].
- Rent: single-storey flat in Akanda (Cap Santa Clara) 170,000 XAF (≈ €259) and a flat in the Amissa social housing at Angondjé 310,000 (≈ €473), Facebook listings Jul–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; a 2-bed mini-villa of 200 m² at Okala-Mikolongo 400,000 (≈ €610) and a 4-bed villa at Okala 2,700,000 (≈ €4,116), Expat.com Nov 2026 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Gozem airport fare to Okala or Angondjé 6,000 XAF (≈ €9.15) [C daily.transport]. During the October 2026 fuel shortage, Libreville–Akanda cost at least 1,500 XAF (≈ €2.29) by taxi [C daily.transport]. No sourced commute time.
- Watch: Angondjé Village: dry taps, makeshift boreholes, dust or mud on an unfinished road (Gabon Media Time, 2025-07-31) [8]. Load-shedding of November 2026 included Akanda [C daily.power].
- Places:
  - CHU d'Angondjé (CHUA, university hospital) — Wikipédia [4]
  - Hôpital d'Instruction des Armées d'Akanda (HIAA) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Market at the Okala crossroads — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Maxi CKDO, Carrefour Jiji, Avorbam (supermarket, Mon–Sat 8:30–20:00, Sun 9:00–13:00) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Arboretum Raponda Walker, Route du Cap Estérias (protected part of the Mondah forest, created 2012) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (room to grow, hospital nearby); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Mondah forest) and "À la plage" (Cap Estérias, Cap Santa Clara).

### Nzeng-Ayong
- Feel: a large popular district on the Voie express, in the 6th arrondissement [1][5]. Expat.com lists it among the newer-build, good-value areas [D]; Africarrières lists it as more affordable (2026-08-07) [C housing.areas].
- Rent: no area-specific listing found; outside-the-centre 1-bed about 300,000 XAF (≈ €457), range 200,000–450,000, Numbeo Aug 2025 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Gozem airport fare 6,000 XAF [C daily.transport]; the pre-2026 bus line L5 ran Sainte Marie–Nzeng Ayong [1] (the new CNT network replaces it; fares not published, Gabonreview 2026-09-20) [C daily.transport].
- Watch: "Nzeng-Ayong behind the prison" is on the France/Belgium vigilance list [C safety.general]. About fifty people were evacuated in the 6th arrondissement, notably Nzeng-Ayong, after the January 2026 floods (Gabonreview, 2026-01-29) [5]. In September 2026 the governorate targeted parts of Nzeng-Ayong, Charbonnages and nearby quarters for demolition to clear an 80 m road right-of-way (Le360 Afrique, 2026-09-03) [11]: check a plot's legal status before renting or buying.
- Places:
  - Nzeng-Ayong market (Thu–Wed 6:30–18:00) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Arboretum de Sibang, listed under Nzeng-Ayong (16 ha, about 170 tree species; arrange ahead for the gate) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [1][2]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" on a budget; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Sibang). Not for Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" in the flagged part.

### Lalala and Owendo (south)
- Feel: popular southern districts toward the port; Owendo handles about 70 % of Gabon's sea trade and is the railway terminus [4]. Wikivoyage FR describes bars along a strip called the "Couloir de la mort" toward Owendo [1].
- Rent: the city's cheapest sourced rents: a "chambre américaine" in Lalala for 50,000–60,000 XAF (≈ €76–91) and a 2-bedroom house for 180,000 (≈ €274) (Le360 Afrique, 2026-05-21), with move-in costs of 450,000 (≈ €686) for that house [C cost.rent]; a Lalala house with its own meter 220,000 (≈ €335), Facebook listing 2026 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Gozem airport fare to Owendo or the PK quarters 8,000 XAF (≈ €12.20), the highest band [C daily.transport]; Gare d'Owendo is about 10 km south of the centre [2]. Pre-CNT bus lines L2, L3 and L4 served Lalala [1].
- Watch: Lalala and Oloumi are on the France/Belgium vigilance list [C safety.general]; PK5, PK9 and PK12 too [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Gare d'Owendo (Transgabonais terminus: ticket desks, restaurant, ATM; book 48 h ahead) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - CHU d'Owendo (orthopaedics and trauma) — Wikivoyage FR [1]; [C health.care]
  - La Forêt Noire, Carrefour SNI, Owendo (bakery, Mon–Sat 6:00–21:30) — Wikivoyage FR [1]
  - Oloumi market, near the Ogombyè river — Wikivoyage FR [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (only if the vigilance list is acceptable); Q5 "Salarié·e" for port and logistics jobs.

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

No coworking space with a confirmed address was found (see Gaps), so the week assumes a home office with a generator-backed building [C housing.areas].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; evening at the Institut français (cinema or concert) [1][2], 1,000 XAF private taxi [2].
  - Tue: groceries at Mbolo's Géant Casino [2]; lunch at Le Shalimar, Rue d'Alsace [2].
  - Wed: work; papers at the Maison de la Presse, Glass [1].
  - Thu: swim at Club Saoti, Haut de Gué-Gué (5,000 XAF a day, 2023) [1]; private course 1,000–3,000 XAF each way [C daily.transport].
  - Fri: after-work at No Stress, Montée de Louis [1][3].
  - Sat: morning at the Village des artisans [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: walk on the Bord de Mer, where locals gather daily [2]; lunch at La Régina, Boulevard Joseph Deemin (mains 4,500–10,000 XAF) [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": New Cotton Club, rue Anselme Mebale (22:00–05:00, drinks from 5,000 XAF, 2023) or VIP Room (Wed–Sun 22:00–06:00) [1]. Take a booked course home, not a shared taxi [C daily.transport].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Rôtisserie, Montée de Louis [10], or Phare du large (menus from 19,000 XAF, Mon–Sat) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": an Institut français screening [2], or home with a Regab (500–2,000 XAF depending on the venue) [C social.nightlife].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Pointe Denis, about 25 minutes by boat from Michel Marine or Port Môle, 10,000–15,000 XAF (≈ €15–23) [2]; guesthouses and restaurants there [1]. Flagged away from the busy spots, especially at night [C safety.general].

  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Lopé National Park, a UNESCO site whose north keeps the last grass savannas [13], on the Transgabonais line from Owendo [1]; a 2–3 night trip, not a weekend: the Lopé fare and timetable were not sourced (Franceville express fares were 42,200–64,300 XAF in 2022) [1].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Base: **Quartier Louis** in a hotel, then a garçonnière in Louis or Glass [C cost.rent].
- Before leaving: e-visa online through the DGDI, 45,000 XAF (≈ €69) plus €15 for a single entry or 120,000 plus €15 for 6 months multi-entry, about 72 h; yellow-fever proof is checked on arrival; stays over 3 months need an open-ended entry authorisation [C admin.visa].
- Week:
  - Day 1: land at Léon-Mba airport (ATM, free Wi-Fi) [1]; Gozem fixed fare to Louis 4,000 XAF [C daily.transport]; check in at Motel La Pirogue de Louis (from 20,000, 2023) or Hôtel Kreg Le Patio (≈ €60) [1][2].
  - Day 2: SIM from an operator shop only (street SIMs are often reused), data loaded by USSD code [C daily.internet]; open Airtel Money or Moov Money [C daily.money]. Cash: cards work only in big hotels, restaurants and supermarkets, and ATM withdrawals are capped [C daily.money].
  - Day 3: flat hunting: the public "Les Infos de Libreville" Facebook group and the "Je libère ma maison" TikTok account (no fee for owner or tenant) [C cost.rent][C housing.search]; brokers charge 5,000 XAF per visit [C housing.search].
  - Day 4: visits in Glass and Montagne-Sainte (garçonnières 160,000–250,000) [C cost.rent]; check the generator and water supply [C daily.power][C daily.water].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Mont-Bouët market when it opens (6:30, closed Monday), the country's largest [1][4]; it is on the vigilance list, so go with little cash and watch your phone [C safety.general][2]. Then the Sainte-Marie mission cathedral, built partly on the remains of Fort d'Aumale [4][2].
- Midday: Musée national des arts, rites et traditions (Tue–Sat, 3,000–4,000 XAF, 2023) [1] and the Village des artisans on Avenue du Colonel Parant (haggle) [1]; lunch at Le Shalimar, Rue d'Alsace [2].
- Afternoon: the Bord de Mer seafront and its sculptures [2]; Mbolo and the Institut français next door [1][2]. Move by private course, 1,000–3,000 XAF a hop [C daily.transport].
- Evening: dinner on the Montée de Louis (La Rôtisserie) [10], then No Stress [1]. Fares double after 21:00; book a course back [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Pointe Denis by boat from Michel Marine or Port Môle (about 25 minutes, 10,000–15,000 XAF) [2]; leatherback nesting beach [3]. Back before dark [C safety.general].
- Day 3: north to Akanda: Arboretum Raponda Walker on the Cap Estérias road [1], then Cap Estérias (about 20 km) or Cap Santa Clara (2,000 XAF bridge toll; a sturdy car needed) [1]; daytime only [C safety.general].
- Day 4, what daily life looks like: morning at Nzeng-Ayong or Nkembo market (Nkembo Sat–Thu 6:30–19:00) [1]; ride a shared taxi at rush hour to see the fare haggling the press calls exhausting [C cost.negotiation]; evening at the Couloir de la mort bars toward Owendo [1] (vigilance-listed area; go with locals) [C safety.general].
- Day 5: Pongara National Park by boat, about 45 minutes; turtles November–March, whales July–October [1][14].
- Day 6: trip out of town, choose one: by boat to Port-Gentil (Logimar 241 about 3 h 15, from 36,000 XAF one way; Sonaga about 4 h, about 35,000; 2023 fares) [1], for the Sogara beach and Cap Lopez [12]; or the Transgabonais from Owendo toward Lopé's savanna and forest, overnight at least [1][13].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at Saint-Michel de Nkembo (31 carved columns) [1][4] or Notre-Dame de Lourdes [1]; afternoon at Plage de la Sablière with Gabonese families [1]; if a match is on, the Stade omnisports Président-Bongo (fixtures not sourced) [C social.sport].
