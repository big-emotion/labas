## Items

- [cost.rent] A 3-bedroom villa costs 300,000–500,000 XAF/month (≈ €457–762); rents outside Libreville are said to be generally 30–50 % lower. — Africarrières "Coût de la vie" Gabon, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Akouakam is the largest and most popular neighbourhood, with a football stadium; other areas include Angone, Adjougou, Meka'a, Mfoul and Essong. — Wikipédia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Oyem is the commercial and service centre of the north, called the "agricultural capital of the north" (cocoa, coffee, cassava, plantain); a Belgian agro-industrial group has revived rubber and palm oil since 2004; little manufacturing. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] A Sobraga plant bottles Régab and Castel beer and soft drinks; the town also hosts the second military region, a gendarmerie legion and a Republican Guard base. — Wikipédia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Cross-border trade hub near Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea; cocoa and coffee are traditionally trucked to Kribi and Douala in Cameroon for export rather than through Libreville. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Gabon and Equatorial Guinea linked their grids in February 2025 to support supply in northern Gabon. — Connaissance des Énergies (AFP), 2026-06-15, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The paved N2 links Oyem to Libreville (about 373 km) and on to Bitam and the Cameroon border; there is no railway in the north. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Oyem airport, about 4 km south, has Afrijet flights to Libreville; the Port-Gentil route was last flown in October 2024. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Libreville–Oyem is one of the first interurban routes of the new state bus company CNT, launched after the September 2026 Council of Ministers. — Gabonreview, 2026-09-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The main public facility is the Centre Hospitalier Régional d'Oyem, locally the "hôpital canadien"; 2010s media reported drug shortages and frequent water and power failures there (old); a rehabilitation contract was signed in 2020 and a new CHU was under construction in 2025. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Oyem has the main secondary schools of the north (Lycée Léon M'ba, Lycée d'Excellence with a preparatory class, Lycée d'État Richard Nguema Bekale, Catholic and Protestant collèges); there is no university — those are in Libreville and Franceville. — Wikipedia "Oyem" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] No school in Oyem is on France's list of 11 approved schools in Gabon for 2025–26. — Gabonreview, 2025-07-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France urges particular vigilance in the north about the risk of crossing into Equatorial Guinea without authorisation; night driving outside the capital is strongly discouraged. — France Diplomatie Sécurité, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] A major centre of Fang culture, including the mvet harp-zither and its oral epics, and of the syncretic Bwiti religion; a Hausa minority settled in 1899. — Wikipedia "Oyem" (EN) / Wikipédia (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Seat of the Catholic diocese of Oyem, with Alliance chrétienne, Assemblies of God and Evangelical churches, mosques, and a small Jewish community. — Wikipedia "Oyem" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] At about 650 m on a plateau, nights are cooler than in Libreville (lows about 19–21 °C, highs 27–31 °C); rains roughly March–May and September–November, main dry season June–August, 1,700–2,000 mm a year. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population 60,685 at the 2013 census; detailed results of the delayed 2023–2026 census were not yet published. — Wikipedia "Oyem", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
