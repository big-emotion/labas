# Port-Gentil: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XAF** (fixed peg, from the dossier). `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gabon/port-gentil.md`; `[CL tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gabon/libreville.md` (national figures); `[D]` = the Gabon dossier `research/countries/gabon.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

**Read first: this sheet is thin.** The shared web-search budget ran out after the first Port-Gentil query, the Wikivoyage FR page is an empty outline, Expat.com's Port-Gentil property page showed only Libreville listings, and the only resident testimony is 8–14 years old [C]. No rent figure is tied to a named neighbourhood: every rent below is a city-wide average. Few venues have a name; most of those that do come from 2020 travel-wiki listings.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Island city: no road to Libreville; you fly (about 30 minutes) or take a boat (3–4 hours), and most food and goods arrive by boat [C daily.transport]. A 93 km road and bridge chain runs south to Omboué [1][3].
- Crime: Canada (2026-10-15) and Belgium (2026-06-25) say violent crime is more frequent in Libreville and Port-Gentil than in rural areas, with night burglaries sometimes by armed men [C safety.general]. France flags the beaches between Pointe Chapuy and Cap Lopez and the river banks, and strongly discourages boating offshore because of Gulf of Guinea piracy (2026-09-15); a French honorary consul is in town [C safety.general].
- Power: cuts of 6 to 24 hours across neighbourhoods in 2024 (Gabonreview, 2024-02-22 and 2024-09-11), weeks of unscheduled cuts in PG2 in December 2025, and a city-wide blackout on 15 June 2026 [C daily.power]. New turbines were on site in September 2026, due online within 40–45 days, with possible test cuts [C daily.power]. Residences usually have generators [C daily.power].
- Water: salt water moving up the Ogooué stopped pumping at Mandorové from 1 August to end-September 2026; residents were told to store water (Gabonreview, 2026-09-06) [C daily.water]. A new plant and water towers at N'Tchengue and the airport are planned from about January 2027 [C daily.water].
- Taxis: shared, no meter, from about 400 XAF (≈ €0.61), negotiated, roughly double at night or off paved roads; rush-hour jams at the Tobia and Léon Mba junctions [C daily.transport]. No ride-hailing app was found [C].
- Addresses: houses have no street numbers; people use PO boxes [C housing.areas].

## Neighbourhoods

### Centre-ville and the Quartier Ancien
- Feel: the harbour-side centre holds the banks, hotels, shops, supermarkets and the casino, between Avenue Savorgnan de Brazza and the seafront [1][3]; the Quartier Ancien keeps colonial wooden houses on stilts [1].
- Rent: 1-bed in the centre about 600,000 XAF/month (≈ €915); 3-bed about 1,216,667 (≈ €1,855) (Expat.com, 2025-12-04) [C cost.rent]. Studios 150,000–300,000 (≈ €229–457), Africarrières 2026-08-07, not tied to an area [C cost.rent]. Deposit of two or three months [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shops and the casino are within walking distance [1]; shared taxis from about 400 XAF [C daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the city-wide frames.
- Places:
  - Casino and Cecado supermarkets — Expat.com [1]
  - Grand Marché (fresh fish, produce, crafts, textiles; exact location not given) — Expat.com [1]
  - Casino de Port-Gentil (gaming tables, slots, bar) — Expat.com [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Cathédrale Saint-Louis, Rue du Révérend Père Bichet (1927 church framed by palms) — Wikivoyage EN [2]; Expat.com [1]
  - Planète Grill, Avenue de la Mosquée (ribs, pizza, burgers; 18:30–23:00, closed Sunday; 2020 listing) — Wikivoyage EN [2]
  - Grilled-seafood stalls along the Promenade du front de mer — Expat.com [1]
  - Clinique du Littoral, Boulevard du Gouverneur Pélieu (private clinic; district not stated) — Wikipedia [3]
  - Appart Hôtel Isis (large rooms, weekend buffet; 2020 listing, no price) — Wikivoyage EN [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e"; Q5 "J'entreprends" and "Télétravail" (with the internet caveat below); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Sogara
- Feel: the area expatriates prefer, with guarded, secure residences; named after the 1960s refinery (Expat.com, 2025-12-04) [1][C housing.areas]. An old forum post calls it a calm seaside area 10–15 minutes from the centre (≈2013, OLD) [9].
- Rent: no Sogara figure; city-wide 1-bed 250,000 (outside the centre) to 600,000 (centre) XAF, 3-bed 500,000–1,216,667 [C cost.rent]. Many expats are housed by their employer [D].
- Getting around: 10–15 minutes to the centre by car (≈2013, OLD) [9]; shared taxi from 400 XAF [C daily.transport].
- Watch: the refinery is next door [3][4]. The nearby coast falls under France's beach caution if it lies between Pointe Chapuy and Cap Lopez; the exact stretch was not confirmed [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Sogara beach, north of town (the town's most popular beach, calm water, grilled seafood nearby) — Expat.com [1]; Wikivoyage EN [2]
  - La Case (beach bar; listed without address, map point north-west of the Sogara beach pin; 2020) — Wikivoyage EN [2]
  - SOGARA refinery, the country's only one — Wikipédia [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (oil employers); Q4 "En couple" and "Avec mes enfants"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Sobraga and the Littoral
- Feel: the school and clinic side of town: the French-approved Collège/Lycée Victor Hugo is in the Sobraga area, and Clinique Mandji is on the route de la Sobraga in the Littoral district [1][3]. The windy western Atlantic coast is thinly settled, though some homes have ocean views [1][3].
- Rent: no area figure; city-wide ranges as above [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no sourced time; shared taxis [C daily.transport].
- Watch: strong winds and currents on the western coast [1][3].
- Places:
  - Collège/Lycée Victor Hugo (French curriculum, 6e to terminale; €3,420–4,395 a year per Expat.com, unverified) — Expat.com [1]; [C family.schools]
  - Clinique Mandji, route de la Sobraga — Wikipedia [3]; [C health.care]
  - IUT de Port-Gentil (since 2007; industrial maintenance, logistics, management; location not given) — Expat.com [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French school to the bac, clinic nearby); Q10 "La santé et l'école"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### N'Tchengue
- Feel: newer residential districts with varied housing; the outer parts are reached by sandy, potholed tracks [1][3][C housing.areas]. The more affordable side of the city by implication; no source says so directly.
- Rent: outside-the-centre averages apply: 1-bed about 250,000 XAF (≈ €381), 3-bed about 500,000 (≈ €762) (Expat.com, 2025-12-04) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: taxis roughly double off paved roads [C daily.transport].
- Watch: unpaved access in the rains (wet season October–May, about 2,000 mm a year) [C climate.daily]. A new water tower is planned here in the post-2027 programme [C daily.water].
- Places:
  - Hôpital de N'Tchengue — Expat.com [1]; [C health.care]
  - Centre du Traitement Ambulatoire, on the N'Tchengue road (outpatient clinic) — Wikipedia [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" on a budget. Not for anyone without a car in the rainy season.

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Centre-ville**, everything on foot [1]. Honest caveat: internet "varies by location" (Expat.com) [C daily.internet], fibre was not verified, WiMax providers Solsi and TLDC existed in older data [D], and power cuts are frequent [C daily.power]. A remote worker needs a generator or inverter and a mobile-data backup. An old forum post found it hard to keep busy without a job (≈2013, OLD) [C social.pace].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; groceries at Casino [1].
  - Tue: Cecado for imported goods [1].
  - Wed: gym (unnamed fitness centres; 37,500 XAF a month) [1][C cost.living].
  - Thu: dinner at Planète Grill, Avenue de la Mosquée [2].
  - Fri: seafood on the Promenade du front de mer [1].
  - Sat: Grand Marché in the morning [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Louis [2]; afternoon at Sogara beach [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no club was named in any source; the city is described as known for hotel bars and nightlife [3]; the Casino de Port-Gentil bar is the only named late venue [1].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Planète Grill (until 23:00, loud music at times) [2]; three-course dinner for two about 40,000 XAF [C cost.living].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": a cinema ticket costs about 2,560 XAF (cinema not named) [1], or home.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Sogara beach [1][2]; Cap Lopez, about 30 minutes from the centre, Gabon's westernmost point with its old lighthouse [1][6] (France flags this coast; go by day) [C safety.general].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no hills on Mandji island; Loango National Park (forest, lagoons) via Omboué and the 93 km road is a multi-day trip [1][7]. Park access and lodge prices not sourced.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Loango also has savanna [7]: realistic only as a 2–3 day trip via Omboué [1].
  - "Je reste en ville": golf course, zoo and tennis [1][3]; the annual Fête de la mer (date not found) [C social.culture].

  - Gym: 37,500 ÷ 4.33 = 8,661 [C cost.living]
  - 10 shared taxis × 400–800 (day to night) = 4,000–8,000 [C daily.transport]

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Grand Marché for the fish landing and produce [1]; walk the Quartier Ancien's colonial stilt houses [1]; Cathédrale Saint-Louis (1927) [2][1].
- Midday: grilled seafood on the Promenade du front de mer [1].
- Afternoon: shared or hired taxi to Cap Lopez, about 30 minutes (old steel lighthouse, oil terminal to the east) [1][6]; back by Sogara beach [1]. By day only on this coast [C safety.general]. Entry fees: none sourced.
- Evening: Planète Grill (from 18:30, closed Sunday) [2], then the Casino de Port-Gentil bar [1]. Taxi fares roughly double at night [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: boat from Libreville's Port Môle (Logimar 241 from 36,000 XAF, about 3 h 15; Sonaga about 35,000, about 4 h, tide-dependent; 2023) [8], or a 30-minute flight [C daily.transport].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: Cap Lopez again for humpback whales in season, watched from shore: France strongly discourages pleasure boating offshore because of piracy [1][C safety.general].
- Day 4, what daily life looks like: Grand Marché at opening [1]; a rush-hour taxi through the Tobia or Léon Mba junction [C daily.transport]; evening walk at Sogara beach where residents gather [1].
- Day 5–6: trip out of town by the 93 km road and bridges to Omboué, on the Fernan-Vaz lagoon [1][11], the gateway to Loango National Park (savanna, beaches, lagoons; elephants, hippos, gorillas) [1][7]. Transport, park entry and lodge prices were not sourced: book through a lodge or operator.
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Louis [2]; afternoon at the golf course [1] or a match at the Stade de Port-Gentil (20,000 seats; fixtures not sourced) [5]; return by boat or plane.
