- [daily.transport] A Transgabonais modernisation deal signed on 24 November 2025 plans to replace 270 km of track and upgrade the Franceville station. — Wikipédia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Train Libreville–Franceville 15,000–35,000 XAF (≈ €23–53); domestic flight 80,000–200,000 XAF (≈ €122–305). — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] M'Vengue airport (about 16 km west) has flights to Libreville by Afrijet and FlyGabon; the RN3 road links to Libreville (about 828 km by the Transgabonaise corridor) with sections under repair; taxis in town, bush taxis to Moanda, Mounana and Okondja. — Wikipédia "Franceville" / Wikipedia (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] In 2023 the state bus company Sogatra had only 38 working buses for Libreville, Port-Gentil and Franceville combined (old, before the CNT merger). — Le360 Afrique, 2023-10-15, intl-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] The Amissa Bongo regional hospital (CHR) is the main public facility; there is also a Sino-Gabonese friendship hospital. — Wikipédia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Sino-Gabonese general hospital, built with Chinese support, has CT and MRI scanners and takes complex cases, according to an expat guide. — Expat.com Guide Gabon (Franceville section), 2025-11-06, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] The CIRMF biomedical research centre has a biosafety-level-4 laboratory and is a national reference lab, known for Ebola research. — Wikipedia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] A conventioned public primary school in Franceville is on France's list of approved schools for 2025–26; the approved Lycée Henri-Sylvoz and COMILOG primary school are in nearby Moanda. — Gabonreview, 2025-07-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Collège Saint-Exupéry follows the French curriculum from primary to collège; fees not given. — Expat.com Guide Gabon (Franceville section), 2025-11-06, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] The USTM (Université des Sciences et Techniques de Masuku, founded 1986) is the country's main science university, with a polytechnic school and an agronomy institute. — Wikipédia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France lists the Poto-Poto market as an area needing extra vigilance and advises against driving at night outside the capital; there is a French honorary consul in Franceville. — France Diplomatie Sécurité, 2026-09-15 (zones 2026-03-05), official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Bushmeat (monkeys, pythons) is sold at Franceville's markets, which the guide calls controversial. — Expat.com Guide Gabon (Franceville section), 2025-11-06, company, confidence: low
- [social.sport] The 22,000-seat stadium hosted Africa Cup of Nations matches in 2012 and 2017. — Wikipedia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mostly Christian (Catholic diocese, Protestant churches, Assemblies of God), with a Muslim minority and mosques. — Wikipédia "Franceville" / Wikipedia (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The main local groups are Obamba and Batéké; the Obamba name for the town is Masuku; landmarks include the brick Saint-Hilaire church (1899) and the Grand Marché. — Wikipédia "Franceville" / Wikipedia (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population was 110,568 at the 2013 census; French Wikipedia gives a 2025 estimate of 294,864 — a large gap, so treat the estimate with caution. — Wikipédia "Franceville", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Subequatorial: rainy season roughly September–May with frequent storms, a cooler dry season June–August; altitude makes the heat a little milder than on the coast; about 1,860 mm of rain a year. — Wikipedia "Franceville" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
