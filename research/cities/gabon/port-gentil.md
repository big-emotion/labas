# Port-Gentil, Gabon

## Items

- [cost.rent] 1-bedroom in the centre about 600,000 XAF/month (≈ €915); outside the centre about 250,000 XAF (≈ €381). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, updated 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] 3-bedroom in the centre about 1,216,667 XAF/month (≈ €1,855); outside the centre about 500,000 XAF (≈ €762). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] The same guide says rents are about 20 % below Libreville (its own centre 1-bed figure, 600,000 XAF, is higher than the 543,000 XAF it gives for Libreville). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Studio 150,000–300,000 XAF/month (≈ €229–457). — Africarrières "Coût de la vie" Gabon, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Landlords ask for a deposit of two or three months' rent. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Single person about 375,000 XAF/month (≈ €572) without rent, 975,000 XAF (≈ €1,486) with a central 1-bed. Family of four about 1,321,667 XAF (≈ €2,015) without rent, including school fees for two children. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Cheap restaurant meal 10,000 XAF (≈ €15); three-course dinner for two 40,000 (≈ €61); local beer 1,300 (≈ €1.98); cappuccino 5,000 (≈ €7.62). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Groceries: milk 1,708 XAF/litre (≈ €2.60), bread 775 XAF/lb (≈ €1.18), a dozen eggs 3,100 (≈ €4.73), chicken 2,268 XAF/lb (≈ €3.46). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Gym membership 37,500 XAF/month (≈ €57); cinema ticket 2,560 XAF (≈ €3.90). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Old forum posts: imported supermarket goods cost several times French prices while local fruit, vegetables and fish are cheap; electricity and water were called very expensive (about 2012–2013, old). — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Port-Gentil", ≈2012–2013, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] National average net salary 253,333 XAF/month (≈ €386) in 2025 — a national figure, not specific to Port-Gentil. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Sogara is the area expats prefer, with guarded, secure residences; N'Tchengue has varied housing but sandy roads on the outskirts. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The centre by the harbour has the banks, hotels, shops and supermarkets; newer districts in N'Tchengue have sandy, potholed tracks; the windy Atlantic coast to the west is thinly settled. — Wikipedia "Port-Gentil", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Houses have no street numbers, so people use PO boxes. — Wikipedia "Port-Gentil", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] An old forum post describes Sogara as a calm seaside area 10–15 minutes from the centre (about 2013, old). — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Port-Gentil", ≈2013, forum, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Oil and gas employ about 7,000 people directly (Total, Perenco, BW Energy, Maurel & Prom, Petronas); construction grew 48 % in 2024; unemployment nationally 20.3 % (2023). — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The city generates about three-quarters of national wealth and has Gabon's only refinery (SOGARA) and main naval base; timber is the other big industry. — Wikipédia "Port-Gentil" / Wikipedia (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Old forum posts: work permits are limited, most jobs go to locals or the oil sector, spouses of expats found it hard, and job hunting worked by word of mouth on site, not remotely (about 2013–2014, old). — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Port-Gentil", ≈2013–2014, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.power] In February 2024 residents marched at night against cuts lasting 9, 10, 12 or even 24 hours across all neighbourhoods; security forces dispersed them. — Gabonreview, 2024-02-22, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] In September 2024 cuts hit 13 neighbourhoods (Badamier, Matanda, Grand-Village, Bornave and others); a resident said each area could go 6 hours without power; families used candles and storm lamps, wealthier ones generators or solar. — Gabonreview, 2024-09-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] In December 2025 residents of PG2 complained of weeks of unscheduled cuts, blamed on a transformer overloaded by over 7,000 households, with lost appliances and spoiled food. — AllAfrica / Gabonews, 2025-12-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] The city's plant had about 25–30 MW from two gas turbines in service since 1977; two new 25 MW turbines were ordered in February 2026. — Ecofin Agency, 2026-02-05, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] A major incident at the Cap Lopez thermal plant cut power to the whole city on 15 June 2026, slowing offices, shops and businesses. — Gabonreview, 2026-06-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] In September 2026 the new turbines were on site, due online within 40–45 days with possible cuts during testing; peak demand is estimated at 45–48 MW. — Gabonreview, 2026-09-09, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] Expat guide: power and water cuts happen and generators are common in residences. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] SEEG warned of drinking-water disruption from 1 August to end-September 2026 because salt water moves up the Ogooué at low water and stops pumping at the Mandorové station; residents were told to store water. — Gabonreview, 2026-09-06, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Demand is estimated at 35,000 m³/day; a new plant for 50,000 m³/day plus water towers at N'Tchengue and the airport is planned, with works from January 2027 for about 24 months, budget 118 billion XAF. — Infos Gabon, 2026-08-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] The water minister presented the same programme at about 100 billion XAF, with 120 km of new network for about 90 % coverage and first works possibly late 2026 or early 2027 (differs from the 118 billion figure). — Gabonreview, 2026-09-09, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Internet is available, but speed and reliability vary by location. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] There is no road to Libreville: travel is by air or sea, and most food and goods come by boat from Libreville. — Wikipédia "Port-Gentil", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] The flight to Libreville takes 30 minutes; the ferry takes 3–4 hours. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Libreville Wikipedia article puts the fast boat to Port-Gentil at about two and a half hours (differs from the 3–4 hours above). — Wikipédia "Libreville", modified 2026-09-25, academic, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Domestic flights cost 80,000–200,000 XAF (≈ €122–305). The same page lists a Libreville–Port-Gentil "bus" at 8,000–12,000 XAF, which does not fit the absence of a road. — Africarrières, 2026-08-07, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Shared taxis have no meter, start around 400 XAF (≈ €0.61), are negotiated and roughly double at night or off paved roads; jams at the Tobia and Léon Mba junctions at rush hour; a 93 km road runs south to Omboué. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04 / Wikipedia (EN), company + academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Most cars are old and spare parts are imported, slow and expensive; many expats get a company car. — Wikipedia (EN) accessed 2026-11-14 / Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04, academic + company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Facilities named: N'Tchengue hospital and the Total Gabon clinic, plus international clinics. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Clinique Mandji (route de la Sobraga), Clinique du Littoral (boulevard du Gouverneur Pélieu) and an outpatient treatment centre on the N'Tchengue road. — Wikipedia "Port-Gentil", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] An old forum post found good GPs and specialists in town (about 2013, old). — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Port-Gentil", ≈2013, forum, confidence: low
- [family.schools] French-approved schools for 2025–26: the conventioned public school of Port-Gentil, École Léopold (Sédar Senghor) and Lycée Victor-Hugo. — Gabonreview, 2025-07-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Collège/Lycée Victor Hugo (Sobraga area, 6e to terminale) charges €3,420–4,395 a year (≈ 2.24–2.88 million XAF); the Gabon-wide guide attributes the same range to a school in Moanda, so check directly. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04 / Expat.com Guide Gabon 2025-11-06, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Canada: violent crime is more frequent in Libreville and Port-Gentil than in rural areas. — Gouvernement du Canada, 2026-10-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Belgium: urban crime has risen in recent years, especially in Libreville and Port-Gentil; night burglaries sometimes by armed men. — SPF Affaires étrangères Belgique, 2026-06-25, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France urges caution on beaches between Pointe Chapuy and Cap Lopez and on river banks, and strongly discourages boating offshore because of Gulf of Guinea piracy; there is a French honorary consul in Port-Gentil. — France Diplomatie Sécurité, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] The city is known for its hotels and nightlife; there is a casino with gaming tables, slot machines and a bar. — Wikipedia (EN) accessed 2026-11-14 / Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04, academic + company, confidence: low
- [social.sport] Golf course, tennis, fitness centres; some oil companies have their own gyms and pools. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [social.food] French, Lebanese, Chinese and international restaurants, seafood grills on the seafront, Casino and Cecado supermarkets and weekly markets for fresh fish. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] An annual sea festival (Fête de la mer); landmarks include St Louis church (1927) and a zoo; musicians Lord Ekomy Ndong and Tiss Warren Jazz are linked to the city. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04 / Wikipedia EN & FR, company + academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mainly Christian (Catholic diocese, Assemblies of God, Evangelical Church of Gabon, Alliance), with mosques too. — Wikipedia "Port-Gentil" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Old forum posts describe a very quiet pace, hard to keep busy without a job or children, beach as the main pastime (about 2013, old). — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Port-Gentil", ≈2013, forum, confidence: low
- [community.expat] Between 5,000 and 7,000 expatriates, mainly French, Lebanese, Chinese, Senegalese and Beninese; Expat.com's local forum has 554 members and 33 threads. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] A November 2026 forum thread asking about life in Port-Gentil had no replies, and the other local threads date from 2012–2018. — Expat.com Forum Gabon and Port-Gentil indexes, accessed 2026-11-14, forum, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] Old forum posts say making friends was easy because of the many expats, and warn that a spouse needs work authorisation to get a residence card (about 2017, old). — Expat.com forum "Vie à Port-Gentil", ≈2017, forum, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Wet season October to May, about 2,000 mm of rain a year, humidity about 84 %; averages about 23.5 °C in July and 27 °C in February–March; record 38 °C (Feb 2016). Air conditioning is common. — Wikipedia (EN) accessed 2026-11-14 / Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil 2025-12-04, academic + company, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Sogara beach in town, Cap Lopez about 30 minutes from the centre, and Loango National Park reachable via Omboué. — Expat.com Guide Port-Gentil, 2025-12-04, company, confidence: medium
- [other] The city is a historic opposition stronghold: riots followed an opposition leader's death in 1990, and clashes after the 2009 election left three dead officially. — Wikipédia "Port-Gentil", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
