# Serrekunda: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 82.3 GMD** (dossier rate, FxFeed, 14 Nov 2026); the Central Bank valuation the same day was 84.87, so EUR figures run about 3% high. USD figures use the city file's CBG cross-rate (1 USD ≈ 0.869 EUR); GBP left in GBP. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gambie/serrekunda.md`; `[B tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gambie/banjul.md`; `[Br tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gambie/brufut.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/gambie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Scope: "Serrekunda" here means the Kanifing urban area plus the coastal strip (Kololi, Kotu, Fajara, Bakau), as in the city file [C housing.areas]. Bijilo/Brusubi/Kerr Serign are just south, in West Coast Region, and are included because guides send families there [4].

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- FCDO (updated 2026-11-11): petty theft in cities and tourist areas, especially at night, on isolated beaches and in markets; passports stolen from hotel rooms; bribe requests "at all levels" [C safety.general][D].
- Presidential election on Saturday 5 Dec 2026, campaign 16 Nov–3 Dec; FCDO expects large gatherings; since 10 Sept 2026 police require a permit for any protest or gathering [D][C safety.general].
- Power: protests over cuts that began in June 2026 broke out on 7 Sept 2026; prepaid "cash power" meters are standard [C daily.power].
- Tap water is not drinkable [C daily.water]. Rainy season June–October, flooding peaks in August [D].
- Rent practice: deposit 1–2 months plus the first month, 6–12-month leases (Expat.com) [C housing.search]; landlords often still ask six months to a year upfront despite a Rent Act amendment (Gambiana editorial 2025-01-01; Jarnias 2026-05-01) [C cost.negotiation]. Agents may charge a non-refundable viewing "movement fee" of about $500 (≈ €435) [C cost.negotiation].
- LGBT: same-sex relations illegal, minimum penalty raised to five years in Sept 2024 [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Serrekunda centre (Sayerr Jobe Avenue, London Corner, Dippakunda, Bundung)
- Feel: the country's largest and densest commercial hub; noise and activity are high, few expats live here and most only come for errands (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. Sayerr Jobe Avenue, from Westfield to London Corner, is a heavy-traffic shopping street with expensive property [5][C housing.areas]. Wolof is the street language; signage is in English [C social.culture].
- Rent: 10,000–25,000 GMD a month for decent housing (≈ €122–304), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; a centre 1-bed about $300 (≈ €261), Jarnias 2026-04-29 [4]; large inland houses £400–650 (Jarnias 2026-04-28) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Westfield Junction sits at the start of the four-lane highway to Banjul, about 18 km [5][C housing.areas]; shared taxi Banjul–Serrekunda 10 → 12 GMD after an undated rise (≈ €0.15) [C daily.transport]; gelly-gelly 7–12 GMD, shared yellow taxi 7–10, private taxi 50–100 by day and 150–250 at night [C daily.transport]. Minibuses upcountry leave from Bundung garage when full [21].
- Watch: petty crime is most common in Kololi, Serrekunda and the Banjul market (Expat.com, 2025-11-03), consistent with FCDO's market warning [C safety.general]. Rainy-season floods cut customers at the market [C climate.daily]. The Bakoteh open dump, on a main road into Serrekunda, is burned in the open [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Serrekunda Market, "Sandika" (the country's largest; vegetables, fish, batik; electronics repair beside it) — Wikipedia [5]; [C social.culture]
  - Serekunda Mosque, Sayerr Jobe Avenue (oldest mosque in the city) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Markaz, Bundung (Tablighi Jamaat centre, mosque for about 2,000; public gatherings on Thursdays; women barred from regular services since 2003) — Wikipedia [5]; [C social.religion]
  - Trinity Methodist Church and Saint Therese's Catholic Church (quarter not stated) — Wikipedia [5]
  - B.O. Semega Janneh arena, Dippakunda (wrestling) and Serrekunda West Mini Stadium (wrestling, football) — Wikipedia [5]; [C social.sport]
  - Serekunda Health Centre (serves about 123,000 people) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Sarawally Guest House, F Section Ampaya near Kairaba Road (rooms from D750 with breakfast, listing Aug 2020) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (the market and Kanifing's businesses); Q8 "Je reste en ville". Not for Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Kanifing inner quarters (Latrikunda, Tallinding, Ebo Town, Kanifing South)
- Feel: the former villages that merged into Serrekunda (Latrikunda) and the dense residential belt around it [5]; residents of Ebo Town and Bakoteh joined the Sept 2026 power-cut protests [C daily.power]. No dated description of who lives here beyond that was found.
- Rent: local-market asking, public Facebook group "Buying and selling in Gambia", 2026-11-14: a 3-bedroom, 2-toilet house in Latrikunda at D10,000 a month (≈ €122) with six months upfront [C cost.rent]. This is a local-standard market, below the Expat.com ranges.
- Getting around: gelly-gelly and shared taxis at 7–12 GMD a ride [C daily.transport]; Westfield Junction for Banjul [5].
- Watch: Ebo Town flooded on 30 Aug 2025; residents blamed a concrete road finished in May 2025 without drainage to the Badala stream (The Voice, 2025-09-01) [18]. In the July 2022 flash floods, Kanifing Municipal Council was the most affected area, with 1,657 households (NDMA via The Point, 2022-08-26) [19].
- Places:

  - YMCA Hostel & Backpackers, MDI Road, Kanifing South (34 rooms, 400 GMD, listing Aug 2020) — Wikivoyage [1][2]
  - Startup Incubator Gambia, MSTE Building, Kanifing Institutional Layout (coworking cubicles, 6-month incubation) — official site [20]; [D]
  - Kanifing General Hospital (public referral, 114 beds, trauma centre) — Wikipedia [5]; [C health.care]
  - Sharab Medical Centre, Kanifing (24/7) — Jarnias healthcare guide [C health.care]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "J'entreprends" (incubator next door); Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" only for readers used to local-standard housing.

### Kololi and the Senegambia Strip
- Feel: "the beating heart of tourist and expat life", commercial and festive, high noise at night on the Strip; European winter retirees, tourism workers, entrepreneurs and international staff (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. Tourist police watch the Strip entrance; visitors are mainly British, Belgian, German and Dutch [8][C social.nightlife]. Residents criticise sex tourism around the Strip [5][C social.nightlife].
- Rent: studios from 20,000 GMD (≈ €243), large flats up to 50,000 (≈ €608), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; 2-bed $250–600 (≈ €217–521), Jarnias 2026-05-01 [C cost.rent]; 1-bed about $350 (≈ €304), Jarnias 2026-04-29 [4].
- Getting around: airport to Kololi 800–1,000 GMD by taxi [C daily.transport]; green tourist taxis 150–250 GMD for short coastal trips, and yellow taxis may not pick up in some tourist zones [C cost.negotiation]. Parking on the Strip is described as a "nightmare" (directory listing) [15].
- Watch: guides name Kololi for bumster pressure and phone or bag theft at night [4]; FCDO's beach and night warnings apply [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Senegambia Strip (restaurants, music venues) — Wikipedia [5][8]
  - Kololi Beach — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Bijilo Forest Park, just south of Kololi (51 ha, 4.5 km trail, monkeys, 133+ bird species; entry D300 as of Dec 2025) — Wikipedia [9]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Maroun's supermarket (imported goods) — Jarnias [4]; AccessGambia, c. 2011 [17]
  - Yasmina Resto Bar, at the Strip entrance by the Senegambia roundabout (daily 9:00 until late; dishes D125–800; live band Saturdays 19:00, listing dated 2024-12-15) — My Gambia [12]
  - Duplex nightclub, Seaview Boulevard (Thu–Sun 20:30–06:00; page © 2009, still used as a landmark in Aug 2025) — AccessGambia [16]; My Gambia [10]
  - Fitness Garage, Bakadaji Hotel (gym, boxing, Mon–Sat); Phenomenal Women, Hendon Court Building (women-only fitness) — My Gambia, Aug 2025 [10]
  - SOS Clinic, Kololi; Africmed, Kombo Coastal Highway — Expat.com / Jarnias [C health.care]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (short lets). Not for Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Kotu
- Feel: laid-back, less crowded than Senegambia, wide beach, fewer touts, much calmer nightlife; couples, retirees, families; often suggested for a first stay (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. Expat.com calls it family-friendly [D].
- Rent: 25,000–50,000 GMD (≈ €304–608), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; 1-bed $200–500 (≈ €174–435), Jarnias 2026-05-01 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: close to Kololi; minibus and taxi links to Serrekunda [4]; same fares as above [C daily.transport].
- Watch: President Barrow planned to inspect NAWEC's Kotu facilities during the 2026 power crisis [C daily.power]; nothing else area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Kotu Beach — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Kotu Stream and Kotu Ponds (birdwatching, bike path) — Jarnias [4]; Wikivoyage Banjul "Go next" [2]
  - British International School The Gambia, Kotu — Jarnias schools guide [C family.schools]
  - AFB Workspace, BB Highway after Kotu Fire Service (day coworking, meeting room for 12; prices not given; article June 2022) — My Gambia [14]
  - Latrikunda market (nearby) — Jarnias [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q4 "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage".

### Fajara and Kairaba Avenue
- Feel: upscale and calm; diplomats, families, NGO staff, retirees; social life around clubs, cafés and restaurants (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. Expat.com calls it the most sought-after area [D]. Embassies sit here: UK High Commission at 48 Atlantic Road, US Embassy at 92 Kairaba Avenue [2].
- Rent: 40,000–80,000 GMD for a 3–4-bed house (≈ €486–972), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; Jarnias gives a 1-bed at $150–300 (2026-05-01) and $400–1,000 (2026-04-29) on two pages, so the low figure is doubtful [C cost.rent][4].
- Getting around: quick taxi ride from Kololi [4]; Kairaba Avenue runs inland toward Serrekunda [5]; Banjul by the highway via Westfield [5].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced beyond the frames above.
- Places:

  - Banjul American International School, Atlantic Road (elementary about $13,400–13,700 a year) — Jarnias schools guide [C family.schools]
  - Pipeline Mosque, Kairaba Avenue (3,000 worshippers, 33 m minaret, opened 1990) — Wikipedia [5]
  - Elite Clinic, Kairaba Avenue; Fajara Medical Centre — Jarnias [4]

### Bakau and Cape Point
- Feel: the old village around the crocodile pool grew into a working town with a lively market and fishing beach; it hosts the UN country office and several embassies; tourism, fishing and gardening are the main activities (Wikipedia; Jarnias 2026-04-29) [6][4][C housing.areas]. Cape Point is "relaxed and chic" with waterfront hotels and less mass tourism than Kololi [4].
- Rent: Cape Point sea-view property from 60,000 GMD (≈ €729), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; large inland houses in Serrekunda or Bakau £400–650, Jarnias 2026-04-28 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared taxi Serrekunda–Bakau 7 → 8 GMD, Banjul–Bakau 10 → 12 (undated rise) [C daily.transport][B daily.transport].
- Watch: a guide advises vigilance at night in Bakau/Cape Point (Jarnias, low) [C housing.areas]; FCDO's beach-at-night warning applies [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Kachikally Crocodile Pool and museum, about 10 blocks inland from the craft market (D200, 04/2026) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [6]
  - Botanical Gardens, Atlantic Boulevard — Wikipedia [6]
  - Bakau fruit-and-vegetable market on the main road; fish market by the town beach — Wikipedia [6]
  - Independence Stadium (the only national stadium) — Wikipedia [6][5]
  - Marina International School, Bakau Newtown (British curriculum) — Jarnias / Wikipedia [C family.schools][6]
  - École Française de Banjul (preschool to première; listed on Atlantic Road by Wikipedia and Kairaba Avenue by Jarnias) — [D][C family.schools]
  - Gibbi's on the Beach, Cape Point beach (seafood) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Tanbi Wetland Complex, Old Cape Road (mangroves) — Wikipedia [6]

### Bijilo, Brusubi and Kerr Serign (coastal belt south of Kololi)
- Feel: Bijilo is "residential beachside", quieter, with expat families and young couples working in Banjul or for NGOs; Brusubi is a fast-growing new town of offices, markets and housing for the Gambian middle class and expats; Kerr Serign mixes traditional houses and new villas, for expats who want everyday Gambian life on a tighter budget (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. Brufut has its own file [Br].
- Rent: Brusubi standard 3-bed D12,500 a month (≈ €152), six months upfront, public Facebook group 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; 3–4-bed compound in the Brufut belt 30,000–60,000 GMD (≈ €365–729), Expat.com [Br cost.rent]; Bijilo 2-bed about $12,000 a year (≈ $1,000 a month, ≈ €869), Jarnias 2026-04-29 [4]; Kerr Serign houses £400–650 (Jarnias) [4].
- Getting around: a car is recommended for the belt [Br daily.transport]; Brusubi is about ten minutes from Kololi; Bijilo a few minutes by car [4]; access via the coastal highway from the Brusubi turntable [Br daily.transport].
- Watch: a guide notes theft on construction sites and rising residential burglaries in Greater Banjul (Jarnias, low; no official source found) [Br safety.general].
- Places:
  - SBEC International School (Bijilo per Jarnias, Brufut per Expat.com) — [Br family.schools][4]
  - Gateway Learning Academy, Brusubi Phase 1 (Cambridge) — Jarnias [Br family.schools]
  - Africmed clinic, Kombo Coastal Highway (Brusubi) — Jarnias [Br health.care]
  - Malkey's Yoga Fitness Club, Bijilo — My Gambia, Aug 2025 [10]

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"

Internet is the risk: outages "that can last several days" (Jarnias) [C work.remote]; expats needing stability pay $75–100 a month across two providers [C daily.internet]. No digital-nomad visa [C work.remote].
- Week:

  - Wed: home office; birdwatching along Kotu Stream at dusk [4].
  - Thu: gelly-gelly to Serrekunda Market for fish and vegetables (7–12 GMD) [C daily.transport][5].
  - Fri: home office; Jumu'ah at Pipeline Mosque, Kairaba Avenue, for Muslim readers [5].
  - Sat: Bijilo Forest Park trail (D300) [1][9]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: gym (Fitness Garage, Kololi) [10]; rest.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Duplex, Seaview Boulevard, Thu–Sun to 06:00 [16]; Aquarius, open to the early hours (location not stated) [15]; Jazz Cafe on the Strip (cheapest bottled beer on the Strip) [15].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Timing: avoid landing during the campaign (16 Nov–3 Dec) and the 5 Dec 2026 vote if you want a calm first week [D]. Entry: FCDO gives British nationals 28 days on arrival, extendable twice at the Immigration Office in Banjul or tourist police stations in the Tourism Development Area; French/EU rules not confirmed [C admin.visa][D]. Visit fees: an agent "movement fee" of about $500 is reported, plus commission up to one month [C cost.negotiation]. View in person; forum users warn against renting from abroad [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: compare a Latrikunda/Brusubi local-standard house (D10,000–12,500, six months upfront) with a Kotu flat (25,000–50,000) [C cost.rent]. Check the cash-power meter, a water source and whether Wi-Fi is included [C daily.power][C daily.internet].

- Day 6: Serrekunda Market morning [5]; evening on the Strip [8].
- Day 7: Jumu'ah was Friday; Sunday Mass at Saint Therese's [5]; Kotu Beach [1]. No returnee or diaspora group was found; the Expat.com forum is mostly inactive [C community.expat].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Dry season roughly November–May; rain June–October [D]. Avoid the election weeks of late Nov–early Dec 2026 for gatherings [D].

### One day
- Morning: Serrekunda Market (Sandika) early, bag closed (FCDO market warning) [5][C safety.general]; then a taxi to Bijilo Forest Park (D300, 4.5 km trail; staff may push a paid guide) [1][9].
- Midday: lunch on the Senegambia Strip, e.g. Yasmina (dishes D300–800) [12].
- Afternoon: Kotu Beach and Kotu Stream for birds [1][4]; or a shared taxi to Bakau (8 GMD) for Kachikally Crocodile Pool (D200) and the fish market at the town beach [C daily.transport][1][6].
- Evening: Solomon's beach bar in Kololi for sunset and live music [15]; dinner at Ali Baba's [15]; late at Duplex (Thu–Sun) [16].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive (airport to Kololi 800–1,000 GMD) [C daily.transport]; evening on the Strip [8].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): rush hour at Westfield Junction, where thousands commute daily to Banjul [C daily.transport][5]; ride the shared taxi to Banjul (12 GMD) for Albert Market, Arch 22 (D200) and the National Museum (D200) [B daily.transport][2].
- Day 4 (trip out): Abuko Nature Reserve (monkeys, crocodiles, 270+ birds) [25], then Makasutu Culture Forest near Brikama (a Banjul taxi with waiting is quoted at D1,300, undated) [22] and the Brikama Woodcarvers' Market [22].
- Day 5 (trip out): Banjul–Barra ferry (pedestrians 25–100 GMD; FCDO calls it often overcrowded) to Juffureh: free Slave Museum, view or boat to Kunta Kinteh Island (UNESCO, 2003) [B daily.transport][B safety.general][24][26].
- Day 6: Tanji fishing village (boats return about 17:00; fish smoking; green taxi D1,000 or yellow 250 from the resorts) [23]; Sanyang Beach on the way back [3].
- Day 7 (daily-life moment): Friday Jumu'ah at the Pipeline Mosque seen from outside, or Sunday Mass at Saint Therese's [5]; a wrestling bout at the B.O. Semega Janneh arena if one is on [5].
