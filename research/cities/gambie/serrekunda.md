> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 82.3 GMD** (FxFeed market aggregator, 14 Nov 2026), rounded. The Central Bank of The Gambia's own daily valuation the same day was 84.87 GMD/EUR (see daily.money), so EUR figures here run about 3% high. USD figures are converted at the CBG cross-rate of the same day (1 USD ≈ 0.869 EUR). GBP figures are left in GBP. Scope: the Kanifing urban area (Serrekunda proper, Kololi/Senegambia, Kotu, Fajara, Bakau/Cape Point).
