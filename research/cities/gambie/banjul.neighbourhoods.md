# Banjul: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 82.3 GMD** (dossier rate, FxFeed, 14 Nov 2026); the Central Bank valuation the same day was 84.87, so EUR figures run about 3% high. USD figures use the CBG cross-rate (1 USD ≈ 0.869 EUR); GBP left in GBP. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gambie/banjul.md`; `[S tag]` = an item in `research/cities/gambie/serrekunda.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/gambie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52. The companion file `serrekunda.neighbourhoods.md` covers the coastal neighbourhoods in more depth.

Why this file reaches beyond the island: Banjul city proper had 26,461 people in 2024 and is shrinking; growth is capped by the island site, so residents and businesses moved to Serrekunda, about 18 km away, and thousands commute in daily [C housing.areas]. A guide says some expats live on the island for work but most foreigners commute from the coast (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]. The neighbourhoods below are therefore the island plus the three mainland areas a Banjul worker actually chooses between.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- FCDO (updated 2026-11-11): petty theft in cities, tourist areas and markets; bribe requests "at all levels"; the Banjul–Barra ferry is often overcrowded and lacks life-saving equipment [C safety.general][D].
- Presidential election on Saturday 5 Dec 2026, campaign 16 Nov–3 Dec; since 10 Sept 2026 police require a permit for all protests and gatherings, three days after Banjul's power-cut protests [C safety.general][C daily.power].
- Rainy season June–October, wettest in August [C climate.daily]. Tap water is not drinkable [S daily.water].
- Rent practice: landlords often want six months to a year upfront plus a deposit, although a Rent Act amendment is said to cap advances at one month (Jarnias, low) [C cost.negotiation].
- LGBT: same-sex relations illegal, "zero tolerance" (FCDO) [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Banjul island (colonial core, Half Die, Banjul North)
- Feel: a small administrative capital on St Mary's Island: ministries, the Central Bank, the port, the national hospital [C housing.areas][D]. Wikivoyage says the city "becomes very quiet at night" and winds down in the evenings and on weekends [2]. Older colonial buildings make up much of the rental stock [C cost.rent].
- Rent: basic apartments 15,000–25,000 GMD a month (≈ €182–304), mostly in older colonial buildings, Expat.com 2025-11-03 [C cost.rent]; a modest 2-bed in greater Banjul 7,000–15,000 (≈ €85–182), Jarnias 2026-05-01, low [C cost.rent]; a centre 1-bed about $300 (≈ €261), Jarnias 2026-04-29 [4].
- Getting around: on foot inside the island; shared taxi within Banjul 7 → 8 GMD after an undated rise [C daily.transport]; the Denton Bridge highway is the only road link to Serrekunda [C daily.transport]; airport to central Banjul 1,000–1,400 GMD by taxi [C daily.transport].
- Watch:
  - Flooding: on 5 Aug 2026 a near-24-hour downpour, the heaviest since 2022, flooded homes, businesses and roads in low-lying Banjul North (The Point, 2026-08-07) [12]. Half Die is named as a low-lying community vulnerable to flooding; land in Banjul sits below sea level and drains through the Bond Polder and lift pumps, whose capacity is reduced by encroachment and sand (The Point, 2026-08-11) [13]. In the July 2022 floods, Banjul was described as particularly affected (NDMA via The Point, 2022-08-26) [14]. The island sits about 2 m above sea level overall [C climate.daily].
  - Power: protests over outages that began in June broke out in Banjul on 7 Sept 2026 [C daily.power].
  - Crime: pickpocketing and bag snatching at Albert Market, ferry terminals and transport hubs (Wikivoyage) [2], consistent with FCDO's market warning [C safety.general]; a second-hand claim of a US "critical" crime rating for some residential areas is undated and unconfirmed [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Albert Market, Liberation Avenue (mid-19th-century market) — Wikipedia [5]; Wikivoyage [2]
  - King Fahad Mosque (largest in Banjul) and Banjul Central Mosque — Wikipedia [6]; [C social.religion]
  - Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption (Catholic Diocese of Banjul) — Wikipedia [7]
  - Arch 22, where Box Bar Road, Independence Drive and Marina Parade meet (35 m, textile museum, view over the port and the Tanbi mangroves; entry D200) — Wikipedia [8]; Wikivoyage [2]
  - Gambia National Museum, Independence Drive (D200; Mon–Thu 9:00–18:00, Fri–Sun 9:00–17:00; listing 04/2026) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Edward Francis Small Teaching Hospital (national referral, 540 beds) — Wikipedia via city file [C health.care]
  - UTG School of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences — Wikipedia via city file [C family.schools]
  - Laico Atlantic Hotel, Marina Parade (pool, private beach; listing Dec 2016) — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (walk to ministries, bank, port, hospital); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Moyen". Not for Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" or for anyone who cannot handle the rainy-season floods.

### Bakau and Cape Point (nearest mainland town)
- Feel: a working town grown from the village around the crocodile pool, with a lively market and fishing beach; the UN country office and several embassies are here; Cape Point is "relaxed and chic" with waterfront hotels (Wikipedia; Jarnias 2026-04-29) [9][4]. The 2024 census figure was not found; 43,098 in 2013 [S housing.areas].
- Rent: Cape Point sea-view from 60,000 GMD (≈ €729), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [S cost.rent]; large inland houses in Serrekunda or Bakau £400–650, Jarnias 2026-04-28 [S cost.rent]. No Bakau flat rent found.
- Getting around: shared taxi Banjul–Bakau 10 → 12 GMD (undated rise) [C daily.transport]; the Old Cape Road runs along the Tanbi wetland toward Banjul [9].
- Watch: a guide advises vigilance at night in Bakau/Cape Point (Jarnias, low) [S housing.areas]; FCDO's isolated-beach-at-night warning [S safety.general].
- Places:
  - Kachikally Crocodile Pool and museum (D200, 04/2026) — Wikivoyage [3]; Wikipedia [9]
  - Botanical Gardens, Atlantic Boulevard — Wikipedia [9]
  - Bakau fruit-and-vegetable market and fish market by the town beach — Wikipedia [9]
  - Independence Stadium (the only national stadium) — Wikipedia [9]
  - Marina International School, Bakau Newtown (British curriculum) — [S family.schools][9]
  - École Française de Banjul, Bakau (preschool to première, CNED from sixième) — [D]
  - Gibbi's on the Beach, Cape Point beach (seafood and rice) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Smiling Coast Guesthouse bar and restaurant, Kofi Anan Street, Cape Point — Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (Marina, École Française); Q6 "Français" (the French school and Alliance Française are the francophone niche [D]); Q5 "Salarié·e" (shortest coastal commute); Q8 "À la plage".

### Fajara (embassy quarter, Atlantic Road and Kairaba Avenue)
- Feel: upscale and calm; diplomats, families, NGO staff, retirees (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4]; the most sought-after area per Expat.com [D]. UK High Commission at 48 Atlantic Road, US Embassy at 92 Kairaba Avenue [2].
- Rent: 40,000–80,000 GMD for a 3–4-bed house (≈ €486–972), Expat.com 2025-11-03 [S cost.rent]; Jarnias 1-bed figures conflict ($150–300 on one page, $400–1,000 on another) [S cost.rent][4].
- Getting around: to Banjul by the highway via Westfield and the Denton Bridge [10]; shared-taxi fare for this exact leg not found (Bakau and Serrekunda legs are 12 GMD) [C daily.transport]. A private Serrekunda–Banjul ride is quoted at about 300–350 GMD [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - The Fajara Club, Atlantic Boulevard (golf, tennis, squash, pool, 19th Hole restaurant; prices not listed) — My Gambia [15]
  - MRC Unit, Atlantic Boulevard — Wikipedia [11]; [S health.care]
  - Banjul American International School, Atlantic Road — [S family.schools]
  - Pipeline Mosque, Kairaba Avenue (3,000 worshippers) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Elite Clinic, Kairaba Avenue; Fajara Medical Centre — Jarnias [4]
  - Fajara War Cemetery — Wikipedia [11]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Westfield–Latrikunda commuter belt (Kanifing)
- Feel: where Banjul's overflow went; Westfield Junction is the start of the four-lane highway that thousands take daily to Banjul [C housing.areas][10]. Dense and busy; few expats (Jarnias, 2026-04-29) [4].
- Rent: Latrikunda 3-bed, 2-toilet house D10,000 a month (≈ €122), six months upfront, public Facebook group 2026-11-14 [S cost.rent]; Serrekunda proper 10,000–25,000 (≈ €122–304), Expat.com [S cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared taxi Banjul–Serrekunda/Buffer Zone 10 → 12 GMD (the same guide also quotes about 20 GMD) [C daily.transport]; gelly-gelly 7–12 GMD inside Kanifing [S daily.transport].
- Watch: KMC was the most affected area in the July 2022 floods (1,657 households) [14]; Ebo Town, next door, flooded again on 30 Aug 2025 [16]; Ebo Town and Bakoteh joined the Sept 2026 power protests [S daily.power]; the Bakoteh dump burns in the open on a main road into Serrekunda [S climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Serrekunda Market "Sandika" (largest in the country) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Latrikunda market — Jarnias [4]
  - YMCA Hostel, MDI Road, Kanifing South (D400, 34 rooms, shared bathrooms) — Wikivoyage [2]
  - Kanifing General Hospital (114 beds) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Startup Incubator Gambia, MSTE Building, Kanifing Institutional Layout — [D]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Salarié·e" on a local salary; Q5 "J'entreprends"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city files, the dossier and the sources below. "Assumption" marks a non-sourced step. No Banjul-specific restaurant price, coworking rate or club fee was found; those lines are left out.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Banjul island**: the cheapest of the expat-standard ranges and quiet after hours [C cost.rent][2]. Honest caveat: a teleworker has no reason to be on the island except calm and price; the 2026 power cuts and flood season hit it directly, and remote workers rely on tourist stays (no digital-nomad visa) [C daily.power][12][S work.remote]. Many would pick Kotu instead (see the Serrekunda file).
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; fixed internet (unlimited 50–60 Mbps 2,500–5,500 GMD a month) [S daily.internet].
  - Tue: Albert Market for food, bag closed [5][2].
  - Wed: home office; evening view from Arch 22 (D200) [8][2].
  - Thu: shared taxi to Bakau (12 GMD) for Kachikally and the botanical gardens [C daily.transport][3][9].
  - Fri: home office; Jumu'ah at King Fahad Mosque for Muslim readers [6].
  - Sat: National Museum (D200) [2]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption for Catholic readers [7]; Cape Point beach [9].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not on the island (quiet after hours [2]); private taxi to Kololi (about 350 GMD each way, Serrekunda–Banjul quote used as a proxy) for Duplex, Thu–Sun to 06:00 [C cost.negotiation][17].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Gibbi's on the Beach, Cape Point [2]; Yasmina Resto Bar on the Senegambia Strip (dishes D125–800) [18].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the island's default [2].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Cape Point beach, Bakau [9]; Kotu and Kololi beaches [3]. Strong currents where the river meets the Atlantic and no lifeguards (Wikivoyage) [2].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": no mountains. Forest: Bijilo Forest Park (D300) [3][19]; Abuko Nature Reserve (107 ha, monkeys, 270+ birds) [20]; Makasutu Culture Forest, about 45 min from Banjul (taxi with waiting D1,300, undated) [21].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": desert not realistic. Savanna and river: Janjanbureh, about 5 h by GTSC bus from Kanifing (185 GMD, 385 air-conditioned) [S daily.transport], with River Gambia National Park boat trips from Kuntaur and the Wassu stone circles [22]. Or across the river: the Banjul–Barra ferry to Juffureh [23].
  - "Je reste en ville": Arch 22, National Museum, African Heritage Museum [2]; the Tanbi mangroves seen from Arch 22 [8].
- Rough weekly spend (one person, GMD):
  - Rent 15,000–25,000 ÷ 4.33 = 3,464–5,774 [C cost.rent]
  - Electricity 2,000–5,000 ÷ 4.33 = 462–1,155 [S cost.living]
  - Fixed internet 2,500–5,500 ÷ 4.33 = 577–1,270 [S daily.internet]
  - Groceries 8,000–12,000 ÷ 4.33 = 1,848–2,771 [S cost.living]
  - 4 local meals × 200–400 = 800–1,600 [S cost.living]
  - 4 shared taxis to Bakau and back × 12 = 48 [C daily.transport]
  - Saturday: private taxi to Kololi and back 2 × 350 = 700 [C cost.negotiation]; 3 local beers × 50–80 = 150–240 [S cost.living]
  - Museum or Arch 22: 200 [2]
  - **Total ≈ 8,249–13,758 GMD a week (≈ €100–167).**

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Bakau**: the nearest mainland town, 12 GMD to Banjul by shared taxi, with a beach and markets [C daily.transport][9]. Banjul's employers are government, the Central Bank, the port and groundnut processing [C cost.salary]; a 30% civil-service rise was promised for January 2027 [C cost.salary]. Foreign hires cost the employer a yearly quota fee (about 50,000 GMD for non-ECOWAS) [S work.jobs]; the Central Bank ordered banks to replace non-Gambian staff (Sept 2026) [S work.jobs].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: shared taxi Bakau–Banjul, 12 GMD each way [C daily.transport]; lunch near the ministries (no venue sourced).
  - Mon: Bakau market on the way home [9].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Westfield–Latrikunda belt**: cheap rents, the market and the incubator in Kanifing, and 12 GMD to Banjul for paperwork [S cost.rent][C daily.transport][D]. The ecosystem sits in Kanifing, not on the island [D]. Banjul is where the administration is: residence permits go to the Director General of Immigration, usually in Banjul (2–3 weeks, Jarnias, low) [C admin.residency]; Gambians report months-long waits for national ID cards at the Banjul and Kanifing centres, which can block opening a bank account (Foroyaa, 2026-04-29) [C admin.nationality].
- Week:
  - Mon: Startup Incubator Gambia, Kanifing [D].
  - Tue: shared taxi to Banjul (12 GMD) for administration; Albert Market for sourcing [C daily.transport][5].
  - Wed: Serrekunda Market suppliers [10].
  - Thu: Banjul port side and Arch 22 area meetings (no venue sourced); back via Westfield at rush hour [10].
  - Fri: Jumu'ah at the Pipeline Mosque, Kairaba Avenue [10]; Wave and bank errands [D].
  - Sat: Latrikunda market [4]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: rest.
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Duplex [17]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — Yasmina [18]; "Tranquille chez moi" — at home.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend (one person, GMD): rent 10,000–25,000 ÷ 4.33 = 2,309–5,774 [S cost.rent] + electricity 462–1,155 + internet 577–1,270 + groceries 1,848–2,771 [S cost.living][S daily.internet] + 5 local lunches × 200–400 = 1,000–2,000 [S cost.living] + 6 shared taxis to Banjul × 12 = 72 [C daily.transport] + 2 private rides × 350 = 700 [C cost.negotiation] + Saturday dinner 500–800 [S cost.living] → **≈ 7,468–14,542 GMD a week (≈ €91–177).**

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Timing: avoid the campaign (16 Nov–3 Dec) and the 5 Dec 2026 vote [D]. British nationals get 28 days on arrival, extendable twice at the Immigration Office in Banjul; French/EU rules not confirmed [C admin.visa][D].
- Where to stay first: few budget beds on the island were found; YMCA Hostel, Kanifing South (D400) [2], or Fajara Guest House, 11 10th Street South (no price, listing 2015) [2]; Laico Atlantic on Marina Parade for a higher budget (no price) [2].
- Day 1: airport to central Banjul 1,000–1,400 GMD, or to Serrekunda 685–765; agree the fare first [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation].
- Day 2: SIM with credit about 50 GMD [S daily.internet]; Africell 1 GB was 450 GMD in April 2023 [24]; Wave for payments [D].
- Day 3: flat hunting on the island and in Bakau: no Banjul listing site was found; a francophone forum member looking near the French school got no area tips [C housing.search]. Use Facebook housing groups and "To Let" signs [S housing.search]. Expect agent viewing fees (about $500 reported) and six months upfront [S cost.negotiation][C cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: check flood exposure: avoid ground floors in Half Die and Banjul North after the Aug 2026 floods [13][12] (assumption drawn from those reports); ask about cash-power meter and water [S daily.power].
- Day 5: bank: Central Bank and banks are in Banjul [C daily.money]; a current account opens with about 500 GMD (Jarnias, low) [S daily.money]; change money only at banks or licensed bureaux (Wikivoyage) [2].
- Day 6: Albert Market morning, then the commute out at evening rush hour via Denton Bridge and Westfield [5][10].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Cathedral or rest; Cape Point beach [7][9].
- Rough first-week spend (GMD): 7 nights × 400 = 2,800 (YMCA, 2020 price) [2] + airport taxi 1,000–1,400 [C daily.transport] + SIM 50 + 1–2 GB data 450–900 [S daily.internet][24] + 14 local meals × 200–400 = 2,800–5,600 [S cost.living] + 10 shared taxis × 12 = 120 [C daily.transport] + 4 private short rides × 50–100 = 200–400 [S daily.transport] → **≈ 7,420–11,270 GMD (≈ €90–137).**

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Dry season roughly November–May [D]. Avoid political gatherings in late Nov–early Dec 2026 [D].

### One day
- Morning: Albert Market, Liberation Avenue, early and with bags closed [5][2]; walk past King Fahad Mosque and the Cathedral of Our Lady of the Assumption [6][7].
- Midday: Arch 22 (D200): the gallery shows the port and the Tanbi mangroves [8][2]; then the Gambia National Museum on Independence Drive (D200, open to 18:00 Mon–Thu, 17:00 Fri–Sun) [2]. Lunch: no Banjul restaurant was sourced; a local meal runs 200–400 GMD [S cost.living].
- Afternoon: shared taxi to Bakau (12 GMD) [C daily.transport]: Kachikally Crocodile Pool (D200) [3], the botanical gardens [9], the fish market at the town beach [9].
- Evening: dinner at Gibbi's on the Beach, Cape Point [2]. Take a licensed taxi back; the island is quiet and poorly lit at night [2].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive (airport to Banjul 1,000–1,400 GMD) [C daily.transport]; evening on Marina Parade [8].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (trip out): Banjul–Barra ferry (pedestrians 25–100 GMD, crossing 20 min to over an hour; FCDO warns it is overcrowded) [C daily.transport][C safety.general] to Juffureh: free Slave Museum, pier restaurant (D200 meal), and Kunta Kinteh Island, UNESCO since 2003, now about a sixth of its former size from erosion [23][25].
- Day 4 (daily-life moment): morning rush hour from Westfield into Banjul by shared taxi (12 GMD) with the commuters who make the island work [10][C daily.transport]; Friday Jumu'ah at King Fahad Mosque seen from outside if the day fits [6].
- Day 5 (trip out): Abuko Nature Reserve [20], then Makasutu Culture Forest (taxi from Banjul with waiting D1,300) [21].
- Day 6: Serrekunda Market and Bijilo Forest Park (D300) [10][3]; evening on the Senegambia Strip [10].
- Day 7: Tanji fishing village (boats in about 17:00; green taxi D1,000 or yellow 250 from the resorts) [26]; or a lazy Cape Point beach day [9].
