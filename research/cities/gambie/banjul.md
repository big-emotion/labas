# Banjul, The Gambia

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 82.3 GMD** (FxFeed market aggregator, 14 Nov 2026), rounded. The Central Bank of The Gambia's daily valuation the same day was 84.87 GMD/EUR. USD figures are converted at the CBG cross-rate (1 USD ≈ 0.869 EUR). Banjul city proper is a small island capital; most people who work here live in Kanifing/Serrekunda (see serrekunda.md). National items (fuel, law, visas) are repeated here only where they matter for a Banjul resident. Web search budget ran out after two queries; everything else comes from direct fetches.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Basic apartments in Banjul, mostly in older colonial buildings: 15,000–25,000 GMD/month (≈ €182–304). — Expat.com "Living in The Gambia", 2025-11-03, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A modest 2-bedroom in greater Banjul: 7,000–15,000 GMD/month (≈ €85–182). — Cyril Jarnias, "Finding Housing in Gambia", 2026-05-01, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Average 1-bed in a "city centre" about 7,384 GMD (≈ €90) and outside about 5,856 (≈ €71); 3-bed about 18,000 (≈ €219) vs 7,500 (≈ €91). The guide does not say which city. — Jarnias housing guide, 2026-05-01, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Landlords often want six months to a year of rent in advance plus a deposit, although the guide says a Rent Act amendment caps advances at one month. — Jarnias housing guide, 2026-05-01, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Private taxis are not metered; agree a flat fare before getting in. A private Serrekunda–Banjul ride is quoted at "about 350" and "over 300" GMD (≈ €4) on the same page. — Jarnias transport guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Pump prices from 1 Sept 2026: petrol D109.80/L (≈ €1.33), diesel D120 (≈ €1.46), kerosene D112.64. — Gambiana, 2026-09-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [cost.living] Headline inflation 6.1% in September 2026; policy rate 14%. — Central Bank of The Gambia, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [cost.salary] Jobs centre on government, the Central Bank, the port and peanut processing; the president promised civil servants a 30% pay rise by January 2027. — Wikipedia "Banjul" / The Point 2026-06-30, academic / local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] Median net local salary about 4,500 GMD/month (≈ €55). — Jarnias cost-of-living guide, 2026-04-28, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The city proper shrank from 31,301 people (2013 census) to 26,461 (2024); the Greater Banjul Area, including Kanifing, holds 405,809. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Banjul's growth is capped by its island site, so Serrekunda, about 18 km away, took overflow residents and businesses; thousands commute in daily. — Wikipedia "Serekunda", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] A francophone forum member asking for a furnished house in Banjul near the French school got no prices or area tips, only a pointer to the site's housing ads. — Expat.com forum thread, c. 2023 (relative date), forum, confidence: low
- [business.invest] Land in Banjul is quoted at roughly 12,000–16,000 GMD/m² (≈ €146–194/m²). — Jarnias investment guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Protests broke out in Banjul on 7 Sept 2026 over "prolonged power outages that began in June". — Wikipedia "2026 in the Gambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Banjul residents joined candlelight protests across Greater Banjul; the editorial also mentions water shortages without detail. — Gambiana editorial, 2026-09-08, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Undated shared-taxi fare rise: intra-Banjul 7 → 8 GMD; Banjul–Bakau 10 → 12; Banjul–Serrekunda/Buffer Zone 10 → 12; Banjul–Brikama 20 → 22 (≈ €0.27). — Jarnias transport guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The same guide also quotes a shared Serrekunda–Banjul taxi at about 20 GMD, which conflicts with its own 12 GMD table. — Jarnias transport guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Airport to central Banjul: 1,000–1,400 GMD (≈ €12–17) by taxi. — Jarnias transport guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Banjul–Barra ferry: pedestrians 25–100 GMD, up to 250 quoted; vehicles about 250; tickets only at the terminal; crossing 20 minutes to over an hour. — Jarnias transport guide, accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A highway crosses the Denton Bridge to Serrekunda; Banjul sits on the Trans-West African Coastal Highway toward Dakar and Bissau. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Central Bank daily valuation on 14 Nov 2026: 1 EUR = 84.87 GMD, 1 USD = 73.77 GMD. The Central Bank is headquartered in Banjul. — Central Bank of The Gambia / Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Edward Francis Small Teaching Hospital (ex-Royal Victoria, founded 1853) is the national referral hospital: 540 beds, the largest in the country; its blood bank is often short, and relatives are sometimes asked to donate. — Wikipedia "Edward Francis Small Teaching Hospital", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The hospital "continues to require assistance"; in Nov 2026 a UK charity ran a free screening day there and paid for a teenager's medication his family could not afford. — The Point, 2026-11-13, local-media, confidence: high
- [health.care] Gambian citizens pay about 25 GMD (≈ €0.30) for an adult public consultation; foreigners pay more. Public hospitals have had long shortages of paracetamol, antibiotics and IV fluids. — Jarnias healthcare guide, 2026-04-28, blog, confidence: low
- [family.schools] UTG's School of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences and School of Engineering and Architecture are in Banjul; the new main campus is at Faraba Banta. — Wikipedia "University of the Gambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] International schools are listed in greater Banjul (Marina, Maarif, SBEC, BISG) and in Bakau (École Française de Banjul), not on the island itself. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Petty crime is most common in Kololi, Serrekunda and the Banjul market. — Expat.com, 2025-11-03, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] A guide says US authorities raised the crime threat to "critical" for some residential areas of Banjul after a rise in petty crime; this is second-hand and undated. — Jarnias safety guide, 2026-04-29, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] The Banjul–Barra ferry is often overcrowded, lacks life-saving equipment and has run aground on sandbanks; FCDO suggests the Senegambia Bridge near Farafenni instead. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Since 10 Sept 2026, three days after the Banjul power protests, police require a permit for all protests and gatherings; the presidential election is on 5 Dec 2026. — Wikipedia "2026 in the Gambia", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.women] Violence against women "remains a serious problem"; Parliament rejected repealing the FGM ban in July 2024. — Freedom House, Freedom in the World 2025, NGO, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Landmarks: Albert Market, the National Museum, Arch 22 (a 35 m gateway housing a textile museum), State House and the Court House. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mostly Muslim, with King Fahad Mosque the largest; the Catholic Diocese of Banjul, Anglican and Assemblies of God churches also operate. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.diaspora] Travellers from Banjul commonly connect via Brussels or Lisbon to reach Paris, Madrid or Barcelona. — Gambiana, 2026-11-14, local-media, confidence: medium
- [admin.visa] British nationals get 28 days on arrival, extendable twice by 28 days at the Immigration Office in Banjul. — UK FCDO entry requirements, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] Residence permits are applied for to the Director General of Immigration, usually in Banjul; about 2–3 weeks once the file is complete; valid one year. — Jarnias admin guide, 2026-04-28, blog, confidence: low
- [admin.nationality] Relevant to dual-national returnees: Gambians report waiting months, some nearly a year, for national ID cards at the Banjul and Kanifing centres; one was refused a new bank account without a current card. — Foroyaa, 2026-04-29, local-media, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex relations are illegal ("zero tolerance", FCDO); the minimum penalty for "homosexual acts" was raised from two to five years in Sept 2024. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14 / Freedom House 2025, official / NGO, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Wet season about five months, rainiest in August (about 350 mm), humidity peaking near 85%; average daily maximum about 32.5 °C. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] The island sits about 2 m above sea level, and a government minister warned it could be submerged by a 1 m sea-level rise. — Wikipedia "Banjul", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
