# Hargeisa, Somalia (Somaliland)

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: prices in Hargeisa are quoted mostly in US dollars. EUR figures use **US$1 ≈ €0.86**, the
rate implied by the dossier's own conversions (`research/countries/somalie.md`: USD 721 ≈ EUR 620),
not re-verified. Somaliland shilling (SlSh) figures are given as the source states them.

Status reminder: France Diplomatie puts Hargeisa in the **orange zone** (2026-11-04); the UK FCDO
advises against all but essential travel to Maroodijeh, the region containing Hargeisa (2026-06-04).
