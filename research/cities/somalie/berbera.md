## Items

- [cost.salary] A 2024 Danish Refugee Council study, cited by the EUAA, puts average income in Berbera at US$737 (≈ €634), against US$806 in Hargeisa. — EUAA COI Report Somalia: Country Focus, 2.2.3, 2025-05, official, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] DP World has run the port since 2017 under a 2016 deal worth about US$442 million; in March 2018 shares were DP World 51 %, Somaliland 30 %, Ethiopia 19 %. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] A second phase announced in June 2021 was to extend the quay from 400 to 1,000 m and bring the port to ten ship-to-shore cranes. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Sheep, camels and cattle are shipped through Berbera to Gulf states such as Saudi Arabia; livestock, gum arabic, frankincense and myrrh are the main exports. — Wikipedia "Economy of Somaliland" and "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Berbera port brought in about two-thirds of Somaliland's revenues in 2020, and a special economic zone was set up to attract investors. — Durham University / The Conversation, 2023-07-10, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Ethiopia announced an agreement to use Berbera's seaport on 1 January 2024; a Berbera–Addis Ababa rail line (reported cost about US$1.5 billion, via Hargeisa) is still only under discussion. — Wikipedia "Berbera" and "Economy of Somaliland", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Electricity is supplied by one private monopoly, Berbera Power House; residents interviewed in early 2023 said prices had not fallen after a 2021 solar project and that only wealthy residents could afford power. — Durham University / The Conversation, 2023-07-10, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Before the 2021 solar investment the price was reportedly US$0.60/kWh (≈ €0.52); officials had proposed US$0.10. — Durham University / The Conversation, 2023-07-10, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] An opinion piece says Berbera's tariff fell to US$0.20/kWh (≈ €0.17) in July 2025, from among the country's highest. — Geeska (opinion), 2025-09-23, local-media, confidence: low
- [daily.power] Berbera was excluded from the national US$0.59/kWh tariff applied from 1 December 2025 and keeps its own pricing. — Geeska, 2025-11-07, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] The port runs its own power supply because it fears blackouts would damage its equipment. — Durham University / The Conversation, 2023-07-10, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Researchers describe many city-centre buildings as abandoned and dilapidated, with roads, water, schools and health services lacking (2023). — Durham University / The Conversation, 2023-07-10, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Berbera lies about 160 km from Hargeisa; there is a customs checkpoint at Hargeisa's north-eastern (Berbera) entrance. — Wikipedia "Berbera" and EUAA 2.2.2 (2025-05), academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Berbera Airport has a 4,140 m runway; after a UAE-funded renovation it reopened to international passengers on 20 November 2021; the page gives no current route list. — Wikipedia "Berbera Airport", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France puts Berbera, like Hargeisa, in the orange zone; the rest of Somaliland is red. It advises escorts, short stays, secure accommodation and discreet routes. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-11-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all but essential travel to Sahil region (which contains Berbera) and against all travel elsewhere in Somalia outside Awdal and Maroodijeh. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [social.religion] Drinking and selling alcohol are banned throughout Somalia, Somaliland included. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [social.culture] Berbera was the capital of British Somaliland from 1884 until 1941, when Hargeisa replaced it. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Mean daily highs exceed 40 °C from June to September (about 42.9 °C in July); winter highs are about 28 °C with humidity near 78 %; only about 52 mm of rain a year. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Batalale and Bathela are the popular local beaches; the coast offers snorkelling, diving and coral reefs. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population was 242,344 in 2019 (urban area about 478,000); it is the capital of Sahil region. — Wikipedia "Berbera", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
