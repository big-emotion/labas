## Items

- [safety.general] France puts Mogadishu in the red zone ("formellement déconseillé"): al-Shabaab carries out attacks there daily (targeted killings, IEDs, grenades, car bombs, suicide commandos) and kidnap risk is "extrêmement élevé". — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-11-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK advises against all travel to Somalia outside three Somaliland regions; Mogadishu is not among the exceptions, and there is no in-person consular support in Mogadishu. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Rocket attacks hit Mogadishu International Airport in February, March and April 2025, two of them in daylight. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] An IED attack on the President's convoy in March 2025 reportedly killed 16 people. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] An attack at Lido Beach in August 2024 killed more than 50 civilians and injured over 200; a July 2024 attack hit Top Coffee Restaurant; a June 2023 attack on the Pearl Beach Hotel killed at least 9. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Security forces and opposition forces clashed in Mogadishu on 3 June 2026; fighting in Middle Shabelle has raised the threat to the city. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The UK says kidnap threat is high across Somalia and that British nationals, including aid workers and business travellers, are seen as legitimate targets. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Al-Shabaab keeps a sizable clandestine presence, taxes businesses and people building houses or shops, and summons residents to its courts; refusing risks reprisals. — EUAA COI Report Somalia: Country Focus, 2.1.1, 2025-05, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Peripheral neighbourhoods are seen as less safe than the protected zones near the airport and centre, while areas with government offices or African Union sites are described as prime targets. — EUAA COI Report, 2.1.1, 2025-05, official, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Road travel is dangerous: checkpoints are run by government forces, militias and other armed groups, and armed militias commit murder, robbery and kidnapping. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] At the end of 2024 Mogadishu had 2,057 displacement sites (mostly Daynile and Kahda); about 197,000 eviction cases affecting displaced people were recorded in Somalia in 2024, mainly in and around Mogadishu. — EUAA COI Report, 2.1.1, 2025-05, official, confidence: medium
- [other] About 300,000 people were newly displaced in Somalia between February and September 2025, per the UN; media reported waves of forced evictions in Mogadishu. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026, 2026 (covers 2025), intl-media, confidence: high ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [other] After the March 2025 convoy bombing at least 22 journalists were briefly detained; on 6 March 2025 the information minister banned reporting on "security threat" issues. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026, 2026 (covers 2025), intl-media, confidence: high
- [admin.visa] Entry to Mogadishu needs an eVisa applied for in advance; the UK warns of an ongoing breach of the eVisa system exposing names, photos, birth details and home addresses. — UK FCDO entry requirements, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [admin.nationality] Somalia treats any British national of Somali descent as a dual national, limiting consular help. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
- [rights.lgbt] Somalia criminalises consensual same-sex conduct with up to three years in prison; the UK advises no public affection. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026 and UK FCDO 2026-06-04, intl-media, confidence: high
- [social.religion] Alcohol is banned; Ramadan daytime rules apply in public; Somali Muslims may not convert or promote another religion. — UK FCDO, updated 2026-06-04, official, confidence: high
