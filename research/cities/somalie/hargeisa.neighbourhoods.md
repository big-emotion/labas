# Hargeisa: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **US$1 ≈ €0.86** (the rate in the city file, implied by the dossier's own conversions, not re-verified). Somaliland shilling figures are given as sources state them (about SlSh 10,000 per US$ in September 2025, low confidence) [C daily.money]. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/somalie/hargeisa.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/somalie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Research depth: the session's shared web-search budget ran out after three searches in this pass (2026-11-15), so almost everything below comes from pages opened directly by URL: Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, the EUAA country-of-origin report, official travel advice, a 2014 UN-Habitat planning study and two local-media pieces. No property portal, local neighbourhood guide or 2025–2026 resident blog could be reached. Neighbourhood-level detail is therefore thin, and several sections say so.

**Read this first: the advisory frame.**
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-11-04) puts Hargeisa in the **orange zone** ("déconseillé sauf raison impérative") and the rest of Somaliland, Berbera excepted, in the **red zone**. For Hargeisa it asks for escorts, short stays, secure housing, venues with armed guards and perimeter walls, discretion about routes, and registration with the French embassy in Nairobi [20][C safety.general].
- The UK FCDO advises against all but essential travel to Maroodijeh (Hargeisa), Awdal and Sahil (Berbera), and against all travel to Togdheer, Sanaag and Sool; it calls the kidnap threat in Somaliland high [21][22][C safety.general].
- Under the product's advisory gate (`docs/criteria/architecture.md` on origin/docs/criteria-architecture, §3.2 `safety`, which points to the §4.3 gate), a city in an orange zone is not proposed. These routes are research material for a reader who already has an imperative reason (family, an employer, an NGO posting); they are not a recommendation to go. Every route below assumes France's measures (secure housing, escorted or pre-booked movements).
- Leaving the city: Wikivoyage says foreigners need a government security escort (Special Protection Unit, SPU) or an exemption and paperwork, or checkpoints turn them back (undated, low) [1]. The EUAA places customs checkpoints at the Gabiley (west) and Berbera (north-east) entrances (2025-05) [5][C daily.transport].

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Eight districts: Mohamoud Haybe, Mohamed Moge, Ibrahim Koodbuur, 31 May, Ahmed Dhagah, 26 June, Ga'an Libah (Gacan Libaax) and Mo'alim Harun; residential areas follow clan lines (EUAA 2025-05) [4][C housing.areas]. A Somaliland Chronicle opinion piece (2025-10-17) speaks of nine districts [3].
- Rent depends mostly on size, a courtyard and a piped-water connection; under 25 % of households have tap water, 48 % rely on water trucks [C housing.areas][C daily.water].
- Power: national tariff cut to US$0.59/kWh from 2025-12-01, still "exceptionally high" per Geeska; a tariff dispute between the regulator and private power firms led to a police operation on 2026-11-01 [C daily.power]. Academics (2023) describe frequent, long power cuts [C daily.power].
- Flooding: the Maroodijeex river flood plain and the gullies are flagged as hazardous zones in UN-Habitat's planning study (file dated 2014) [30]; homes and shops built on dry riverbeds block stormwater, and many roads lack drainage (Somaliland Chronicle opinion, 2025-10-17) [3]; FCDO: localised flooding can block access [C climate.daily].
- Few public open spaces apart from the stadium and a few UNICEF-refurbished playgrounds (UN-Habitat, 2014) [30]; children play football on roads or rented private pitches (2025-10-17) [3].
- Getting around: big public buses SlSh 2,000–3,000 (US$0.20–0.40, ≈ €0.17–0.34) per ride (IOM 2022 via EUAA); Dhaweeye app or short code \*3000#, US$1 minimum + US$0.50/km (≈ €0.86 + €0.43/km) [5][C daily.transport]. Wikivoyage advises booking taxis through the hotel and names the DALAB Taxi app (undated) [1]. No checkpoints inside the city except ad hoc ones [5].
- Distances below are **straight-line** from Wikipedia coordinates, measured from the MiG memorial on Freedom Square (the city's recognised centre) [29]; road distances are longer. No sourced travel times inside the city were found.
- Alcohol is banned; Ramadan daytime eating, drinking and loud music in public are to be avoided [C social.religion]. Wikivoyage: women in ankle-length skirts or dresses with hair and upper arms covered; men avoid shorts and sleeveless shirts (undated) [1].

## Neighbourhoods

### City centre (Independence Avenue, Freedom Square, 26 June district)
- Feel: the dense inner core mixes shop fronts and small workshops; the central Wahen market draws formal and informal traders and ribbon shops line the main axis (UN-Habitat, 2014) [30]. Wikipedia describes 26 June district as central and busy, where much of the daily commercial and administrative activity happens (undated stub) [6]. The EUAA lists 26 June as predominantly Isaaq/Habar Awal [4]. Major commercial hubs suffer persistent congestion and lack pedestrian walkways (2025-10-17, opinion) [3].
- Rent: no centre-specific listing. Aggregator model, 1-bed 40 m² in the centre US$199/month (≈ €171); 3-bed 80 m² US$355 (≈ €305) (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21, modelled, low) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot inside the core in daytime; Dhaweeye or hotel taxi otherwise; the airport is about 5.4 km south-east in a straight line [13][29], at the end of the city–airport road that UN-Habitat calls one of the city's main axes (2014) [30].
- Watch: the State House area in the centre is a squatter settlement under eviction threat (Durham University, 2022-08-19) [17], and water is especially scarce around State House (EUAA 2025-05) [C daily.water]. Markets are exposed to fires linked to overhead power cables and to flooding (2025-10-17, opinion) [3]. Wikivoyage mentions occasional rock or bottle throwing in some areas such as the livestock market; that is a wiki remark only, not an official one (undated, low) [1].
- Places:
  - Central Market / Suuqa Bacadlaha, Independence Road (main market: electronics, food, clothing) — Wikivoyage [1]; the central Wahen market in UN-Habitat [30]
  - Jama Mosque, Independence Road (central mosque; non-Muslims cannot enter, Friday midday prayers fill the street) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [2]
  - Hargeisa War Memorial (MiG-17 on Freedom Square, Independence Avenue) — Wikipedia [29]
  - National Museum of Somaliland, 26 June Street (opened 29 July 2024) — Wikipedia [14]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Dahabshiil Business Centre, June Avenue (described as the tallest building and largest mall in Somaliland), with Damal Hotel inside and Damal Restaurant next door (Somali and international food) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Oriental Hotel, Arwada Road, next to the gold market (budget, US$15–30) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Gobonimo market (26 June district per Wikipedia; UN-Habitat says it serves peripheral areas, so its exact position is uncertain) — [6][30]
  - Edna Adan Maternity Hospital ("near the city center", about 1.2 km south of the memorial; obstetrics, emergency blood bank, nurse and midwife training) — Wikipedia [10][C health.care]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" and "J'entreprends" (banks, trade, offices within walking distance); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" (by Hargeisa's measure; nothing sourced supports "Vibre jour et nuit"); Q3 "Moyen"; Q8 "Je reste en ville".

### Jigjiga Yar (Ibrahim Koodbuur district)
- Feel: the EUAA places the most expensive houses in Ibrahim Koodbuur, including the Jigjiga Yar sub-district (2025-05; seen in a search snippet of section 2.2.3, consistent with the city file) [C cost.rent]. Ibrahim Koodbuur is predominantly Isaaq/Habar Awal [4]. No dated description of the street life or noise was found; aggregator descriptions ("upscale, popular with diaspora and NGO workers") come from an unsourced AI-generated site already rejected in the city file and are not used [C Gaps].
- Rent: four-bedroom house with courtyard and tap water up to US$500/month (≈ €430) (EUAA 2025-05) [C cost.rent]. No smaller-flat figure.
- Getting around: no distance or road time sourced; Dhaweeye at US$1 + US$0.50/km [5].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Sultan Restaurant, Jigjiga-Yar Road (budget; open 18:00–24:00 per Trip.com; Middle Eastern grills per a 2016 article) — Trip.com [26]; How We Made It In Africa, 2016-09-05 [27]
  - Timeout Hargeisa, Burj Omaar 2 building (upscale; Chinese, American and international dishes; listing updated November 2026) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Only two places could be sourced here (see Gaps).

### Airport Road corridor (south)
- Feel: the airport lies in the south of the city (EUAA 2025-05) [5]. UN-Habitat (2014) saw the city–airport axis as a growth corridor for commerce and services [30]. No resident description was found.
- Rent: no figure; use the city-wide model (1-bed US$131–199, ≈ €113–171) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Airport Road links the city to Egal International Airport [13]; the airport is about 5.4 km from the memorial in a straight line [13][29]. Hotel taxis were about US$25 to the airport from Maan-Soor in 2011 (old, Wikivoyage) [1].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced (the airport rocket attacks in the dossier concern Mogadishu, not Hargeisa) [D].
- Places:
  - Ambassador Hotel, Airport Road, about 1.4 km north-west of the airport (gym, restaurants, non-alcoholic bar; deluxe US$52.50, en suite US$78.75; staff help with visas and tours) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Egal International Airport (Addis Ababa, Dubai, Djibouti, Jeddah, Nairobi, Mogadishu, Cairo and others listed; route list flagged as disputed) — Wikipedia [13]; EUAA list as of June 2024 [C daily.transport]
  - Hargeisa Stadium (national stadium, artificial turf, 30,000 seats per Wikipedia, home of the Somaliland national team; about 1.4 km south of the memorial, so between the centre and this corridor) — Wikipedia [15]
- Fits: Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport access); Q5 "Salarié·e" for anyone whose employer houses staff near the airport; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (the first nights in a hotel that helps with paperwork).

### North-west edge (Mohamed Warsame Road)
- Feel: UN-Habitat (2014) describes the north–south road on the western side of the city as cutting through some of Hargeisa's better-off neighbourhoods [30]. The Maan-Soor Hotel sits on the north-western edge of town [1]. The study does not name these neighbourhoods, and no 2020s description was found.
- Rent: no figure; the EUAA's upper bound for a large house with courtyard and water (US$500, ≈ €430) is the only sourced reference for a better-off area [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no time sourced; hotel taxi or Dhaweeye.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced. Malawle, a resettlement scheme on the far western outskirts, lacks roads and public transport and has water shortages (Durham 2022; EUAA 2025-05) [17][C daily.water]; it is not a newcomer area.
- Places:
  - Maan-Soor Hotel, Mohamed Warsame Road (sprawling grounds, restaurant, café, sports centre; rooms US$40–90; a Berbera branch offers diving) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Only one place could be sourced here (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (no rent figure, but described as better-off).

### Ahmed Dhagah (Sinai sub-district)
- Feel: the EUAA names Sinai, in Ahmed Dhagah district, as the cheapest area; the district is populated by Isaaq/Arab and Isaaq/Ayub clans (2025-05) [4][C cost.rent].
- Rent: a basic four-bedroom house with no courtyard and no tap water about US$200/month (≈ €172) (EUAA 2025-05) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no distance or time sourced; buses at SlSh 2,000–3,000 per ride [5].
- Watch: no tap water in the cited example, so budget for trucked water (a household of 8–9 spends US$25–30/month, ≈ €22–26) [C daily.water].
- Places: none located inside Ahmed Dhagah could be sourced (see Gaps).
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; only for a reader with family on site, Somali and their own transport. Thinly sourced; treat as a lead.

### New Hargeisa (Ga'an Libah district)
- Feel: a sub-district of Ga'an Libah, mainly Isaaq/Garhajis; the same district also contains Sheikh Madar (Habar Awal) and Daami (mainly Gabooye) (EUAA 2025-05) [4][9].
- Rent: no figure.
- Getting around: no time sourced.
- Watch: Daami, in the north-east of the city, is an impoverished area with frequent evictions (Durham, 2022-08-19) [17], water scarcity (EUAA 2025-05) [C daily.water] and documented discrimination against the Gabooye minority [C rights.racism]. It is listed here so a newcomer recognises the name, not as a recommendation.
- Places: none sourced (see Gaps).
- Fits: no profile can be supported with sourced detail; listed for completeness because returnees with Garhajis family ties may be offered housing here [4].

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come from the city file: the EUAA report (2025-05, medium confidence), Telesom's published bundles (accessed 2026-11-14), and livingcost.org's model (2026-06-21, low confidence; it even lists a pub beer in a city where alcohol is banned [C social.nightlife]). ### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Jigjiga Yar**, because the only sourced houses with courtyard and piped water are there [C cost.rent], and remote work depends on staying home through water and power problems. Expect a house, not a studio: no 1-bed listing exists for this area.
- Week:
  - Mon: home office on Telesom unlimited data (US$15/30 days) or fibre (modelled US$23.20/month) [C daily.internet]; check the power supplier's tariff (dispute ongoing in November 2026) [C daily.power].
  - Tue: home office; spiced tea (shaah) as the morning staple, per a 2017 resident blog (old) [C social.food].
  - Wed: Dhaweeye to the centre (assume 3 km, about US$2.50); Central Market and the gold-market area; lunch at Damal Restaurant [1].
  - Thu: home office; evening at Sultan Restaurant on Jigjiga-Yar Road (from 18:00) [26].
  - Fri: Friday midday prayers at Jama Mosque fill the street [1]; quiet afternoon.
  - Sat: National Museum of Somaliland, 26 June Street [14]; evening per Q2 below.
  - Sun: home; groceries; work prep.
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": not realistic. Alcohol is banned [C social.religion] and no club, live-music venue or late-night dance place was found [C Gaps].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Timeout Hargeisa (upscale, Burj Omaar 2) [1] or Sultan Restaurant (open until midnight) [26].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the realistic default; France advises secure housing and discretion on movements [20].
- Weekend outings (Q8):
  - "À la plage": Berbera's Batalale and Bathela beaches, about 160 km and three hours by road [19][1]. Berbera is orange like Hargeisa, but the road crosses the red zone (France) and needs an SPU escort (Wikivoyage) [20][1]; summer highs exceed 40 °C from June to September [19]. Realistic only as an organised, escorted weekend.
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Naasa Hablood, the twin hills on the city's outskirts, about 7 km north-east of the centre in a straight line; Wikipedia lists hiking but describes no route or access [12]. No forest sourced. Whether the hills fall inside France's orange perimeter is not stated.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic as such from Hargeisa on the sources found. The nearest sourced outing is Laas Geel (rock art, see Visiting), whose landscape no source describes.
  - "Je reste en ville": museum, market, Friday prayers crowd, a match at Hargeisa Stadium if one is scheduled (no fixture list found) [15].

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Jigjiga Yar**, the only area with a sourced family house that has a courtyard and tap water (up to US$500/month) [C cost.rent]. Edna Adan Maternity Hospital is near the centre [10]; private hospitals named by the EUAA are Gaargaar, Manhal, Haldoor, Hussein Deeqle and Edna Adan (minor treatment US$50–100) [C health.care]; Hargeisa Group Hospital (public, about 400 beds per Wikipedia, opened 1953) charges no admission fee [23][C health.care].
- Schools: private schools about US$20/month (elementary) and US$50/month (secondary); public schools officially free but about US$10/month collected per child; Koranic school about US$10 (EUAA 2025-05) [C family.schools]. Abaarso School of Science and Technology is an English-language boarding school about 18 km west of the city [16]; Smart Secondary School is about 13 km west [2]. No international or French school was found [C family.schools].
- Week:
  - Mon: school run by Dhaweeye (assume 2 km each way); Mon–Thu similar.
  - Tue: market for fresh food at the Central Market [1].
  - Wed: paediatric check-ups, if needed, at a private hospital (US$50–100 for minor treatment) [C health.care].
  - Thu: after school, children's play is the hard part: few public open spaces besides the stadium and a few UNICEF playgrounds (2014) [30], and children often play on roads or rented pitches (2025-10-17) [3].
  - Fri: Friday prayers at Jama Mosque; family day [1].
  - Sat: National Museum of Somaliland [14]; the Hargeysa International Book Fair if visiting in late July (2026 edition 25–30 July) [18].
  - Sun: home; Koranic school if chosen [C family.schools].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not realistic; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at Timeout Hargeisa or Sultan Restaurant [1][26]; "Tranquille chez moi" is the default with children.
- Weekend outings (Q8): as profile 1; Berbera's summer heat (above 40 °C, June–September) rules out a family beach trip in those months [19].
- Rough weekly spend (family of four, two primary pupils and one secondary pupil assumed): rent 500 / 4.33 = 115.47 + data × 2 = 7.00 + utilities 7.09 + trucked water if the tap fails 27.50 / 4.33 = 6.35 + groceries × 2.5 = 57.55 + 2 private primary × 20 / 4.33 = 9.24 + 1 private secondary 50 / 4.33 = 11.55 + 1 dinner for two 17.40 + 10 Dhaweeye school rides × 2.00 (2 km, assumed) = 20.00 → **≈ US$252/week (≈ €216)**; add a watchman (23.09). For scale, the aggregator models a family of four at US$1,626/month with rent (≈ US$376/week) [C cost.living]. ### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: a hotel that handles security and transport. Options sourced: Oriental Hotel, centre (US$15–30) [1]; Maan-Soor Hotel, north-west edge (US$40–90) [1]; Ambassador Hotel, Airport Road (US$52.50–78.75; staff help with visas and tours) [1]; Serene Sarovar Premiere Hargeisa, the first internationally branded five-star hotel (134 rooms, pool, spa, conference halls; announced 2025-08-28, address not given) [28]. Wikivoyage prices are undated. France asks for secure accommodation [20].
- Week:
  - Day 1 (arrival): one-month single-entry visa on arrival at Hargeisa airport; a federal eVisa may be needed to board but is not valid for entry; an invitation letter may be asked for; passport valid 6 months [C admin.visa]. Ask about the street exchange rate, which beats the airport's (Wikivoyage) [1].
  - Day 2: Telesom SIM and bundle (unlimited data 30 days US$15; unlimited calls and data US$20) [C daily.internet]; ZAAD mobile money runs on Telesom [D][C daily.money]. Documents needed for a SIM or a ZAAD account: not found. Withdraw US$ at an ATM with an international card [C daily.money].
  - Day 3: bank visit to ask about an account (Dahabshil Bank International, Premier Bank) [C daily.money]; requirements for foreigners not found.
  - Day 4: flat hunting in Jigjiga Yar and the centre. No portal, agency or visit-fee information was found; rent depends on size, courtyard and water connection [C housing.areas]. Ask about the water source and the power supplier first [C daily.water][C daily.power].
  - Day 5: Friday prayers crowd at Jama Mosque, Central Market [1].
  - Day 6: second round of visits; Sinai (Ahmed Dhagah) if the budget is tight [C cost.rent].
  - Day 7: decide; check residency: an April 2025 interview says there are no formal requirements to settle, and no public document list was found [C admin.residency].
- Advance rent and deposit customs: not found (see Gaps).
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" not realistic; "Un bon dîner entre amis" at the hotel restaurant or Damal Restaurant [1]; "Tranquille chez moi" at the hotel.
- Weekend outings (Q8): not advised in the first week; escort paperwork (SPU) comes first [1].
- Rough weekly spend: 7 nights at the Oriental Hotel midpoint 22.50 = 157.50 + data 3.50 + 3 lunches × 5.04 = 15.12 + 8 Dhaweeye rides × 3.00 (4 km, assumed) = 24.00 + half a groceries basket 11.51 → **≈ US$212 (≈ €182)**; at Maan-Soor's lowest rate (7 × 40 = 280) ≈ US$334 (≈ €287). ## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". France and the UK both advise against non-essential travel to Hargeisa, and France calls for escorts, short stays and secure housing [20][21]. A holiday is not an "imperative reason" in France's sense, so this section documents what a visit would look like, not that one is advisable.

### One day
- Morning: livestock market, south-east of town, best early; ask before photographing animals [1]. Then the Central Market (Suuqa Bacadlaha) on Independence Road; ask before photographing stallholders [1]. Hotel car or Dhaweeye between them [1][5].
- Midday: Freedom Square and the MiG-17 War Memorial on Independence Avenue [29]; lunch at Damal Restaurant next to the Dahabshiil Business Centre (Somali and international food) [1]. Lunch modelled at US$5.04 [C cost.living].
- Afternoon: National Museum of Somaliland, 26 June Street (opened July 2024; entry fee and hours not found) [14]. On a Friday, watch the midday prayer crowd outside Jama Mosque instead (non-Muslims cannot enter) [1].
- Evening: dinner at Sultan Restaurant on Jigjiga-Yar Road (opens 18:00) or Timeout Hargeisa [26][1]; a camel-milk milkshake, popular per Wikivoyage [1]. Back to the hotel by pre-booked car.

### One week
- Day 1: arrival, visa on arrival [C admin.visa]; hotel (Ambassador on Airport Road helps with visas and tours) [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: Laas Geel rock art, about 50 km north-east off the Berbera road (Wikivoyage; about 48 km in a straight line from Wikipedia coordinates) [1][11]. Ten painted alcoves of cattle, herders, a dog and a giraffe, brought to international attention by a French team in 2002 [11]. Wikivoyage: a ministry letter and a US$20 fee, a guide, possibly an SPU escort; a 4x4 with driver and guard cost US$80 in 2017 (old) [1]. Outside the city, France's red zone applies [20].
- Day 4 (daily life): a morning at Gobonimo market or the Central Market, then Friday prayers at Jama Mosque if the day falls on a Friday [1][6]; or a match at Hargeisa Stadium if one is scheduled (no fixture source) [15]. The point is to see a normal weekday and the traffic at commercial hubs [3].
- Day 5: Jigjiga Yar and the centre as a resident would see them (house visits if relocating); Naasa Hablood seen from town [12]. Evening at Timeout Hargeisa [1].
- Day 6–7: Berbera, about 160 km and three hours by road, for Batalale or Bathela beach, snorkelling or diving (the Maan-Soor branch there offers scuba) [19][1]. Berbera is orange; the road is red per France and needs an SPU escort [20][1]. Avoid June–September heat [19]. Fly out from Hargeisa, or from Berbera airport if a route exists (none confirmed) [C daily.transport].
- Seasonal option: the Hargeysa International Book Fair at the Hargeysa Cultural Centre each July (2026: 25–30 July) [18].
