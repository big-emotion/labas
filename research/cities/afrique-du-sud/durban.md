- [business.startup] StartupBlink 2026 places Durban 601st globally (up 43 places, 47% growth), far behind Cape Town (114th) and Johannesburg (122nd). — ITWeb, 2026-05-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] Several roads in Springtown (north Durban) had no running water for nine days from 4 Feb 2026; municipal tankers were not enough; the city blamed low pressure and heatwave demand. — Sunday Tribune (IOL), ~2026-02-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.water] Springtown residents say disruptions have recurred since mid-2025; the outage hit the Sherwood reservoir zone (Morningside, Musgrave, Sydenham, Springfield and others) too. — Sunday Tribune (IOL), ~2026-02-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Minibus taxis are what most locals use (R10–25 per trip ≈ €0.53–1.34); municipal buses from R15; the People Mover links centre and seafront; Uber/Bolt ~R10/km. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Monthly public-transport pass ~R464 (≈ €25), but many expats rely on a car because public transport is thin outside the centre. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Minibus taxis are rated higher-risk nationally (theft, violence, erratic driving). — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Ethiopian Airlines is due to resume daily Durban–Addis Ababa flights on 11 Dec 2026 — a direct hub link to the rest of Africa. — Wikipedia "Durban", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [health.care] Private clinics are run by Netcare and Life Healthcare; Umhlanga has several reference private hospitals; private health insurance R3,000–8,000/month (≈ €160–428). — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Doctors at Inkosi Albert Luthuli Central Hospital (the province's main public referral hospital) said it ran ~40% below surgical capacity, with an orthopaedic waiting list over 1,300 and first elective dates in March 2028 (claims not confirmed by the department). — Spotlight, 2025-09-01, local-media, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] No French lycée in Durban: options are CNED distance learning, the Alliance Française, or boarding at the Johannesburg or Cape Town lycées. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Private schools R84,000–130,000/year (≈ €4,492–6,952), e.g. Crawford International (La Lucia) and Reddam House (Umhlanga). — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Good public schools R30,000–60,000/year (≈ €1,604–3,209); Westville Boys' High and Durban Girls' High cited with >96% pass rates; school year January–December. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] UKZN has 35,000+ students on five campuses; non-SADC international undergraduates pay about US$18,300/year. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Jan–Mar 2026: Phoenix ranked 9th nationally for serious crime (1,612 cases), Chatsworth 15th (1,463), and Verulam 2nd in KwaZulu-Natal for murder (26). — The Post (IOL), ~2026-05-25, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Lower crime is reported in the north (Umhlanga, La Lucia, Durban North) and west (Westville, Hillcrest) — no statistics cited; homes typically have alarms, electric fencing and private security. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: low

- [safety.general] The UK safety page carries no Durban- or King Shaka airport-specific warning. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high

- [safety.women] Women and solo travellers have reported unwanted attention and assaults; drink spiking occurs (national advice). — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.food] Bunny chow (curry in a hollowed loaf) is the signature dish; Florida Road has restaurants, bars and cafés; Umhlanga Village has upscale seaside dining. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium

- [social.nightlife] Described as a "sleepy town" with little nightlife compared with Johannesburg and Pretoria. — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [social.culture] First languages (2011, city): English 49.8%, Zulu 33.1%, Xhosa 5.9%, Afrikaans 3.6%. — Wikipedia "Durban", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Museums: Phansi (Zulu beadwork, traditional art), KwaMuhle (apartheid history), Natural Science Museum (free, in the city hall). — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Alliance Française de Durban (nearly 90 years old) sold its Morningside building in 2025 and now runs classes online and at partner venues; it is the only DELF/DALF/TEF centre in Durban and also teaches isiZulu. — Alliance Française de Durban, undated (© 2026), company, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Golden Mile is a patrolled beachfront for swimming, surfing and walking; uShaka Marine World adult entry ~R200 (≈ €11). — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Drakensberg mountains are 2–3 hours' drive for hiking. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium

- [climate.daily] Humid subtropical: summers ~24 °C average with ~80% humidity in Jan–Feb; wettest months Nov–Jan (~120 mm each); winters ~17 °C, dry and frost-free; sunniest May–July. — Wikipedia "Durban", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] 300+ sunny days a year, 20–25 °C average year-round. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: low
- [community.expat] About 150,000 expats, mainly Indian, British, Zimbabwean, Mozambican and Chinese; the Durban section of Expat.com's forum has ~601 members. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: low

- [rights.racism] On 17 Apr 2026 about ten men broke into a foreign national's shop in Durban and assaulted those inside with whips, golf clubs, pepper spray and stun guns; no police came. — Human Rights Watch, 2026-05-20, intl-media, confidence: high

- [rights.racism] Nationally, 86,596 foreign nationals were processed for deportation or repatriation between 14 June and 20 Aug 2026 (mostly Malawians, Zimbabweans, Mozambicans). — Al Jazeera (citing the Inter-Ministerial Committee on Migration), 2026-09-28, intl-media, confidence: high

- [admin.visa] Retired Person Visa: no minimum age, ≥R37,000/month guaranteed income (≈ €1,979), 4 years renewable, no work allowed, private health insurance and police clearances required. — Expat.com, 2025-12-09, company, confidence: medium
