## Neighbourhoods

### Rosebank
- Feel: mixed residential and commercial suburb that grew around Rosebank Mall (1976) and drew creative businesses north from the CBD after apartheid; small resident population (1,085 in 2011) but busy by day, with an arts precinct (Keyes Art Mile) [14]. In Your Pocket lists two Rosebank dance or live-music venues and four rooftop bars, so it is livelier at night than the "Parks" suburbs [4][9].
- Rent: 1-bed R8,500–13,000/month (≈ €455–695), The Rio Times 2026-09-14 (city file). Annual lease, two-month deposit, utilities and security often billed on top (Expat.com 2025-12-23, city file).
- Getting around: ~6.8 km north of the CBD; Rosebank Gautrain station (opened 2011) links to Sandton, Pretoria and OR Tambo; Oxford Road (M9) and Jan Smuts Avenue (M27) are the road corridors [14]. Metrobus route 5C runs along Oxford Road to Sandton City and the Rosebank malls, weekends included, every 30–60 min, fare not given [11]. Gautrain airport trips carry a R155–185 surcharge (2025); the card costs R21 unless you tap a contactless bank card [10].
- Watch: do not walk to or from Gautrain stations after dark; taxi vs ride-hailing violence has hit areas near Gautrain stations — check plate and driver ID in the app (UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, city file).
- Places:
  - Rosebank Sunday Market (market, rooftop level 4 of Rosebank Mall, every Sunday 09:00–16:00, free) — In Your Pocket event page [5]
  - Rosebank Mall (mall) — Wikipedia "Rosebank, Gauteng" [14]
  - African Craft Market (craft market, Rosebank Mall, daily 9–18) — Wikivoyage Johannesburg/Sandton [11]
  - EWJ Park (small park next to Rosebank Library, Keyes Ave, closed weekends) — In Your Pocket picnic spots, 2026-10-22 [7]
  - Katzy's (bar, jazz/soul/house, The Firs) — In Your Pocket dancing guide [4]; Wikivoyage [11]
  - Marble (restaurant, Keyes Art Mile rooftop) — In Your Pocket rooftops [9]
  - St Martin's in-the-Veld (Anglican church, Cradock Ave) and Rosebank Catholic Church (Keyes Ave) — Wikipedia [14]
  - St Teresa's School (private girls' school) — Wikipedia [14]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" (walkable mall-and-gallery core, train to the airport); Q10 "Voyager facilement" (Gautrain to OR Tambo); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" at a moderate level; Q3 "Moyen".

### Sandton (with Morningside)
- Feel: the business and office core; sold as "maximum security" by The Rio Times (2026-09-14, city file); expats cluster in gated complexes in Sandton and Morningside (Expat.com, dossier). Joburg Pride drew thousands to Sandton on 31 Oct 2026 (Time Out, city file).
- Rent: 1-bed R9,000–14,000 (≈ €481–749), The Rio Times 2026-09-14; Sandton or Morningside 1-bed R8,000–14,000 (≈ €428–749), Expat.com 2025-12-23; 3-bed in a secure complex R25,000–45,000 (≈ €1,337–2,406) (all city file). A 1-bed 46 m² flat for sale in Morningside asked R699,000 (≈ €37,380) on Property24, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: Sandton Gautrain station to OR Tambo, Rosebank and Hatfield (Pretoria); avoid weekday rush 7–9 and 16–19; street-hailing taxis is illegal, book by phone or use the rank at Sandton City entrance 12 [11]. Wikivoyage quotes Johannesburg–Pretoria Gautrain at R46 and a R15 card, undated and older than the R21 card (2025) on the main page [10][11] — not used for budgets.
- Watch: same Gautrain-after-dark and ride-hailing warnings as Rosebank (FCDO, city file). Gauteng recorded 2,404 kidnappings in Jan–Mar 2026, 1,307 linked to carjackings (IOL ~2026-06, city file) — provincial, not Sandton-specific.
- Places:
  - Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square (mall and square, Mon–Sat 9–18, Sun 10–16) — Wikivoyage Johannesburg/Sandton [11]
  - Theatre on the Square (theatre, usually R100, undated) — [11]
  - Wanderers Stadium (cricket ground, Illovo) — [11]
  - Lycée Français Jules-Verne, Johannesburg campus (French AEFE school, Morningside) — Expat.com 2025-12-23 (city file)
  - Booth Night Club (club, amapiano/R&B/afrobeats, Parkmore) — In Your Pocket dancing guide [4]
  - Alto234 Bar (rooftop bar at The Leonardo, R390 entry with a glass of Champagne, booking essential) — In Your Pocket rooftops [9]
  - Hugh Wyndham Park (fenced playground, Dunkeld West) — In Your Pocket picnic spots [7]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" and Q10 "Ma carrière" (office core, Gautrain); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" as far as gated complexes go; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" via the Lycée campus in Morningside.

### Fourways and Bryanston
- Feel: walled-estate suburbs (The Rio Times 2026-09-14, city file); Wikipedia calls Fourways the fastest-developing hub in the Sandton region and says its estates (Dainfern, Cedar Lakes, Fourways Gardens…) grew partly because of crime [16].
- Rent: 1-bed Fourways R6,500–9,500 (≈ €348–508); 1-bed Bryanston R7,500–11,000 (≈ €401–588); 3-bed house from ~R16,000 in Fourways (≈ €856) (The Rio Times 2026-09-14, city file). A 3-bed house in Bryanston asked R5,915,000 to buy (≈ €316,310), Property24 read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: car-based; access via the N1 at Winnie Mandela Drive (R511) or Witkoppen Road (R564); Pretoria ~55 km, Hartbeespoort and the Magaliesberg ~30 min north-west [16]. A car is described as "indispensable" in Johannesburg; petrol R29.81/l nationally on 2026-11-09 (city file).
- Watch: Midrand (north, not Fourways itself) went ~6 days without water after the Zuikerbosch pump failure in Jan–Feb 2026 (Daily Maverick 2026-02-06, city file). Nothing Fourways-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Fourways Mall (mall, Mon–Sat 9–19) — Wikivoyage Johannesburg/Sandton [11]; Wikipedia [16]
  - Montecasino and Montecasino Bird Gardens (leisure complex, bird park Mon–Fri 8:30–17:00, weekends to 18:00) — Wikivoyage [11]
  - Dainfern College (school, grades 0–12) — Wikipedia "Fourways" (unsourced section) [16]
  - Crawford schools, Lonehill and Craigavon (private schools) — Wikipedia [16]
  - Fourways High School (government school, grades 8–12) — Wikipedia [16]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (estates, schools, 3-bed houses); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Magaliesberg half an hour away); Q3 "Moyen".

### Melville
- Feel: "bohemian" student suburb near the University of Johannesburg and Wits; cafés, restaurants and bars along 7th Street; 3,355 residents (2011) [15]. A search result summary describes it as more suited to adults than children (expat guide, not opened).
- Rent: no Melville figure found. Citywide Numbeo 1-bed outside the centre ~R6,747 (≈ €361), 2026-11-13 (city file) — a proxy only.
- Getting around: west of the CBD, Region B [15]; no transport line or fare sourced; ride-hailing (8 km taxi ≈ R136, livingcost 2026-06-21, city file).
- Watch: in Feb 2026 Melville had no water for more than 14 days and 7th Street businesses closed (Daily Maverick 2026-02-06, city file).
- Places:

### Braamfontein and the inner city (Newtown, Maboneng)
- Feel: Wits University's district, known for a Saturday market and nightlife [10]; Maboneng has 24-hour security and a Sunday market at Arts on Main [10]. Wikivoyage says walking alone late at night is not recommended and to detour around squatted buildings [12].
- Rent: no suburb figure found. Numbeo city-centre 1-bed ~R8,565 (≈ €458), 2026-11-13 (city file) — city-centre proxy.
- Getting around: Rea Vaya bus lanes with stops every ~500 m, fares not found [10]; Park Station is the rail hub.
- Watch: some residents describe Park Station as dirty and dangerous with frequent robberies (Daily Maverick 2026-07-24); the 6 Sep 2026 storm flooded parts of the CBD (EWN); on 29 Apr 2026 an anti-"illegal foreigner" march went from Mary Fitzgerald Square to Beyers Naude Square in Newtown and shops closed (Daily Maverick 2026-04-30); vigilante groups targeted immigrant-run shops in nearby Yeoville (Daily Maverick/GroundUp 2026-08-03) (all city file).

### Soweto (Orlando West)
- Feel: the historic township where Mandela and Tutu both lived on Vilakazi Street; Wikivoyage calls it fairly safe for visitors but hard to drive alone, and suggests meeting B&B hosts on a main road [13]. Native Rebels in Jabavu is on the city's dance map [4].
- Rent: none found (Property24 page 404). No proxy given: the Numbeo outside-centre figure is not representative of a township.
- Getting around: MetroRail from Park Station to Naledi, Oberholzer or Vereeniging; Phefeni station serves Orlando West [13]. Minibus taxis are what many use; FCDO rates them higher-risk (city file).
- Watch: nothing Soweto-specific dated found beyond the national xenophobia items in the city file.
- Places:
  - Nelson Mandela National Museum / Mandela House (museum, Vilakazi St, daily 09:30–17:00) — Wikivoyage Soweto [13]
  - Hector Pieterson Museum (museum, Mon–Sat 10–17, Sun 10–16) — [13]
  - Regina Mundi (Catholic church, South Africa's largest) — [13]
  - Maponya Mall (mall, Mon–Sat 09–18) — [13]
  - Sakhumzi Restaurant (Vilakazi St) and Wandies (Dube, mogodu and pap) — [13]
  - Native Rebels (bar/club, Jabavu) — In Your Pocket dancing guide [4]
  - Petrus Molefe Eco Park (park) — [13]

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Apartheid Museum, Ormonde (Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00; fee via webtickets, not shown) [17]. Ride-hail.
- Midday: Constitution Hill, Braamfontein (daily 09:00–16:00) and lunch in Braamfontein [6][12].

- Evening: sunset at The Wilds, Houghton (to 18:00) or Flames terrace at The Westcliff; dinner on Parkhurst's 4th Avenue or Melville's 7th Street [9][3][15].

### One week
- Day 1: Rosebank — Keyes Art Mile, African Craft Market, rooftop dinner (Marble) [9][11][14].
- Day 2: Inner city with a guided walk — Dlala Nje (Hillbrow on Saturday, Yeoville on Sunday; ~R480 adult, Oct 2026) or Johannesburg Heritage Foundation (~R300, Oct 2026) [10][8].
- Day 3: Soweto — Mandela House, Hector Pieterson Museum, lunch at Sakhumzi on Vilakazi Street; Wikivoyage recommends an accredited guide [13]. City Sightseeing's Soweto add-on R679 (Dec 2024) [10].
- Day 4 (trip out): Cradle of Humankind — Maropeng and Sterkfontein Caves (daily 09:00–16:00, booked online) [6][10].
- Day 5 (trip out): Pretoria by Gautrain from Rosebank or Sandton [10][11].
- Day 6: Daily-life moment — morning commute on the Gautrain at Sandton during the 7–9 rush [11], then Sunday-style Mass at Regina Mundi in Soweto if the day is a Sunday [13]; evening Melville.
- Day 7: Rosebank Sunday Market (if Sunday) and the Johannesburg Botanical Gardens at Emmarentia Dam [5][7]. Alternative 2-day trip out: Pilanesberg (~2 h) [10].
