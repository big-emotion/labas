## Neighbourhoods

### Sea Point (Atlantic Seaboard)
- Feel: densely populated seafront suburb between Signal Hill and the Atlantic, long the city's Jewish hub; Time Out called it one of the world's coolest neighbourhoods in 2022 and 2023; the promenade is used for walking, jogging and gatherings [8]. Expat.com names Sea Point and Camps Bay as the expat favourites (city file).
- Rent: unfurnished 40 m² studio R16,000–17,000/month (≈ €856–909); furnished 1-bed 45 m² R19,000 (≈ €1,016); 2-bed 104 m² R30,000 (≈ €1,604) — Property24, 238 listings, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: a few km west of the CBD [8]; MyCiTi routes 108 and 109 to the V&A Waterfront, Adderley Street (CBD) and Hout Bay [8]. MyCiTi 0–5 km R19.50 peak / R15 off-peak, 7-day pass R420, from 2026-07-01 (city file).
- Watch: attacks on hikers on Lion's Head and Signal Hill — stay on busy trails, avoid early mornings and evenings, groups of 4+ (UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14); a couple was knifed in daylight on Lion's Head in Jan 2026 (EWN 2026-01-21); rabies found in seals along the Peninsula; an anti-immigrant march on 16 June 2026 drew ~40 people (Daily Maverick 2026-06-21) (all city file).
- Places:
  - Sea Point Promenade (seafront walkway) — Wikipedia "Sea Point" [8]
  - Sea Point seawater pool (Olympic-size public pool next to the beach) — [8]
  - The Duchess of Wisbeach (restaurant/bar, Regent Road) and Mojo Market (food market with live entertainment, Regent Road) — Cape Town Tourism, 2026-10-20 [5]
  - Sea Point Primary and Sea Point High School (public schools) — [8]
  - French School of Cape Town, primary campus (French school) — [8]
  - Sea Point Methodist Church (church, Main Road) — [8]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" (walkable, MyCiTi, promenade); Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (≈ €850+ for a studio); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### City Bowl: Gardens, Bo-Kaap and the CBD
- Feel: Gardens is an affluent residential suburb under Table Mountain and the hub of the city's creative industry, with Kloof Street's restaurants and boutiques [12]; Bo-Kaap, on Signal Hill's slopes, is the historic centre of Cape Malay culture, about 57% Muslim, with gentrification pressure on long-term residents [13]. Expatica describes the CBD as feeling quieter and safer than other areas (2025-06-03, city file). Long, Bree and Kloof Streets are the three central night-out strips (city file).
- Rent: Numbeo city-centre 1-bed ~R17,188 (≈ €919), 2026-11-13; an 18 m² City Centre micro-studio advertised at R9,950 (≈ €532), Property24, low confidence (city file).
- Getting around: walkable centre; MyCiTi routes 101 (Vredehoek, Civic Centre), 113 (Adderley St, V&A) and 107 (Camps Bay) serve Gardens [12]. Table Mountain trailhead via MyCiTi to Kloof Nek plus free shuttle [4].
- Watch: Cape Town Central police station ranked first nationally for community-reported serious crimes in Oct–Dec 2025 (EWN 2026-03-03, city file).
- Places:
  - Company's Garden (public park, South Africa's oldest garden) — Wikipedia "Gardens, Cape Town" [12]
  - Lycée Français du Cap, secondary division (French AEFE school, Gardens) — [12]; fees R100,000–135,000/year (Expat.com, city file)
  - Labia Theatre (art-house cinema, 68 Orange St, R90, Jul 2018 price) — [4][12]
  - Auwal Mosque (mosque, Dorp Street, built 1794) — Wikipedia "Bo-Kaap" [13]
  - Bo-Kaap Museum (museum, 71 Wale St, Mon–Sat 10–17, R20) — [4][13]
  - Greenmarket Square (flea market, 54 Shortmarket St, prices negotiable) — Wikivoyage [4]
  - The Story Horse (bar, Bree St; daily happy hour 16:00–17:30) — Cape Town Tourism [5]
  - Rita's Cocktail Bar and Yours Truly (bar; café/social venue, Kloof St) — Cape Town Tourism [5]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" and Q5 "Création, culture" (creative industry, central networking); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Bo-Kaap, District Six nearby); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" for the Lycée; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Woodstock
- Feel: one of the oldest suburbs, ~1 km east of the CBD, mixed residential/commercial/light-industrial; Victorian terraces renovated by young professionals and warehouses turned into restaurants and offices since the late 1990s [11]. Time Out links remote workers and short-term rentals to rising rents in central neighbourhoods (2026-01-27, city file).
- Rent: 1-bed 41 m² R12,500/month (≈ €668); a room in a 4-bed house share R6,500 (≈ €348); only 104 of 483 Woodstock rental listings are residential — Property24, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: Woodstock railway station [11]; MyCiTi route 261 stops outside the Old Biscuit Mill [7].
- Watch: nothing dated and area-specific sourced (Wikipedia's crime remarks are historical) [11].
- Places:
  - The Old Biscuit Mill and Neighbourgoods Market (market on Saturdays, 373–375 Albert Road; R10 all-day parking at the College of Cape Town opposite) — Old Biscuit Mill site [7]
  - Espresso Lab Microroasters (café, at the Mill) — [7]
  - The Potluck Club (restaurant, at the Mill) — [7]
  - The Test Kitchen (restaurant, Woodstock) — Wikivoyage [4]
  - Woodstock railway station (Metrorail) — Wikipedia "Woodstock, Cape Town" [11]
- Fits: Q5 "Création, culture" and Q5 "Télétravail"; Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen" (cheapest sourced 1-bed near the centre, or a room share for Q3 "Serré"); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Observatory ("Obs")
- Feel: student neighbourhood linked to UCT and Groote Schuur Hospital, with an alternative character; Lower Main Road is the commercial heart with restaurants, bars and pool halls [10][4]. Cape Town Tourism lists it for live bands and alternative nights (city file).
- Rent: 31 m² studio R12,500/month (≈ €668); 2-bed 75 m² R19,000 (≈ €1,016); 3-bed 90 m² R34,000 (≈ €1,818) — Property24, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: Observatory station on the Southern Line, trains about every 40 min on weekdays and hourly on Sundays [10]. Metrorail runs Mon–Sat only per Wikivoyage, which conflicts with Wikipedia's Sunday service note [4][10] — check before relying on it.
- Watch: nothing area-specific dated sourced.
- Places:
  - Armchair Theatre (live music, comedy, quizzes, Lower Main Road) — Cape Town Tourism, 2026-10-20 [5]
  - Trenchtown (karaoke venue with garden decks) — [5]
  - Observatory Liquors (bottle store, ~95 years old) and St Peter's Square (shopping complex) — Wikipedia "Observatory, Cape Town" [10]
  - Hartleyvale Stadium (soccer and hockey) and the Obs outdoor swimming pool — [10]
  - Cape Town Science Centre (science museum, Mon–Sat 09:00–16:30, R55) — Wikivoyage [4]
  - St Michael and All Angels (Anglican church) — [10]
  - Groote Schuur Hospital (public academic hospital) — [10]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (bands, karaoke); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (hospital and UCT nearby); Q3 "Moyen".

### Southern Suburbs: Rondebosch, Newlands, Claremont
- Feel: Rondebosch is primarily residential, with UCT's main campus on Devil's Peak and a high density of schools; Main Road is its commercial centre [9]. Expatica says the Southern Suburbs rank highly for schools (2025-06-03, city file). Rainfall here is nearer 1,000 mm/year vs ~515 mm citywide (Wikipedia, city file).
- Rent: no suburb figure found. Numbeo outside-centre 1-bed ~R11,098 (≈ €593), 2026-11-13 (city file), as a proxy; for a family home the closest sourced figure is Observatory's 3-bed R34,000 (≈ €1,818).
- Getting around: Rondebosch railway station on the Southern Line is the main public transport [9]; Cape Town had the worst congestion in South Africa in 2023 (Expatica, city file).
- Watch: on 20 June 2026 an anti-immigrant march from Wynberg toward Newlands drew ~60–100 people and most shops on the route closed (Daily Maverick 2026-06-21, city file).
- Places:
  - Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden (garden, Rhodes Dr, Newlands, daily 08:00–18:00/19:00, R65 adult, R15 child, undated) — Wikivoyage [4]
  - Rondebosch Common (fynbos conservation area and recreation ground) — Wikipedia "Rondebosch" [9]
  - Cavendish Square (mall, Claremont, Mon–Sat 09–19, Sun 10–17) — Wikivoyage [4]
  - Rondebosch Boys' High, Rustenburg Girls' High, SACS, Westerford High (schools) and Diocesan College/Bishops (private) — [9]; Bishops, SACS and Rondebosch cited at R70,000–180,000/year (Expat.com, city file)
  - Stones Claremont (pool and live-music venue, Main Road) — Cape Town Tourism [5]
  - University of Cape Town, main campus — [9]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and Q10 "La santé et l'école"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" (Kirstenbosch and Table Mountain's back slopes).

### Muizenberg (False Bay)
- Feel: Art Deco former resort and the city's main surf spot, ~27 km south-east of the CBD by road; mixed population (39% Black African, 28% White, 26% Coloured in 2011) [6]. Cape Town Tourism lists its beach bars (city file).
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: Muizenberg station on the Southern Line to Cape Town, Kalk Bay, Fish Hoek and Simon's Town [6]; Wikivoyage advises avoiding the Simon's Town line after 18:30 [4].
- Watch: beachfront seawall upgrade under way since Feb 2025, beach open, completion targeted end 2026; historic beach huts dismantled July 2026 [6]; shark-spotting service operates; strong rip currents — swim between red and yellow flags (FCDO, city file).
- Places:
  - Surfer's Corner (surf beach with surf shops and cafés) — Wikipedia "Muizenberg" [6]
  - Hank's Bar & Vinyl Lounge and The Village Inn (bars) — Cape Town Tourism [5]
  - Casa Labia (restaurant and music venue) — [6]
  - Muizenberg Shul (synagogue, Camp Road) — [6]
  - Zandvlei estuary (yacht and canoe clubs) — [6]
  - The Brass Bell, Kalk Bay (fish restaurant, next stop south) — Wikivoyage [4]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q5 "Télétravail" (if commuting is rare); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices are from the city file (dated there) unless marked. Weekly = monthly ÷ 4.33. Quantities are stated assumptions.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Sea Point — promenade, MyCiTi to the centre, studios from R16,000 [8].
- Mon: work from home; promenade run at lunchtime [8].
- Tue: MyCiTi 108/109 to the V&A Waterfront; laptop day near Time Out Market, where Nomad Week opened in Jan 2026 [8][15].
- Wed: Sea Point seawater pool after work [8].

- Sat: Oranjezicht City Farm Market at Granger Bay (Sat 08:00–14:30) [14]; evening per Q2.

- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — The Waiting Room, Long Street [5]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — The Duchess of Wisbeach, Regent Road [5]; "Tranquille chez moi" — sunset from the promenade, then home.
- Weekend outings (Q8): "À la plage" — Clifton or Camps Bay [4]. "En montagne, en forêt" — Platteklip Gorge up Table Mountain (~2 h) or the cable car (R450 online) [4]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" — no savanna or desert destination sourced within a weekend from Cape Town; not realistic on current sources. "Je reste en ville" — Zeitz MOCAA (R190) and the V&A [4].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent R16,000–17,000 ÷ 4.33 = R3,695–3,926 (Property24)

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Table Mountain cable car (R450 return online, R490 at the station) or the Platteklip Gorge hike in a group [4]; MyCiTi to Kloof Nek.
- Midday: Bo-Kaap — Bo-Kaap Museum (R20), the Noon Gun at 12:00 Mon–Sat, Auwal Mosque from outside [4][13]; walk down to Company's Garden [12].
- Afternoon: District Six Museum (R40, R70 guided, Mon–Sat 09:00–16:00) or Zeitz MOCAA at the V&A (R190) [4].
- Evening: sunset on the Sea Point Promenade (MyCiTi 108/109), dinner on Bree Street [8][5].

### One week
- Day 1: City Bowl as above.
- Day 2: Robben Island ferry from the Nelson Mandela Gateway, V&A (~3.5 h; the site shows two price sets for non-South Africans, R600 or R1,400 adult — confirm) [17]; afternoon at the Waterfront.
- Day 3: Kirstenbosch (R65) and the Southern Suburbs; Groot Constantia tasting R75 [4].
- Day 4 (trip out): False Bay by Southern Line train — Muizenberg Surfer's Corner, Kalk Bay (Brass Bell), Simon's Town and Boulders penguins; avoid the line after 18:30 [4][6].
- Day 5 (trip out): Stellenbosch (~60 km) — Metrorail via Bellville or a ~R120 hostel van; Tourism Bureau walking tour R80; Vine Hopper R300 [16].
- Day 6: Daily-life moment — a Saturday morning at Neighbourgoods Market in Woodstock, then Langa: Guga S'thebe Arts & Culture Centre with a registered township tour (e.g. Sam's Cultural Tours) [7][4][19]. FCDO advises against MyCiTi to townships.
- Day 7: Sunday — Oranjezicht City Farm Market (08:30–14:30) and a swim at Clifton between the flags; or Sea Point Promenade alongside residents' Sunday walk [14][4][8].
