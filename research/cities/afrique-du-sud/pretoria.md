## Items

- [cost.rent] 1-bed: R5,500–9,500/month (≈ €294–508), average ~R7,300 (≈ €390); cheaper in Pretoria Central, Sunnyside, Pretoria North and parts of Arcadia, dearer in Brooklyn, Hatfield, Menlyn, Waterkloof, Lynnwood. — The African Vestor, updated 2026-09-21 (June 2026 data), blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Studio: R4,300–8,000/month (≈ €230–428), average ~R5,800 (≈ €310). — The African Vestor, 2026-09-21, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] 2-bed: R6,800–15,000/month (≈ €364–802), average ~R9,800 (≈ €524); R13,000–20,000 (≈ €695–1,070) in Waterkloof, Brooklyn and Menlyn. — The African Vestor, 2026-09-21, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Expat-oriented rentals in Waterkloof, Brooklyn and Groenkloof run ~R10,000–25,000/month (≈ €535–1,337); furnished units add ~R800–2,500. — The African Vestor, 2026-09-21, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Students make up ~25% of rental demand, around Hatfield, Arcadia and Sunnyside, peaking at the University of Pretoria's January–February intake. — The African Vestor, 2026-09-21, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Embassy demand drives rents up around Waterkloof and Brooklyn; diplomats are a main user of furnished rentals. — The African Vestor, 2026-09-21, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Cost of living is described as lower than the other big cities; many residents commute ~50 minutes to Johannesburg; heavy traffic is "inevitable". — Expatica, updated 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Pretoria hosts 134 embassies and high commissions — the second-largest concentration in the world after Washington, D.C. — Wikipedia "Pretoria", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Major employers in research and education: University of Pretoria, UNISA, Tshwane University of Technology, CSIR, HSRC, National Research Foundation. — Wikipedia "Pretoria", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [business.startup] StartupBlink 2026: Pretoria 377th globally, up 52 places, with 64.8% growth — the fastest-growing ecosystem among South African cities. — ITWeb, 2026-05-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] An explosion at the Watloo substation on 29 Sep 2026 knocked out at least 11 substations and large parts of Pretoria; about 30% of affected customers were still without power two days later. — EWN, 2026-10-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] The Gautrain links Hatfield and Pretoria station to Centurion, Midrand, Sandton, OR Tambo airport, Rosebank and Johannesburg. — Wikipedia "Pretoria", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Gautrain is considered a generally safer option between Pretoria, Johannesburg and OR Tambo, but do not walk to or from stations after dark. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Lycée Français Jules-Verne (AEFE) has a Pretoria annex to its Johannesburg campus; fees ~R100,000–135,000/year (≈ €5,348–7,219). — Expat.com Afrique du Sud guide, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Criminals have followed travellers from OR Tambo airport and robbed them, often at gunpoint (applies to Pretoria arrivals). — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] High crime rates; mass demonstrations often take place near the Union Buildings. — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [rights.racism] On 28 Apr 2026 an anti-immigrant march of 300+ (about 50 carrying knobkerries and shields) passed through Sunnyside; a street vendor was attacked, most shops shut, and the memorandum asked for army deployment in Sunnyside and Hillbrow. — GroundUp, 2026-04-28, local-media, confidence: high
- [rights.racism] On 30 June 2026 marchers went from Church Square to Sunnyside police station; some pointed sjamboks at foreign nationals watching from balconies while chanting "Mabahambe!" ("they must go"); heavy police presence with armoured vehicles. — IOL, 2026-06-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [social.culture] First languages (2011, city proper): Afrikaans 47.7%, English 16.4%, Sepedi 8.0%, Setswana 5.4% — Afrikaans is far more present than in Johannesburg. — Wikipedia "Pretoria", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Described as orderly and reserved, with quieter nightlife and a more leisurely pace; rugby is popular. — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [nature.access] About 70,000 jacaranda trees ("Jacaranda City"); locals use the National Botanical Garden and Rietvlei Nature Reserve. — Wikipedia "Pretoria" (accessed 2026-11-14), intl-media (encyclopaedia); Expatica (2025-06-03), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] At ~1,339 m: hot, rainy summers (Jan average max ~29 °C), dry mild winters with nights around 4.6 °C in July and recent lows of 2 to −5 °C; ~662 mm rain, almost all in summer. — Wikipedia "Pretoria", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
