- [cost.rent] 1-bed in the city centre ~R17,188/month (≈ €919); outside the centre ~R11,098 (≈ €593) — roughly double Johannesburg or Durban. — Numbeo, updated 2026-11-13 (via dossier verification log), company (crowdsourced), confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Average Cape Town rent was R12,561/month in Q2 2026 (≈ €672), up R1,107 or 9.7% in a year; the Western Cape is the most expensive province for renters. — EWN (PayProp Rental Index), 2026-09-18, local-media, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Sea Point and Camps Bay are named as the neighbourhoods popular with expats. — Expat.com Afrique du Sud guide, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Remote workers and short-term rentals are "frequently linked" to rising rents, gentrification and shrinking affordable housing in central neighbourhoods. — Time Out Cape Town, 2026-01-27, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] About 60% of residents live in townships and informal settlements (undated figure). — Wikipedia "Cape Town", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Yzerfontein, Churchhaven and St Helena Bay (West Coast, outside the metro core) are cited as among the safest areas; the Southern Suburbs rank highly for schools. — Expatica, updated 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Inexpensive meal ~R200 (≈ €10.70); dinner for two ~R700 (≈ €37); single person's monthly costs excluding rent ~R12,054 (≈ €645). — Numbeo, updated 2026-11-13 (via dossier), company (crowdsourced), confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Expatica sums Cape Town up as high cost of living with low wages (editorial assessment). — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Private security fees R500–1,000/month for a single person (≈ €27–53); domestic help R800–1,600 (≈ €43–86) — national estimates. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.salary] National average take-home pay was just over R21,500 in March 2026 (≈ €1,150), down 1% in real terms in Q1 2026 — not Cape Town-specific. — EWN (PayInc index), 2026-04-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] Unemployment was 23% in 2024, nearly 10 points below the national average; growth sectors are technology, finance, real estate and tourism. — Wikipedia "Cape Town", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Sought-after profiles nationally: engineers, developers, data scientists, cybersecurity, accountants, medical staff, renewable-energy specialists; English dominates work life; work permit mandatory. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] StartupBlink 2026 ranks Cape Town 114th in the world (up 24 places), South Africa's top ecosystem and 3rd in Africa, with 39% year-on-year growth. — Wesgro, 2026-06-05, official, confidence: high
- [business.startup] Fintech leads the local scene (e.g. Stitch's US$55 m Series B), alongside clean energy, biotech and edtech via UVU Africa. — Wesgro, 2026-06-05, official, confidence: medium
- [work.remote] Cape Town's second Nomad Week (Jan 2026) drew about 200 remote workers, freelancers and founders, with a mentorship scheme pairing them with local startups. — Time Out Cape Town, 2026-01-27, local-media, confidence: high
- [work.remote] Remote-work visa: income above ~R650,000/year (≈ €34,759) from a foreign employer, valid up to 36 months, applied for at a South African mission abroad; the source is inconsistent on the threshold. — Xpatweb FAQ, undated (© 2026), company, confidence: low
- [daily.water] In Sept 2026 the city's dams were at ~75.5%, 16.7 points lower than a year earlier, and water restrictions were under consideration with a decision expected by October. — EWN, 2026-09-17, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.water] During the 2015–2018 drought daily consumption fell from 1.2 bn to 516 m litres — the memory of "Day Zero" shapes water habits. — Wikipedia "Cape Town" (citing Oxfam), accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] MyCiTi bus fares from 1 July 2026: 0–5 km R19.50 peak / R15 off-peak (≈ €1.04 / €0.80); 10–20 km R32.50 / R25.50; myconnect card R40. — MyCiTi (City of Cape Town), 2026 (fares effective 2026-07-01), official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] MyCiTi passes 2026/27: day R130 (≈ €7), 7-day R420 (≈ €22), monthly R1,500 (≈ €80); peak fares only weekdays 06:45–08:00 and 16:15–17:30. — MyCiTi, 2026, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] MyCiTi is considered safer in the CBD and Atlantic Seaboard; FCDO advises against using it to reach townships after violent attacks in Khayelitsha. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] After attacks on the airport road, use the M3 and N2 rather than the R300, and the airport approach road (N2 exit 16) rather than Borcherd's Quarry Road. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Cape Town had the worst traffic congestion in South Africa in 2023 and the 9th worst in the world. — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A 2023 minibus-taxi strike caused 5 deaths, after a 2021 taxi turf war that killed 83. — Wikipedia "Cape Town", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media (encyclopaedia), confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Mobile plan with 10 GB R290–1,000/month; WhatsApp is the main channel for personal and professional messages. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Opening a local account early is strongly advised; daily payment/withdrawal limits are low by default and can be raised in the bank app. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] Private care preferred and paid upfront (GP R1,000–2,000 ≈ €53–107); the French consulate can supply lists of French-speaking doctors; hepatitis A and typhoid jabs recommended for long stays. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Lycée Français du Cap (AEFE) charges ~R100,000–135,000/year (≈ €5,348–7,219) depending on level. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Local private schools ~R70,000–180,000/year (≈ €3,743–9,626): Bishops, SACS, Rondebosch (boys), Herschel and St Cyprian's (girls), Camps Bay School, Reddam (co-ed). — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] International options: Deutsche Internationale Schule Kapstadt and the International School of Cape Town; places limited, some need pre-registration or English tests. — Expat.com, 2025-12-23, company, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Western Cape recorded 1,071 murders in Apr–Jun 2026 (77 fewer than a year earlier); Mfuleni was the national murder hotspot with 73; ten Cape Town police stations sit in the national top 30 for murder. — EWN, 2026-09-15, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Cape Town Central police station ranked first nationally for community-reported serious crimes in Oct–Dec 2025; Mfuleni, Delft, Mitchells Plain, Kraaifontein and Nyanga also in the top 30. — EWN, 2026-03-03, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In Jan 2026 a couple was attacked with knives in daylight on Lion's Head, outside patrolled zones; the City's safety MMC said the mountain is "very difficult to securitise". — EWN, 2026-01-21, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Attacks on hikers on Lion's Head and Signal Hill: stay on busy trails, avoid early mornings/evenings, hike in groups of 4 or more. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Strong rip currents drown people every year — swim between red and yellow flags; rabies has been found in seals along the Cape Peninsula. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Wildfires can occur in the city and in Table Mountain National Park. — UK FCDO, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Murder rate ~68 per 100,000 (2023); the CBD is described as feeling quieter and safer than other areas. — Expatica, 2025-06-03, intl-media, confidence: medium
