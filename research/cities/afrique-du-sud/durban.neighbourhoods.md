## Neighbourhoods

### uMhlanga (Umhlanga Rocks and Umhlanga Ridge)
- Feel: resort village on the coast plus a newer inland office-and-retail hub (Umhlanga Ridge, Ridgeside) on former cane land; population swells in summer; residents split between missing the village feel and valuing new infrastructure [6]. Expat.com calls it the most popular area with expat professionals and the most expensive (2025-12-09, city file).
- Rent: furnished 1-bed 45 m² in New Town Centre R11,500/month (≈ €615); 2-bed 78 m² R12,500 (≈ €668); 3-bed 250 m² R28,000 (≈ €1,497) — Property24, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: ~16 km from Durban's centre; M4 coastal road to Durban and Ballito, N2 to King Shaka airport [6]; the airport is about 20 km away [11]. Car-based; Uber/Bolt ~R10/km (Expat.com, city file).
- Watch: beaches closed for an extended period by sewage contamination in 2022, pump station repaired by 23 Nov 2022 (old); the Durban North police precinct, which includes uMhlanga, had a murder rate of about 7 per 100,000 in 2016–19 (old figures) [6]. Expat.com lists the north as lower-crime without statistics (city file).
- Places:
  - uMhlanga promenade (Ken O'Connor Promenade), lighthouse and Whalebone Pier (seafront walk) — Wikipedia "uMhlanga" [6]; Wikivoyage [11]
  - Bronze Beach and uMhlanga Main Beach (lifeguard-patrolled beaches) — [6]
  - uMhlanga Lagoon Nature Reserve (forest reserve, daily 06:00–17:00) — [11]
  - Gateway Theatre of Shopping (mall, 1 Palm Blvd) — [6][11]
  - KZN Sharks Board (shark-safety institution and visitor centre, 1a Herrwood Dr, Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00) — [11]
  - Netcare uMhlanga Hospital and Busamed Gateway Private Hospital (private hospitals) — [6]
  - Reddam House Umhlanga (private school, R84,000–130,000/year band) — Expat.com (city file)
  - Chris Saunders Park (park, Umhlanga Ridge) — [6]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (Ridgeside office node); Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" relative to the rest of the city (old figures only); Q10 "Voyager facilement" (airport ~20 km).

### Morningside and the Berea (Florida Road, Musgrave)
- Feel: affluent ridge suburb above the city with Edwardian and Victorian houses; Florida Road in its south-west is the nightlife strip of shops, restaurants, pubs and bistros [7]. Expat.com describes an urban feel (city file).
- Rent: 1-bed 70 m² R6,500/month (≈ €348); 1-bed 85 m² R8,165 (≈ €437); 2-bed 104 m² R8,000 (≈ €428); 3-bed 110 m² R13,500 (≈ €722) — Property24, read 2026-11-14 (city file).
- Getting around: ~2 km inland from the Indian Ocean on the south bank of the Umgeni; closest residential suburb to Moses Mabhida Stadium [7]. Minibus taxis R10–25 a trip, municipal buses from R15, monthly pass ~R464 (Expat.com, city file).
- Watch: the Feb 2026 outage in the Sherwood reservoir zone hit Morningside and Musgrave among others; residents of nearby areas report recurring cuts since mid-2025 (Sunday Tribune ~2026-02-10, city file).
- Places:
  - Florida Road (restaurant and bar strip) — Wikipedia "Morningside, Durban" [7]; Wikivoyage [10]
  - Mitchell Park (park with small zoo, 10 Ferndale Rd, daily 08:00–16:00) — [7][10]
  - Durban Botanic Gardens (garden, 9A John Zikhali Road, Musgrave, daily 07:30–17:45 in summer) — official site [16]; Wikipedia [15]
  - Morningside Sports Club (sports club) — [7]
  - Kenmore Primary School (school) — [7]
  - Moses Mabhida Stadium (stadium, home of AmaZulu FC; funicular to the arch, 550-step walk) — Wikipedia [14]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and Q4 "Seul·e" (cheapest sourced 1-bed, walkable strip); Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Durban North
- Feel: between the N2 and the coast north of the Umgeni; residential areas among Durban's most affluent; Broadway is its commercial and school hub [8]. Expat.com: near the beach and cheaper than uMhlanga (city file).
- Rent: no suburb figure found; Expat.com's outskirts 1-bed R6,136 (≈ €328) is a citywide proxy (city file).
- Getting around: M12 (Kenneth Kaunda Rd) to uMhlanga Ridge and Stamford Hill; M4 to the city [8].
- Watch: Springtown (north Durban) had no running water for nine days from 4 Feb 2026 (Sunday Tribune, city file); Blue Lagoon was among beaches closed for E. coli in Dec 2025 (Time Out Durban 2026-01-21, city file); Glenashley Beach is officially non-swimming [8].
- Places:
  - Durban North Japanese Gardens (park, free) — Wikivoyage [10]; Wikipedia [8]
  - Umgeni River Bird Park (bird park) — [8]
  - Virginia Beach and Beachwood Mangroves Nature Reserve (beach; mangrove reserve) — [8]
  - Northwood School, Durban North College, Northlands Girls' High (government schools) — [8]
  - Virginia Bush Nature Reserve (reserve) — [8]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (government schools, parks); Q3 "Moyen"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Westville
- Feel: mainly residential inland suburb, mixed (42% White, 32.5% Black African, 22% Indian/Asian in 2011), ~10 km west of the CBD [9]. Expat.com: green, quiet, good schools, popular with families (city file).
- Rent: no suburb figure; Expat.com 3-bed R10,446 on the outskirts to R14,377 in the centre (≈ €559–769) as the citywide range (city file).
- Getting around: N2, N3, M13 and M19 meet near the E.B. Cloete interchange ("Spaghetti Junction") [9]; a car is the norm (Expat.com, city file).
- Watch: nothing Westville-specific dated sourced.
- Places:
  - The Pavilion (mall, second largest in Greater Durban) — Wikipedia "Westville" [9]
  - Palmiet Nature Reserve (reserve with ~15 km of trails) — [9]
  - Westville Boys' High School and Westville Girls' High School (public schools; Westville Boys' cited at >96% pass, R30,000–60,000/year band for good public schools) — [9]; Expat.com (city file)
  - Deutsche Schule Durban (German school) — [9]
  - UKZN Westville campus (university) — [9]
  - Bergtheil Museum (house museum, 1840s) — [9]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and Q10 "La santé et l'école"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "En montagne, en forêt" in a small way (Palmiet trails).

### Ballito (Dolphin Coast)
- Feel: affluent coastal town ~40 km north of Durban, with large gated estates (Zimbali, Simbithi) [12]; Expat.com and Expatica call it the calmer beach-town option, popular for raising children (city file).
- Rent: none found.
- Getting around: N2 toll road with off-ramps at Ballito Drive; M4 coastal road [12]. Car only on current sources.
- Watch: nothing dated sourced.
- Places:
  - Willard Beach (main swimming beach) and the ~2.5 km promenade — Wikipedia "Ballito" [12]
  - Ballito Junction Regional Shopping Centre (mall) — [12]
  - Ballito Lifestyle Centre (shopping centre) — [12]
  - Zimbali Estate and Simbithi (gated estates) — [12]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (estates).

### City centre and beachfront (Golden Mile, Point, Grey Street area)
- Feel: Indian-South African and Zulu commercial heart around the Juma Masjid, with the Golden Mile beachfront to the east [13][10]. English 50%, Zulu 33% in the city (city file).
- Rent: Numbeo city-centre 1-bed ~R7,125 (≈ €381), 2026-11-13; Expat.com R7,248 (≈ €388) (city file).
- Getting around: People Mover buses every 15 min 06:00–23:00 between centre and seafront (R4 a trip, R10 day pass — undated, likely old); Mynah buses R5–6 (undated) [10].
- Watch: Country Club and Reunion beaches shut 21 Jan 2026 as a precaution; Battery, Country Club, eThekwini and Blue Lagoon closed for E. coli in Dec 2025 (Time Out Durban, city file). In June 2026 ~200 migrants camped outside Home Affairs and ~200 near the central police station after attacks (Daily Maverick/Reuters 2026-06-18); in Sept 2026 vigilantes demanded ID at the gates of the Lancers Road and Gateway clinics (Daily Maverick 2026-09-29); HRW reported a 17 Apr 2026 attack on a foreign national's shop in Durban (all city file). Victoria Street Market: beware of pickpockets (Wikivoyage, city file).
- Places:
  - Golden Mile (patrolled beachfront for swimming, surfing, walking) — Expat.com (city file); Wikivoyage [10]
  - uShaka Marine World (aquarium and water park, 1 King Shaka Avenue, Point; tickets only on site; adult ~R200 per Expat.com) — official site [17]; city file
  - Juma Masjid (mosque, Dr Yusuf Dadoo St and Cathedral Rd) — Wikipedia [13]
  - Victoria Street Market (market, haggle) — Wikivoyage [10]
  - KwaMuhle Museum (apartheid-history museum, 130 Bram Fischer Rd, free) and Old Court House Museum (77 Aliwal St, free) — [10]
  - Durban Natural Science Museum (free, in the City Hall) — Expat.com (city file)
  - Wilson's Wharf (harbour seafood, daily 08:00–23:00) — [10]
  - Kingsmead (cricket ground) — [10]
- Fits: Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (Indian indenture and apartheid history: Durban Cultural and Document Centre, KwaMuhle) [10]; Q3 "Serré"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" only by Durban standards — Expatica calls Durban a "sleepy town" for nightlife (city file).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — the first week
- Where to stay first: a backpackers near the beachfront — Tekweni Backpackers (dorm R135) or Ansteys Beach (dorm R140), Wikivoyage, undated prices [10].
- Day 1: King Shaka airport shuttle R80 per person, versus taxis "well over R400" (Wikivoyage, city file).
- Day 2: SIM at a Vodacom or MTN store with passport and proof of address (RICA) [22]. Mobile money: not found.
- Day 3: bank — opening an account needs passport, proof of local address, work contract and residence permit (Expat.com national rule, via the Johannesburg file); Wise/Revolut meanwhile (dossier). Use ATMs inside malls; bank ATM fees R50–75 (2023) [22].
- Day 4: flat search on Property24 and Private Property; deposit usually 1–2 months, leases at least 12 months (Expat.com, city file). Agency fees and negotiation margins: not found; Property24 shows few "Reduced" tags in uMhlanga and none in Morningside (city file).
- Day 5: viewings in Morningside and Durban North by Uber (~R10/km) [city file].
- Day 6: Saturday — Golden Mile, Victoria Street Market [10].
- Day 7: Sunday — Botanic Gardens [16].
- Rough weekly spend: 7 nights × R135 = R945; shuttle R80; 7 simple meals 7×R130 = R910; 4 ride-hail trips 4×R103 = R412 → ≈ R2,350 (≈ €126), plus SIM bundle.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Golden Mile walk from uShaka (1 King Shaka Avenue) north towards Moses Mabhida; stadium funicular or 550-step walk [17][14]. People Mover along the seafront [10].
- Midday: Juma Masjid and the Grey Street area, Victoria Street Market; bunny chow for lunch [13][10]; city file.
- Afternoon: KwaMuhle Museum (free) or the Durban Cultural and Document Centre (free) [10]; then the Botanic Gardens (to 17:45 in summer) [16]. Uber between them.
- Evening: dinner on Florida Road [7].

### One week
- Day 1: Central Durban as above.
- Day 2: uMhlanga — promenade, lighthouse, Sharks Board (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00), lagoon reserve [11].
- Day 3 (trip out): Valley of a Thousand Hills on the R103 — PheZulu Safari Park; Shongweni market if Saturday [21].
- Day 4–5 (trip out): Drakensberg, 2–3 h by car — Champagne Valley or Giant's Castle; Three Pools or Gxalingenwa River trail [18]; city file.
- Day 6 (trip out, long): St Lucia (N2 ~250 km + R618, 26 km) for iSimangaliso hippo and croc cruises, and Hluhluwe–iMfolozi game drive [20][19].
- Day 7: Daily-life moment — an AmaZulu FC home match at Moses Mabhida (no fixture or ticket price sourced) [14], or the Sunday visit to the Hare Krishna Temple in Chatsworth (Sat–Sun 10:00–13:00) [10]; or a weekday morning on the Mynah bus at rush hour [10].
