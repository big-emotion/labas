# Oran, Algeria

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the dossier gives no EUR rate (only ≈ 146 DZD per USD, Dec 2025). EUR equivalents here use the **Bank of Algeria official rate of €1 ≈ 152 DZD (9 Nov 2026)**, as reported by Mediaterranee (2026-11-10); USD figures are converted at the same day's 136 DZD per USD and marked "USD-converted". Cash euros change at about 276 DZD on the informal market (Algiers figure), which nearly halves every EUR figure for someone paid abroad. Numbeo (dossier source [6]) returned HTTP 429 and is not re-cited. Items marked "national" apply country-wide and are kept short; see alger.md for the full national set.

- [daily.water] The wilaya has five desalination plants, including Macta (up to 500,000 m³/day); supply is about 51% surface water, 38% desalination, 11% groundwater. — Wikipedia "Oran", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
