# Alger, Algeria

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: the dossier gives no EUR rate (only ≈ 146 DZD per USD, Dec 2025). EUR equivalents here use the **Bank of Algeria official rate of €1 ≈ 152 DZD (9 Nov 2026)**, as reported by Mediaterranee (2026-11-10). Sources quoting USD are converted at the same day's official 136 DZD per USD, then to EUR, and say "USD-converted". Daily life for anyone holding euros in cash runs at the parallel ("square") rate, about 276 DZD per euro, which nearly halves every EUR figure below — see the [daily.money] items. Numbeo (the dossier's source [5]) returned HTTP 429 on every attempt, so its figures are not re-cited here.
