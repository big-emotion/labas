# Kampala: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate: the dossier's **1 EUR ≈ 4,550 UGX** (`research/countries/ouganda.md`, derived from Numbeo, 2026-11-06) and the city file's **1 USD ≈ 3,800 UGX ≈ €0.84**; both rough. "[city file]" means `research/cities/ouganda/kampala.md`, "[Jinja city file]" `research/cities/ouganda/jinja.md`, "[dossier]" the country dossier; bracketed numbers refer to "Sources read" at the end. Method note: the shared web-search budget ran out after one search in this pass, so places come from pages opened directly (Wikipedia neighbourhood articles, Wikivoyage, operator sites). Wikivoyage prices are often undated or old; each is flagged where it matters.

Shared caveats for every neighbourhood below:

- Crime: France says crime is rising in the capital, including normally secure areas (Kololo, Nakasero, Naguru), with snatch thefts, burglaries and sometimes violent robberies, especially around Christmas (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [city file]. The UK says foreigners on boda-bodas have been mugged, some with weapons, and advises not walking after dark (FCDO, read 2026-11-14) [city file]. Wikivoyage also notes phone and bag snatching from cars [3].
- Terrorism: France advises extra vigilance in churches, malls, bars and hotels; the UK calls attacks "very likely" (2026-09-15 / 2026-11-14) [city file].
- Ebola: France lists Kampala and Wakiso districts at "very high risk" (alert updated 2026-11-10); the UK says the WHO declared Uganda's outbreak over on 27 Aug 2026. The two disagree [city file]. Lubowa, Najjera and Namugongo are in Wakiso district [11][13][23].
- Power and water: UEDCL took over from Umeme on 1 April 2025; outage complaints have grown and its leadership was removed in May 2026 (URN, 2026-05-02) [city file]. A resident blogger reports near-daily cuts in the rainy season and occasional water cuts; tap water must be boiled (2026-08-22) [city file]. Knight Frank says backup power and water are now the baseline for lettable stock (H1 2026) [city file].
- Up-front cash: most landlords ask 3–6 months in advance plus one month's deposit (Expat.com, 2025-11-24); a blogger paid a broker UGX 100,000–150,000 (2026-08-22) [city file]. Knight Frank says tenants held the upper hand in negotiations in H1 2026 [city file].
- Traffic: rush hours run about 7–9 am and 5–8 pm (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [city file]. No sourced door-to-door commute times were found for any neighbourhood; distances below are Wikipedia road distances to the central business district (CBD).
- Fares: boda 2–3 km UGX 2,000–3,000 (≈ €0.44–0.66), 7–8 km about 8,000 (≈ €1.76), 10–12 km 12,000–15,000 (≈ €2.64–3.30); Uber halfway across town about 35,000 (≈ €7.70) (blogger, 2026-08-22) [city file]. Matatu $0.50–1.50 (≈ UGX 1,900–5,700) (Expat.com) [city file]; taxi fares rose UGX 2,000 → 3,000 on some routes in Oct 2026 (Daily Monitor) [city file]. France advises apps with an emergency button and a helmet on bodas [city file].
- Elections and internet: mobile internet was cut from 13 Jan 2026 around the general election [city file].
- Air: Wikivoyage calls the air pollution among the worst in the world [3]; a blogger left Kampala for Entebbe because of the haze (2026-08-22) [city file].

## Neighbourhoods

### Kololo (with Kamwokya and Naguru)

- Feel: an upscale residential hill since the 1950s, next to the CBD on Nakasero Hill, with more than a dozen embassies; since the 2000s hotels, banks, hospitals and companies have moved in, and residents sued seven bars over noise (case still in court in Feb 2019) (Wikipedia) [4]. Naguru next door has upscale mansions on its upper slopes, a public housing estate slated for redevelopment, and slum areas (Wikipedia) [9]. Knight Frank reports Ugandan senior professionals, executives and returning diaspora now replacing Western expats as prime tenants (H1 2026) [city file].
- Rent: premium flats in Kololo, Nakasero and Naguru UGX 2.5M–5M/month (≈ €549–1,099); expat-oriented units 2.5M–6M (≈ €549–1,319) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]. Expat.com quotes Kololo/Nakasero 2-beds at $1,500–2,500 (≈ €1,260–2,100) (2025-11-24) [city file]; a blogger puts luxury Kololo flats with pool, guard, gym and generator at $800–1,000 (≈ €670–840) (2026-08-22) [city file]. Prime 2-bed rents rose only about 1.7% year on year in H1 2026 (Knight Frank) [city file].
- Getting around: adjoins the CBD (Nakasero) [4]; Naguru is about 4.5 km north-east of the CBD [9]. Short boda hops (2–3 km, UGX 2,000–3,000) [city file].
- Watch: France names Kololo and Naguru among the normally secure areas where crime is rising (2026-09-15) [city file]. Bar noise has led to a residents' lawsuit (Wikipedia, status Feb 2019) [4].
- Places:

  - Alliance Française Kampala with the Déjà Brew café (French courses, library, events; Plot 52 Bukoto Street, Kamwokya, near Acacia Mall) — afkampala.org [24]
  - The Lawns Restaurant & Lounge Bar (Plot 34 Impala Ave; mains UGX 18,000–40,000, undated) — Wikivoyage [3]

### Bugolobi (with Mbuya)

- Feel: one of Kampala's most developed areas with "some of the most valuable properties", upscale commercial property, bungalows on the slopes and summit, and a large apartment complex with lower-middle-class tenants on the eastern slope (Wikipedia) [7]. Named among the five safest areas by a real-estate guide (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21; low confidence) [city file].
- Rent: family 2–3-beds in Mbuya and Bugolobi UGX 1.5M–4M/month (≈ €330–879); young-professional units in Bugolobi UGX 700k–1.8M (≈ €154–396) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]. Expat.com: 1-bed $600–1,200 (≈ €500–1,010), 3-bed $1,000–2,000 (≈ €840–1,680) (2025-11-24) [city file].

### Muyenga, Kabalagala and Kansanga

- Feel: Muyenga ("Tank Hill") was a wealthy area before the 1960s; after 1986 it grew without planning, so good houses sit on poor roads with poor drainage; views over the city and Lake Victoria (Wikipedia) [6]. Kabalagala below is known for restaurants, bars and nightclubs, many open round the clock, and is popular with foreigners; it is also becoming a business hub (Wikipedia) [15]. Kansanga turned upscale in the 1990s and is described as cosmopolitan (Wikipedia) [8].
- Rent: family 2–3-beds in Muyenga UGX 1.5M–4M (≈ €330–879); young-professional units in Kansanga and Makindye UGX 700k–1.8M (≈ €154–396) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]. Expat.com: Muyenga 1-bed $600–1,200 (≈ €500–1,010); Kansanga 2-bed $400–800 (≈ €335–670) (2025-11-24) [city file]. Older agent quotes: Makindye 1-bed UGX 400k (≈ €88), Kisugu 200k (≈ €44) (Real Muloodi, 2023) [city file].
- Getting around: Kabalagala about 6 km, Kansanga about 7 km and Muyenga about 8 km south-east of the CBD (Wikipedia) [15][8][6]. Matatus for Ggaba and Kabalagala routes stop at Cooper Complex in town (Wikivoyage) [3].
- Watch: Ethiopian Village in Kabalagala was one of the sites hit in the July 2010 Kampala bombings (Wikipedia) [15]; France's 2026 advice still asks for vigilance in bars [city file]. UK advice flags drink spiking in bars (read 2026-11-14) [city file]. Muyenga's poor roads and drainage (Wikipedia, undated) [6].
- Places:

  - Ethiopian Village (restaurant, bar, nightclub) — Wikipedia [15]; Club Venom (club) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - The British School of Kampala (school, on or near Muyenga hill) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Rainbow International School (school, Kansanga; UGX 2.4–5.5M a term per Expat.com) — Wikipedia [8], [city file]

### Ntinda (with Bukoto)

- Feel: a trading centre with railway staff housing in the 1960s–70s that drew wealthier residents and upscale housing after the 1980s; one part became known as "Ministers Village" (Wikipedia) [5]. Bukoto next door is a township with private hospitals and bars around Bukoto–Kira Road (Wikipedia, Wikivoyage) [10][3].
- Rent: family 2–3-beds in Ntinda UGX 1.5M–4M (≈ €330–879); young-professional units in Ntinda and Bukoto 700k–1.8M (≈ €154–396) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]. Expat.com: Ntinda 1-bed $600–1,200 (≈ €500–1,010) (2025-11-24) [city file]. Sale prices in Ntinda UGX 2.8–4.2M/m² [city file].
- Getting around: Ntinda about 7 km and Bukoto about 8 km north-east of the CBD (Wikipedia) [5][10]; the Northern Bypass crosses northern Bukoto [10].

### Kisaasi, Kyanja and Najjera (the northern belt)

- Feel: Kisaasi is "a middle class residential neighborhood, with clusters of upscale residencies"; KCCA has paved roads there since 2011 (Wikipedia) [12]. Najjera is a bedroom community with upscale housing, flats under construction, workshops, small restaurants and motels (Wikipedia) [13]. A real-estate guide lists Kisaasi, Kyanja and Najjera among the cheaper 1–2-bed areas (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]. No Wikipedia page for Kyanja was found.
- Rent: young-professional units UGX 700k–1.8M (≈ €154–396) (Africanvestor, 2026-09-21) [city file]; a blogger paid UGX 800,000 (≈ €176) for a 2-bed in Kyanja (2026-08-22) [city file]; Expat.com quotes Kyanja/Kulambiro 2-beds at $400–800 (≈ €335–670) (2025-11-24) [city file]; Najjera 1-bed 500k–700k (≈ €110–154), Real Muloodi 2023 [city file].
- Getting around: Kisaasi about 7.5 km and Najjera about 12 km north-east of the CBD (Wikipedia) [12][13]; the Northern Bypass passes through southern Kisaasi [12]. The Entebbe express taxi charges UGX 7,000 to northern areas such as Kisaasi (blogger, 2026-08-24) [city file].

### Lubowa and Munyonyo (the southern lakeside belt)

### Ggaba

- Feel: low-lying lakeside neighbourhood at the city's southern tip; a small beach, fishing landing, fish market, shopping centre, a resort with a private beach and several bars; lower-income housing to the south and west, more upscale homes with lake views to the north and east (Wikipedia) [22].
- Rent: no Ggaba-specific figure; Ggaba is in Makindye Division, where an agent quoted a 1-bed at UGX 400k (≈ €88) in 2023 (Real Muloodi) [city file] and Africanvestor lists "parts of Makindye" among cheaper areas (2026-09-21) [city file].
- Getting around: about 11 km from the CBD (Wikipedia) [22]; Ggaba-route matatus use the Cooper Complex stop in town (Wikivoyage) [3].
- Watch: ferry and boat accidents on Lake Victoria have killed many passengers, often through overloading (FCDO, national, read 2026-11-14) [Jinja city file].
- Places:
  - Ggaba landing site and fish market — Wikipedia [22]
  - Ggaba Beach and KK Beach (UGX 5,000 listed in the shared entry, not explicitly an entry fee, undated) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Ggaba Community Health Centre, Wentz Medical Centre, Martyrs Family Clinic (clinics) — Wikipedia [22]
  - Ggaba National Seminary (Catholic) and Kampala University main campus — Wikipedia [22]
  - Bbunga Primary School and Bbunga Islamic School — Wikipedia [22]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q8 "À la plage" (lake beach only); Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q4 "Seul·e" or "En couple". Thin for Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (no international school sourced nearby beyond Lubowa).

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Fees below come from Wikivoyage unless stated, and many are undated; check on arrival.

### One day

- Morning: Uganda National Mosque on Old Kampala Hill, guided entry and the 272-step minaret climb for UGX 20,000 [3]; then down to Nakasero Market, between the old taxi park, Kampala Road and Entebbe Road [3]. Walk or boda.
- Midday: lunch at The Roots, Plot 4 Kafu Rd, Nakasero (traditional Ugandan food) [3], or a rolex from a street stall (UGX 2,000 per the blogger) [city file].
- Afternoon: Kasubi Tombs (UNESCO site, about 5 km north-west) by boda, then Namirembe Cathedral on the next hill [3]. Bodas: agree the fare first; a 2–3 km hop is UGX 2,000–3,000 [city file].
- Evening: Ndere Centre dinner and traditional dance show on Kira Road (UGX 50,000) [3]; Uber back rather than walking after dark (FCDO) [city file].

### One week

- Day 1: arrive at Entebbe; Entebbe Botanical Gardens and Aero Beach (UGX 5,000) before the express taxi into town [20][city file].
- Day 2: the one-day loop above.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): Owino (St Balikuddembe) market at opening, then a matatu out of the old taxi park at evening rush hour (5–8 pm), phone out of sight [3][city file]. Walk Kabalagala in the evening to see a working-week night out [15].
- Day 4 (trip out): Jinja by Gateway or YY coach (UGX 7,000–10,000, 2026) or matatu (10,000–13,000) [27]: Source of the Nile (UGX 30,000 non-residents) and a two-hour Nile sunset cruise (US$45) [27]. Overnight in Jinja (see `jinja.neighbourhoods.md`).
- Day 5 (trip out, continued): Mabira Forest on the way back (walks, zip-lining) [26].
- Day 6: house-hunting eyes: a morning walk around Ntinda (Capital Shoppers, St. Luke's) and Bugolobi (Village Mall) to see where families and returnees live [5][7]; afternoon at Munyonyo Martyrs Shrine and Speke Resort's free grounds [14][3].
- Day 7 (Sunday, daily-life moment): Mass at Rubaga Cathedral or the Anglican service at Namirembe [3]; Ggaba landing site and fish market in the afternoon [22]; if KCCA FC is at home, a Uganda Premier League match at MTN Omondi Stadium, Lugogo (ticket price not found) [16]. Optional extra day: Ngamba Island chimpanzees from Entebbe (US$70) [3].
- Note: Martyrs Day on 3 June brings hundreds of thousands (estimates up to 2 million in 2015) to Namugongo, about 12 km north-east; plan around it [23].
