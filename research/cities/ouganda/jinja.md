# Jinja, Uganda

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 4,550 UGX**; USD figures are converted at about 3,800 UGX per USD, so **1 USD ≈ €0.84**. Jinja has almost no local price reporting online: most price items below come from cost-of-living aggregators that mix crowdsourced and AI-generated figures and disagree with each other, so every one is low confidence. Items marked "(national)" are not Jinja-specific.
