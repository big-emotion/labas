# Lubumbashi: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (from the dossier and the city file): **1 USD ≈ 0.86 EUR** (editorial, unsourced, flagged in the dossier) and **2,265 CDF per USD** (Banque centrale indicative rate reported by ACP, 30 Oct 2026), i.e. **1 EUR ≈ 2,634 CDF**. Expat.com's French guide quotes in EUR and those figures are kept as published; its English guide quotes USD. `[L tag]` = an item in `research/cities/rd-congo/lubumbashi.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/rd-congo.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- Belgium (updated 2026-10-27): security "volatile", heightened vigilance in the evening and at night; violent banditry regularly reported "notably in the city of Lubumbashi"; militia activity rising across Haut-Katanga, Lualaba, Haut-Lomami and Tanganyika; have someone meet you at the airport [L safety.general]. France (2026-09-15): crime rising in the provinces, Lubumbashi named [L safety.general]. Haut-Katanga is not a red zone for France or Belgium [D].
- Assaults on young women in unknown taxis at night are recurrent (France, national advice) [L safety.women]. The routes below assume a known driver after dark; most expatriates drive or have a driver [L daily.transport].
- Power and water are intermittent, cuts from hours to several days; housing with a generator and water tanks is advised (Expat.com, 2025-12-19) [L daily.power]. Cholera is endemic in the Katanga region: bottled water [L health.care].
- No organised public transport: shared minibuses, moto-taxis and private taxis [L daily.transport]. Motorbikes and tricycles were banned from the city centre in Jan 2026 (Radio Okapi; earlier bans ignored) [L daily.transport].
- Upfront housing costs: 3 months' deposit plus one month's agency commission (Expat.com) [L housing.search]; OpenShores says 1–3 months [L housing.search].
- Language: Swahili is the street lingua franca, French the official language [L social.pace].

## Neighbourhoods

### Golf (Lido Golf, Golf Météo, La Plage)
- Feel: the area foreigners prefer, with "enhanced security measures", on the edge of the business district; most expatriates live in secured residences or walled houses with guards, often employer-provided (Expat.com, 2025-12-19) [L housing.areas][9]. Bars and clubs with live rumba and soukous gather "around the Golf area and the city center" [9]. "Lido golf" and "Météo" are quartiers of Lubumbashi commune (Wikipedia FR) [6].
- Rent: a 3-bed house €2,700–4,500/month, unfurnished, from €2,700 before charges and staff (Expat.com FR, 2025-12-19) [L cost.rent]; the English page says an unfurnished house from USD 3,000 (≈ €2,580) [9].
- Getting around: next to the business district [D]; a short taxi ride costs about €0.50–2 (Expat.com) [L daily.transport]; cross-town taxi or ride-hailing USD 3–8 (OpenShores, 2026-04-15, low confidence) [L daily.transport].
- Watch: in Golf Météo a resident said the area went more than 7 months without electricity until SNEL replaced a transformer in Oct 2026; residents link dark new neighbourhoods to burglaries and armed robberies (Radio Okapi, 2026-10-18) [L daily.power]. The Nov 2025 collapse of a mining retention pond sent acid water into Golf-les-Battants, Kamisepe II and Kasapa; residents said wells were still unusable in March 2026 (Mongabay, 2026-03-26) [L daily.water].
- Places:
  - Lubumbashi Golf Club, Route du Golf (designed 1934, open to guests) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Pullman Lubumbashi Grand Karavia, 55 Route du Golf (hotel) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - The lake by the golf course with "La Plage" on its south shore (pedalo rental, swimming prohibited; Côte Ouest and Mykonos cafés on the far side) — Wikivoyage FR, which labels it Lac Kipopo [2]; Wikivoyage EN calls it Lake Tshombe, with Plage Tshombe bars and restaurants on Route du Golf [1]. Name conflict unresolved.
  - Le Boucher, La Plage Golf (high-end restaurant) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Lattelicious, Golf area (American-style bar, restaurant and café) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Chez Marc, Météo (restaurant, 06:30–00:00, updated Nov 2024) — Wikivoyage FR [2]
  - Kin Marché Golf, KM Express Golf, Kin Marché Lido Golf (supermarkets; addresses not read) — kinmarche.com [10]
  - Bougian Villa, 12 avenue Lukonzolwa (guest house USD 145–185, restaurant and bar) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple" (secured houses, employer packages); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats".

### Centre-ville (Lubumbashi commune: Gambela, Makutano, Baudouin, Kiwele)
- Feel: the colonial centre around the cathedral, railway station and Palais de Justice; Expat.com says it gives convenient access to shops, restaurants and business facilities (2025-12-19) [9][3]. OpenShores names Centre-ville and Kampemba as the most sought-after areas (2026-04-15) [L cost.rent]. Lubumbashi commune had about 185,000 people in 2001 (Wikipedia FR) [6].
- Rent: 1-bed in the centre €550–1,350 (Expat.com FR) [L cost.rent], USD 600–1,500 (Expat.com EN) [9]; OpenShores gives about USD 400 (≈ €345) for a 1-bed and USD 720 for a two-room flat, which conflicts [L cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot or short taxi rides (€0.50–2) [L daily.transport]; the motorbike ban covers the centre bounded by avenues Kambove, Likasi, Sendwe, Kamanyola and Adoula (Jan 2026) [L daily.transport].
- Watch: in Feb 2026 police presented 39 suspects accused of attacking residents and stealing phones around Marché Mzee, avenue Sendwe, avenue des Usines, Mama Yemo and Route Likasi (ACP, 2026-02-02) [L safety.general].
- Places:
  - Cathédrale Saints-Pierre-et-Paul (Catholic cathedral, colonial era) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]; Wikipedia FR [3]
  - Musée national de Lubumbashi, 750 avenue du Musée (archaeology and ethnography) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Lubumbashi Zoo and botanical garden, boulevard Kamanyola (zoo with a termite museum and restaurant; about USD 10 for foreign visitors) — Wikivoyage EN [1]; Expat.com [L cost.living]
  - Cercle Wallon, 2 avenue de la Révolution (club with eatery, bar, tennis with lessons, 10-pin bowling) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Park Hotel (Hotel Léopold II), 50 avenue Munongo (USD 100; courtyard restaurant with live jazz most evenings) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Latte Lounge and Planet Hollybum (pizza), avenue Kilela Balanda — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Waza Art Center & Spilulu'Studio, 588 avenue Adoula (exhibitions, recording) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Cinéma Climax, 2nd floor of the Hypnose building opposite the cathedral — Wikipedia FR [3]; KM Express Hypnose and Kin Marché 30 Juin (supermarkets) — kinmarche.com [10]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (walkable services, Trust Merchant Bank's head office in town [L work.jobs]); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q5 "Création, culture" (Waza, museum, biennale [L social.culture]).

### Kampemba (Bel-Air, Kafubu, Bongonga)
- Feel: the eastern commune split from Lubumbashi commune in 1972, about 307,000 people in 2017; quartiers Bel-Air I and II, Bongonga, Industriel, Kafubu, Kinka-ville, Kampemba, Kigoma, Hewa-bora (Wikipedia FR) [5]. OpenShores lists it among the most sought-after areas [L cost.rent].
- Rent: no rental listing found; a 900 m² plot with a 2-bed house on avenue Luvungi for sale at USD 250,000 (≈ €215,000), "négociable" (MediaCongo, 2026-05-13) [L cost.rent]. Periphery 1-bed €270–720 (Expat.com FR) as the closest range [L cost.rent].
- Getting around: no sourced time or fare to the centre.
- Watch: a cordon-and-search at the Major Vangu military camp in Kampemba led to over 200 arrests and the seizure of 19 assault rifles (Radio Okapi, 2026-03-30) [L safety.general].
- Places:
  - Christ-Sauveur church, Centre Interviens et Vois church, Bana Bampeta church — Wikipedia FR [5]
  - KM Express Belair (supermarket) — kinmarche.com [10]
  - Camp Vangu maternity (maternity ward) — Wikipedia FR [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (maternity nearby); thin on sourced daily places.

### Kamalondo (historic quarter, TP Mazembe)
- Feel: the former "Quartier Albert", the old African quarter south of the centre (Wikipedia EN/FR) [4][3]; home of TP Mazembe's stadium [7].
- Rent: no rental listing; a 375 m² plot for sale at USD 150,000 (≈ €129,000), "discutable" (MediaCongo, 2026-04-08) [L cost.rent]. Simple house in a local neighbourhood €180–450 (Expat.com FR) [L cost.rent].
- Getting around: close to the centre (south of it) [4]; short taxi rides €0.50–2 [L daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - Stade TP Mazembe, avenue des Sports (18,000–18,500 seats, opened 2012) — Wikipedia EN [7]
  - TP Mazembe (five African Champions League titles) home matches — [L social.sport]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (the lowest sourced house range applies to local neighbourhoods); Q8 "Je reste en ville" for football fans (Mazembe's stadium on the doorstep). No source on everyday pace or noise.

### Ruashi (Rwashi, north-east)
- Feel: one of the main suburbs, originally an African quarter (Wikipedia EN) [4]; OpenShores lists Rwashi among expat-oriented areas and says peripheral areas are calmer and cheaper (2026-04-15, low confidence) [L housing.areas].
- Rent: outskirts 1-bed about USD 150 (≈ €130, OpenShores) [L cost.rent]; periphery 1-bed €270–720 (Expat.com FR) [L cost.rent]. The two conflict.
- Getting around: no sourced time or fare.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced; city-wide banditry frame applies.
- Places:
  - Kin Marché Ruashi (supermarket) — kinmarche.com [10]
  - Maternité de Rwashi — Wikipedia FR [3]
  - Université de Lubumbashi, north of the city west of the airport (about 33,000 students; quartier not named) — Wikipedia EN [8]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (near the university, location approximate); Q7 "Prend son temps".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city file, the dossier and the sources listed here. "Assumption" marks a non-sourced step. No coworking, gym, club entry, generator, fuel, mobile-money or in-city bus fare from 2025–2026 was found (latest bus/taxi fares are from 2022) [L cost.negotiation]. Weekly sums are in EUR because the main rent source quotes EUR; USD items are converted at 0.86.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Centre-ville**. Cafés and clubs within short rides, the cathedral, museum and Cercle Wallon nearby [1]. Remote work depends on a generator and a fixed line: internet is "slow and expensive" (Expat.com), USD 20–40/month (OpenShores) [L daily.internet]. No coworking space found (Kadea has an office, no public desks sourced) [L work.jobs].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; groceries at Kin Marché 30 Juin [10].
  - Tue: work from Latte Lounge, avenue Kilela Balanda (assumption: laptop-friendly; not sourced) [1].
  - Wed: home office; tennis lesson at Cercle Wallon [1].
  - Thu: home office; evening at Cinéma Climax [3].
  - Fri: live jazz at the Park Hotel courtyard [1].
  - Sat: zoo and botanical garden in the morning (USD 10) [1][L cost.living]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saints-Pierre-et-Paul [1]; afternoon at La Plage by the golf lake [2].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": live rumba and soukous bars around Golf and the centre (no venue named by Expat.com) [9]; Village Beau Site and Matshipisha (listed as drinking spots, no detail) [1]; the Novotel's nightclub (Wikivoyage FR, Aug 2024) [2].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Boucher or Chez Marc [1][2]; Cercle Grec's taverna (mezze) [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Ciné Bétamax also screens football and concerts [4].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Lubumbashi (inland) [D]. Substitute: pedalos at La Plage on the golf lake; swimming prohibited [2].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Muyambo Park, about 15 km out (a large garden for play and rest, opened 2010) [4]; Mikembo on the Kasenga road [4][L nature.access]. No mountains near the city.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Expat.com mentions unnamed mini-safari parks near the city with giraffes and hyenas [9]. Kundelungu National Park (Lofoi falls, 340 m) [11] is not advised: the army ran an operation there against Mai Mai rebels in Jan 2023 [11] and Belgium reports rising militia activity in Haut-Katanga (2026-10-27) [L safety.general].
  - "Je reste en ville": Musée national [1]; a TP Mazembe match [7].
- Rough weekly spend (one person, EUR):
  - Rent: €550–1,350 ÷ 4.33 = 127.0–311.8 [L cost.rent]
  - Internet: USD 20–40 ÷ 4.33 = 4.6–9.2 USD = €4.0–7.9 [L daily.internet]
  - 7 local meals × USD 4–9 = 28–63 USD = €24.1–54.2; 2 mid-range meals × USD 18 = 36 USD = €31.0 [L cost.living]
  - 10 short taxi rides × €0.50–2 = €5–20 [L daily.transport]
  - Going out: USD 150–300/month ÷ 4.33 = 34.6–69.3 USD = €29.8–59.6 (Expat.com EN, low confidence) [L cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ €221–484 a week (≈ USD 257–563)**, before generator, utilities (USD 40 or €90–180 a month, conflicting) and groceries [L daily.power].

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Golf**. Where expatriates and mining staff mostly live, often in employer-provided housing [L housing.areas]; next to the business district [D]. Mining employers dominate [L work.jobs]. Cheaper alternative: a periphery 1-bed at €270–720 [L cost.rent].
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: drive or driver to the centre or a mine office; daily transport €3–10 (Expat.com) [L daily.transport].
  - Mon: groceries at Kin Marché Lido Golf [10].
  - Tue: golf at the Lubumbashi Golf Club [1].
  - Wed: dinner at Lattelicious [1].
  - Thu: bowling at Cercle Wallon [1].
  - Fri: drinks at La Plage / Plage Tshombe bars [1][2].
  - Sat: market errands with a driver; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: brunch at the Pullman Grand Karavia (no price sourced) [1]; pedalos on the lake [2].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": rumba bars around Golf [9]; Novotel nightclub [2].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Boucher, La Plage Golf [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home with a generator [L daily.power].
- Weekend outing: as profile 1 ("À la plage" not realistic; Muyambo for green space; mini-safari parks; museum and TP Mazembe in town). Day trip to Likasi on one of the very few asphalted roads in the country (France, 2026-09-15) [L daily.transport].
- Rough weekly spend (two people, EUR):
  - Rent (Golf house): €2,700–4,500 ÷ 4.33 = 623.6–1,039.3 [L cost.rent]

### 4. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q4 "Avec mes enfants"
- Base: **Golf**. Secured houses, employer packages that often include schooling allowances [L work.jobs][L housing.areas]. Schools: AFEEL (British curriculum, nursery to university entrance), British International School (preschool to grade 12), École privée belge, École française Blaise-Pascal (levels unconfirmed); Expat.com says no accredited French lycée [L family.schools][4]. Ifri: only the French and Belgian schools give equivalences recognised in France [L family.schools]. School addresses: not found.
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: school runs by driver; staff €270–540/month for a family [L cost.living].
  - Mon: groceries at Kin Marché Golf [10].
  - Tue: tennis lessons at Cercle Wallon [1].
  - Wed: Cercle Grec's sports grounds and playground [1].
  - Thu: medical check-ups: consultation USD 15–50 (OpenShores); good private doctors exist in Lubumbashi, care paid upfront [L health.care]. Named private clinics: not found; Wikipedia lists the Centre médical de la communauté (CMC) and the Centre de cardiologie [3].
  - Fri: family dinner at Lattelicious [1].
  - Sat: the zoo (termite museum, USD 10 per foreign visitor) [1][L cost.living]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Cathedral Mass; afternoon horse-riding at the Cercle Hippique, the country's last equestrian club (no price) [4].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin": adults only, with a driver [L safety.women]; "Un bon dîner entre amis": Le Boucher [1]; "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outing: "À la plage": not realistic from Lubumbashi; pedalos at La Plage [2]. "En montagne, en forêt": Muyambo Park, about 15 km [4]. "Dans la savane ou le désert": the unnamed mini-safari parks [9]; not Kundelungu [11][L safety.general]. "Je reste en ville": museum and a TP Mazembe match [1][7].
- Rough weekly spend (family, one child in an international school, EUR):

  - Staff: €270–540 ÷ 4.33 = 62.4–124.7 [L cost.living]
  - Health insurance (family of four): €270–720 ÷ 4.33 = 62.4–166.3 [L health.care]
  - 4 mid-range meals × USD 18 = 72 USD = €61.9 [L cost.living]
  - Zoo for four: 4 × USD 10 = 40 USD = €34.4 (child price not sourced) [L cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ €931–1,859 a week (≈ USD 1,083–2,162)**, before groceries and generator. Expat.com's estimate for a family of four is €2,950/month (≈ €681/week), which does not appear to include Golf rents [L cost.living].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": first week of a trial stay
- Before landing: visa required; stays over 6 months need a residence permit requested in the DRC; working needs a VET plus the employer's VETS [L admin.visa]. Bring recent, clean USD; USD 1 notes accepted almost nowhere [L daily.money].
- Day 1: arrive at Lubumbashi international airport; Belgium advises having someone meet you [L safety.general]. Stay first at the Park Hotel (USD 100) or Hotel Belle-Vue, avenue Kasai, one block from the station (USD 50) [1]; Bougian Villa in Golf (USD 145–185) [1].
- Day 2: SIM card: Vodacom, Airtel and Orange cover the town [L daily.internet]; where to buy and mobile money: not sourced (gap).
- Day 3: money: carry cash, ATMs are rare (Expat.com) [L daily.money]; cards increasingly accepted in hotels, restaurants and supermarkets (Belgium, 2026-10-27) [L daily.money][12]. Opening an account: no source (gap); Trust Merchant Bank is headquartered in the city [L work.jobs].
- Days 4–6: flat hunting through agencies and expat Facebook groups (OpenShores; no local property portal found) [L housing.search]; budget 3 months' deposit plus one month's commission [L housing.search]; prefer housing with a generator and water tanks [L daily.power]. Check the street's recent power history (Golf Météo: 7 months without power until Oct 2026) [L daily.power].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the cathedral, then the zoo [1].
- Consulates: Belgian consulate-general, avenue Lufira 990; French consular agency [1][L admin.residency]. Customs on household goods: not sourced (gap).
- Spend for the week: hotel 7 × USD 50–100 = 350–700; 14 local meals × USD 4–9 = 56–126 [1][L cost.living]; 10 short taxi rides × €0.50–2 = €5–20. **≈ USD 406–826 + €5–20 (≈ €354–730)** before any deposit.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Arrange an airport pick-up [L safety.general] and a car with driver for outings.

### One day
- Morning: Cathédrale Saints-Pierre-et-Paul, then the Musée national, 750 avenue du Musée (fee not sourced) [1].
- Midday: lunch at Cercle Wallon or Latte Lounge [1].
- Afternoon: the zoo and botanical garden, boulevard Kamanyola (about USD 10 for foreign visitors; termite museum) [1][L cost.living]; a look at the copper slag heap (terril) by the Gécamines plant, the city's symbol [2].
- Evening: sunset at La Plage on the golf lake, then dinner at Le Boucher [2][1]; or live jazz at the Park Hotel [1].

### One week
- Day 1: arrival, airport pick-up, check in [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3: Golf area: a round or a walk at the Golf Club, pedalos at La Plage, lunch at Lattelicious [1][2].
- Day 4 (daily life): a morning in the centre's markets around Marché Mzee with a local guide, phones out of sight (robberies reported Feb 2026) [L safety.general]; groceries at a Kin Marché to compare prices [10]; afternoon at Waza Art Center [1].
- Day 5 (out of town): Muyambo Park, about 15 km [4]; or the Kasenga road for Mikembo [4].
- Day 6 (out of town): Likasi by the asphalted Lubumbashi–Likasi road (France, 2026-09-15) [L daily.transport]; distance and time not sourced.
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the cathedral, then a TP Mazembe match at the Stade TP Mazembe, avenue des Sports, Kamalondo, if one is on (no fixture or ticket price sourced) [7][L social.sport]. If you visit in October of an even year, the photography biennial at Picha [L social.culture].
