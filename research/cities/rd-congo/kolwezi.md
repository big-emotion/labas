## Items

- [cost.rent] Cities near mines such as Kolwezi and Lubumbashi see prices inflated by expatriate demand from the mining sector; the guide gives no Kolwezi figure. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère de RD Congo", 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Rond-Point de l'Indépendance is the traditional centre (offices, guesthouses, banks, restaurants); Dilala is described as the upscale area with foreign workers and guesthouses. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The colonial-era city centre, where most shops are, has lost nearly half its space in about ten years to mining; residents blame blasting by the Chinese-owned Compagnie minière de Musonoi (Comus) for hundreds of destroyed houses, and some families were relocated. — RFI via AllAfrica, 2026-02-09, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Residents of Dilungu say the Ruashi Mining plant under 1,000 m from homes makes the air "suffocating" and kills garden crops; a tailings basin is reportedly being built about 100 m from houses. — RFI via AllAfrica, 2026-02-05, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] A Kolwezi NGO report (IBGDH) found cadmium and other heavy metals above WHO guide values in wells and rainwater in Biashara (Dilungu, Bel-Air, Jacques Masengo cells), with residents reporting coughs, nosebleeds and skin irritation. — Actualite.cd, 2026-03-13, local-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Kolwezi is the capital of Lualaba province and a major copper and cobalt centre (also uranium deposits); population has surged with the cobalt boom. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Kadea (tech training and software) names Kolwezi as one of its locations, though not in its office list. — Kadea website, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] A new 120 kV line (Nseke–RO) built by Metalkol with SNEL raised transfer capacity to about 260 MW for the Kolwezi network (Oct 2025); the article is about mining reliability and gives no household outage data. — Logistics Business Africa, 2025-10-11, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Transafrican Highway 9 links Kolwezi to Lubumbashi; the station has daily mineral and passenger trains to Likasi and Lubumbashi and connects to the Benguela railway; the airport is about 6 km south. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] France says good private doctors are concentrated in Kinshasa and Lubumbashi; Kolwezi has "several clinics and hospitals" per Wikipedia, none named. Cholera is endemic in the Katanga region. — France Diplomatie 2026-09-15 / Wikipedia "Kolwezi", official, confidence: medium
- [health.care] In March 2026 the government declared a "radiological emergency" around the T17 tailings dump of Kamoto Copper Company (Glencore) after radioactive material was confirmed; the president ordered an urgent commission. — RFI via AllAfrica, 2026-03-29, intl-media, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Dozens of primary and secondary schools; the University of Kolwezi launched the country's first digital library; many schoolchildren have also worked in the mines. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Belgium (27 Oct 2026) reports rising militia activity across Grand Katanga, including Lualaba (Kolwezi's province); new actions "cannot be excluded". Lualaba is not a red zone. — Belgian FPS Foreign Affairs, 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Gangs called "Biongozi", largely artisanal miners, are accused of night-time burglaries, assaults and arson in Kapata, Musonoie, Tshabula, Kanina, Hewa Bora, Sabat, Cinq Ans and the Gécamines estate; authorities did not respond to Radio Okapi. — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-08-10, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] In June 2026, fights between youth gangs ("shegués") armed with blades in the Bel Air 2 cell led to looting and home break-ins; residents fled towards Dilungu and shops closed early. — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-06-17, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Historical marker: in May 1978 Katangese rebels occupied the city and French and Belgian paratroopers intervened; about 170 European hostages and some 700 Africans were killed. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Population about 573,000 (2015 figure, the latest given), about 480,000 urban; altitude 1,448 m. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Tropical savanna at altitude: mean highs around 27 °C, lows around 14 °C with cool dry-season mornings; about 1,160 mm of rain mostly Nov–Mar, June–August essentially dry. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Lake Nzilo, created by damming the Lualaba river, supplies hydropower and water to the mines. — Wikipedia "Kolwezi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
