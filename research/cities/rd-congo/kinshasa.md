# Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: Kinshasa runs on two currencies. Rent, schools, clinics and supermarkets are priced in **USD**; markets, buses, taxis and moto-taxis in **Congolese francs (CDF)**. EUR equivalents use the dossier's editorial rate **1 USD ≈ 0.86 EUR** (unsourced, flagged in the dossier) and, for CDF, the Banque centrale indicative rate reported by ACP: **2,265 CDF per USD** at close on 30 Oct 2026 (parallel market 2,327), i.e. **1 EUR ≈ 2,634 CDF**. Listings are dated asking prices, not transaction prices.
