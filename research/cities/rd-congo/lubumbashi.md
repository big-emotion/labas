# Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: housing, schools and services are priced in **USD**, small purchases and transport in **Congolese francs (CDF)**. EUR equivalents use the dossier's editorial rate **1 USD ≈ 0.86 EUR** (unsourced, flagged in the dossier) and, for CDF, the Banque centrale indicative rate reported by ACP: **2,265 CDF per USD** at close on 30 Oct 2026, i.e. **1 EUR ≈ 2,634 CDF**. Expat.com's French Lubumbashi guide quotes in EUR directly; those figures are kept as published. Listings are dated asking prices.

## Items

- [cost.rent] 1-bed in the centre €550–1,350/month; periphery €270–720; a 3-bed house in Golf €2,700–4,500 (unfurnished, from €2,700 before charges and staff); a simple house in a local neighbourhood €180–450. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A second guide gives much lower figures: centre 1-bed about USD 400 (≈ €345), centre two-room (deux-pièces) USD 720 (≈ €620); outskirts 1-bed USD 150 (≈ €130); no 3-bed figure on the page. Centre-ville and Kampemba are named as the most sought-after. Conflicts with Expat.com above. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Lubumbashi", 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [cost.rent] Lubumbashi rents are put at 30–50 % below Kinshasa by one guide and 20–40 % below by another; neither gives absolute figures for the city. — Africarrières 2026-08-07 / OpenShores 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Prices are pushed up around mining towns (Lubumbashi, Kolwezi) by expatriate demand from the mining sector. — OpenShores "Ville la moins chère de RD Congo", 2026-05-02, company, confidence: low

- [cost.rent] Asking sale price, Kamalondo: a plot of 375 m² (dimensions given as 17×25 m, which do not match) at USD 150,000 (≈ €129,000), "discutable". — MediaCongo classified ad, 2026-04-08, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Single person excluding rent: groceries USD 200–400, transport USD 90–300, utilities USD 100–200, going out USD 150–300; the same guide elsewhere says USD 1,500–3,000 excluding housing, which contradicts it. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (EN), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Estimated monthly cost: €1,100 for one person, €2,950 for a family of four; domestic staff (driver, housekeeper) €270–540/month for a family. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Groceries for one about USD 180/month (≈ €155); local restaurant meal USD 4–9 (≈ €3.40–7.70); mid-range meal about USD 18 (≈ €15.50); lean monthly budget USD 484, comfortable USD 1,020 (≈ €415–875). — OpenShores, 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Imported products and Western-standard amenities carry premium prices. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (EN), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Lubumbashi Zoo and Botanical Gardens charge about USD 10 (≈ €8.60) entry for foreign visitors. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (EN), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] Both property sale ads read for Lubumbashi (Kampemba and Kamalondo) mark the asking price "négociable" or "discutable". — MediaCongo classified ads, 2026-04-08 and 2026-05-13, company, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] In early 2022 drivers doubled fares (taxi 1,000 → 2,000 CDF, bus 500 → 1,000 CDF) blaming fuel shortages; the city set fines of 250,000 CDF (taxi) and 400,000 CDF (minibus) for illegal fare rises and for "demi-terrain", i.e. selling a route as several partial rides. Old item (2022). — Actualite.cd, 2022-02-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.negotiation] When petrol rose from 4,000 to 5,000 CDF/L at Kasumbalesa (Jan 2025), there was no fixed fare: each driver set his own price on the Kasumbalesa–Lubumbashi run (then 12,000–15,000 CDF per passenger, ≈ €4.60–5.70). — ACP, 2025-01-20, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Golf is the area foreigners prefer, with "enhanced security measures"; most expatriates live in secured residences or walled houses with guards, often provided by the employer. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] The city's communes are Annexe, Kamalondo, Kampemba, Katuba, Kenya, Lubumbashi and Rwashi. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [housing.areas] Expat-oriented areas named by a relocation blog: Centre-ville, Kampemba, Golf and Rwashi; peripheral areas are calmer and cheaper. — OpenShores "Vivre à Lubumbashi", 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Deposit of 3 months' rent plus one month's agency commission. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Another guide says 1 to 3 months' deposit, and points to local property platforms, expat Facebook groups and agencies to find housing. — OpenShores "Vivre à Lubumbashi", 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Lubumbashi is the hub of copper and cobalt mining (the DRC produces about half the world's cobalt, mostly from Katanga); also copper smelting, textiles, food and drink, the Simba brewery (Tembo beer) and Trust Merchant Bank's headquarters. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Many mining employers include schooling allowances in expatriate packages. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide, updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Kadea (tech training and software, Kadea Academy since 2019) lists an office in Lubumbashi. — Kadea website, accessed 2026-11-14, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Power and water are intermittent, with "frequent cuts" lasting from hours to several days; housing with a generator and water tanks is advised. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] In Golf Météo, a resident said the area went "more than 7 months without electricity" until SNEL replaced a transformer in October 2026; residents link dark new neighbourhoods to burglaries and armed robberies. — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-10-18, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Joli Site, a newer peripheral area with hotels and hospitals, got electricity for the first time on 24 Jan 2026 (one 630 kVA transformer for up to 400 households). — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-01-26, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Utilities (water, power, internet) for one person: about USD 40/month per OpenShores (≈ €34) vs €90–180 per Expat.com. Conflicting figures. — OpenShores 2026-04-15, company / Expat.com 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] Mobile coverage is fine in town (Vodacom, Airtel, Orange); internet is available but "slow and expensive". OpenShores gives internet at USD 20–40/month (≈ €17–34). — Expat.com 2025-12-19, blog / OpenShores 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [daily.water] The 4 Nov 2025 collapse of a retention pond at Congo Dongfang Mining (CDM) sent acid water into outlying areas (Golf-les-Battants, Kamisepe II, Kasapa); 670 people were treated at Sendwe hospital and residents say wells are still unusable in March 2026. — Mongabay, 2026-03-26, intl-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] No organised public transport: shared minibuses, moto-taxis and private taxis; a short taxi ride costs about €0.50–2, daily transport €3–10; most expats drive or have a driver. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] OpenShores gives a cross-town taxi or ride-hailing trip at USD 3–8 (≈ €2.60–6.90) and transport at USD 20–50/month; it also mentions a metro or tram, which no other source supports. — OpenShores, 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] In Jan 2026 the mairie banned motorbikes and tricycles from the city centre (bounded by avenues Kambove, Likasi, Sendwe, Kamanyola, Adoula); the article notes earlier bans were ignored. — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-01-11, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Lubumbashi–Likasi is one of the very few asphalted roads in the country; domestic flights are discouraged by France as most domestic carriers are EU-banned. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Transafrican Highway 9 links the city to Lusaka and Kolwezi; rail links to Ndola, Kindu, Ilebo and, via Tenke, to Kolwezi and Lobito; Lubumbashi international airport is a main entry point. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] The economy runs mainly in USD, with CDF in circulation; ATMs are rare, so carry cash. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide, updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] Cards are increasingly accepted in hotels, restaurants and supermarkets in Kinshasa and Lubumbashi; USD 1 notes are accepted almost nowhere. — Belgian FPS Foreign Affairs, 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Good GPs and specialists, mostly in Kinshasa or Lubumbashi, work in private clinics, a few close to European standards; public hospitals are very run-down; all care paid upfront. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Expat.com mentions "weaknesses of the health system" without naming facilities and advises international insurance with repatriation (€270–720/month for a family of four). — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [health.care] A medical consultation costs USD 15–50 (≈ €13–43); international insurance USD 100–200/month. — OpenShores, 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] Cholera is endemic in the Katanga region; drink bottled water. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] AFEEL (British curriculum, nursery to university entrance) and the British International School (BIS, preschool to grade 12); international fees €4,500–22,500/year, local private schools €450–2,700/year. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (FR), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Expat.com says there is no accredited French lycée (CNED or AEFE boarding in southern Africa instead); France Diplomatie does list an École française Blaise Pascal de Lubumbashi, and Wikipedia also cites a Belgian school. Levels covered by Blaise Pascal not confirmed. — France Diplomatie 2026-09-15 (with Expat.com 2025-12-19 and Wikipedia "Lubumbashi"), official, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Ifri says only the French and Belgian schools of Kinshasa and Lubumbashi offer equivalences recognised in the French system. — Ifri study, 2026-10, academic, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Belgium (27 Oct 2026): security is "volatile", heightened vigilance in the evening and at night; violent banditry is regularly reported "notably in the city of Lubumbashi"; militia activity has risen across Haut-Katanga, Lualaba, Haut-Lomami and Tanganyika. — Belgian FPS, 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France (15 Sept 2026) says crime is increasing in the provinces, naming Lubumbashi among the cities. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In Feb 2026 police presented 39 suspects, 13 of them alleged armed robbers, accused of attacking residents and stealing phones around Marché Mzee, avenue Sendwe, avenue des Usines, Mama Yemo and Route Likasi. — ACP, 2026-02-02, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] A cordon-and-search at the Major Vangu military camp (Kampemba) in March 2026 led to over 200 arrests and the seizure of 19 AK-47-type rifles. — Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, 2026-03-30, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] In Jan 2025 the governor asked Kinshasa to approve an 11 p.m.–5 a.m. curfew, citing machete attacks and motorbike-borne gangs; the article does not say it was applied. — infos.cd, 2025-01-19, local-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Have someone meet you at Luano airport (Belgian advice). — Belgian FPS, 2026-10-27, official, confidence: high
- [safety.women] France lists sexual assault among the violent crimes reported in the country and warns that assaults on young women in unknown taxis at night are recurrent (national advice, not Lubumbashi-specific). — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Nightlife features live rumba and soukous, with venues around Golf and the city centre; Italian, Lebanese and Congolese restaurants. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide (EN), updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] The Biennale de Lubumbashi runs every two years; the Picha Art Center hosts the photography biennial in October of even years. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi" / Expat.com (EN) 2025-12-19, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Ciné Bétamax is the main modern cinema; the Institut français offers films, plays and a library; the national museum and botanical garden are in town. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] In April 2026 the city hosted the Amani Festival, moved from Goma because of the war in the east. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Football rules: TP Mazembe (5 African Champions League titles), FC Saint-Éloi Lupopo, CS Don Bosco; also the country's last equestrian club (Cercle Hippique) and a growing street-basketball scene. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mainly Christian (Catholic, Kimbanguist, Baptist, Anglican, Presbyterian, Assemblies of God), with mosques too. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Urban population about 2.6 million (2021 estimate); Swahili is the street lingua franca, French the official language, with Kiluba, Chokwe and Bemba also spoken. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [community.expat] An estimated 15,000–20,000 expatriates (Chinese, Belgian, Indian, Lebanese, South African); the Expat.com forum has 667 members. — Expat.com Lubumbashi guide, updated 2025-12-19, blog, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] A French-speaking community is described as active mainly through social-media groups. — OpenShores "Vivre à Lubumbashi", 2026-04-15, company, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] Visa required for French citizens; stays over 6 months need a residence permit requested in the DRC; working needs a VET plus the employer's VETS. Lubumbashi airport is one of the main entry points. — France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [admin.residency] Belgium has a consulate-general in Lubumbashi; France has a consular agency there. — Belgian FPS 2026-10-27 / France Diplomatie 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [admin.nationality] Congolese nationality is exclusive (art. 10); the government announced in Feb 2026 a gradual reform starting with a special diaspora status. — Actualite.cd, 2026-02-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Altitude 1,208 m: highs about 25–31 °C, October the hottest month, June–July nights down to about 8 °C; rain mostly Nov–Mar (≈ 1,238 mm/year), almost none May–Sept; UV index 11+ most of the year. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Muyambo Park (opened 2010, about 15 km out) and Mikembo on the Kasenga road; the zoo is run by the non-profit AZLU; Lac Kipopo is named as an outing. — Wikipedia "Lubumbashi" / Expat.com (EN) 2025-12-19, academic, confidence: medium
- [rights.lgbt] Same-sex acts are legal, marriage is restricted to opposite-sex couples (Constitution art. 40, 2006), and indecency laws have been used against same-sex couples (US State Dept 2021). — Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in the DRC", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
