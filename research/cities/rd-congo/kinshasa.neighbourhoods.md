# Kinshasa: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (from the dossier and the city file): **1 USD ≈ 0.86 EUR** (editorial, unsourced, flagged in the dossier) and **2,265 CDF per USD** (Banque centrale indicative rate reported by ACP, 30 Oct 2026), i.e. **1 EUR ≈ 2,634 CDF**. Rent, schools, clinics and supermarkets are priced in USD; markets, buses and taxis in CDF. `[K tag]` = an item in `research/cities/rd-congo/kinshasa.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/rd-congo.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15): the whole country is orange ("sauf raison impérative"); insecurity in the capital is worsening with the economy: armed robbery, burglary, car-jacking, kidnapping and fake police checks; permanent home guarding is advised; foreigners alone at night spots are favoured targets for blackmail [K safety.general][D].
- Belgium (updated 2026-10-27): urban crime "very high"; avoid taxis, public transport and walking, especially at night; protests are regularly announced [K safety.general]. Assaults on passengers, especially young women, in unknown taxis at night are recurrent; France advises daytime rides with known, privatised drivers [K safety.women]. The routes below therefore assume a known driver or a booked car for anything after dark.
- Power cuts can last weeks in some neighbourhoods (Radio Okapi, 2026-10-06) [K daily.power]; expat housing is in compounds with guards, generators and water storage (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [K housing.areas]. Budget a generator wherever you live.
- Flooding: the Kinshasa plain floods in the rainy season (Oct–May) [K climate.daily]; Wikipedia lists Kalamu, Bandalungwa, Ngiri-Ngiri, Kinshasa, Barumbu, Lingwala and Limete on the flood-prone plain [12].
- Upfront housing costs: three months' rent in advance plus a deposit of one to three months (Expat.com) [K housing.search]; the law caps the deposit at 3 months but 6–10 are often asked (Radio Okapi, 2026-01-21), and an agent reports 8–12 months in central communes (ACP, 2026-02-26) [K housing.search]. Viewing-fee scams of about USD 10 are reported [K housing.search].
- Health context: Ebola outbreak since 15 May 2026, eight provinces as of 16 Oct 2026, Kinshasa not among them; a few mpox cases and chikungunya in Kinshasa [D][K health.care]. A Kin+ post of 14 Nov 2026 refers to an ongoing Ebola response involving Hôpital du Cinquantenaire; to be checked against WHO bulletins [K health.care].
- Ride-hailing: Yango is the only authorised app since July 2025 [K daily.transport]; Wikivoyage (undated) says fares show in-app and are paid cash, and also names SwiftRide and ALBO Taxi [1]. No Yango fare in CDF was found.

## Neighbourhoods

### Gombe (business and government centre)
- Feel: the administrative and economic heart, tree-lined, both residential and business, historically the European expatriates' quarter; north and west hold institutions and embassies and a riverside promenade, the east holds the port, Beach Ngobila and Gare de l'Est (Wikipedia FR, read 2026-11-14) [3]. Expat.com calls it the area with the most reliable services [D]. Water use is about 306 L per person per day, against 2 L in Kimbanseke [K housing.areas].
- Rent: flats USD 1,500–5,000 (≈ €1,290–4,300), villas USD 2,500–10,000; a 3-bed USD 2,500–3,000 (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [K cost.rent]; furnished 2-bed of 125 m² USD 3,500 (≈ €3,010, MediaCongo, 2026-05-06); furnished 3-bed with pool and gym at INA USD 6,000 (≈ €5,160, 2026-07-17) [K cost.rent]; a flat at USD 4,500 "négociable légèrement" (Kinshasa Expats Marketplace, 2026-11-14) [K cost.rent]. Studio USD 400–800 in the residential communes incl. Gombe per Africarrières (2026-08-07), cheaper than the other guides [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: this is the centre; Boulevard du 30 Juin runs through it [3]. Ndjili → Gombe bus 2,000 → 3,000–4,000 CDF after Dec 2025 (≈ €1.14–1.52) [K daily.transport]. A 25 km trip can take 2–3 hours at peak [D].
- Watch: France names Gombe, where most foreigners live, as affected by traffic-jam robberies (wing mirrors stolen; keep doors locked), 2026-09-15 [K safety.general]. A protest targeted embassies on 28 Jan 2025 (Belgium, 2026-10-27) [K safety.general].
- Places:
  - Silikin Village, Concession COTEX, 63 avenue Colonel Mondjiba (coworking: 200+ seats, 50+ private offices, meeting rooms, food court, backup power; Mon–Sat 07:00–22:00; prices not published) — silikinvillage.com [23]; inaugurated Oct 2024 — Actualite.cd [24]
  - Jardin botanique de Kinshasa, avenue Kasa-Vubu next to the Grand Marché (7 ha park, 100+ tree species; hours and fee not stated) — Wikipedia FR [15]
  - Grand Marché (Marché central), just east of the botanical garden, administratively in Kinshasa commune (the city centre's largest market) — Wikipedia FR [16][15]
  - Lycée français René Descartes, avenue de la Gombe, with a second site on avenue Kalemie (French school, nursery to terminale) — Wikipedia EN [13]
  - Lycée Prince de Liège, quartier universitaire de Gombe (Belgian curriculum, nursery to CESS, about 900 pupils) — Wikipedia FR [14]
  - Hôpital général de Kinshasa, avenue de l'Hôpital / avenue Tombalbaye (reference public hospital) — Wikipedia FR [3]; France says public hospitals rarely give quality care [K health.care]
  - Sultani Hotel, 30 avenue de la Justice: Marie Kabuang restaurant (business lunch USD 15) and bar with live jazz on Fridays (cocktails from about USD 8) — Wikivoyage EN/FR [1][2]
  - Hotel Memling, 5 avenue de la République du Tchad: Cockpit Bar (happy hour 18:00–20:00), Mangoustan (weekday lunch buffet USD 40) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Chacha Bar, top of Immeuble Forescom, 4 avenue du Port (terrace bar) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Paroisse Sainte-Anne (Catholic parish) — Wikipedia EN [12]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (secure rents start around USD 1,500); Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (Silikin Village, banks, the DGM and the Banque centrale are here [3]); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French and Belgian schools on site); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Ngaliema: Ma Campagne, Binza, UPN, Joli Parc
- Feel: a large western commune running from Kintambo up Mont Ngaliema along the route de Matadi, about 683,000 people in 2004 (Wikipedia FR) [4]; holds the French embassy (Basoko), the Lebanese embassy (Joli Parc / Ma Campagne), Camp Tshatshi and the Cité de l'Union africaine [4]. Expat.com names Ngaliema (river views) and Ma Campagne (families) among the expat areas [D][K housing.areas].
- Rent: from USD 2,000 (≈ €1,720) on the expat market (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [K cost.rent]; an unfurnished new 3-bed, 3-bath, air-conditioned flat near UPN at USD 900 (≈ €775), deposit "3+1" (MediaCongo, 2026-03-04) [K cost.rent]; residential-commune ranges: studio USD 400–800, 2-bed USD 800–1,500 (Africarrières, 2026-08-07) [K cost.rent]. A Ngaliema villa for sale at USD 1.1 M (MediaCongo, 2026-11-14) [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: Binza-Ozone has no Transco line; a 30-minute ride to the centre can take 2–3 hours at peak, and some drivers leave at 3 a.m. (Radio Okapi, 2026-09-08) [K daily.transport]. During the Jan 2026 police checks, one woman walked about 14 km from UPN to Kintambo Magasin (Radio Okapi, 2026-01-26) [K daily.transport].
- Watch: nothing area-specific beyond the transport isolation above; city-wide frames apply.
- Places:
  - TASOK (American School of Kinshasa), route de Matadi (IB from preschool to grade 12; fees USD 15,000–25,000/yr per Expat.com) — tasok.net [25]; [K family.schools]
  - GGMart hypermarket, near the Rêve Congo residential complex — Wikipedia EN [12]
  - Kin Marché branches named "UPN", "Delvaux" and "Binza Ozone (Centre Sup)" (supermarket; addresses not read) — kinmarche.com store list [26]
  - Saint-Léopold Catholic parish (founded 1899) — Wikipedia EN [12]
  - Parc du mont Ngaliema and the Académie des Beaux-Arts, avenue du 24 Novembre / Pierre Mulele (art school with upmarket art for sale) — Wikivoyage FR/EN [2][1]
  - Snake park at Binza Pigeon — Wikivoyage FR [2] (no hours or fee)
  - Hotel Livana, Ma Campagne (hotel, no price) — Wikivoyage FR [2]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (TASOK on the route de Matadi); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" or "Moyen" (UPN listings near USD 900); Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q7 "Prend son temps" only if you rarely need the centre.

### Kintambo
- Feel: the historic first trading post, at the junction of the boulevard du 30 Juin / avenue Colonel Mondjiba, avenue Kasa-Vubu and the route de Matadi; small (2.7 km²) and dense, about 107,000 people in 2004, with colonial-style concessions (Wikipedia FR) [10]. Expat.com lists it among the expat areas [K housing.areas].
- Rent: from USD 1,000 (≈ €860) on the expat market (Expat.com, 2025-11-24) [K cost.rent]. Deposits of 8–12 months reported in central communes, Kintambo included (ACP, 2026-02-26) [K housing.search].
- Getting around: the city centre moved about 5 km east of Kintambo in the 1940s (Wikipedia FR) [10]; Silikin Village sits on avenue Colonel Mondjiba, the extension of the boulevard that meets Kintambo's centre [23][10]. No sourced door-to-door time.
- Watch: a women's foundation filed a memorandum over frequent unplanned power cuts, saying dark streets become crime hotspots (Radio Okapi, 2026-01-23) [K daily.power].
- Places:
  - Kintambo-Magasins commercial centre — Wikipedia EN [12]; Kin Marché "Kintambo Magasin" branch — kinmarche.com [26]
  - Vélodrome de Kintambo (stadium, built 1936) — Wikipedia EN [12]
  - Saint Francis parish (Catholic, 1939) and Église francophone CBCO Kintambo (Baptist) — Wikipedia EN [12]
  - Silikin Village coworking, a short hop east (see Gombe) — [23]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (cheapest expat-listed area near Silikin Village); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit".

### Limete (east, residential and industrial)
- Feel: an eastern commune between the Pool Malebo and boulevard Lumumba, with many industries, most port installations, the FIKIN fairground and the Cité du Fleuve development; about 376,000 people in 2004 (Wikipedia FR) [5]. Named as middle-to-upper by Africarrières and OpenShores [D]. Home of the Connexion Kin arts festival since 2009 [K social.culture].
- Rent: flats on Limete's 8e rue USD 400–450 (≈ €344–387); a ground-floor flat USD 400 + 20 water and power (Kinshasa Expats Marketplace, 2026-11-14) [K cost.rent]; residential-commune ranges studio USD 400–800, 2-bed 800–1,500 (Africarrières) [K cost.rent]; a tenant says a USD 150 house now goes for 250–300 (MediaCongo, 2026-08-04) [K cost.rent]. Plots: Limete Mombele 13×25 m USD 79,000 (2026-11-13/14) [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: Rond-point Ngaba → Zando moto-taxi 7,000 → 10,000 CDF (≈ €2.70 → 3.80, Dec 2025) [K daily.transport] gives an order of magnitude for a cross-town moto; France and Belgium advise foreigners against motos and public transport [K safety.general]. The ONATRA airport train stops in Limete (Wikivoyage FR, undated) [2].
- Watch: in Jan 2024 rising water flooded much of Cité du Fleuve [K climate.daily]; the April 2025 Ndjili river floods killed 72 people, mainly in Ndjili, Matete, Limete and Masina (infos.cd, 2025-04-14) [K climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Limete Tower (monument; garden open, tower closed as of 2022) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - FIKIN (Foire internationale de Kinshasa, fairground, main entrance facing Lemba) — Wikipedia FR [5]
  - Collège Saint-Raphaël and Complexe scolaire Cardinal Malula (schools) — Wikipedia FR [5]
  - Kin Marché branch "7em Rue, Place Commerciale" (supermarket; commune not stated on the page, numbered "rues" are a Limete feature per the 8e rue listing) — kinmarche.com [26]; [K cost.rent]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen"; Q4 "En couple"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (port and industry); Q5 "Création, culture" (Connexion Kin).

### Lemba (university hill)
- Feel: a commune of about 350,000 (2005), home to the Université de Kinshasa on Mont Amba and several higher-education institutes (Wikipedia FR) [8]. Africarrières lists it among the popular communes [K cost.rent]; ACP names it among areas where rents are rising [K cost.rent].
- Rent: new 2-bed, 2-bath flats in Salongo Sud at USD 500 (≈ €430), deposit "3+1" (MediaCongo, 2026-04-19) [K cost.rent]; a single-storey house USD 450 (≈ €387, Kinshasa Expats Marketplace, 2026-11-14) [K cost.rent]; popular-commune ranges: room USD 80–200, simple house 150–400, 2-bed 200–600 (Africarrières, 2026-08-07) [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: no Lemba-specific fare or time found; city-wide shared taxi 1,000–2,500 CDF, bus 500–1,000 CDF (Africarrières) [K daily.transport].
- Watch: deposits of 8–12 months reported in Lemba among central communes (ACP, 2026-02-26) [K housing.search].
- Places:
  - Université de Kinshasa (former Lovanium), Mont Amba — Wikipedia FR [8]; its history and ethnography museums — Wikivoyage FR [2]
  - Kin Marché branches "Lemba" (KM 14, Lemba commune), "Lemba Terminus", "Righini" and "Terre Jaune" (Righini and Terre Jaune are Lemba sub-quarters per Wikipedia) — kinmarche.com [26]; [8]
  - Groupe scolaire du Mont-Amba (school) — Wikipedia FR [8]
  - FIKIN fairground entrance faces Lemba — Wikipedia FR [5]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q5 "Santé, enseignement" (university and medical-technology institute); Q4 "En couple"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" is not claimed (no source on pace).

### Kalamu (Matonge, Victoire) and Bandalungwa
- Feel: Matonge, in northern Kalamu between the Victoire quarter and Stade Tata Raphaël, is one of the city's main nightlife areas with many bars and recording studios (Wikipedia FR) [9]; Kalamu is very dense (about 47,500/km²) [6]. Bandalungwa ("Bandal") is a modest-income, dense commune where, per Wikipedia FR (undated text), burglaries are frequent and night-time noise is daily; culturally lively, home to Fally Ipupa and Wenge 4x4 [7]. Wikivoyage names Matonge, Bandal and Bonmarché as nightlife neighbourhoods [1].
- Rent: no Kalamu or Bandalungwa listing found. Closest sourced proxy: a Barumbu agent's central popular-commune rents, 1 bed + living USD 200 → 250 (≈ €215), 2-bed 300 → 350–400 (ACP, 2026-02-26) [K cost.rent]. Kalamu families say they were pushed further out by rising rents (MediaCongo, 2026-08-04) [K cost.rent]. Cheap hotels in Kalamu: La Crèche from USD 20, BIBI-PLAY'S USD 15–25 (Wikivoyage FR, July 2023) [2].
- Getting around: Victoire → Zando bus 1,000 → 1,500 CDF (≈ €0.57, Dec 2025) [K daily.transport]; on Point Chaud–Victoire passengers often paid 1,500 CDF instead of the official 1,000 (infos.cd, 2025-01-13) [K cost.negotiation]; a rumoured strike pushed Yolo-Nord → Huilerie from 2,000 to 4,000 CDF (ACP, 2026-01-26) [K cost.negotiation].
- Watch: both communes sit on the flood-prone plain (Wikipedia EN) [12]; Bandal burglary remark is Wikipedia's undated text, not a dated official source.
- Places:
  - Stade Tata Raphaël (60,000-seat football stadium, site of the 1974 "Rumble in the Jungle"; tenants DCMP and AS Vita Club) — Wikipedia EN [20]; Wikipedia FR [6]
  - Rond-point Victoire and Matonge's bars (no venue named in a readable source) — Wikipedia FR [9][6]
  - Avenue Kapela ("busy and lively") and Couloir Kimbuta ("heart of the atmosphere"; commune not stated) — Wikivoyage FR [2]; Kin Marché "Kapela" and "Victoire" branches — kinmarche.com [26]
  - La Bloque, Bandalungwa (well-known roadside stall) and Mama Colonel, Bandal (barbecued chicken, fish, fries, plantain) — Wikivoyage EN [1]
  - Terrain Allemagne, Terrain Ngoma, Terrain Municipal (football grounds, Bandalungwa) — Wikipedia FR [7]
  - Kin Marché "Sendwe (Tata Raphael)" branch — kinmarche.com [26]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q3 "Serré"; Q5 "Création, culture" (studios, rumba). Not for Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Considered and set aside
- **Mont-Ngafula**: urban-rural, hilly, isolated, mostly poor, short of drinking water and electricity, with erosion and insecurity (Wikipedia FR) [11]. It holds Clinique Monkole and Hôpital Mama Koko, the UNIKIN campus side, Lac Ma Vallée and Lola ya Bonobo [11]; Monkole is one of the four facilities France names for expatriates [K health.care]. Treated below as a day-trip area, not a base.
- **Masina, Ndjili, Kingasani**: cheapest rents (Kingasani studio USD 100–120, Masina 1-bed USD 200, ACP 2026-02-26) [K cost.rent], but far east, flood-hit in April 2025 [K climate.daily], and Masina → Zando bus doubled to 4,000 CDF [K daily.transport]. Marché de la Liberté is on boulevard Lumumba in Masina [16].

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city file, the dossier and the sources listed here. "Assumption" marks a non-sourced step. No coworking price, gym price, club entry, Yango fare or mobile-money tariff was found, so those lines are left out. Car rides use the Africarrières private-taxi range 20,000–50,000 CDF (≈ USD 8.83–22.08) [K daily.transport], because the official advice rules out buses and motos for foreigners [K safety.general].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Kintambo**. The cheapest area on Expat.com's expat list (from USD 1,000) [K cost.rent], next to Silikin Village's coworking and backup power [23][10]. Fall-back if cuts on your street drag on: Gombe [K daily.power].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office on generator power; groceries at Kin Marché Kintambo Magasin [26].
  - Tue: coworking at Silikin Village (Mon–Sat 07:00–22:00) [23], lunch at its food court; short car ride, time not sourced.
  - Wed: home office; late afternoon walk in the Jardin botanique, Gombe (by car) [15].
  - Thu: Silikin Village day and evening event if listed [23]; dinner at Marie Kabuang, Sultani Hotel (USD 15) [1].
  - Fri: home office; after-work jazz at the Sultani bar (Fridays, no cover charge) [2].
  - Sat: Grand Marché morning with a local companion (Wikivoyage advises keeping a local with you) [16][1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at Saint Francis parish, Kintambo [12]; quiet afternoon at home (Silikin Village closes on Sundays) [23].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Village of Chez Ntemba, avenue du Port (busy after midnight) or Millionaire Club, 95 boulevard du 30 Juin [1]; Matonge bars with a known driver both ways [9][K safety.women].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": La Brasserie (Belgian-French, Memling) or Chez Gaby (Portuguese-style) [1]; cossa cossa river prawns USD 25–40 a plate [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; no delivery service sourced. Hotel Memling's Wednesday jazz is the quiet live-music alternative on a weeknight [2].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": not realistic from Kinshasa (river city, no sea) [D]. Nearest substitute: a Yacht Club boat with driver to a Congo River sandbank for a barbecue, about USD 150 a day [1].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Lac Ma Vallée, Mont-Ngafula, about 30 km and an hour without traffic via the route de Matadi (6 km lake loop, pedalo, kayak, zip-line; no swimming; 4x4 often needed in the rainy season) [17]; Lola ya Bonobo at the Petites Chutes de la Lukaya, about 50 minutes from the centre [2][18].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Parc de la Vallée de la N'Sele, a drive-through park with lions and giraffes (fee not sourced) [1]. The Bombo-Lumene reserve, about 130 km out toward Kikwit [1], is on the Batéké plateau where Belgium reports militia activity on RN1/RN17 (2026-10-27) [K safety.general]: not advised.
  - "Je reste en ville": National Museum of the DRC (opened Nov 2019, 6,000 m² of galleries; fee not sourced) [22]; Académie des Beaux-Arts [1].
- Rough weekly spend (one person, USD):
  - Rent: 1,000 ÷ 4.33 = 230.9 (Expat.com "from", so a floor) [K cost.rent]
  - Home internet: 30–80/month ÷ 4.33 = 6.9–18.5 (OpenShores low end, Africarrières higher) [K daily.internet]
  - Mobile data backup: 15,000–25,000 CDF/month ÷ 4.33 = 3,464–5,774 CDF = 1.5–2.6 [K daily.internet]
  - Generator: 200–600/month ÷ 4.33 = 46.2–138.6 [K daily.power]
  - 7 local meals × 5–10 = 35–70; 2 mid-range meals × 15–25 = 30–50 [K cost.living]
  - 4 private-taxi rides × 20,000–50,000 CDF = 80,000–200,000 CDF = 35.3–88.3 [K daily.transport]
  - Saturday: 4 local beers × 2,000–3,000 CDF = 8,000–12,000 CDF = 3.5–5.3 [K cost.living]
  - **Total ≈ USD 389–604 a week (≈ €335–520)**, before coworking (price not published), electricity bills and groceries. Cross-check: Expat.com's single budget is USD 2,130/month (≈ USD 492/week) [K cost.living].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Gombe**. Silikin Village (offices, coworking, events) [23], the Banque centrale, the DGM and ministries [3]. Ifri (Oct 2026) warns that returnee businesses often hit established circles and some founders went back in debt [K business.invest]; Kadea Academy trains and places tech talent in Kinshasa [K work.jobs].
- Week:
  - Mon: Silikin Village desk; paperwork at the DGM headquarters, Gombe (residence permit beyond 6 months, VET/VETS rules for work) [3][K admin.visa].
  - Tue: client meetings; business lunch at Marie Kabuang (USD 15) [1].
  - Wed: Silikin Village event or networking [23]; evening Memling jazz [2].
  - Thu: supplier visit to the Grand Marché (by car, with a local) [16][1].
  - Fri: Mangoustan lunch buffet at the Memling (USD 40) [1]; Sultani jazz at night [2].
  - Sat: Académie des Beaux-Arts and the "marché des valeurs" art market, where asking prices run 60–75 % above the final price [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: rest; riverside promenade in front of the British and German embassies [1].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": K Lounge, 124 boulevard du 30 Juin, or Coco Jambo Lounge, 11 avenue de la Presse [1].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": first week of a trial stay
- Before landing: tourist visa for stays under 6 months, passport valid 6 months, yellow-fever certificate mandatory [K admin.visa]; beyond 6 months a residence permit from the DGM [K admin.visa]. Bring recent, undamaged USD (worn USD 100 notes refused; USD 1 notes accepted almost nowhere) [K daily.money].
- Day 1: N'djili airport; taxi into town USD 30–50, 2 hours or more (up to 6 in traffic); a visitor escort into the terminal USD 10; keep your luggage tag stub (bribe requests reported) [1]. Stay first in Gombe: Hotel Pour Vous, 22 avenue de la République du Tchad (USD 59) or Léon Hôtel, 41 avenue Luambo Makiadi (USD 60, Mar 2022) [1]; Sultani from USD 175 [2].
- Day 2: SIM card at the Airtel, Orange or Vodacom stores at the Pullman Grand Hotel [1]; mobile data 15,000–25,000 CDF/month [K daily.internet]. Mobile money: no source read on how to open a wallet (gap).
- Day 3: money: cards work in big hotels and some supermarkets [K daily.money][1]; Wikivoyage reports a Pro Credit Bank ATM working with an international Visa card [1]. Opening a local account: no source found (gap).
- Days 4–6: flat hunting. Read MediaCongo listings and the Kinshasa Expats Marketplace group [K cost.rent], then visit in person (listings are often outdated) [K cost.negotiation]. Expect 3 months' advance plus deposit, "4+1" or "5 mois de garantie" on the group; the legal deposit cap is 3 months [K housing.search]. Refuse to pay "travel fees" before seeing a flat (USD 10 scams reported) [K housing.search]. Prices are often marked "à discuter" (12 of 20 MediaCongo listings) [K cost.negotiation].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at Notre-Dame du Congo [21] and a first look at Kintambo or Limete with a hired car.
- Customs on household goods: no source found (gap).
- Spend for the week: hotel 7 × 59–60 = USD 413–420; airport taxi 30–50; SIM and data 3,464–5,774 CDF a week = 1.5–2.6; 14 local meals × 5–10 = 70–140 [1][K cost.living][K daily.internet]. **≈ USD 515–613 (≈ €442–527)** before any rent advance.

## Visiting: discovery

### One day
- Morning: Jardin botanique, Gombe (7 ha, 100+ tree species) [15], then the Grand Marché next door with a local companion [16][1].
- Midday: lunch at Marie Kabuang, Sultani Hotel (USD 15) [1], or Al Dar shawarma near the Memling (about USD 3) [1].

### One week
- Day 1: arrival via N'djili, check in Gombe [1].
- Day 2: the one-day route above.
- Day 3 (out of town): Lola ya Bonobo at the Petites Chutes de la Lukaya, then Lac Ma Vallée (Mont-Ngafula, about 1 hour without traffic) for the lake walk and lunch at its terrace restaurant [18][17][2].
- Day 4 (daily life): rush hour on the Victoire → Zando run (bus 1,500 CDF since Dec 2025) watched from the car, then morning at the Grand Marché and a Kin Marché branch; afternoon in Matonge and Rond-point Victoire [K daily.transport][16][26][9].
