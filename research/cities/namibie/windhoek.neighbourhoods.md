# Windhoek: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **N$20 ≈ €1** (dossier editorial rounding; the N$ is pegged 1:1 to the rand). USD figures from aggregators are converted at **US$1 ≈ €0.86**, the city file's convention. Wikivoyage Namibia gives €1 ≈ N$19 "as of October 2026" [4], so EUR figures here are slightly low. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/namibie/windhoek.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/namibie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read".

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Car city.** Public transport is scarce and most trips are by taxi [9][C daily.transport]. Shared minibus/sedan taxis cost **N$15 a trip since 18 May 2026** (set nationally, not haggled) [C daily.transport; cost.negotiation]; Wikivoyage (pre-rise, N$13) adds that "out of the way" trips cost double and fares double after midnight [3]. Pre-booked radio taxis start at about N$80 a trip; Yango from about N$30 [3]. The FCDO advises against hailing taxis in the street, especially in Windhoek [C safety.general].
- **Safety (FCDO, read 2026-11-14, page date not confirmed):** muggers target busy central areas at any time of day; gangs sometimes try to enter cars at busy intersections; do not enter townships at night without someone who knows the area [C safety.general]. City Police counted 100+ pedestrian collisions in the first half of 2026 (New Era, 2026-07-23) [C safety.general].
- **Water:** Category D restrictions were lifted on 11 Aug 2025 with dams at 86.2% (The Namibian, 2025-08-08) [C daily.water]. **Power:** no 2025–26 outage reporting found [C daily.power].
- **Before signing a lease:** the City disconnects accounts more than 30 days in arrears and owners stay liable for tenants' unpaid bills (The Brief, 2026-11-06) — ask to see the flat's municipal account [C housing.search].
- **Rents moved fast:** a Brief op-ed (2026-08-03) says 1-beds are now advertised at N$12,000–15,000 excl. utilities, roughly double the Jan 2024 survey averages quoted per area below [C cost.rent]. Treat 2024 area averages as a floor.
- **Shops and alcohol:** many shops close Sunday or at 13:00; take-away alcohol is not sold from Saturday 13:00 to Monday morning [3][4].
- **Rent search:** Facebook posts, WhatsApp groups and the free NamSpace portal (launched Sept 2026) [C housing.search].
- **LGBT readers:** sodomy offences struck down in June 2024 (appeal outcome not found); a Dec 2024 law bans recognition of same-sex marriages; Pride has marched in Windhoek since 2013 [C rights.lgbt]. Applies to every neighbourhood.

## Neighbourhoods

### Klein Windhoek
- Feel: the oldest part of the city, an affluent suburb [7]; hillside homes behind trees and stone walls, mixed with embassies, churches and offices, wealth "understated rather than announced" (Namibian Sun, 2025-12-15) [5]; a leafy valley just east of the central business district that a property portal pitches at families, professionals and diplomats [12].
- Rent: 2-bed average N$9,600 (≈ €480; The Brief survey, Jan 2024) and 3-bed sectional title N$12,000 (≈ €600) [C cost.rent]; furnished 2-beds N$15,000–32,000 (≈ €750–1,600; MyProperty, read 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; the portal's suburb page shows a median 2-bed rent of N$25,000 over a small sample (read 2026-11-15, undated) [12]. Expat.com gives N$12,000–25,000 for a 2–3-bed flat here (2025-10-31, self-contradictory page) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: "just east" of the CBD [12] (no sourced minutes). Radio taxi ≥N$80, Yango from N$30 [3]; shared taxis are hard to find at night in the posh suburbs (Wikivoyage) [3].
- Watch: no area-specific dated safety source; see the citywide FCDO frame.
- Places:
  - Luigi and the Fish, 320 Sam Nujoma Dr (restaurant; game, seafood, pizza; book Fri–Sat) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - The Stellenbosch Wine Bar & Bistro, Bougain Villas, Sam Nujoma Dr (wine bar, restaurant) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Jenny's Place, Bougain Villa 78, Sam Nujoma Dr (arts and crafts shop with classes) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Klein Windhoek Guest House, 2 Hofmeyer St (guest house) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Joe's Beer House, 160 Nelson Mandela Ave (Namibian food and beer hall; main N$150, large beer N$25) — Wikivoyage [3]; 0.8 km per portal [12]
  - St Paul's College (private secondary; N$75,000–110,000/yr in 2026) — portal amenity list [12]; fees [C family.schools]
  - Deutsche Höhere Privatschule, DHPS (German private school; N$65,000–90,000/yr) — portal, about 2 km [12]; fees [C family.schools]
  - Klein Windhoek SuperSpar (supermarket) — portal amenity list only, not independently confirmed [12]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (the city's restaurant cluster); Q5 "Télétravail" (fibre suburbs [D]); Q7 "Prend son temps".

### Eros

- Rent: 2-bed average N$8,000 (≈ €400; Brief survey Jan 2024) [C cost.rent]; two 1-bed listings at N$13,000 and N$16,500 (≈ €650–825; MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [D]; furnished 2-beds N$25,000–27,000, some priced in rand [C cost.rent]; portal median 2-bed N$25,000 (7 rentals) [14].
- Getting around: Windhoek Central about 2.3 km (portal) [14]; Eros Airport (small aircraft) 7 km south of the centre [9].
- Places:

### Olympia and Kleine Kuppe (south)
- Feel: Olympia is townhouses and small units for young professionals, dual-income households and first-time buyers; Kleine Kuppe and Auasblick are hillside homes and gated complexes for executives, diplomats and expatriates, "inward-facing" (Namibian Sun, 2025-12-15) [5]. Portal: Kleine Kuppe mixes houses, townhouses and security complexes [11].
- Rent: Kleine Kuppe 2-bed average N$8,000 (Jan 2024) [C cost.rent]; a tenant's Kleine Kuppe 1-bed at N$12,000 excl. utilities (op-ed, 2026-08-03, testimony) [C cost.rent]; 3-bed townhouse in Kleine Kuppe N$40,000 and 3-bed house in Olympia N$50,000 (≈ €2,000–2,500; MyProperty 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: B1 highway about 1 km from Olympia, Western Bypass 3.5 km from Kleine Kuppe (portal) [15][11]; airport 42 km [11]. No sourced commute time to the CBD; peak-hour traffic is described as dense [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Independence Stadium, Olympia (25,000-seat football stadium; CAF-decommissioned in 2021, used for state ceremonies in 2024–25) — Wikipedia [20]
  - Maerua Mall (mall: cinema, Virgin Active gym, Mugg & Bean, FNB; opposite Centaurus High School) — Wikipedia [18]; Jan Jonker Road per Wikivoyage [3]
  - Lady Pohamba Private Hospital (private hospital; about 1.2 km from Kleine Kuppe) — Wikivoyage lists it [3]; distance from portal [11]
  - Grove Mall of Namibia (mall with Ster-Kinekor cinema) — portal amenity lists only [11][15]
  - Windhoek Gymnasium (private school; N$70,000–90,000/yr in 2026) — portal [11]; fees [C family.schools]
  - PepperCorn, Shop 59, Maerua Mall (restaurant, 09:00–22:00) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q4 "En couple" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q5 "Salarié·e" (a car commute from a mall-equipped suburb); Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (gated complexes [5]).

### Pionierspark and Academia
- Feel: steady middle-class suburbs of teachers, nurses, technicians and civil servants, single-storey homes, townhouses and small flats (Namibian Sun, 2025-12-15) [5]. Academia also houses students sharing rooms (op-ed, 2026-08-03) [C cost.rent].
- Rent: Pionierspark 1-bed N$6,800, 2-bed N$8,500, 3-bed N$11,000 (averages, Jan 2024) [C cost.rent]; furnished 1-bed in Pionierspark N$16,500 with utilities (MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; furnished room in Academia N$5,500 with water and power + N$3,000 deposit (Facebook "Windhoek Rentals", 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; 3-bed house in Academia N$30,000 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared taxi N$15 [C daily.transport]; Heroes' Acre is 13 km south on the B1 [3]. No sourced commute time.
- Places:
  - Windhoek International School, Scheppmann St, Pioneers Park (IB primary and diploma, IGCSE; ages 3–19; N$85,000–160,000/yr in 2026) — Wikipedia [17]; fees [C family.schools]
  - AJ du Plooy Sports Fields, Socrates St, Academia (free football, rugby, tennis, basketball fields, picnic areas) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Hotel Casa Blanca, 52 Fritsche St, Pioneers Park (mid-range hotel) — Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (WIS is in the suburb); Q3 "Moyen"; Q10 "La santé et l'école".

### Windhoek Central and Windhoek West
- Feel: Windhoek West is older houses, flats and converted homes close to the core for professionals, students and renters (Namibian Sun) [5]; the portal calls it mature and tree-lined [10]. The centre itself is small and easy to walk by day (Wikivoyage) [3].
- Rent: CBD 1-bed average N$6,800 (≈ €340; Jan 2024) and the same CBD complex advertising 1-beds at N$9,500 and N$6,500 [C cost.rent]; Windhoek West 3-bed N$11,000 (Jan 2024) [C cost.rent]; furnished 1-bed in Windhoek Central N$16,000 and furnished 2-beds N$15,000–26,000 (MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Windhoek West lies under 1 km from Windhoek Central (portal) [10]; walking distance to Independence Avenue; the railway station is on Bahnhof St [3].
- Watch: FCDO says muggers often target busy central areas at any time of day and gangs try to enter cars at busy intersections (read 2026-11-14) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Christuskirche, traffic circle at Robert Mugabe Ave and Fidel Castro St (Lutheran church) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [9]
  - Independence Memorial Museum (museum; Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, free; rooftop restaurant) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Tintenpalast and Parliament Gardens (parliament and public gardens) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [9]
  - Zoo Park, Independence Ave (park, playground, open-air theatre) — Wikipedia [9]; La Marmite Royale – Café Zoo at its corner (pan-African restaurant, karaoke upstairs) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Post Street Mall (craft market; outdoor stalls cheaper, haggling expected) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Pick n Pay, Wernhill Centre (supermarket; internet café upstairs, N$12/30 min) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Old Location Ohungi, Purcell St, Windhoek West (Namibian food incl. kapana and mopane; main N$130, draught N$35; updated Jan 2026) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Franco-Namibian Cultural Centre, Robert Mugabe Ave (French cultural centre, classes) — French embassy [C social.culture]; Wikivoyage [3]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" and "Création, culture" (walk to banks, FNCC, galleries); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q4 "Seul·e"; Q6 "Français" (only francophone anchor is here).

### Khomasdal
- Feel: historically the Coloured residential area, still mainly Coloured, about 15 sub-neighbourhoods, nicknamed "Die Dal" with a party reputation [8]; brick houses, many with backyard rooms, working-class and middle-income families and small-business owners, fully formal (Namibian Sun, 2025-12-15) [5].
- Rent: 1-bed average N$4,500 (≈ €225; Jan 2024, with Otjomuise), 3-bed N$8,500 [C cost.rent]; 2-bed with water N$9,000 (≈ €450; MyProperty 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. The suburb portal showed no rentals on 2026-11-15 [13].
- Getting around: city centre about 5 km (portal) [13]; Monte Christo Road is the arterial [13]; shared taxi N$15 [C daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Khomasdal informal market, corner of Mahatma Gandhi and Hans-Dietrich Genscher streets (municipal SME market) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Windhoek College of Education (teacher-training college) — Wikipedia [8]
  - Khomasdal Health Centre (public clinic) — portal amenity list only [13]
  - Checkers Khomasdal and Khomasdal Mall (supermarket, mall) — portal amenity list only [13]
  - Jan Jonker Afrikaner Secondary School (state secondary) — portal amenity list only [13]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (reputation only [8]); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" relative to the leafy suburbs.

### Katutura
- Feel: the township created in 1961 by forced removal from the Old Location, about 8 km north of the centre; the name is Otjiherero for "we do not settle" [6]. Working-class; parts planned and serviced, others informal, with access to services "uneven even within a few streets" (Namibian Sun, 2025-12-15) [5].
- Rent: 1-bed average N$4,000 (≈ €200), mostly backyard flats with utilities often included; 2-bed N$5,250; 3-bed N$6,800 (Jan 2024) [C cost.rent]. Sale median N$800,000 over 21 homes; one rental listed (portal, 2026-11-15) [16].
- Getting around: shared taxis are how most people move (N$15) [C daily.transport]; northern intercity minibuses leave from the Hakahana service station [3].
- Watch: FCDO advises not entering townships at night without someone who knows the area (read 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]; New Era's crime briefs (2026-03-31) report a fatal assault in Katutura and a rise in housebreakings [22]; a guest column describes burst sewers in Katutura and shacks without water or power in nearby Havana and Babylon (The Namibian, 2026-06-21) [C housing.areas]; the Single Quarters market had blocked drains and broken toilets (New Era, 2025-01-17) [21].
- Places:
  - Single Quarters open market, Katutura Central, the "Kapana" area (one of the region's largest open markets, meat stalls) — New Era [21]
  - Sam Nujoma Stadium (10,300-seat football stadium; CAF-decommissioned in 2021) — Wikipedia [19]
  - Katutura State Hospital (one of the two state hospitals) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Herero Mall (informal shopping area) — Wikipedia [6]
  - Katutura Community Radio (community station) — Wikipedia [6]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" (cheapest formal rents, utilities often included); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (the Old Location history and Human Rights Day, 10 December [6]). Not a first base for a newcomer without local contacts, per the FCDO township advice.

## Living: typical weeks

Prices in these weeks: only figures in the city file, the dossier or the sources below. The aggregator "single person without rent" budget (livingcost, US$525/month ≈ €451, data 2026-06-21) [C cost.living] is used as the everyday-living line (food, utilities, transport) and is not double-counted with groceries.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Klein Windhoek** — walkable restaurant strip on Sam Nujoma Dr, fibre suburbs (Telecom Namibia, MTC, Paratus about N$800/month [C daily.internet]), quiet streets.
- Mon: work from home on fibre; groceries at Klein Windhoek SuperSpar (portal-listed, unconfirmed) [12].
- Tue: day at a co-working desk — CcHUB Namibia reports 100+ co-working members (The Brief, 2026-04-10) [23]; its address was not found. Yango from N$30 each way [3].
- Wed: lunch in town at Old Continental Cafe (meals N$30–80) [3]; walk the centre by day.
- Thu: evening class or film at the Franco-Namibian Cultural Centre, Robert Mugabe Ave [C social.culture]; radio taxi home at night (≥N$80) [3].
- Fri: after-work beer at Joe's Beer House, 160 Nelson Mandela Ave (large beer N$25) [3].
- Sat: morning hike on Farm Windhoek trails [C nature.access] or Avis Dam (N$30, daylight only) [3]; buy any weekend alcohol before 13:00 [3].
- Sun: most shops shut or close at 13:00 [3]; Lutheran service at Christuskirche [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Chez Ntemba, Dr Kenneth Kaunda St (Wed–Sun, Fri–Sun entry N$30, small beer N$20) or Bump, Southern Industrial (house and rave) [3]; radio taxi both ways.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Luigi and the Fish (book) or The Stellenbosch Wine Bar & Bistro, both on Sam Nujoma Dr [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": cook a braai at home; the cinema at Maerua Mall is 2.5 km away [18][12].
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": not realistic as a day trip from Windhoek — Swakopmund is 352 km / about 4 hours by the B2 [C daily.transport]; a weekend there works (minibus about N$120, undated) [26].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": highland hiking at Farm Windhoek and Daan Viljoen (on the C28, trails, giraffe, ostrich) [3][C nature.access]; no forest.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Daan Viljoen's game reserve for a day [3]; Sossusvlei is a full day's drive and needs a night (Sesriem campsite about N$490 pp, Dec 2025) [24].
  - "Je reste en ville": Independence Memorial Museum (free, weekdays), Zoo Park, Post Street Mall [3].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent 1-bed N$12,000–15,000/month (op-ed, 2026-08-03) [C cost.rent] ÷ 4.33 = N$2,771–3,464 ≈ **€139–173**
  - Everyday living US$525/month ≈ €451 ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€104** [C cost.living]
  - Saturday dancing: entry N$30 + 3 small beers N$60 + 2 radio taxis ≥N$160 = N$250 ≈ **€12.50** [3]
  - Total ≈ **€255–290 a week**. Co-working fees: not found.
- Fits also: Q7 "Prend son temps", Q9 "Sec et ensoleillé" (300+ sunny days [C climate.daily]).

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: Chameleon Backpackers & Guesthouse, 5–7 Voight St (dorm N$350, private room N$700, breakfast included; updated Apr 2023) [3] or Klein Windhoek Guest House [3].
- Day 1 (arrival): visa on arrival or e-visa, N$1,600 (≈ €80), no work allowed [C admin.visa]; taxi from Hosea Kutako (45 km) about N$400 per person [3].
- Day 2: SIM at an MTC "Mobile Home" or Telecom "TeleShop", passport required, starter pack under N$20 [3]; weekly bundles N$30–50 [4]. Mobile money is not widespread; bank e-wallets dominate [D]. ATMs: limits N$2,000–5,000, foreign-card fee about N$30 [3].
- Day 3: flat hunting in the Facebook group "Windhoek Rentals", WhatsApp groups and NamSpace [C housing.search]; visit Academia and Pionierspark by Yango.
- Day 4: visits. Example ask: a furnished room in Academia N$5,500/month with utilities + N$3,000 deposit [C cost.rent]. The 1977 ordinance caps deposits at half a month's rent but is "largely non-functional" [C cost.rent]; deposits are hard to recover [C housing.search]. Ask to see the municipal account [C housing.search]. Agency fees: not found.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: walk the centre by day — Christuskirche and the Tintenpalast with its Parliament Gardens, then the Independence Memorial Museum (Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, free) [3]. All within a short walk.
- Midday: lunch on the museum's rooftop restaurant or at Old Continental Cafe (meals N$30–80) [3].
- Afternoon: Post Street Mall craft market (haggle at the outdoor stalls) and Zoo Park on Independence Ave [3][9]; or Yango (from N$30) to Heroes' Acre, 13 km south (N$50) [3].
- Evening: radio taxi (≥N$80) to Joe's Beer House (main N$150) or Luigi and the Fish (book) [3].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: "what daily life looks like" — a weekday morning at the Single Quarters open market in Katutura Central with a local companion (FCDO township advice) [21][C safety.general], then shared taxi back (N$15) at rush hour [C daily.transport].
- Day 3: Daan Viljoen on the C28: hiking, giraffe and ostrich [3].
- Day 4: Okahandja, about an hour north: Mbangura Woodcarvers Craft Market, Gross Barmen hot springs [3][25].
- Day 5: museums — National Museum (Alte Feste, Owela), National Art Gallery; evening class or event at the FNCC [9][C social.culture].
- Day 6–7: out of town — either Swakopmund (352 km, about 4 h by B2; minibus about N$120) [C daily.transport][26] or Sossusvlei (full day's drive; Sesriem campsite about N$490 pp; dune shuttle N$260, Dec 2025) [24]. Alternative for a Sunday spent in town: the Lutheran service at Christuskirche [3].
