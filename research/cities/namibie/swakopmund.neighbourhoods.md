# Swakopmund: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **N$20 ≈ €1** (dossier editorial rounding; the N$ is pegged 1:1 to the rand). USD figures from aggregators are converted at **US$1 ≈ €0.86**, the city file's convention. Weekly figures divide a monthly figure by 4.33. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/namibie/swakopmund.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/namibie.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read".

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **Small, walkable town** of about 75,921 people (2023) [D], growing fast from about 45,000 in 2011; the town CEO calls growth one of its biggest challenges (New Era, 2026-07-14) [C housing.search]. No public transport; the centre is walkable; taxis are hard to find downtown and none run on Saturdays after 19:00 (Wikivoyage, undated) [3]. Shared taxi fares are set nationally at **N$15 a trip since 18 May 2026** [C cost.negotiation].
- **Rent pressure:** building approvals rose 119% y/y in Oct 2025 and small/mid-priced home sales rose 41.5% in 2025 (The Brief) [C cost.rent; housing.search]; oil and gas are adding to demand [D]. The main portal listed no 1-bed rental on its first page (2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. Rentals are found mostly on Facebook and WhatsApp, plus the free NamSpace portal (Sept 2026) [C housing.search].
- **Safety:** the UK FCDO gives no Swakopmund-specific advice; nationally it says avoid townships at night without local company and do not drive outside towns at night (read 2026-11-14) [C safety.general]. No dated city-level crime statistics were found.
- **Climate:** under 20 mm of rain a year and 180+ foggy days on the coast [C climate.daily]; damp and salt affect houses and cars [D].
- **Water:** much of the drinking water comes from the Erongo desalination plant, about 35 km north (record output in 2025, New Era 2026-01-12) [C daily.water]. **Power:** no outage reporting or tariff found [C gaps].
- **Windhoek is 352 km away by the B2** (about 4 hours) [C daily.transport]; the nearest scheduled airport is Walvis Bay, 35 km south, with shuttles to Swakopmund at about N$200 [3].
- **LGBT readers:** Swakopmund has held a Pride parade since June 2016; a Dec 2024 law bans recognition of same-sex marriages [C rights.lgbt].

## Neighbourhoods

### Town Centre (Swakopmund Central)
- Feel: German colonial architecture and German street names, partly renamed after 1990 [4]; museums, cafés and the promenade; the town's tourist core [3][4].
- Rent: 2-bed flat in the centre N$15,000/month (≈ €750) and 3-bed house N$22,000 (≈ €1,100) (MyProperty, read 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; aggregator 1-bed in the centre US$469 (≈ €403; livingcost, data 2026-06-21) [C cost.rent]; Expat.com sea-view flat N$10,000–20,000 (2025-10-31, self-contradictory) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: on foot [3].
- Places:
  - Swakopmund Jetty (pier, sunsets) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Swakopmund Museum, Strand St (museum; adults N$30, children N$10) — Wikivoyage [3], Wikipedia [4]
  - National Marine Aquarium of Namibia, Strand St (aquarium; Tue–Sun 10:00–16:00, N$30, feeding at 15:00) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Woermann Brock Mall, Ankerplatz (mall with supermarket) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Shoprite, Sam Nujoma Ave (supermarket) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Café Treffpunkt, 102 Sam Nujoma Ave (German café, breakfasts) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Open market, Am Zoll (street market; obvious tourists may be quoted 3–4 times the price) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Deutsche Evangelisch-Lutherische Church (Lutheran church, central) — Wikipedia [4]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends" (everything on foot); Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q9 "Doux".

### Vineta and the northern coast (Vogelstrand, Ocean View, Mile 4)
- Feel: Vineta is home to well-off Black and white residents [C housing.areas]; mainly standalone houses with wide streets and sea breezes (MyProperty) [7]. Ocean View and Vogelstrand are named among the upmarket coastal areas [D].
- Rent: furnished 2-bed flat in Vineta N$25,000 (≈ €1,250); 2-bed townhouse in Ocean View N$20,000 (≈ €1,000); furnished 4-bed at Mile 4 N$22,300 (MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. Vineta portal medians: 2-bed N$25,000, 3-bed N$29,500 (small sample, undated, read 2026-11-15) [7].
- Getting around: City Centre about 2.2 km from Vineta (portal, approximate) [7]; car or bicycle; C34 coast road north is dangerous in fog [3].
- Places:
  - Platz am Meer, Vineta (shopping mall) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Strand Hotel area and the breakwater beach where locals swim (cold water, strong northward current elsewhere) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Thick Lip and The Wreck (surf spots, north of town) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Mile 4 salt works (salt pans with flamingos) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Vogelstrand Guest House, 33 Tsavorite St, Vogelstrand (guest house; listing dead-linked) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Pro-Ed Akademie (private school, about 1.2 km) and Vineta Spar (supermarket) — portal amenity list only [7]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q4 "En couple"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (no sourced crime remark for the area, and a well-off residential profile [C housing.areas]).

### Kramersdorf
- Feel: a quiet inland suburb near the Swakop River, sheltered from the sea mist, mostly detached and modern houses, pitched at families settling long-term (MyProperty) [8]; named among the upmarket areas [D].
- Rent: partly furnished 3-bed townhouse N$16,725 (≈ €836; MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]; portal median N$16,700, 3-bed N$17,800 (3 rentals, undated) [8]. Rossmund Golf Resort nearby: 2-bed townhouse N$28,600 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: Swakopmund Central about 1.2 km (portal, approximate) [8]; walk or a N$15 taxi [C cost.negotiation].
- Places:
  - Swakop River mouth (small freshwater lagoon, birdwatching) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Alternative Space Guesthouse, Anton-Lubowski St 167, Kramersdorf (guest house, N$600) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Rossmund Desert Golf Course, outside town (all-grass desert golf course) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Okakambe Trails, about 8 km out (horse riding) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Pro-Ed Akademie (private school, about 0.8 km) and SuperSpar Kramersdorf (supermarket) — portal amenity list only [8]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "Pour fonder une famille"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q9 "Doux" (less fog [8]).

### Tamariskia
- Feel: built in the 1970s for Coloured residents [C housing.areas]; a working-class area [D]; quiet and neighbourly, away from the tourist centre, older freestanding houses and newer townhouses (MyProperty) [9].
- Rent: no rental listed on the portal on 2026-11-15; sale median N$1.75m over 37 homes [9]. Only citywide aggregator figures: cheaper 1-bed US$286 (≈ €246) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: city centre about 2.8 km, B2 about 1 km, airport about 6 km (portal, approximate) [9]; N$15 taxi [C cost.negotiation].
- Watch: Demshi open-access fibre covers all of Tamariskia, Mondesa and Matutura (Paratus, Echo, IT Guru; The Brief, 2024-09-03) [C daily.internet] — a plus for remote work.
- Places:
  - Tamariskia Clinic (public clinic) — Wikipedia [4]; New Era names it as backup for Mondesa patients (2025-02-05) [15]
  - Tamariskia Primary School (state primary) — Wikipedia [4]
  - West Side High School (state secondary) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Tamariskia Shopping Centre (neighbourhood centre) — portal amenity list only [9]
  - Swakopmund Sports Complex (sports ground) — portal amenity list only [9]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q5 "Télétravail" (open-access fibre); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Mondesa
- Feel: built in the 1960s as the Black township [C housing.areas]; the fastest-growing district, in the north-east; the municipality built a wall in 2017 hiding it from the entrance road, called a "Wall of shame" by The Namibian [5]. The DRC informal settlement (40,000+ residents) lies about 1 km further north-east [5][C housing.areas].
- Rent: no rental listed on the portal (2026-11-15) [10]; sale asking prices: 1-bed townhouse N$430,000, 3-bed house N$950,000 (MyProperty, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent]. Room and backyard-flat prices: not found.
- Getting around: city centre about 3 km (portal, approximate) [10]; minibuses to Walvis Bay leave from the taxi rank out of town (N$25, undated) [11].
- Watch: a fire in Feb 2025 damaged the Mondesa clinic's pharmacy and records and halted services temporarily (New Era, 2025-02-06) [15]; in the neighbouring DRC, Rössing funded a N$23.2m police station after its workers were repeatedly victims of crime on the way to bus stops (The Brief, 2025-03-10) [C safety.general].
- Places:
  - Hanganeni Primary School (state primary; #Cricket4All youth cricket hub, ground broken Dec 2024) — Wikipedia [4], New Era [15]
  - Vrede Rede Primary School (state primary) — Wikipedia [4]
  - Mondesa clinic (public clinic) — New Era [15]
  - Planned 10,000-seat football stadium linked to footballer Deon Hotto's foundation (announced June 2024; status not found) — New Era [15]
  - Mondesa Shopping Center (neighbourhood centre) — portal amenity list only [10]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (township history, and the genocide memory in town [4]). Not a first base for a newcomer without local contacts, per the FCDO township advice.

## Living: typical weeks

The aggregator "single person without rent" budget (livingcost, US$405/month ≈ €348, data 2026-06-21) [C cost.living] is the everyday-living line (food, utilities, transport).

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Town Centre** — everything on foot [3]; internet about US$73/month (≈ €63) [C daily.internet]. Alternative with cheaper fibre access: Tamariskia (Demshi) [C daily.internet].
- Mon: work from home; morning fog usually lifts by mid-morning [C climate.daily]; groceries at Woermann Brock Mall [3].
- Tue: lunch at Café Tiffany, Libertina Amathila Ave (fish, reasonable prices) [3].
- Wed: sunset walk on the jetty [3].
- Thu: surf or walk at the breakwater beach [3].
- Fri: drink at Bacchus Taverne, 44 Bismarck St [3].
- Sat: Am Zoll market [3]; buy weekend alcohol before 13:00 (no take-away alcohol from Saturday 13:00 to Monday) [14].
- Sun: German Lutheran service [4]; Hansa Hotel's Sunday lunch [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Wikipedia mentions nightclubs [C social.culture] but no club was sourced; Rafters Action Pub, Woermann St, is a sports bar [3]. No taxis after 19:00 on Saturdays [3]. Realistically: Windhoek.
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": The Tug at the jetty (book), Napolitana, Zur Kupferpfanne [3]; sit-down meals about N$150–300 + about 10% tip [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home, sea view.
- Weekend outings:
  - "À la plage": town beaches and surf spots [3].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Spitzkoppe (granite peaks, via Usakos off the B2; robberies after staged breakdowns reported) [13]; no forest.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": Moon Landscape and Welwitschia drive (half day, deep sand) [3]; dunes south of town [3].
  - "Je reste en ville": Swakopmund Museum (N$30), Aquarium (N$30), Kristall Galerie (N$20) [3].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: US$469/month (≈ €403, 1-bed centre) to N$20,000 (sea-view flat top of Expat range) [C cost.rent] ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€93–231**
  - Everyday living €348 ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€80** [C cost.living]
  - Internet €63 ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€15** (may overlap the living line) [C daily.internet]
  - One dinner out: N$165–330 incl. tip ≈ **€8–17** [3]
  - Total ≈ **€196–342 a week**. Co-working: none found.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Kramersdorf** — family suburb near the river, less fog [8]; jobs are in tourism, trade, Rössing uranium (60–70 km) and the port and energy projects of Walvis Bay (35 km) [C work.jobs][D].
- Mon–Fri commute: one partner to Walvis Bay by car (about 20 minutes) or minibus (N$25, undated) [11]; a Swakopmund–Walvis Bay taxi should cost no more than N$40 (Wikivoyage, undated) [3]. Rössing is 60–70 km east [4]; transport not sourced.
- Tue: groceries at Shoprite, Sam Nujoma Ave [3].
- Wed: birdwatching at the Swakop River mouth [3].
- Thu: horse ride with Okakambe Trails [3].
- Fri: sundowner at Tiger Reef beach bar [3].
- Sat: golf at Rossmund [4] or skydiving with the Swakopmund Skydiving Club [C social.sport].
- Sun: walk on the beach; Sunday lunch at the Hansa Hotel [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no club sourced; Rafters Action Pub [3].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": The Jetty 1905 (book via Don Pedro's) or Kücki's Pub [3].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home.
- Weekend outings: as profile 1, plus Walvis Bay lagoon flamingos and Dune 7 [11].
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent 2-bed N$15,000–20,000 (centre flat to Ocean View townhouse, 2026-11-14) [C cost.rent] ÷ 4.33 = N$3,464–4,619 ≈ **€173–231**
  - Everyday living for two: 2 × €348 (my assumption: double the single line) ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€161**
  - Commute by minibus: 10 × N$25 = N$250 ≈ **€12.50** [11]
  - One dinner for two US$36.60 ≈ **€31.50** [C cost.living]
  - Total ≈ **€378–436 a week**.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Town Centre** — tourism is the core trade [4]; the municipality now decides complete planning applications within a month (New Era, 2026-07-14) [C business.admin]; the town plans technology hubs but none is named [D]. Company registration (BIPA) and NIPDB are in Windhoek [D].
- Mon: client meetings in town on foot.
- Tue: visit the Am Zoll market and Brauhaus Arcade traders [3].
- Wed: day trip to Walvis Bay port businesses (minibus N$25) [11].
- Thu: lunch meeting at Café Treffpunkt [3].
- Fri: networking over drinks at Bacchus Taverne [3].
- Sat: adventure-tourism operators at work (Swakopmund named in Africa's best adventure-destination award, Nov 2025) [C nature.access].
- Sun: rest; supermarkets are usually open every day, but take-away alcohol is off until Monday [14].
- Saturday night, outings: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend:
  - Rent: 1-bed US$469 (≈ €403) to 2-bed centre N$15,000 (≈ €750) per month [C cost.rent] ÷ 4.33 ≈ **€93–173**
  - Everyday living ≈ **€80** [C cost.living]
  - Three lunches: 3 × US$6.17 ≈ **€16** [C cost.living]
  - Total ≈ **€189–269 a week**. Licence and permit fees: not found.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week

- Day 1: visa on arrival or e-visa N$1,600 (≈ €80), no work allowed [C admin.visa].
- Day 2: SIM — MTC has the best coverage; SIM N$10 or less with passport, weekly bundles N$30–50 [14]. Mobile money not widespread [D].
- Day 3: flat search on Facebook, WhatsApp and NamSpace [C housing.search]; no 1-bed was listed on the portal [C cost.rent].
- Day 4: visits in Kramersdorf and Tamariskia (N$15 taxis, or walk) [C cost.negotiation]. Deposit and agency-fee norms in Swakopmund: not found.
- Day 5: bank account rules for a visitor: not found; Namibian banks convert N$ and rand without charge [14].
- Day 6: commute test to Walvis Bay by minibus [11].
- Day 7: Sunday — jetty walk, review the week's visits [3].
- Rough first-week cash:
  - Visa ≈ €80; shuttle N$200 ≈ €10; SIM + bundle ≈ €3.50 [C admin.visa][3][14]
  - 7 nights at N$250–450 = N$1,750–3,150 ≈ €88–158 [3]
  - Everyday living ≈ €80 [C cost.living]
  - First month of a 1-bed (aggregator) US$469 ≈ €403; deposit unknown [C cost.rent]
  - Total ≈ **€664–734** for the first week, before any deposit.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Swakopmund Jetty, then the Swakopmund Museum on Strand St (from 10:00, N$30) and the Woermannhaus on Bismarck St [3]. All on foot.
- Midday: breakfast-lunch at Café Anton in the Schweizerhaus Hotel or Café Treffpunkt [3].
- Afternoon: National Marine Aquarium (N$30; feeding 15:00), Kristall Galerie (N$20, Mon–Sat to 17:00), the Am Zoll market [3].
- Evening: walk south from the aquarium to Tiger Reef for a sundowner, then dinner at The Tug (book ahead) [3].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route.
- Day 2: Walvis Bay (35 km, 20 min; minibus N$25): lagoon flamingos, Dune 7 sandboarding (N$350–480), lunch at Crazy Mama's [11][3].
- Day 3: Sandwich Harbour by 4x4 tour; permit from the Ministry of Environment and Tourism office in Swakopmund [12]. Distance is given as about 80 km south of Walvis Bay by Wikipedia [12] and 45 km by Wikivoyage [11].
- Day 4: Moon Landscape and Welwitschia drive (half day, deep sand, river crossing) and the 1896 Martin Luther steam engine on the B2 [3]; the Swakopmund Genocide Museum on the town's outskirts [4].
- Day 5: "what daily life looks like" — take the early minibus to Walvis Bay with commuters from the out-of-town taxi rank [11], or go to a Sunday service at the German Lutheran church [4]; afternoon in Tamariskia and Mondesa by day (Hanganeni Primary's cricket hub, Tamariskia Clinic area) with a local contact, per FCDO township advice [15][C safety.general].
- Day 6: Cape Cross seal colony, about 120 km north (N$10 entry, old figure; breeding Oct–Dec); the C34 is dangerous in fog [3].
- Day 7: Spitzkoppe via Usakos (bring water; beware staged breakdowns) [13], or a calm day at the beach and Hansa Hotel Sunday lunch [3].
