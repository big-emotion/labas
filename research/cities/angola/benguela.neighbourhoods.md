# Benguela: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rates used throughout (from the city file and dossier): **1,012 AOA (Kz) = €1** (Numbeo Luanda, 2026-11-12) and **USD 1 ≈ €0.86**. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/angola/benguela.md`; `[CL tag]` = an item in `research/cities/angola/luanda.md`; `[D]` = the dossier `research/countries/angola.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

**Read first: this sheet is thin.** The shared web-search budget ran out part-way through this pass. No source describes Benguela's residential districts one by one: the municipality is a single commune split into Zona A to F [C housing.areas], and no rent figure is tied to a named Benguela neighbourhood. Benguela and Lobito (33 km north) work as one metropolitan area with Catumbela and Baía Farta [4][D], so two Lobito areas are included because that is where Expat.com places newcomers [5]. Most venue listings come from Ver Angola's "Onde comer?" pages (2017–2024) and Wikivoyage.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- **April 2026 floods.** On 12 April 2026 the Cavaco river dike broke in at least three places; official data cited by HRW count at least 19 dead, 31 missing, more than 8,000 families affected and 1,540 homes destroyed, with parts of Lobito and Catumbela also hit (HRW, 2026-05-12) [16]. In mid-May about 10,000 families were still in reception centres and displaced people protested at the entrance to Cauango on the EN-100 (Ver Angola, 2026-05-14) [17].
- **Protests.** Benguela province was one of four where police used excessive force during the 28–30 July 2025 taxi strike (HRW World Report 2026) [C safety.general].
- **Roads.** FCDO: avoid night road travel, use convoys outside major centres, expect checkpoints, watch for landmines on verges outside provincial capitals [C safety.general].
- **Health.** The FCDO calls care outside Luanda "very limited" [C health.care]; Hospital Geral de Benguela is the referral hospital [C health.care].
- **Power and water.** Cuts and shortages are called common in Lobito, where homes keep generators and tanks; no Benguela-specific source [C daily.power].
- **Getting around.** The Yango app works in Benguela and Lobito [3][6]; a local transport ticket is about USD 0.49 (≈ €0.42) [C cost.living]. Candongueiros and motorbike taxis (kupapatas) run between the two cities; Expat.com says they may not meet Western safety standards [5].

## Neighbourhoods

### Centro and Praia Morena (the colonial core)
- Feel: the colonial core keeps relatively good-quality houses while most people who arrived during the civil war live in informal areas [C housing.areas]. Squares and landmarks include Largo da Feira, Praça do Embondeiro and the Cine-Teatro Monumental [4]. Praia Morena, the city beach, is a narrow strip between road and ocean, crowded on holidays and usually empty otherwise [3]. Expat.com calls Benguela calmer than the capital [C social.pace].
- Rent: no centre-specific listing. City-wide: 1-bed in the centre USD 247 (≈ €212), "cheap" 1-bed USD 233 (livingcost.org, 2026-06-21) [C cost.rent]; 2-bed USD 500–1,200 (≈ €430–1,030) (Expat.com, 2025-10-28) [C cost.rent]; 1-bed USD 300–800 (AI-assisted page, 2026-08-06) [C cost.rent]. The main portal showed no Benguela rental on 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: walkable centre; Yango [3]; Benguela railway station on Rua 31 de Janeiro, with daily commuter trains to and from Lobito [3]. Coach terminals of Huambo Expresso (R. Domingos do Ó) and Macon (R. Celestino Madeira) are south-east of the centre [3].
- Watch: nothing centre-specific sourced beyond the city-wide frames.
- Places:
  - Praia Morena (city beach) — Wikivoyage [3]; Ver Angola [14]
  - Hotel Praia Morena, R. José Estêvão 25 (123 rooms, pool, gym, spa, two restaurants) — Wikivoyage [3]; Ver Angola [12]
  - Joana's, Avenida Comandante Kassanji (adjacency to the Mercado Municipal not confirmed by the source; Angolan grill, seafood rice; terrace under a large tree) — Ver Angola [7][8]
  - Mercado Municipal (city market, early 20th century) — Ver Angola [7]; Wikipedia PT [4]
  - Espaço Gourmet, R. Domingos do Ó 14 (buffet by weight; Jul 2024) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Restaurante Cassanga, R. Dr. João Omelas (daily menu, terrace; Tue–Wed 08:00–01:00, Thu–Sun 08:00–02:00, closed Monday) — Wikivoyage [3]; Ver Angola [9]
  - Clínica Sagrada Esperança Benguela, Rua Cidade de Malange 12 (private clinic) — UK FCDO medical list [18]
  - Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Pópulo (historic church; location in town not stated) — Wikivoyage [3]; Wikipedia PT [4]
- Fits: Q4 "Seul·e" and "En couple"; Q5 "Télétravail" and "J'entreprends"; Q7 "Reste à taille humaine" or "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen".

### The Cavaco riverside bairros: Massangarala, Calomanga, Cotel, Santa Teresa, Seta Antiga
- Feel: popular neighbourhoods along the Cavaco river; nothing sourced on who lives there beyond the general note that civil-war arrivals live mostly in informal areas [C housing.areas]. The Portuguese Wikipedia names Camanigã and Calohombo for poor construction, waste and polluted drainage [C housing.areas].
- Rent: no figure. The city-wide "cheap" 1-bed of USD 233 (≈ €200) is the only low-end reference [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: no sourced route or time; the railway and a pedestrian bridge over the river were destroyed in April 2026 [16].
- Watch: all five bairros are on HRW's list of areas flooded when the Cavaco dike broke on 12 April 2026; on 3 May more than 300 people were still sleeping outdoors in Calomanga (HRW, 2026-05-12) [16]. HRW asks for an independent inquiry into years of unmaintained infrastructure [16].
- Places:
  - Estádio Nacional de Ombaka (used as a flood shelter in April–May 2026; 35,000 seats; home of Girabola club 1º de Maio) — HRW [16]; [C social.sport]
  - No everyday venue in these bairros could be sourced.
- Fits: none recommended for a newcomer until a repaired dike is documented (editorial reading of [16]). Listed so a reader who finds a cheap offer there knows why.

### Restinga do Lobito
- Feel: a peninsula into the ocean with beachfront homes, hotels and restaurants; Expat.com calls it the most sought-after area of Lobito, with the best security and premium rents (2025-12-16; a perception, no crime data) [5]. Bars and restaurants line the Restinga [6].
- Rent: house with garden USD 1,000–2,000/month (≈ €860–1,720); rent paid 3–12 months in advance (Expat.com, 2025-12-16) [5].
- Getting around: Benguela is 33 km away by the EN-100 or the commuter train [5][6]; Catumbela airport is 13 km [5]. Yango works in Lobito [6].
- Watch: parts of the Lobito municipality were flooded in April 2026; HRW does not name the Restinga [16].
- Places:
  - Praia da Restinga (swimming, surfing, kayaking) — Expat.com [5]
  - Luna (bar-lounge with food, opened 2014) — Wikivoyage [6]; Ver Angola's "Luna Ocean Club", facing the Restinga beach, is probably the same venue [7]
  - Batuk (restaurant next to the beach) — Ver Angola [7]
  - Alfa Bar (pizza, street tables) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Zulu (bar-restaurant at the far end of the Restinga) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Jango Hotel Restinga (deck and beach chairs) — Wikivoyage [6]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (port and Lobito Corridor jobs) [D]; Q4 "En couple"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu"; Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (as a perception).

### Lobito town centre and port side
- Feel: convenient for shops, banks and services, with colonial and newer buildings; the port side suits logistics professionals but is more industrial (Expat.com, 2025-12-16) [5]. Lobito is the terminus of the Benguela Railway and the hub of the Lobito Corridor [D].
- Rent: 1-bed in the centre USD 300–500 (≈ €258–430); 3-bed USD 600–1,000 (≈ €516–860); a 3-bed family house USD 800–1,500 (Expat.com, 2025-12-16) [5].
- Getting around: 33 km to Benguela; Lobito station is a major rail terminal [6].
- Watch: parts of the Lobito municipality were flooded in April 2026 (no street named) [16].
- Places:
  - Hotel Terminus, R. Robert Williams 16 (hotel with sea-view terrace and jango) — Wikivoyage [6]; Ver Angola [7]
  - Lobito railway station (Benguela Railway terminus) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Lobito Lighthouse (restored) and the Fort of São Sebastião (location in town not stated) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Lobito Ethnography Museum and Cine-Flamingo (location in town not stated) — Wikivoyage [6]
  - Banco BIC and BFA (banks named for Lobito, no branch address) — Expat.com [5]
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" in logistics; Q4 "Avec mes enfants" on a budget (3-bed range); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

## Living: typical weeks

Common to all five: Portuguese is required [D]; Benguela has no international school, only Portuguese-medium private schools [C family.schools]; complex medical cases go to Luanda or abroad [C health.care]. Luanda is 11–12 hours by coach (11,000 Kz, ≈ €10.9, June 2024) [C daily.transport] or a flight from Catumbela [3].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Centro / Praia Morena**: walkable, beach at the door, the private clinic in town [3][18]. Caveats: home internet at USD 41.60 a month for 50 Mbps+ is a crowdsourced figure [C daily.internet]; no fibre map or outage data was found; plan for a generator and backup data as in Lobito [C daily.power]. No remote-work visa exists [D].
- Week:
  - Mon: work from home; lunch at Espaço Gourmet [3].
  - Tue: morning at the Mercado Municipal [7]; lunch at Joana's [8].
  - Wed: gym at Hotel Praia Morena (hotel gym with instructors; access for non-guests not stated) [12].
  - Thu: work; evening on the Cassanga terrace [9].
  - Fri: swim at Praia Morena [3].
  - Sat: commuter train to Lobito, afternoon on the Restinga [3][6]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Pópulo [3][4].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": no nightclub was named in any source; the nearest late venue is Luna on the Restinga in Lobito [6]. Return by Yango [6]; the FCDO warns against road travel at night outside towns [C safety.general].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Batuk on the Restinga [7], or Joana's in town [8].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": home; Cassanga's terrace stays open until 02:00 Thursday to Sunday [9].
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": the Caotinha coves just south, some deserted, walkable between them [3]; Baía Azul, a 3 km beach in the Baía Farta bay with few restaurants [3][14].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": not realistic as a weekend; the highland city Lubango is 7–8 hours by coach (9,000 Kz, June 2024) [C daily.transport].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Benguela on sourced information; no desert or park within a day was documented.
  - "Je reste en ville": Museu Nacional de Arqueologia, in a 17th–18th-century former slave warehouse, reopened May 2023 [13].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Legal frame: 30 days per visit, 90 per calendar year, tourism only, for French/EU passports [D]. Yellow-fever vaccination is checked on arrival [CL health.care].
- Day 1: fly Luanda–Catumbela (the Benguela airport is "borderline defunct") [3], or take the Macon or Huambo Expresso coach (11,000 Kz, 11–12 h, June 2024) [3]. Stay at Hotel Praia Morena (a 2015 listing gave 32,000 Kz for a double with breakfast, likely outdated) [3][12].
- Day 2: SIM and money. Unitel and Movicel are the operators [CL daily.money]; SIM price and documents not confirmed (Gaps). Bring US dollars; card acceptance is limited and ATMs may cap withdrawals [5].
- Day 3: bank. BFA and Banco BIC are named in Lobito [5]; no source confirms a visa-exempt visitor can open an account (the 2020 BNA rule covers foreign workers) [CL daily.money].
- Days 4–6: flat hunting. No Benguela rental was online on the main portal on 2026-11-14 [C cost.rent]; no local rental group was reached. In Lobito, rent is typically paid 3–12 months ahead [5]. Luanda listings charge 3,000–5,000 Kz per visit; whether Benguela brokers do the same is not sourced [CL housing.search]. Before signing, check whether the street was in the April 2026 flood zone [16].
- Day 7: Sunday at Praia Morena and dinner on the Cassanga terrace [3][9].
- Rough first-week spend: seven nights at the 2015 hotel rate (7 × 32,000 Kz = 224,000 Kz ≈ €221) is the only priced lodging and is eleven years old [3]; coach from Luanda 11,000 Kz (≈ €10.9) [3]; food at livingcost.org's USD 211/month → ≈ €41.9 [C cost.living]. **Indicative total ≈ €274**, with the lodging figure the weakest part. If a Lobito centre 1-bed is signed at USD 300–500 with 3 months ahead [5]: USD 900–1,500 ≈ €774–1,290 up front.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Move by Yango [3]; avoid road travel at night outside towns [C safety.general].

### One day
- Morning: Museu Nacional de Arqueologia, in a former slave warehouse of the 17th–18th centuries, founded 1976 and reopened May 2023 [13]; hours and fee not sourced. Walk past the colonial buildings and the Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Pópulo [3][4].
- Midday: the Mercado Municipal, then lunch at Joana's [7][8].
- Afternoon: Yango to the Caotinha coves just south of town for a walk between the inlets [3], or stay on Praia Morena [3].
- Evening: Morena Beach restaurant near the sea (national dishes, oysters and prawns) [10], or the Cassanga terrace (open until 01:00–02:00, closed Monday) [9].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (trip out): Baía Azul, a 3 km bay of very blue water in the Baía Farta area, few restaurants; Hotel Duas Faces overlooks it (2016 listing) [3][14].
- Day 3 (trip out): commuter train to Lobito [3][6]; the lighthouse, the Fort of São Sebastião and the Ethnography Museum [6]; late lunch at Batuk on the Restinga and sunset at Luna [6][7].
- Day 4: Caotinha coves hike [3]; evening at Joana's [8].
- Day 5 ("what daily life looks like"): a market morning at the Mercado Municipal [7], then the Kero hypermarket, which says it sells mostly local produce [15], to compare informal and formal prices. Rush-hour candongueiros and kupapatas on the Benguela–Lobito road show how most people commute [5].
- Day 6: birdwatching at Chimalavera Park or the Catumbela estuary mangroves [5].
- Day 7: Sunday Mass at the Catedral de Nossa Senhora de Fátima [4]; a Girabola match at Ombaka if 1º de Maio or Nacional de Benguela play at home (no fixture checked) [C social.sport]. The Festas de Maio (city anniversary) and carnival are the times when the city celebrates [C social.culture].
