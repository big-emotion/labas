- [housing.areas] The Restinga do Lobito, a beachfront peninsula of hotels and restaurants, is the most sought-after area, with the best security and premium rents. The city centre suits shops, banks and services; the port areas suit logistics workers but are industrial. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Port and logistics dominate employment, followed by railway operations, fishing, oil services and construction. The Lobito Atlantic Railway consortium (Trafigura, Mota-Engil, Vecturis), holder of a 30-year concession, recruits engineers, technicians and logistics staff. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Industry carries the largest wage bill: bottling, agro-processing, textiles, ship-related metalwork and fuel storage. A Sonangol refinery is planned. The port and railway terminals form the Lobito Corridor. — Wikipedia (PT) "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The port handles about 2 million tonnes of cargo and 370 ships a year, and its facilities are being expanded. — Wikipedia "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [admin.visa] Foreign workers need a Type D work visa sponsored by an Angolan employer, which must show that no qualified local candidate exists. At least 70% of a company's workforce must be Angolan. The visa lasts up to 12 months and can be renewed to 36 months in total. Processing takes about 2–3 months, with documents translated into Portuguese and notarised. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Power cuts and water shortages are common, and many properties have backup generators and water tanks. Utilities (electricity, water, internet) cost USD 100–200/month (≈ €86–172). — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] 50 Mbps+ unlimited home internet USD 41.50/month (≈ €36); utilities for one person USD 46.20 (≈ €40). — livingcost.org "Lobito", updated 2026-06-21, company (crowdsourced), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Shared minibuses (candongueiros) and motorcycle taxis (kupapatas) are cheap but may not meet Western safety standards; many expats rent or buy a car. The Benguela Railway now runs passenger services. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Lobito has no airport of its own: Catumbela airport is 13 km away and Benguela airport 33 km. The EN-100 links it to Luanda and Benguela. — Wikipedia "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Yango ride-hailing app works in Lobito. — Wikivoyage "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.money] Cards are of limited use, so carry cash. ATMs may limit withdrawals, and US dollars are sometimes accepted. Banks named: BIC and Banco de Fomento Angola. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The guide gives little detail: health infrastructure is "still developing", and international health insurance is recommended (USD 400–800/month for a family, ≈ €344–688). — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] FCDO: outside Luanda, health care is "very limited". — UK FCDO Angola travel advice: Health, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] Public schools are free but short of teachers and overcrowded, and teaching is in Portuguese. Private Portuguese-medium options nearby are Colégio Angolano and Colégio Bankazi in Benguela. International schools are in Luanda, about 500 km away, with waiting lists of over two years. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] University tuition for international students in the province is USD 500–1,000/year for undergraduates (≈ €430–860) and USD 1,000–3,750 for postgraduates. There is a Portuguese entrance exam, and the year starts in February or March. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Benguela province, which includes Lobito, was one of four provinces where police used excessive force during the 28–30 July 2025 taxi strike over fuel prices. At least 29 deaths and over 1,200 arrests were reported in total. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026 (Angola), 2026, intl-media (NGO report), confidence: high

- [social.pace] Umbundu is widely spoken alongside Portuguese, and English is rare outside business. Business hours are roughly 8:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Bars on the Restinga: Alfa Bar (pizzas, tables near the beach), Zulu at the far end, Luna lounge (opened 2014), and the Jango of Hotel Restinga with a deck and beach chairs. — Wikivoyage "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [social.culture] The Saints Peter and Paul festivities are the main religious tradition; heritage sites include the lighthouse, Fort São Sebastião (Egipto), the Cine-Flamingo and the Lobito Ethnography Museum. — Wikipedia (PT) "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Académica Petróleos do Lobito plays in the Girabola (national runner-up in 1999) at the Estádio do Buraco. — Wikipedia (PT) "Lobito", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] The Restinga beach offers surfing, kayaking and fishing trips. Chimalavera Park (a small reserve founded in 1974) and the Catumbela estuary mangroves suit birdwatching. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: low
