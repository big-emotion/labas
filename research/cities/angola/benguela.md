- [cost.living] Total monthly cost USD 631 (≈ €543) for one person with rent, USD 307 (≈ €264) without; USD 1,609 (≈ €1,384) for a family of four. - [cost.living] Two Benguela restaurants (Espaço Gourmet, Restaurante Cassanga) serve buffets paid by weight; no prices given. — Wikivoyage "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14 (listings updated up to July 2024), blog (travel wiki), confidence: low

- [housing.areas] The municipality is a single commune organised into six zones (Zona A to Zona F). Camanigã and Calohombo are named as neighbourhoods with poor construction, waste and polluted drainage. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Most people who arrived during the civil war live in slum areas, while the colonial core has relatively good-quality houses. — Wikipedia "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Jobs are in fishing, agriculture and services, and there are far fewer than in Luanda. — Expat.com "Living in Angola", 2025-10-28, company, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The city is an agro-distribution hub (coffee, maize, tobacco, sugar cane, sisal) with fish processing, soap, tools and ceramics. Manganese deposits south of the city provide jobs. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] The Yango ride-hailing app works in Benguela. — Wikivoyage "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Coaches (Huambo Expresso, Macon) cost 11,000 Kz to Luanda (≈ €10.9, 11–12 h) and 9,000 Kz to Lubango (≈ €8.9, 7–8 h), as of June 2024. — Wikivoyage "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Luanda–Benguela by intercity transport costs USD 15–35 per passenger (≈ €12.9–30); AI-assisted page. — Africarrières "Coût de la vie" Angola, 2026-08-06, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Benguela has its own airport (17 de Setembro) and is also served by Catumbela International Airport. The Benguela Railway links it to Lobito and runs east toward Luau and the DRC. Roads EN-100 go to Luanda and Moçâmedes, EN-105 to Lubango and Huambo. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] 50 Mbps+ unlimited home internet USD 41.60/month (≈ €36). — livingcost.org "Benguela", updated 2026-06-21, company (crowdsourced), confidence: low
- [daily.power] In nearby Lobito, power cuts and water shortages are called common, and many homes have generators and water tanks. No Benguela-specific source was found. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] Hospital Geral de Benguela is the main referral hospital, supplemented by private clinics and health centres. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] FCDO: outside Luanda, health care is "very limited". — UK FCDO Angola travel advice: Health, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] International schooling is limited in Benguela. — Expat.com "Living in Angola", 2025-10-28, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Local private Portuguese-medium schools named in Benguela: Colégio Angolano and Colégio Bankazi. International schools are in Luanda, about 500 km away. — Expat.com "Living in Lobito", 2025-12-16, company, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Higher education: Universidade Katyavala Bwila (headquartered here), ISCED Benguela and Instituto Superior Politécnico Jean Piaget. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] Benguela province was one of four provinces (with Luanda, Huambo and Huíla) where the 28–30 July 2025 taxi strike over fuel prices saw police use excessive force. At least 29 deaths and over 1,200 arrests were reported in total. — Human Rights Watch World Report 2026 (Angola), 2026, intl-media (NGO report), confidence: high
- [safety.general] The FCDO safety page has no Benguela-specific line. Its general advice applies: avoid night road travel, use convoys outside major urban centres, expect frequent checkpoints, and watch for landmines on verges outside provincial capitals. — UK FCDO Angola travel advice: Safety and security, current 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [social.pace] The guide describes Benguela as calmer than the capital. — Expat.com "Living in Angola", 2025-10-28, company, confidence: low
- [social.culture] Annual events: Festas de Maio (city anniversary), a candlelight procession for Our Lady of Fátima, Corpus Christi and carnival. Venues include the Cine-Teatro Monumental; writers Alda Lara and Pepetela are from Benguela. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] Estádio Nacional de Ombaka (35,000 seats, opened 2009) is home to Girabola (top-flight) club 1º de Maio; Clube Nacional de Benguela (founded 1920) also plays in the Girabola. — Wikipedia (PT) "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Mostly Christian: Catholic Diocese of Benguela plus Evangelical Congregational, Evangelical Reformed, Baptist, Universal Church and Assemblies of God congregations. — Wikipedia "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Beaches: Baía Azul (Baía Farta, quiet, few restaurants), the Caotinha coves just south of the city (some deserted, walkable between them), and Praia Morena, the city beach (crowded on holidays). The wiki warns that "beaches are not well-maintained". — Wikivoyage "Benguela", accessed 2026-11-14, blog (travel wiki), confidence: low
- [nature.access] The guide calls Benguela's beaches among the country's finest, with cooler water than in the north. — Expat.com "Living in Angola", 2025-10-28, company, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Classified as desert (BWh) in the English article and hot semi-arid (BSh) in the Portuguese one, with irregular rains followed by prolonged droughts. Neither gives a monthly table. — Wikipedia "Benguela" (EN and PT), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
- [rights.lgbt] Iris Angola, the legally recognised LGBT association (since June 2018), has an office in Benguela. — Wikipedia "LGBTQ rights in Angola", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
