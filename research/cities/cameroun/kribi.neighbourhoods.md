# Kribi: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 ≈ 656 XAF** (the dossier's rate, from France Diplomatie). Most Kribi prices come from OpenShores in US dollars; they are shown at the city file's conversion of **605 XAF per USD** and marked "USD-converted". `[K tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cameroun/kribi.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/cameroun.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- Kribi is thinly covered. No Numbeo page, no listing site, no named clinic, school, bank or supermarket was found [K Gaps]. Almost every living cost comes from one commercial blog (OpenShores, 2026-04-12/13) that cites no data. Treat figures as orders of magnitude. Expat guides present Kribi mainly as a weekend destination with holiday and semi-residential homes [10].
- Rent sources disagree: 20,000–100,000 XAF for Kribi as a whole (infospratiques, 2026-05-05) [K cost.rent] against 100,000–300,000 for the centre and seafront (OpenShores "Vivre à Kribi") [4]. Holiday and year-end rentals can cost about double [K cost.negotiation].
- Distance: about 180 km south of Douala, where the international airport is (fr.Wikipedia) [3]; the dossier says about 150 km [Dos]; OpenShores says 280 km [4]. Travel time is about 3 hours by road from Douala in two sources [K daily.transport][11] and about 4 hours from Yaoundé [K daily.transport].
- Safety frame: France places the South region in yellow (heightened vigilance) and does not name Kribi; it formally discourages recreational boating in the Gulf of Guinea, especially anchoring near the coast, and forbids approaching within 500 m of oil platforms (updated 2026-11-05) [8][K safety.general].
- Sea: more than thirty drownings were recorded on the Wamie, Elabe and Ngoye beaches between December 2017 and July 2018, mostly during holidays; the urban community advised not to go more than three metres into the water (Camer.be, 2018-07-10) [7]. A January 2024 study found intestinal worm eggs and larvae along the beaches [K health.care].
- Services: basic care locally, specialist care usually in Douala [K health.care]; OpenShores says there is no international school [4]; cash is essential because only major hotels take cards (blog) [K daily.money].
- Search note: the shared web-search budget ran out after four searches in this pass; one Kribi search ran, the rest are pages opened directly.

## Neighbourhoods

Kribi is split into Kribi Ier (seat Massaka; quarters include Mokolo, Petit-Paris, New-Town 1, Ngoye administratif, Mboamanga, Lobé and Louma/Grand Batanga I) and Kribi IIe (seat Dombé; quarters include Afan-Mabé, Mpolongwe, Londji I and II, Elabe, Ngoye-Wamie and Ngoye-Réserve) [3]. No source describes daily life quarter by quarter; the blocks below follow the areas OpenShores prices [4].

### Centre-ville, seafront and Ngoye
- Feel: the old town with the German-era cathedral above the port [3]; the nightlife and grill area clusters around Ngoye, the fish-market quarter, and the beaches (blog) [5][K social.nightlife]. Visitors come mostly from Yaoundé and Douala, and local youths often guide them informally [K social.pace]; OpenShores calls bars and maquis at night "modest" [K social.nightlife].
- Rent: centre and seafront 100,000–300,000 XAF (≈ €152–457) (OpenShores, 2026-04-12) [4]; centre 1-bedroom about 108,000 (≈ €165) and 2-room about 240,000 (≈ €366) on the main road and seafront (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: moto-taxi 90–300 XAF a ride, city taxi 180–605, about 6,050 a month (USD-converted) [K daily.transport]. A nightlife blog advises avoiding moto-taxis at night [5].
- Watch: the Wamie, Elabe and Ngoye beaches had more than thirty drownings in seven months (Camer.be, 2018-07-10) [7]; worm eggs and larvae in beach water (study, Jan 2024) [K health.care]. Power cuts described as frequent; many residents keep a generator (blog) [K daily.power].
- Places:
  - Cathédrale Saint-Joseph (Catholic cathedral, German period, seat of the diocese since 2008) — fr.Wikipedia [3]
  - Phare de Kribi (1906 lighthouse, south side of town, open daily 10:00–22:00) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Public Beach Kribi (beach; the guide notes litter) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Débarcadères de Kribi (fishing landings, five in town) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Marché aux poissons de Ngoye (fish market with grill women, "braiseuses") — Cyril Jarnias [5]
  - Pieds dans l'Eau, just south of the lighthouse (beach restaurant, daily 13:00–21:00; chicken and fish $7–9, ≈ 4,235–5,445 XAF USD-converted) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; Cyril Jarnias [5]
  - Chez Amélie (restaurant with tables on the water, about 8,000 XAF, 2018) and La Marina, near the marina (pizza, about 7,000) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Tara plage (beach hotel, about 30 rooms, from 25,000 XAF, Mar 2018) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q5 "Télétravail" (with the power and 4G caveats below).

### Lobé road and Grand Batanga (south coast)
- Feel: villages south of town with larger houses, often with ocean views, at prices similar to town (OpenShores) [K housing.areas]; Grand Batanga has developed beaches [3]; Grand Batanga beach is quieter, with the port and offshore platforms in view [2][K nature.access]. The Batanga mark their return from exile every 14 February and 9 May [3].
- Rent: Grand Batanga / Londji seafront villas 80,000–250,000 XAF (≈ €122–381) [4]; outer neighbourhoods from about 72,000 for a 1-bedroom [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: the Lobé falls are about 7 km south, about 15 minutes by car [K nature.access]; Les Gîtes de Kribi is 1–2 km south of town on the P8 and 10 minutes by car from the falls [2][1].
- Watch: strong undertow noted by travel guides [K nature.access]; no dated official or media source specific to these villages.
- Places:
  - Chutes de la Lobé (waterfall into the sea; daily 08:00–20:30; pirogues to the foot of the falls) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; fr.Wikipedia [3]
  - Les Gîtes de Kribi (seafront bungalows with pool and restaurant; about $53 ≈ 32,065 XAF USD-converted) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Au Plaisir du Goût, on the road to the Lobé falls, 300 m past the Blue Sea casino (grilled fish, 6:00–24:00, listing Sep 2016) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Le Bamboo Beach Bar & Grill, on the banks of the Lobé (DJ sets and concerts) — Cyril Jarnias [5]
  - Grand Batanga beaches at Luma and Bongahélé, with the royal palace, King Madola's tomb and the Musée des arts nègres de la grande Batanga in Luma — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Mémorial de la tragédie de Batanga (memorial to 1880 and 1914–1916 colonial violence) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hôtel le Lagon Resort, between downtown and Lobé (oceanfront, infinity pool, gym) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage"; Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q1 "Investir sans y vivre" (holiday rentals).

### Londji and Mpolongwe (north, Kribi IIe)
- Feel: Londji is a fishing village on a bay north of Kribi near the mouths of the Nyong and Lokoundjé, known for its mangroves [3]; about 15 km out on the N7 [1]. Mpolongwe, about 10 km north, hosts the 216 MW gas power station (2013) [K daily.power].
- Rent: as Grand Batanga, 80,000–250,000 XAF for seafront villas [4]. Bare land between Londji and the port area sells for 4,000–250,000 per m² in a forum post, often unserviced [K cost.rent].
- Getting around: 15 km on the N7 to town [1]; no sourced fare.
- Watch: nothing sourced.
- Places:
  - Londji fish market hall — fr.Wikivoyage [1]; [K nature.access]
  - Plage de Londji (beach) — OpenShores [4]
  - Kribi gas power station, Mpolongwe (Globeleq) — [K daily.power]
- Fits: Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q1 "Investir sans y vivre" (land market, with scam warnings [K housing.search]).

### Port quarter (south, "en développement")
- Feel: OpenShores lists a developing "Quartier du Port" near the port area without describing it [4]. The deep-sea port's container terminals opened from 2018, run by KCT (CMA CGM / China Harbour / AGL) [K work.jobs][Dos]. Its distance from town differs: about 35 km south (fr.Wikivoyage) [1] or about 17 km south on the P8 (en.Wikivoyage) [2].
- Rent: 60,000–150,000 XAF (≈ €91–229) [4].
- Getting around: no transport source; a 130 km rail link to Edéa is planned, not built [K daily.transport].
- Places:
  - Port Autonome de Kribi (deep-water port, two container terminals, mineral and oil terminals) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Rocher du loup (18 m rock visible at low tide between Kribi and Campo) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Only two places sourced; see Gaps.
- Fits: Q5 "Salarié·e" (port and logistics jobs); Q3 "Serré".

## Living: typical weeks

Prices come only from the city file and sources below; "assumption" marks a non-sourced quantity. Kribi-specific mobile data prices were not found; the Douala mobile plan (15,000 XAF a month, combien-coute.net) [see douala.md cost.living] is used and flagged. Groceries for one: about 72,000 XAF a month (OpenShores) [K cost.living] → 16,628 a week.

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: **Centre-ville / seafront**: walkable to the cathedral, lighthouse and Ngoye fish market [3][2][5]. Connection: OpenShores calls 4G from MTN and Orange unstable, says fibre is not deployed everywhere and suggests Starlink [4]; plan for power cuts and a generator [K daily.power].
- Week:
  - Mon: home office; dawn at a town débarcadère for fish [1].
  - Tue: home office; Ngoye fish market and grill lunch [5].
  - Wed: lighthouse walk at sunset (open to 22:00) [2].
  - Thu: home office; dinner at Chez Amélie [1].
  - Fri: Pieds dans l'Eau [2].
  - Sat: Lobé falls by taxi, 15 minutes [K nature.access]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Joseph [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": limited; Ocean Bleu night-club and Le Bamboo Beach Bar & Grill (DJ sets, concerts) are named without prices [5][K social.nightlife]. A real club night means Douala, 3 hours away, and France advises against night driving [K daily.transport][Dos].
  - "Un bon dîner entre amis": Ngoye braiseuses [5]; beach seafood platter 3,000–6,000 XAF (USD-converted) [K social.food]; Chez Amélie [1].
  - "Tranquille chez moi": the default here.
- Weekend outing:
  - "À la plage": Grand Batanga beaches (Luma, Bongahélé) [1], Londji and Ebodjé [4]; stay close to shore (2018 drowning campaign) [7].
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Campo Ma'an national park (gorillas, chimpanzees, forest elephants, mandrills) [K nature.access]; guided forest hike $10–15 (≈ 6,000–9,000 XAF, USD-converted) [K nature.access]; Mbikiliki falls on the Lolodorf road [1]. No mountains nearby.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Kribi (the north is far, and the far north is red for France) [Dos].
  - "Je reste en ville": Kribi is small; Douala is about 3 hours away for museums and shops (see `douala.neighbourhoods.md`).
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent: centre 1-bedroom 108,000 ÷ 4.33 = 24,942 [K cost.rent]
  - Groceries: 16,628 [K cost.living]
  - Water and electricity: about 15,000 ÷ 4.33 = 3,464 [K daily.power]
  - Local transport: about 6,050 a month ÷ 4.33 = 1,397 [K daily.transport]
  - Mobile plan (Douala figure): 15,000 ÷ 4.33 = 3,464
  - 3 seafood platters × 3,000–6,000 = 9,000–18,000; 2 grilled-fish meals × 1,815–3,025 = 3,630–6,050 [K social.food]
  - **≈ 62,500–73,900 XAF a week (≈ €95–113)**; Starlink not priced.

### 2. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Salarié·e" + Q4 "En couple"
- Base: **Port quarter** if one partner works at the port (60,000–150,000 XAF) [4], or **Centre-ville**. Honest frame: jobs are in port logistics, energy, tourism and fishing, with few outside these sectors [Dos][K work.jobs]; no salary figure for Kribi was found.
- Week:
  - Mon–Fri: commute to the port (no transport source; distance 17 or 35 km depending on the guide) [2][1]; the other partner's options are tourism and hospitality [K work.jobs].
  - Wed: Ngoye fish market [5].
  - Fri: dinner at Pieds dans l'Eau [2].
  - Sat: Grand Batanga beaches [1]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the cathedral [3]; lunch at Au Plaisir du Goût on the Lobé road [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" — Ocean Bleu [5]; "Un bon dîner entre amis" — hotel restaurants $15–30 a meal (≈ 9,075–18,150 XAF, USD-converted) [K social.food]; "Tranquille chez moi" — home.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1; a weekend in Douala or Yaoundé by bus (3,000–4,200 / 3,600–5,400 XAF, USD-converted) [K daily.transport].
- Rough weekly spend (two people):
  - Rent: 60,000–150,000 ÷ 4.33 = 13,857–34,642 [4]
  - Groceries: 2 × 16,628 = 33,256 (assumption: doubled)
  - Utilities 3,464; transport 2 × 1,397 = 2,794; two mobile plans (Douala figure) 6,928
  - 1 hotel-restaurant dinner for two: 2 × 9,075–18,150 = 18,150–36,300 [K social.food]
  - 2 beach seafood platters × 3,000–6,000 = 6,000–12,000 [K social.food]
  - **≈ 84,400–129,400 XAF a week (≈ €129–197)**.

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: **Centre-ville**, where visitors and the beach bars are [5]. Forum posters report rising rents and a growth in Airbnb-style short lets [K business.invest]; land sells for 5,000–40,000 XAF/m² [K cost.rent]. Company registration happens at a CFCE in Douala or Yaoundé (one-stop shops listed for Yaoundé, Douala, Bafoussam and Bamenda) [see yaounde.md business.startup].
- Week:
  - Mon: bus to Douala (3 hours, 3,000–4,200 XAF) for CFCE registration [K daily.transport][see douala.md business.startup].
  - Tue: back in Kribi; visit plots with a trusted contact; forum members warn that online land listings for Kribi are often scams or overpriced and one title took about four years [K housing.search].
  - Wed: Ngoye market suppliers [5].
  - Thu: meet the informal guides (a badge scheme for guides began in 2013) [K social.pace].
  - Fri: Le Bamboo Beach Bar & Grill [5].
  - Sat: Lobé falls; pirogue operators at the foot of the falls [2]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Mass at the cathedral [3].
- Saturday night: as profile 1.
- Weekend outing: as profile 1.
- Rough weekly spend (one person): as profile 1 minus the grilled-fish line, plus one Douala round trip: 24,942 + 16,628 + 3,464 + 1,397 + 3,464 + 2 bus legs × 3,000–4,200 = 6,000–8,400 + 3 platters 9,000–18,000 → **≈ 64,900–76,300 XAF a week (≈ €99–116)**.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Tara plage (from 25,000 XAF, 2018) [1]; Les Gîtes de Kribi (about $53, ≈ 32,065 XAF USD-converted) [2]; Maison d'accueil Sœurs Trinitaires de Valence, 250 P8 (guesthouse, no price) [2]; Immeuble NIKE, 250 P8 (furnished apartments, no price) [2].
- Day 1: fly into Douala with an e-visa (no visa on arrival) [Dos]; road to Kribi about 3 hours, 3,000–4,200 XAF (USD-converted) [K daily.transport]; do not travel at night [Dos].
- Day 2: SIM and data (mobile plan priced only for Douala, 15,000 XAF a month [see douala.md cost.living]); mobile money as in Douala [Dos]; withdraw cash, since only major hotels take cards [K daily.money].
- Day 3: flat hunting: visit in person (photos mislead) [K housing.search]; negotiate an annual lease directly with the owner to escape seasonal rises [K cost.negotiation]; displayed rents are often negotiable by 10–20 % [K cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: check power backup and 4G at the address [K daily.power][4].
- Day 5: bank: no bank branch in Kribi was found in any source (see Gaps); the Douala file lists the national banks [see douala.md daily.money].
- Day 6: Ngoye fish market and the débarcadères [5][1].
- Day 7: Mass at the cathedral, then the Lobé falls [3][2].
- Rough first-week spend: lodging 7 × 25,000–32,065 = 175,000–224,455; mobile plan 15,000; bus from Douala 3,000–4,200; 10 moto-taxi rides × 90–300 = 900–3,000; 14 grilled-fish meals × 1,815–3,025 = 25,410–42,350 → **≈ 219,300–289,000 XAF (≈ €334–441)**.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Kribi is usually reached from Douala (3 hours) or Yaoundé (4 hours) by bus [K daily.transport]; a Yaoundé regular bus costs 5,000 XAF and leaves when full (2018) [1]. Bring cash [K daily.money]. Do not swim far out or alone [7]; boat trips at sea fall under France's advice against recreational navigation in the Gulf of Guinea [8].

### One day
- Morning: the Cathédrale Saint-Joseph above the port [3]; walk along the beach to the 1906 lighthouse [2][1]; a town débarcadère as the boats come in [1].
- Midday: Ngoye fish market, grilled fish from the braiseuses [5] (grilled fish with sides 1,815–3,025 XAF, USD-converted) [K social.food].
- Afternoon: taxi to the Lobé falls (about 15 minutes by road [6]; city taxi 180–605 XAF [K daily.transport]); pirogue to the foot of the falls [2]; excursion $5–10 (≈ 3,000–6,000 XAF, USD-converted) [K nature.access]; drink at Le Bamboo Beach Bar & Grill [5].
- Evening: dinner at Pieds dans l'Eau (closes 21:00) [2]; taxi back rather than a moto-taxi [5].

### One week
- Day 1: arrive from Douala or Yaoundé; the lighthouse at sunset [2].
- Day 2: the one-day route.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): dawn at a débarcadère and the Ngoye market morning with fishermen and grill women [1][5].
- Day 4 (trip out): Grand Batanga: the royal palace, King Madola's tomb and the Musée des arts nègres in Luma, the Mémorial de la tragédie de Batanga, lunch on the beach [1].
- Day 5 (trip out): Londji fishing village and its fish hall, 15 km north on the N7 [1].
- Day 6 (trip out): south towards Campo: Ebodjé, a turtle-monitoring and ecotourism site [9][3], or Campo Ma'an national park with a guide [K nature.access]; Bipindi and the Lala Bagyeli camps, about 70 km from Kribi [1], are an alternative. No sourced travel times or entry fees.
- Day 7 (daily-life moment): Sunday Mass at the Cathédrale Saint-Joseph [3]; if in Kribi on 14 February or 9 May, the Batanga return-from-exile commemoration [3]. OFTA Kribi plays football at top level (promoted for 2022) [K social.sport], but no stadium or fixture was found.
