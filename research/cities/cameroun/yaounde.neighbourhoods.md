# Yaoundé: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 ≈ 656 XAF** (the dossier's rate, from France Diplomatie). Items quoted in US dollars by their source are shown at the city file's conversion of **605 XAF per USD** and marked "USD-converted". `[Y tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cameroun/yaounde.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/cameroun.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- Rent sources disagree by a factor of three or more: crowd-sourced figures give a centre 1-bedroom at about 82,800 XAF [Y cost.rent], expat guides give 150,000–300,000 outside Bastos and 350,000–500,000 in Bastos [Y cost.rent]. Rents are reported to have doubled in under three years (Camer.be, 2025-08-14) [Y cost.rent]. Deposits run 3–6 months [Y cost.negotiation].
- City-wide safety frame: the FCDO names Briqueterie and Mokolo market as areas to avoid when isolated, says improvised explosive devices have been used in Yaoundé, and advises against hailing yellow taxis in the street (accessed 2026-11-14) [Y safety.general]. France advises formally against taxis from nightfall and against unaccompanied visits to popular markets and disadvantaged neighbourhoods (updated 2026-11-05) [Y safety.general][10]. Yaoundé saw a partial shutdown during the Nov 2025 lockdown [Y safety.general].
- Floods: Melen, Ekounou, Nkoldongo and Nsam, along the Mfoundi river, were flooded in early June 2026; the ministry acknowledged part of the blame and the 209.6 billion XAF PADDY drainage project was cited as the structural answer (Camer.be, 2026-06-09) [9].
- Search note: the shared web-search budget ran out after four searches in this pass; places come mostly from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage fr/en, Wikipedia fr, OpenShores, Expat.com, Cyril Jarnias). See Gaps.

## Neighbourhoods

### Bastos
- Feel: a "quartier résidentiel chic" in the north with several embassies and restaurants (fr.Wikipedia) [3]; hilltop, embassies, diplomatic residences, highest rents (Expat.com) [5]; calm, villas with gardens, expat-heavy (OpenShores, 2026-04-12) [4]; a blog calls it green and calm (2026-03-05) [6].
- Rent: 1-bedroom 350,000–500,000 XAF (≈ €535–760), 3-bedroom 1,000,000–1,500,000 (≈ €1,525–2,290) (Expat.com) [Y cost.rent]; 2-bedroom around 600,000 (≈ €915), luxury up to 1.6 million (blog) [Y cost.rent]; OpenShores apartment 200,000–500,000 [4]. Residential 3 rooms in Bastos, Golf or Quartier du Lac 150,000–400,000 (infospratiques, 2026-05-05) [Y cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared yellow taxi about 250–300 XAF for short trips [Y cost.negotiation]; private "dépôt" about 1,000–1,500 by day, about 2,000 at night [2][1]; Yango, Bolt and TakeTako named [Y daily.transport]. OpenShores recommends a car because of the hills [4].
- Watch: no dated official or media remark specific to Bastos was found; the blog descriptions of it as "very safe" are not an official source [6].
- Places:

  - École internationale Le Flamboyant (AEFE partner school, ages 2–10) — Expat.com [5]; fees about 950,000–1.5 million XAF a year [Y family.schools]

### Nlongkak and Etoa-Meki
- Feel: next to Bastos, quiet, popular with international-organisation staff (Expat.com) [5]; calm, green, residential, better value than Bastos (OpenShores) [4]. Nlongkak is the seat of Yaoundé I [3].
- Rent: intermediate areas (Mvog-Mbi, Essos, Nlongkak): studio 30,000–50,000 XAF (≈ €46–76), 2 rooms 50,000–90,000 (≈ €76–137) (infospratiques) [Y cost.rent]; OpenShores 120,000–300,000 (≈ €183–457) [4]; 3 rooms in Bastos/Nlongkak 180,000–350,000 (Africarrières) [Y cost.rent].
- Getting around: next to Bastos [5]; shared taxi 250–300 XAF [Y cost.negotiation].
- Watch: fr.Wikipedia lists Elig-Edzoa and Bata-Nlongkak among the most dangerous neighbourhoods (encyclopedia, undated statement) [Y safety.general][3]. Ask which side of Nlongkak a flat is on.
- Places:
  - Nlongkak roundabout (street food; beans 200–500 XAF) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Centre culturel camerounais, Nlongkak — fr.Wikipedia [3]
  - Mahima du haut and Select, Etoa-Meki (supermarkets, listings Feb 2018) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Les Feuilles Vertes, Etoa-Meki (smoked chicken) — en.Wikivoyage [2]

### Centre, Quartier du Lac and Hippodrome
- Feel: busy by day, quiet at night, ministries and shops (OpenShores' "Mvan / Centre administratif") [4]; Camp SIC Hippodrome is an older residential quarter with two ministries [3].
- Rent: OpenShores centre 100,000–250,000 XAF (≈ €152–381) [4]; Quartier du Lac 3 rooms 150,000–400,000 [Y cost.rent].
- Getting around: the cathedral, Marché Mfoundi and the Casino supermarket all sit around Rond-Point de la Poste [2][3]; La Fraîcheur in Mvog-Mbi is 5 minutes away by taxi [2].
- Watch: France advises against unaccompanied visits to popular markets (2026-11-05) [10]; the FCDO avoid-list names Mokolo market, not the centre (accessed 2026-11-14) [Y safety.general].

### Essos, Mvog-Mbi and Mvog Ada

### Biyem-Assi and Mendong (Yaoundé VI)
- Feel: Biyem-Assi is a popular quarter that has grown densely since the 1980s [3]; a blog describes it as lively and young with many students [6]. Mendong grew around the SIC estate and has a gendarmerie and police station, which fr.Wikipedia says makes it relatively secure [3][Y housing.areas].
- Rent: popular areas (Melen, Biyem-Assi, Nkolbisson, Emana): studio 15,000–25,000 XAF (≈ €23–38), 2 rooms 30,000–50,000 (≈ €46–76) [Y cost.rent]; blog 1-bedroom 70,000–150,000 [6].
- Getting around: Biyem-Assi bus station serves Douala and the north-west [2]; land in Biyem-Assi sells for 25,000–50,000 XAF/m² [Y cost.rent].
- Watch: no area-specific dated source; Melen, next on the infospratiques list, flooded in June 2026 [9].
- Places:
  - Biyem-Assi market and Mendong market — fr.Wikipedia [3]
  - Biyem-Assi bus station (intercity) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Supermarché Dovv, Mendong branch — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hotel Jouvance, Biyem-Assi (from 35,000 XAF) and Residence Toundalia (6,116 XAF, Aug 2020) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Mont Eloundem, Mendong (forested hill, about 4 hours' climb) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". E-visa only, yellow-fever certificate required [Dos]. Do not photograph official buildings [Y safety.general]; en.Wikivoyage adds that embassies and military sites are off-limits for photos [2]. Book taxis rather than hailing them [Y safety.general].

### One day
- Morning: taxi up Mont Fébé for the city view and the Benedictine monastery's museum [1][2]; down to the Musée national, Quartier du Lac (foreigners 5,000 XAF) [5].
- Midday: Le Rondo buffet at Hôtel du Mont Fébé (10,000 XAF, 2018) or Le St Tropez, Bastos (2,000–2,500) [1][2].
- Afternoon: Bois Sainte-Anastasie (photo fee 2,500) and Lac municipal on foot [2][1]; Cathédrale Notre-Dame-des-Victoires at Rond-Point de la Poste [3]; Monument de la Réunification, Ngoa-Ekele [2].
- Evening: Panoramique bar at the Hilton for happy hour (16:00–18:00) [2]; dinner at La Maison Blanche, Mvog Ada [2]; booked taxi back.
