## Items

- [cost.rent] Rents in Kribi (and Limbe) run 20,000–100,000 XAF/month (≈ €30–152), higher in tourist areas; no neighbourhood breakdown. — infospratiques.cm rent guide, 2026-05-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Centre 1-bedroom about 108,000 XAF/month (≈ €165) and 2-room about 240,000 (≈ €366), on the main road and seafront; outer neighbourhoods from about 72,000 (≈ €110) for a 1-bedroom. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Kribi", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Land sale prices in Kribi: 5,000–40,000 XAF/m² (≈ €8–61), no neighbourhood breakdown. — infospratiques.cm "Prix du terrain", 2026-05-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] A forum member reports buying 2,700 m² of titled land about 1 km from the sea at about 4,500 XAF/m² (≈ €6.90), found through a family member. — Expat.com forum "Investissements immobiliers au Cameroun", ≈2026-01/02, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Another member describes bare land between Londji and the port area at 4,000 to 250,000 per m² (written as XOF in the post), often unserviced and sold in multi-hectare lots, with seafront plots far dearer. — Expat.com forum "Investissements immobiliers", ≈2026-01/02, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] A blog advises negotiating a long-term or annual lease directly with the owner to escape tourism-driven seasonal rises; holiday and year-end rentals can cost about double. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Displayed rents in Cameroon are often negotiable by 10–20 % (national guide covering Kribi). — infospratiques.cm rent guide, 2026-05-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Visit before renting: photos can be misleading and condition varies a lot. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Forum members say online and Facebook land listings for Kribi are often tourist scams or heavily overpriced; one buyer's title took about four years, partly because officials asked for bribes the buyers refused to pay. — Expat.com forum "Investissements immobiliers", ≈2026-01/02, forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] The villages of Londji and Grand Batanga offer larger houses at prices similar to town, often with ocean views. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Kribi is split into two communes, Kribi Ier (Massaka) and Kribi IIe (Dombé). — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [cost.living] Monthly budgets for one person: economical about $335 (≈ €309), comfortable about $510 (≈ €470), large with a sea-view rent of $400 about $905 (≈ €835) (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Groceries about 72,000 XAF a month (≈ €110) for one person. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Seafood: fresh shrimp $3–5/kg (≈ 1,800–3,000 XAF, €2.80–4.60), fish (bar, capitaine) $2–4/kg (≈ 1,200–2,400 XAF), langoustines $5–8/kg (≈ 3,000–4,800 XAF), crab $1–2 each (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Produce: coconut $0.20 (≈ 120 XAF), pineapple $0.50 (≈ 300), mangoes in season $0.30/kg (≈ 180), cassava $0.25/kg (≈ 150) (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fuel is sold at the same official price nationwide (super 840 XAF/litre, ≈ €1.28, since March 2026). — infospratiques.cm "Prix du carburant", 2026-05-04, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Moto-taxi $0.15–0.50 a ride (≈ 90–300 XAF), city taxi $0.30–1 (≈ 180–605 XAF), about $10 a month (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Kribi–Douala about 3 h by road for $5–7 (≈ 3,000–4,200 XAF, €4.60–6.40); Kribi–Yaoundé about 4 h for $6–9 (≈ 3,600–5,400 XAF) (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A regular bus from Yaoundé costs 5,000 XAF (≈ €7.60) and leaves when full; VIP coaches cost more but run closer to schedule (listing last updated March 2018, old). — Wikivoyage "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] National Route 7 links Kribi to Edéa on the Douala–Yaoundé axis; Douala's international airport is about 180 km away. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A 130 km rail link from the port to the metre-gauge network at Edéa is planned, not built. — Wikipedia "Kribi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] Utilities (water, electricity) about $25 a month (≈ 15,000 XAF, ≈ €23); power cuts are described as frequent and many residents keep a backup generator. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] The 216 MW Kribi gas power station at Mpolongwe, about 10 km north of town, came online in 2013 (now owned by Globeleq); a second phase is planned. — Wikipedia "Kribi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.money] A blog says cash is essential in Kribi because only major hotels take cards. — Cyril Jarnias blog "La vie nocturne au Cameroun", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] GP $5–10 (≈ 3,000–6,000 XAF), specialist $10–20 (USD-converted); basic care is available locally but specialist care usually means going to Douala; the blog recommends insurance with evacuation cover. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] A January 2024 study found intestinal worm eggs and larvae in waters along Kribi's beaches, likely from human waste. — Wikipedia "Kribi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The deep-sea port's first container terminal opened in March 2018 under a 25-year concession to a CMA CGM / China Harbour / Bolloré (now AGL) consortium; a second terminal from 2019 lifts capacity above one million TEU a year. — Wikipedia "Kribi", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Fishing and tourism have been the main sectors since the Edéa–Kribi road was paved in 1990; port extension plans include aluminium, hydrocarbon, ore and LNG terminals. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Forum posters report rising rents and growth of Airbnb-style short-term rentals; one plans a high-end multi-unit rental building with a pool for tourists, another a rental housing project on land already owned. — Expat.com forum "Investissements immobiliers" ≈2026-01/02 and "Comment financer un investissement immobilier locatif" ≈2026-09, forum, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France puts the South region in yellow (heightened vigilance); Kribi itself is not named in any zone. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", updated 2026-11-05, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] France formally discourages recreational boating in the Gulf of Guinea, especially anchoring near the coast, and says to stay at least 500 m from oil platforms. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", 2026-11-05, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Wikivoyage warns of a strong undertow at Kribi's beaches and notes litter on the public beach; Grand Batanga beach is quieter, with the port and offshore platforms in view. — Wikivoyage "Kribi" (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] The Lobé falls, where the river drops straight into the Atlantic, lie about 7 km south of town (about 15 minutes by car); pirogues go to the foot of the falls. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR) / Cyril Jarnias blog "Sites touristiques" 2026-03-05, academic + blog, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Activity prices: half-day pirogue fishing trip $10–20, Lobé falls excursion $5–10, guided forest hike $10–15, scuba dive $30–50; the beach is free (USD-converted ≈ 6,000–12,000 / 3,000–6,000 / 6,000–9,000 / 18,000–30,000 XAF). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Campo Ma'an National Park (2,680 km², South region) holds western lowland gorillas, chimpanzees, forest elephants and mandrills; threats named include logging, poaching and the Kribi deep-sea port. — Wikipedia "Campo Ma'an National Park", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Nearby: Londji fishing village (about 15 km on the N7, with a fish hall), the Ebodjé turtle-monitoring site, the Grand Batanga beaches, and a 1906 German lighthouse within walking distance of the beach. — Wikivoyage "Kribi" (FR) / Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, blog + academic, confidence: medium
- [social.food] Beach "maquis" seafood platter $5–10 (≈ 3,000–6,000 XAF), grilled fish with sides $3–5, hotel restaurants $15–30 a meal (USD-converted). — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [social.food] Kribi is known for seafood restaurants and "crevettes à la kribienne"; local vendors will roast fish bought straight from fishermen. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR) / Wikivoyage (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic + blog, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Nightlife clusters around Ngoye (the fish market and grill area) and the beaches; venues named include Ocean Bleu (night-club), Le Bamboo Beach Bar & Grill, Kribeach and Le Tétraodon Lounge, with afrobeat and live beach music. — Cyril Jarnias blog "La vie nocturne", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [social.nightlife] Bars and maquis at night are described as modest. — OpenShores Kribi, 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [social.religion] The Saint-Joseph cathedral, built under German rule, dominates the port; Kribi became a Catholic diocese in 2008. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Batanga people mark their return from exile each year on 14 February and 9 May; Bagyeli (Bakola) forest communities are part of local culture; the local language is Batanga. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR) / Wikivoyage (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic + blog, confidence: medium
- [social.sport] OFTA Kribi was promoted to the top football division (Elite One) for the 2022 season; a nine-hole private golf course has existed since 2003. — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Beach visitors come mostly from Yaoundé and Douala; local youths often guide visitors informally (a badge scheme for guides began in 2013). — Wikipédia "Kribi" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] Kribi has semi-residential holiday homes and is presented in expat guides mainly as a weekend destination from Douala and Yaoundé. — Cyril Jarnias blog "Quartiers prisés", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Humid all year, about 25.7 °C on average; February is hottest (average high 32 °C), August coolest (28 °C); September wettest (483 mm), December driest (59 mm); about 2,870 mm over roughly 204 rainy days. — Wikipedia "Kribi" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population figures conflict: 70,565 at the 2005 census, 80,957 (2012 infobox) and an estimated 55,401 elsewhere on the same page. — Wikipedia "Kribi" (EN) / Wikipédia (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
