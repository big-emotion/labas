# Douala: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **€1 ≈ 656 XAF** (the dossier's rate, from France Diplomatie: 1,000,000 FCFA ≈ €1,524.49). Items quoted in US dollars by their source are shown at the city file's conversion of **605 XAF per USD** and marked "USD-converted". `[D tag]` = an item in `research/cities/cameroun/douala.md`; `[Dos]` = the dossier `research/countries/cameroun.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33.

Read this first:
- Rents vary wildly between sources for the same area (a Bonapriso 3-bedroom is listed at 120,000 and at 1,800,000 XAF on the same page) [D cost.rent]. Upfront demands are the real barrier: deposit 2–6 months plus one month's agency fee [D housing.search], or 6–12 months' advance [D housing.search], or 8 months plus 2 months' deposit in a Nov 2026 listing [D housing.search].
- City-wide safety frame: France advises formally against taxis after nightfall and recommends day and night guards for rented homes (updated 2026-11-05) [D safety.general][13]; the UK FCDO names Nkololun, New Bell, Akwa, Bonabéri and Village as areas to avoid when isolated, and advises against hailing yellow taxis in the street (accessed 2026-11-14) [D safety.general].
- Floods: Douala has been paralysed by floods almost every year since 2000 [D health.care]; on 10–11 Sep 2026 Bonamoussadi, Sable, Denver and Makèpè-Missokè flooded and a woman died in Bonabo [10][11].
- Search note: the shared web-search budget ran out after four searches in this pass, so most places come from pages opened directly (Wikivoyage, Wikipedia, OpenShores, Expat.com, Cyril Jarnias). See Gaps.

## Neighbourhoods

### Bonapriso
- Feel: one of the wealthiest areas in the city, with commercial galleries, restaurants and shops (en.Wikipedia) [3]; "ambiance relativement calme pour Douala" (OpenShores, 2026-04-12) [5]; a blog calls it an "expat village" of French, Lebanese and European residents and multinational staff (2026-03-05) [7]. The most sought-after area for international residents, with the highest rents [D housing.areas][6].
- Rent: studio about 200,000 XAF (≈ €305); 2-bed 90 m² 500,000 (≈ €762); 3-bed 1,000,000–1,800,000 (≈ €1,524–2,744) (Expat.com listings, Aug–Nov 2026) [D cost.rent]. In a Nov 2026 Facebook feed: 2-bedroom at Bonapriso–Youpwè with 24 h guard 150,000 (≈ €229), furnished 3-bedroom 700,000 (≈ €1,067) [D cost.rent]. Expat.com guide: 1-bed 300,000–600,000 (≈ €457–915) [6]. OpenShores: villas 200,000–500,000 [5].
- Getting around: close to the airport, the centre and large companies (no times given) [7][6]. Shared yellow taxi 200–400 XAF, private "course" 2,000–3,000 [D daily.transport]; Yango and Heetch named as ride-hailing apps [5].
- Watch: buildings often have 24 h guards, generator and water reserve, which a blog says adds to the rent (generator 15–25 %, guard and parking 10–15 %) [7][D cost.rent]. No area-specific incident found.
- Places:
  - Lycée français Dominique Savio (AEFE school, maternelle to terminale) — dossier [Dos]; OpenShores places it "near Bonapriso" [5]
  - American School of Douala (international school) — Cyril Jarnias [7]
  - ABZ Café / Le Grand Café (Lebanese tea room, chawarma 1,500 XAF, no alcohol) and ABZ supermarket-bakery — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Aladin, rue Dominique Savio (Lebanese restaurant; mezze for two about 22,000 XAF) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]; en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - La Sorento, Carrefour de l'Air (pizzeria) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Galerie Gondwana and Galerie Bwo (contemporary art galleries) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hôtel La Falaise, rue Njo-Njo (hotel with gym, pool and Sunday brunch; 83,087 XAF, Aug 2020) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Casino and Santa Lucia supermarkets — OpenShores [5]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (French and American schools on the doorstep); Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q10 "Retrouver d'autres repats".

### Bonanjo
- Feel: the administrative and business district with government offices, company headquarters, banks and taller buildings [3]; colonial-era architecture [6]; "structured" and suited to managers, singles and couples without children (OpenShores) [5].
- Rent: 2 rooms 100,000–200,000 XAF (≈ €152–305), 3 rooms 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534) for the residential areas Bonanjo, Bonapriso, Bonamoussadi (infospratiques, 2026-05-05) [D cost.rent]; OpenShores: 1–2 bedroom 150,000–350,000 (≈ €229–534) [5]; a blog puts Bonanjo at a 60–100 % premium over the city average [7].
- Getting around: near the airport (fr.Wikivoyage) [1]; the airport is about 8 km from the centre [1]. Peak-hour jams on Ndokoti–Bonanjo [D housing.areas].
- Places:

### Akwa (with Bali)
- Feel: the business district, historically Plateau Joss [3]; "bruyant et encombré" with nightlife (OpenShores) [5]; the historic heart of Douala nightlife, young local and international professionals (blog) [7][8]; fr.Wikivoyage calls it a festive, colourful centre [1]. Bali, next to Bonanjo, is a housing area [3] that Expat.com says families choose for space [6].
- Rent: intermediate areas (Akwa, Deido, Bépanda): studio 35,000–60,000 XAF (≈ €53–91), 2 rooms 60,000–100,000 (≈ €91–152) [D cost.rent]; OpenShores: 80,000–200,000 (≈ €122–305) [5].
- Getting around: central; chronic peak-hour jams on boulevard de la Liberté and the Wouri bridge [5]; Bonabéri–Akwa is a named jam [D housing.areas].
- Watch: the FCDO names Akwa among areas to avoid when isolated, with armed robbery and mugging warnings (accessed 2026-11-14) [D safety.general].
- Places:
  - Cathédrale Saint-Pierre-et-Saint-Paul, rue Joss (Catholic cathedral, seat of the archdiocese) — en.Wikivoyage [2]; [D social.religion]
  - Collège Libermann, behind the cathedral (19th-century Jesuit school) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Bolo, l'espace art et culture (free; Mon–Fri 9:00–18:30, Sat 9:30–15:00) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Boulangerie Pâtisserie Zepol (bakery with a small grocery; pizza slice 400–600 XAF) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - ActivSpaces, Immeuble TECNO, boulevard de la Liberté (startup hub; coworking free for founders building a startup) — dossier [Dos]; AfriLabs [14]
  - Saga Africa and Nice Cream, boulevard de la Liberté (regional food; pizza, ice cream, shawarma) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Caf'art (Chez Tapie), rue de la Joie, Bali (café, about 500 XAF; bar street) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]. OpenShores places rue de la Joie in Akwa [5]; the two sources disagree on the quarter.
  - Hôpital Laquintinie (public hospital, old buildings in Akwa; crowded) — fr.Wikipedia [4]; [D health.care]

### Deido and Bessengue
- Feel: Deido is a historical neighbourhood in the north-central city, around the Rond-Point Deido junction [3]; both sit in Douala I [4]. No dated source on daily feel.
- Rent: intermediate prices as for Akwa (studio 35,000–60,000 XAF) [D cost.rent]; a blog says a furnished 1-bedroom in Deido can be under 200,000 (≈ €305) against 350,000–450,000 in Bonapriso [D cost.rent].
- Getting around: Bessengue station is off boulevard de la Réunification [2]; Douala–Yaoundé train about 4h45–5h, Camrail Express 6,000–10,000 XAF [D daily.transport] (en.Wikivoyage says about 3h45 [2]).
- Places:
  - La Nouvelle Liberté, Rond-Point Deido (12 m recycled-metal sculpture) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]; en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - New Deido market (food market) — OpenShores via [D cost.living]
  - CanalOlympia Bessengué (cinema and performance venue) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Gare de Bessengue (railway station) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q3 "Moyen"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit"; Q1 "Tester quelques mois" (station and cheaper furnished flats).

### Bonamoussadi and Denver
- Feel: the main housing area in the north, which recently gained malls, supermarkets, restaurants and night clubs [3]; mid-range [D housing.areas]; "newer, better planned and more affordable than Bonapriso" (OpenShores) [5]. Denver and Bonamoussadi are growing fast, with young Cameroonian professionals and expat families (blog) [7]; Denver offers larger properties chosen by families [D housing.areas][6]. Nightlife here is "more popular" than Bonapriso [D social.nightlife].
- Rent: new studio 180,000 XAF (≈ €274), high-end 3-bed 750,000 (≈ €1,143) (Expat.com listings, 2026) [D cost.rent]; 3-bed house 600,000–1,300,000 (≈ €915–1,982), Denver villa with pool 1,000,000–1,700,000 (Expat.com guide) [6]; a blog cites a Denver 3-bed at about €500 (≈ 328,000 XAF) against about €1,000 in Bonapriso [7]; OpenShores: 100,000–250,000 [5].
- Getting around: no sourced time to the centre. Shared taxi zone-to-zone about 350 XAF by day, 400 at night [D daily.transport].
- Watch: on 11 Sep 2026 streets in Sable and Bonamoussadi went under muddy water and the roads to Makepe became impassable; the Wouri weather alert listed Bonamoussadi as vulnerable and the urban community asked residents to limit non-essential trips (Camer.be, 2026-09-11) [10]. Denver also flooded that night (Beninwebtv, 2026-09-12) [11]. OpenShores' claim of "fewer flooding problems" (April 2026) is contradicted by these reports [5]. Bonamoussadi market: blocked lanes, mud and poor hygiene (Cameroon Tribune, 2022-05-23) [12].
- Places:
  - Marché de Bonamoussadi (fish, poultry, plantain, second-hand clothes) — Cameroon Tribune [12]
  - Santa Lucia Bonamoussadi and Super U Bonamoussadi (supermarkets) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Manège Bonamoussadi, opposite Tchop & Yamo (children's rides, 500 XAF a ride, 10:00–23:00) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - The Bridge International School (Denver–Bonamoussadi) — Cyril Jarnias [7]; fees about 2 million XAF a year [D family.schools]
  - Bonamoussadi Baptist Hospital — [D health.care]
  - Yoro Joss residence, Denver, 300 m from Bonamoussadi market (apartment hotel, 50,000 XAF a night in 2020) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (space, an international school; check flood exposure street by street); Q3 "Moyen"; Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" (popular clubs nearby); Q5 "Télétravail".

### Makepe, Kotto and Logpom
- Feel: Douala V neighbourhoods next to Bonamoussadi [4]; Kotto is a modern residential area home to a growing middle and upper class [3]; a blog groups Logpom and Kotto with Bonamoussadi as calm streets of villas and new buildings [7]. Makepe, Logbessou and Ndogbong are named as the affordable periphery with long commutes [D housing.areas].
- Rent: Makepe 2-bed 80 m² 270,000 XAF (≈ €412) (Expat.com listing, 2026) [D cost.rent]; Logpom studio 200,000 including charges and new high-standard flats 300,000 (≈ €305 / €457), with discounts for annual payment (Facebook feed, 14 Nov 2026) [D cost.rent][D cost.negotiation]; a blog says Kotto and Logpom have some of the lowest 1–2 bedroom rents [D cost.rent].
- Getting around: Kotto has reasonable access to the centre but serious peak-hour jams (blog) [7]. Monthly transport 10,000–50,000 XAF [D daily.transport].
- Watch: Makepe-Missoke floods regularly in the rainy season and its bridge was submerged on 11 Sep 2026 (Camer.be) [10][11].
- Places:
  - Peninsula Plaza Hotel, Voie Makepe Est (budget hotel, 22,117 XAF, Mar 2022) — en.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Résidence au lycée Maképé (apartment rooms 25,000–40,000 XAF a night, 2021) — fr.Wikivoyage [1]
  - Only two places could be sourced for this area; see Gaps.
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi"; Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" for buyers (land 50,000–200,000 XAF/m² in Makepe, Kotto, Logpom [D cost.rent]).

### Considered and set aside
- New Bell (Central market and Marché Nkololoun [3]), Bonabéri and Village: the cheapest rents (studio 15,000–30,000 XAF) [D cost.rent], but named by the FCDO as areas to avoid when isolated [D safety.general]. New Bell is described as having "very tough conditions" [3].
- Bépanda: University of Douala, very dense [3]; floods each rainy season (Camer.be, 2026-09-11) [10].

## Living: typical weeks

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: Loumia Residency, Bonapriso (apartments suited to longer stays, gym; 37,471 XAF, Aug 2020) [2]; Peninsula Plaza Hotel, Makepe (22,117, Mar 2022) [2]; Yoro Joss residence, Denver (50,000 with kitchen, 2020) [1].
- Day 1: e-visa obtained before departure (short stay 100,000 XAF; stays over 30 days need a temporary visa) [D admin.visa]; airport is 8 km from the centre [1]; airport taxi about 5,000 or more [D daily.transport]; departure tax of 10,000 XAF cash at the airport when leaving [2].
- Day 2: SIM and data: mobile plan about 15,000 XAF a month [D cost.living]; MTN MoMo and Orange Money are ubiquitous by observation only [Dos]; mobile-money transfers and withdrawals carry a 0.2 % tax plus 4 XAF [D daily.money]. Install Yango [2].
- Day 3: flat hunting: visit in person, check water, power and toilets [D housing.search]; never pay "frais d'inspection ou de dossier" before seeing the address [D housing.search]. Expect 2–6 months' deposit plus one month's agency fee, often more upfront [D housing.search]; counter 10–15 % under the asking rent before paying [D cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: test the commute from the chosen quarter at rush hour (Ndokoti, Bonabéri–Akwa) [D housing.areas].
- Day 5: bank: Afriland, Ecobank, SG, UBA, BICEC; non-resident account about 200,000 XAF to open (blog) [D daily.money]; France advises avoiding card payments outside international hotels [D daily.money].
- Day 6: Marché Mboppi or Bonamoussadi market with a local contact [2][12][13].
