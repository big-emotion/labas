Extra cities suggested by the dossier under one heading ("additional: anglophone coast and 'Silicon Mountain', currently not recommended"); the two towns sit near each other at the foot of Mount Cameroon, so they share one file and each item says which town it concerns. ## Items

- [cost.rent] Limbe and Buea: land sells for 15,000–55,000 XAF/m² (≈ €23–84), one shared range, no neighbourhood breakdown. — infospratiques.cm "Prix du terrain", 2026-05-05, local-media, confidence: medium
- [cost.rent] Buea aggregator rents: 1-bedroom centre about 40,000 XAF (≈ €61), outside 35,000 (≈ €53); 3-bedroom centre about 90,000 (≈ €137) but outside 120,000 (≈ €183), an inconsistency in the data. — combien-coute.net Buéa, surveyed 2026-11, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Buea aggregator prices: lunch 997 XAF (≈ €1.50), local beer 0.5 L 597 (≈ €0.90), bus ticket 197, taxi pickup 151; the site puts Buea 12 % below the national average. — combien-coute.net Buéa, 2026-11, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] The same page lists petrol at 689 XAF/litre, below the official nationwide price of 840 set in March 2026 — likely stale or informal-market data. — combien-coute.net Buéa 2026-11 / infospratiques.cm "Prix du carburant" 2026-05-04, company + local-media, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Buea average net salary quoted at about 75,000 XAF a month (≈ €114). — combien-coute.net Buéa, 2026-11, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Yaoundé–Buea/Limbe intercity bus: about 5,000 XAF classic (≈ €7.60), 8,000–10,000 VIP. — Cyril Jarnias blog "Les transports en commun", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Douala–Limbe takes 1–2 hours by bus or shared taxi; moto-taxis (bendskins) are common in Limbe. — Cyril Jarnias blog "Sites touristiques", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Buea: taxis are the main local transport; Douala airport is the practical airport, Tiko has little activity. — Wikipedia "Buea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Limbe: National Highway 3 links it to Yaoundé via Tiko, Douala and Edéa; ferries run to Calabar, Bakassi and Douala; a deep-sea port upgrade has stalled, which Wikipedia links to the Anglophone crisis. — Wikipedia "Limbe, Cameroon", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.internet] Authorities imposed several internet shutdowns in the English-speaking regions in 2017, which hurt Buea's tech businesses. — Wikipedia "Silicon Mountain", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] Buea's tech scene ("Silicon Mountain", a name first used publicly in 2013) grew around the University of Buea; ActivSpaces launched in 2009; the annual Silicon Mountain conference, started in 2015, draws 500+ people. — Wikipedia "Silicon Mountain" / "Buea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] Limbe: the CDC (Cameroon Development Corporation) head office is at Bota; SONARA is the refinery company; a cement works opened in 2008; fishing and tourism matter. — Wikipedia "Limbe, Cameroon", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Buea: the University of Buea was Cameroon's first anglophone university; Hope Academy International School (British orientation) costs about 1.5–3 million XAF a year (≈ €2,287–4,573). — Wikipedia "Buea" 2026-11-14 / Cyril Jarnias blog "Écoles internationales" 2026-03-05, academic + blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France classes the South-West region orange, with red zones on the Bakassi peninsula, Korup park and west of Kumba and Mamfe; it advises avoiding gatherings, respecting curfews and keeping a low profile on "villes mortes" days. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", updated 2026-11-05, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] The FCDO reports improvised explosive devices used against civilians in the North-West and South-West regions; its kidnapping warning names the Far-North, North and Adamawa, not the South-West. — UK FCDO Cameroon safety page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [safety.general] The Anglophone conflict has run since 9 Sept 2017; separatists claimed Buea as capital; separatists set up roadblocks and attacked soldiers in Buea (29 Jun 2018) and attacked in Buea again (1 Jul 2018); totals cited are 6,000+ civilians killed (as of Jan 2023) and about 700,000 internally displaced (as of Mar 2021). — Wikipedia "Anglophone Crisis" / "Buea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium ✎ spot-check 2026-11-14: corrected
- [nature.access] Limbe Botanic Garden (founded 1892): entry about 1,000 XAF for foreigners, about 2,000 per camera, guide 1,000–2,000; the Limbe Wildlife Centre rehabilitates gorillas and chimpanzees. — Cyril Jarnias blog "Sites touristiques", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Limbe's black volcanic-sand beaches (Down Beach, Seme, Ngeme, Batoke) have strong waves; with Kribi it is one of the two coastal towns popular with Western visitors. — Cyril Jarnias blog 2026-03-05 / Wikipedia "Limbe, Cameroon" 2026-11-14, blog + academic, confidence: medium
- [nature.access] Buea sits at about 870 m on Mount Cameroon (about 4,040 m, last eruptions 1999, 2000, 2012); the classic summit hike takes about seven hours of sustained effort; the Mount Cameroon Race of Hope is held every year. — Wikipedia "Buea" / Cyril Jarnias blog "Sites touristiques" 2026-03-05, academic + blog, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Buea: subtropical highland climate, average high about 21 °C and low about 16 °C, weeks of drizzle and mountain fog in the rainy season. — Wikipedia "Buea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Limbe FESTAC, an annual arts and culture festival, has run since 2014; English and Cameroonian Pidgin are the everyday languages; Bakweri is the native language. — Wikipedia "Limbe, Cameroon", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.nightlife] Limbe: restaurants generally close between 22:00 and 23:00, while beach grills stay open later; music runs to afrobeat, urban and Caribbean sounds. — Cyril Jarnias blog "La vie nocturne", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [other] Population: Limbe 84,223 (2005 census); Buea's page gives both about 300,000 (2013) and 47,300 (2025), a contradiction. — Wikipedia "Limbe, Cameroon" / "Buea", accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
