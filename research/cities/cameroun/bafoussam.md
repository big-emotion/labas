- [cost.living] Secondary cities (Bafoussam, Bamenda, Garoua) are described as 20–40 % cheaper than Douala and Yaoundé. — infospratiques.cm "Coût de la vie au Cameroun 2026", 2026-05-05, local-media, confidence: medium

- [cost.living] Aggregator prices: lunch 2,499 XAF (≈ €3.80), local beer 0.5 L 702 (≈ €1.10), bread 243, eggs ×12 1,115, rice 1 kg 1,292, home internet 103,333 a month (≈ €158) — values identical to the Douala page. — combien-coute.net Bafoussam, 2026-11, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Fuel is sold at the same official price nationwide: super 840 XAF/litre, diesel 828 (since March 2026). — infospratiques.cm "Prix du carburant", 2026-05-04, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Intercity classic bus Bafoussam–Buea about 5,000 XAF (≈ €7.60). — Cyril Jarnias blog "Les transports en commun au Cameroun", 2026-03-05, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] The city lies on the Trans-African Highway 8 (Lagos–Mombasa) and on National Route 4 from Yaoundé (about 294 km per Wikipédia); Bafoussam-Bamougoum airport (BFX) is northeast of the city. — Wikipédia "Bafoussam" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The West region's trade centre: coffee, potatoes, maize and beans; a coffee processing facility, a brewery (Brasseries du Cameroun) and a BEAC branch; ranked third in Cameroon by financial importance. — Wikipedia "Bafoussam" (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [business.startup] One of four cities with a CFCE one-stop shop for company registration (with Yaoundé, Douala and Bamenda). — infospratiques.cm "Création d'entreprise", 2026-05-04, local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] Main quartiers include Bamendzi, Banengo, Djeleng, Famla, Kamkop, Tamdja, Toket and Tyo; periodic markets Marché A (most popular), B, C, Socada and Casablanca ("Caza"). — Wikipédia "Bafoussam" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [health.care] The Hôpital régional de l'Ouest is in Tyo-Ville; district medical centres in Djeleng, Lafé and Tyo, plus about a dozen integrated health centres. — Wikipédia "Bafoussam" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] Collège Polyvalent de l'Assomption ranked first in the West region (sixth nationally) in the 2017 baccalauréat ranking; Lycée Bilingue is named the best public high school; most students leave for Dschang, Yaoundé, Douala or Buea for university. — Wikipedia "Bafoussam" (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [family.schools] École française le Baobab is listed with fees of €336–1,829 a year (data from 2009, old; current status unverified). — Expat.com "Écoles françaises au Cameroun", 2009-08-05, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] France classes parts of the West region orange: the border areas with the North-West and South-West regions, the area within 20 km of Lake Bamendji, and the D66 axis between Kouomboum and Kouhouat; Bafoussam itself is not named. — France Diplomatie "Sécurité", updated 2026-11-05, official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] The Akwa area is known for nightlife and street vendors selling grilled meat and fish. — Wikipedia "Bafoussam" (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] The Nyang Nyang festival is the main celebration; the chefferie is led by the 97th king, on the throne since 10 Dec 1988; the traditional eight-day week sets market days. — Wikipédia "Bafoussam" (FR) / Wikipedia (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [social.religion] Catholic diocese since 1970 (Saint Joseph de Baleng cathedral, 11 parishes), Église évangélique du Cameroun parishes, and several mosques including a central mosque. — Wikipédia "Bafoussam" (FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Altitude about 1,420–1,520 m; temperatures usually 15–27 °C; dry season mid-November to mid-March; about 1,500–1,800 mm of rain a year, wettest in September–October; average humidity 74 %. — Wikipedia "Bafoussam" (EN/FR), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: medium
- [other] Population figures vary: 347,517 (2008 census, urban), about 1.15 million for the urban community (2023 estimate). — Wikipedia "Bafoussam" (EN), accessed 2026-11-14, academic, confidence: low
