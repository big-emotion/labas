# Porto-Novo, Benin

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier: the official capital, about 30 km east of Cotonou. Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF**; USD figures are kept as stated. Most price items come from one commercial relocation blog (OpenShores, April 2026) that cites no data.

## Items

- [cost.rent] 1-bed $150/month in the centre, $80–100 on the outskirts; 2-bed $200–300 / $130–180; 3-bed $300–450 / $200–300; house with courtyard $400–600 / $250–400 (USD as stated). — OpenShores "Vivre à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] The same publisher's cost page gives a slightly different grid: 1-bed $150 centre / $80 outskirts, 2-bed $220 / $140, 3-bed $350 / $220 (USD as stated). — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A third OpenShores page puts expat-level rent at €500–900/month — several times its own figures above. — OpenShores "Les meilleures villes du Bénin", 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Utilities $20–45 electricity, $5–12 water, $20–35 internet a month; groceries about $100; maquis meal $1.50–3 (USD as stated). Budget tables total $280 (economy) and $490 (comfortable), while the intro says $350–500. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Ouando is described as central and commercial near government offices (studios ~$100–150); Djègan-Daho as quiet and residential with colonial-style houses (~$150–250); Tokpota as a developing area with newer buildings (~$120–200) (USD as stated). — OpenShores "Vivre à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] Sales of 173 economic F4 villas at the Cité CNSS de Ouando (9.56 ha) opened on 8 May 2025, by rent-to-own or cash, advertised as "without initial deposit"; prices not published. — gouv.bj, 2025-05-09, official, confidence: high
- [work.jobs] Jobs outside public administration are scarce and most expatriates look for work in Cotonou. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] The blog claims power cuts are "more frequent and longer" than in Cotonou, without figures. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.power] National load-shedding since early November 2026; the government says it may last several months (about 60% of power imported). — Bénin Web TV (English), 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] Distance and time to Cotonou disagree between pages of the same publisher: about 30 km / 30 min by the express road in one, about 42 km / one hour by car in another; minibus fare about $1. — OpenShores "Vivre à Porto-Novo" 2026-04-13 / "Les meilleures villes" 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Daily commuting to Cotonou is described as exhausting, especially in the rainy season. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] The departmental hospital handles routine care; complex cases are often transferred to Cotonou. — OpenShores "Vivre à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Porto-Novo is not named by France Diplomatie and falls in the general "vigilance renforcée" zone; the ministry flags road accidents, night driving and roadside robbers on secondary roads at night. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [social.nightlife] Few restaurants and bars, no cinema, nightlife "almost non-existent"; few supermarkets and imported goods hard to find. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [social.culture] A government press kit announces an International Vodun Museum in Porto-Novo; nearby Adjarra is one of five towns in the pilot "Route des Couvents Vodun". — Vodun Days 2026 press kit (government), 2026-01, official, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Named attractions: Royal Palace (Honmè museum), Jardin des Plantes et de la Nature, Centre Songhaï, an ethnographic museum and Afro-Brazilian architecture. — OpenShores "Vivre à Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [community.expat] The international community is described as very small, with a risk of isolation. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Porto-Novo", 2026-04-13, blog, confidence: low
- [admin.residency] OpenShores says the Direction de l'Immigration is located in Porto-Novo. — OpenShores "Les meilleures villes du Bénin", 2026-04-30, blog, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] The city faces degraded lagoon banks and recurrent flooding of lagoon-side neighbourhoods, per the final evaluation of the Porto-Novo Ville Verte project. — FFEM evaluation summary (Insuco), 2026-06-15, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Porto-Novo Ville Verte (2015–2023, FFEM share €1.2 M alongside AFD) rehabilitated the lagoon waterfront; the evaluation found better access to the lagoon front, more street lighting and some neighbourhoods opened up, but no lasting rise in residents' incomes. — FFEM evaluation summary (Insuco), 2026-06-15, official, confidence: high
- [nature.access] Brut profiled a market gardener whose work changed with the lagoon-bank programme, which also aims at flood protection. — Brut Afrique, 2025-09-09, intl-media, confidence: medium

## Gaps
- No independent rent, salary or price data; everything numeric except the CNSS housing programme comes from OpenShores, whose pages contradict each other. The OpenShores best-cities page also lists "Bembèrèkè" (a northern town) as a Porto-Novo neighbourhood, which lowers trust.
- AFD project page for Porto-Novo Ville Verte did not load (two URLs tried); the dossier's 9.2 M EUR figure was not re-verified.
- No source found on schools, internet speed, water, women's safety, nightlife venues or the diaspora/returnee community in Porto-Novo. Proximity to the Nigerian border is not covered by any source read.
- The search budget ran out mid-session, so local media (La Nouvelle Tribune, Bénin Web TV) were not searched for Porto-Novo-specific daily-life stories.
