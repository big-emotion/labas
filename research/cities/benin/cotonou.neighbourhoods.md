# Cotonou: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (fixed peg; the dossier rounds it to ≈ 656). USD figures are kept as their source states them and are not converted. `[C tag]` = an item in `research/cities/benin/cotonou.md`; `[D]` = the Benin dossier `research/countries/benin.md`; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33 and yearly ones by 52.

Frames that apply to every neighbourhood:
- France Diplomatie (updated 2026-09-15) puts Cotonou in "vigilance renforcée", its lowest tier; at night avoid the port and the streets along the railway in the centre; the city's beaches are unsafe day and night; thefts are frequent at Dantokpa [C safety.general][D]. UK FCDO (undated, post-Dec 2025): street crime and mugging are common, carjacking risk, never walk on the beach alone, avoid Dantokpa at night [C safety.general].
- Swimming is discouraged along the whole coast all year; currents kill several people each year (France Diplomatie, 2026-09-15) [C nature.access].
- France Diplomatie advises against moto-taxis (frequent accidents) and night driving [C daily.transport]. The routes below use car rides (Gozem or taxi) by default; zémidjan fares are given because they are what most residents use.
- National load-shedding since 3 Nov 2026, "could continue for several months" (government, 11 Nov 2026) [C daily.power]. Plan an inverter or generator wherever you live.
- Ride-hailing status is uncertain: Yango was suspended and Gozem told to regularise in Dec 2024; the 2026 status was not confirmed [C daily.transport]. The travel wiki still quotes Gozem and Yango fares [3].
- The attempted coup of 7 Dec 2025 was put down within a day [D].

## Neighbourhoods

### Haie Vive and Les Cocotiers (12th arrondissement)
- Feel: the expat comfort zone, with the French embassy and the Montaigne French school nearby; Le Petit Journal calls the 12th-arrondissement areas the most upmarket ("plus huppés") and pleasant for families [1][C housing.areas]. Haie Vive is the expat bar and restaurant strip, good for walking [3][C social.nightlife]; OpenShores (2026-04-13) adds secured residences and a cosmopolitan feel [2].
- Rent: central 1-bed 150,000–300,000 XOF/month (≈ €229–457), Le Petit Journal 2024-06-13 [C cost.rent]; modern flats in Haie Vive / Les Cocotiers $300–500 (USD as stated), OpenShores 2026-04-13 [C cost.rent]; furnished 3–4-bed flats in Haie Vive 1.5–2 M XOF (≈ €2,287–3,049), undated listings [D]. Upfront: legal advance of 3 months, often more [C housing.search].
- Getting around: the airport sits in the Haie Vive / Cadjèhoun area, "very close to the centre" [3]. Gozem about 1,700 XOF (≈ €2.59) for a 10-minute car ride; hotel and airport taxis cost 2–5 times the street rate [C daily.transport][C cost.negotiation]. No sourced door-to-door time to Ganhi.
- Watch: nothing area-specific sourced; the city-wide frames above apply (FCDO names bars and beach resorts among possible terrorism targets nationally [D]).
- Places:
  - EFE Montaigne, Les Cocotiers (AEFE French school, nursery to lycée, ~1,137 pupils) — Le Petit Journal [4]; fees [C family.schools]
  - Sèmè One, opposite the Montaigne school (state innovation campus with coworking; Epitech hosted at Sèmè City) — semecity.bj [5]; [C business.startup]
  - EtriLabs Cocotiers, 57 Rue 12.247, and its Haie Vive annexe (coworking/incubator, Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00, no prices published) — etrilabs.com [6]
  - Le Livingstone, Haie Vive main strip (expat bar, 10:00–02:00, drinks 1,500–5,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - MAD Lounge, end of the Haie Vive strip (hookah bar, 09:00–03:00) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Shamiana (Indian, 4,000–8,000 XOF) and Rousski Dom (Russian, 6,000–15,000 XOF), in the lane next to the Trésor Public, Haie Vive — Wikivoyage [3]
  - La Galette à Sucre, Route de l'Aéroport (bakery-brunch, 06:00–23:00, 500–2,500 XOF) and Mandarine, Route de l'Aéroport (Lebanese, no alcohol) — Wikivoyage [3]; on the airport road, exact quarter not confirmed
  - Erevan hypermarket, beyond Code Bar at Tri-Postal, after the airport roundabout (supermarket; imported goods moderately to highly priced, 2020; quarter not confirmed) — Wikivoyage [3]; Talesmag via [C cost.living]; OpenShores [2]

### Fidjrossè (west, beachside)
- Feel: one of the "most beautiful and richest" quarters per Wikipedia, built around its beach, tourism, entertainment and fishing [10]; the beachside area with many local bars and restaurants, busiest at weekends [3]; Nigerians in Cotonou describe beach restaurants and kizomba nights as their circuit (2023) [C social.nightlife].
- Rent: $200–400 (USD as stated) depending on distance to the beach, OpenShores 2026-04-13 [C cost.rent]; furnished 2-room flat at Fidjrossè-plage 350,000 XOF (≈ €534) and a sea-view 4-bed 550,000 (≈ €838), Expat.com Oct–Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]; a furnished 2-bed one minute from the beach 600,000 XOF (≈ €915), with generator, fibre and 24/7 guard, c. Jul 2026 [C cost.rent]; a house at 1.2 M XOF unfurnished / 1.6 M furnished (≈ €1,829 / €2,439), 14 Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. Next door, Togbin-plage: 4-bed villa 1.35 M XOF (≈ €2,058) [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: same arrondissement as the airport [10]; the Route des Pêches runs west from Fidjrossè to Ouidah's Djègbadji beach, about 30 km [C daily.transport][11]. No sourced commute time to Ganhi.
- Watch: the beach frame above applies with full force here (unsafe day and night; never walk it alone) [C safety.general]; Wikivoyage warns about surf and not going after dark [3]. Amnesty documented about 3,000 people evicted at Fiyégnon 1 (Cotonou coast) for a plantation, 2023 report [C housing.search]; the exact location relative to Fidjrossè was not confirmed.
- Places:
  - Fidjrossè beach and Place du Calvaire — Wikipedia [10]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Saint François d'Assise parish (Catholic church) — Wikipedia [10]
  - Le Parking, Place Calvaire (bar in a converted VW van, 500–1,500 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Djunta, end of the paved road (bar, live music Friday nights, beer 500–2,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - La Cabane du Pêcheur, Route des Pêches, about 7 km past the paved end (seafood, Tue–Sun, mains 5,000–7,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Route des Pêches beach bars: Cali Cali, La Plage by Code Bar, Zone5 Playa — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Jackot fishing sector (where much of the local fish lands) — Wikipedia [10]
- Fits: Q8 "À la plage" (with the swimming caveat); Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and "Un bon dîner entre amis" at weekends; Q4 "En couple"; Q3 "Moyen" to "Le prix compte peu".

### Around the Stade de l'Amitié: Zogbo, Vêdoko, Sètovi, Kouhounou (10th arrondissement)
- Feel: Le Petit Journal calls the 10th "le must" for students wanting a social life, around the Stade de l'Amitié Général Mathieu Kérékou and its ring of bars and restaurants [1][C housing.areas]; busy on weekend evenings [3].
- Rent: no area-specific figure found; city-wide central 150,000–300,000 and outlying 100,000–200,000 XOF for a 1-bed (≈ €229–457 / €152–305) [C cost.rent]; unfurnished 2-room 120,000–250,000 XOF in "Cotonou and surroundings" (≈ €183–381), Africarrières 2026-08-07 [C cost.rent].
- Getting around: shared fixed-route taxis about 200 XOF a segment (e.g. Dantokpa–Étoile Rouge) [C daily.transport]; zémidjan 100–500 XOF [C daily.transport].
- Watch: until the World Bank-funded drainage works, Sètovi was a low basin where some streets held water all year; Sètovi, Vêdoko, Zogbo, Midédji and Kouhounou now have collectors and paved streets, and floods are "significantly reduced" (World Bank, 2025-05-15) [12][C housing.areas]. The 29–30 June 2026 floods still hit several Cotonou neighbourhoods [C climate.daily].
- Places:
  - Stade de l'Amitié Général Mathieu Kérékou, Rue 2651 (national stadium) — Le Petit Journal [1]; Wikivoyage [3]
  - Drink City, esplanade of the Stade de l'Amitié (bar, draught beer 500–2,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Fifadji Pork Maquis, Rue 2650, the street number next to the stadium's Rue 2651 (grilled pork, 12:00–18:00, 500–1,200 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - Nearby, arrondissement not confirmed: Africa Sound City, Kindonou, opposite Pharmacie Kindonou (live music venue, entry up to 2,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]; Blolab, Kindonou (digital fabrication lab, described as Cotonou's first coworking) — Wikipedia Cotonou [13]; [D]; Le Yes Papa, Place de l'Étoile Rouge (weekend concerts, entry 1,000 XOF) — Wikivoyage [3]

### Akpakpa (east bank)
- Feel: the traditional residential area on the eastern bank of the lagoon channel [13]; settled from the 1930s by families from Porto-Novo [15]. OpenShores (2026-04-13) calls it a good balance of price and quality of life [2].
- Rent: 1-bed $150–220 (USD as stated), OpenShores 2026-04-13 [C cost.rent]; a flat at 230,000 XOF (≈ €351) and a single-storey villa at Sodjèatinmè 350,000 XOF (≈ €534), public expat group 14 Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. Le Petit Journal lists an agency in Akpakpa Dédokpo (IMMO Bénin) [1].
- Getting around: three bridges cross the channel to the west bank; Wikipedia illustrates jams on the Dantokpa bridge [13]. No sourced commute time.
- Watch: Akpakpa is named among low-lying areas hit by floods [D]; drainage works covered Enagnon and Akpakpa-Dodomè in the 4th arrondissement (World Bank, 2025-05-15) [12]; Wikipedia notes Akpakpa-Dodomè was recently razed and cites coastal-protection works on the east coast (2017 article) [15]. Amnesty documented 368+ households evicted in Xwlacodji for a commercial centre (2023 report) [C housing.search]; its arrondissement was not confirmed here.
- Places:
  - Stade René Pleven d'Akpakpa (stadium, second-largest in the Littoral department) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Aire de jeux sportifs de l'amitié sino-béninoise (former Centre sportif municipal SOWETO), Dédokpo (sports ground, opened 9 Sep 2020) — Wikipedia [15]
  - Librairie Savoir d'Afrique, Carrefour la Roche (bookshop) — fr.Wikivoyage, listing updated Mar 2025 [16]
  - Hôtel Tahiti Bénin, Rue 1531, east bank of the lagoon (hotel with beach and pool, listed at ≈ 27,366 XOF, unit not stated) — Wikivoyage [3]
  - USTB campus, Kpondéhou, and Lycée technique et professionnel de Kpondéhou — Wikipedia [15][13]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" to "Moyen"; Q4 "Pour fonder une famille" (larger homes for the price); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Considered and set aside
- **Ganhi, Jonquet, Dantokpa (commercial centre, 5th arrondissement for Jonquet [17])**: the main commercial districts [3], but Le Petit Journal lists Jonquet and Dantokpa among places to avoid, especially at night, and France Diplomatie flags the port and railway streets at night [1][C safety.general]. Used below for visits, not proposed as a base.
- **Agla (13th arrondissement)**: one of the largest and most populated quarters (44,145 residents in 2013) [18]; a 4-bed house listed at 400,000 XOF (≈ €610) on 14 Nov 2026 [C cost.rent]. Flood damage was recurrent and the quarter is said to be "getting out of the water" thanks to road asphalting, undated [18]; the dossier names Agla among flood-prone areas [D]. Only one place (a tax office) could be sourced, so no block.
- **Abomey-Calavi / Godomey**: cheaper (e.g. 2-bed at Arconville 100,000 XOF) and home to the CHIC hospital, but the Calavi–Cotonou axis is among the most congested in the country and police dismantled 11 "ghettos" in Godomey in Oct 2026; see `research/cities/benin/abomey-calavi.md`.

  - Thu: Sèmè One events or coworking [5]; dinner at Shamiana [3].
  - Fri: home office; drinks at Le Livingstone [3].
  - Sat: Dantokpa in the morning by car, not at night (thefts) [3][C safety.general]; Saturday night below.
  - Sun: Fondation Zinsou is closed (Mon–Sat) [3]; walk Haie Vive; brunch at La Galette à Sucre [3].
- Saturday night:
  - "Danser jusqu'au matin": Code Bar, Tri-Postal (Fri–Sat 18:00 to dawn, free entry, drinks 1,500–3,000 XOF) [3]; Africa Sound City, Kindonou (entry up to 2,000 XOF) [3].

  - "Tranquille chez moi": delivery of pizza, Lebanese and Thai food was available in 2020 (OLD) [C social.food]; or an Institut français screening [9].
- Weekend outing:

  - "En montagne, en forêt": Dassa's granite hills are listed as a weekend trip from Cotonou [3] (no distance or fare sourced); the Lama classified forest (16,250 ha, 7 of Benin's 10 primate species, an ecotourism trail) [19], access details not found. No mountains near Cotonou.
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Cotonou: the savanna parks (W and Pendjari) are in France Diplomatie's advised-against zone (zones of 2026-04-21) and the UK advises against all but essential travel to Alibori and Atacora (Dec 2025) [D].

  - Mobile data backup (Celtiis 24.4 GB / 30 days): 15,000 ÷ 4.33 = 3,464 [C daily.internet]
  - 10 maquis meals × 2,000–5,000 = 20,000–50,000 [C cost.living]
  - 4 rice-with-sauce lunches × 500–1,000 = 2,000–4,000 [C cost.living]
  - 6 Gozem car rides × ≈1,700 (10-minute ride) = 10,200 [C daily.transport]
  - Saturday: 3 drinks × 1,500–5,000 (Haie Vive expat bar) = 4,500–15,000 [C social.nightlife]
  - **Total ≈ 78,247–155,389 XOF a week (≈ €119–237)**, before electricity and groceries. Staples for reference (USD as stated): rice $0.80/kg, chicken $3.50/kg, 12 eggs $1.80 [C cost.living].

  - "À la plage": Air de Jeux Plage Erevan [3]; Casa del Papa beach resort near Ouidah (mini-golf, kayaks; rooms from €99) [23]. No swimming in the sea.
  - "En montagne, en forêt": Possotomè and Lac Ahémé [21].
  - "Dans la savane ou le désert": not realistic from Cotonou [D].
  - "Je reste en ville": Ganvié by boat [3][22]; Fondation Zinsou [3].
- Rough weekly spend (two adults, two children):
  - Rent: 4-room villa with garden 350,000–800,000 XOF ÷ 4.33 = 80,831–184,758 [C cost.rent]
  - School, 2 children at EFE Montaigne 2026–27: French-family rate 2 × 1,824,000–2,904,000 ÷ 52 = 70,154–111,692; Beninese-family rate 2 × 2,382,000–3,825,000 ÷ 52 = 91,615–147,115 [C family.schools]; plus a one-off first registration of 500,000 XOF per child (350,000 for a sibling) [C family.schools]
  - Sunday buffet: 4 × 10,000 = 40,000 (assumption: children pay the adult price) [3]
  - 10 Gozem car rides × ≈1,700 = 17,000 [C daily.transport]
  - Home fibre: 3,441 [C daily.internet]
  - **Total ≈ 211,426–392,314 XOF a week (≈ €322–598)** (low end: French rate, nursery; high end: Beninese rate, lycée), before groceries and electricity. A domestic worker adds $50–80/month (USD as stated) [C cost.living]. Africarrières puts a family of four "living decently" at 300,000–500,000 XOF a month, a far lower frame that excludes French-school fees [C cost.living].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois" — first week of a trial stay
- Where to stay first: a furnished short let (25,000–35,000 XOF a night on Expat.com, Oct–Nov 2026; 30,000–80,000 in the expat group, electricity often billed on top) [C cost.rent][C housing.search], or Hotel du Lac (50,000–90,000 XOF, free airport shuttle) [3]. Le Petit Journal advises starting the search before arrival and staying in a hotel meanwhile [1].
- Day 1: e-visa obtained beforehand (€50 for 30 days single entry, €75 multiple, €100 for 90 days) [C admin.visa]; the airport is close to the centre; avoid the airport taxi mark-up (2–5 × street rate) [3]. ATMs are in the departure area only [3].
- Day 2: SIM and data: Celtiis 24.4 GB for 15,000 XOF / 30 days, or a 4G FlyBox with 10 GB for 17,900 [C daily.internet]; MTN and Moov also sell bundles [2]. Mobile money: MTN MoMo, Moov Money, Celtiis Cash [D]; cash still dominates and ATMs can run dry [C daily.money].
- Day 3: viewings in Haie Vive, Fidjrossè or Akpakpa. Expect a 5,000 XOF visit fee in some listings, a legal 3-month advance, and in some Calavi listings 3 + 3 + 3 months (≈ 9 months) before moving in [C housing.search]; a démarcheur typically takes 10% of the rent (2017 guide, OLD) and their activity is described as illegal [C housing.search][1]. On Expat.com, 15 of 20 listings were marked "négociable" (14 Nov 2026) [C cost.negotiation].
- Day 4: test the commute at rush hour from the flat you like; on the Calavi side, trips under an hour sometimes take "several hours" (Jan 2025) [abomey-calavi.md daily.transport].
- Day 5: ask about the generator or inverter (load-shedding since Nov 2026) and water: SONEB subscription 14,400–16,200 XOF, mostly a deposit [C daily.power][C cost.living]. Tap water is not safe to drink [C daily.water].
- Day 6: meet people: Cotonou Accueil and UFE Bénin are recommended for leads [1]; InterNations meets at the Institut français [D]. Opening a bank account: no source found on what a foreigner needs (banks named: Ecobank, BOA, Société Générale [2]).
- Day 7: Sunday at the Cathedral or Fidjrossè, then the Hotel du Lac buffet [3][10].
- Rough first-week spend: 7 nights × 25,000–35,000 = 175,000–245,000 + data 15,000 + 14 meals × 2,000–5,000 = 28,000–70,000 + 12 Gozem rides × ≈1,700 = 20,400 + 3 visit fees × 5,000 = 15,000 → **≈ 253,400–365,400 XOF (≈ €386–557)**, excluding the visa and any lease advance [C cost.rent; daily.internet; cost.living; daily.transport; housing.search].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Read the frames at the top (beach, night, moto-taxis) first. Visa: e-visa from €50 [C admin.visa].

### One day
- Morning: Dantokpa market, Boulevard Saint-Michel (bargaining expected; keep valuables close, thefts are frequent) [3][C safety.general]; then Fondation Zinsou, Quartier Zongo (free contemporary art, Mon–Sat) and the Centre Artisanal one block south-west (07:30–20:00) [3]. Short taxi hops; a shared taxi is about 200 XOF a segment [C daily.transport].
- Midday: Chez Maman Bénin, Rue 201A (local dishes, 1,000–3,000 XOF, from 11:00) [3].
- Afternoon: the Cathedral Notre-Dame near the Ancien Pont, then Place de l'Amazone and Étoile Rouge by Gozem [3]; the Grande Mosquée de Zongo from outside [3].
- Evening: Haie Vive: drinks at Le Livingstone and dinner at Shamiana or Rousski Dom [3]. Car, not zem, after dark [C daily.transport].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2 (Ouidah): 45–60 minutes by car [3]; Python Temple (1,000 XOF, photos 2,000) and the Route des Esclaves to the Porte du Non-Retour [24]; see `ouidah.neighbourhoods.md`.
- Day 3 (daily-life moment): leave at dawn for the Calavi landing stage: the Calavi–Cotonou axis at rush hour is the commute many residents live with [C daily.transport][abomey-calavi.md]; then the Ganvié stilt village on Lake Nokoué (on UNESCO's tentative list since 1996) [22]; avoid touching the water [3].
- Day 4 (Porto-Novo): about 60 minutes by car [3]; Royal Palace (Honmè museum), Jardin des Plantes et de la Nature, Centre Songhaï [porto-novo.md social.culture]; Rivière Noire boat outing at Adjarra [3].
- Day 5 (Abomey, long day or overnight): Royal Palaces (UNESCO) and the Musée Historique d'Abomey (2,500 XOF with a ~1 h guided tour; Mon–Fri 8:30–18:00, weekends to 17:00); bush taxi about 3 hours, ~2,000 XOF to Bohicon, then 300 XOF to Abomey (fares dated 2018) [25].
- Day 6 (Saturday, Fidjrossè): lunch at La Cabane du Pêcheur, Route des Pêches beach bars, Djunta or Le Parking at night [3]; no swimming [C nature.access].
- Day 7 (Sunday): Mass at the Cathedral or Saint François d'Assise [3][10]; Hotel du Lac buffet [3]; an evening at the Stade de l'Amitié esplanade (busy on weekend evenings) [3].
