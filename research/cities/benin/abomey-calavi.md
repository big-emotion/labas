# Abomey-Calavi, Benin

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier: Cotonou's northern suburb and university town, where many Cotonou workers live. Currency note: EUR equivalents use the fixed peg **1 EUR = 655.957 XOF** (the dossier rounds it to ≈ 656).

## Items

- [cost.rent] Agency listing: furnished, air-conditioned 3-bedroom flat in Atrokpocodji at 650,000 XOF/month (≈ €991), "negotiable", with generator, Wi-Fi and security agent; 10% agency commission; 30,000 XOF (≈ €46) a night for short stays. — Expat.com housing listing (agency), posted c. 2026-10, company, confidence: medium (asking price)
- [cost.rent] Student rentals near the UAC campus: shared room about 23,000–35,000 XOF/month (≈ €35–53); self-contained room or studio 35,000–55,000 XOF (≈ €53–84); landlords want several months in advance plus a deposit, and prepaid SBEE electricity is often extra. — Uni2Study, 2026-08-07, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Campus residences run by COUS-AC are subsidised: shared cabins from about 2,500 XOF/month and studios from about 5,000 XOF, plus about two months' deposit (indicative, changes yearly). — Uni2Study, 2026-08-07, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Student rentals cluster in Dossounou, Tankpè and Aidegnon near the campus; many students who study in Cotonou live in Godomey because Cotonou rents are higher. — Uni2Study, 2026-08-07, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.search] COUS-AC opened its 2025 campus-housing applications on 22 Sep–3 Oct 2025, in person only; demand exceeds supply every year. — Bénin Web TV (English), 2025-09-20, local-media, confidence: high
- [business.invest] Land asking prices per m² claimed by a savings-app blog: Godomey centre 35,000–50,000 XOF (≈ €53–76); Calavi centre 30,000–45,000 (≈ €46–69); Akassato 15,000–25,000 (≈ €23–38); Ouèdo 10,000–18,000 (≈ €15–27); Glo-Djigbé 8,000–15,000 (≈ €12–23). Unsourced. — I am Beezy blog, 2026-01-28, company, confidence: low
- [business.invest] The same blog ranks land documents: a titre foncier is safest and dearest, an attestation de recasement 10–20% cheaper, a bare sale agreement 30–50% cheaper but risky; it warns of plots sold to several buyers and advises checking with the ANDF and paying by traceable transfer. — I am Beezy blog, 2026-01-28, company, confidence: low
- [business.startup] Fondation UAC's Startup Valley (Abomey-Calavi) was one of 15 support organisations sharing $1.49 M in World Bank-backed grants in February 2026. — gouv.bj, 2026-02-11, official, confidence: high
- [business.startup] A wholesale market at Zopah (Calavi) was under construction in January 2025 to relieve Cotonou's Dantokpa market. — La Nouvelle Tribune, 2025-01-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] National load-shedding since early November 2026; the government says it may last several months (about 60% of power is imported). — Bénin Web TV (English), 2026-11-11, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] On 20 Jan 2025, drivers took over an hour between Calavi-Kpota and the IITA junction at dawn; a commuter from Akassato said trips that should take under an hour sometimes take "several hours". — La Nouvelle Tribune, 2025-01-23, local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] A forum member in 2018 described Calavi as calm and about 25 minutes from central Cotonou (anecdote, likely off-peak). — Expat.com forum "Vie au Bénin", c. 2018, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Students living in Calavi or Godomey commute to Cotonou by zémidjan or shared taxi. — Uni2Study, 2026-08-07, blog, confidence: low
- [health.care] The Centre Hospitalier International de Calavi (CHIC), a public hospital with 434 beds inaugurated in June 2025, is open to all; it offers dialysis, oncology with chemotherapy, cardiology, nephrology, CT and MRI. — gouv.bj, 2025-11-02, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] The CHIC opened radiotherapy on 1 April 2026 (first patient 15 April) and added laparoscopic surgery and 24/7 services. — gouv.bj, 2026-04-25, official, confidence: high
- [family.schools] The Université d'Abomey-Calavi is said to have over 100,000 students and the commune over 1.5 million inhabitants (promotional blog, unsourced). — I am Beezy blog, 2026-01-28, company, confidence: low
- [safety.general] Police arrested 182 people and dismantled 11 "ghettos" in Godomey on 20–31 Oct 2026, targeting drugs, illegal gambling, taxi robberies with false plates and motorbike theft. — Bénin Web TV, 2026-11-02, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Mob justice: on the night of 16–17 Sep 2025 villagers in Bozoun (Kpanroun arrondissement) burned alive a suspected moto-taxi robber before police arrived. — Bénin Web TV, 2025-09-18, local-media, confidence: high
- [safety.general] Abomey-Calavi is not named by France Diplomatie and falls in the general "vigilance renforcée" zone; road accidents and night driving are flagged nationally. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-09-15, official, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] 141 mm of rain fell near the university on the night of 29–30 June 2026; Cotonou and Calavi neighbourhoods flooded and commuters were stranded. — AllAfrica (fr), 2026-07-01, local-media, confidence: high
- [climate.daily] Water covered the Djonou bridge at Godomey on the Calavi–Cotonou road in early July 2026; civil protection pumping continued where water still disrupted daily life. — La Nouvelle Tribune, 2026-07-18, local-media, confidence: high
