# Antananarivo: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA (Ar)**, the dossier rate (`research/countries/madagascar.md`). One source used below (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) converts at 4,800 Ar; its ariary figures are kept and re-converted at 4,500. USD figures (OpenShores) are kept as stated and not converted, as in the city file. `[A tag]` = an item in `research/cities/madagascar/antananarivo.md`; `[D]` = the dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33. Targeted fetches were made on 2026-11-15; the shared web-search budget ran out after five searches, so the rest of the pass used direct fetches of known pages (travel wikis, Wikipedia, Madagascar National Parks, relocation blogs).

Frames that apply to the whole city:
- Safety (official): FCDO advises not walking alone after dark in city centres or poorly lit areas, keeping car doors locked and windows closed in Tana, and holding business meetings in secure places because of occasional kidnappings for ransom, mostly of Malagasy people seen as wealthy [A safety.general][D]. It advises against multi-passenger taxi vans (robberies, road deaths) and recommends pre-booked taxis [A safety.general]. France Diplomatie (updated 2026-11-13): calm for several months, extra vigilance around 25 September, the anniversary of the 2025 protests [A safety.general].
- Utilities: power and water cuts of up to 12 h set off the 2025 uprising [D]; a generator and water filter are described as close to essential (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [A daily.power]. Tap water is unsafe (FCDO) [A daily.water].
- Housing stock: about 40% of the capital's housing is old and dilapidated; about fifty houses collapsed or were damaged in the 2025–26 rainy season, mostly in hillside "red zones" of the 2nd, 4th and 6th arrondissements (L'Express, Nov 2026) [A housing.areas]. Which quarters below fall in those arrondissements was not found.
- Traffic: daily commutes of two to three hours; over 500 km of roads in flood-prone zones (World Bank via Midi Madagasikara, 2026-01-23) [A daily.transport]; OpenShores gives 5 km in an hour in heavy traffic [6]. No neighbourhood-to-centre travel time was found in any source; distances below are given only where sourced.
- Moving around: taxi-be 600–800 Ar a leg (≈ €0.13–0.18) [A daily.transport]; city taxis have no meter, about 5,000 Ar (≈ €1.10) for a short ride and 20,000 Ar (≈ €4.40) for a longer one, agree the fare first (Wikivoyage) [1]; taxis often double at night while app fares do not (L'Express, Apr 2025) [A cost.negotiation]; app cars about 5,000 Ar/km with a 25,000 Ar minimum on some trips, app moto-taxis about 3,000 Ar/km, 10,000 minimum [A daily.transport]. The Misy app (cars, motorbikes, minivans; fares shown in advance; cash or mobile money) is named by Wikivoyage, which says Uber and Bolt do not operate [1]; OpenShores names "Taxis Gasy" [6].
- Ivato airport is about 15 km from the centre (Wikipedia) [3]; private transfer 60,000–80,000 Ar (≈ €13–18, June 2023), 30 minutes to over an hour; taxi 40,000–60,000 Ar one way, 60,000 at night (Wikivoyage) [1].

## Neighbourhoods

### Ivandry (with Alarobia and the Masay lake shore)
- Feel: the quarter expats name first: spacious villas, international schools, guarded residences, embassies (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06 and 2026-11-06) [4][5]. Expat.com (2015, old) calls it "hautement résidentiel" [7].
- Rent: furnished T2 60 m² €600/month (≈ 2,700,000 Ar); furnished duplex T3 €1,200, both "négociable"; T4 penthouse €1,400; villas €1,700–4,000 (Expat.com listings, 2026-11-14) [A cost.rent]. Cyril Jarnias (Nov 2026): modern F3 €800–1,500, prestige villa from €1,800 [4]. Forum user (2026): agencies quoted 12–20 million Ar (≈ €2,670–4,440) for expat-type housing (forum, low confidence) [A cost.rent].
- Getting around: next to the Ankorondrano business zone (listings advertise flats "à 2 pas des écoles et d'Ankorondrano", Expat.com, 2026) [A cost.rent]. No minutes to downtown sourced. L A City Mall in Alarobia is about 3 km north of downtown (Wikivoyage, listing updated Apr 2018) [1].
- Watch: leaving the quarter is slow at peak hours (Expat.com 2015) [7]; the Ivandry crossroads is "saturé entre 17 h et 19 h" (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) [4]. Standing costs: Jirama water and power 150,000–300,000 Ar/month (≈ €33–67), generator diesel 200,000–500,000 Ar/month (≈ €44–111) [4].
- Places:
  - Leader Price (supermarket), 24 h pharmacies and international banks "à cinq minutes" — Cyril Jarnias, 2026-11-06 [4]
  - La City / L A City Mall (two-floor shopping centre with shops and restaurants; Wikivoyage lists 52 stores and a Super U, 09:30–19:30, updated Apr 2018) — madacamp, modified 2025-05-01 [9]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Dzama Cocktail Café (restaurant with gastronomic, tapas and fast-food areas) — fr.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Terrace Exotic (Indian restaurant, Lot II J17A Ivandry) — madacamp, seen as a search-result summary only [10]
  - Parc de Tsarasaotra (bird-watching park; OpenShores names it without an address; its Alarobia location is not confirmed by a fetched page) — OpenShores, 2026-04-13 [6]
  - American School of Antananarivo (IB, Alarobia; 2019–20 fees 12,500–21,320 USD, outdated) — Expat.com via dossier [D]
  - Lycée Français de Tananarive school D is to move to Ivandry — Wikipedia [11][D]
- Fits: Q4 "Avec mes enfants" and "En couple" (schools, guarded residences); Q3 "Le prix compte peu" (the city's highest rents); Q2 "Un bon dîner entre amis" (restaurants in the mall and quarter); Q10 "Me sentir en sécurité" (guards, generators advertised in listings).

### Ambatobe and Analamahitsy (north-east, around the French lycée)
- Feel: hilltop, more residential than Ivandry and somewhat isolated; families choose it for the walk to the French lycée (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06 and 2026-11-06) [4][5]; Expat.com (2015) notes strong demand and distance from the centre [7].
- Rent: furnished T3 3,000,000 Ar (≈ €667); semi-furnished T5 €1,750; 5-bedroom villa 8,500,000 Ar (≈ €1,890) (Expat.com listings, 2026-11-14) [A cost.rent]. Cyril Jarnias (Nov 2026): 4-bedroom house with garden €600–1,200; flat in a secure residence €500–900 [4]. An Analamahitsy T3 "de standing" is advertised as a few minutes from the Ivandry school and the Ankorondrano business quarter (Expat.com listing, price not read) [20].
- Getting around: school D of the Lycée Français sits along National Route 3 (Wikipedia) [11]; no travel time to the centre sourced.
- Watch: utility networks "have not always followed" the area's expansion (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) [4].
- Places:
  - Lycée Français de Tananarive, secondary campus (AEFE; about 28 million Ar per child per year, Cyril Jarnias Nov 2026) — Cyril Jarnias [4][5]; Wikipedia [11]
  - École D "Les Charmilles", Analamahitsy, along RN3 (French primary) — Wikipedia [11]

### Isoraka and the Haute-Ville (Andohalo, around the Rova)
- Feel: Isoraka is in the "ville moyenne", with nightlife and old houses turned into restaurants and inns; the Haute-Ville around the Rova is mainly residential and considered prestigious (Wikipedia) [3]. Andohalo and Isoraka are chosen for colonial charm; Isoraka is near restaurants and shops, with galleries and music events (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06) [5]. OpenShores notes Isoraka's "restaurants gastronomiques" [6].
- Rent: no Isoraka-specific asking rent found. City-centre benchmarks: 1-bed ≈ 1,700,000 Ar (≈ €378) (Numbeo via Expat.com, Sept 2025) [A cost.rent]; 1-bed centre about $200 (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [A cost.rent]; studio in the centre about 1,000,000 Ar (Expat.com, 2015, old) [7].
- Getting around: central; taxis 5,000 Ar for a short ride [1]. Analakely is walkable (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Watch: FCDO's after-dark walking advice applies to city centres [A safety.general]. Nothing quarter-specific found.
- Places:

  - Sakamanga (hotel-restaurant, downtown; known for its ravitoto, serves late) — Wikivoyage [1]; fr.Wikivoyage [2]; Cyril Jarnias [8]
  - Tomb of Prime Minister Rainiharo (monument, Isoraka) — Wikipedia [3]

  - Andohalo Catholic Cathedral (1890, Lalana Justin Rajoro) — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Grill du Rova (small-menu restaurant near the Rova) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin" and "Un bon dîner entre amis"; Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (by Tana standards); Q10 "La mémoire de la diaspora" (royal and martyr sites).

### Analakely, Antaninarenina and Tsaralalana (downtown)
- Feel: Analakely is the commercial centre on the valley floor, site of the first marketplace (Wikipedia) [3]; "centre-ville animé" (OpenShores) [6]. Antaninarenina holds the historic Hôtel Colbert, jewellers, luxury shops and offices [3].
- Rent: centre benchmarks as for Isoraka [A cost.rent]; Hotel Niaouly (Ambatovinaky) 55,000–85,000 Ar a night (≈ €12–19, Jan 2022) for a first stay [2].
- Getting around: the hub of the taxi-be network; Gare Soarano is here [1]. Taxi-be are banned from the very centre but enter it anyway (L'Express, Mar 2026) [A daily.transport].
- Watch: in the December 2025 floods residents paid 500–1,000 Ar to be carried across water or 700–1,000 Ar to rent boots in Besarety, Andravoahangy and Tsaralalana (Midi Madagasikara, 2025-12-13) [A daily.transport]. On 25 Sept 2025 looters hit shops, a bank, a hotel and cable-car stations (Wikipedia citing AP) [A safety.general]. FCDO after-dark advice [A safety.general].
- Places:
  - Analakely Market (daily 06:00–18:00; covered section; bargain) — Wikivoyage, updated Aug 2020 [1]; OpenShores [6]
  - Avenue de l'Indépendance (1 km shopping and dining street) and Ambohijatovo gardens at its start — Wikivoyage [1]; Wikipedia [3]
  - Institut français de Madagascar (14 avenue de l'Indépendance; médiathèque, Campus France) — fr.Wikipedia [15]
  - Jasmin (vegetarian-friendly restaurant, Palace Analakely 101; about €7 for two courses and a drink) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Hôtel Colbert (historic hotel, Antaninarenina) — Wikipedia [3]; Wikivoyage [1]
  - Café de la gare (café near Gare Soarano) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - Lac Anosy (lake with war memorial; jacarandas in October) — Wikipedia [3]; fr.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Andravoahangy (artisan market, eastern edge) — Wikipedia [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Serré" for a first stay (cheap hotels, market prices "3–5 times lower" than supermarkets per OpenShores [A cost.negotiation]); Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (by day); not for Q4 "Avec mes enfants".

### Ampandrana, Ankadivato, Ambohipo and Ambatoroka (east of the centre, cheaper)
- Feel: Ambohipo and Ambatoroka are further out with pleasant housing and "nettement moins élevés" rents (Expat.com, 2015, old) [7]; Ankadivato is described as residential and near the centre in a 2026 listing [A cost.rent].

## Living: typical weeks

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Isoraka, for walkable evenings and the centre's restaurants [3][5]; the alternative is a furnished Ivandry T2 (€600) in a residence advertising a generator, if power matters more than evenings [A cost.rent]. Coworking is thin: Red Zone Coworking is listed in Antsakaviro (no price, no date) [18]; Coworker.com lists it at Alarobia Ivandry too (unverified, page returned 403) [20].
- Monday: work from home; lunch at a gargote ($1–2 a dish, OpenShores) [A cost.living]; evening errands at Analakely Market on foot [1].
- Tuesday: taxi (≈ 5,000 Ar) to Red Zone Coworking in Antsakaviro if it is open; check address first [18][1].
- Wednesday: home; evening French-language talk or film programme at the Institut français, avenue de l'Indépendance, on foot [15].
- Thursday: taxi to La City mall (Ivandry) for imported groceries and a meal; allow for the 17–19 h jam on the way back [9][4].
- Friday: dinner at KudeTa, then Le Manson By Marco [1][8]; taxi home, agreed price, as fares double at night [A cost.negotiation].
- Saturday: Malagasy lesson ($5–10/hour, OpenShores) [A cost.living]; walk up to Andohalo and the Rova [1].
- Sunday: Lac Anosy walk; slow day [3].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" → Le Manson By Marco (club at weekends) or Club 43 (DJs, themed nights; no address sourced) [8]; most bars close around 2 am per the same blog [8]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" → La Table de Mariette (high-end Malagasy, 11 rue George-V, Faravohitra) or Sakamanga [1]. "Tranquille chez moi" → home; the Isoraka flat's evening cost is just the fibre [A cost.living].
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" → not realistic by road from Tana (inland at 1,280 m [D]); Nosy Be is about an hour's flight (OpenShores, in `research/cities/madagascar/nosy-be.md` [health.care]), fares not sourced. "En montagne, en forêt" → Andasibe-Mantadia, about 140–150 km east on the RN2, about 3 h by road, licensed guide compulsory [13][14]; or Lemurs' Park, RN1 PK 22, 70,000 Ar with guide (Feb 2025) [1].   - Food: 5 gargote lunches × $1–2 = $5–10; 3 mid-range dinners × $5–10 = $15–30; groceries: rice 5 kg $3.50 + eggs $1.50 + chicken 1 kg $3 + tomatoes 1 kg $0.60 + milk 2 L $2.40 + 7 baguettes $1.75 = $12.75 [A cost.living]
  - Going out: one bar or club night $10–20; Malagasy lesson 1 h $5–10 [A cost.living]

  - Total ≈ 417,000 Ar + €27.70 + $49–85 ≈ €120 + $49–85. Cross-check: Expat.com's single budget excluding rent, €380–490/month ÷ 4.33 = €88–113 a week, plus rent €87 = €175–200 [A cost.living].

  - Lycée Français ≈ 28,000,000 Ar per child per year ÷ 52 = 538,500 Ar a week per child; two children 1,077,000 Ar (≈ €239) (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) [4]
  - Jirama 150,000–300,000 ÷ 4.33 = 34,600–69,300 Ar; generator 46,200–115,500 Ar [4]
  - Housekeeper $50–80 + guard $40–70 a month ÷ 4.33 = $20.80–34.60 [A cost.living]
  - Groceries ×4 = $51 [A cost.living]
  - Saturday Tsimbazaza 2 × 10,000 + 2 × 4,000 = 28,000 Ar (2018 fees) [2]
  - Total ≈ 1,879,000–1,983,000 Ar (≈ €417–441) + $72–86. Cross-check: Expat.com's family-of-four budget excluding rent, €2,900–4,700 ÷ 4.33 = €670–1,085 a week, mostly school fees [A cost.living].

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- Where to stay first: night 1 at Manoir Rouge, 1 km from Ivato (double €9–15; transfer 15,000 Ar) [1]; then downtown at Hotel Niaouly (55,000–85,000 Ar) or Sakamanga (€19–98, book ahead) [2][1].
- Entry: up to 15 days without a visa with a 10 € fee and an onward ticket; visa on arrival for 30 or 60 days, extendable to 90 in total [D]. Declare foreign currency above 7,500 € [D].
- Day 1: taxi-be "D" (2 × 800 Ar) or private transfer (60,000–80,000 Ar) into town [1].
- Day 2: SIM: operators are Yas (ex-Telma), Orange and Airtel, each with mobile money (MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money) [D]; a 5 GB plan costs $5–10 a month [A cost.living]. Exchange euros in town rather than at the airport; ATMs: BNI, Bank of Africa, BMOI, MCB (Wikivoyage, several dead links) [1]. Cash is still the main means of payment, and some sellers quote in FMG (5 FMG = 1 Ar) [A daily.money].
- Day 3: flat hunt: Expat.com listings and the "Bon coin des expat" Facebook group [A cost.rent]; agencies charge a "droit de visite" of 10,000–20,000 Ar a viewing [A housing.search]; budget four viewings (Ivandry, Ambatobe, Ankadivato, Ambohibao).
- Day 4: more viewings; visit on a rainy day, test outlets with breakers off, photograph the property for the lease, fix rent in ariary with indexation (forum, 2026) [A housing.search].
- Day 5: negotiate: almost every Ivandry/Ambatobe listing is "négociable" [A cost.negotiation]. Advance customs: deposit usually 2 months (Expat.com) [A cost.rent], but listings ask for one month (Ankadivato) or 50% (Ambohibao) [A cost.rent]; an agency fee of one month's rent, or about 50% of the first month to a neighbourhood middleman (forum, c. 2023–24) [A cost.negotiation].
- Day 6: bank account: OpenShores says opening one can take weeks [A business.admin]; no bank's documents list was found. Mobile money is the workable bridge [D].
- Day 7: Sunday at Andohalo Cathedral and the Haute-Ville to feel the city at rest [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" → Le Manson By Marco [8]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" → Sakamanga, near the hotel [1]. "Tranquille chez moi" → the hotel.
- Weekend outing: Lemurs' Park half-day by taxi (70,000 Ar) [1]. "À la plage" and "Dans la savane ou le désert" → not realistic from Antananarivo.
- Rough first-week spend (one person):
  - Manoir Rouge 1 night €9–15 + transfer 15,000 Ar [1]
  - Hotel Niaouly 6 nights × 55,000–85,000 = 330,000–510,000 Ar [2]
  - 4 viewings × 10,000–20,000 = 40,000–80,000 Ar [A housing.search]
  - Mobile plan $5–10 [A cost.living]
  - Food: 7 gargote lunches × $1–2 = $7–14; 7 mid-range dinners × $5–10 = $35–70 [A cost.living]
  - Taxis: 8 × 5,000–20,000 = 40,000–160,000 Ar [1]
  - Total ≈ 425,000–765,000 Ar (≈ €94–170) + €9–15 + $47–94. To sign a lease on an Ivandry T2 at €600: 2 months' deposit + 1st month = €1,800, plus a one-month agency fee €600 if an agency is used [A cost.rent][A cost.negotiation].

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances".

### One day
- Morning: Analakely Market (opens 06:00), then up Avenue de l'Indépendance past the Ambohijatovo gardens [1][3]. Carry few valuables; daytime is generally fine (Wikivoyage) [1].
- Midday: taxi (≈ 5,000 Ar) up to the Haute-Ville; lunch at Grill du Rova [1].
- Afternoon: the Rova, Andafiavaratra Palace (3,000 Ar, Sat–Thu 10:00–17:00) and Andohalo Cathedral, on foot between them [1]; in October, the jacarandas round Lac Anosy on the way down [3].
- Evening: dinner in Isoraka at KudeTa or Sakamanga; Le Manson By Marco if it is a weekend [1][8]. Taxi back at an agreed price; do not walk alone after dark (FCDO) [A safety.general].

### One week
- Day 1: the one-day route above.
- Day 2: Ambohimanga, about 24 km north-east (UNESCO 2001; 10,000 Ar for foreigners, c. 2012–13) [12]; on the way back, Croc Farm near Ivato (25,000 Ar) [1].
- Day 3: leave for Andasibe-Mantadia by the RN2, about 3 h, 140–150 km [13][14]; Cotisse coaches run Tana–Toamasina, but fares to Andasibe were not found [1].
- Day 4: Andasibe-Mantadia, indri walk with a compulsory licensed guide; return to Tana [14][13].
- Day 5, daily life: catch a taxi-be at morning rush hour (600–800 Ar a leg; about 70% of trips are made this way) [A daily.transport], ride to Analakely and walk the market at its busiest [1]. FCDO warns about robberies in multi-passenger vans, so carry nothing of value and ride by day only [A safety.general].
- Day 6: scout where people settle: Ivandry (La City mall, Leader Price) and Ankorondrano (Jumbo Score), then Tsarasaotra park [9][4][1][6]; an evening film at Cinépax [2].
- Day 7, daily life: Sunday Mass at Andohalo Cathedral (the churches weigh heavily in public life; the FFKM council mediated in the 2025 crisis [D]), then Lac Anosy [1][3]. If a match is on, Mahamasina stadium (rugby and football; fixtures not sourced; a crush killed at least 12 at the 2023 Indian Ocean Island Games opening) [17].
- Alternative for days 3–4: Antsirabe, about 5 h by VIP coach (25,000–26,000 Ar) [1].
