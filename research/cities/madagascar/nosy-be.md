# Nosy Be, Madagascar

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA** (implied by Expat.com's rent tables, see `research/countries/madagascar.md`), rounded. OpenShores gives prices in USD only; they are kept in USD and not converted. Most city-specific prices come from one commercial relocation site (OpenShores) and from old forum threads; treat them as indicative. No current Nosy Be rental or sale listing could be found on Expat.com's portal (its "à louer" pages showed Tana and Toliara only).

## Items

- [cost.rent] Rents (USD/month): studio or 1-bed about $250; 2-bedroom bungalow $350–500; villa with pool $700–1,500. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Nosy Be", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives, for coastal cities including Nosy Be: T2 near the beach from 400,000 MGA/month (≈ €90); sea-view house 1,000,000–2,000,000 MGA (≈ €220–445). — Cyril Jarnias, "Trouver un logement à Madagascar", 2025-03-04, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Forum users (c. 2020) report a hillside villa with pool and sea view at €600/month (≈ 2,700,000 MGA), found through a local business contact, and a villa near Ambatoloaka at 80,000 MGA/day (≈ €18), called exceptional. — Expat.com forum "Vivre Nosy Bay", c. 2020 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Forum users (c. 2020) say beachfront land in good legal order costs at least €60,000; a 1980s house of about 400 m² on 1,500 m² was asked at €500,000. — Expat.com forum "Vivre Nosy Bay", c. 2020 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Monthly budget (USD): rent $250, groceries $140, transport $30–80, health $30–50, telecoms $20–40, leisure $80–150 — total $550–710. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Nosy Be", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Food (USD): fresh fish 1 kg $2–4; shrimp 1 kg $5–8; rice 5 kg $4; baguette $0.30; farm chicken 1 kg $3.50; local beer $1; bottle of rum arrangé $3–5. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Eating out (USD): gargote $2–3; mid-range beach restaurant $8–15; fine dining $20–35; seafood platter for two $15–25. A sister page puts dinner for two at a good restaurant at $20–30. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Utilities (USD/month): electricity $30–60 (more with air-con); water $10–20; gas $5–10. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Telecoms (USD): local SIM $2; 5 GB data $8–12; fixed internet, where available, $30–50/month. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Leisure (USD): two dives $50–80; full-day boat trip $30–60; kitesurf lesson $50–80; one-hour massage $8–15. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Domestic staff (USD/month): full-time housekeeper $50–70; cook $60–100; guard $40–60. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Forum users (c. 2016) call Nosy Be "relativement cher" compared with Toliara, Morondava or Diego, because most goods are imported. — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Nosy Be", c. 2016 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] A real-estate blogger says a qualified mason earns about €150–200 a month (up to 800,000 MGA). — Cyril Jarnias "Nosy Be : atouts pour investisseurs", updated 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.salary] Forum users (c. 2020) say a bar worker earns about €50 a month; another says a waitress is paid 6,000 MGA a day (≈ €1.30). — Expat.com forum "Vivre Nosy Bay", c. 2020 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] OpenShores says off-season seasonal rentals can drop to about 50% of the high-season rate, long-term tenants get discounts, and dives and excursions are negotiable outside high season. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] OpenShores says Hell-Ville market is 30–50% cheaper than tourist shops. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] A forum user (c. 2020) reports a house asked at €500,000 that sold for about €180,000 after negotiation; another got a chalet cut from €38 to €32 a night for 21 days by booking off-platform (2018). — Expat.com forum "Vivre Nosy Bay", c. 2020 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Hell-Ville (Andoany) is the main town and port (about 30,000 people); Ambatoloaka is the busy tourist village with bars, a casino and most excursion departures. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] Foreigners cannot yet own land in their own name; they can take a long-term "bail emphytéotique" (long lease) and build on it. — Cyril Jarnias, updated 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: medium
- [business.invest] The same blogger says hotel occupancy reaches 100% in peak season and two Marriott-brand hotels (over 200 rooms) are planned. — Cyril Jarnias, updated 2026-04-27, blog, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] The SIRAMA sugar company, once employing over 3,000 people, has been insolvent since 2006, and its land is being sold for hotels and housing; tourism has been the main activity since 1960. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.power] OpenShores says power cuts become more frequent in the rainy season and some tracks become impassable. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Nosy Be", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [daily.internet] OpenShores describes internet as slow and unstable with frequent outages, worse in the rainy season; 4G only in covered areas. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Petrol about $1.20/L; scooter rental $100–150/month; tuk-tuk ride $1–3; car rental $25–40/day; boat to nearby islands $5–20. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] One paved ring road circles the island with unpaved tracks beyond; boats run from Ankify on the mainland to Hell-Ville throughout the day until nightfall. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Fascene airport has scheduled flights from Antananarivo, Réunion, Mayotte, Paris, Marseille, Milan and Rome; OpenShores puts a trip to Europe at 15+ hours with a stopover. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be" + OpenShores, accessed 2026-11-14 / 2026-04-13, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] OpenShores: a few basic clinics, no MRI, no CT scanner and no specialised surgery on the island; evacuation goes to Antananarivo (about 1 hour's flight) or Réunion. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] GP consultation $15–30; basic pharmacy $5–15; international health insurance $80–200/month, described as essential. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] Forum users report a dental crown at about 600,000 MGA (≈ €135) on the road out of Hell-Ville; others say implants are unrealistic locally. — Expat.com forum "Recherche bon dentiste sur Diego, Nosy Be", c. 2019 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [health.care] Nosy Be is an active malaria transmission zone; prophylaxis, nets and repellent are advised. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] The French Collège Alphonse de Lamartine has run since 1974 (English Wikipedia lists the French school as primary only); for university, students must go to Antsiranana. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] FCDO lists Nosy Be among coastal places with violent robberies and assaults on beaches, some in daylight; get local advice before going to isolated beaches. — UK FCDO safety page, current at 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 3 Oct 2013 two Europeans and a Malagasy man were lynched at Ambatoloaka after child-trafficking rumours; in Oct 2015 four people got life sentences. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] Italian is widely spoken by tourism workers because of the many Italian visitors; events include the Donia festival (Pentecost weekend) and Nosy Be Symphonies (late August, since 2014). — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [social.pace] Forum users (c. 2020) say bakeries are scarce and the atmosphere is calmer than in South America; OpenShores recommends a three-month trial stay. — Expat.com forum "Vivre à Nosy Be" + OpenShores, c. 2020 / 2026-04-13, forum, confidence: low
- [community.expat] OpenShores describes the community as welcoming, with friendships forming quickly; older forum posts describe a split between unhappy expats and quietly content ones. — OpenShores 2026-04-13 + Expat.com forum c. 2016, company, confidence: low
- [climate.daily] Air 25–35 °C and sea about 28 °C all year; dry season May–October, rainy season November–March; humpback whales July–November. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Lokobe reserve (primary forest, lemurs), Nosy Komba and the Nosy Tanikely marine park are next to the island; planned rare-earth mining near Ampasindava Bay could send toxic effluent toward Nosy Be's waters. — Wikipedia FR "Nosy Be", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
