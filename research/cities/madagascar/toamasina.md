# Toamasina (Tamatave), Madagascar

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier ("for work"). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA** (implied by Expat.com's rent tables), rounded. OpenShores gives USD only; kept in USD and not converted. **Cyclone Gezani destroyed most of the city on 10 Feb 2026**, so every price or housing figure dated before February 2026 may no longer hold.

## Items

- [cost.rent] Rents (USD/month): city-centre studio $80–120; 1-bed $120; 2-bed $150–250; house with garden $200–400; seafront villa $300–600. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie à Toamasina", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A forum user (March 2023) paid 1,700,000 MGA/month (≈ €380) for a 1-bedroom flat, water and power extra; the first payment included one month's deposit and one month's agent fee. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks in Toamasina or in Antananarivo", 2023-03, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A family (Aug 2019) said houses and flats ran 300,000–1,000,000 MGA (≈ €65–220), most often 1,000,000, and agency listings mostly 1,400,000 MGA and up. — Expat.com forum "Living in Tamatave", c. 2019-09 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Expat.com's housing guide gave about 900,000 MGA/month for a small home on the outskirts and 1,400,000 in the centre, plus about 500,000 more for three bedrooms. — Expat.com "Se loger à Tamatave", 2015-05-06 (OLD), company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Monthly budget (USD): 1-bed rent $120, groceries $75, transport $10–25, health $20–40, telecoms $15–35, leisure $40–80 — table total $280–375, though the intro says $350–500. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Food (USD): rice 5 kg $3; fresh fish 1 kg $1.50–3; shrimp 1 kg $4–7; chicken 1 kg $2.50; baguette $0.20; dozen eggs $1.30; large beer $0.70. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A forum user (2023) paid about 550 MGA per egg at Bazar Kely and 2,700 MGA for 2 L of bottled water. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks…", 2023-03, forum, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Eating out and leisure (USD): gargote $1–2; mid-range $4–8; sea-view restaurant $10–18; local cinema $2–3; gym $15–25/month; bar night $5–10. — OpenShores "Coût de la vie", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Utilities and telecoms (USD/month): electricity $15–35; water $5–10; 5 GB mobile $5–10; fixed internet $20–40. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.living] Domestic staff (USD/month): full-time housekeeper $35–60; cook $40–70; guard $30–50. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] A family (2019) got a new 4-bedroom house with five air-conditioners near the French lycée for €450/month after the owner first asked €575; they said rents are negotiable and early August has the best choice. — Expat.com forum "Living in Tamatave", c. 2019-09 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] The same family reports neighbourhood middlemen charging 5,000 MGA per viewing plus 60% of the first month's rent; agencies charge one month's rent. — Expat.com forum "Living in Tamatave", c. 2019-09 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] Forum users say locals still quote prices in old francs (FMG, 5 FMG = 1 MGA) and that answering in ariary brings higher prices; tuk-tuk fares can be bargained. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks…", c. 2023–2024, forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Expat.com's guide calls Tahiti Kely and Salazamay (near the sea and the airport) upscale, and the area around the French lycée and consulate popular with French-speaking families. — Expat.com "Se loger à Tamatave", 2015-05-06 (OLD), company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] A family (2019) found Valpinson and Morafeno not prone to flooding, about 7 minutes' drive from the French lycée (20–30 min in school traffic). — Expat.com forum "Living in Tamatave", c. 2019-09 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Cyclone Gezani (10 Feb 2026) passed its eye over the city; about 75% of buildings were destroyed, 16,318 people displaced, and at one point the city had about 5% of its power and no clean water. — Wikipedia "Cyclone Gezani" (citing RFI 2026-02-12, France 24 2026-02-13), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.areas] French Wikipedia gives a wider damage estimate of 75–90% of the city and 41 deaths in the Toamasina region by 16 Feb 2026; earlier cyclones razed the city in 1927 and 1994 (Geralda, ~80%). — Wikipedia FR "Toamasina", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] The port handles about 80% of Madagascar's sea freight; the city also has the oil refinery that supplies the capital and, since 2007, the Ambatovy nickel-cobalt processing plant on its southern edge. — Wikipedia FR "Toamasina", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [work.jobs] OpenShores sees real openings in the port, logistics, trade and industry for technical profiles, but a narrow market for everyone else. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients de Toamasina", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [daily.power] In Nov 2026 the state requisitioned Enelec's thermal plant for up to 24 months; Jirama says it should give 32 MW but delivers 14 MW at most, one cause of the city's outages. — L'Express de Madagascar "Toamasina : l'État réquisitionne la centrale", 2026-11 (URL date), local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] A resident (c. May 2026) said Jirama restored power and water in Ampasimazava about 12 days after Gezani, but many other districts were still cut when they left, with poles still on the ground. — Expat.com forum "TAMATAVE et Alentour", ~2026-05, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.water] OpenShores says water and power cuts are frequent and cyclones can cut both for days; a 2023 forum user advised against drinking or cooking with tap water. — OpenShores 2026-04-13 + Expat.com forum 2023-03, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] A forum user (March 2023) paid about 1,500 MGA per person for a tuk-tuk by day and 2,500 at night (≈ €0.33–0.55); a pousse-pousse about 1,000 MGA by day. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks…", 2023-03, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Public buses were removed after Covid, so residents rely on cyclo-pousses and tuk-tuks; passenger trains to Moramanga resumed on 1 June 2023; a highway from Tana (target ~2.5 h) is under construction. — Wikipedia FR "Toamasina", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] OpenShores puts the RN2 drive to Antananarivo (about 350 km) at 8–10 hours and taxi-brousse to the capital at $8–12; local roads degrade fast in the rain. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] FCDO warns that ferries from Tamatave/Soanierana to Sainte-Marie have had accidents from overcrowding, poor maintenance and sudden squalls. — UK FCDO safety page, current at 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [daily.money] A forum user (2023) paid 10,200 MGA per foreign-card withdrawal at BOA and 8,000 MGA at Société Générale, both capped at 800,000 MGA. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks…", 2023-03, forum, confidence: low
- [admin.visa] A forum user (2023) extended a visa in Toamasina for 120,000 MGA (≈ €27) with photos, copies and a return ticket and got the passport back the next day; another says extensions are easier in port towns than in Tana. — Expat.com forum "Life hacks…", 2023-03, forum, confidence: low
- [health.care] CHU Morafeno is the university hospital; malaria and chikungunya outbreaks still occur. — Wikipedia "Toamasina", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [health.care] OpenShores says local facilities are basic with no reference hospital; GP $10–20, specialist $20–40. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [family.schools] There is a French lycée (Lycée Français de Tamatave), one public lycée (Jacques Rabemananjara, about 4,000 pupils), mostly church-run private schools, and the University of Toamasina (1977). — Wikipedia FR "Toamasina", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] On 25 Sept 2025 a protest rally turned to looting and vandalism; curfews from 7–8 pm to 4–5 am followed. — Wikipedia "2025 Malagasy protests" (citing Midi Madagasikara 2025-09-26/27), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [social.culture] OpenShores says there is no cinema, few varied restaurants and few cultural events (its own price table lists a local cinema at $2–3). — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [community.expat] The city has a large Chinese community (often of mixed descent), an Indo-Pakistani community and a European minority; OpenShores calls the expat community small but close. — Wikipedia FR "Toamasina" + OpenShores, accessed 2026-11-14 / 2026-04-13, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Over 3,000 mm of rain a year and humidity often above 80%, causing mould on clothes and electronics; a 2019 forum user left the area over black mould in buildings. — OpenShores 2026-04-13 + Expat.com forum c. 2019, company, confidence: low
- [nature.access] The Pangalanes canal (600+ km), Foulpointe beaches (about 1 hour) and Sainte-Marie island are within weekend reach; Parc Ivoloina is 12 km from the centre. — OpenShores 2026-04-13 + Wikipedia "Toamasina", company, confidence: medium
