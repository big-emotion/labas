# Nosy Be: neighbourhoods and routes

> Built 2026-11-14 from the city file and targeted searches. Research material, not published text. Every named place has a source; nothing is invented. Prices are dated asking prices, not what people pay.

Rate used throughout: **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA (Ar)**, the dossier rate (`research/countries/madagascar.md`). USD figures (OpenShores) are kept as stated and not converted, as in the city file. `[N tag]` = an item in `research/cities/madagascar/nosy-be.md`; `[A tag]` = an item in `research/cities/madagascar/antananarivo.md`; `[D]` = the dossier; numbers in brackets point to "Sources read" below. Weekly figures divide monthly ones by 4.33. Fetches were made on 2026-11-15; the shared web-search budget was used up early in the pass, so this sheet rests on direct fetches of travel wikis, Wikipedia, Madagascar National Parks pages and relocation blogs.

Read this first: no source gives a rent **by locality** on Nosy Be. Every rent below is island-wide, mostly from one commercial relocation site (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) and from old forum threads [N cost.rent]; Expat.com's portal showed no Nosy Be listing on 2026-11-14 [N Gaps]. Treat rents as orders of magnitude, and expect them to be seasonal: off-season seasonal rentals can drop to about half the high-season rate (OpenShores) [N cost.negotiation].

Frames that apply to the whole island:
- Safety (official): FCDO (current at 2026-11-14) lists Nosy Be among coastal places with violent robberies and assaults on beaches, some in daylight; get local advice before going to isolated beaches, and do not go alone [N safety.general][D]. On 3 Oct 2013 two Europeans and a Malagasy man were lynched at Ambatoloaka after child-trafficking rumours (Wikipedia FR) [N safety.general].
- Health: a few basic clinics, no MRI or CT scanner, evacuation to Antananarivo (about 1 hour's flight) or Réunion (OpenShores) [N health.care]; OpenShores names "l'hôpital de Hell-Ville" without detail [6]. Nosy Be is an active malaria zone (OpenShores) [N health.care].
- Utilities: power cuts more frequent in the rainy season; internet slow and unstable, 4G only in covered areas (OpenShores) [N daily.power][N daily.internet].
- Getting around: one paved ring road with unpaved tracks beyond (Wikipedia FR) [N daily.transport]. In Hell-Ville, tuk-tuk 500 Ar a person a ride and Renault 4L taxi 1,000 Ar a person (fr.Wikivoyage, undated) [2]; OpenShores gives tuk-tuk $1–3, taxi $3–10 by distance, scooter rental $100–150/month, car rental $25–40/day [6][N daily.transport]. Fascene airport is about 15 km NNE of Hell-Ville [2]; airport taxi about 10,000 Ar a person in a four-seat car, about 20 minutes to the centre (madacamp, 2017 prices) [4]. No distance between Hell-Ville and the west-coast beach villages was found.
- Mainland: speedboat Ankify–Hell-Ville 20,000 Ar (≈ €4.40), about 45 minutes (Wikivoyage, 2025) [1]; madacamp's 2017 fares were 6,000 Ar (slow boat, 1.5 h), 9,000 (faster), 13,000 (shared speedboat), 80,000 (private); touts may push 13,000 to 20,000, so buy at the gate [4]. FCDO names Ankify and Ambanja too for robberies [D].

## Neighbourhoods

### Hell-Ville (Andoany), the town and port
- Feel: the main town and port, about 30,000 people (Wikipedia FR) [3]; "plus urbaine", shops nearby (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [6]; colonial houses, modern flats and the island's economic centre (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06) [8]. Wikivoyage advises most travellers to sleep on the beaches instead [1].
- Rent: island-wide only: studio or 1-bed about $250/month; 2-bed bungalow $350–500; independent bungalow $200–400; room in a shared house $100–150 (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [N cost.rent][6].
- Getting around: tuk-tuk 500 Ar, 4L taxi 1,000 Ar a ride inside town [2]; port for Ankify, Nosy Komba (about 20 min) and the islands [2]; Lokobe park office at Marodoka is about 7 km by car from Ambarivato port [11].
- Watch: nothing town-specific from an official or media source.
- Places:
  - Hell-Ville central market place (market) — madacamp, modified 2025-05-01 [4]; OpenShores says Hell-Ville market is 30–50% cheaper than tourist shops [N cost.negotiation]
  - Hell-Ville harbour (boats to Ankify and the islands) — madacamp [4]; fr.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Hôpital de Hell-Ville (public hospital, unnamed beyond the town) — OpenShores, 2026-04-13 [6]
  - L'Oasis Nosy Be and Casa Mofo (cafés-restaurants) — madacamp [4][5]
  - Container Bar (bar) — madacamp [4][5]
  - Hotel de la Mer and La Plantation (hotel-restaurants with bars) — madacamp [4]
  - Musée CNRO (oceanographic research centre museum, Sarodravoay Ambanoro, on the road to Marodoka-Lokobe; Mon–Fri 8–12 h and 14–16 h; updated Jun 2026) — fr.Wikivoyage [2]
- Fits: Q5 "J'entreprends" (port and economic centre); Q4 "Avec mes enfants" (hospital and market at hand); Q3 "Serré" and "Moyen" (town prices); Q7 "Reste à taille humaine".

### Ambatoloaka, the tourist village
- Feel: former fishing village on the west coast, lively after dark, mid-range hotels, restaurants, a casino and bars; boats leave from the beach for the islands (Wikipedia FR) [3]. "Idéal pour les célibataires" (OpenShores) [6]; the island's tourist heart (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06) [8]; restaurants, nightlife and beach, but seasonal and expensive per m² (Cyril Jarnias, 2026-11-06) [7].
- Rent: island-wide figures above [N cost.rent]. A villa near Ambatoloaka at 80,000 Ar a day (≈ €18), described as exceptional (forum, c. 2020) [N cost.rent]; most budget hotels cost €15–20 a night (fr.Wikivoyage) [2]. Sea-view villa purchase $150,000–400,000 (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) [7].
- Getting around: tuk-tuk or taxi [6]; island boat departures from the beach [3]; Nosy Tanikely boats also leave from Ambatoloaka [12].
- Watch: the 2013 lynching took place here (Wikipedia FR) [N safety.general]; FCDO beach-robbery warning applies [N safety.general].
- Places:
  - Ambatoloaka beach (boat departures for the islands) — Wikipedia FR [3]
  - Ambatoloaka evening street vendors (brochettes and local dishes) — fr.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Coucher du Soleil (huts off the main road uphill, Madirokely/Ambatoloaka; double with bathroom €25, breakfast €4, Feb 2025) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - The village's casino — Wikipedia FR [3] (name not given)
- Fits: Q2 "Danser jusqu'au matin"; Q4 "Seul·e"; Q7 "Vibre jour et nuit" (by island standards); Q8 "À la plage".

### Madirokely, the residential beach
- Feel: "la plage résidentielle la plus recherchée" (Cyril Jarnias, 2026-11-06) [7]; a compromise between calm and nearby services, "plage magnifique" (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [6]. The Libertalia festival moved here from the mainland (Wikipedia FR) [3].
- Rent: island-wide figures [N cost.rent]; sea-view villa $500–1,200 a month (OpenShores) [6]; purchase $150,000–400,000 [7].
- Getting around: next to Ambatoloaka (Coucher du Soleil is listed as Madirokely/Ambatoloaka) [1]; tuk-tuk or scooter [6].
- Watch: FCDO beach-robbery warning applies [N safety.general].
- Places:
  - Madirokely beach — OpenShores [6]
  - Tatie Chris (beach party venue, every Sunday from noon until late) — Wikivoyage, updated Feb 2025 [1]
  - Coucher du Soleil (guesthouse) — Wikivoyage [1]
- Fits: Q5 "Télétravail" (calm with services, if the connection holds); Q4 "En couple"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage".

### Dzamandzar, the old sugar town
- Feel: "plus authentique", lower prices, local atmosphere (OpenShores, 2026-04-13) [6]; a major sugar industry from the 1920s and a well-known rum distillery (Wikipedia FR) [3]; the SIRAMA sugar company has been insolvent since 2006 and its land is being sold for hotels and housing [N work.jobs].
- Rent: island-wide figures [N cost.rent]; no Dzamandzar figure.
- Getting around: on the road to Andilana [3]; scooter or taxi [6].
- Watch: nothing locality-specific sourced.
- Places:
  - La Banane, Dzamandzar (moraingy, traditional bare-fisted fighting, every Sunday afternoon; entry 10,000 Ar, Feb 2025) — Wikivoyage [1]
  - 18-hole golf course on the road to Andilana — Wikipedia FR [3]
  - The former rum distillery — Wikipedia FR [3] (no visiting information)
- Fits: Q3 "Serré"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q10 "Une vie abordable"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

### Andilana, the northern beach (upper end)
- Feel: the top of the villa and hotel market (Cyril Jarnias, 2026-11-06) [7]; more exclusive, white sand, calm (Cyril Jarnias, 2025-06-06) [8]; a hotel complex was built in the 1960s–70s, and a Holiday Inn later (Wikipedia FR) [3].
- Rent: no rent figure; purchases at the top of the $150,000–400,000 range [7]; turnkey villas with pool from a local developer €299,000–599,000 [7].
- Getting around: end of the road past Dzamandzar [3]; Nosy Tanikely boats also leave from Andilana [12].
- Watch: FCDO beach-robbery warning [N safety.general].
- Places:
  - Le Grand Bleu (2-star hotel with pool, diving, sport fishing; bungalow from €39 or €60 by season, Jan 2023) — fr.Wikivoyage [2]
  - Andilana beach — Cyril Jarnias [8]; Wikipedia FR [3]
- Fits: Q3 "Le prix compte peu"; Q7 "Prend son temps"; Q8 "À la plage"; Q2 "Tranquille chez moi".

## Living: typical weeks

Read this before the profiles: Nosy Be's job market is small and tourism-only (hospitality, diving, property) [D]; reported local wages are low (a bar worker about €50 a month, a waitress 6,000 Ar a day, forum c. 2020; a qualified mason €150–200 a month, blog 2026) [N cost.salary]. Secondary schooling and serious health care are thin [N family.schools][N health.care].

### 1. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "Télétravail" + Q4 "Seul·e"
- Base: Madirokely, calm with services nearby [6][7]. Internet is the risk: slow, unstable, worse in the rainy season; fixed lines only where available ($30–50/month) [N daily.internet][N cost.living]. No coworking space was found.
- Monday: work from home; lunch at a gargote ($2–3) [N cost.living].
- Tuesday: tuk-tuk to Hell-Ville market and the harbour for errands [4][6].
- Wednesday: home; evening swim at Madirokely beach [6].
- Thursday: work; dinner at a beach restaurant in Ambatoloaka ($8–15) [N cost.living][3].
- Friday: Ambatoloaka street brochettes, then the bars [2][3].
- Saturday: boat day to Nosy Tanikely (book online; full-day boat trip $30–60) [12][N cost.living].
- Sunday: Tatie Chris from noon [1], or moraingy at La Banane, Dzamandzar [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" → Ambatoloaka's bars and casino [3]; the open-air bar-club "Taxi Be" is named by one blog without a location [9]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" → seafood platter for two ($15–25) in Ambatoloaka [N cost.living]; Ylang Ylang Restaurant is named by the same blog without a location [9]. "Tranquille chez moi" → home; a one-hour massage costs $8–15 [N cost.living].
- Weekend outings: "À la plage" → Andilana, or Nosy Iranja, about 90 minutes by boat, two islands joined by a 750 m sandbar at low tide [2][1]. "En montagne, en forêt" → Lokobe (primary forest, black lemurs; Lakana pirogue circuit before 11 am) [11][10]; Mont Passot (329 m, by car, sunset) [2]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" → not realistic from Nosy Be. "Je reste en ville" → Hell-Ville is small: market, harbour, Musée CNRO [4][2].
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - Rent $250 ÷ 4.33 = $57.70 [N cost.rent]
  - Utilities: electricity $30–60 + water $10–20 + gas $5–10 = $45–90 ÷ 4.33 = $10.40–20.80 [N cost.living]
  - Fixed internet $30–50 ÷ 4.33 = $6.90–11.50 [N cost.living]
  - Groceries $140 ÷ 4.33 = $32.30 [N cost.living]
  - 3 gargote lunches × $2–3 = $6–9; 2 beach dinners × $8–15 = $16–30 [N cost.living]
  - 6 tuk-tuk rides × $1–3 = $6–18 [N daily.transport]; one massage $8–15 [N cost.living]
  - Total ≈ $143–194. Cross-check: OpenShores' monthly $550–710 ÷ 4.33 = $127–164 [N cost.living].

### 3. Q1 "M'installer pour de bon" + Q5 "J'entreprends"
- Base: Hell-Ville, the economic centre and port [8][3]. The entrepreneurial angle is tourism and property: foreigners cannot own land in their own name yet but can take a long lease ("bail emphytéotique") and build [N business.invest]; beach plot $40,000–90,000, new reinforced-concrete build $400–700 per m², plus about 10% for notary, land registry and contingencies (Cyril Jarnias, Nov 2026) [7]. Peak-season hotel occupancy is said to reach 100% (blog, low confidence) [N business.invest]. Company creation goes through the EDBM in Antananarivo [D].
- Monday: Hell-Ville harbour and market: suppliers, prices [4].
- Tuesday: visits to plots or premises by 4L taxi (1,000 Ar a ride in town) [2].
- Wednesday: meetings in Ambatoloaka's hotels and restaurants [3].
- Thursday: work from home; power cuts likely in the rainy season [N daily.power].
- Friday: Andilana and Dzamandzar to study the upper market [7][3].
- Saturday: Container Bar or L'Oasis in town [4].
- Sunday: Tatie Chris, Madirokely, where the island's crowd meets [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" → Ambatoloaka [3]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" → Hotel de la Mer or La Plantation, Hell-Ville [4]. "Tranquille chez moi" → home.
- Weekend outings: as profile 1; "Dans la savane ou le désert" → not realistic from Nosy Be.
- Rough weekly spend (one person):
  - 1-bed $250 ÷ 4.33 = $57.70 [N cost.rent]; utilities $10.40–20.80; fixed internet $6.90–11.50; groceries $32.30 [N cost.living]
  - 2 mid-range dinners × $8–15 = $16–30 [N cost.living]
  - 10 town taxi rides × 1,000 Ar = 10,000 Ar [2]
  - Total ≈ $123–152 + 10,000 Ar (≈ €2.20), before business costs.

### 5. Q1 "Tester quelques mois": the first week
- OpenShores recommends a three-month trial stay [N social.pace]. Entry rules as for the whole country: 15 days visa-free with a 10 € fee; visa on arrival 30 or 60 days, up to 90 [D].
- Day 1: Fascene airport, taxi about 10,000 Ar a person to town (2017) [4]; check in at Coucher du Soleil (€25 double, €4 breakfast, Feb 2025) [1] or a budget hotel in Ambatoloaka (€15–20) [2].
- Day 2: SIM ($2) and 5 GB data ($8–12) [N cost.living]; operators and mobile money as in Tana (Yas, Orange, Airtel; MVola, Orange Money, Airtel Money) [D]. Cash: no ATM or bank in Nosy Be was sourced; Wikivoyage says to change money before check-in at Fascene on departure, as there is no exchange counter airside [1]. Bring euros [D].
- Day 3: housing: no portal listings exist [N Gaps]; forum users found villas through local business contacts (c. 2020) [N cost.rent]. Ask in Hell-Ville and Madirokely.
- Day 4: viewings by scooter (rent one for the stay: $100–150/month) [N daily.transport]. Negotiate: long-term tenants get discounts, off-season rents can halve [N cost.negotiation]; a forum user cut a chalet from €38 to €32 a night for 21 days by booking off-platform (2018) [N cost.negotiation]. Deposit and advance customs specific to Nosy Be were not found; Tana's usual 2 months' deposit is the only national benchmark [A cost.rent].
- Day 5: health check: locate the Hell-Ville hospital and a pharmacy; buy repellent [6][N health.care].
- Day 6: bank account: OpenShores says opening one in Madagascar can take weeks [A business.admin]; no Nosy Be bank was named.
- Day 7: Sunday at La Banane (moraingy) and Tatie Chris to meet residents [1].
- Saturday night: "Danser jusqu'au matin" → Ambatoloaka [3]. "Un bon dîner entre amis" → seafood platter [N cost.living]. "Tranquille chez moi" → the guesthouse.
- Weekend outing: Nosy Tanikely by boat (booking online) [12]. "Dans la savane ou le désert" → not realistic from Nosy Be.
- Rough first-week spend (one person):
  - Coucher du Soleil 7 × €25 + 7 × €4 breakfast = €203 [1]
  - Airport taxi 10,000 Ar [4]
  - SIM $2 + data $8–12 = $10–14 [N cost.living]
  - 7 gargote lunches × $2–3 = $14–21; 7 dinners × $8–15 = $56–105 [N cost.living]
  - 10 tuk-tuk rides × $1–3 = $10–30 [N daily.transport]
  - Total ≈ €203 + 10,000 Ar + $90–170.

## Visiting: discovery

Q1 "D'abord des vacances". Season: dry May–October, rainy November–March; humpback whales July–November (OpenShores) [N climate.daily].

### One day
- Morning: taxi to the Lokobe park office at Marodoka (about 7 km from Hell-Ville's port) for the Lakana pirogue circuit, which runs before 11 am [11]; black lemurs, Nosy Be panther chameleon [10]. Entry fee not found.
- Midday: back in Hell-Ville by taxi; the central market, then lunch at L'Oasis or Casa Mofo [4].
- Afternoon: taxi or tuk-tuk to Madirokely beach [6]; do not walk to isolated stretches alone (FCDO) [N safety.general].
- Evening: Mont Passot (329 m) for the sunset, by car [2]; dinner of street brochettes in Ambatoloaka [2].

### One week
- Day 1: Hell-Ville: harbour, market, Musée CNRO if a weekday [4][2].
- Day 2: Nosy Tanikely marine park (snorkelling, diving at 12–20 m, lighthouse walk; online booking required; boats from Hell-Ville, Ambatoloaka or Andilana) [12][14]; full-day boat trip $30–60, two dives $50–80 [N cost.living].
- Day 3: Lokobe, morning pirogue circuit, Ampasipohy village near the reserve [11][3].
- Day 4: Nosy Komba, 3 km south of Nosy Be: black lemurs, villages, crafts [13]; boat about 20 min from Hell-Ville [2].
- Day 5: Nosy Iranja, about 90 minutes, the sandbar between its two islands [2][1].
- Day 6, daily life: early at Hell-Ville market, when residents shop, then the Ankify speedboat (20,000 Ar, about 45 min) to see the mainland commute and Ambanja by taxi (6,000 Ar, about 1 h) [1]. FCDO names Ankify and Ambanja for robberies, so go by day and get local advice [D].
- Day 7, daily life: Sunday afternoon moraingy at La Banane, Dzamandzar (10,000 Ar), the fight games locals attend every week, then Tatie Chris at Madirokely [1].
- Events to time a visit: Donia festival (Pentecost weekend) and Nosy Be Symphonies (late August) [N social.culture].
