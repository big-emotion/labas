# Antsiranana (Diego-Suarez), Madagascar

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Extra city suggested by the dossier ("for a quieter coastal life"). Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA**, rounded. This file is thin on current prices: OpenShores has no Antsiranana page, Expat.com has no listings for the city, and the session's web-search budget ran out before local press could be searched. Most price items below are old forum posts, flagged by date.

## Items

- [cost.rent] A relocation blog gives, for coastal cities including Diego: T2 near the beach from 400,000 MGA/month (≈ €90); sea-view house 1,000,000–2,000,000 MGA (≈ €220–445); deposits are often 2–3 months' rent (Madagascar-wide). — Cyril Jarnias, "Trouver un logement à Madagascar", 2025-03-04, blog, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] Forum users (c. 2017) report a seafront studio at €250–270/month including water and power, and a 300 m² villa on 2,000 m² rented for 750,000 MGA around 2010–2012. — Expat.com forum "Le coût des locations à l'année par villes", c. 2017 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.rent] A prospective resident (Dec 2014) saw a decent, secured house advertised online at €500–600/month. — Routard forum "Vivre à Diego-Suarez en famille", 2014-12-20 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.living] A resident (Dec 2014) called living costs relatively high but in line with Western habits; another said food is cheap except imported goods, and electricity costs depend on appliances. — Routard forum, 2014-12-21/23 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [cost.negotiation] A resident (Dec 2014) said bargaining over housing and food is normal everywhere except chain stores. — Routard forum, 2014-12-21 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] La Corniche is described as calm with bay views and popular with expats; the centre mixes colonial buildings and busy street life. — Cyril Jarnias, 2025-03-04, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Neighbourhoods named include Cap Diego, Place Bazary, Grand Pavois, the Tanambao districts, Lazaret Nord and Sud, Mahatsara and Tsaramandroso; Bazary Kely is the commercial heart. — Wikipedia "Antsiranana" + Wikipedia FR, accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] A forum user warns that a Diego tenant was blamed for an illegal power connection they did not make (2011 case) and advises testing outlets with breakers off before signing. — Expat.com forum "Location à Madagascar : vos pires expériences logement", ~2026-10, forum, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] Main employers named: the Star brewery, the Compagnie Salinière de Madagascar (salt), the SECREN shipyard and PFOI (fish and tuna), plus tourism and the port (sugar, salt and cocoa exports); sources are older. — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [work.jobs] A forum user (c. 2020) left Diego for Nosy Be for work and described the city as in steady decline. — Expat.com forum "Demande d'information sur Diégo Suarez", c. 2020 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [daily.power] On 20 July 2026 the interim president warned Enelec over persistent outages in Toamasina, Mahajanga and Antsiranana, cities fed by Enelec plants; Enelec cited major technical problems. — L'Express de Madagascar "Toamasina : l'État réquisitionne la centrale", 2026-11 (URL date), local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] A remote worker who moved to the Avenir district (late 2025) reported water and power cuts "continuing, even intensifying" and was buying a generator, since renters cannot fit solar. — Expat.com forum "Arrived in Diego Suarez – Recommendations for a Generator?", ~2025-12 to 2026-01, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.water] French Wikipedia estimates daily water demand at 24,000 m³ against about 18,000 m³ supplied by treatment plants (undated figure). — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Tuk-tuks ("bajaj") have largely replaced pousse-pousses and taxis since about 2010; Arrachart airport has domestic flights (two a week by Tsaradia per Wikipedia); taxi-brousse from the capital takes 26–36 hours in the dry season. — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [health.care] Forum users say a Diego dentist has an X-ray unit and autoclave, but implants are not realistic locally. — Expat.com forum "Recherche bon dentiste sur Diego, Nosy Be", c. 2019 (OLD), forum, confidence: low
- [family.schools] Lycée Français Sadi-Carnot, the Alliance française, the University of Antsiranana (1976, including a medical faculty) and the IST technical institute (1992) are in the city. — Wikipedia "Antsiranana" + Wikipedia FR, accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] On 26 Sept 2025 six people, including a student, were reportedly killed and about thirty injured in clashes with police during the national protests. — Wikipedia "2025 Malagasy protests" (citing Midi Madagasikara 2025-09-27), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [community.expat] France closed its chancellery in 2015 and keeps only an honorary consulate; about 1,200 French nationals are registered in northern Madagascar. — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [social.culture] French Wikipedia describes a cosmopolitan city: Antakarana and Sakalava people, Comorians, Mahorais, Arabs, Indians, Yemenis, Chinese and Europeans; churches and mosques (Bambao, Chadouli). — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] Dry tropical climate: about 942 mm of rain and 3,039 hours of sun a year, wet season December–March, daily highs about 29–32 °C. — Wikipedia "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
- [climate.daily] A strong easterly trade wind, the varatraza, blows through the long dry season (April–November) and can reach 22 m/s. — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [nature.access] Montagne d'Ambre national park (rainforest) is about an hour away; the Emerald Sea, Ramena beach, the Three Bays and Nosy Lonjo are nearby. — Wikipedia FR "Antsiranana", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: medium
