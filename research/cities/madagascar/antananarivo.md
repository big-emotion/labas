# Antananarivo, Madagascar

> Curated 2026-11-14. Raw items for Là-bas — not published text. Testimonies are anecdotes, not data.

Currency note: EUR equivalents use the dossier rate **1 EUR ≈ 4,500 MGA** (implied by Expat.com's rent tables, see `research/countries/madagascar.md`), rounded. OpenShores gives prices in USD only; they are kept in USD and not converted (the dossier gives no USD rate). Listing prices are asking prices seen on 2026-11-14; Expat.com forum dates are relative ("5 months ago") and converted to an approximate month. The session web-search budget ran out early, so most sources were reached by direct fetch.

- [housing.areas] Expat.com's housing guide names Ambatobe (near the French lycée, pricey), Ivandry (spacious, high rents, peak-hour traffic), Ivato/Ambohibao (big gardens, airport traffic) and Ambohipo/Ambatoroka (noticeably cheaper). — Expat.com "Se loger à Antananarivo", 2015-05-06 (OLD), company, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Ivandry, Ankorondrano, Ambatobe and Isoraka/Andohalo (colonial houses) are named as residential or expat-favoured areas. — Cyril Jarnias, 2025-03-04, blog, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] Forum users list low-lying districts (Anosibe, Isotry, Andavamamba, 67 ha, Andravoahangy among others) as flood-prone and advise families to choose by commute time to school. — Expat.com forum "Quels quartiers recommanderiez-vous", c. 2022, forum, confidence: low
- [housing.areas] About 40% of the capital's housing is old and dilapidated (Orsec estimate); about fifty houses collapsed or were damaged in the 2025–26 rainy season, mostly in hillside "red zones" of the 2nd, 4th and 6th arrondissements. — L'Express de Madagascar "Risque d'effondrement des maisons", 2026-11 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [housing.search] Renters' advice from 2026 threads: visit on a rainy day, test every outlet with breakers off to detect meter bypasses, photograph the property in the lease, and fix rent in ariary with indexation. — Expat.com forum "Location à Madagascar : vos pires expériences logement", ~2026-09 to 2026-11, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Taxi-be fares are 600–800 MGA a leg (≈ €0.13–0.18); cooperatives must notify the city 15 days before any fare change. — L'Express de Madagascar, 2026-10 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] On Line E, passengers say crews charge a second 600 MGA fare if they ride past the announced terminus; taxi-be also enter the banned city centre. — L'Express de Madagascar "L'anarchie gagne les rues de Tanà", 2026-03 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Taxi-be have nearly 70% modal share: about 7,000 minibuses on roughly 100 routes carrying nearly 1.5 million passengers (PMUD 2024). — CODATU, 2025-09-18, academic, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Wikipedia gives a smaller fleet: about 4,400 minibuses on 90 routes carrying about 700,000 passengers a day. — Wikipedia "Antananarivo", accessed 2026-11-14, intl-media, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Ride-hailing cars cost about 5,000 MGA/km (≈ €1.10) with a 25,000 MGA minimum (≈ €5.60) on some trips; app moto-taxis about 3,000 MGA/km, 10,000 minimum. No app is named. — L'Express de Madagascar, 2025-04 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Of about 7,800 registered city taxis, only about half still run, squeezed by moto- and bicycle-taxis. — L'Express de Madagascar "Les taxi-villes en perte de vitesse", 2026-05 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] More than 3,000 moto-taxis operate though they have never been formally regulated; the city proposed a 320,000 MGA annual licence, drivers countered with 50,000. — L'Express de Madagascar, 2025-09 (URL date), local-media, confidence: medium
- [daily.transport] Daily commutes take two to three hours; the World Bank puts congestion's cost at about $40 million a year and counts over 500 km of the city's roads in flood-prone zones. — Midi Madagasikara via AllAfrica, 2026-01-23, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] During December 2025 floods, people paid 500–1,000 MGA to be carried across water ("taxi-olona") or 700–1,000 MGA to rent rubber boots in Besarety, Andravoahangy and Tsaralalana. — Midi Madagasikara via AllAfrica, 2025-12-13, local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.transport] A forum user paid 70,000 MGA (≈ €15.50) for a taxi from Ivato airport to the bus station. — Expat.com forum "Prix d'un taxi conventionné Ivato", ~2026-06, forum, confidence: low
- [daily.transport] Intercity: taxi-brousse Antananarivo–Mahajanga capped at 70,000 MGA in high season (≈ €15.50); Cotisse coaches 65,000 (Lite) to 125,000 (VIP). — L'Express de Madagascar "Taxi-brousse", 2026-06 (URL date), local-media, confidence: high
- [daily.power] OpenShores says power cuts are frequent and can last hours, water is not always available, and a generator and water filter are close to essential. — OpenShores "Avantages et inconvénients d'Antananarivo", 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low

- [business.admin] OpenShores says work permits, vehicle registration and bank-account opening can take weeks and that corruption persists in some services. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
- [health.care] FCDO: public and private hospitals in Antananarivo handle only routine operations; complex surgery means evacuation to Mauritius, South Africa or Réunion. — UK FCDO health page, accessed 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [health.care] Private care (USD): GP $15–30; specialist $25–50; standard blood tests $10–20; international insurance $80–200/month. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low

- [safety.general] FCDO: keep car doors locked and windows closed, particularly in Antananarivo; occasional kidnappings for ransom mostly target Malagasy people seen as wealthy. — UK FCDO safety page, current at 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] FCDO advises against multi-passenger taxi vans because of robberies and frequent road deaths; use pre-booked taxis. — UK FCDO safety page, current at 2026-11-14, official, confidence: high
- [safety.general] On 25 Sept 2025 looters hit shops, a bank, a hotel and cable-car stations; a 7 pm–5 am curfew followed, relaxed to 8 pm–4 am on 29 Sept. — Wikipedia "2025 Malagasy protests" (citing AP 2025-09-25 and Midi Madagasikara 2025-09-30), intl-media, confidence: medium
- [safety.general] France Diplomatie says public order has been calm in Tana for several months and asks for extra vigilance around 25 September, the anniversary of the protests. — France Diplomatie, updated 2026-11-13, official, confidence: high

- [nature.access] Weekend trips named: Andasibe, Ranomafana and the Anja reserve. — OpenShores, 2026-04-13, company, confidence: low
